The Commuter Problem — Solved

The average commute is 27 minutes each way. That's nearly an hour a day of potential learning time — if you use it right. The problem? Most podcast episodes run 45–90 minutes. You start listening, realize it's not what you thought, and switch. You waste 10 minutes just figuring out what to play.

PodBrief fixes this by giving you the full picture before you press play. Read the summary, decide in 2 minutes, then commit to the listen knowing it'll be worth it. It's how the best commuters stay ahead of the curve.

Browse summaries from your favorite shows, discover new ones by topic, or just scroll the daily feed. PodBrief is the pre-commute habit that makes the commute itself 10x better.
