The Indicator from Planet Money

Why are veterinarian bills getting so ruff on the wallet?

Key Takeaways

  • The integration of practical and scientific knowledge spurred significant technological advancements and economic growth.
  • A nation's openness to change and institutional adaptability are crucial for sustained economic development.
  • Technological innovation, despite facing resistance from established interests, drives progress and addresses global challenges.

Deep Dive

  • Nobel laureates Philippe Aghion, Peter Howitt, and Oliver Williamson are recognized for their contributions to understanding economic growth and innovation.
  • The discussion focuses on Joel Mokyr's economic history of the European Enlightenment.

  • Medieval Europe experienced limited progress, characterized by hard labor, food scarcity, and high infant mortality.
  • Knowledge during medieval times was categorized into 'prescriptive' practical skills and 'propositional' scientific laws, which were largely separate.
  • The Scientific Revolution, influenced by figures like Francis Bacon, fostered the integration of these two knowledge types through empirical testing.
  • This integration led to technological advancements and more persuasive communication of new ideas.

  • Joel Mokyr highlights a nation's openness to change as crucial for sustained economic growth.
  • Established powers often resist new technologies and changes, hindering progress.
  • The United Kingdom's parliamentary evolution in 1800 is cited as an example of increased openness.

  • Economic growth driven by technology is not guaranteed and remains vulnerable to external changes.
  • The application of technology, whether constructive or destructive, determines its ultimate outcome.
  • Joel emphasizes that technological innovation is crucial for addressing major global challenges like climate change and demographic shifts.
  • Continued investment in invention is urged to tackle future challenges.
