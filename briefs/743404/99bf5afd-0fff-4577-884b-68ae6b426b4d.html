Why Trump resurrected the Monroe Doctrine

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump is reinterpreting the 200-year-old Monroe Doctrine as the "Donroe Doctrine."
  • The "Donroe Doctrine" seeks to reassert U.S. influence in the Western Hemisphere for strategic interests.
  • U.S. actions in the Americas are driven by desires for resource control and managing oil prices.
  • This policy reflects a shift towards spheres of influence and a more interventionist regional role for the U.S.

Deep Dive

  • The Trump administration's actions in Venezuela are a deliberate callback to the 200-year-old Monroe Doctrine.
  • This reinterpreted policy, now dubbed the "Donroe Doctrine," dictates global power influence in the Western Hemisphere.
  • The reinterpretation is notably influenced by President Trump's specific interest in oil resources.

  • The "Donroe Doctrine" reflects the Trump administration's shift from global cooperation to establishing spheres of influence.
  • This policy partly responds to China's growing economic influence in regions such as Latin America.
  • The White House explicitly stated goals include discouraging mass migration, combating criminal groups, and securing critical supply chains.

  • Bloomberg columnist Javier Blas suggests U.S. actions in Venezuela are driven by President Trump's desire for resource control under an "American security umbrella."
  • Trump's long-standing obsession with low oil prices is identified as a motivator to keep inflation and interest rates low.
  • The White House officially states its motivation is to stop the influx of drugs and criminals.

  • The "Donroe Doctrine"'s security umbrella over the Americas covers 40% of the world's oil production.
  • This approach insulates the U.S. from geopolitical fallout, allowing for more assertive foreign policy by mitigating oil price spikes, a constraint present since the 1970s.
  • Instances of U.S. influence include interventions in Venezuela, discussions about controlling the Panama Canal, and exploring the acquisition of Greenland and Canada.
