The Indicator from Planet Money

A school cellphone ban study, white collar jobs wither, and spooky candy prices

Key Takeaways

  • AI is contributing to white-collar job reductions at major companies.
  • Cell phone bans in schools show a small boost in student test scores.
  • Halloween candy prices significantly increased this year, fueled by tariffs.
  • Some cell phone ban policies correlate with higher student suspensions.

Deep Dive

  • Amazon cut 14,000 corporate jobs, citing AI for innovation and leaner operations.
  • Companies like Salesforce, Accenture, and Goldman Sachs are also announcing layoffs.
  • St. Louis Fed research suggests AI contributes to knowledge worker displacement.

  • Florida's 2023 statewide cell phone ban resulted in a 1.1 percentile boost in student test scores.
  • Researchers used cell tower data to compare schools with varying reductions in phone usage.
  • Schools with greater reductions in phone use also saw increased student suspensions.

  • The average price of Halloween candy rose by 10.8% compared to the previous year.
  • Tariffs on cocoa products are a contributing factor to the price increases.
  • Tootsie Roll Pops saw a 34% price hike, while Mars products increased by 12%.
