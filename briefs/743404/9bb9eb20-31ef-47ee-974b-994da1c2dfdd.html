The Indicator from Planet Money

Vice Series: The evolving business of crime

Key Takeaways

  • Crime has shifted from traditional rackets to advanced digital schemes.
  • Modern criminal methods exploit technologies like deepfakes and AI.
  • "The Indicator" will feature a week-long series on crime's business evolution.

Deep Dive

  • The episode opens with Detective Billy Hull addressing a case from an earlier era.
  • The case involved a plea for help concerning involvement in the "numbers racket."
  • Mob threats were also a central component of the traditional criminal activity.

  • The current crime landscape includes synthetic drugs and widespread data breaches.
  • Deepfakes are utilized to create convincing voice scams, targeting relatives for money.
  • AI bots demonstrate capabilities for collusion within financial markets.

  • Detective Hull agrees to help, but first must listen to five podcast episodes.
  • This introduces "The Indicator from Planet Money's" week-long series.
  • The series will explore the evolving business of crime in detail.
