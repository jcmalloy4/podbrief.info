'The View' Hosts Jaws Drop as Zohran Mamdani Accidentally Reveals How Radical He Really Is

Key Takeaways

  • Zohran Mamdani doubled down on extreme rhetoric regarding an ICE shooting, sparking debate on agency abolition.
  • Ilhan Omar's rhetoric linking white men to public safety threats drew criticism amid financial investigation reports.
  • Donald Trump criticized Don Lemon's protest participation and delivered a blistering speech at the World Economic Forum.
  • Trump declared globalism dead at Davos, emphasizing national sovereignty and an "America First" model.
  • The strategic importance of Greenland to US national security was highlighted by Donald Trump, advocating for US control.

Deep Dive

  • Zohran Mamdani appeared on "The View" doubling down on rhetoric about the ICE shooting of Renee Nicole Macklin Good, labeling it a murder.
  • The host refuted Mamdani's murder claim, citing video evidence and defining murder as unlawful, premeditated killing.
  • Mamdani supports abolishing ICE, believing it "terrorizes" people without fulfilling its duties or considering immigration law.
  • The host argued that ICE operates within its legal mandate, established by the Homeland Security Act of 2002.

  • The host criticized comments on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI), suggesting it could lead to systemic racism.
  • Representative Jasmine Crockett's claim of recent lynchings was challenged, with the host citing no recorded instances in 2025 according to Google.
  • Arguments that Black people and women cannot obtain identification were critiqued as a tactic for voter suppression.
  • The host argued that past claims of widespread police brutality against Black people were false, citing figures like Larry Elder.

  • Ilhan Omar's statement calling white men the biggest threat to public safety drew strong criticism from the host.
  • A Fox News report detailed an investigation into Omar's finances, showing her family's net worth increased from under $100,000 to $30 million.
  • This financial surge was linked to a reported $19 billion social services fraud scandal in Minnesota, questioning if it was merit-based.
  • Donald Trump publicly addressed these issues, expressing a desire for certain individuals to leave Minnesota, calling the state corrupt.

  • Donald Trump criticized Don Lemon for participating in an ICE protest inside a Minneapolis church during mass, describing him as a "loser."
  • The host deemed Lemon's actions a violation of a place of worship and a potential criminal act, displaying an image showing parishioners and children.
  • Polymarket users indicated 26% believed Don Lemon would face criminal charges for his actions.
  • Ellen DeGeneres expressed support for anti-ICE protesters in Minneapolis, though the host questioned her perceived hypocrisy regarding undocumented workers on her properties.

  • The World Economic Forum meeting in Davos was criticized for its globalist agenda, framed as undermining sovereign nations and advocating for a top-down world order.
  • California Governor Gavin Newsom's appearance at the WEF was interpreted as encouraging European opposition to Donald Trump and American interests.
  • Donald Trump's speech at Davos was presented as a counterforce, arguing against globalism and offering help to states like California to reduce crime by deporting criminals.
  • The host criticized European Union policies, citing Hungary's daily payment of 1 million Euros to the EU for maintaining closed borders.

  • The host argued that the U.S. provides global security, contrasting this with leaders like Emmanuel Macron and Mark Carney.
  • Carney's view of a 'rupture' in global integration was criticized, attributed to decades of lopsided trade deals that benefited other countries at the U.S.'s expense.
  • The speaker warned that if the U.S. withdraws its global influence, China is poised to step in.
  • French President Emmanuel Macron's appeal to China for foreign investment and technology transfer was noted.

  • Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick stated at the World Economic Forum that globalization failed the West and the U.S., advocating for an 'America First' model.
  • Lutnick prioritized domestic workers and industries, criticizing the offshoring of essential production like medicine and semiconductors.
  • The discussion referenced Yoram Hazzoni's book 'The Virtue of Nationalism,' which explains that global community participation requires individual nations to first manage their own affairs.
  • The host noted Europe's reliance on China for batteries in its pursuit of net-zero emissions by 2030, highlighting issues of national dependency.

  • Donald Trump articulated Greenland's vital strategic importance to US national security at the World Economic Forum.
  • Trump asserted Greenland's geographical position is critical to counter Russian and Chinese influence in the Arctic.
  • He suggested U.S. control of Greenland would enhance its security, referencing a 'golden dome' defense concept.
  • Mark Carney's response to the proposal was characterized as 'word salad,' acknowledging Russia as a threat in the Arctic but criticizing Canada's perceived inaction.

  • A map was discussed to illustrate Greenland's size and proximity to Canada and the United States, highlighting its strategic position relative to Denmark.
  • The host criticized Denmark's commitment to Greenland's defense, stating they spent only a fraction of their promised $200 million.
  • Scott Besson argued for the U.S. to acquire Greenland, citing potential threats from Russia and China in the Arctic and questioning Denmark's custodianship.
  • Donald Trump reiterated that Greenland is crucial for national and world security, and the U.S. must maintain peace through strength.
