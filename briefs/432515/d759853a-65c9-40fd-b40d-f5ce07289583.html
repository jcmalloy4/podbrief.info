Host Goes Quiet as Press Sec Destroys Jimmy Kimmel Narrative w/ Facts in Under 1 Minute

Key Takeaways

  • White House Press Secretary Leavitt clarified ABC's decision on Jimmy Kimmel was independent.
  • Charlie Kirk was an instrumental early investor and advocate for Rumble's free speech mission.
  • Bill Maher condemned rhetoric implying Charlie Kirk's responsibility for a death.
  • The host criticized perceived media hypocrisy and mainstream journalistic practices.
  • Charlie Kirk's memorial event was a massive gathering akin to a political rally.
  • "Wokeism" was characterized as a "mind virus" impacting secular liberal values.

Deep Dive

  • The host discussed media fallout from Jimmy Kimmel's joke about Charlie Kirk's death, noting Kimmel's alleged refusal to apologize and subsequent show cancellation.
  • White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt stated ABC executives made the decision regarding Kimmel's show, not the White House.
  • The host expressed belief in Leavitt's account, citing no evidence of government pressure, contrasting it with alleged Biden administration tech collusion.

  • Rumble CEO Chris Pavlovsky highlighted Charlie Kirk's early and active involvement as an initial investor in April 2021, alongside JD Vance and Vivek Ramaswamy.
  • Kirk was instrumental in helping Rumble find its current headquarters in Sarasota, Florida, envisioning it as the 'free speech capital of the world.'
  • Kirk's commitment to Rumble's free speech mission extended beyond typical investor contributions, and the company credits him for its current standing.
  • The CEO emphasized Kirk's significant, often unknown, impact on Rumble, especially when it was a primary free speech platform.

  • The host expressed irritation with perceived hypocrisy regarding free speech, citing the ABC Jimmy Kimmel situation compared to the Trump administration.
  • It was argued that ABC changing talent is not a free speech violation, unlike the deplatforming of a president.
  • Democrats were criticized for decrying free speech violations while media companies like Fox News and CNN have also removed talent.
  • A meme comparing reactions to Roseanne Barr and Jimmy Kimmel situations was referenced regarding accountability.

  • The host described attending Charlie Kirk's memorial service at a large football stadium, noting significant traffic and numerous American flags and signs.
  • The atmosphere was compared to an 'old school Trump rally,' with visual elements and religious undertones.
  • Notable attendees included Sage Steele, Elon Musk, Bobby Kennedy, Tulsi Gabbard, and Dan Bongino, leading to a 'Muppet show' comparison.
  • The event underscored Charlie Kirk's unique significance and impact.

  • The host affirmed that genuine religiosity is possible without belonging to an organized institution, though community aspects are beneficial.
  • "Wokeism" was characterized as a "mind virus" or parasitic entity, contrasting with the historically beneficial secular liberal world.
  • It was suggested that Enlightenment values, potentially underpinned by Judeo-Christian principles, were compromised by prioritizing "tolerance" excessively.

  • The host criticized CNN for rehiring figures like Brian Stelter and Jeffrey Toobin, citing their past actions and subsequent media roles.
  • It was argued that mainstream media outlets retain such figures because they generate discussion, regardless of journalistic merit.
  • This practice was contrasted with the host's online platform, which claims success by providing value to its audience.
