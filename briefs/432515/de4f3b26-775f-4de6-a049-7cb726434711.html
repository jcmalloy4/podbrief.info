Assassination Attempt Lies & the Ugly Attacks to Take Down My Family | Eric Trump

Key Takeaways

  • Eric Trump's book, "Under Siege," documents the family's perceived coordinated legal and media attacks.
  • The Trump family relocated their organization to Florida, citing New York's political climate and legal challenges.
  • Eric Trump asserts the Trump Organization is now stronger than ever, despite numerous investigations and legal actions.
  • The guest claims mainstream media's influence has diminished, contributing to Donald Trump's political effectiveness.
  • Eric Trump suggested the 2020 election outcome ultimately benefited Republicans, leading to gains in Congress and the Supreme Court.

Deep Dive

  • Eric Trump's book "Under Siege: My Family's Fight to Save Our Nation" reached number one in multiple categories.
  • The book's premise is to counter what the family describes as coordinated efforts, including impeachments, the Russia investigation, social media deplatforming, and numerous subpoenas, to destroy their reputation and business.
  • The guest compares his family's situation to that of Hunter Biden, highlighting perceived differential treatment.
  • The title refers to incidents at Butler and a Palm Beach golf course, which Eric Trump suggests were attempts to harm his father and eliminate independent voices.

  • The Trump family's move to Florida was prompted by New York Attorney General Letitia James's campaign promise to sue them, which Eric Trump described as efforts to bankrupt and de-platform the Trump Organization.
  • Eric Trump stated moving to Florida was "the best decision he ever made," calling it a "center of freedom" and contrasting it with his perception of New York City potentially becoming a "communist nightmare."
  • The guest recalled Donald Trump's warning to expect to learn who their true friends were at the start of his political career.

  • Eric Trump acknowledged "so many" moments of doubt regarding the Trump Organization's survival amidst legal actions.
  • He recounted daily presence in New York courtrooms for what he describes as "sham proceedings," stating journalists recognized them as such and that convictions were later overturned.
  • A $600 million penalty imposed on the family was reversed by appellate courts, with banks attesting to their good borrowing history.
  • Eric Trump expressed anger about the "Butler" incident, questioning the investigation's focus by contrasting it with the scrutiny given to January 6th protesters.

  • Eric Trump questioned the legitimacy of the Biden family's business activities, contrasting them with the Trump family's transparent operations in the hotel, golf, and real estate sectors.
  • He suggested that mainstream media has lost influence and credibility due to the rise of independent voices like Dave Rubin, Joe Rogan, and Donald Trump.
  • Eric Trump claimed that the diminished power of traditional media outlets has altered the Democratic Party's strategy, preventing narratives like the Russia collusion story from gaining traction as they did in 2016.
  • He characterized the lack of scrutiny on Hunter Biden's art sales as a "greatest cover-up," implying it was a means to gain access to his father.

  • Eric Trump asserts that Donald Trump is now more effective due to the media's loss of influence and a perceived weakness in the Democratic Party.
  • He reflected on the Trump family's initial unfamiliarity with political processes in 2017 but highlighted their ability to connect with voters through family unity and work ethic.
  • Eric Trump stated that he and his father have matured politically, learning to choose their battles and gaining confidence.
  • He expressed skepticism about the 2020 election results, questioning how Joe Biden could receive more votes than Barack Obama.
  • He suggested that losing the 2020 election was ultimately beneficial, leading to a Republican majority in the House and Senate, a 6-3 Supreme Court majority, and a strong cabinet.

  • Eric Trump asserted that the Trump Organization is now at its strongest point, despite facing what he described as coordinated attacks from banks, attorneys general, and subpoenas aimed at destroying their reputation and business.
  • He noted the business had to adapt to political polarization, with roughly 50% of the country supporting his father while other segments boycotted their products and services.
  • The guest expressed nostalgia for the "electric vibe" of the Trump Organization's hotel in Washington D.C. during his father's first term, describing it as a hub for conservative thought.
  • He stated hopes to return to D.C. with a similar hospitality venture in the future.
