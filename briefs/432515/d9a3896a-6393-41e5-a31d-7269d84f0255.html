The Rubin Report

Listen to ‘The View’ Crowd Gasp as Host Starts Lying to Her Co-Hosts Faces

Key Takeaways

  • Immigration enforcement is accelerating with ICE operations targeting illegal immigrants nationwide, while mysterious drops in fentanyl seizures suggest Trump's border policies are already having impact before he takes office
  • Political realignment is underway as Trump successfully expanded the Republican base by creating a sense of belonging and community, while Democrats increasingly alienate voters through divisive messaging focused on race and gender rather than practical solutions
  • Mental health correlates with political affiliation, with conservatives showing significantly better mental health outcomes according to recent data, suggesting a deeper philosophical divide between reality-based versus emotion-driven worldviews
  • Urban governance failures are becoming more apparent as Democratic-led cities struggle with basic competency issues, from overpaid municipal executives to flawed policing databases, driving migration patterns toward more sensibly-governed areas
  • Spiritual and philosophical awakening is emerging as people seek truth and meaning beyond politics, with emphasis on personal responsibility and collective action guided by foundational principles rather than progressive narratives

Deep Dive

Setting and Historical Context

  • Dave Rubin broadcasts from Jerusalem, Israel, specifically from a rooftop with a view of the Western Wall, describing Jerusalem as the holiest city on the planet with significant religious sites for Judaism, Islam, and Christianity
  • Historical overview of Israel's formation: After the British mandate of Palestine, Jews accepted the land partition plan while Arab armies rejected it; Jerusalem was controlled by Jordan from 1948-1967, during which Jews were not allowed to pray in the city
  • Current tensions: Discussion occurs against the backdrop of ongoing conflict and challenges facing Israel, with references to tensions since the October 7th attack

Immigration and Law Enforcement

  • Recent anti-Semitic incidents in the US: Highlights a specific case of an illegal immigrant who committed a firebombing against Jewish Americans, with the White House announcing the suspect's family is in ICE custody for potential deportation
  • Muslim Brotherhood designation: Senator Ted Cruz plans to reintroduce the Muslim Brotherhood Terrorist Designation Act, with support for designating the group as a terrorist organization, claiming they aim to "destroy" host countries rather than create Palestine
  • ICE operations and deportations:
- Discussion of ongoing ICE arrests of undocumented immigrants across the country - Emphasis that enforcement targets illegal immigrants, not legal citizens - Current approach described as giving people opportunity to "self-deport" - Critique of Anna Navarro's comments on The View about deportations affecting the Latino community

  • Border security developments:
- Washington Post reported mysterious drop in fentanyl seizures at US-Mexico border, attributed to Trump's border policies - Claims of 2 million "gotaways" crossing the border, described as a national security risk - Tom Homan's perspective that Biden administration's border policy created "the biggest national security vulnerability"

Political Dynamics and Criticism

  • Democratic opposition tactics: Introduction of the "TACO" meme (Trump Always Chickens Out) by Democrats, suggesting Trump doesn't follow through on tariff threats, which the speaker strongly disputes by citing Trump's resilience and achievements
  • Trump's political appeal:
- Successfully expanded Republican party's base through sense of belonging (rallies, red hats, slogans) - Attracts younger voters through both policies and entertainment value - Understanding of group dynamics and community building - Drew support from former Democrats

  • Critique of Democratic messaging:
- Accused of alienating young men through rhetoric about race and gender - Campaign messaging focused on calling opponents "racists" and "misogynists" - References Jordan Peterson's commentary on Democrats' approach to masculinity

  • Specific political figures criticized:
- Calls for Ilhan Omar's removal from Congress, with accusations of illegal marriage and terrorist connections - Criticism of Hakeem Jeffries and Democrats' stance on ICE agents - Eric Swalwell criticized over Iran negotiations and past Chinese spy controversy

Election Aftermath and Mental Health

  • 2020 election reaction: Speakers describe Trump's victory as bringing profound relief rather than pure joy, preventing potential negative scenarios under a Kamala Harris presidency including open borders and increased "woke" cultural influences
  • Mental health and political affiliation: Reference to Nate Silver's data suggesting conservatives are up 31 points among people with "excellent mental health," while Democrats are increasingly associated with poor mental health
  • Philosophical divide: Framed as "common sense and reality versus neuroses and craziness," with conservatives having stable belief systems grounded in reality versus more fluid left-leaning perspectives

Urban Governance and Free Speech

  • Los Angeles leadership criticism:
- Critique of Janice Quinones, DWP executive earning $750,000 annually - Mayor Karen Bass's defense of the salary as competitive nationally - Discussion of water reservoir issues and municipal management

  • New York City policing: Features NYC Councilwoman Althea Stevens discussing NYPD's gang database, suggesting it's fundamentally flawed due to racist origins
  • Free speech concerns: Discussion of threats against ICE agents, with bystander comments about putting "a bullet in their heads" raising questions about free speech violations and normalization of violence

Economic and Social Grievances

  • Economic stagnation: Bottom 20% of population experiencing wage stagnation amid concerns about government spending on EV subsidies and green initiatives
  • Educational system critique: Concerns that educational systems aren't providing necessary skills, contributing to broader government incompetence and unfairness
  • Migration patterns: Observation of "sane people" migrating from blue states/cities, with people "waking up" and pushing back against perceived progressive narratives

Philosophical and Spiritual Reflections

  • Jordan Peterson's influence: Discussion of understanding God through transforming oneself, embracing responsibility, and serving others, with reference to Nietzsche's "God is dead" concept
  • Truth and meaning: Emphasis on orienting oneself towards truth as a path to building a better world, returning to foundational principles established by the founders
  • Divine presence in Jerusalem: Speaker reflects on experiencing what feels like divine presence or strange coincidences in the holy city
  • Call for collective action: Themes include societal honesty and transparency, rejecting past deceptions, and belief that collective effort can address serious global issues and create meaningful change, potentially with guidance from a "higher authority" beyond political solutions

Conclusion

  • Practical note: Promotion of live show in Israel with tickets available at DaveRubin.com/events, as the speaker prepares to walk through Jerusalem's old city
