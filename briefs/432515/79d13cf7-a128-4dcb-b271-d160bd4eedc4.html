Watch Joe Rogan's Face as Elon Musk Exposes How Dems Are Cheating in Plain Sight

Key Takeaways

  • California's Proposition 50 is scrutinized for potential gerrymandering and its impact on congressional seats.
  • Elon Musk suggests census population counts, including non-citizens, drive Democratic electoral strategy.
  • The government shutdown is linked to partisan disagreements over funding for immigrant-related programs.
  • Donald Trump's administration defends ICE actions and criticizes liberal judicial and mayoral policies.
  • Concerns are raised about Democratic border security failures and increasing Southwest border encounters.
  • Kamala Harris's past presidential campaign and her relationship with Joe Biden are critically analyzed.
  • New York City's sanctuary city policies and its mayoral race candidates are discussed amid federal funding concerns.
  • Barack Obama's recent campaigning and the perceived direction of the Democratic Party face criticism.

Deep Dive

  • Elon Musk detailed how California's Proposition 50 could enable the legislature to redraw congressional districts for the 2026, 2028, and 2030 elections.
  • Musk explained the U.S. census counts all persons, not just citizens, influencing congressional seat apportionment and electoral college votes.
  • He argued this system incentivizes Democrats to encourage immigration to increase population counts, potentially flipping five congressional seats in California.
  • Arnold Schwarzenegger criticized Prop 50 as a 'scam' for political manipulation, while Gavin Newsom defended it as a temporary measure.

  • The host questioned Gavin Newsom about his presidential aspirations.
  • Newsom denied wanting to be president, stating his dislike for politicians who lie.
  • He clarified his comments were made in response to others.
  • The discussion criticized politicians who lie and shift focus to presidential aspirations, with one speaker calling a politician a 'lizard person'.

  • Elon Musk suggested the five-week government shutdown was linked to a Democratic strategy to attract and retain voters through financial incentives for illegal immigrants.
  • The host claimed the standoff required only seven Senate Democrats to end it, implying internal party division.
  • It was estimated that around 30 million illegal immigrants are in the US, with Democrats allegedly maintaining their support via government assistance.
  • Donald Trump, in an interview, stated he would not be extorted by Democrats regarding shutdown demands for funding programs for undocumented immigrants.

  • Donald Trump defended ICE's actions as necessary to remove criminals, attributing hindrances to liberal judges.
  • He criticized Democratic mayors for perceived failures in city cleanup and asserted his administration's success in crime reduction.
  • A group of tenants in Rogers Park, Chicago, protested rent hikes and unsafe conditions, linking issues to increased ICE activity.
  • Minnesota politician Omar Fateh allegedly planned to warn undocumented immigrants about impending ICE raids, an action deemed illegal by the speaker.

  • Chamath Palihapitiya of the "All In Podcast" stated he felt safer under a Donald Trump presidency than a Biden presidency regarding immigration policies.
  • John Fetterman acknowledged the Democratic Party's failure in border security and expressed support for securing the border.
  • The host criticized Democrats for being hesitant to voice border concerns, leading to public confusion.
  • A chart was presented showing a significant increase in Southwest border encounters, particularly under the Biden administration.

  • George Clooney suggested Kamala Harris made a mistake by having to run against her own record during her presidential campaign.
  • Clooney implied a primary battle might have been more beneficial for the Democratic Party.
  • The host referenced a video of Joe Biden appearing disoriented at a fundraiser hosted by Clooney, implying Clooney's responsibility.

  • Kamala Harris, during an interview on "Diary of a CEO," discussed whether Joe Biden wanted her to win the election.
  • She recounted a call from then-President Biden before a debate, feeling he focused on negative comments about her rather than offering support.
  • Harris expressed feeling angry and disappointed by Biden's perceived self-centered motivation.
  • The host suggested this reaction highlighted why she might not have been suited for the presidency.

  • New York City's sanctuary city policy was criticized, with one speaker urging residents to leave and stating that coordination with ICE is akin to capitulating to Donald Trump's agenda.
  • Donald Trump commented on "60 Minutes" about Zorhan Mamdami, described as a 34-year-old communist and potential NYC mayor.
  • Trump stated he would not send federal money to a city run by a communist, preferring a "bad Democrat" like Andrew Cuomo.
  • It was predicted that if Mamdami maintains NYC as a sanctuary city and refuses ICE cooperation, it would lead to increased violence.

  • Barack Obama's recent speech was discussed, with the speaker claiming Obama has "lost his juice" while campaigning against Donald Trump.
  • Obama endorsed Virginia gubernatorial candidate Abigail Spanberger, and was noted to be campaigning against Winsome Sears, a Black woman.
  • The host criticized Obama's use of race in politics, contrasting past statements with his current stance.
  • The speaker questioned the direction of the Democratic Party, suggesting it has become "crazy."
