The Simple Rules to Fight Crime Blue Cities Choose to Ignore | Jay Collins

Key Takeaways

  • Florida actively evacuated citizens from war zones, including Israel, under Governor DeSantis's leadership.
  • Lt. Governor Collins, a Green Beret, details his combat injury and successful re-qualification after leg amputation.
  • Florida's conservative governance is presented as a model for policy, particularly regarding COVID-19 response.
  • The state prioritizes illegal immigration control, property tax relief, and strengthening law enforcement.
  • Florida addresses insurance market challenges through tort reform, leading to stabilizing rates.
  • Lt. Governor Collins is considering a run for higher office in 2026 to continue Governor DeSantis's legacy.

Deep Dive

  • Collins joined the military after 9/11, serving as a Green Beret in various global conflicts.
  • He suffered injuries from a mortar attack, leading to the amputation of his leg in January 2014.
  • Collins re-qualified as a Green Beret within a year, requiring him to relearn fundamental skills with a prosthetic leg.
  • His experience contributed to a policy shift, enabling one-legged Green Berets to serve in the Army.

  • Florida's positive trajectory is attributed to Governor DeSantis's leadership and moral compass during COVID-19.
  • DeSantis's approach prevented economic consequences seen in other states during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • Florida passed significant conservative legislation, including school choice and parental rights, serving as a national model.

  • Florida maintains a proactive stance on illegal immigration with initiatives like 287G agreements and detention programs.
  • Property tax relief is a key policy goal, focusing on primary residences over corporate holdings.
  • The guest highlights nearly $200 million in waste found in Orange County's municipal budgets.
  • He advocates prioritizing funds for essential services such as law enforcement and infrastructure.

  • Florida aims to strengthen law enforcement, contrasting its policies with those in California regarding theft.
  • Concerns are raised about future threats like cybersecurity and drone warfare, necessitating law enforcement training and funding.
  • A legislative act supports recruiting veterans and their spouses to Florida, connecting them with opportunities.
  • The guest highlights his desire for veterans to retire and serve in Florida, including in business and politics.

  • The guest notes jealousy from other states regarding Florida's accomplishments, including educational choice and gun rights.
  • He acknowledges a challenging legislative session but commends Governor DeSantis for upholding conservative values.
  • Governor DeSantis is described as actively pursuing policy in his final term, not acting as a 'lame duck'.
  • Florida's leadership reaffirms its commitment to principles and maintaining its resolute approach.

  • Florida's insurance market faced challenges following two major hurricanes, Ian and Nicole.
  • State tort reform efforts have led to stabilizing and decreasing insurance rates.
  • Several insurance companies have returned to the Florida market as a result of these reforms.
  • Maintaining these reforms is deemed crucial to prevent future insurance rate hikes.
