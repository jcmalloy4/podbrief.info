‘The View’ Hosts Aghast When Mamdani Accidentally Repeats Hamas Talking Points

Key Takeaways

  • NYC Mayoral candidate Zohran Mamdani's 'The View' appearance faced scrutiny over his Israel stance.
  • Elon Musk highlighted Netflix trans content in a children's show, prompting subscription cancellations.
  • J.K. Rowling publicly responded to Emma Watson's perceived attempt at reconciliation regarding trans issues.
  • Scott Jennings' logical commentary on CNN was discussed for disrupting typical network format.
  • A man-on-the-street segment revealed Mamdani supporters' opposition to his radical policy positions.

Deep Dive

  • NYC Mayoral candidate Zohran Mamdani appeared on 'The View' on October 2nd, 2025, to discuss his response to Donald Trump's threats regarding federal funding for New York City.
  • Mamdani condemned Hamas and the October 7th attacks as war crimes but also labeled the situation in Gaza as a genocide due to Palestinian children killed.
  • He faced questions regarding his past alleged evasiveness in condemning Hamas and accusations of hypocrisy for criticizing Israel's response while not fully addressing Hamas's actions.
  • A post from X detailed Mamdani's alleged past actions, including refusing to sign a Holocaust resolution and comparing the Intifada to the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising.

  • Content creator Zach Sage conducted 'man-on-the-street' interviews in New York City, posing as a canvasser for Zohran Mamdani.
  • Sage presented Mamdani's policies from his website to supporters, which often led to confusion and opposition.
  • Radical policy positions, such as higher taxes on white neighborhoods and not condemning Sharia law, were met with negative reactions from many supporters.

  • Elon Musk drew attention to Netflix's children's show 'Dead End: Paranormal Park,' which is accused of promoting pro-trans ideology, leading to calls for subscription cancellations.
  • Musk reportedly reposted a meme suggesting the transgender agenda is a 'Trojan horse' for children, linking to his estranged relationship with his transgender daughter.
  • The host questioned Netflix's promotion of such content, including a documentary about trans children, particularly with auto-play features leading to unexpected material.
  • Concerns were raised about the long-term implications of gender transitions and the push of related content on platforms like Netflix.

  • J.K. Rowling responded to Emma Watson's comments expressing love and gratitude, stating she is not owed 'eternal agreement' from actors.
  • Rowling contrasted her own past struggles with Watson's privilege and noted Watson's past participation in trans-inclusive charity work like Mermaids.
  • Watson's shift in stance is suggested to be due to changing cultural opinions or a desire for future career opportunities.

  • The host and a guest discussed Scott Jennings' 'sensible commentary' on CNN, noting its disruptive effect on the network's typical format.
  • Jennings' calm demeanor and logical arguments were highlighted as making other CNN hosts appear less rational.
  • His appearances were posited to expose the mainstream media's embrace of certain narratives.

  • The host engaged in a question-and-answer session with the Rubin Report Locals community on a wide range of topics.
  • He confirmed Brock, his former social media manager, now works for the White House, expressing happiness for his success.
  • The host outlined a busy itinerary for an upcoming Australia trip, including live shows and private events, anticipating potential off days for cultural experiences.

  • A viewer asked about Zohran Mamdani's high polling numbers for NYC Mayor, prompting the host to analyze the current candidate landscape.
  • The host predicted a 'disaster' for New York and the United States if Curtis Sliwa's supporters shifted to Andrew Cuomo.
  • Reflections were shared on New York City's direction, contrasting observations after 'Charlie's assassination' with the host's early years there, questioning if he would want to return.

  • The host discussed Ron DeSantis's proposal to remove property taxes for Florida primary residences, considering its potential impacts on 'snowbirds' and the state's booming economy.
  • He responded to a question about fasting for 25 hours, noting it as a family tradition and detailing the difficulty, particularly abstaining from morning coffee.
