Crowd Roars for Cuomo’s Blistering Attack on Zohran Mamdani

Key Takeaways

  • Andrew Cuomo criticized NYC mayoral candidate Zohran Mamdani's record and policy proposals.
  • Florida Governor Ron DeSantis is advancing efforts to eliminate property taxes by November 2026.
  • An FBI investigation uncovered a major NBA gambling scandal involving point-shaving and numerous arrests.
  • A fatal DUI crash by an undocumented truck driver sparked debate on California's CDL policies.
  • A U.S. vessel successfully intercepted and sank a narco-terrorist boat carrying illegal drugs.

Deep Dive

  • Andrew Cuomo criticized Zohran Mamdani in the second NYC mayoral debate for lacking government experience and legislative accomplishments.
  • Cuomo highlighted Mamdani's inconsistent attendance in the New York State Assembly despite a significant pay raise.
  • Mamdani's proposal for 911 dispatchers to assess police response to mental health crises was deemed 'unhinged'.
  • His stance on antisemitism and the slogan 'globalize the intifada' were interpreted as calls to 'kill Jews everywhere'.
  • The host expressed concern over Mamdani potentially winning the NYC mayoral race, characterizing it as a move towards communism.

  • New York City's ranked choice voting system shaped candidate discussions and strategies.
  • Zohran Mamdani was questioned for his political strategy, including an endorsement of both himself and Curtis Sliwa.
  • A poll suggested Curtis Sliwa had a 0% chance of winning, but potential vote shifts could make the election competitive if votes move to Andrew Cuomo.
  • Bill Ackman claimed Curtis Sliwa remained in the race to benefit from New York City's 8-to-1 matching fund program.
  • Allegations suggested Sliwa and his associates were profiting from campaign funds, potentially avoiding repayment.

  • Governor Ron DeSantis is pursuing the elimination of property taxes in Florida, highlighting the state's success in education and safety.
  • This proposal could go to a public vote in November 2026, requiring 60% voter approval to pass.
  • Record tourism has increased sales tax revenue, which is presented as a means to offset property tax losses.
  • The policy aims to provide property tax relief to long-time residents affected by rising property values.

  • A major NBA gambling scandal involving point-shaving and alleged game rigging was detailed in a report.
  • FBI investigations led to over 30 arrests across 11 states, including former players and coaches Chauncey Billups, Damon Jones, and Terry Rozier.
  • Terry Rozier allegedly faked an injury to influence bets exceeding $200,000 on his under-statistics.
  • This alleged action resulted in tens of thousands of dollars in profit from rigged bets.

  • An illegal immigrant truck driver, Jashanpreet Singh, allegedly caused a fatal DUI crash in California that killed three people.
  • The incident sparked criticism of the Biden administration for releasing the individual and California for issuing commercial driver's licenses to undocumented immigrants.
  • Questions were raised regarding English proficiency standards for commercial driver's licenses issued to undocumented immigrants.
  • Democrats were criticized for not supporting federal immigration laws and ICE agents, which was linked to increased danger for law enforcement.

  • A U.S. vessel, under the command of Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, sank a narco-terrorist boat in the Eastern Pacific.
  • The intercepted boat was carrying fentanyl and methamphetamines, resulting in three fatalities.
  • Senator Corey Booker criticized the operation, citing concerns over accountability and transparency.
  • U.S. authorities track these vessels, knowing their origins and occupants, stating they are heading towards the U.S. with hostile intent.
