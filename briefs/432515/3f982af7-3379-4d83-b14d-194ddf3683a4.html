The Rubin Report

Is Joe Rogan Wrong on the Basic Facts of ICE?

Key Takeaways

  • Joe Rogan questioned ICE tactics and optics, prompting a factual counterpoint on arrest data.
  • Donald Trump addressed the Renee Good shooting, acknowledging tragedy while defending ICE actions.
  • Democrats, including Ilhan Omar and AOC, outlined plans to defund ICE or refuse engagement.
  • Joy Behar's speculation about Trump canceling midterms was critically examined by the host.
  • Donald Trump publicly condemned protester killings in Iran, indicating potential U.S. support.
  • Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman compared Iran's leader to Hitler, raising nuclear concerns.
  • New York City's universal child care initiative faced strong criticism for its implications.
  • Antisemitic protests in NYC and Australia's response to extremism highlight growing societal tensions.

Deep Dive

  • Joe Rogan expressed concern about ICE agents' appearance on city streets, likening masked agents to the Gestapo after an incident in Minneapolis.
  • Rubin countered with DHS data, stating that between 2025 and the current time, ICE made over 700,000 arrests and deportations.
  • Fewer than 0.1% of these incidents involved U.S. citizens, with no U.S. citizens ultimately deported.

  • An ICE agent fired his weapon after a woman, Renee Nicole Macklin Good, nearly drove over him, an incident that fueled unrest in Minneapolis.
  • Donald Trump, when asked by Tony Dokoupil what he would say to Good's father, stated he loves all his people and acknowledged the tragedy.
  • Trump noted the victim's actions were 'tough' and suggested the victim might have been on his side politically.

  • Ilhan Omar announced the Democrat Congressional Progressive Caucus's formal position to oppose all funding for immigration enforcement.
  • This opposition is contingent on enacting 'meaningful reforms' to end 'militarized policing practices.'
  • Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez stated she was unwilling to discuss ICE with the Trump administration, citing alleged actions against citizens.

  • Joy Behar on 'The View' speculated that Donald Trump might use recent events to cancel the upcoming midterm elections.
  • The host criticized Behar's claims as untrue, likening her audience to 'seals being fed fish.'
  • Bill O'Reilly observed that the Democrats' 'over-the-top resistance' negatively impacts public perception, even concerning incidents like Renee Good's death.

  • Donald Trump posted on Truth Social condemning the killing of protesters in Iran, canceling meetings, and stating 'Help is on the way.'
  • An image of an Iranian protester asking Trump for help was highlighted, amidst reports of over 12,000 people allegedly killed.
  • Discussion explored potential American military intervention in Iran without ground troops, drawing parallels to past actions in Venezuela.

  • Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, in a resurfaced '60 Minutes' interview, compared Iran's Ayatollah Khamenei to Hitler due to expansionist ambitions.
  • Bin Salman stated Saudi Arabia would pursue nuclear weapons if Iran developed them.
  • A report indicated at least 12,000 people have been killed in recent protests across Iran.

  • New York City announced a universal child care initiative featuring Mayor Eric Adams and Ms. Rachel.
  • The host strongly criticized the program, likening it to a 'commie jihad' and a method for 'brainwashing' a generation.
  • Ms. Rachel's past controversial statements were referenced during the critique of the initiative.

  • Protesters chanting 'we are Hamas' outside a New York synagogue were interpreted as a 'mask off moment,' revealing antisemitic intent.
  • Concerns were raised about the lack of consequences for supporting Hamas, which killed 47 Americans, contrasting with Australia's stricter hate speech laws.
  • The Australian Prime Minister's initial response to a Sydney shooting focused on gun laws rather than Islamic extremism.
  • Discussion also touched on Australia's legal immigration challenges and infrastructure strain due to high population intake.
