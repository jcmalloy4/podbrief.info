The Rubin Report

Cenk Vs. Dave, the Left Turns Violent & the Best and Worst of 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Co-hosts reflected on 2025, balancing personal successes with global concerns about political polarization.
  • Discussions highlighted the internet's role in amplifying fringe views and challenges to free speech.
  • Predictions for 2026 covered political shifts, evolving pop culture, and rapid Artificial Intelligence advancements.
  • Guests recounted their most impactful internet moments, including public confrontations and personal advocacy.

Deep Dive

  • Sage Steele recited a poem summarizing 2025 pop culture, including TikTok's continued operation.
  • The poem noted the popularity of "lababoo" dolls, celebrity space trips, and Taylor Swift's engagement to Travis Kelce.
  • "Laboo boos" were discussed as expensive collectible items, compared to Cabbage Patch Kids, featured in South Park.

  • Dave Rubin described 2025 as a personally great year with his young child, despite deep concerns for the world's state.
  • Dr. Drew Pinsky noted a return to reality post-COVID, albeit in an unusual manner.
  • Sage Steele shared she had a great year, having met and married her husband.

  • Dr. Drew Pinsky expressed concern about a perceived rise in "alt-right" positions and backsliding on LGBTQ+ rights.
  • Dave Rubin criticized the silencing of dissenting voices and discussed challenges faced by black conservatives.
  • The hosts emphasized staying true to oneself despite criticism, highlighting the value of friendships.
  • Concerns were raised about the rise of extremism, political violence on the left, and increasing identitarianism, homophobia, and antisemitism on the alt-right.

  • The discussion explored how internet architecture can centralize fringe ideas, leading them to go viral.
  • Speakers expressed gratitude for their audience and the importance of communicating with like-minded individuals.
  • Sage Steele highlighted Dave Rubin's ability to calm her during stressful public scrutiny.

  • Jillian Michaels shared an optimistic view on parenting teenagers.
  • Hosts discussed personal goals, including prioritizing health after age 50 and achieving proper squat form.
  • Predictions included the decline of oversized 'big pants' and the potential rapid fall of TikTok's influence.
  • Concerns were raised about the potential negative long-term effects of weight loss drugs leading to accelerated aging.

  • Predictions included volatile reactions from the left if Republicans win midterms, or widespread concern on the right if Trump's influence wanes.
  • A potential collapse of the European Union was suggested as a 2026 prediction.
  • The rapid and staggering evolution of Artificial Intelligence was highlighted, with applications like personalized fitness trainers.
  • One guest predicted the Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce wedding would significantly influence 2026 wedding trends.

  • Dave Rubin recounted his best internet moment: confronting Cenk Uygur on Piers Morgan Uncensored about past negative comments on Charlie Kirk.
  • Dr. Drew Pinsky's highlight was re-sharing past television appearances questioning COVID-19 lockdowns and school closures.
  • Sage Steele cited a positive White House press briefing question but a negative Piers Morgan Uncensored encounter involving bigotry accusations.
  • Jillian Michaels' best moment was appearing on the Joe Rogan podcast, while a debate on Piers Morgan Uncensored and a panel with Riley Gaines were among her worst.

  • Dave Rubin expressed gratitude for the "Actual Friends" podcast and strengthened friendships despite personal losses in 2025.
  • Sage Steele thanked her parents for their support through difficult personal and professional times.
  • Dr. Drew Pinsky expressed gratitude to Dave Rubin for enabling his podcasting activities while handling childcare.
  • Dave Rubin challenged co-hosts and the audience to make 2026 a year of normalcy and decency, coinciding with America's 250th anniversary, to foster patriotism.
