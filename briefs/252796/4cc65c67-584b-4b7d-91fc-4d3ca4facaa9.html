Dry Powder: The Private Equity Podcast

Bending the Revenue Curve w/ Permira’s Dipan Patel

Key Takeaways

  • Permira employs a sector-embedded, operator-led value creation team focused on top-line revenue growth across its portfolio.
  • The firm prioritizes future revenue growth and business quality to achieve better exits, even with lower initial margins.
  • Permira identifies the private wealth market as a crucial capital source, targeting ultra-high net worth individuals through private banks.
  • Cultivating top talent in private equity involves fostering an entrepreneurial culture and providing opportunities for development.

Deep Dive

  • Permira's approach leverages investor experience, a dedicated 25-person value creation team, and senior advisors.
  • The model embeds ex-operators, such as a former surgeon and a Booking.com SEO lead, directly within sector teams.
  • The primary focus is on driving top-line revenue growth across the portfolio, including expansion from Europe to the US.

  • Permira values future revenue growth more highly than historic growth or potential margin expansion for better exits.
  • The strategy involves intentionally underleveraging, overgrowing, and under-margining businesses.
  • Examples include Universidad Europea and PNI achieving significant growth, and Altadomis shifting from 100% Europe to a 50-50 US-Europe split and increasing fund administration from 20-30% to 70%.
  • These changes are underwritten to fundamentally improve the quality of earnings, driven by growth and competitive moats.

  • The guest emphasizes the private wealth channel's significance for the next decade.
  • Permira targets ultra-high net worth individuals through private banks, highlighting the appeal of illiquidity for higher returns and diversification across U.S. and European markets.
  • The traditional private equity promise of 2x net returns and 20% net IRRs is considered a compelling product for high-net-worth individuals.
  • Permira ensures any new product offerings are additive and sensible for both existing and new clients.

  • Alternative investment firms must attract and retain talent by offering opportunities to become great investors and strong compensation.
  • Permira fosters an entrepreneurial culture, supporting emerging talent in developing investment themes and executing deals.
  • An anecdote from 2009 illustrates this ethos, where a team member proactively developed an education technology theme and took a U.S. public company private.
