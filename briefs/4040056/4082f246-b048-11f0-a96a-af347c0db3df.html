Honestly with Bari Weiss

Trailer | Spiral: Murder in Detroit

Key Takeaways

  • Samantha Woll, president of Detroit's last synagogue, was found stabbed to death in October 2023.
  • The murder case became complex, raising questions about antisemitism, race, and justice.
  • The "Spiral murder in Detroit" podcast investigates the unsolved case through exclusive interviews.

Deep Dive

  • Detroit community leader Samantha Woll was found brutally stabbed to death outside her home on October 21, 2023.
  • Woll was the president of Detroit's last synagogue; the case initially looked like a hate crime but police ruled that out.
  • The investigation led to two unrelated suspects, and charges against one raised questions about antisemitism, race, and justice in America.

  • The "Spiral Murder in Detroit" podcast features exclusive interviews with individuals connected to Samantha Woll's case.
  • The series includes conversations with Woll's family, lawyers, jury members, an ex-boyfriend, and authorities.
  • Produced by The Free Press, the podcast is available on major podcast platforms, with the full series accessible to paid subscribers.
