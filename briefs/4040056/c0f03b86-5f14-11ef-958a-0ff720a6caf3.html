A Note from Bari on Honestly

Key Takeaways

  • Bari Weiss's podcast "Honestly" is taking a temporary hiatus.
  • The pause is due to Weiss's new role as CBS News editor-in-chief.
  • "Honestly" plans to return in a few months.
  • The Free Press is expanding with new podcasts, live streams, and columnists.

Deep Dive

  • Bari Weiss announced a temporary pause for "Honestly" after nearly 400 episodes.
  • The hiatus is attributed to Weiss's new role as editor-in-chief at CBS News.
  • The podcast had covered diverse topics from politics to war since its inception in May 2021.

  • "Honestly" is taking a temporary pause but is expected to return in a few months.
  • The Free Press is expanding, introducing new podcasts and live streams for subscribers.
  • New columnists have been added to The Free Press platform.

  • Bari Weiss encouraged listeners to watch CBS News programs like "60 Minutes" and "CBS Evening News."
  • Listeners are directed to thefp.com to support The Free Press through subscriptions.
  • Weiss thanked listeners for their understanding during the podcast's temporary pause.
