5 Things to Know Before the Opening Bell 6/6/25

Key Takeaways

  • Jobs market showing resilience with forecasters expecting modest but steady employment growth of 125,000 new jobs in May, suggesting continued economic stability despite broader market uncertainties.
  • AI boom driving selective winners as Broadcom's 70% surge since April demonstrates how companies positioned in artificial intelligence infrastructure continue to significantly outperform, though questions remain about growth sustainability.
  • Consumer discretionary showing strain with Lululemon missing sales growth targets and cutting guidance while proactively raising prices for tariff protection, reflecting broader retail headwinds and defensive positioning.
  • Stablecoin sector experiencing explosive volatility as Circle's dramatic pre-market jump from $31 to $94 highlights the continued speculative nature and regulatory uncertainty in cryptocurrency-adjacent businesses.

Deep Dive

Opening and Market Context

The hosts begin by acknowledging National Donut Day and noting the absence of co-host Andrew, while teasing an upcoming Squawk Pod podcast episode.

Jobs Report Preview

May Employment Expectations: Forecasters are predicting a modest increase of 125,000 non-farm payrolls for May, with the unemployment rate expected to hold steady at current levels.

Individual Stock Performance Analysis

Circle (Stablecoin Company)

Dramatic Pre-Market Surge: Shares experienced explosive growth, jumping from $31 to $94 in pre-market trading - representing one of the day's most significant movers.

Lululemon - Mixed Results and Strategic Adjustments

Financial Performance:
  • Both earnings and revenue fell short of expectations
  • Comparable sales growth came in at just 1%, missing analyst estimates of 3%
  • Management cut full-year guidance in response to weaker performance
Strategic Response:
  • Company announced plans for strategic price increases specifically designed to mitigate potential tariff impacts

DocuSign - Beat Tempered by Concerns

Quarterly Results:
  • Earnings and revenue both exceeded analyst estimates
  • However, first quarter billings came in weaker than expected
  • Management provided lower guidance for the current quarter, suggesting cautious outlook ahead

Broadcom - AI Growth Story Continues

Strong Performance Metrics:
  • Both earnings and revenue came in slightly above analyst expectations
  • Particularly strong AI revenue growth driving results
  • Stock has surged 70% since its early April low
Growth Sustainability Questions:
  • Despite strong performance, some market observers are expressing concerns about potential limitations to AI growth momentum going forward
