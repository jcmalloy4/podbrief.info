Episode

Key Takeaways

  • Fixed income is regaining attractiveness, offering competitive yields after a decade of low rates.
  • The S&P 500's strong performance is heavily concentrated in AI-driven technology stocks, posing potential risk.
  • Current market valuations share similarities with the dot-com era, but underlying companies show stronger fundamentals.
  • Philip Morris International is actively transitioning to profitable smoke-free products for future growth.
  • Despite high valuations, long-term S&P 500 returns are still expected to be attractive, albeit lower.

Deep Dive

  • Over 40% of the S&P 500's current market capitalization is attributed to AI-related companies.
  • A 20% decrease in data center capital expenditures could lead to a 10-13% market derating.
  • An earlier January sell-off saw NVIDIA's stock drop 17% after China's AI model launch.
  • Despite NVIDIA's drop, the S&P 500 index fund only fell approximately 1.5% due to gains in other sectors.

  • Concerns exist regarding potential double-ordering and inventory issues in AI-related data center construction.
  • Data center growth is projected to be in the low double digits through the current decade.
  • Few sectors beyond technology and communication services currently profit from AI, which remains an expense for many.
  • Sectors like industrials and utilities are expected to benefit indirectly from data center build-out and increased electricity demand.

  • Favored sectors for outperformance through 2026 include technology, industrials, utilities, and financials.
  • Scott Wren suggests a 10% market correction presents a buying opportunity, but advises against small-cap stocks.
  • The expert expresses an underweight stance on emerging and developed international markets like Europe.
  • Attractive, albeit lower, returns are anticipated from the S&P 500 over the next 10 years, recommending investment during market pullbacks.

  • AppLovin's valuation has increased from $100 billion to over $200 billion since a previous podcast mention in November.
  • The company specializes in marketing and user acquisition for mobile gaming and is expanding into e-commerce.
  • A Wedbush note predicts continued phenomenal growth for AppLovin, supporting its valuation increase.

  • CEO Jacek Olczak detailed the company's shift towards non-combustible products like Icos heated tobacco and nicotine pouches.
  • 41% of Philip Morris International's revenue and 42-43% of its profits now come from smoke-free products.
  • These newer products offer better margins than traditional combustible products, driving the company's strategic focus.
  • Wall Street estimates project double-digit earnings per share growth for the company over the next few years.

  • Analysts project continued margin gains for Philip Morris International, expecting close to a 1% increase next year.
  • The company states cash from traditional cigarette sales helps finance its transition to smoke-free products.
  • Philip Morris International's stock valuation has risen to 22 times projected earnings, nearing market averages.
  • This valuation shift positions the company to appeal more to growth investors, moving beyond its historical appeal to income investors.
