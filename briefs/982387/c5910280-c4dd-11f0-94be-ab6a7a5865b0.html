Invest Like the Best with Patrick O'Shaughnessy

Ari Emanuel - The Anti-AI Bet - [Invest Like the Best, EP.448]

Key Takeaways

  • Ari Emanuel's core strategy centers on an "anti-AI bet," investing in live, physical experiences as digital content costs decrease.
  • His operational success is driven by a relentless "make things happen" mindset, over-communication, and continuous follow-up.
  • The media landscape is shifting towards creators as independent media channels and increasing value for premium live sports and entertainment.
  • Emanuel emphasizes the crucial role of taste, intellectual property, and learning from mistakes in navigating evolving business challenges.

Deep Dive

  • Ari Emanuel oversees TKO (UFC, WWE), chairs WME Group, and founded MARI, a new company focused on global events and live experiences.
  • His "anti-AI bet" posits that as AI automates digital content and everyday work, value will concentrate in live, physical experiences.
  • Emanuel anticipates increased demand for social interaction and extended weekends will further drive the value of live events.

  • Emanuel's core operating principle is relentlessly making things happen and not accepting 'no,' attributing his fearlessness to lessons from dyslexia.
  • He translates the mental fortitude and emotional endurance required for high school wrestling, including severe weight cutting, to handling business challenges.
  • The conversation highlights evolving content ownership and distribution, noting major studios increasingly supply each other while independent producers are less prevalent.
  • AI is expected to drastically reduce production costs and enhance top talent's capabilities, prompting questions about where value will ultimately accrue.

  • Emanuel's new venture, MARI, aims to create a platform for live events by integrating existing assets like Frieze and Barrett Jackson with a new ticketing business.
  • A great user experience in live events incorporates surrounding elements such as food, lectures, and ancillary activities, enhancing communal engagement.
  • There is significant demand for premium and tiered experiences, with Super Bowl and UFC tickets commanding tens to hundreds of thousands of dollars.
  • His new boxing business intends to create a league where top boxers fight each other, with the potential to increase fighter pay.

  • Emanuel predicts creators like Dwayne Johnson will become their own media channels as digital content production costs approach zero.
  • He anticipates significant price increases for sports teams, driven by private equity and debt leverage, necessitating tiered access for end-users.
  • The expansion of gambling and betting is viewed positively for its ability to generate data, prevent cheating, and enhance engagement.
  • Emanuel expresses concern for his children's future in an AI-dominated world, though Elon Musk believes live experiences are resilient to AI disruption.
