Invest Like the Best with Patrick O'Shaughnessy

Luca Ferrari - Building Bending Spoons - [Invest Like the Best, EP.446]

Key Takeaways

  • Bending Spoons operates as a hybrid 25% private equity, 75% technology company.
  • They acquire and extensively rebuild digital businesses for long-term, 'forever' ownership.
  • A core vision is to build a defining European institution, inspired by Berkshire Hathaway.
  • Bending Spoons prioritizes attracting and developing exceptional, often inexperienced, talent.
  • CEO Luca Ferrari's 'perennially unhappy' mindset fuels the company's pursuit of excellence.
  • Centralized operations yield cost savings and shared R&D across their acquired companies.
  • Financing evolves from organic cash flow to commercial debt and secondary equity raises.
  • Consensus is considered overrated; an uncompromising vision drives significant achievements.

Deep Dive

  • Bending Spoons is described as 25% private equity and 75% technology, founded in 2013.
  • It acquires and operates digital companies like Evernote, Meetup, Vimeo, and AOL, intending to own them 'forever'.
  • Unlike typical private equity, Bending Spoons undertakes deep, extensive rebuilding of acquired companies.

  • The failure of previous startup Evertail, which aimed to create an AI-powered life diary and faced near-bankruptcy with €40,000 remaining, shaped Bending Spoons' acquisition strategy.
  • Initial seed capital for Bending Spoons in 2013 was €40,000, necessitating a low burn rate and co-living arrangements.
  • The insight to become an acquisition-driven company emerged from observing numerous startup journeys, focusing on functional expertise over founder passion.

  • Bending Spoons achieves cost savings through bulk purchasing (e.g., cloud infrastructure) and leverages R&D investments across multiple businesses.
  • The company can fluidly move R&D and marketing resources between its acquired companies.
  • Bending Spoons attracts stronger talent than individual businesses, handling 800,000 applications for 250 hires annually.
  • Key to success includes building a strong employer brand, prioritizing talent density, and viewing job offerings as their most important product.

  • The Evernote acquisition marked Bending Spoons' shift to larger, structured companies, despite paying a premium for a strong brand with a large user base.
  • A rapid improvement process led to approximately 250 product improvements in 2.5 years, significantly accelerating innovation.
  • Operational improvements included rebuilding the codebase and infrastructure, boosting performance and customer satisfaction, even with a 60% price increase.

  • Bending Spoons employs sophisticated valuation, discipline, and negotiation, making fair, immediate offers to build a reputation for reliability.
  • Their approach, informed by deep operational experience, results in a high offer acceptance and low walkaway rate.
  • Financing evolved from organic free cash flow to commercial bank debt, with recent equity raises primarily for secondary transactions and modest dilution.
  • Attracting international investors enhances credibility and firepower for large acquisitions, though dilution is carefully managed.

  • AOL is described as a strong business with tens of millions of loyal users, functioning as an email inbox and content aggregator.
  • Plans include improving AOL's product, offering, and monetization by simplifying its often-excessive features.
  • The strategy emphasizes focusing on a few impactful initiatives driven by customer needs rather than broad R&D.

  • Exceptional investors assess businesses on fundamental merits, discerning underlying 'laws of physics' to identify overlooked opportunities.
  • They deeply understand people, distinguishing intelligence from promotional skills, and logically deduce business success and leadership viability.
  • Bending Spoons motivates employees with a fixed salary, no variable pay or stock options, offering the chance to invest at a discount.
  • This incentive structure relies on high integrity and professional pride, treating employees with respect and assuming best interests.

  • Bending Spoons recently raised a $700 million debt round at an $11 billion valuation, demonstrating significant financial activity.
  • Lenders focus on mitigating downside risk, unlike equity investors, but demonstrate surprising vision and thoroughness.
  • Raising debt involves engaging anchor lenders who commit funds and help shape the deal, followed by syndication, described as more efficient than VC markets.
  • AI is primarily positive for Bending Spoons, accelerating quality and efficiency across its diversified model, widening the gap between advanced users and laggards.

  • Bending Spoons emphasizes aligning on principles and values, with leaders embodying them, fostered through biannual 'State of the Spoon' internal events.
  • Annual company retreats, lasting 8-9 days in exotic locations, build trust and camaraderie among employees, seen as crucial for willingness to sacrifice and perform.
  • Building this company model is described as 'dauntingly painful,' requiring 7-12 years to develop the necessary talent, culture, and technology, explaining its rarity.
