Invest Like the Best with Patrick O'Shaughnessy

Peter Lacaillade - Backing The Best Managers In Private Markets - [Invest Like the Best, EP.437]

Key Takeaways

  • SCS Financial uniquely accesses top private equity funds for wealthy clients.
  • Private equity delivers consistent high returns through long-term operational improvements.
  • Lower middle market buyouts offer significant alpha via professionalization and roll-ups.
  • Successful private equity partnerships depend on deep character alignment and authenticity.

Deep Dives

Elite Access

  • SCS Financial bypasses adverse selection common in wealth management by securing top-tier private equity funds.
  • Their pooled vehicle structure, established every two years, guarantees client access to high-quality funds.
  • Managing over $50 billion across 500 clients enables SCS to mirror top endowment strategies.
  • SCS's fee structure avoids bias, focusing solely on alpha generation within private equity.

Persistent Alpha

  • Private equity's long-term capital enables systemic business changes, impossible with quarterly public market pressures.
  • SCS targets high teens to low 20s IRRs, aiming for 2.5x multiples, by consistently investing across market cycles.
  • Missing opportune vintages during downturns severely impacts returns; consistent allocation mitigates this.

Untapped Value

  • SCS focuses on the smaller end of private equity, buying assets cheaply to improve and sell to larger firms.
  • A growing trend sees individuals becoming independent sponsors, building platforms in fragmented industries like HVAC and youth sports.
  • Successful independent sponsors exhibit immense hustle, passion, and entrepreneurial drive, often with prior firm experience.
  • Alpha is generated by professionalizing businesses or through complex carve-outs, rather than consensus trades.

Character Focus

  • Peter emphasizes understanding the character of firm leaders, as private equity partnerships can last 15+ years.
  • Actively seeking contrary views beyond positive references is crucial to avoid problematic investments and ensure authenticity.
  • Successful general partners prioritize deep relationships, seeking LPs who offer stable capital and strategic value with long-term alignment.
