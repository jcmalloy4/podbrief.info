Episode

Key Takeaways

  • John O'Leary dedicated the episode to his friend John Rulin's legacy.
  • John Rulin lived a life of radical generosity, elevating others through influence.
  • Rulin's unexpected death at 44 inspired a call for purposeful living.
  • Listeners are encouraged to live inspired and support the 'Soul on Fire' film.

Deep Dive

  • Met host O'Leary nearly two decades ago, facilitating his involvement with the Entrepreneurs Organization.
  • Used his influence to secure over a dozen new speaking opportunities for O'Leary.
  • Practiced radical generosity, consistently asking, "What's the most I can possibly offer?"

  • Died unexpectedly at age 44 while on vacation with his family.
  • Collapsed and could not be revived.
  • A final text exchange with O'Leary about perseverance occurred on the same day as his passing.

  • Rulin left a wife, four daughters, and a global community, leaving a legacy of service to others.
  • Host O'Leary urged listeners to embody Rulin's principles of generosity.
  • Encouraged living with purpose and giving, as "tomorrow is not promised."

  • The upcoming film "Soul on Fire" is promoted, with more information at soulonfiremovie.com.
  • Portrays people bringing hope and inspiration to a child's life through generosity.
  • Aims to counter negative Hollywood narratives by highlighting positive individual impact.
