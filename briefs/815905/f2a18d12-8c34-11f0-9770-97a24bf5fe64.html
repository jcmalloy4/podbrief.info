Kalshi is Scaring DraftKings & How Do You Sell $102M of Louvre Loot?

Key Takeaways

  • Prediction markets like Kalshi and Polymarket are disrupting sports betting through federal regulation advantages.
  • Meta is reorganizing its AI division, cutting approximately 600 roles amid a broader debate on AI's future.
  • Harvard's PhD program admissions are significantly declining due to financial pressures, a trend seen nationwide.
  • Florida is dramatically increasing Burmese python removal efforts via a unique partnership with a leather company.

Deep Dive

  • The current government shutdown has become the second longest in U.S. history, with no clear end in sight.
  • Negotiations are complicated by President Trump's planned trip to Asia and stalled congressional talks over healthcare subsidies.
  • Speculation suggests the shutdown could become the longest on record, potentially causing airport disruptions by Thanksgiving.

  • The NHL is partnering with prediction market platforms Kalshi and Polymarket, allowing official league branding.
  • This challenges traditional sportsbooks like DraftKings and FanDuel, with DraftKings acquiring Railbird Technologies for $250 million to compete.
  • Prediction markets function as federally regulated financial exchanges, circumventing patchwork state-by-state gambling laws.
  • They are gaining traction in states like California and Texas, where sports gambling is restricted, with advice from Donald Trump Jr.

  • More than 850 prominent figures, including Steve Wozniak, urged a pause in advanced AI development due to existential risks.
  • Meta is reportedly cutting approximately 600 roles from its AI division, citing a reorganization for improved efficiency and research focus.
  • Only 5% of Americans support rapid, unregulated AI progress, contrasting with timelines from figures like Sam Altman and Elon Musk.

  • The Louvre reopened after a daylight heist of Napoleonic-era jewels, valued at an estimated $102 million.
  • Eight stolen objects, including items belonging to French queens, present a challenge for thieves to sell on the black market.
  • Thieves would need to break down and recut 9,000 gems, a process that significantly reduces their value and takes time.

  • Harvard's PhD admission slots have significantly declined, with drops of 75% in science and 60% in humanities.
  • This trend is attributed to Harvard's financial pressures, including a $300 million endowment tax and a $113 million operating deficit.
  • A broader survey of American higher education shows 40 out of 45 biology departments and 33 astronomy and physics programs are downsizing or eliminating admitted students.

  • Florida successfully removed 748 Burmese pythons this summer, a significant increase from 235 last year.
  • This increase is attributed to an innovative partnership with Inversa Leathers, which converts python skins into fashion items.
  • The partnership creates a market incentive for hunting, aiding Florida's efforts in managing the invasive python population.
