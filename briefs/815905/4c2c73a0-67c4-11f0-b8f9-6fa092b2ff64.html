Air Canada Union Defies the Government & Americans Are Stuck…Literally

Key Takeaways

  • Air Canada's flight attendants continue their strike, defying a government return-to-work order.
  • American mobility has reached a record low, impacting housing and economic productivity.
  • Six Flags faces declining attendance and operational issues post-merger, despite season pass efforts.
  • #RushTok has evolved into a lucrative industry, boosting sorority recruitment and related businesses.
  • Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway increased stakes in UnitedHealth and Domino's Pizza.

Deep Dive

  • The union representing Air Canada's 10,000 flight attendants is defying a government order to return to work, prolonging a strike.
  • Prime Minister Mark Carney granted Air Canada's appeal for binding arbitration, but the union rejected the offer, citing insufficient compensation for inflation.
  • The union demands 'boarding pay' for time before aircraft doors close, a practice followed by airlines like Delta and American, but not Air Canada.
  • Air Canada offered a 38% total compensation increase over four years, including a 12-16% hourly pay rise for the first year.

  • Americans are relocating at the lowest rate on record, with only 1 in 13 (7.7%) moving annually, down from 1 in 5 in the 1960s.
  • Economists attribute this decline to a slowed labor market with fewer job opportunities and high home prices combined with increased mortgage rates.
  • The lack of mobility impacts the housing market and individual earnings, with a University of Chicago study linking improved mobility to a potential $2 trillion U.S. GDP boost by 2009.
  • Dual-income households exhibit the lowest interstate mobility, further contributing to the trend.

  • The 2023 merger between Six Flags and Cedar Fair has seen a 9% drop in attendance, resulting in 1.4 million fewer parkgoers and a third consecutive quarterly loss.
  • Operational issues include the new Sirens Curse roller coaster in Ohio, which broke down five times since its June debut.
  • The company experienced a challenging summer marked by reliability issues, delayed rides, and mechanical breakdowns, impacting season pass sales.
  • An Ohio state representative proposed a law requiring real-time ride closure updates for major amusement parks due to frequent breakdowns.

  • 'RushTok,' documenting sorority recruitment at the University of Alabama, has become a significant cultural and financial influence.
  • The phenomenon, described as 'reality television for the TikTok masses,' gained global attention four years ago and was featured in an HBO series in 2023.
  • Consultants now charge up to $10,000 for coaching, and small businesses in areas like Tuscaloosa have seen a boost from participants.
  • Nationwide sorority recruitment registrations have increased 13% since 2022, indicating continued growth.

  • Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway amassed a stake exceeding $1.5 billion in UnitedHealth Group, causing the stock to rally nearly 11%.
  • This investment occurred despite UnitedHealth's recent struggles, including missed earnings, a CEO resignation, and a Justice Department investigation.
  • For the fourth consecutive quarter, Berkshire Hathaway has invested in Domino's Pizza, now owning 7.8% of the company.
  • Other hedge funds, including David Tepper's and Lone Pine Capital, also acquired significant shares in UnitedHealth Group.
