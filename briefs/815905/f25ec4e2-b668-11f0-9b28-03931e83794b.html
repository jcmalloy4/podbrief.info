Meta Ruled Not a Monopoly & “Baby Shark” Company Goes Public

Key Takeaways

  • A federal judge ruled Meta's acquisitions did not violate antitrust law, marking a loss for the FTC.
  • Home Depot reported weak Q3 sales and cut forecasts, signaling broader economic weakness.
  • Panera Bread is implementing a turnaround plan to address declining customer value and sales.
  • Pinkfong, creator of "Baby Shark," went public but faces monetization challenges despite billions of views.
  • Google launched its new AI model, Gemini 3, intensifying competition in the AI sector.
  • A Cloudflare system crash caused a four-hour widespread internet outage affecting major websites.

Deep Dive

  • A federal judge dismissed the FTC's antitrust case against Meta, ruling its acquisitions of Instagram and WhatsApp did not violate U.S. antitrust law.
  • The judge cited the rapidly changing social media landscape and the FTC's inability to clearly define the relevant market.
  • Meta successfully argued its platforms now function like content-driven media, facing broader competition beyond personal networking sites.
  • Antitrust trials against large tech companies are challenging as new competitors emerge rapidly, making legal battles seem outdated by verdict time.

  • Home Depot reported stagnant sales and cut its profit forecast, signaling broader economic weakness.
  • The company's performance is seen as a bellwether for the consumer and housing markets, which are frozen due to economic uncertainty and high mortgage rates.
  • Home Depot is boosting sales by focusing on the business-to-business (B2B) sector, acquiring companies like SRS Distribution and GMS to sell to professional contractors.
  • Individual homeowners, despite rising home values, are primarily undertaking cosmetic upgrades rather than major renovations.

  • Panera Bread is implementing a turnaround plan, 'Panera Rise,' to regain its standing in the fast-casual market.
  • The plan addresses past cost-cutting measures, such as using iceberg lettuce and reducing staff and portion sizes, which negatively impacted customer value and sales.
  • Panera faces competition from both fast-casual and coffee chains, compounded by issues like lawsuits over highly caffeinated 'charged sips'.
  • The company has experienced high C-suite turnover, with three CEOs since 2020.

  • South Korean company Pinkfong, creator of 'Baby Shark Dance,' has gone public with a market cap of approximately $400 million.
  • Despite its viral YouTube video accumulating over 16 billion views, profitability is reportedly impacted by YouTube's restrictions on children's content, including ad limitations and disabled comments.
  • The 'Baby Shark Dance' video's views surpass 'Despacito' and exceed the combined views of Taylor Swift's top 10 YouTube videos.
  • Pinkfong plans to leverage its IPO to develop new intellectual property and monetize existing characters, with Bebe Finn reportedly out-earning Baby Shark.

  • Google launched its new AI model, Gemini 3, described as natively multimodal and claiming to be more intelligent and accurate than previous models, directly compared to OpenAI's GPT-5.
  • Google's Gemini app reports 650 million monthly users, while ChatGPT has 800 million weekly users, indicating significant market competition.
  • Major AI players NVIDIA and Microsoft are making significant investments in Anthropic, a competitor to OpenAI, diversifying their stakes in the AI industry.
  • These developments suggest a rapid evolution in AI capabilities and a shift away from traditional search engine results.

  • A widespread internet outage affected numerous popular websites, including X, Spotify, and Morning Brew, for approximately four hours.
  • The outage was caused by a system crash at Cloudflare, attributed to an oversized file impacting its traffic management system.
  • This incident highlighted the internet's reliance on a few key infrastructure companies and the vulnerabilities present in modern internet architecture.
  • The outage impacted various services, including Down Detector, underscoring Cloudflare's role as a critical choke point for internet traffic.
