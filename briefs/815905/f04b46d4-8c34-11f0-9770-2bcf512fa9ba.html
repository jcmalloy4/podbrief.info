OpenAI is the World's Most Valuable Private Company & Tesla Sales Are… Up?

Key Takeaways

  • OpenAI secured a $500 billion valuation, becoming the world's most valuable private company.
  • Tesla's Q3 deliveries rose 7.4%, yet its stock fell due to expiring EV tax credits.
  • Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) is allowing direct access to scores for mortgage lenders.
  • Reddit saw its traffic from ChatGPT plummet, raising concerns for its advertising revenue.
  • Taylor Swift launched her 12th album and a new concert film, both with massive pre-release engagement.
  • Berkshire Hathaway acquired Occidental's chemical business, OxyChem, for $9.7 billion.

Deep Dive

  • OpenAI reached a $500 billion valuation, making it the world's most valuable private company after a secondary share sale.
  • The company reported $4.3 billion in revenue for the first half of the year, with projections of $13 billion for the full year.
  • OpenAI is pursuing multiple revenue streams, including instant checkout within ChatGPT, potential hardware products with Jony Ive, and an advertising network.
  • Its valuation rise positively impacted public markets, contributing to the NASDAQ reaching a record high and signaling strength in the AI sector.

  • Tesla's Q3 deliveries increased 7.4% year-over-year, with nearly 500,000 vehicles sold globally.
  • Despite beating analyst expectations, Tesla's stock fell 5% due to demand being pulled forward by a $7,500 federal EV tax credit that is set to expire.
  • Elon Musk's net worth surpassed $500 billion; however, Tesla faces headwinds from disappearing EV incentives.
  • Tesla's future strategy is shifting focus from EV manufacturing to AI, robotics, and energy systems, including a RoboTaxi program with tests in Austin and the Bay Area.

  • Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) stock rose 18% after announcing mortgage lenders can now access FICO scores directly.
  • This move bypasses traditional credit reporting agencies like Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion.
  • The initiative is influenced by White House pressure to reduce home loan costs and is seen as a step toward market competition.

  • Reddit experienced a significant decrease in traffic from ChatGPT, falling from 14% to 2% of responses.
  • This decline poses a risk to Reddit's business model, which relies on user-generated content for search queries and advertising revenue.
  • Reddit's stock has fallen 15% amid concerns over its reliance on traffic from platforms like Google and AI search engines.
  • Despite these concerns, Reddit's core advertising business saw 84% year-over-year revenue growth in Q2.

  • Taylor Swift released her 12th studio album, 'The Life of a Showgirl,' with over 5 million pre-saves on Spotify.
  • The album, a collaboration with Swedish songwriter Max Martin, aims for a more pop-oriented sound.
  • Swift's new movie, 'The Official Release Party of a Showgirl,' is projected to debut at $35-40 million at the box office.
  • This film follows her 'The Eras Tour' movie, which earned $93 million in 2023.

  • Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway is acquiring Occidental's chemical business, OxyChem, for $9.7 billion.
  • This marks Berkshire Hathaway's largest deal in three years.
  • The acquisition of the petrochemical business is considered a characteristic move by Buffett, despite a skeptical initial investor reaction.
  • The deal is expected to generate significant revenue for Berkshire Hathaway.
