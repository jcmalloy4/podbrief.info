Microsoft Will Pick Up its Data Center Tab? & Your Groceries Got More Expensive

Key Takeaways

  • December inflation showed 2.7% annual rise, with elevated grocery prices but moderating overall.
  • Microsoft committed to funding AI data center expansion without taxpayer subsidies due to community pushback.
  • US greenhouse gas emissions increased 2.4% in 2025, driven by heating demand and data center power.
  • Anthropic launched Cowork, an agentic AI tool for non-technical users, creating competition for Microsoft Copilot.

Deep Dive

  • December inflation rose 2.7% annually and 0.3% monthly, indicating slowing but still elevated price growth.
  • Groceries, particularly beef, saw significant price jumps, while gas and used cars became cheaper.
  • Shelter costs, a major component of the Consumer Price Index, showed potential stabilization after low increases.
  • Shelter data in the report remains uncertain due to previous government shutdown data distortions.

  • Microsoft announced a five-point plan to address community concerns regarding new data center construction.
  • The plan includes the company covering electricity cost increases and replenishing water usage, without relying on taxpayer subsidies.
  • Former President Trump's opposition to higher electricity bills for data centers added political pressure.
  • Microsoft aims to double its data center footprint in two years, making expansion a significant public affairs issue.

  • US greenhouse gas emissions increased by 2.4% in 2025, reversing a two-year decline, according to the Rhodium Group.
  • The rise is attributed to colder temperatures increasing heating demand and a surge in electricity consumption from data centers and crypto mining.
  • Coal usage by electric utilities rose 13% in 2025, contributing to the emissions increase.
  • Solar generation saw a 34% increase in 2025, but this growth was insufficient to offset the overall rise in demand and emissions.

  • Anthropic launched Cowork, an agentic AI tool designed for non-technical users to execute multi-step tasks on a computer.
  • The tool was developed in approximately 1.5 weeks using Anthropic's own Claude Code.
  • Cowork is available at a $200 monthly tier, designed for general computing without coding.
  • The introduction of Cowork immediately sparked debate about its potential impact on productivity and employment.

  • Anthropic's Cowork tool poses a direct competitive threat to Microsoft's Copilot and smaller startups specializing in single AI functions.
  • Anthropic has seen significant enterprise adoption for its AI technology.
  • The company recently raised funds, achieving a $183 billion valuation with plans to reach $350 billion.
  • Cowork's broad capabilities include tasks like receipt processing and document drafting.

  • Tensions escalated with Iran following President Trump's support for protesters, leading to a violent government crackdown, over 12,000 reported deaths, and internet shutdowns.
  • Elon Musk's Starlink service became crucial for Iranians seeking outside communication but faced government jamming and tracking, described as 'electronic warfare'.
  • JPMorgan Chase CFO Jeremy Barnum indicated the bank is planning for a potential 10% cap on credit card interest rates, proposed by President Trump.
  • JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon addressed an investigation into Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell, emphasizing the importance of Fed independence.
