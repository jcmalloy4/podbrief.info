Morning Brew Daily

AI Energy Gets $90B Boost & HBO Max Dominates Emmy Noms

Key Takeaways

  • June's inflation report indicates subtle price increases in tariff-sensitive sectors, maintaining the Federal Reserve's cautious interest rate stance.
  • Pennsylvania is emerging as a significant AI and energy hub, drawing multi-billion dollar investments from tech giants like Google and Blackstone.
  • Apple TV+ is making notable strides in the streaming wars, with "Severance" and "The Studio" leading Emmy nominations and signaling platform growth.
  • Major League Baseball debuted automated robo-umpires at the All-Star Game, marking a significant step toward modernizing the sport with enhanced accuracy.
  • Apple's new partnership with MP Materials aims to onshore critical rare earth supply, strengthening the U.S. supply chain and boosting the American miner.

Deep Dives

Topic 1: Inflation's Nuances and Tariff Impact

  • June's Consumer Price Index saw a 0.2% monthly rise and 2.7% year-over-year, largely meeting forecasts but revealing specific price hikes in apparel, toys, and electronics linked to tariffs.
  • Core inflation continued to undershoot projections for the fifth consecutive month, suggesting the Federal Reserve may maintain current interest rates with potential cuts anticipated in the fall.
  • Market reactions were mixed, with the NASDAQ hitting a new high driven partly by NVIDIA's AI chip sales, not solely a direct response to the inflation data.

Topic 2: Pennsylvania's Rise as an AI Energy Hub

  • A Pittsburgh summit saw approximately $70 billion in investment pledges from companies like Google, Blackstone, and CoreWeave, aiming to establish Pennsylvania as a leading AI and energy center.
  • The state's existing power infrastructure and strong university presence, including Carnegie Mellon, make it an attractive location for the massive data centers required by AI development.
  • This surge in data center construction, which consumes substantial electricity, signifies a new industrial revolution driven by the demand for AI power.

Topic 3: Apple TV+'s Ascent in the Streaming Wars

  • Apple TV+ garnered significant Emmy nominations, with "Severance" leading all shows with 27 nods and "The Studio" receiving a record 23 for a comedy series, surpassing "Ted Lasso."
  • These critical successes, alongside commercial hits like the $400 million grossing "F1" film and interest in acquiring F1 racing rights, highlight Apple's growing momentum in entertainment.
  • Despite having fewer subscribers than rivals, Apple's strategic investments and quality content are positioning it as a serious contender against HBO Max and Netflix.

Topic 4: MLB Embraces Robotic Umpires

  • Major League Baseball introduced automated ball-strike systems during the All-Star Game, marking its prime-time debut after extensive testing in minor leagues and spring training.
  • The system, powered by Sony's Hawkeye computer vision, aims for higher accuracy than human umpires, with an 88.2% correct call rate, and allows teams two challenges per game.
  • This technological shift, part of Commissioner Rob Manfred's broader efforts to modernize baseball, represents a move towards greater precision and has faced some resistance from the umpires union.

Topic 5: Securing U.S. Rare Earth Supply with Apple's Investment

  • MP Materials, operator of the only rare earths mine in the U.S., secured a significant deal with Apple to supply rare earth magnets from its Texas facility and establish a California recycling line.
  • This partnership, valued at $500 million, aims to build a more U.S.-based supply chain, significantly reducing reliance on China for these critical minerals.
  • The announcement propelled MP Materials' stock, nearly doubling its market capitalization and highlighting the strategic importance of rare earth minerals in global trade relations.
