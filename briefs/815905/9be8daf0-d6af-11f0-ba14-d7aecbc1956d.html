2026 Lookahead: World Cup, Moon Travel, Grand Theft Auto VI and More

Key Takeaways

  • 2026 will feature significant national anniversaries and major global sporting events.
  • AI's energy demands are emerging as a key issue in upcoming elections.
  • NASA is planning the next phase of human exploration around the moon.
  • Major anniversaries will be observed across technology, geopolitics, and history.
  • One host offered bold predictions concerning tech companies and cultural figures for 2026.

Deep Dive

  • The United States will celebrate its 250th anniversary in 2026, with planned events nationwide.
  • Reported patriotism has seen a decline, with 58% of Americans proud of the country, down from 87% post-9/11.
  • Cities including Philadelphia, Washington D.C., and New York are vying to be central locations for the celebrations.

  • The significant electricity demands of AI data centers are becoming a major election issue for 2026.
  • These facilities can consume enough power for 100,000 homes, leading to increased energy costs in battleground states.
  • A 'pro-AI versus anti-AI' debate is influencing political campaigns and voter concerns.

  • The 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan-Cortina will feature American snowboarder Chloe Kim aiming for a third gold and the return of NHL players.
  • The World Baseball Classic in March 2026 will include top MLB stars and 20 national teams, following 2023's dramatic Japan-USA final.
  • The 2026 FIFA World Cup will expand to 48 teams and 104 games across North America, with Argentina defending their title.

  • NASA's Artemis II mission, aiming for a sustainable human presence on the moon, is slated for April 2026, potentially launching in February.
  • This 10-day mission will orbit the moon, serving as a precursor to a Mars landing.
  • Victor Glover will be the first Black astronaut and Jeremy Hansen the first Canadian to fly around the moon; Artemis III, the first moon landing since 1972, is targeted for 2027.

  • The year 2026 marks the 50th anniversary of Apple Computer.
  • Significant geopolitical events include the 25th anniversary of 9/11 and the 10th anniversary of Brexit.
  • The Mughal Empire will celebrate its 500th anniversary, adding a historical milestone.

  • Toby Howell predicts OpenAI will cease operations by the end of 2026 due to economic unsustainability and competition.
  • He forecasts Netflix will acquire AMC, citing Netflix's market capitalization and a desire to preserve theatrical releases.
  • Howell also suggests TikTok will fall out of the top 10 most downloaded apps due to a perceived loss of cultural relevance and increased focus on TikTok Shop.
