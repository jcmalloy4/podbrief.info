Waymo’s Are A Lot Safer Than You Think & Meet Italy’s ‘Berkshire Hathaway’

Key Takeaways

  • Former President Trump's administration proposes relaxing federal vehicle fuel efficiency standards.
  • Spotify Wrapped 2023 launched, introducing new personalized music insights and a redesigned interface.
  • Italian tech firm Bending Spoons acquired Eventbrite for $500 million, continuing its strategy of rebuilding online businesses.
  • Waymo's self-driving cars show a significant safety advantage, reducing injury crashes by 80-91% compared to human drivers.
  • A new 'no-find, no-fee' search for missing Malaysia Airlines Flight 370 has commenced in the Indian Ocean.
  • Boston's rental market is softening, with average asking rents falling for the first time since 2021.

Deep Dive

  • Former President Trump's administration plans to slash federal fuel economy rules for passenger vehicles.
  • The required average would decrease from 50 miles per gallon by 2031 to 34 miles per gallon.
  • This move is supported by automakers and the oil industry but criticized by environmentalists.
  • The rollback could lead consumers to higher fuel costs if gasoline prices increase, as automakers prioritize less fuel-efficient vehicles.

  • Former President Trump proposes a significant reduction in vehicle fuel efficiency standards.

  • Spotify Wrapped 2023 launched, featuring personalized music insights, new play counts for favorite songs, and a 'listening age' metric.
  • The design aesthetic shifted to a hand-drawn, analog style this year, moving away from last year's tech-focused theme and receiving positive reviews.
  • One host reported a 'listening age' of 92, attributed to listening to classical music from the 1940s and 50s.
  • The user interface changed from a story-like format to a vertical feed, similar to TikTok, reflecting a broader trend towards vertical video content.

  • Italian tech conglomerate Bending Spoons acquired Eventbrite for $500 million, adding it to a portfolio that includes AOL and Vimeo.
  • The firm, founded 12 years ago, employs a strategy of buying cash-flowing but stagnant online businesses, cutting staff, and rebuilding them.
  • Backed by investors like Andre Agassi and former Google leader Eric Schmidt, Bending Spoons raised $270 million at an $11 billion valuation and an additional $2.8 billion in debt financing.
  • An example of their redevelopment approach is Evernote, which involved staff reductions, price increases, and a complete code rewrite.

  • Waymo's self-driving cars were involved in 91% fewer serious injury crashes and 80% fewer crashes causing any injury compared to human drivers over 100 million miles.
  • This data, presented in the New York Times, suggests self-driving technology could drastically reduce the 39,000+ annual traffic deaths in the US.
  • Waymo has updated its software in San Francisco to be more assertively driven, leading to more confident maneuvers like crossing double yellow lines to avoid traffic congestion.

  • Boston's average asking rents fell to approximately $3,000 in October, marking the first decline since 2021.
  • Vacancy rates have risen, benefiting renters but signaling economic struggles in the city.
  • This trend is attributed to decreased funding for biotech due to high interest rates and fewer international students enrolling in universities because of past immigration policies.

  • Ocean Infinity is launching a new 'no-find, no-fee' search for Malaysia Airlines Flight 370 in the Indian Ocean, with a $70 million payout if successful.
  • The company will scan a 6,000 square mile area using three autonomous underwater vehicles.
  • This renewed effort utilizes prior knowledge of the search area and could potentially locate the aircraft within a month.
