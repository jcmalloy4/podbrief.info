The Unemployment Rate Hits 4-Year High & Elon Getting $1T from Tesla?

Key Takeaways

  • The U.S. labor market showed significant weakness in August, with job growth falling short and unemployment rising.
  • Tesla's board proposed a $1 trillion pay package for Elon Musk, contingent on ambitious performance targets.
  • Authors secured a $1.5 billion settlement from AI company Anthropic for copyright infringement from AI training.
  • The Catholic Church canonized Carlo Acutis, its first millennial saint, to engage younger, digitally-oriented audiences.

Deep Dive

  • The U.S. economy added 22,000 jobs in August, missing expectations, while the unemployment rate increased to a four-year high of 4.3%.
  • Blue-collar sectors, including manufacturing, saw declines, with manufacturing losing 12,000 jobs in August and 78,000 yearly.
  • Unemployment rates for young workers (16-24) and Black workers also increased.
  • The Federal Reserve is now anticipated to cut interest rates, with traders expecting multiple cuts by year-end.

  • Tesla's board proposed a new $1 trillion pay package for CEO Elon Musk, tied to aggressive company performance targets.
  • Targets include reaching specific valuation, EBITDA, vehicle delivery, and autonomous driving milestones.
  • The proposal follows a Delaware judge striking down Musk's previous $56 billion pay package.
  • Shareholders are required to approve the new package on November 6th.

  • Authors secured a $1.5 billion settlement from AI company Anthropic for copyright infringement related to training its AI models.
  • The settlement establishes a precedent that while training AI on legally obtained books may be fair use, using pirated works is a copyright violation.
  • Anthropic confirmed using a 'legacy system' to acquire digitized books, including from shadow libraries, for its AI training data.
  • This marks the largest copyright payout in history and could influence over 40 similar lawsuits against AI companies.

  • Carlo Acutis, who died at 15 in 2006, was canonized as the Catholic Church's first millennial saint, drawing 80,000 attendees.
  • Known as 'God's Influencer,' Acutis gained popularity for his tech-savviness and a website tracking miracles.
  • His relatable image, often depicted with jeans, Nikes, and a laptop, aims to connect with younger, digitally-oriented generations.
  • His canonization, expedited by two confirmed miracles, aligns with the church's strategy to address declining religious affiliation among youth.

  • Apple's hardware event is expected to unveil four iPhone 17 variants, an updated watch, AirPods Pro 3, and AirTag 2.
  • Six companies are set to go public via IPOs, including the significant 'buy now, pay later' firm Klarna and crypto exchange Gemini Space Station.
  • Monthly Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Producer Price (PPI) reports will indicate inflation trends, following a three-year high in producer prices.
  • A Powerball jackpot drawing will occur, resetting to $20 million after a previous nearly $1.8 billion win.
