SCOTUS Takes on Music Piracy Showdown & NYC Casinos Get Lucky Break

Key Takeaways

  • The Supreme Court is hearing a case on ISP liability for copyright infringement, potentially impacting internet access.
  • New York State approved three new casino licenses in Queens and the Bronx, expecting billions in tax revenue.
  • Airbus stock declined following reports of quality issues with its A320 aircraft, including a software glitch and fuselage panel defects.
  • Shopify experienced significant downtime on Cyber Monday, affecting numerous e-commerce merchants during a critical sales period.
  • The quarter-zip is making a comeback in men's fashion as a symbol of "soft professionalism."

Deep Dive

  • The Supreme Court is hearing a case between major music labels and internet provider Cox Communications regarding ISP liability for user copyright infringement.
  • The lawsuit highlights Cox's "13-strike" policy, with only 32 users terminated for infringement between 2013-2014, contrasted with 620,000 terminations for non-payment.
  • A 2017 Supreme Court ruling for Twitter (now X), stating that hosting content does not equate to aiding copyright infringement, may serve as a precedent.

  • New York State approved three new casino licenses, including an $8 billion complex by Mets owner Steve Cohen and Hard Rock near Citi Field in Queens.
  • Other approved projects include a $4 billion Bally's casino in the Bronx and an expansion of the Resorts World Casino near JFK Airport.
  • These casinos are projected to generate billions in tax revenue for New York State and the MTA, despite local concerns about increased traffic and crime.

  • Airbus stock declined up to 10% after reports of quality issues with its A320 aircraft, impacting airlines like JetBlue during Thanksgiving travel.
  • A software glitch, attributed to increased solar radiation, caused 15 passenger injuries and emergency landings, disrupting flights globally.
  • A separate issue involved fuselage panels on several dozen aircraft, prompting inspections as Airbus aims to deliver a record 160 jets in December.

  • The quarter-zip (Q-zip) is experiencing a resurgence in men's fashion, popularized by TikTok videos showcasing a shift towards a more "elegant" and "presentable" style.
  • This trend, dubbed "quarter zip dudes," signifies a move away from athletic brands towards "soft professionalism."
  • The style is gaining traction as professional attire, drawing parallels to past generational fashion shifts and LinkedIn's aesthetic.

  • Shopify experienced significant issues on Cyber Monday, a critical e-commerce shopping day.
  • The platform's downtime severely impacted merchants during one of the year's busiest sales periods.
  • This incident underscored Shopify's crucial role in the e-commerce infrastructure.
