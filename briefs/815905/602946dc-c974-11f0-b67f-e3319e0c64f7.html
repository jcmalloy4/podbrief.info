Morning Brew Daily

Producers Show! The Year of Fast-Casual Dining, Viral Marketing Moments and more

Key Takeaways

  • Fast-casual dining establishments saw a resurgence, driven by nostalgia and consumer demand for value.
  • Americans are increasingly reducing alcohol consumption, influenced by health concerns and rising costs.
  • Legacy media is undergoing significant transformation, exemplified by high-profile show cancellations and industry layoffs.
  • Controversial marketing campaigns can generate substantial financial and brand recognition benefits for companies.

Deep Dive

  • Chili's reported a 24% increase in quarterly sales, with unit volume rising significantly.
  • Dave & Buster's leveraged nostalgic appeal for its end-of-year holiday event, capitalizing on customers' youth memories.
  • Ambiance and atmosphere, alongside food, are key factors influencing consumer dining choices, as seen with Cracker Barrel's interior changes.
  • Taco Bell's popular menu item returns address consumer value-seeking amidst rising dining costs at establishments like McDonald's.

  • A Gallup poll indicated 53% of Americans view moderate drinking as unhealthy, a significant increase from 2015.
  • Average weekly alcohol consumption reached a multi-decade low of 2.8 drinks.
  • Trends include increased participation in 'Dry January' and Gen Z's evolving habits, such as not opening bar tabs.
  • High cocktail costs, up to $20 in cities like New York, contribute to younger demographics opting for less expensive alternatives.
  • The increasing availability and quality of non-alcoholic beverage options, and cannabis, are noted as alternatives.

  • CBS cancelled 'The Late Show with Stephen Colbert', citing financial challenges.
  • The decision followed Colbert's on-air criticism of a settlement by CBS's parent company, Paramount.
  • This event sparked speculation regarding the true reasons behind the show's termination.

  • The media landscape is experiencing a rapid transformation, marked by a ripple effect of cancellations and layoffs in legacy media.
  • Companies like Sinclair are making significant decisions impacting traditional programming, reflecting industry changes.
  • The success of news podcasts, such as Morning Brew Daily, highlights evolving opportunities in the dynamic media environment.
  • Producers noted the contrast between legacy media's 'well-oiled machine' production and the current fluid media landscape.

  • American Eagle's July ad campaign featuring Sydney Sweeney, under the slogan 'Sydney Has Great Jeans,' faced accusations of employing eugenics-like language.
  • Despite the controversy, including commentary from Donald Trump, the campaign reportedly boosted American Eagle's stock by 10%.
  • The campaign doubled web traffic and added an estimated $400 million in market value for American Eagle.
  • Other recent viral campaigns, such as those from Beyonce for Levi's and Gap, were debated for their effectiveness even with controversy.
