Trump Threatens Euro Tariffs Over Greenland & ChatGPT Adds Ads

Key Takeaways

  • A trade war between the U.S. and Europe over Greenland threatens global markets.
  • OpenAI plans to integrate ads into ChatGPT, aiming for $200 billion annual revenue by 2030.
  • A new $1 billion 'mini-sphere' entertainment venue is planned for National Harbor, D.C.
  • Netflix adapts content for distracted viewers, emphasizing repeated plot points and early action.
  • Chris Hone's TCI hedge fund achieved a record $18.9 billion gain in 2025.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump threatened a 10% tariff on eight European allies by February 1st, escalating to 25% by June, if Greenland is not purchased.
  • European allies are unified against the threats, preparing over $100 billion in retaliatory tariffs and an 'anti-coercion instrument'.
  • Markets reacted negatively, with major European automakers like Volkswagen, BMW, and Mercedes-Benz experiencing stock declines.

  • OpenAI is implementing ads in ChatGPT's free and lower-cost tiers, displayed above or below responses and clearly labeled.
  • This move aims to increase annual revenue from $20 billion to $200 billion by 2030 to achieve profitability.
  • Ads in ChatGPT will be contextually matched to conversation topics and allow for follow-up questions, differing from traditional internet advertising.

  • Sphere Entertainment plans a 6,000-seat 'mini-sphere' venue near Washington D.C., located at National Harbor, Maryland.
  • The project is expected to cost over $1 billion, with $200 million in financing already secured.
  • Local politicians view the venue as an economic boost for the region, which has experienced job losses.

  • Morning Brew Daily is broadcasting live from the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, describing the 'chaos energy' of the conference.
  • The broadcast is in partnership with Lightspeed Venture Partners, who created a 'lighthouse' space to showcase portfolio companies and founders.
  • Lightspeed is featuring special interviews with founders on their YouTube channel, including Deepak Partak of Skilled AI.

  • Netflix is adapting its content for viewers who often multitask while watching, typically on their phones.
  • This strategy involves incorporating earlier action sequences and verbally repeating plot details within films to maintain audience engagement.
  • The approach is a response to changing viewing habits that necessitate clearer exposition than traditional cinema.

  • Chris Hone's hedge fund, TCI, achieved a record-breaking year in 2025, earning $18.9 billion.
  • The fund's success was driven by a concentrated portfolio consisting of nine holdings.
  • TCI's top-performing holding, General Electric, gained 86% during the year.
