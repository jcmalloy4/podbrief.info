Pardon My Take

NFL Week 11, Fastest 2 Minutes, Broncos Huge Win Against The Chiefs, Rams Control NFC West, Bears 7-3 And The Eagles Win Ugly + Who's Back Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 11 featured multiple close matchups, including overtime victories for the Dolphins and Panthers, and significant upsets.
  • Notable player performances included Josh Allen's dual-threat game for the Bills and Christian McCaffrey's multi-touchdown effort for the 49ers.
  • Coaching decisions and their impact on game outcomes and playoff standings were central themes in several team analyses.
  • The 'Internet Invitational' segment discussed a cheating scandal, an emotional tribute, and conspiracy theories surrounding the event.
  • Media accountability was a focus, highlighted by a specific instance of sports news misinformation and its consequences.

Deep Dive

  • Pittsburgh Steelers defeated Cincinnati Bengals 34-12, aided by a key fumble return touchdown.
  • Buffalo Bills secured a 44-32 victory over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, with Josh Allen throwing multiple touchdowns.
  • Los Angeles Rams narrowly beat the Seattle Seahawks 21-19, as the Seahawks committed four costly interceptions.

  • Buccaneers coach Todd Bowles was criticized for punting on fourth and two from the Bills' 40-yard line.
  • Despite their loss, the Buccaneers are projected to win their division due to a favorable remaining schedule.
  • The Panthers face a more challenging upcoming schedule, including games against the Rams, 49ers, and Seahawks.

  • The Chicago Bears defeated the Minnesota Vikings 19-17, prompting debate on their status as a 'good' team due to winning close games.
  • The Lions and Bears were identified as the most likely NFC North playoff representatives.
  • Concerns were raised about the Green Bay Packers' playoff resume following a significant loss to the Browns.
  • Aaron Rodgers' potential return against the Bears was discussed, with reports of a broken wrist possibly sidelining him for Mason Rudolph.

  • The Green Bay Packers secured a 27-20 victory over the New York Giants in windy conditions, leading to three missed extra points.
  • Key offensive plays included Jameis Winston's 10-yard 'Crab Walk' quarterback sneak and Jordan Love's critical third-down throw to Savion Williams.
  • Christian Watson also contributed with touchdown catches, but fan frustration with the offense was noted despite the win.

  • The Pittsburgh Steelers secured a 34-12 victory over the Cincinnati Bengals, but hosts questioned their status as a 'good' team despite a 6-4 record.
  • Tight end Darnell Washington was highlighted for his physical impact, described as a 'rhinoceros' on the field.
  • Discussion also touched on the potential performance of Mason Rudolph if Aaron Rodgers' season ends due to a broken hand.

  • The Los Angeles Rams defeated the Seattle Seahawks 21-19, despite Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold throwing four interceptions.
  • The Seahawks' defense was credited for keeping the game close by limiting the Rams' yards and first downs in the final three quarters.
  • Hosts debated Darnold's potential, questioning his ability to lead a Super Bowl-caliber team given his turnovers.

  • The San Francisco 49ers defeated the Arizona Cardinals 41-22, with Brock Purdy returning and the offense performing strongly.
  • Christian McCaffrey was a key player, scoring three touchdowns and accumulating 120 yards from scrimmage.
  • Jacoby Brissett set a record with 47 completions in the regular season game, while the Cardinals committed 17 penalties in their loss.

  • Hosts engaged in a debate about the 'best year for movies,' proposing and discussing various years including 1992, 1994, 1995, 1998, and 1999.
  • Films mentioned included 'The Matrix,' 'Fight Club,' 'Forrest Gump,' 'Saving Private Ryan,' 'The Lion King,' and 'Dumb and Dumber.'
  • The discussion highlighted the hosts' self-proclaimed lack of extensive movie knowledge.

  • The Denver Broncos achieved a 22-19 upset victory over the Kansas City Chiefs, improving their home record to 6-0.
  • Hosts debated if the Broncos could secure the AFC's number one seed, potentially making the road to the Super Bowl run through Denver.
  • Bo Nix's performance and the team's strong offensive line were highlighted as factors making them a difficult playoff opponent.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles defeated the Detroit Lions 16-9, with discussion focusing on defensive performance.
  • A controversial play where A.J. Brown appeared to stop running on a deep pass indicated potential offensive dissatisfaction.
  • Debate also covered coach Nick Sirianni's decision to go for it on fourth down late in the game while up by 10 points, a move some considered statistically risky despite the team's 8-2 record.

  • Detroit Lions coach Dan Campbell's aggressive play-calling was a key topic, particularly his decision-making in windy conditions.
  • A controversial late-game field goal attempt against the Eagles was highlighted as an example of his aggressive approach.
  • The hosts noted the 'chaotic factor' of Campbell handling multiple responsibilities, impacting the team's strategies in critical moments.

  • PFT's 'Who's Back of the Week' segment highlighted a false rumor spread by 'JMU Sports News' regarding ESPN College GameDay visiting James Madison University.
  • The blog's subsequent apology and potential closure were discussed, with hosts questioning the severity of shutting down an entire site for one incorrect report.
  • Past instances of misinformation, including a fake quote attributed to Greg Sankey, were also referenced.
