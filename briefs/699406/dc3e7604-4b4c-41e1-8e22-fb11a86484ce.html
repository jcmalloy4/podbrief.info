Seahawks Win TNF Classic In OT, Week 16 Picks And Preview, Mike Florio, Joey Harrington + Fyre Fest Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • The Seahawks secured an overtime victory against the Rams in a thrilling Thursday Night Football game.
  • NFL Week 16 features numerous high-stakes matchups with significant playoff implications for multiple teams.
  • Concerns are raised regarding Joe Burrow's long-term contentment and future with the Cincinnati Bengals.
  • Discussions cover the complex NFL playoff picture and the potential for league expansion, including international teams.
  • Mike Florio and Joey Harrington provide expert analysis on current NFL news and college football playoff contenders.

Deep Dive

  • The Wisconsin Badgers volleyball team experienced a season-ending loss, with player Eva Hudson praised.
  • Discussions shifted to the upcoming College Football National Championship game.
  • Hosts provided picks for college football playoff games, favoring Oregon and JMU.

  • The hosts discussed upcoming streamed Saturday games, including Commanders-Eagles and Bears-Packers.
  • Opinions were shared on the Commanders' chances and betting strategies.
  • Consideration was given to Week 18 'win and get in' scenarios for various teams.

  • A perceived shift in the Dallas Cowboys' performance and team mood was analyzed.
  • Wide receiver George Pickens' apparent dip in form was compared to an 'energy vampire'.
  • Hosts suggested bad vibes within the team and potential Jerry Jones influence.

  • A statistic highlights that 22 quarterbacks have made their starting debut against the Bengals.
  • The Bengals have a winning record in those 22 games.
  • The upcoming week will mark the 23rd such quarterback debut against Cincinnati.

  • Discussions centered on rookie receiver A-Buka and failures to adjust fantasy lineups.
  • The pitfalls of not setting lineups and overvaluing players after a few good games were highlighted.
  • One host recounted forfeiting a game in a two-quarterback league due to bye weeks and injuries.

  • Potential consequences for Pete Carroll and Geno Smith were discussed if they return next season.
  • Hosts debated whether Carroll's tenure, age, or personality would influence the Seahawks' decision.

  • TD Parlay picks included Stefon Diggs, Trey McBride, and Jameson Williams.
  • Best bets covered Eagles -6.5, Lions/Steelers over 51, Buccaneers -3, Vikings/Giants over 43.5, and 49ers -6.
  • Additional bets included Chargers/Cowboys over 49.5 and the 'Shuckster' -4.5.

  • Discussions centered on Joe Burrow's potential desire to be traded from the Bengals.
  • One host suggested Burrow is attempting a 'Jedi mind trick' to prompt a trade without explicitly asking.
  • Burrow's careful press conference responses and past openness to XFL opportunities were noted.

  • The strength of the NFC was noted, with six teams competing for limited playoff spots.
  • The volatility of teams with younger quarterbacks was discussed.
  • Potential for NFL expansion, including international teams and a 40-team league with 16 playoff teams, was considered.
