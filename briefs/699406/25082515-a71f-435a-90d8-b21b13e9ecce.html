Pardon My Take

Stanford Steve Talking Ball, NFL Week 14 Picks & Preview, Lions End The Cowboys, Lebron's Streak Ends, Fyre Fest And More

Key Takeaways

  • The Detroit Lions significantly improved their playoff odds to 53% after a crucial win over the Cowboys.
  • LeBron James' impressive 1,297-game scoring streak ended, influencing both fans and bettors.
  • NFL Week 14 presented multiple games with significant playoff implications for various teams.
  • The College Football Playoff committee faced criticism for its ranking methodologies.
  • Mike Tomlin's coaching future with the Pittsburgh Steelers became a topic of ongoing speculation.
  • Stanford Steve joined the podcast, sharing his insights on college and NFL football.

Deep Dive

  • Stanford Steve, from ESPN's Game Day and SVP podcast, joined as a guest.
  • The hosts designated him their "number one dude" due to his strength of schedule.
  • Steve questioned if his top ranking was a committee decision, which was affirmed by the hosts with "strong data points."

  • The Detroit Lions secured a significant win over the Dallas Cowboys, boosting their playoff odds to 53%.
  • Amon-Ra St. Brown and Jahmyr Gibbs were key offensive contributors; Dallas's turnovers were a factor in their loss.
  • The Cowboys' playoff probability consequently decreased to 8% after the defeat.
  • Jameson Williams' "electric playmaking ability" was noted.

  • Week 14 NFL picks highlighted several games with significant playoff implications.
  • Matchups included Ravens-Steelers, Bears-Packers, and Texans-Chiefs.
  • The Bengals-Bills game was identified as the most anticipated early game, marking the last week with bye teams.

  • Discussion noted Mike Tomlin's historical success as an underdog coach for the Steelers-Ravens game.
  • Rumors suggested Tomlin might move to Penn State, impacting both the university and the Steelers.
  • Tomlin's recent quotes included agreement with fans calling for his firing.

  • Hosts debated whether Jalen Daniels or Marcus Mariota should play, weighing injury risk vs. momentum.
  • A blind resume comparison favored Daniels despite some lower statistical rankings.
  • Statistics cited included EPA per dropback and completion percentage.

  • The Broncos, favored by 7.5 points vs. Raiders, faced scrutiny over close games.
  • A win against the Patriots held significant NFL playoff seeding implications.
  • Riley Moss leads the NFL in pass interference calls.

  • Matthew Stafford's high number of pick-sixes, second only to Brett Favre, was discussed.
  • This statistic was deemed a testament to his longevity and overall skill over an extended period.
  • Sean McVay's strong December record was noted.

  • The discussion focused on identifying teams capable of winning a national championship.
  • Oklahoma's potential playoff appearance was noted, but hosts doubted their championship capabilities.
  • Texas Tech was identified as a strong defensive contender, despite offensive line concerns.

  • Speculation continued regarding Mike Tomlin's future with the Steelers, with predictions of a mutual departure.
  • Hosts debated if a coaching change was due and his immediate hiring elsewhere.
  • Tennessee Titans or New York Giants were discussed as potential landing spots if he were to leave Pittsburgh.
