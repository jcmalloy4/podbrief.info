Pardon My Take

NFL Week 5, Fastest 2 Minutes, Broncos Big Win, Bucs Win In A Shootout, Jayden Daniels Back + Who's Back And Playoff Baseball

Key Takeaways

  • Several NFL Week 5 games featured surprising finishes, including comeback victories by the Broncos and Panthers.
  • The Vikings defeated the Browns 21-17 in London, highlighting strong performances and a game-winning drive.
  • The Cowboys secured a dominant 37-22 win over the Jets, who remain the last winless team in the league.
  • The Colts achieved a decisive 40-6 victory against the Raiders, raising questions about Geno Smith's performance.
  • The Cardinals suffered a dramatic 22-21 loss to the Titans after blowing a 28-6 fourth-quarter lead.
  • A heated argument between hosts Hank and Max, fueled by sports betting outcomes, escalated physically.
  • The retirement of the Carolina Panthers podcast 'Meow Mix' sparked discussion on fan dedication and mental health.
  • Playoff baseball discussions highlighted critical managerial decisions and team performances across multiple series.

Deep Dive

  • The Buccaneers narrowly defeated the Seahawks 38-35 in Seattle.
  • Rashad White scored a touchdown for the Buccaneers.
  • Geno Smith connected with Jaxon Smith-Njigba for the Seahawks in the high-scoring game.

  • The Broncos secured a significant 21-17 road victory against the Eagles.
  • Bo Nix led a fourth-quarter comeback, completing 9 of 10 passes for 127 yards and a touchdown.
  • The Broncos' defense sacked Jalen Hurts six times, limiting Saquon Barkley to six carries.
  • The Eagles' offensive strategy shifted to frequent passing with only one handoff in the second half.

  • A dispute between Hank and Max in the 'gambling cave' escalated physically over Hank's parlay bet on the Broncos.
  • Max became angry during a football game, punching a wall and throwing a hat due to betting outcomes, including Broncos and Commanders.
  • One host admitted to intentionally stirring the pot at times, linking incidents to the emotional toll of sports betting.

  • The hosts discussed the emotional toll of sports betting and team losses following recent Phillies, Penn State, and Eagles games.
  • A blind resume comparison between Jalen Hurts and Russell Wilson was presented, noting their common opponents in the Cowboys and Chiefs.

  • Dak Prescott's strong performance, including scoring multiple touchdowns, put him in the MVP conversation for the Cowboys.
  • The Cowboys secured a 37-22 victory over the Jets in New York.
  • The Jets are noted as the last team without a win and have a historically unprecedented lack of takeaways through five games.
  • Jake Ferguson and Javonte Williams were highlighted for their contributions to the Cowboys' 'electric' offense.

  • The Indianapolis Colts secured a 40-6 victory over the Las Vegas Raiders.
  • Daniel Jones demonstrated improved play, connecting for a touchdown pass with Michael Pittman.
  • Geno Smith's performance for the Raiders was questioned, noting his nine interceptions through five weeks and his career earnings estimated at $107 million.

  • The Carolina Panthers podcast 'Meow Mix' retired after its creators, Steven and Jerry, realized covering the team's losing record since 2019 negatively impacted their lives and families.
  • The hosts debated if team-specific podcasts improve mental health, suggesting disclaimers or releasing episodes only after a team win.
  • Speculation arose about the financial aspects of personal podcasts, including hypothetical sponsorship from entities like the Saudis or PFT.

  • The Titans defeated the Cardinals 22-21 in a chaotic fourth quarter, despite Arizona holding a 28-6 lead.
  • The Cardinals committed several costly errors, including fumbling at the one-inch line before scoring.
  • An interception was returned for a touchdown against the Cardinals, marking their third consecutive loss on a final-play score.

  • The Lions defeated the Bengals 37-24, extending their streak of covering the spread with multiple players scoring touchdowns.
  • Michigan teams were noted for currently outperforming Ohio teams, including the Lions beating the Bengals and Browns, and the Tigers defeating the Guardians.
  • A 'stripe out' attempt at the Lions-Bengals game by Bengals fans was reportedly overridden by Lions fans.

  • The Commanders' season outlook included acknowledgment of a 'scheduled loss' in their recent game.
  • Hope was expressed for Terry McLaurin's return, and positive performances from Debo and Luke were noted.
  • Discussion touched on perceived negativity towards Jaden Daniels from Bears fans and a plea for kindness towards PFT in public.

  • PFT was selected as 'Who's Back' for holding a football, and Taylor Swift was also nominated for her new album release.
  • Swift's new album met with mixed reactions and fan dissection, including online discourse and 'Gaylor Swift' theories.
  • Hank was named 'Who's Back' for his support of Drake May, and his upcoming $5,000 golf match against PFT was discussed.

  • The Mariners' series against the Tigers went to extra innings, with the Mariners winning Game 2 and gaining support due to their long playoff drought.
  • The Blue Jays delivered a dominant 13-7 performance against the Yankees, featuring Vlad Jr.'s grand slam.
  • Questionable managerial decisions were highlighted for the Cubs, specifically pitching Matthew Boyd on short rest in Game 1, and for the Phillies' struggles with their top of the order.
