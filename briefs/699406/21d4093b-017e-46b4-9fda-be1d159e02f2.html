  • Terry McLaurin signed a three-year, $96 million extension with the Commanders, ending his holdout.
  • Trey Hendrickson received a $14 million raise from the Bengals, increasing his 2025 salary.
  • Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's reported engagement was a significant topic of discussion.

Deep Dive

  • Washington Commander Terry McLaurin signed a three-year, $96 million extension, including a $30 million signing bonus, ending his holdout.
  • Cincinnati Bengals' Trey Hendrickson received a $14 million raise for the current year, boosting his 2025 salary while retaining free agency status after the season.
  • Concerns over McLaurin potentially missing training camp were expressed, though confidence in his professionalism remained high.
  • Quarterback Tommy DeVito was unexpectedly released; Spencer Rattler was named the New Orleans Saints' Week 1 starter.

  • Attendees of the Burning Man festival faced extreme weather conditions, including high winds and flash flood warnings.
  • The festival experienced chaotic traffic and a reportedly damaged 'orgy dome.'
  • The hosts humorously compared the event's troubles to other challenging situations.

  • The podcast segment 'Mount Rushmore of things girls hate about guys' began, with the hosts defining competitive parameters.
  • An honorable mention included a partner not attending physical therapy for an injury, claiming they will be fine.
  • Early listed grievances included forgetting to leave the toilet seat up and an inability to find items in plain sight.

  • Further 'Mount Rushmore' entries included men not talking about emotions, 'man-splaining,' and experiencing road rage.
  • Other points highlighted men's varied hobbies, interest in parlays, and urinating on the side of the road.
  • Fashion choices, specifically repeatedly wearing the same outfit, and watching numerous documentaries on a single topic were also mentioned.

  • Golfer Tommy Fleetwood discussed his Tour Championship victory in Atlanta and the support received from figures like LeBron James and Caitlin Clark.
  • He detailed maintaining focus on the challenging 15th hole and managing thoughts of past close calls.
  • Fleetwood acknowledged monitoring Scottie Scheffler's leaderboard climb but prioritized his own game.
  • His fan favorite catchphrase, 'I'm just trying my hardest,' became a meme chanted by fans.

  • Tommy Fleetwood discussed the emotional process after a close golf loss, emphasizing the importance of positive self-talk for future success.
  • He confirmed that moments of doubt are common in athletic careers, but perseverance and mental fortitude are key motivators.
  • Fleetwood noted his growing interest in American sports, specifically basketball, naming Steph Curry and the Golden State Warriors as favorites.
  • He confirmed no change in play during his win, attributing success to circumstances aligning.

  • Yahoo Sports writer Ross Dellinger discussed Barstool Sports' reported ban from Ohio State's campus for their game coverage.
  • Ohio State's decision to deny Barstool College Football's presence was viewed as a move to control media access and avoid negative optics.
  • This situation was suggested to potentially benefit Barstool's founder, Dave Portnoy, who thrives in the spotlight.

  • The SEC Championship game was projected to be LSU versus Georgia, with LSU predicted to win the conference.
  • Penn State was selected to win the Big Ten, potentially beating Ohio State, citing defensive coordinator Jim Knowles and quarterback Drew Allar's improvements.
  • Clemson was projected to win the ACC and make the playoff.
  • Baylor was picked to win the unpredictable Big 12 due to returning players and significant portal additions.

  • The hosts addressed a listener question regarding the acceptable age for a second television in the living room, debating its status as a symbol of personal space.
  • They discussed the functionality of an ice maker, confirming its positive performance.
  • The hosts announced their office turtle, Mr. Pear, is seeking adoption due to perceived unhappiness and need for a new home.
