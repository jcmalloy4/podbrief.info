Pardon My Take

NFL Week 1, Fastest 2 Minutes, Dolphins Are Terrible, Bills/Ravens Game Of The Year (Through 1 Week) And Packers Look Unstoppable + Who's Back Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 1 featured several competitive matchups, with pivotal plays influencing game outcomes.
  • Quarterback performances, including those of rookies and veterans, were closely scrutinized.
  • Defensive units proved decisive in multiple games, demonstrating significant impact.
  • Special teams, notably clutch field goals, emerged as critical factors in securing victories.
  • Off-field human interest stories, such as a fan wedding, added unique elements to the season's start.

Deep Dive

  • The Steelers defeated the Jets 34-32 in a high-scoring game that spoiled Aaron Rodgers' debut.
  • Justin Fields' performance and plays by Jets defender Will McDonald were highlighted.
  • Steelers kicker Chris Boswell secured the win with a critical 60-yard field goal.

  • The Steelers offense was noted as 'kind of fun,' with quarterback play drawing comparisons to pre-injury Ben Roethlisberger.
  • Wide receiver DK Metcalf was recognized for his speed and contributions to the offense.
  • Chris Boswell was a primary candidate in a debate over the best kicker in the NFL, due to his performance in big moments.

  • The Bengals-Browns game in Cleveland was preceded by a fan wedding held in the muni lot.
  • The wedding featured Browns-themed registry items, drawing the podcast hosts' attention.
  • The Bengals secured a narrow 30-29 victory over the Browns, despite a sluggish offensive second half with only seven net yards.

  • The Washington Commanders defeated the New York Giants 21-6, with their defense highlighted as a key factor.
  • The Commanders' defensive tackles were noted for their effectiveness, contributing to the team's first shutout since 2020.
  • Quarterback Jaden Daniels, a seventh-round pick, impressed with his play and reportedly wore a Japanese flag decal on his helmet, revealing his Japanese heritage.

  • The Indianapolis Colts secured a dominant 33-8 victory over the Miami Dolphins in Indianapolis.
  • Colts quarterback Daniel Jones led the team to score on every drive, a feat noted as not achieved by Peyton Manning or Andrew Luck.
  • Dolphins quarterback Tua Tagovailoa turned over the ball multiple times, contributing to the team's offensive struggles.

  • The New England Patriots suffered a loss to the Dolphins, raising questions about the team's overall ranking.
  • The pairing of Drake Maye and Josh McDaniels was discussed regarding player development and system adaptation.
  • The Dolphins secured a dominant 30-3 victory over the Patriots in their Week 1 matchup.

  • The Jaguars defeated the Panthers 26-10 in a game that experienced a lightning delay.
  • Trevor Lawrence matched Blake Bortles' passing record during the game.
  • Liam Cohen's coaching record and Jordan Lewis's defensive performance were noted in the Jaguars' win.

  • The Rams defeated the Texans 14-9 in a low-scoring affair.
  • The Texans' offensive struggles were attributed to D'Amico Ryans' defensive schemes and a lack of offensive weapons, including a weak running game and offensive line.
  • Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford reached a milestone of 60,000 career passing yards, while Puka Nacua recorded 10 catches for 130 yards.

  • The Denver Broncos defeated the Titans 20-12, despite their offense committing four turnovers.
  • Bo Nix's performance for the Broncos was questioned, while Titans' quarterback Will Levis showed potential due to his composure under pressure.
  • The Titans accumulated 133 penalty yards, matching their total offensive yards, significantly hindering their chances.
