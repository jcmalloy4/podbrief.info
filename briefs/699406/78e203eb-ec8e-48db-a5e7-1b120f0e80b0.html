CFB Quarterfinals, Georgia, Ohio State and Bama Go Down, Week 18 Picks And Preview, Jerry O’Connell Recapping Our Fantasy Season And Fyre Fest Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • College Football Playoff quarterfinals featured major upsets, reshaping the semifinal picture.
  • NFL Week 18 playoff implications and individual player incentives are significant.
  • Hosts shared personal New Year's resolutions for fitness, languages, and new experiences.
  • A fantasy football championship debrief occurred with guest Jerry O'Connell.
  • The episode concluded with "Fyre Fest of the Week" segment highlighting various anecdotes.

Deep Dive

  • Miami upset Ohio State, with coach Mario Cristobal's physical play touted for a national title run.
  • Indiana dominated Alabama, with quarterback Mendoza playing a "perfect game" and the team praised as superior.
  • Ole Miss achieved an upset victory over Georgia, propelled by quarterback Trinidad Chambliss and kicker Mendoza.
  • The semifinals now feature Indiana vs. Oregon and Ole Miss vs. Miami, a shift from traditional powerhouses.

  • Drake Maye is discussed as the likely MVP candidate due to recent performance.
  • The Broncos vs. Chargers game is previewed, questioning the Chargers' motivation under Jim Harbaugh.
  • The Jaguars' potential to win and affect playoff seeding in the AFC is a key storyline.
  • Concerns are raised about Quinn Ewers and the Patriots' historical end-of-season struggles against the Dolphins.

  • Hosts reviewed past resolutions, with one achieving physical and dental check-ups, resulting in a cavity and oral surgery.
  • New fitness goals include attending cycling classes, trying jiu-jitsu, and becoming an ice bath user.
  • One host expressed a desire to learn conversational Spanish for future international travel.
  • Another host is considering Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT) as part of fitness and weight loss goals.

  • The Seahawks vs. 49ers Saturday night game is highlighted as crucial for NFC Super Bowl aspirations.
  • The winner of Seahawks-49ers could secure home-field advantage throughout the playoffs, potentially reducing games played.
  • Brock Purdy's strong performance since returning from injury, with a 6-0 record and leading statistics, is noted.
  • The Colts vs. Texans game is emphasized for the AFC South title, with flexible scheduling proposed for divisional finales.

  • The Houston Texans seek home-field advantage due to a strong home record and weak road performance.
  • The Chargers are identified as the likely seventh seed in the AFC playoffs.
  • Opposing quarterbacks against the New York Jets have collectively achieved an MVP-caliber season with 32 touchdowns and zero interceptions.
  • Buffalo Bills coach Sean McDermott is prioritizing James Cook potentially winning the rushing title, with Cook 47 yards ahead of Jonathan Taylor.

  • Guest Jerry O'Connell joined for an exit interview following the fantasy team's runner-up finish.
  • Discussion included the cost of a new heating system in relation to the fantasy entry fee.
  • Roster decisions, such as using Jacoby Brissett as a backup and overlooking Tyler Shuck, were questioned.
  • Adam Schefter provided input via video message, congratulating O'Connell and offering co-management for next season.

  • One host recounted a golf outing in Mexico where a left-handed social media post while hungover drew negative reactions.
  • Another shared an incident in Florida where two individuals presented a printed photo of the 2015 Duke basketball team.
  • A host described a child's bowel movement closing a condo pool and disposing of soiled underwear in an Airbnb.
  • A rideshare experience involved a spider dropping from the visor onto the driver, causing the car to swerve.
