NFL Week 14, Fastest 2 Minutes, Bills Snow Bowl Win, Packers Beat The Bears, Chiefs Might Be Done + CFP Bracket Reveal And Who's Back Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 14 featured significant upsets and dominant performances impacting playoff races.
  • Officiating controversies and quarterback performances were central themes across multiple NFL matchups.
  • The College Football Playoff selection process generated considerable debate regarding team inclusions and exclusions.
  • Several teams, including the Bills, Packers, and Texans, demonstrated surprising strengths or weaknesses.
  • The episode covered a 'Who's Back of the Week' segment and a discussion on a 'positivity bunny' in the Eagles' locker room.

Deep Dive

  • The Pittsburgh Steelers defeated the Baltimore Ravens 27-22.
  • Officiating controversies, including two calls favoring the Steelers and an overturned Isaiah Likely touchdown, were discussed.
  • The Ravens' red zone struggles and Mike Tomlin's motivated performance were noted following criticism.

  • The New Orleans Saints defeated the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 24-20.
  • Quarterback Tyler Shuck contributed a key scramble to break a tie and a long touchdown pass.
  • Buccaneers' Baker Mayfield experienced a potential injury and the team struggled with key drops and offensive inconsistencies.

  • The Tampa Bay Buccaneers currently hold a 4-seed in the NFC despite a 7-6 record.
  • Baker Mayfield's potential injury and offensive inconsistencies raised questions about the team's quality.
  • The close game against the Saints and key drops by Buccaneers players contributed to speculation about their playoff chances.

  • Potential NFL playoff matchups involving the Los Angeles Chargers and Jacksonville Jaguars were discussed.
  • The complexity of playoff seeding was highlighted, including the possibility of the Houston Texans overtaking the Chargers for a spot.
  • Differing prediction models were referenced for analyzing potential game outcomes and their impact on seeding.

  • The Minnesota Vikings achieved a dominant 31-0 victory over the Commanders.
  • Quarterback '9' led a 98-yard touchdown drive, contributing to the Vikings' scoring.
  • The Commanders were labeled a bottom-five team, with quarterback Jaden Daniels re-injuring his elbow and coach Dan Quinn's future questioned.

  • The Miami Dolphins secured a fourth consecutive win, improving their record to 6-7 and keeping them technically in playoff contention.
  • Despite a dominant 34-10 win over the Jets, hosts expressed skepticism about the Dolphins' overall quality and estimated playoff chances at 1-3%.
  • Zach Wilson's two-yard performance and an exceptional incompletion by Brady Cook (negated by pass interference) were noted in the Jets-Dolphins game.

  • The Denver Broncos defeated the Raiders 24-17 in a game featuring a controversial penalty that allowed the Raiders to cover the spread.
  • Denver is now in first place in the AFC, controlling their playoff tiebreaker despite a challenging remaining schedule.
  • The Raiders' disastrous season included a touchdown pass from Kenny Pickett to Shedrick Jackson, their first from a player over 24 years old this season.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles introduced an inflatable "positivity bunny" into their locker room.
  • Hosts debated whether this constitutes a "drastic change" and its potential impact on team morale and performance.
  • The bunny's presence was discussed in the context of recent team performance and alleged incidents involving the offensive coordinator.

  • The Green Bay Packers defeated the Chicago Bears 28-21, despite the Bears' improved second-half performance.
  • Jordan Love's performance during a 'Toyota-thon' period (late November to early January) was highlighted, with 36 TDs and 2 INTs compared to his career 44 TDs and 27 INTs.
  • The Packers are being considered potential Super Bowl contenders, drawing comparisons to teams like the Rams and Seahawks.

  • The Kansas City Chiefs lost to the Houston Texans, raising questions about Andy Reid's decision to go for it on fourth down near his own 30-yard line.
  • The Texans displayed a strong defensive performance, while the Chiefs struggled offensively with dropped passes and injuries.
  • Rookie quarterback CJ Stroud played impressively, leading hosts to believe the Texans are a legitimate playoff contender.

  • The final College Football Playoff bracket sparked controversy, particularly regarding Notre Dame's exclusion and Alabama's inclusion despite a conference championship loss.
  • A Memorandum of Understanding was discussed, ensuring Notre Dame a CFP berth if ranked in the top 12 starting next year.
  • The possibility of a 16-team college football playoff bracket and its implications for teams like Michigan and JMU were also debated.

  • The FIFA World Cup draw was humorously discussed, with comments on perceived bias and the presence of figures like Tom Brady and Wayne Gretzky.
  • Shaquille O'Neal drew Australia for the U.S. men's national team, which was deemed the easiest path to the World Cup.
  • Wayne Gretzky's pronunciation issues and Kevin Hart's role as host during the lengthy FIFA presentation were also noted.
