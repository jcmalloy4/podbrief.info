Pardon My Take

Future Hall of Famer Zack Martin In Studio, Week 8 Picks And Preview, NBA Gambling Controversy, Carson Wentz Horrible TNF + Fyre Fest Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • Carson Wentz's poor Thursday Night Football performance drew concern, contrasting with the Los Angeles Chargers' improved play.
  • The NBA is embroiled in a significant gambling scandal, including player arrests and alleged game manipulation.
  • The podcast provided an extensive Week 8 NFL preview, covering multiple games, betting trends, and fantasy football advice.
  • Dallas Cowboys offensive lineman Zack Martin offered candid insights into his career, retirement, and the team's internal dynamics.
  • Discussions ranged from specific player statistics and coaching critiques to broader issues like team complacency and gambling ethics.

Deep Dive

  • Carson Wentz displayed a difficult Thursday Night Football performance, accumulating numerous sacks.
  • The Los Angeles Chargers demonstrated improved play with Joe Alt's return, highlighting their strong rushing game.
  • Speculation arose regarding Wentz's potential shoulder injury and the Chargers' prospects for a Super Bowl victory.

  • The NBA is facing widespread gambling issues, with legal experts discussing player arrests and ongoing investigations.
  • Allegations include the use of modified automatic shufflers and off-site communication systems by players.
  • Hosts questioned motivations for high-earning athletes to engage in illicit betting, citing past incidents like Chauncey Billups' alleged involvement.
  • The NBA's prop betting environment is noted as highly susceptible to player influence compared to other major sports.
  • Discussions included suggestions for lifetime bans versus allowing players to bet freely on games.

  • DraftKings offered a new customer promotion: bet $5, get $300 in bonus bets instantly if the bet wins.
  • 'Jerry's Fantasy Minute' provided advice on streaming quarterbacks and defenses, mentioning Dalton Kincaid and Hunter Henry.
  • Current standings in the best bets competition show Memes and Max leading with eight points.
  • Week's picks included Falcons -7.5, Cowboys +3.5, Packers -3, and the Dolphins-Falcons over 44.5.

  • NFL Week 8 features six teams on bye (Lions, Raiders, Rams, Cardinals, Seahawks, Jaguars) and no London game.
  • The Sunday night game showcases Aaron Rodgers' Jets against his former team, the Green Bay Packers.
  • NFC teams have a record of 26-12 against the spread over AFC teams since 2022.
  • Specific game analyses included the Dolphins vs. Falcons (Falcons 7-point favorites) and Bijan Robinson's 46 forced missed tackles.

  • New York Jets owner Woody Johnson's comments on his quarterback's inability to complete a pass signaled internal dysfunction.
  • Statistical anomalies were noted, such as Justin Fields' zero interceptions but zero wins in his starts.
  • Joe Flacco was discussed as a Comeback Player of the Year candidate based on his return to play.
  • The Bengals are favored by seven points against the Jets, despite their own defensive and offensive line issues.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles are 7.5-point favorites against the Giants, with an undefeated record in their Kelly Green uniforms.
  • Discussion included A.J. Brown's potential discontent and Jalen Hurts' perceived need to strategically utilize him.
  • Concerns arose over the Eagles' offensive line injuries, specifically Cam Jurgens' absence, impacting their game against the Giants' defensive line.

  • The Baltimore Ravens recently removed ping pong tables from their locker room.
  • The Chicago Bears are noted as improving under coach Ben Johnson, exceeding expectations due to a high, but unsustainable, rate of takeaways.
  • Hosts debated the Bears' chances against the Ravens and the potential narrative shift a Bears victory could create.

  • Brandon Aubrey of the Cowboys has -110 odds for a record-breaking 70-yard field goal attempt.
  • Sean Payton's public criticism of Russell Wilson was a point of discussion regarding the Cowboys vs. Broncos game.
  • The segment included 'Hungry Dog' picks for the week, featuring teams like the Cowboys, Bears, and Titans.
  • The hosts recalled a previous competitive golf match with a $6,000 stake, featured on their YouTube channel.

  • Dallas Cowboys offensive lineman Zach Martin discussed his career, including seven First-Team All-Pros and nine Pro Bowls.
  • He recounted winning the Pinstripe Bowl MVP and detailed how the trophy was lost in a lake.
  • Martin stated he felt excitement at the start of the season but does not regret his retirement decision, feeling it was the 'perfect time'.
  • He shared a story about his son's admiration for Cowboys player CeeDee Lamb and meeting him on the field.

  • Discussion covered Zach Martin's experience with Tony Romo during his rookie year.
  • Dak Prescott took over as the Cowboys' starter in 2016 following Romo's injuries.
  • Martin shared a humorous anecdote about encountering Romo at a golf event in Tahoe.
  • The segment highlighted the immense pressure Dak Prescott faces as the Dallas Cowboys' quarterback.

  • The Dallas Cowboys' offense was praised as one of the league's best.
  • Inconsistencies on defense were noted, despite the team losing a key player before the season began.
  • The dynamic play of offensive players like CeeDee Lamb and Jake Ferguson forces the Cowboys to score on nearly every possession.
  • Hosts expressed a belief that the Cowboys' defense cannot worsen.

  • Zach Martin discusses his post-retirement focus on family and golf, noting his wife is pregnant.
  • He confirmed the Cowboys made the correct draft pick choosing him over Johnny Manziel.
  • Martin expressed willingness to scuba dive to locate his Pinstripe Bowl trophy, submerged for approximately eight to nine years.
  • He recalled a host jokingly calling him a 'bust' on draft night, a prediction the host applied to many players.

  • The 'Fyre Fest' segment focused on 'bad vibes' associated with early winter, Chicago weather, and early sunsets.
  • One host considered a diet of soup, salad, and smoothies, while another admitted to weight gain from an 'ice cream machine' and late-night eating.
  • A host recounted being kicked out of a fantasy football league mid-season due to alleged commissioner manipulation and rule changes.
