Pardon My Take

CFB With Brandon Walker, CFP Rankings, Patriots Crush The Giants, Hot Seat/Cool Throne + Listener FAQ's

Key Takeaways

  • College Football Playoff rankings drew significant criticism, particularly for James Madison's inclusion.
  • The New England Patriots secured an 11-2 record with a decisive win against the New York Giants.
  • Mike Tomlin's Pittsburgh Steelers face scrutiny amid a decade without playoff wins and offensive struggles.
  • Lane Kiffin's controversial move from Ole Miss to LSU escalated the rivalry and disappointed fans.
  • Formula 1's US popularity was debated, while NASCAR faced an antitrust lawsuit involving Denny Hamlin.
  • Penn State's prolonged coaching search and perceived hesitation regarding James Franklin were discussed.
  • Ohio State's recent improvement positions them as a strong College Football Playoff contender.
  • Listener questions covered athlete loyalty and discussions on NFL physicality and team adoption.

Deep Dive

  • James Madison University (JMU) entered the Top 25, sparking debate on Group of Five inclusion and playoff fairness.
  • The committee's decisions were questioned, particularly regarding potential scenarios for Championship Saturday.
  • A loss by Texas Tech or Alabama could complicate choices for teams like Miami and Notre Dame, leading to difficult committee decisions.

  • The New York Giants' offensive line struggles and overall performance were scrutinized.
  • Player Abdul Carter was benched for tardiness, contributing to ongoing team issues.
  • The discussion included the potential for rival players to target the Giants' quarterback due to team struggles.

  • Speculation centered on Brian Hartline and Mark Stoops for the Penn State head coach position.
  • The trend of interim coaches becoming permanent was discussed, citing Coach O at LSU and Pete Golding at Ole Miss.
  • The team's coaching search was labeled a disaster, with no head coach near National Signing Day and a perceived lengthy, unsuccessful process.

  • Formula 1's popularity in the US was questioned, with hosts debating if media companies had over-promoted the sport.
  • An antitrust lawsuit involving Denny Hamlin and Michael Jordan's NASCAR team, 23XI, was discussed.
  • Hosts opined that NASCAR might be over-monetizing and limiting competition, expressing support for Hamlin.

  • An anecdote resurfaced about Lane Kiffin's coaching beginnings at Fresno State, where he allegedly skipped practice as a backup quarterback.
  • Fan reactions to coaches were discussed, including an incident where Philadelphia Eagles fans egged coach Kevin Petullo's house.
  • The debate covered appropriate boundaries for fan criticism, distinguishing between public booing and actions targeting private residences.

  • Mike Tomlin and the Pittsburgh Steelers were identified as being in a "hot seat" situation, lacking playoff wins for a decade.
  • The team's offense was criticized for not completing a downfield pass in 36 days, and its defense for its cost versus performance.
  • Stagnant performance of teams like the Bills, who allegedly ran the same play 27 times, was noted as a broader coaching issue.

  • Lane Kiffin's move from Ole Miss to LSU caused disappointment among Ole Miss fans, who felt betrayed by his prior statements about not leaving for money.
  • The host defended Kiffin, noting that coaches often prioritize better opportunities throughout their careers.
  • Kiffin's move significantly escalated the animosity between the Ole Miss and LSU fan bases.

  • Penn State's standing as a top national job was debated, with hosts ranking it below Ohio State, Michigan, USC, and potentially Oregon within the Big Ten.
  • Mike Elko's consideration of Penn State before returning to Texas A&M was viewed as a tactic to leverage more money.
  • The team's perceived hesitation in firing James Franklin despite consistent 10-2 or 11-1 seasons was observed amidst a lengthy coaching search.

  • Texas A&M was labeled a 'fraudulent' team, with a controversial win against Notre Dame and a schedule of lower-ranked SEC opponents cited.
  • Oklahoma's playoff chances were discussed, with one host arguing for their strong resume but predicting a low-scoring playoff loss due to limited offense.
  • College football fan bases, particularly Oklahoma and Tennessee, were discussed regarding perceived obsession and annoyance.

  • Ohio State's improvement since their loss to Michigan last year was highlighted, leading to an impressive playoff run.
  • The team's defensive talent and player development, specifically mentioning Julian Sayyon, were noted as key upgrades.
  • Potential playoff contenders like Georgia, Notre Dame, and Texas Tech were considered challenges for Ohio State.

  • The College Football Playoff committee's rankings were debated, with hosts outlining a 'doomsday scenario' involving specific game outcomes impacting Texas Tech's inclusion.
  • Hypothetical scenarios for teams like BYU, Notre Dame, and Miami were discussed, considering how the committee might handle difficult decisions with an expanded playoff field.
  • Ole Miss's playoff chances were considered, factoring in their record, the status of their coaching staff, and Lane Kiffin's potential actions.

  • Listeners posed questions about athletes they would 'die on a hill for,' including Joakim Noah, Kyle Schwarber, Drake May, and Tom Wilson.
  • The hosts debated the historical physicality of NFL games, agreeing that the game was more violent in previous eras compared to the present day.
  • San Diego FC was adopted as a universal team to support, with discussions on MLS team naming conventions like 'Real Salt Lake' being critiqued.
