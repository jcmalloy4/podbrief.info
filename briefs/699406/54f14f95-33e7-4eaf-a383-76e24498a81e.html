Pardon My Take

Indiana Wins The Natty, Adam Thielen On Retirement, Paddy Pimblett Ahead Of UFC 324, Coaching Carousel + Listener Pardon Your Takes

Key Takeaways

  • The Indiana Hoosiers clinched the CFB National Championship, overcoming 150-to-1 pre-season odds.
  • Major NFL coaching changes were discussed, including Sean McDermott's dismissal from the Buffalo Bills.
  • Adam Thielen reflected on his 13-season NFL career, retirement, and influential teammates like Justin Jefferson.
  • UFC fighter Paddy Pimblett detailed his preparation and mindset for his interim lightweight championship fight at UFC 324.
  • Segments explored Brooklyn Beckham's family drama, Jake Paul's expansive new ranch, and a theory regarding NFL microchips.

Deep Dive

  • Coach Curt Cignetti led Indiana to a national championship in two years, a rapid success contrasting with longer rebuilds like Jim Calhoun at UConn and Bobby Bowden at FSU.
  • Indiana's pre-season odds were 150-to-1, with historical struggles including only six total bowl victories, three of which occurred within the last 19 days.
  • The team was the losingest in college football history before Cignetti's arrival, achieving numerous program-first records within his two-year tenure.

  • Sean McDermott was fired as head coach of the Buffalo Bills, making the position a top opening.
  • Robert Saleh was hired by the Tennessee Titans, while Jeff Hafley joined the Dolphins.
  • Drew Petzing was appointed as the Detroit Lions' Offensive Coordinator.

  • The upcoming AFC Championship game will feature the Denver Broncos against the New England Patriots.
  • The hosts expressed skepticism regarding the Broncos' viability with Jared Stidham as quarterback.
  • One host predicted a 40-0 Patriots victory in the matchup.

  • A blind resume comparison was presented for quarterbacks Caleb Williams and C.J. Stroud.
  • The hosts debated Stroud's regression and compared his playoff performance to Williams', considering factors such as fumbles and defensive support.
  • The discussion noted the influence of media and statistics on quarterback perceptions versus actual game viewing.

  • Drama surrounding Brooklyn Beckham's marriage to Nicola Peltz led to a feud with his parents, David and Victoria Beckham.
  • Discussion centered on Peltz's family wealth, Victoria Beckham's alleged refusal to design the wedding dress, and a disputed 'mommy son dance' at the reception.
  • The hosts noted the Beckham family's use of media in addressing their public disputes.

  • Jake Paul acquired a 6,000-acre ranch in Decatur County, Georgia, a property noted to be larger than half of Manhattan.
  • The ranch includes amenities such as a gun range, four miles of lakefront, and plans for an F1 track with surrounding housing.
  • Future developments for the property reportedly include a private jet landing strip and features suitable for various activities like water sports and dirt track racing.

  • Adam Thielen retired after 13 NFL seasons, stating he is content and looks forward to golf and coaching his children.
  • He highlighted Aaron Rodgers's intense practice preparation and Mike Tomlin's emotional final team meeting, where Tomlin felt he hadn't done a good enough job.
  • Thielen expressed strong support for Bryce Young, noting the challenges Young faced during his rookie year with the Carolina Panthers, including coaching instability and team struggles.

  • Adam Thielen identified Justin Jefferson as the best player he played with, citing Jefferson's consistent performance despite defenses focusing on stopping him.
  • Thielen observed significant intensity and competitiveness in Jefferson during their offseason throwing sessions, which he saw as a key indicator of future success.
  • Jefferson was affirmed as 'Teammate of the Year' due to his consistent effort, positive attitude, and leadership despite inconsistent quarterback play and team struggles for the Minnesota Vikings.

  • UFC fighter Paddy Pimblett reported feeling in peak physical condition for his upcoming interim lightweight championship fight at UFC 324, aiming to reach 155 pounds.
  • He described his eight-week fight camp as his best ever, training over the Christmas and New Year period.
  • Pimblett acknowledged being labeled the 'most hated man' in the UFC, stating that both love and hate from fans contribute to viewership.

  • A theory was discussed suggesting that increased occurrences of field goal posts being hit ('doinks') are due to NFL microchips in the ball.
  • This theory has reportedly made the 'no doink' bet a popular choice among bettors.
  • One host noted a personal viewing habit change to 4K resolution on YouTube TV, which caused awareness of live game spoilers due to broadcast delays.
