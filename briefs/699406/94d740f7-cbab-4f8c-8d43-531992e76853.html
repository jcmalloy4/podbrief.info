Playoff Baseball, CFB With Brandon Walker, Ole Miss Head Coach Lane Kiffin, Jets And Bengals Are A Disaster + Guys On Chicks

Key Takeaways

  • Playoff baseball wild card games featured strong pitching, with the Cubs securing their first home playoff win with fans since 2017.
  • The WNBA is experiencing public conflict between the WNBPA head Nafisa Collier and Commissioner Cathy Engelbert over player concerns.
  • Monday Night Football games showcased dismal performances from the Bengals and Jets, leading to extensive discussion about their futures.
  • The Saudi Public Investment Fund acquired EA Sports for $55 billion, aiming to diversify investments and enhance global perception.
  • Ole Miss Head Coach Lane Kiffin discussed his team's win against LSU, recruiting strategies, and unique coaching style, including hiring former NFL coaches.
  • College football analysis focused on Penn State's inability to win major games and Kirby Smart's 'Alabama problem' with Georgia.
  • The 12-team college football playoff expansion was welcomed, despite concerns over conference realignment and field storming traditions.

Deep Dive

  • The Cubs secured their first playoff victory since 2017, marked by back-to-back home runs and strong bullpen management under Craig Counsell.
  • Tarek Skubel delivered a 14-strikeout performance in game one of the playoffs, despite a ninth-inning scare involving a Javi Baez error.
  • Yankees closer Aroldis Chapman entered in the eighth inning of a playoff game, but the team failed to score in the ninth with bases loaded, elevating panic levels for fans.
  • Dodgers showcased dominance with Shohei Ohtani's leadoff home run and multiple other home runs; Blake Snell pitched six innings, his first time in postseason play.

  • Discussion addressed a statement regarding WNBA player endorsements and pay scales attributed to Nafisa Collier and the league.
  • Reports indicate conflict between WNBPA head Nafisa Collier and WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert over player concerns.
  • Players like Paige Bueckers and Elena Delle Donne have publicly supported Collier, criticizing Engelbert's handling of these issues.

  • The Cincinnati Bengals' offense was described as dismal, with 19 of their last 22 drives ending in a punt or turnover, prompting a humorous suggestion to 'GoFundMe' for Jameis Winston.
  • The Denver Broncos showcased a dominant defense in their successful game, contrasting sharply with the Bengals' offensive struggles.
  • The New York Jets' performance was a 'tough watch,' characterized by effective running undermined by fumbles and a significant collapse after a promising initial drive.
  • Concerns were raised about the Jets' future and a 'two-year plan' proposed by 'Memes,' involving drafting defense this year and offense next year.

  • The Jets' 'two-year plan' involves prioritizing a defensive draft in the current year and an offensive draft in the following year, despite the team's historically poor defense.
  • Debate covered potential trades for players like Quinn Williams and Garrett Wilson, alongside speculation about specific quarterback prospects.
  • A fan expressed dark humor about having 'one more rebuild' left before becoming a casual fan, highlighting the emotional toll of supporting the team.
  • Discussion noted efforts to address the team's status as the most penalized, alongside criticism of officiating fairness in games.

  • NBA Media Day featured Kawhi Leonard addressing allegations of suspicious sponsorships, which he dismissed as a 'conspiracy' and 'clickbait'.
  • The Public Investment Fund (PIF) of Saudi Arabia, along with Jared Kushner's company, acquired EA Sports for $55 billion, taking the company private.
  • The acquisition is speculated to be a strategy for diversification and improving Saudi Arabia's global perception through sports and entertainment investment.

  • Golfer Rory McIlroy's conduct during the Ryder Cup was debated, including his reactions to fan behavior and interactions with Bryson DeChambeau.
  • The European Ryder Cup team's perceived camaraderie and strong performance made it difficult for hosts to dislike them, contrasting with the U.S. team's dynamic.
  • Ludvig Åberg's unique background, dual Austrian and American identity, and playing style were noted, alongside the team's overall celebratory atmosphere.
  • Specific European players like Tommy Fleetwood, Justin Rose, Tyrrell Hatton, and Jon Rahm were highlighted for their admired performances.

  • Ole Miss Head Coach Lane Kiffin downplayed their win against LSU, stating the team played as expected and was favored.
  • Kiffin humorously addressed his dog, Juice, tweeting critical comments about the team's run game.
  • He recounted recruiting quarterback Jaxson Dart from Ferris State, highlighting Dart's initial surprise at amenities and his 'magic' on the field.
  • Kiffin discussed media attention around his daughter's relationship with an LSU player, drawing parallels to past career challenges.

  • Coach Kiffin discussed Ole Miss's focus on improvement and preparation for upcoming games, including scouting Georgia during a bye week.
  • He explained his adoption of the visor as a nod to Steve Spurrier, whom he admired, and his practice of offering opportunities to coaches like Joe Judge.
  • Kiffin detailed his recruiting strategy, emphasizing selling his program over negative recruiting, and using available 'bulletin board material' for motivation.
  • His staff receives daily updates on the transfer portal during specific open periods to track potential new players.

  • Penn State consistently struggles to win major games under James Franklin, creating a 'narrative tipping point' for fans.
  • Kirby Smart's decision-making in a game against Alabama, including not kicking a field goal or going for it on fourth down, led to debate about his 'Alabama problem'.
  • The perceived regression of top SEC teams like Alabama and Georgia sparked questions about the conference's overall dominance compared to previous years.

  • The AP Top 25 rankings are often considered a 'disaster' but effectively generate debate among college football enthusiasts.
  • Brandon Walker's personal top five teams included Ohio State, Oregon, Miami, Alabama, and Texas, emphasizing the uncertainty surrounding many programs.
  • Notre Dame's playoff chances were debated, with one host predicting a 10-2 record and a playoff berth despite defensive concerns, citing a strong offense and talented players.
  • Heisman Trophy futures were discussed, noting inconsistent quarterback performances and potential dark horses among wide receivers and running backs this season.

  • Bobby Petrino's immediate impact as interim coach at Arkansas, including firing assistants upon arrival, sparked discussion about coaching decisions.
  • Dabo Swinney's current situation at Clemson led to debate about his willingness to adapt to the transfer portal and NIL rules for a premiere job like Florida.
  • Concerns were raised about LSU Head Coach Brian Kelly's fit with the program's culture and fanbase, citing perceived unlikability despite recruiting prowess.
  • The chaotic scheduling of Pac-12 teams Washington State and Oregon State amid conference realignment garnered sympathy, with questions about a new Pac-12 formation.

  • The expansion to a 12-team college football playoff was generally approved, with suggestions that 16 teams would be ideal.
  • The expanded format is expected to increase the number of meaningful games late in the season and provide opportunities for teams like Wisconsin or Ole Miss.
  • The importance of on-campus playoff games was emphasized for preserving the unique atmosphere of college football Saturdays.
  • Concerns were raised that large-scale field storming incidents might lead to the tradition being banned, noting financial penalties for conferences.
