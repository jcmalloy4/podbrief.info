NFL Divsional Round, Bears Lose In OT, Bills Heartbreak, Patriots And Seahawks Advance + Who's Back Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • Broncos secured a 33-30 overtime victory against the Bills.
  • Seahawks dominated the 49ers 41-6, showcasing strong defense.
  • Rams defeated the Bears 20-17 in overtime at Soldier Field.
  • Patriots beat the Texans 28-16 despite C.J. Stroud's struggles.
  • Bills' consistent playoff exits raise questions about future and coaching.
  • MLB offseason featured significant spending by teams like the Dodgers.
  • NFL saw notable head coach hirings, including Kevin Stefanski to the Falcons.

Deep Dive

  • The Broncos secured a 33-30 overtime victory against the Bills on January 19th.
  • Key plays included a touchdown pass from Josh Allen to Miko Hardman, an offensive tackle Frank Crumb touchdown reception, and Jordan Humphrey's go-ahead touchdown for the Broncos.
  • Bo Nix sustained a broken ankle after the game, which Sean Payton announced immediately.
  • The Bills tied the game before halftime, but ultimately lost in overtime after failing to convert a deep pass.

  • The Seahawks secured a dominant 41-6 victory against the 49ers.
  • Rasheed Shaheed scored an opening kickoff touchdown for Seattle, contributing to their swift, decisive performance.
  • The 49ers struggled offensively, including Brock Purdy throwing an interception to Christian McCaffrey.
  • Kenneth Walker was effective against a struggling 49ers defense, and the Seahawks defense held Kyle Shanahan's teams under seven points twice recently.

  • The Dodgers' extensive spending for a long-term dynasty led to a hypothetical 2026 opening day lineup featuring Shohei Ohtani and Kyle Tucker.
  • Shohei Ohtani's contract involves deferred payments, allowing him to secure future income streams.
  • The Philadelphia Phillies signed Trea Turner to a seven-year, $200 million contract.
  • Steve Cohen of the Mets tweeted cryptically about upcoming moves, preceding the Mets signing Trea Turner after the Phillies' acquisition.

  • The Rams secured a 20-17 overtime victory over the Bears in a cold game at Soldier Field.
  • Sean McVay's game plan, including limited run plays and fourth-down decisions, was questioned.
  • The Bears' defense, led by Puna Ford, disrupted offensive shifts and employed effective blitzing during the playoff games.
  • Matt Stafford had fumbles and an interception on fourth down, though the Rams ultimately secured the win.

  • A fan who flew from Hawaii to see the Bears play struggled with the Chicago cold, purchasing a sweatshirt and not displaying his sign in the second half.
  • Former NFL player Kyle Long attempted to attend a Bears game incognito but was easily recognized.
  • Leaving the stadium was described as a chaotic experience, including the temporary loss of a friend and concerns about the cold weather impacting attendees.

  • The Patriots defeated the Texans 28-16, with a touchdown from Drake Maye to Miko Douglas.
  • Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud struggled with multiple interceptions and fumbles, characterized as 'ass' and 'horrendous' by hosts.
  • Marcus Mike Jones returned an interception for a Patriots touchdown, extending their lead.
  • A statistic about Drake Maye's performance on third-and-six was mentioned.

  • The hosts discussed post-Super Bowl plans, with one mentioning a trip to Orlando and another planning to withdraw $50,000 in cash for a bet.
  • They analyzed the Patriots' Super Bowl prospects and the 'winning DNA' attributed to them.
  • Nick Foles posted on Twitter, offering a 'positive note' for the Broncos regarding their past struggles against backup quarterbacks in championship games.

  • The hosts asserted the Seahawks are the best team in the NFL after their dominant 41-6 victory over the 49ers.
  • They highlighted the Seahawks' perfect defense and offensive efficiency.
  • Sam Darnold's performance and oblique injury were noted, with initial doubts about its reality dismissed.
  • The Seahawks' strong offseason outlook includes having the fourth-most cap space.

  • The Bills' playoff loss to the Broncos highlighted critical turnovers by Josh Allen, including a fumble and a missed game-tying touchdown.
  • Despite a strong regular-season record (83-33) since 2019, the Bills have not won a Super Bowl.
  • Josh Allen now holds the record for the most playoff wins without a Super Bowl appearance.
  • Head coach Sean McDermott's job security is debated, with suggestions the team has become stagnant after repeating playoff failures annually.

  • A controversial overturned catch by Brandon Cooks in overtime led to debate about the NFL's review process and consistency.
  • Pass interference calls and non-calls were argued to be inconsistently applied, particularly in critical overtime situations.
  • The hosts leaned towards the interception call on Cooks being correct by rule, despite its visual ambiguity and lack of on-field referee review.

  • The upcoming College Football National Championship game features Indiana and Miami.
  • Indiana is favored by 8.5 points (-340 on the moneyline) and is predicted to win by double digits, remaining undefeated.
  • Miami's coach, Mario Cristobal, is noted for his unique qualifications to address cheating allegations.
  • One host considered hedging a 16-1 future bet on the Miami Hurricanes.

  • Recent NFL head coach hirings include Jim Harbaugh to the Giants and Kevin Stefanski becoming the Atlanta Falcons head coach.
  • Kevin Stefanski's new role with the Atlanta Falcons, dubbed 'Philly South,' highlights the Falcons' connections to Philadelphia.
  • Falcons owner Arthur Blank personally cooked ribeyes for Stefanski and Matt Ryan during their meeting, with a photo of the group being discussed.
  • The hosts humorously debated awkward group photos, particularly among men, and explored alternative poses.
