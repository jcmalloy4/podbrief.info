Pardon My Take

Miami HC Mario Cristobal, CFB With Tom Fornelli, Jerry From Meow Mix + Playoff Baseball, Jaguars Big Win And FAQ's

Key Takeaways

  • Aaron Judge's clutch three-run homer helped the Yankees avoid elimination, winning Game 3 9-6.
  • The Mariners took a 2-1 series lead, including a fan-predicted Cal Raleigh home run.
  • NL playoff teams, the Phillies and Cubs, are struggling offensively, down 0-2 in their series.
  • The Jacksonville Jaguars secured a notable win against the Kansas City Chiefs, marking a rare week of losses for top NFL teams.
  • Joe Flacco signed with the Cincinnati Bengals to address quarterback depth concerns.
  • College football analysis covered Penn State coaching speculation, Ohio State's team evolution, and North Carolina's program struggles.
  • Miami Hurricanes Head Coach Mario Cristobal detailed his strategies for team building and fostering a competitive culture.
  • The Carolina Panthers' poor performance prompted a team-specific podcast, "Meow Mix," to go on an unexpected hiatus.
  • Fan Q&A segments addressed navigating difficult team rebuilds and shared memorable podcast road trip anecdotes.

Deep Dive

  • Aaron Judge's three-run home run in the Bronx tied Game 3, ultimately leading to a 9-6 Yankees victory, preventing elimination.
  • Despite initial skepticism, a host acknowledged the significance of Judge's moment, noting his impressive .500 playoff average.
  • Judge demonstrated his ability to hit a 100 mph inside pitch, a key highlight of the game.

  • Hosts expressed a lack of hope for the Phillies and Cubs, both down 0-2 in their respective playoff series.
  • The Cubs' offensive struggles were highlighted, noting consistent low-scoring games and issues with striking out.
  • Brewers reliever Jacob Misiorowski's high velocity (104.3 mph) and dominant performance against the Cubs were discussed.

  • The Jacksonville Jaguars achieved a rare win against the Kansas City Chiefs.
  • Trevor Lawrence's performance was highlighted, alongside discussions of a controversial goal-line leap and critical penalties.
  • The Chiefs, Bills, and Ravens all losing in the same week was noted as a rarity since 2017.

  • Tom Fornelli discussed the situation at Penn State, including head coach James Franklin and the potential for a buyout.
  • Kurtis Townsend, currently at Indiana, was mentioned as a possible replacement for Franklin.
  • Penn State could secure an at-large playoff spot if they win out, including against Ohio State, though odds are considered low.

  • The current Ohio State team's effort level is considered higher due to new players and the integration of Julian Sayin.
  • The defense is praised for confusing opposing quarterbacks, though the offense is still developing.
  • Last year's Ohio State team was ultimately considered better despite the current team's effort improvements.

  • The University of North Carolina's football program was described as a 'dumpster fire' lacking preparedness.
  • Reports included poor family relations and the cancellation of a Hulu documentary under Bill Belichick's leadership.
  • Concerns were raised about UNC becoming an afterthought in college football, especially with conference realignments.

  • Miami Hurricanes Head Coach Mario Cristobal attributes program turnarounds to hiring driven individuals and changing team DNA.
  • Cristobal shared lessons learned from Nick Saban, particularly regarding combating complacency.
  • He emphasized continuous improvement, especially in trench play and by perimeter defensive players.

  • Jerry from the Carolina Panthers podcast, Meow Mix, announced a temporary hiatus due to the team's poor performance.
  • The podcast gained national media attention for ending its run mid-season.
  • Jerry explained the emotional toll of consistent Panthers losses impacted their ability to produce content effectively.

  • A St. Louis Cardinals fan inquired about navigating a full team rebuild and a non-contending season.
  • Hosts debated that while rebuilds can be fun, failed rebuilds are not.
  • The conversation touched on how the experience of a rebuild differs for teams with winning versus losing backgrounds.
