Pardon My Take

NFL Talk With Chris Long, NFL Week 10 Picks And Preview, Windmill Deep Dive, TNF Stinker + Fyre Fest Of The Week

Key Takeaways

  • The Thursday Night Football game between the Broncos and Raiders was heavily criticized for its poor quality and high penalty count.
  • Week 10 NFL previews included detailed analysis of multiple matchups, team performances, and associated betting lines.
  • A discussion explored the environmental impacts of wind turbines, balancing energy benefits against bird mortality and offshore risks.
  • Chris Long provided insights into NFL trade dynamics, player evaluations, and coach-player interactions during his interview.
  • The 'Fyre Fest of the Week' segment covered a dodgeball stream and a host's personal struggle with sleep deprivation.

Deep Dive

  • The Broncos-Raiders game was labeled 'dog shit' with low total yardage (just over 400) and 22 combined penalties.
  • Bo Nix's interceptions were discussed, though hosts noted they weren't entirely his fault, with his play not improving in the fourth quarter.
  • Al Michaels' commentary was described as cathartic when he expressed frustration, with his energy speculated to correlate with travel length.
  • The discussion included a specific critique of a Sean Payton 'trick play' that resulted in a sack on third-and-short.

  • The Bears' offense showed more success using pre-snap motion against the Giants' defense.
  • The team had a low number of punts this season, indicating offensive efficiency.
  • Offensive coordinator Ben Johnson was credited with effectively utilizing various players for trick plays.
  • Quarterback Justin Fields' performance and the team's commitment to a blocking culture were noted.

  • The hosts returned to discussing wind turbines after a listener noted bird mortality.
  • Estimates suggest house cats are responsible for significantly more bird deaths annually than wind turbines.
  • One host expressed an anti-wind turbine stance due to a broken offshore turbine damaging an ocean ecosystem.
  • The debate concluded with a primary concern for offshore wind turbines, less so for land-based ones.

  • The Jaguars-Texans game preview saw Texans as 1.5-point favorites with an over/under of 37.5.
  • Jaguars GM James Gladstone's comment on Jacoby Myers avoiding drops was interpreted as an indirect critique of receiver Brian Thomas.
  • One host favored the Jaguars, citing the Texans' offensive struggles regardless of quarterback.
  • A betting strategy suggested betting against the Texans when they face strong pass rush teams like the Jaguars.

  • Teams coached by Dan Campbell are 81-57-3 against the spread after a straight-up loss as a favorite of seven points or more.
  • This statistic suggested the Lions could heavily defeat the Commanders following their recent loss.
  • The discussion highlighted the potential for teams to perform well against the spread after a significant loss as a favorite.

  • The hosts reviewed current standings for their weekly picks: Big Cat 12-6, PFT Commenter 10-8, Zach 9-9.
  • Memes, Hank, and PFT were trailing at 8-10 in the standings.
  • Touchdown parlay bets were discussed, including George Kittle and a player referred to as 'Lad' due to a scoring drought.
  • Zachary selected Colts at -6.5 in the Falcons-Colts game; another host picked under 48.5 points for the same matchup.

  • Chris Long's four former NFL teams (Eagles, Patriots, Rams) held a combined record of 27-7.
  • The Buccaneers were mentioned as a current pick to reach the Super Bowl.
  • A debate ensued about Big Dom's visit to an injured player, Cam Scataboo, questioning if the actions were performative or genuine.
  • The impact of the NFL trade deadline on locker rooms and team dynamics was discussed.

  • The Detroit Lions showed inconsistent rushing and vulnerability in the secondary.
  • Concerns were raised about their offense's consistency and play-calling, particularly Jared Goff's protection.
  • The hosts questioned if the Lions, desperate after a loss, were a good bet against the Commanders given recent trends.
  • The intense atmosphere in NFL team facilities after losses, contrasting with complacency after victories, was described.

  • The concept of an NFL 'soft benching,' where a player is sidelined without explicit disciplinary action, was discussed.
  • Kyler Murray's situation in Arizona was labeled a clear soft benching, suggesting his tenure with the Cardinals was ending.
  • The Cardinals' ownership was criticized for appearing to undermine the coach in their handling of team situations.
  • The Seahawks were identified as a top-three NFC Super Bowl contender following their victory over the Commanders.

  • The 'Fyre Fest of the Week' segment featured a recent dodgeball stream event.
  • The hosts' team experienced a significant losing streak, winning only two matches against college teams.
  • Hosts recounted physical soreness and injuries from the dodgeball game, including a hit to the face and shoulder pain.
  • The team lost the championship game with a score of 20-3.

  • One host described a recurring issue with his four-year-old daughter waking up nightly.
  • The daughter refused to stay in her room, leading to the host's significant lack of sleep.
  • Humorous, drastic measures were considered to encourage the daughter to remain in her room.
  • The host sought opinions on the normalcy of going out to dinner alone, finding general support for 'solo dates'.
