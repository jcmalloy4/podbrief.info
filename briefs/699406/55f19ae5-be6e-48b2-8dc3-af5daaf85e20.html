Pardon My Take

NFL Week 13 Recap, Fastest 2 Minutes, Bears Smash The Eagles, Chiefs On The Ropes, Surging Texans + Lane Kiffin Drama And Rivalry Week

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 13 saw multiple upsets and tight contests, significantly impacting playoff pictures in both conferences.
  • The Chicago Bears and Houston Texans demonstrated notable improvements, securing key victories against strong opponents.
  • Philadelphia Eagles' offense faced significant criticism for predictable play-calling and struggles, leading to fan displeasure.
  • Contenders like the Kansas City Chiefs and Detroit Lions experienced setbacks, prompting discussions about their playoff viability and roster adjustments.
  • College football's coaching carousel was active, highlighted by the controversial timing of coach departures amidst critical regular season and playoff discussions.
  • Lane Kiffin's departure from Ole Miss to LSU was characterized by a publicly contentious and dramatic exit.
  • The College Football Playoff landscape remains complex, with ongoing debates surrounding team rankings and 'strength of loss' metrics.

Deep Dive

  • The Houston Texans defeated the Indianapolis Colts 20-16, extending their winning streak to four games.
  • The team's rapid improvement is highlighted by their comeback from an 0-3 start to being one game behind the Colts in the AFC South.
  • C.J. Stroud's return and a strong defensive performance, particularly from Derek Stingley, were key to their success.

  • The Tampa Bay Buccaneers secured a 20-17 victory over the Arizona Cardinals.
  • Baker Mayfield played effectively despite a shoulder injury, leading to discussions about the Buccaneers' performance and future schedule.
  • Analysts questioned Mayfield's decision to slide in one game but then avoid sliding later, impacting game dynamics.

  • The Tampa Bay Buccaneers defeated the Arizona Cardinals 20-17 in a narrow victory.
  • Baker Mayfield connected with Cade Otton for a touchdown, with Rachaad White also contributing a score.
  • Chase McLaughlin secured the win for the Buccaneers with a 57-yard field goal.
  • The win contributes to discussions about the Buccaneers' future schedule and potential investment in the team.

  • The possibility of the Kansas City Chiefs missing the playoffs was discussed as a 'shocking' outcome given Patrick Mahomes' prime.
  • Missing the playoffs could prompt the Chiefs to make significant roster adjustments, potentially benefiting the franchise long-term.
  • Analysts agreed the Chiefs are not mathematically eliminated and should remain in contention discussions.

  • The Chicago Bears secured a significant 24-15 victory over the Philadelphia Eagles on Black Friday, their most impactful win since 2018.
  • The Bears' rushing attack was formidable, accumulating over 281 yards with two running backs exceeding 120 yards each.
  • The win sparked debate on the Bears' identity and potential as a Super Bowl contender, especially in cold weather games.

  • Frustration mounted over the Philadelphia Eagles' offensive performance, with hosts citing a lack of adjustments and predictable play-calling.
  • Fans expressed displeasure directly to owner Jeffrey Lurie, questioning whether Jalen Hurts or the coaching staff bears more responsibility.
  • The team's 8-4 record was noted, but the offense's poor showing justified fan boos, a common occurrence in Philadelphia.

  • The Jacksonville Jaguars defeated the Tennessee Titans 25-3, securing their first-place position in the division.
  • Coach Liam Cohen's leadership has driven a significant turnaround, leading the team to an 8-4 record, their fourth time achieving eight or more wins in 15 years.
  • The Titans' coaching situation was deemed to lack direction, and a decision to kick a field goal on fourth and three was criticized as analytically unsound.

  • The San Francisco 49ers defeated the Browns 26-8, a victory that included a key muffed punt by Cleveland.
  • Christian McCaffrey achieved over 800 rushing and 800 receiving yards for the third time in his career, highlighting his statistical dominance.
  • George Kittle also contributed to the 49ers' strong performance, cementing their status as a formidable team.

  • The Seahawks secured a 26-0 shutout victory over the Vikings.
  • Vikings quarterback Max Brosmer had a particularly poor performance, throwing four interceptions with a QBR lower than spiking the ball every play.
  • His stat line of 126 passing yards, four interceptions, and zero touchdowns was compared to historical lows, frustrating Justin Jefferson.

  • The Denver Broncos defeated the Commanders 27-26, with the Broncos holding a record of reaching the Super Bowl in all four previous seasons they started 10-2.
  • Quarterback Bo Nix's performance was mixed, featuring a confusing interception but also clutch plays and an overtime drive.
  • The Commanders' defense showed improvement, but Marcus Mariota's 50 pass attempts were deemed an ineffective winning formula.

  • Lane Kiffin's departure from Ole Miss to LSU was characterized by a contentious and public exit, consistent with his coaching history.
  • Florida withdrew from negotiations with Kiffin due to inconsistent communication, further illustrating his unpredictable behavior.
  • The timing of his departure amidst the transfer portal and early signing day was criticized as detrimental to college football.

  • The College Football Playoff picture was discussed, with Alabama's two losses potentially keeping them out of contention.
  • Michigan's players were scrutinized for their focus on protecting the midfield logo after their loss to Ohio State.
  • The debate included the concept of 'strength of loss' as a ranking metric and whether Michigan should remain in playoff contention despite their loss.
