The Matt Walsh Show

America Will Never Be The Same After Charlie Kirk’s Assassination

Key Takeaways

  • Podcast deviated from normal format to address Charlie Kirk's death.
  • Host praised Kirk's humble character, patriotism, and political influence.
  • Kirk's public death occurred during a debate, sparking host's strong emotions.
  • Host rejected unity calls, criticized media, and vowed to confront 'evil'.
  • Call to action urged listeners to continue Kirk's fight and mission.

Deep Dive

  • The host announced a show format deviation, expressing devastation and anger, to discuss Charlie Kirk's legacy and the path forward.
  • Kirk was characterized as humble, deflecting compliments, and crediting others, notably for Trump's victory.
  • He was described as a "rare individual," a "great man, builder, organizer, and fighter," skilled in public engagement and strategic orchestration.

  • Charlie Kirk was lauded as a patriot who loved and fought for America, with 'Make America Great Again' being his mission, and showed devotion to his family.
  • His death occurred publicly during a debate, described as engaging in civil discourse without insults or shouting.
  • The host expressed personal devastation and anger, asserting Kirk was killed for being effective and beloved, and rejected calls for unity.
  • He criticized media figures who previously stoked hatred against Kirk but then advocated for de-escalation post-death.

  • The host contended it is too late to de-escalate tensions, citing media celebrations of Charlie Kirk's death and political violence fueled by groups like Antifa and BLM.
  • He expressed anger and devastation over Kirk's death, condemning celebratory reactions from "leftist demons" and noting Congressional Democrats jeered during a moment of silence.
  • The enemies' gloating was framed as a tribute to Charlie's impact and a sign of his success as a "culture warrior."

  • The host called for listeners to emulate Charlie's effectiveness, asserting that a martyred fighter is more powerful than a living one.
  • He emphasized that the cause, rooted in love of God, country, and family, cannot be killed by individuals.
  • Amidst death threats, the host vowed not to back down or hide, fearing a copycat event but committing to fight harder for the truth.
  • He recalled Charlie's final post, stating "America will never be the same" and will be better due to Charlie's work, vowing to continue the fight.
