The Matt Walsh Show

Ep. 1661 - We Just Got Proof Of A Huge Attack On Free Speech, And It Has Nothing To Do With Jimmy Kimmel

Key Takeaways

  • Google admitted to censoring conservative content at the Biden administration's request, impacting free speech.
  • The host criticized media's disproportionate outrage over Jimmy Kimmel's absence compared to systemic censorship.
  • Concerns were raised regarding left-wing political violence, with calls for Antifa to be designated a terrorist organization.
  • Pregnant women are reportedly taking Tylenol on TikTok in protest of Donald Trump, despite potential fetal health risks.

Deep Dive

  • The host recounted YouTube demonetizing his channel years ago for refusing to use preferred pronouns for transgender individuals.
  • The channel also faced penalties for critiquing gender ideology, leading to financial losses.
  • YouTube eventually reversed its decision regarding the channel's demonetization.

  • Google admitted to House Republicans that Biden administration officials pressured the company regarding content not violating existing policies.
  • The host criticized Google's admission as 'too little, too late,' comparing it to an NFL team admitting to cheating post-Super Bowl.
  • YouTube is accused of conspiring with the government, potentially committing a crime against civil liberties.
  • Google's letter reportedly indicated policy changes addressing COVID-19 and election integrity issues.

  • The host argued that media outrage over Jimmy Kimmel's temporary suspension was disproportionate to the censorship of conservatives.
  • Other examples cited included the jailing of pro-lifers and January 6th protestors, which received less media focus.
  • A lack of leftist outrage was noted when Apple canceled 'The Savant,' a show depicting conservatives as a terror threat.

  • A shooting at an ICE facility in Dallas, where the shooter died by suicide, was labeled 'left-wing terrorism' due to anti-ICE messages on bullets.
  • The host advocated for designating Antifa as a domestic terrorist organization, arguing its members should face arrests akin to ISIS affiliates.
  • The host called for a declaration of a national emergency and a crackdown on left-wing radicals.

  • Jimmy Kimmel returned to his show after a 72-hour absence, which the host sarcastically framed as a minor event.
  • Kimmel's apology for a controversial joke was critiqued as self-pity, with the host claiming Kimmel falsely implied a shooter was conservative.
  • Following the controversy, Kimmel's viewership and YouTube views significantly increased, with Google searches spiking over 10,000%.

  • Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth closed a nearly century-old committee focused on expanding women's roles in the military.
  • This action was framed as an effort to redefine the armed forces' image and remove 'woke' initiatives from the Pentagon.
  • The host supported the closure, arguing that actively recruiting and expanding roles for women has not historically strengthened any military.

  • An NIH-funded study in BMC Environmental Health linked prenatal Tylenol (acetaminophen) exposure to increased neurodevelopmental disorders, including autism.
  • Autism cases among young children have reportedly increased by 400% in the last two decades.
  • Pregnant liberal women on TikTok are reportedly consuming Tylenol publicly in protest of Donald Trump's statements.
  • The host criticized these women for disregarding potential fetal health risks, such as links to brain disorders, for internet attention.
