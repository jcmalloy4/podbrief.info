I Have A DISTURBING Update About Target

Key Takeaways

  • Target implemented a new '10-4 program' requiring employees to smile and offer assistance to customers.
  • Online reactions were largely critical, questioning the sincerity of Target's mandatory customer interaction policy.
  • The host criticized Target's policy, viewing it as a symptom of declining customer service standards across industries.
  • Discussion highlighted a perceived widespread decline in the quality of work and professionalism in various sectors.
  • The host connected skilled labor shortages to immigration policies, arguing for a focus on training American workers.

Deep Dive

  • Target introduced a "10-4 program" requiring employees to smile at customers within 10 feet and offer assistance within 4 feet during the holiday season.
  • Chief stores officer Adrian Costanzo stated the initiative aims to enhance customer connection, representing a stricter mandate than previous guidelines.
  • Online reactions were largely critical, with commenters sarcastically noting mandatory happiness and questioning the policy's sincerity following recent employee layoffs.

  • The host criticized Target's '10-4 program', arguing that requiring employees to smile and offer basic assistance should be standard practice.
  • He contrasted current service with past expectations, noting a general decline in service quality across various industries.
  • Chick-fil-A was cited as a notable exception maintaining high customer service standards.

  • The host discussed a widespread decline in the quality of work and customer service across various sectors, including retail and contracted services.
  • He shared personal experiences observing a general lack of basic engagement and professionalism from employees.
  • This decline is presented as a systemic issue impacting overall service standards and customer satisfaction.

  • The discussion addressed skilled labor shortages, with the host referencing former President Donald Trump's past statements.
  • The host argued that policies like the H-1B visa program and mass migration are not solutions and may contribute to a collapse of standards.
  • He suggested a focus on training and raising standards for American workers as an alternative approach.
