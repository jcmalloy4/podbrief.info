The Matt Walsh Show

Ep. 1609 - How Biden’s Border Policy Turned Into A Child Trafficking Operation

Key Takeaways

  • Unaccompanied migrant children crisis: The Biden administration's sponsor system has resulted in approximately 85,000-300,000 missing children, with inadequate vetting creating conditions for potential trafficking, sexual exploitation, and abuse while blocking congressional oversight efforts.
  • Religious revival among Gen Z: Church attendance has quadrupled among young people in Britain, representing a significant generational shift toward faith as a response to what the hosts describe as a fundamental human need for meaning and purpose.
  • Cultural and political pushback: Conservative resistance to Pride Month initiatives has achieved measurable results through canceled parades, reduced corporate sponsorship, and companies replacing LGBT merchandise with patriotic alternatives.
  • Consciousness as evidence for divine existence: The emergence of self-awareness from inanimate matter represents an inexplicable phenomenon that the hosts argue can only be explained through supernatural intervention, positioning this as the strongest rational argument for God's existence.
  • Administrative accountability failures: Government agencies are systematically avoiding oversight on critical issues, from the Harvey Milk ship renaming during Pride Month to blocking investigations into missing migrant children, highlighting broader concerns about transparency and institutional responsibility.

Deep Dive

Unaccompanied Migrant Children Crisis

The podcast begins with an extensive examination of the Biden administration's handling of unaccompanied migrant children, contrasting it sharply with the Trump-era "kids in cages" controversy that drew heavy criticism from Democrats and celebrities who condemned detention facilities as inhumane.

The Sponsor System Implementation

  • The Biden administration sought to avoid using detention facilities by implementing a "sponsor" system for unaccompanied minors
  • Children were placed with adults inside the U.S., often attempting to work with parents or relatives
  • The system created perverse incentives for parents to send children across the border alone
  • Unaccompanied minor entries increased to over 130,000 per year under Biden
  • HHS admitted they lost track of children after placing them with sponsors, with no legal mechanism to follow up on children's whereabouts
Alarming Government Testimony A government official's testimony revealed the scope of the crisis:
  • 85,000 minors were reported missing from the system
  • The agency no longer has jurisdiction over these children once placed
  • Children are being placed with unrelated adults who are inadequately vetted
  • Sponsors have no obligation to communicate with government agencies after placement
  • The DHS inspector general reported approximately 300,000 minors are unaccounted for
Specific Cases and Systemic Failures
  • A documented case involved a 16-year-old girl placed with a 37-year-old illegal immigrant who allegedly sexually assaulted her
  • Federal agencies like ICE have limited authority to intervene in cases of unsafe placements
  • Children are potentially being sexually exploited and involved in drug and weapons trafficking
  • Democrats reportedly blocked HHS from informing local schools about these children
  • No systematic way exists to verify children's well-being or school attendance
Administrative Stonewalling The podcast details how the Biden administration allegedly obstructed congressional investigations:
  • Senator Bill Cassidy and Governor Ron DeSantis faced resistance from HHS when attempting to investigate
  • HHS reportedly refused to provide witnesses or documents to investigators
  • The situation received minimal media or political left-wing scrutiny
The speakers argue this represents a "human trafficking operation" and call for mass deportations, secure border enforcement, and breaking up what they describe as a large human trafficking network affecting tens of thousands of potentially at-risk children.

Harvey Milk Ship Renaming Controversy

The Decision and Context Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth ordered the Navy to remove Harvey Milk's name from a ship, with the timing during Pride Month reportedly being intentional. The podcast provides background on Harvey Milk as the first openly gay man elected to public office in the U.S. (San Francisco Board of Supervisors, 1977), a Navy veteran who served during the Korean War, and was assassinated in 1978.

Arguments About Historical Preservation The speakers criticize perceived hypocrisy in the left's reaction:

  • The left previously supported removing Confederate monuments and other historical statues
  • The Harvey Milk ship was only named in 2016, making removal less significant as historical erasure
  • The ship itself continues to exist, just with a different name
Character Assassination Claims The podcast makes serious allegations against Harvey Milk:
  • Claims he was a sexual predator who engaged with underage boys
  • Alleges he was responsible for at least one victim's suicide
  • Questions his minimal political achievement and positive impact on society
  • Argues he doesn't meet standards for historical honor (significant positive contributions, helping shape civilization, being virtuous on balance)
Historical Comparison The speakers contrast Milk with Christopher Columbus, portraying Columbus as a transformative historical figure whose actions potentially altered the course of human existence, while criticizing inconsistent standards for judging historical figures and selective application of "understanding historical context."

Legal Case and Religious Revival

Miami-Dade Deputy Case A Miami-Dade County deputy, Daniel Chala, was cleared of all charges after an incident with a trans-identified individual. Initially arrested for allegedly battering and preventing the person from leaving his residence, prosecutors dropped charges due to the alleged victim's refusal to cooperate. The hosts suggest the trans-identified individual should face charges for attempted sexual assault, gaining entry under false pretenses, and assault for misrepresenting gender.

Gen Z Religious Revival A Financial Times article highlighted a significant religious trend:

  • Church attendance among Gen Z in Britain has quadrupled in recent years
  • This generation is making the country more religious, contrasting with millennial skepticism toward religion
  • The trend is now supported by data rather than just anecdotal observation

Philosophical Arguments for Faith

The Meaning Crisis The speakers present a fundamental argument about human nature:

  • Human beings fundamentally NEED meaning in their lives, not just desire it
  • Without meaning, societies cannot sustainably exist and will either collapse or be revived
  • They attribute the generational spiritual shift to divine intervention
Critique of New Atheism The podcast extensively critiques the "Four Horsemen" of atheism (Hitchens, Dennett, Dawkins, Harris):
  • These philosophers ultimately failed to provide compelling explanations for life's meaning
  • Their arguments consistently break down when confronting "Why does life matter?"
  • Without God, human existence is reduced to accidental formation destined for obliteration
Consciousness as Divine Evidence The speakers present consciousness as the strongest rational argument for God's existence:
  • Inanimate matter becoming self-aware is inexplicable without supernatural intervention
  • Consciousness evidences a "transcendent, immaterial realm"
  • The emergence of consciousness, love, and self-awareness is too complex to be random accident
  • Existence itself "testifies to God's existence"
They argue humans face two existential options: belief in God or viewing existence as a meaningless "cosmic joke," with the horror and absurdity of a godless universe suggesting divine likelihood.

Cultural Pushback Against Pride Month

Conservative Victory Claims The podcast celebrates what they view as successful challenges to Pride Month:

  • Canceled parades and reduced corporate sponsorship
  • Successful political and cultural resistance to gender ideology
  • Many major corporations not displaying LGBT logos
  • Companies like Target replacing Pride merchandise with patriotic clothing
Ongoing Concerns Despite claimed victories, they highlight continued concerns:
  • Sesame Street promoting drag performers and Pride content targeting young children
  • An Air Canada flight featuring an all 2SLGBTQIA+ crew, which they sarcastically dismiss
  • New England Patriots and Philadelphia Museum of Art displaying Pride-related imagery
The speakers frame these corporate and institutional displays as inappropriate "propaganda" particularly targeting children and those with less developed cognitive abilities, concluding by declaring various Pride-related communications as "canceled."
