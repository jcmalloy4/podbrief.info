Ep. 1686 - New Yorkers Hand Their City Over To A Third World Islamic Communist

Key Takeaways

  • Democrats secured a sweep in recent elections, electing officials described as radical in New York City and Virginia.
  • The host argues that conservative infighting and purity tests contrast with unified Democratic efforts, hindering electoral success.
  • A Gold's Gym in Los Angeles terminated a woman's membership after her complaint about a man in the women's locker room.
  • The host details a strategic conservative counteroffensive against the transgender agenda, yielding cultural victories.
  • The political influence of celebrities is reportedly declining, with some expressing a desire to disengage from politics.

Deep Dive

  • Democrats achieved a sweep in recent elections, electing officials characterized as radical in New York City and Virginia.
  • In Virginia, Democrats flipped seats in the House, and top-ticket candidates were described as holding significant power.
  • A Muslim socialist from Uganda was elected in New York City, advocating policies like government-run grocery stores and free public transport.
  • The host views these elections as a clarifying moment, illuminating battle lines and strategic disagreements on the right since 2016.

  • New York City's long-term decline is discussed, with an increase in foreign-born residents, particularly in Queens.
  • The white population in New York decreased from 98% in 1910 to 32% in 2020, leading to cultural changes.
  • Queens is highlighted as a complex, multinational environment, with a diverse population representing numerous ethnicities and religions.
  • The host critiques the newly elected NYC official's statement that government can solve all problems, contrasting it with Ronald Reagan's skepticism.

  • Recent election results in New York, Georgia, and Pennsylvania Supreme Court races are categorized as a significant defeat for the conservative movement.
  • The host asserts these outcomes represent a serious situation with potential consequences, including economic decline and loss of life.
  • The host criticizes the perceived embrace of ethnic nationalism by Democrats, notably excluding white people from their coalition.

  • The host criticizes internal disputes and purity tests among conservatives, contrasting this with the unified Democratic Party's focus on winning.
  • Conservatives are urged to unite against perceived threats, stating that the stakes involve their lives, children's futures, and the nation itself.
  • Infighting within the conservative movement is described as strategically detrimental, particularly in the weeks leading up to recent elections.

  • Tish Hyman's Gold's Gym membership was terminated after she complained about a man in the women's locker room.
  • Hyman described feeling violated by the man's presence while she was naked, noting he asserted he identified as a woman.
  • The host applauded Hyman's angry reaction as appropriate and necessary, suggesting past strong reactions could have prevented normalization.
  • He posits that consistent, strong reactions from women and men could have prevented transgender individuals from routinely entering women's spaces.

  • The host attributes a perceived decline of the 'trans agenda' to a deliberate and strategic counteroffensive from conservatives.
  • Public opinion has reportedly turned against the trans agenda, citing a lack of mainstream media condemnation for a woman confronting a trans-identified male.
  • The host advises speaking up immediately against perceived wrongs with openly enraged, disgusted, but peaceful reactions to protect family.

  • The host explains that an aggressive and sometimes mocking tone on issues like transgender ideology was strategic, providing cover for others to speak out.
  • This approach, exemplified by the Dylan Mulvaney monologue, reportedly led to YouTube demonetization for the host.
  • Opposing men in women's sports is now characterized as a low-risk and popular position, with the left reportedly avoiding the topic.

  • A strategy of targeting vulnerable corporations, such as Bud Light, aimed to make an example and signal to others to back off from 'woke' initiatives.
  • This approach reportedly caused billions in losses for targeted brands, influencing corporate behavior.
  • The strategy also extended to changing laws, recognizing their power to influence cultural and social norms.
  • The host asserts that strident communication provides cover for more moderate expressions of concern, empowering the public.

  • The host notes the decline of the 'monoculture' and diminishing political influence of celebrities.
  • Jennifer Lawrence recently acknowledged the pointlessness of celebrity political endorsements, contrasting it with her past outspokenness.
  • Lawrence expressed a desire to stop alienating audiences with her political opinions and protect her artistic craft.
  • The host concludes that Lawrence's realization signifies the end of celebrity influence in politics, viewing it as a positive outcome.
