The Matt Walsh Show

Ep. 1664 - A Real Insurrection Is Happening And It Needs To Be Stopped

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump's directive for 'full force' against 'leftist terrorists' has been questioned regarding its implementation.
  • Law enforcement responses to alleged left-wing federal crimes are contrasted with actions taken against January 6th defendants.
  • A mass shooting at a Mormon church in Michigan prompted discussion on motives beyond mental health.
  • The host addressed significant backlash for comments characterizing Dearborn, Michigan, as an 'Islamic capital'.
  • Concerns were raised about demographic shifts in Dearborn's police force and broader cultural changes.

Deep Dive

  • The host argued the Biden administration did not punish law enforcement officials for alleged excessive force against conservatives.
  • Incidents cited included January 6th, the FBI's raid on James O'Keefe's home, and the case of Mark Houck.
  • This approach was contrasted with the administration's alleged crackdown on conservatives.

  • The host questioned who in the Trump administration authorized a DHS statement condemning an ICE officer, calling for their firing.
  • The DHS reportedly placed the ICE officer back on active duty within 48 hours, but the host criticized the initial condemnation without a full review.
  • President Trump's directive for 'full force' against domestic terrorists was questioned regarding actual implementation and lack of follow-through.
  • The host criticized the lack of mass arrests for 'leftist terrorists' despite videos of their alleged federal crimes, unlike January 6th defendants.

  • Footage showed individuals blocking a federal vehicle, which the host argued constituted kidnapping, justifying force for escape.
  • The host questioned why individuals allegedly blocking a federal vehicle, such as Kat Abuguzella, were not arrested.
  • Federal agents in Portland were seen backing away from harassed and threatened crowds, avoiding arrests despite alleged domestic terror organization presence.

  • Democrats are accused of inciting violence with rhetoric like calling Stephen Miller a fascist.
  • The New York Times' obituary for convicted terrorist Assata Shakur is cited as evidence of the left's endorsement of violence.
  • The Chicago Teachers Union's praise for Assata Shakur is presented as an example of the modern left's mainstream embrace of revolutionary violence.

  • The host characterized the situation as an insurrection supported by a major political party, expressing concern about authorities' preparedness.
  • Concerns were raised that any use of force against 'leftist terrorists' could lead to backlash, similar to past cases.
  • Law enforcement is reportedly instructed to stand by while being harassed, leading to a choice between continued terrorism or treating groups as terrorists.

  • A mass shooting occurred at a Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Grand Blanc Township, Michigan, where at least four people were killed.
  • The shooter, a U.S. Marine veteran, drove into the church and opened fire before being killed in a firefight with police.
  • The host questioned the motive, considering political or personal grudges, and criticized focusing solely on mental health.

  • The host contrasted Congresswoman Maxine Waters' social media post of a park in Portland with independent journalist footage showing urban decay and drug use.
  • The host suggested the failure to detain individuals deemed unfit for civilized society contributes to urban issues observed in Portland.

  • The host addressed backlash for calling Dearborn, Michigan, an 'Islamic capital', dismissing accusations of racism or Islamophobia.
  • A hypothetical scenario was used, questioning how Americans would react if their culture were supplanted in an Arab country.
  • The host asserted a desire for America to remain culturally American, not adopting Middle Eastern culture, while not advocating persecution of Muslims.

  • The host highlighted Dearborn's police force changing from 3% to 45% Arab American, according to the city's police chief.
  • The chief's use of Islamic religious phrases during an official address was cited as an example of cultural shift.
  • The demographic shift is suggested as an intentional transformation, which the host implied would be unsettling to non-Muslims.
  • The host criticized the perceived intentional replacement of white Christians with Arabs in institutions, arguing it leads to Islamicization.
