Ep. 1670 - Democrats Have Been Trying To Cover Up One The Biggest Healthcare Scams Ever

Key Takeaways

  • The government shutdown is linked to debates over Obamacare subsidies and Medicaid for undocumented immigrants.
  • Leaked texts indicate Charlie Kirk considered abandoning the pro-Israel cause due to donor pressure.
  • A Democratic gubernatorial candidate's on-camera reaction to a routine question drew criticism.
  • China's rapid infrastructure development contrasts with perceived delays in U.S. projects.
  • Bureaucracy and a perceived loss of national will are cited as reasons for U.S. engineering decline.

Deep Dive

  • The host argues the government shutdown is primarily driven by Democratic proposals.
  • These proposals include renewing Obamacare subsidies and reversing Medicaid cuts.
  • Specific work requirements for asylum seekers are also part of the legislative debate.

  • Democrats are accused of using the shutdown to fund healthcare for undocumented immigrants.
  • Specific state examples (New York, Massachusetts, Illinois) are cited for providing Medicaid to undocumented immigrants.
  • A California scheme reportedly allocates nearly $4 billion for Medicaid to undocumented immigrants.
  • Reports indicate 1.5 million undocumented immigrants receive Medicaid benefits nationally.

  • Providing healthcare to undocumented immigrants is alleged to cost hundreds of millions nationwide.
  • Texas hospitals report over $120 million in costs monthly for emergency care to undocumented patients.
  • The host argues this strains emergency rooms and doctor's offices, hindering access for citizens.
  • The Affordable Care Act is criticized as a "debacle" for most Americans.

  • Leaked text messages suggest Charlie Kirk considered leaving the "pro-Israel cause."
  • This consideration was reportedly due to pressure from Jewish donors over his association with Tucker Carlson.
  • A potential loss of $2 million in annual funding was mentioned in the texts.
  • A Turning Point USA spokesperson confirmed the authenticity of these texts.

  • Democratic candidate Katie Porter reportedly had a "meltdown" during an interview.
  • The incident occurred when asked a question about reaching out to Trump voters.
  • The host characterized her reaction as excessive for a "softball question."
  • The reaction is attributed to factors including perceived animosity towards opposing voters.

  • The Secretary of Transportation cited challenges in enforcing DUI laws for marijuana.
  • Challenges arise from the lack of immediate telltale signs and reliable roadside testing.
  • The host argued against legalizing marijuana due to risks like addiction and increased potency of modern strains.

  • China recently opened the world's tallest bridge, completed in under four years.
  • The bridge includes features like a restaurant, theme park, glass skywalk, and waterfall.
  • This project is contrasted with slower infrastructure development in the U.S.

  • The U.S. is questioned for its current inability to undertake large-scale engineering projects.
  • Historical achievements like the Panama Canal and Hoover Dam are cited as contrasts.
  • Factors like excessive bureaucracy, permits, and zoning laws are blamed for project delays.
  • A loss of national will and desire is identified as a primary reason for this decline.
