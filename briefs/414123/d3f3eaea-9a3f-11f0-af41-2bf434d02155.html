The Matt Walsh Show

Ep. 1662 - This Is What Must Be Done To Put An End To The Left Wing Terrorism Epidemic

Key Takeaways

  • Multiple ICE facilities have been targeted in violent attacks, described as acts of left-wing terrorism, from June to September.
  • New polling data indicates voters prefer Republicans on most key issues, coinciding with increased conservative visibility on social media.
  • A predicted rapture event failed to materialize, prompting discussions on the psychological allure of apocalyptic prophecies.
  • The host advocates for age verification laws for adult websites, emphasizing child protection over free speech objections.
  • Allegations of online platforms and universities inciting violence against federal agents have led to calls for legal action.

Deep Dive

  • The host presented a hypothetical scenario where Democrats hold power and right-wing radicals commit violent acts.
  • It was suggested that Democrats would respond with a 'full-scale war on conservatives,' involving arrests and media shutdowns.

  • A sniper attack targeted an ICE facility in Dallas, perpetrated by an individual with a history of communist posts and anti-ICE sentiments.
  • The shooter was allegedly inspired by a previous assassination and mistakenly killed ICE detainees instead of intended targets.
  • The FBI released information regarding the shooter's motives and influences shortly before this report.

  • The host criticized Reddit for allegedly allowing content that incites violence against federal agents while banning conservative speech.
  • A flyer at Georgetown University reportedly incites violence against fascists and directs students to a communist group, citing words from a political assassin.
  • The host asserted that this constitutes incitement, and those involved, including the university administration, should face arrest and prosecution.

  • The host contrasted the potential Republican response to left-wing violence with a hypothetical Democratic reaction to similar right-wing acts.
  • It was suggested that if roles were reversed, Democrats would 'throw the rules out' to eliminate conservatism from the country.
  • The host argued for the prosecution of left-wing individuals involved in political violence, comparing groups like Antifa to terrorist organizations, stating that inaction is unsustainable.

  • Reuters polling data indicates voters prefer Republicans on most issues, including crime, immigration, and the economy.
  • Democrats reportedly lead on issues such as respect for democracy, healthcare, women's rights, and the environment.
  • Increased access for conservative voices on social media platforms like X (formerly Twitter) and YouTube is cited as contributing to a shift towards the right, particularly among young people, especially during 2018-2022.

  • A predicted rapture, reportedly scheduled for the previous Tuesday, did not occur, leading to reports of depression among some who had quit jobs and sold possessions.
  • An African preacher predicted the rapture for September 2025, based on a supposed vision and a calculation of 77 years from Israel's founding in 1948.
  • The host expressed skepticism, noting the lack of biblical basis and the 19th-century origin of such predictions, referencing biblical statements that no one knows the day or hour.

  • The host explored psychological reasons why individuals are drawn to apocalypse and rapture predictions, suggesting it stems from a fear of death.
  • The idea of the rapture is presented as offering an appealing alternative to traditional dying, allowing believers to avoid the fear of mortality.
  • The fear of death is attributed to its loneliness and unpredictability, with apocalypse predictions offering a false sense of a communal and predictable end.

  • Arizona's new law requires age verification for adult websites, drawing objections over free speech chilling effects, user non-compliance, and definitional ambiguity.
  • The host argued that age verification for pornography is a widely accepted concept, comparable to regulations for alcohol or tobacco.
  • The host contended that pornography is not speech but prostitution, and that protecting children should be prioritized over arguments for free speech protection, particularly online.
  • It was suggested that if 95% of users refuse age verification, it indicates a significant portion are underage or adults are ashamed of their consumption habits.
