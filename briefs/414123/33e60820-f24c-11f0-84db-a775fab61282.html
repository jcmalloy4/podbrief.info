Ep. 1718 - Domestic Terrorists Have Officially Taken Over Minneapolis. Here's What Needs To Be Done

Key Takeaways

  • Incidents involving alleged left-wing agitators targeting ICE in Minneapolis resulted in vehicle destruction, stolen documents, and firearms.
  • The host criticized Minnesota Governor Tim Walz for allegedly inciting agitators against ICE actions, labeling his claims as fabrications.
  • The host advocated for invoking the Insurrection Act to address perceived domestic terrorism in Minneapolis.
  • Oprah Winfrey's claims about an 'obesity gene' were debated by the host, who emphasized caloric intake and exercise.
  • Senator Josh Hawley questioned an expert witness on the possibility of men getting pregnant during a congressional hearing.
  • The host critiqued modern pet ownership, particularly the view of pets as replacements for children, and the 'pagan worship' of dogs.

Deep Dive

  • The host criticized Minnesota Governor Tim Walz's speech regarding ICE operations, alleging Walz incited agitators and is corrupt.
  • The host dismissed Walz's claims about ICE as fabrications, arguing they were designed to incite violence and advance an open-borders agenda.

  • An incident involved an illegal immigrant attacking ICE officers, leading to an officer firing shots in self-defense.
  • Following the shooting, a mob allegedly destroyed law enforcement vehicles and stole firearms and documents, including names and hotel room numbers of ICE agents and FBI badges.
  • The host characterized these events as domestic terrorism and called for prosecutions, noting thousands of federal agents were expected in Minneapolis.

  • The host discussed polling data and a special election victory in Alabama, suggesting a waning of the left's perceived power.
  • He praised President Trump's assertive approach, citing actions like giving the middle finger to hecklers and halting visa processing from 75 countries.
  • Trump's engagement with Denmark regarding Greenland was noted, with Danish officials discussing a working group after Trump's initial mockery.

  • The host argued for the invocation of the Insurrection Act, stating current law enforcement is insufficient against perceived 'domestic terrorists'.
  • He emphasized that the administration must use all legal force at its disposal, which he believes should be overwhelming, to restore order in Minneapolis.
  • The host noted that ICE and the federal government have not backed down in Minneapolis, with federal authorities responding to incidents with tear gas and rubber bullets.

  • Oprah Winfrey appeared on 'The View' to promote her new book, stating an 'obesity gene' causes overeating, rather than overeating leading to obesity.
  • The host criticized Winfrey's explanation as a denial of personal accountability regarding weight gain.

  • The host asserted that there is no 'obesity gene,' stating weight gain is a simple matter of consuming more calories than expended.
  • He argued that while genetics may influence fat distribution, the fundamental cause of obesity is overeating and lack of exercise.
  • The host noted the decline of the body positivity movement with the availability of weight-loss drugs like Ozempic, which reduce hunger.

  • During a congressional hearing on abortion pills, Senator Josh Hawley questioned expert witness Dr. Nisha Verma on whether men can get pregnant.
  • The host framed this as a test of basic biological reality against political influence, highlighting the expert's difficulty in providing a direct 'yes' or 'no' answer.

  • The host clarified his stance on dogs, stating he criticizes people's 'pagan' worship of them rather than the animals themselves.
  • He shared a personal anecdote about searching for his dog, Penny, in the cold to illustrate his affection for animals.

  • The host critiqued the modern tendency to treat pets as replacements for children or as their equals.
  • He argued this trend stems from a self-centered worldview, contrasting it with human relationships requiring work and sacrifice.
  • The host stated that dogs, while not committing evil acts, also do not make moral choices or exhibit virtue.
