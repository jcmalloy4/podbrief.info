The Matt Walsh Show

Ep. 1692 - "America Only" Should Not Be Controversial, So Why Is It?

Key Takeaways

  • The host defended his 'America Only' philosophy, advocating for national leaders to prioritize U.S. interests above all else.
  • He criticized U.S. Catholic bishops for condemning Trump's immigration policies while allegedly ignoring other political issues.
  • Marjorie Taylor Greene's apology on CNN was condemned as cowardly and undermining her political movement.
  • The host critiqued the feminist concept of 'mankeeping,' arguing it reflects transactional views of relationships and highlights gender differences.

Deep Dive

  • The host introduced his 'America Only' stance, advocating leaders focus solely on the United States and defining himself as an 'American chauvinist'.
  • He stated that even normal statements can cause controversy, citing significant backlash from the right over his position.
  • The host drew parallels between prioritizing America and prioritizing one's own family, referencing founding fathers like George Washington and Thomas Jefferson.

  • The host addressed criticism from Ethan Isaacson, who argued against ignoring international relations and global supply chains.
  • He contended that Isaacson misrepresented his statement, which focused on leaders exclusively benefiting their citizens, similar to a sports team.
  • The host agreed on the need to increase domestic antibiotic production, noting U.S. reliance on foreign sources, particularly China, and past shortages.
  • He clarified his stance on prioritizing national interests does not equate to ending the U.S. dollar's global reserve status.

  • The host refuted accusations that his 'America First' stance conflicts with Christian ethics, citing comments from Peter Laugh, Philip Goff, and Evan Wickham.
  • He argued against the idea that Christian ethics mandate open borders or extensive foreign aid, stating such interpretations lack scriptural support.
  • The host asserted that ICE agents should deport illegal immigrants, criticizing the term 'undocumented neighbor' as weak and aligned with communism.

  • The host discussed a video by U.S. Catholic bishops condemning the Trump administration's immigration enforcement.
  • He contrasted this with the bishops' alleged silence on abortion and other Biden White House policies.
  • The host challenged the bishops to provide specific examples of immigrants arbitrarily losing legal status under Trump, calling the claim a 'lie'.
  • He argued that deporting foreign communists who lied on visa applications is not arbitrary.

  • A protest occurred outside an ICE facility in Portland where participants engaged in aerobics while wearing spandex.
  • The host humorously suggested this was the most 'normal' activity in Portland that weekend.
  • He argued that while such events are easy to mock, conservatives fail if they do not dismantle the leftist movement.

  • The host discussed Marjorie Taylor Greene's recent CNN interview where she apologized for her past rhetoric.
  • He criticized her for not confronting the media directly and instead offering an apology.
  • The host stated that loyalty to politicians must be earned and that elected officials are constantly evaluated.

  • The host viewed Marjorie Taylor Greene's public apology on CNN as 'cowardly' and an attempt to appease the network.
  • He argued that instead of apologizing, she should have challenged CNN's hosts on perceived hypocrisy, citing the Attorney General of Virginia as an example.
  • The host asserted that such apologies debase the individual and the movement they represent, ultimately serving CNN's narrative.

  • The host introduced 'mankeeping' as women caring for men in romantic relationships, criticizing a recent Vice article and tweet on the topic.
  • He argued that the concept of 'unpaid labor' in relationships, as presented by feminists, reveals a desire for transactional relationships.
  • A Vice article was cited linking 'mankeeping' to a 'male loneliness epidemic' where partners are expected to provide emotional support.
  • Pew Research data indicates fewer single women seek relationships compared to single men, suggesting women are opting out of being 'therapists'.

  • The host argued that recent complaints about 'mankeeping' highlight women's selfishness and the negative impact of feminism, leading to demanding behavior.
  • He posited a biological double standard where women are not equipped to handle the same emotional vulnerability from men as vice versa.
  • The host asserted that men are biologically predisposed to carry emotional burdens privately, unlike the expectation for men to comfort wives.
  • He suggested a 50-50 approach to marriage labor is unworkable due to varying 'weight' of tasks between genders, with women often better suited for household management.
