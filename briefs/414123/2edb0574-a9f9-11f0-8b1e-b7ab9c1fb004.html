The Matt Walsh Show

Ep. 1673 - POLITICO Releases An Egregious Hit Piece And Many Republicans Are Falling For It

Key Takeaways

  • Politico's report on young Republicans' group chat jokes drew criticism.
  • Democrats are accused of tolerating violence while condemning jokes.
  • Conservative organizations swiftly condemned the young Republicans' remarks.
  • Left-wing protests feature public nudity and art vandalism.
  • CNN's "male gaze" article on gender shifts was criticized by the host.

Deep Dive

  • The host asserts Democrats are a party of political violence, citing examples of celebrated violence against figures like Charlie Kirk and Donald Trump.
  • Attacks on ICE officers and instances of vandalism are also mentioned.
  • Democratic politicians are noted for defending individuals fantasizing about harming children.

  • Politico published an article about "edgy jokes," including "I love Hitler" messages, in a private group chat among young Republicans.
  • The article implicated a young Republican in the Trump administration, noting the offensive language was intended as jokes.
  • Politico focused on a "sombrero meme" against Hakeem Jeffries, which the host found factually representative of the Democratic platform.

  • Prominent Republicans and state and national GOP organizations denounced the group chat content.
  • The National Young Republicans' board of directors issued a statement calling the language "vile and inexcusable."
  • The board demanded resignations and adherence to high standards, while Senator Chuck Schumer also strongly condemned the jokes.

  • The host argues the left is not genuinely concerned about group chat jokes but aims to destroy conservatives, especially youth outreach organizations.
  • This strategy is suggested to be a form of "terrorism" to win back young voters.
  • The host advocates for defending young people or refusing to participate in attacks against them.

  • The host clarifies he avoids group chats due to criticisms regarding his stance on Politico's reporting.
  • He describes his minimal participation in group chats as that of a silent observer or a rare cicada.
  • This stance is maintained despite ongoing discussions about private communication.

  • Two climate activists from Futuro Vegetal vandalized a Christopher Columbus mural at a Spanish museum with red paint, causing damage.
  • The host suggested severe legal penalties, including amputation, for art vandalism, labeling it a crime against humanity and ancestors.

  • Leftists in Portland engaged in a nude protest against ICE, which included a nude bike ride and a "die-in."
  • The host posits that the prevalence of nudity in left-wing protests stems from the belief that everything for the left relates back to sex.
  • This behavior is suggested to be a desire to destroy traditional identity and culture.

  • CNN published an article titled 'After years of progress on gender, the male gaze is back,' which the host criticized as a repetitive feminist essay.
  • The article discussed a perceived resurgence of the 'male gaze' in media, possibly linked to 2024 or the overturn of Roe v. Wade.
  • The host argued against the concept, suggesting it unfairly blames men for women's choices and preferences.
