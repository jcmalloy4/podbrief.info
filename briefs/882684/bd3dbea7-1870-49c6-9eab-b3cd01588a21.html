The Charlie Kirk Show

The Left's War on ICE + Voter ID for CA

Key Takeaways

  • Local jurisdictions are escalating tensions by threatening federal ICE operations, raising constitutional crisis concerns.
  • A California grassroots initiative to implement voter ID has collected over one million signatures for the 2026 ballot.
  • Widespread protests against Iran's ruling mullahs continue, with minimal coverage in U.S. media outlets.
  • The episode analyzed left-wing ideologies, framing them as anti-Western and contributing to perceived lawlessness.

Deep Dive

  • Blake Neff discussed rhetoric against ICE, labeling agents as "criminals" and "Gestapo," aiming to delegitimize the agency.
  • The goal is to create a situation where local authorities can openly defy federal immigration law enforcement.
  • Concerns were raised about a potential constitutional crisis from local jurisdictions threatening federal law enforcement, specifically ICE.

  • The conversation focused on the left's alleged desire for confrontation between local police and ICE, potentially leading to violence.
  • The discussion referenced the Renee Goode case, suggesting her death was viewed as a "martyrdom opportunity" by some.
  • Speakers posited the left wants ICE to cease enforcing immigration laws, fearing a breakdown in law and order.

  • The host criticized left-wing rhetoric and leaders for allegedly hindering ICE deportation efforts, potentially leading to "civil war."
  • The discussion characterized the left's worldview as viewing the U.S. as illegitimate due to its wealth and European origins, promoting an "anti-white movement."
  • This movement allegedly seeks to dismantle property ownership and redistribute wealth, citing an example in New York City.

  • A grassroots initiative in California to implement voter ID has collected over 1 million signatures, aiming for the 2026 ballot.
  • Representative Ken Calvert discussed the need for voter ID due to concerns about election integrity and California's permissive registration laws allowing non-citizens to vote.
  • Organizers plan to gather 1.2 million signatures by March 15th to verify the required 873,000 for ballot inclusion.

  • Discussions addressed challenges for the voter ID initiative in California, including existing "motor voter" laws, issuance of IDs to undocumented immigrants, and expansive mail-in voting.
  • The initiative is viewed as a significant first step to clean up voter rolls, with organizers encouraging support via voteridcalifornia.org.

  • Polling data suggests strong support for voter ID across party lines in California.
  • The discussion shifted to the upcoming election for California Secretary of State, with support encouraged for Republican candidate Don Wagner.
  • The episode emphasized the need for a more efficient voter system, similar to Florida's, to implement stricter voter ID laws.

  • The discussion shifted to recent protests in Iran, noting minimal coverage in U.S. media compared to other events.
  • Ongoing demonstrations against the ruling mullahs are highlighted, occurring in multiple Iranian cities.
  • Reports indicate government buildings set on fire and alleged control of some cities by protesters, with the regime attempting to control the situation by blacking out internet and other communications.

  • Protests in Iran are linked to a dramatic collapse of the currency, the rial, and soaring inflation of 40-50%.
  • Reza Pahlavi, son of the last Shah, urged President Trump to support protesters, warning of a brutal crackdown and communication blackout.
  • President Trump previously stated on Truth Social that the U.S. was "locked and loaded" to intervene if Iran violently killed protesters.
