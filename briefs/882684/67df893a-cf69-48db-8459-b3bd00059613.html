The Charlie Kirk Show

Remembering the Boss: The Staff's Favorite Charlie Memories

Key Takeaways

  • Staff members of The Charlie Kirk Show shared personal anecdotes and memories of their time working with Charlie Kirk.
  • Themes of Kirk's generosity, mentorship, and distinctive personality traits were frequently highlighted by his colleagues.
  • The episode commemorated Charlie Kirk's legacy, emphasizing his dedication to work, faith, and personal connections.
  • A promotional segment featured YReFi.com, endorsed by Kirk, for refinancing private student loans.

Deep Dive

  • Danny recalled Charlie Kirk's team trash-talking him after an Ohio State loss to Oregon.
  • He retaliated by sending a picture of Mikey wearing an Ohio State sweatshirt depicting a mascot shooting ducks.
  • Mikey reportedly did not wear the Ohio State sweatshirt in front of Charlie Kirk.

  • Charlie Kirk discussed YReFi.com, a service for refinancing approximately $300 billion in distressed or defaulted private student loans.
  • The service offers plans adaptable to monthly budgets and co-borrower release options.
  • Customers may skip up to 12 payments without penalty, though state availability varies.

  • Nate recounted his job interview with Charlie Kirk, which occurred during a drive to the airport.
  • Kirk engaged Nate with personal questions and offered him a job directly, advising against attending college.
  • This approach exemplified Kirk's genuine interest in individuals' life stories.

  • Kylie recalled Charlie Kirk's distinctive pronunciation of the word 'Racist'.
  • Michael noted Kirk's preference for specific intro and outro songs for his show.
  • Staff members also discussed the excitement surrounding Kirk's appearance on a South Park episode.

  • The team recalled Charlie Kirk inviting a man named Rio from Asia to spend Christmas with his family.
  • Kirk was highlighted for his exceptional ability to remember names and specific details about people he met at events.
  • This personal approach was noted as a significant aspect of his character.

  • Staff members recalled Charlie Kirk's dedication to his children, including shopping for them in Tokyo.
  • He would immediately shift to "dad mode" after his first child's birth.
  • Kirk showed excitement for a colleague's baby, born on his birthday, and provided support during a hospital stay.

  • Dallin shared extensive history working with Charlie Kirk from the early days of building Turning Point USA on campus.
  • Colleagues highlighted Charlie Kirk's loyalty to his core team members.
  • This loyalty was a key factor in the long tenure of many of his staff.

  • Heidi recalled Charlie Kirk's reaction to a 'Rush Was Right' shirt and his admiration for Rush Limbaugh's on-air persona.
  • The podcast staff reflected on the impact of Rush Limbaugh's final Christmas message.
  • They noted how the message's meaning changed following Charlie Kirk's passing, committing to remembering both.
