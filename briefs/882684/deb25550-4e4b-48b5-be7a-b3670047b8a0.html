Ask Us Anything 237: Charlie's Favorite Books? 2026 Plans? Surprised by Grace?

Key Takeaways

  • Turning Point Action is focused on building a "new red wall" in Sunbelt states for the 2026 midterms.
  • Charlie Kirk's broad knowledge requires team role segmentation to continue his extensive work.
  • Following Charlie Kirk's passing, team members and listeners report experiences of divine peace and spiritual revival.
  • Charlie Kirk drew significant influence from mentors including Dennis Prager and maintained a wide reading list.
  • Turning Point USA is managing a substantial increase in campus chapter inquiries, streamlining its vetting process.
  • Erica Kirk is actively maintaining political relationships and is noted for her strategic thinking.

Deep Dive

  • Turning Point Action plans to build a "new red wall" in Sunbelt states for the 2026 midterms.
  • Key states for this strategy include Arizona, Florida, Texas, and potentially Nevada and New Hampshire.
  • The Turning Point Action app is highlighted as a tool to identify and mobilize low-propensity Republican voters.
  • Listeners are directed to tpaction.com for volunteering and grassroots organizing opportunities.

  • Charlie Kirk's expertise spanned political, historical, and philosophical subjects; no single team member matches his breadth.
  • The team anticipates future role segmentation, with individuals specializing in specific areas like politics or faith.
  • Kirk's dedication included completing all 31 Hillsdale College online courses and deepening his understanding of scripture.
  • A new free online course, 'The Federalist,' is available via CharlieForHillsdale.com, honoring Kirk's commitment.

  • Team members reported experiencing God's peace and operating on "autopilot" following Charlie Kirk's passing.
  • Witnessing the global impact of Kirk's life has inspired individuals to re-engage with faith and community.
  • The team believes Kirk's death has sparked a revival, citing messages about improved relationships and renewed faith.
  • One couple on the brink of divorce reconciled and improved their marriage, attributing it to Kirk's legacy.

  • Key mentors included Bill Montgomery, Dennis Prager, Frank Turek (for apologetics), and David Horowitz (for networking/fundraising).
  • Dr. James Orr was a more recent mentor who provided insights on classics.
  • Frequent book recommendations included 'Darkness at Noon,' '1984,' 'Age of Entitlement,' and Victor Frankl's 'Man's Search for Meaning'.
  • He also appreciated C.S. Lewis works like 'Mere Christianity' and Frank Turek's 'Not Enough Faith to be an Atheist.'

  • Turning Point USA has received over 120,000 inquiries for new campus chapters, a significant increase from 30-50 per week.
  • The vetting process involves field teams, batch processing, and securing school sponsors.
  • The organization aims to establish Club America chapters in every high school.
  • Applicants are expected to be contacted within one month, with the vetting process ongoing.

  • Charlie Kirk's "secret weapon" for debates involved using Socratic questioning, such as "What evidence do you have?".
  • He emphasized remaining calm, kind, and acting as the "bigger person" while representing views responsibly.
  • Kirk approached opponents with respect while effectively upholding his own arguments.

  • Erica Kirk is maintaining ongoing conversations with contacts from the Trump administration, who offered support post-tragedy.
  • She is described as strategic, measured, and clear-thinking, complementing Charlie Kirk's vision.
  • Her conservatism has reportedly increased, influenced by marriage and motherhood.
  • Erica Kirk is scheduled to address the audience directly about Charlie's impact.
