The Charlie Kirk Show

A Look Back: The Best Clips of Charlie's 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Charlie Kirk, founder of Turning Point USA, emphasized fighting evil and proclaiming truth.
  • The episode commemorates Charlie Kirk's peak influence, reviewing key moments from 2025.
  • Kirk secretly visited Greenland regarding President Trump's proposal to purchase the island.
  • He attended President Trump's 2025 inauguration, signifying his esteemed position.
  • Kirk praised Elon Musk's Twitter acquisition as vital for free speech against censorship.
  • He engaged in high-profile podcast interviews with Gavin Newsom and Bill Maher.
  • Kirk consistently challenged progressive viewpoints, particularly on the definition of a woman.
  • His final TikTok video amassed 152 million views, demonstrating his broad reach.

Deep Dive

  • Charlie Kirk, Don Jr., and others undertook a secret mission to Greenland in 2025 related to President Trump's proposal to purchase the island.
  • Kirk documented the experience, noting locals' affinity for America and Trump.
  • He appreciated Greenland's natural beauty and low population density, contrasting it with his dislike for urban environments.

  • Charlie Kirk and Erica attended President Trump's 2025 inauguration ball, which was moved indoors due to extreme cold.
  • Kirk's prominent seating was noted as a sign of President Trump's esteem.
  • A crowded Turning Point-hosted ball featured Vice President Vance, causing delays for scheduled performances.
  • Charlie Kirk was JD Vance's first guest on Air Force 2.

  • Charlie Kirk admired Elon Musk and his involvement with Tesla, predicting Musk and Trump would reconcile.
  • Kirk considered Musk's purchase of Twitter historically significant for free speech and American society.
  • He contrasted this with an era of cancel culture and censorship that Kirk's actions aimed to counter.

  • Charlie Kirk appeared on Gavin Newsom's podcast, stating his rationale was to ensure a conservative voice was present.
  • A viral moment occurred when Newsom admitted his son was a fan of Kirk, an admission Newsom later downplayed after Kirk's death.
  • During the interview, Newsom also stated his opposition to men competing in women's sports, which reportedly caused reaction within the Democratic party.

  • Charlie Kirk's appearance on Bill Maher's podcast, 'Club Random,' became one of the podcast's most-watched episodes.
  • Kirk experienced initial distress post-interview due to Maher smoking marijuana during the show, contrasting with Kirk's non-smoking, non-drinking habits.
  • A March clip featured Charlie Kirk participating in a viral morning routine challenge involving applying banana to the face, to the surprise of the host.

  • Charlie Kirk enjoyed posing the rhetorical question 'What is a woman?' to challenge audiences.
  • A rapid exchange on the definition of a woman resulted in agreement on XX chromosomes and a declaration of a culture war victory.
  • Kirk and his wife, Erica, celebrated their four-year anniversary, offering advice emphasizing partnership.
  • The hosts reflected on Erica's courage and faith following Charlie's death.

  • YReFi Chief Investment Officer Lane Schoenberger expressed gratitude for the partnership with Turning Point and Charlie Kirk's endorsement.
  • YREFY.com helps refinance approximately $300 billion in private student loan debt in the U.S.
  • Listeners Tim and Judy emphasized Turning Point USA's continued work with young people, citing a critical period in 2024.
  • The host reflected on Kirk's poetic life, dedicated to saving a generation and witnessing young people support Donald Trump.

  • Charlie Kirk opposed Juneteenth becoming a federal holiday, viewing it as a distraction from July 4th and a transformed Texas observance.
  • He made a highly anticipated appearance on 'South Park,' featuring a comedic debate scenario.
  • The 'South Park' segment referenced Kirk's 'Prove Me Wrong' series and included his biblical arguments concerning abortion, citing Jeremiah 1:5.
