Erika Kirk's Opening Speech at AmFest 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Erika Kirk opened AmericaFest, emphasizing improvisation after a technical difficulty and reinforcing Charlie Kirk's mission.
  • AmericaFest 2025 showcased significant growth, attracting 80% new attendees, a third being students, and representatives from 50 states and 25 countries.
  • Turning Point USA reported substantial expansion, including over 1 million student involvements and thousands of new chapters by December 2025.
  • Future initiatives for 2026 include a national tour, campus debates, and a strategic focus on upcoming elections in key states.

Deep Dive

  • Erika Kirk addressed AmericaFest, referencing her husband Charlie Kirk's prior statement, 'Here I am, Lord. Use me.'
  • She began her address by noting a technical difficulty with her iPad, which resulted in the loss of her prepared speech and necessitated improvisation.
  • Charlie Kirk views AmericaFest as his 'Super Bowl,' planning to experience the event from the back of the arena to ensure optimal sound quality.

  • AmericaFest attendance included 80% of individuals new to Turning Point USA events.
  • One-third of attendees were students, and 54% identified as female.
  • The event featured 40 breakout sessions, hosted 178 sponsors, and drew attendees from all 50 states, 25 countries, and Puerto Rico.

  • Erika Kirk stated her commitment to honoring Charlie Kirk by fulfilling his media obligations for his book and maintaining their household's principle of upholding their word.
  • She encouraged the audience to actively participate in conservative causes and connect with like-minded individuals.
  • Attendees, particularly students, were urged to become involved on their campuses by starting or joining 'Club America' chapters.

  • Turning Point USA recorded over 140,000 'get-involved' requests since September 10th and over 1 million students involved.
  • As of December 18th, 2025, the organization established 3,082 high school chapters and 1,432 college chapters.
  • The group is currently starting 50 new chapters daily and has initiated state partnerships with 'Club America' in Oklahoma, Florida, Indiana, Texas, and Tennessee.
  • The 'Make Heaven Crowded Tour' for 2026 was announced, planning to tour the country with specific city stops to be revealed.

  • Turning Point USA has established over 8,000 church partnerships since September 10th and will launch 'Pick Up the Mic' campus debates in Spring and Fall 2026.
  • Turning Point Action plans to focus on the 2026 and 2028 elections, aiming to build a 'red wall' in Arizona, Nevada, and New Hampshire.
  • The organization's goals include electing figures like JD Vance and ensuring congressional support for President Trump throughout all four years of his term.
  • The movement encourages engaging with opposing views with clarity, courage, and grace, asserting that disagreement is part of America.
