AMD Rallies 24% on OpenAI Deal & Bari Weiss Takes Over CBS News

Key Takeaways

  • OpenAI is securing massive compute capacity through multi-billion dollar deals, including a 10% stake in AMD.
  • OpenAI faces significant unprofitability, raising questions about financing its vast infrastructure investments.
  • The company is exploring advertising as a new monetization strategy to reach billions of users.
  • Paramount acquired The Free Press for $150 million, appointing Bari Weiss as CBS News Editor-in-Chief.
  • The Free Press acquisition is viewed as culturally significant but economically questionable.

Deep Dive

  • The SP 500 hit a record high, NASDAQ climbed, and the Dow fell.
  • Bitcoin surpassed $125,000, and gold neared $4,000.
  • Tesla shares rose over 5% following a teaser video release.

  • OpenAI secured a deal with AMD involving a 10% stake in AMD and the purchase of six gigawatts of compute capacity.
  • This partnership is valued at tens of billions of dollars, serving as a bespoke model for OpenAI to secure necessary compute power.
  • AMD's stock increased by 24% on the news of the deal.

  • OpenAI leadership, previously resistant to advertising, is now seriously considering it as a necessary model to reach billions of users.
  • While ChatGPT has a significant annual run rate, scaling subscription models globally faces challenges due to varying price sensitivities.
  • Advertising is predicted to be introduced to ChatGPT within the next year.

  • OpenAI's compute capacity deals total over $882 billion over the next few years, including $200 billion for six gigawatts from AMD and $350 billion for ten gigawatts from NVIDIA.
  • The company reported a net loss of nearly $14 billion in the first half of the year on over $4 billion in revenue.
  • Projected gross spending, including a 20% revenue share with Microsoft, totals $930 billion, with an estimated $780 billion shortfall.

  • OpenAI's financial situation significantly impacts the public stock market, especially AI-related stocks like NVIDIA and AMD.
  • The company, though private, influences stock prices through its announcements, creating potential systemic risk if commitments are unmet.
  • Investors are advised to be critical of AI-driven market rallies and avoid decisions based solely on hype.

  • Paramount acquired The Free Press for $150 million.
  • Founder Bari Weiss will become the new Editor-in-Chief of CBS News, reporting to David Ellison.
  • The Free Press, launched in 2021, has 1.5 million subscribers, including 170,000 paying, and generates an estimated $15 million annually.

  • Paramount's $150 million acquisition of The Free Press is valued at 10 times its estimated $15 million annual revenue.
  • This multiple is considered rich when compared to Netflix's 12 times revenue multiple.
  • The host and Scott Galloway question the economic rationale, suggesting the deal is overvalued.

  • Scott Galloway expressed skepticism about Bari Weiss's ability to reshape the editorial direction of CBS News, likening the attempt to 'bug slapping against the windshield.'
  • He predicted Weiss may leave CBS News within 24 to 36 months, citing challenges in influencing a resistant legacy newsroom.
  • Galloway anticipates massive layoffs and a further diminishment of the CBS newsroom under Weiss's leadership.
