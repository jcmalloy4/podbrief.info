Zohran Mamdani Wins — Why NYC’s Mayor Race Was a Referendum on Inequality

Key Takeaways

  • Shopify reported robust Q3 revenue and GMV growth despite a net income decline driven by rising operating expenses.
  • Consumer demand demonstrates resilience across categories, with Shopify reporting nine consecutive quarters of over 20% GMV growth.
  • The longest U.S. government shutdown to date inflicted a multi-billion dollar economic toll and widespread disruptions to federal services.
  • Public opinion shifted against the party in power during the shutdown, providing Democrats leverage on healthcare issues.
  • New York City's mayoral election results highlight growing dissatisfaction with capitalism and calls for addressing inequality.

Deep Dive

  • Shopify reported a 32% year-over-year revenue increase to $2.8 billion in Q3, surpassing estimates.
  • Net income fell 68% in Q3 due to rising operating expenses.
  • Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) grew 32% year-over-year to $92 billion.

  • Shopify data indicates resilient consumer demand across all categories, with Q3 GMV growth of 32%, marking nine consecutive quarters over 20%.
  • Harley Finkelstein views quarterly earnings as storytelling opportunities for a broad audience beyond investors, including merchants and partners.
  • Founder-led companies are noted for superior modern investor relations, driven by a deep mission and focus on entrepreneurship.

  • The U.S. government shutdown, on its 36th day, caused an estimated $7 billion in lost output, projected to reach $11 billion if prolonged.
  • Approximately 1.4 million federal workers missed paychecks, and 42 million Americans faced reduced SNAP benefits.
  • The reduction in SNAP benefits led to an increased reliance on food pantries.

  • Democrats are showing increased resistance due to concerns over rising ACA premiums and the administration's stance.
  • Polls indicate Republicans are being blamed for the shutdown's economic consequences, giving Democrats a perceived advantage.
  • Democrats are focusing on healthcare issues, specifically restoring Medicaid cuts and addressing ACA premium increases, as leverage in funding negotiations.

  • The shutdown's impact is expanding, with potential U.S. airspace closures due to air traffic control shortages.
  • Services like SNAP benefits and Head Start programs face significant disruptions.
  • A short-term deal on Obamacare subsidies is considered a likely resolution, addressing premium increases affecting Republican states.
  • Public criticism mounted over President Trump's actions during the shutdown, including White House ballroom construction and a Mar-a-Lago party.

  • The likely election of Eric Adams as NYC mayor signals a potential structural overhaul of the current system.
  • This reflects widespread dissatisfaction with capitalism due to income inequality, unaffordable housing, and stagnant wages.
  • The host emphasized that unchecked inequality in America is no longer acceptable and reaching a breaking point.
