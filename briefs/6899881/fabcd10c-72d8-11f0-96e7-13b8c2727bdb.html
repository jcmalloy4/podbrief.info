Prof G Markets

Fed Holds Rates — Inflation Back in Focus

Key Takeaways

  • The Federal Reserve maintained interest rates, signaling a 'dovish' stance on future cuts tied to inflation deceleration.
  • The U.S. dollar's recent devaluation prompts global concerns over U.S. country risk and investor hedging strategies.
  • Big Tech earnings revealed Microsoft's Azure growth slightly missed, while Meta's core advertising business surged.
  • Major tech firms' AI investments are driving genuine economic growth, distinguishing them from speculative market behavior.
  • Adobe's stock is currently undervalued despite strong fundamentals and significant revenue generation from its AI tools.

Deep Dive

  • The Federal Reserve maintained interest rates, citing stabilizing unemployment and somewhat elevated inflation (2:28).
  • Chair Powell indicated an improved economic outlook, though two governors dissented, voting for a rate cut (2:28).
  • Michael Gapen, Chief U.S. Economist at Morgan Stanley, characterized the Fed's hold as 'dovish,' anticipating future rate cuts (3:04).

  • The Fed believes the economy is improving, the labor market is stabilizing, and inflation risks are not escalating (4:19).
  • Inflation remains above the 2% target, but the Fed expects it to decrease later in the year (6:41).
  • Tariffs are pushing goods prices higher, but services inflation is decelerating, and overall goods prices stabilize (7:24).
  • A government shutdown adds uncertainty to inflation figures, with potential catch-up effects in future reports (8:57, 9:45).
  • The Federal Reserve maintains a modestly restrictive policy to manage inflation expectations and ensure tariff-related inflation is transitory (10:50).

  • The U.S. dollar fell to a four-year low, coinciding with a significant rise in gold prices, sparking 'dollar debasement' discussions (11:36).
  • The Federal Reserve does not directly target currency levels or gold prices but acknowledges their impact on growth and inflation (12:37).
  • The global financial system holds approximately $62 trillion in U.S. dollar-denominated assets; concerns about U.S. country risk are leading investors to seek hedges (13:36).
  • U.S. Treasury officials stated they are not intervening to weaken the dollar, contrasting with market concerns about a weaker dollar (15:00, 15:56).

  • Rick Reeder is emerging as a strong possibility for the next Federal Reserve chair, according to prediction markets (17:52).
  • Analysts consider Reeder's extensive financial markets experience and communication skills relevant for the role (17:52).
  • Kevin Hassett is unlikely to be the next chair, leaving Rick Reeder, Kevin Warsh, and Chris Waller as potential candidates (19:51).

  • Microsoft's Azure growth slightly missed expectations at 38% versus a projected 39%, leading to a post-hours stock dip (23:55).
  • Microsoft's cloud growth still outperforms Google Cloud and AWS (23:55).
  • Future growth is secured by remaining performance obligations, with 39% tied to OpenAI (23:55).
  • Microsoft holds a preferential claim to OpenAI's future revenue as both an investor and primary compute provider (23:55).

  • Meta's Q4 2023 earnings saw a positive market reaction despite similar capital expenditure guidance of $115 to $135 billion in 2026 (27:06, 27:23).
  • The core advertising business grew at 24-25%, significantly outpacing Google's ad growth (27:23).
  • Growth in ad volume and higher prices, driven by AI-driven improvements in feed algorithms and ad targeting, fueled the ad business (28:42).

  • Microsoft trades at a trailing PE of 34, a discount to its historical average, while Meta's PE is 29, in its typical lower range (29:46).
  • Google's PE is at a premium despite slower growth in its cloud and ad businesses compared to Microsoft and Meta (29:46).
  • Microsoft and NVIDIA are better positioned for actual AI profit generation in the coming year, with NVIDIA leading (30:54).
  • Core AI investment by major tech firms like Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and NVIDIA is a genuine economic growth driver (32:41).

  • Adobe's stock price declined 33% year-over-year and 50% from two years ago, attributed to slowed revenue growth from 20% to 10% (34:16).
  • Increased competition from Figma and Canva, along with investor concerns about AI impacting designers, contributed to the downturn (34:16).
  • Adobe has substantial revenue of $24 billion and double-digit growth, demonstrating efficiency with over $750,000 revenue per employee and 90% gross margins (35:42).
  • Adobe's AI tools generate over $5 billion in annual recurring revenue and increased revenue per employee by 20% since 2022, contrary to investor sentiment (35:42).
  • The stock is trading at historically low valuations despite strong fundamentals, presenting a 'wonderful company at a fair price' (39:30).
