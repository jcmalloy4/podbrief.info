Prof G Markets

Did Anthropic Just Kill Software?

Key Takeaways

  • Software stocks experienced a significant sell-off driven by fears of AI disruption, but high switching costs may protect established players.
  • Entertainment industry debates shifted from antitrust concerns to political content bias during a Senate hearing.
  • Disney's valuation is impacted by underperforming linear assets, with a new CEO potentially leading divestiture.
  • Anthropic's Super Bowl ad positioned it against OpenAI, highlighting brand strategy differences and a potential market turning point.
  • Antitrust enforcement faces ongoing challenges, with new technologies like AI used as justification to avoid breaking up monopolies.

Deep Dive

  • Scott Galloway was tangentially mentioned in the Epstein files via an email recommending an article he authored.
  • The discussion notes a lack of trusted institutions to sift public information from mere gossip.
  • The mixing of serious allegations with trivial matters may be intentional, contributing to a loss of trust and difficulty in discerning credible information.

  • Cloudflare, Atlassian, and Shopify experienced significant stock declines triggered by fears AI tools from Anthropic and OpenAI could disrupt traditional software revenue models.
  • One host views the market's reaction as an overreaction, similar to Meta's 70% drop after TikTok emerged, presenting potential buying opportunities.
  • High switching costs for enterprise SaaS contracts, including lengthy implementation and contractual penalties for 100% of remaining fees, create strong barriers to adopting new AI tools.

  • Adobe, Salesforce, and ServiceNow are identified as potential buys, having experienced significant stock declines and currently trading below historical averages.
  • Microsoft is considered a potential buy due to its ownership stake in OpenAI.
  • Gartner's stock fell 72% in the past year, serving as an example of a company significantly impacted by the AI thesis.

  • A Senate antitrust hearing involving Netflix and Warner Bros. Discovery executives initially focused on antitrust concerns.
  • The hearing reportedly devolved into a debate about political bias and 'wokeness' in content, with specific accusations made against Netflix regarding LGBTQIA+ content in children's programming.
  • One speaker suggested political motivations and donor influence, rather than core antitrust issues, drove the controversy.

  • Senate hearings are criticized as political theater, with senators often defaulting to 'wokeness' arguments when unsure about complex antitrust issues.
  • The discussion critiques political approaches to inflation, advocating for free trade and massive antitrust action as primary solutions to lower prices.
  • It is noted that the political landscape makes it difficult to side with any specific party due to varied relationships with media companies and figures.

  • Josh D'Amaro is set to take over as Disney CEO from Bob Iger on March 18th, having previously led the successful parks and experiences division.
  • Disney's linear assets (Disney, Nat Geo, ABC) are identified as an overhang, contributing to an 8% revenue decline and 14% EPS decline in that segment.
  • Divesting these assets, even for a nominal price, is deemed crucial to improving Disney's stock performance, potentially through a 'good bank, bad bank' strategy or a spin-off.

  • Opportunities are seen in oversold software stocks, with Adobe, Salesforce, and ServiceNow cited as potential buys.
  • Disney is considered a target for activist investors due to its new CEO, with speculation about a 'Peltz-like figure' acquiring shares.
  • The potential sale of ABC News is noted, with savvy buyers seeking market value, which the seller may not accept due to declining asset value, creating an opportunity for private equity.

  • Anthropic's Super Bowl ad mocked OpenAI's impending introduction of ads into ChatGPT, generating significant buzz and millions of views before its official airing.
  • The ad is described as a 'significant win' for Anthropic, contrasting its brand strategy with OpenAI's and potentially marking a turning point for Anthropic's market valuation.
  • The Super Bowl's continued importance as a marketing platform is highlighted by the ad's impact.

  • Anthropic's ad moment is likened to Apple's 1984 commercial, suggesting it could become the 'heavyweight champion of AI by 2026.'
  • One host predicts Anthropic will surpass OpenAI in market value within 12 months, drawing an analogy to the Dell vs. Gateway computer market dynamics.
  • Anthropic's focus on the enterprise market is seen as a strategic advantage over OpenAI's more consumer-centric approach.
