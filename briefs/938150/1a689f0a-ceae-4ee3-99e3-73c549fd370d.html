20Product: How AI Changes Product Design | Does the Design Phase Become Irrelevant in a World of Vibe Coding | The Five Pillars of Truly Great Product Design with Carl Rivera, Chief Design Officer at Shopify

Key Takeaways

  • Tictail's launch demonstrated ambitious product strategy, despite initial inexperience.
  • Shopify's product approach includes diverse methodologies, from lean to long-term development.
  • Overemphasis on Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) hurt Shopify's Shop App user engagement.
  • Truly great product design prioritizes low latency, fluid motion, and personalized experiences.
  • AI will amplify both low-quality products and potential for outstanding, magical experiences.
  • AI may favor incumbents controlling distribution, network effects, and unavailable data.
  • Centralized design teams will focus on systems and components, enabling broader impact.
  • Remote work is less effective for design and product teams; intentional in-person sessions are vital.
  • Product Managers evolve as orchestrators, aligning teams and ensuring project collaboration.

Deep Dive

  • Tictail, described as the "Tumblr for e-commerce," was founded with an "incredible startup adventure" mindset.
  • The team worked intensely for 1.5 years before launching at a conference.
  • Launch strategy involved a website countdown timer that refreshed to the live service.

  • Shopify's organizational structure includes 'areas' like Financial Services and POS, operating as independent businesses.
  • Each area has leadership teams and multiple product teams, functioning with autonomy.
  • These areas align with company-wide missions and mandates, guided by the founder and CEO.

  • Good design maximizes every aspect of user experience to a '10,' emphasizing speed, ease, and emotional connection.
  • Low latency, fluid motion, and delightful micro-interactions are crucial for a personalized, adaptive experience.
  • Design can intentionally introduce friction to highlight actions but can hinder usability with overly complex interfaces.
  • Shopify's Shop App development revealed the importance of understanding the merchant story, challenging conventional funnel-shortening.

  • AI may controversially favor incumbents due to their control over distribution, consumer network effects, and access to unavailable data.
  • Successful incumbents, such as Shopify and Stripe, are those that remain "hungry, paranoid, and actively innovate."
  • The guest notes increased difficulty for startups to gain visibility and distribution since approximately 2014.

  • Designers are expected to create opportunity spaces and imagine future possibilities, with faster learning curves for new categories.
  • 'Vibe designing' prototypes are increasingly constructed using code and existing components, shifting Figma to a final refinement tool.
  • Centralized design teams will focus on design systems and components, leading to broader impact across user journeys.
  • Shopify's teams are free to choose design tools, with Cursor being the most used, prioritizing productivity over cost.

  • Remote work is less effective for design and product teams, with Shopify utilizing intentional in-person sessions.
  • Starting a company in-person is ideal until approximately 150 employees, then remote structure is preferred for talent access.
  • Hybrid models are considered the least effective due to information asymmetry between co-located and remote team members.
  • The guest's decision to move to New York aimed to reduce "background noise" and achieve focus, diverging from typical startup optionality.

  • Shopify's CEO, Toby, reviews all projects on a rolling two-month cadence, ensuring focus on long-term goals.
  • Monthly executive team meetings are intensive, in-person, and directly examine prototypes, code, or data.
  • This process avoids presentations and pre-reads to maintain focus on actual work and customer experience.
  • Overly complex solutions and ambitious timelines were cited as pitfalls in a unified KYC project, leading to delays.

  • Product organizations are moving towards generalists, with only "best in the world" niche specialists retaining distinct roles.
  • Shopify has dropped 'UX' from designer titles, expecting broader skill sets including information architecture, UX, and prototyping.
  • Product Managers are evolving from "CEO of the product" to orchestrators, serving context and aligning teams.
  • PMs' roles emphasize project management, ensuring collaboration and connection between different teams.

  • Advice for new parents includes having a second child sooner, noting minimal workload increase and mutual entertainment.
  • Partner selection involves a three-part framework: physical attraction, intellectual capability, and shared laughter.
  • Relationships require significant hard work and understanding each other through challenges.
  • A stringent phone and work schedule, leaving phones outside the bedroom and office in the evenings, is a life hack for improved sleep.
