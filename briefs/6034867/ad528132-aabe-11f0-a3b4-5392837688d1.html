Deep Dive

  • Samantha Woll, president of Detroit's only downtown synagogue, was found brutally stabbed to death outside her home in October 2023.
  • The city of Detroit was initially shocked by the high-profile murder.
  • Despite police investigations, no one has been convicted of her murder, contributing to an unresolved mystery.
  • Police swiftly ruled out the possibility of a hate crime, initially considered due to the timing of her death two weeks after October 7.

  • A new podcast series, 'Spiral, Murder in Detroit,' produced by The Free Press, investigates Samantha Woll's murder.
  • The series features exclusive interviews with individuals involved, including Woll's family, lawyers, and jury members.
  • An ex-boyfriend and various authorities connected to the case are also interviewed for the podcast.
  • The podcast, hosted by Frannie Block, will be available starting October 21, 2025.
