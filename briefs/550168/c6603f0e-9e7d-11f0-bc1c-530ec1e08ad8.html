The Joe Rogan Experience

#2387 - Gregg Braden

Key Takeaways

  • Evidence suggests non-natural structures exist on Mars and the Moon, some possibly 50,000 years old.
  • Human chromosome 2 shows evidence of fusion, challenging traditional evolutionary timelines and theories.
  • The guest argues the universe is a living, conscious entity, influencing human origins and capabilities.
  • Current societal trends promote technology integration, potentially diminishing "pure human" abilities.
  • Humans possess innate self-healing, intuition, and resilience, suppressed by external agendas.
  • Global entities are pushing a 2030 agenda toward merging human and digital realms.
  • Climate change is re-evaluated, with data suggesting CO2 levels and ocean warming have deeper cosmic origins.
  • Heart-brain coherence techniques can significantly enhance physical and cognitive well-being.
  • Ancient texts and scientific findings indicate humans possess an extraordinary internal force.

Deep Dive

  • Early Mars images of Cydonia showed a 'Face on Mars' and structures with unnatural 90-degree angles, suggesting intentionality.
  • Freedom of Information requests reportedly reveal NASA pixelated tower-like structures on the Moon, implying obscuration.
  • Similar geometric and artificial-looking formations, including various-sided pyramids, are observed on both Mars and the lunar surface.

  • Modern humans shared Earth with Neanderthals, not descending from them, challenging traditional evolutionary models.
  • Human chromosome 2, vital for advanced cognitive abilities, is cited as a 'smoking gun' for understanding human origins.
  • The Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences concluded human chromosome 2 resulted from an ancestral fusion of two chromosomes approximately 200,000 years ago, deemed unnatural.

  • Prolonged virtual reality (VR) use in children can lead to diminished cognitive, language, and communication skills.
  • A societal trajectory encourages external technology adoption, fueled by a perceived human flaw, evident in the transhuman movement.
  • It is predicted that by 2032, most people encountered publicly may have technology embedded in their bodies.
  • Replacing natural human functions with synthetics risks the atrophy of inherent abilities, following a "use it or lose it" principle.

  • A battle for human essence is underway, with external forces working to deny human capabilities if not actively claimed.
  • Limiting self-expression and fostering division, amplified by social media, are primary methods of societal control.
  • Digital IDs in Europe and the UK, and over 12,000 arrests in the UK for social media posts, are highlighted as tactics restricting freedoms.
  • Populations are allegedly 'programmed' to focus on trivial matters, creating a false sense of power while diverting from broader control issues.

  • Gnostic texts and Dead Sea Scrolls suggest humans were imbued with a force greater than their creators, access to which has been denied.
  • The Nag Hammadi discovery involved a woman finding ancient clay vases containing these documents in a tomb in Egypt.
  • Excluded ancient New Testament texts, such as the Gospel of Thomas and the Apocryphon of John, date to the late 1st and early 2nd centuries.
  • These texts reveal an internal power enabling sovereignty, also noted in Sumerian texts which describe created beings possessing more light.

  • Excluded ancient texts reveal humans possess unique 'force' and 'intelligence,' enabling sovereignty and critical thinking.
  • The Genesis narrative's plural term 'Elohim' implies a collective of intelligences in human creation, linked to DNA mutations 200,000 years ago.
  • Increased global chaos, including wars and economic issues, is posited as a distraction to prevent humanity from 'awakening' to its inherent internal power.

  • Trauma therapy may require addressing both the brain and the heart, as the heart contains its own neural network.
  • The Institute of Heart Math offers techniques to harmonize heart and brain networks, creating a potent, unified system.
  • Humans can consciously create coherence at 0.1 hertz, activating a supercharged immune system, longevity enzymes, and stem cells.
  • Benefits include increased resilience to change, enhanced intuition, precognition, and over 1,300 positive biochemical reactions.

  • NASA data and geological records indicate current atmospheric CO2 levels are not dangerously high and have been significantly higher in Earth's history, correlating with lush environments.
  • Vostok ice core data, spanning 420,000 years, suggests temperature increases often precede CO2 rises, challenging the premise that CO2 drives temperature change.
  • Paul Laviolette's 1980s theory posits cosmic rays from the Milky Way's center can heat Earth's core, causing ocean warming and 90% of CO2 outgassing.
  • Thorium is presented as an abundant, safe, and non-weaponizable clean energy source, with reactors existing in the 1980s.
