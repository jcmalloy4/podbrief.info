The Joe Rogan Experience

#2411 - Gavin de Becker

Key Takeaways

  • Governments engage in covert operations and information warfare to influence public opinion.
  • Public skepticism is crucial for accountability, as institutions often operate without transparency.
  • The pharmaceutical industry faces scrutiny for historical misconduct, profit motives, and alleged suppression of adverse health effects.
  • Historical events and public health crises are re-examined through a lens of potential systemic control and profit influence.
  • Vaccine safety and efficacy are questioned, including ingredients, reported adverse events, and alleged data suppression.
  • Population reduction policies, originating from reports like Kissinger's, have allegedly been implemented globally through medical interventions.
  • The episode critiques mainstream media and scientific institutions for perceived corruption and bias, citing pharmaceutical funding.

Deep Dive

  • The 1972 Kissinger Report, a 1975 U.S. presidential directive, reportedly aimed to reduce populations in 12 specific foreign countries.
  • Methods discussed included medicalizing birth control and incentivizing vasectomies or temporary fertility reduction injections for men.
  • The U.S. allegedly funded vaccines containing HCG in India, Peru, and Africa, which reduced fertility in women.
  • The World Health Organization (WHO) is cited with a mission of population reduction, referencing Bill Gates' comments about using vaccines for this purpose.

  • The Smith-Mundt Act modernization allegedly legalized propaganda use on American citizens, blurring lines between truth and reality.
  • Project Mockingbird is referenced as a historical precedent for intelligence agencies allegedly lying to the public.
  • Saudi Arabia reportedly utilized Twitter bots in 2018-2019 to manipulate trending topics and spread specific narratives.

  • The AIDS epidemic is critically examined, questioning HIV as the direct cause and the pharmaceutical industry's role, referencing the 'House of Numbers' documentary.
  • The guest questions the effectiveness and dangers of the drug AZT, suggesting it caused more deaths than the disease itself.
  • Peter Duesberg, a UC Berkeley professor, proposed that AZT, not HIV, was the cause of AIDS, with a reported case of a child recovering after discontinuing AZT.

  • Technology, particularly the internet, is discussed as a tool for both disseminating truth and obscuring it through information warfare.
  • Prominent scientists were reportedly removed from platforms like Twitter in 2020 for expressing dissenting views on scientific topics.
  • A Henry Ford Medical Center study comparing vaccinated and unvaccinated children was allegedly suppressed, with a doctor reportedly reluctant to release findings due to career repercussions.

  • The guest argues that vaccine safety assessment should focus on investigating rare adverse effects, drawing an analogy to how plane crashes are thoroughly studied.
  • Questions are raised about the necessity of widespread tetanus vaccination, noting its low incidence in the United States and suggesting post-injury treatment.
  • CDC data is cited, suggesting that a significant portion of polio paralysis cases are linked to the vaccine itself rather than natural infection.

  • Strong reservations are expressed about the COVID-19 mRNA vaccine for children, citing a lack of benefit and potential risks, including death or long-term cardiac issues like myocarditis.
  • A significant increase in sudden deaths among young athletes post-vaccination is referenced, contrasted with historical data.
  • The guest notes flu vaccines reportedly contained mercury until Robert F. Kennedy's involvement, stating it was removed from childhood vaccines by 2001 but remained in some flu vaccines.

  • Vaccine ingredients cited include gelatin, chicken embryo protein, cow fetus blood, human fetus DNA, shark liver oil, and monkey kidney DNA.
  • Formaldehyde, polysorbate 80, and potassium chloride (used in lethal injections) are also listed as modern vaccine components, alongside sodium borate and ethylmercury.
  • The inclusion of potentially harmful ingredients like mercury in vaccines for infants is criticized, contrasting its administration with its classification as a hazardous material.

  • Systemic corruption, driven by profit motives, allegedly leads to sacrifices of human lives and avoidable negative health outcomes, with studies like one on the Amish being ignored.
  • People tend to ignore inconvenient truths or conform under fear and social ostracization, contrasting with admired figures like professional fighters.
  • The guest shares personal COVID-19 experiences, noting mild symptoms in his family and his subsequent decision to forgo vaccination after observing concerning events.

  • Pharmaceutical companies, including Pfizer, Johnson & Johnson, and GlaxoSmithKline, have faced financial penalties and criminal convictions for deceptive marketing, fraud, and illegal kickbacks.
  • Specific cases include Pfizer's record fine and Johnson & Johnson's penalties related to baby powder and opioid distribution.
  • Merck's drug Vioxx is cited for allegedly causing approximately 50,000 deaths in America, despite an initial figure of 200 deaths being mentioned.

  • Leaked transcripts from an Institute of Medicine report on vaccines and autism reportedly reveal internal discussions where the CDC requested a specific outcome.
  • The study allegedly sought to avoid claims of causality between vaccines and autism and to not investigate mercury as a potential factor.
  • The report concluded no link between vaccines and autism and advised against further study, a process described as a 'debunking' campaign.

  • The guest suggests that 60 U.S.-funded bioweapons labs in Ukraine are a reason for Russia's actions.
  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is described as an apolitical comedian whose rise to presidency was reportedly aided by an oligarch and U.S. intelligence funding.
  • Zelensky's election and subsequent declaration of martial law, ending future elections, are highlighted as outcomes of U.S. democracy initiatives.
  • The guest concludes Zelensky is a 'construct' or 'created entity,' questioning his current popularity given the ongoing war and casualties.

  • The guest critiques government attempts to promote a monkeypox (mpox) vaccine despite its limited cases and low death toll.
  • The renaming of monkeypox to mpox is suggested as an attempt to make the disease sound more serious and frightening.
  • Optimism is noted from the increased sales of George Orwell's '1984' during the pandemic, and the guest's new book, 'Forbidden Facts,' is promoted to encourage skepticism.
  • COVID shots for children as young as six months are reportedly being halted by Robert F. Kennedy Jr.
