GOOD FOLLOW - All Things Athletes Unlimited, Friendship & More with Sydney Colson & Theresa Plaisance

Key Takeaways

  • Athletes Unlimited (AU) selected Nashville, Tennessee, for its upcoming season due to positive reception.
  • AU provides a crucial alternative to playing overseas, fostering player growth and new opportunities.
  • Sydney Colson is 11 weeks into ACL rehabilitation, progressing well with a positive outlook.
  • The unique AU format emphasizes self-expression, individual scoring, and continuous player development.
  • Theresa Plaisance aims to conclude her basketball career at AU on her own terms, focusing on enjoyment.

Deep Dive

  • Athletes Unlimited (AU) is hosting its welcome weekend and season in Nashville, Tennessee.
  • Nashville was selected over initial considerations for Dallas due to its viability for professional women's basketball.
  • The decision was influenced by positive fan and player reception during the previous season in Nashville.

  • Athletes Unlimited offered Sydney Colson a critical opportunity after her departure from the WNBA.
  • Participation in the AU Las Vegas season directly impacted her career trajectory and competitiveness.
  • The league facilitated new ventures for Colson, including her podcast with Theresa Plaisance.

  • Sydney Colson reports her ACL rehabilitation is progressing well at 11 weeks post-surgery.
  • She describes the recovery as a mental and physical journey, emphasizing a positive outlook and trusting the process.
  • Colson plans to support Athletes Unlimited and its players in the upcoming season, even if not fully recovered.

  • Athletes Unlimited emphasizes self-expression and individuality for players, allowing personal style within uniform requirements.
  • The league's format fosters individual and athletic growth through peer interaction, with one guest participating for four seasons.
  • It features five-on-five play, rotating teams, individual scoring, and strategic 'games within the game,' such as intentional fouling.

  • Theresa Plaisance (TP) and Sydney Colson (Sid) formed their friendship and podcast collaboration naturally during a 2022 training camp.
  • They recognized each other as kindred spirits despite previously knowing of each other but not interacting directly.
  • TP describes Colson as generous, supportive, and influential, particularly for young individuals.

  • Theresa Plaisance aims to finish her basketball career strongly at Athletes Unlimited on her own terms.
  • Her focus is on enjoyment and self-confidence, rather than external professional pressures.
  • Plaisance transitioned her humor to television, crediting Sydney Colson with encouraging her first 10-episode series.
