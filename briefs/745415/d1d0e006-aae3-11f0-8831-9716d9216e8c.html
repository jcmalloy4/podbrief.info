The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Top Five NFL Games To Watch in Week 7, Falcons Soar with Bijan Robinson, Dan Campbell Wants Detroit Rocking Again, Plus USC Legend Matt Leinart Shares What's Great About South Bend, Indiana

Key Takeaways

  • The episode previewed significant upcoming college and NFL rivalry games, including Notre Dame vs. USC and Chiefs vs. Raiders.
  • USC legend Matt Leinart discussed the national blood shortage, emphasizing that one donation saves three lives, part of the Big Ten/Abbott Labs drive.
  • Expert analysis was provided on the complexities of evaluating NFL quarterbacks, citing factors such as system fit and coaching impact.
  • Detailed predictions and insights for various NFL Week 7 matchups were offered, involving teams like the Packers, Cardinals, Falcons, and 49ers.
  • A "Pick Six" segment featured specific betting predictions for individual player performances and game outcomes, including interception and touchdown forecasts.

Deep Dive

  • The host ranked the top six football rivalries, led by Notre Dame vs. USC.
  • Other significant rivalries included Tom Brady vs. the Manning Brothers, Cowboys vs. 49ers, Cowboys vs. Packers, and Cowboys vs. Steelers.
  • Army vs. Navy was identified as the top rivalry overall.

  • USC legend Matt Leinart highlighted a national blood shortage during the Big Ten and Abbott Labs' 'We Give Blood' drive.
  • He stated one blood donation can save three lives, and the campaign has already saved over 110,000 lives this year.
  • Participation is encouraged via big10.org/Abbott or by texting DONATE to 222-688, with winning schools receiving $1 million for community health advancement.

  • The discussion focused on the enduring challenges of evaluating NFL quarterbacks, even in the modern era.
  • Factors such as system fit, coaching, and game evolution were cited as key difficulties.
  • Examples like Baker Mayfield, Sam Darnold, and Daniel Jones illustrated struggles, while Brock Purdy and the San Francisco 49ers' West Coast offense were noted as successful system examples.

  • USC legend Matt Leinart detailed the intensity of the USC vs. Notre Dame rivalry, contrasting the environments of South Bend, Indiana, with Los Angeles.
  • Leinart recalled an instance where Notre Dame's student body significantly greeted the USC team bus, with coach Sarkeesian acknowledging the intense atmosphere.
  • He noted the rivalry felt "bigger" than UCLA due to its history.

  • A discussion on favorite cookies ensued, prompted by the mention of cookies as a reward for blood donation.
  • Oreos, particularly when dunked in milk, were highlighted as a preferred choice.
  • The segment also touched on warm chocolate chip cookies and a debate regarding optimal microwaving times and the superiority of air fryers for reheating food.

  • Week 7 NFL picks included the Green Bay Packers versus the Arizona Cardinals, with Henry Rollins picking the Packers.
  • The Jacksonville Jaguars' inconsistency was discussed, with Chris Cote picking the Rams minus three against them.
  • For the Atlanta Falcons versus San Francisco 49ers game, Chris Cote picked the Falcons plus two and a half.

  • The Indianapolis Colts versus the Los Angeles Chargers game was analyzed, with the Chargers initially favored by 1.5 points and a total of 48.5.
  • One speaker advocated for betting on the Colts plus 1.5, citing the Chargers' offensive line injuries and overall struggles.
  • This betting strategy was contrasted with the Colts' perceived resilience.

  • The host's "Pick Six" predictions for Week 7 included Justin Herbert and Matthew Stafford to throw interceptions.
  • Jalen Hurts was predicted to score a rushing touchdown, while Lad McConkie, Travis Kelce, and Quincey Judkins were predicted to score touchdowns.
  • Chris Cote offered his own picks, including a Travis Hunter receiving yards under and Saquon Barkley to score a touchdown.
