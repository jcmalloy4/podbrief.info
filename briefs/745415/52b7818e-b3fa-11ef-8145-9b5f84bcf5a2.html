The Big Suey: The College Football Power Hour

Key Takeaways

  • Several NFL teams, including the Patriots and Texans, displayed significant offensive struggles and controversial coaching decisions.
  • Baker Mayfield is gaining MVP consideration due to multiple game-winning drives and consistent performance despite team injuries.
  • Clemson coach Dabo Swinney faces intense criticism and scrutiny after a major upset loss and questions about his coaching philosophy.
  • College football featured controversial officiating, notable team upsets, and standout defensive performances.
  • The Miami Hurricanes under Mario Cristobal are demonstrating physical dominance and a strong, confident team identity.

Deep Dive

  • The Patriots recorded five turnovers in their game against the Jets.
  • Titans coach Mike Vrabel controversially attempted a fourth-down conversion from his own 16-yard line.
  • The Texans were identified as having the lowest-scoring offense, with specific criticism directed at quarterback C.J. Stroud's performance.

  • Baker Mayfield led two game-winning drives in a recent game, contributing to four in three games this season.
  • He is positioned as an MVP candidate, delivering wins despite key player injuries like Mike Evans.
  • While noted for his ability to win, the discussion acknowledged his capacity to lose games, contrasting him with Daniel Jones.

  • Dabo Swinney faced criticism for perceived arrogance, particularly after a loss to Syracuse as a 17-point underdog at home.
  • His contract includes varying buyout clauses depending on potential hiring schools like Alabama, where Clemson might now hope he leaves.
  • Paul Finebaum suggested Swinney consider other job offers or a transition to television, citing his stubbornness with modern college football trends.

  • Texas Tech demonstrated an impressive performance with a backup quarterback and a defense heavily invested in offseason spending.
  • A controversial play involving Oklahoma's Jackson Arnold against Auburn saw SEC refs allegedly admit to a blown call, leading to an unguarded touchdown.
  • Arkansas lost a late lead and a potential game-winning field goal attempt against Memphis, contributing to a weekend of upsets.

  • Dabo Swinney's recent performance includes three or more losses in three of his last four seasons.
  • Clemson suffered an unexpected 17.5-point home loss to Syracuse, a game in which the team was never in control.
  • This uncharacteristic performance for a top-10 ranked team has raised questions about his adaptability to college football's transfer portal era.

  • Saszlo recounted tailgating at an ESPN Radio campus tour event alongside Mike Ryan.
  • The event featured Willis McGahee's boisterous behavior and the presence of Florida Panthers personnel.
  • Mike Ryan's tailgating skills and the comfortable game-watching setup were highlighted as positive aspects of the experience.

  • The Miami Hurricanes showcased physical dominance against an in-state rival, positioning them as potential contenders.
  • Coach Mario Cristobal is described as having extreme confidence in his team's roster, contrasting with his past stress levels.
  • The team's identity has shifted towards a physically dominant running game, moving away from reliance on a quarterback like Cam Ward.
