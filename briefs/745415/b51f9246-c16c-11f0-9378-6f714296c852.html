The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Alley Oop 136: Jokić Drops 55, Beal’s Season Ends, Nico Fired, Hidalgo Goes Nuclear & Steph Still Elite!

Key Takeaways

  • Nikola Jokic's exceptional scoring performance and Bradley Beal's season-ending injury made NBA headlines.
  • Women's college basketball saw dominant performances from stars Hannah Hidalgo and Audi Crooks.
  • Steph Curry, at 37, continued to deliver elite scoring displays, defying social media criticisms.
  • The Dallas Mavericks front office faced scrutiny regarding personnel decisions and strategic direction.
  • Unconventional fan interactions, from court invasions to unique attire, added memorable moments to sports.

Deep Dive

  • Nikola Jokic delivered an exceptional performance, noted for his potential to score 80 points.
  • Bradley Beal suffered a season-ending hip fracture, prompting discussions of his retirement and impact on the Phoenix Suns.
  • Norm Powell was mentioned as a solid alternative performer who has played well.

  • Hannah Hidalgo achieved 44 points, 9 rebounds, 4 assists, and 16 steals in an outstanding performance.
  • Audi Crooks scored 43 points in just 19 minutes during a women's college basketball game.
  • The hosts pledged to increase coverage of women's college basketball on their show, 'Alley Hoop'.

  • Discussion addressed comments on the Golden State Warriors' team toughness and focus, including player Pajemski.
  • Steph Curry, at 37, responded to aspirations of surpassing him with a 46-point performance against the San Antonio Spurs.
  • This performance defied social media criticisms about his age and speed, reaffirming his elite status.

  • Speculation arose about a potential trade involving Nico Harrison, whose contract extension made a trade impossible until January.
  • The firing of Nico Harrison was interpreted as an admission of error by the Dallas Mavericks organization.
  • Owner Patrick Dumont faced criticism for perceived lack of basketball knowledge, with a fan in a Luka Doncic jersey cited as a 'final straw' for his judgment.
  • Strategic options for the Mavericks included tanking for draft picks or evaluating potential future rosters with players like Kyrie, Cam Boozer, Cooper Flagg, and Anthony Davis.

  • A fan ran onto the court during a Clippers game, with his evasive maneuvers likened to 'AI agility'.
  • A video was analyzed of a fan scoring a goal during a soccer match after putting on a jersey.
  • A baseball fan wore a provocative t-shirt referencing a player's physique during the ALCS, prompting speculation about intentions and humor effectiveness.
  • The hosts reflected on a 'big dumper' theme that they felt was poorly understood, suggesting a Vlad Guerrero Jr. jersey would have been more impactful.
