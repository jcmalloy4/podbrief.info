The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

GOOD FOLLOW - The Aces Win the Championship!! Angel Reese’s Motion is Undeniable, Ros Roses & More!

Key Takeaways

  • The "Good Follow" podcast recorded 4,000 YouTube subscribers and significant audience growth.
  • The Las Vegas Aces secured a championship following a season marked by significant adversity.
  • A'ja Wilson delivered a historic individual season, earning both league and Finals MVP titles.
  • Jewel Loyd showcased a resurgent performance during the Aces' playoff run, overcoming past struggles.
  • Angel Reese expanded her influence through major partnerships and cultural milestones like the Met Gala.
  • DraftKings partnered with the Larry Fitzgerald Foundation for Breast Cancer Awareness Month, aiming to raise $100,000.

Deep Dive

  • The "Good Follow" podcast, relaunched in January, achieved 4,000 YouTube subscribers.
  • Hosts Roz Gold-Onwude and Angel McCoughtry noted significant audience growth across podcast downloads and social media engagement.
  • The hosts expressed gratitude to their listeners for the show's milestones.

  • The Las Vegas Aces' championship was considered their most difficult due to significant adversity, including a 53-point loss.
  • The team proved doubters wrong, demonstrating season-long resiliency and adapting to roster changes.
  • A coach expressed deep investment in the players' greatness, highlighting the emotional challenge of this particular championship.

  • A'ja Wilson received a 'rose' for her historic season, during which she won nearly every individual award.
  • Her accolades included both league MVP and Finals MVP.
  • The speaker emphasized the importance of recognizing her consistent greatness, urging listeners to avoid 'greatness fatigue'.

  • Jewel Loyd was recognized for her significant impact on the Aces' championship run.
  • She overcame past struggles with performance and bullying, experiencing a resurgence in the playoffs.
  • An emotional post-game moment with coach Becky Hammon highlighted Loyd's gratitude for belief, enabling her to 'play free'.

  • Bam Adebayo provided a supportive role as A'ja Wilson's boyfriend.
  • He publicly displayed support during the WNBA finals by attending games.
  • Adebayo also referenced Wilson's past MVP and Defensive Player of the Year awards.

  • Angel Reese is noted for her significant cultural and financial impact beyond her WNBA season.
  • She secured partnerships with Juicy Couture and Reebok, including a signature sneaker, and a role in 'House of Dynamite'.
  • Reese became a Met Gala committee member and appeared on the cover of Vogue, highlighting her broad appeal, particularly to Gen Z.
