Good Follow - Connecticut Sun’s Bria Hartley Talks Sophie Cunningham, This Season’s Sun Team, Motherhood, & More!

Key Takeaways

  • Bria Hartley made a successful WNBA comeback with the Connecticut Sun after a three-year injury hiatus.
  • Hartley navigated severe physical and mental challenges, including five surgeries, with resilience, faith, and therapy.
  • The WNBA game has intensified in physicality and speed, with increased social media attention impacting player-fan interactions.
  • Motherhood provided Bria Hartley with significant motivation, contributing to her personal growth and best basketball years.
  • An on-court altercation occurred between the Connecticut Sun and Indiana Fever, with Hartley clarifying a specific injury was unintentional.

Deep Dive

  • WNBA star Bria Hartley returned to the league with the Connecticut Sun after a three-year absence due to multiple injuries.
  • She described her comeback season as a success, though she aimed for greater consistency in her performance.
  • Hartley noted the WNBA game is now significantly more physical and faster, requiring adjustment to a more aggressive style of play.

  • Hartley sustained a meniscus injury in practice, attributing it to fatigue after a strenuous three-game road trip with increased playing time.
  • She detailed recovery from five surgeries, including two ACL tears, and admitted losing belief in another WNBA opportunity.
  • Her resilience stems from faith, church attendance, and utilizing resources like sports psychology and therapy.
  • The guest identified the biggest mental hurdle as the absence of basketball after not being signed in 2023, leading her to explore youth coaching.

  • The Connecticut Sun finished near the bottom of the league, navigating significant roster turnover and a new coaching staff.
  • Despite initial difficulties, the team demonstrated growth and resilience throughout the season.
  • Team chemistry developed later in the season, particularly among former UConn teammates, as hard work began yielding results on and off the court.

  • An on-court altercation occurred between the Connecticut Sun and Indiana Fever, involving multiple players and escalating emotions, with inconsistent refereeing suggested as a contributing factor.
  • The guest clarified that a previous incident leading to Sophie Cunningham's injury was unintentional.
  • Hartley emphasized her positive relationship with Cunningham and the subsequent support she received online regarding the incident.

  • Hartley's journey as a mother began with her son's birth in 2017, following her 2014 WNBA draft.
  • Motherhood, which became publicly known via an ESPN ticker, helped her become more unselfish and coincided with her best years in basketball.
  • Having a child provided motivation and a 'chip on your shoulder,' influencing her to set an example through actions and deal with adversity, which her son has witnessed.
  • Her son, described as mature and outgoing, often interacts with adults and sometimes encourages her to socialize, helping her step out of her shell.
