The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: Serving An On-Time God (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Guest Juju Gotti presented DraftKings betting picks, plus Top 5 lists for rewatchable dramas and things he should have said.
  • The hosts celebrated Chris Whittingham's engagement and discussed his successful sports broadcasting career.
  • Audience polls debated the offensiveness of the term "peg leg" and assessed various phrases as "douche" behavior.
  • The host expressed frustration regarding a lack of commitment for a live stream to watch an upcoming University of Miami game.

Deep Dive

  • Juju Gotti provided betting picks for "Thursday Thunder," presented by DraftKings.
  • Picks focused on player statistics such as catches and carries.
  • Players mentioned included Harrison Wallace III, Design Stribbling, Keely Marion, and Big Flesh.

  • Juju Gotti presented his "Top 5 Dramas to Rewatch" list, explicitly excluding comedies.
  • His list included "Shameless" at number five and "The Wire" at number four.
  • "Fargo" was number three, prompting a discussion about the quality of its different seasons.
  • "True Detective" and "Breaking Bad" were also discussed, with one participant admitting to not having watched "Breaking Bad" completely.
  • An audience poll later indicated 78% found "Family Ties" not as good as "The Sopranos" as a rewatchable drama.

  • Juju Gotti presented his "Top 5 Things He Should Have Said This Week" list.
  • Topics included Miles Garrett's on-field actions and a controversial statement comparing "The Wire" and "Breaking Bad."
  • The list also covered issues with amusement park rides and the Oklahoma City Thunder's significant home loss to the Hornets.
  • Snoop Dogg being called a hero for his political involvement was discussed.
  • The final item referenced "Zaslow trying to steal that brother's job," concluding with a congratulatory note to Chris Whittingham.

  • The podcast hosts congratulated Chris Whittingham on his engagement.
  • They discussed interviewing him and proposing a "true or false" game show segment about him.
  • Whittingham's success in sports broadcasting, particularly with the upcoming World Cup, was acknowledged.

  • The hosts debated whether the term "peg leg" is offensive in reference to prosthetic legs, particularly concerning pirates.
  • Audience polls explored whether Ed Orgeron resembles a particular woman, with 97% responding affirmatively.
  • Another poll questioned if one can be shot without being surprised, with 53% of the audience answering yes.
  • Calling an airplane a "bird" was labeled "douche" behavior by 82% of the audience.
  • Mike Ryan's phrasing "Miami's dialed" instead of "Miami's dialed in" was also deemed "douche" behavior by the audience.
