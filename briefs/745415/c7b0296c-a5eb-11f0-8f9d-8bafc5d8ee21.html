The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 1: Making Time (feat. Dave Dameshek & Matthew Berry)

Key Takeaways

  • Deion Sanders' health and coaching longevity at Colorado are under scrutiny.
  • John Harbaugh's future with the Baltimore Ravens is a topic of internal debate.
  • Perceptions of NFL head coach intellect, particularly Nick Sirianni's, are discussed.
  • The "tush push" play is effective but faces probable elimination post-season.
  • Matthew Stafford's quarterback value and late-game performance are being re-assessed.
  • Baker Mayfield's departure from the Browns is attributed to injury and locker room issues.
  • Matthew Berry offers specific fantasy football predictions and favored matchups for the week.

Deep Dive

  • Deion Sanders' health issues include amputated toes and bladder cancer complications requiring a portable toilet on the sidelines.
  • The host questioned if coaching is worth the physical toll for Sanders given his past accomplishments.
  • A guest argued Sanders' commitment despite potential departures of Shadur Sanders and Travis Hunter.
  • Dave Dameshek compared Sanders' situation to a gambler walking away from a winning streak.
  • Speculation suggests Sanders will not remain head coach at Colorado for more than a few years due to health and perceived peak relevance.

  • Discussion centered on Lamar Jackson's career, injury history, and the Baltimore Ravens' struggles.
  • A debate emerged about reassessing Coach John Harbaugh's tenure, with suggestions it might be nearing an end.
  • A hypothetical trade scenario was proposed, exchanging John Harbaugh for the Pittsburgh Steelers' Mike Tomlin.

  • The discussion centered on historical quarterback decisions, drawing parallels between Tom Brady's early career and current coaching dilemmas.
  • Specific reference was made to Carson Wentz and Brian Daboll's situations with the Giants.
  • The segment also touched upon Jackson Dart as part of this broader comparison.

  • The conversation addressed the perception of intellect assigned to NFL football leaders.
  • Nick Sirianni was compared to coaches like Andy Reid, Kevin O'Connell (KOC), Sean McVay, and Kyle Shanahan.
  • Questions were raised regarding Sirianni's success given his perceived demeanor.

  • Participants agreed the "tush push" play is boring but acknowledged its effectiveness.
  • Mac Jones was cited for successfully using the play for a first down, tearing his jersey in the process.
  • The play presents a competitive disadvantage for players with back issues, such as Matthew Stafford.
  • It is suggested the "tush push" play will likely be eliminated after the current NFL season.

  • Discussion centered on Matthew Stafford's performance and perceived value as a quarterback.
  • His ability to convert on fourth-and-short was highlighted.
  • Stafford's avoidance of late-game interceptions was contrasted with earlier perceptions of his play in Detroit.

  • Analysis focused on Baker Mayfield's situation with the Cleveland Browns.
  • His injury and locker room dynamics were suggested as contributing factors to his departure.
  • A player named Miles was referenced as potentially influencing these dynamics.
  • The analysis was based on information from "plugged-in sources."

  • Matthew Berry provided fantasy football predictions for the upcoming week.
  • He highlighted specific matchups like Tetero McMillan against the Cowboys and Rashad White against the 49ers as surest bets.
  • Berry identified Mason Taylor of the Jets as having the highest utilization score among tight ends over two weeks.
  • His advice is based on researched probabilities, with content available on fantasylife.com and YouTube/Peacock.
