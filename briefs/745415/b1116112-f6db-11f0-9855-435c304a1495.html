The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: The Cock Of The Walk (feat. Jarrett Stickem)

Key Takeaways

  • Jonathan Zaslow faced scrutiny for failed sports predictions, including the Nets' 54-point loss.
  • Giannis Antetokounmpo's declining performance fueled trade speculation, with Miami as a potential destination.
  • College football's transfer portal created chaos, notably Duke suing quarterback Darian Stidham.
  • The Florida Panthers' playoff chances are a concern due to recent team performance and injuries.
  • Mike Ryan highlighted top "badass plays" from the University of Miami's football season.

Deep Dive

  • Jonathan Zaslow's prediction for the Nets resulted in a 54-point loss (120-66) to the Knicks.
  • This score was noted as significantly lower than any other team's season low.
  • His prediction that Mike Brown would be fired also proved incorrect.

  • "Mad Dog" was noted for mispronouncing quarterback Jared Stidham's name.
  • The Denver Broncos were five-and-a-half-point underdogs in a conference championship game.
  • This spread was the largest for a home team in such a game since 1970.
  • The large spread was attributed to the opposing defense's strength against quarterbacks with limited experience.

  • Discussion arose regarding Giannis Antetokounmpo's post-loss reactions and Doc Rivers' coaching legacy.
  • The 2008 Florida Gators football team's championship roster reportedly included 41 players who were later arrested.
  • This fact was noted as being omitted from a Netflix documentary about the team.

  • The Milwaukee Bucks held an 18-25 record following a recent loss.
  • Giannis Antetokounmpo's shot attempts decreased over the last six games.
  • A debate centered on whether Giannis should request a trade from the Bucks.
  • Some argued his passive approach could be detrimental, potentially turning fans against him.

  • Giannis Antetokounmpo's comments about teammates being selfish and not knowing how to win were discussed.
  • Miami reportedly believes it will be a preferred destination for Giannis if he becomes available.
  • The Heat would prioritize acquiring Giannis over making smaller roster moves.
  • Prior communication between Giannis's camp and the Heat was noted before the season.

  • Jonathan Zaslow expressed concern for the Florida Panthers, returning early from vacation due to recent team performance.
  • The Panthers were described as five points out of a wild card spot with 34 games remaining.
  • Key injuries, including Seth Jones and Matthew Tkachuk, were discussed as factors impacting the team.

  • Mike Ryan presented his top five "badass plays" from the University of Miami's football season.
  • Highlighted plays included Malachi Tony's fourth-down reception and Mark Fletcher's "beast mode" run.
  • Ruben Bain's interception against Notre Dame and Keontae Scott's pick-six were also featured.
  • A touchdown by Marty Brown was mentioned, but it was later nullified.
