The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 1: Michelle's Horrible Advice (feat. Michelle Beadle)

Key Takeaways

  • The San Antonio Spurs are projected for a playoff run, contingent on Victor Wembanyama's anticipated healthy return.
  • Zion Williamson's recent weight loss has sparked debate regarding its potential impact on his athletic performance.
  • Michelle Beadle advised against deleting phone numbers, sharing her method of tracking contact changes over time.
  • MLB Wild Card playoff results and the "Rust vs. Rest" dynamic for bye teams were extensively analyzed.
  • Shohei Ohtani's strong postseason performance was contrasted with Aaron Judge's playoff batting struggles.
  • Michelle Beadle discussed her upcoming 50th birthday, humorously requesting monetary gifts from financially stable friends.

Deep Dive

  • The host expresses a strong "homer" outlook for the San Antonio Spurs to make the playoffs.
  • Expectations are high, emphasizing Victor Wembanyama's health and readiness for success.
  • This optimism comes despite the team's quiet performance after Wembanyama's injury last season.

  • Zion Williamson is noted for his significant weight loss and improved appearance.
  • Discussion prompts a debate on whether this weight loss impacts his inherent strength and athleticism.

  • Billy asks Michelle Beadle for advice on managing phone contacts, specifically when to purge numbers.
  • He recounts mistakenly texting a former coworker from nearly 20 years ago while testing positive for COVID-19.
  • The incident led to confusion and highlighted potential complications from outdated contact information.

  • Michelle Beadle advises against deleting phone numbers, sharing her method of updating contact entries with dates.
  • The conversation touches on the potential for accidental contact with old acquaintances.
  • It also addresses the risks associated with outdated contact information.

  • A baseball discussion begins with McMahon's play and its significance in Yankees-Red Sox history.
  • The crew analyzes McMahon's play over the dugout railing, noting Red Sox players' indifference except for one assistant.
  • The hosts debate whether the added playoff round dilutes the impact of postseason victories.
  • The host expresses a preference for the division round over the wildcard round.

  • The panel discusses the concept of 'Rust vs. Rest' for teams with byes in the playoffs.
  • It is noted that teams with time off held scrimmages to stay sharp during their bye periods.
  • The segment examines national interest in teams such as the Brewers, Blue Jays, Mariners, and Phillies, favoring the advancement of the Dodgers and Yankees.

  • Shohei Ohtani's powerful home run against a 101 mph fastball in the postseason is highlighted.
  • Ohtani's playoff success is contrasted with Aaron Judge's regular-season power numbers and postseason drop-off in OPS and batting average.
  • The discussion questions Judge's postseason impact compared to Giancarlo Stanton's role in the Yankees' past World Series run.
