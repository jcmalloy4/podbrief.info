The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: Playoffs For Dummies

Key Takeaways

  • WrestleMania ticket prices reached $9,000 for top seats before the fight card was announced.
  • The Miami Marlins secured a mathematical chance at the postseason with a seven-game winning streak.
  • Debate continued on the Miami Hurricanes' football performance and coach Mario Cristobal's game management.
  • Discussions included the Detroit Tigers' significant September collapse and other notable sports failures.

Deep Dive

  • Cheapest seats for WrestleMania were priced at $900, with top seats reportedly costing $9,000.
  • These prices were described as 'egregious' as the fight card for the event had not yet been announced.
  • A strategy of waiting for last-minute ticket releases was noted as a potential approach to managing high costs.

  • Mike Ryan argued Miami's offense has averaged 11 drives per game this season, a decrease from 15 last year, impacting scoring and 'style points'.
  • It was contended that game statistics, rather than the final score, made a recent Miami game appear poor, despite a late nine-minute drive by Miami.
  • Despite defensive strength and forcing opponents into three-and-outs, Miami's games against Notre Dame and Florida remained competitive.
  • Coach Mario Cristobal's game management was debated, with some suggesting his decisions hinder the team despite analytics from last season.

  • Analysis suggests the Florida Gators have a significant chance of finishing the season with a 1-11 record.
  • The team was criticized for 'choking away' a game against South Florida, with a missed field goal by kicker Franco Grimaldi cited as a pivotal moment.

  • The conversation touched upon former LSU coach Ed Orgeron's career, including a national championship with Joe Burrow, and subsequent reports of misconduct.
  • Orgeron's buyout was referenced at $17 million, alongside a related divorce settlement.

  • The Marlins have won seven consecutive games, bringing their record to three games under .500.
  • Winning their remaining five games could lead to an 82-80 record, triggering potential two-team tie scenarios against teams like the Giants and Cardinals.
  • For a postseason berth, numerous other teams, including the Reds, Diamondbacks, and Mets, must lose games.
  • In a three-team tie at 82-80, the Marlins would win the tiebreaker in five out of six potential scenarios.
  • The only scenario where the Marlins would not clinch the final wildcard spot is if they are tied with both the Diamondbacks and Cardinals, and the Diamondbacks win based on division record.

  • The Detroit Tigers' collapse was highlighted, having lost a significant number of September games after holding a 10.5-game lead.
  • Pitcher Eduardo Rodríguez's errors, including struggles with bunts and flipping the ball between his legs, were described as unique and significant gaffes.
  • Collapses are argued to be more impactful in baseball due to the daily schedule creating a 'slow death' over final weeks.
  • Other examples of notable failures include the 9-2 Dolphins' 1993 season-ending losing streak and the undefeated Patriots' Super Bowl loss.

  • Green Bay Packers quarterback Jordan Love's average depth of target was notably low at 1.9 yards against Cleveland.
  • This low depth was attributed to the absence of deep-threat receivers, specifically Jaron Reed and Christian Watson.
  • The NFL's increased emphasis on avoiding turnovers was questioned as a factor potentially reducing offensive explosiveness and high-yardage passing games.
