Postgame Show: Top 5 Things That Deserve More Credit (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Juju proposed a college football playoff solution involving an extra week for seeding.
  • Juju provided DraftKings betting insights for the Cowboys-Lions game, including CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens.
  • The Buffalo Bills acquired defensive back Darius Slay.
  • Club 520 podcast announced a pioneering shoe deal with Adidas.
  • Audience polls covered topics from sports figures' fame to jersey retirements and team performance.

Deep Dive

  • Juju offered DraftKings betting insights for the Cowboys-Lions game, starting at 1:04.
  • Predictions included CeeDee Lamb over 84.5 receiving yards and George Pickens over 70 yards.
  • Tom Kennedy was predicted over 2.5 catches, citing injuries in the Lions' secondary.

  • Juju presented a "Top 5" list of individuals and items deserving more credit, starting at 4:19.
  • The list included Aaron Gordon for his performance despite personal tragedy.
  • Other mentions were self-driving cars, Eagles' player Lane Johnson, Bryce Young's inconsistent performance, and Caleb Williams's potential.

  • The episode announced that Darius Slay has been picked up by the Buffalo Bills, at 6:35.
  • This news concluded a segment discussing a "Top 5" list.

  • A series of audience polls began at 9:53, discussing various sports and pop culture topics.
  • One poll asked about retiring Jimmy Butler's jersey in Miami; 87% of the audience voted no.
  • Another poll questioned if Pablo Torre ruined the Clippers, with 89% of the audience responding yes.
