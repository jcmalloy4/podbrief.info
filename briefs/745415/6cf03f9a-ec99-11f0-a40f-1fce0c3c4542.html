The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: It's Today? (feat. Tig Notaro)

Key Takeaways

  • Comedian Tig Notaro shared her unique perspectives on sports, navigating pop culture, and airplane etiquette during her guest appearance.
  • Speculation surrounding the Miami Dolphins' coaching future, specifically John Harbaugh, and franchise stability was a significant discussion point.
  • The trade of Trae Young to the Washington Wizards prompted analysis of his value, team strategy, and comparisons to other NBA stars.
  • A reporter's musical side hustle, including a song advocating for John Harbaugh to coach the Dolphins, was featured.
  • Tig Notaro detailed her 'Handsome' podcast, which features comedians discussing questions from celebrities for lighthearted conversation.

Deep Dive

  • Comedian Tig Notaro joined the podcast to provide expertise on Mississippi for an Ole Miss vs. Miami game breakdown.
  • Notaro admitted she does not follow sports but mentioned her family's connections to Ole Miss and LSU football.
  • The host expressed admiration for Notaro's career, best-selling author status, and resilience after breast cancer.

  • Tig Notaro discussed a past Funny or Die segment where she pretended not to recognize celebrities for comedic effect.
  • She detailed her childhood TV viewing habits, mentioning shows like 'Family Ties,' 'Good Times,' and 'The Waltons.'
  • Notaro elaborated on the mental health benefits of disconnecting from popular culture and news, finding her Apple Watch heart rate monitor more engaging.

  • Tig Notaro explained her podcast 'Handsome,' which features comedians discussing questions submitted by celebrities.
  • The podcast aims for lighthearted conversation and a break from external noise, with questions ranging from serious topics like forgiveness to personal inquiries.
  • Notaro mentioned her first touring dates in years were scheduled for Florida, following a two-and-a-half-year break for filming a Star Trek series.

  • Discussion centered on the Miami Dolphins potentially hiring John Harbaugh after firing Mike McDaniel, with reports suggesting the team's interest.
  • The team's instability, owner Stephen Ross, the Brian Flores lawsuit, and quarterback Tua Tagovailoa's performance were noted.
  • Debate arose over granting a coach significant power, drawing parallels to past situations with Nick Saban and Bill Parcells, and emphasizing the importance of the quarterback position.

  • Trae Young was traded to the Washington Wizards, sparking initial reactions of surprise at his destination.
  • Initial desired destinations for Young included Minnesota and Brooklyn, with Washington showing subsequent mutual interest.
  • The trade highlighted financial implications due to Young's contract extension and Washington's salary cap standing.

  • The Washington Wizards' franchise history and recent asset management strategy were critiqued, highlighting prolonged playoff struggles.
  • The team's pattern of acquiring veteran players as temporary assets rather than building a core was noted.
  • The discussion questioned the rationale behind comparing Trae Young to Stephen Curry, especially against Luka Dončić's generational talent.

  • Trae Young's career arc and the Atlanta Hawks' strategy were analyzed, comparing it to the Oklahoma City Thunder's model of accumulating depressed assets.
  • The Washington Wizards' front office connection to OKC was noted as relevant context for the trade.
  • Despite Young averaging 25 points and 10 assists, being compared to Mike Conley was suggested as a potential overachievement, contrasting with initial Steph Curry aspirations.

  • A song was featured imploring the Miami Dolphins to hire John Harbaugh as their next coach, following another 'season to nowhere'.
  • The proposed salary for Harbaugh in the song was discussed, starting at $50 million and escalating to $150 million.
  • The segment highlighted a reporter's side hustle of creating songs and impressions, prompting questions about its professional implications.
