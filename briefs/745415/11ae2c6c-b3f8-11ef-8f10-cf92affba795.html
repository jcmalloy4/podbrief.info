The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: The Rob Ryan Tweet

Key Takeaways

  • Wrestler Mick Foley resigned from WWE due to political disagreement over the company's association with President Trump.
  • The New York Knicks opted not to raise an NBA Cup championship banner at Madison Square Garden.
  • A Las Vegas trip included a companion's $6,700 slot machine win, while the speaker experienced a loss.
  • Audience polls revealed 74% report flying in dreams and 68% have experienced a dream within a dream.

Deep Dive

  • Wrestler Mick Foley departed WWE over a political disagreement with the company's association.
  • Foley disagreed specifically with WWE's relationship with President Trump.
  • He formally quit his Legends contract following this association.

  • The New York Knicks will not raise an NBA Cup championship banner at Madison Square Garden.
  • This decision contrasts with other teams, such as the Lakers and Bucks, who did raise banners.
  • The rationale behind the Knicks' decision is subject to debate, with fan sentiment cited as a factor.

  • A trip to Las Vegas included a missed opportunity to see 'The Wizard of Oz' at The Sphere.
  • Companion Michelle Beetle achieved a significant slot machine win of $6,700.
  • The speaker, in contrast, reported a $500 loss during their gambling session.

  • A poll indicated 74% of the audience reports being able to fly in their dreams, describing it as effortless.
  • Another poll revealed 68% of the audience has experienced a "dream within a dream."
  • A third audience poll found 77% of respondents described their wife as a "total stud," with 23% disagreeing.
