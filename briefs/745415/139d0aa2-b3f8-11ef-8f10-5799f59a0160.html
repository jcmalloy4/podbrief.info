The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Hockey Show: Every Foot Matters

Key Takeaways

  • Thanksgiving marks a critical point for NHL teams to assess playoff standings.
  • Concerns are mounting over the Milan Olympic hockey arena's size and readiness.
  • The NHL is enforcing helmet rules, fining teams for pre-game skate non-compliance.
  • Connor Bedard's exceptional play continues to impress, igniting Olympic participation hopes.
  • The Edmonton Oilers are experiencing unexpected struggles due to injuries and defensive play.
  • The Florida Panthers are in a significant slump, attributed to injuries and blown leads.

Deep Dive

  • Hosts acknowledge Thanksgiving as a key point for assessing NHL team standings and playoff chances.
  • Brad Marchand suggested media in Toronto overreact to the Maple Leafs' season, noting they are only 4 points out of a playoff spot.
  • The discussion debated the sincerity of Marchand's comments, given his past reputation.

  • Construction issues in Milan may result in an Olympic hockey rink smaller than standard NHL dimensions due to a metric-customary system mix-up.
  • NHL Deputy Commissioner Bill Daly suggested NHL players might not participate if the venue is not completed in time.
  • Torino, which hosted the 2006 Olympics, was mentioned as a renovated, albeit distant, alternative arena option.

  • The San Jose Sharks and Ottawa Senators were fined by the NHL for players not wearing helmets during pre-game skate.
  • This rule was established for players drafted in 2019-2020 and later to enhance player safety.
  • One host debated the balance between player safety regulations and individual player choice regarding equipment.

  • Connor Bedard showcased two spectacular goals in a recent game against Vegas, demonstrating improved speed and shot release.
  • The hosts discussed Bedard's eligibility and strong desire for him to play in the Olympics, potentially alongside Celebrini.

  • DJ Bean of the 'What Chaos! Podcast' discussed his collection of Seth Jones Winter Classic jerseys, noting they were personally purchased.
  • Bean clarified that many items on his show's set are bought by him rather than provided by sponsors.
  • He defended Seth Jones as a 'very good player,' despite not being considered elite, and acquired the jerseys after games.

  • During the Panthers' overtime loss to Nashville, Philip Forsberg attempted a 'Michigan goal' which was successfully defended by goalie Bobrovsky.
  • The play prompted a discussion on whether the 'Michigan goal' is becoming outdated or if NHL goalies are adapting to defend it more effectively.

  • DJ Bean attributed the Edmonton Oilers' early struggles to injuries and inconsistent play from key defensemen like Darnell Nurse and Evan Bouchard.
  • The Oilers recently secured a 9-4 victory over the Seattle Kraken.
  • Bean expressed optimism for an Oilers' turnaround, though he anticipates a more challenging recovery compared to the Panthers.

  • The Florida Panthers are currently in a slump, having lost 5 of their last 6 games, including 4 in regulation.
  • Goaltender Bobrovsky's poor performance and blowing two-goal leads in four straight home games have exacerbated the team's issues.
  • The slump is largely attributed to significant player attrition, with 7 key players missing, forcing reliance on less experienced players.
