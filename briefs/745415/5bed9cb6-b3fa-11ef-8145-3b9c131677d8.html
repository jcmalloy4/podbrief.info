The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The B** S***: SO DID WE! (feat. Chuckle F***)

Key Takeaways

  • The College Football Playoff selection process, particularly regarding Miami and Notre Dame, generated significant debate.
  • Broadcasters Bob Wishusen and Joey Galloway faced criticism for their commentary and perceived biases in football rankings.
  • A controversial foul call in a basketball game and a football player's alleged groin punch sparked heated discussions.
  • The show delved into internal dynamics, including host conflicts and a producer's travel plans.

Deep Dive

  • A hypothetical college football game between Miami and Notre Dame was discussed, with Notre Dame favored by 4.5 points on a neutral field.
  • One perspective argued Notre Dame would win on a neutral field, referencing a 70-7 victory over Syracuse.
  • Conversely, Miami was noted to have controlled Notre Dame in their earlier matchup, despite a close score.
  • A speaker asserted Miami deserves a playoff spot due to improved performance since earlier losses.

  • The College Football Playoff committee's decision-making was questioned, citing Notre Dame's favorability over Miami despite a head-to-head loss.
  • Speakers expressed frustration that on-field results appeared to be disregarded, impacting the perceived fairness of rankings.
  • The host suggested that if Miami is unfairly excluded from the College Football Playoff due to biased perspectives, they should be crowned national champions.
  • A comparison to Ichiro Suzuki's contract negotiations was used to argue for the importance of head-to-head results in rankings.

  • Disrespect towards broadcasters Bob 'Chuckle F***' Washusen and Joey Galloway was a central topic, with the host defending Washusen as a South Florida legend.
  • The discussion questioned prioritizing an ESPN personality over local talent and friendship in perceived slights.
  • Joey Galloway's commentary was critiqued, labeling him a 'shitstirrer' and questioning his sincerity and consistency.
  • AP voters, including Jordan McPherson, were criticized for not ranking Miami higher despite a shared record and a head-to-head win against Notre Dame.

  • A controversial foul call during a Creighton vs. Baylor basketball game was debated, specifically a blocking foul on a dunk attempt.
  • Broadcaster John Bishop's strong reaction to the call was highlighted, with hosts debating its validity.
  • One host labeled the call 'horse crap' and a 'bailout call by an incompetent referee,' involving a player in the restricted area.
  • The hosts analyzed a play-by-play announcer's call, debating whether they said 'effing foul' or 'blocking foul'.

  • Mike was reported to be criticizing Bob Wishusen's coverage of the Miami Hurricanes, with Wishusen declining a show invitation.
  • Zaslow's upcoming trip to Norman, Oklahoma, for a game was discussed, including concerns about potential extreme cold.
  • The hosts discussed the show's potential to be its 'most bleeped hour' in history during a Thanksgiving-themed discussion.
  • A 'Frito microphone' reference was clarified as referring to a person, Josh Friedman, rather than the snack company.

  • A controversial incident during a football game involved a player appearing to punch an opponent in the groin.
  • Participants debated whether the punch was intentional or accidental and if it warranted criminal charges.
  • The play was replayed and analyzed, with arguments for it being an attempt to hit the ball versus a criminal act, referencing a player with a cast.
  • The sound of a player being hit in the groin was played, prompting discussion on player toughness.
