Postgame Show: Spliff Notes with JuJu Gotti

Key Takeaways

  • Football's Rams-49ers expected to outrate baseball's Yankees-Red Sox, despite streaming.
  • Juju described physical discomfort after undergoing a unique bed-rest punishment.
  • Hosts critiqued NFL player conduct regarding officials and potential broadcaster styles.
  • Discussions covered quarterback media obligations and Shador's professional interactions.
  • Dan Le Batard received a listener-voted punishment regarding attire and condiments.

Deep Dive

  • Discussion compared expected viewership for the Yankees-Red Sox game and the Rams-49ers Thursday Night Football matchup.
  • Prediction indicated football would dominate ratings, even with its Amazon Prime streaming platform.

  • The conversation addressed Juju's punishment, which involved him staying in bed.
  • Juju described experiencing physical discomfort from lying in one position for an extended period.

  • Hosts critiqued potential broadcasting styles, specifically disliking commentators who predict penalties before officials.
  • Players surrounding officials and signaling penalties were criticized as contrary to desired dramatic reveals.
  • Quarterbacks like Aaron Rodgers and Jared Goff signaling 'we're good' after penalties were deemed unsportsmanlike.

  • The discussion addressed media interaction of third-string quarterbacks.
  • The opinion was shared that players are not obligated to speak with media during availability, despite ESPN interest.
  • Quarterback Shador's media availability was highlighted, noting his professional demeanor and perceived fatigue with questions.
  • His recent lighthearted interactions and appearance, linked to his Sanders heritage, were also discussed.
