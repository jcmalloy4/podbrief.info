The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Greg Gives Chris An Aneurysm (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Greg Cody's knee injury prompts questions about his rehabilitation and workout claims.
  • Hosts repeatedly struggled with name recall, notably Wilt Chamberlain and George Harrison.
  • The "Jeremy as George Harrison" prank fooled many, but not Greg Cody.
  • An Adele tribute led to analyzing Miami Heat player dynamics and UCF's 2017 season.
  • NFL "Shoulda Couldas" segment critically reviewed game decisions and team strategies.
  • A privacy concern arose regarding a teammate filmed without consent, prompting an HR report call.

Deep Dive

  • Greg Cody's son questioned his use of a microphone, sparking discussion about past performances.
  • The conversation touched on identifying performers, including a reference to Ethel Merman.
  • Kirsten's singing voice was praised, leading to a playful argument about identifying people.

  • Discussion centered on the death of Jimmy Cliff, recognizing his role in introducing reggae through 'The Harder They Come' soundtrack.
  • Toots Hibbert of Toots and the Maytals also noted his death "a few years prior."
  • A host struggled to recall names, mentioning Jimmy Cliff, leading into a debate on merits of spoken versus written word.

  • One host struggled to recall Wilt Chamberlain's name, experiencing a moment of panic compared to the movie "Inside Out."
  • This was linked to prior confusion regarding George Harrison and a "show-long bit."
  • Discussion touched on claims of seeing the Beatles on Ed Sullivan in 1964 and attending a Wilt Chamberlain game.

  • Greg Cody explained his foot was on the desk due to an aching, surgically repaired knee.
  • Hosts questioned if he was skipping rehab sessions and doubted his exercise bike use.
  • It was suggested he uses the exercise bike in his "Barbie room" office for comedic bits, not actual workouts, despite claiming to have added "10 years" to a career.

  • An Adele tribute segued into analyzing Miami Heat player dynamics, focusing on Tyler Herro and Norman Powell.
  • The discussion drew parallels to UCF's 2017 undefeated season and alleged injustices.
  • A participant noted Miami has lost "twice" to 'unwrecked Jeeps,' referencing a prior discussion about Norman Powell.

  • The show's ongoing "Jeremy" bit was revealed to involve an impersonator mistakenly identified as George Harrison.
  • Greg Cody was called out for being the only one who realized it wasn't Jeremy and for not reacting.
  • This lack of reaction frustrated the hosts, who noted the bit fooled the audience but not him.

  • The hosts introduced a "Top Five Shoulda Couldas" segment, starting with the Buffalo Bills' performance.
  • They questioned a Cardinals kicker's missed field goal and Trevor Lawrence's performance with the Jaguars.
  • The Giants' decision not to kick a field goal, which prevented overtime, was analyzed.

  • The "Shoulda Coulda" segment concluded with questions about the Minnesota Vikings' top-10 quarterback draft decision and their process.
  • Separately, a host discussed an uncomfortable situation where a teammate was filmed without consent, advocating for an HR report.
  • NFL statistics were highlighted, comparing George Pickens' receiving yards to the entire Pittsburgh Steelers wide receiver corps this season.
