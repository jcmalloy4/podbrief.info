AFC and NFC Championships: Time to Cheer for The Team We All Hate

Key Takeaways

  • The NFL season concludes, but its influence continues year-round through offseason activities.
  • The AFC Championship pits the Patriots against the Broncos, prompting debate on fan rooting interests and backup quarterback narratives.
  • The NFC Championship features the Rams as favorites against the Seahawks, with predictions for a close contest.
  • NFL coaching changes and speculation are active topics, impacting both current teams and potential future hires.

Deep Dive

  • The NFL season concludes, marking a 7-month gap until its return to on-field play.
  • Despite the break, the NFL maintains year-round relevance through free agency, the draft, and training camps.

  • The Patriots vs. Broncos AFC Championship prompts debate on fan rooting interests, considering the Patriots' historical dominance.
  • The possibility of Jarrett Stidham, who reportedly hasn't thrown a pass in two years, reaching the Super Bowl is discussed as potentially "underwhelming."
  • Comparisons are drawn to past backup quarterback Super Bowl winners like Nick Foles.

  • The NFL coaching carousel is a central topic, with Mike McDaniel's job security noted.
  • Bills owner Terry Pegula's critical comments regarding coach Sean McDermott and player Keon Coleman are cited, potentially diminishing the Bills' coaching job desirability.
  • Bill Belichick is mentioned as a hypothetical candidate for the Bills.

  • For the AFC Championship, the Denver Broncos are 4.5-point underdogs against the New England Patriots.
  • Guest Nick Kostos predicts the total score will be "over" 42.5 points and expects the Patriots to cover the spread.
  • Historical games, including the 2006 Colts-Patriots and 2015 Patriots-Broncos, are referenced.

  • In the NFC Championship, the Los Angeles Rams are 2.5-point favorites against the Seattle Seahawks.
  • Nick Kostos asserts the winner of this game will win the Super Bowl.
  • He predicts a Rams victory, contingent on an "A-plus" performance, with a final score of Rams 28-27.
  • The Rams' healthy defensive front seven and Sam Darnold's play are cited as key factors.
