The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Dave Called It: Tomlin Out, MJD In & the Best Weekend of the Year

Key Takeaways

  • Mike Tomlin's 19-year tenure as Steelers coach concluded, prompting debate on his legacy.
  • NFL coaching trends highlight the success of offensive coordinators from "McVay tree" and "Shanahan tree" systems.
  • The divisional round features high-stakes matchups where weather conditions and team health are critical factors.
  • The Los Angeles Rams are considered a highly talented team but face challenges from cold weather environments.
  • Expert predictions were offered for key playoff games, including specific outcomes and DraftKings "Pick Six" prop bets.

Deep Dive

  • Coach Mike Tomlin departed the Pittsburgh Steelers after 19 years, with opinions split on his tenure.
  • His success in consistently raising the team's performance floor was noted.
  • However, his failure to reach a Super Bowl in later years despite strong personnel was also highlighted.

  • Maurice Jones-Drew, who covers the Rams, hosts the "Between the Horns" podcast focusing on the team's weekly activities and injury updates.
  • Jones-Drew shared an anecdote about being quarantined with the Rams due to a flu outbreak in Charlotte before a game.
  • The incident highlighted the team's efforts to prevent illness from impacting player performance.

  • Coaches from the "McVay tree" and "Shanahan tree," such as Liam Cohen, Mike LaFleur, KOC, and Zach Taylor, have achieved success in new roles.
  • This trend drives other NFL teams to seek innovative offensive strategies, making coaches with ties to these systems highly sought after.
  • Offensive coordinators are argued to be more critical than the quarterback in certain situations, as demonstrated by examples like Shanahan with Brock Purdy.

  • The Houston Texans' defense is identified as championship-caliber, drawing comparisons to Seattle's dominant units.
  • Their defense, featuring multiple pass rushers and versatile defensive backs, reduces pressure on the team's offense.
  • Despite his talent, CJ Stroud's current inconsistency, marked by turnovers followed by successful plays, significantly impacts the team's overall performance.

  • The Rams missed the number one seed in Seattle and are now slated to play in frigid Chicago.
  • Despite a controversial two-point conversion call and player frustration over the loss, the team recorded 500 yards of offense and scored 37 points.
  • Early defensive injuries allowed backups to gain experience, contributing to key plays in later games, such as Kobe Parkinson's game-winning touchdown in Carolina.

  • Maurice Jones-Drew reflected on his NFL career, where he ranks 50th among all-time rushers, and his non-selection as a Hall of Fame finalist.
  • He expressed a wish to have taken more breaks and pursued other aspirations earlier in his career.
  • Jones-Drew confirmed he possesses a player's Super Bowl ring from his time with the Los Angeles Rams in 2021.

  • The Chicago Bears vs. Los Angeles Rams game is projected to be affected by cold weather, with temperatures in the low 20s and winds.
  • This frigid forecast is highlighted as a significant factor, particularly for Matthew Stafford, who holds a 1-9 career record in cold-weather games.
  • Pundits predict a Rams victory, citing their offensive talent and ability to establish a run game against the Bears' defense.

  • The San Francisco 49ers, despite strong offensive potential, face significant hindrances due to potential injuries to Fred Warner and George Kittle.
  • Sam Darnold is noted as facing pressure in the rivalry matchup against the Seattle Seahawks.
  • One prediction suggests the Seattle Seahawks will win the game and cover the spread.
