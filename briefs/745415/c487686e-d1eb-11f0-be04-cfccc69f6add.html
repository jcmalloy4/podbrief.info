The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: Being Attacked In a Public Forum (feat. Dave Dameshek)

Key Takeaways

  • The Baltimore Ravens extended tight end Mark Andrews, sparking discussions on his age and team receiving history.
  • Debates emerged on the greatest NFL rivalries and the diminishing careers of aging athletes like LeBron James.
  • College football playoff evolution and subjective rankings continue to fuel discussion and perceived biases.
  • The history of the football term 'safety' was explored, alongside proposals for significant rule changes.
  • Podcast hosts shared personal anecdotes and debated appropriate payments for the Tooth Fairy.

Deep Dive

  • The Ravens extended tight end Mark Andrews, who is 30 years old.
  • Discussion covered Andrews' career achievements for the team, including becoming the all-time receiving leader.
  • The conversation also touched on the Ravens' historical struggles to find top receiving talent.

  • Potential consequences of Lamar Jackson losing a key game were discussed.
  • Focus centered on whether Jackson's career trajectory would require a significant adjustment in playing style.
  • Comparisons were drawn to LeBron James's career transition and adaptation over time.

  • Surprise was expressed that a team vying for an ACC championship against a ranked opponent was not more successful.
  • Discussion covered the evolution of college football playoff systems from bowl games determining champions.
  • Commentary highlighted the current 12-team playoff format, noting persistent debates over the best team.

  • Skepticism was expressed regarding the Super Bowl contention of the Denver Broncos, New England Patriots, and Chicago Bears.
  • An argument was made that quarterbacks in their second to sixth seasons are in a 'sweet spot' for winning the Super Bowl.
  • This assertion was supported by historical data gathered from the last 25 years, emphasizing the importance of securing the top seed.

  • Critique was leveled against the term 'safety' in football, specifically its application when a player is tackled in the end zone.
  • A comparison between Jameer Gibbs and Barry Sanders was made, with Gibbs asserted as the modern-day equivalent.
  • A hypothetical question about preferring a gold jacket (Hall of Fame) or a Lombardi Trophy (Super Bowl win) was posed, citing Cam Newton and Antonio Brown choosing the jacket.

  • The origin and reasoning behind the football term 'safety' were discussed.
  • It was explained that the term traces back to rugby strategy, where a 'safety' represented a last line of defense.
  • This strategic concept was subsequently carried over into the rules of football.

  • A proposal was made to eliminate the quarterback sneak and 'tush-push' plays in favor of more traditional, contested short-yardage plays.
  • The host advocated for bringing back the fullback position and running plays like the fullback trap.
  • A suggestion to move the goalposts back to the goal line to 'jazz up' football sparked debate about historical rules and safety concerns.

  • One host recounted an elaborate 'tooth fairy' setup for his daughter's first tooth, involving a special receptacle and a $20 payment.
  • The appropriate amount for lost teeth was debated, with $20 for the first tooth and $5 for subsequent ones being discussed.
  • The conversation noted some parents reportedly give $50, contrasting with the hosts' perspectives on what is considered 'rich' for a child.
