The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: Leading With Love (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • The "Against the Spread" segment offered NFL picks for multiple games, including the Raiders, Dolphins, and Texans.
  • Guest Juju provided detailed corrections and criticisms regarding previous show discussions.
  • A top five list of memorable sports celebrations was presented, alongside an investigation into their origins.
  • Humorous alternative names for the injury 'turf toe' were suggested.
  • Discussion covered the Emmy-winning show 'Adolescence' and Twitter poll results on boxing and dog farts.

Deep Dive

  • Mike Ryan favored the Raiders against the spread in the Monday Night Doubleheader, citing Geno Smith's statistically better performance in domes.
  • The Dolphins were picked to cover the 12.5-point spread against the 2-0 Bills in the Thursday night game, influenced by Greg Cody's opinion.
  • The Buccaneers were considered overhyped, leading to a pick for Houston and CJ Stroud in their Monday Night Football game.

  • Juju corrected the "Two America" segment, noting a lack of shared host interest in Terrence Crawford vs. Canelo.
  • Corrections were made regarding Max Kellerman's return and the specific nature of Josh Allen's injury.
  • Juju defended Puka Nacua's running style against host commentary, emphasizing the physical toll players endure.
  • Juju corrected Dan's assessment of Ken Rosenthal's demeanor, stating Rosenthal displayed disgust, not fear, during an interview.

  • A top five list included Joe Horn's flip phone, Carl Edwards' NASCAR backflip, LeBron James' 'Silencer,' Jamal Anderson's 'Dirty Bird,' and Dusty Baker/Glenn Burke's high five.
  • The segment investigated the origins of celebrations, clarifying the timeline of T.O.'s actions versus Joe Horn's.
  • Homer Jones was credited with inventing the spike celebration in 1965.

  • Discussion moved to injuries, specifically Joe Burrow's turf toe, prompting a comedic top five list.
  • Alternative names included 'Nosferatu toe,' 'Torn Achilles toe,' and 'Texas Chainsaw Massacre toe.'
  • Other suggested names were 'Terminator T-1000 toe,' and 'severed toe.'

  • The Emmy-winning show 'Adolescence' by Seth Rogen was discussed, noting surprise at its lack of wider acknowledgment despite multiple awards.
  • Twitter poll results showed 62% of respondents favored Terrence Crawford as the face of boxing over Jake Paul.
  • A separate poll revealed 67% of the audience confirmed they know the smell of a dog's fart.
