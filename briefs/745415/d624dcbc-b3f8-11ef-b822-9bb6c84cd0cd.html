The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 1: The House I Could Have Built If They Let Me (feat. Tony Calatayud)

Key Takeaways

  • Tony recounted his unfulfilled FIU football tryout experience as a kicker.
  • NFL weekend observations covered team dynamics, player performances, and strategic decisions.
  • The podcast featured a segment critically deconstructing Miami Dolphins' names and leadership.
  • Shohei Ohtani's multi-faceted playoff performance was lauded as historically unprecedented.
  • A host detailed a perplexing American Airlines travel experience involving a bus leg.

Deep Dive

  • Tony claimed he was not given a football during his failed walk-on tryout as a kicker at FIU Stadium.
  • He stated into a hot mic that FIU Stadium was "the house I could have built if they had let me."
  • The coaches at the time were identified as possibly Ron Turner or Long Kruger, not Mario Cristobal.
  • Lack of footballs was attributed to NCAA practice time rules.

  • Observations included frustration over Jacksonville Jaguars' inconsistency, citing numerous penalties.
  • The Kansas City Chiefs' performance was acknowledged as potentially underestimated, with Rashi Rice noted.
  • Brian Flores's strategy to counter the 'tush push' involves placing a player horizontally on the field.
  • The Cowboys were projected as a 12-win team with a healthy Dak Prescott, despite Trayvon Diggs' injury.
  • Bo Nix's impactful fourth quarter for the Giants included two rushing and two passing touchdowns.

  • Hosts offered humorous, critical interpretations of Miami Dolphins associated names and roles.
  • "Mike McDaniel" was deconstructed as "Miserable/Muerto" and "Chris Greer" as "Come Mieda" (shit eater).
  • Tua's name was interpreted as "Tremenda Mieda" (tremendous turd), and Stephen Ross's 'E' as "Encojonau" (upset or lacking 'balls').
  • The discussion concluded with a call for the organization to "clean house."

  • Tony announced a potential role as honorary captain for an upcoming FIU Panthers football game at FIU Stadium.
  • The segment referenced his access to the stadium and past NCAA violations.
  • A visual gag involving a large, impractical goalpost sparked humorous discussion about its size and weight.

  • The Rams' strategy under Sean McVay was highlighted for its meticulous approach with quarterback Matthew Stafford.
  • Wide receiver Devontae Adams' dedication to executing routes at exact yardage was emphasized.
  • The discussion underscored the necessity for adaptability and strategic superiority in high-stakes NFL games.

  • Shohei Ohtani recorded an extraordinary performance in a baseball playoff game, hitting three home runs and 10 strikeouts.
  • This feat was described as an unprecedented athletic achievement, surpassing historical accomplishments by Babe Ruth.
  • Ohtani's athleticism was termed "superhero athleticism," with his dual-threat abilities lauded as unique.

  • Shohei Ohtani's unique dual-threat abilities were compared directly to those of baseball legend Babe Ruth.
  • The Los Angeles Angels' past team performance was questioned despite having stars like Ohtani, Mike Trout, and Albert Pujols.
  • The host expressed surprise at Ohtani's $2 million salary, contrasting it with Babe Ruth's off-season income from vaudeville shows.

  • A host recounted a confusing American Airlines travel experience to South Bend for an ESPN college football assignment.
  • The journey included an unexpectedly long layover in Chicago and a segment where passengers boarded a bus at the gate.
  • The bus, labeled American Airlines, was confirmed by the driver to be the transportation to South Bend, not a plane.
  • The host questioned the airline's practice of booking bus transportation as part of a flight itinerary.
