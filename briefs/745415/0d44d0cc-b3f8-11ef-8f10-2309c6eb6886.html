Postgame Show: Let's Monetize As We Realize (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Prime Video announced a Black Friday game day featuring NFL, NBA, and golf events.
  • The episode discussed local Miami sports reporters and broader sports media figures.
  • Juju offered his perspective on Shador Sanders, noting fan divisions.
  • A new poll format for audience engagement was introduced to the podcast.
  • A 'Top 5 powerful black men in sports' list was presented and debated.
  • Another 'Top 5' list detailed essential items for Thanksgiving celebrations.

Deep Dive

  • Prime Video is set to feature an NFL game between the Chicago Bears and Philadelphia Eagles at 3 p.m. ET.
  • Two NBA Cup games are scheduled: Milwaukee Bucks vs. New York Knicks at 7 p.m. ET and Dallas Mavericks vs. Los Angeles Lakers at 10 p.m. ET.
  • The Capital One's The Match golf event, offering $4 million, will commence at 9 a.m. ET.

  • A poll was introduced asking if one wishes for their enemies to thrive, with a guest stating this approach eliminates pettiness.
  • Discussion covered group chat etiquette and the importance of direct communication for public figures.
  • A speaker suggested inviting individuals onto shows like 'South Beat sessions' for direct conversations instead of secondhand discussions.
  • A debate ensued regarding public versus private arguments, with one speaker preferring private resolution first.

  • The host posed a question about the most powerful black men in sports, drawing comparisons to Shadur Sanders.
  • Kendrick Perkins was mentioned and criticized for his opinion regarding Sanders' power.
  • A Top 5 list included Norman Powell, LeBron James, Steph Curry (noted for a new shoe deal), Michael Jordan, and Dwayne Wade.

  • The speaker thanked Zaslow for information about Steph Curry not playing, which led to a profitable bet on Brandon P. points.
  • Zaslow was acknowledged for breaking stories, including Kiffin to LSU and mentions of Jeff Fish.
  • The conversation also touched on efforts to help Barry Jackson recover his hacked account, noting his new truck and potential house purchase.

  • JuJu presented his Top 5 list, starting with stuffing.
  • Collard greens were listed at number four, and mac and cheese at number three.
  • Fried turkey was number two, with number one being a TV with football games, specifically Bengals-Ravens and Chiefs-Cowboys matchups.
