The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: Pops Witherspoon (feat. JuJu Gotti & Bryant McKinnie)

Key Takeaways

  • Tony selected Dan Le Batard's outfit for a live stream, raising host concern.
  • Analysis covered the University of Miami's offensive line and running back Fletcher's performance.
  • Bryant McKinney offered a hypothetical scenario regarding defensive tackle Ruben Bain.
  • Suggestions were given to Dan Le Batard for improving his standing in the Miami community.
  • Cleveland Cavaliers' Donovan Mitchell was discussed as a potential NBA MVP candidate.

Deep Dive

  • Host Dan Le Batard expressed concern after allowing Tony to select his outfit for an upcoming Monday live stream.
  • Tony presented the selected attire: Giovanni shoes (size 18), Daitlos pants, a shirt, and a hat.
  • The host referenced a past instance where a similar outfit choice led to embarrassment.

  • Brian, an expert on running backs, analyzed the University of Miami's offensive line, noting its size and toughness.
  • Running back Fletcher's performance was highlighted, emphasizing his patience and second effort.
  • Discussion included Markel Bell, a six-foot-nine junior college transfer, and techniques like knee bend and hand positioning for large players.

  • A hypothetical scenario was presented regarding defensive tackle Ruben Bain's prime years.
  • Brian McKinney questioned if Bain, playing defensive end against him, would have allowed any sacks.
  • The unlikelihood was suggested due to Bain's limited attempts at defensive end.

  • Advice was sought on improving standing with the Miami community, particularly regarding perceived lack of support for local teams.
  • Three suggestions were offered: attending a game and being visibly present.
  • Other recommendations included donating turkeys in a local neighborhood and creating 'Dan Le Batard croquetas' for global sales.

  • An update on the Cleveland Cavaliers noted their current record and a recent loss.
  • Donovan Mitchell was highlighted as a potential MVP candidate, with recent performance statistics provided.
  • A previous "MVP of the day" selection, Stephon Castle, from December 11th, was also mentioned.
