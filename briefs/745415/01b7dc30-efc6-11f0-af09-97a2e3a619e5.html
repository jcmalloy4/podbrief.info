The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: F*** Billy Corben (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • A debate centered on collaborating with producer Billy Corbin for University of Miami sports content.
  • Mike Ryan expressed skepticism regarding Billy Corbin's motivations and past criticisms of the UM team.
  • The role of media in supporting sports teams was discussed, contrasting journalistic detachment with fan loyalty.
  • Justin Herbert's performance and blame for a loss were debated, with comparisons to various pop culture figures.
  • NCAA recruiting rules were criticized as 'goofy' due to their scheduling overlap with championship games.

Deep Dive

  • The host questioned Mike Ryan's dismissal of Billy Corbin, a producer of University of Miami content and documentaries.
  • Mike Ryan expressed skepticism about Corbin's emotional investment in UM, contrasting it with deeply invested fans.
  • Ryan clarified his issue was Corbin's past criticism, not skepticism, viewing it as counterproductive to shared team support.
  • Juju acknowledged Corbin's work on 'The U' documentaries and willingness for more content, while Ryan maintained reservations linked to team performance and superstitions.

  • The host criticized Mike Ryan's superstitions about jinxing teams, comparing it to his handling of the Panthers, suggesting an unhealthy emotional investment.
  • The conversation initiated a discussion on supporting the University of Miami, contrasting journalistic neutrality with fan investment.
  • A participant stated their emotional investment in Miami's team differs from a journalist's role, asserting they are a journalist, not a fan or an ally.
  • One participant apologized, agreeing with Mike Ryan, and observed 'hater' behavior among some Miami Hurricanes fans on social media who do not actively support the team.

  • A social media poll was proposed on the Le Batard Show regarding whether to respect 'hating ass' content.
  • A participant asserted that regardless of content origin or creator's fandom status, good content will always be taken.

  • The 'Stat of the Day' sponsored by Miller Light, highlighted 49ers wide receiver Jawan Jennings has 2 career touchdown passes, equaling Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert's total.
  • The discussion analyzed Justin Herbert's play, comparing him to various pop culture figures and memes, including Martin Lawrence, a Saturday Night Live skit, and a hockey fight.
  • Josh Allen's performance, overcoming multiple injuries including to his head, elbow, and foot to lead the Buffalo Bills to a win, was contrasted with Herbert's situation.

  • Juju raised concerns about NCAA recruiting rules, specifically highlighting the overlap between the championship game and the start of recruiting.
  • The existing NCAA system was described as 'goofy as hell' due to its scheduling.
  • Wins by Indiana and Miami were mentioned during the discussion.
