The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: START SPREADIN’ THE NEWS, B******

Key Takeaways

  • The New York Yankees experienced a significant postseason loss, extending their World Series drought since 2009.
  • Hosts expressed strong enjoyment over the New York Yankees' recent defeat.
  • Disparity in NFL fines for GMs and owners, notably Jerry Jones' middle finger incident, was debated.
  • Rolling Stone's "250 Best Songs" list sparked host criticism regarding song placements.
  • Odell Beckham Jr.'s performance with the Miami Dolphins was contrasted with his Los Angeles Rams tenure.

Deep Dive

  • The New York Yankees have not won a World Series since 2009.
  • Teams including the Cubs, Red Sox, Nationals, and Rangers have won titles more recently.
  • Brian Cashman's job security was questioned in parallel to manager troubles with the Florida Dolphins.

  • Yankees' postseason performance highlighted individual struggles from players like Trent Grisham, Volpe, Ben Rice, Jazz, and Cody Bellinger, who combined for a low hit rate.
  • Their offensive approach, relying on home runs, was contrasted with the Toronto Blue Jays' contact strategy, exemplified by player Clement.
  • Manager Aaron Boone's future was questioned following repeated postseason failures, presented as a key reason for the team's struggles.

  • The discussion covered the Phillies vs. Dodgers game, with a spoiler that the Phillies forced a game five.
  • The host humorously noted this spoiler and contrasted it with Stugotz's time zone advantage.
  • The Yankees' reliance on Aaron Judge and the short right-field porch were mentioned in relation to their recent loss.

  • Discussion covered Tarik Skubal and Ariel Hawani, noting Hawani's presence at Canadian events.
  • Skubal's role in the Mariners' success was highlighted, drawing parallels to historical Seattle teams.
  • His performance was juxtaposed with expectations for Aaron Judge and the Yankees.

  • The host's fandom transitioned from being a Yankees fan to enjoying their losses.
  • This shift was attributed to the perceived negativity of New York sports culture.
  • A personal anecdote shared the host's father's intense reaction to a Miami Heat playoff game, including removing his slacks.

  • The hosts discussed Rolling Stone's "250 Best Songs" list.
  • One host expressed frustration about receiving the list without context.
  • Opinions were shared on the placement of the song 'Runaway'.

  • Odell Beckham Jr.'s poor performance with the Miami Dolphins (9 catches, 55 yards in 9 games) was highlighted.
  • This was contrasted with his impactful Super Bowl appearance for the Los Angeles Rams.
  • His Rams tenure included a career-altering ACL injury.

  • A $250,000 fine was levied against Jerry Jones for an inadvertent middle finger display.
  • This was compared to Jonathan Gannon's $100,000 fine for physical contact with an employee.
  • The discussion questioned the disparity and rationale behind fines for team personnel and owners, including Bud Adams and David Tepper.
