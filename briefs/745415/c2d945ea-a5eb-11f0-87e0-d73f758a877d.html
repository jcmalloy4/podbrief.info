The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Alley Oop: A’ja Wilson DOMINATES Game 3 | WNBA Finals Recap | Thomas & Sabally Go Cold

Key Takeaways

  • A'ja Wilson's dominant Game 3 performance, including a game-winning shot, was crucial for the Las Vegas Aces.
  • The Phoenix Mercury faced significant offensive struggles from key players like Alyssa Thomas and Satou Sabally.
  • The Las Vegas Aces are poised for a dynasty, drawing comparisons to the 2022 Golden State Warriors.
  • A Lakers fan has filed a lawsuit against LeBron James over a perceived misleading advertisement for a game.

Deep Dive

  • The Las Vegas Aces secured a 3-0 lead over the Phoenix Mercury in the WNBA Finals due to A'ja Wilson's Game 3 performance.
  • Wilson's game-winning shot was analyzed, noting her jersey number 22 corresponded to the 2.2 seconds left in the game.
  • The shot demonstrated Wilson's dominance over defenders Alyssa Thomas and DeWanna Bonner.

  • Hosts discussed the challenge of stopping A'ja Wilson, noting her ability to hit three-pointers.
  • Wilson's all-around game was highlighted, emphasizing her footwork, ball skills, and athleticism, placing her on a higher level.
  • Her dominance was compared to NBA player Bam Adebayo, citing her height and vertical leap as advantages for unreachable shots.

  • Alyssa Thomas's offensive game and shot selection, including dribbling and missed opportunities, faced criticism during the Finals.
  • Hosts questioned if Thomas was playing through an injury, suggesting she should consider sitting out if not fully healthy.
  • Her physicality was compared to defensive strategies against Giannis Antetokounmpo, noting her inability to shoot allows teams to foul her.

  • Satou Sabally sustained a concussion, raising concerns she might miss the next game.
  • Sabally's Game 2 shot selection, specifically deep, contested three-pointers over driving, was criticized as a factor in the blowout loss.
  • The Mercury's overall Game 3 struggles included a lack of offensive contribution from players like Sammy Smith, Laura Makani, and Kalani Brown.
  • Hosts questioned the team's bench utilization, especially as the Las Vegas Aces approach a dynasty.

  • Breaking NBA news reported a lawsuit filed against LeBron James by a Lakers fan.
  • The fan claimed to be misled by an advertisement regarding a game against the Cavaliers, believing it was James's retirement game.
  • The fan is reportedly seeking compensation for the perceived deception.
  • Hosts reacted by calling the fan the 'dummy of the day' for buying tickets based on the advertisement.

  • The hosts concluded the current episode by looking ahead to upcoming content.
  • The next episode is slated to focus on the NBA.
  • Anticipated topics include the end of the Phoenix Mercury's season and Giannis Antetokounmpo's potential departure from Milwaukee.
