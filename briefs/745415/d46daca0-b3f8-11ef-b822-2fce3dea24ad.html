The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: The Middle Seat of Middle Seats (feat. Senator Mark Kelly)

Key Takeaways

  • Airplane etiquette, including intentional bathroom floor urination, was a debated topic.
  • Dolphins owner Stephen Ross expressed frustration over his private jet's parked car distance.
  • George Clooney's reaction to a 7-minute Louvre heist sparked host speculation about his involvement.
  • Senator Mark Kelly detailed government shutdown impacts on healthcare, federal employees, and food stamps.
  • Senator Kelly discussed his astronaut experience and the critical importance of the U.S. space program.
  • Hurricane Melissa caused catastrophic damage in Jamaica, severely impacting impoverished populations.
  • The MLS playoff schedule and potential World Cup venue changes were discussed.

Deep Dive

  • Hurricane Melissa demonstrated unprecedented strength and catastrophic effects on Jamaica.
  • The storm disproportionately impacted impoverished populations, with significant danger from flooding.
  • Calls for aid support were made, mentioning organizations like American Friends of Jamaica and Global Empowerment Mission.

  • Comedian Mark Schlerith expressed irritation with airplane passengers wearing flip-flops or revealing clothing.
  • One host admitted to intentionally urinating on an airplane bathroom floor to deter others from walking in socks.
  • The discussion compared this act to removing shoes or eating pungent food, with one host citing exotic fried fish on a flight from China as the "worst airplane crime."

  • Hosts compared themselves to "Moneyball" and "Ocean's Eleven" characters in a hypothetical heist.
  • Discussion centered on George Clooney's comments regarding a recent Louvre heist and potential black market implications.
  • The planning and execution of such a crime were speculated upon, drawing parallels to heist movies.

  • George Clooney expressed amusement and disbelief at the audacity of the Louvre heist, noting it occurred in broad daylight and lasted seven minutes.
  • Hosts questioned Clooney's apparent pride in the heist, suggesting it was suspicious and reminiscent of his "Ocean's" movie characters.
  • Speculation arose about Clooney potentially acting as a fence for the stolen goods or being the lone remaining thief.
  • The discussion questioned the impact of stealing crown jewels and Clooney's sincerity about the crime being "terrible."

  • Discussion covered the long break between MLS playoff games.
  • Donald Trump threatened to revoke World Cup host city status, specifically for the final at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey, not New York.
  • FIFA president Gianni Infantino acknowledged that venue changes for the World Cup are a possibility.
  • The hosts humorously associated FIFA presidents Sepp Blatter and Gianni Infantino with criminal activity based purely on their names.

  • Senator Mark Kelly expressed concern over the Arizona Cardinals' two-and-five record and a recent "impossible loss" to the Tennessee Titans.
  • Kelly outlined concerns about the government shutdown's impact, including increased healthcare costs and issues with healthcare.gov.
  • He emphasized the need for a shutdown resolution, criticizing the President's absence and the impact on federal employees and the military.

  • Senator Kelly stated 40 million Americans could lose access to food stamps, citing Donald Trump's unwillingness to use a contingency fund.
  • Kelly called for the President to negotiate with Congress to fix the "easily fixable" shutdown, highlighting broad impacts.
  • He described dire consequences, including citizens choosing between rent, premiums, and groceries, attributing issues to tax cuts for the wealthy funded by Medicaid and ACA cuts.

  • Senator Kelly expressed concern about democracy's erosion, Justice Department politicization, and societal division weakening the U.S. against global adversaries.
  • He described the exhilarating experience of launching into space on four missions to the space station, totaling 56 days in orbit.
  • Kelly highlighted the space program's importance for national interests and scientific advancement, stressing the U.S. must lead lunar exploration over China.
