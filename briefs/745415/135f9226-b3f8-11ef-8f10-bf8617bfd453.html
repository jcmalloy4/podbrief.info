The Hockey Show: The Oilers Make The Move

Key Takeaways

  • The Edmonton Oilers acquired goaltender Tristan Jarry from the Pittsburgh Penguins, sparking debate on if it is an upgrade.
  • The Vegas Golden Knights faced criticism for their handling of Carter Hart's media availability amidst sexual assault allegations.
  • Proposed NHL rule changes included redefining shots on goal, adjusting penalty enforcement, and modernizing warm-up routines.
  • The Florida Panthers are navigating a challenging schedule, which could impact their playoff positioning and wild card aspirations.

Deep Dive

  • The Edmonton Oilers acquired goaltender Tristan Jarry from the Pittsburgh Penguins in a trade.
  • Pittsburgh received Stuart Skinner, Brett Kulak, and a 2029 second-round pick in the deal.
  • Jarry's save percentage above expected was 9.8, compared to Skinner's 6.9, sparking debate on if it is an upgrade.
  • Some hosts questioned the move as potentially lateral for the Oilers given the cost.

  • The Vegas Golden Knights faced criticism for not making goaltender Carter Hart available to the media in Philadelphia.
  • Extra security was present around the team's bench during the game due to anticipated fan reactions.
  • The panel condemned the team's handling, comparing it to the Florida Panthers' response to Pride jersey situations.
  • Concerns were raised about the league's general leniency towards players with problematic pasts.

  • David's "Puck Luck" betting picks included Tyler Bertuzzi (over 1.5 shots) and Brandon Hagel (over 2.5 shots) with combined parlay odds of +504.
  • Nathan MacKinnon was involved in 44 five-on-five goals, surpassing the entire Seattle Kraken team's 42 goals.
  • The Toronto Maple Leafs had a shutout win against Tampa Bay, with Auston Matthews pursuing a scoring record.
  • The NHL is actively encouraging color-on-color jersey matchups in games for visual appeal.

  • Dave's win was the visual appeal of color-on-color NHL jersey matchups, which the league encourages.
  • His fail cited defensive lapses that cost teams points in the final second of two games.
  • Ethan's win celebrated an "unreal assist" from Alex Ovechkin in a Mighty Mites game.
  • Rose attributed her fail to the Edmonton Oilers' losses, specifically due to a last-second goal.

  • Guest Jordan McPherson presented scenarios for improving the NHL, sparking debate.
  • A debate centered on whether shots hitting the goal post should count as shots on goal for statistics and player props.
  • New analytics such as "shots off posts" were proposed to refine existing metrics.
  • Discussions included why an embellishment call doesn't nullify the original penalty.

  • A proposal suggested players on the ice during a penalty commitment should take the subsequent penalty kill shift.
  • The discussion covered modernizing pre-game warm-ups to allow more flexibility for specific skill practice.
  • Innovations in warm-ups and practice drills from coaches like Paul Maurice were referenced for success.

  • Fan behavior, specifically performing "the wave" during close games, was criticized as premature celebration.
  • A "jailbreak rule" was proposed, where a shorthanded goal would end the penalty and grant a power play.
  • Discussion included having a penalized player remain on the ice for the opening penalty kill shift, a PWHL rule.
  • A more radical suggestion involved installing mirrors in the penalty box for fan antagonism.

  • Guest Jordan McPherson discussed the Florida Panthers' three-game home winning streak, broken by a loss to a league-leading team.
  • The loss to the Colorado Avalanche involved aggressive play and Panthers' defensive breakdowns on a back-to-back road game at altitude.
  • The Panthers face a difficult upcoming schedule with multiple back-to-back games against top teams.
  • Currently trailing in standings, the team is fighting for a wild card spot, making upcoming games crucial.
