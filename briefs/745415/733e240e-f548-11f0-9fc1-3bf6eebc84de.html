The Big Suey: Rose's Cigarettes

Key Takeaways

  • Josh Allen's playoff performance faced scrutiny after a divisional round loss.
  • Buffalo Bills players, including Josh Allen, expressed significant emotional reactions post-game.
  • NFL officiating and specific rule interpretations, such as pass interference, were debated.
  • A co-worker's chain-smoking incident and Miami's unique local features were discussed.

Deep Dive

  • Josh Allen's turnovers were cited as a key factor in the Buffalo Bills' recent playoff loss.
  • Emotional responses were observed from Bills players including Josh Allen, Dion Dawkins, and Stefon Diggs.
  • Quarterback Josh Allen's 'superhero' performance is considered essential for coach Sean McDermott's effectiveness, who holds a 98-50 record.

  • Josh Allen's 0 for 9 performance on deep passes in the recent playoff loss contributed to his team's exit.
  • His overall postseason record stands at 8-7, with the recent loss marking his first with multiple interceptions.
