The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: I Need Your Support

Key Takeaways

  • Host Greg Cody faced criticism for perceived collusion in the PFPI Fantasy League, involving his father's influence.
  • The Miami Hurricanes' recent loss to Louisville sparked concerns over player discipline and coaching strategies.
  • A potential conflict emerged for the Hurricanes' playoff hopes, involving a scheduled La Liga soccer match at their home stadium.
  • Stadium game day operations for the Miami Hurricanes, including PA system and music, drew significant criticism.

Deep Dive

  • Greg Cody expressed frustration over comments made about his father on a prior show, feeling Dan Le Batard unfairly criticized him.
  • Le Batard was accused of calling Cody's father a 'fool' regarding a story about Billy Gill.
  • Cody claimed Le Batard violated a rule of criticizing actions, not actors.

  • The Dan Le Batard Bakery announced a Saturday 1 p.m. deadline for PFPI Fantasy League picks.
  • Failure to submit picks by the deadline would result in consequences, including texts and potential calls.
  • A participant recounted a recent family Oktoberfest event unrelated to the fantasy league.

  • The hosts briefly discussed a past instance where Greg Cody was accused of faking interest in a solar eclipse.
  • The conversation touched on a potential trip to Augusta and reminiscing about visiting a planetarium in their youth.

  • Accusations of collusion arose in the fantasy league, involving Greg's father allegedly pressuring another owner with a personal relationship.
  • Greg, acting as league commissioner, discussed potential punishments for late picks with his wife, who is also a league owner.
  • This situation was compared to NFL commissioners conferring with owners like Roger Goodell.

  • The Miami Hurricanes suffered a 24-21 loss to Louisville, described as an 'ass-kicking' despite the close score.
  • Concerns were raised about quarterback Carson Beck's multiple interceptions and the team's perceived lack of discipline.
  • Louisville coach Jeff Brohm was credited for out-coaching Miami's Mario Cristobal by effectively disrupting Miami's offensive plays.

  • The hosts discussed a news story about a woman reportedly swallowed whole by a shark.
  • Reports indicated her friends did not notice her disappearance while swimming.

  • The Miami Hurricanes' potential path to the ACC championship and College Football Playoff is discussed, noting their destiny is no longer in their own hands.
  • A conflict arises as Stephen Ross, owner of Hard Rock Stadium, is bringing a La Liga soccer match to the venue.
  • This potentially displaces the Hurricanes if they host a CFP game, leading to contingency plans for them to play in Orlando.

  • Criticism targeted the Hard Rock Stadium's Public Address (PA) system and music choices, including playing 'conga' before a critical third down.
  • Questionable song selections like 'Sweet Caroline' and Black Eyed Peas were highlighted as detracting from the atmosphere.
  • A fake field goal attempt was criticized for being anticipated by the opponent and poorly executed, leading to a first down for the opponent.
