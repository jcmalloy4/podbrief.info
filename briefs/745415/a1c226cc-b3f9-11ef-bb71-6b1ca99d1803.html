The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 1: Judge Pope Zaslow (feat. Jessica Smetana)

Key Takeaways

  • Jessica Smetana's CFP predictions for Notre Dame and Miami proved accurate.
  • Lane Kiffin denies an ultimatum from Ole Miss amid conflicting media reports.
  • The College Football Playoff committee's ranking criteria faces scrutiny for inconsistencies.
  • Debate surrounds the University of Miami's offense and historical team comparisons.
  • A "Zaslow's courtroom" segment ruled on a disputed question's intended recipient.

Deep Dive

  • Jessica Smetana's College Football Playoff predictions for Notre Dame and Miami have unfolded as she anticipated.
  • The segment followed a "useless sound montage" as noted by the hosts.
  • Discussions after a challenging game reflected on missed offensive opportunities and the importance of execution for a win, despite scoring 32 points.

  • Lane Kiffin has denied receiving an ultimatum from Ole Miss, despite reports from The Athletic and On3 suggesting otherwise.
  • Kim Mulkey's comments about Kiffin's presence in Gainesville were discussed amidst the debate over his denial versus media reports.
  • The conversation used a relationship analogy, suggesting an ultimatum implies a breakup has already occurred, noting Kiffin has not yet signed an extension.

  • The College Football Playoff committee's criteria were questioned, specifically why Notre Dame is in a different tier than Miami despite a head-to-head loss.
  • Frustration was expressed over subjective human judgment in rankings compared to computer-generated ones, with perceived cheating by Miami, including simulated snap counts, also noted.
  • Conflicting logic was cited regarding Oregon's win over Penn State and Louisville's impact on Virginia's ranking, raising questions about committee credibility.

  • The committee's selective use of advanced analytics was critiqued, noting Miami's strength of schedule metrics were not fully considered while Notre Dame's SAD ratings placed them ahead.
  • The concept of a 'quality loss' was debated, particularly concerning Notre Dame's narrow defeat to Texas A&M.
  • Participants questioned the rankings of teams like Arizona State, Tennessee, and Missouri, suggesting the committee might bolster the resumes of playoff contenders.

  • Discussion touched on Lane Kiffin's relationship status and coaching position at North Carolina, with Jessica Smetana providing commentary.
  • A segment covered political reporter Olivia Luzy, her alleged affair with RFK Jr., and past relationships with other political figures and Keith Olbermann.
  • The conversation included a description of a Dolphins logo in relation to Ms. Luzy's journalistic career.

  • Debate centered on whether the current University of Miami offense ranks as the second-best in program history.
  • Comparisons were drawn to Miami offenses from 1986 and the early 2000s.
  • The discussion considered the changing nature of college football and its impact on statistical analysis.

  • NFL coaching salaries were discussed, mentioning Chip Kelly's $6 million salary as the highest-paid offensive coordinator.
  • Skepticism was raised regarding Tom Brady's offensive contributions given the team's poor performance, questioning his headset role.
  • The conversation debated whether Brady is actively practicing broadcasting or providing valuable input, noting his slow improvement in timing.

  • A dispute regarding whether a question was posed to Zaslow or Amin led to a mock courtroom scenario, with Zaslow ruling it was directed at him.
  • Eyewitness accounts were sought to determine the intended recipient, with suggestions the question might have initially been for Zaslow but pivoted to Amin.
  • The segment touched on LeBron James's recent injury and its impact on the Lakers, contrasting his absence with team performance when Luka Doncic dominates the ball.
