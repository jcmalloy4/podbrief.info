The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: I'm Not Letting That Guy Beat Me (feat. Amin Elhassan and Jonathan Zaslow)

Key Takeaways

  • A 'Battle Court' league leader's decision to forfeit a match for player protection sparked internal team disagreement.
  • The hosts engaged in a frank discussion regarding the function and use of a butt plug, questioning audience experience.
  • NFL announcer commentary, including that of Rob Gronkowski and Tom Brady, was critiqued for its perceived lack of analytical insight.
  • Zaslow reported from Paris, sharing travel anecdotes and mixing in observations on NFL games, including the Panthers' 3-0 start.
  • Amin Alhassen delivered 'Weekend Observations,' highlighting Geno Smith's football performance and incorporating humorous remarks.
  • Discussions focused on Brian Branch's potential suspension for admitting to bullying and Haynes King's standout quarterback performance.
  • Justin Fields' recent performance was heavily criticized, leading to a comedic 'Top 5 Things Colder Than Justin Fields' list.
  • James Franklin's coaching tenure at Penn State faced scrutiny following a series of critical losses impacting playoff chances.

Deep Dive

  • The Cyclones team, despite numerous injuries, held first place in the 'Battle Court' league.
  • A leader made an unpopular decision to forfeit a relatively meaningless match to protect injured players, including one with a torn bicep.
  • The forfeit decision led to a disagreement with a player named Unda and touched on potential conflicts of interest regarding betting on games.

  • The conversation veered into a confusing and explicit topic about the function and use of a butt plug.
  • Participants debated its purpose, with one individual claiming ignorance and another attempting to clarify its non-toilet-related function.
  • The discussion concluded with a direct question posed to the audience about who had worn one, followed by Amin's discussion of 'The Naked Gun' movie.

  • The segment included a Detroit Tigers announcer's on-air frustration about their season ending and discussed the Baltimore Ravens' failed 'tush push' attempts.
  • NFL announcers like Rob Gronkowski were described as having a 'court jester' style, while Tom Brady was perceived to lack insightful commentary.
  • The hosts questioned the value of high salaries for announcers whose analytical contributions were seen as limited.

  • Zaslow joined the conversation from Paris, describing visits to the Louvre and the Eiffel Tower, and plans for a river cruise on the Seine.
  • His travel choices were humorously questioned by others, especially as it coincided with Indigenous Peoples Day in America.
  • Zaslow mentioned being unable to watch the Panthers due to time differences but noted their 3-0 record, and discussed the Dolphins game, highlighting three interceptions.

  • Amin Alhassen began his 'Weekend Observations' segment, including a crude joke about needing a 'butt plug' after setup for a 'pickle game'.
  • He focused on Geno Smith's football performance, noting 174 yards and one touchdown in a win against a perceived weaker team.
  • The discussion also touched on the performance of Drake the quarterback versus Drake the musician, and a college football game where 'Kool-Aid' lost to 'Booty'.

  • The conversation revisited Brian Branch's situation, emphasizing the negative repercussions of admitting to being bullied within Dan Campbell's coaching philosophy, with a potential multiple-game suspension mentioned.
  • Haynes King's performance was highlighted, with 204 passing yards, one touchdown, 60 rushing yards, and two touchdowns, suggesting he might be a Heisman contender.
  • A humorous reference was made to Nick Wright's alleged gambling budget of $5,000 per week, estimated to exceed a quarter-million dollars annually.

  • A discussion about a lawsuit against LeBron James concerning 'The Decision' preceded football commentary.
  • A historical parallel was drawn between the 1778 Cherry Valley Massacre and a recent 'boring game' between the Broncos and Jets, highlighting Justin Fields' poor performance.
  • A 'Top 5 Things Colder Than Justin Fields' list was presented, including the North Pole (despite global warming), the dairy section of Costco, and airplane cabin temperatures.

  • The discussion critiqued James Franklin's job security at Penn State despite recent success, questioning the timing of a potential firing.
  • His tenure was noted for a series of losses, including to UCLA and Northwestern, which were highlighted as key factors impacting playoff chances.
  • Despite a reputation for not winning 'the big game,' Franklin's ability to consistently reach playoffs was seen as a saving grace until recent performance declined.
