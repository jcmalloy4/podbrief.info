Hour 1: Jeremy Teaches 3rd Grade Math

Key Takeaways

  • Fantasy football scoring differences, particularly PPR versus half-PPR, significantly impact player rankings and valuations.
  • Roy Bellamy faced a scheduling conflict between a hockey championship game and a media skate requiring specific training.
  • The potential presence of actor George Clooney was utilized as a primary incentive to convince wives to attend various events.
  • John Cena's anticipated final WWE match against Gunther was a major topic, including discussions on its placement and wrestling traditions.
  • The Dan Le Batard Show's team, the Cyclones, competed against Kay Marie's Devils in the Battle Corps Championship.

Deep Dive

  • Peepo was named the 2025 "Nuttiest Fan" for his college football season performance, which included six catches for 117 yards and three touchdowns in a half-PPR game as a tight end.
  • A discussion confirmed that half-PPR and full-PPR scoring formats significantly affect player rankings due to the value assigned to receptions.

  • Mike introduced David Dwork, a colleague from "The Hockey Show with Roy Bellamy," leading to a discussion about the "Battle Corps Championship" and a "media skate" event.
  • Roy Bellamy stated he would not attend the Devils versus Cyclones championship game, citing training for a casual skate as his reason, which was questioned by hosts as a potentially poor excuse.
  • Hosts urged Roy to reconsider his decision to attend the skate over the championship game, suggesting he change his plans to support the event.

  • The conversation shifted to convincing wives to attend an event by highlighting features such as a spinning photo platform and the possibility of George Clooney and Greg Cody attending a match at the Magic City Fronton at 7 p.m.
  • The discussion emphasized George Clooney's presence as a key factor in Roy's wife's potential reaction to attending an event.
  • It was revealed that one host's wife was "tricked" into attending an event by the mention of George Clooney, leading to a humorous exchange about maintaining the deception and event logistics.

  • Discussion began about the 2026 World Cup returning to North America, noting it will feature 48 teams and utilize large stadiums.
  • The Game Time app was introduced as a solution for obtaining difficult-to-get tickets, emphasizing its real-time price drop tracking, alerts, and a $20 off promo code DAN.
  • The high cost of tickets for major sporting events, including upcoming World Cup matches in Miami, was cited as unaffordable for typical fans, contrasting with lower prices at events like a Royal Rumble in Saudi Arabia.

  • The host announced that WWE's Saturday Night's Main Event would feature John Cena's final match in Washington, D.C., a detail he expressed excitement about.
  • John Cena's upcoming match was identified as Saturday Night's Main Event against Gunther, noting its unusual placement as an opening match.
  • Hosts discussed the tradition of retiring wrestlers losing their final match, questioning if John Cena would break this tradition against Gunther, and speculated on a possible 'jumping' after the match if he loses.

  • The conversation delved into the history of Saturday Night's Main Event, recalling significant moments such as Hulk Hogan turning heel and the Mega Powers' exploding drama, alongside Jack Tunney's role as president and his safety-focused decisions.
  • The wrestling match card for an upcoming event was noted to include Cody Rhodes in the main event and a champion vs. champion matchup.
  • Hosts debated John Cena's potential match placement, considering whether he should go on first for a longer match or last, and the implications for audience energy and time constraints on streaming platforms like Peacock.

  • Hosts discussed the tradition of retiring wrestlers losing their final match, speculating if John Cena would break this against Gunther or if he might be 'jumped' after the match to protect his opponent.
  • The tradition of wrestlers placing their boots in the center of the ring to signify retirement was discussed, contrasting it with John Cena's likely use of sneakers for his potential final match.
  • Anticipation for the emotional impact of Cena's potential final match was expressed, with one host predicting tears and another expressing skepticism.

  • The "Battle Corps Championship" featured the Cyclones, owned by The Dan Le Batard Show, against the Devils, owned by Kay Marie from Y100.
  • The DJ for the games was described as energetic, utilizing a mobile deck and playing custom music, including the Undertaker theme for player 'Undo'.
  • The narrative recounted how the group's energy at previous games positively influenced Ray Lewis, transforming him from a distant observer to an engaged participant, despite initial minimal crowds.

  • Jeremy was introduced to present fantasy football statistics, specifically focusing on tight end rankings for half-PPR leagues from 37th to 41st place.
  • The discussion centered on the impact of receptions in PPR and half-PPR formats, using players like Algi Krumpler, Kellen Winslow Sr., and George Kittle as examples to demonstrate how additional points for catches alter player rankings.
