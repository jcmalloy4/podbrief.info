The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Zaslow Makes The List

Key Takeaways

  • A major debate centered on identifying Patrick Dempsey, not Michael Rappaport, as 'Patient Zero' in the 1995 movie 'Outbreak'.
  • NFL discussions covered Philip Rivers' potential return, quarterback comparisons, and recent team performances.
  • The 2026 FIFA World Cup expansion and the inaugural NBA Emirates Cup received promotional coverage.
  • Updates were provided on the 'Nuttiest Fan' contest finalists and new player additions to Team USA and the Dominican Republic.

Deep Dive

  • Kyle Schwarber signed a five-year, $150 million contract with the Philadelphia Phillies.
  • Schwarber was also named to Team USA, joining Bryce Harper.

  • The 1995 film 'Outbreak' was discussed, with participants acknowledging its renewed relevance during the pandemic.
  • Actors Michael Rappaport and Rene Russo were mentioned in the context of the movie.
  • One participant noted their father showed the film to children.

  • A debate arose over whether Michael Rappaport was 'Patient Zero' in the 1995 film 'Outbreak'.
  • After checking Google and IMDb, it was confirmed that Patrick Dempsey played the role of 'Patient Zero'.
  • The speaker who incorrectly identified Rappaport apologized for the misinformation regarding Dempsey's role.

  • The upcoming World Cup in North America will expand to include 48 teams, marking a significant increase in scale.
  • Difficulty in obtaining tickets for the expanded tournament was noted.
  • The GameTime app was promoted as a resource for real-time price tracking and ticket alerts.

  • The discussion explored Philip Rivers' potential motivation for playing a few games with the Indianapolis Colts.
  • One perspective suggested Rivers might be restarting his five-year eligibility clock for the Hall of Fame.
  • An alternative view proposed Rivers sought to enhance his resume by helping the Colts reach the playoffs.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles' recent losing streak and the Dallas Cowboys' division standing were analyzed.
  • The Chargers secured a win despite Justin Herbert's poor performance, which included low yardage, multiple sacks, and five turnovers.
  • The selection process for post-game interviews was briefly explained, noting PR directors provide lists for interviewers.

  • Gunner Henderson was added to Team USA, while Sandy Alcantara joined the Dominican Republic team.
  • Listeners inquired about 'Landman,' prompting a host to express disinterest in the individual's actions.
  • Jessica Smetana's regular Wednesday appearances were mentioned, with anticipation for a segment where hosts 'drink her tears'.
