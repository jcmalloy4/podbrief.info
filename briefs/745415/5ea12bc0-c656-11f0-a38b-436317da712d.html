GOOD FOLLOW - Lexie Brown Talks CBA Negotiations, Seattle’s New Head Coach, Athletes Unlimited & More!

Key Takeaways

  • Lexie Brown secured her Seattle Storm contract, providing crucial health insurance amid WNBA free agency and CBA renegotiations.
  • Brown is deeply involved in current WNBA CBA negotiations, advocating for increased player leverage and a fairer share of revenue.
  • Players seek greater transparency from WNBA owners regarding investments and facilities, moving beyond a "survival mode" mentality.
  • Lexie Brown clarified her health and inconsistent playing time with the Seattle Storm, seeking a reintroduction to fans.
  • Brown serves on the Athletes Unlimited player executive committee, shaping league operations and involving her father, Dee Brown.

Deep Dive

  • Lexie Brown reflected on her nine-season WNBA career, including overcoming challenges like Crohn's disease and roster changes.
  • She secured her contract with the Seattle Storm, providing essential health insurance crucial after past health setbacks.
  • This security positions her uniquely amidst ongoing free agency uncertainties for other players.

  • Lexie Brown expressed excitement about the Seattle Storm's new head coach, Sonia Raman.
  • Brown praised Coach Raman's intellect and historical significance as the first WNBA head coach of Indian descent.
  • She looks forward to building a strong relationship with Raman, who has already shown interest in Brown's podcast.

  • Lexie Brown is deeply invested in the current Collective Bargaining Agreement negotiations, participating in calls and seeking updates.
  • She acknowledges negotiations are "spicy" but remains optimistic due to the league's growth and increased player leverage.
  • Players are shifting from gratitude to recognizing themselves as partners, leveraging alternative platforms like Athletes Unlimited and NIL.

  • Brown stated that WNBA CBA negotiations became personal, with statements from Commissioner Cathy Engelbert and Sue Bird.
  • She highlighted the WNBA's complex ownership structure, with 42% owned by NBA owners and 16% by an independent group.
  • Brown criticized owners for insufficient player investment, citing lack of adequate facilities for some teams, and called for greater transparency on finances.

  • Lexie Brown desires a reintroduction to fans, clarifying that her 'podcast girl' perception stemmed from injury-hampered seasons.
  • She states her most recent season in Seattle was her first fully healthy year, yet she was not given consistent playing time despite being told her three-point shooting was needed.
  • Brown used the media to clarify her healthy status, noting she was not playing due to a lack of minutes, not illness.

  • Lexie Brown serves on the Athletes Unlimited (AU) player executive committee, where players handle all operational aspects, including roster selection, uniform, and rule changes.
  • The committee collectively decided on Nashville as the season's location.
  • Brown's father, Dee Brown, will join the AU staff for skill development, offering valuable front-office experience and father-daughter bonding.
