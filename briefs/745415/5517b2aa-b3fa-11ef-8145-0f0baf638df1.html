The Big Suey: Nick Wright's Highly Rate and Very Successful Television Show

Key Takeaways

  • Podcast hosts and guests debated Patrick Mahomes' performance, statistics, and 'dink and dunk' playstyle.
  • A prominent sports commentator clarified his quarterback tier list that ranked Lamar Jackson ahead of Patrick Mahomes.
  • The episode analyzed Mark Cuban's podcast appearance and the freedom of public figures to discuss broad topics.
  • Discussions covered Travis Kelce's early-season struggles and Daniel Jones' potential as an MVP candidate.
  • The conversation touched upon differing approaches in sports journalism and the debate over attributing wins to quarterbacks versus coaches.

Deep Dive

  • A discussion ensued regarding past comments comparing Tom Brady and Rex Grossman, contrasting with Patrick Mahomes' performance.
  • The host defended statements about Mahomes being a 'dink and dunk' quarterback, citing statistics like yards per attempt against Baker Mayfield's play.
  • It was argued that Mahomes has performed exceptionally well after Tyreek Hill's departure, securing MVP awards and maintaining high offensive rankings.
  • Mahomes' early-season touchdown to interception ratio from 2018-2022 was noted to have declined in the last two seasons, suggesting a shift to a 'dink and dunk' offense.

  • The conversation shifted to the Chiefs' defensive performance, with one speaker asserting a transition from an elite offensive to defensive unit.
  • Another speaker disputed the claim that the defense carried the team last year, specifically pointing to Mahomes' clutch performances.
  • The host expressed frustration over a recurring four-year cycle of doubt surrounding the Chiefs and Mahomes, highlighting instances where Mahomes consistently proved skeptics wrong after losses or trades.

  • One speaker expressed disappointment that Lamar Jackson was placed ahead of Patrick Mahomes on a recent quarterback tier list.
  • The speaker clarified that his tier list was presented on a television show, not his podcast, and defended his rankings as honest and unbiased.
  • The discussion touched on Mahomes' 30th birthday and explored whether Lamar Jackson could be the player to help the Chiefs overcome struggles, noting Jackson's consistent regular-season performance.

  • The discussion centered on Mark Cuban's appearance on a podcast where he answered questions from former NBA players Channing Frye and Kendrick Perkins.
  • Cuban's commentary included topics potentially outside his direct knowledge, prompting debate among the participants.
  • Participants debated the nature of podcast conversations and Mark Cuban's freedom to discuss various subjects without being subjected to a formal inquiry.

  • Analysis focused on Travis Kelce's performance and impact on the Kansas City Chiefs, questioning if he is receiving undue criticism for early-season struggles.
  • Specific instances mentioned included a dropped pass that could have resulted in an interception and a miscommunication with Patrick Mahomes.
  • The debate acknowledged Kelce's statistical achievements at his age, drawing comparisons to career trajectories of players like Rob Gronkowski and Tony Gonzalez.

  • The host shared a text exchange with Nick Wright concerning their divergent approaches to sports journalism.
  • One approach emphasized reporting on specific figures like Kawhi Leonard.
  • The other involved generating 'fart clicks' through quick takes, highlighting a perceived difference in journalistic priorities.

  • A listener submitted a take comparing Daniel Jones, nicknamed 'Indiana Jones,' to the AK-47, and discussed coaching changes at Indiana.
  • The nickname 'Indiana Jones' for Daniel Jones was re-evaluated by the hosts.
  • The possibility of Daniel Jones becoming an MVP candidate and Brian Daboll being fired was raised, connecting player performance to coaching stability.

  • A debate ensued regarding whether Brian Daboll achieved more with Daniel Jones than Mike McDaniel did with Tua Tagovailoa, citing Daboll's playoff win with the Giants as evidence.
  • The conversation analyzed if wins are primarily a quarterback or coach statistic, particularly in the playoffs, contrasting the Dolphins' situation with Daboll's success.
  • Quarterback performance metrics were discussed, with Joe Burrow noted for his low yards per attempt, and Patrick Mahomes highlighted for his deep-arm talent and ability to make plays.
