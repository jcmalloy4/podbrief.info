The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: I Happen to Have Heard (feat. Jessica Smetana)

Key Takeaways

  • Amin mistakenly texted Jessica's old number for months, thinking she was ghosting him.
  • Debate surrounds LeBron James's injury credibility, with speculation of strategic faking for leverage.
  • Notre Dame's football playoff hopes hinge on winning out, amidst coaching retention speculation.
  • College bowl games face financial strain, prompting considerations like hosting games in Saudi Arabia.
  • Texas Tech's tortilla-throwing tradition is under threat of penalties from the Big 12 conference.

Deep Dive

  • Jessica accused Zaslow of lying, while Zaslow, in turn, accused LeBron James of lying.
  • Sean Penn's story from 'Cops' training involved mistaking his own reflection for a gun during a drug bust.
  • This error led to an overreaction from law enforcement officers present during the incident.

  • A public dispute between NFL figures Russell Wilson and Sean Payton was discussed, with participants generally siding with Wilson.
  • Payton's public criticism of Wilson was largely viewed as classless by the hosts.
  • Unverified gossip suggests Wilson had a luxury box for a game he was benched for, which he couldn't resell.

  • Notre Dame's playoff prospects rely on winning their remaining schedule; a single loss would end their hopes.
  • The team's performance against ranked opponents and the impact of earlier losses to Marshall and NIU were noted.
  • Discussion included concerns about coach Marcus Freeman viewing the Notre Dame job as a stepping stone.
  • Speculation arose about Freeman's agents using NFL interest to secure a raise if the team loses more games.

  • The Holiday Bowl is exploring a potential game in Saudi Arabia due to financial strain from COVID-related cancellations.
  • Financial struggles for many bowl games are exacerbated by the expanded 12-team playoff format.
  • The new playoff system is diminishing the historical prestige of bowl games, leading to unconventional financial considerations.

  • Texas Tech's tradition of throwing tortillas is under scrutiny, with the Big 12 conference threatening penalties.
  • The athletic director has asked fans to cease the popular tradition to avoid sanctions.
  • The conversation also touched on the absurdity of stadium food rules and methods to discard tortillas discreetly.

  • Amin confessed to Jessica that he had been texting her old phone number for months.
  • He was unaware it was her, mistakenly believing she had been ignoring him or ghosting him during this period.

  • A debate centered on LeBron James's injury credibility, with questions raised about whether he is genuinely injured or faking it.
  • One host suggested LeBron might be faking a mid-November sciatica re-evaluation as a tactic to prove a point to the team.
  • This speculation includes the idea of LeBron seeking leverage with the front office, potentially to secure a multi-year contract.

  • Discussion included a hypothetical scenario of LeBron James influencing Anthony Davis's departure to acquire Luka Dončić.
  • Jimmy Butler's recent performance was highlighted, including making all 16 free throws in a game.
  • The Houston Rockets' strategy of tanking was noted, leading to a roster of many young, tall players described as 6'11".
