The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: Don't Shoot The Messenger (feat. Tim Kurkjian)

Key Takeaways

  • Tim Kurkjian analyzed Hall of Fame votes and major offseason MLB transactions.
  • Greg Cote's Baseball Hall of Fame voting decisions, particularly on Andruw Jones, sparked debate.
  • The "Looks Like" segment featured humorous public figure comparisons, including Dave Dameshek.
  • Baker Mayfield's criticism of Browns coach Kevin Stefanski's past handling was revisited.
  • Listeners shared embarrassing personal stories of eating specific foods while driving.

Deep Dive

  • Tim Kurkjian joined the show to discuss the Hall of Fame vote, with Greg Cote expressing disappointment in the ballot.
  • Greg Cote voted for only three players: Carlos Beltran, Andruw Jones, and Chase Utley.
  • Tim Kurkjian noted it was not a strong year for first-time candidates, though he was surprised Cole Hamels received 25% of the vote.

  • 'Looks Like' submissions included comparing Dave Dameshek to a billboard-posing realtor, a comparison Tim Kurkjian was unfamiliar with.
  • Jonathan Zaslow received comparisons to a stubbed big toe and an an off-duty mime.
  • Other comparisons featured Kurt Signetti as a dad who never says 'I love you' and an accountant in a crippling divorce.
  • NBA referee Ed Malloy and Adam Silver were also subjects of humorous 'Looks Like' comparisons.

  • The Kyle Tucker trade to the Dodgers was discussed, alongside potential for a work stoppage due to payroll disparities.
  • Dave Dombrowski called Bo Naylor's move to the Mets a 'gut punch,' with Naylor anticipated to impact third base.
  • Tim Kurkjian ranked Dylan Cease's $210 million trade to the Blue Jays as the third biggest offseason transaction.
  • The Dodgers' acquisition of closer Edwin Diaz for $69 million was identified as the second biggest offseason transaction.

  • Baker Mayfield publicly criticized Cleveland Browns coach Kevin Stefanski, stating he felt abandoned and treated like 'garbage.'
  • The Browns' decision to trade Mayfield despite his playoff win and a bum shoulder, then sign Deshaun Watson, was questioned.
  • Debate arose over Stefanski's coaching record, including two Coach of the Year awards, versus his tenure's overall effectiveness.
  • Upcoming matchups between Mayfield and Stefanski were discussed, with interest in the narrative mirroring professional wrestling drama.
