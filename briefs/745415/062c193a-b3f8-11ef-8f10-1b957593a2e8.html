The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: We're Here! Don't Go There! (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Journalist Jane Levy's book "Make Me Commissioner" prompted discussion on baseball strategy and player performances.
  • A segment explored Bill Parcells' coaching methods and locker room humor, including comparisons to modern football.
  • NFL betting selections were made, including the Giants (+2.5) and a confident prediction for the Miami Hurricanes.
  • Guest Juju Gotti offered WNBA analysis on the Indiana Fever and discussed perceived 'overcorrection' in Lamar Jackson criticism.
  • Debates occurred regarding controversial sports statues, celebrity appearances, and the authority of name pronunciation.

Deep Dive

  • The discussion centered on journalist Jane Levy's book, "Make Me Commissioner."
  • Conversations with journalist Brian Ross covered baseball strategy and specific player performances.
  • The segment began at 0:47 following advertisements, including for Liberty Mutual and Sierra workout gear.

  • The conversation touched on Bill Parcells' unique coaching methods and the "Ultimate Parody League."
  • A humorous comparison was made regarding a coach potentially placing thousands of mousetraps in a locker room, referencing Parcells and Dave Wanstead.
  • The discussion also covered complementary football across three phases and the ease of scoring in modern games.

  • The host selected the Giants plus 2.5 points against the Saints, citing a feeling from their previous performance against the Chargers.
  • Greg Cody predicted the Miami Hurricanes would defeat Florida State by at least a touchdown, attributing this confidence to the Hurricanes' improved pass defense.
  • Cody defended his perceived bias toward local teams, noting a previous pick where he chose the Dolphins to lose to the Bills.

  • Guest Juju Gotti expressed admiration for the Indiana Fever's resilience against the Aces, commending coach Stephanie White's leadership despite the team being short-handed.
  • The discussion addressed an "overcorrection" in criticism toward Lamar Jackson, emphasizing his perceived need to secure victories in significant games.
  • Criticism was directed at the Buffalo Bills' defense, with a mention of a past trade involving player Kyer Elam to the Cowboys.

  • Greg Cody's controversial stance on the proliferation of statues in sports was highlighted, alongside initial reactions to a newly unveiled Tina Turner statue described as "rough."
  • The hosts debated Tony Romo's appearance, specifically his hair, face, and teeth, questioning whether these features were natural or enhanced, with one jokingly calling it a 'hat-trick' of enhancements.
  • A segment also addressed the pronunciation of 'Castellanos,' using the analogy of Matt Diaz to discuss who holds the authority to dictate a name's pronunciation.
