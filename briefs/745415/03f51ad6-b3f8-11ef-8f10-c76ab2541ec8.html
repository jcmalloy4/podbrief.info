The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: The VIN Number (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Kirby Smart was identified as the 'Joker of the Week' for appearing to plead with a referee after a timeout.
  • The Las Vegas Aces secured a championship, demonstrating resilience and a 17-game winning streak.
  • Guest Juju provided five television show recommendations, including 'Gen V' and 'Slow Horses'.
  • Audience polls covered diverse topics, from Lad McConkey's perceived fear factor to Baker Mayfield's cultural fit.

Deep Dive

  • Host Juju identified Kirby Smart as the day's 'Joker of the Week' during the segment.
  • Audio clips played showcased Smart appearing to plead with a referee.
  • The incident occurred after Smart had called a timeout during a game, presented as a moment of poor judgment.

  • The Las Vegas Aces won the championship, featuring player A'ja Wilson and coach Becky Hammon.
  • WNBA Commissioner Kathy Engelbert was booed, suggesting potential challenges for future CBA negotiations.
  • The team achieved a mid-season turnaround and a 17-game winning streak, attributed to Jewel Loyd coming off the bench.
  • A Miller Light watch party on October 30th was announced, with Christy from Beota, Texas, winning a prediction pool.

  • Juju provided five television show recommendations, starting with 'Gen V' on Prime Video and 'Slow Horses' on Apple TV+.
  • Additional recommended shows included 'The Lowdown' on Hulu, 'Task' on Max, and 'Peacemaker' also on Max.
  • The hosts reacted to the variety of streaming platforms and the premises of the suggested shows.

  • Poll results showed 71% of the audience does not fear a person named Lad McConkey, and 90% believe he sounds like a Key West mayor.
  • 93% of the audience believed Baker Mayfield would fit in at Gasparilla.
  • Additional polls indicated 89% of the audience had not encountered a 'Black Ethan' and 92% had never worn a butt plug.
