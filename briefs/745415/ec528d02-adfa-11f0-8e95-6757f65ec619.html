The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Patrick Mahomes is Unstoppable, Dave's Mom Has a Mike Ditka Story, Film Nerd Showdown with Marc Sessler, Points Per Game with Geoff Schwartz

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 7 reactions focused on the Kansas City Chiefs' dominance and 49ers' critical players.
  • Guest Marc Sessler contributed to discussions on Quentin Tarantino films and the episode's 'Jerk List'.
  • A unique childhood anecdote about NFL legend Mike Ditka was shared by a guest's mother.
  • Jeff Schwartz provided insights into college football rules and analyzed various NFL team performances.
  • The AFC landscape was debated, including the New England Patriots' future and the Las Vegas Raiders' struggles.

Deep Dive

  • A guest, 'Mama Mo', shared a childhood story about growing up on the same block in Aliquippa, PA, as Mike Ditka.
  • She recounted being restricted from playing with Ditka due to his past pranks.
  • The segment was introduced at 3:11 into the episode.

  • The hosts debated a previous character draft where Colleen Wolf selected Rick Dalton over Cliff Booth from 'Once Upon a Time in Hollywood'.
  • The host argued Rick Dalton would offer more adventure as a weekend companion.
  • Guest Marc Sessler's pick of Mr. White was clarified as not the ear-cutting character, referencing 'Reservoir Dogs'.

  • Mike Fuentes suggested that the Kansas City Chiefs' consistent dominance makes their Super Bowl appearance inevitable, advocating to skip the regular season.
  • Guest Marc Sessler concurred that the Chiefs appear to be the best team in the AFC.
  • The discussion playfully framed the Chiefs' dominance as potentially 'ruining a marriage'.

  • The host criticized the practice of on-field jersey swaps after NFL games.
  • This practice was unfavorably compared to retired wrestling antics.
  • It was suggested that such displays should occur privately to enhance the professional presentation of football.

  • The media's perception of the Indianapolis Colts was questioned due to their surprising success and favorable betting lines.
  • Coach Shane Steichen was praised, with a prediction of a potential 12-5 season followed by a decline.
  • The Colts' success was described as a possible mirage, attributed to the running back, quarterback performance, and defensive coordinator Lou Anarumo.

  • A hypothetical scenario was presented: choosing to watch only Quentin Tarantino's or Paul Thomas Anderson's movies for life, including future productions.
  • The discussion weighed the rewatchability of Paul Thomas Anderson's films like 'There Will Be Blood' and 'Boogie Nights' against Tarantino's extensive library.
  • Practicalities were considered, such as the technology available on a deserted island to watch films, like VCRs or laser disc players.

  • Guest Jeff Schwartz questioned how the New York Jets consistently perform poorly in a salary-capped league.
  • Issues were attributed to ownership, coaching, player acquisition, and a perceived lack of buy-in from the team.
  • A comparison highlighted the winning atmosphere of the New York Giants' facility versus the perceived losing culture of the Jets.

  • The conversation questioned how far Drake Maye could lead the New England Patriots, given that the AFC is not perceived as exceptionally strong.
  • The Kansas City Chiefs were identified as the best team in the conference.
  • The host expressed regret for not predicting the Patriots' improvement, attributing it to their collective youth and Drake Maye's development.
