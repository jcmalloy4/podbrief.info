Postgame Show: Top 10 Things That Will Happen This NBA Season (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • The NBA season kickoff included discussion of player movement and team histories.
  • Predictions for the upcoming NBA season covered team standings and player-specific events.
  • NBA playoff forecasts included specific team matchups and anticipated player performances.
  • The segment also provided sports betting insights across multiple leagues.

Deep Dive

  • The NBA season commenced with two games broadcast on NBC and Peacock.
  • Juju expressed excitement for the league's return, mentioning the "DLS Hoops" show with Trista.
  • Initial discussion included speculation about Kevin Durant's potential move to the Rockets and the Oklahoma City Thunder's past player trade practices, such as James Harden's departure.

  • Juju presented top 10 predictions for the upcoming NBA season, beginning with the Golden State Warriors securing the four seed, attributed to Al Horford.
  • Another prediction involved Ja Morant potentially introducing a new celebratory item, alongside a debate on shoe design.
  • The eighth prediction suggested the Miami Heat would trade either Bam Adebayo or Tyler Herro, discussing the implications of the extension deadline passing.

  • NBA playoff predictions included the Dallas Mavericks potentially defeating the Los Angeles Lakers.
  • Discussion also covered a possible confrontation between Russell Westbrook and LeBron James.
  • The segment touched on the Washington Wizards' future prospects and Victor Wembanyama's anticipated unique performances in the league.

  • The "Against the Spread" segment, presented by DraftKings, featured bets on the Toronto Maple Leafs covering a 1.5 spread against the Devils.
  • Another bet was placed on the Oklahoma City Thunder minus 7.5 against the Houston Rockets, with discussion on the Rockets' offensive struggles and injuries.
  • Mike made a separate bet on USF minus 3.5 against Memphis, expressing low confidence in the Memphis team.
