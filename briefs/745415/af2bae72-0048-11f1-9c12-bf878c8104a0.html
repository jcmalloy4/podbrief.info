The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: Happy Black History Month (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Show members revisited past "odd takes" and debated celebrity status.
  • David Sampson's comments on Chevy Chase prompted show discussion.
  • Guest Juju presented his "Top 5 Bad Movies of 2025" list.
  • The episode featured listener polls on sports and hypothetical scenarios.

Deep Dive

  • Guest Juju reviewed previous "odd takes" from show members, initiating a segment for retractions.
  • A host defended an earlier statement about watching Mike Vrabel perform a drastic act.
  • The conversation included a debate on whether Kai Cenat qualifies as a 'high-end celebrity' based on his primary platform, Twitch.

  • The hosts prepared to discuss David Sampson's recent comments supporting actor Chevy Chase.
  • This segment highlighted accusations that Chase used racial slurs, contrasting with Sampson's positive portrayal.
  • The discussion transitioned to praise for 'The Traitors,' featuring Ron Funches, with one host calling it potentially the best show on television.

  • Guest Juju presented his "Top 5 Ass Movies of 2025" list to the hosts.
  • The list began with 'War of the Worlds' starring Ice Cube, citing its 4% rating on Rotten Tomatoes.
  • Other films criticized included 'Nova Cain,' 'The Running Man,' and 'Bugonia,' which was described as weird despite starring well-known actors.
  • The number one 'ass movie' of 2025 was identified as a Marlon Wayans quarterback horror film, criticized for lacking originality.

  • The hosts conducted multiple listener polls, comparing D-Wade and Giannis for best basketball player.
  • Polls also assessed fan sentiment regarding the Patriots and Tom Brady, and discussed hypothetical physical feats.
  • Additional polls covered topics such as the best way to kick off Super Bowl week and the perceived likelihood of boxers wearing toupees.
