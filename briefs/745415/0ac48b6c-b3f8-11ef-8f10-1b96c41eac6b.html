The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: The Fibbing Ejection (feat. Julian G.)

Key Takeaways

  • Juju Gotti presented a "Top 5 Free Him" list, highlighting NFL players in challenging team situations.
  • A proposal for NFL pass interference rules suggests immediate player ejection for clear holding on review.
  • Luka Doncic's performance in a Lakers win garnered attention in the NBA MVP candidate discussion.
  • Audience polls covered topics ranging from punctuality and NFL player performance to hypothetical scenarios.

Deep Dive

  • Hosts suggested the Cleveland Browns release Myles Garrett, arguing for an act of mercy due to his talent and the team's performance.
  • A poll was proposed to gauge audience opinion on whether the Browns should cut Garrett.
  • Juju Gotti's 'Top 5 Free Him' list included Drake London (Atlanta Falcons), Garrett Wilson (New York Jets), Chris Olave (New Orleans Saints), Justin Jefferson (Minnesota Vikings), and Myles Garrett (Cleveland Browns).
  • teaser": "Juju Gotti shares his 'Top 5 Free Him' list for NFL players stuck on underperforming teams."

  • A proposal suggested that if slow-motion review clearly shows holding on a pass interference call, the player should be immediately ejected.
  • This proposal was compared to an NBA challenge system where a false claim results in losing a timeout.
  • A poll was posed to the audience asking if the NFL should eject players for lying about pass interference calls.

  • Luka Doncic's performance was highlighted, specifically his 41 points in a win against the Lakers.
  • Discussion focused on Doncic's effectiveness, even without LeBron James on the court, and questions about team integration.
  • Nikola Jokic was also mentioned in the MVP discussion, with one participant reporting him as the 2025-26 NBA MVP.

  • An audience poll showed 54% of respondents believe being on time is not difficult.
  • 98% of the audience agreed that Jameis Winston's performance is consistently 'trick-or-treat.'
  • Another poll found 85% of listeners believe being spit on is not worse than other negative interactions.
  • 84% of the audience agreed that losing to a specific individual named 'Let's' is particularly painful.
