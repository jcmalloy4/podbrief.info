The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: The JuJu Gotti Experience (feat. Haley Jones, JuJu Gotti, and Trysta Krick)

Key Takeaways

  • Tom Brady's neutrality regarding the Patriots generates debate among hosts and fans.
  • Lindsey Vonn's Olympic aspiration with a ruptured ACL sparks discussion about her competitive readiness.
  • Juju shares his unique stance on rooting for the Patriots and details his "Thursday Thunder" betting picks.
  • A segment highlights Juju's selection of the top five halftime shows deserving more recognition.
  • The episode examines diversity in NFL offensive coordinator roles and features listener poll results.
  • WNBA player Haley Jones discusses her professional basketball career and new ventures, including pickleball team ownership.

Deep Dive

  • Hosts questioned why Patriot fans were reportedly upset with Tom Brady despite his long tenure and success.
  • Debate centered on Brady's 'Last Dance' documentary and his perceived neutrality regarding the Patriots' success, possibly not wanting them to win quickly.
  • Comparison was made to Dwayne Wade's vocal support for the Miami Heat, contrasting Brady's public statements.
  • A later discussion recounted how Patriots fans defended Brady throughout his career and continued to root for him when he moved to Tampa Bay.

  • Discussion focused on skier Lindsey Vonn's determination to compete in the Olympics with a ruptured ACL.
  • Some expressed doubt about her ability to compete at a high level given the severe injury.
  • A past controversial take comparing wrestler Anthony Robles's success to an 'advantage' was revisited, paralleling Vonn's situation.

  • Buffalo Bills supporter Juju explained his unusual stance of rooting for the Patriots in the Super Bowl, citing a changed perspective on sports fandom.
  • His 'Thursday Thunder' betting picks included Kenneth Walker III for a touchdown and Seattle's defense.
  • Juju also picked Trevor 'Trevy R' Henderson for over 0.5 receptions and Jake Bobo for over 0.5 reception yards.

  • Juju presented his 'Top 5 Halftime Shows That Deserve More Fanfare,' starting with a chihuahua performing a 360-degree spin.
  • The list included a strong individual on roller skates holding a person, and a woman performing trick archery in a handstand.
  • Other acts featured were the 'red panda' mascot and a mascot performing a full backward, over-the-head, half-court shot, described as a 'Hall of Fame act.'

  • The conversation addressed the perceived lack of diversity in NFL offensive coordinator positions, with a critical view of the Rooney Rule's effectiveness.
  • It was noted that Michael Fleur was hired as an offensive coordinator, with discussion on whether this was an internal promotion.
  • Audience poll results were shared on topics including ESPN's treatment of Wendy, Pat Riley's legacy, and the pronunciation of Chris Stapleton's last name.

  • Juju Gotti and Trista Crick introduced Haley Jones to 'Good Follow,' discussing her basketball career and recent debut in the WNBA's 'Unrivaled' league in Philadelphia.
  • Jones compared the intimate atmospheres of the Philadelphia and Miami 'Unrivaled' arenas to her experience at Stanford, noting differences in crowd size.
  • She shared insights on her resilience, including being drafted by Atlanta, her subsequent release, and earning a starting role with the Dallas team.

  • Haley Jones discussed her surprise engagement party planned by her fiancé, emphasizing his supportive role and their three-year foundation as best friends.
  • She revealed her new role as a part-owner of a Major League Pickleball team, expressing excitement about the opportunity and her growing passion for the sport.
  • Jones identified her 'basketball ick' as people wearing extremely high socks, tucking knee pads into socks, flopping, and complaining to referees.
