The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: What Is Cleveland Doing? (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • The Cleveland Cavaliers are reportedly in advanced trade talks for James Harden while also exploring options for Giannis Antetokounmpo or Anthony Davis.
  • Guest JuJu Gotti presented a Top 5 list of highly skilled individuals, sparking debate on Mina Kimes's NFL analysis.
  • Tyrese Maxie's standout performance in the 76ers' victory over the Clippers generated discussion regarding his MVP potential.
  • Audience polls revealed preferences for 'white chocolate' over 'the glove' and found the Summer Olympics significantly more popular than the Winter Olympics.

Deep Dive

  • The Cleveland Cavaliers are reportedly in advanced trade discussions with the Los Angeles Clippers for James Harden.
  • Cleveland's team performance this season and the potential fit of Harden with Donovan Mitchell are being questioned due to similar high usage rates.
  • The team is also reportedly considering 'blowing things up' to pursue players like Giannis Antetokounmpo or Anthony Davis.
  • Guest Juju Gotti expressed surprise at the Harden rumors, calling them 'smoke and mirrors' and suggesting Dennis Schroder as a potential acquisition despite Evan Mobley's injury.

  • JuJu Gotti presented his top five individuals as talented at their jobs as NFL linebacker Fred Warner.
  • The list included Ariel Helwani at number five and Mina Kimes at number four, leading to debate about Kimes's standing as an NFL analyst.
  • Brian McKinney was discussed for his potential Hall of Fame career and compared to Fred Warner.
  • Alicia "Bomb" Gardner and Timmy (Tom Kirchen) were also mentioned as part of the compiled list.

  • The Philadelphia 76ers secured a victory over the Los Angeles Clippers in an NBA matchup.
  • Tyrese Maxie was highlighted for his performance during the game.
  • Hosts debated Maxie's MVP candidacy and exceptional speed on the court.

  • Polls covered topics including championship desirability and the impact of game count on MVP status.
  • Audience members favored the nickname 'white chocolate' over 'the glove' in a head-to-head comparison.
  • Another poll compared the stardom of Bad Bunny against LeBron James and Lionel Messi, with audiences choosing LeBron and Messi respectively.
  • The final poll indicated that 74% of the audience believes the Winter Olympics are not more popular than the Summer Olympics.
