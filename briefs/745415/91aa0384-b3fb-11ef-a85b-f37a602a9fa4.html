The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: That Jezebel Amber Wilson

Key Takeaways

  • Reporter Pablo's actions concerning the Clippers raise questions about journalistic ethics versus self-promotion.
  • The Panthers face a significant challenge due to Alexander Barkov's severe knee injury impacting playoff prospects.
  • The Miami Marlins' team payroll is nearly matched by Juan Soto's projected 2025 salary, underscoring salary disparities.
  • Fan behavior at the Ryder Cup drew sharp criticism for exceeding acceptable sportsmanship and golf etiquette.
  • Pop culture debate ensued regarding Bad Bunny versus Taylor Swift as the current biggest global artist.

Deep Dive

  • Discussions centered on reporter Pablo and the Los Angeles Clippers' PR staff being contacted late at night.
  • The situation was described as bumbling, with questions raised about the effectiveness of an NBA investigation, especially if timed around the All-Star game and a new arena.
  • One host suggested the reporter is intentionally slow-playing information and driven by a need for attention, impacting NBA and Clippers responses.

  • The Panthers received 'worst news' with Alexander Barkov suffering ACL and MCL tears, potentially missing the regular season and impacting playoff chances.
  • The injury's severity was compared to significant past local sports injuries, including Dan Marino's in 1992-93 and Dwyane Wade's in 2005.
  • Despite being two-time defending champions, the loss of Barkov, considered the best two-way player capable of neutralizing Connor McDavid, makes making the playoffs in a strong division uncertain.

  • A speaker was accused of being a 'fraud' regarding their college football picks.
  • It was alleged that the speaker privately predicted Georgia would win to a fan, despite publicly picking Alabama.
  • The speaker humorously confirmed the story, attributing the leak to 'Amber Wilson' and reaffirming their correct public pick of Alabama.

  • Juan Soto's projected 2025 salary is $61 million, while the entire Marlins payroll is $67 million.
  • After accounting for Sandy Alcantara's $17 million salary, the remaining Marlins team payroll is approximately $50 million.
  • Only three players on the Marlins team earn $1 million or more, including Braxton Garrett and Anthony Bender.

  • The Ravens' defense is noted for significant struggles, with a substantial increase in missed games by defensive starters compared to the previous year.
  • Despite Lamar Jackson's streak of not losing by more than one score since 2021, the team's defense is described as being in disarray, considered worse than the Dolphins'.
  • Speculation arose regarding Lamar Jackson's benching or injury, with a report from the 'Zazzle Mansion family' claiming he quit.

  • The Ryder Cup discussion highlighted perceived obnoxious behavior from U.S. fans, described as crossing lines and causing reactions from European players like Shane Lowry and Rory McIlroy.
  • Incidents included the use of squeaky rubber ducks to taunt European players and perceived trolling of Donald Trump on social media.
  • Audio from the Ryder Cup featured Rory McIlroy's reaction to the crowd and his satisfaction in performing well amidst the taunting.

  • Hosts debated if the fan conduct observed at the Ryder Cup was unacceptable for golf, emphasizing that the sport should uphold higher standards of etiquette and respect.
  • The behavior was contrasted with expectations for the 2027 Ryder Cup in Ireland, noting that aggressive chanting, even directed at specific players, crosses a line.
  • Specific mention was made of the crowd singing chants directed at Donald Trump, with speculation about his likely negative reaction to the songs.

  • The upcoming Jets vs. Dolphins game was discussed, noting that the last three Jets coaches started their seasons 0-3 or worse.
  • Criticism was directed at Tyreek Hill for reportedly skipping practice to attend a birthday party.
  • The conversation touched on player performance, referencing a 'Metallica pace' and disappointment regarding Chris Godwin's post-injury state.
