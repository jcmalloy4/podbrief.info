The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: From 3 p.m. (feat. Chris Wittyngham)

Key Takeaways

  • Witty paid tribute to Ray Hudson's broadcasting career and ranked his top four calls.
  • Concerns were raised about Tua Tagovailoa's post-injury mobility and the Dolphins' upcoming game.
  • Witty maintained his skeptical 'bad take' on Mario Cristobal's game-day coaching ability.
  • Chris Whittingham expressed a profound fear of losing his voice, preferring to lose an arm instead.

Deep Dive

  • The discussion opened with a historical account of tequila's invention by Cuervo in 1795.
  • The brand's long-standing family legacy, land connection, and passion were highlighted.
  • This historical note set an initial context before the primary sports and media discussions.

  • The episode featured a series of clips showcasing commentator Ray Hudson's distinctive calls.
  • Witty reflected on Hudson's impending retirement, sharing personal experiences and admiration for his dedication to broadcasting.
  • Hudson's retirement from broadcasting was officially announced, marking the end of a notable career.

  • Chris Whittingham presented his top four Ray Hudson calls, offering vivid descriptions for each.
  • The segment was part of a larger discussion to celebrate Hudson's commentary style.
  • The conversation also briefly touched on Lionel Messi's potential contract extension with Inter Miami.

  • Concerns were raised about Tua Tagovailoa's perceived inability to escape pressure and improvise off-script, unlike other NFL quarterbacks.
  • The physical impact of Tagovailoa's injuries, particularly a hip injury, was cited as a reason for his altered athleticism and mobility.
  • It was suggested that Tagovailoa's college performance differed significantly from his current play due to these physical changes.

  • Witty maintained his critical stance on Mario Cristobal's game-day coaching ability despite acknowledging Cristobal's success in recruiting and securing resources.
  • He cited unexpected losses last season despite a talented team as grounds for his skepticism.
  • Witty stated a personal preference for a coach who is a strong tactician over an elite recruiter for college football.

  • The discussion explored the most crucial skill for a college coach, considering factors like quarterback play, schematic advantage, or recruiting.
  • Mario Cristobal's ability to address issues through coordinator changes and recruiting was noted.
  • The importance of strong offensive lines and success in the trenches, exemplified by Cristobal's history at Oregon and Miami, was highlighted.

  • Chris Whittingham expressed significant concern about losing his voice due to a demanding schedule of five games in six days, including Champions League and Inter Miami matches.
  • He described the difficulty of broadcasting while sick and the anxiety associated with potential illness.
  • Whittingham emphatically stated he would prefer to have his arm chopped off than get a common cold, believing a prosthetic arm would still allow him to broadcast.

  • A 'gearhead update' announced the reduction of the NASCAR playoffs to the final 12 drivers.
  • Shane van Gisbergen was confirmed to be out of the competition.
  • The series' return to New Hampshire Motor Speedway was also noted.
