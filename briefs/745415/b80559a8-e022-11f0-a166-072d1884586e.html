Alley Oop 147: DENNIS SCHRÖDER HITS THE GAME-WINNING BUZZER BEATER Knicks vs Celtics, Brunson Drops 47 & Trae Young Exposed

Key Takeaways

  • Jalen Brunson's 47-point performance for the Knicks fueled debate on their Eastern Conference contention.
  • Dennis Schröder drilled a game-winning buzzer-beater for the Sacramento Kings in overtime.
  • Trae Young's impact and late-game decision-making are under scrutiny for the Atlanta Hawks' success.
  • The Boston Celtics are considered favorites for the Eastern Conference due to their strong team play.
  • The Clippers face significant challenges with Ivica Zubac's 6-week injury and a 7-28 record.
  • Team roster moves and player acquisitions, including John Collins and Bradley Beal, faced critical evaluation.

Deep Dive

  • Jalen Brunson scored 47 points for the New York Knicks against the Miami Heat.
  • In the same game, Karl-Anthony Towns recorded only 2 points.
  • The hosts emphasized the significance of Brunson's individual scoring versus team success.

  • Hosts critically assessed Trae Young's overall impact, suggesting the Hawks might be better without him at times.
  • Questions were raised about his ability to lead winning basketball in critical moments.
  • Specific criticism targeted his late-game decision-making, where he attempted to draw a foul instead of taking a shot.
  • Young contributed 35 points in the Hawks' 152-150 loss to the Bulls.

  • Dennis Schröder made a game-winning shot for the Sacramento Kings.
  • Russell Westbrook forced overtime by hitting a three-pointer in the same game.
  • Schröder's clutch play was highlighted, referencing his EuroBasket MVP status.

  • Dylan Brooks' aggressive actions against Steph Curry of the Golden State Warriors were discussed.
  • His play was compared to Draymond Green's 'villain' persona on the court.

  • Ivica Zubac is reported to be out for approximately six weeks due to injury.
  • The Los Angeles Clippers currently have only 7 wins through 28 games into the season.
  • The team has notably struggled in road games, exacerbating their challenges.

  • The discussion critiqued recent roster changes, specifically questioning the additions of players like Brooke Lopez and John Collins.
  • John Collins' career production and trade rumors were reviewed, raising doubts about teams acquiring him.
  • Bradley Beal's perceived entitlement was criticized, with comparisons made to Chris Paul's career achievements.
