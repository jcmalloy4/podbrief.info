The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Micah Parsons Returns to Dallas, Lamar and Harbaugh Battle The Chiefs, a List Off with JuJu Gotti, and the Five Most Interesting Games of The Week with Matt Verderame

Key Takeaways

  • Week 4 NFL matchups and betting strategies were extensively analyzed, including specific player projections.
  • A debate on the greatest NFL players to wear number 11 and a list of the worst NFL jerseys of all time were featured.
  • Divisional quality was questioned, specifically regarding the consistent underperformance of the AFC South and NFC South.
  • Super Bowl 60 predictions were updated, shifting from Bills vs. 49ers to Bills vs. Packers.

Deep Dive

  • The host discussed the Baltimore Ravens' past coaching decisions, referencing the 2018 Tavin Bryan draft pick.
  • The team's subsequent success with quarterback Lamar Jackson under coach John Harbaugh was noted.
  • Current struggles were contrasted with past success, suggesting Harbaugh might face similar job security issues as Doug Marrone.

  • The host and guests debated the greatest players to wear number 11, including Danny White, Drew Bledsoe, Tony Eason, Alex Smith, and Larry Fitzgerald.
  • Discussion centered on Julian Edelman's Hall of Fame case, alongside other notable number 11s like Steve Grogan and Dante Culpepper.
  • Kicker Sebastian Janikowski was also mentioned in the debate among these prominent players.

  • The 'Pick Six' segment, presented by DraftKings, aimed to build a collective lineup for a million-dollar prize in Week 4.
  • Selections included Quentin Johnson for over half a touchdown and Jordan Mason for over 78.5 rushing yards.
  • Patrick Mahomes was selected for over 25.5 rushing yards, and Jalen Warren for over 23.5 receiving yards.
  • Additional picks were Josh Jacobs for over half a touchdown against the Cowboys and Caleb Williams for over 1.5 touchdowns against the Raiders.

  • The discussion ranked states based on their current football fan experience, considering both NFL and college teams.
  • Pennsylvania was proposed as a top state due to the Eagles and Steelers' performance, despite the latter's recent loss.
  • California (USC, Rams, Chargers, 49ers), New York (Bills, Jets), Michigan (Lions, Michigan), and Arizona (Cardinals, college teams) were also considered.
  • Florida was discussed, leading to a debate about fans' ability to root for multiple in-state teams.

  • JuJu Gotti introduced a 'Top 10 Worst NFL Jerseys of All Time' list, reduced to nine entries for time.
  • Criticized jerseys included the Miami Dolphins' orange tops, Chicago Bears' 1925 jerseys, and Seattle Seahawks' neon green and blue uniforms.
  • The Buffalo Bills' Takeo Spikes era uniforms were described as a 'visual atrocity' with multiple conflicting colors.
  • The Philadelphia Eagles' Carolina blue with yellow jerseys from the Donovan McNabb era were also highlighted as a poor design choice.

  • The podcast conducted an analysis of the AFC South and NFC South divisions.
  • Discussion focused on questioning the overall quality of these divisions.
  • Exploration of potential reasons for their consistent underperformance in the NFL was a key point.

  • Matt Verderame joined to discuss the Packers vs. Cowboys game as one of the '5 Most Interesting Games of The Week'.
  • Betting lines and analysis were provided, with one pundit predicting a Packers win and cover.
  • The prediction cited Dallas's offensive line struggles and Dak Prescott's high pass attempts as factors.
  • The strong performance of Packers' player Rashawn Gary was also highlighted during the preview.

  • Analysts discussed the Colts vs. Rams game, with one predicting Vic Fangio-level success for the Colts.
  • Concerns were raised over Matthew Stafford's back injury for the Rams.
  • Gardner Minshew's surprisingly strong play for the Colts was highlighted, alongside questions about their defense's ability to stop Kyren Williams' run game.
  • The Rams' league-leading sack numbers were also noted as a significant factor in the matchup.

  • One commentator predicted the Buccaneers to win 24-23, attributing it to Baker Mayfield's strong play despite a depleted receiving corps.
  • Another commentator predicted the Chiefs would win 24-21, noting the Ravens' significant injury issues and the Chiefs' historical dominance in this matchup.
  • The Eagles vs. Buccaneers game was also briefly mentioned, with its winner potentially earning the NFC's number one seed.
