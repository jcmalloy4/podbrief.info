The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: The 10 AM Ludacris Concert (feat. Jessica Smetana)

Key Takeaways

  • Mike Gundy's firing and John Mateer's injury are key college football headlines.
  • Analysts previewed major Week 5 college football games and betting lines.
  • Podcast hosts discussed personal scheduling conflicts and satirical news reports.
  • Negative NFL player statistics for Derrick Henry, Daniel Jones, and Ashton Gentry were highlighted.

Deep Dive

  • Oklahoma State coach Mike Gundy was fired during game week, following a winless Big 12 record.
  • The decision came after a blowout loss to Tulsa, raising questions about organizational timing.
  • Jessica agreed the firing was overdue given the team's decline and competitive struggles.

  • The potential impact of an Alabama victory over Georgia on Kalen DeBoer's coaching is discussed.
  • Kirby Smart's record against Alabama includes a single regular-season victory.
  • Nick Saban's departure is framed as an opportunity for Georgia in the SEC landscape.

  • Upcoming Week 5 games include USC at Illinois and Notre Dame at Arkansas.
  • Notre Dame's defensive player Leonard Moore may be absent due to secondary injuries.
  • Discussion included potential 'look-ahead' factors for Arkansas after a loss to Memphis.

  • A Clemson-UNC game pre-game concert featuring Ludacris was scheduled for 10 AM, sparking debate.
  • Chris expressed a preference for early morning activities, contrasting with Dan's desire for more sleep.
  • The discussion touched on lifestyle preferences and the concept of early bird specials.

  • Sister Jean, a 106-year-old figure associated with Notre Dame basketball, has retired.
  • A host mistakenly joked about her 'rapture' in a group chat, thinking she had passed away.
  • He clarified his relief that she simply retired after a long life and wished her well.

  • The hosts discussed a satirical Onion report claiming Pat McAfee Show co-host AJ Hawk had been 'dead for three days'.
  • The report humorously suggested the show's content remained unaffected by his alleged demise.
  • Hosts reacted with amusement and surprise upon realizing the report was satirical.

  • Tony expressed frustration over a 3 PM Sunday birthday party conflicting with NFL games.
  • The conflict included the Ravens vs. Chiefs game at 4:25 PM, which he could not watch due to the party's park location without a TV.
  • He noted the scheduling was made by his wife and was part of their friend group's activities.

  • Derrick Henry recorded two fourth-quarter fumbles this season for his team.
  • Daniel Jones' record since coach Daboll's strong start was highlighted negatively.
  • Ashton Gentry had a high rate of being hit behind the line of scrimmage, indicating offensive line struggles.
