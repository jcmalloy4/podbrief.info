GOOD FOLLOW - Deja Kelly Talks Athletes Unlimited, Staying Ready For The W & What Good Follow Is Thankful For!

Key Takeaways

  • Deja Kelly utilizes Athletes Unlimited as a platform to prepare for potential WNBA opportunities.
  • Kelly maintains rigorous training and mental fortitude after being waived by the Las Vegas Aces.
  • WNBA Collective Bargaining Agreement renegotiations and expansion are critical for player roles and league growth.
  • Deja Kelly is expanding her career beyond basketball into modeling, hosting, and brand partnerships.
  • The episode highlights gratitude for the growth, authenticity, and increased visibility in women's basketball.

Deep Dive

  • Deja Kelly details her positive experience in Nashville for Athletes Unlimited, noting player camaraderie and Tennessee Titans support.
  • She joined AU to showcase skills for WNBA opportunities, valuing its individual scoring format over team politics.
  • Kelly sees AU as a clean slate and her first opportunity to play a full season in a professional league, emphasizing free-minded, joyful play.

  • Kelly was waived by the Las Vegas Aces after training camp due to limited roster space.
  • She has maintained readiness by working out and focusing on mental strength.
  • Kelly clarifies that basketball is her primary vehicle for opportunities, not a necessity.

  • The WNBA's Collective Bargaining Agreement renegotiations impact roster spots and future team expansion.
  • Kelly expresses concern about potential lockouts or strikes impacting player opportunities.
  • She views WNBA expansion teams as beneficial for players seeking new roles and believes league growth creates a positive career environment.

  • Kelly's business ventures extend beyond basketball to include hosting, podcasting, modeling, brand deals, and events.
  • She walked the runway for Actively Black during New York Fashion Week, describing it as a dream come true.
  • Kelly also walked a second runway for Romeo Hunt, practicing distinct styles for each designer's presentation.

  • Hosts and guests expressed gratitude for the audience, the show's team, and the athletes' hard work.
  • Appreciation was shared for off-season leagues like Athletes Unlimited and Unrivaled, which provide opportunities and proper compensation.
  • The discussion highlights the positive environment created by new leagues and consistent audience support.

  • Gratitude was expressed for 'stud buds' in women's basketball for their authentic and unapologetic presence.
  • Speakers highlighted the increased visibility and representation of masculine-presenting Black women on major platforms like Vogue.
  • The WNBA All-Star 72-hour livestream was noted for its authenticity and humorous moments, contributing to the fun of the year.
