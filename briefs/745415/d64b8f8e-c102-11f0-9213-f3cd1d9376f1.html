The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Top Five NFL Games to Watch in Week 11 with former NFL Defensive Tackle Gerald McCoy, Plus Best Notre Dame Players in History with Jessica Smetana

Key Takeaways

  • Jessica Smetana expressed disappointment regarding the Pittsburgh Steelers' recent performance and quarterback situation.
  • The conversation included a debate on the greatest college football triplets in history, focusing on Oklahoma's talent.
  • Experts analyzed the top five NFL Week 11 games, providing predictions and breaking down key matchups.
  • The discussion covered college football rivalries, rankings, and a comparison of stadium experiences.

Deep Dive

  • Guest Jessica Smetana voiced frustration over the Pittsburgh Steelers' recent performance.
  • Smetana expressed skepticism about potential quarterback targets like Drew Allar.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Aaron Rodgers potentially joining the Steelers.
  • Smetana concluded by stating her disappointment with the Steelers' current season.

  • The discussion focused on the upcoming Notre Dame football game against Pitt.
  • Pitt coach Pat Narduzzi's statement that it's not a 'must-win' game was debated regarding playoff implications.
  • The Notre Dame-Navy rivalry was noted for its historical significance during World War II, making it Notre Dame's longest-standing rivalry.

  • Participants compared Pittsburgh and South Bend, favoring Pittsburgh overall but acknowledging Notre Dame's superior college football stadium.
  • Notre Dame's stadium was highlighted for its proximity to tailgating areas.
  • Preference was given to on-campus venues like Notre Dame's over professional stadiums such as SoFi.
  • The distance of the Rose Bowl from the UCLA campus was noted as a geographical challenge for fans.

  • Predictions for the Notre Dame vs. Pitt game focused on Pittsburgh's defensive strength against the run.
  • Notre Dame's CJ Carr's passing ability was discussed as a key factor.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Notre Dame's offensive line depth for the upcoming game.

  • Former NFL defensive lineman Gerald McCoy was asked to identify the greatest college football triplets.
  • McCoy selected Adrian Peterson, Sam Bradford, and Ryan Broyles as Oklahoma's top three.
  • Mike Fuentes countered with Jim Kelly, Adrian Peterson, and Michael Irvin for Oklahoma.
  • The conversation briefly touched on Kyler Murray's performance and Sam Bradford's career perception.

  • The hosts debated the best portrayal of Spider-Man in film, with some favoring Tobey Maguire for capturing the character's essence.
  • Others argued Andrew Garfield better embodied the role's humor and dorkiness.
  • Tom Holland's portrayal was discussed, highlighting his youth and emotional struggles such as wanting loved ones to remember him.
  • The discussion briefly touched on the emotional impact of Uncle Ben's death across the films.

  • The first NFL Week 11 game discussed was the Tampa Bay Buccaneers versus the Buffalo Bills.
  • The host expressed concern over the Bills' recent struggles and their Super Bowl prospects.
  • Gerald McCoy noted the Bills' weakness against the run, favoring the Buccaneers.
  • McCoy viewed the game as a potential trap for the Bills due to their inability to consistently rush the passer against a mobile quarterback like Josh Allen.

  • The Kansas City Chiefs were favored over the Denver Broncos, citing Andy Reid's post-bye record and the strength of the Chiefs' defense and offense led by Patrick Mahomes.
  • The Los Angeles Rams were favored over the Seattle Seahawks, highlighting Matthew Stafford's performance and Sean McVay's offensive schemes.
  • The Rams' pass rush was predicted to force a critical mistake from Seahawks' quarterback Sam Darnold.
  • The Detroit Lions versus Philadelphia Eagles game was analyzed, with the Eagles ultimately favored due to their proven ability to win games despite offensive struggles.
