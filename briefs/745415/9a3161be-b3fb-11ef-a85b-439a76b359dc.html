The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: Desecrating The Cane

Key Takeaways

  • Hosts engaged in a linguistic debate on the precise definitions of crying, including weeping and sobbing.
  • Greg Cote's use of his late father's cane for a costume ignited discussions on honor and remembrance.
  • Correct cane usage for injuries was clarified by a physical therapist, challenging a common misconception.
  • The Miami Heat's offensive strategy and a controversial late-game shot by Donovan Mitchell were analyzed.

Deep Dive

  • The hosts debated precise definitions of "weeped," "sobbed," and "bawled," offering varying interpretations of emotional expressions.
  • Discussions noted that sobbing often involves audible heaving and can be perceived even with eyes closed.
  • Nancy Kerrigan's reaction was brought up as a different form of emotional display to exemplify distinctions.

  • Greg Cote used his late father's cane, which had been in a garage for over 10 years, for a humorous "Cyclones" team costume.
  • Debate arose on whether Cote's action honored or desecrated his father's memory, prompting Judge Zaslow for a ruling.
  • A playful exchange referenced a "penalty" for desecrating a cane, linking it to a previous disrespect towards Warren Sapp.
  • Cote later shared an emotional story about kissing the cane to connect with his late father and acknowledge his own mortality.

  • The dog, Jumping Charlie, is gaining weight due to insufficient exercise and tore apart a dog blanket.
  • Hosts discussed chaotic pet behavior, with Le Batard describing animals hissing and clawing during a conversation.
  • Le Batard recounted falling face-first at a dog park due to his own dog, highlighting the risk of similar incidents.
  • Concerns were raised about the potential danger of the dog to its owner, especially when using a cane.

  • Greg Cote, using a cane for a right knee injury, initially believed he was holding it correctly in his left hand.
  • A physical therapist informed Cote that the cane should be held in the opposite hand to the injured leg for proper weight-bearing.
  • Cote consulted Google AI for confirmation, verifying the physical therapist's advice.
  • The group celebrated Cote's vindication, acknowledging the counterintuitive logic of proper cane usage.

  • Donovan Mitchell executed a "special shot" at the end of a game against the Miami Heat, sparking debate on if he was out of bounds.
  • The play's success was attributed to a pre-designed strategy by Eric Spoelstra, diagrammed by assistant Chris Quinn.
  • Strategic decisions made by the Cleveland Cavaliers' assistant coaches were analyzed after head coach Kenny Atkinson was ejected.
  • Sam Merrill's missed assignment on the play was analyzed as a factor in the outcome.

  • Hosts debated Norman Powell's role, questioning if he could be considered a primary offensive option.
  • The Miami Heat's strategy emphasizes positionless basketball and player development, rather than acquiring a star like Kevin Durant.
  • The team achieved a high offensive rating with a low number of picks in an overtime game.
  • This approach was contrasted with traditional reliance on a single alpha scorer, with Jimmy Butler and Tyler Herro also mentioned.
