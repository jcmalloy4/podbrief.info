The Big Suey: Zaslow's Source

Key Takeaways

  • Lane Kiffin's coaching decisions are heavily influenced by financial incentives and career advancement strategies.
  • The podcast features an internal debate regarding journalistic credit and attribution, particularly concerning reporting on coaching changes.
  • College football's coaching carousel involves significant salary increases and complex negotiations between universities and coaches.
  • The ethical implications of coaches actively recruiting for a new team while still employed by another are discussed.
  • Media coverage of high-profile coaching moves, like Kiffin's, can be intense and nontraditional, leading to challenges for reporters.

Deep Dive

  • A 25-year-old sports controversy involved Miami being controversially excluded from a championship game despite high rankings.
  • The team had beaten a top opponent but was still kept out of contention, prompting strong reactions.
  • Undefeated records in college football only guarantee a playoff 'buy' for top teams, highlighting the pragmatic, business-oriented nature of coaching.
  • The discussion touched on the perceived importance of winning the Egg Bowl for team standing.

  • Lane Kiffin's tenure at Ole Miss was viewed as a strategic stepping stone for aspirational roles, such as at Florida or the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.
  • Kiffin's decision-making process reportedly disregards conventional coaching timelines and personal relationships, focusing on his contract and future opportunities.
  • The hosts examined the financial and strategic implications of Kiffin's potential move to LSU, including negotiations over his contract.
  • Kiffin is perceived to use programs in regions like Mississippi, known for its poverty, as leverage for career progression.

  • Jonathan Zaslow claimed to have broken the Lane Kiffin story on ESPN radio over a week prior to other reports.
  • A dispute arose over who reported specific coaching news first, with text messages presented as proof of reporting integrity.
  • The concept of 'hat tipping' in sports reporting, giving credit to sources on Twitter, was discussed.
  • Zaslow had accurately predicted Nico Harrison's firing after sharing the news internally, highlighting his predictive reporting ability.

  • Journalist Marty Smith described covering the Lane Kiffin story as the 'worst' assignment of his career due to its intensity.
  • Smith appeared visibly aged after an intense weekend chasing Kiffin in Mississippi.
  • The coverage questioned whether Kiffin, who has never coached a playoff game, receives disproportionate media attention.
  • Kiffin eventually provided Smith with a five-minute interview, suggesting a degree of familiarity between them.

  • Text messages from Friday, February 7th, detailed predictions for John Summerall going to Florida by Sunday and Lane Kiffin to LSU by Monday.
  • Florida Athletic Director Scott Strickland favored John Summerall over Eli Drinkwitz for the head coach position.
  • Boosters at Florida reportedly desired control over the head coach, influencing Strickland's decision.
  • A social media poll was proposed to assess if Mike Ryan 'played himself' during a discussion about sources.

  • Lane Kiffin's move to LSU was presented as a reaction to Florida's pursuit, with LSU offering a similar amount of money.
  • Coaching salaries have seen significant increases, with Missouri's Eli Drinkwitz reportedly earning $10-11 million annually.
  • Kiffin's move to LSU for a similar figure positioned LSU as a superior job opportunity compared to Ole Miss.
  • Concerns were raised about Kiffin's coaching record against ranked teams and his recruiting approach at Ole Miss.

  • Lane Kiffin's actions, such as recruiting Ole Miss players while still coaching, were attributed to financial motivations.
  • The discussion raised logistical and ethical questions about a coach actively recruiting for another team while still employed.
  • Reporter Marty Smith's challenging experience covering Kiffin's decision-making process was highlighted, including desperate text messages for a resolution.
  • The media's intense portrayal of Kiffin was critiqued, suggesting it's similar to coverage of Nick Saban despite Kiffin's different approach.

  • Lane Kiffin expressed feelings of anger and hurt over being unable to return and coach his Ole Miss team, respecting AD Keith Carter's decision.
  • Kiffin revealed an agreement was being worked on to protect Ole Miss during his departure, but it proved insufficient given his new position.
  • The conflict between a coach committing to a rival and staying in their current job for an extended period was analyzed.
  • The impact of coaches splitting time and recruiting for a new team while still coaching their current one was examined, noting potential negative outcomes.
