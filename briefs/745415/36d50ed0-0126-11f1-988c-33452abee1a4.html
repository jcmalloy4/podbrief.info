The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Alley Oop 156: Jason Kidd SNAPS at Reporters Over Flagg at PG, Jokic Returns, SGA MVP Talk & Wild Viral Chaos

Key Takeaways

  • Jason Kidd faces scrutiny over coaching decisions for Cooper Flagg.
  • Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is a strong candidate for NBA MVP honors.
  • Paul George received a 25-game suspension for a substance abuse policy violation.
  • Viral clips feature an unexpected rodeo incident and a boxer's toupee mishap.
  • Reggie Miller's Caitlin Clark comparison sparked significant debate.

Deep Dive

  • Jason Kidd's past decision to make Giannis Antetokounmpo a point guard is compared to his current strategy with Cooper Flagg for the Dallas Mavericks.
  • Kidd dismissively responded to reporters' questions regarding his coaching decisions for rookie Cooper Flagg.
  • He asserted his experience as a player grants him authority over external criticism of his coaching.

  • Nikola Jokic returned to the Denver Nuggets, who subsequently lost to the Oklahoma City Thunder despite his presence.
  • Hosts Juju Gatti and Trysta Crick debated Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's (SGA) MVP level, referencing his recent performance.
  • Juju and Trysta agreed SGA is the likely MVP, citing injury as the only potential obstacle to him receiving the award.
  • Jalen Brown was briefly mentioned as an MVP candidate if the award were based solely on his value to his team.

  • Paul George was suspended for 25 games by the NBA for violating the league's substance abuse policy.
  • The specific details of the violation remain undisclosed by the league.
  • The discussion touched on a potential suspension for mental health treatment involving benzodiazepines, questioning its acceptability if not performance-enhancing.
  • The group expressed a need for more information regarding the violation before issuing judgment.

  • The discussion covered a viral rodeo clip involving women on seesaws and a bull's interaction with them.
  • Participants described the clip, expressing shock and disbelief at the severe outcomes that ensued.

  • The crew discussed a viral video of a boxer whose toupee, referred to as a 'hair system,' flew off during a match.
  • They humorously debated the terminology and the apparent adhesive failure, questioning the boxer's preparation for the fight.
  • Humorous speculation arose about whether the flying toupee constituted a weapon or a self-inflicted injury in the match.
  • A boxer recounted a story about using ammonia instead of shampoo, which damaged his hair and led him to get a toupee.

  • The discussion covered Caitlin Clark's appearance on Sunday Night Basketball.
  • Reggie Miller compared Caitlin Clark to Peyton Pritchard, citing their ability to handle the ball and make clutch shots.
  • This comparison sparked debate and disbelief among the podcast hosts and listeners.
  • Lenny Kravitz's song lyric 'a little less conversation, a little more action' was interpreted as a critique of sports commentary shows.
