The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: NBA Beef

Key Takeaways

  • NHL analysis highlights Maple Leaves' struggle to cover the spread and Blue Jackets' strong home record.
  • College football discussion favors Texas over Vanderbilt, with Arch Manning's potential play noted.
  • NBA drama unfolds as Patrick Beverly publicly feuds with Trey Young after recent game outcomes.
  • Audience polls reveal strong opinions on the Charlotte Hornets and unique sports names.

Deep Dive

  • The Maple Leaves have struggled to cover the spread in seven consecutive games when playing the second leg of a back-to-back.
  • The Blue Jackets maintain a six-game home winning streak against opponents entering with winning streaks.

  • Patrick Beverly tweeted 'BTA' (later clarified as 'Bulls Way To Beat the Hawks') after the Hawks' loss to the Bulls.
  • Beverly followed up by mocking Trey Young's poor shooting performance.
  • The Hawks subsequently experienced another loss to the Raptors.

  • A poll showed 96.5% of the audience believes the Charlotte Hornets have been running up and down the court for 37 years for no good reason.
  • Another poll, questioning if a better name exists in sports than 'mustachioed Simone Fontechio,' resulted in an even 50% split.
