The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: The Doritos Lie (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • NBA 40-point games are increasingly common, suggesting a devaluation of the scoring feat.
  • Juju presented 'Thursday Thunder' betting picks, despite a reported end to his winning streak.
  • FBI investigations targeted large-scale illegal sports betting and poker operations.
  • Audience polls revealed strong opinions on athlete accountability and sports betting legality.

Deep Dive

  • Discussions centered on the perceived devaluation of 40-point performances in the NBA.
  • A historical number of players achieved 40-point games early in the current season.

  • Juju presented his 'Thursday Thunder' picks, following a reported four-game winning streak.
  • Picks included projections for Carson Wentz's receptions and a mention of Aronde Gadson.

  • Discussions included FBI investigations into 'Operation Nothing But Bet,' targeting a sports betting operation.
  • Another investigation, 'Operation Royal Flush,' focused on an illegal poker ring.

  • An audience poll questioned if Terry Rozier should be arrested for his field goal percentage, with 95% responding yes.
  • Another poll found 69% of the audience agreed that betting on one's own team should be legal for athletes.
  • A final poll showed 67% of the audience believed Terry Rozier should not be afforded due process regarding his field goal percentage.
