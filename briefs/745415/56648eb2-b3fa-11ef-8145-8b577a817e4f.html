The Big Suey: Baker Mayfield's 8-Foot Bravado

Key Takeaways

  • Dan Le Batard suggested a colleague's marital issues for show content, raising HR concerns.
  • The Miami Dolphins' defense was criticized as historically poor against opposing running backs.
  • Baker Mayfield delivered an MVP-caliber performance for the Buccaneers, overcoming early career struggles.
  • Former NFL player Mark Sanchez was arrested after an alleged loading dock altercation.
  • The Tennessee Titans secured a chaotic, fumbled touchdown victory in an improbable comeback.
  • The podcast featured multiple nominations for the 'Nuttiest Fan' award, including Michael Irvin.

Deep Dive

  • Dan Le Batard expressed a desire for a colleague's marriage to experience trouble for podcast content.
  • This controversial suggestion was met with resistance, partly due to the colleague having a child.
  • The discussion explored the idea of using personal lives as material for show content.
  • An update on Roy's burnt hand also contributed to the segment's personal content focus.

  • Carson Wentz executed a highly efficient late-game drive against the Browns' defense, despite Miles Garrett's fatigue.
  • Baker Mayfield delivered an MVP-caliber performance for the Buccaneers against the Lions, recalling his early career struggles.
  • Jalen Hurts and the Eagles' offensive strategy were analyzed in their game against the Broncos.
  • The Browns' consistent low scoring and past quarterback issues, including Johnny Manziel and Joe Flacco, were revisited.

  • Cardinals coach Jonathan Gannon was criticized for his perceived lack of emotion after Emari Demercado's costly fumble.
  • Demercado's fumble prevented the Cardinals from securing a 28-6 lead, contributing to their eventual loss.
  • The Cardinals' inexplicable loss to the Titans included a specific play where a player dropped the ball before the goal line.

  • The Tennessee Titans secured a victory through a bizarre touchdown play involving multiple fumbles and deflections.
  • Despite the chaotic sequence, the Titans' win led to criticism regarding coaching decisions and receiver play.
  • The unlikelihood of the Titans' comeback victory was emphasized, contrasting with the Cardinals' failure to secure their lead.
  • Kyler Murray's injury and the Arizona Cardinals' performance were paralleled with the Titans' improbable game.

  • Michael Irvin was nominated for his actions as the University of Miami mascot and his interactions with fans.
  • A fan in a shark costume, who exited a North Carolina game after a 35-3 deficit, was another nominee.
  • Ohio State fans collectively removing their shoes in unison at a game was presented as a nominee.
  • Michael Irvin's heated, yet controlled, banter with an FSU fan after a Miami loss also received a nomination.

  • Initial reports indicated former NFL player Mark Sanchez was stabbed and in stable condition in Indianapolis.
  • Subsequent information emerged that Sanchez was arrested at the airport.
  • The New York Jets' season was deemed unsalvageable following their 0-5 record and missed tackles.
  • A hypothetical scenario involving Daniel Day-Lewis performing an action was humorously discussed by the hosts.

  • Mark Sanchez allegedly attacked a 69-year-old delivery driver in a loading dock over parking.
  • The driver reportedly defended himself with pepper spray, but Sanchez continued the assault.
  • The driver sustained severe injuries, and public intoxication was listed as a factor in Sanchez's arrest.
  • The truck driver's hearing aids being turned down was noted as a potential contributing factor to the dispute.

  • The Cincinnati Bengals' season was deemed dependent on Joe Burrow, with backup Jake Browning considered unviable due to interceptions.
  • The New York Jets' season was concluded as finished after their first month of play.
  • Baker Mayfield's "bravado" and ability to throw 30-yard passes were highlighted, alongside the Tampa Bay Buccaneers' potential.
  • A rookie receiver from Ohio State achieved over 100 receiving yards in an NFL weekend game.
