The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Alley Oop: Cathy Gets BOOED?! Aces Dynasty Talk + Devin Carter’s HAIR + NBA Preseason Gamblers

Key Takeaways

  • The Las Vegas Aces secured their third championship in four years, sparking dynasty debates.
  • WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert was booed by fans during the Aces' trophy ceremony.
  • Potential WNBA lockout discussions predict free agency delays and chaotic roster finalization.
  • The hosts identified the Denver Nuggets as their early favorite for the upcoming NBA championship.

Deep Dive

  • The Las Vegas Aces clinched their third championship in four years, prompting discussions regarding their dynasty status.
  • Mid-season roster changes, including the additions of Jewel Lloyd and Alysa Smith, contributed to the Aces' significant turnaround.
  • The team's recovery was described as one of the greatest in basketball history, drawing parallels to the Golden State Warriors' ability to adjust and win.

  • WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert was met with significant boos during the Las Vegas Aces' trophy ceremony.
  • The booing was characterized as a legitimate, impromptu expression of disapproval from the crowd.
  • Aces players, including A'ja Wilson, were observed laughing when asked about the Commissioner receiving boos.

  • The hosts expressed strong belief in the Denver Nuggets as their pick for the upcoming NBA championship.
  • The Nuggets had a close series against the Thunder last season and made significant offseason additions.
  • Additions cited include Bruce Brown, a new center, Cam Johnson, and Tim Hardaway Jr., enhancing the team's depth.

  • The hosts expressed disbelief and concern regarding the practice of betting on preseason basketball games and player props.
  • The discussion highlighted the inherent risks and unpredictability associated with preseason wagering.

  • The hosts questioned why untied shoes are a recurring issue for basketball players during games.
  • Discussion centered on ankle injuries sustained from untied shoes or stepping on opponents' shoes.
  • One host shared a personal experience of a severe double ankle injury attributed to such an incident.

  • A 'Bosch Stop' segment focused on Devin Carter's hair, with one host expressing amusement at his 'pissy energy'.
  • The discussion explored whether Carter's distinct hair is a personal choice or a dare.
  • Carter's athletic lineage was highlighted, comparing him to his NBA father and bald-playing grandfather.
