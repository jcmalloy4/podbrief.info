Indiana Fever’s Syd Colson Talks Getting To Know Caitlin Clark, ACL Injuries, Brand Identity & More!

Key Takeaways

  • Sydney Colson navigated an injury-plagued Indiana Fever season with humor and leadership.
  • Colson's WNBA career demonstrates resilience, overcoming setbacks to win two championships.
  • She actively builds an authentic personal brand through humor and fashion for post-basketball endeavors.
  • Colson highlights Kelsey Mitchell's under-recognized work ethic and resilience on the court.

Deep Dive

  • The guest joined the Indiana Fever for personal growth and faith, despite initial discomfort and external perceptions.
  • The team achieved success, including winning the Commissioner's Cup, while navigating adversity and injuries.
  • Humor and camaraderie served as key coping mechanisms for the team's injury-plagued season, which included Colson tearing her ACL.
  • The team joked about a 'Phoenix curse' after losing multiple players there; Colson also discussed interactions with Caitlin Clark.

  • The guest expressed admiration for Kelsey Mitchell's strong work ethic and comprehensive all-around play.
  • Mitchell demonstrated resilience by consistently performing despite facing double teams and increased defensive pressure.
  • Her sustained performance and developments throughout the season may not have received due credit.

  • Colson's career journey includes an ACL tear in college, being waived by the Connecticut Sun, and playing overseas for three years.
  • She won two WNBA championships with the Las Vegas Aces before joining the Indiana Fever.
  • Her endurance is attributed to her faith and a belief in gaining valuable lessons from team experiences and relationships.
  • Doubts arose in 2019 while playing overseas in Poland, where she considered ending her career due to difficult coaching and low pay.

  • Colson connects with fans through her authenticity and self-deprecating humor, noting basketball is not her sole focus.
  • She embraces a multifaceted life and emphasizes self-belief and the importance of her voice.
  • Her strategy for handling online haters includes not valuing their opinions and sometimes trolling them back, noting some negative comments come from bots.

  • Colson served as an assistant coach at Rice University, enjoying the relationship-building and impact on young women, indicating a potential return to coaching.
  • Her girlfriend and stylist, Amadi Brooks, has significantly elevated Colson's personal brand and style.
  • This visual transformation is preparing Colson for a post-basketball career in acting, with increased industry recognition.
  • Her personal style is described as 'versatile' and 'elevated streetwear,' often incorporating colorful and outgoing elements.
