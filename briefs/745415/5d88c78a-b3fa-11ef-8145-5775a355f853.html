The Big Suey: Zaslow Wins Sucka of the Day

Key Takeaways

  • "Pablo Torre Finds Out" podcast achieved a Top 10 ranking on Apple Podcasts for 2025.
  • The episode explored generational disconnects regarding respect for comedic legends like Eddie Murphy.
  • Lane Kiffin's coaching future at Ole Miss and potential College Football Playoff implications were debated.
  • A radio host's rapid retraction of the phrase "shut up" prompted discussion on workplace communication.
  • Bill Belichick's presence at an adult cheerleading competition sparked humorous analysis from hosts.

Deep Dive

  • "Pablo Torre Finds Out" was recognized as one of Apple Podcasts' Top 10 episodes of 2025.
  • Amin was congratulated for his involvement in the Kawhi Leonard episode, noted for its narrative structure.
  • The hosts considered some Top 10 entries, like "New Heights" (Taylor Swift) and a Lady Gaga episode, as 'cheating' due to celebrity involvement.
  • An award nomination was acknowledged, with gratitude extended to David Sampson, Dan Le Batard, and Metalog Media.

  • Discussion centered on a perceived lack of historical respect for comedy among younger audiences, questioning recognition of icons like Eddie Murphy.
  • The speaker argued Eddie Murphy's significant talent and impact, comparing him to the LeBron James of comedy.
  • Murphy's early career included joining "Saturday Night Live" at 19 and starring in "Trading Places" at 21.
  • Unmade movies and casting choices included Richard Pryor for a buddy movie, Charlie Sheen for "The Karate Kid," and Martin Lawrence for "Rush Hour."

  • Lane Kiffin's appearance on The Pat McAfee Show, discussing self-help and yoga, drew criticism for his perceived mercenary approach.
  • The host questioned outrage over Kiffin's perceived lack of loyalty to Ole Miss, arguing coaches are temporary hires.
  • Mississippi was characterized as a stepping stone, suggesting the state has no claim to loyalty from coaches like Kiffin.
  • Kiffin is speculated as the "stone-cold lead" for the next Florida coach position, with meetings reportedly occurring this week.

  • A debate arose regarding whether Ole Miss should be penalized in playoff rankings if head coach Lane Kiffin were to leave.
  • One speaker argued Kiffin's influence as a "cult of personality" and offensive strategist makes his departure more impactful.
  • His potential departure was contrasted with a quarterback injury, such as Florida State's situation with Jordan Travis, in terms of playoff impact.

  • Discussion focused on a radio host's rapid retraction after using the phrase "shut up" during a segment.
  • Participants speculated on the reasons for the quick apology, considering potential HR violations or pressure from a spouse.
  • The speed of the apology was highlighted, suggesting the host may have apologized before the other person was even offended.
  • The phrase was noted as an "HR edict" in some contexts, leading to its removal from workplace interactions.

  • The conversation shifted to Bill Belichick's attendance at an adult cheerleading competition in Raleigh, North Carolina.
  • Hosts debated Belichick's potential dislike for the activity, contrasting it with other photo opportunities he has enjoyed.
  • Analysis of a picture suggested Belichick appeared displeased, possibly looking at his phone, during the event.
  • The discussion questioned whether anyone genuinely enjoys watching cheerleading competitions as a spectator, distinguishing from participants.
