The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Marty Smith Will Not Complain (feat. Lucy Rohden and Marty Smith)

Key Takeaways

  • The Michigan-Ohio State rivalry generated intense debate and trash talk among reporters.
  • Lane Kiffin's controversial behavior at Ole Miss was heavily scrutinized, including his pilates class antics and fake dog.
  • Reporter Marty Smith detailed his challenging assignment literally chasing Lane Kiffin across Mississippi.
  • A viral Turkey Trot prank sparked debate about its humor versus humiliation.

Deep Dive

  • Reporters expressed disdain for Ohio State's colors and performance, emphasizing Michigan's perceived superiority in the long-standing rivalry.
  • Individuals at the game engaged in humorous insults, with one finding the other soft and comparing him to Ohio State's defense and coach.
  • A reporter confidently asserted Michigan has never lost to Ohio State during his time as a student and predicted continued success, including a championship win.

  • Dan Le Batard discussed a recent Lane Kiffin tweet involving a Bible verse and the flag of Trinidad, which received negative reactions.
  • Humorous anecdotes from women who attended Kiffin's Pilates classes described his disruptive behavior during sessions.
  • The discussion questioned the fairness of criticism leveled at Kiffin, considering his consistent behavior and portrayal as a 'victim.'

  • The host criticized Lane Kiffin's attention-seeking method of departure from Ole Miss and the decision to allow him to coach in the playoff run.
  • Lucy argued Ole Miss should not allow Kiffin to coach due to the rivalry with LSU and potential player poaching, stating Ole Miss fans hate LSU.
  • Kiffin's controversial move was contrasted with Tulane coach John Summerall's amicable transition to Florida, where Summerall did not attempt to sway Tulane commits.

  • Earlier rumors about Lane Kiffin not having custody of his dog, 'Juice,' were confirmed.
  • It was revealed that 'Juice' was a prop dog belonging to Ole Miss university, used to improve Kiffin's public image and likability.
  • The dog, formerly known as Juice, has been renamed 'Rebel' and is confirmed to remain in Mississippi, not with Kiffin.

  • Lucy discussed a viral TikTok prank where a family told their son's fianc e she was participating in a Turkey Trot 10K.
  • The family revealed the prank on race day after the fianc e had trained for months, posting the footage online.
  • The video sparked a debate among viewers regarding whether the prank was funny or humiliating.

  • Following an on-air apology, the show transitioned to discussing Marty Smith's recent assignment.
  • The host questioned Marty Smith about the 'Turkey Trot prank' orchestrated by Lucy, involving a family member training for a non-existent race.
  • Marty Smith described his recent experience chasing Lane Kiffin across Mississippi for an assignment.

  • Marty Smith recounted the logistical challenges and near misses involved in his pursuit of Lane Kiffin at the Oxford airport.
  • Smith described driving through a field to bypass fire trucks to reach Kiffin, who was waiting atop a hill.
  • Kiffin reacted with surprise to the pursuit, comparing Smith to 'Scooby-Doo.'

  • The discussion shifted to NASCAR, focusing on Denny Hamlin's upcoming season following a recent devastating loss.
  • Marty Smith discussed Hamlin's mindset after the significant loss, suggesting the event took a toll.
  • Smith noted Hamlin's ability to appear composed even when potentially sad, mentioning Hamlin's father's illness.
