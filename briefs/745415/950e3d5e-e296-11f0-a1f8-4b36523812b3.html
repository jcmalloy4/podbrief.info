The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Consequential Countdown: Lions, Lamar, and the 49ers' Path Home

Key Takeaways

  • Lamar Jackson is expected to miss the Ravens' Week 17 game, and DK Metcalf is sidelined for the Seahawks.
  • The DK Metcalf fan altercation generated debate regarding player conduct, fan accountability, and security effectiveness.
  • Discussions on quarterback legacies highlight Lombardi Trophies as a key, though potentially unfair, metric for historical greatness.
  • NFL Week 17 game previews analyze critical matchups, including the Eagles-Bills and Steelers-Browns, with playoff seeding implications.
  • The San Francisco 49ers are considered underestimated, with a strong defense and coaching despite significant injuries.

Deep Dive

  • Lamar Jackson is expected to miss the Ravens' game against the Packers in Week 17.
  • DK Metcalf is out for the Seahawks' game against the Browns.
  • The host noted the transition from Travis Kelce's career to young quarterbacks like Bo Nix, Caleb Williams, and Drake May.
  • Both players' absences impact key Week 17 matchups crucial for playoff contention.

  • The conversation addressed the recurring debate of whether 'Die Hard' is a Christmas movie.
  • One speaker asserted that 'Die Hard' unequivocally qualifies as a Christmas movie.
  • The hosts expressed weariness with the longevity of this particular holiday season debate.

  • Lamar Jackson's availability for an upcoming game was discussed amid rumors of a potential 'divorce' between Jackson and coach John Harbaugh.
  • Whispers suggested Jackson is not trying hard enough and spends excessive time playing video games.
  • The Ravens have reportedly adjusted practice schedules later in the afternoon to accommodate Jackson, drawing comparisons to Michael Jordan and Kobe Bryant's mid-career adjustments.
  • Jackson's spectacular talent but inconsistent availability were highlighted.

  • DK Metcalf received a two-game suspension for interacting with a fan during a game, with the guest stating Metcalf should have alerted security instead.
  • The host argued the Steelers were fortunate officials did not see the incident in real-time, which could have resulted in an immediate ejection and altered game outcomes.
  • Both speakers expressed surprise and disapproval that the fan involved in the altercation has not been banned.
  • The incident also prompted a critique of the ineffectiveness of security and police during the on-field altercation.

  • One host expressed doubt about Patrick Mahomes winning another Super Bowl, prompting a comparison to Tom Brady's sustained success.
  • The discussion highlighted the pressure to associate current star quarterbacks like Lamar Jackson, Josh Allen, and Joe Burrow with historical greatness.
  • Lombardi trophies are presented as a key, albeit potentially unfair, metric impacting Hall of Fame considerations, with Dan Marino cited as an example of a great QB without one.
  • A prediction was made that at least two of Jackson, Allen, and Burrow might finish their careers without a Super Bowl win, with Justin Herbert added to the list of quarterbacks facing challenging odds.

  • The Eagles versus Bills matchup was previewed, with Buffalo favored by 1.5 points and a total of 43.5; the guest predicted a Buffalo win due to playoff seeding motivation, while the host predicted a 31-27 Eagles victory.
  • The Steelers versus Browns game was discussed, with the Browns favored by three points and a low total of 34.5.
  • A bet was placed on the Steelers at plus three, with the potential involvement of Mason Rudolph at quarterback noted as a factor that could benefit Miles Garrett in his pursuit of a sack record.
  • The Jaguars and Colts game was also analyzed, with a bet placed on the Colts at plus six at home; the guest expressed admiration for Philip Rivers' veteran play and believed the Colts could win outright.

  • The discussion highlighted the potential for the AFC's fifth seed, such as Buffalo, to be favored against an AFC North champion like Baltimore or Pittsburgh.
  • The Seahawks versus Panthers game was analyzed, with the Panthers receiving seven points at home; the guest expressed a preference for the Seahawks.
  • The potential for the Seahawks to secure the number one seed in the NFC was highlighted as a significant factor in their Week 17 game.
  • The host and guest debated the prevalence of 'big cat' team names versus 'big dog' names in the NFL.

  • The San Francisco 49ers are discussed as being underestimated, despite recent injuries to key players like Nick Bosa and Fred Warner.
  • The team's defense, under Bob Sala, is performing well.
  • Kyle Shanahan's coaching ability to develop quarterbacks, including Trey Lance, Mac Jones, Sam Darnold, and Daniel Jones, was praised.
  • The 49ers' potential to secure home-field advantage throughout the playoffs, a rarely achieved scenario, was highlighted.
  • The Ravens versus Packers game was also discussed, with the Packers favored by 4.5 points due to Lamar Jackson's doubtful status.
