The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Keep Your Third Lie Open (feat. Amin Elhassan)

Key Takeaways

  • NBA trade discussions focused on Giannis Antetokounmpo's potential future and various team offers.
  • Transparency issues persisted regarding NBA player suspensions and health-related disclosures.
  • The 'Doomsday Clock' setting sparked debate about its scientific validity and funding mechanisms.
  • LeBron James's All-Star selections and comments generated controversy, leading to comparisons with other players.

Deep Dive

  • Giannis Antetokounmpo is reportedly leaning against a trade this week, with improved offers anticipated over the summer.
  • The Miami Heat's detailed trade package for Antetokounmpo included players like Khalil Ware and Tyler Hero, alongside future draft picks.
  • A trade package offering salary cap relief for the Milwaukee Bucks was suggested as the most attractive option.
  • Rumors surrounding the Golden State Warriors' offer, specifically mentioning Jimmy Butler, were theorized as posturing or misinformation.

  • The NBA exhibited an unusual lack of transparency regarding Paul George's suspension, which he reportedly attributed to mental health medication.
  • Hosts debated the prevalence of performance-enhancing drug use in professional basketball, with one recalling a past conversation suggesting it is more common than believed.
  • One host hypothesized Paul George might have taken Xanax, leading to a discussion about the drug's effects and its implications for playing professional basketball.
  • The colloquialism 'get pinched' in relation to being caught using performance-enhancing drugs was discussed, along with its potential origins.

  • The 'Doomsday Clock' was set at 85 seconds to midnight on January 27, 2026, by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists.
  • The Bulletin called for urgent action on nuclear arsenals, artificial intelligence, and biological threats.
  • One participant expressed skepticism, labeling it 'bullshit' and a funding concern, suggesting the clock is kept near midnight to ensure continued financial support.

  • LeBron James's comments about the All-Star game were anticipated, with hosts preparing to discuss his statements.
  • Giannis Antetokounmpo's statement about not aiming to be an All-Star was questioned for sincerity after his All-Star starter status was announced.
  • Reggie Miller's comparison of Caitlin Clark to NBA player Peyton Pritchard sparked debate about its validity and the NBA's efforts to promote the WNBA.

  • Amin's 'Weekend Observations' included a playful categorization of people based on their interest in major sports events like the Super Bowl and trade deadlines.
  • The Cleveland Cavaliers acquired Keon Ellis and Dennis Schroeder in exchange for DeAndre Hunter.
  • Potential trade destinations for Giannis Antetokounmpo were listed as Golden State, Atlanta, New York, and Miami.
  • Miami's Norm Powell was congratulated on his first All-Star selection, which occurred with the team that traded him.
  • LeBron James's ambiguous comments about this potentially being his last year were discussed as a strategy to secure his All-Star selection.

  • A wrestling match preview between Darren Peterson and AJ DeBansa was mentioned.
  • Carlos Alcaraz was congratulated for becoming the youngest player to complete a career Grand Slam.
  • A bet placed on Yannick Sinner to beat Djokovic ultimately resulted in a loss due to injury.
  • The 'Weekend Observations' revisited a video clip, focusing on bouncers' reactions, including amusement and an attempt to use a stanchion before a chair.

  • A trade of Giannis Antetokounmpo to the Oklahoma City Thunder was deemed unlikely due to the team's financial situation and potential disruption to their current progress.
  • Kawhi Leonard's exclusion from the All-Star team drew criticism, with his consistent strong performance for the Clippers highlighted.
  • The format of USA vs. World spots and players not taking the selection process seriously were cited as reasons for perceived All-Star injustices.
  • The debate continued regarding LeBron James's All-Star selection over Kawhi Leonard, with arguments favoring Leonard's consistent season performance.

  • LeBron James's anticipation of his All-Star selection was debated, with Amin disputing the notion that LeBron didn't expect to be chosen.
  • Jason Kidd responded to criticism regarding his coaching decisions, specifically playing Giannis Antetokounmpo at point guard, asserting his experience as a player as justification.
  • The panel acknowledged Kidd's role in developing players like Giannis but questioned his dismissal of all criticism from non-players.
  • A debate comparing Giannis Antetokounmpo and Dwyane Wade concluded that Giannis possessed a higher peak and a more accomplished career due to MVP awards and sustained performance.
