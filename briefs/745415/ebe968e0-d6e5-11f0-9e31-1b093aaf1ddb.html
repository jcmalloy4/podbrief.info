The Return of Old Man Rivers: Savior or Season Ender?

Key Takeaways

  • Philip Rivers' NFL comeback with the Colts is discussed as a boost for his Hall of Fame candidacy.
  • The podcast explores several 'unanswerable' NFL questions regarding player injuries and team decisions.
  • Joe Burrow's recent reflections on his career's toll are compared to Andrew Luck's early retirement.
  • Dean 'Mojo' Muhtadi provides expert analysis and predictions for consequential NFL Week 15 games.
  • A segment evaluates top quarterbacks like Mahomes, Jackson, Allen, and Burrow for future franchise leadership.

Deep Dive

  • Host Dave Dameshek opened by discussing Philip Rivers' potential return to the NFL with the Colts.
  • The comeback is theorized to enhance Rivers' Hall of Fame candidacy by potentially securing a playoff appearance.
  • Significant events since Rivers last played include Shohei Ohtani's MVPs and Alex Ovechkin's goal record.

  • A story recounts former Chargers cornerback Quentin Jammer allegedly playing professional games while intoxicated for years.
  • The hosts debated the feasibility and consequences of playing football drunk versus at age 44.
  • Questions were raised about the logistics of alcohol consumption during games and potential coaching staff awareness, calling for further investigation.

  • Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow's recent comments on the mental and physical toll of his career were discussed.
  • The host expressed skepticism about Burrow's complaints given his financial status and career success.
  • Analysts compared Burrow's mindset to Andrew Luck's, citing Burrow's injury history and team circumstances as contributing factors.

  • Mike Fuentes introduced a segment on 'unanswerable questions' within the NFL.
  • Topics included JJ McCarthy's 'mysterious injury' and low completion percentage.
  • The discussion also questioned the Colts' decision to sign 44-year-old Philip Rivers as Anthony Richardson's replacement after an orbital bone fracture.

  • Speculation arose regarding Michigan football, questioning if the administration had long-standing knowledge of program issues.
  • Questions were also raised about hiring John Harbaugh, considering his brother Jim Harbaugh's history with the Michigan program.
  • The host expressed hope for Tom Brady's involvement to aid the Michigan Wolverines football program.

  • Dean 'Mojo' Muhtadi joined to discuss consequential NFL Week 15 games, starting with the Los Angeles Chargers at Kansas City Chiefs.
  • The Chiefs were favored by 4.5 points, with an over/under total of 41.5 points.
  • Mojo predicted a Chiefs victory, attributing it to Patrick Mahomes and Travis Kelce's ability to rally.

  • The Los Angeles Rams were favored by six points at home against the Detroit Lions, with a 42.5 total.
  • The Broncos were discussed as home underdogs against the Packers, with the host picking Denver motivated by their top seed race.
  • Skepticism was expressed about Philip Rivers' readiness for the Colts against the Seahawks, who were favored by 13.5 points.

  • Dean 'Mojo' Muhtadi picked the Minnesota Vikings against the spread versus the Dallas Cowboys.
  • The Buffalo Bills were favored over the New England Patriots in a Week 15 matchup.
  • The discussion concluded by evaluating Lamar Jackson, Patrick Mahomes, Josh Allen, and Joe Burrow as top quarterback choices for the next half-decade.
