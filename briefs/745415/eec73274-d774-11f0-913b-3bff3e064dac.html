The Big Suey: A Language Reserved For Muppets

Key Takeaways

  • Waymo self-driving cars sparked apprehension regarding I-95 usage.
  • The Tampa Bay Buccaneers' playoff hopes are diminishing after recent losses.
  • Joe Burrow is publicly questioning his enjoyment of football amid injuries.
  • Kyle Pitts delivered a historically significant fantasy football performance.
  • Former Michigan coach Shrone Moore faces arraignment, fueling intense college football fan reactions.
  • Michigan Athletic Director Warde Manuel's actions in a high-profile firing draw criticism.
  • Analysts offer skeptical predictions for Philip Rivers' hypothetical NFL return.
  • The 2026 World Cup's North American return highlights ticket acquisition challenges.

Deep Dive

  • A host observed a Waymo vehicle on the highway, noting its distinctive spinning device.
  • Apprehension was expressed regarding the prospect of riding in a driverless vehicle on I-95.
  • The service is a self-driving car operation.

  • The World Cup is set to return to North America in 2026, marking its first appearance since 1994.
  • The tournament will feature 48 teams playing in large stadiums.
  • Discussion highlighted potential difficulties in obtaining tickets for the event.

  • Atlanta Falcons tight end Kyle Pitts achieved a career-high game with 11 catches for 166 yards and three touchdowns.
  • This was noted as one of the best fantasy tight end performances in NFL history since 1983.
  • Pitts is now ranked fifth all-time in half-PPR points among tight ends.

  • Quarterback Joe Burrow stated he needs to enjoy playing football, questioning his involvement if it's not fun after extensive physical and emotional tolls.
  • Analysts interpret his comments as a message to the Bengals for improved team infrastructure, particularly the offensive line.
  • Comparisons were drawn to Andrew Luck's career, suggesting Burrow may consider retirement without improvements.

  • Bengals teammate Jamar Chase expressed surprise and disbelief at Joe Burrow's public comments.
  • Chase initially thought Burrow's statements about his struggles might have been AI-generated.
  • The unusual nature of Burrow's public expression of frustration was highlighted.

  • The potential return of 44-year-old quarterback Philip Rivers for the Colts against the Seahawks was discussed.
  • Analysts predicted a challenging performance, forecasting low completion rates (e.g., 9 for 21) and multiple interceptions (one or two).
  • Predictions cited Rivers' age and lack of recent play as factors for his performance (e.g., 105 or 93 yards).

  • The impending arraignment of former Michigan coach Shrone Moore was discussed, with charges against him soon to be revealed.
  • Hosts noted the unusual circumstances of his detention without formal arrest.
  • Disapproval was expressed regarding public reactions to the situation, particularly towards women involved.

  • Michigan Athletic Director Warde Manuel's job security was discussed given the university's history with similar issues.
  • NBC Sports reporter Nicole Auerbach reported Manuel fired Shrone Moore without HR or security present.
  • The handling of Moore's firing was criticized, with suggestions that Manuel's actions might lead to his dismissal amidst scandals.
