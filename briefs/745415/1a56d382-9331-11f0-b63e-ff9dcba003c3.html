WNBA Milk Bag Puffer Jackets and It’s Ring Season | Alley Oop | Ep 22

Key Takeaways

  • WNBA player appearance and post-game media interactions sparked ethical and practical discussions.
  • The Seattle Storm faced significant challenges and performance questions in the WNBA playoffs.
  • Alyssa Thomas's critical errors impacted her MVP candidacy discussion during the playoffs.
  • The Minnesota Lynx showed strong WNBA championship potential due to a key player's performance.
  • Courtney Williams' improved play and the 'StudBudz' movement are gaining significant traction in the WNBA.

Deep Dive

  • Discussion centered on Skylar Diggins' distinctive 'mother's milk' puffer jacket, worn following a WNBA playoff loss where the Aces defeated the Sun.
  • Hosts debated journalistic ethics regarding following up on a player who covered her shorts with a towel after a significant loss.
  • Conversation included practical considerations for WNBA players' attire during periods, specifically addressing white shorts and the concept of 'period shorts' for stain avoidance.
  • Skylar Diggins' intense demeanor post-loss was noted, with advice to give her space.

  • The Seattle Storm suffered a significant loss to the Las Vegas Aces in the playoffs, despite the Aces' winning streak.
  • Speakers expressed disappointment with the Storm's uncharacteristic turnovers and lack of aggression, particularly from player NECA.
  • The Aces' dominance was highlighted by Asia Wilson's heavy scoring against a Storm roster featuring high-caliber players like Dominique Melanca, Gabby Williams, and Skylar Diggins.

  • Alyssa Thomas's MVP candidacy was questioned due to critical errors, including airballing shots, missing layups, and turning the ball over in crucial moments.
  • Missed free throws were identified as a significant lapse in her WNBA playoff performance, alongside a defensive lapse.
  • Her performance was contrasted with Natasha Cloud's 20-point performance and the New York Liberty's win.

  • One speaker expressed strong confidence in the Minnesota Lynx's championship potential for the WNBA playoffs.
  • This confidence was based on a dominant performance from player Natisha Heideman, who achieved 18 points, 4 assists, 3 rebounds, and a +33 rating.

  • Courtney Williams' improved conditioning, mid-range game, and confidence, attributed to playing with a close friend, have driven the 'StudBudz' movement.
  • Her success has garnered significant off-court attention, including a Vogue cover and interest from Twitch's CMO, with fans showing engagement like pink wigs.
  • Her past with the Atlanta Dream was discussed, including a critical view that she was undervalued and an offseason altercation involving physical aggression towards a man.
  • A host expressed strong personal support for Courtney's skills and success since 2021, noting her rare professional achievement from Folkestone, Georgia.
