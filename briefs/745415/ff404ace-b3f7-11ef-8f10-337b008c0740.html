The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: The GOAT Conversation (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Juju expressed concern about the Kansas City Chiefs' offensive performance.
  • A'ja Wilson received her fourth WNBA MVP award amidst greatness debate.
  • Arch Manning and a Rams player were named 'Jokers of the weekend'.
  • The Indiana Fever secured a surprising Game 1 WNBA playoff victory.

Deep Dive

  • Juju voiced concern for the Kansas City Chiefs' offense, suggesting Rashi Rice and Xavier Worthy could improve performance.
  • The Chiefs face a difficult upcoming schedule, including games against the Ravens and Jaguars.
  • This could lead to a 2-4 record before Rice's return.

  • A'ja Wilson won her fourth WNBA MVP award, despite Nafisa Collier being a strong contender.
  • Her MVP was attributed to the Las Vegas Aces' 17-game winning streak.
  • Las Vegas Raiders owner Mark Davis wore a pink wig in support during Wilson's award ceremony.
  • Juju debated if Wilson's fourth MVP solidifies her as the greatest WNBA player, leaning yes while acknowledging Maya Moore.

  • Arch Manning was labeled a 'Joker of the weekend' for flexing on a Sam Houston player after a touchdown.
  • Rams player number 47 was also highlighted for poor positioning on an epic block kick play.

  • The Indiana Fever secured a stunning Game 1 victory against the Las Vegas Aces in Las Vegas.
  • The Fever advanced after eliminating the Atlanta Dream, with Caitlin Clark noted for her performance.
  • The conversation also touched on the Minnesota Lynx's comeback victory against the Phoenix Mercury.
  • The WNBA playoff structure has shifted from best-of-three to best-of-five series.
