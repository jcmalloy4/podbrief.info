The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Tom Brady Shenanigans, Mike Ryan Ruiz and the Problem with the Raiders' Logo, and the Five Most Interesting Games of Week 3 with Nick Kostos

Key Takeaways

  • Tom Brady's part-ownership of the Las Vegas Raiders is scrutinized for potential conflicts of interest within the NFL.
  • A new segment, 'The Drip Drill,' analyzes and ranks NFL team uniforms, focusing on AFC West designs.
  • Dave Dameshek presents his DraftKings 'Pick Six Fantasy' selections for Week 3, including specific player prop bets.
  • Key Week 3 NFL matchups are previewed, with analysis on quarterback injuries, defensive strengths, and betting lines.

Deep Dive

  • The segment questions the optics and potential conflicts of interest regarding Tom Brady's new role as a part-owner of the Las Vegas Raiders.
  • Discussion centers on Brady's historical ties to the league and his current broadcasting position.
  • The analysis suggests the NFL's stance on Brady's involvement may be viewed skeptically by some observers.

  • The new fashion segment, 'The Drip Drill,' begins with an examination of NFL uniforms, particularly in the AFC West.
  • The Las Vegas Raiders' classic black and silver uniforms are highlighted, while the Los Angeles Chargers' powder blue pants receive specific critique.
  • The discussion includes modern sock trends, uniform violations, helmet decal placement issues for the Raiders, and the perceived decline of ornate striped socks post-2000.
  • The segment ranks AFC West uniforms, criticizing the Denver Broncos' current bright neon orange designs compared to their 1990s look, and praising the Kansas City Chiefs' red pants and white tops as a top road uniform.

  • Dave Dameshek introduces his 'Pick Six Fantasy' selections for the week, presented by DraftKings.
  • His picks include Justin Herbert over 20.5 rushing yards and Patrick Mahomes to throw at least two touchdown passes.
  • Other selections include B.J. Robinson over 21.5 receiving yards, Russell Wilson under 227.5 passing yards, Drake Maye to throw at least one interception, and Kareem Hunt over 8.5 rushing attempts.
  • Guests Mike and Gino Fuentes offer insights, particularly on Herbert's rushing yards and Mahomes' touchdown potential.

  • The upcoming game between the Cincinnati Bengals and Minnesota Vikings is discussed, noting both teams are expected to start backup quarterbacks due to injuries.
  • The total points line for the game dropped significantly after Joe Burrow's confirmed absence.
  • Nick Kostos analyzes the Bengals' defense and recalls Jake Browning's past performance against the Vikings, including an overtime victory where Browning threw for over 340 yards.
  • Kostos suggests the Bengals possess capable offensive weapons and anticipates a close game.

  • Concerns are raised about the Baltimore Ravens' pass rush and their ability to generate interior pressure against the Detroit Lions' offensive line.
  • Despite the Ravens being favored by 4.5 points, a lean towards betting the Lions to win outright is expressed due to perceived value.
  • Analysis suggests the Lions' physicality advantage might be neutralized by the Ravens' strength, especially with changes to the Lions' offensive line.
  • The Ravens are predicted to win, potentially by a significant margin.
