Alley Oop: LYNX COACH CHERYL REEVE SUSPENDED, Minnesota Finals Hopes CRUSHED, Fever SHOCK the Aces

Key Takeaways

  • Minnesota Lynx faced playoff elimination after coach Cheryl Reeve's suspension and team struggles.
  • The Indiana Fever delivered a significant upset, forcing a Game 5 against the Las Vegas Aces.
  • NBA star Anthony Davis must now wear protective eyewear for the remainder of his career.
  • Virginia Cavaliers fans celebrated a college football victory by storming the field.
  • WNBA officiating and player conduct prompted debate and scrutiny.

Deep Dive

  • The Minnesota Lynx were eliminated from the WNBA playoffs, with disappointment expressed over blown leads and their series exit.
  • Key players and coach Cheryl Reeve were absent due to suspension during the Lynx's playoff run.
  • Reeve's heated post-game conduct, including profanity, led to questions about the severity of her suspension compared to player fines.

  • The Indiana Fever delivered a surprising performance against the Las Vegas Aces, forcing a Game 5 in their series.
  • Conversation highlighted the Indiana Fever's resilience and Aaliyah Boston's performance, contrasting with the Aces' struggles and questioning officiating fairness, including player comments about 'special whistles'.
  • WNBA player seating for Chloe Bibby and Aerie McDonald was criticized, suggesting preferential treatment, following a controversial photo by Bibby.
  • Aaliyah Boston's comments about officiating and Becky Hammon's response fueled discourse on 'messiness' in WNBA post-game press conferences.

  • The Phoenix Mercury awaits their WNBA finals opponent, noted for their surprising success and past wins against the Atlanta Dream.
  • The Las Vegas Aces are favored to advance in the WNBA finals due to their pedigree, despite the Fever's challenging performance.
  • The WNBA was compared to "The Hunger Games" due to numerous player injuries and team struggles, including specific mentions of players Fi and DJ Nae.

  • Minnesota Lynx coach Cheryl Reeve was observed yelling at a fan on the sideline, prompting analysis from the hosts.
  • The incident was compared to other public outbursts, with speculation regarding the fan's actions.
  • Reeve's earlier suspension and heated post-game conduct, involving profanity, had already raised questions about her behavior.

  • Breaking news announced that NBA star Anthony Davis must now permanently wear protective eyewear.
  • This decision was based on a doctor's recommendation following an injury scare.
  • The change is anticipated to impact Davis's public image and potentially his career trajectory.

  • Virginia Cavaliers fans stormed the field after their win against Florida State, with the speed of the storming notably fast.
  • Discussion included a brief mention of a player potentially being run over during the field-storming event.
  • One speaker approved of field storming, recalling an incident at an Oregon vs. Penn State game where they hugged the athletic director.
