The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: Top 5 People In Sports Who Need To Keep Their Beard (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • The Buffalo Bills' recent loss to the Miami Dolphins was analyzed, citing receiver issues and play-calling.
  • A 'Top 5' list of sports figures who should not cut their beards was presented and debated.
  • Tyrese Maxey of the Philadelphia 76ers emerged as a significant NBA MVP candidate.
  • Audience polls revealed listener opinions on airport etiquette, driving, and various other topics.

Deep Dive

  • Guest Juju expressed frustration over the Bills' loss to the Miami Dolphins, attributing it to issues like a lack of a number one receiver.
  • Questionable play-calling on third downs was highlighted as a specific concern for the Bills' performance.
  • Player Jordan Poyer's on-field struggles were mentioned, alongside the team's significant injury concerns.

  • A 'Top 5' list of sports figures who should not cut their beards was discussed by the guest.
  • Aaron Rodgers was ranked fifth, with his appearance without a beard described as 'aging and unusual'.
  • Ryan Fitzpatrick was listed at number four, and the dynamic duo of Jason and Travis Kelce were placed at number three.

  • The conversation returned to guest Juju, who was wearing a Miami Heat Vice Nights jersey, before an MVP update.
  • Tyrese Maxey of the Philadelphia 76ers was highlighted as a potential MVP candidate, recently averaging over 33 points and eight assists.
  • Nikola Jokic was also mentioned as a strong contender in the current NBA MVP race.

  • A specific poll asked, 'Do you judge someone who sleeps on the airport floor?', with 68% of respondents indicating they do.
  • Audience poll results also covered topics including judging in general, airplane mode usage, and pizza preferences.
  • Listeners' perceptions of Davis Mills compared to Patrick Mahomes, and aspirations to taste 'wetness' were also polled.
