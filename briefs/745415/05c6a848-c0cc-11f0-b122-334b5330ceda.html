GOOD FOLLOW - Aneesah Morrow Talks LSU, Connecticut Sun, Athletes Unlimited, Her Fashion Identity, & More!

Key Takeaways

  • Aneesah Morrow blends tomboyish and chic in her personal fashion, with aspirations for modeling.
  • Morrow values the community aspect of Athletes Unlimited, contrasting it with the WNBA's competitive nature.
  • She attributes her rebounding prowess and mental toughness to her South Chicago upbringing and athletic parents.
  • Morrow's collegiate career included national accolades at DePaul and a positive transfer to LSU under coach Kim Mulkey.
  • She envisions a Final Four appearance and a national championship for LSU this season.

Deep Dive

  • Morrow describes her personal style as a blend of tomboyish and chic, evolving over three years with inspiration from Chicago fashion.
  • She collaborates with stylist Tisha to achieve a bold and confident aesthetic, highlighting her interest in fashion.
  • Morrow attended New York Fashion Week with Coach, expressing a desire to pursue modeling and fashion in the future.
  • She views her hair journey, including braids, ponytails, and wigs, as an alter ego that boosts her confidence to 'look good, play good'.

  • Morrow experienced a welcoming community at Athletes Unlimited, contrasting it with the WNBA's competitive environment and highlighting the importance of relationships.
  • She aims to build connections and balance competitive drive with camaraderie, with winning as her ultimate goal.
  • Morrow identified her primary season goals as continuing her legacy, winning, making connections, and pioneering from South Chicago.
  • She views Athletes Unlimited as beneficial for players with specific career trajectories, including those aiming for the WNBA, and for balancing personal dreams with family connections.

  • Despite her 6'1" height, Morrow dominates rebounding, attributing her prowess to will, effort, and high school volleyball experience.
  • She describes rebounding as an unselfish act requiring extra effort that significantly benefits her team.
  • Morrow's physical strength and mental toughness stem from her genetics and upbringing in South Chicago, enabling her to hold her ground as an undersized player.
  • Her motivation is rooted in proving herself right rather than disproving others.

  • Morrow is adapting to the Connecticut Sun's rebuilding phase and new coaching staff after a dominant college career.
  • She is giving herself grace and focusing on personal development, emphasizing the importance of learning from veteran players, even off the court.
  • Morrow is working to incorporate efficiency and the ability to slow down the game into her play, inspired by WNBA veteran Tina Charles.

  • Morrow earned significant accolades at DePaul, including National Freshman of the Year, and finished with the third-most rebounds in Division I history.
  • She transferred to LSU, citing family proximity during COVID-19 and the appeal of coach Kim Mulkey's program and new opportunities.
  • Coach Kim Mulkey challenged Morrow to be a better teammate and person, encouraging her to use her voice and be a leader, recognizing untapped potential.
  • Morrow credits the NCAA transfer portal for its positive impact on her career, providing financial leverage and opportunities for women athletes.

  • Morrow expresses hope that the current LSU team surpasses her achievements, aiming for a Final Four appearance and a national championship.
  • She advises the team to focus on fundamental aspects of the game, including rebounding, setting screens, and running the floor.
  • Morrow emphasizes the importance of extra effort and hard work, stressing consistent contributions for team success.
