The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: Happy Thanksgiling Day 2!

Key Takeaways

  • The episode serves as a tribute to Billy Gil on his last day, featuring emotional farewells and show history.
  • Joe Flacco's unexpected performance has revitalized the Bengals' season, surprising critics and rival coaches.
  • Billy Gil's early years on the show included a quiet presence and a controversial on-air debut.
  • Billy Gil encountered disciplinary issues at ESPN Bristol, including unauthorized studio use and a 'Masked Singer' incident.
  • The show is beginning auditions for Billy Gil's replacement, following Jeremy's musical tribute.
  • A significant segment analyzes the upcoming Miami versus Louisville college football game.

Deep Dive

  • The hosts dedicated the episode to Billy Gil's last day, acknowledging his significant contributions and positive feedback from the previous day's show.
  • Jeremy and Greg Cody planned to serenade Billy Gil with songs; Chris confirmed Greg Cody's song was a 'tearjerker'.
  • A song with lyrics expressing gratitude and calling Billy Gil their 'favorite hypochondriac' was played.

  • Joe Flacco's unexpected performance led the Bengals, outperforming expectations and leading to a high number of targets for Ja'Marr Chase.
  • Steelers coach Mike Tomlin reportedly fears Flacco, referencing past warnings about the quarterback's potential to cause disruption.
  • The Cleveland Browns' decision to trade a quarterback to a division rival is suggested to validate Tomlin's concerns about facing Flacco.
  • Flacco is framed as a 'bridge quarterback' revitalizing franchises, with an 'urban legend' about him yielding the Comeback Player of the Year award to Damar Hamlin.

  • Hosts recalled Billy Gil's very first words on the program, which elicited an immediate negative reaction from listeners via a text message declaring him 'the worst'.
  • Billy Gil allegedly remained silent for two years early in his tenure, only answering the phone.

  • Hosts discussed Billy Gil's reaction to encountering ESPN personality Linda Cohn in Bristol, comparing it to children's reactions to Disney characters, exploring themes of fear and recognition.
  • Billy Gil's early days at ESPN in Bristol highlighted his initial feelings of not belonging, including an anecdote about missing a meeting while trying to film a segment.

  • The group discussed getting in trouble at ESPN for using a studio without proper protocol.
  • An incident involved Matthew Berry's fantasy studio and puppets, alongside strict security around the SportsCenter set.
  • A 'Dancing with the Stars' segment involving a ladder was mentioned, clarified as separate from a later 'Masked Singer' incident.

  • The hosts began a segment on Billy Gil's top five times he got in trouble at ESPN, starting with a 'Masked Singer' incident after the Super Bowl.
  • A ladder incident occurred during a later season of 'The Masked Singer' segments.
  • Audio of a ladder being purposefully grabbed to fall was played for analysis, including the sound of a tree and a ladder missing its cue.

  • Jeremy expressed gratitude and admiration for Billy Gil, stating he dressed as Billy as a tribute and struggled to find words for his goodbye, preparing a farewell song.
  • Discussions ensued regarding 'open seat auditions' for Billy's position, with Jeremy noted for talking extensively in an attempt to 'yammer more than Billy'.
  • Potential successors for Billy's role, including Stevie and Yudonis Haslam, were mentioned.

  • The conversation focused on the upcoming college football game between Louisville and Miami, described as the 'battle for the bronze boots'.
  • Analysis included comparing Miami's current defense to past seasons and discussing the offensive strengths and weaknesses of both teams, mentioning players like Miller Moss and Brown.
  • Louisville was identified as Miami's most talented opponent remaining on their schedule, alongside tough road tests against Pitt and SMU.
