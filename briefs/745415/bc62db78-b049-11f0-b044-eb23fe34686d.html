GOOD FOLLOW - Michael Grady talks New York Liberty, The Face of The WNBA, Finding His Style & More!

Key Takeaways

  • Michael Grady is a prominent play-by-play announcer for NBA and WNBA, including the New York Liberty and Amazon Prime.
  • His first professional play-by-play experience was with the New York Liberty during their Westchester phase.
  • Grady serves as Amazon Prime's lead WNBA announcer, a role developed from early experiences with the Indiana Fever.
  • His signature "Cash" play-calling phrase originated during his Minnesota Timberwolves broadcasts.
  • A'ja Wilson is identified as the WNBA's current face; Caitlin Clark and Paige Bueckers are recognized as emerging stars.

Deep Dive

  • Michael Grady serves as a play-by-play announcer for NBC and Amazon Prime, covering both NBA and WNBA.
  • He is also the voice for the Minnesota Timberwolves and the New York Liberty.
  • Grady and the host, the Liberty's color commentator, humorously discussed who was the "Beyoncé" of their broadcast team.
  • They underscored the honor of covering the New York Liberty, especially following the team's 2024 championship win.

  • Michael Grady's first professional play-by-play basketball experience occurred with the New York Liberty while they played in Westchester.
  • He characterized the team as being in a transitional phase before their relocation to Brooklyn.
  • The New York Liberty has become "appointment viewing" in New York City, offering exciting basketball and entertainment, culminating in a championship.
  • Grady recounted a humorous incident involving the mascot, Ellie the Elephant, during an intense game, highlighting broadcasters' need for quick decisions.

  • Michael Grady was selected as the lead play-by-play announcer for Amazon Prime's WNBA broadcasts, partnering with Candace Parker.
  • This selection was prestigious given the competitive field of play-by-play announcers, with both Amazon and NBC seeking Grady's services.
  • His entry into covering women's basketball was shaped by early experiences with the Indiana Fever and Tamika Catchings.
  • He began by covering the Indiana Fever to gain experience as the PA announcer for the Pacers, which broadened his opportunities.

  • Grady drew inspiration from strong female athletes, including his mother, recalling watching the inaugural WNBA game.
  • He expressed gratitude for his career progression, advancing from early play-by-play roles to covering games on Amazon with Candace Parker.
  • His signature play-calling phrase, "Cash," originated during his time announcing for the Minnesota Timberwolves.
  • This signature call aims to capture impactful moments that resonate on social media, irrespective of the game's quarter.

  • Michael Grady identified A'ja Wilson as the current face of the WNBA, citing her "GOAT-level" resume before age 30.
  • Wilson has led the Las Vegas Aces to championship runs, and her resilience and continued hunger for achievements were highlighted.
  • Emerging WNBA talents discussed included Caitlin Clark and Paige Bueckers, with Grady expressing excitement about Bueckers' skills and potential.
