The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

GOOD FOLLOW - Lisa Leslie Talks Angel Reese’s Motion, A’ja Wilson’s GOAT Status, Cathy Engelbert Gets Booed & More

Key Takeaways

  • Lisa Leslie emphasizes authentic, responsible storytelling in podcasting to avoid negativity.
  • Angel Reese became the first WNBA player to walk in the Victoria's Secret runway show.
  • Reese and Caitlin Clark are credited with significantly growing women's basketball's popularity.
  • Lisa Leslie advises Angel Reese to develop offensive skills for WNBA success.
  • The conversation debates A'ja Wilson's GOAT status against past WNBA legends.
  • WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert was booed, signaling fan support for player profit sharing.

Deep Dive

  • Lisa Leslie stated her podcasting approach focuses on speaking from the heart and being a truth-teller.
  • She prioritizes using her platform responsibly to avoid negativity and clickbait content.
  • Leslie aims to produce content that is genuine rather than sensationalized for views.

  • Angel Reese made history as the first WNBA player to walk in a Victoria's Secret runway show.
  • Lisa Leslie, who has a modeling background, expressed pride in Reese's confidence and nerve.
  • The event is seen as opening doors for other female athletes in modeling and broader cultural influence.

  • The guest compared her past diverse career to Angel Reese's current impact, advocating against confining young women to a single career path.
  • Angel Reese's breakthrough is attributed to her personality, confidence, social media, and the growing popularity of women's basketball.
  • Both Angel Reese and Caitlin Clark are recognized for their significant impact, fueling an explosion in women's basketball growth, with both navigating careers authentically.

  • Lisa Leslie assessed Angel Reese's WNBA performance, highlighting her rebounding leadership.
  • Leslie advocated for Reese's inclusion in defensive accolades despite early season injuries.
  • She suggested Reese focus on developing offensive skills and noted the absence of veteran leadership in Chicago as a factor impacting her rookie transition.

  • A'ja Wilson's accomplishments include four WNBA MVP awards, Defensive Player of the Year honors, and three championships in eight seasons.
  • Lisa Leslie acknowledged Wilson's dominance but questioned if her current competition level allows for a true GOAT comparison across eras, citing players like Cheryl Miller and Diana Taurasi.
  • Leslie advocated for players like Wilson to receive more off-court opportunities and endorsement deals, noting her existing partnerships with Nike and Gatorade.

  • WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert was loudly booed by fans while presenting the championship trophy to the Las Vegas Aces.
  • The guest interpreted the booing as fans supporting players and wanting them to benefit from the league's success, including sellouts and full arenas.
  • Lisa Leslie discussed the critical importance of profit sharing within the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA).
