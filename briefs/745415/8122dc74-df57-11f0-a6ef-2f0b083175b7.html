Best of DLS: It's Beginning To Look A Lot Like Fishmas

Key Takeaways

  • The Christmas episode compiles favorite holiday-themed moments from past shows.
  • Segments include Greg Cody's 'Back in My Day' critiques of modern Christmas traditions.
  • Dan Le Batard's 2014 essay on LeBron James' Miami return is featured.
  • The compilation showcases Tom Izzo's accordion playing and a Dolphins fan's 1-15 season song.
  • Roy's Top 10 holiday songs list elicits humorous reactions from participants.
  • A 'Fishmas' song celebrates the Miami Dolphins' winning streak.
  • A parody song details the 1-15 Miami Dolphins' challenging season.

Deep Dive

  • The podcast episode celebrates Christmas with a compilation of past segments.
  • It includes two 'Back in My Days' from Greg Cody and a 'Roy's Top 10' list.
  • Dan Le Batard's 2014 welcome essay for LeBron James' return to Miami is featured.
  • The compilation also includes Tom Izzo playing the accordion and singing Christmas carols, plus a fan-made song honoring the 1-15 Dolphins.

  • The conversation critiques how the Christmas season has deteriorated, focusing on artificial trees and impersonal gift-giving like gift cards.
  • The speaker expresses dissatisfaction with modern holiday trends, contrasting them with a nostalgic view of past traditions.
  • The segment criticizes the convenience of laser lights over manual decoration and the perceived superficiality of contemporary Christmas songs.

  • Greg Cody's 'Back in My Day' segment contrasts old-school Christmas light installation with modern laser light technology.
  • Cody argues that the effort involved in traditional methods makes them earned, while laser lights are seen as a shortcut that disrespects Santa Claus.
  • He criticizes the perceived laziness associated with artificial trees and the decline in traditional Christmas music.
  • Another segment by Cody focuses on the artificial Christmas tree as a symbol of a declining national spirit, recalling a childhood psychedelic silver Christmas tree.

  • A Christmas-themed song about the Miami Dolphins, referred to as 'Fishmas,' is played.
  • The song celebrates their winning streak and key players.
  • It provides a festive take on the team's recent performance.

  • Roy presents his Top 10 list of holiday songs and stories.
  • Participants react humorously to his choices, particularly to number 10, 'White Woman'.
  • Questions are raised regarding the ranking of other songs on his list.

  • A narrative reflects on LeBron James's four-year tenure with the Miami Heat.
  • The relationship is described as passionate but ultimately temporary.
  • The narrative contrasts Miami's potential reaction to his return with the rest of the country's.

  • Participants begin by singing 'Jingle Bells,' 'Silent Night,' and 'Joy to the World.'
  • Legendary coach Tom Izzo of Michigan State is mentioned and encouraged to sing.
  • The discussion references his past coaching achievements, including recruiting players like Zach Randolph and Draymond Green to Michigan State.
  • The group transitions to singing 'Deck the Halls,' with focus on performance quality.

  • A parody song details the struggles of the 1-15 Miami Dolphins during a season under coach Cam Cameron.
  • Each verse highlights a specific week of the season, enumerating the team's losses and poor performance metrics.
  • Commentary mentions Billick, missed plays, and Wayne Huizinga's emotional state.
  • The song shifts to 'The big tuna,' attributing blame and noting a decade since his last playoff win.
