The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Six Defining Games of NFL Week 14; Deshaun Watson Cleared to Practice & Von Miller Speaks on Parsons

Key Takeaways

  • DraftKings' 'Pick Six' segment features predictions for player touchdowns in NFL Week 14 games.
  • Pro Bowler Von Miller discussed Hall of Fame criteria, emphasizing Super Bowl wins over individual stats.
  • Miller offered insights on NFL team building, player influences, and the league's evolution over 15 years.
  • Sports Illustrated writer Matt Verderame analyzed pivotal NFL Week 14 matchups and playoff implications.
  • Key games include Colts-Jaguars, Steelers-Ravens, and Bengals-Bills, with detailed predictions for each.

Deep Dive

  • The podcast transitioned to the 'Pick Six' segment, sponsored by DraftKings, focusing on players expected to score touchdowns.
  • Initial player selections for Week 14 included Derrick Henry, James Cook, Josh Allen, Joe Burrow, Baker Mayfield, and Jordan Love.
  • DraftKings' 'Pick Six' game allows users to bet on player statistics, with payouts based on pick accuracy; new users receive $50 in credits with a $5 entry using code Damashek.

  • Von Miller detailed his jersey number selections, wearing 58 with the Denver Broncos to honor Derrick Thomas.
  • He adopted number 40 with the Rams and Bills, and number 24 with the Washington Commanders, inspired by Kobe Bryant and Champ Bailey.
  • Miller discussed studying Derrick Thomas's game film and interviews extensively, drawing parallels between their physical play.

  • Von Miller proposed a Hall of Fame criterion: whether a player's story is essential to telling the NFL's history 4,000-5,000 years in the future.
  • This contrasts with Rod Woodson's rule of being a top-two player at one's position for a season.
  • Miller suggested the NFL's story cannot be told without Cam Newton due to his unique impact, choosing Newton over Jalen Hurts to win a single game based on Super Bowl 50 performance.

  • Von Miller reflected on the NFL's evolution over his 15-year career, noting the increased prevalence of cameras capturing every play.
  • He highlighted a general increase in player size, speed, and strength across the league over time.
  • The discussion also touched upon NFL team building strategies, including trading high-priced wide receivers for multiple lower-cost options.

  • Sports Illustrated NFL Staff Writer Matt Verderame joined to preview pivotal Week 14 matchups, including rivalries like Bears-Packers and Steelers-Ravens.
  • The Colts-Jaguars game was highlighted for its significant playoff implications in the AFC South.
  • Discussion covered the fluctuating relevance of rivalries, emphasizing the importance of both teams competing for substantial stakes.

  • The importance of securing the number one seed in the NFL playoffs was debated, highlighting the advantage of a first-round bye and fewer games.
  • Discussions covered the significance of homefield advantage, particularly how cold weather or dome stadiums can impact game outcomes.
  • Teams like the Packers, Bears, Texans, and Bills were mentioned, with a preference expressed for open-air stadiums over domes, criticizing the Browns' consideration for a dome.

  • Matt Verderame picked the Jacksonville Jaguars to defeat the Indianapolis Colts, citing Daniel Jones's mobility issues due to a fractured leg as a disadvantage against Jacksonville's pass rush.
  • For the Pittsburgh Steelers vs. Baltimore Ravens game, Verderame predicted a 30-22 Ravens victory, highlighting Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers' offensive struggles.
  • Verderame noted Lamar Jackson is not at 100% and Mark Andrews' recent contract extension was questioned due to low receiving yardage, with a final score prediction of 24-20 for Baltimore.

  • The AFC North race was discussed, noting the Bengals' position behind the Ravens and their upcoming game against the Bills.
  • The Bills were favored by 5.5 points against the Bengals, with a total of 53.5; T. Higgins was expected to play.
  • Matt Verderame predicted a 34-27 Bills victory, noting the Bengals' inability to stop opposing run offenses and favoring James Cook and Josh Allen's mobility.
