The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: Lollipops In The Oven (feat. Dianna, Joey, and Mikey Russini)

Key Takeaways

  • Alexander Barkov's recovery timeline for the Florida Panthers includes conflicting projections from the player and medical staff.
  • NFL insider Dianna Roussini shared personal anecdotes ranging from chaotic children and a secret candy stash to a birdseed-fueled grill fire.
  • NFL team performances this season highlight the Eagles' defensive strength amidst dysfunction and the Bills' inconsistency, while the Texans emerge as a playoff dark horse.
  • The latest Baseball Hall of Fame ballot is considered weak, notably prompting a revisit to Ryan Braun's controversial drug test and denial.
  • Quarterback discussions covered Jalen Hurts' record, Justin Fields' future, and Sam Darnold's performance under pressure.

Deep Dive

  • The Florida Panthers set a record with 16 players scoring in a single game, including five goals in an 11-minute span.
  • The Miami Heat struggled with rebounding without Bam Adebayo.
  • Offensive rebounding was distinguished as a "hunter's skill" versus defensive rebounding's positional, IQ-based nature.

  • NFL insider Dianna Roussini described her children's "witching hour" chaos, including a child near an oven.
  • She revealed using a bottom oven as a "candy stash" to hide treats from her children.
  • A family incident involved her father storing birdseed in a grill, which later caught fire when her husband ignited it.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles secured a victory over the Detroit Lions, with their defense performing strongly amidst internal dysfunction.
  • The Buffalo Bills exhibited inconsistent performance over the last month, raising questions about their conference championship prospects.
  • Multiple teams, including the Colts, Rams, Seahawks, and Patriots, are making a case for contention this season, with the Patriots considered stronger than the Bills.

  • Bill Belichick was not seriously considered by the New York Giants for their coaching job, and no NFL team currently lists him as a candidate.
  • The Houston Texans were endorsed as a surprise team potentially making a playoff run, improving with backup quarterback Davis Mills and a strong defense.
  • A previous prediction for a team's quarterback situation outlined Flacco starting, then Dylan Gabriel, followed by Shadur Sanders due to public demand.

  • The discussion covered Jalen Hurts' record against top opponents and Tom Brady's playoff road wins.
  • Hosts debated the Philadelphia Eagles' success with many star players and the Dolphins' Super Bowl chances.
  • The conversation included whether Daniel Jones could outperform Patrick Mahomes and if the Colts could beat the Chiefs.

  • Questions were raised regarding Justin Fields' future as an NFL starting quarterback, given his previous stints with the Pittsburgh Steelers and New York Jets.
  • Sam Darnold's ability to handle pressure and recent performance against the Rams raised concerns.
  • The weak list of newcomers to the Baseball Hall of Fame ballot was noted, including Ryan Braun and Edwin Encarnacion.

  • Discussion centered on Ryan Braun's positive drug test and his subsequent "Whopper press conference" denying the accusation.
  • The test was clarified to be related to a urine sample collector, not a FedEx person, as Braun had previously claimed.
  • The segment highlighted the perceived incompetence of the baseball steroid era, mentioning South Florida's role and players like A-Rod and Manny Ramirez.
  • Braun's adamant defiance was compared to Lance Armstrong's response to accusations.
