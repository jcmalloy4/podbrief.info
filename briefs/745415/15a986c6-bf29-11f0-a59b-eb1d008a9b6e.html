Alley Oop 136: De’Andre Hunter’s Filthy Cross, Sengun’s Magic Footwork & Mavs GM Talk

Key Takeaways

  • Alperen Sengun's controversial play against Giannis Antetokounmpo sparked debate during a Rockets victory.
  • De'Andre Hunter's 'crossover of the year' candidate potentially injured Josh Giddey.
  • The Dallas Mavericks are reportedly considering a mid-season General Manager change due to internal negativity.
  • Mavericks GM Nico Harrison's decisions are under scrutiny amid growing fan pressure.
  • A viral image of food in a public restroom prompted a debate on hygiene standards.

Deep Dive

  • Alperen Sengun's crossover and move against Giannis Antetokounmpo occurred during the Houston Rockets' 122-115 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks.
  • Hosts described Sengun's move as 'shady' and disrespectful, leading to a controversial foul call on Giannis.
  • Kevin Durant's performance in another game was briefly mentioned, registering 31 points, 3 assists, and 7 rebounds.

  • De'Andre Hunter's crossover on Josh Giddey was identified by the hosts as a candidate for 'crossover of the year.'
  • The play potentially caused an injury to Giddey, possibly leaving him unable to play in the next game.
  • The hosts debated whether Giddey embellished his fall and how he would emotionally recover from such a play.

  • Reports suggest the Dallas Mavericks are considering a mid-season General Manager change due to pervasive negativity.
  • Ownership has reportedly lost faith in the current front office structure.
  • The timing of a potential change, especially before Kyrie Irving's return and his effectiveness on the current roster, was debated.

  • The conversation touched on whether Mavericks General Manager Nico Harrison has made poor decisions.
  • A parallel was drawn to public pressure shifting policy, citing an example in Portland involving ICE agents and protests.
  • This comparison suggested fan sentiment could exert similar influence on the Dallas Mavericks' front office.

  • A segment titled 'Boy Stop' featured discussion of a viral image depicting a man with a DoorDash McDonald's bag on a bathroom floor.
  • Hosts debated the implications of bringing food into a bathroom, with one host referencing a past incident on an airplane.
  • There was general agreement that food should not be brought into such environments, regardless of circumstances, with suggestions for consequences for the individual.
