The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Help, I've Been Orbitally Altered! (feat. Amin Elhassan)

Key Takeaways

  • Jacksonville Jaguars are on an eight-game winning streak due to offensive improvements.
  • Seattle Seahawks fired Head Coach Pete Carroll after a 3-14 season.
  • Draymond Green was ejected from a game after receiving two technical fouls.
  • Amin's Weekend Observations validated Mike Ryan's sports predictions on various teams.
  • The Winter Classic at LoanDepot Park suffered widespread criticism for operational issues and poor fan experience.
  • Severe eye injuries and orbital damage in combat sports were a significant discussion point.
  • Netflix is expanding into live sports broadcasting, including an NFL Christmas Day game.

Deep Dive

  • The Jacksonville Jaguars achieved an eight-game winning streak.
  • Success is attributed to the acquisition of Jacoby Myers and an improved offense, becoming formidable over the last six weeks.
  • Offensive Coordinator Liam Cohen's impact with Trevor Lawrence is compared to Baker Mayfield's performance in Tampa Bay.
  • The team's current talent level is being compared to that of the Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Rams.

  • Adam Schefter initially reported the Raiders fired Pete Carroll, later clarified to be the Seahawks.
  • Pete Carroll was fired by the Seattle Seahawks following a 3-14 season.
  • A question was raised regarding the reason for his termination despite a recent win, drawing a parallel to the Atlanta Falcons and Raheem Morris.

  • Amin's 'Weekend Observations' began with Mike Ryan being correct about the Kansas team's success against Ohio State and Michigan.
  • The Kansas team secured a victory, validating Mike Ryan's commentary.
  • The team later lost after multiple red zone turnovers, but Ryan's initial prediction was still noted as correct.

  • The Boston Celtics are highlighted for their recent success and strong record.
  • Jaylen Brown achieved a 50-point game against the Clippers.
  • The Celtics are leading the Eastern Conference standings, with Jayson Tatum expected to return soon.
  • Jaylen Brown publicly complained on social media about not winning Player of the Month, comparing his performance to SGA for the Western Conference award.

  • The Panthers lost 5-1 to the Rangers in the Winter Classic at LoanDepot Park, which was described as a disaster.
  • Criticism was directed at LoanDepot Park's operations for major events, citing issues with concessions, fan experience, and long lines.
  • The ballpark's infrastructure and location in Little Havana were noted as contributing factors to operational difficulties.

  • Despite significant operational issues, the overall atmosphere at the Winter Classic had a good buzz, with attendees appearing happy.
  • The Florida Panthers' arrival with Ferraris created an impressive spectacle, but participants felt the actual game experience was less engaging.
  • The event was ultimately described as a 'horseshit experience' for in-person attendees, despite looking good on television.
  • Issues included poor sound quality, capacity problems, and the unusual choice of a South Florida location for an outdoor hockey event.

  • Strong negative reactions were voiced against holding another Winter Classic at LoanDepot Park due to perceived failures.
  • The possibility of hosting future events like the Stadium Series was discussed.
  • The stadium's surrounding environment was noted for its lack of amenities, with only a Wendy's mentioned as present.
  • Historical context of the Orange Bowl and its surrounding area, including a place called 'Bowl Bar', was also brought up.

  • Tom Aspinall sustained a potential career-ending eye injury during a fight with Ciryl Gane, having previously made a comeback from an ACL tear.
  • The culture of fighting was discussed, highlighting the requirement for fighters to finish bouts despite severe damage like orbital alteration, often requiring multiple surgeries and leading to lasting vision impairment.
  • Netflix is entering the live sports business, specifically with a Christmas Day NFL broadcast featuring former players like Emmitt Smith and Clinton Portis.
