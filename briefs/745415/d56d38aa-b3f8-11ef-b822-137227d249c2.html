The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Ally Mike Ryan (feat. Ros Gold-Onwude and Adnan Virk)

Key Takeaways

  • WNBA championship rings are highly valued, with some estimated at $70,000.
  • A'ja Wilson delivered an iconic game-winning shot in Game 3 of the WNBA Finals.
  • LeBron James's recent endorsements, including Hennessy, mark a shift in his public persona.
  • The segment "The Pitch Clock" featured baseball trivia focused on 2001 ALDS rosters.
  • MLB playoff recaps highlighted the Blue Jays' ALDS victory and NLDS series analysis.

Deep Dive

  • Roz confirmed her return to Los Angeles and displayed her New York Liberty championship ring.
  • Championship rings, like those for the Liberty, Panthers, and Warriors, are highly valuable.
  • Liberty player rings were described as large, diamond-encrusted, with a locket-like opening, potentially valued at $70,000 each.

  • A'ja Wilson delivered a game-winning shot in Game 3 of the WNBA Finals, described as an iconic moment.
  • Wilson achieved 34 points and 14 rebounds, leading the Aces to rally from a 17-point deficit and take a 3-0 series lead.
  • Mainstream media coverage of women's basketball was criticized for focusing on controversy over the game itself.

  • Discussion covered player leverage with the league's Unrivaled project and potential work stoppages, linked to money.
  • The Aces' championship potential was noted despite mid-season struggles and a significant loss to the Lynx.
  • Becky Hammond received recognition for coaching the Aces through player changes toward a potential third championship in four years.

  • LeBron James's endorsement of Hennessy surprised guests, questioning his embrace of a humorous persona.
  • A guest criticized James's Hennessy commercial as "grotesquely commercial," suggesting a significant payment for the deal.
  • Recent commercial ventures, including sports gambling apps, align with a phase where James plays into public perception.

  • "The Pitch Clock" segment was introduced as a two-part baseball feature with trivia and expert interviews.
  • The trivia game involved naming players from the 2001 ALDS Game 5 roster for the Mariners and Cleveland.
  • Players like Kenny Lofton, Roberto Alomar, Mike Cameron, Jim Thome, Omar Vizquel, and Brett Boone were identified.

  • The Toronto Blue Jays eliminated the New York Yankees in four games, despite Aaron Judge's performance.
  • The Blue Jays advanced to the ALCS for the first time since 2016, highlighting their offensive success against Yankees pitching.
  • Discussion previewed a Game 5 matchup between the Detroit Tigers and Seattle Mariners, with predictions favoring the Tigers.

  • National League playoff games focused on the Phillies-Dodgers series, mentioning Christopher Sanchez and Tyler Glasnow.
  • Analysis highlighted the Phillies' strong home and road postseason performance and Kyle Schwarber's home run record.
  • The Dodgers' bullpen weakness was a key discussion point in the series analysis.

  • Despite the Cubs winning Game 4, the Brewers were favored to advance in the NLDS due to better matchups, particularly in pitching.
  • The hosts discussed the Milwaukee Brewers' playoff performance, expressing doubt about their World Series chances against teams like the Dodgers or Phillies.
