The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: Zas Is In A Bed

Key Takeaways

  • The show's crew faced challenges with absences and injuries, sparking liability concerns.
  • Heisman Trophy conversations included defensive player eligibility and 2024 odds.
  • Erik Spoelstra's comments on Aleksander Barkov's recovery raised questions.
  • The Miami Heat's Nikola Jovic signed a significant 4-year, $62 million contract.
  • Potential friction was discussed between Dolphins' coach Mike McDaniel and GM Chris Greer.
  • Analysis of the Miami-FSU game included a surprising FSU advisory source.

Deep Dive

  • Discussion focused on Ruben Bain for Heisman, questioning eligibility for defensive players.
  • 2024 Heisman odds mentioned Dante Moore, Ty Simpson, Jeremiah Smith, and Carson Beck.
  • Travis Hunter was identified as a defensive player who previously won the Heisman.

  • Discussion centered on which sports figures, such as Lou Holtz or Miles Garrett, might wear a gown and nightcap.
  • Russell Wilson and Shannon Sharpe were also mentioned in relation to sleepwear.
  • C.K. Metcalf was dismissed as a nightcap wearer, while Miles Garrett was considered due to his 'old soul'.

  • Erik Spoelstra appeared on 'Zaslow Show 2.0' despite two-and-a-half years of attempts by the host to get him on 'South Beach Sessions'.
  • Audio clips from the Spoelstra/Zaslow interaction were played, including well wishes for Zaslow's career.
  • Spoelstra's past as a 'video nerd' and current role as head coach were highlighted.

  • The Miami Heat signed Nikola Jovic to a four-year, $62 million contract.
  • Skepticism arose regarding the $15 million annual salary for a player with limited performance.
  • The Heat reportedly would not trade Jovic even for Kevin Durant, trusting his potential and character.

  • Coach Erik Spoelstra's comments suggested a potential 5-6 month recovery for Barkov's ACL and MCL tear.
  • Paul Maurice stated there is no 'next man up' for Barkov, emphasizing his irreplaceable role.
  • Barkov is described as the 'leader and heart of the locker room' for the Florida Panthers.

  • Matthew Tkachuk was identified as a player who could step up in Barkov's absence.
  • Discussion focused on who would receive an 'A' (alternate captain) designation, with Sam Bennett and Sam Reinhart mentioned.
  • The team's depth at center was highlighted, with Lundell and another player being out.

  • Discussion centered on potential friction between Coach Mike McDaniel and GM Chris Greer, drawing parallels to Brian Flores' tenure.
  • Both McDaniel and Greer were argued to have underperformed, questioning Greer's position after 10 years without a playoff win.
  • Tua Tagovailoa's dependency on Tyreek Hill was predicted to lead to a Dolphins loss against the Carolina Panthers.

  • A prediction was made that the Miami Hurricanes would trail for the first time this season against Florida State.
  • Former Miami quarterback Cameron Ward was revealed to be giving advice to current FSU quarterback Jordan Travis, which was viewed as problematic.
  • Miami's defense was praised for its discipline in avoiding penalties and preventing big plays.
