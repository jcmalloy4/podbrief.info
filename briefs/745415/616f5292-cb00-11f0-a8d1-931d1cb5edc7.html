The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

FA! Black Friday Football Deal! Celebrity Chef Capon Ranks All the Meats, Sam Darnold Revenge Game Against The Vikings, And Jeremy Taché Vets Dave's Best Position Group by Franchise in History List

Key Takeaways

  • Dave Dameshek's 100-entry list of the Best Position Group by Franchise in History sparked notable debate.
  • Week 13 NFL matchups were analyzed, with hosts providing predictions for five key games.
  • Celebrity Chef Josh Capon discussed Thanksgiving leftover innovations and ranked various meats.
  • The episode explored personal Thanksgiving traditions and identified signature foods for several NFL cities.

Deep Dive

  • The discussion honored players associated with jersey number 29, including Eric Dickerson and his reasons for choosing the number.
  • Guest Jeremy Taché added notable athletes such as baseball player Rod Carew and NHL players Nathan McKinnon and Leon Draisaitl.
  • NBA player Paul Silas was also mentioned among the athletes who wore number 29.

  • Dave Dameshek's 100-entry list, 'Best Position Group by Franchise in History,' was introduced, with a focus on the top 10 or 25 entries.
  • The Green Bay Packers' quarterback position, featuring Bart Starr, Brett Favre, and Aaron Rodgers, was highlighted as the first NFL group.
  • The Dallas Cowboys' wide receivers, including Michael Irvin, Drew Pearson, and Terrell Owens, were also discussed as an iconic group.

  • Jeremy Tache vetted Dave Dameshek's list, considering the Dodgers' starting pitchers as a strong contender from Sandy Koufax to Clayton Kershaw.
  • A debate arose over the Pittsburgh Penguins' centers ranking at number two, with Tache citing potential 'homer bias.'
  • Dave Dameshek defended the Penguins' ranking by highlighting Mario Lemieux, Sidney Crosby, Evgeni Malkin, and Ron Francis.

  • The Lakers' centers were discussed as the best positional group in basketball history, citing a lineage of talent.
  • Shaquille O'Neal, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, and Anthony Davis were specifically mentioned among the Lakers' dominant centers.
  • The conversation also briefly touched upon the Cardinals' first basemen, including Mark McGwire, Albert Pujols, and Paul Goldschmidt.

  • The hosts discussed the top five most consequential NFL games of Week 13, analyzing betting trends for favorites and underdogs.
  • Jeremy Tache predicted a Rams win over the Panthers, but questioned if they would cover the spread.
  • Predictions included a 49ers win over the Browns by a score of 23-20, and a Seahawks victory against the Vikings.
  • The Bills were predicted to win against the Steelers, and differing opinions were noted for the Texans versus Colts game.

  • Chef Josh Capon joined the show to discuss Thanksgiving leftovers, specifically his preference for making sausage cornbread waffles.
  • Capon suggested combining turkey and cranberry sauce with the waffles.
  • Producer Mike Fuentes proposed carnitas as a preferred meat over turkey, which Capon countered by advocating for both meats.

  • Chef Capon discussed his preference for duck and pork over venison, specifically highlighting the preparation of duck skin.
  • His signature Duroc pork chop was mentioned, noting pork as a more economical choice than beef.
  • Turkey was ranked fifth among various meats, leading to a culinary debate on the best cooking method for a whole turkey versus deconstructed parts.

  • The discussion transitioned to identifying the best signature foods for NFL towns.
  • New York pizza was proposed and agreed upon as a top contender, with deli sandwiches also considered for the Jets.
  • Philadelphia cheesesteaks were deemed the city's signature food, but debate arose regarding the use of cheese whiz and bread quality.
  • One speaker controversially called the cheesesteak a 'bullshit sandwich,' preferring provolone cheese.
