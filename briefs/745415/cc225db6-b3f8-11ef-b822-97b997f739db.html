The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Billy Breaks

Key Takeaways

  • Tony's 'Tony's Top 5' field goal challenge resulted in a hip injury, prompting debate among hosts.
  • NFL discussions covered the Chiefs' offense, Seahawks' playoff potential, and Ravens' AFC North prediction.
  • Jeremy's planned live stream was canceled due to a new friendship opportunity and Yankees-Red Sox game attendance.
  • MLB wild card games were analyzed in the 'Big Game or Not a Big Game' segment, highlighting key matchups.
  • Clippers Media Day featured discussions on Kawhi Leonard's endorsement deal allegations and a past financial dispute.
  • Fuentes attempted a field goal following Tony's injury but did not convert.
  • Podcast segments included 'Tony's Top 5,' 'Big Game or Not a Big Game,' and reactions to Clippers Media Day.

Deep Dive

  • Tony prepared for his 'Tony's Top 5' field goal segment from Dural Legacy Park, aiming to attempt five field goals.
  • One ball was already lost over a net before attempts began.
  • The segment was sponsored by Smirnoff.

  • Tony attempted field goals from 40 yards, having previously kicked a 35-yard attempt over the net.
  • A bet was placed on Tony to make at least two of his four remaining field goals.
  • One kick went wide, and another fell short during a discussion about field goal difficulty and technique.
  • Coaching advice was given on technique for a kick described as being too far left, with a yardage estimate provided.

  • The Kansas City Chiefs' offense was critiqued as a 'mirage,' with Rashi Rice's return noted as a potential improvement.
  • The Seattle Seahawks were identified as a 'sneaky playoff team' due to their strong defense, with Sam Darnold's performance highlighted.
  • A bold prediction was made that the Baltimore Ravens would win the AFC North division despite perceived weakness.
  • The Steelers were mentioned as a contender in the AFC North.

  • Tony experienced a hip injury during a field goal attempt, rendering him unable to complete his kicks.
  • His struggles were attributed to a 'dad body' due to lack of rest, sleep, and proper nutrition.
  • The legitimacy of Tony's injury was debated, with some suggesting his pride was more affected than his hip.
  • Consensus leaned towards the injury being genuine, exacerbated by pride and pressure.
  • Tony confirmed feeling a 'click' during his final kick attempt, forcing him to pull up.

  • Following Tony's injury, the hosts encouraged someone, possibly Fuentes, to complete the five promised field goal attempts.
  • Fuentes attempted a kick, but missed, drawing humorous and critical commentary.
  • Jeremy apologized for a canceled live stream, explaining Jane Levy's absence due to Yankees-Red Sox tickets.
  • Jeremy discussed his new friendship with Jane Levy and plans to attend a Yankees-Red Sox game in New York.

  • Zaslow delivered an MLB playoff edition of 'Big Game or Not a Big Game,' focusing on wild card matchups.
  • The Detroit Tigers vs. Cleveland Guardians game featured players like Dylan Dingler and 'Big Christmas' Jonkenzie Noel.
  • The Padres vs. Cubs game was deemed a 'big game,' with Tatis leading off for the Padres and an ABC broadcast.
  • The Red Sox vs. Yankees matchup was highlighted as a 'big game' due to its historical rivalry and Yankee Stadium setting.
  • The Reds vs. Dodgers game concluded the segment, noting Cincinnati's entry into the postseason due to the Marlins beating the Mets.

  • Ramona Shelburne questioned Kawhi Leonard at Clippers Media Day regarding an endorsement deal and allegations of not performing services.
  • Leonard stated he understood the contract, performed services, and characterized the company's bankruptcy as fraud.
  • The conversation touched on a past financial dispute, with an alleged $7 million owed and skepticism about Leonard's performance claims.
  • Concerns were raised about Kawhi Leonard's public speaking ability and suitability as an endorser, particularly regarding sensitive accusations.
  • Clippers president Lawrence Frank affirmed that Kawhi, his uncle Dennis Robertson, and Mitch Frankl understand NBA rules on extra benefits and salary cap.
