The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 1: Dan's Favorite Rohden (feat. Lucy Rohden & Lucy's Dad)

Key Takeaways

  • Iowa's electric game day includes a decade-long charity initiative for its Children's Hospital.
  • A podcast personality's football throw nearly hit a baby, raising questions about judgment.
  • College football coaching market is volatile, impacting decisions at Penn State, Florida, and Notre Dame.
  • Wisconsin's Luke Fickle faces scrutiny for a failed offensive identity shift causing team struggles.
  • Miami's ACC championship hopes are complicated by inconsistent quarterback play and tough scheduling.
  • Vanderbilt achieved a significant upset over a top-10 SEC team, challenging conference rankings.
  • Baseball playoff discussions highlight the Blue Jays' bullpen strength and George Springer's clutch hitting.

Deep Dive

  • Kinnick Stadium's game day experience is described as 'electric' and 'heavenly', featuring extensive tailgating and drinking.
  • Game tickets were acquired using the Game Time app, with a mentioned discount code.
  • The 'Hawks to Go' tailgate in Iowa City has raised over $400,000 for the University of Iowa Children's Hospital through shot sales over a decade.

  • Footage shows Rose attempting to throw a football, an unusual event due to prior concerns about her judgment.
  • Despite multiple warnings, Rose nearly hit a baby in a stroller during the throwing attempt.
  • Her judgment in the incident was publicly questioned, and no apology was noted afterward.
  • Rose reportedly requested to throw the football with 12-year-old boys, who were overwhelmed and agreed.

  • Billy Napier's firing and James Franklin's situation are speculated to influence top coaching talent competition.
  • The job market for Penn State versus Florida is debated based on resources, fan bases, and perceived job security.
  • Marcus Freeman's potential move from Notre Dame to Penn State is discussed, aligning with his coaching style.
  • Matt Ruhl is mentioned as a potential hiring candidate, despite his team losing significantly to Minnesota.

  • Luke Fickle's retention at Wisconsin is questioned following recent losses and a deviation from the team's traditional run-heavy identity.
  • The attempt to implement an air raid offense with an incompatible offensive coordinator led to a loss of talent suited for the original identity.
  • This identity shift negatively impacted both the offense and defense, alienating fans and affecting scoring abilities.

  • Miami's loss to Louisville is attributed to quarterback Carson Beck's inconsistent performance, including four interceptions.
  • Despite a talented roster, Miami's path to the ACC championship is complicated, requiring other teams to lose.
  • Coaching decisions and an inability to effectively run the ball contributed to key losses.
  • A one-loss Power Four team, such as a hypothetical one-loss Miami, is generally expected to make the College Football Playoff.

  • The atmosphere of Baton Rouge at night for an LSU versus Texas A&M game is anticipated, with speculation of a 'Brian Kelly gets fired game'.
  • The SEC's ranking system is critiqued, questioning Tennessee's high standing without a significant win.
  • Vanderbilt's notable victory over a top-10 team is highlighted, alongside its avoidance of an SEC fine by delaying field entry against LSU.

  • The Toronto Blue Jays are discussed as a formidable opponent against the Dodgers due to their bullpen strength.
  • George Springer is highlighted for his postseason home run record and clutch performance after a recent knee injury.
  • Discussions included a bet where Greg Cody would walk 10 miles from Bellevue, Washington, if the Mariners lost in the playoffs.
