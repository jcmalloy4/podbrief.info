Postgame Show: Top 5 Voices In Sports JuJu Can Think Of (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Juju provided his DraftKings "Thursday Thunder" picks, including bets on Bijan Robinson and Ameka Ebuka.
  • Juju unveiled his "Top 5" list of sports media voices, with John Madden ranked as number one.
  • The NBA Cup "MVP of the Day" honored Spurs players Stefan Castle and Victor Wembanyama.
  • Notre Dame's preferential playoff access agreement is causing controversy and scheduling threats from other athletic directors.

Deep Dive

  • The host introduced the "Thursday Thunder" segment, sponsored by DraftKings, where Juju shares his game picks.
  • Juju's betting selections included specific wagers on Bijan Robinson's rush attempts and Ameka Ebuka's receptions.

  • Juju introduced his "Top 5" list of sports media voices, explaining his preference for lists when other topics are already covered.
  • His list featured Rebecca Lobo at #5, Dickie V at #4, Ian and Noah Eagle at #3, Kevin Harlan at #2, and John Madden at #1.
  • Other notable voices considered for the list included Spiro D, Gus Johnson, Marv Albert, and Tony Romo.

  • The host announced an "MVP of the Day" segment in celebration of the NBA Cup, following the Lakers' recent loss.
  • Stefan Castle achieved 30 points and 10 rebounds in a game, leading the Spurs to the NBA Cup semifinals.
  • Castle was recognized alongside Victor Wembanyama as one of two Spurs players on Zaslow's MVP of the Day list.

  • Notre Dame's new preferential playoff access agreement is reportedly leading to threats from other athletic directors.
  • These directors are considering excluding Notre Dame from future schedules due to concerns over fairness.
  • The agreement has also raised issues regarding scheduling flexibility for other college athletic programs.
