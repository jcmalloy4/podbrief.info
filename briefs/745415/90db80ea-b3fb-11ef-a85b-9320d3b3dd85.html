The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: Dan Says "The Chiefs Are Dead"

Key Takeaways

  • Greg Cody's traffic woes and controversial competition exit sparked early show discussion.
  • Jeff Conine finally responded to Chris Cote's text messages regarding his handshake and show experience.
  • The evolution of NFL kicking, including accuracy and player value, was a prominent discussion point.
  • Baker Mayfield is identified as an emerging top-five quarterback in crucial late-game situations.
  • Tony presented his Top 5 QBs trusted most at the end of games, leading to a debate on Patrick Mahomes.
  • The Kansas City Chiefs' offense is scrutinized for perceived struggles attributed to injuries and lack of weapons.
  • The Miami Dolphins' 0-2 start was critically assessed as the league's 'worst' despite team talent.
  • Cam Newton's NFL career was reevaluated, drawing an unusual comparison to MVP kicker Mark Moseley.
  • Mike McDaniel's coaching faced criticism following the Dolphins' disappointing early season performance.

Deep Dive

  • Greg Cody's arrival was delayed by downtown Miami traffic, sparking a debate about his punctuality for the 8:55 a.m. show start.
  • Cody claimed unawareness of the consistent 8:55 a.m. start time for over a year, drawing laughter from Zaslow.
  • He announced his withdrawal from future competitions, citing a perceived 'rigged' outcome, specifically a defied plan to let him win on his birthday.
  • Producer Billy denied rigging the competition, leading to a humorous exchange.
  • Cody questioned the need to leave his house earlier, stating he already departs between 7 and 7:15 a.m.

  • Geno Smith's statistically poor performance in a recent game included a low completion rate on deep passes against a strong Chargers defense.
  • The Chargers' victory was primarily attributed to their defense rather than quarterback play, despite the position's importance.
  • Jim Harbaugh's successful coaching career at Michigan and with the 49ers was highlighted during the discussion.

  • Baker Mayfield is emerging as a top-five quarterback in crucial late-game situations, drawing comparisons to Tua Tagovailoa's performance.
  • His performance in the Buccaneers' recent game against the Texans saw scoring only in the first four drives and the final five minutes.
  • Tony prepared to present his Top 5 QBs he trusts most at the end of games, referencing statistics since 2023, particularly regarding Patrick Mahomes.

  • The conversation noted a high number of quarterback injuries early in the season, with five starters reportedly out after two games.
  • Matthew Stafford was listed at number five in a quarterback ranking, followed by Baker Mayfield at number four.
  • A brief debate occurred regarding whether Baker Mayfield was ranked above Tom Brady and Patrick Mahomes.

  • One speaker asserted the Kansas City Chiefs' offense is 'dead' and no longer entertaining, blaming current defensive schemes.
  • Others countered that Patrick Mahomes is not at fault, citing injuries to Travis Kelce and a lack of offensive weapons, including Tyreek Hill's absence.
  • A disagreement arose regarding claims that the team has been 'dead' for three years, despite recent Super Bowl appearances.

  • Patrick Mahomes' ongoing elite status was discussed despite his team's struggles due to offensive line injuries and a lack of elite receivers.
  • Joe Burrow was ranked second on a list of top quarterbacks trusted in the final two minutes of a game, with Mahomes named number one.
  • The Dolphins' 0-2 start was critically analyzed, with one speaker arguing they are the 'worst 0-2' in the league despite having significant talent.

  • A debate focused on Cam Newton's career, with one participant deeming it disappointing despite his talent and strong start, including an MVP and Super Bowl appearance.
  • Others argued his achievements, such as leading a 15-1 team to the Super Bowl, signify a successful career.
  • The conversation included a comparison to Mark Moseley, an MVP kicker, leading to a request for a side-by-side statistical comparison of Newton and Moseley.

  • Mike McDaniel's coaching was criticized, with a speaker stating the Dolphins are a 'dumpster fire' and that McDaniel is responsible for recent losses.
  • The discussion also included a brief segment on a kicker's ability to make a 64-yard field goal.
  • A suggestion was made to narrow the goalposts to improve NFL watchability.
