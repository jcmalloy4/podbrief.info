The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: JuJu Finds Out (feat. JuJu Gotti)

Key Takeaways

  • Reporters' questioning techniques, including repetition and length, were critically discussed.
  • The college basketball season began with notable team performances and a controversial injury handling incident.
  • NFL trade speculation centered on Kyler Murray's future and a list of potential trade destinations.
  • An audience poll revealed a significant preference (78%) for Jacoby Brissett over Kyler Murray as quarterback.

Deep Dive

  • The hosts discussed 'worst questions' reporters ask athletes, often being overly long or repetitive.
  • Guest Juju shared an example of asking about Doritos flavors and expressed frustration with repetitive questions posed at different podiums.

  • The college basketball season began, featuring wins for the University of Miami.
  • Arizona demonstrated a strong performance against Florida, and Loyola College achieved a buzzer-beater win after Sister Gene's passing.
  • A concerning incident involved a player reportedly dragged off the court by a trainer at 4:19, raising questions about injury handling.

  • Discussion initiated with Kyler Murray and Jacoby Brissett, with one speaker suggesting Brissett should finish the season as Cardinals quarterback.
  • A list of top five trade destinations for Murray included the Cleveland Browns, New Orleans Saints, New York Jets, Las Vegas Raiders, and Miami Dolphins.

  • Breaking news announced the Jacksonville Jaguars traded for former Raiders wide receiver Jacoby Myers, exchanging a fourth and sixth-round pick.
  • A show poll comparing Jacoby Brissett and Kyler Murray revealed 78% of the audience voted for Brissett.
