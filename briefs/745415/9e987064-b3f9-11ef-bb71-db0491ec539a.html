The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 1: You Know What You Did, Mike!

Key Takeaways

  • Lamar Jackson's injury status and its impact on betting lines for the Dolphins-Ravens game were debated.
  • A controversial heavyweight boxing eye poke and unusual Steelers throwback uniforms sparked discussion.
  • The physical toll on quarterbacks like Cam Newton was contrasted with the durability of players like Justin Herbert.
  • Myles Garrett's career choices with the Cleveland Browns, including trade desires and contract extension, were heavily discussed.
  • Joe Flacco's mid-game departure for a restroom break during a critical play generated humorous speculation.

Deep Dive

  • Uncertainty surrounds Lamar Jackson's playing status for the upcoming Thursday night game against the Dolphins.
  • His injury, referred to as a 'hammy,' impacts betting lines for the game.
  • Discussion included whether the Ravens would remain favored with a backup quarterback due to their defensive performance.

  • Heavyweight boxer Tom Aspinall was awarded a title after Cyril Gane delivered an eye poke, which replays showed as a full-hand strike.
  • The 'no contest' decision in the Aspinall-Gane fight disappointed the commentators.
  • The Pittsburgh Steelers' mustard-yellow throwback uniforms were criticized as a merchandising gimmick and a departure from classic style.

  • During a Giants-Eagles game, a New York Giants player accidentally punched a teammate in the helmet while attempting to punch the ball out of an Eagles ball carrier's hands, causing a concussion.
  • The legality and frequency of such ball-punching plays were discussed by commentators.
  • A humorous incident involved Jalen Brown's hair smudging onto an opponent's jersey, creating a 'potential scandal'.

  • Justin Fields' performance was analyzed, noting a poor jump pass and his record in games where opponents score 21 points.
  • The New York Jets' win against the Bengals was discussed, along with owner Woody Johnson's public criticism of the team's quarterback.
  • Fields' post-game comments addressed how he handles outside noise.

  • Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs are perceived to have a playoff advantage over other quarterbacks like Herbert, Nix, and Daniel Jones.
  • The Colts' strong offensive performance, led by Daniel Jones and Jonathan Taylor, was highlighted as a potential challenge.
  • Drake Maye's performance, including deep ball accuracy and rushing ability, was compared to other top quarterbacks.

  • Drake Maye was credited with making big plays and elevating the Buffalo Bills offense.
  • Comparisons were drawn between Maye's performance and Josh Allen's past play, as well as the Patriots' offensive struggles.
  • The Bills' receiving corps, including Kayshawn Booty and Mac Hollins, was discussed, with a potential need for an 'alpha number one wide receiver' mentioned.

  • Debate centered on whether Myles Garrett 'crossed the line' by expressing a desire to be traded from the Cleveland Browns due to their lack of winning.
  • Commentators questioned Garrett's decision to accept a lucrative contract extension, dubbed a 'Godfather offer,' after previous trade demands.
  • Garrett's sideline behavior, including throwing his helmet after a recent game, was contrasted with past statements about wanting to leave Cleveland.

  • Joe Flacco departed the field during a critical moment in the Jets-Bengals game, occurring after the Jets scored 15 points to trail 39-38 with under two minutes remaining.
  • Hosts speculated Flacco needed to use the restroom, humorously referring to the sensation as 'dancing swords.'
  • An anecdote about former NFL player Larry Izzo receiving a game ball for defecating in a bucket on the sideline was recounted, contrasting with Flacco's apparent need for a private restroom.
