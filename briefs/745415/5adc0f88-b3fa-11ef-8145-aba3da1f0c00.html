The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: The 2019 NBA Draft Class (feat. Peanut Pete)

Key Takeaways

  • Ja Morant is reportedly facing a trade from the Memphis Grizzlies just six games into the season.
  • Morant's recent struggles include career lows in performance and a suspension due to conflict with his new coach.
  • The Miami Heat is a potential trade destination for Ja Morant, with debate over team fit and risk.
  • A hypothetical redraft of the 2019 NBA class reignited discussions about Morant and Zion Williamson.
  • NBA organizations granting rookie stars excessive concessions may lead to entitlement issues.

Deep Dive

  • Ja Morant of the Memphis Grizzlies is subject to trade speculation just six games into the season, despite the general manager's prior statements.
  • Morant's post-game interview following a loss featured terse responses, deflecting questions about his performance and issues with the coaching staff.
  • Hosts suggested Morant's responses were an attempt to sanitize remarks made directly to the coaching staff, following conflict with new head coach Tomas Isalo.

  • Discussion focused on Ja Morant's accountability and past actions, alongside speculation about his career trajectory.
  • Consideration was given to whether the Miami Heat would pursue a trade for Morant, factoring in team culture and potential fit.

  • Discussion questioned the lack of adult leadership and veteran presence in the Memphis Grizzlies' locker room.
  • This deficiency may have contributed to Ja Morant's off-court issues and pattern of behavior, including prior incidents.

  • NBA organizations often create entitlement issues by giving rookie stars too many concessions, with a trade sometimes being the only reset.
  • Ja Morant objectively improves his team's performance on the court, but off-court actions have led to personal and team disappointments.
  • Speculation suggests the Miami Heat's organizational culture could provide discipline for Morant, despite potential conflicts with their motion-based offense.

  • The 'Nuttiest Fans' segment, sponsored by Hampton Farms, presented nominees from a Colorado Boulders game.
  • Nominees included a fan with 'Boulders' shaved into his belly and another shirtless fan identified as potentially Andy Reid.
  • A third nominee from college basketball was introduced, following a player's missed dunk and feigned injury.

  • The 2019 NBA draft class was reviewed, including top picks Zion Williamson, Ja Morant, and RJ Barrett.
  • Commentary covered player performance, potential, and career management, specifically addressing Zion Williamson's health and habits.
  • The discussion also evaluated players such as Jackson Hayes, Rui Hachimura, and Cam Reddish.

  • A hypothetical redraft of the 2019 class debated Ja Morant and Zion Williamson for the number one pick, considering their talent and off-court issues.
  • Darius Garland and Tyler Herro were discussed as potential picks 3 and 4 in a redraft, with a preference voiced for Garland.
  • The possibility of the Miami Heat trading Tyler Herro for Ja Morant was explored, analyzing whether the Grizzlies would accept without draft picks and the Heat's commitment to Morant's $42-44 million contract.

  • The Miami Heat's trade flexibility is questioned, with discussion comparing potential acquisitions to a 'mystery box' scenario rather than pursuing immediate opportunities.
  • Rookie Jaime Jaquez Jr. delivered an impressive performance with 31 points and 8 rebounds off the bench.
  • LeBron James's 'sciatica' holdout plan is asserted to have backfired, as the Lakers achieved a 5-2 record in his absence.
