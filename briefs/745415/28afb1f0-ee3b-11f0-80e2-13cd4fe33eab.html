Alley Oop 150: Trae Young on the Move?! Peyton Watson Buzz, Deni in Portland & MJ Hate Talk

Key Takeaways

  • Deni Avdija's versatile play and scoring ability garnered attention, with hosts debating his All-Star potential.
  • Trae Young trade speculation to Washington intensified, impacting Atlanta's hip-hop culture and fan base.
  • Washington Wizards' appeal, potential rebranding, and reluctance to attract star players were discussed.
  • Payton Watson's strong performance for the Denver Nuggets is expected to lead to a significant contract.
  • Isaiah Thomas and Michael Jordan's long-standing rivalry was revisited, with hosts defending Jordan's legacy.
  • A viral video of a kangaroo fighting a man in Australia provided a lighthearted moment.

Deep Dive

  • One host expressed optimism about Deni Avdija's potential as a top-10 player in the league.
  • Avdija is described as 6'9" and 215 lbs, possessing guard-like skills and ability to drive to the lane.
  • He was compared to players like Jayson Tatum, LeBron James, and Luka Dončić.
  • Hosts debated whether Avdija's league-leading free-throw attempts and 27 points per game scoring average were due to genuine contact or flopping.

  • Speculation arose regarding a potential trade involving Trae Young to Washington.
  • The unusual timing of trade rumors, with a reporter tweeting about a player's preference, suggested an imminent deal.
  • Hosts reflected on the visual of Trae Young leaving an arena, possibly for the last time.
  • A mid-game trade announcement seemed to genuinely upset fans, highlighting Young's cultural impact in Atlanta's hip-hop scene.

  • Discussion centered on whether acquiring Trae Young would improve the Washington Wizards' standing.
  • Hosts questioned if Washington may be intentionally losing games and why players are reluctant to join the team.
  • Proposals were made to rename the Wizards to 'Chocolate City,' 'DMV Stars,' or 'DMV Go-Go's' to better reflect D.C.'s local culture and improve marketability.

  • Payton Watson was noted for his strong performance for the Denver Nuggets, particularly in Nikola Jokic's absence.
  • Hosts questioned why Watson had not received contract recognition comparable to Christian Brown.
  • It was suggested that Watson is earning a significant contract for next season based on his recent play.

  • The conversation shifted to a viral video from Australia depicting a kangaroo fighting a man.
  • Hosts humorously described the kangaroo's aggressive tactics and the man's defense.
  • Physical attributes of kangaroos, including their height and weight, were discussed and compared to humans.

  • The discussion covered the ongoing 'beef' between Isaiah Thomas and Michael Jordan, with one host suggesting Thomas will never move past their interactions.
  • A clip argued that Michael Jordan's era was not the 'golden era,' noting LeBron James holds numerous records.
  • Hosts defended LeBron James's record-breaking career as sustained greatness over 20 years, acknowledging potential 'compiler' greatness.
  • Arguments were made against reducing Jordan's legacy to just shoes, emphasizing his defensive prowess, two-way play, multiple retirements, comebacks, and dynastic runs.
