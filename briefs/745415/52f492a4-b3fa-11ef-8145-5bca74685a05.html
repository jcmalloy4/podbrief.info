The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: Taller Van Dyke

Key Takeaways

  • NFL games featured critical turnovers, including a dropped ball after a 75-yard catch, and controversial overturned plays.
  • College football saw aggressive fan behavior, leading to field storming incidents and ACC fines for violations.
  • Coaching records, such as James Franklin's 4-21 against top 10 teams, faced scrutiny for performance in high-stakes games.
  • Major program quarterback decisions, specifically Georgia's choice of Gunner Stockton, were critically analyzed for championship implications.

Deep Dive

  • Baker Mayfield demonstrated a high number of "turnover worthy plays" this season, but divisional weakness mitigated issues.
  • Colts receiver Adonai Mitchell made a 75-yard extraordinary catch using his elbow but dropped the ball near the goal line, resulting in a turnover.
  • The incident highlights the minimal margin for error in football.

  • Brandon Cooks' controversial catch against the Bills was initially ruled a touchdown but later overturned.
  • Cooks displayed visible panic and attempted to adjust his cup upon realizing he lost possession before scoring.
  • The play's reversal significantly impacted the game's outcome.

  • Colts receiver Adonai Mitchell incurred a holding penalty during a long run by Jonathan Taylor.
  • The penalty nullified what would have been a touchdown run by Taylor.
  • This incident, combined with Taylor's subsequent 50-plus yard touchdown, highlighted critical moments affecting the game's outcome.

  • The "Hampton Farms nuttiest fan" segment featured a shirtless Virginia fan who climbed a field goal upright during a game.
  • Other nominees included an Oregon fan at a Penn State game and Virginia fans storming the field after an upset win.
  • Virginia's field rush was described as aggressive and fast, compared to scenes from "World War Z."

  • The hosts expressed confusion regarding new NFL kickoff rules and the "drop zone" concept.
  • They questioned the practical application of these rules, seeking clarity on their implementation.
  • A recent 65-yard field goal went largely unremarked upon, contrasting with historical expectations for such long kicks.

  • Discussions included outcomes of USC vs. Illinois, Georgia Tech vs. Wake Forest, and Ole Miss vs. LSU, with notes on betting implications.
  • A close game highlighted player Trinidad Shambliss and a late-game mistake by Trevor Lawrence.
  • Mississippi had a yardage advantage over LSU despite penalties and turnovers, while LSU struggled to run the ball.

  • James Franklin holds a 4-21 record against top 10 teams and 1-18 in the Big Ten against top 10 teams since 1937.
  • He is noted as the third-worst coach against top 10 teams since 1937 among those discussed.
  • Coaches like Tom Allen (1-14), Mike Loxley (0-7), and Matt Rhule (0-11) also have poor records against top 10 opponents.

  • Analysis suggested Georgia made a poor decision in choosing Gunner Stockton over Carson Beck as quarterback.
  • This decision potentially wasted a championship-caliber season for Georgia.
  • Stockton was compared unfavorably to numerous other SEC quarterbacks across 9 different teams.
