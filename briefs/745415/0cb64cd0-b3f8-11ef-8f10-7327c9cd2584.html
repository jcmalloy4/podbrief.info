The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Postgame Show: The Mountain Dew Baja Blast™ Pie

Key Takeaways

  • Taco Bell's new Mountain Dew Baja Blast Pie sparked mixed opinions on its artificiality and flavor.
  • Hosts discussed early holiday celebrations, with one noting a Friendsgiving on November 12th.
  • Personal dessert preferences were debated, including strong opinions on pie versus ice cream cake.
  • Dietary choices extended to fast food avoidance, specific cuisine preferences, and fast-food exceptions.
  • Logistical and social aspects of using Uber Eats for multiple orders were discussed.

Deep Dive

  • Participants discussed the new Mountain Dew Baja Blast Pie, specifically its flavor, texture, and artificiality.
  • The pie was compared to key lime pie and candy during the conversation.

  • One participant noted celebrating Friendsgiving on November 12th, initiating a discussion on early holiday events.
  • Thanksgiving was identified by one individual as their favorite holiday.

  • One participant expressed a strong preference for ice cream cake over pie, specifically disliking key lime pie.
  • Another participant claimed to avoid soda and fast food, favoring Thai and Italian cuisine, particularly Sicilian pizza.
  • Despite avoiding fast food, Wendy's was identified as a preferred option for its fries and spicy chicken.

  • The hosts discussed ordering food from multiple restaurants via Uber Eats, noting instances of two drivers arriving simultaneously.
  • One host mentioned feeling perceived as an alcoholic when ordering alcohol through the delivery app.
