The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: The Tiers of Tears (feat. Dianna Russini)

Key Takeaways

  • Dianna Russini projected the Miami Dolphins as Super Bowl contenders.
  • Buffalo Bills' offensive issues, including run game and failed trades, were analyzed.
  • Creative, yet controversial, parenting techniques involving threats were discussed.
  • NFL quarterback futures for Burrow and Murray, plus draft prospects, were updated.
  • "Mad Dog" criticized the government shutdown's impact, particularly on air travel.
  • The show explored the nuances and various types of emotional crying.

Deep Dive

  • Dianna Russini stated her belief that the Miami Dolphins can win the Super Bowl.
  • The team's schedule includes challenging opponents such as the Commanders, Saints, Steelers, Bengals, and Buccaneers.
  • The initial part of their schedule was noted for its ease.

  • Discussion began on public reaction to Brian Daboll's firing despite recent struggles, attributed to New York media and Daniel Jones' development.
  • Participants debated if the Giants need an offensive mind or a 'leader of men,' citing candidates like Anthony Weaver, Lou Anarumo, and Mike Tomlin.

  • Parenting techniques discussed included using threats of Santa Claus or police to discipline young children.
  • Dianna Russini shared her experiences using threats involving deer, dinosaurs, and isolation as consequences.
  • The hosts debated the effectiveness and potential downsides of these methods, with Dan Le Batard expressing horror.

  • The New England Patriots are perceived as "very real" and ahead of schedule, despite their remote location.
  • The Philadelphia Eagles hold the NFC's top spot, but their offense draws complaints, with limited targets for A.J. Brown.
  • The Green Bay Packers allowed historically low points in losses since 1940, contextualizing the Eagles' recent win.
  • Lane Johnson is considered the Eagles' best player and among the league's top right tackles.
  • Dianna Russini confirmed the Patriots inquired about A.J. Brown's availability during the offseason.

  • Dianna Russini reported Joe Burrow is unlikely to return in December.
  • Russini also expressed belief that Kyler Murray's tenure with the Arizona Cardinals is nearing its end.
  • Top draft quarterbacks discussed included Drake May, Caleb Williams, and Jaden Daniels, with Caleb Williams highlighted for his "clutch gene."

  • The hosts engaged in a humorous discussion to rank various emotional expressions related to crying.
  • Expressions included 'frog in the throat,' 'sniffling,' 'whimpering,' 'sobbing,' and 'hysterical crying.'
  • The debate also covered 'shower cry' and 'car cry,' with 'shower cry' favored for its privacy and cathartic release.

  • A segment featured "Mad Dog" complaining about the government shutdown and strongly criticizing elected officials.
  • Concerns were raised about the shutdown's impact on air travel and the danger of flying without air traffic controllers.
  • A specific instance highlighted a 75-year-old man stranded at Miami International Airport for three days.
  • The speaker expressed extreme dissatisfaction, contemplating moving out of the country due to the government's failures.
