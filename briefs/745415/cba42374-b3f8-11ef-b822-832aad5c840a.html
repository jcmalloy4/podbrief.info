The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: The Man Who Blamed His Dog (feat. Chauncey Swallows & Gout Gout)

Key Takeaways

  • Terence Crawford's boxing prowess sparks debate against Canelo Alvarez's assessment.
  • Greg Cote is considering retirement from writing and promoting an upcoming fan event.
  • NFL coaching accountability, specifically Mike McDaniel's, is under scrutiny amid team struggles.
  • Malik Neighbors' exceptional receiving ability is highlighted despite his team's quarterback limitations.
  • A track runner named Gout Gout is drawing comparisons to Usain Bolt ahead of Tokyo championships.
  • Greg Cote publicly announced he has stopped farting, describing himself as a "recovered fartaholic".

Deep Dive

  • Canelo Alvarez acknowledged Terence Crawford's challenging style, noting its difficulty for him.
  • Crawford asserts himself as the best pound-for-pound fighter, a claim debated by the hosts.
  • The discussion highlighted Crawford's perceived relative anonymity despite his elite status.

  • Greg Cote is considering retirement from writing, with humorous discussion about a 'Greg Cody Show without Greg Cody'.
  • Cote will host an event at Sports and Social in Dolphin Mall on Tuesday from 6 to 8 PM, featuring Omar Kelly, Isaiah Smalls, and Jordan McPherson.
  • Rumors emerged about a potential new logo for the Greg Cody Show, seemingly contradicting talks of his retirement.
  • A recap of major news included Cote's potential writing retirement, podcast succession planning, new logo rumors, and the Dolphin Mall appearance.

  • Tom Brady's upcoming Fox announcement and 10-part Apple TV series are compared to Michael Jordan's legacy.
  • Discussion focused on player perception, specifically if an unnamed athlete, 'Mike,' is perceived as boring despite his success.
  • The conversation touched on the 'mythical' status of athletes due to limited public access and the influence of branding like Nike.

  • Wide receiver Malik Neighbors demonstrated exceptional performance despite his team's quarterback play and offensive struggles.
  • Neighbors remained productive even when double-covered, accumulating high catch numbers through 20 games.
  • Russell Wilson completed 7 of 9 deep pass attempts for 264 yards, with Neighbors identified as a key factor in this performance.

  • Mike McDaniel took full accountability for communication and substitution errors during a crucial game, stating 'everything falling on me'.
  • McDaniel's statement was analyzed and compared to Brian Kelly's perceived attitude under pressure.
  • Several NFL teams, including the Dolphins, Panthers, Saints, Bengals, Chiefs, Browns, and Jets, are experiencing difficult periods, with some seasons nearing an end.

  • The discussion ranked LSU receivers, including Jamar Chase, Justin Jefferson, Malik Neighbors, and Odell Beckham Jr., highlighting their performances and potential.
  • A receiver's performance against the Dallas Cowboys was cited, with speculation suggesting he might be the best LSU has produced.
  • The New York Giants, under Brian Daboll, are facing questions about job security and the need for a franchise quarterback.

  • A runner named Gout Gout is generating hype as potentially the next Usain Bolt.
  • He is set to race against Noah Lyles at the World Track and Field Championships in Tokyo.
  • The discussion included the unusual nature of his name and its backstory.

  • Greg Cote declared he has stopped farting, calling himself a 'recovered fartaholic' and expressing pride in this accomplishment.
  • He compared quitting farting to giving up cigars, but noted he has not given up beer.
  • Cote humorously clarified that women do fart but often do not admit it, drawing a parallel to men.
