The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

The Big Suey: Football Movies (feat. Tony D'Amato)

Key Takeaways

  • The Patriots' dynasty under Bill Belichick was debated, questioning luck and Tom Brady's influence.
  • A recent Patriots-Broncos game received harsh criticism for its low quality and 'Smash Mountain' style.
  • Hosts revisited classic football films, debating the existence and quality of truly 'good' movies in the genre.
  • Analysis covered the Seahawks' offseason, including Sam Darnold's performance and JSN's emergence.
  • NFL outcomes are heavily influenced by random plays and clutch moments, differing from sports with larger sample sizes.

Deep Dive

  • The Patriots' sustained success, including 17 AFC East titles and six Super Bowl wins, was discussed.
  • The role of luck and support in coaching success was analyzed in contrast to the Dolphins' recent playoff victory.
  • A recent docuseries was critiqued for its portrayal of Bill Belichick's contributions versus Tom Brady's role.

  • Initial speculation surrounded the Steelers hiring Mike McCarthy, with potential links to Aaron Rodgers.
  • Surprise was expressed at Mike Tomlin's quiet departure after a streak of no losing seasons.
  • A factual correction clarified the Steelers have had more than three head coaches since the NFL merger.

  • Hosts questioned if five truly 'good' football movies exist, initiating a genre debate.
  • The quality of 'Any Given Sunday' was debated, with one host finding it holds up while another found it poor upon rewatching.

  • The recent Patriots-Broncos game was criticized as an 'abomination' and 'stupid' to watch.
  • Hosts expressed disbelief that anyone enjoyed the game's style, described as 'Smash Mountain football'.
  • The game was characterized by constant running, poor visibility, and generally low quality.

  • Discussion covered Mina Kimes' negative reaction to potentially trading Geno Smith and the success of Sam Darnold under new coaching.
  • JSN's emergence as a top wide receiver for the Seahawks was highlighted.
  • Sam Darnold's inconsistent play was noted despite a winning record, prompting speculation other teams may regret not acquiring him.
  • The Rams' defense was critiqued for allowing points on 40% of drives in recent games.

  • NFL games are often won by clutch situations and narrow margins, not solely by perceived team superiority.
  • It was noted that if the 49ers' situation were different, their standing might also be higher, highlighting fine margins.

  • The random nature of football game outcomes was contrasted with sports like hockey and baseball, which have larger sample sizes.
  • It was argued that a single play can dramatically alter a game's result and historical memory.
  • A hypothetical seven-game series between the Rams and Seahawks illustrated that neither team would be counted out despite recent outcomes.

  • The original 'The Longest Yard' (1974) was claimed as the best football movie ever made.
  • 'Friday Night Lights', 'Remember the Titans', and 'Varsity Blues' were proposed as alternatives.
  • Criticisms were raised about the realism of certain plays in 'Remember the Titans', such as a long reverse.
  • 'The Program' and 'North Dallas 40' were also mentioned in the ongoing debate.
