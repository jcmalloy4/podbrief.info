The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Local Hour: Happy Thanksgiling!

Key Takeaways

  • The episode is a Thanksgiving special, 'Thanksgiling,' celebrating former employee Billy Gil's best moments.
  • Pablo Torre released a significant investigative report on Kawhi Leonard's alleged $28 million 'no-show job.'
  • The report suggests potential salary cap violations by the Clippers and owner Steve Ballmer.
  • The Miami Dolphins' 'must-win' game against the Colts and Tyreek Hill's captaincy were key discussion points.
  • Hosts debated the report's origins, implications, and the perceived absurdity of the alleged fraud.

Deep Dive

  • The episode serves as a special Thanksgiving tribute, 'Thanksgiling,' for former employee Billy Gil.
  • It features replays of Billy Gil's best moments from his last year on the show.
  • A highlight included Gil discussing aspirations and Kawhi Leonard, noted as a beneficial segment for Pablo Torre's reporting.
  • Teasers for Billy Gil's 'epic' final show mentioned the return of a character named Peepo.

  • Hosts debated whether the Miami Dolphins' upcoming game against the Indianapolis Colts constituted a 'must-win.'
  • Discussion covered the betting line of a 1.5-point spread, including analysis of the Colts' quarterback situation.
  • It was agreed that a loss to the Colts would be demoralizing for the Dolphins' fanbase.

  • The conversation highlighted a significant increase in coffee prices across various brands.
  • Cuban coffee bricks reportedly saw a price jump from approximately $1.99 to nearly $5.
  • Contributing factors mentioned included tariffs and the limited domestic coffee-growing regions.

  • Pablo Torre released a new seven-month investigative report, in partnership with The Athletic, focusing on Kawhi Leonard.
  • The report details an alleged $28 million 'no-show job' for Leonard, suggesting potential salary cap violations by the Clippers.
  • One host initially expressed skepticism about the story but was convinced by the report's substantial findings.

  • Pablo Torre stated he learned about Kawhi Leonard's alleged $28 million endorsement deal with Aspiration within 30 days of joining his company.
  • He was informed the deal was intended to circumvent the NBA salary cap.
  • The report claims there was no proof of actual endorsement work by Kawhi Leonard for Aspiration.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Pablo Torre's security due to potential backlash from powerful individuals.

  • The hosts discussed their general lack of proficiency with voice modulation technology.
  • Greg Cody's past use of a voice modulator was recalled for a 1993 column where he suggested trading Dan Marino.
  • The modulator was used in an interview to conceal Cody's identity for the controversial piece.

  • The state of Florida currently features three college football teams ranked within the top 15 nationally.
  • Florida State University (FSU) made a significant jump in the rankings following a win against Alabama.

  • The group expressed skepticism and disinterest in the alleged $28 million 'no-show job,' criticizing the perceived absurdity of the fraud.
  • They questioned why the Clippers, if cheating, would not 'cheat better' to secure championships.
  • Discussion included whether owner Steve Ballmer was the first to attempt such a strategy, noting other teams might be investigating similar actions.
  • Past instances of salary cap manipulation, including Chinese endorsements and MLS with Messi, were referenced.
