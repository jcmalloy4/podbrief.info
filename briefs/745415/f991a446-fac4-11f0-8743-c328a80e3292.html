The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hour 2: Jessica's Leeancé (feat. Jessica Smetana)

Key Takeaways

  • Jessica expresses frustration over weeks of deception surrounding her surprise engagement.
  • Unconfirmed reports suggest Giannis Antetokounmpo is open to leaving the Milwaukee Bucks.
  • Discussions covered college football playoff agreement changes and transfer portal impact.
  • Mike McCarthy's reported move to the Pittsburgh Steelers generated critical assessment.
  • Conspiracy theories, from fake snow to the Leviathan, were a surprising segment topic.
  • Hall of Fame voting principles were debated, questioning merit versus personal judgment.

Deep Dive

  • Guest expressed discomfort with constant well-wishes after her engagement, preferring less earnest reactions.
  • The proposal, filmed by partner Leonce on Friday after eight years of dating, included flowers and props.
  • Friend Lucy assisted with planning; guest's initial reaction was "okay, yes" despite having picked out the ring.
  • The elaborate setup was unexpected, especially during a New York snowstorm.

  • College football playoff agreements may allow the highest-ranked Group of Six team to enter without being a conference champion.
  • The significance of conference championship games is questioned under certain agreement scenarios.
  • The transfer portal's impact on college sports is noted, with Miami acquiring two top players from Duke.
  • Fans expressed frustration when players announced returns then entered the transfer portal.

  • NFL coaching hires are discussed, with a critical assessment of Mike McCarthy reportedly joining the Pittsburgh Steelers.
  • The speaker expressed surprise and disappointment, comparing it unfavorably to previous hirings.
  • The speaker admitted feeling "punked" by the news but noted McCarthy "won the press conference" by being emotional.
  • Uncertainty surrounds the Steelers' offense, especially with the potential return of Aaron Rodgers.

  • Jessica's internet minute debunks a conspiracy theory about fake plastic snow, explaining blackening is due to incomplete combustion and sublimation.
  • Discussion includes the idea of a Leviathan sea creature linked to mail-in voting and the Book of Revelation.
  • The hosts questioned how Mike took over the segment to discuss these theories, mentioning a Dr. Fauci bobblehead.

  • A report states Giannis Antetokounmpo is "ready for a new home," referencing the February 5th NBA trade deadline.
  • Despite the report, Giannis has not formally requested a trade, leading to commentary on the phrasing.
  • It's noted Giannis lives in Chicago and commutes to Milwaukee, fueling the "new home" narrative.

  • Hypothetical trade scenarios involving Giannis Antetokounmpo, Terry Rozier, and the Miami Heat were discussed.
  • Speculation arose about the NBA potentially voiding such a contract.
  • Damian Lillard's trade to Milwaukee and subsequent injury may have influenced Giannis's desire to move.
  • Potential destinations for Giannis include the Heat, Knicks, Blazers, and Pelicans.
