GOOD FOLLOW - Can UConn Repeat? Will MiLaysia Fulwiley Fit at LSU? Who to Watch & NCAA Preview with Autumn Johnson!

Key Takeaways

  • UConn, led by Sarah Strong and Azzi Fudd, is a top contender for another women's college basketball championship.
  • South Carolina's Dawn Staley integrates transfer Ta'Niya Latson to address perimeter scoring needs.
  • MiLaysia Fulwiley's transfer to LSU signals her pursuit of a central on-ball, starting point guard role.
  • UCLA and Texas aim to build on previous Final Four appearances by bolstering rosters and refining offensive strategies.
  • Bracketologist Autumn Johnson detailed bracket building criteria and identified several 'sleeper' teams for the season.

Deep Dive

  • UConn demonstrates depth, skill, and experience, positioning them for another championship run in the 2025-26 season.
  • Key players include Sarah Strong, whose impressive freshman season drew comparisons to Maya Moore, and Azzi Fudd.
  • The addition of Sarah Williams, a transfer from Wisconsin averaging 19 points and 10 rebounds, significantly strengthens UConn's frontcourt.
  • Coach Geno Auriemma's program culture, emphasizing pace, execution, and conditioning, underpins the team's consistent success.

  • Dawn Staley's Gamecocks face a season after losing significant scoring from their previous roster.
  • The team acquired transfer Ta'Niya Latson from Florida State, where she averaged 25 points per game.
  • Latson's addition is intended to address South Carolina's need for consistent perimeter scoring, identified during their championship loss to UConn.
  • Latson will adapt her game from taking 20 shots and playing 30 minutes per game at Florida State to Staley's system of depth, defense, and balanced rotations.

  • MiLaysia Fulwiley transferred from South Carolina to LSU, seeking a more significant on-ball and starting point guard role.
  • Under Kim Mulkey at LSU, Fulwiley is expected to take on a central position, contrasting with her previous bench contributions.
  • Her move is viewed as a challenge for personal growth, potentially forming an explosive offense with Flau'Jae Johnson and MiKayla Williams.
  • Flau'Jae Johnson is anticipated to lead LSU with championship experience, while MiKayla Williams offers three-level scoring for the 2025-26 season.

  • Following their Final Four appearance, UCLA has reloaded its roster with talent, including Lauren Betts, Kiki Rice, and transfer Gianna Niekamp.
  • The team gained valuable experience from their Final Four loss to UConn, focusing on developing skills for high-level competition.
  • UCLA is positioned as a potential contender for the upcoming season.
  • Key factors for the Bruins' success are identified as depth, experience, and player health.

  • The Texas Longhorns' season outlook centers on rising junior Madison Booker, who previously averaged 16 points and 7 rebounds.
  • The team must enhance its offensive scoring to complement its established toughness and defensive capabilities.
  • Improved offensive performance is crucial for meeting expectations, particularly after their Final Four loss to South Carolina.

  • NCAA tournament bracketology involves analyzing resume metrics, extensive game watching, and understanding team performance against tournament-caliber opponents.
  • Logistical considerations for bracket building include travel constraints and avoiding early conference rematches.
  • Autumn Johnson identified several 'sleeper' teams for the upcoming women's college basketball season, including Richmond, Oklahoma, Tennessee, Ole Miss, Michigan, and Iowa State.
  • Monitoring key players and team dynamics within these identified sleeper teams is crucial.
