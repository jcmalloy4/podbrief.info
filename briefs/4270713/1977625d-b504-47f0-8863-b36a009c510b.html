Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump Plans Shutdown Job Cuts, OpenAI Valuation Soars to $500 Billion

Key Takeaways

  • White House threatens federal worker layoffs and infrastructure funding cuts to pressure Democrats.
  • Federal Reserve warns that the government shutdown is hindering economic interpretation due to data gaps.
  • OpenAI's valuation surged to $500 billion, making it the world's largest startup after a stock sale.
  • Apple is reportedly shifting focus from its Vision Pro headset to developing AI-driven smart glasses.
  • Renowned conservationist Jane Goodall, celebrated for her chimpanzee research, has died at 91.
  • Trump administration targets billions in infrastructure funds for Democrat-led states.

Deep Dive

  • White House Budget Director Russell Vought plans swift dismissal of federal workers to pressure Democrats to end a government shutdown.
  • Vought informed House lawmakers that some federal agencies will move to terminate workers within one to two days, according to sources.

  • The Trump administration plans to cancel billions of dollars earmarked for hydrogen projects in California and the Pacific Northwest.
  • An additional $18 billion in infrastructure funding for the New York metro region is also targeted.
  • Democrats describe these actions as political payback to pressure them during government shutdown negotiations, while the White House claims funds are under review for compliance.

  • Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee stated a government shutdown hinders the Federal Reserve's ability to interpret the economy due to a lack of official data.
  • Goolsbee expressed concerns about a recent pickup in services inflation, suggesting persistent price pressures in parts of the economy.
  • The absence of data like the September jobs report makes economic assessment harder for central bankers.

  • OpenAI completed a deal to help employees sell shares, reaching a $500 billion valuation.
  • This propels the ChatGPT owner past Elon Musk’s SpaceX to become the world’s largest startup.
  • Current and former employees sold approximately $6.6 billion of stock to investors, including Thrive Capital and SoftBank Group Corp.

  • Apple is reportedly pausing its Vision Pro headset overhaul to focus on developing smart glasses.
  • Plans include at least two smart glass models that would rely heavily on voice interaction and artificial intelligence.

  • Renowned conservationist Jane Goodall has died at the age of 91.
  • Goodall was known for her groundbreaking chimpanzee research.
  • She also became a vocal advocate for environmental protection and conservation.
