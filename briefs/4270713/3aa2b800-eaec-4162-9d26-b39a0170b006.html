Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Nvidia Gives Strong Forecast, Countering Fears of AI Bubble

Key Takeaways

  • NVIDIA's fiscal Q3 results surpassed expectations, with a strong $65 billion Q4 revenue forecast.
  • Concerns about AI spending sustainability persist, focusing on circular financing and GPU depreciation.
  • Analysts note the absence of China from NVIDIA's forecasts and question 'froth' in AI capacity utilization.
  • Despite market skepticism, AI is widely considered a transformative movement with lasting impact on business.

Deep Dive

  • NVIDIA reported strong fiscal third-quarter results, beating analyst expectations.
  • The company provided a fiscal fourth-quarter outlook of $65 billion in sales.
  • This forecast exceeds analyst consensus estimates by approximately $3 billion.

  • Investor worries include circular financing, exemplified by NVIDIA's investment in Anthropic, which then purchases NVIDIA chips.
  • Market fixation on depreciation of AI hardware is another key concern for investors.
  • The latest NVIDIA press release notably omits any mention of China, despite its significance as a data center market.

  • NVIDIA's annual GPU refresh cycle creates pressure on customers, particularly 'neo clouds,' to continuously upgrade.
  • This frequent refresh impacts the economic life of servers, potentially leading to punishing price curves.
  • Coreweave, a 'neo cloud' company, shows strong market reaction and high utilization rates for older GPU generations like Hopper.

  • Jay Goldberg questions high utilization claims for GPUs, pointing to NVIDIA and AMD backstopping capacity at 'neo clouds'.
  • He believes this backstopping indicates 'froth' and friction in the AI sector, justifying a cautious outlook.
  • Skepticism about NVIDIA's market position is partly due to its significant equity investment in major customer Coreweave.

  • NVIDIA's inventory increased, which is typically a worrying sign for investors.
  • However, the company states this inventory build-up is to secure components for future demand.
  • This strategy specifically targets upcoming Blackwell systems, indicating a coordinated supply chain effort.

  • Concerns regarding the AI market's sustainability are not entirely gone, despite NVIDIA's positive forecast.
  • Potential issues include NVIDIA indirectly funding demand for its chips and uncertainty surrounding sales to China.
  • Questions remain about the crucial need for physical infrastructure and energy to support data centers, and the long-term viability of the 'NVIDIA economy'.

  • From a Silicon Valley perspective, AI is viewed as the beginning of a transformative movement with new applications beyond current LLMs.
  • The AI revolution is changing jobs and personal decision-making, creating eagerness for new products and services.
  • While a financial correction is possible, AI's fundamental role in changing business and daily life is expected to persist, similar to the internet's lasting impact despite the dot-com bubble.
