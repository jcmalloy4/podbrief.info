Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

US Offers Ukraine Security Guarantee; Brown University Shooter Remains at Large

Key Takeaways

  • US offers "Article 5-like" security guarantees to Ukraine, pushing peace talks and territorial concessions.
  • Hollywood director Rob Reiner and wife Michele Singer Reiner found dead; son Nick Reiner arrested for murder.
  • Brown University shooting investigation struggles with lack of video footage, after two killed and nine injured.
  • Ford Motor Company reduces electric vehicle production, taking a $19.5 billion charge, to focus on hybrids.

Deep Dive

  • US negotiators offered Kyiv "Article 5-like" security guarantees as part of renewed peace efforts.
  • President Trump stated the conflict is "closer than ever" to resolution, hinting at territorial concessions.
  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed an agreement with the US for legally binding security guarantees via a congressional vote.

  • A deadly attack at a Bondi Beach Hanukkah celebration in Australia killed at least 15 people.
  • Authorities believe the attack was inspired by the Islamic State, finding two homemade ISIS flags in a suspect's car.
  • The attackers are believed to be a father and son who recently traveled to the Philippines.

  • Ford Motor Company is taking a $19.5 billion charge due to a significant pullback from electric vehicles.
  • The company cited overbuilt capacity and challenges in profitability for the shift.
  • Ford is canceling electric F-Series truck production to focus on gas and hybrid vehicles, noting a customer shift.

  • Leading Jewish groups in the US are advising increased security measures for public events.
  • These recommendations follow a deadly mass shooting in Australia.
  • Groups suggest pre-registration and screening for attendees at public gatherings.

  • Bloomberg Senior Europe correspondent Oliver Crook discussed US talks on security guarantees for Ukraine in more detail.
  • Ukrainian President Zelensky considers "Article 5-like" guarantees essential to prevent future invasions.
  • Analysis suggests Russia would reject direct NATO involvement or a seat at the NATO policy table.

  • US negotiators offered enhanced security guarantees to Kyiv, while pressuring President Zelenskyy on territorial concessions.
  • The US aims to bridge the gap between Ukraine's refusal to cede land and Russian demands for the Donbass region.
  • Options like a demilitarized zone are being explored, but an agreement has not yet been reached.
