Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Fed's Cook Defies Trump Pressure; Jackson Hole Conference Starts

Key Takeaways

  • Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook is resisting calls from President Trump for her resignation amid mortgage fraud allegations.
  • US stock futures fell, led by tech declines, as investors anticipate Federal Reserve Chair Powell's remarks at Jackson Hole.
  • Negotiations to end the Russia-Ukraine war are underway, focusing on Ukraine's security guarantees and Russian territorial claims.
  • Walmart's Q2 earnings are expected to show continued strength in grocery and effective inventory management.

Deep Dive

  • President Donald Trump called for Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook's resignation, citing alleged mortgage fraud after Bill Pultey, his head of housing finance, urged an investigation.
  • Cook has refused to step down, stating she will 'not be bullied.'
  • Pultey maintains the president has cause to fire her, and Cook's departure would grant a future President Trump a majority on the seven-member Fed Board of Governors.
  • This marks a significant escalation in Trump's ongoing campaign against the central bank.

  • The Federal Reserve's annual economic policy symposium in Jackson Hole, Wyoming, will feature remarks from Fed Chair Jay Powell, anticipated to signal future Fed policy direction.
  • The symposium is expected to focus less on academic papers and more on the future of the Fed, especially after most officials prioritized inflation risk over labor market concerns.
  • Central bank officials, including those from the ECB, BOE, and BOJ, are present, providing an opportunity to demonstrate support for central bank independence.
  • Prior Trump appointees to the Fed, Michelle Bowman and Christopher Waller, have dissented from his rate preferences, emphasizing the central bank's independent actions.

  • Negotiations to end the Russia-Ukraine war are focused on security guarantees for Ukraine and territory Russia seeks to control, including land it doesn't currently occupy.
  • Vice President J.D. Vance indicated significant U.S. progress in facilitating talks between the two nations to halt fighting.
  • Ukrainian President Vladimir Zelensky will hold intensive meetings on security guarantees but rejected Russia's proposal to include China in a peacekeeping force.
  • Ukraine reported one of Russia's heaviest overnight attacks, involving 40 missiles and nearly 600 drones, killing at least one person in Lviv.

  • The New York Yankees achieved their fifth consecutive win, defeating the Rays 6-4 in 10 innings, hitting five home runs and tying a major league record with 14 home runs in a two-game series.
  • Other MLB results included the Nationals beating the Mets 5-4, the Phillies sweeping Seattle 11-2, and the Rockies defeating the Dodgers 8-3.
  • Shohei Ohtani gave up five runs, reducing the Dodgers' lead over the Padres to one game after San Diego defeated the Giants 8-1.
  • In the WNBA, the LA team secured a one-point victory over Dallas, featuring rookie Paige Beckers' 44 points.
  • Upcoming NFL matchups include the Giants vs. Patriots and Jets vs. Eagles, with the Jets bolstering their defensive line.
