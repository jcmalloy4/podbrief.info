Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

US Sanctions Russian Oil Giants; China Says US Trade Talks Set for Friday

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump sanctioned Russia's major oil producers, prompting a 5% crude oil price rally.
  • US and China are set for new trade talks in Kuala Lumpur to ease economic tensions.
  • New York City's mayoral debate saw Andrew Cuomo challenge frontrunner Zohran Mamdani.
  • Tesla's profit declined despite record sales, while Wall Street bonuses are projected for a record year.
  • US envoys visited Israel supporting a Gaza ceasefire amid complex Trump-Zelensky dynamics.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump announced new sanctions on Russia's state-run oil giants, Rosneft and Lukoil.
  • This action marks a U-turn for Trump, who had previously indicated he believed Russia wanted to end the war, and is part of a fresh bid to end the conflict in Ukraine.
  • The Treasury Department blacklisted the entities due to "Russia's lack of serious commitment to a peace process to end the war in Ukraine."

  • Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng plans to meet with US officials in Kuala Lumpur from Oct. 24 to 27.
  • The meeting is aimed at defusing a standoff between the world's two largest economies.
  • He, Beijing's top trade negotiator, held a call with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent last week ahead of the planned in-person summit.

  • Former New York Governor Andrew Cuomo sought to cast State Assemblyman Zohran Mamdani as unprepared for mayor in the final debate.
  • Mamdani, a 34-year-old democratic socialist, won the Democratic primary in June, besting Cuomo by more than 12 points.
  • Some Republicans and prominent donors are calling upon GOP candidate Curtis Sliwa to exit the race to help improve Cuomo's odds against Mamdani.

  • Tesla's shares dropped as profit decreased due to rising costs, despite the company achieving record vehicle sales.
  • CEO Elon Musk urged shareholders to approve his controversial $1 trillion pay package.
  • Musk criticized advisory firms as 'corporate terrorists' in relation to their stance on his compensation.

  • Wall Street bonuses are projected to reach a record high this year.
  • New York State Comptroller Thomas D'Anapoli's report indicates profits at 130 NYSE firms reached $30.4 billion in the first half of the year.
  • This performance is on track for a record high, following last year's average bonus of $244,700.

  • The U.S. military conducted its ninth strike against a drug-carrying vessel in the eastern Pacific.
  • The operation resulted in three fatalities as part of an expanded campaign against drug trafficking.
  • President Trump indicated that further strikes could occur in the ongoing efforts.

  • Vice President J.D. Vance and other U.S. envoys are visiting Israel to support the Gaza ceasefire agreement.
  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio is scheduled to meet with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.
  • The visits underscore U.S. efforts to maintain regional stability following the agreement.

  • New US sanctions on Russia's largest oil producers, Rosneft and Lukoil, have caused a 5% rally in WTI and Brent crude prices.
  • The sanctions represent a shift from previous price-capping efforts and may impact Western economies.
  • Coordination with European allies remains unclear, though China has already criticized similar EU measures.

  • The relationship between President Trump and Ukrainian President Zelensky is complex, with Trump's support appearing to fluctuate.
  • The EU is working to maintain consistent pressure on Trump regarding Ukraine's situation.
  • Despite previous dealings, President Zelensky and EU leaders currently appear satisfied with the recent US actions.
