Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Daybreak Holiday: Stock Market Records, Fed Independence, The Future of DEI

Key Takeaways

  • Stock markets face potential volatility in 2026 due to high valuations, earnings expectations, and election year dynamics.
  • The Federal Reserve's independence is under scrutiny amidst a Justice Department investigation into Chair Jay Powell.
  • Analysts describe a K-shaped economy, where large companies show strong performance while smaller firms lag.
  • Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives are experiencing a 'wholesale dismantling' in the current climate.

Deep Dive

  • Cameron Dawson of NewEdge Wealth and Lori Calvasina of RBC Capital Markets foresee stock market volatility in 2026.
  • Dawson cites high valuations, earnings expectations, and the midterm election year as potential headwinds.
  • Calvasina projects a 13% drawdown for the S&P 500, noting concerns about fiscal policy and geopolitical risk.

  • Cameron Dawson analyzes the K-shaped economy, noting that strong market-weighted data is influenced by large companies.
  • Smaller companies and consumer sentiment surveys reflect weaker performance despite resilient aggregate consumer data.
  • Lori Calvasina notes client interest in fairly valued small-cap stocks, but advises a cautious, day-by-day approach.

  • The Federal Reserve faces an upcoming rate decision amidst an investigation into Chair Jay Powell.
  • Powell stated he has always served without political fear or favor, focusing on the Fed's mandate.
  • Anna Wong, Chief US Economist for Bloomberg Economics, observed a change in Powell's demeanor, suggesting White House pressure has taken a toll.

  • Discussion centers on the potential for political influence on the Federal Reserve's monetary policy decisions.
  • Analysts express concern that political pressure could sway policy away from data-driven interest rate cuts.
  • The upcoming Fed leadership composition could lead to 'mischief' if loyalty to the president shifts the committee's balance.

  • The Justice Department's probe into the Fed is debated for its market impact, with some seeing it as temporary and others warning of significant damage.
  • While Fed officials believe the threat to independence has increased due to potential jail time, they do not anticipate charges or indictments.
  • Markets have shown limited reaction, with a belief that the issue will fade without significant consequences, unless the next Fed rate decision brings surprises.

  • Bloomberg's Jeff Green describes the changes in Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives as a 'wholesale dismantling'.
  • He suggests that the current state of DEI initiatives will be difficult to reverse.
  • Rebuilding DEI initiatives would require a significant shift in the political climate.
