Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Winter Storm Pummels East Coast; Dems Threaten Shutdown After Latest Minneapolis Killing

Key Takeaways

  • US power grids face unprecedented demand and blackout threats from a damaging winter storm, with 800,000 outages.
  • A Border Patrol agent fatally shot an ICU nurse in Minnesota, escalating tensions and prompting calls for investigation.
  • Senate Democrats threaten a US government shutdown over Homeland Security funding, intensifying a political standoff.
  • President Trump issued a 100% tariff threat against Canada if it pursues a trade deal with China.

Deep Dive

  • A severe winter storm led to over 3,600 flight cancellations nationwide.
  • More than 800,000 homes and businesses experienced power outages, straining US grids.
  • Lingering ice and snow continued to disrupt travel, particularly in upstate New York and New England.
  • Bitterly cold temperatures are forecast across the eastern US for the entire week.

  • A Border Patrol agent fatally shot ICU nurse Alex Pretti in Minnesota during immigration crackdown protests.
  • Governor Tim Waltz criticized federal authorities, and some Republicans called for investigations into the incident.
  • Questions arose about whether Pretti resisted or brandished a firearm, with video evidence suggesting he held a phone.
  • The NRA called for a full investigation, emphasizing the right to lawfully carry a weapon.

  • President Trump threatened 100% tariffs on Canadian exports to the US.
  • The threat is in response to Canada's potential trade deal with China, allowing increased electric vehicle exports.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Besson criticized the deal, fearing Chinese goods could enter the U.S. tariff-free.
  • This escalates tensions and complicates upcoming US-Mexico-Canada free agreement renegotiations.

  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky announced a security agreement with the US is finalized.
  • The agreement is awaiting a signing date.
  • Zelensky reiterated Ukraine's goal of achieving EU membership by 2027.
  • He acknowledged significant differences with Russia regarding territorial integrity.

  • Senate Democrats are threatening to block a large spending package aimed at averting a US government shutdown.
  • They demand the removal of funding for the Department of Homeland Security.
  • Republicans are reportedly resistant, increasing the risk of a shutdown by week's end.
  • This political pressure point occurs amidst ongoing discussions about immigration enforcement in Minneapolis.
