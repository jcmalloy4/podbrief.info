Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump Readies Tariff Cuts in Affordability Push; Fed Rate Cut Doubts Grow

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump plans tariff reductions on common grocery items and new trade deals to lower food prices.
  • Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem announced $10,000 bonus checks for TSA officers post-government shutdown.
  • Traders have lowered expectations for a December US interest rate cut due to Federal Reserve officials' skepticism.
  • New Jersey authorities arrested 14 individuals in a multi-million dollar betting ring targeting Gen Z gamblers.
  • Boeing factory workers in St. Louis accepted a new contract, ending a strike and increasing wages by 24%.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump is preparing substantial tariff cuts on common grocery items such as beef, bananas, and coffee beans.
  • These cuts are part of new trade agreements with Argentina, Guatemala, El Salvador, and Ecuador.
  • The initiative aims to address high food prices, following recent Democratic electoral victories where affordability was a key issue.
  • The administration expects retailers to pass the savings onto consumers.

  • Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem announced $10,000 bonus checks for Transportation Security Administration (TSA) officers.
  • These payments reward officers who worked without pay during the six-week government shutdown, calling it 'exemplary service'.
  • TSA officers took on extra shifts to keep security lines moving despite paychecks stopping.
  • The shutdown, the longest in US history, shuttered parts of the federal government for 43 days and left over 800,000 workers unpaid.

  • Traders slashed the odds of a December US interest rate cut to below 50% after Fed officials voiced skepticism.
  • Minneapolis Fed President Neel Kashkari did not support the last rate cut, and San Francisco Fed chief Mary Daly called a December decision 'premature'.
  • Stock markets are seeing futures lower after a tech-driven sell-off, impacted by concerns about steady interest rates.
  • Citigroup CEO Jane Frazier expressed caution on the economic outlook, suggesting another 'shoe to drop' related to tariffs, the labor market, or asset prices.

  • New Jersey authorities arrested 14 individuals involved in a multi-million dollar betting ring.
  • The operation allegedly targeted Gen Z gamblers, including college students and athletes.
  • Prosecutors claim a Lucchese crime family soldier financed his son to operate the illegal ring.
  • The betting ring is alleged to have generated over $2 million in illicit funds.

  • President Trump's new trade deals include tariff cuts for common grocery items like beef, bananas, and coffee with Latin American countries.
  • This move aims to address voter concerns about rising prices, particularly after recent Democratic election wins where affordability was a key issue.
  • The impact on overall trade flows and specific sectors, such as American cattle ranchers, is being questioned.
  • Democrats are expected to leverage concerns about rising grocery and healthcare costs, including Affordable Care Act subsidies, as midterms approach.
