Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

President Trump Pushes Powell on Rates; Bessent Says China Talks Stalled

Key Takeaways

  • Legal uncertainty around tariffs is creating market volatility and hampering trade negotiations, with a federal appeals court temporarily upholding Trump's tariffs while the administration prepares for a potential Supreme Court battle over whether trade deficits constitute a "national emergency."
  • Trade relations with China have stalled, requiring potential direct intervention between Trump and Xi Jinping to break the impasse, while the U.S. simultaneously plans to increase weapons sales to Taiwan with missiles and drones.
  • Trump is pressuring the Federal Reserve to lower interest rates through direct meetings with Chair Powell, believing current rates disadvantage the U.S. economy, as Powell's term expires in May.
  • Elon Musk is stepping back from politics, distancing himself from political donations and reducing his role in the "Department of Government Efficiency" despite an upcoming White House news conference with Trump.
  • A Gaza ceasefire proposal has been accepted by Israel but criticized by Hamas as one-sided, featuring a 60-day pause, restored UN aid, and hostage exchanges that could reshape Middle East dynamics.

Deep Dive

Trade and Economic Policy Developments

Tariff Legal Battle and Trade Relations:

  • Federal Appeals Court temporarily allowed Trump administration tariffs to remain in effect, pausing a previous trade court ruling that had deemed most tariffs since January illegal
  • The White House is prepared to potentially escalate the case to the Supreme Court if necessary
  • Key legal questions center on whether trade deficits and fentanyl issues constitute a "national emergency" sufficient to justify broad tariff implementation
  • Trade talks with China have reached a standstill, with Treasury Secretary Scott Besson suggesting a potential direct call between Trump and President Xi Jinping may be needed to break the impasse
  • The White House remains committed to maintaining its tariff policy, though alternative policy tools exist that are more time-consuming and legally complex
  • Uncertainty about the final judicial outcome continues to impact ongoing trade negotiations and America's negotiating leverage
Taiwan Arms Sales:
  • Reuters reports Trump is planning to increase weapons sales to Taiwan, with focus on missiles, munitions, and drones

Federal Reserve and Monetary Policy

Trump-Powell Meeting:

  • Trump met with Fed Chair Jay Powell at the White House, reportedly pushing Powell to lower interest rates
  • Trump believes current interest rates put the U.S. at an economic disadvantage
  • Powell's term as Fed chair expires in May of next year

Political Developments

Elon Musk's Changing Role:

  • Trump and Musk are scheduled to hold a White House news conference
  • Musk has recently distanced himself politically, swearing off future political donations
  • Musk is stepping back from his involvement with the "Department of Government Efficiency"

Middle East Developments

Gaza Ceasefire Proposal:

  • Israel has accepted a U.S. ceasefire proposal for Gaza
  • The proposal includes a 60-day fighting pause, restoration of U.N. aid distribution, Hamas releasing 10 live hostages, and return of remains of 18 deceased hostages
  • Hamas has criticized the proposal as one-sided and has not officially responded

Market Analysis and Economic Indicators

Market Outlook:

  • Futures are lower as traders navigate uncertainty about tariff policies
  • Expectations of continued market volatility and potential upward pressure on U.S. bond yields
  • Upcoming focus on Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index, a key inflation gauge
Corporate Performance:
  • Gap stock down 15% due to revenue decline and potential tariff impacts
  • Costco navigating tariffs through strategic sourcing and pricing adjustments
  • Luxury watch market experienced 160% higher trading volume in late April, particularly in U.S. and U.K. markets

Notable News and Sports

General News:

  • Bernard Carrick, NYPD Commissioner during 9/11, died at age 69
  • Two NYPD officers placed on modified duty related to a crypto entrepreneur's kidnapping case
  • Harvard University President Alan Garber emphasized international reach during commencement
Sports Updates:
  • New York Knicks won Game 5 against Pacers (111-94), cutting series deficit to 3-2, with Jalen Brunson scoring 32 points
  • Florida Panthers and Edmonton Oilers set to meet in Stanley Cup playoffs finals for the sixth time in history
  • Yankees vs. Dodgers game featuring Aaron Judge and Shohei Otani, with Yankees on a hot streak (5 straight wins, 9 of last 10, 16 of last 20)
  • Max Fried pitching with impressive 7-0 record and 1.29 ERA
  • French Open second-round action with Novak Djokovic advancing, pursuing his 25th Grand Slam
