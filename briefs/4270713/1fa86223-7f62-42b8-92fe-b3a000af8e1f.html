Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump Moves to Preserve Xi Truce While Reassuring Ally Japan, Moscow and Kyiv Trade Fire

Key Takeaways

  • US President Trump held calls with China and Japan as Taiwan tensions threaten a trade truce.
  • A US official is meeting Russian counterparts in Abu Dhabi for Ukraine peace talks.
  • Nvidia shares declined on reports that Meta is in talks to acquire Google's AI chips.
  • The White House is developing a plan to address rising Obamacare health insurance premiums.
  • Investors are increasing bets on a Federal Reserve rate cut in December due to labor market weakness.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump held calls with China's Xi Jinping and Japan's prime minister regarding escalating tensions over Taiwan.
  • The US aims to preserve its weeks-old trade truce with Beijing amidst the rising geopolitical concerns.
  • China's Foreign Ministry stated Xi informed Trump that Taiwan's return is an 'integral part of the postwar international order'.
  • Trump's public statements on the calls focused on trade issues, including soybean purchases and fentanyl interdiction.

  • A US official is currently meeting with a Russian delegation in Abu Dhabi to discuss a peace proposal for the ongoing Ukraine conflict.
  • President Trump previously reported progress on a peace proposal amid continued fighting.
  • Russia has stated it will not accept a peace plan that deviates from previous agreements, indicating remaining points of disagreement concerning a ceasefire.
  • Ukraine has put forward a revised 19-point plan for resolution.

  • Nvidia shares fell following a report that Meta is in talks to purchase billions of dollars worth of Google's AI chips, known as tensor processing units (TPUs).
  • Meta is reportedly in discussions to use these Google chips in its data centers by 2027.
  • This development suggests Google's TPUs are making headway in efforts to compete with Nvidia's widely adopted AI accelerators.
  • Meta may also rent chips from Google's cloud division as early as next year, according to the report.

  • The Pentagon has initiated an investigation into Senator Mark Kelly.
  • The inquiry focuses on potential breaches of military law.
  • The investigation stems from Kelly's participation in a video where he urged troops to defy illegal orders.
