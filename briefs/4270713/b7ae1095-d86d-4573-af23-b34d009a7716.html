Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump Accuses Xi of ‘Conspiring Against US’ With Putin, Kim; Global Bond Selloff Deepens

Key Takeaways

  • US-China tensions heightened after Beijing's military parade and President Trump's remarks.
  • Global bond markets are experiencing a sell-off amid inflation and debt concerns.
  • Google avoided a Chrome browser sale in its federal antitrust case.
  • Thousands of Jeffrey Epstein case records have been released, with more blocked.
  • President Trump plans National Guard deployment in Chicago, facing objections.

Deep Dive

  • President Xi Jinping hosted leaders including Vladimir Putin and Kim Jong-un at a military parade.
  • China emphasized self-reliance and resilience, showcasing its military modernization.
  • President Trump commented on social media, accusing the leaders of conspiring against the U.S.
  • Stephen Engel reported from Beijing on the parade's significance for China's WWII history assertion.

  • Global bond markets are experiencing renewed selling pressure, impacting previously safe assets.
  • Yields on U.S. Treasuries, European, and Japanese bonds are rising.
  • Analysts express concern over market de-anchoring driven by inflation jitters and debt sales.

  • A federal judge ruled Alphabet Inc.'s Google must share some search data with competitors.
  • The court decision, however, will not force Google to sell its popular Chrome web browser.
  • Google shares rose 6% following the ruling, which avoids a severe remedy request from the U.S. government.

  • President Trump plans to send the National Guard to Chicago as part of a crime crackdown.
  • A judge previously ruled a similar deployment in Los Angeles illegal.
  • Illinois Governor J.B. Pritzker has voiced objections to the proposed deployment.

  • Thousands of Justice Department records related to the Jeffrey Epstein case have been released.
  • The release followed meetings with victims of the case.
  • House Speaker Mike Johnson is reportedly blocking a full public release of these documents.

  • Bloomberg Asia correspondent Rosalind Matheson discussed President Xi Jinping's projection of power, internally and externally.
  • Xi's alignment with leaders from Russia, North Korea, Iran, and Cuba signifies a move to form alternate power blocks.
  • This display, following the military parade, emphasizes China's strength and resistance to intimidation.
