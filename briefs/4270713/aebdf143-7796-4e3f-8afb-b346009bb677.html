Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump May Extend Influence to Fed Banks; SpaceX Deploys Satellites in Space

Key Takeaways

  • The Trump administration is exploring ways to increase its influence over the Federal Reserve.
  • SpaceX's Starship rocket achieved new flight milestones after a successful test.
  • NVIDIA's upcoming earnings report is highly anticipated by investors focused on AI growth.
  • The U.S. imposed a 50% tariff on India's exports, doubling previous duties.

Deep Dive

  • The Trump administration is exploring ways to increase influence over the Federal Reserve's regional banks, potentially beyond presidential appointments.
  • President Trump seeks to remove Fed Governor Lisa Cook over alleged mortgage fraud to appoint a majority to the Fed's board.
  • Former Fed Vice Chair Lale Brainerd expressed concern about political interference undermining Fed independence and potentially increasing inflation and interest rates.
  • Trump stated readiness for a legal battle to remove Governor Cook, aiming to lower housing interest rates.

  • Investors are anticipating NVIDIA's quarterly earnings report, with expectations of continued growth driven by AI demand and its Blackwell chips.
  • Bloomberg Consensus estimates revenue of $46.23 billion and adjusted earnings per share of $1.01.
  • U.S. restrictions on chip sales to China and China's recommendations for using older chip models pose a potential challenge for the company.
  • Investors focus on revenue sources, Blackwell chip development, and the H-20 business to China.

  • SpaceX successfully conducted a test flight of its Starship rocket.
  • The booster separated and splashed down as planned.
  • Starship is designed for satellite deployment and future human spaceflight.

  • The Israeli military stated a strike on a Gaza hospital killed 20 people, including five journalists.
  • The military claimed it targeted what it believed to be a Hamas surveillance camera.
