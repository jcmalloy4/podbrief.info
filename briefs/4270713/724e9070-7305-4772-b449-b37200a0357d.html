Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Israeli Cabinet Backs Gaza Deal; New York AG James Indicted

Key Takeaways

  • Israel approved a deal for Hamas to release all remaining hostages in exchange for Palestinian prisoners.
  • The US government shutdown has entered its tenth day, impacting federal operations and key economic reports.
  • New York Attorney General Letitia James faces federal indictment on charges of bank fraud and false statements.
  • France navigates new government leadership, while Japan's ruling coalition has collapsed.

Deep Dive

  • Israel's cabinet approved a ceasefire deal, involving the release of Palestinian prisoners in exchange for remaining hostages in Gaza.
  • This initial phase includes the Israeli army withdrawing to pre-agreed lines over four days.
  • President Trump is expected to travel to Israel for the hostage release, according to the agreement details.

  • The US government shutdown has entered its tenth day, with both political parties blaming each other for the ongoing stalemate.
  • President Trump reiterated threats to use the shutdown to implement sweeping cuts to popular Democratic programs and federal bureaucracy.
  • The White House Office of Management and Budget recalled Bureau of Labor Statistics staff to assemble the September Consumer Price Index report.
  • The CPI report, crucial for determining next year's Social Security checks, is unlikely to meet its October 15th deadline.

  • New York Attorney General Letitia James was indicted by a federal grand jury in Virginia on charges of bank fraud and making false statements to a financial institution.
  • The indictment stems from a 2020 property purchase and marks the second such action by a U.S. Attorney recently appointed by President Trump.
  • James stated she will fight the charges, calling it a 'weaponization of our justice system'; her first court appearance is scheduled for October 24th.

  • Bloomberg News Israel Bureau Chief Ethan Bronner reports from Tel Aviv that a ceasefire is beginning as Israel redeploys its forces.
  • The presence of a U.S. envoy and Jared Kushner alongside Prime Minister Netanyahu during the deal's approval signifies solidarity.
  • Hamas is releasing all hostages upfront, with further negotiations to follow regarding Gaza's future governance and Hamas's potential disarmament.

  • The podcast explores potential political ramifications in Israel following the deal, including the possibility of early elections for Prime Minister Netanyahu.
  • Public sentiment is noted to be shifting towards ending the conflict once the hostages are returned.
