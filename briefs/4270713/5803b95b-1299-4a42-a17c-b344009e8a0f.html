Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Jackson Hole Points to Hard Road Ahead for Powell; Wall Street Awaits Nvidia Earnings

Key Takeaways

  • Federal Reserve Chair Powell signaled a potential September interest rate cut amid internal divisions.
  • SpaceX delayed its Starship rocket test flight due to a ground system liquid oxygen leak.
  • Upcoming Nvidia earnings will test "artificial intelligence euphoria" in the stock market.
  • President Trump targeted major news networks and faced pushback on National Guard deployment.
  • Western U.S. endures dangerous heat and growing wildfires threatening thousands of homes.

Deep Dive

  • Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell signaled a potential interest rate cut as soon as September during the Jackson Hole symposium.
  • Traders are anticipating the cut, while ECB President Christine Lagarde warned against political interference in central banking.
  • Upcoming economic data, including the Personal Consumption Expenditures price index, jobs reports, and CPI, will be crucial for the September decision.

  • President Trump threatened NBC and ABC with FCC license revocation, claiming 97% of their stories about him are unfavorable.
  • Maryland Governor Wes Moore opposed President Trump's threat to deploy National Guard troops to cities, calling it disrespectful.
  • Millions in the Western U.S. are facing dangerous heat and dry conditions, fueling wildfires, with Oregon's "flat fire" growing over 21,000 acres.
  • The LA County Fire Department is taking extra precautions due to extreme temperatures and low humidity.

  • SpaceX delayed a critical test flight of its massive Starship rocket due to a ground-side liquid oxygen leak identified by CEO Elon Musk.
  • Tommy Fleetwood won the Tor Championship in Atlanta with a score of 18-under-par, earning $10 million.
  • The U.S. Open tennis tournament has begun, with notable early wins for players including Novak Djokovic, Taylor Fritz, and Jessica Pegula.

  • Political pressure from the White House and former President Trump, advocating for interest rate cuts, adds complexity to the Federal Reserve's decision-making.
  • European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde's comments underscored the importance of central bank independence.
  • Divisions among Federal Reserve policymakers are evident, with two members dissenting in the previous month's decision.
  • Future data releases will be crucial for assessing the Fed's unified stance on September's policy.
