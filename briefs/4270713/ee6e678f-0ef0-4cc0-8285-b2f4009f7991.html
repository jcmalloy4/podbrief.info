Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump, Musk Trade Threats; US-China Tensions Cool

Key Takeaways

  • Trump-Musk relationship explodes into a public feud with massive financial consequences, resulting in Tesla shares falling 14% and Musk losing over $30 billion in personal wealth after Trump threatened to cut government contracts.
  • International trade momentum builds as the U.S. resumes talks with China over rare earth minerals, while Trump exchanges visit invitations with Xi Jinping and continues negotiations with Japan and Canada ahead of the G7 summit.
  • Economic weakness signals emerge with expectations for the weakest jobs report since October (126,000 payrolls) and major corporate disappointments from Lululemon (-21%) and Broadcom amid concerns about AI spending slowdown.
  • Ukraine conflict takes controversial turn as Trump suggests letting Russia and Ukraine "continue fighting" like children while Russia launches deadly strikes on civilian targets in Kyiv.
  • Political reconciliation attempts surface as Republicans seek to maintain Musk's support for midterms while Musk shows openness to "cooling off" the feud, though he maintains criticism of deficit-expanding legislation.

Deep Dive

Trump-Musk Feud Erupts

The conversation begins with coverage of a significant public falling out between Elon Musk and Donald Trump that has escalated into a full-blown feud with major financial and political implications:

  • Initial Conflict: Trump expressed disappointment in Musk, claiming their relationship has deteriorated, while Musk fired back by calling Trump an "ingrate" and suggesting Trump wouldn't have won the election without his help
  • Escalating Threats: Trump threatened to cut off Musk's government contracts in response to the public criticism
  • Financial Impact: The feud resulted in devastating financial consequences for Musk, with Tesla shares falling 14% and Musk losing over $30 billion in personal wealth - a $34 billion total loss to his net worth
  • Broader Implications: The conflict poses potential brand damage risks for Musk, particularly among Democrats and in key markets like California and Europe

International Relations and Trade Developments

Amid the domestic political drama, several significant international developments unfolded:

  • U.S.-China Relations: The U.S. and China agreed to resume trade talks after 90 minutes of discussions, with rare earth minerals serving as a key negotiation point. Trump accepted an invitation to visit China and extended a reciprocal invitation to Xi Jinping to visit the U.S.
  • Additional Trade Negotiations: The U.S. began trade negotiations with Japan, with Japan's delegation seeking tariff reprieve, while U.S.-Canada trade discussions continued ahead of the G7 summit

Russia-Ukraine Conflict Commentary

President Trump offered controversial commentary on the ongoing Russia-Ukraine conflict:

  • Trump's Approach: Trump suggested letting Russia and Ukraine continue fighting, comparing the situation to "children fighting in a park," while hinting at potential sanctions if the conflict continues too long
  • Ground Reality: Meanwhile, Russia launched drone and missile strikes on Ukrainian civilian targets, killing at least 3 people in Kyiv and wounding over a dozen

Economic and Market Analysis

Several key economic indicators and corporate developments emerged:

  • Jobs Report Expectations: The upcoming May jobs report was expected to show 126,000 nonfarm payrolls, potentially the lowest since October, with labor market indicators suggesting weakness after ADP reported only 37,000 jobs created
  • Corporate Performance:
- Lululemon stock plunged 21% due to reduced earnings outlook and sales estimates - Broadcom shares fell 4% on lackluster revenue forecasts, potentially impacted by AI spending slowdown - HSBC Chairman Mark Tucker announced his departure after 8 years

  • Tax Policy: President Trump indicated openness to a lower state and local tax deduction cap in tax bill negotiations

Legal and Immigration News

Several notable legal developments were covered:

  • Criminal Charges: Mohammed Suleiman was charged with 118 counts, including attempted murder, for an attack on pro-Israel demonstrators
  • High-Profile Cases: Sean Diddy Combs was warned by a judge about courtroom behavior during his sex trafficking trial
  • Immigration Issues: Marcelo Gomes De Silva was released from ICE custody after being detained while driving to volleyball practice

Sports Highlights

The conversation included significant sports updates:

  • NBA Finals: Indiana Pacers defeated Oklahoma City Thunder 111-110 in Game 1, with Tyrese Halliburton hitting a game-winning shot with 0.3 seconds remaining - continuing his history of clutch playoff performances
  • Other Sports: Edmonton led Florida 1-0 in the Stanley Cup Finals, the Yankees beat Cleveland 4-0 with notable performances by Cody Bellinger and Max Fried, and Aaron Rodgers agreed to play quarterback for the Steelers

Musk's Path Forward

The discussion concluded with signs of potential de-escalation and political maneuvering:

  • Cooling Off Period: Musk appeared open to "cooling off" after the conflict, responding positively to social media suggestions to take a step back
  • NASA Commitments: Despite the political tensions, Musk confirmed he would not cancel the NASA Dragon Space program for ferrying astronauts to the International Space Station
  • Political Positioning: Republicans were actively seeking Musk's continued support for midterm elections, hoping to maintain his backing without further alienating him
  • Fiscal Concerns: Musk criticized a bill estimated by the CBO to add $2.4 trillion to the deficit over 10 years, viewing this potential impact through both personal and professional lenses as he navigates complex political and business relationships
