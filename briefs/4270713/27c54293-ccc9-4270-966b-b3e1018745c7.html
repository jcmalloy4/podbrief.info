Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Instant Reaction: Apple Delivers Upbeat Forecast

Key Takeaways

  • Apple reported record quarterly revenue of $143.76 billion, exceeding analyst expectations and showing strong growth.
  • Exceptional iPhone sales, particularly in Greater China, were the primary driver behind Apple's strong financial performance.
  • Apple successfully navigated rising component costs by leveraging its scale and engaging in extensive advance purchasing.
  • The company's AI strategy heavily relies on third-party partnerships, notably with Google for Gemini, highlighting internal development gaps.
  • Long-term AI development and executive succession planning remain key strategic challenges for Apple's future viability.

Deep Dive

  • Apple announced record quarterly revenue of $143.76 billion, surpassing analyst expectations of $138.4 billion.
  • Earnings per share reached $2.84, exceeding the estimated $2.68 for the period.
  • Greater China revenue surged to $25.53 billion, beating the $21.82 billion forecast, largely driven by the new iPhone 17 design.
  • Bloomberg News Managing Editor Mark Gurman characterized the quarter as a 'home run' and Apple's 'greatest quarter ever'.

  • Apple successfully navigated rising component and memory chip costs by purchasing components and securing deals months or even years in advance.
  • This strategic advance purchasing and Apple's substantial scale enabled the company to secure favorable pricing, protecting its profit margins.
  • Despite high memory chip prices driven by scarcity and data center demand, Apple's robust supply chain team ensures its leverage is maintained.

  • Apple is currently partnering with Google for AI models due to a perceived lack of internal development and unfavorable pricing from other partners like Anthropic and OpenAI.
  • Mark Gurman emphasized the critical need for Apple to address its future AI strategy, despite strong current performance buying the company time.
  • Apple recently acquired the Israeli AI startup QAI, which is expected to enhance future wearables but is not central to the company's overall AI strategy.

  • Apple CEO Tim Cook described the demand for the iPhone as 'unprecedented,' contributing to record quarterly sales.
  • The new design of the iPhone 17 is credited with attracting new customers and driving strong sales in Greater China.
  • Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported an interim agreement for Apple's Siri to be underpinned by Google's Gemini, valued at $1 billion annually.

  • Apple's recent acquisition of an Israeli AI startup focuses on facial movement interpretation, with potential for future product development.
  • Amazon is reportedly considering a $50 billion investment in OpenAI, which could value the AI company at over $800 billion.
  • This potential deal highlights a trend of hyperscale companies diversifying their AI partnerships and signals OpenAI's need for capital, possibly for an IPO.
