Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Mamdani Sworn In as NYC Mayor; US Sanctions Chinese Companies, Tankers With Venezuela Links

Key Takeaways

  • Zohran Mamdani inaugurated as NYC Mayor, pledging democratic socialist policies.
  • U.S. sanctioned Chinese firms over Venezuela oil exports, escalating pressure on Maduro.
  • President Trump vowed U.S. support for Iranian protesters facing government action.
  • Global stock futures began the new year higher, but volatility is anticipated.
  • College football playoffs saw significant victories for Oregon, Indiana, and Ole Miss.

Deep Dive

  • Zohran Mamdani, 34, was inaugurated as New York City's first South Asian and Muslim mayor, and its youngest in over a century.
  • He promised to govern as a democratic socialist, outlining an agenda of safety, affordability, and abundance.
  • Key proposals include free child care, free bus transportation, and a rent freeze for stabilized apartments.
  • Mamdani plans a $6 billion universal child care program funded by new taxes on corporations and millionaires.

  • The Trump administration sanctioned four Hong Kong and mainland China-based companies.
  • Four oil tankers linked to these firms were also sanctioned, accused of evading restrictions on Venezuela's oil exports.
  • This targets Chinese firms, signaling a broader U.S. pressure campaign against Nicolás Maduro's regime.
  • China remains Venezuela's largest customer for oil exports.

  • President Trump pledged U.S. rescue for Iranian protesters if attacked by Islamic Republic authorities.
  • The tweet followed protests in Tehran ignited by a record low currency and worsening economic crisis.
  • Demonstrations have spread across Iran, leading to clashes between civilians and security forces.
  • Trump did not specify what actions the U.S. would consider taking.

  • Global stock futures are higher at the start of the new year, following a strong performance in the previous year driven by earnings and AI optimism.
  • Market strategists anticipate increased volatility and potential early-year sell-offs, possibly foreshadowing a modest recession.
  • The S&P 500 rose 16% in 2025, following two years of over 20% gains.
  • Historically, the S&P 500's median change on the first trading day of the year since 1953 is a 0.3% drop, having declined for the past three years.

  • Oregon shut out Texas Tech 23-0 in the Orange Bowl.
  • Indiana defeated Alabama 38-3 in the Rose Bowl.
  • Ole Miss beat Georgia 39-34 in the Sugar Bowl with a last-second field goal.

  • Bloomberg News editor-at-large Marty Shanker highlighted Mayor Mamdani is not moderating his democratic socialist campaign promises.
  • Discussions included potential concerns regarding federal funding for Mamdani's ambitious plans.
  • The 'tax the rich' promise for funding faces implementation challenges.
  • President Trump's actions were acknowledged as an unpredictable factor impacting future policies.
