Bloomberg Daybreak: US Edition

Trump's War on Media Expands; Trump, Xi to Hold Call

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump is escalating pressure on broadcast networks, threatening license reviews over critical coverage.
  • A call between President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping aims to finalize a deal for TikTok's US operations.
  • The Trump administration is appealing to the Supreme Court to remove Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook.
  • Adobe launched new AI-powered PDF features for business applications.
  • Three Pennsylvania police officers were killed in an ambush while serving a warrant.

Deep Dive

  • Adobe Acrobat Studio has launched a new AI-powered PDF feature.
  • The feature can summarize market research, create sales proposal templates, and tailor report tones.

  • President Trump threatened to review US broadcast network licenses if they are too critical of him, following the suspension of Jimmy Kimmel's show.
  • Media executives and academics suggest companies are 'bending to the will' of the Trump administration, making them resemble state-run outlets.

  • The Trump administration appealed to the Supreme Court to allow the removal of Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook.
  • The Justice Department cited alleged mortgage fraud in its request.
  • Cook denies wrongdoing and currently remains in her position due to a Washington federal judge's ruling.

  • Three Pennsylvania police officers were killed by a suspected stalker who ambushed them with a rifle.
  • The incident occurred as officers attempted to serve a warrant at the suspect's ex-girlfriend's home.

  • A call between President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping is scheduled to discuss TikTok's U.S. operations.
  • They will address a framework agreement to shift control from Chinese parent ByteDance to a consortium of American investors.
  • The proposed structure involves ByteDance's stake dropping to 20% or less, with Oracle among the American companies taking majority control.
