Call Her Daddy

Ayesha Curry: Marriage, Motherhood & My Identity

Key Takeaways

  • Ayesha Curry's perspective on marriage and motherhood evolved from early career focus.
  • She details her relationship with Steph Curry, including initial pursuit and milestones.
  • Public scrutiny and misogynistic stigmas impact her and other athletes' wives.
  • Ayesha navigates challenges as a female entrepreneur in male-dominated industries.
  • Her work extends to philanthropy and a skincare brand rooted in personal experience.

Deep Dive

  • Ayesha Curry initially stated she did not desire marriage or children, aiming to focus on her career.
  • Her perspective shifted early in life, leading to a change in her personal timeline.
  • She noted her family's July is chaotic, including three children's birthdays and her anniversary, even during her husband's NBA offseason.

  • Ayesha Curry met Steph Curry as friends at age 14 in North Carolina, after moving from Markham, Ontario.
  • They were dating during her appearance on Disney Channel's "Hannah Montana."
  • Steph initiated contact with a flirtatious Facebook message, leading to their first "real date" in Hollywood when they were 18-19 years old.
  • Ayesha described their first date, which included visiting Hollywood Boulevard and getting chai lattes, as "one of the best days of my life."

  • Ayesha Curry confirmed she and Steph Curry were official when he was drafted into the NBA.
  • She was the first to say "I love you" during a visit to him at college; Steph's delayed response was due to a past regret, which Ayesha later understood as sincere.
  • Steph first reciprocated his love during a viewing of the movie "Seven Pounds" with her family present, in her uncle's elevator.

  • Ayesha Curry discussed balancing her personal identity with family life, acknowledging resilience gained from the experience.
  • She still experiences "mom guilt" but emphasized the importance of maintaining personal interests for self-confidence and family well-being.
  • Ayesha advised embracing unexpected life changes by rejecting strict timelines, noting life's "ebbs and flows" and the possibility of recovery.
  • She identified losing control over her entrepreneurial aspirations as the hardest part of being a young mother, describing herself and Steph as "trailblazers" among their friends for having children early.

  • Ayesha Curry expressed gratitude for opportunities stemming from her husband's fame but initially disliked the public aspect and unexpected scrutiny.
  • She recounted a traumatic postpartum incident with a fan and noted how early social media contributed to her self-criticism.
  • Ayesha described differing levels of public scrutiny between male and female figures, and her husband, Steph, struggles to empathize with her feelings about criticism, often dismissing it as untrue.
  • She faced public criticism including a comparison to 'SpongeBob's mom' and comments implying her husband's infidelity, noting the 'tired' and 'strange' trend of judging athletes' wives based on looks.

  • Ayesha Curry detailed her career progression from starting a food blog for family to publishing a book and appearing on Food Network.
  • She faced "noes" in the food industry due to her age and lack of an existing demographic for women of color.
  • Ayesha highlighted the difficulty of being taken seriously, encountering questions about why she works despite her husband's income, and noted feeling "crazy" when people comment on her career.
  • She admitted to initially lacking confidence in male-dominated industries, requiring support from a female colleague, but has developed stronger self-advocacy skills in her 30s.

  • Ayesha Curry co-founded the Eat Learn Play Foundation with Steph Curry, aiming to provide quality nutrition, education, and safe places to play for children in the Oakland Unified School District.
  • She launched Sweet July Skin, a brand inspired by her happiest life moments in July, using traditional Caribbean island remedies backed by science.
  • Ayesha addressed the challenges of owning a skincare brand while experiencing uncontrollable skin issues, such as those associated with PCOS.
  • Her products are described as clean, vegan, anti-inflammatory, and non-irritating, designed to manage skin problems.
