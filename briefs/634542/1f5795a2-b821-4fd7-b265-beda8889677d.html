Call Her Daddy

Dave Franco: Blunt Rotations, Body Counts, & Bad Meat

Key Takeaways

  • Dave Franco surprisingly reveals a chaotic past, from a Molly-fueled first date to a secret Disneyland hookup.
  • His strong, collaborative partnership with wife Allison Brie deeply influences his personal and professional life.
  • Franco candidly discusses overcoming extreme shyness to build a diverse and confident acting career.
  • Podcast games unveil hilarious and bizarre anecdotes from his film career and personal interactions.

Deep Dives

Wild Revelations

  • Franco candidly recounts taking Molly with his now-wife Allison Brie on their very first meeting, leading to an 'insane weekend' in New Orleans and an immediate, lasting connection.
  • He admits to having had sex on the Pirates of the Caribbean ride at Disneyland, stating they were discreet despite potential cameras.
  • Franco also revisits a disastrous surprise birthday party thrown by Allison early in their relationship, where he had his first panic attack after consuming a weed cookie amidst an overwhelming crowd and unexpected elements like someone dressed as cocaine.

Partnership Power

  • Franco highlights his positive and easy collaborative dynamic with his wife, Allison Brie, on film projects, noting her talent makes his job simpler, especially when he directs.
  • He reveals his skincare routine is entirely 'stolen' from Allison, reflecting their deep comfort and shared life.
  • The couple's new film sees them play a codependent pair who physically fuse together, a concept Franco felt would translate authentically given their over a decade-long relationship.

Shy Guy Evolution

  • Franco describes himself as 'debilitatingly shy' in middle school, recounting an anxiety-driven moment where he threw a bag of candy at a crush instead of giving it to her.
  • He explains his 'piss boy' nickname from 'Superbad' motivated him to pursue more roles, helping him overcome the public stereotype.
  • His early passion for movies stemmed from working at a video store in 1999, which eventually led him to acting classes that helped him break out of his shell and pursue his diverse career.

Unexpected Anecdotes

  • Franco recalls a 'Neighbors' scene where Zac Efron dislocated his thumb grabbing Franco's crotch, clarifying it was due to force, not an erection.
  • In a game, Franco revealed he married Channing Tatum, 'f-cked' Jonah Hill due to already 'exploring sexuality' with Zac Efron, and saved Zac.
  • He participated in a text game, sending outlandish messages to friends and colleagues, including asking to borrow money for a strip club and sharing STD results, which Alex found a sign of healthy friendship.
