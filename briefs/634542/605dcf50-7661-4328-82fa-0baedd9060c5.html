Call Her Daddy

Kid Cudi: The Pursuit Paid Off

Key Takeaways

  • Early family dynamics and unaddressed grief profoundly shaped Kid Cudi's formative years.
  • Fame’s jarring reality led to self-medication, addiction, and a period of deep suicidal ideation.
  • Rehab offered a pivotal path to confront past trauma, release anger, and embrace self-acceptance.
  • Meeting his wife Lola brought profound peace, completing his journey toward self-love and stability.

Deep Dives

Formative Years

  • Kid Cudi described a happy childhood until his father’s death at age 11, a grief he internalized.
  • He used humor as a class clown to mask inner pain and anger that persisted into his early career.
  • An early vivid memory of his parents' shouting, foreshadowing their divorce at age three, set a tone for family dysfunction in his memoir.
  • His mother's unwavering support for his father during illness inspired Kid Cudi’s desire for a 'ride or die' relationship.

Fame's Shadow

  • The transition from ordinary person to public figure was jarring, leading Kid Cudi to use cocaine to manage anxiety.
  • Early fan interactions felt uncomfortable, making it difficult to discern genuine connections from superficial recognition.
  • Navigating relationships became challenging, with Kid Cudi questioning motives and feeling used by those around him.
  • His 2015-2016 period was marked by constant suicidal thoughts, culminating in the album 'Speeding Bullet to Heaven' as a potential farewell.

Path to Healing

  • A relapse and the desire to avoid past mistakes served as a wake-up call, leading Kid Cudi to seek help in rehab in 2016.
  • In rehab, discussing his past trauma, including his father’s death, felt freeing and helped him process long-held hurts.
  • He confronted and released pent-up anger, realizing his true nature as a good person, which alleviated self-judgment.

Finding Solace

  • Kid Cudi met Lola at Virgil Abloh’s Louis Vuitton show, instantly drawn to her energy and genuine nature, leading to a several-year friendship before dating.
  • He proposed to Lola in a privately rented temple in Kyoto, a moment he described as perfectly imperfect with partially obscured footage.
  • Lola has brought him profound peace and a sense of completion, fulfilling his long-held dream of marriage and a family, and inspiring his new album 'Free'.
