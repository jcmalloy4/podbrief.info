Saweetie: Blocked Numbers & Birkin Bags (FBF)

Key Takeaways

  • Saweetie discussed her intricate nail art, often taking up to 10 hours.
  • She shared unique food preferences, including hot Cheetos on tuna rolls.
  • Saweetie elaborated on balancing her public persona with her private identity.
  • She recounted early career housing challenges using Craigslist sublets.
  • Her evolving perspectives on relationships and infidelity were explored.
  • Saweetie shared anecdotes about persistent ex-partners and gift-keeping.
  • She outlined upcoming music and her goal to star in an action film.

Deep Dive

  • Saweetie discussed her custom, jewel-adorned nail designs.
  • The intricate process can take up to 10 hours.
  • She utilizes this time for work meetings, multitasking during sessions.

  • Saweetie differentiates between her public persona, "Saweetie," and her private self, "Diamante."
  • She discussed reaching a point of peace with her inner self and faith amid public scrutiny.
  • Her grandmother's prayers and concerns over negative press affect her.

  • Saweetie recounted using Craigslist to find sublet rooms due to financial limitations.
  • She emphasized the importance of trusting one's instincts when dealing with overly eager landlords.
  • Her career gained traction following the release of her song 'Icy Girl'.

  • Saweetie believes a partner's infidelity stems from their own issues, not the wronged party's actions.
  • Her ideal partner is financially successful and emotionally intelligent.
  • She noted dating rappers and athletes due to proximity, distinguishing between baseball players (potentially more prone to infidelity due to travel) and football players.

  • Saweetie confirmed she has made the first move in relationships.
  • She described a "bold" high school plan to approach a shy football player.
  • She stated a lack of attraction to athletes, likening it to dating a cousin, due to growing up with many athletes in her family and their demanding schedules.

  • Saweetie shared two instances where ex-partners found her hotel room after breakups and ordered room service.
  • She typically does not reconcile with exes unless they take extreme measures.
  • She keeps "custom one of one" gifts, including a personalized Birkin bag received before dating the man.

  • Saweetie detailed the "most dramatic" way she discovered infidelity: a partner's accusations after her call with a male friend led her to find evidence of him cheating with multiple women.
  • She described feeling sick and numb, initially considering infidelity a dealbreaker, a stance she currently maintains.
  • She observed that a partner's accusations of cheating can reveal their own behaviors.

  • Saweetie confirmed an upcoming album next year, describing it as her "diamond era" after enduring industry pressures.
  • She also has two new Christmas songs, including 'Big Santa' and a softer track.
  • Her career goal is to star in an action film, drawing inspiration from movies like "Tomb Raider" and "Columbiana," expressing a desire to play a strong heroine.
