Call Her Daddy

Heather McMahan: Golf Wives, Hall Passes & Frat Daddies

Key Takeaways

  • Heather McMahan and Alex Cooper discuss personal anxieties and relatable pet-related challenges.
  • Conversations delve into college life at Ole Miss, including sorority rush and dorm experiences.
  • Millennial fashion and beauty debates cover topics like side parts, skinny jeans, and thin eyebrows.
  • Discussions explore footwear preferences, school uniforms, and early career challenges in Los Angeles.
  • Explicit details regarding oral sex techniques and living arrangements with family are shared.
  • A husband's 'hall pass' encounter and gift-giving mishaps provide humorous storytelling.
  • Heather McMahan announces her self-produced comedy special 'Breadwinner' and an upcoming comedy cruise.

Deep Dive

  • Heather McMahan recounts a sleepless night dealing with her dog's explosive diarrhea, causing anxiety and leading to excessive coffee consumption.
  • Alex Cooper describes waking to her dog defecating on the carpet multiple times and her husband's lack of assistance in cleaning.
  • McMahan explains her anxiety stems from being overwhelmed by responsibilities, leading to a feeling of short-circuiting, self-medicated with Benadryl.
  • Both host and guest discuss experiencing increased anxiety as their lives improve, attributing it to having more to lose and heightened responsibilities.

  • The guest recounts her college experiences at the University of Mississippi, including how she ended up there and sorority life.
  • She details the "door song" tradition during sorority rush at Ole Miss, where members formed a "dog pile" to welcome potential new members.
  • The guest reflects on the intense aspects of sorority rush but emphasizes the positive experience of forming friendships and dismisses concerns about hazing.
  • A comparison is drawn between current TikTok trends in college dorms and sororities versus their past experiences, noting social media's impact on expectations.

  • The guest, identifying as an "elder millennial," expresses enthusiasm for discussing millennial trends, starting with the "side part" hairstyle.
  • She recounts finding an old sorority composite photo featuring her deep side part and discusses her struggle with a middle part due to a cowlick.
  • The hosts reflect on childhood fashion and beauty choices, with one recalling a past desire to look emo and the other mentioning a preference for skinny jeans and band t-shirts.

  • The host discusses footwear preferences, advocating for Nike to offer more wide-fit options, particularly for models like the Air Force One, to accommodate wider feet.
  • She recalls wearing tall Uggs with her Catholic school uniform, which included plaid skirts and knee-high socks, and successfully advocated for pants due to cold weather.
  • The host describes her difficulty securing restaurant jobs in Los Angeles after moving there, attributing it to the city's preference for 'porn star' aesthetics over traditional qualifications.

  • The guest discusses her large breasts, noting their naturalness and the aesthetic appeal of her areolae, contrasting this with thin ankles.
  • Both speakers strongly oppose the return of thin eyebrows, with the guest humorously likening it to a "crime against humanity."
  • The conversation debates the return of gaucho pants, with one speaker advocating for them due to their practicality for intimacy, contrasting them with the impracticality of barrel jeans for similar activities.

  • The guest discusses her prolonged stay in her childhood home with her mother and fiancé, attributing it to her mother's well-being and her own financial comfort.
  • She humorously recounts past wilder sexual encounters in her childhood bedroom, with her mother potentially overhearing.
  • Explicit details about oral sex techniques are discussed, including descriptions of specific anatomy and variations in approach, with a humorous comparison to a spouse experiencing diarrhea.
  • The guest clarifies her stance on anal sex, stating she is not opposed to it as an accessory to oral sex, and humorously describes the act of touching or 'tickling' the perineum.

  • The guest expresses frustration with men's lack of understanding regarding desired gifts or experiences, referencing the Sydney Bridge incident as an example of a failed surprise.
  • She recounts her husband's 'hall pass' situation involving model Kate Upton, where he met Upton's husband, Justin Verlander, at a cocktail event instead.
  • The guest details her positive experience attending Taylor Swift's Eras Tour in London, praising the production and her enjoyment of the show.
  • Plans for an upcoming four-day comedy cruise in April are discussed, described as an event at sea with approximately 2,000 fans and other comedians.
