Call Her Daddy

Lisa Vanderpump: Bravo, Drama, & Beverly Hills (FBF)

Key Takeaways

  • Lisa Vanderpump discussed her public persona and how it influenced perception.
  • She detailed her unique connection with Andy Cohen and reasons for exiting RHOBH.
  • Vanderpump revealed knowing about a Bravo castmate's infidelity, but kept silent.
  • She shared her philosophy on holding grudges and reasons for firing employees.
  • Vanderpump offered insights into 'Scandoval' and proposed a future for Vanderpump Rules.
  • She spoke about her parents' 1960s swinging and her own 42-year marriage.

Deep Dive

  • Vanderpump describes her home, 'Villarosa,' as a sanctuary, enjoying downtime activities such as cooking and flower arranging.
  • She maintains high standards for ambiance, including fresh flowers and good wine, but is personally low-maintenance, doing her own nails and makeup.
  • Initially hesitant about joining 'The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills' due to perceived drama, she was encouraged by Jennifer Stallone at 4:00.
  • Her flamboyant public persona, including a love for pink and a pampered dog, contributed to an image of excess, despite her charitable work and long marriage.

  • Vanderpump recounted a rumor that Andy Cohen initially doubted 'The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills,' but her tape convinced him, a sentiment she believes might have annoyed other cast members (9:25, 10:40).
  • She believes Cohen has a strong connection with her, vociferously defending him against recent 'absolute bullshit' allegations at 11:05.
  • Vanderpump enjoys playful interactions with Cohen, noting the sexual innuendos are jokes because he is 'gold star gay' (11:40).
  • She left 'RHOBH' due to personal struggles following her brother's suicide and the show's ongoing drama, stating she drew a line and would not return at 12:24.

  • When asked for advice on dealing with dislike, Vanderpump advises considering that out of billions of people, not everyone will like you; she suggests self-reflection but ultimately encourages moving on (16:18).
  • During a 'Never Have I Ever' game, she stated she has never had a drink thrown in her face, though she acknowledged it as common on Bravo (18:04).
  • Vanderpump identified lying as the quickest way to anger her, followed by lateness.

  • Vanderpump denied prior knowledge of Tom Sandoval and Raquel Leviss's affair, but recalled telling Ariana Madix she would not be comfortable with another woman in a jacuzzi with her husband (22:48).
  • She confirmed knowing a castmate's partner was cheating but chose not to intervene, noting that individuals often reject such truths, and revealing can alienate everyone involved (19:14).
  • Vanderpump contrasted the perceived inauthenticity of some reality TV couples with her own 42-year marriage to Ken Todd, highlighting shared passions like their seven dogs.

  • Vanderpump revealed her parents participated in swinging in the 1960s and shared anecdotes about celebrity discussions regarding anatomy (27:04).
  • Having been married for 42 years, she stated at 28:25 that she does not feel the need to pursue swinging herself.
  • She admits to forgetting secrets rather than intentionally keeping them and does not recall sharing a secret with a castmate who then revealed it on camera (26:02, 29:41).
  • Vanderpump explained her reliance on speaking 'from experience' on reality television, stating her memory is not good enough to lie about events (30:34).

  • Vanderpump highlighted James Kennedy as an entertaining example of authenticity and growth on reality TV, despite perceived missteps (31:39).
  • She does not believe she will reconcile with her former 'Real Housewives of Beverly Hills' castmates, explaining that she doesn't expect a genuine apology (33:41).
  • Vanderpump advocates holding grudges against those who consistently show they don't have your best interests at heart, emphasizing addressing personal distress (34:04, 35:00).
  • Reasons for firing employees include stealing and 'shagging in the back,' issues she clarified were general restaurant occurrences, not specifically on her show (35:51).

  • Vanderpump identifies with Stassi in her 20s and notes her growth, also suggesting Lala and Schwartz as a potential Vanderpump Rules couple (38:10).
  • She expressed empathy for Tom Sandoval's situation post-'Scandoval,' despite his actions, noting the widespread attention it garnered, even at high-profile events (40:51).
  • Vanderpump reflected on the potential dangers of public backlash, referencing her brother's experience with suicidal ideation.

  • Vanderpump proposed a hypothetical scenario for the next season of 'Vanderpump Rules': firing the current cast and starting fresh with new talent at one of her restaurants (43:43).
  • She described her dynamic with Gordon Ramsay as a back-and-forth exchange, admitting she enjoys putting him in his place (47:22).
  • Vanderpump stated the worst aspect of living life on television is lies, while the best is the documentation of beautiful family moments (48:00).
  • Despite fame, her material life remains unchanged, but she values experiences like advocating for animal welfare in Congress and being recognized by the State Assembly (49:00).
