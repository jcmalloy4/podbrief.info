Call Her Daddy

Dove Cameron: Toxic Patterns, Engagement, & Entering 30's

Key Takeaways

  • Dove Cameron shared a positive perspective on aging, challenging societal pressures on women.
  • Childhood traumas, including a friend's murder and father's suicide, profoundly shaped Cameron.
  • Her Disney Channel role provided financial support, necessitating a persona adjustment.
  • Cameron detailed her engagement, emphasizing healing and finding a supportive, communicative partner.

Deep Dive

  • Dove Cameron celebrated her 30th birthday, expressing positive views on aging and feeling more respected.
  • She critiqued societal messaging glorifying youth as a mechanism for female disempowerment, noting her 20s were her "worst years."
  • Cameron discussed overcoming a past fear of not being perceived as 18, influenced by early Disney Channel career culture.
  • She recounted a recent shift, stopping self-harm, processing trauma via therapy and writing, and embracing aging.

  • The death of a childhood friend at age eight accelerated existing marital issues for Cameron's parents.
  • Her parents' relationship was not a "love match," with factors including her father's unacknowledged sexual orientation.
  • An unplanned pregnancy also contributed to the relationship's difficulties.
  • Cameron identified age eight as a pivotal time when many difficult life events began for her.

  • Cameron recounted adopting behaviors like dissociation or "survival mode" during traumatic moments with her father, who struggled with depression and bipolar disorder.
  • She wrote a song about her desperation to escape to Hollywood, viewing it as a haven from her father's inconsistent mental state.
  • While acknowledging his struggles, Cameron stated her childhood included beautiful moments and her mother was supportive.
  • She notes her father's positive qualities, which she now recognizes, are emerging in her as she ages.

  • Cameron took a two-year hiatus from dating after recognizing unhealthy relationship patterns stemming from her childhood.
  • She described her adolescence as difficult, marked by depression and homeschooled isolation.
  • Her desire to move to Hollywood was driven by a perception of it as a place for emotional understanding and opportunity.
  • Cameron's parents' marriage ended after a decade of "slowly burning out," leading to her move to Los Angeles with her mother.

  • Upon moving to Los Angeles for her Disney role, Cameron lived in a small apartment with her recently separated mother.
  • This living arrangement provided her with more freedom to see friends.
  • Her sister also returned home from college around the same time.
  • This period followed a difficult adolescence for Cameron, marked by depression and isolation.

  • The host noted Cameron's consistent attempts to connect with her father, which were often unreciprocated due to his mental state.
  • Cameron received news of her father's death by suicide at age 15 via a phone call from a family friend, triggering an immediate panic attack.
  • She reflected on how sudden, unexpected loss via phone calls can lead to intense trauma and anxiety.
  • Cameron's experiences highlighted the difficulty of reconciling a person's highs and lows when battling bipolar disorder.

  • Experiences of loss and death fostered Cameron's deeper appreciation for loved ones and a more compassionate view of humanity.
  • These experiences made her less fearful of everyday concerns.
  • Cameron legally changed her name from Chloe to Dove as a tribute to her father, who used the nickname "Dovey."
  • This name change served to maintain a connection with him and to feel like his daughter.

  • Cameron clarified public misconceptions regarding her relationship with Damiano David, stating their first meeting was a brief 14-second encounter at the 2022 VMAs.
  • She addressed rumors of "sliding into his DMs," explaining she had publicly tagged his band out of admiration, not as a flirtatious message.
  • Cameron stated her relationship with David began in earnest after they both attended the 2023 VMAs, when both were emotionally ready.
  • She emphasized her practical approach to relationships, preferring in-person meetings over extensive texting.

  • Cameron shared that her partner, Damiano, a "by the books" Capricorn, openly discussed marriage and future plans with her for a significant period.
  • Leading up to the engagement, her partner hinted at the ring design for months, and she speculated on the timing.
  • The proposal occurred during a casual, everyday moment while they were packing, with Cameron in casual attire.
  • She was moved by his explanation that he wanted to marry her for their daily life, including mundane tasks, emphasizing its personal and authentic nature.

  • Cameron stressed the importance of finding a supportive partner and not believing oneself "too damaged" for love, contrasting this with past trauma bonds.
  • She spent two years actively working on herself to prepare for a healthy relationship, shifting her personal narrative.
  • Cameron discussed her new show, "56 Days," a pseudo-sexual thriller based on a novel, featuring a dual-timeline structure.
  • She views nudity in the show as courageous and vulnerable, challenging internalized misogyny and asserting bodies are innocent.
