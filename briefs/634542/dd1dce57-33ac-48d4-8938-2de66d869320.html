Call Her Daddy

Hannah Berner: How to Scare Men (FBF)

Key Takeaways

  • Hannah Berner joined Alex Cooper for a "Call Her Daddy" live tour, navigating chaotic crowd interactions.
  • Berner shared viral anecdotes, including her rejection by the Wisconsin Badgers mascot, Bucky.
  • She detailed negative experiences drinking on reality TV, leading her to stop alcohol consumption during filming.
  • The hosts openly discussed sexual experiences, normalizing imperfections and sharing cautionary tales about intimacy.
  • Conversations touched on dating former athletes, relationship dynamics, and methods women use to deter unwanted attention.
  • Discussions included personal anecdotes about "petty theft" from ex-partners' apartments.
  • The host reflected on ex-partners as career motivators and the role of maternal advice in relationships.
  • Hannah Berner recounted her journey from reality TV to a successful stand-up comedy career and an upcoming Netflix special.
  • The episode explored themes of female empowerment, shared struggles in competitive industries, and mutual support between the hosts.

Deep Dive

  • Host Alex Cooper invited Hannah Berner to join her live tour, specifically for the Boston opening.
  • Cooper sought a comfortable and capable co-host for the event.
  • Berner accepted, viewing the invitation as a "teammate" opportunity and trusting Cooper's non-judgmental approach.

  • Hannah Berner detailed negative experiences with drinking while filming reality television.
  • She stated she stopped drinking after filming concluded due to these experiences.
  • Berner recounted blacking out during filming, leading to a rule where she jumped in the pool to remove her microphone, once requiring an audio technician to retrieve her.

  • The hosts normalized that sexual encounters are not always positive for women, including faking orgasms or enduring discomfort like bleeding.
  • They discussed the emotional impact of faking orgasms and injuries, such as a friend experiencing prolonged bleeding after attempting anal sex without preparation.
  • A cautionary tale advised against using water as a lubricant for anal sex, describing it as painful and potentially bloody.

  • The host recounted a scary encounter with a former athlete, describing him as "psychotic" and the "most ill person" she had met.
  • She detailed a one-night encounter with an attractive hockey player in New York City who denied being in a relationship, only for her to later discover he was engaged.
  • The host also experienced significant pain during sex with the player due to his "large, curved penis."

  • Hannah Berner shared her experience wearing a tie, finding it empowering and comparing the feeling to a salesman or Avril Lavigne.
  • The hosts discussed a past incident involving using photo editing tools, including basic coding methods, to significantly alter their appearance for online content.

  • The host and guest discussed unusual intimate experiences, with one host stating earwax removal was "better than sex."
  • They also explored the ethics of stealing from wealthy ex-partners, with one guest recounting taking an unopened GoPro after suspecting infidelity.
  • A previous "Call Her Daddy" episode topic was revisited, where one host achieved orgasm with legs straight, which the other host confirmed trying.

  • The host discussed maintaining contact with only "British Dave" among ex-partners, humorously suggesting a man blocking her implies he is "still in love."
  • An ex-partner blocked the host on multiple platforms, including after she secured a Netflix special, an event the host framed as a career motivator.
  • She noted that anger can be a motivator, but "true healing" occurs when the reason for the initial anger is forgotten.

  • The guest shared her mother's advice on partner selection, stating she avoids men her mother dislikes.
  • Both hosts discussed the wisdom of older women, referencing Julia Louis-Dreyfus, and advocated for leveraging mothers as an "asset" for advice.
  • The conversation touched on dating double standards, contrasting a man's ability to casually date with a woman's potential for negative labeling.

  • After touring success reported by Variety magazine, Hannah Berner received a call from Netflix regarding a comedy special.
  • Her agent announced the offer, but her partner advised delaying filming to refine her material, a suggestion she followed.
  • Berner withheld the news for a month to internalize the achievement, prioritizing creating "proud work" over seeking immediate public attention.
  • The host praised Berner's personal growth and confidence, noting their shared struggles in competitive fields.
