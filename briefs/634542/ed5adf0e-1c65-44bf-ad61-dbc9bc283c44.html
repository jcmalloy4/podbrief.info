Call Her Daddy

KIM KARDASHIAN

Key Takeaways

  • Kim Kardashian detailed iconic career moments, including her 'break the internet' Paper magazine cover and the family's reaction.
  • She discussed her personal relationships, addressing dating rumors, past infidelity, and her current single status.
  • Kardashian offered insights into the complexities of co-parenting with Kanye West and her public approach to family dynamics.
  • Her business ventures, including Skims, and her journey into a non-traditional law career were highlighted.
  • She shared anecdotes about motherhood and her children's influence, particularly daughter North's humor and creative spirit.

Deep Dive

  • Kim Kardashian identified her 2014 Paper magazine cover shoot, featuring a glass balanced on her backside with padding, as her most iconic 'break the internet' moment.
  • Her mother initially reacted with shock but later accepted a 10% cut from the venture.
  • The family has become desensitized to major media events, such as multiple members appearing on Vogue covers in the same month.
  • Kardashian and Alex Cooper reflected on past insensitive interview questions, particularly regarding physical appearance, that would not be acceptable today.

  • Kardashian dismisses recent false dating rumors, stating they no longer bother her as they once did.
  • She estimates her annual spending on glam to be in the high six figures or low seven figures, prioritizing work-related coverage.
  • Kardashian clarified her hair extensions are glued-on tracks, not K-tips, and her natural hair is shorter.
  • She identified a past photoshoot with then-16-year-old Justin Bieber as potentially misconstrued, despite no inappropriate behavior.

  • Kardashian denied rumors of hooking up with Travis Barker, clarifying their past platonic friendship during his relationship with Paris Hilton.
  • She cited getting married in Vegas without informing her parents, and it becoming public record, as her 'dumbest decision' made on Molly.
  • Kardashian dispelled rumors of a romantic connection with Post Malone, noting their work for Skims and his past performance at Kylie's birthday.
  • She confirmed Kris Jenner and Corey Gamble are still together, addressing breakup rumors.

  • Kardashian identified her stepfather's transition and her mother's subsequent divorce as the family's hardest public challenges.
  • She reflected on the profound impact of losing her father and going through her first divorce at age 22, both occurring in the same year.
  • Kardashian discussed accountability for past relationship decisions and her initial attraction to Kanye West's 'larger-than-life persona' and intelligence.
  • She noted the difficulty of leaving a partner unwilling to make healthy changes, particularly when children are involved.

  • Kardashian reflected on the difficulty of relationship decisions for public figures, acknowledging it can be easier to stay than to leave.
  • She cited feelings of unsafety in a past relationship due to her partner giving away multiple Lamborghinis without her knowledge, creating financial instability.
  • Following her departure from that relationship, she found new projects and strengthened connections in fashion and art, contrary to her initial belief of abandonment.
  • She concluded that a decade-long relationship with four children should not be considered a failure despite multiple divorces.

  • Kardashian stated she is currently single and content, prioritizing her children but open to a truly special person.
  • She believes her 'person' would not be intimidated by her fame and finds herself approachable, with friends often facilitating introductions.
  • Her best qualities as a partner include being a team player, good communication, and support, while meshing her life with someone else's is a challenge.
  • She has shifted from dating primarily music or athlete industries to preferring someone intellectually stimulating, such as a lawyer or scientist.

  • Kardashian identified Tristan Thompson and Scott Disick as her closest co-parents, also speaking positively about Travis Barker.
  • She described co-parenting with Kanye West as difficult but emphasized raising their children full-time and actively pursuing a healthy relationship with him.
  • Kardashian highlighted positive co-parenting examples, such as Tristan's involvement, countering narratives that she 'kidnaps' the children.
  • She maintains a commitment to allowing her children to see their father and fostering positive narratives, despite public criticism.

  • Kardashian identified her extreme closeness with her children, who confide everything in her, as her best quality as a mother.
  • Her home is frequently filled with 8-12 children sleeping over on weekends, reflecting her close relationship with her own.
  • Kardashian revealed Kanye West initially wanted to name their daughter Noriega or Donda, but she preferred unique names, leading to North.
  • North often humbles her mother, recently telling her she needs to remarry because she is 'getting too old' and criticizing the home's 'all beige' decor.

  • Kardashian emphasized that her successful business ventures stem from genuine passion and love for the product, not solely financial gain.
  • The initial launch of Skims was under the name 'Kimono,' which she believed was a clever play on her name but caused backlash and confusion.
  • She quickly pivoted the brand to 'Skims' and donated the original domain, emphasizing respect for culture and business accountability.
  • Kardashian detailed her deep involvement in Skims, including creative direction, fabric selection, color palettes, and overseeing campaigns.

  • Kardashian took the bar exam in July after a four-month hiatus dedicated to studying, expressing a desire to reunite families through legal advocacy.
  • Her motivation to pursue law stemmed from feeling embarrassed by her lack of understanding of political jargon during a clemency meeting at the White House.
  • She detailed her non-traditional six-year path to law, involving self-study, hired tutors, and a law firm job, including passing the baby bar exam.
  • Kardashian discussed balancing acting roles, like Ryan Murphy's offer for 'American Horror Story,' with motherhood and business through efficient time management.
