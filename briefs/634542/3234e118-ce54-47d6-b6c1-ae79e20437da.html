Kristin Cavallari: So You Want to Date an Athlete? (FBF)

Key Takeaways

  • Kristin Cavallari's early reality TV on "Laguna Beach" involved producer manipulation, fostering trust issues.
  • Her divorce from Jay Cutler stemmed from a toxic relationship, a decision taking years to finalize.
  • Post-divorce, she seeks partners who pursue her, identifying insecurity and lack of accountability as deal-breakers.
  • Cavallari balances public life for Uncommon James with a desire for a private, normal existence.

Deep Dive

  • Cavallari initially aspired to broadcast journalism but joined MTV's "Laguna Beach" shortly after starting college at Loyola Marymount.
  • Her mother expressed reservations about her joining MTV, while her father supported the opportunity, unaware of the show's future impact.
  • The move to Laguna Beach in high school, necessitated by trouble in Chicago and family issues, became a positive turning point.
  • She projected confidence on "Laguna Beach" despite internal insecurity stemming from challenging family dynamics and not feeling close to her mother at the time.

  • MTV cameras created an unnatural dynamic for high schoolers, with adult manipulation contributing to trust issues that persist.
  • Producers allegedly created storylines and manipulated situations on "Laguna Beach," including a manufactured conflict between Cavallari and Lauren Conrad.
  • The guest states that MTV should provide therapy for cast members due to the intense and often misleading narratives presented during filming.

  • Producer Adam D'Vello allegedly created false storylines and fed them to tabloids, generating a false public perception of Cavallari.
  • D'Vello reportedly bribed castmates to falsely accuse her of drug use on camera, which was then publicized by Us Weekly, leading to significant conflict.
  • Cavallari clarified that while she partied, she never required rehab, contrasting her experience with the show's portrayal of staged interventions on "The Hills."
  • Tabloids and clickbait sensationalism continue to negatively impact her, leading her to now avoid the press unless it benefits her company, Uncommon James.

  • Kristin Cavallari met former NFL player Jay Cutler, reconnecting a year after initially declining his invitation, and became engaged after eight months.
  • Her prior experience dating professional athletes prepared her for that world, and she notes she avoids musicians.
  • A benefit of dating Cutler was his ability to secure dinner reservations, and she notes she fit in with other players' wives.
  • The hardest aspect of being married to a football player was witnessing his weekly physical injuries, especially after they had children.

  • The guest postponed her wedding and engagement due to the same reasons that led to her eventual divorce from Jay Cutler, emphasizing that people do not change.
  • Her divorce was attributed to an inability to continue in a toxic relationship, not a loss of love, with caution taken due to their three children.
  • While she initially hid some issues from friends, her mother was aware throughout; a close friend only realized the extent after moving to Nashville.
  • The decision to end the marriage took a few years and was primarily due to time passing, as she ultimately "just knew" it was time.

  • She now prioritizes her happiness and aims to set a positive example for her children by ending unhealthy relationships.
  • The guest recounts a difficult recent date with an actor in Los Angeles who was performing stunts in a crowded restaurant, leading to an awkward interaction.
  • Cavallari admits difficulty in ghosting, preferring to be direct about a lack of chemistry, which has resulted in negative reactions from some individuals and their friends.
  • She now uses short drink dates to avoid prolonged uncomfortable situations when no romantic chemistry is present.

  • Following her 10-year marriage, Cavallari now wants to be pursued and courted, stating she will not make the first move on social media.
  • She describes the current dating scene as a "shit show," desiring men to put in more effort.
  • While she prefers not to DM first, she noted that posting a story about attending a musician's show led to him initiating contact.
  • She and the host discussed hypothetical opening lines for initiating contact, considering location and context.

  • Kristin Cavallari and the host agree that shirtless selfies and photos of men holding large fish are undesirable on Instagram profiles.
  • She prefers partners not heavily involved in social media, citing a positive experience meeting a partner with no Instagram account.
  • Non-negotiable traits in a partner include an inability to take accountability and insecurity, as she emphasizes needing someone comfortable with themselves.
  • While dating athletes is not a current priority, she is attracting younger men in their 20s despite being 35 and a mother of three.
