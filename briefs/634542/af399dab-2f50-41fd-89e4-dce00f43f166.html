Call Her Daddy

I’m Not Ready For A Baby…

Key Takeaways

  • Content boundaries evolved naturally as Alex transitioned from sharing explicit dating details to protecting her relationship with Matt, demonstrating how authentic creators must balance public persona with private intimacy.
  • Coming off birth control after 16 years caused severe physical and emotional challenges including vertigo, irregular periods, and potential PCOS, highlighting the significant but often undiscussed health impacts of hormonal contraception.
  • Professional success can conflict with personal timelines - Alex chose to pause trying for children despite hitting career milestones at 30, prioritizing her readiness over societal expectations about when women should start families.
  • Communication and mutual support proved crucial when Alex shared her uncertainty about having children with Matt, who responded with complete understanding and reassurance that they would start a family when she felt ready.
  • Rejecting external timelines and embracing "selfishness" allows women to make authentic life decisions based on personal readiness rather than societal pressure, emphasizing the importance of trusting one's instincts about major life choices.

Deep Dive

Relationship Evolution and Content Boundaries

  • Personal Life Update: Alex Cooper opens by sharing a significant life update with her podcast audience, acknowledging how her content approach has evolved over the years since meeting her husband Matt
  • Anniversary and Origin Story: Alex and Matt recently celebrated their one-year wedding anniversary in Hawaii, marking a milestone in a relationship that began during a pandemic-era work trip to LA after just one prior Zoom meeting
  • Initial Dating Approach: Originally, Alex's podcast was built on openly discussing dating experiences in explicit detail, sharing comprehensive information about men she was dating - an approach she planned to continue with Matt on their first date
  • The First Date:
- Alex was upfront with Matt about her intention to discuss their date on the podcast - Matt was relatively relaxed about her podcasting approach - The date lasted over three hours with both being mutually surprised by their connection - Alex initially viewed the potential relationship as podcast content

The Turning Point: "Sloppy Toppy Delight" Episode

  • Early Relationship Dynamic: After their successful first date, Alex and Matt continued spending time together casually due to geographic distance and different life stages
  • The Controversial Episode: Alex decided to create an extremely graphic follow-up episode detailing their sexual encounter, titled "Sloppy Toppy Delight" with explicit descriptions including details like "cum in his beard" and "pussy eating skills"
  • Strategic Timing Backfires: Alex strategically uploaded the episode while Matt was flying from LA to New York to visit her for her birthday
  • Family Fallout: When Matt landed, he immediately called via FaceTime, revealing that his father Steve had listened to the explicit episode after googling Alex - creating immediate concern about intimate details being shared publicly

Navigating Public vs. Private Dynamics

  • Fundamental Incompatibility: The couple faced a core challenge - Alex was very public while Matt was extremely private, with her podcast stories potentially impacting his personal and professional life
  • Matt's Initial Concerns: Matt was initially worried about being "taken down" by Alex's public persona and the potential consequences of their relationship being broadcast
  • Alex's Internal Shift:
- She began feeling protective of Matt and hesitant to share everything about their relationship - Experienced an internal conflict between her public career and desire for a private relationship - Recognized that sharing relationships for content can blur lines between authenticity and performance

  • Mutual Understanding: Matt eventually expressed full trust in Alex, saying he was "all in" and trusted her judgment about what to share, leading to a more nuanced approach to privacy
  • Relationship Principles: They established key principles including keeping their relationship private, making decisions together, and avoiding performing for public approval or "couple goals" status

Health Journey and Family Planning

  • Fertility Decision: Alex and Matt decided to start trying for children last summer, prompting Alex to go off birth control after being on it since age 16
  • Physical Health Challenges: Coming off birth control caused significant bodily changes:
- Extreme vertigo and dizziness - Emotional fluctuations and passing out during interviews - Skin breakouts and irregular periods - Taking 4-5 months to get her period back with extremely painful cramps - Doctor considering potential PCOS diagnosis

  • Professional Success vs. Personal Timeline: While experiencing health challenges, Alex was also hitting professional milestones:
- Feeling confident as CEO of her company started two years ago - Signing a major new deal and turning 30 - Experiencing creative stimulation and excitement about work

The Decision to Pause

  • Internal Conflict: Alex realized she wasn't ready to have a baby, causing guilt about doubting her previous excitement and anxiety about "losing time" on her imaginary timeline for children
  • Communication Hesitation: She was initially hesitant to share these feelings with Matt - not from fear of his reaction, but because feeling uncertain was unusual for her and she knew Matt was ready to start a family
  • Matt's Support: When Alex finally shared her feelings, Matt responded with incredible support, listening compassionately and assuring her they would have children when the time felt right
  • Mutual Decision: The couple decided to pause their pregnancy plans, prioritizing Alex's health and well-being

Current Life Philosophy

  • Rejecting External Timelines: Alex advocates for not letting external pressures or arbitrary timelines control life decisions, emphasizing the importance of trusting personal instincts about relationships, career, and family planning
  • Embracing the Present: Currently enjoying her "newlywed era," prioritizing personal and professional experiences, traveling, hosting events, and spending quality time with Matt
  • Redefining "Selfish": Alex encourages women to be "selfish" and prioritize their own needs and desires, challenging societal expectations about women's life milestones and emphasizing personal agency
  • Future Motherhood Vision: She wants to be a present, motivated mother like her own mom, recognizing life's unpredictability while believing in enjoying her current life phase before having children
  • Audience Connection: Alex expresses gratitude toward her audience ("daddy gang") and offers continued dialogue through follow-up episodes, feeling more confident about sharing her perspective now compared to a year ago, believing her experience might help other women facing similar decisions
