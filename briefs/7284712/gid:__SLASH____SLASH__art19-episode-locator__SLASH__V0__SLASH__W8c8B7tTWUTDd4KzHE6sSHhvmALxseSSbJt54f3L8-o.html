Adam Carolla Show

Teresa Strasser + Jordan Morris (Carolla Classics)

Overview

  • Adam Carolla shares his profound frustration with municipal services and law enforcement in Los Angeles, arguing they prioritize revenue generation over public safety, as exemplified by his experiences with an unresponsive 911 system and a dismissive court system following damage to his car.
  • The podcast reveals pivotal moments in Carolla's career transition from carpenter to comedian, particularly highlighting validation from celebrities like Henry Winkler who told him he was "gifted" and "touched by God" - recognition that proved transformative for his self-perception.
  • Discussions range across contemporary issues including political campaigns, the "glitter bombing" of candidates, crime stories, and reality television, with Carolla consistently advocating for personal accountability and challenging institutional incompetence.
  • Carolla emphasizes the importance of seeking knowledge from experts and remaining open to criticism, sharing personal examples from his racing experience where implementing professional advice significantly improved his performance.

Content

Podcast Context and Initial Setup

  • This episode of "Cruel Classics" features highlights from a 2012 Adam Carolla Show with guests Greg Fitzsimmons, Allison Rosen, and Brian Bishop
  • Host Giovanni introduces the episode, which begins with Adam Carolla recovering from an injury or medical procedure
  • Carolla is being driven around by his mother (jokingly called "news mom")
  • He discusses his limited mobility, specifically mentioning difficulty drying himself after showering due to a weak left arm

Car Incident and Legal System Frustrations

  • Carolla describes witnessing his car being destroyed while on the phone with Jimmy Kimmel
  • He recounts his frustrating court experience related to the car damage:
- Felt treated as a nuisance rather than a victim - Questioned the court's accounting methods for tracking restitution payments - Felt the system was inefficient and dismissive

Criticism of Law Enforcement and Municipal Services

  • Carolla expresses strong criticism of law enforcement in Los Angeles:
- Describes driving in L.A. as feeling like a criminal experience - Criticizes police for constantly trying to extract money through tickets and penalties - Claims people are afraid to challenge or pass police vehicles - Argues law enforcement treats citizens like a "piggy bank"

  • He discusses perceived government and municipal greed, particularly in Los Angeles:
- Excessive fees and tickets driving people away from cities/businesses - Municipal systems prioritizing revenue generation over public service - Inefficient city services, with parking enforcement being the most efficient due to its revenue potential

The Drunk Driver Incident

  • Carolla shares a specific incident where his wife called 911 after a drunk driver crashed near their house:
- 911 dispatch was reportedly uninterested in responding - Dispatchers asked overly specific questions about the severity of the incident - They seemed unwilling to send a patrol car - His wife was home alone with children - The disoriented person crashed into a stationary object - The drunk driver threw a Jack Daniels bottle into bushes - An OnStar tow truck eventually arrived and helped the drunk driver

  • Carolla expresses extreme frustration about:
- Paying high property taxes without receiving adequate public safety services - The perceived indifference of 911 dispatchers and local law enforcement - Wanting to withhold part of his tax payment as a "security deposit" for non-responsive services

Suggestions for Improving City Services

  • Carolla suggests people need to:
- Demand better service from city workers - Be more aggressive when reporting emergencies (potentially exaggerating details) - Challenge and report bad behavior - "Reclaim the city" by confronting poor service

  • He implies language barriers and cultural tensions contribute to poor service
  • Suggests city workers feel no pressure to improve due to lack of competition
  • Criticizes law enforcement for focusing on minor infractions like traffic tickets

Career Turning Point Story

  • A caller named Jordan from Fresno asks Adam Carolla about his transition from carpentry to comedy
  • Carolla shares a significant career moment at a K-Rock acoustic Christmas event:
- He was backstage as his radio persona "Mr. Burcham" - Henry Winkler (the "Fonz") approached Carolla and gave him high praise - Winkler specifically complimented Carolla's improvisational skills, saying "Nobody can do what you do" - This moment was significant for Carolla, as it was when he was still working as a carpenter and doing comedy part-time

  • Carolla shares additional celebrity validation stories:
- The Fonz (Henry Winkler) told him he was "gifted" and "touched by God" - Duff from Guns N' Roses mentioned that Axl Rose was talking about him - He emphasizes the significance of these moments for an ordinary person - Sees these encounters as peak moments in his life

Medical Discussion

  • A female guest discusses her recent medical procedure:
- Underwent removal of an endometrial polyp - Has endometriosis and was concerned about potential extensive surgical intervention - Signed consent forms allowing for potential removal of multiple internal organs if cancer was discovered - Woke up from anesthesia feeling uncertain about the exact details of her procedure - A DNC (dilation and curettage) was performed - Some cysts were removed during the surgery - She was anxious about potentially losing an ovary or requiring a hysterectomy

Political Discussion

  • The group discusses the Iowa caucuses results:
- Santorum won by 109 votes (29,017 votes) - Romney received 28,908 votes - Ron Paul received 25,025 votes

  • They analyze voter priorities:
- One-third focused on federal budget deficit - 40% prioritized the economy - One-third wanted a candidate who could defeat Obama - 60% identified as evangelical or born-again Christian

  • The speakers express skepticism about budget discussions:
- View budget concerns as a recurring, abstract issue - Characterized as an "idle threat" that never gets resolved - Compared to a parent with no boundaries who doesn't enforce spending limits

"Glitter Bombing" and Demographics Discussion

  • The discussion centers around "glitter bombing" as a form of protest by gay rights activists:
- Rick Santorum was glitter bombed at a campaign event in Iowa - Newt Gingrich was also glitter bombed - An unidentified man threw purple glitter at Santorum, shouting "stop the hate, taste the rainbow"

  • This leads to a discussion about demographics in the United States:
- The speakers debate the percentage of gay and Black populations - They reference a survey suggesting 15-16% of men have engaged in same-sex oral sexual activities - They speculate that the Black population is likely larger than the gay population - They note that race is more visibly identifiable compared to sexual orientation

News Stories and Crime Discussion

  • The group discusses an L.A. arsonist story:
- Both the arsonist and his mother were arrested - The mother was charged with 19 fraud counts, including unpaid breast augmentation - The mother claims her son is mentally ill and suggests he might have been "handed over to the Nazis"

  • They provide a detailed recollection of the infamous North Hollywood bank robbery:
- Robbers had modified AK-47s, armor-piercing bullets, body armor, police scanner, and ski masks - They critique the legal system's inability to arrest suspects for potential criminal intent - They debate about preventative arrest and potential criminal intent

Television and Entertainment Discussion

  • The Bachelor TV show discussion:
- 16th season premiered with 7.74 million viewers - Down 13% from previous year's viewership - Speakers discuss the show's dynamics (women competing for one man, dramatic interpersonal conflicts)

  • Super Bowl advertising:
- Super Bowl ads have sold out at record prices, averaging $3.5 million per 30-second spot - 70 30-second spots have been sold - Last year's Super Bowl was the highest-rated television show in history

  • Personal connections to Jimmy Kimmel:
- Discussion about being invited to Kimmel's Super Bowl parties - Comparison of Kimmel's close-knit family to Corolla's more distant family relationships - Dawson was reportedly banned from Football Sundays for bringing a calzone with a cheese blend Kimmel didn't approve of

Lisa Loeb Interaction Story

  • The host shares a story about a potentially awkward interaction with Lisa Loeb:
- He was interested in asking her out during her appearance to promote a reality TV show about finding a husband - He planned to ask her out on-air without her prior consent - His co-hosts were skeptical about the likelihood of success - The host believed they were at similar career stages - Unexpected revelation: the host claims to have made out with Lisa Loeb's sister about 15 years prior

Personal Growth and Learning Philosophy

  • Carolla emphasizes the importance of learning and being open to advice from those more experienced:
- Shares a personal anecdote about improving racing performance by seeking advice from professional driver Tanner Faust - Highlights the value of implementing expert tips during practice and racing - Stresses the importance of not being defensive about receiving advice - Encourages seeking out people who are more skilled/successful than you

  • He discusses human tendencies that inhibit growth:
- A knee-jerk hostility towards people who know more than them - Defensiveness when someone demonstrates superior knowledge - A resistance to learning and implementing new information

Professional Incompetence Complaints

  • Carolla shares a story about racing Scion cars and receiving commemorative die-cast models with his name repeatedly misspelled
  • He uses this as an example of professional incompetence
  • Raises a rhetorical question about whether job performance has deteriorated over time
  • Suggests there might be too many jobs leading to lower quality work

Sea Turtle Commentary

  • Carolla criticizes sea turtle reproductive behavior:
- Questions why turtles lay eggs far from the ocean - Notes that 99% of hatchlings get eaten by predators before reaching water - Wonders why turtles travel so far from the surf to lay eggs and move extremely slowly - Suggests sea turtles should lay eggs closer to the shoreline to improve offspring survival chances - Expresses that he would intervene to protect baby sea turtles from predatory seagulls

Dinner Etiquette Story

  • Carolla discusses a dining experience involving pork chops, leftovers, and payment etiquette:
- There's a restaurant policy about taking food home to prevent over-ordering when someone announces they're paying - He ate what he believed was Lynette's pork chop, leading to confusion about whose leftovers belonged to whom - He describes eating someone else's partially eaten pork chop as an oddly intimate act - Recounts a previous incident at Mr. Chow's where his wife Lynette insisted on paying the entire bill, despite him being unemployed

Pregnancy and Genetics Discussion

  • A female guest announces she is pregnant with a second child, a boy
  • She already has a son named Nathaniel who is tall, blonde, adventurous, and loves motorcycles and bacon
  • Discussion about "half-Jews" and genetic traits:
- Speculation about a "powerful generation" of mixed-race children - Reduced likelihood of chromosomal abnormalities - Potential genetic advantages from mixed heritage

Little League Coaching Controversy

  • Carolla refuses to get fingerprinted to coach his children's team, viewing the requirement as:
- An overreaching safety measure - Unnecessarily treating all potential coaches as potential criminals - An example of excessive bureaucratic policy
  • He frames his refusal as a principled stand against bureaucratic overreach
  • There's tension with his wife, who suggests his stance might negatively impact their children's ability to participate in sports

Bullying Incident Discussion

  • The group discusses a story about teenage bullying:
- Three 14-year-old girls targeted another high school classmate - They drew swastikas on the victim's house using maple syrup - Left feces on the victim's porch - One of the bullies' mothers drove them to commit the act - John Lovitz (a family friend of the victim) strongly disagrees with the LAPD's assessment that it wasn't a hate crime - Lovitz tweeted a photo of the three girls, calling them out publicly - The victim's father is the son of Holocaust survivors

Thrillist Tour and Flight Experience

  • Matt discusses a road trip called "Tour de Thrillist" with Jordan Morris:
- Traveling from L.A. to New York and creating videos - Cooking with a Top Chef contestant in L.A. - Flying fighter jets in Vegas - Matt became nauseous during the flight and ultimately vomited - Matt jokingly comments on the pilot potentially trying to make him vomit

Zimmerman Case Discussion

  • Brief news segment about the George Zimmerman case:
- Mark O'Meara (Zimmerman's lawyer) filed paperwork to have Judge Jessica Rex Leidler step down - O'Meara cited potential conflict of interest due to case scrutiny - Zimmerman is scheduled to return to court Friday for a bail hearing - The speakers discuss the challenge of finding jurors who are unaware of the case

Celebrity Incidents and Diet Trends

  • Discussion about Pippa Middleton in Paris:
- She was in a convertible with three others - The car's unidentified driver pointed a gun at paparazzi trying to take photos - Comparisons to Princess Diana's fatal paparazzi encounter

  • The KE Diet:
- A medical weight loss method involving a feeding tube inserted through the nose into the stomach - Promises 20 pounds of weight loss in 10 days - Involves a constant drip of protein and fat mixed with water - Diet contains zero carbohydrates and totals 800 calories per day - Patients remain on an outpatient basis, carrying a food bag with them

Technology and Entertainment

  • Discussion about a Coachella performance featuring a Tupac hologram:
- Performed alongside Dr. Dre and Snoop Dogg - Participants speculate on future uses of hologram technology - Potential applications include bringing deceased celebrities to events or reanimating deceased family members
