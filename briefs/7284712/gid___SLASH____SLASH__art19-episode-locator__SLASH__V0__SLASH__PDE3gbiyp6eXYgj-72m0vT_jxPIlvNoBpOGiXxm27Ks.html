Adam Carolla Show

Jason Ellis + Deaf Frat Guy (Carolla Classics)

Key Takeaways

  • Modern society has shifted from hiding narcissistic tendencies to openly embracing them, with media personalities and reality TV figures like the Kardashians normalizing self-promotion and attention-seeking behavior as desirable traits.
  • Successful relationships require partners who are "on the same page" - sharing similar tastes, supporting each other realistically, and knowing when to encourage versus when to provide constructive boundaries, rather than just focusing on political alignment.
  • Career success in creative fields demands extensive preparation and stage time before seeking major opportunities, as industry professionals typically only give one chance - whether in comedy clubs or entertainment casting decisions.
  • Personal safety and violence have become increasingly unpredictable, with seemingly senseless crimes affecting even kind-hearted people, while traditional safety advice (like earthquake protocols) may be less effective than commonly believed.
  • Cultural trends reflect broader societal changes - from men's fashion and body image shifts to the decline of shared experiences like video rental culture, illustrating how technology and social norms continuously reshape everyday life.

Deep Dive

Podcast Introduction and Gratitude

  • This episode of "Cruel Classics" features highlights from Adam Carolla Show #1268 from 2014, with Adam, Allison, and Brian
  • Adam expresses gratitude to listeners for supporting his podcast over the past five years
  • Announces upcoming live shows including Hollywood Improv (March 8th), Redondo Beach Performing Arts Center (March 27th for Legal Defense Fund benefit), and Hollywood Outpost (February 27th)

Personal Observations and Family Dynamics

  • Adam discusses the paradox of wanting different experiences - being busy and wanting to relax versus being bored and wanting to travel
  • Shares a humorous tangent about saunas and temperature settings, plus nostalgic references to old tropes like using chairs to block doors

Family Outing and Brand Loyalty

  • Describes a family trip to Malibu where his wife Lynette bought subs for everyone from one shop, but specifically got their son Sonny a Subway sub from a different location
  • Adam finds this behavior unusual, arguing they were all getting subs already and Sonny should eat from the same place
  • Notes Sonny's brand preference for Subway seems influenced by Olympic athlete commercials and his admiration for Jay Glazer (MMA personality)
  • Also comments on Sonny being barefoot in the restaurant, which he finds odd

Cultural and Social Commentary

Changing Fashion and Body Image Trends

  • Discussion of evolving male body types and fashion choices over time
  • Observes men are now less likely to go shirtless due to weight gain compared to the "lanky" physiques of the 1970s
  • Notes the trend of men wearing beards to complement fuller faces
  • Identifies an emerging trend of going barefoot in public spaces, even restaurants
  • Humorously observes that calves remain the most attractive body part when gaining weight
  • Critiques modern male body types as "stoned and confused"

Media Personalities and Narcissism

  • Brief discussion of Sam Rubin's embarrassing on-air mistake involving Samuel L. Jackson
  • Extended conversation about Piers Morgan's arrival and perception in American media
  • Critique of Morgan as an overhyped media personality, though later acknowledges appreciation for his confrontational interviewing style
  • Broader commentary on how modern media interviews focus more on the interviewer than the interviewee
  • Observation that society has become increasingly tolerant of narcissistic behavior, with people attracted to confident, self-promoting personalities
  • Notes societal shift "from pretending we weren't all insane narcissists" to openly embracing it, exemplified by figures like the Kardashians

Personal Anecdotes and Relationships

High School Friend Reunion

  • Adam reconnects with old friend Chris in Las Vegas, who brought his brother Greg
  • Greg has a medical condition requiring a tracheostomy and communicates using a device held to his neck that produces robotic-like speech
  • Despite the unusual communication method, Greg's speech remains intelligible

Job Interview Insights

  • Caller Jared from Chicago discusses interviewing at NBC for a snowboarding event coordinator position
  • Adam provides practical job search advice: if not hearing back after two weeks, likely not selected
  • Discusses subtle signs of genuine interest from interviewers and suggests exploring opportunities with other networks

Industry Experience and Casting Stories

  • Contrasts hiring approaches between construction (word-of-mouth, practical skills) and entertainment industries
  • Shares detailed casting story from a CBS pilot where they hired an actor from Montreal based solely on video submission
  • Despite team preferring a short, brown-haired kid with great chemistry, network chose a blonde alternative
  • Years later discovered the originally preferred kid became successful in "Diary of a Wimpy Kid" movies
  • Expresses frustration with producers' decision-making and lack of accountability

Relationship Advice and Communication

  • Discusses importance of being a "good person" in relationships beyond political differences
  • Emphasizes value of having similar tastes and being "on the same page"
  • Good partners should support you, be realistic, bring out your best version, and "rein you in" when needed
  • Explores communication differences between men and women, particularly around giving and receiving advice
  • Suggests men might be more receptive to tips in sports-like environments where coaching is normalized
  • Notes women may perceive corrections as attempts to assert dominance

Specific Relationship Scenarios

  • Caller Michael discusses moving in with girlfriend and her tendency to quickly say "no" to suggestions
  • Conversation about dealing with someone who has a "no" mentality and the importance of tone when offering help
  • Later caller Jim seeks advice about reconciling with ex-girlfriend after three-month relationship
  • Hosts discuss dynamics of relationship reconciliation and strategic assessment of intentions

Entertainment and Media Analysis

Movie Discussions

  • Extended discussion of 2014 RoboCop remake directed by Jose Padilla
  • Notes the PG-13 rating versus original's R-rated violent style
  • Contrasts with original 1987 Paul Verhoeven film known for violent content and satirical approach
  • Considers it unremarkable with 50% Rotten Tomatoes rating

Harold Ramis Tribute

  • Notes Harold Ramis's death at 69 from autoimmune disease
  • Strong recommendation for "Groundhog Day" as a "perfect movie"
  • Dan Aykroyd's statement praising Ramis as a "brilliant, gifted, funny friend"

Media Format Preferences

  • Critique of formal interview setups, preferring casual at-home interviews like Johnny Carson
  • Criticism of "Cribs" TV show for staged luxury items and unrealistic home presentations
  • Discussion of changing wealth representation from 1980s stereotypes to modern figures like Mark Cuban

Nostalgic Cultural References

  • Extended conversation about video rental culture, VHS tapes, and Blockbuster
  • Discussion of late fees, rewinding practices, and battery-operated VCR rewinders
  • Anecdote about woman arrested in 2021 for not returning 2005 VHS rental
  • Humorous observations about labeling tapes with China markers

Comedy and Career Development

  • Advice to comedian Frank about career locations
  • Emphasizes that comedy success requires logging stage time, similar to pilot training
  • Starting in smaller clubs allows for more stage minutes and skill development
  • Industry professionals may only give comedians one chance, so preparation is crucial

Sports and Physical Activities

Earthquake Experiences and Safety

  • Adam describes experiencing a 4.4 magnitude earthquake at 6:25 AM near Encino
  • Shares skepticism about traditional earthquake safety advice (hiding under desks, doorways)
  • References 1994 earthquake as pivotal moment when he was working as carpenter at "Bodies in Motion" gym
  • Describes pulling partner Lynette and dog on top of him for protection during recent quake

Boxing and Fighting Background

  • Adam participated in Golden Gloves at age 18-19 in 1984, fighting three times
  • Trained at Olympic gym in East LA, felt he started competing too soon
  • Discusses weight cutting difficulties and current weight around 210 pounds
  • Shares experience of emotional responses after being knocked out, including crying in shower as physical response to head trauma

Extreme Sports Discussion

  • Jason Ellis discusses his entry into radio and skateboarding background
  • Detailed account of breaking world record for skateboard drop (14-foot ramp, estimated 16-foot actual drop)
  • Vivid description of Jake Brown's severe X Games mega ramp crash
  • Discussion of safety insights: when approaching ramp "out of shape," athletes should step off immediately
  • Ellis describes challenging desert gap stunt and psychological aspects of extreme skateboarding

Personal Tragedy and Violence

  • Adam shares story of close friend Billy Burchette's murder in Colorado
  • Burchette was bass player in Adam's first band, described as kind person who "brings in strays"
  • 54-year-old victim was stabbed multiple times and burned by 22-year-old roommate Thomas Kevin Leperch
  • Suspect had responded to Craigslist ad, motive related to rent disputes
  • Adam expresses frustration with seemingly senseless modern violence

Boxing Violence Anecdote

  • Story of "Harlem Hammer" boxer who punched opponent after 9/11 benefit fight
  • Same boxer later killed Max Kellerman's brother after being offered place to stay
  • Discussion of post-fight emotional volatility and potential for reflexive aggression in concussed fighters

Podcast Logistics and Promotions

  • Multiple references to upcoming events including San Francisco podcast event
  • Mentions of show personalities (Alison Rosen, Brian Bishop, Mike August)
  • References to Mangria tasting event in Westminster, Colorado
  • Jason Ellis segment includes promotion of Sirius XM channel 41 and Ellismania.com

Miscellaneous Cultural Observations

  • Discussion of March Madness and criticism of excessive sports media coverage
  • Brief mention of Malaysian Airlines Flight 370 theory from Asian publicist
  • Conversation about "JV" versus "all balls" categorization of various cultural elements
  • Commentary on holiday drinking culture (St. Patrick's Day, New Year's Eve, Cinco de Mayo)
  • Observations about nature's power, including drone footage of Niagara Falls and hawk attacking seagull
