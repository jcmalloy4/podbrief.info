Capital Allocators – Inside the Institutional Investment Industry

Dave Thornton – Unlocking Venture Access Through Stock Options at Vested (EP.469)

Key Takeaways

  • Vested offers liquidity for startup employee stock options, addressing the issue of abandoned equity.
  • The platform proactively sources deals by monitoring employees leaving preferred companies.
  • Vested's investment strategy focuses on diversified exposure to the top 20% of venture-backed startups.
  • A proprietary 'data moat' and accurate pricing model are key to Vested's competitive advantage.
  • The company's long-term vision includes developing private market indexing and providing extensive liquidity data.

Deep Dive

  • Dave Thornton built trading and risk models at a Citigroup internal hedge fund before moving into entrepreneurship.
  • He developed an illiquid asset pricing model, drawing lessons from his hedge fund experience.
  • An anecdote from his second startup, a healthcare analytics business, highlighted employee struggles with equity management, a key insight for Vested.
  • Earlier ventures included machine learning models for NBA teams and algorithms for poker and daily fantasy sports.

  • Vested initially launched as an equity education platform, providing free content and tools to attract startup employees.
  • The goal was to later offer financial products and referrals, such as mortgages and wealth management.
  • About 18 months in, users primarily sought capital to exercise stock options before their 90-day post-employment window closed.
  • This emerged as the dominant use case, despite initial hypotheses of other capital needs like home down payments.

  • Many startup employees struggle with managing their equity, often abandoning stock options even with financial incentives.
  • Vested observes diverse signals from employees regarding their stock options, highlighting a common challenge.
  • The guest notes that slow feedback loops in private markets over the last three years complicate hypothesis testing on employee signals.

  • Vested's current sourcing method is entirely proactive, identifying employees of preferred companies who have recently left.
  • The platform monitors job websites like LinkedIn for employees leaving Vested's preferred list of companies.
  • Vested automatically reaches out to these individuals when an end date is posted.
  • Long-term, Vested aims to provide value throughout an employee's startup equity lifecycle, re-engaging in equity education to reach people earlier.

  • Vested utilizes regression models for company assessment, avoiding large language models (LLMs) for this purpose.
  • The portfolio naturally weights towards Series A through F+ stages, with slight underweights in the earliest and latest stages.
  • Diversification is emphasized due to the difficulty of picking winners in early and mid-stage venture capital.
  • Sector weighting generally aligns with the broader venture market, primarily tech, with significant allocations to healthcare and smaller positions in energy and financial services.

  • Vested identifies top-tier venture capital firms by analyzing historical cash-on-cash returns, moving beyond brand names.
  • An algorithm determines the cutoff for investment based on these returns, in response to market volatility and risks.
  • The biggest risk to Vested's strategy is identified as increased competition from well-funded asset management firms.
  • Vested aims to build a 'data moat' through proprietary data collection and an accurate pricing model to maintain competitive advantage.

  • The market for abandoned stock options is estimated at $200 to $300 billion over a market cycle, indicating substantial scaling potential.
  • Vested is building its brand to become the go-to platform for startup employees seeking liquidity, starting with proactive outreach.
  • Vested aims to partner with companies to offer employee liquidity programs, highlighting recruiting and retention benefits.
  • The guest anticipates potential pushback from primary venture funds in the future as Vested's scale grows.

  • Vested envisions a future business phase focused on indexing private markets, providing liquidity and data to secondary markets.
  • The guest notes the current anemic state of private secondary markets, characterized by limited trading volume.
  • Vested believes its extensive data set on private companies can characterize startups for buyers, leading to a profitable index business.
  • This vision draws parallels to the historical development of public market indices and recent industry moves like BlackRock's acquisition of Preqin.
