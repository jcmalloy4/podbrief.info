Capital Allocators – Inside the Institutional Investment Industry

Jack Kokko – Building the Google of Finance at AlphaSense (EP.461)

Key Takeaways

  • AlphaSense addresses financial analysts' inefficiencies by providing AI-powered research reports.
  • The platform leverages advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance market intelligence capabilities and search accuracy.
  • AlphaSense's 'AI interviewer' product allows scalable expert insights and automated channel checks.
  • The company envisions an 'always-on intelligence machine' proactively monitoring market information 24/7.

Deep Dive

  • Jack Kokko, CEO of AlphaSense, developed the platform to address his frustrations as an investment banking analyst at Morgan Stanley in London and San Francisco.
  • He noted inadequate tools led to missed information, stress, and an inability to consume data at the required scale and speed.
  • The initial vision for AlphaSense was a semantic search engine, nicknamed 'Google for Finance', designed to efficiently find specific data points and topics.

  • AlphaSense initially used simpler AI for tasks like sentiment and document classification, requiring years of manual data tagging and training before LLMs.
  • Core data sources include public information such as SEC filings, global exchange filings, and earnings call transcripts.
  • Key acquisitions, including TIGUS for expert transcripts, expanded AlphaSense's proprietary content library with insights from customers, suppliers, and private company data.

  • AlphaSense allows users to ask natural language questions across a vast document repository, providing narrative answers with granular citations to original documents.
  • The platform provides information from high-quality sources, enabling users to see data origins and gain comprehensive views, saving significant research time.
  • This enhances research efficiency, breadth, and decision-making confidence, simultaneously addressing quality, speed, and trust.

  • Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT are considered a breakthrough, enabling AlphaSense to refine its platform by shifting from keywords to full prompts for improved accuracy.
  • AlphaSense launched an AI interviewer designed to replicate buy-side firm expert interviews, a high-friction task, enabling new content sets and monthly channel checks across industries.
  • This groundbreaking AI interviewer can engage in sophisticated conversations with human experts to gain nuanced market insights more scalably and efficiently.

  • Leveraging LLMs involves technical challenges, including selecting appropriate models, managing context absorption for lengthy research reports, and enabling dynamic questioning by the AI.
  • AlphaSense employs an LLM-agnostic orchestrator, allowing systematic testing and deployment of various leading models and adaptation to new breakthroughs.
  • Their process involves evaluating models by both humans and other LLMs, comparing performance against established metrics to determine optimal model usage.

  • AlphaSense's customer base has evolved from initial hedge fund users to a broad range of corporate clients in investor relations, strategy, and competitive intelligence.
  • The company is evolving its unit costs and pricing models due to the integration of LLM intelligence and the operational costs of its 'intelligence factory.'
  • Pricing models are moving towards more flexible and enterprise-level structures to accommodate the diverse information access needs of its expanding client base.

  • AlphaSense's primary metric is recurring revenue growth, which is performing well and attracting investor interest, despite increasing costs associated with AI and language models.
  • The company maintains strong growth margins, allowing it to absorb these AI-related expenses while accelerating overall growth.
  • CEO Jack Kokko has taken a hands-on role as head of product, directly managing more reports to remove roadblocks and enable the 2,000+ employee team to meet ambitious growth goals.

  • AlphaSense aims to build an 'always-on' intelligent machine, accessible to financial firms, banks, consultancies, and corporations.
  • This system will proactively monitor portfolios and analyze information 24/7, providing clients with crucial insights around the clock.
  • CEO Jack Kokko envisions an AI-powered system that autonomously identifies and researches emerging issues by generating expert calls and analyzing new information.
