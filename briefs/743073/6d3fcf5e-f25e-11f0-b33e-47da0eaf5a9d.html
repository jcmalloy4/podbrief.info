My Hot Take On Trump's Accomplishments So Far

Key Takeaways

  • Trump secured an Israel-Gaza peace deal and acted against Iran's nuclear facilities in 2025.
  • His administration executed a "police action" in Venezuela and achieved the closure of the southern border.
  • Trump advocated for eliminating the filibuster but faced concerns regarding increased Chinese student visas.
  • The administration lowered prescription drug prices and actively challenged Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives.
  • Decisions concerning AI chip sales to China, worldwide tariffs, and easing marijuana restrictions sparked debate.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump initiated a peace deal between Israel and Gaza in 2025, seen as promoting regional stability.
  • This move aimed to normalize relations between Israel and its neighbors.
  • Trump's actions against Iran's nuclear facilities were cited as contributing to a safer global environment.

  • Trump closed the southern border, an achievement noted for its speed and being overlooked by media.
  • The host disagreed with Trump's reported decision to double visas for Chinese students, citing espionage concerns.
  • Criticism was raised regarding Trump's acceptance of a new Air Force One from Qatar, questioning its benefits and potential influence.

  • Trump lowered prescription drug prices, addressing the issue of high U.S. costs due to funding global research and development.
  • He negotiated 'favored nation status' for drug prices, with a preference expressed for competitive healthcare markets.
  • Trump's actions to slow down Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives were praised as positive.

  • Trump's decision to allow advanced AI chips to be sold to China was questioned, with its rationale deemed unclear.
  • He implemented worldwide tariffs, interpreting them as a strategy to create fairer international trading systems.
  • These tariffs were also intended to counter China's influence and strengthen America for a potential Cold War.
