The Andrew Klavan Show

My List of The Best–and Worst–007 Movies

Key Takeaways

  • The host ranked James Bond films from various eras, starting with Sean Connery.
  • 'From Russia With Love' and Daniel Craig's 'Casino Royale' received top film ratings.
  • Critiques included Roger Moore's Bond portrayal and fatigue with Daniel Craig's series.

Deep Dive

  • 'From Russia With Love' was rated 'S' as the best Sean Connery film by the host.
  • 'Thunderball' received a 'B+' due to its underwater scenes, while 'You Only Live Twice' was rated 'B-'.
  • George Lazenby's 'Her Majesty's Secret Service' was rated 'B' for being exciting and featuring Blofeld.
  • Sean Connery's return in 'Diamonds Are Forever' was assessed as 'B-'.
  • Roger Moore's 'Live and Let Die' was rated 'C', and 'Moonraker' a 'B'.

  • Roger Moore's final Bond film, 'A View to a Kill,' was found decent despite earlier entries.
  • Pierce Brosnan's portrayal of Bond received praise from the host.
  • Comedy writer Bruce Fierstein was credited for successful Bond scripts, including 'GoldenEye' and 'Die Another Day'.

  • 'Casino Royale' with Daniel Craig was identified as the greatest James Bond film, lauded for its loyalty to the book.
  • The host praised Craig's portrayal of the character's violence in 'Casino Royale'.
  • 'Quantum of Solace' was deemed terrible, and 'Skyfall' focused on a personal rather than global threat.
  • The host stopped watching Daniel Craig's Bond films after 'No Time to Die' due to series fatigue.
  • A 1967 spoof of 'Casino Royale' starring Woody Allen and Peter Sellers was described as unfunny.
