What The Right Must Build To Encourage Conservative Filmmaking w/Dallas Sonnier

Key Takeaways

  • Dallas Sonnier detailed his filmmaking approach for "Frontier Crucible," a traditional Western.
  • Sonnier advocates for conservatives to build a film industry presence amid Hollywood shifts.
  • Filmmakers should prioritize quality and authentic character portrayal over pandering.
  • Conservative investors must adapt their mindset and overcome distribution challenges.

Deep Dive

  • Dallas Sonnier describes 'Frontier Crucible' as a traditional Western inspired by John Ford and 1970s counterculture films.
  • The film aims for a pure, traditional feel with a moral, stoic hero and is based on Harry Whittington's novel 'Desert Stakeout'.
  • It was meticulously adapted and filmed extensively in Monument Valley, leveraging the iconic landscape.

  • Guest Dallas Sonnier ensures quality for Daily Wire films, securing financing for low-budget projects.
  • He successfully cast notable actors like Armie Hammer and William H. Macy in films like 'Frontier Crucible'.
  • The film 'Frontier Crucible' was made with a budget of $1.4 million.

  • Dallas Sonnier advises aspiring conservative filmmakers to give audiences "what they need" rather than "what they want," citing success with films like 'Muzzle' and 'Dragged Across Concrete'.
  • He discussed creating complex conservative characters and negotiating studio fees.
  • This approach allowed 'Frontier Crucible' to feature actors like William H. Macy and Thomas Jane on a $1.4 million budget.

  • Sonnier notes many in Hollywood are unemployed and political discussions are waning, even among left-leaning individuals.
  • He identifies a declining "woke" culture as an opportunity for conservatives to establish a strong film industry presence.
  • He advocates for investment in film schools and production to counter "morally bankrupt, politically driven movies" amid industry turmoil.

  • The guest encourages conservatives to study film history, classic movies, and filmmaking techniques to effectively reclaim the industry.
  • He emphasizes the need for more conservative talent, noting the current "bench is short."
  • Effective storytelling requires authentically portraying men as men and women as women.

  • Conservative investors' focus on principal and rate of return differs from liberal investors' pursuit of billion-dollar profits and world-changing impact.
  • This mindset, combined with a lack of an established ecosystem and distribution platforms, creates hurdles for conservative filmmakers.
  • 'Frontier Crucible' faced distribution challenges due to its content and casting decisions.
