Inside The Startup Launching AI Into Space

Key Takeaways

  • StarCloud launched the first NVIDIA H100 GPU into orbit for space data center testing.
  • The company aims to build 40-megawatt orbital data centers, utilizing constant solar power and space cooling.
  • StarCloud developed and tested its initial satellite design in just 15 months, demonstrating rapid prototyping.
  • This approach seeks to alleviate terrestrial energy and water demands for massive data compute.
  • Major tech firms, including Google, SpaceX, and Amazon, are also exploring orbital data centers.

Deep Dive

  • StarCloud successfully launched a satellite with an NVIDIA H100 GPU into orbit.
  • This marks the first deployment of such a powerful terrestrial GPU operating in space, a prototype 100 times more powerful than previous space computers.
  • Starcloud 1 was launched on November 2nd via SpaceX's Bandwagon 4 rideshare mission, aiming to test modern data center hardware in orbit.

  • Co-founder Philip Johnston's background spans software engineering, applied math, and theoretical physics.
  • StarCloud's inspiration stems from societal demand for energy and the slow pace of terrestrial infrastructure development.
  • The company pivoted from exploring space-based solar power to orbital data centers due to launch cost adjustments.
  • Break-even launch cost calculations were revised from $50/kg to $500/kg, making orbital data centers a more feasible concept.

  • StarCloud rapidly developed its first demo satellite in under two years, showcasing significant prototyping speed.
  • The co-founders possess complementary expertise, including extensive data center and SpaceX experience, and a decade in satellite design for missions like NASA's Lunar Pathfinder.
  • They designed and built the satellite structure, compute module, and payload entirely in-house.

  • StarCloud achieved a significant milestone by having a satellite design built, ready, and tested in just 15 months.
  • This timeframe is notably faster than the approximately four years typically required for similar startups.
  • The rapid progress was facilitated by intense work and in-house development of the compute module and antennas.
  • The first satellite, Starcloud 1, is a 60kg payload the size of a small fridge, featuring an NVIDIA H100 GPU.
