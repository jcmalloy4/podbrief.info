INTRODUCING: We Need A Fourth with Brian Baumgartner, Cooper Manning and Kenny Mayne

Key Takeaways

  • Will Arnett introduced the hosts of the new golf podcast 'We Need a Fourth'.
  • The hosts discussed their varying golf skills and passion for the game.
  • 'We Need a Fourth' aims to explore guests' full personalities beyond golf topics.
  • Actor Chris Pratt sustained a back injury during a golf game in New Jersey.
  • The 'We Need a Fourth' podcast premiered September 15 and releases new episodes every Monday.

Deep Dive

  • Kenny Mayne is noted as the most enthusiastic of the 'We Need a Fourth' hosts but is still learning the game.
  • Cooper Manning prioritizes playing golf with people he enjoys over the quality of the course.

  • The 'We Need a Fourth' podcast frequently goes on tangents beyond golf to understand the whole person.
  • The hosts aim to create a relaxed, social atmosphere for their conversations.
  • Cooper Manning describes golf as a form of meditation and a way to disconnect from daily stresses.
  • Will Arnett observes that extended golf play uniquely allows one to truly get to know another person.

  • Chris Pratt injured his back on a New Jersey golf course during a game with producer Mark.
  • The injury caused Pratt to withdraw from the game prematurely.

  • Chris Pratt was later found lying on the ground behind the 18th green, appearing to have waited for hours.
  • A client was injured by a golf ball during another incident discussed by the hosts.
  • A host shared a personal anecdote about wearing a leg brace due to a past ankle injury.
