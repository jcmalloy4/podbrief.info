Small Town Dicks

Episode

Key Takeaways

  • A kindergarten teacher reported sexual assault by a county deputy sheriff, creating a complex conflict of interest case.
  • The victim, Victoria, faced challenges reporting due to shame; a 2.5-week delay impacted forensic evidence collection.
  • The suspect, Darren, a 47-year-old corrections deputy, denied non-consensual acts and attempted to manipulate the narrative.
  • Investigators used disclosure witnesses and recorded phone calls to corroborate Victoria's account and gather evidence.

Deep Dive

  • The episode introduces a "two-part doozy" case from Detective Robert, described as a frequent guest and passionate investigator.
  • Guests include Detective Dan, Detective James, and Detective Robert, welcomed as an honorary co-host due to frequent appearances.
  • The podcast format involves personal accounts from investigating detectives, with names and details altered for privacy.

  • The case became a "conflict case" because the suspect, Darren, was a deputy sheriff in an adjacent county, creating a conflict of interest.
  • Both the suspect's county and the speaker's District Attorney's office declined prosecution; the State Department of Justice assigned three deputy attorneys general.
  • Darren, a 47-year-old corrections deputy assigned to court security, possessed extensive courthouse connections.

  • Victoria, a kindergarten teacher, agreed to watch her ex-boyfriend Bob's house and dog with his friend Darren.
  • She states Darren unexpectedly arrived, she let him in to watch a movie, but he then aggressively kissed her and commented on Bob's infidelity.
  • Victoria recounts being subjected to unwanted sexual acts by Darren, attempting to escape, expressing distress, and later reporting STI concerns.

  • Victoria left Bob's house, washed her clothes, and reported no contact with Darren for 2.5 weeks following the assault.
  • She revealed Darren, a deputy sheriff, allegedly engaged in sexual misconduct at the courthouse with a judge and other female employees.
  • Victoria reported the assault to a sexual assault hotline approximately two weeks after it occurred, prior to speaking with Bob.

  • Victoria did not receive a sex assault kit as she reported 2.5 weeks later, past the typical 84-hour window, challenging forensic evidence collection.
  • Darren, as a law enforcement officer, was aware of legal strategies and Miranda rights, suggesting he would likely remain silent if questioned.
  • While internal bodily evidence may be lost, investigators instruct victims to provide clothing worn during the assault for potential DNA evidence.

  • Victoria's decision to come forward was motivated by her belief Darren stalked and planned the attack, a desire to prevent future victims, and her own stress.
  • Bob was absent for two weeks; the assault occurred near the end of that period after Victoria observed a suspicious vehicle before Darren emerged.
  • A week-long bureaucratic delay after Victoria's initial report limited forensic opportunities, as Bob had disposed of the couch.

  • The investigation emphasized disclosure witnesses—individuals Victoria informed about the incident—as crucial due to the lack of DNA evidence.
  • Victoria reached out to numerous people, providing consistent text messages and voicemails; her sister was a key disclosure witness.
  • While hotline personnel are confidential, phone records showing call dates and times to the hotline served as corroborating evidence.

  • Four days after the case was taken, Victoria, diagnosed with STIs, confronted Darren in a recorded phone call, identifying him and Bob as her only recent partners.
  • Darren framed the assault as consensual, downplayed its severity, and advised Victoria not to inform Bob, later calling it a "perfect storm."
  • He dismissed Victoria's STI claims by admitting he had them since high school, asserting their perspectives differed, and claiming he did not force her.

  • Victoria informed Darren she wanted no further contact, a statement reinforced by state law making continued communication a misdemeanor.
  • Victoria's sister, beautician, teaching partner, and an ex-boyfriend all provided consistent accounts, corroborating her allegations.
  • Detectives arranged for Bob to call Darren; Darren repeatedly offered to visit Bob's house during a 45-minute call, ending abruptly as he arrived.
