Ep. 2256 - Is Trump WINNING The Tariff Wars?

Key Takeaways

  • Gina Carano's settlement highlights Disney's shift toward less "woke" content after box office underperformance.
  • President Trump's aggressive new tariffs create economic uncertainty and harm American consumers.
  • AI is viewed as Western economies' last hope for productivity growth despite bubble concerns.
  • The Democratic Party's increasingly radical agenda includes controversial constitutional and social proposals.

Deep Dives

Disney's Pivot

  • Gina Carano settled her lawsuit against Disney and Lucasfilm, backed by Elon Musk, following her 2021 social media firing.
  • Disney is reportedly shifting to more moderate content after recent "woke" films like "Jungle Cruise" and "Strange World" underperformed.
  • Successful films such as "Encanto" and "Inside Out 2," which avoided social messaging, suggest a business-driven move away from politically charged themes.

Tariff Concerns

  • President Trump's new tariffs, up to 35% on Canada and 20-25% on India, now exceed Smoot-Hawley era rates, creating business uncertainty.
  • The speaker argues these tariffs are primarily paid by American consumers, making them counterproductive, especially against allies like Canada.
  • While tariffs generate significant revenue, the speaker suggests better strategies to counter China involve free trade alliances and secured supply chains, not tariff wars.
  • The economy's true potential is seen as hampered by tariff volatility, despite other positive Trump administration policies.

AI's Economic Hope

  • AI is considered the primary avenue for Western economic growth, despite current stock market gains being concentrated in big tech and concerns over a potential AI bubble.
  • While transformative advancements like OpenAI's GPT-5 show promise, the speaker anticipates a slower, more challenging integration of AI into broad business productivity.
  • The U.S. is prioritizing AI development to compete with China, focusing on job creation and energy exploration to support AI's high demands.

Radical Agendas

  • The Democratic Party is described as moving further left, with figures like James Carville proposing adding states and Supreme Court judges and eliminating the filibuster.
  • The discussion highlights "luxury beliefs" like "defund the police" and certain Gaza conflict narratives, arguing they signal virtue for the affluent while negatively impacting the less fortunate.
  • President Trump's proposal to exclude illegal immigrants from the census count, citing the 14th Amendment, reflects a broader debate on who should be counted for representation.
