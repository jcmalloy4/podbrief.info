The Ben Shapiro Show

Ep. 2252 - Texas Democrats RUN AWAY…Plus, The REAL Gaza Starvation

Key Takeaways

  • Texas Democrats' quorum break highlights the pervasive, bipartisan nature of gerrymandering as a political tactic.
  • Hamas weaponizes aid and starves hostages to generate propaganda, blaming Israel for Gaza's humanitarian crisis.
  • President Trump's firing of the BLS commissioner raises concerns about the politicization of crucial economic data.
  • The Democratic Party's embrace of radical socialist agendas is evident in rising progressive candidates and policy proposals.

Deep Dives

Quorum Politics

  • Shapiro critiques Texas Democrats for breaking quorum to block redistricting, calling it a desperate political move.
  • He argues gerrymandering is a long-standing, common political practice used by both parties across states like California.
  • The host emphasized that such tactics reflect partisan struggles over electoral advantage, not a unique ethical failing.

Gaza Propaganda

  • Hamas deliberately starves Israeli hostages, including one forced to dig his own grave, for manipulative propaganda.
  • The host contended Hamas diverts humanitarian aid, preventing distribution and even shooting civilians seeking assistance.
  • Hamas seeks international recognition for a Palestinian state by fabricating a humanitarian crisis, blaming Israel.
  • Shapiro proposed Israel establish protected aid zones and annex territory daily until Hamas surrenders and releases hostages.

Data Trust

  • President Trump fired the BLS commissioner after a weak jobs report, attributing negative data to political manipulation.
  • Shapiro explained that job report revisions are routine, occurring due to incomplete survey data, not necessarily manipulation.
  • Critics, including Governor Jared Polis, viewed the dismissal as "shooting the messenger," undermining public trust in official statistics.
  • The host argued the action was politically unwise, fostering suspicion that economic data is manipulated for political gain.

Leftward Shift

  • The speaker highlighted Zoran Mamdani, a "commie" candidate leading New York polls, proposing radical policies like abolishing private property.
  • Mamdani's platform, supported by Democratic Socialists, includes providing free gender-affirming care nationwide via telehealth.
  • Shapiro noted the Democratic Party's overall "apex state" leftward shift, evidenced by foreign policy stances and radical elements gaining prominence.
