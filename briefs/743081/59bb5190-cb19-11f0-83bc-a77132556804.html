Do You Know About This Secret Military Game?

Key Takeaways

  • The annual Secretary's Cup football game highlights the U.S. Coast Guard and Merchant Marine Academies.
  • These academies are crucial for national infrastructure, defense, and supply chain logistics.
  • The episode featured insights into the academies' challenges and their critical national roles.
  • The U.S. Coast Guard Academy won the Secretary's Cup against the Merchant Marine Academy 39-38.

Deep Dive

  • Mary Margaret Olohan reported from Fenway Park covering the annual Secretary's Cup.
  • The game is between the U.S. Coast Guard Academy and the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy.
  • Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy's department oversees the Merchant Marine Academy.
  • The event was described as an important rivalry for both academies.

  • Secretary Sean Duffy made a wager with Secretary Christy Noem: if Merchant Marine wins, Noem hosts a party.
  • Pre-game activities at Fenway Park included a coin toss and attendees taking their seats.
  • Mary Margaret Olohan interviewed Secretary Duffy and another individual, noting team morale and 'smack talk'.
  • A reporter observed enthusiasm from both Secretary Duffy and Secretary Noam.

  • The U.S. Coast Guard Academy and U.S. Merchant Marine Academy are critical to national infrastructure and defense.
  • The Merchant Marine Academy specifically supports national security through sea lifts and supply chain movement.
  • Midshipmen highlighted their roles in global logistics, providing essential goods and services.
  • Secretary Sean Duffy noted these academies are often overlooked compared to other military branches.

  • Secretary Sean Duffy mentioned the U.S. Coast Guard Academy was previously called "black sheep."
  • This nickname was attributed to past underinvestment and poor conditions, including lack of hot water and mold.
  • Discussion included failed green energy initiatives involving electric boilers.
  • A historic mural of Christ was removed from the academy, with a promise for its reinstatement.

  • The U.S. Merchant Marine Academy (Mariners) led 26-21 during the game.
  • With two minutes remaining, the U.S. Coast Guard Academy took a 38-35 lead.
  • The Coast Guard Academy won the Secretary's Cup 39-38 in the final seconds.
  • Secretary Duffy was interviewed by ESPN, highlighting the event's significance for national defense.
