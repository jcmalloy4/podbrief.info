The Ben Shapiro Show

Ep. 2351 - Trump Prepares To Drop Davos BOMBS

Key Takeaways

  • The Trump administration reviewed its first year, citing economic growth and border enforcement achievements.
  • President Trump's approval ratings saw a decline, reaching 42.5% despite perceived successes.
  • The US delegation at Davos presented an 'America First' vision, challenging traditional globalization.
  • European leaders pushed back against US economic pressure, advocating for a multipolar global order.
  • A federal investigation was launched into Minnesota officials regarding alleged obstruction of an immigration operation.
  • The debate over the abolition of ICE intensified amidst ongoing enforcement operations.
  • The documentary 'The 1916 Project' explored the controversial historical origins of Planned Parenthood.

Deep Dive

  • The White House reported 365 wins during its first year, including economic growth and a booming stock market.
  • Key achievements cited included strong GDP numbers, reduced inflation, and increased private sector job growth.
  • The administration also highlighted a net loss in the migrant population due to stricter immigration enforcement efforts.

  • The Trump administration dispatched a large delegation to the World Economic Forum, including the President, Secretary of State, and Treasury Secretary.
  • Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick declared globalization had failed the West, advocating for policies prioritizing domestic workers and national sovereignty.
  • This approach aimed to prevent economic hollowing out and dependency by discouraging offshore production of essential goods like semiconductors.

  • The discussion centered on a critique of the post-Cold War vision of global order, articulated by George H.W. Bush in 1990 as a 'New World Order.'
  • This vision, pursued by several administrations, posited international cooperation and universal aspirations, but the host argued it never fully materialized.
  • President Trump's actions suggest a belief that a 'law of the jungle' still applies, raising questions about the future of US geopolitical strategy.

  • President Trump's stated interest in acquiring Greenland for US territory raised concerns about potential alienation of NATO allies.
  • Prediction markets on Kalshi indicate a significant probability that President Trump will purchase part of Greenland before January 20, 2029.
  • This move, alongside trade wars with Canada, has reportedly impacted US relations and potentially benefited China.

  • European leaders at Davos, including Emmanuel Macron, called for a reorientation away from U.S. global leadership toward a multipolar world.
  • President Trump's potential tariff threat against European allies over Greenland represents an unprecedented use of tariffs for strategic goals.
  • The Dow Industrials declined 871 points following Trump's tariff announcements, prompting EU President Ursula von der Leyen to announce a potential trade deal with India to counter US trade pressure.

  • A federal investigation was initiated into Minnesota officials, including Governor Tim Walz and Attorney General Keith Ellison, for allegedly obstructing an immigration operation.
  • Subpoenas were issued seeking records from state and county officials regarding public statements about the operation.
  • President Trump displayed mugshots of individuals arrested in Minnesota, highlighting their criminal backgrounds and alleged immigration status, alongside comments on Somali Americans and refugees.

  • Zorin Mamdani, featured on 'The View,' advocated for the abolition of ICE, arguing the agency 'terrorizes people.'
  • Protesters, including a group allegedly funded by the Chinese Communist Party, reportedly took over a church in response to ICE operations.
  • New Jersey Governor Mikey Sherrill was mentioned for arguing President Trump maintains standing armies in peacetime, an assertion challenged by the host who stated ICE is a domestic law enforcement agency.

  • The documentary 'The 1916 Project' explores the origins of Planned Parenthood, focusing on its founder Margaret Sanger and her first clinic in 1916.
  • The film details alleged historical connections between Sanger's American Birth Control League, eugenics movements, and figures like Lothrop Stoddard, who met Hitler in 1939.
  • Planned Parenthood disavowed Margaret Sanger in 2020 due to pressure from movements like the 1619 Project and Black Lives Matter.
