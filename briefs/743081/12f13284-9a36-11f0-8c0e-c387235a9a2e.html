Ep. 2286 - MORE Left-Wing Violence: Deadly Shooting at ICE Facility!

Key Takeaways

  • A sniper attack at a Dallas ICE facility killed two detainees; bullets were engraved with anti-ICE messages.
  • The FBI is investigating the ICE shooting as ideologically motivated, noting the shooter's reported left-leaning social media.
  • Medical experts refute President Trump's assertion of a link between Tylenol use during pregnancy and autism risk.
  • Jimmy Kimmel returned to television, clarifying past remarks and receiving bipartisan support for free speech.
  • Former FBI Director James Comey faces potential indictment for allegedly lying to Congress in September 2020.
  • President Trump's UN speech, delivered without a teleprompter after a malfunction, included strong criticism of the United Nations.
  • A suspect in the attempted assassination of Justice Brett Kavanaugh identifies as transgender; the DOJ seeks 30 years prison.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump suggested a link between Tylenol use during pregnancy and increased autism risk.
  • Medical evidence and studies, including meta-analyses, indicate no convincing evidence that acetaminophen causes autism.
  • Experts like Dr. Mark Siegel state no proof exists, distinguishing association from causation.
  • Tylenol is often the only pregnancy-safe option for fever reduction; doctors advise on fever severity and Tylenol use.

  • A sniper killed two detainees and wounded others at a Dallas ICE facility; the shooter died from a self-inflicted gunshot.
  • Engraved bullets with "anti-ICE" messages were found on the shooter, identified as 29-year-old Joshua John.
  • The FBI is investigating the shooting as ideologically motivated, noting the shooter's social media appeared left-leaning.
  • Texas Governor Greg Abbott characterized the incident as an attempted assassination targeting law enforcement, with detainees hit accidentally.

  • ICE Director Todd Lyon reported a 1000% increase in assaults on ICE agents.
  • Previous attacks occurred on ICE facilities in Yakima, Washington, and Alvarado, Texas, as well as a Border Patrol office.
  • A July 4th incident involved fireworks used to lure agents, resulting in a police officer being shot.
  • The Dallas shooter's brother stated the suspect lacked strong political ideology or feelings towards ICE.

  • Polling data indicates Americans increasingly view neighbors as enemies, leading to "unbridgeable partisan polarization."
  • The host attributes rising hatred to the left's "demonization of Republicans" and rhetoric suggesting the end of democracy.
  • California Governor Gavin Newsom's "two-faced" strategy involves inflammatory rhetoric for political gain.
  • A Quinnipiac University poll shows 79% of voters perceive a U.S. political crisis.

  • A majority of voters believe political rhetoric will not de-escalate and political violence will worsen.
  • 54% of voters think democracy is not working, especially if their party loses elections.
  • An event at Tennessee State University saw a DEI debate disrupted by a near-riot, with participants' belongings stolen.
  • The host attributes disproportionate political violence to the left.

  • Jimmy Kimmel returned to television after a suspension, with the host questioning the cause.
  • Kimmel expressed regret if his remarks about Charlie Kirk's death were misinterpreted and praised Kirk's widow, Erica, for forgiveness.
  • Kimmel received bipartisan support for his right to free speech from individuals and groups.
  • The host argued that questioning the system and suggesting government overreach, as by J.D. Vance concerning the FCC, undermines trust.

  • Former FBI Director James Comey is reportedly facing indictment for allegedly lying to Congress in September 2020.
  • The charges relate to authorizing a leak of his notes concerning Donald Trump.
  • Prosecutors are expected to seek an indictment before the perjury statute of limitations expires.
  • The host warns that using the DOJ to target political enemies, as suggested by Trump, undermines public trust.

  • President Trump's UN speech was impacted by a reported escalator stop and teleprompter freeze.
  • Trump delivered his speech without a teleprompter, suspecting foul play.
  • The host criticized the United Nations as a "wretched hive," advocating for its defunding and dissolution.
  • Trump criticized European countries for uncontrolled migration and "green energy scams."

  • Daily Wire correspondent Mary Margaret Olohan reported on the attempted assassination of Supreme Court Justice Brett Kavanaugh.
  • The suspect, identified as 'Sophie' and using 'she/her' pronouns in defense filings, identifies as transgender.
  • The suspect traveled from California to Maryland with tools to kill Kavanaugh and his family.
  • The Department of Justice is seeking 30 years in prison for the suspect, who is described as mentally ill.
