The Ben Shapiro Show

Ep. 2255 - America Needs Big Balls, NOT Mahmoud Khalil

Key Takeaways

  • Media shows blatant double standards, praising anti-American activists while disparaging a heroic citizen.
  • The Democratic Party increasingly embraces radical left-wing ideologies, abandoning traditional moderate stances.
  • President Trump's "America First" economic policies prioritize American workers through strategic tariffs and domestic investment.
  • Republican leaders emphasize core conservative principles: strong economy, limited government, and foundational values.

Deep Dives

Media Double Standards

  • The media initially portrayed Edward Coristine, known as 'Big Balls,' negatively despite his heroic intervention protecting a woman from assault.
  • Conversely, Mahmoud Khalil, an activist described as anti-American and anti-Israel, received favorable media treatment, even after downplaying anti-Semitism and justifying October 7th attacks.
  • Ben Shapiro argues the left's support for figures like Khalil, questioning the wisdom of importing individuals who openly despise the United States.
  • The podcast highlights reports of 'Palliwood Online,' where images of suffering in Gaza are allegedly staged, and discusses how Hamas censored AP to skew conflict narratives.

Leftward Shift

  • The Democratic Party is shifting towards extreme left ideologies, exemplified by figures like Zoran Mamdani and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez's support for them.
  • Shapiro contends the Democratic Party has been overtaken by the left, lacking courage to maintain moderate convictions.
  • He criticizes the embrace of radical ideas such as abortions in churches and family abolition, citing statistics on negative impacts of single-mother households.

Trump's Economic Vision

  • President Trump's 'America First' strategy emphasizes re-shoring manufacturing through tariffs on imports and incentives for domestic production, as seen with Apple's $600 billion U.S. silicon investment.
  • The strategy sparks debate on exporting advanced chips to China; some argue for restrictions like Cold War policies, while others suggest allowing chip flow leverages American tech superiority.
  • Trump's approach to international conflicts, such as Gaza and Ukraine, involves prioritizing American interests and seeking resolution, including potential talks with Vladimir Putin.
  • Ben Shapiro suggests radical deregulation could offer a more sustainable path for long-term business growth than tariffs alone, while acknowledging technology's impact on manufacturing jobs.

Conservative Priorities

  • Senator Tim Scott's book, 'One Nation Always Under God,' underscores the importance of the nation's Judeo-Christian foundation and celebrates individuals embodying Christian courage.
  • Scott credits the Trump administration with empowering Americans through tax cuts, border security, and prioritizing the American worker via trade reciprocity.
  • Senator Marsha Blackburn announced her Tennessee gubernatorial campaign, focusing on a strong economy with low taxes, efficient government, and addressing crime, immigration, and healthcare.
