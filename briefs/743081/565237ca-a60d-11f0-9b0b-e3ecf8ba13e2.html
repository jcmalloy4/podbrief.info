The Ben Shapiro Show

New Releases this October on DW+

Key Takeaways

  • A new series investigates the 2000 USS Cole bombing, focusing on ignored intelligence pre-9/11.
  • Daily Wire+ announced its October content slate, including new series, films, and documentaries.
  • The platform reaffirmed its mission to expand content and amplify voices in response to perceived attacks.

Deep Dive

  • A three-part series focuses on the USS Cole bombing, an attack that occurred on October 12, 2000.
  • The bombing resulted in 17 American sailors killed and dozens wounded.
  • The series highlights previously ignored intelligence and Al-Qaeda's declaration of war before 9/11.

  • October programming includes the 'USS Cole' series and 'Friendly Fire,' premiering October 16th with conservative media voices.
  • 'The 1916 Project' is scheduled to expose Planned Parenthood's origins.
  • The thriller 'Nefarious' premieres October 24th, followed by 'Hiding in Plain Sight' on October 30th, detailing digital predators targeting children.

  • The host articulated the Daily Wire+ mission: to respond to attacks by increasing content and volume.
  • The platform emphasizes its commitment to fighting harder and refusing to be silenced.
  • Viewers are encouraged to join the platform's mission by subscribing at DailyWire.com/Subscribe.
