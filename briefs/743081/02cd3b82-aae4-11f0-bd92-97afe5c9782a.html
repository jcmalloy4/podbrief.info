The Ben Shapiro Show

Friendly Fire: The Next Decade Begins

Key Takeaways

  • The Daily Wire marked its 10th anniversary with new content and a 'Friendly Fire' debate.
  • Legal discussions focused on Justice Katanji Brown Jackson's voting rights remarks and the ADA.
  • A Politico leak of young Republican group chats sparked debate on political strategy and rhetoric.
  • Gen Z cultural trends, particularly non-binary and transgender identities, showed a reported decline.
  • Conservative unity and differentiating internal 'friendly fire' from external threats were discussed.
  • Former President Donald Trump's Middle East foreign policy received praise for its effectiveness.
  • The Daily Wire launched a new app, new merchandise, and a limited Lifetime Membership.
  • A teaser for The Daily Wire's ambitious new TV series, 'The Pendragon Cycle,' premiered.

Deep Dive

  • Speakers reminisced about The Daily Wire's initial period, recalling personal anecdotes.
  • Matt Walsh humorously recounted broadcasting from his car in the company's early years.
  • Andrew Klavan's unchanging appearance over the past decade was noted by hosts.

  • Discussion focused on Justice Katanji Brown Jackson's statements concerning special voting districts.
  • Remarks debated regarding whether 'blackness' as a disability implies a need for special accommodations.
  • The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) was referenced as a legal precedent in arguments.

  • Politico reported a leak of private group chat conversations among young Republicans.
  • Messages contained 'edgy jokes and offensive language,' some characterized as taken out of context.
  • A speaker criticized media focus as a political distraction, contrasting it with leftist political violence.

  • Speakers debated the maturity of individuals in political discourse, asserting those in their 30s and 40s are not 'kids'.
  • Concerns were raised about increased personal security needs due to threats from both the left and the right.
  • Discussion centered on the impact of 'radical elements' within political movements and scrutiny for public figures.

  • Discussion covered excluding individuals from the conservative movement based on viewpoints or associations, citing Charlie Kirk's unity approach.
  • Debate occurred on whether acceptance of Charlie Kirk's alleged murder by a 'trans furry lover' should be a leadership requirement.
  • A call for a conservative truce against common threats was made, with one speaker offering to appear with Tucker Carlson.

  • Hosts discussed Jordan Peterson's health, stating he is 'on the mend'.
  • Peterson's mother expressed a desire for his messages to continue to be heard.
  • New Daily Wire merchandise, including shirts, hats, and a camo hoodie, was also highlighted.

  • A University of Buckingham study suggested a decline in queer and transgender identities among Gen Z in the UK.
  • Non-binary identity reportedly collapsed after peaking in 2023, while 'L' and 'G' identities remained stable.
  • Isabel Brown and speakers discussed whether Gen Z is becoming less 'gay' or identifying with transgender/non-binary identities.

  • A new Daily Wire app launched with enhanced features and an all-access experience.
  • Daily Wire Plus includes a new feed for shows, articles, and shorts, improved search, and upcoming offline downloads.
  • DWTV, a 24/7 live streaming channel, will debut this fall, accessible via the new app on major streaming platforms.

  • A teaser premiered for 'The Pendragon Cycle,' described as an ambitious TV project by an independent studio.
  • Jeremy Boreing made a special appearance to mark the launch and reflect on The Daily Wire's 10-year history.
  • The trailer depicted a land in chaos, with numerous rulers and a prophecy about a Merlin figure bringing peace.
