Ep. 2339 - 2026 SHOCK: Trump DEPOSES Maduro!

Key Takeaways

  • The Trump administration launched a covert mission, "Operation Absolute Resolve," to arrest Venezuelan dictator Nicolas Maduro.
  • Maduro was apprehended and extradited to the U.S. to face narco-terrorism charges, ending his nearly 13-year rule.
  • The mission sparked diverse international condemnations and domestic political debates regarding its legality and strategic implications.
  • Venezuela faces a complex future, with U.S. pressure on Vice President Delcy Rodriguez for democratic transition and oil concessions.
  • Ongoing protests in Iran over basic necessities and Minnesota Governor Tim Walz's re-election withdrawal also featured in the news.

Deep Dive

  • The host predicts 2026 will be a 'year of clarity,' where individuals and events reveal true nature, testing character.
  • Adversity is anticipated from international sources, including China's ambitions, the Russia-Ukraine conflict, and Iran's nuclear pursuits.
  • Domestic adversity may stem from economic turmoil, government bureaucracy, and reactionary political posturing, with increased "lies" from both political extremes.

  • The U.S. launched "Operation Absolute Resolve" on January 2nd to extradite Venezuelan dictator Nicolas Maduro for narco-terrorism charges.
  • The mission involved months of secret planning and rehearsals, including a replica of Maduro's compound, aided by an inside source.
  • U.S. forces, including Delta Force, utilized F-18s, F-22s, F-35s, and B-1 bombers to disable Venezuelan air defenses, apprehending Maduro and his wife, Cilia Maduro.
  • Maduro was transported on the USS Iwo Jima and appeared in a Manhattan federal courthouse on cocaine trafficking charges.

  • The host characterized President Trump's foreign policy as 'peace through strength' and 'America First,' focused on U.S. interests, not isolationism.
  • Trump's policy aims to surround Venezuela with stability and secure its energy resources for U.S. and global benefit.
  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio stated the actions were in the U.S. national interest, intending to stop drug trafficking and Iran's presence in Venezuela.

  • Nicolas Maduro succeeded Hugo Chavez in 2013, overseeing Venezuela's transition from an oil-rich democracy to a socialist dictatorship.
  • Venezuela experienced a 75% drop in GDP per capita and oil production, leading to the exodus of nearly 8 million people.
  • The country formed alliances with Russia, China, and Iran, engaging in weapon shipments, oil trade, and drone manufacturing.

  • The host dismissed international law as unenforceable, asserting that international relations operate under a 'law of the jungle' where strength dictates terms.
  • Secretary of State explained congressional notification was withheld to protect mission secrecy, as it was a short-term operation to arrest indicted fugitives.
  • Attorney General William Barr's 1989 memo regarding the Noriega case and Jonathan Turley's analysis were cited as legal precedents supporting the mission's legality.

  • International reactions to Maduro's arrest were mixed; his supporters were upset, while Venezuelans globally expressed happiness.
  • China and Russia condemned the action, invoking international law, while UK Prime Minister Kier Starmer stated non-involvement.
  • Domestically, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Marjorie Taylor Greene criticized the action, citing oil interests/regime change and U.S. job losses, respectively.

  • Marjorie Taylor Greene and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez publicly aligned in criticizing the mission, with Rashida Tlaib calling it a 'war for oil'.
  • New York Mayor Eric Adams questioned the legality, claiming it violates international law, while host questioned Adams' briefing accuracy.
  • Bernie Sanders condemned the action as a violation of the Constitution and international law, while the host argued Congress should pass a war powers resolution.

  • Under Maduro, Venezuela is economically left-wing but socially conservative, banning gay marriage, abortion, and sex changes due to its Catholic identity.
  • The U.S. is reportedly pressuring Vice President Delcy Rodriguez to moderate, allow U.S. oil interests, and transition towards democracy, risking a "second strike."
  • Venezuelans are celebrating Maduro's departure, hoping for freedom and an orderly transition, with the potential for the Rodriguez regime to seek refuge abroad if they do not comply with U.S. demands.

  • Protests in Iran have continued for over a week due to shortages of water and electricity, with approximately 41 people reportedly killed.
  • The U.S. President warned of consequences if Iran continues to kill protesters, while the host discussed potential regime change and Ayatollah Ali Khomeini possibly fleeing.
  • Minnesota Governor Tim Walz is not seeking re-election due to low approval ratings and scrutiny over alleged fraud in statewide daycare programs, costing taxpayers billions.
