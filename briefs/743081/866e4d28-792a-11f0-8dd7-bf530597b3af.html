The Ben Shapiro Show

Ep. 2258 - NYC’s Whiny Brats Pick A Mayor

Key Takeaways

  • Youthful political shifts: Zohran Mamdani's appeal to young voters highlights changing aspirations and New York's unique economic landscape.
  • Trump's direct action: President Trump advocates federal intervention on crime and challenges 'woke' cultural narratives.
  • Texas legislative battle: Governor Abbott presses forward with redistricting amid Democratic walkouts, defending state and border policies.
  • Trump's geopolitical approach: Concerns emerge regarding President Trump's potential concessions in upcoming talks with Vladimir Putin.

Deep Dives

Mamdani's Appeal

  • Ben Shapiro scrutinizes Zohran Mamdani's unconventional resume, likening his career path to Bernie Sanders as a 'career useless person'.
  • The show posits that younger Generation Z voters view Mamdani's lifestyle, including his rent-stabilized apartment, as aspirational.
  • Mamdani's political ascent is attributed to the structure of Democratic Party power in major cities, heavily reliant on publicly funded non-profit organizations.
  • New York City's $8-to-$1 matching program for small donations and its economy's reliance on government and non-profit sectors contribute to dependency and affordability issues.

Trump's Agenda

  • President Trump's plan to deploy federal law enforcement in Washington D.C. to combat crime is discussed, with Shapiro noting Democratic failures on crime despite their criticisms.
  • The podcast suggests a double standard exists where similar crime-fighting actions by a Democratic governor are praised, while Trump's initiatives are demonized.
  • Trump also seeks to counter 'woke' cultural influences by reviewing institutions like Smithsonian museums and selecting figures for honors who align with his perspective.

Texas Standoff

  • Governor Greg Abbott describes the absent Texas Democrats, who fled to block redistricting, as exhibiting 'very un-Texan' behavior.
  • Abbott confirms his commitment to passing new congressional maps, citing a court decision and a recent shift of Hispanic voters towards Republicans as justification.
  • Beto O'Rourke's alleged involvement in funding the absent Texas Democrats is highlighted, with the Texas Attorney General investigating potential bribery charges.
  • Governor Abbott contrasts the current secure border situation under President Trump with the previous year, noting a significant decrease in illegal crossings due to strong enforcement.

Foreign Policy Outlook

  • Concerns are raised about potential concessions as President Trump prepares for an upcoming meeting with Vladimir Putin in Alaska.
  • European leaders, including President Zelensky, have reportedly established 'red lines' with Trump, emphasizing Ukraine's need for a ceasefire and security guarantees, but not NATO membership.
  • The discussion highlights Putin's attempts to influence Trump, while Zelensky views Putin's rhetoric on sanctions as a bluff, confirming sanctions have been effective.
