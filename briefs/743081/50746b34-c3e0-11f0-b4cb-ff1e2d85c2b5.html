The Ben Shapiro Show

Ep. 2319 - MTG Revolts Against Trump, MAGA!

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump publicly withdrew support for Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene, criticizing her attacks and questioning her political motives.
  • The Jeffrey Epstein files controversy became a political wedge, fueling accusations and factionalism within the MAGA movement.
  • The Trump administration explored various proposals to address affordability, facing economic critiques regarding direct payments and tariffs.
  • The podcast analyzed the historical roots and policy challenges contributing to rising U.S. healthcare costs and proposed market-based reforms.
  • Michelle Obama commented on American sexism as a barrier to female presidency and on professional expectations for black women's hair.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump publicly criticized Representative Marjorie Taylor Greene at 0:46, stating he is withdrawing his support and endorsement.
  • Greene's claims included Trump protecting pedophiles in relation to the Jeffrey Epstein case, which the host dismissed as 'ridiculous' at 1:47.
  • Greene responded to Trump's criticism by accusing him of attacking and lying about her at 4:26, asserting her independence from political figures.

  • Greene's focus on the Epstein files and criticism of Trump is linked to her disapproval of his Middle East foreign policy and stance on Israel, with Greene quoted blaming Israel without evidence at 10:39.
  • The host suggests Trump may have withheld Epstein files due to a lack of evidence and to avoid further discussion, contrasting this with Greene's theories about Israeli involvement at 11:45.
  • Some theorize Trump's reluctance on Epstein files is to protect wealthy friends and donors, not due to personal implication, at 15:02.

  • Steve Bannon's career is characterized as opportunistic, using powerful figures for personal advancement, and his recent praise for Nick Fuentes is noted at 16:14.
  • Bannon's past relationship with Jeffrey Epstein is cited, including BBC reports of frequent correspondence and alleged plans for an Epstein documentary at 18:09.
  • The host links Bannon's actions to efforts to shift policy away from pro-Israel stances and undermine Trump's coalition.

  • The Trump administration considered proposals such as $2,000 direct payments for middle and low-income individuals, an antitrust probe into meatpacking companies, and tariff reductions at 21:42.
  • The host critiqued direct payments as 'helicopter money' that could increase prices, and argued tariffs cause price spikes, decreased demand, and ultimately a deflationary spiral with job losses at 23:39.
  • Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin cited decreasing 30-year mortgage rates from 7.8% to 6.22% as an indicator of improved affordability at 26:34.

  • The host criticized Democrats for creating programs necessitating continuous government subsidies, leading to a cycle of rising costs that require more funding, exemplified by SNAP expansion at 27:39.
  • Republicans are critiqued for attempting to outbid Democrats on large government redistribution programs, illustrated by pressure to support increased Obamacare subsidies at 30:14.
  • President Trump's plan to lower healthcare costs by providing funds for health savings accounts (HSAs) is contrasted with the ACA's subsidized marketplaces at 32:49, with Trump arguing HSAs foster competition.
  • Extending ACA tax credits would increase insurance coverage but cost taxpayers $350 billion over a decade at 33:59.

  • U.S. healthcare spending per capita began exceeding comparable wealthy nations in the 1980s and accelerated through the 90s and 2000s, outpacing GDP growth, as shown by data from Comet at 34:49.
  • Medicare, Medicaid, and the ACA have significantly contributed to rising U.S. healthcare spending, with Medicare's share of national health expenditure increasing notably since 1970 at 35:40.
  • Avic Roy, co-founder of the Foundation for Research on Equal Opportunity, attributes high U.S. healthcare costs to World War II wage controls, which led employers to offer health insurance as a tax-exempt benefit at 41:22.

  • The host identifies employer-provided health insurance as a fundamental distortion in the US healthcare system at 42:37, proposing a shift to individual health savings accounts (HSAs).
  • This approach aims for individuals, particularly younger ones, to purchase their own insurance, moving away from a one-size-fits-all federal regulatory architecture stemming from the ACA at 42:37.
  • A strategic proposal at 44:52 suggests trading a temporary extension of Biden-era enhanced subsidies (extended to 2025) for bipartisan deregulation of the ACA, aiming for a more affordable individual health insurance market.

  • New York City politicians, including Zorn Mamdani, are pledging not to cooperate with ICE officials, a stance the New York Post suggests could lead to chaos at 47:34.
  • Operation Midway Blitz targeted undocumented immigrants in Chicago, leading to 3,000 arrests, with planned escalations in Charlotte and New Orleans, while Border Patrol Chief Mike Banks reported increased recruitment under the Trump administration at 48:47.
  • Michelle Obama stated she would not run for president due to American sexism at 51:02, citing men who 'do not feel like they can be led by a woman.'
