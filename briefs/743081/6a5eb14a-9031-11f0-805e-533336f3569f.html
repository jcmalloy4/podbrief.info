The Ben Shapiro Show

How Charlie Kirk Transformed America.

Key Takeaways

  • The episode serves as a tribute to Charlie Kirk, featuring a re-released interview discussing his life and political contributions.
  • Charlie Kirk's Turning Point USA built a significant voter turnout operation, instrumental in President Trump's electoral successes.
  • Kirk's organization aimed to transform the Republican electorate by mobilizing low-propensity and MAGA-aligned voters.
  • The discussion highlights future conservative political strategies, including party alignment, education reform, and addressing cultural issues.
  • A new documentary, 'Identity Crisis,' is announced, focusing on the detransitioner movement and critiquing gender-affirming care.

Deep Dive

  • Ben Shapiro announced a re-released December 2024 interview covering Kirk's origin story and views on Donald Trump.
  • Kirk founded Turning Point USA and built a substantial organization from early efforts.
  • He discussed the success of his organization in the recent election cycle, including President Trump's victory and their voter turnout operation.

  • Charlie Kirk detailed his entrepreneurial drive to disrupt the nonprofit space, focusing on college and high school outreach.
  • He credited President Trump's presidency for merging culture and politics, aiding broader audience reach.
  • Turning Point USA has grown to 400,000 donors, engages hundreds of thousands of students, and hosted a major event with 20,000 attendees and President Trump.

  • The discussion shifted to the political agenda for upcoming years, emphasizing aligning the Republican Party with its voters.
  • Turning Point Action aims to ensure senators from red states reflect conservative values and confirm President Trump's cabinet picks, citing potential examples like Tulsi Gabbard.
  • The agenda includes transforming the party's philosophy towards long-term winning strategies and cultural change.

  • President Trump is characterized as a pragmatist focused on winning, suggesting he will adapt policies based on effectiveness.
  • The speaker asserts Trump will implement campaigned policies such as mass deportations and barring men from women's sports.
  • It is suggested Trump's time out of power has allowed for introspection, potentially leading to an improved and strong 'second first term' with a clear agenda.

  • The conversation explored how President Trump is now perceived to represent 'normalcy' compared to perceived Democratic extremism, citing examples like transgender protests at the White House.
  • Matt Walsh was credited for his stance on the transgender issue, which was considered a key factor in a recent election win.
  • Trump's campaign ad opposing taxpayer-funded surgeries for illegal immigrants was highlighted as particularly effective.

  • A new documentary, 'Identity Crisis,' is announced, focusing on the detransitioner movement and the 'graphic brutality' of gender transition procedures, particularly for minors.
  • The film is slated for free release on X on January 10th, followed by an exclusive showing on Daily Wire Plus.
  • The project aims to influence White House staff and government officials to make ending gender-affirming care a public policy agenda item.
