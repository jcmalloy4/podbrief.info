Ben Shapiro Reacts To Woke TikToks: Venezuela Meltdowns

Key Takeaways

  • The host critiqued 'woke' TikTok narratives regarding Venezuelan support for Nicolas Maduro.

  • The host satirized pro-Maduro TikTok reactions, including a mock campaign for Venezuela.

Deep Dive

  • US forces conducted an operation in Venezuela's capital at the host's direction.
  • The host reacted to 'woke' TikTok videos regarding Nicolas Maduro's deposition.
  • A TikTok claim of majority Venezuelan support for Maduro was challenged, citing a dictatorship where freedom of speech is suppressed.

  • Zara Mamdani opposed the US action in Venezuela, citing international law.
  • The host dismissed her concerns, satirized her call to then-President Trump, and questioned her credibility, referring to her as a 'terrorism supporter'.

  • Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez tweeted about the arrest of a Venezuelan dictator.
  • The host disputed her claim that the operation was about oil and regime change, clarifying Trump's statements.
  • Ocasio-Cortez's tweet connecting the Venezuelan operation to Epstein and healthcare costs was mocked as a distraction.

  • A segment presented 'woke TikTok reactions' where participants expressed a desire to trade Trump for Maduro.
  • Participants joked about Maduro's appearance and heritage, stating preference for a bus driver over a billionaire.
