The Ben Shapiro Show

Ep. 2353 - Another Ridiculous Media LIE About ICE!

Key Takeaways

  • Media narratives regarding federal agents' actions in immigration enforcement are being scrutinized.
  • The Department of Justice is prosecuting individuals for disrupting church services under the FACE Act.
  • The Trump administration is reportedly engaged in efforts to promote regime change in Cuba.
  • Virginia lawmakers are advancing proposals for a wealth tax and protections for illegal immigrants.
  • A $25 billion plan for Gaza's reconstruction was proposed, conditioned on Hamas demilitarization.
  • Oscar nominations for 2026 were announced, with some films receiving critical assessments.

Deep Dive

  • Legacy media reports were critiqued regarding federal agents allegedly using a 5-year-old as 'bait' to capture an undocumented immigrant.
  • An alternative account states the father fled, leaving the child behind, though both reportedly had active asylum claims.
  • Discussion addressed the complexities of detaining children and referenced the Flores Agreement and its impact on immigration policy.

  • A new ICE policy allows agents to enter homes based on administrative warrants for individuals with final deportation orders.
  • A Wall Street Journal report detailed a secret Trump administration memo that expanded this authority.
  • The legal basis for using administrative warrants and deportation orders as justification for home entry is expected to be litigated in court.

  • The Department of Justice (DOJ) is prosecuting three individuals for allegedly violating the FACE Act by disrupting a church service in Minnesota.
  • Protesters targeted a church where a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement official serves as a pastor; the FBI announced a third arrest.
  • Attorneys for Cities Church in St. Paul asserted the First Amendment does not permit disrupting worship or intimidating children.

  • Virginia lawmakers are considering a wealth tax with new tax brackets for high earners, potentially doubling state income tax for those making over $1 million annually.
  • Bills are also advancing to reduce penalties for certain crimes and protect illegal immigrants by barring arrests in courthouses and penalizing employers paying below minimum wage.
  • Representative Eric Swalwell proposed making ICE agents unhireable in California, which was questioned for its benefit to Democrats.

  • The Wall Street Journal reported the Trump administration is seeking Cuban government insiders to help oust the communist regime, potentially by year-end.
  • President Trump publicly urged Cuba to make a deal, stating 'no more oil or money,' with U.S. officials believing Cuba's economy is near collapse.
  • U.S. officials are reportedly seeking a 'palace coup' in Cuba rather than a military invasion, using Venezuela's situation as a potential blueprint.

  • Jared Kushner proposed a $25 billion reconstruction plan for Gaza at Davos, detailing plans for new cities and rebuilding Rafah within three years.
  • The plan highlighted Hamas demilitarization within 100 days as a key component for success.
  • Questions were raised about enforcement if Hamas refuses to disarm, and the necessity of 'denazification' efforts, including educational programs.
