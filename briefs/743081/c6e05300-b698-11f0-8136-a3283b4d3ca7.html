Can You Finish The Movie Quote? w/ Ben Shapiro

Key Takeaways

  • Ben Shapiro participated in a movie quote identification game with producers.
  • The game featured quotes from films like 'Back to the Future' and 'Casablanca'.
  • Shapiro shared his critical opinions on various films and director Stanley Kubrick.
  • Discussions included film humor, casting considerations, and censorship in comedy.

Deep Dive

  • The host initiated a segment to test his knowledge of famous movie quotes.
  • The game featured lines such as 'A center for ants?', which the host related to the movie 'Ant-Man'.
  • Another quote, 'Wait a minute, Doc. Are you telling me you built a time machine out of a blank?', was identified from 'Back to the Future' at 1:45.
  • Discussion also briefly touched on 'Zoolander 2' and the phrase 'Wickness is the essence of beauty' at 1:29.

  • The host identified 'Play it, Sam' as a quote from 'Casablanca', despite initially struggling to recall the full line and song title at 4:02.
  • He praised the film, discussing its cast and the potential historical casting of Ronald Reagan as Rick Blaine at 4:42.
  • The scene involving Victor Laszlo was specifically mentioned during the segment.

  • The host discussed the works of director Stanley Kubrick.
  • He expressed a preference for 'Paths of Glory' within Kubrick's filmography.
  • The first half of 'Full Metal Jacket' also received specific praise from the host.
  • This discussion followed the identification of a quote from Kubrick's 'Dr. Strangelove' at 6:18, highlighting George C. Scott's role.

  • The movie quote game continued with a line from the film 'Airplane!', leading to participants mimicking accents.
  • Discussion covered the movie's humor and its appropriateness for children, referencing a specific scene.
  • A joke involving the actress who played Beaver Cleaver's mother was also mentioned during this segment.
