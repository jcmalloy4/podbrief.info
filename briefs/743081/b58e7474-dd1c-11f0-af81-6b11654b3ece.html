Ben Shapiro Reviews: "Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery"

Key Takeaways

  • The host expressed strong negative opinions regarding "Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery."
  • The film was criticized for its mystery structure, specifically insufficient clues for the viewer.
  • The narrative's portrayal of Catholicism and its political messaging drew negative commentary.
  • Director Rian Johnson's storytelling approach was critiqued for prioritizing subversion over coherent plotting.
  • Despite a large cast and significant budget, the film's production was considered overstuffed.

Deep Dive

  • The host introduced "Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery" by Rian Johnson, noting polarized audience reactions.
  • His review immediately expressed strong negative opinions on the film's characters, writing, and director.
  • Despite a "gigantic" ensemble cast including Daniel Craig, Josh O'Connor, and Josh Brolin, disappointment was conveyed.
  • The host characterized the film as a failed dramedy, with most characters lacking sufficient depth to maintain interest.

  • The host critiqued "Wake Up Dead Man," stating it violated a "cardinal rule" of mystery filmmaking, similar to "Glass Onion."
  • He argued the film presents insufficient clues, making it impossible for viewers to solve the mystery independently.
  • This structural flaw hinders viewer engagement and the intellectual puzzle aspect of the genre.

  • The plot focuses on Josh O'Connor as a young priest in a parish led by the charismatic, morally corrupt Monsignor Jefferson Wicks (Josh Brolin).
  • The host interpreted the film's narrative as a critique of religion, specifically Catholicism, suggesting it is portrayed negatively unless offering psychological comfort.
  • Political messaging was noted, with villainous characters depicted as radically right-wing, and heroic figures either ignorant or holding similar views.
  • The film's portrayal of Catholicism was described as scornful, with religion serving only a superficial purpose.

  • Despite an extensive cast, only a few performances were noted as memorable.
  • The host questioned the film's $152 million budget and deemed its 144-minute runtime excessive and overstuffed.
  • Director Rian Johnson's storytelling was criticized for not constructing a coherent narrative or presenting puzzle pieces logically.
  • This approach, according to the host, aims to subvert audience expectations rather than create a clever or well-plotted film.
