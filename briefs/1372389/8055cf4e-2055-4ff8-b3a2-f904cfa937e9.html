Last Podcast On The Left

Side Stories: The Haunting of Ed Larson

Key Takeaways

  • Podcast hosts recount personal Halloween adventures and spooky experiences.
  • Listeners submit ghost stories, including an unexplained incident at a Texas sandwich shop.
  • Discussions range from paranormal perception to the realities of historical atrocities.
  • Upcoming live shows and podcast-related content are announced for November and December.

Deep Dive

  • Listeners sent the hosts vials containing ground brick dust from the former Silver Shamrock Factory.
  • The factory was the filming location for "Halloween 3".
  • The hosts considered the gift "cool" and unique, signifying audience engagement.

  • The hosts discussed a recurring problem of underwear falling down within pants.
  • They appealed to the audience for solutions, suggesting options like suspenders.
  • An email address, sidestoriesLPOTL@gmail.com, was provided for engineers to collaborate on specialized underwear for "egg-shaped" physiques.
  • One host expressed dissatisfaction with Jockey briefs and humorously referenced revealing underwear advertisements.

  • The episode shifted to a Halloween theme, contrasting chilling paranormal events with historical atrocities like the Einsatzgruppen.
  • One host, Eddie, stated he would share five personal, unexplained spooky experiences he has encountered.
  • Despite preparing to detail these events, Eddie typically does not believe in ghosts.

  • A host recounted a paranormal experience in an old Linden, New Jersey house known for supernatural occurrences.
  • The house, frequented by mafia figures, had a history of unexplained events experienced by the speaker's aunt, mother, and grandmother.
  • Incidents included hearing breathing through an intercom, feeling held down in a specific room, and lights turning on by themselves in an attic.
  • A medium allegedly identified the house as haunted by a ghost named Vito and his children, who were killed by the mafia for stealing money.

  • A particularly frightening story from Florida involved a phone call to Aunt Patty on the anniversary of her twin brother's death and her mother's death.
  • The call appeared to be from her deceased mother and connected her to the morgue regarding her brother.
  • This incident was part of a series of strange events occurring in Florida.

  • A host described paranormal activity in his new Mission Bay, West Boca Raton house, initially attributing it to construction.
  • Experiences included items disappearing and reappearing, a phenomenon also reported by neighbors.
  • A specific incident involved a "JFK assassination" book repeatedly falling from a speaker, eventually landing open, leading to its discard.
  • The host and a friend saw a man sitting in his father's chair at the end of the hallway.
  • Other incidents included a shadowy figure and aggressive shaking of front and sliding glass doors, leading to a theory that the neighborhood might be built on a former plantation.

  • After moving, the speaker reported no further paranormal experiences, speculating on factors such as age, brain solidification around 25, or heavy marijuana use.
  • The discussion explored whether marijuana use hinders paranormal connection by "gunking up the pituitary gland" or simply causes paranoia.
  • Other individuals, including Julie, Natalie, and Rob, stated they had never experienced anything paranormal, though Rob sensed presences while plumbing old houses.

  • The hosts read listener-submitted ghost stories, including one about a "haunted sandwich shop" in Texas.
  • Incidents at the sandwich shop included moving chips, handprints on glass, whispers, and a mysterious man in a coat appearing and disappearing inside the locked, closed restaurant.
  • Another story recounted an old lady seen on camera footage inside a closed Hooters in Tallahassee without triggering alarms.
  • The Luxor in Las Vegas was briefly mentioned as a reportedly haunted location where ghosts supposedly fold clothes.

  • The hosts announced upcoming live shows and events, including a stand-up show in Miami before a "Crime Wave at Sea" cruise.
  • A "Dark Disney" show in Orlando is scheduled after the cruise.
  • Solo performances by Eddie are planned for San Diego on November 16th, and Side Stories in Columbus, Ohio on November 30th, and Las Vegas on December 7th.
  • Listeners were encouraged to send in "murder at sea" stories and subscribe to the LPNTV YouTube channel for series like 'LPNRPG Bloodbath'.
