Last Podcast On The Left

Side Stories: One Story After Another

Key Takeaways

  • The hosts reviewed several horror films for their '31 for 31' Halloween challenge.
  • A listener recounted an encounter with Aaron Hernandez prior to his murder arrest.
  • Unconfirmed drone sightings led to airport shutdowns in Germany, sparking speculation.
  • A Texas man was charged after holding a driver at gunpoint over his dog's death.
  • An Australian dock worker was reinstated after a disputed termination.
  • Listeners submitted two distinct stories detailing paranormal experiences.
  • New tour dates for 2026 were announced for multiple U.S. cities.

Deep Dive

  • Henry Zabrowski reviewed 'The Gate' and Ed Larson watched 'The Strangers' as part of the '31 for 31' Halloween movie challenge.
  • Discussions included the rubric for the challenge, which explores different horror subgenres, created by Jackie Zabrowski.
  • The hosts debated the definition of 'old' horror movies, citing 1950s and 60s Hammer films and requiring action or gore for engagement.
  • 'Sleepaway Camp 2,' starring Pamela Springsteen, was noted for its nudity and non-traditional slasher elements.
  • Henry Zabrowski chose 'Halloween 3' as his first film for the challenge due to its absence of Michael Myers.

  • A listener recounted working at an escape room near Gillette Stadium in 2013, interacting with Patriots players including Aaron Hernandez.
  • She reprimanded Hernandez for not paying for tour tickets during his visit.
  • Later, she demanded payment from Hernandez for the tickets before returning forgotten protein powder.
  • Her manager informed her of Hernandez's arrest for murder shortly after his interactions at the escape room.
  • The small size of Massachusetts was highlighted by the subsequent discovery that a friend's mother was the detective who arrested Hernandez.

  • Munich Airport experienced a shutdown due to unconfirmed drone sightings, restricting flights for two consecutive days.
  • Similar incidents have been reported in Denmark and Norway, with speculation about Russian involvement or advanced military technology.
  • Congressman Tim Burchett's statements regarding extraterrestrial contact and underwater alien activity near San Diego were discussed.
  • The military's perspective on potential adversaries having ample opportunity to attack without doing so was considered.

  • In Mineola, Texas, Alberto Joshua Hernandez is accused of holding a driver at gunpoint after the driver accidentally killed his dog.
  • The victim was allegedly forced to dig a grave for the dog and withdraw $200 from an ATM under duress.
  • Hernandez also reportedly took the driver's license, threatening further action if the money was insufficient.
  • Hernandez's mother was present when police arrived on the scene.

  • Hosts acknowledged sympathy for Alberto Hernandez's grief over his dog but clarified they do not endorse his actions.
  • The Texas man was subsequently arrested and faced charges including aggravated robbery, kidnapping, and drug offenses.
  • The conversation touched on the emotional impact of a pet's death and individual coping mechanisms.

  • An Australian dock worker was reportedly fired for putting his penis in a co-worker's sandwich.
  • The worker was later rehired because the employer deemed the termination unreasonable due to a lack of physical evidence.
  • The hosts debated the fairness of the firing, noting the absence of concrete proof.

  • A listener story detailed aggressive knocking at a door during a girls' night in West Hollywood, with no one present when opened.
  • The knocking occurred multiple times, accompanied by sounds of coins dropping and white shapes appearing outside the window.
  • Fear led one guest to leave, and the remaining friends sought refuge elsewhere.
  • Upon returning days later, dimes and pennies were found scattered, and mail was wedged between the balcony and window, suggesting a potential explanation for the shapes.

  • A host shared a personal ghost story from a dorm room at the University of Wisconsin-Madison on January 23rd.
  • They witnessed a figure in a white nightgown in a mirror and later their roommate observed the same figure near a coffee machine.
  • A friend later provided video evidence of a girl in a white nightgown walking through a doorway, similar to the initial sighting.
  • The hosts discussed the difference between 'apportation' and 'apparition' in the context of ghost stories.
