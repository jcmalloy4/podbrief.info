Side Stories: 'Tis The Season

Key Takeaways

  • Schizophrenia therapy focuses on functional stabilization rather than delusion elimination.
  • A man in a Nazi uniform died by suicide after a police standoff in Stamford, Connecticut.
  • Holiday season crimes include child torture in Utah and a pregnant daughter's murder in Michigan.
  • A Michigan couple faces charges for murdering a pregnant daughter and removing the baby.
  • A security guard shot a shoplifter at a Spirit Halloween store, leading to manslaughter charges.
  • Unusual discoveries include human foot soles in socks and a swallowed Fabergé egg.
  • Listener emails explored consensual BDSM practices involving controlled risk.

Deep Dive

  • Audience feedback discussed past podcast coverage of Morgan Geyser.
  • Schizophrenia therapy aims for functional stabilization by grounding patients, not curing delusions.
  • Delusions do not inherently predict risk; behavior is the determining factor.

  • Jed Parkington, wearing a homemade Nazi uniform, engaged in a standoff with Stamford, CT police.
  • Parkington opened fire on officers and had explosive devices.
  • He refused a tater-tot hot dish lure after releasing his wife and dog.
  • Parkington died by suicide after shooting down police drones.

  • Micah Smith faces child torture charges for a strenuous winter hike in Big Cottonwood Canyon, Utah.
  • His two-, four-, and eight-year-old children were involved; the youngest died and required multiple revivals.
  • Smith allegedly filmed the children and quoted Gandalf to his 8-year-old daughter during the ordeal.

  • Courtney and Bradley Bartholomew are accused of murdering Courtney's pregnant daughter.
  • They allegedly removed the baby, who also died; the daughter was three days from her due date.
  • The hosts questioned the lack of a clear motive in this "disgusting" crime.

  • A security guard with a gun shot a shoplifter at a Spirit Halloween store.
  • The guard, whose mother is the district attorney, was charged with second-degree manslaughter.
  • The incident involved pepper spray before the fatal shooting.

  • A Scottish widow found the soles of her deceased husband's feet in his socks among his belongings.
  • Her husband had fallen overboard from a ferry due to medication side effects.
  • The hosts humorously speculated on the socks' durability.

  • A listener shared experiences with kinks involving risk and consensual strangulation (death play).
  • Emphasis was placed on consent, safety measures in BDSM, and distinguishing it from criminal activity.
  • The hosts discussed early consensual asphyxiation experiences and personal "vanilla" preferences.
