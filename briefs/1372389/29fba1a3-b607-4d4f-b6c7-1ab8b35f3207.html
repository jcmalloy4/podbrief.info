Side Stories: The Slenderman Escape

Key Takeaways

  • Morgan Geyser, a convicted 'Slender Man stabber,' recently escaped a mental health facility and was recaptured.
  • Two men were convicted for conspiring to overthrow a Haitian island and planning to enslave its population.
  • A UK woman traveled to Florida to arrange her own murder, leading to charges against the perpetrator.
  • Three individuals were arrested in the Florida Keys for public indecency in a Winn-Dixie parking lot.
  • Listener stories detail personal experiences with sextortion attempts and various elaborate scam operations.

Deep Dive

  • The hosts detailed food experiences in Ohio, including Luigi's Italian restaurant in Akron, noting its high cheese-to-patio ratio.
  • They also visited Fred's Diner, located near Jeffrey Dahmer's birthplace, and mentioned the venue's connection to Pantera.
  • Positive experiences were highlighted at Akron Coffee Roasters, contrary to prior assumptions about the city.
  • In Columbus, the discussion centered on Warios, known for its cheese steaks and pork roll sandwiches.

  • Morgan Geyser and Anissa Weire, both 12, attacked their friend Peyton Leitner in 2014, motivated by delusions involving Slender Man.
  • Geyser completed the stabbing after Weire hesitated; Peyton Leitner survived the attack.
  • Geyser was committed to a mental health facility for a potential 40-year stay, with an initial schizophrenia diagnosis.
  • Concerns arose when Geyser received a book titled 'Rent Boy,' reportedly containing themes of sexual sadism and violence, leading to stricter parameters in the facility.

  • Morgan Geyser escaped on November 23rd by cutting off her ankle monitor and left with 43-year-old Chad Mecca, who identifies as Charlie.
  • They were apprehended the night before Thanksgiving after obtaining a Greyhound bus ticket.
  • Bodycam footage reportedly showed Geyser appearing to be in control during their attempted escape to Nashville.
  • Legal repercussions for Charlie are being considered, potentially including charges for trafficking if he paid for Geyser's bus ticket.

  • The ongoing case of Morgan Geyser continues to raise questions regarding her motives and mental state.
  • The role of Chad Mecca, who has a history of sexual assault, in Geyser's escape is being analyzed.
  • Geyser reportedly continues to claim interactions with Slender Man, which is viewed as a sign of her dangerousness.

  • Gavin Weisenberg (20) and Tanner Thomas (21) conspired to amass homeless men from Washington D.C. to invade Gunave Island, intending to enslave women and children.
  • Tanner enlisted in the Air Force, allegedly to gain training, while Weisenberg was reassigned to be closer to D.C. for the project.
  • Their attempts included enrolling in a fire academy and trying to join a sailing school in Thailand, which were unsuccessful.
  • They were convicted on charges of conspiracy to murder, maim, or kidnap in a foreign country, and production of child pornography, facing life in prison.

  • Ryan Wedding, a former Canadian Olympic snowboarder, allegedly became a leader in the Sinaloa cartel.
  • Wedding is currently wanted by the FBI, with a reward of up to $15 million offered for information leading to his capture.
  • The discussion highlighted his evasion of authorities despite his alleged high-level involvement in drug trafficking.

  • Sonia Excelby, a woman from the UK, traveled to Ocala, Florida, to hire Dwayne Hall through a fetish website to murder her.
  • The victim reportedly rescinded her agreement to be killed, but the perpetrator proceeded with the act.
  • Evidence included communications about the victim's change of mind, the purchase of a new shovel used for burial, and the shovel containing the victim's DNA.

  • Sharon Helen Saplinski (45), Marshall Adam Lowry (43), and Michael McDonald Howard (59) were arrested in Marathon, Florida.
  • The individuals were charged with public indecency after engaging in sexual activity in a Winn-Dixie parking lot.
  • Reports indicated that all three individuals were heavily intoxicated at the time of their arrest.

  • One listener shared a story of their father being sextorted after receiving a compromising video from a cloned account.
  • Another listener recounted their grandmother's multiple experiences with scams, including sending computers to Africa and money to fake captors.
  • The grandmother was also reportedly blackmailed with fabricated nude photos of her husband, highlighting persistent scam tactics.
