Fox News Makes Fatal Admission on Trump’s Public Collapse

Key Takeaways

  • Fox News hosts reportedly admitted potential Republican midterm losses and Donald Trump's declining influence.
  • Controversial policy suggestions included a US invasion of Venezuela for oil by a Republican Congresswoman.
  • Discussions highlighted Russia's negotiation tactics, with a NATO ambassador noting "no concessions" from Moscow.
  • Several Fox News commentators were criticized for statements on military service and political rhetoric.
  • Allegations were made on Fox News that Democrats unfairly targeted Donald Trump, with warnings of retaliation.

Deep Dive

  • Fox News is reported to have admitted potential significant losses for Donald Trump and the Republican Party in upcoming midterms.
