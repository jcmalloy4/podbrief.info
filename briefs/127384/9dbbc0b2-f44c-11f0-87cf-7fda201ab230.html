Oops! Melania Makes Fatal Error in Court over Epstein Claims

Key Takeaways

  • Melania Trump faces a lawsuit from author Michael Wolf over alleged defamation threats.
  • Wolf's lawsuit claims violations of First Amendment rights and anti-SLAPP statutes.
  • Melania Trump moved the case to federal court, citing Florida residency, a claim disputed by Wolf.
  • The legal strategy appears aimed at delaying discovery and avoiding sworn testimony regarding Epstein allegations.
  • Extensive jurisdictional discovery is being sought, potentially involving Melania Trump's deposition and family testimony.

Deep Dive

  • Melania Trump allegedly threatened journalists for reporting on her connections to Jeffrey Epstein.
  • Author Michael Wolf was threatened with a $1 billion lawsuit by Melania Trump over articles linking her to Epstein.
  • Wolf filed a lawsuit in New York, alleging Melania Trump's threats violated his First Amendment rights and anti-SLAPP statutes.

  • Melania Trump removed Wolf's state court case to federal court, arguing for diversity jurisdiction and Florida residency.
  • The host questioned why Melania Trump has not countersued Michael Wolf for defamation if her claims are false.
  • Her attempt to transfer the case to Florida, potentially to Judge Eileen Cannon, is viewed as a strategy to cause delays and avoid discovery.

  • The core of Melania Trump's legal strategy is to delay discovery and prevent a deposition where she would answer questions under oath about Epstein and Donald Trump.
  • Her claim of Florida residency is challenged by Wolf, who seeks jurisdictional discovery to prove her New York residency.
  • Wolf's counsel aims to prove New York residency to prevent the case from potentially being heard by Judge Eileen Cannon.

  • Wolf's lawyers intend to seek extensive jurisdictional discovery, including Melania Trump's deposition, to establish her primary residence in New York.
  • Testimony from Baron Trump may be sought as part of efforts to determine Melania Trump's primary residence.
  • Evidence such as flight manifests, travel documents, and financial records will be examined to clarify her time spent in New York versus Florida.
