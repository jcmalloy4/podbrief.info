Trump Can’t Sleep and Goes on Rampage as Term Collapses

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump reacted late-night to Epstein file release, criticizing Kimmel and defending his economic record.
  • Claims of a strong economy by Trump were contrasted with rising consumer prices and economic hardship.
  • A top military lawyer raised concerns about anti-drug operations near Venezuela, citing war crime allegations.
  • Trump Media's stock value significantly decreased, resulting in an estimated $5 billion loss in paper wealth.
  • Trump's claims of brokering peace deals and ending the Ukraine war were disputed as unfulfilled or fabricated.

Deep Dive

  • The host contrasted Donald Trump's claims of a strong economy with rising prices on essential goods like coffee, ground beef, and turkey, citing specific year-over-year percentage increases.
  • A Fox News clip showed economic concerns, with a significant percentage viewing U.S. economic conditions as fair or poor.
  • American economic struggles include increased food bank lines, credit card delinquencies, and rising jobless claims.

  • An NBC exclusive reported a top lawyer at U.S. Southern Command disagreed with the Trump administration on the legality of anti-drug smuggling operations near Venezuela.
  • The lawyer's concerns were reportedly overruled by the Justice Department.
  • Allegations suggest strikes on boats off Venezuela and Colombia may constitute war crimes, leading to over 82 deaths.
  • Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth and others in the chain of command were mentioned in connection to potential future prosecution.

  • The Trump family reportedly lost approximately $5 billion in paper wealth from Trump Media, the parent company of Truth Social.
  • Trump Media's stock decreased by nearly 70% in the past year, trading at $10.29, essentially at its starting SPAC value.
  • The host questioned Trump's business acumen, suggesting an inability to run companies successfully even with significant investments from Saudi Arabia and Qatar.

  • The host refuted Donald Trump's claims of brokering peace deals, stating he fabricates them and takes credit for conflicts that persist.
  • Trump's unfulfilled promise to end the war in Ukraine within 24 hours was highlighted, noting recent civilian deaths.
  • Secret negotiations involving the Trump regime and Russian sovereign wealth fund leader Kirill Dmitriev were detailed, referencing a missed meeting in Istanbul.
  • Trump allegedly planned to tell Ukrainian President Zelensky to surrender in Istanbul, but Ukraine reportedly exposed his intentions.
