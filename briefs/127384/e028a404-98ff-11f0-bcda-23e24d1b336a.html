Trump Loses It in Late Night Meltdown in Front of World

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump threatened lawsuits against ABC and Jimmy Kimmel, alleging DNC political bias.
  • Trump's UN speech faced criticism for malfunctions and controversial remarks.
  • The US government faces a shutdown after Trump halted bipartisan budget negotiations.
  • US taxpayer money is bailing out Argentina, while global alliances are shifting away from the US.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump threatened to sue ABC and Disney over Jimmy Kimmel's show.
  • Trump alleged Kimmel's program is a "DNC operation" and an "illegal campaign contribution."
  • He also disparaged Kimmel's audience and talent.

  • Trump's team reportedly blamed the UN for a broken escalator and malfunctioning teleprompter during his UN appearance.
  • Influential MAGA figures called for military action against the UN and deporting the UN.
  • Governor Newsom detailed Trump's controversial remarks about nations "going to hell" and windmills during the speech.
  • The host displayed a large-print page from Trump's speech, indicating teleprompter issues.

  • Donald Trump canceled a planned meeting with Democratic leaders Chuck Schumer and Hakeem Jeffries.
  • He ordered Republicans not to negotiate with Democrats regarding the impending government shutdown.
  • Trump cited Democrat demands, such as protecting Affordable Care Act tax credits, as "too extreme."
  • Democrats had proposed meeting at the White House despite congressional recess for Jewish holidays.

  • The US government is allocating taxpayer money to bail out Argentina, led by President Javier Milei.
  • Milei is supported by MAGA followers and figures like Elon Musk, but Argentina is reportedly nearing bankruptcy.
  • This financial aid occurs concurrently with calls for cuts to American domestic services.
  • The bailout is also seen as benefiting US investors and corporations interested in Argentine lithium.

  • Allies such as Portugal and Denmark are procuring military equipment from Europe, not the United States.
  • Thailand signed a submarine deal with China, indicating a shift in global military partnerships.
  • Canada's Prime Minister Mark Carney affirmed Canada's recognition of the state of Palestine.
  • The host criticized Donald Trump's "schizophrenic" stance on the Ukraine conflict, labeling him a "Putin bootlicker."
