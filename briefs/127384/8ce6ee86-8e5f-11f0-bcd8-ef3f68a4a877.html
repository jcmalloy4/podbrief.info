Trump Gets Booed Inside DC Restaurant as War Erupts

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump was publicly heckled at Joe's Seafood in Washington, D.C.
  • Russian drone activity over Poland prompted concerns about NATO's Article 5.
  • Trump denied an Epstein letter, calling related questioning a "waste of time."
  • Trump commented on an Israeli airstrike, expressing mixed sentiments but implying support.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump was booed and heckled at Joe's Seafood restaurant in Washington, D.C., both outside and inside the establishment.
  • Attendees reportedly referred to him as a "modern-day Hitler."
  • The incident was reported early in the podcast and revisited later at 14:01.

  • Russian drone activity was reported over Poland, raising concerns about NATO's Article 5.
  • Congressman Joe Wilson described the activity as an "act of war."
  • Reports indicated Russian drones entered Polish airspace, potentially targeting Zamosk.
  • Warsaw International Airport was closed due to military activity, and Polish fighter jets were observed.

  • During his restaurant visit, Donald Trump was questioned about an Epstein letter.
  • He denied the signature and language were his, stating the questioning was a waste of time.
  • Trump also indicated he had not considered meeting Epstein victims.

  • Donald Trump commented on an Israeli airstrike in Qatar that hit a residential area.
  • He stated he was "not thrilled" by the situation and desired the return of hostages.
  • Trump's remarks implied support for Israeli actions despite his stated reservations.
