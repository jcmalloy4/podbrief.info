MeidasTouch Full Podcast - 9/30/25

Key Takeaways

  • Trump's regime allegedly uses a government shutdown to hide economic data and Epstein files.
  • Donald Trump promotes unverified "MedBeds" linked to alien technology, a tactic for distraction.
  • U.S. military actions in Venezuela mistakenly killed fishermen, not "narco-terrorists."
  • Federal troop deployments in cities like Portland face lawsuits for constitutional violations.
  • Trump's "golden age" claims contradict data on high grocery prices and farmer bankruptcies.
  • Republican rhetoric for shutdown includes fabricated claims about Democrat spending priorities.
  • MAGA media employs disinformation tactics and shows bias in mass shooting coverage.
  • Trump prioritizes personal branding and luxury, impacting American economic and trade interests.

Deep Dive

  • A potential government shutdown is discussed, with Donald Trump reportedly blocking press from a White House meeting.
  • The shutdown is suggested as a tactic to halt Bureau of Labor Statistics economic data reporting and conceal Epstein files.
  • It is also linked to hiding alleged economic hardship on American citizens, including farmers, and blaming Democrats.
  • Hosts refer to the potential event as the 'Trump Jeffrey Epstein shutdown.'

  • Donald Trump promoted unverified 'MedBeds' based on alien technology, via an AI video, as a healthcare plan.
  • Hosts highlighted this as an example of unsubstantiated claims and a deviation from past healthcare promises.
  • The dissemination of this fabricated video is criticized while Republicans seek to cut actual healthcare.
  • This disinformation campaign is labeled as manipulative and predatory, impacting livelihoods and healthcare access.

  • New York Times reported individuals killed by U.S. military actions in Venezuela, previously called 'narco-terrorists,' were fishermen.
  • Family members identified victims as fishermen, challenging the Trump administration's characterization.
  • The tactic of labeling groups as 'narco-terrorists' to justify lethal action is criticized.
  • One victim was reportedly a fisherman with four children; their boats were described as small fishing vessels.

  • Trump deployed troops to Portland, Oregon, and conducted ICE crackdowns in Chicago, framed as an invasion against citizens.
  • Oregon's Attorney General filed a lawsuit against the Trump administration, citing violations of the Constitution and the Posse Comitatus Act.
  • Trump's ambiguous command to 'utilize full force' caused confusion within the Pentagon.
  • Lawsuits challenge these deployments on grounds of violating the Posse Comitatus Act, the 10th Amendment, and executive powers.

  • Donald Trump's claims of a 'golden age' in America are contrasted with data on record high grocery prices, unaffordable housing, and surging inflation.
  • Farmers are reported on the verge of bankruptcy due to issues like China's reduced purchases.
  • Trump's claims of $17 trillion in revenue are questioned, noting a $2 trillion fiscal deficit and a $150 billion decrease in funding for rural hospitals.
  • The US bailing out Argentina is argued to undercut American soybean farmers.

  • Fabricated Republican talking points include claims that Democrats want to fund transgender surgeries and provide healthcare to illegal immigrants.
  • This rhetoric is used to justify a government shutdown, contrasting with Democrats' focus on affordable healthcare and Obamacare tax credits.
  • Republican statements are characterized as lies about 'woke policies' and healthcare for illegal aliens, aiming to strip citizens' benefits.
  • The strategy of using inflammatory language to obscure truth and deny essential services like healthcare is discussed.

  • Governor Gavin Newsom's view that MAGA Republicans flood the zone with disinformation and bend reality is discussed.
  • Corporate media is criticized for not challenging false claims like 'Peruvian transgender surgeries' or misrepresented events.
  • The New York Post allegedly falsely linked a Penn State 'whiteout' event to Charlie Kirk, distributing 'freedom' shirts.
  • This is described as fascist propaganda designed to associate conservative figures with popular culture.

  • Conservative media is criticized for downplaying mass shooters' Trump affiliations and altering photos, such as by the New York Post.
  • Two recent mass shootings involved U.S. combat veterans, often white men and Trump supporters, contrasting with previous 'left-wing maniacs' labels.
  • 97-98% of mass shooters between 2000-2019 were male, and over 52% were white men.
  • Republicans are accused of defunding mental health services while scapegoating specific groups and ignoring common-sense gun reform.

  • Donald Trump's focus on personal branding and luxury, including social media posts about 24-carat gold in the White House, is highlighted.
  • He is accused of sacrificing leverage in trade negotiations with China and neglecting American farmers.
  • Allegations include efforts to secure a TikTok subsidiary for his associates and Benjamin Netanyahu, despite national security concerns.
  • Text messages from the UN General Assembly indicated China outmaneuvering the US, leading to falling soy prices.
