Trump Has Disaster Morning as War Explodes

Key Takeaways

  • Russia's missile and drone attacks targeted Kyiv, hitting foreign delegation buildings, while Donald Trump focused on campaign messaging.
  • Reports indicated international leaders, including Japan and India, distanced themselves from Donald Trump.
  • Former Trump administration officials, including Federal Reserve and CDC appointees, initiated legal action over alleged misconduct.
  • Concerns were raised about the Department of Homeland Security's counterterrorism capabilities and Donald Trump's rhetoric and alleged actions regarding sensitive files.

Deep Dive

  • Russia launched ballistic missiles and drones on Kyiv and other Ukrainian cities, resulting in 16 deaths, including four children.
  • The European Union delegation building and British Council building in Kyiv were hit during the attacks.
  • Donald Trump was criticized for not acknowledging these attacks, instead focusing on 'Doge checks' and 'tariff rebate checks' for campaign outreach.

  • Foreign countries, specifically Japan and India, reportedly ignored Donald Trump, canceling meetings and not returning calls.
  • Japan's trade representative canceled a meeting with Trump, attributing it to his tendency to misrepresent such encounters.
  • A claimed $550 billion trade deal with Japan did not materialize.

  • Donald Trump is accused of firing Federal Reserve governor Lisa Cook based on fabricated mortgage fraud allegations, prompting a lawsuit from Cook against the Trump administration.
  • Susan Monarez, the CDC head and a Trump appointee, is preparing to sue the administration after being asked to resign due to her opposition to RFK Jr.'s stance against vaccine accessibility.

  • The Department of Homeland Security's counterterrorism and threat prevention division was reportedly gutted and led by a 22-year-old intern named Thomas Fugate.
  • Donald Trump's administration is alleged to have covered up the Epstein files, a behavior the media has been accused of normalizing, exemplified by a New York Times headline on Trump's "power quest."
  • Trump's rhetoric and behavior are compared to that of a dictator, with an AFP headline describing his style as 'dictator chic.'
