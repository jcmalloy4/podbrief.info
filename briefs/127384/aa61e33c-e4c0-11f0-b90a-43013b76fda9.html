Trump’s Health Deteriorates as Urgent Warning Surfaces

Key Takeaways

  • Observations of discolored hands on Donald Trump during a meeting with Zelenskyy raised health concerns.
  • Speculation arose linking Trump's symptoms to the Alzheimer's drug Lekembi and his family's history of dementia.
  • Experts analyzed Trump's cognitive assessments, public behavior, and speech patterns for signs of decline.
  • A code for the 25th Amendment was allegedly used within the Trump White House due to cognitive concerns.
  • Donald Trump's recent public statements, including claims about bombing Nigeria, were characterized as erratic.

Deep Dive

  • The host noted concerning signs observed during Donald Trump's Sunday meeting with Zelenskyy, specifically the discoloration of both of Trump's hands.
  • Speculation arose regarding the discolored appearance, suggesting potential IV infusions or rot as causes.
  • A social media post highlighted a small hole at the site of discoloration on Trump's hand, fueling health inquiries.

  • Congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene speculated a link between Donald Trump's symptoms and Lekembi, an Alzheimer's drug administered via infusion.
  • Images comparing a Lekembi patient's hand to Trump's were discussed, alongside the Alzheimer's treatment and its potential side effects.
  • A Fox 5 report showed a patient receiving Lekembi, illustrating the infusion process and drawing comparisons to Trump's situation.
  • Parallels were drawn to the rapid progression of Alzheimer's in Donald Trump's father, Fred.

  • Amanda Markot speculated that Donald Trump's MRI results influenced GOP House members regarding Epstein files, and stated White House denials of health issues are untrustworthy.
  • The host criticized Trump's press conference with Zelenskyy, noting his speech veered into unsubstantiated claims about the 2020 election and a 'Russia hoax'.
  • Trump's interactions with reporters about food were presented as erratic behavior, contributing to public evidence of his decline.

  • Dr. John Gartner, discussing the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MOCA) in a Daily Beast article, suggested individuals only take it if they have significant cognitive issues.
  • Gartner indicated that repeated MOCA testing points to a monitoring of dementia rather than a routine baseline assessment.
  • The discussion highlighted concerns about the necessity and frequency of cognitive assessments for Donald Trump.

  • Dr. Vin Gupta questioned the necessity of a full-body MRI and frequent MOCA tests, suggesting these actions indicate monitoring of cognitive decline rather than a standard check-up.
  • Gupta pointed to Trump's physical presentation, speech patterns, and the frequency of cognitive tests as potential indicators of concern.
  • Michael Wolf, Trump's former biographer, discussed Trump's disinhibited behavior, citing a peculiar statement after Rob Reiner's death.
  • Wolf posited Trump's behavior might be akin to dementia due to a lack of reflex control, comparing it to dealing with an alcoholic as suggested by Susie Wiles.

  • Dr. Gardner reported that individuals within the Trump White House allegedly used '25' as a code for the 25th Amendment, indicating concerns about Trump's cognitive abilities.
  • This code was reportedly used to signal when Trump was exhibiting behavior potentially requiring removal from office.
  • The host discussed Donald Trump's reposting of a statement alleging Democrats want to replace Americans with uneducated Mexicans.
  • Trump claimed to have recently bombed Nigeria, asserting the target was ISIS for attacking Christians, a claim the host disputed, suggesting it was related to oil and lithium interests.
