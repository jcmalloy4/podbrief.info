Trump Can't Sleep and has Late Night Breakdown

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump reposted QAnon memes, including calls for mass executions and violent rhetoric.
  • Trump's claims of economic success are contradicted by rising jobless claims and foreclosures.
  • Trump-affiliated investments, such as a meme coin and Trump Media stock, experienced significant value drops.
  • Trump's G20 summit boycott in South Africa led to U.S. isolation from global cooperation.
  • Ukraine rejected Russian surrender terms, prompting critical social media posts from Donald Trump.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump engaged in late-night social media activity, reposting QAnon memes.
  • Posts included calls for mass executions and violent rhetoric against political opponents.
  • Further QAnon memes referenced military tribunals and threatened a January government shutdown over the SAVE Act.
  • The host criticized media for focusing on Trump's Ukraine policy while ignoring his QAnon meme posting.

  • The host refuted Donald Trump's claims of economic success and the 'best economy ever'.
  • Negative economic indicators cited included rising jobless claims, credit card delinquencies, and foreclosures.
  • Inflation rose to 3% year-over-year, with increases observed from April through September.
  • A CBS poll indicated Americans perceive the economy as poor and believe Trump is mishandling economic issues.

  • Investors in a Trump-associated meme coin reportedly lost nearly its entire value.
  • The Trump family allegedly profited from transaction fees related to the meme coin.
  • Trump Media's stock crashed approximately 70% from its initial SPAC value.

  • Donald Trump boycotted the G20 summit in South Africa, citing a 'white genocide' claim.
  • This boycott occurred during the first G20 summit held on the African continent.
  • Other world leaders formed a 'G19,' conducting business and issuing a declaration of purpose without U.S. involvement.
  • The U.S. later requested participation in the gavel ceremony for the next G20, scheduled to be hosted at Trump's Doral property.

  • Ukrainian President Zelensky and European allies rejected Russian surrender terms, refusing to disarm or abandon EU/NATO aspirations.
  • Donald Trump reacted to the Ukrainian stance with posts calling leaders ungrateful and accusing them of failing to thank him.
  • Trump claimed he could have ended the war in 24 hours, but allegedly tried to sell out Ukraine for personal business deals.
