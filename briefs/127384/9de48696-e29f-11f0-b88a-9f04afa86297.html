Furious Zelenskyy Finally Turns the Tables on Trump

Key Takeaways

  • Ukrainian President Zelenskyy's Christmas message challenged alleged plots by Trump and Putin.
  • Reports detailed an alleged plan for Putin and Trump to jointly manage the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant.
  • Russian military forces are overstretched and facing criticism amidst Ukrainian counteroffensives.
  • Declassified US documents from 2008 indicate Putin's long-held view of Ukraine as an 'artificial state'.
  • Ukrainian forces successfully struck a Russian oil refinery with British-French Storm Shadow missiles.
