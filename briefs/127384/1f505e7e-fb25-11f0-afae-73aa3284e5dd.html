The MeidasTouch Podcast

MeidasTouch Full Podcast - 1/27/26

Key Takeaways

  • Backlash grows over Donald Trump administration's response to Alex Pretty's Border Patrol killing.
  • Reports detail Donald Trump's alleged deteriorating health, including memory issues and threats to sue.
  • Trump's threats of 100% tariffs against Canada highlight his erratic international trade approach.
  • The Department of Justice faces criticism for perceived ongoing cover-ups related to the Epstein files.
  • House Republicans passed a $1.2 trillion appropriations bill, criticized for bundling ICE and Border Patrol funding.
  • Border Patrol Commander-at-Large Greg Bovino was demoted following aggressive tactics and a fatality.
  • Professor Justin Wolfers explains how undermining institutions harms long-term economic prosperity.
  • The United States' international reputation is suffering, contrary to Trump's claims of renewed respect.

Deep Dive

  • Criticism focused on the Department of Justice for alleged ongoing cover-ups related to the Epstein files.
  • Hosts allege a pattern of extortion by the DOJ towards Minnesota's governor regarding election information.
  • Bill Barr wrote an op-ed supporting Pam Bondi's handling of the Epstein files, suggesting a move by Donald Trump to bring Barr back into his orbit, further raising questions about perceived cover-ups.

  • The Trump administration's response to the killing of Alex Pretty (also referred to as Perea, Preciado, Pineda, and Predi) by Border Patrol agents was described as morally depraved and a rush to defame the victim.
  • Officials like Stephen Miller and Greg Bovino conducted a coordinated campaign to label Pretty a domestic terrorist, despite video evidence allegedly showing he was not holding a gun and was not a threat.
  • Pretty was described as intervening to aid a woman, then pepper-sprayed, thrown to the ground, and shot multiple times, with his concealed handgun removed before he was shot.

  • House Republicans, with aid from seven Democrats, passed a $1.2 trillion 'Minibus' appropriations bill.
  • The bill includes funding for defense and FEMA, but also additional money for ICE and Border Patrol, which was criticized as a tactic to pressure support for these agencies by linking them to disaster relief.
  • The bill moved to the Senate while the House entered recess, with Senate Democrats outlining non-negotiables including accountability for officials, troop withdrawal from Minnesota, and no further funding for ICE and Border Patrol.
  • Senator Susan Collins expressed concerns about a potential government shutdown, acknowledging the killing of Alex Preciado and criticizing the bundling of funding as an 'extortion racket'.

  • Alarming reports from a New York Magazine exposé detail Donald Trump's deteriorating health, including alleged memory issues where he struggled to recall the word 'Alzheimer's'.
  • The article, while having a favorable headline, included critical reporting, an unflattering photo, and described Trump's interactions with doctors.
  • Trump reportedly threatened to sue the magazine over the article's contents.

  • Donald Trump threatened to impose 100% tariffs on Canada if they engage in trade deals with China.
  • Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau responded firmly, adhering to existing trade agreements like CUSMA.
  • This action is viewed by some as erratic and meaningless, in contrast to other countries like Canada and Europe, which are forging alliances.

  • Professor Justin Wolfers from the University of Michigan explained that the foundations of prosperity—strong institutions, the rule of law, and stable economic policies—are being undermined by the current administration's actions.
  • This undermining is predicted to lead to a loss of future economic opportunities and innovation, despite short-term economic indicators.
  • The hosts plan to integrate more of Professor Wolfers' economic analysis into their network.

  • Bill Barr wrote an op-ed supporting Pam Bondi's handling of the Epstein files, suggesting a move by Donald Trump to bring Barr back into his orbit.
  • This adds to concerns about perceived cover-ups related to the Epstein files, with one host questioning the slow release of information given personal experience with document production.
  • Barr's connection to the case also includes his father hiring Epstein and Barr's oversight of the case during Trump's term.

  • Border Patrol Commander-at-Large Greg Bovino, previously deployed to Minneapolis, was demoted and will return to his former role in El Centro, California, with likely retirement plans.
  • This demotion is viewed as a reconsideration of aggressive tactics following the death of Alex Pretty.
  • Hosts noted that Trump's actions were driven by concern over negative media coverage, not remorse for the killing, and identified Stephen Miller as the primary force behind policies, with Bovino serving as a scapegoat.

  • The United States is experiencing negative international perception, with a former FIFA president, Sepp Blatter, reportedly advising World Cup fans to avoid the U.S. due to its chaotic state.
  • This contrasts sharply with Donald Trump's claims of renewed respect for the U.S., which the hosts assert has brought shame.
  • The MeidasTouch Network is expanding its international coverage, including reporter Ken Harbaugh's work in Greenland and Ukraine, noting negative sentiment towards Donald Trump in Greenland.
