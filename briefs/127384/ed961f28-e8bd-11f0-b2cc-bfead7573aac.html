BREAKING: Trump Invades Venezuela

Key Takeaways

  • The United States military initiated a full-scale invasion of Venezuela.
  • US airstrikes targeted Venezuelan air bases, causing power outages.
  • The host states the invasion lacks Congressional authorization and is unlawful.

Deep Dive

  • The United States military launched a full-scale invasion of Venezuela, as reported by the host.
  • Initial targets included the Carlota Air Base in Caracas and Isla Margarita.
  • Footage displayed explosions, smoke, and U.S. Army helicopters over the capital.
  • The host asserted the invasion is unlawful due to a lack of authorization from the U.S. Congress.

  • Airstrikes targeted specific Venezuelan air bases, including El Libertador Air Base and Higuerote airport.
  • These actions resulted in power outages across various Caracas suburbs.
  • The invasion is described as ongoing, with continuous updates expected.
