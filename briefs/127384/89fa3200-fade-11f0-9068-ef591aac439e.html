Trump Loses It and Backs Down in Minnesota

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump adjusted his position on Border Patrol and ICE deployment in Minnesota after public pressure and protests.
  • The death of Alex Pretty in Minneapolis during an encounter with federal agents triggered significant national discussion and calls for accountability.
  • Internal criticism of the Trump administration's actions emerged from both his supporter base and within the Republican party.
  • Political gridlock over government funding, including appropriations for ICE, and a 2025 measles surge present national challenges.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump asserted a respectful call with Minnesota Governor Tim Walls, retracting previous attacks on the state and governor, following public pressure regarding Border Patrol and ICE.
  • Trump's social media posts detailed conversations with Governor Walls and Tom Homan, while also referencing alleged welfare fraud amounting to $20 billion and Congresswoman Ilhan Omar.
  • The host challenged Trump's claims of crime reduction in Minnesota, attributing it to Governor Walls and local law enforcement efforts, not federal intervention.

  • A Fox host questioned labeling Alex Pretty a domestic terrorist following his actions in Minneapolis, while a DOJ representative stated they are not prejudging facts and await video evidence.
  • Stephen Miller labeled Pretty a domestic terrorist, accusing Democrats of inciting insurrection to protect undocumented immigrants.
  • Official accounts describe Alex Pretty as an armed suspect who reacted violently during an encounter with Border Patrol and ICE, leading to his death at the scene.

  • A Wall Street Journal op-ed, 'Time for ICE to pause in Minneapolis,' argued against federal law enforcement shooting people or arresting children, stating Pretty was not a domestic terrorist.
  • It was alleged that Alex Pretty was peacefully protesting and attempting to aid a woman being pepper-sprayed by ICE and Border Patrol, legally carrying a concealed handgun without brandishing it before being killed.
  • Critiques were raised against Trump regime supporters who claimed Pretty deserved to be killed for having a gun at a protest, arguing this undermines Second Amendment rights.

  • Donald Trump faces backlash from some supporters expressing disillusionment over his policies and actions, particularly concerning ICE and Border Patrol.
  • Florida Governor Ron DeSantis publicly criticized the Trump administration and the U.S. dollar's performance.
  • Ron Filipkowski questioned government transparency regarding the agents involved in Alex Pretty's death and the existence of a federal secret police force.

  • Minnesota Republican gubernatorial candidate Chris Maydell withdrew from the race due to disagreements with the national Republican Party's actions towards the state.
  • U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations, Mike Walls, faced criticism for allegedly spreading misinformation about healthcare in Greenland and Denmark.
  • The U.S. experienced a significant surge in measles cases in 2025, described as astounding, while political gridlock delayed a government funding bill, including increased ICE funding, due to House Speaker Mike Johnson sending the House on recess.
