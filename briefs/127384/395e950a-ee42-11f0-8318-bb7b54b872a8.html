Ben Folds Responds to Performers Leaving Kennedy Center

Key Takeaways

  • Approximately a dozen performers resigned from the Kennedy Center following its alleged renaming.
  • The renaming of the Kennedy Center to the 'Trump Kennedy Center' drew strong public and artistic condemnation.
  • Musician Ben Folds' prior resignation as artistic advisor due to perceived propaganda was discussed.
  • Artists are navigating difficult decisions regarding performances at the Kennedy Center amidst political interference.
  • Concerns were raised about authoritarian control over cultural institutions and its impact on artistic freedom.

Deep Dive

  • Approximately a dozen performers resigned from the Kennedy Center in protest.
  • The protest concerns the alleged 'vandalism' and renaming of the venue to the 'Trump Kennedy Center.'
  • The Washington National Opera is moving after decades, and numerous other acts including Hamilton and Chuck Redd canceled performances.

  • Ben Meiselas spoke with musician Ben Folds regarding his February resignation as artistic advisor to the National Symphony Orchestra.
  • Folds had resigned in protest of board changes and perceived propaganda, with his initial interview going viral.
  • The conversation framed the mass resignations as a continuation of authoritarian actions initiated by the Trump administration.

  • The discussion highlighted the historical significance of the Kennedy Center and the National Symphony Orchestra.
  • It referenced Mstislav Rostropovich, a conductor who fled the Soviet Union for artistic freedom in the US.
  • The segment contrasted this legacy with current political interference at the institution.

  • The guest explained artists have varying timelines for taking a stand, influenced by job security and fear of legal repercussions.
  • The guest expressed personal reluctance to perform at the Kennedy Center due to safety concerns and perceived lack of competent leadership.
  • Empathy was conveyed for arts professionals continuing to work at the Kennedy Center despite difficult circumstances.

  • The host and guest discussed the significance of authoritarian control over cultural institutions like the Kennedy Center.
  • They noted that artists should express themselves honestly and politically.
  • The conversation highlighted the resilience of people as institutions face political pressures.
