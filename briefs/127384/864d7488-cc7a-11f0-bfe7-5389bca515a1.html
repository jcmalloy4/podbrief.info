Trump and GOP Panic as Easy Special Election Slips Away!!!

Key Takeaways

  • Polling indicates a tightening race in Tennessee's 7th Congressional District special election.
  • MAGA Republican Matt Van Epps faces criticism for his culture war focus and policy stances.
  • Democrat Afton Bain campaigns on economic affordability and effective governance.
  • The race is seen as a potential upset for Democrats in a traditionally Republican stronghold.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump and MAGA Republicans reportedly express panic over potential Democratic gains in Tennessee's 7th congressional district special election.
  • An Emerson College poll shows Republican Matt Van Epps leading Democrat Afton Bain by only 2 points (48% to 46%), with 5% undecided.
  • Independent and late-breaking voters are trending towards the Democratic candidate, potentially leading to a significant defeat for Republicans.
  • Democratic candidate Afton Bain is within striking distance due to surging Democratic votes in Davidson County and low Republican enthusiasm in Montgomery County (+1 for Van Epps).

  • MAGA Republican candidate Matt Van Epps focuses on culture war issues and supports Trump's tariffs.
  • The host criticized Van Epps for a perceived disconnect between the wealthy and the middle class regarding Trump's tax bill.
  • Van Epps avoided direct answers on the Epstein files, stating support for the Speaker's plan for transparency via an oversight committee with subpoena power.
  • A political ad criticized Van Epps as a 'puppet for the billionaire elite,' funded by Wall Street, and against social security and healthcare access for Tennesseans.

  • A TV ad contrasted Van Epps's stance on healthcare, tariffs, and tax breaks for billionaires with Bain's focus on lower costs and ending the grocery tax.
  • Democratic candidate Afton Bain was shown canvassing in a selfie video, expressing energy about a new poll showing the race within 2 points.
  • Bain urged voters to go to the polls before Thanksgiving, while Van Epps is presented as focusing on cultural issues like transgender rights and bathroom policies.

  • Matt Van Epps criticized Afton Bain's platform as a 'socialist agenda,' accusing her of supporting transgender surgery for minors and believing men can give birth.
  • The host criticized Van Epps's stance on immigration, comparing his support for ICE operations and deportation of undocumented immigrants to concentration camps.
  • Van Epps avoided reporters' questions regarding tariffs impacting farmers, the Epstein files, and campaign funding, often running away from queries.
  • Van Epps publicly expressed gratitude for Donald Trump's endorsement, pledging to 'have his back in Congress'.

  • Democratic candidate Afton Bain's campaign focuses on Tennessee's affordability crisis, criticizing Washington Republicans for exacerbating issues.
  • Bain entered the race after a bill she described as a 'transfer of wealth' passed, asserting she is running to represent working people against corporate donors.
  • Bain criticized opponent Matt Van Epps for employing the 'MAGA playbook' of culture war issues as a distraction from economic problems.
  • She suggested a 'rupture' in the paradigm, with people realizing scapegoating immigrants is not the cause of high grocery prices.
