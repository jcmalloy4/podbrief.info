Total Havoc Breaks Loose as Trump Caught on Camera Over Holiday

Key Takeaways

  • Allegations surfaced of escalated ICE operations and use of force over Halloween weekend.
  • Multiple incidents involved ICE agents near schools and public confrontations with civilians.
  • A family separation by ICE led to protests and alleged agent misconduct in Durango, Colorado.
  • An ICE agent fired shots into a vehicle in Phoenix, prompting an FBI investigation.
  • A mother of eight was deported shortly after filing a sexual harassment lawsuit against an ICE contractor.

Deep Dive

  • The host alleged ICE agents accelerated assaults and invasions across the US over Halloween, aiming to create a pretext for military intervention.
  • Incidents included a mother of four with a work permit detained in Salt Lake City and another mother arrested for not memorizing her social security number.
  • Footage reportedly showed ICE agents firing into a car windshield with children inside, and federal agents hitting a man on the ground in Evanston.

  • ICE operations near elementary schools were highlighted, with allegations they aim to terrorize communities.
  • An incident in Gurney, Illinois, involved ICE agents in a high-speed chase in a school parking lot, nearly hitting parents and students.
  • In Cicero, Illinois, videos allegedly showed ICE agents with batons shattering truck windows, dragging individuals out, and detaining them in unmarked cars.
  • Staff at the Cicero location were reportedly instructed to delete the footage of the incident.

  • ICE reportedly detained a Colombian father and his two children (ages 15 and 12), separating them and sending the father hundreds of miles away to Texas.
  • The children were allegedly moved to an undisclosed location, sparking protests in Durango, Colorado.
  • Local police attempted a welfare check on the detained children and to deliver food but were denied access by ICE.
  • During protests, agents allegedly knocked over a fire pit and used pepper spray and rubber bullets on demonstrators, with one woman reportedly physically assaulted.

  • In Phoenix, Arizona, an ICE agent fired two shots at an SUV during a traffic stop, creating two bullet holes in the driver's side window.
  • The agent claimed to fear for his life as the vehicle drove away; the FBI is investigating the incident.
  • An expert noted less transparency in federal agent deadly force investigations compared to local police.
  • It is unknown if the agent's body-worn camera was active during the shooting, despite ICE policy requirements.

  • Silvia Reina Mendoza, a mother of eight, was deported from Northern California after filing a civil lawsuit against an ICE contractor for sexual harassment.
  • Her attorneys question the timing, stating she was deported the morning after her story aired and suspecting retaliation.
  • Mendoza alleges her ICE monitor, Luis Ruiz, sent explicit messages and her supervisor took no action.
  • The lawsuit against BI Incorporated is expected to continue, with Mendoza claiming her deportation was illegal and that she had an emergency stay.
