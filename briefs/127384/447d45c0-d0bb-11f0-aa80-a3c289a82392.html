Trump Looks Very Sick as He Falls Apart at WH Presser

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's press conference included unusual behavior and statements regarding his appearance.
  • Trump made claims about affordability being a "con job" and tariff-generated revenue.
  • Controversial remarks were made concerning Somalian immigrants and Representative Ilhan Omar.
  • The House Judiciary Committee's interest in Jack Smith's deposition was discussed, alongside Trump's "war" comments.

Deep Dive

  • During a press conference, Donald Trump's appearance was described as disastrous, with attempts noted to hide his hand.
  • Trump reportedly made sounds like 'bootajed jedge' while others spoke, drawing laughter from attendees.
  • He appeared to fall asleep during Senator Marsha Blackburn's speech, only to be startled when Senator Ted Cruz appeared.

  • Donald Trump attacked the concept of affordability, labeling it a 'massive con job' and a 'joke,' while claiming high inflation.
  • He stated he found $30 billion from tariffs, recounting a story where he insisted tariffs had already generated revenue.
  • When questioned about Putin's statements regarding Ukraine ceding eastern territory, Trump mentioned Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff had a 'reasonably good meeting' with Putin.

  • Donald Trump criticized Somalians and Representative Ilhan Omar, stating they complain about the U.S. and should 'go back to their own country.'
  • The host labeled Trump's remarks about Somalian immigrants and Ilhan Omar as racist.
  • Trump claimed that under his presidency, the war in Ukraine would not have occurred, and 100% of Ukraine's territory would be intact.

  • The House Judiciary Committee reportedly desires a secret deposition of Jack Smith regarding his investigation of Donald Trump, with MAGA Republicans preferring privacy due to alleged fear of Smith.
  • A reporter questioned Trump about potential punishment for individuals involved if survivors were killed clinging to a boat, to which Trump characterized the situation as war.
  • Trump stated that 'millions' of Americans were killed in the context of the situation and accused those piloting the boats of trying to kill people. The speaker subsequently labeled Trump a 'war criminal.'
