Whoa! Trump Panics as Presser Resurfaces on his Dark Past

Key Takeaways

  • A 2019 press conference by Trump's federal prosecutors regarding Jeffrey Epstein has resurfaced.
  • Host asserts government shutdown is linked to concealing Epstein files and preventing their release.
  • Federal prosecutors detailed sex trafficking charges against Epstein, involving dozens of minor girls.
  • Jeffrey Epstein's neighbor, Howard Lutnick, described him as a "creep" and "ultimate blackmailer."

Deep Dive

  • A July 8, 2019, press conference by Trump's federal prosecutors detailed Jeffrey Epstein's arrest.
  • A search warrant on Epstein's mansion led to the seizure of evidence, including nude photographs of underage girls.
  • The host asserts that Donald Trump is intentionally prolonging the government shutdown to conceal these files.

  • Federal prosecutors detailed sex trafficking charges against Jeffrey Epstein, including conspiracy and exploitation of underage girls as young as 14.
  • Charges were unsealed for alleged exploitation of dozens of minor girls between 2002 and 2005.
  • The investigation was a joint effort involving the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York, FBI, NYPD, and Customs and Border Patrol.

  • Bill Sweeney of the FBI detailed Epstein's arrest at Teterboro Airport and the charges he faced.
  • The FBI emphasized its commitment to protecting children and bringing perpetrators to justice.
  • Victims and those with information were urged to call 1-800-CALL-FBI.

  • The FBI acknowledged the difficulty for victims and assured them they would be prioritized.
  • Jeffrey Epstein's non-prosecution agreement with the Southern District of Florida did not bind the Southern District of New York.
  • The investigation's origin was not disclosed, though investigative journalism provided assistance.

  • Howard Lutnick, Trump's former commerce secretary and Epstein's neighbor, described Epstein as a "creep" and "ultimate blackmailer."
  • Lutnick claimed Epstein blackmailed his friends and stated he immediately recognized him as "bad business."
  • An encounter at Epstein's NYC mansion, involving a massage table and suggestive comments, prompted Lutnick and his wife to leave.
