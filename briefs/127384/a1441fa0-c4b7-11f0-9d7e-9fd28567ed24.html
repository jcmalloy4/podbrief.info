Trump Blows Up in Oval at Press Event with Saudi Prince

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump reacted aggressively to reporters at a joint press event with Saudi Crown Prince MBS.
  • Trump defended MBS and Saudi Arabia, dismissing US intelligence on the Khashoggi murder allegations.
  • Saudi Arabia announced a $1 trillion investment pledge in the US, which Trump reacted to with surprise.
  • Trump made various claims about the US economy and criticized the Biden administration's record.

Deep Dive

  • During a press conference with Saudi Crown Prince MBS, Donald Trump displayed aggressive reactions to reporters, particularly female journalists.
  • Trump dismissed a reporter's network as 'fake news' when questioned about his family's business with Saudi Arabia and the Khashoggi murder.
  • He defended MBS, calling him a respected figure who allegedly knew nothing about the Khashoggi situation, while criticizing a reporter's 'insubordinate' question.
  • Trump also attacked another reporter's attitude regarding questions about delaying the release of the Epstein files.

  • Donald Trump stated he and Saudi Arabia have 'always been on the same side of every issue,' a claim questioned by the host referencing 9/11.
  • Trump referenced a previous meeting in the Oval Office with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and his father, the king.
  • He credited himself with preventing Iran's nuclear capacity, associating this with the US-Saudi relationship.

  • Saudi Crown Prince MBS announced an investment of $1 trillion in the US, an increase from a previously mentioned $600 billion.
  • A reporter questioned the source of this trillion-dollar investment, asking if the numbers were being fabricated.
  • MBS clarified these were real investment opportunities in sectors like AI and semiconductors, with $50 billion planned for short-term semiconductor investments and hundreds of billions long-term.
  • Trump reportedly joked about not being able to ask MBS to build a ballroom at the White House due to restrictions on foreign donations.

  • Donald Trump asked MBS if he was the 'best president,' leading to Trump grabbing MBS's hand and sticking out his tongue.
  • MBS stated he has worked with all US presidents, acknowledging Trump's close ties to Saudi Arabia.
  • Trump called MBS a 'great ally' after the $1 trillion investment announcement, linking it to national security and job creation.
  • Trump expressed entitlement to applause during the press conference, stating he was dealing with the 'future king of Saudi Arabia' and didn't have to be there.

  • Donald Trump claimed MBS stated the US inherited 'a mess from Biden' and that he was doing a 'great job' fixing it.
  • Trump asserted the US is the 'hottest country in the world' and is bringing down prices substantially, including gasoline.
  • He claimed $21 trillion would be invested in the US, an amount the host disputed, questioning why the national debt and deficits were surging.
  • Trump described visitors to the Oval Office as impressed with its condition, attributing the positive state to his administration.
