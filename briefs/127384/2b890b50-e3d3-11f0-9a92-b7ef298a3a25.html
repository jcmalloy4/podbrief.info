Melania Throws Donald Under the Bus on Epstein Files

Key Takeaways

  • Melania Trump is reportedly evading service of a lawsuit filed by author Michael Wolff.
  • Wolff's preemptive lawsuit stems from Melania Trump's $1 billion defamation threat.
  • The legal battle could lead to extensive discovery, including depositions of Donald and Melania Trump.
  • Questions surrounding Melania Trump's immigration history and connections have resurfaced.

Deep Dive

  • Melania Trump is reportedly evading service of a lawsuit filed by author Michael Wolff.
  • Wolff claims her threats to sue him over statements about her connection to Jeffrey Epstein constituted abusive process.
  • Process servers have been reportedly prevented from serving her at Trump Tower, and her lawyer has refused to accept the lawsuit.

  • Michael Wolff filed a lawsuit against Melania Trump on October 21st, 2025, predating the release of the Epstein files.
  • This action followed a demand letter from Melania Trump's attorney on October 15th, 2025.
  • The letter threatened a $1 billion lawsuit if Wolff did not retract statements concerning Melania Trump's alleged involvement in the Epstein scandal.
  • Wolff claims this threat created a "chilling impact" on his First Amendment rights.

  • Melania Trump's lawyer, Alejandro Brito, sent a letter on October 15th, 2025, demanding retraction of statements in a Daily Beast article claiming Melania was "very involved" in the Epstein scandal.
  • The lawsuit details alleged defamatory statements, including claims Melania was the "missing link" in Trump's ties to Jeffrey Epstein and was introduced through Epstein-linked individuals.
  • Wolff's lawsuit aims to obtain discovery, including potential subpoenas for Donald and Melania Trump, their phones, text messages, and emails.

  • Michael Wolff has stated his intention to pursue discovery, including depositions of both Donald Trump and Melania Trump, regarding their connections to Jeffrey Epstein.
  • The host discussed the desire for Donald Trump's deposition to uncover details of his financial and alleged sexual involvement with Epstein.
  • The situation is compared to a previous instance where Melania Trump threatened to sue Hunter Biden, which could have allowed for discovery into the Trump-Epstein relationship.

  • Michael Wolff reportedly possesses knowledge regarding Jeffrey Epstein, including claims that Epstein showed him photographs.
  • These images allegedly included topless girls in Donald Trump's lap and one depicting a stain on Trump's pants.
  • It is speculated that these photographs might have been stored in Epstein's safe, which the FBI subsequently emptied.

  • Questions are raised regarding Melania Trump's immigration to the U.S., including potential visa discrepancies and the nature of her "Einstein visa."
  • Paulo Zampoli, a former modeling agent, is credited with introducing Donald and Melania Trump at a 1998 party and allegedly helped secure Melania's U.S. visa.
  • Donald Trump appointed Zampoli as special envoy for global partnership in March 2015.
  • Potential deposition questions for Melania Trump may cover her work visas, green card acquisition in 2001, and travel to the U.S. before 1996, amid reports she may have entered or worked illegally prior to her 1996 work visa.
