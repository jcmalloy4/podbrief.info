Trump Has Disaster Saturday as He Rushes Out of White House

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump golfed during a government shutdown, avoiding press interactions.
  • The government shutdown threatens healthcare access for millions and led to federal worker dismissals.
  • Trump's social media posts on tariffs contributed to significant market drops.
  • The U.S. Treasury executed a $20 billion currency swap with Argentina's central bank.
  • House Speaker Mike Johnson linked the government shutdown to upcoming protests.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump left the White House at 8:45 a.m. to golf, avoiding press interaction, marking his second consecutive week golfing during a government shutdown.
  • Speculation regarding Trump's health, specifically chronic venous cardiovascular issues, was discussed despite a recent White House report stating excellent health.

  • The government shutdown is projected to cause 20 million Americans to lose healthcare coverage or face unaffordable costs without Affordable Care Act subsidies.
  • The shutdown is being utilized to advance Project 2025, and 4,000 federal workers have been fired, including dozens of disease detectives and scientists at the CDC.

  • Market indices including the Dow, S&P, and NASDAQ dropped significantly following Donald Trump's social media posts regarding tariffs on China.
  • An agreement was announced for a Qatari Air Force base in Idaho, hosting Qatari F-15s and pilots for joint training.
  • The host criticized Trump for focusing on culture war issues, such as a Columbus Day Proclamation, while neglecting critical matters like healthcare and housing affordability.

  • The Qatari Air Force base agreement was contrasted with the absence of MAGA Republicans from Congress, with Democrats reportedly active while Republicans were on vacation.
  • The U.S. Treasury directly purchased Argentine pesos and enacted a $20 billion currency swap with Argentina's central bank to assist the country amidst economic turmoil.
  • Argentina's President Javier Milei's efforts to reform the country's economic cycle and reduce China's influence were highlighted, with the U.S. program noted as an investment opportunity rather than a bailout.

  • Venezuela requested a UN Security Council session due to an anticipated U.S. armed attack, amidst accusations that the Trump regime destroyed a Colombian fishing boat.
  • House Speaker Mike Johnson attributed the government shutdown to pro-Hamas and Antifa protests scheduled for October 18th, criticizing Democrats for allegedly supporting these events.
