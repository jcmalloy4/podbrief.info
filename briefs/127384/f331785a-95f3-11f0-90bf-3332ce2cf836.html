Trump’s Dark Past Surfaces as Prosecutor Folds Under Oath

Key Takeaways

  • Alex Acosta faced congressional questioning over the Jeffrey Epstein plea deal.
  • Acosta admitted responsibility for the deal but not reading victim statements.
  • Congressional members criticized Acosta's evasiveness and credibility.
  • Donald Trump defended Acosta's actions prior to his resignation in 2019.
  • Democrats plan to pursue Epstein-related files, potentially post-midterms.

Deep Dive

  • Alex Acosta took responsibility for Jeffrey Epstein's sweetheart deal but admitted not reading statements from over 40 victims.
  • His prosecutors had a 50-page indictment prepared, but Acosta and others allegedly prevented Epstein's federal prosecution.
  • Congressmembers Stansbury and Garcia highlighted Acosta's refusal to acknowledge victim suffering or admit the non-prosecution agreement was a 'sweetheart deal.'

  • Congressman Dave Min described Alex Acosta as a non-credible and evasive witness during his deposition.
  • Min cited Acosta's lack of believable answers regarding his alleged non-involvement in reviewing case evidence, despite Epstein's high-profile status and Acosta's meetings with Epstein's attorneys.
  • Congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez also found Acosta evasive, struggling to get straightforward answers on his decisions not to charge Epstein federally.

  • Alex Acosta resigned as Labor Secretary on July 12, 2019, stating he wanted to focus on the economy instead of the 'old Epstein case.'
  • Donald Trump publicly supported Acosta on July 9, 2019, claiming unawareness of Epstein and asserting Acosta did nothing wrong.
  • The podcast host contrasted Trump's claims with later public knowledge of Trump's association with Epstein.

  • A speaker suggested the decision regarding Jeffrey Epstein was made by multiple individuals, not just one, and would undergo careful examination.
  • Another individual expressed sympathy for Secretary Acosta concerning the situation surrounding Epstein.
  • Multiple speakers mentioned knowing Jeffrey Epstein from Palm Beach but had a falling out with him approximately 15 years prior.
