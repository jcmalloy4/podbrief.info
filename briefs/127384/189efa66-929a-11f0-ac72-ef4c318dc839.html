MeidasTouch Full Podcast - 9/16/25

Key Takeaways

  • The aftermath of the Charlie Kirk shooting sparked controversy over media narratives and political exploitation.
  • Extreme rhetoric from political figures is increasingly normalized by some media outlets.
  • Allegations surfaced regarding financial dealings by the Trump regime involving foreign entities.
  • Concerns were raised about the FBI's leadership and potential political manipulation of information.
  • US military actions in Venezuela are questioned amidst claims of unsubstantiated justifications.
  • The issue of presidential authority and its potential limits was debated by a judicial nominee.
  • Donald Trump's foreign policy claims and domestic threats against cities faced scrutiny.

Deep Dive

  • The shooter of Charlie Kirk was identified as a 22-year-old male from a religious, gun-owning family in Utah.
  • Initial right-wing media reports blamed the trans community, despite the shooter being a young white male from a pro-Trump household.
  • A transgender individual reportedly aided authorities in the aftermath of the shooting.

  • Donald Trump claimed unfamiliarity with a request to lower flags for Minnesota House Speaker Melissa Hortman, assassinated by a Trump supporter, stating he would have complied if the governor had asked.
  • Charlie Kirk made disparaging remarks about Kamala Harris and called for the death penalty for Joe Biden in a clip played on the podcast.
  • Corporate news media is criticized for normalizing such hate speech and conflating random social media users with major Republican figures.

  • Donald Trump is accused of removing experienced FBI personnel focused on domestic terrorism.
  • A 22-year-old intern with no relevant experience was allegedly appointed to a leadership role within the FBI.
  • The FBI is criticized for potentially leaking or fabricating information under political influence to satisfy followers and deflect from inconvenient truths.

  • A New York Times exposé detailed a deal where the UAE received advanced semiconductor technology from the Trump regime.
  • Entities linked to the Trump and Witkoff families allegedly received $1-2 billion in funding for a crypto venture in return for the UAE deal.
  • This arrangement raised national security concerns, including the potential sharing of technology with China.

  • The current administration is alleged to be orchestrating a 'massive grift and swindle,' profiting from chaos and division.
  • A multi-billion dollar crypto scheme involving World Liberty Financial and the UAE is highlighted as a major potential scandal.
  • This situation is contrasted with past presidents, such as Jimmy Carter, who placed assets in a blind trust.

  • Prosecutor Maureen Comey, involved in the Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell cases, is suing the Trump administration for alleged unlawful firing.
  • Cash Patel previously claimed the FBI controlled and withheld Epstein's 'black book' and files.
  • The handling of the Charlie Kirk manifesto is compared to the Nashville school shooting manifesto, suggesting FBI intervention to prevent certain information releases.

  • US military actions off the coast of Venezuela involved the destruction of fishing boats.
  • Donald Trump released 'execution-style videos' of these actions, which were compared to ISIS propaganda.
  • Trump made unsubstantiated claims that the fishing boat was carrying cocaine and used grossly inaccurate drug-related death statistics to justify military actions.

  • A judicial nominee refused to give a direct 'no' when asked if Donald Trump could order the killing of political opponents.
  • This raised concerns about the implications of presidential rhetoric labeling individuals as 'narco-terrorists,' potentially leading to lethal action without due process.
  • The hosts advocated for comprehensive strategies against drug smuggling over indiscriminate killing, questioning the military's adherence to potentially illegal orders.

  • Donald Trump's claims of resolving seven wars, including peace deals in Congo and India, were questioned.
  • His unfulfilled promise to bring peace to Ukraine within 24 hours was noted, as Russia escalated drone attacks during his tenure.
  • Trump allegedly threatened to withhold federal funds from New York City if mayoral candidate Zoran Mamdani was elected, which hosts called 'impeachable and criminal.'
