Fox News Collapses on Air as Trump Crashes and Burns

Key Takeaways

  • Fox News struggled to frame economic data and Epstein file release positively for Donald Trump.
  • Scrutiny focuses on authenticity and beneficiaries of multi-trillion-dollar foreign investment claims linked to Donald Trump.
  • Senator Lindsey Graham plans lawsuit over Jan 6th phone record subpoena, citing emotional damages.
  • Charlotte protests grew chaotic following deployment of ICE and Border Patrol agents.
  • New economic proposals and Trump's Federal Reserve suitability faced debate.

Deep Dive

  • Fox News anchors reportedly reacted with confusion on air to the Epstein file vote and negative jobs data.
  • The host characterized the Trump economy as a 'disaster' due to low approval ratings and negative job reports.
  • A Fox News clip showed a host expressing confusion over low consumer confidence despite claims of falling prices.
  • Greg Gutfeld discussed Donald Trump's reaction to the Epstein file release, suggesting Trump was concerned about missed leverage opportunities with figures like Prince Andrew.

  • The host criticized Fox News for reporting a $1.1 trillion Saudi investment, noting Saudi Arabia's GDP is approximately $1 trillion.
  • It was suggested that purported deals for this sum were with Donald Trump's private company, rather than directly benefiting the United States.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessant confirmed a $14.2 billion GE Vernova deal in South Carolina, creating 1,800 jobs.
  • Bessant referenced Donald Trump's figure of $20 trillion in total investment, stating he believes those commitments are real.
  • The host questioned the validity of Trump's financial figures, citing increasing deficits, past bankruptcies, and contrasting them with Biden administration investment totals.

  • Senator Lindsey Graham announced on Fox News his intention to sue the government.
  • He seeks tens of millions of dollars for alleged emotional damage.
  • The lawsuit stems from his phone records being subpoenaed in connection with the January 6th investigation.
  • Graham claimed a lack of defenses against such lawsuits, describing it as a self-created cause of action.

  • Fox News reported on protests in Charlotte, linked to Donald Trump's deployment of ICE and Border Patrol agents.
  • An on-scene reporter described the protest as growing, chaotic, and potentially dangerous.
  • Observations included people burning rubber and the presence of children at the protest.

  • A proposal for a $2,000 dividend check was discussed, raising concerns about its potential inflationary impact.
  • Criticism was directed at Treasury Secretary Scott Bessett's suggestion of Donald Trump as a suitable Federal Reserve chair, citing Trump's business bankruptcies.
  • A new proposal suggested government accounts for every child born in the next three years, invested in the stock market.
  • This initiative aimed to give young people a direct stake in the economy.
