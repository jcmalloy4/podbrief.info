All Hell Breaks Loose as Trump Turns City into War Zone!!

Key Takeaways

  • Federal agents allegedly used chemical weapons and flashbang grenades against protesters in Minneapolis and St. Paul.
  • Multiple reports detail ICE and Border Patrol agents allegedly assaulting and unlawfully detaining U.S. citizens.
  • Incidents include alleged groping, body slams, child kidnapping, and severe injuries from projectiles.
  • The podcast criticizes federal agents' conduct and questions their motivations for widespread aggression.
  • Allegations of excessive force by federal agents are reported nationwide, extending beyond the Twin Cities.

Deep Dive

  • Minneapolis and St. Paul were described as a 'war zone' by the host, citing 'war crimes' by federal agents.
  • ICE agents allegedly deployed flash bang grenades and gaseous agents against protesters in Minneapolis at 0:06.
  • Footage from Status Coup reportedly shows Border Patrol agents ramming vehicles, causing injuries, and deploying tear gas and pepper spray at 0:48.
  • A U.S. Navy veteran expressed shock and fear, questioning if this was the America they served.

  • ICE and Border Patrol agents were allegedly shown assaulting U.S. citizens, including instances of body slamming and unwarranted arrests from 12:14.
  • Agents allegedly used flashlights as a pretext for detainment, leading to the arrest of a woman with a small flashlight.
  • Footage at 14:38 showed ICE agents allegedly kidnapping a child in freezing conditions.

  • Masked, armed Border Patrol agents were depicted at 16:01 allegedly detaining U.S. citizens in Minneapolis, threatening them in their own neighborhood.
  • In St. Paul at 17:31, four ICE agents allegedly detained a U.S. citizen woman, groping her, exposing her midriff, and shoving her into an SUV, causing her to hit her head.
  • An incident in Santa Ana at 19:18 described a 21-year-old allegedly blinded in one eye by a 'non-lethal' projectile fired by ICE agents.

  • Footage at 20:08 showed a protester with a bleeding leg injury from shrapnel caused by flashbang grenades fired by ICE at unarmed individuals.
  • A U.S. veteran expressed strong opposition to ICE agents from 21:15, stating they were destroying the country and harming citizens.
  • ICE agents in Minneapolis were shown at 22:50 allegedly detaining, threatening, and arresting a U.S. citizen who refused to show identification, escalating to physical force.
  • During an arrest at 23:42, an ICE agent allegedly threatened to tase the citizen and handled a legally registered firearm recklessly.

  • The host questioned the motivations of ICE agents at 25:02, asking why they are aggressive towards immigrants and citizens.
  • A video at 26:54 showed ICE agents deploying tear gas at a special education school in St. Paul during student drop-off while arresting a teacher.
  • An incident in West Virginia was cited where fire department personnel allegedly assisted ICE in detaining roofers who reportedly possessed work permits.
