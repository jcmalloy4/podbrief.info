Sickly Trump Gets Terrible News as City Runs Him Out

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's "Operation Midway Blitz" in Chicago faced protests and was ultimately shelved.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Donald Trump's physical deterioration and its potential implications.
  • The national deficit is projected to reach $2 trillion, amid questions about fiscal responsibility.
  • Economic indicators show inflation at 4.3%, shrinking manufacturing, and a record low job-finding probability.
  • Pentagon leadership directed staff to search for negative comments about Charlie Kirk from military members.

Deep Dive

  • An individual was fatally shot by ICE in Franklin Park, Chicago, during a traffic stop, with agents claiming self-defense.
  • ICE agents were observed wearing Trump pins, leading to allegations of Hatch Act violations and political bias within the agency.
  • Protesters at an ICE facility in Broadview, Illinois, were confronted by agents, one of whom displayed a Trump pin.
  • CNN reported that plans for a Trump-ordered crackdown in Chicago were shelved due to advisor concerns over potential legal issues.

  • Axios reported inflation at 4.3%, marking the highest level since 2021.
  • Manufacturing activity is shrinking, and there are more unemployed workers than job openings.
  • Howard Lutnick, Trump's commerce secretary, stated the economy would not be considered Trump's until 2026.
  • JP Morgan's analysis indicates that tariffs are contributing to increased inflation.

  • The national deficit is projected to reach $2 trillion for the fiscal year, exceeding the previous year.
  • The host critiqued MAGA Republicans for increasing national debt and deficits during their administrations.
  • A New York Fed survey indicated the probability of finding a job dropped to 44.91%, an all-time low.
  • California trucking firms are facing bankruptcies due to a prolonged freight slowdown.

  • Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth directed Pentagon staff to search for negative comments about Charlie Kirk made by military members.
  • This directive is seen as unusual and raises concerns given Kirk's history of making offensive remarks.
  • The host clarified that Kirk, despite not being a "hero," is subject to criticism for his past hateful statements.
