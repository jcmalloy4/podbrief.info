Trump Has Disaster Veterans Day and Loses It in Public

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump posted controversial content on Veterans Day, including calls for Obama's arrest.
  • Trump's social media included politically charged claims on racism and the US economy.
  • Economic hardship concerns voters, with Christmas spending projected to decrease by 1.3% in 2025.
  • President Biden and former President Obama honored veterans through traditional tributes and direct engagement.
  • Global partners like the UK and China are engaged in new intelligence and trade disputes with the US.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump posted controversial social media content, including calls for Barack Obama's arrest and sexually suggestive material, instead of directly acknowledging Veterans Day.
  • The host highlighted Trump's lack of specific Veterans Day posts, contrasting it with his other online activity.

  • Donald Trump's Veterans Day posts included patriotic pinups featuring women in American flag bikinis and underwear.
  • Trump also posted that Barack Obama should go to prison for creating the 'Russian collision' story, a claim the host noted as ironic.

  • On Veterans Day, Donald Trump posted an image asserting that racism was nearly gone until the Obamas 'set America back 100 years'.
  • He also commented on the vacancy rate of Chicago's Magnificent Mile, linking it to crime.

  • President Biden and Congressmember Eric Swalwell shared posts honoring veterans, contrasting with former President Trump's social media activity.
  • Former President Barack Obama surprised veterans on a flight, a gesture praised by the host as a demonstration of leadership.

  • Voters across political affiliations expressed concern about rising costs, with the economy identified as a top concern.
  • Donald Trump stated that affordability has improved and will continue to do so, citing mortgage rates as proof.
  • The host suggested economic issues are being framed as a 'con job' by Democrats manipulating media narratives, noting the term 'manufactured economy'.
  • Gasoline prices have significantly decreased since the Biden administration, impacting the overall economy.
