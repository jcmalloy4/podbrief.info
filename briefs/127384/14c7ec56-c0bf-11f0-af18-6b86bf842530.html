Trump Goes Silent on Thursday after Nightmare Scenario

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump remained silent on social media following the release of new emails linking him to Jeffrey Epstein.
  • Reports indicate Donald Trump's approval ratings are declining, particularly concerning the Jeffrey Epstein case.
  • Legal controversies surrounding Trump's former attorney, Todd Blanche, are intensifying amidst accusations.
  • Donald Trump's company filed 566 foreign worker visa applications over five years, contrasting with his public rhetoric.
  • The Department of Justice is accused of withholding documents implicating Donald Trump in Jeffrey Epstein emails.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump was silent on social media for 21 hours following the release of 20,000 new emails.
  • The emails reportedly link Trump to Jeffrey Epstein and suggest his awareness of Epstein's activities.
  • UK media reports revealed emails indicating Trump spent hours with an Epstein victim, prompting a question from Epstein's brother about Trump's trustworthiness.
  • A CNN report shows Trump's net approval rating is 39 points underwater concerning the Epstein case, worse than other issues.

  • California Governor Gavin Newsom publicly criticized Donald Trump, displaying a 'pedo' sign on the Walk of Fame.
  • George Conway criticized Trump's former attorney, Todd Blanche, regarding his interview with Ghislaine Maxwell.
  • Conway suggested Blanche was either incompetent or intentionally avoided crucial follow-up questions related to Trump and Epstein.
  • Blanche responded by questioning Conway's qualifications and stated law enforcement lacked current Epstein estate materials during his Maxwell interview.

  • The host accused Todd Blanche and Donald Trump of being part of a 'pedo protection program' and covering up a child sex trafficking ring.
  • Blanche was criticized for allegedly not utilizing available Epstein files and trial transcripts, and for firing prosecutors.
  • A potential admission of lying by Todd Blanche regarding a witness was discussed.
  • Lindsay Halligan, an attorney for Donald Trump, faces a hearing for potential disqualification as a U.S. attorney.

  • Donald Trump's company submitted 566 H-2A and H-2B visa applications for foreign workers over five years.
  • This record number of applications for foreign workers contrasts with Trump's public statements.
  • Concerns were raised about potential inaccuracies in economic data, like the Consumer Price Index, under a possible future Trump administration.

  • Congressman Jamie Raskin was mentioned in relation to the Jeffrey Epstein emails and Department of Justice documents.
  • It is asserted that the Department of Justice is withholding information implicating Donald Trump.
  • This alleged withholding of information relates to the ongoing investigation into the Epstein case.
