Trump Bends the Knee in White House... to Zohran?!

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump publicly praised NYC Mayor-elect Zohran Mamdani, contradicting prior criticisms.
  • The meeting focused on shared concerns like New York City's affordability and cost of living.
  • Trump expressed confidence in Mamdani's ability to address crime and housing development.
  • Trump's positive remarks created potential conflict with existing Republican campaign strategies.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump held a White House meeting with New York City Mayor-elect Zohran Mamdani.
  • Trump praised Mamdani and dismissed attacks against him, including being called a "fascist."
  • Trump noted that many of Mamdani's voters were also Trump supporters.

  • Mamdani stated the meeting focused on shared purposes, including serving New Yorkers and addressing the cost of living crisis.
  • Trump praised Mamdani for his potential role in crime reduction and benefiting New York City, congratulating him on his election.
  • Mamdani expressed appreciation for the productive meeting, discussing rent, groceries, and utilities to improve affordability.
  • He noted his campaign's focus on cost of living resonated with voters, including those who supported Trump.

  • Mamdani discussed efforts to lower energy costs, specifically addressing Con Edison rates, which haven't reflected decreased fuel prices.
  • When asked about calling Mamdani a communist, Trump stated they did not discuss the specific topic.
  • Trump acknowledged Mamdani has "views a little out there" but expressed confidence in his ability to do a good job for the city.

  • Zohran Mamdani discussed New York's tax dollars funding foreign conflicts and addressing city homelessness.
  • He expressed appreciation for Donald Trump's efforts towards peace.
  • Trump stated he would be comfortable living in New York City under a Mamdani administration.
  • Trump cited agreement on issues like trade and economic policy, acknowledging past connections with Bernie Sanders voters.

  • Donald Trump's praise for Mamdani conflicts with House Republicans' plans to use Mamdani in their 2026 campaign strategy.
  • Trump had previously criticized Mamdani, calling him a "communist" and falsely suggesting he was in the country illegally.
  • Trump now believes Mamdani will be effective in stopping crime and building housing to lower rents.
  • The host described Trump's positive comments as "bending the knee" given his past rhetoric.
