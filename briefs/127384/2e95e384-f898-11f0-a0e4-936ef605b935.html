Fox News Panics On Air Over Trump’s Catastrophic Trip

Key Takeaways

  • A general strike, originating in Minnesota, is spreading across the U.S. with calls for pro-democracy solidarity.
  • Donald Trump's Davos trip faced criticism over his cognitive state and a disputed Greenland deal.
  • Trump's comments on global warming, NATO allies, and fallen soldiers drew renewed scrutiny.
  • The U.S. Supreme Court's immunity decision for Trump contrasts with Brazil's Bolsonaro insurrection sentencing.
  • Senator J.D. Vance was criticized for labeling protesters and striking workers as domestic terrorists.

  • Senator J.D. Vance was criticized for labeling protesters and striking workers as sex traffickers and domestic terrorists.
