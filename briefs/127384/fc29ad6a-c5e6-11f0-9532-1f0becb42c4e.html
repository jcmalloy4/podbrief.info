Trump’s Health Takes Turn for Worse and He Can’t Hide It

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's observable physical health, including issues with his hands and walking, was discussed.
  • Instances of Donald Trump's disinhibited behavior were analyzed as potential symptoms of cognitive decline, referencing specific actions and expert explanations of frontal lobe damage.
  • Medical professionals and reports raised questions about Donald Trump's cognitive status, noting changes over the past 8-10 years and discussing the relevance of specific dementia symptoms.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump's apparent deteriorating health was discussed, citing visible issues with his hands, described as 'flesh-eating' or chronic venous insufficiency, and his unsteady walking.
  • His disinhibition was presented as a potential dementia symptom, exemplified by calling a reporter 'piggy' and repeatedly shouting 'eggs'.
  • A clip from Dementia Support Australia explained that frontal lobe damage can remove social filters, leading to disinhibition.
  • Further examples cited included Trump spraying people with perfume and sniffing them, and spraying cologne on a visiting leader in the Oval Office.
  • Past incidents, such as being found liable for sexual assault and alleged conduct with pageant contestants, were mentioned to contextualize his behavior.

  • A Daily Beast report was referenced, detailing doctors questioning Donald Trump's claims regarding an MRI.
  • Dr. Gupta discussed Trump's age, stress, and potential cognitive decline, noting a visible change over the past eight to ten years.

  • The host and a guest discussed the appropriateness of medical screenings, such as MRIs, for a world leader if there are specific neurological concerns or existing conditions being monitored.
  • A video clip from the Meidas Touch Network outlined symptoms linking peculiar behavior, mixing fiction and reality, and an inability to complete sentences to frontotemporal dementia.
  • Specific symptoms highlighted included slurred speech, struggling to find words, detached ideas, and impaired motor function, with advice to seek medical attention if these signs are present.
