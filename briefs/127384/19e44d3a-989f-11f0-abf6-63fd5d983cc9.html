Trump Gives Disaster UN Speech as Teleprompter Breaks

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's United Nations speech was characterized by the host as "bizarre" and "disastrous," featuring technical malfunctions.
  • Trump delivered controversial remarks concerning international policy, including criticisms of Europe, Brazil, and the London mayor.
  • The former President made strong assertions against renewable energy and dismissed climate change predictions.
  • Trump evaluated his speech positively afterward, citing technical issues as adding interest, while some media coverage was criticized for "sainwashing."
