The MeidasTouch Podcast

Trump Gets Caught on Tape as Hell Is Unleashed

Key Takeaways

  • The podcast highlights alleged aggressive and unlawful tactics by ICE agents in Chicago and Portland, including the use of chemical agents.
  • Federal court orders against using chemical warfare on citizens and journalists were reportedly violated by ICE personnel.
  • Citizen journalists and community members actively documented and confronted ICE actions, leading to direct clashes.
  • ICE detained the parents of a U.S. Marine, who possessed valid work visas, while they were visiting their pregnant daughter.

Deep Dive

  • The host alleges Donald Trump is captured on tape orchestrating invasions with chemical warfare across the U.S., claiming disappearances, beatings, killings, and concentration camps.
  • A video from Status Coo News reportedly shows ICE agents chasing an individual at a facility in Chicago.
  • The host emphasizes the importance of sharing this information globally to inform the public.

  • ICE agents in Chicago reportedly used tear gas on families and citizens filming, despite federal court orders preventing chemical warfare against citizens and journalists.
  • An ICE agent in Chicago allegedly refused to remove his mask, then threatened to arrest a U.S. citizen for impeding operations and pointed a gun at neighbors, actions claimed to violate federal court orders.
  • Footage from Gregory Royal Pratt shows ICE agents in Chicago threatening to arrest a cyclist for recording them.
  • ICE agents allegedly trapped volunteers in an alley in Chicago, aimed pepper bullet guns into a car, and then drew their weapons on bystanders documenting the scene.

  • ICE reportedly detained the parents of U.S. Marine Steve Rios, who held valid work visas and had no criminal record.
  • The parents were taken into custody while visiting their pregnant daughter at Camp Pendleton.
  • They were later released with ankle monitors, having been sponsored by their son for green cards.

  • Audio captures individuals in Chicago confronting ICE agents, expressing anger and using expletives, calling them ",domestic terrorists,".
  • A clip from Migrant Insider features a detained individual and shows the community responding by confronting ICE agents in Chicago.

  • Levi Rolls, a protester at the Broadview Detention Center, reports being shot with non-lethal rounds by ICE agents and describes fear among his community.
  • In Beaverton, Oregon, protesters attempted to protect migrants from ICE on a roof, expressing fear of being targeted.
  • Footage is presented of Portland protesters being confronted by individuals described as 'Trump's goons,' involving aggressive language.
