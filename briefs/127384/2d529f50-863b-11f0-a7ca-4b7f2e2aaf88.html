The MeidasTouch Podcast

Canada MP Destroys Trump with What He Fears Most

Key Takeaways

  • Canada is actively resisting perceived political and trade challenges from the U.S.
  • Former Canadian MP Charlie Angus launched Meidas Canada to counter political movements.
  • Allegations of U.S. interference in Canada, including funding for separatist groups, were discussed.
  • Concerns were raised regarding threats to international law and order, specifically concerning the ICC.

Deep Dive

  • Former Canadian Member of Parliament Charlie Angus announced the launch of Meidas Canada.
  • The initiative aims to counter political movements and build resistance against fascism.
  • The launch event, a town hall, will take place in North Rustico, Prince Edward Island, on Labor Day.
  • The guest urged listeners to subscribe to the Charlie Angus Meidas Canada channel, aiming for 100,000 subscribers by year-end.

  • Canada is responding to political and trade challenges from the U.S., shifting focus to European relations.
  • U.S. Ambassador Pete Hoekstra accused Canada of boycotting U.S. products and engaging in retaliatory tariffs.
  • 75% of Canadians are reportedly willing to boycott American products and want them removed from store shelves.
  • Trump's threats of tariffs against Canada were discussed following Canada's recognition of Palestine.

  • Allegations were made of U.S. interference in Canada, including espionage in Greenland and funding separatist groups in Alberta.
  • The guest reported allegations of $500 million in funding from the Trump administration for separatist groups, labeling it 'treason'.
  • Canadian mainstream media was criticized for not adequately covering these issues.
  • The guest attributed these actions to a 'gangster crew' involving Trump, Putin, and Orban.

  • The Meidas Touch Network discussed its international focus and the global threat of fascism.
  • The guest's international speaking engagements address the threat posed by the U.S. to the world order.
  • The guest criticized Donald Trump, describing him as a "thug" and a "malignant, self-centered, thin-skinned narcissist."
  • Canadians are asserted to possess backbone, pride, and determination, believing in strength through diversity, equity, and inclusion against such threats.
