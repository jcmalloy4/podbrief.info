Trump Loses It in Disaster Briefing With GOP Over Invasion

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump briefed on a Venezuela invasion, citing Maduro's dancing and a $100 billion oil extraction plan.
  • Trump suggested canceling elections, criticized Barack Obama, and revisited his dancing remarks.
  • Claims of widespread support for events in Venezuela were contrasted with reports of fear and paramilitary activity.
  • Trump questioned press credibility, citing negative coverage, and commented on soldiers in Washington D.C.
  • He made claims about reducing drug prices and his record in Boeing sales, also mentioning tariffs.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump held a briefing at the Kennedy Center on a planned Venezuela invasion.
  • He cited Venezuelan President Maduro's perceived imitation of his dancing as a humorous rationale.
  • Trump discussed meeting oil companies to promote 'drill, baby, drill' in Venezuela.
  • A potential $100 billion taxpayer subsidy was mentioned for this venture, as oil companies considered it unwise.

  • Trump suggested canceling elections to run against Democrats, avoiding the term 'dictator'.
  • He called Barack Obama the worst president, citing his policies.
  • Trump revisited his dancing rivalry with Maduro, noting his wife's 'unpresidential' comment.

  • The host discussed Donald Trump's claims of widespread support for an unspecified event in Venezuela.
  • These claims contrasted with reports of paramilitary groups and fear within the country.
  • Trump claimed people were marching at the border and 'love it' regarding Venezuela.
  • He disparaged the appearance and intelligence of protestors.

  • Trump asserted the press lacks credibility, citing high negative coverage percentages.
  • He presented his election victory as evidence against press credibility.
  • Trump commented on soldiers in D.C., stating they 'look good' and make him feel safe.

  • Trump claimed to reduce drug prices by a 'thousand percent,' which the host refuted as mathematically impossible and contrary to actual rising prices.
  • He referenced Senator Tim Scott and claimed to be 'the king' for selling 'more Boeings than any human being on earth.'
  • Trump stated he received a 'salesman of the year' award.
  • The conversation included Trump allegedly instructing an aide to check a 'tariff shelf' regarding Boeing issues.
