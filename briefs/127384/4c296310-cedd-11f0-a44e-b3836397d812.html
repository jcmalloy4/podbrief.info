Trump Loses It on Monday and Summons War Cabinet

Key Takeaways

  • Republican lawmakers discussed potential U.S. military action in Venezuela and advocated for regime change.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Donald Trump's business performance, including multiple bankruptcies and recent stock crashes.
  • An alleged 115-page FBI dossier detailed criticisms against FBI Director Kash Patel's leadership and conduct.
  • Attorney Alina Habba was disqualified from a U.S. Attorney position by the Third Circuit Court of Appeals.

Deep Dive

  • MAGA Republican Congressmember Scott Fitzgerald stated U.S. commitment to military action in Venezuela.
  • Members of Congress have reportedly been briefed on the situation regarding Venezuela.

  • Senator Markwayne Mullin stated readiness for lethal force against foreign and domestic enemies, citing ICE and Border Patrol actions.
  • Congressman Greg Murphy advocated for Venezuela regime change, drawing parallels to the 1990 Panama intervention.
  • Congressman Carlos Jimenez commented that a reported second strike on a fishing boat constitutes war crimes.

  • Critiques cited multiple bankruptcies of Donald Trump's ventures and endorsed products.
  • Recent stock crashes of Trump Media and Trump Memes were highlighted.
  • Doubts were reiterated regarding his negotiation abilities and business acumen.

  • An alleged 115-page FBI dossier against FBI Director Kash Patel was reported.
  • Current and former agents criticized Patel's leadership, inexperience, and perceived insecurity.
  • The report mentioned Dan Bongino, and some contributing agents were sympathetic to Donald Trump.

  • FBI Director Kash Patel allegedly refused to leave his plane in Utah until given an FBI raid jacket.
  • He reportedly threw a tantrum over a raid jacket lacking desired patches and required Velcro.
  • FBI agents described the situation as embarrassing, noting the jackets were too large for him.

  • The Third Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed a district court's order disqualifying Alina Habba.
  • Habba was disqualified as U.S. Attorney in New Jersey.
  • The ruling stated she unlawfully claimed the position after her interim term expired and her nomination failed.
