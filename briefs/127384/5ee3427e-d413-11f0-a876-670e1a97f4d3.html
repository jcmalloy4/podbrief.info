Entire World Goes to War as Trump makes Fool of Himself

Key Takeaways

  • Global conflicts are escalating across multiple regions, including the Thailand-Cambodia border and Congo-Rwanda.
  • The US national security strategy is reportedly shifting to undermine European unity and NATO expansion, alarming allies.
  • The war in Ukraine continues with intense Russian aerial attacks, while diplomatic solutions face obstacles.
  • Donald Trump's past and current foreign policy statements are criticized amid global tensions.

Deep Dive

  • Airstrikes occurred along the Thailand-Cambodia border following clashes, with both nations blaming each other.
  • The Royal Thai Army reported multiple clashes, deployed air assets, and confirmed one soldier killed.
  • Four villages were evacuated and schools and colleges were closed due to the escalation.
  • Donald Trump had previously referenced a “Kuala Lumpur peace accord” between Thailand and Cambodia.
  • Correspondent Saturday Sambar provided an update from Bangkok on the escalating situation.

  • The conflict between Congo and Rwanda escalated despite a purported peace deal involving Donald Trump.
  • The key M23 rebel group was reportedly not included in the talks, leading to continued warfare.
  • The situation in eastern DRC worsened after a peace accord signed by Congo and Rwanda's presidents.
  • The M23 rebel group and the Congolese army are blaming each other for renewed attacks.

  • A new US national security strategy aims to cultivate resistance to Europe's trajectory and end NATO's expansion.
  • Former US Army Europe commander Ben Hodges described this strategy as a “giant middle finger to Europe” benefiting Vladimir Putin.
  • Hodges warned that a full-scale war between Russia and Europe is likely if Ukraine fails, emphasizing the need for European support.
  • Dmitry Peskov expressed Russia's satisfaction with the US aligning with Russian positions on Europe and NATO.
  • Germany's parliament is reportedly increasing military spending as Europe prepares for potential conflict.

  • Ukrainian President Zelensky stated Ukraine deserves a peaceful resolution, contingent on Russia's actions and collective pressure.
  • Zelensky detailed Russia's weekly attacks, including over 1,600 attack drones, 1,200 guided aerial bombs, and 70 missiles.
  • These Russian attacks primarily targeted Ukrainian infrastructure and residential areas.
  • Donald Trump claimed Ukrainian President Zelensky had not read a peace proposal that Trump said Russia and Zelensky's people approved of.

  • Japan filed diplomatic complaints against China after Chinese fighter jets locked fire control radars onto Japanese F-15Js near Okinawa.
  • This incident followed reports that Donald Trump advised Japanese Prime Minister Kishida to appease China regarding Taiwan.
  • Secret meetings reportedly occurred involving Israel, Qatar, and Donald Trump in New York.
  • Trump referred to himself as a peacemaker, claiming to have settled eight wars and working on a ninth.
  • Drones, suspected Russian, were seen over French nuclear submarines and possibly linked to a Zelensky assassination attempt.
