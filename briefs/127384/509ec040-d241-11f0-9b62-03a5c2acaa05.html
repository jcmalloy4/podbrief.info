Furious World Leaders Blast Trump After Nuclear Subs Attacked!!!

Key Takeaways

  • Unidentified drones were detected near French nuclear submarines and Ukrainian President Zelensky's plane.
  • Leaked reports suggest European leaders deeply distrust the U.S., fearing betrayal and alleged 'agents of Putin'.
  • The U.S. National Security Strategy (November 2025) reportedly aims to 'destroy the European Union'.
  • European leaders privately warned Zelensky about potential U.S. pressure for territorial concessions without security guarantees.
  • International engagements, notably excluding the U.S., suggest a reordering of global alliances.

Deep Dive

  • Unidentified drones, possibly of Russian origin, were detected and engaged by the French military over nuclear submarines during President Macron's visit to China.
  • A potential assassination attempt on Ukrainian President Zelensky involved Russian drones near his plane's landing in Ireland.
  • The host criticized the lack of significant media attention and US support regarding these incidents, occurring from 2:22 to 3:11.

  • The United States is alleged to be betraying Europe by lifting sanctions on Russian oil and gas companies like Lukoil and lobbying against using frozen Russian assets for Ukraine (4:02).
  • A leaked transcript of a call revealed European leaders advised Ukrainian President Zelensky to be wary of US agents like Witkoff and Kushner, described as 'agents of Putin' (5:19).
  • The US National Security Strategy Report, published in November 2025, explicitly states a goal to 'destroy the European Union' by supporting 'patriotic European parties,' identified as far-right, nationalist, and pro-Putin groups (6:08).

  • A report detailed U.S. interests in Ukraine, including stabilizing European economies and preventing escalation (8:02).
  • The report also suggested a U.S. desire to reestablish strategic stability with Russia and reintegrate it into global affairs.

  • A Der Spiegel report alleged that European leaders, including Macron and German Chancellor Maerz, privately warned Ukrainian President Zelensky of potential U.S. pressure for territorial concessions without security guarantees (10:13).
  • Macron reportedly used the term 'betrayal' in discussions with Zelensky regarding U.S. policy.
  • European leaders expressed concerns about unofficial U.S. envoys pushing a Trump agenda, with NATO's Mark Rut emphasizing the need to protect Zelensky (11:12).
  • State visits between French President Macron and Chinese President Xi Jinping, and Indian Prime Minister Modi and Russian President Putin, occurred as the U.S. is perceived as alienating allies (12:38).

  • U.S. Senators J.D. Vance and Marco Rubio criticized the European Commission's fine against Elon Musk's X (formerly Twitter), viewing it as an attack on American tech platforms and free speech (13:26).
  • The host interpreted the U.S. stance as treating Europe like Russia, indicating a departure from traditional alliances (14:07).
  • Donald Trump allegedly lobbied against European Union sanctions on frozen Russian assets, a move seen as benefiting Putin and aiming to destroy Ukraine (15:00).
  • Ukrainian President Zelensky's team is seeking information about discussions between Trump's envoys and Russian officials to understand Putin's war prolongation tactics.
