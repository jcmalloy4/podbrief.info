Furious Zelenskyy Calls Trump’s Bluff Right Into His Face

Key Takeaways

  • President Zelenskyy presented a 20-point peace proposal to Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin.
  • Donald Trump asserted himself as the final arbiter for any Ukraine-Russia peace agreement.
  • Russia launched missile strikes across Ukraine following Zelenskyy's peace proposal.
  • Allegations surfaced regarding potential Putin-Trump proposals, including cryptocurrency mining at a nuclear plant.

Deep Dive

  • President Zelenskyy presented a 20-point peace proposal to Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin.
  • The plan includes a 60-day ceasefire and a referendum for eastern Ukraine residents to decide allegiance.
  • This initiative aims to expose any insincere peace intentions from Russia and its allies.

  • Russia launched missile strikes across Ukraine following Zelenskyy's peace proposal.
  • Donald Trump stated Zelenskyy needs his approval for any peace deal, positioning himself as the final arbiter.
  • Zelenskyy plans to present his 20-point peace framework, including a demilitarized zone and U.S. security guarantees, to Trump.

  • Donald Trump expressed lukewarm interest in Zelenskyy's proposal, stating nothing is approved until he says so.
  • This stance underscored Ukraine's dependence on Trump's approval, as Russia maintained its maximalist position.
  • The host characterized Zelenskyy's actions as outmaneuvering Trump and aligning with Europe.

  • Discussion centered on alleged Putin proposals to Trump, including jointly controlling the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant for cryptocurrency mining.
  • Russian officials, including Lavrov, consistently demanded full surrender of eastern Ukraine, viewing it as historical Russian territory.
  • The host suggested Trump's motivation was personal financial gain, despite Russia's economy being smaller than Italy's and Canada's.
