Trump Loses it as Protests Explode All Over

Key Takeaways

  • Large protests in Minnesota and a New York City nurses' strike highlight growing public unrest.
  • Allegations surface against ICE regarding child abductions, chemical weapon use, and protester databases.
  • Donald Trump is criticized for using AI-generated vanity images while protests expand.
  • DHS issued guidance advising against using "ice" in storm messaging to avoid negative public association.
  • Musician Rod Stewart publicly condemned Donald Trump's comments on NATO allies.

Deep Dive

  • Organizers estimate 50,000 to 100,000 participants in a Minnesota general strike, despite sub-zero temperatures.
  • Protests are expanding to other U.S. cities, including Minneapolis, which Fox News also acknowledged.
  • 15,000 nurses went on strike in New York City, receiving support from the city's mayor.

  • The Department of Homeland Security advised FEMA to avoid using "ice" in storm messaging.
  • The suggested alternative term is "freezing rain," aiming to prevent negative public association and memes.
  • Critics argue this strategy downplays the danger of destructive ice storms that threaten infrastructure and lives.

  • Reporter Jessica Tarlov presented Donald Trump's poor polling numbers on Fox News.
  • Trump shows significant deficits in public perception regarding foreign relations, cost of living, immigration, and the economy.
  • The 2020 voter coalition, including young, Black, and Latino voters, reportedly remains supportive of the Democratic Party.

  • Rod Stewart publicly criticized Donald Trump, labeling him a "draft dodger" and "coward."
  • Stewart specifically condemned Trump's remarks questioning the combat efforts of NATO allies in Afghanistan.
  • Stewart called for Trump to issue an apology to U.S. troops and their families for his comments.

  • The Trump administration is accused of covering up the Epstein files while signing the Trafficking Survivors Relief Act.
  • Critics characterize this as hypocrisy, alleging Trump protects child sex traffickers.
  • Claims suggest Trump deliberately delayed signing the Trafficking Survivors Relief Act.
