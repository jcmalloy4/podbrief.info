Mayor Barbara Lee Discusses Her Lawsuit against Trump for J6

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's poll numbers indicate significant unfavorability across various comparisons.
  • Trump continues to assert the 2020 election was rigged, linking it to foreign policy and Vladimir Putin.
  • He faces a civil lawsuit regarding his January 6th conduct, asserting claims of absolute immunity.
  • Mayor Barbara Lee is a lead plaintiff in a NAACP suit against Trump, emphasizing accountability and the rule of law.
  • Oakland's leadership focuses on local issues and community resilience, pushing back against federal political interference.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump's national favorability rating has dropped to negative 14%.
  • In New York State, his net unfavorable rating is 30 points, a 45-point difference from Mayor Eric Adams' +15 rating.
  • Trump's polling numbers are unfavorably compared to President Zelensky, Vladimir Putin, and New York City Mayor Eric Adams.

  • Donald Trump consistently asserts the 2020 election was rigged and stolen.
  • He linked a non-rigged 2020 election to preventing the war in Ukraine, claiming good relations with President Putin.
  • Trump stated Vladimir Putin informed him the 2020 election was rigged due to mail-in voting.
  • He focused on these election claims during a Politico interview, rather than healthcare or affordability.

  • Donald Trump faces lawsuits concerning his conduct on January 6th, seeking to block document production.
  • He claims absolute immunity for his January 6th actions, questioning why if he believes he did nothing wrong.
  • Mayor Barbara Lee is a lead plaintiff in a NAACP civil suit against Trump regarding his J6 conduct, a case filed five years ago.
  • Mayor Lee emphasizes the principle that no one is above the law in the context of attempts to thwart the peaceful transfer of power.

  • Mayor Barbara Lee discusses Oakland's resilience, including its refusal to cooperate with ICE.
  • The city seeks federal support for infrastructure and services while pushing back against federal militarization attempts.
  • Oakland focuses on improving public safety, reducing crime rates, managing affordable housing, and supporting economic development.
  • Mayor Lee expresses optimism for 2026, citing youth engagement and Oakland's cultural and economic vitality.
