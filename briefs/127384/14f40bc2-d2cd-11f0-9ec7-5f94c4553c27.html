Trump Gets Crushed on Saturday AM with Terrible News

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's social media activity and public remarks are criticized for inconsistency and unsubstantiated claims regarding his domestic policy agenda.
  • The FIFA Peace Prize awarded to Donald Trump, subsequently mocked by Governor Gavin Newsom, generated controversy regarding its legitimacy and FIFA's leadership.
  • The episode includes commentary on the U.S. economic situation, with claims about President Biden's role in the affordability crisis and an analysis of Treasury bond performance.
  • The 2025 U.S. National Security Strategy is discussed, outlining objectives related to NATO expansion and the potential dismantling of the European Union.
  • White House social media posts and recent public statements have drawn scrutiny, leading to discussions about Donald Trump's cognitive health and specific policy promises.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump's social media posts on his 'affordability crusade' and 'tiny truck solution' were dismissed as nonsensical by the host.
  • Trump was criticized for attributing economic issues to President Biden and for a perceived lack of clarity in his proposals.
  • Trump's predictions of rising prices were contrasted with data indicating gas prices at $1.99, among other price decreases.
  • The host also noted Trump's criticism of Fox and Friends for featuring stockbroker Peter Schiff, whom Trump labeled a 'Trump-hating loser'.

  • California Governor Gavin Newsom publicly mocked Donald Trump for receiving a FIFA Peace Prize, including a 'Kohl's cash' component.
  • FIFA leadership was characterized as corrupt, and the validity of a FIFA peace prize was questioned, particularly as Trump was the sole nominee and recipient.
  • The host criticized Donald Trump for allegedly creating and awarding himself a 'fake' peace prize.
  • Laura Ingraham later discussed Trump receiving the FIFA Peace Award, describing him as being honored for helping end conflicts.

  • The host claimed President Biden caused the affordability crisis but is actively working to fix it, citing border improvements.
  • Treasury bonds are noted to be experiencing their best year since 2020.
  • Current high yields for Treasury bonds were suggested to reflect a negative economic outlook, linked to inflation and increased borrowing costs.

  • The 2025 U.S. National Security Strategy outlines a policy to end the perception of NATO as a perpetually expanding alliance.
  • The strategy aims to cultivate resistance to Europe's trajectory within European nations.
  • A stated goal of the strategy is to dismantle the European Union.

  • The White House posted, then deleted, a video using Sabrina Carpenter's music to depict migrants being brutalized by ICE, after Carpenter denounced the video.
  • White House Instagram posts featuring Donald Trump and FIFA President Gianni Infantino had limited comments; another post depicted Trump as 'daddy,' drawing critical reactions and comparisons to his cognitive state.
  • Signs were reportedly placed in the White House to guide Trump due to concerns about his declining mental state, with the term 'Lekembi' mentioned in relation to cognitive health.
  • The speaker criticized Trump's promises, including ending the war in Ukraine in '24 hours,' imposing tariffs, and the 'Gestapo' treatment of immigrants.
