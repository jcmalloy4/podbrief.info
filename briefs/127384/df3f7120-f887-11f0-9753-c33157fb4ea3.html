Trump Loses It as General Strike Spreads

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump reacted to a nationwide general strike, reportedly the largest in 70 years, protesting fascism.
  • The host criticized Trump's social media posts, linking his climate change denials to severe weather.
  • Trump's "suckers and losers" comments on fallen allied soldiers caused international backlash and political risk.
  • A kidnapped child's case, Liam Canejo Ramos, involved criticism of Border Patrol and DHS statements.
  • Senator J.D. Vance drew criticism for labeling protesters as "sex traffickers" and "domestic terrorists."

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump reacted to a spreading general strike across the U.S., including Minnesota, described as the largest in approximately 70 years.
  • Protests occurred in Minneapolis and St. Paul despite freezing temperatures.
  • The strike aims to protest fascism and advocate for workers' rights, with solidarity expressed for striking nurses in New York City.

  • The host mentioned Donald Trump's prior "humiliating" trip to Davos and subsequent criticism of the United Kingdom.
  • The MeidasTouch network plans to launch "Midas UK" and "Midas Europe" in response to Trump's statements concerning NATO allies not adequately sacrificing for the U.S.
  • This criticism sparked significant uproar internationally.

  • Donald Trump repeatedly used the terms "suckers and losers" to describe fallen soldiers from allied nations, including the UK, Denmark, and Canada.
  • The host noted that leaders who do not challenge Trump on such statements risk losing elections.
  • Discussions touched on shifts in Danish politics and Trump's alignment with authoritarian leaders like Putin, Lukashenko, and Orban.
  • Claims about a deal with NATO on Greenland were refuted by the Greenlandic premier, who emphasized that only Greenland and Denmark hold decision-making power.

  • The podcast addressed the case of Liam Canejo Ramos, a kidnapped child, and his father, who were allegedly sent to "concentration camps."
  • Greg Bavino of Border Patrol and DHS spokesperson Tricia McLaughlin were criticized for their statements regarding the child's well-being and the father's alleged abandonment.
  • Law enforcement officers later took custody of a child whose mother refused to care for him, providing care and food.
  • During a press conference, officials faced questions about the father's criminal record and the child's legal status, stating more information would be provided.

  • Senator J.D. Vance faced criticism for labeling protesters as "sex traffickers" and "domestic terrorists."
  • The host compared Vance's rhetoric to authoritarian language.
  • A clip showcased protesters enduring extreme cold, with one affirming their commitment and resilience despite negative temperatures.
