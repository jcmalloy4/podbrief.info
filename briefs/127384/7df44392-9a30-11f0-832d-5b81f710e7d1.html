Trump Loses It in Disaster Meeting with Turkey Prez

Key Takeaways

  • During a press conference with Turkish President Erdogan, Donald Trump called American elections rigged and likened himself to Erdogan.
  • Trump claimed tariffs generated significant revenue, which he stated would be used to support farmers temporarily impacted by the tariffs.
  • Turkish President Erdogan publicly criticized Donald Trump's claims of quickly resolving the Ukraine and Gaza wars.
  • Donald Trump blamed the "radical left" for a shooting at an ICE detention facility and was allegedly involved in efforts to prosecute James Comey.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump, during a press conference with Turkish President Erdogan, described American elections as rigged and compared himself to Erdogan, whom he characterized as a tough, authoritarian leader.
  • Trump praised Erdogan as a tough, opinionated leader, despite stating he typically dislikes such individuals, adding that Erdogan performs an "amazing job" in Turkey.
  • When asked about Erdogan's potential role in mediating the Ukraine war, Trump called Erdogan a "very neutral man," similar to himself, and claimed to have resolved "seven wars."

  • Donald Trump blamed the "radical left" for a shooting at an ICE detention facility, asserting they are dangerous and will escalate violence.
  • The host criticized Trump's blame of the "radical left" for the ICE facility shooting, noting that bullets engraved with anti-ICE messages were reportedly found.
  • The discussion covered Donald Trump's alleged involvement in an effort to prosecute former FBI Director James Comey as the statute of limitations was expiring, with Trump describing Comey as a "bad person" and a "sick guy."

  • Turkish President Erdogan criticized Donald Trump's claims that he could quickly resolve the Ukraine and Gaza wars.
  • Erdogan's skepticism regarding Trump's conflict resolution assertions was highlighted during a press conference following their meeting.

  • Donald Trump claimed tariffs are generating significant revenue for the US, stating he found "$31 billion on a tariff shelf" and that some funds would support farmers impacted by tariffs.
  • The host contrasted Trump's claim of finding $31 billion with the struggles of American farmers and citizens, likening the tariff revenue distribution to a Ponzi scheme.
  • Trump discussed his trade tariffs' revenue, the support for farmers, and expressed disappointment with Putin's military gains in Ukraine, noting his tendency to align with whoever he last spoke with.

  • Donald Trump criticized Democrats for allegedly wanting to support transgender individuals and provide healthcare to undocumented immigrants, while claiming to have secured the border.
  • Trump asserted that Democrats seek to open borders and provide money to "illegal immigrants."
  • The host played a clip of Donald Trump discussing President Erdogan's alleged role in regime change in Syria, questioning Trump's attribution of credit and suggesting Erdogan might not appreciate such comments.
