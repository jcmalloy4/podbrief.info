MeidasTouch Full Podcast - 9/12/2025

Key Takeaways

  • Political violence and inflammatory rhetoric are criticized as pervasive, with blame often assigned prematurely.
  • Economic indicators show persistent inflation, rising jobless claims, and large federal deficits contradicting claims.
  • Concerns are raised regarding Donald Trump's health and the alleged politicization of federal agencies.
  • Foreign policy critiques highlight Trump's perceived weak responses to international incidents.
  • The Epstein files controversy continues, with detailed accusations against Trump and criticism of media coverage.
  • Immigration enforcement tactics by ICE are described as racially discriminatory and creating community fear.

Deep Dive

  • Charlie Kirk was shot at Utah Valley University, leading to immediate blame on liberals by Donald Trump and MAGA Republicans.
  • Hosts criticized premature accusations without knowing the shooter's identity or motive.
  • Additional violence noted includes multiple death threats at HBCUs and a school shooting in Colorado.

  • Donald Trump and Republican figures, including Nancy Mace, were criticized for prematurely blaming 'radical leftists' for the Kirk incident.
  • This rhetoric is deemed dangerous, potentially inciting further violence, and has been linked to ongoing swatting incidents.
  • The assassination of former Minnesota Speaker Melissa Hortman by a Trump supporter was cited, where MAGA Republicans initially blamed Democrats.

  • A concerted messaging push from senators, Congress members, and Donald Trump has been linked to violence across the country.
  • Recent incidents include a Colorado school shooting and an active shooter at the U.S. Naval Academy in Annapolis resulting in at least 3 casualties.
  • The hosts noted 85% of Americans support common-sense gun reform, but MAGA Republicans oppose these measures while cutting mental health funding.

  • Cash Patel and the FBI's handling of two suspects was labeled a 'colossal failure' as they were not the primary individuals sought.
  • Concerns were raised about appointing influencers over experienced professionals, exemplified by the firing of a former head of a force.
  • A lawsuit by top FBI officials warned against prioritizing social media engagement and criticized a 22-year-old former Trump intern leading a domestic terrorist task force.

  • Donald Trump's response to Russia's drone invasion of Poland, which led Poland to invoke Article 4 of NATO, was characterized as 'weak and inadequate.'
  • Trump commented that the drone incident 'could have been a mistake,' drawing criticism from Poland's foreign minister who stated it demonstrated weakness.
  • The hosts suggested Trump's perceived alignment with Russian President Vladimir Putin negatively impacts Republican Party leadership.

  • Masked ICE agents were accused of racially profiling and detaining Latinos, drawing comparisons to concentration camps.
  • The Supreme Court's decision was criticized, along with sports teams like the New York Yankees for appearing to endorse these actions.
  • Concerns were raised about ICE agents patrolling neighborhoods, schools, and churches, impacting daily lives and causing fear.
  • ICE operations were highlighted for focusing on workers and vendors rather than criminals, causing distress to long-term residents in 'Operation Midway Blitz' in Chicago.

  • Headline inflation was noted at 2.9% year-over-year and core inflation at 3.1%, indicating an increase and a reversal of a downward trend.
  • Kamala Harris, citing Nobel laureates and the Wharton School, predicted Donald Trump's economic plan would increase inflation and lead to a recession.
  • Trump's claims of a $17 trillion surplus were contrasted with a projected $2 trillion deficit for 2025, with tariffs identified as taxes on American consumers leading to increased bankruptcies, including a 50% tariff on goods from countries like India and Brazil.

  • The podcast discussed Donald Trump's alleged cover-up of Epstein files and Republican efforts to block the release of documents.
  • Congressmembers questioned the investigation's integrity and the authenticity of a signature on a birthday card, suggesting a signature expert examine the document.
  • The hosts argued Trump cannot lie his way out of the Epstein files controversy due to public focus on details from Epstein's Island.

  • Mainstream news outlets, particularly CNN and CNBC, were criticized for allegedly downplaying evidence connecting Donald Trump to the Epstein case.
  • Hosts cited Trump being found liable for sexual assault, audio of him bragging about assaulting women, and allegations of inspecting underage girls at beauty pageants he owned.
  • Mar-a-Lago's alleged use for sex trafficking and Trump's contradictory statements about Jeffrey Epstein were also mentioned as evidence.
