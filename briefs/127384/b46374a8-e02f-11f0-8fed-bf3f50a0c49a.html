Trump Loses it as 30,000 New Epstein Files are Very Bad

Key Takeaways

  • New information from Epstein files, data set eight, mentions Donald Trump.
  • A 2019 DOJ list named 10 redacted Epstein co-conspirators, contradicting prior testimony.
  • A grand jury subpoena was sent to Mar-a-Lago in October 2021 for the Ghislaine Maxwell investigation.
  • Flight records and witness accounts connect Donald Trump to Epstein's private jet and concerning alleged events.

Deep Dive

  • New information from the Epstein files, specifically data set eight, has been released.
  • Donald Trump's name is mentioned within these newly public documents.
  • The Department of Justice is releasing these documents despite previous claims that their content was 'unfounded and false'.
  • A document in data set eight, possibly a letter from Jeffrey Epstein to Larry Nasser, references shared interests in young women.

  • A 2019 Department of Justice list identified 10 Epstein co-conspirators, whose names are now redacted in recently released documents.
  • This contradicts previous FBI testimony and DOJ memos regarding uncharged third parties in the Epstein case.
  • Internal communications show investigators tracked these 10 individuals, with some located in Florida, New York, Boston, and Connecticut.
  • Investigators vetted approximately 45 calls and interviewed 25 victims related to Epstein's bail arguments in July and August 2019.

  • A grand jury subpoena was issued to Mar-a-Lago in October 2021.
  • The subpoena compelled a representative to appear in court as part of the Ghislaine Maxwell investigation.
  • Attempts were made to contact potential co-conspirators in the Epstein case, including efforts to reach Maxwell and Lex Wexner.
  • Discussions took place regarding the locations of these individuals, specifically in New York.

  • Cash Patel's sworn testimony, stating no credible information existed about Epstein trafficking young women to others, is contrasted with newly released records.
  • Investigators found a photo of Donald Trump and Ghislaine Maxwell on Steve Bannon's phone in June 2021 during the 'We Build the Wall' investigation.
  • An email dated January 8, 2020, from a federal prosecutor, indicates Donald Trump traveled on Epstein's private jet multiple times between 1993 and 1996.
  • Ghislaine Maxwell was reportedly present on some of these flights with Donald Trump.

  • A redacted FBI message from 2020 details a limo driver's account of a concerning 1995 car ride with Donald Trump.
  • During this 1995 ride, Trump reportedly mentioned Jeffrey Epstein and potentially abusing a girl.
  • Another redacted FBI message, dated October 9, 2020, concerns a party at Mar-a-Lago allegedly invited by Trump.
  • A victim claimed Ghislaine Maxwell told her the Mar-a-Lago party was for prostitutes, though Maxwell later denied this.
