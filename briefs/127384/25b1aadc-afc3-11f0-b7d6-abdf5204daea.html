Trump Caught on Camera as Plan Spins Out of Control

Key Takeaways

  • Allegations of widespread ICE abuses are detailed across multiple U.S. cities.
  • Eyewitness accounts describe military-style immigration sweeps and aggressive tactics by ICE agents.
  • The host alleges that Trump is utilizing ICE to terrorize citizens and provoke conflict.
  • Criticism was raised against corporations and leaders for alleged complicity in human rights violations.

Deep Dive

  • The host alleges Donald Trump is using ICE as a personal military force to terrorize Americans in various cities and towns.
  • This assertion is supported by photos and videos presented during the podcast.

  • Footage from a Canal Street, New York City, ICE raid was shown, with the host stating these actions aim to provoke anger and create pretexts for military intervention.
  • An ICE raid at a Home Depot in the San Fernando Valley reportedly resulted in at least three detentions, with bystanders expressing anger.
  • A video also showed a citizen confronting ICE agents, questioning their actions.

  • A video from Canal Street depicts 'military-style immigration sweeps,' where ICE agents allegedly used full body weight on a man for over 2 minutes.
  • The man reportedly stated 'I can't breathe' in Spanish during the incident.

  • A video from Portland, Oregon, reportedly showed ICE agents pinning a man to the ground while his wife pleaded for help; the man was said to be non-resisting.
  • Another video depicted agents pointing a gun at a pregnant U.S. citizen and her son; an agent reportedly stated, 'It's your fault I did that.'
  • In Illinois, ICE agents allegedly held a state legislator and a staffer at gunpoint without warning.

  • ICE agents detained blind protester Quinn Haberoll in Montrose, allegedly dropping him on the pavement, causing him to hit his head.
  • Witnesses described Haberoll as typically joyful and dancing during protests, making his rough handling particularly shocking.
  • ICE claimed Haberoll obstructed law enforcement and made threats, though video reportedly shows agents dragging him.
