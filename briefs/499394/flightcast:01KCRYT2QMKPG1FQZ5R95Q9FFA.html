The Diary Of A CEO with Steven Bartlett

Episode

Key Takeaways

  • Modern lifestyles contribute to a decline in daily walking habits.
  • Increasing daily steps, even incrementally, significantly reduces mortality risks.
  • Walking offers substantial mental health benefits and combats isolation.
  • Strategic footwear choices are crucial for foot health and injury prevention.
  • Regular movement, including "micro-walks," enhances physical and cognitive well-being.

Deep Dive

  • Walking has declined due to ride-sharing services and sedentary office work.
  • Global average step counts range from 4,500 to 4,900 steps daily.
  • Increasing daily steps by 500 can reduce cardiovascular mortality risk by 7%.
  • A 1,000-step increase can lower all-cause mortality risk by 15%.

  • A 27-year-old patient with chronic heel pain experienced depression and isolation from activity restriction.
  • A strategy involving "micro walks" (5-10 minutes, approximately 500-1,000 steps) was implemented to gradually increase his step count.
  • Research indicates 5,000 daily steps are linked to reduced symptoms of depression.
  • Achieving 7,500 daily steps is associated with a lower prevalence of depression diagnosis.
  • The patient reported walking 5,000-6,000 steps daily and experiencing more good days one month prior to the conversation.

  • Common footwear choices, such as high heels, can weaken foot tissues due to unnatural foot positioning.
  • Functional footwear features a wide toe box, allowing toes to splay naturally.
  • These shoes are "zero drop," meaning the heel and toe are on the same plane.
  • They are characterized by thin, flexible soles to strengthen the foot's tissues and bones.
  • Transitioning to minimalist or functional footwear requires a gradual approach to prevent injuries like plantar fasciopathy.

  • High-performance running shoes, like the Nike Alpha Fly, are characterized by toe spring and a rocker sole.
  • These shoes can improve running economy by 2-4% and facilitate gait.
  • However, prolonged training in them can weaken intrinsic foot muscles and potentially lead to injuries.
  • The guest advocates for using a spectrum of footwear, distinguishing between training shoes that build strength and race-day performance shoes.

  • Short, frequent bouts of activity, termed "movement snacks" or "micro-walks," are crucial breaks from sedentary work.
  • Incorporating micro-walks, even just five minutes a few times daily, helps maintain bodily systems and increases activity.
  • Research indicates that taking 9,800 daily steps can reduce the risk of dementia.
  • Notably, 3,800 daily steps can achieve 50% of the maximum dementia risk reduction benefits.
  • 'Relationship walking,' particularly among the elderly, enhances social connection and combats loneliness.
