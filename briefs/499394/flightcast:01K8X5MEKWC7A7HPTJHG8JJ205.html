The Diary Of A CEO with Steven Bartlett

Episode

Key Takeaways

  • Guest Brené Brown discussed how fear prompts individuals to adopt 'armor'.
  • Identifying this protective 'armor' is presented as a learned skill.

Deep Dive

  • Brené Brown, a frequently requested guest, was welcomed to the podcast.
  • The conversation delves into bravery, fear, and mechanisms of self-protection.
  • Brown highlights that 'armor' used in fear distances individuals from love, connection, and core values.
  • The guest clarifies that recognizing this 'armor' is not automatic but a learned skill.
