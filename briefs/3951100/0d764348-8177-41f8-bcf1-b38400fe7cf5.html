Breaking Points with Krystal and Saagar

10/28/25: Kamala 2028 Run, Trump's 2026 Election Plot, NBA Gambling Scandal

Key Takeaways

  • Kamala Harris's 2028 presidential prospects face skepticism due to past electoral performance and perceived lack of voter connection.
  • The Democratic Party's platform has shifted from economic issues to cultural and identity politics, potentially alienating working-class voters.
  • A 'maximalist strategy' is detailed for Republicans to secure the House in 2026, involving election mechanics control and gerrymandering.
  • An NBA gambling scandal involves organized crime, rigged poker games, and concerns over prop bets and league integrity.
  • Concerns are raised about traditional sports journalism's independence due to league business relationships and corporate advertising.

Deep Dive

  • Krystal Ball criticized Kamala Harris's past primary and general election failures, noting a perceived lack of authenticity and connection with the Democratic base.
  • A clip played where Harris discussed her past results and confirmed, 'I am not done' politically, stating 'Possibly' to a woman leading the White House.
  • Figures like California Governor Gavin Newsom and Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez reportedly poll higher than Harris among Democrats.
  • One host suggested Harris's advisors have a financial incentive to encourage a presidential run despite skepticism about her electability.

  • Between 2012 and 2014, Democratic platform language saw a significant decrease in terms like 'jobs' and 'economic'.
  • Concurrently, terms related to identity politics such as 'equity,' 'reproductive,' 'LGBT,' 'guns,' and racial demographics saw dramatic increases.
  • This shift is characterized as a cultural takeover over economic messaging, potentially alienating working-class voters.

  • Discussed unpopular Democratic policy positions include 'abolish the police,' providing free healthcare to undocumented immigrants, and lowering the voting age to 16.
  • These specific cultural issues are cited as potentially alienating voters and contributing to conservative electoral success.
  • Donald Trump's strategy of pushing a forceful, consistent narrative is contrasted with the Democratic Party's approach of analyzing polls and seeking centrist positions.

  • Attention shifted to the upcoming New Jersey gubernatorial election, where a narrow one-point Republican lead was noted.
  • A Republican victory in New Jersey, a traditionally Democratic state, is highlighted as a potential significant development.
  • This election reflects a broader discussion on the effectiveness of cultural mentions versus clear policy platforms for political movements.

  • An analysis detailed a 'sophisticated plot' by the Trump administration to influence the 2026 midterm elections.
  • Key elements include gutting election integrity departments, replacing staff with 'Stop the Steal' proponents like Heather Honey and Kurt Olson, and consolidating control over election mechanics.
  • Executive orders involve stringent ID requirements, recertification of voting machines, and granting access to voter rolls for 'fraud audits,' with the Justice Department demanding voter rolls from 30 states.
  • A mass gerrymandering push is underway, with 3 states redrawing maps to favor Republicans and 5 more considering it; J.D. Vance and Trump are reportedly pressuring Indiana lawmakers.

  • A memo detailed the establishment of a National Guard response force, trained in crowd control and civil unrest.
  • This force is slated to be deployed in all 50 states by April 2026.
  • The authenticity of the memo was questioned, with a response confirming multiple layers of National Guard response forces can be employed when necessary.

  • Guest Pablo Torre detailed allegations of rigged private poker games, mentioning former athletes like Kevin Garnett were present.
  • Rigging methods allegedly included x-ray machines, marked cards with special sunglasses, and rigged shuffling machines.
  • These games are linked to 'Operation Royal Flush' and 'Operation Nothing But Bet,' with overlaps to the Jontay Porter scandal and alleged mob associations.
  • The NBA's incentive structure, established by Commissioner Adam Silver, encouraged legalized gambling and prop bets, contributing to current issues.

  • Calls for NBA Commissioner Adam Silver to appear before Congress have arisen after the Department of Justice contradicted the NBA's internal investigation clearing player Terry Rozier of wrongdoing.
  • Concerns highlight a conflict of interest due to legalized sports betting partnerships.
  • The discussion explains how prop bets, allowing wagers on specific player outcomes, facilitate scandals more easily than traditional game-fixing.

  • The podcast discussed a major NBA gambling and salary cap scandal involving Steve Ballmer, Aspiration, and Kawhi Leonard.
  • The issue highlights how ultra-wealthy owners may attempt to circumvent league rules for acquiring star players.
  • This circumvention could impact sports ownership and team valuations, with potential IRS and league implications.
