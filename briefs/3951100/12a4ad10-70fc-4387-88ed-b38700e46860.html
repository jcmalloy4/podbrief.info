Breaking Points with Krystal and Saagar

BP EXCLUSIVE: Zohran On ICE, Eric Adams, Bibi Arrest

Key Takeaways

  • Zohran Mamdani stresses listening to New Yorkers' concerns, especially cost of living.
  • Mamdani highlights anti-Muslim rhetoric from both Democratic and Republican parties in NYC.
  • His "Freeze the Rent" policy aims to protect over 2 million rent-stabilized tenants.
  • Mamdani advocates for holding ICE accountable for unlawful actions in New York City.
  • He proposes universal child care, and free, fast buses as key campaign priorities.

Deep Dive

  • Mamdani discussed his experience as a Muslim in New York City, stating he received support from Democratic leaders.
  • He noted many Muslim New Yorkers face daily indignities, citing anti-Muslim rhetoric from both Republican and Democratic parties.
  • Specific instances involving Andrew Cuomo and Eric Adams were cited regarding the presence of anti-Muslim rhetoric.

  • The guest questioned if the NYPD would arrest federal agents who break the law in New York City.
  • He stated anyone breaking the law, including ICE, must be held accountable for recklessness and intimidation tactics.
  • A recent raid on Canal Street was cited as an example of ICE's actions, linking it to the mayor's stance on civil immigration enforcement.

  • With six days remaining before the election, Mamdani outlined key proposals: freezing rent, universal child care, and making buses fast and free.
  • He urged the national audience to support his campaign by volunteering for canvassing or phone banking via Zohranfornyc.com/gotv.
  • He highlighted the significant impact of volunteer efforts in past campaigns.
