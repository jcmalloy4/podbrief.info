Breaking Points with Krystal and Saagar

1/21/26: Zohran Calls For ICE Abolition, IDF Buckles To Ms Rachel, India Joins Fight Against Saudis

Key Takeaways

  • New York City Mayor Eric Adams advocated for ICE abolition and addressed anti-Semitism concerns during a TV appearance.
  • Ms. Rachel's intervention influenced the IDF's plans for a West Bank soccer field, drawing international attention.
  • Donald Trump proposed a $1 billion Gaza Peace Board, reportedly with UN blessing and Vladimir Putin invited.
  • Texas congressional candidate Zeeshan Hafiz challenged incumbents, criticizing pro-Israel funding and emphasizing progressive domestic policies.
  • Saudi Arabia shifted its gold purchases from Sudan away from the UAE, intensifying regional geopolitical rivalries.
  • The Sudan conflict involves UAE backing the RSF, while Saudi Arabia and Pakistan support the Sudanese army.
  • Media coverage disparities exist between the Gaza and Sudan conflicts, despite comparable death tolls in Darfur.

Deep Dive

  • New York City Mayor Eric Adams appeared on "The View" to discuss his administration's actions and policies.
  • Mayor Adams advocated for the abolition of ICE, stating the agency terrorizes people regardless of immigration status.
  • He addressed concerns from Jewish New Yorkers regarding anti-Semitism policies, reaffirming his commitment to combating anti-Semitism.
  • Adams explained his decision to revoke previous executive orders while asserting commitments to increase funding for hate crime prevention and security.

  • New York's budget negotiations involve Governor Kathy Hochul's budget proposal.
  • The proposed budget notably excludes tax increases on the wealthy.
  • This exclusion stands despite the governor's expressed support for universal childcare initiatives.
  • The situation indicates a potential conflict in Albany concerning funding priorities.

  • Ms. Rachel, along with Swiss MPs, advocated for saving a soccer field in the West Bank claimed by the IDF as illegally constructed, citing children's right to play.
  • The broader fight against the field's demolition is linked to UEFA's potential suspension of Israel from European soccer leagues due to teams hosting games in the West Bank.
  • Israel is reportedly preparing land in Rafah, bulldozing and compacting areas, previously intact with greenhouses and homes, to corral Palestinians.

  • Donald Trump proposed a Gaza Peace Board, reportedly inviting Vladimir Putin to join for a $1 billion cost.
  • Tony Blair is identified as a key figure on the board, described as Larry Ellison's proxy.
  • The UN has reportedly blessed Trump's Peace Board, which requires a $1 billion contribution and exclusivity to Gaza, potentially replacing UN functions.
  • In a related development, the Israeli government reportedly demolished the UNRWA headquarters in Jerusalem, condemned by the UN Secretary-General.

  • Zeeshan Hafiz is running for Congress in Texas's 33rd district, aiming to unseat two incumbent Democrats.
  • Hafiz criticized opponents for receiving $700,000 from pro-Israel groups, stating that a "genocide continues" in Gaza.
  • She contends the issue of Israeli military actions in Gaza energizes voters, including younger demographics and people of color.
  • Hafiz advocates for progressive policies such as abolishing ICE, a living wage, universal healthcare, and free public education from kindergarten through university.

  • Lindsey Graham warned against the Syrian government's use of force against Kurds and the SDF, threatening to revive "bone-crushing" Caesar Act sanctions.
  • Congressional candidate Zeeshan Hafiz critiqued Donald Trump's foreign policy and establishment Democrats and Republicans.
  • Hafiz argued that establishment parties prioritize foreign conflicts and corporate interests over American citizens' needs.
  • She advocates for addressing domestic issues like affordable housing and veteran support, emphasizing fighting the 'war on homelessness.'

  • Saudi Arabia has agreed to purchase gold from Sudan, shifting away from the UAE, which previously bought nearly all of Sudan's gold.
  • The UAE's gold purchases from Sudan often occurred through unofficial channels.
  • This deal takes place amid surging global gold prices and a geopolitical rift between Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

  • India is forming strategic ties with the UAE, driven by a search for energy and financial services.
  • The partnership is also influenced by a shared animosity towards Muslims, especially groups like the Muslim Brotherhood, and opposition to the Arab Spring.
  • This alliance occurs within a complex geopolitical landscape in the Red Sea region, which is crucial for global trade.
  • The UAE has established significant influence in Yemen and Libya, further complicating regional dynamics.

  • The International Criminal Court (ICC) is focusing on Israel and potential charges against the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) in Sudan.
  • Intense fighting continues in Darfur, with reports of systematic torture, rape, and executions, exacerbated by a near-collapse of the health system in South Kordofan.
  • Media attention on the Gaza conflict is contrasted with lesser coverage of the Sudanese conflict, despite comparable or higher death tolls in areas like El Fasher.
  • The UAE's significant role in the Sudanese conflict is highlighted as a point of leverage for the United States, given the UAE's status as a key ally and its economic ties.
