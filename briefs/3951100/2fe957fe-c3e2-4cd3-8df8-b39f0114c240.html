Breaking Points with Krystal and Saagar

11/24/25: MTG Resigns, Zohran Trump Meeting, Zohran Endorses Hakeem Jeffries, Ukraine Peace Deal

Key Takeaways

  • Marjorie Taylor Greene announced her resignation from Congress, effective January 5, 2026.
  • Greene's departure narrows the House Republican majority and solidifies Donald Trump's control.
  • Zohran Mamdani met with Donald Trump to discuss the New York affordability crisis.
  • Mamdani endorsed Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker, potentially swaying a DSA electoral working group vote.
  • A potential Ukraine peace deal includes limiting Ukraine's military and non-membership in NATO.
  • The proposed Ukraine peace deal requires territorial concessions and is viewed as unjust.
  • Economic issues and voter anger are impacting Republican midterm election prospects.

Deep Dive

  • Marjorie Taylor Greene announced her resignation from Congress, effective January 5th, 2026, citing a desire to avoid a contentious primary and frustration with the political system.
  • Potential motivations for her departure included a presidential run, though Greene publicly denied a Time magazine report as fabrication.
  • Her resignation narrows the Republican majority in the House and is seen by hosts as a loss for the Republican Party due to her influence and ability to sway close votes.

  • Donald Trump publicly stated his disagreement with Marjorie Taylor Greene's support for Thomas Massie, claiming she resigned due to an inability to survive a primary challenge.
  • Trump's social media post initially referred to Greene as 'Marjorie Trader Brown' due to her perceived 'rotting' political stance.
  • Alex Jones expressed being 'inches away' from no longer supporting Trump, citing concerns about Trump's perceived failure to deliver on campaign promises, potentially impacting the MAGA contingent.

  • Donald Trump met with Zohran Mamdani, who discussed taking responsibility for broken politics and prioritizing working people.
  • Trump appeared unfazed by past criticisms from Mamdani and seemingly found common ground, potentially signaling a shift in Republican strategy.
  • Saagar initially believed a confrontation would benefit Zohran politically but now views the meeting positively, suggesting Zohran is prioritizing policy goals like affordable housing.

  • Saagar described Trump's demeanor during the meeting as 'obsequious,' highlighting Trump's statements about shared ideas and potential to 'make New York great again.'
  • Zohran was characterized as cordial yet principled, managing to secure a meeting and partnership opportunity without compromising his stance, which reportedly impressed many observers.
  • On Meet the Press, Zohran explained his meeting with Trump was focused on finding common ground on New York's affordability crisis, despite his continued labeling of Trump as a fascist.

  • White House Deputy Chief of Staff James Blair tweeted confirmation of Zohran and Trump's shared commitment to an affordability agenda, indicating its widespread appeal.
  • Republicans, including those involved with Marjorie Taylor Greene, are working to address voter anger and mitigate backlash for upcoming midterm elections due to significant economic issues.
  • Trump is considered strategically astute for understanding his brand and the appeal of figures like Zohran who challenge the establishment, aligning with an affordability agenda.

  • Representative Zohran Mamdani endorsed Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker of the House, despite Jeffries being a primary challenger to a DSA-endorsed candidate.
  • This intervention by Zohran reportedly swayed the DSA electoral working group's vote against endorsing the challenger.
  • One speaker questioned the effectiveness of Zohran's pragmatic approach with establishment Democrats like Jeffries, who are perceived to respond more to demonstrable power than endorsements.

  • A critical analysis questioned the Democratic Socialists of America's (DSA) stance on endorsing Hakeem Jeffries, arguing against supporting his primary opponent and questioning their alignment with core principles.
  • Speakers proposed a more muscular approach to political action, advocating for backing numerous insurgent candidates rather than solely focusing on electoral calculations.
  • A pragmatic, opportunistic approach, as seen with the Tea Party wave and MAGA adoption, is suggested to achieve political victories by forcing politicians to align with popular stances.

  • Discussion began on a potential peace plan for Ukraine following a meeting in Geneva with the U.S. delegation, with a Ukrainian negotiator expressing optimism for a 'just and lasting peace.'
  • Key proposed terms include limiting the Ukrainian armed forces to 600,000 personnel, a move described as a Russian request and a significant sticking point.
  • Another major point is Ukraine agreeing not to join NATO, with NATO statutes to reflect this, identified as the primary reason for Russia's invasion.

  • One speaker stated that no weapon systems, short of nuclear, could help Ukraine achieve its military objectives against Russia.
  • The proposed peace deal is described as disgusting and unjust, requiring Ukraine to cede territory including Crimea, Luhansk, and Donetsk, and limiting its military size.
  • While the deal is considered hideous, negotiations might lead to slightly more favorable terms; the current situation presents a choice between this unjust deal and continued war, which could lead to an even worse outcome.
