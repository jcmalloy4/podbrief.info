11/19/25: Trump Meets MBS, MTG Calls Trump Traitor, Epstein Pursued Rothschild Bank, Obama Official Laments Holocaust Education

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump defended Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and granted Saudi Arabia major non-NATO ally status.
  • Marjorie Taylor Greene criticized Trump over delays in releasing Epstein files and for labeling her a "traitor."
  • Jeffrey Epstein reportedly sought to fund an Israeli cyber weapons empire through Rothschild Bank.
  • An Obama official connected rising anti-Semitism to social media content showing "carnage in Gaza" and Holocaust education.
  • Concerns were raised regarding potential suppression of incriminating information within the Jeffrey Epstein files.

Deep Dive

  • The podcast previewed Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's White House visit with Donald Trump, alongside new Jeffrey Epstein reporting.
  • The U.S. designated Saudi Arabia as a major non-NATO ally, a status also extended to Qatar, drawing little immediate reaction from the right.
  • Critics framed Saudi Arabia's non-NATO ally designation as a humiliation, alleging the US president defended MBS regarding Jamal Khashoggi's murder.
  • Donald Trump claimed he 'saved MBS's ass' by intervening with Congress on the Khashoggi assassination, stating MBS knew nothing about the event.
  • MBS acknowledged Khashoggi's death as a "painful mistake" and referenced Saudi investigations, while praising Trump's work on human rights.
