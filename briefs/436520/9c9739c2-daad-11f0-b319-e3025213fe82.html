  • Parathyroid glands control blood calcium levels, which are critical for nerve function, maintaining calcium within a narrow range of 9.5-10 in the US.
  • Primary hyperparathyroidism, often caused by a tumor in one or more of the four glands, leads to elevated blood calcium, osteoporosis, kidney stones, and heart disease.
  • Common symptoms include chronic fatigue and high blood pressure, which is four times more common in affected individuals.
  • Treatment involves surgically removing the tumor, a procedure that has shortened to about 20 minutes and results in a cure.
  • Diagnosis requires measuring parathyroid hormone, calcium, and vitamin D levels together, as a persistent high calcium level (above 10.0 for adults over 40) typically indicates a problem.
