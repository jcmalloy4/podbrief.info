The Glenn Beck Program

Glenn Makes a BIG Announcement About His Future | Guest: Brandon Tseng | 10/27/25

Key Takeaways

  • The ongoing government shutdown is impacting SNAP benefits for approximately 42 million Americans.
  • Glenn Beck launched 'The Torch' app and a new foundation focused on American history and verified information.
  • Shield AI is developing AI pilots for advanced military fighter jets and unmanned systems.
  • AI is projected to have a quadrillion-dollar economic impact and fundamentally change future warfare.
  • The program discusses political polarization, societal unrest, and calls for non-revolutionary change through community action.

Deep Dive

  • The federal government entered a shutdown on October 1st due to Congress's failure to pass appropriations bills.
  • This shutdown endangers SNAP benefits for approximately 42 million Americans monthly, potentially depleting state emergency funds.
  • Democrats are criticized for allegedly weaponizing hunger by not agreeing to funding bills, threatening to leave millions without benefits.

  • Listeners are urged to support food banks and assist the hungry through kindness and service, rather than through politics.
  • Political inaction on the government shutdown could devastate the economy and lead to civil unrest, potentially affecting 40 million SNAP beneficiaries.
  • The host emphasized that principles of love and responsibility are more important than political considerations in times of crisis.

  • A perceived global effort by anarchists, communists, socialists, and Islamists seeks to destabilize the West, with allegations of Democrat politicians aiding this.
  • Democrats are criticized for refusing compromise during the shutdown, arguing they are alienating vulnerable populations and potentially inciting chaos.
  • The DNC chair's comments on 'resistance' are interpreted as promoting unrest, suggesting elections may lose relevance.
  • Chuck Schumer and Gavin Newsom are alleged to be fueling public rage, drawing parallels to historical calls for harsh treatment of political opponents.

  • The host advocates for self-sufficiency, individual charity, and mutual support as solutions, rather than causing chaos by dismantling government programs.
  • Change should occur through being 'peacemakers,' acting with love and charity, and adopting a constitutionally based stance.
  • Values are demonstrated through small, quiet decisions, such as choosing a phone service provider.
  • Patriot Mobile is presented as a Christian conservative wireless provider supporting religious liberty.

  • The host recounted founding The Blaze after leaving Fox News, foreseeing potential for independent, internet-based media networks.
  • The Blaze was established as an independent media network pioneering streaming across radio, television, and online platforms.
  • The host announced his mission with The Blaze is complete, signaling his transition to a new venture.

  • "The Torch," a new app launching January 5th, will feature a digitized library of over 30 years of Glenn Beck's work, including books and research.
  • This platform aims to restore curiosity and provide unbiased historical information, with new history shows and investigations available on demand.
  • The Glenn and Tanya Beck Foundation for American History will launch in January as a privately funded trust to preserve and educate on American history.
  • The foundation's collection houses over a million documents and items, proving America's founding on Judeo-Christian values.

  • Listeners expressed excitement for "The Torch" project, with suggestions for online exhibits and VR integration.
  • The host confirmed online exhibits are coming and announced a physical museum early next year.
  • Concerns were raised about the potential for AI to rewrite history in such projects, acknowledging ethical considerations and high costs.
  • The host confirmed he would continue radio and podcasts, with this new project dramatically changing his career.

  • George AI, integrated with The Torch app, will analyze legislation, identify unconstitutional content, and provide constitutional reasoning based on founding documents.
  • The AI's capability extends to predicting Supreme Court rulings based on the Constitution and founding documents, providing rationale.
  • The initiative plans to make historical documents, including thousands of 17th and 18th-century sermons, accessible to the public through The Torch app.
  • The app will translate archaic language into modern English and is expected to generate educational podcasts, with a major update planned by July 4th, 2025.

  • Brandon Tseng, co-founder and president of Shield AI, is developing advanced technology for future warfare, focusing on AI pilots and next-generation aircraft.
  • Shield AI, founded 10 years prior, has approximately 1,400 employees and is valued at $5.5 billion.
  • Their X-BAT is a new vertical takeoff and landing fighter jet, F-16 sized with an F-100 class engine, designed to operate collaboratively in swarms.
  • First flight tests for the X-BAT are planned for the following fall, with operational deployment anticipated in 2028 with U.S. and allied militaries.

  • Future conflicts are predicted to be rapid, AI-driven engagements with fewer human participants, accelerating warfare.
  • Taiwan is seriously approaching defense against potential swarm drone attacks by increasing its military budget and capabilities.
  • While China leads in industrial production of drones, the U.S. is considered ahead in AI and autonomy capabilities, despite China's rapid iteration.

  • Shield AI's HiveMind framework is an AI and autonomy system for unmanned vehicles, including aircraft, surface vessels, and ground vehicles.
  • The system integrates perception, cognition, and action modules, processing various data feeds in real-time.
  • Humans remain in the loop for critical decisions, such as lethal force, aligning with Shield AI, U.S., and NATO policy.
  • AI-driven military technology is becoming significantly cheaper compared to traditional systems.

  • AI and autonomy could enable new forms of deterrence, with legacy systems like aircraft carriers becoming less respected due to advanced anti-ship missiles.
  • The guest suggests AI could allow the U.S. to field millions of drones managed by small groups, leading to a dominant military that deters conflict.
  • AI's economic impact is optimistically projected to generate $4.5 quadrillion between 2025 and 2050, significantly more than the internet's impact.
