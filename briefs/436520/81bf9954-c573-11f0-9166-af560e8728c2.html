The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | 11/19/25

Key Takeaways

  • Texas Governor Greg Abbott designated the Muslim Brotherhood and CAIR as terrorist organizations, citing historical connections to anti-Semitism.
  • Journalist Olivia Nuzzi's career from an Anthony Weiner intern to a prominent Washington correspondent, including a professional scandal, was detailed.
  • President Trump is reportedly authorizing CIA covert actions in Venezuela amid a video from Democrats urging military members to defy illegal orders.
  • The host and Stu discussed the Democrats' video as a potential 'color revolution' tactic to undermine presidential credibility and divide the nation.

Deep Dive

  • The discussion centered on Haj Amin al-Husani, the Grand Mufti of Jerusalem, and his alleged collaboration with Adolf Hitler and promotion of anti-Semitism.
  • This historical context was presented as foundational for understanding current groups like the Muslim Brotherhood and CAIR.
  • Texas Governor Greg Abbott designated the Muslim Brotherhood and the Council on American-Islamic Relations as terrorist organizations.
  • The host asserted the Grand Mufti of Jerusalem systematized hatred and linked fascism to Islamism, forming ideological origins for groups like Hamas and Hezbollah.

  • Journalist Olivia Nuzzi began her career interning for Anthony Weiner's failed mayoral campaign, later reporting on it for the Daily News.
  • Her rapid ascent included covering presidential campaigns for the Daily Beast while still in college and becoming Washington correspondent for New York Magazine at age 24.
  • By age 25, Nuzzi had interviewed President Trump in the Oval Office, received an award, and was featured in a documentary and TV show.
  • Nuzzi's affair with RFK Jr. while profiling him and being engaged to another journalist led to her dismissal from New York magazine.
  • A subsequent book by Nuzzi about her experiences was favorably profiled by the New York Times, a move questioned by the speakers.

  • President Trump is reportedly poised to authorize CIA plans for covert action in Venezuela, a report noted as not new information by the speakers.
  • A group of Democrats, including Senators Alyssa Slotkin and Mark Kelly, released a video urging military and intelligence personnel to refuse illegal orders.
  • The host questioned the timing and specificity of the Democrats' video, suggesting it is a calculated move to sow doubt and position the president as radical.
  • Stu and the host interpreted the video as a 'color revolution' tactic aimed at building a case against the president for illegal actions and creating national division.
