The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | 1/15/26

Key Takeaways

  • Glenn Beck announced a 5-day deadline for a "fundamental transformation" in his approach to Attorney General Pam Bondi.
  • The episode explored perceptions of a lack of accountability for public figures and discussed systemic issues.
  • Oprah Winfrey's remarks on obesity and GLP1 medications sparked a debate on the causes of weight gain.
  • The host detailed global geopolitical pressures, potential war, and an alleged "Great Reset" plan by elites.

Deep Dive

  • The host announced a 5-day deadline for a "fundamental transformation" in his treatment of Attorney General Pam Bondi, citing listener poll support over 56%.
  • A listener expressed discouragement regarding perceived lack of accountability for figures like Fauci.
  • The host validated frustration over lack of legal action for alleged wrongdoings at the Department of Justice.

  • The host framed current global events as a prelude to war, drawing parallels to 1939 and FDR's 'arsenal of democracy' strategy.
  • He suggested the president is actively securing resources and allies for potential conflict, citing travel and resource acquisition.
  • The host expressed belief that war is likely due to geopolitical pressures and economic instability, having developed since 2008.
  • Discussion included "insider" comments predicting civil war or a French-style revolution if figures like the Clintons were arrested.

  • A listener urged the host to "unleash now" regarding Attorney General Pam Bondi, but the host stated he needed five more days to prepare his case for January 20th.
  • The host criticized the Clintons, alleging they are architects of current problems, funded by organizations like the Tides Foundation and George Soros.
  • He implicated them in scandals including Uranium One and money laundering with China.

  • The host connected NGOs, giant corporations, the World Economic Forum, and the Clinton Foundation, labeling them 'arsonists' enabled by Congress to fund America's destruction.
  • He described a "great reset" plan by the World Economic Forum, suggesting a deliberate societal collapse orchestrated by elites to establish a new system.
  • The host alleged a massive taxpayer money heist in Minnesota involving the state government, banking system, and the airport, with Somalis being unfairly blamed.

  • The host expressed skepticism about uncovering truth regarding the Clintons' involvement with Jeffrey Epstein due to past evidence destruction by political parties.
  • He speculated on their deep involvement in financial dealings similar to Epstein's, including offshore money movement and shell corporations, citing the Clinton Initiative's Haiti fund as an example.
  • The host asserted that both Bill and Hillary Clinton possess expertise in financial manipulation and illicit activities, suggesting a high likelihood of their involvement in Epstein's operations.
  • He argued against arresting them for Epstein due to insufficient evidence for a conviction, fearing it would appear political rather than just.

  • The conversation confirmed an upcoming "fundamental transformation" in the host's treatment of Attorney General Pam Bondi, scheduled for January 20th, a year into Donald Trump's administration.
  • The host reiterated his view that Congress should not arrest the Clintons for contempt of Congress over the Epstein case, framing it as a potential trap due to a lack of solid evidence.

  • A clip from "The View" featured Oprah Winfrey discussing obesity, stating it causes overeating and "food noise," which GLP1 medications help quiet.
  • The host questioned whether an "obesity gene" has been identified and expressed doubt that overeating does not lead to obesity.
  • The podcast introduced a segment categorizing individuals by "fat" based on shirt size and perceived privilege, with participants humorously assigning themselves categories like "small fat."

  • The exiled Crown Prince of Iran delivered a message outlining a future free Iran's foreign policy, including ending the nuclear program, ceasing support for terrorism, recognizing Israel, and normalizing relations with the US and Arab nations.
  • The host criticized the left's opposition to the Iranian prince's message and protests against ICE, emphasizing the need to support the Iranian people.
  • He then criticized media coverage, specifically the New York Times, for an inaccurate headline regarding an incident involving an ICE agent, framing it as an attack on an officer by an immigrant.
