The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | Guest: Brandon Tseng | 10/27/25

Key Takeaways

  • The ongoing government shutdown is severely impacting SNAP benefits for 42 million Americans.
  • The shutdown's impact on food aid is attributed to political maneuvering by Chuck Schumer.
  • Glenn Beck announced his transition from The Blaze to a new venture focused on education.
  • Beck's new initiative, "The Torch," includes an AI-driven historical library named "George."
  • SHIELD AI is developing advanced AI-piloted aircraft like the X-BAT for modern warfare.
  • Future conflicts are predicted to be hyper-accelerated due to AI and autonomous drones.

Deep Dive

  • The government shutdown, which began October 1st, is attributed to the Democratic Party's refusal to pass a continuing resolution.
  • It impacts SNAP benefits for approximately 42 million Americans, with the USDA warning funds may be insufficient for November.
  • Virginia's governor has already declared a state of emergency due to potential strain on food banks and state resources.

  • The host criticized Chuck Schumer, attributing the shutdown's impact on 40 million Americans' SNAP benefits to efforts to avoid a primary challenge, describing it as "weaponizing hunger."
  • Listeners were urged to contact Democratic representatives and support local food banks to address potential food insecurity.
  • The host predicted increased civil unrest, theft, and violence if the situation is not addressed through kindness and service.

  • The host announced a major career transition, reflecting on the media landscape's shift since he founded The Blaze.
  • He recalled Roger Ailes dismissing the internet's potential, while he pioneered live, subscription-based networks for independent thinkers like Buck Sexton.
  • He stated his mission with The Blaze is complete, having built an ecosystem for new voices.

  • The host announced a new educational and non-governmental rescue venture, launching the Glenn and Tanya Beck Foundation for American History in January.
  • The foundation will use an AI-driven historical library named "George" to preserve and teach American history with over 1 million verified documents.
  • This resource, described as a final career step, will be accessible via a blockchain-secured digital vault and a new app called "The Torch."

  • Brandon Tseng, co-founder of SHIELD AI, discussed the company's development of AI systems and pilots for next-generation aircraft, valued at $5.5 billion with 1,400 employees.
  • SHIELD AI's X-BAT, an AI-piloted vertical takeoff and landing fighter jet using an F-100 class engine, is designed for collaborative flight with up to four other aircraft.
  • The company received an order for 150 AI pilots, indicating scaling technology for uncrewed aircraft.

  • Brandon Tseng, a former Navy SEAL, described future warfare as an accelerated version of the current Ukraine conflict, heavily influenced by AI and autonomous drones.
  • He stated that future conflicts will be "hyper-accelerated," necessitating dominant technology for deterrence or victory.
  • The discussion highlighted the increasing challenges for warfighters due to relentless drone and missile threats, and the strategic concern of overwhelming drone swarms.
