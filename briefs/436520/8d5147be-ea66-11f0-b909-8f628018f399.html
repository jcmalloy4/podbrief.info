The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | 1/5/26

Key Takeaways

  • US actions in Venezuela reportedly aimed beyond regime change, offering an honest vote and addressing systemic issues.
  • A sophisticated US military operation successfully disabled advanced Chinese and Russian defense systems in Venezuela in two hours.
  • Donald Trump's foreign policy doctrine prioritized strategic resources like rare earth minerals for future AI development.
  • The Glenn Beck Program is launching 'Torch', a platform offering exclusive content, fewer commercials, and AI-generated analysis.
  • The Venezuela operation was a limited military action focused on capturing a key target rather than a full regime change.

Deep Dive

  • The host introduced discussion on multifaceted reasons for US actions in Venezuela, beyond solely regime change, to include offering citizens an honest vote.
  • Past US presidents have taken similar actions, suggesting a systemic issue rather a Trump-specific problem.
  • The initial discussion also noted the launch of 'Torch', a new platform for exclusive content.

  • Venezuela's military technology includes Chinese and Russian systems, which were targeted in a sophisticated operation.
  • The operation involved cyber warfare capabilities, demonstrated by U.S. aircraft flying into Venezuela, disabling detection and power systems.
  • Months of planning by CIA operatives culminated in a two-hour operation that disabled advanced Russian and Chinese S-300 missile systems.
  • The operation targeted Maduro's location at Venezuela's 'Pentagon,' with 34 Cubans reportedly killed.

  • Donald Trump's foreign policy doctrine began with a focus on strategic resources like rare earth minerals in Panama Canal and Greenland.
  • This 'America-first' strategy aimed to secure resources for future AI development and establish strategic alliances.

  • The operation in Venezuela was clarified as a limited military action to capture a priority target, not a full regime change.
  • This approach aimed to avoid escalating conflict or losing American lives, focusing on removing a key leader while leaving the existing regime in place.
  • The host used the analogy of capturing Hitler in World War II to illustrate the strategic choice.
  • World leaders reportedly took note of the U.S. military's capability to execute such a complex mission without casualties.

  • Potential reasons for the Venezuela operation included oil, drugs, terror, hemispheric control, and military proof of concept.
  • The involvement of SmartMatic and voting machines was speculated as a factor, raising the possibility of Maduro testifying.
  • The host suggested Trump's actions served multiple purposes, including legal strategy and international influence.
  • Maduro reportedly lost a past election by 35 points but retained power, suggesting a precedent for challenging leadership.

  • The concept of soft power was discussed, suggesting Trump's perceived 'locked and loaded' stance created significant international leverage.
  • This strategy was considered applicable to Venezuela, Iran, Mexico, and Cuba, even without direct military action.

  • 'Glenn AI' is being developed to synthesize 30 years of host content for in-depth analysis on topics like the communist movement and Venezuela.
  • 'George AI' is designed to answer what the founders would say about specific actions, such as the US intervention in Venezuela, drawing parallels to historical events like the Barbary Pirates war.
  • Exclusive content, including the 'American Story' series, is available on Glenn Beck's Torch platform, free for the first month, then $10 per month.

  • The host introduced a narrative about a "confidence collapse" rather than a financial crash, describing a neighbor's desperate search for medicine during societal breakdown.
  • The scenario depicted riots, looting, and violence over basic necessities, highlighting failures in supply chains and law enforcement.
  • The program is expanding to three hours, with an additional hour on Glenbeck.com, to cover topics like capital controls in a special Wednesday night series.
  • The subscription for Glenbeck.com offers the first month for $10 with an inflation-proof lifetime price lock.
