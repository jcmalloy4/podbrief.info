The Glenn Beck Program

Are Russia & Ukraine FINALLY Nearing a Peace Deal? | 11/25/25

Key Takeaways

  • Charges against James Comey and Letitia James dismissed due to an unlawfully appointed prosecutor.
  • Senator Mark Kelly faces a Pentagon investigation over a video urging military to defy Trump's orders.
  • Obamacare's subsidy expansions from 2021-2025 and lack of reform were heavily criticized.
  • Reports suggest a potential Ukraine-Russia peace deal includes significant territorial and military concessions for Ukraine.
  • The hosts expressed strong opposition to biological men competing in women's sports due to physical advantages.
  • Persecution of Nigerian Christians by the Fulani tribe, including mass killings, was highlighted.

Deep Dive

  • U.S. District Judge Cameron McGowan Currie dismissed charges against former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James.
  • The dismissal was based on the argument that Lindsey Halligan, the prosecutor, was unlawfully serving as interim U.S. attorney.
  • The court ruled that layering temporary roles without Senate advice and consent is improper, setting a precedent.
  • The White House is reportedly angered by the decision, with an appeal expected.

  • Sean Duffy introduced a 'civility campaign' to encourage considerate behavior during the upcoming holiday travel season.
  • One host recommended dressing more respectfully when traveling, specifically advising against pajamas and slippers at the airport.
  • The campaign aims to foster better behavior in crowded airports and during potentially frustrating travel experiences.

  • The Pentagon is investigating Senator Mark Kelly (D-Ariz.) for potentially breaching military law.
  • This investigation stems from Kelly's participation in a video urging troops to defy "illegal" orders from President Trump.
  • As a retired reservist, Kelly is subject to military law, raising constitutional questions regarding separation of powers.
  • The hosts questioned the legal standing of the case, suggesting the intent might be to uncover further evidence or motivations.

  • The hosts discussed Senator Mark Kelly's congressional actions beyond his astronaut background and his wife's shooting incident.
  • One host criticized Kelly for allegedly exploiting his wife's tragedy for political gain, a claim disputed by another host.
  • They clarified that the perpetrator of his wife's shooting was insane and the incident was not politically motivated.
  • The discussion suggests Kelly brought up political violence in relation to his wife's shooting.

  • The hosts discussed past opposition to Obamacare, with one humorously recalling a satirical desire for unnecessary medical procedures.
  • They criticized how the COVID-19 pandemic was used to expand subsidies, which were extended by the Biden administration from 2021 to 2025.
  • This led to increased government spending and a debate over income limits for eligibility.
  • Concerns were raised that proposed changes to Obamacare aim to limit damage rather than fundamentally solve problems, citing little political will for reform.

  • Reports indicate Ukraine and the U.S. have agreed on the essence of a peace deal with Russia, possibly based on a 28-point plan.
  • Proposed terms include Russia gaining 20% of Ukrainian territory and rejoining the G7.
  • Ukraine would reportedly limit its army and agree not to join NATO, seen as significant concessions.
  • The hosts expressed surprise at these potential terms.

  • Further discussion questioned the validity of Ukraine agreeing to territorial concessions and military limitations, likening it to a surrender.
  • A Ukrainian official reportedly stated the U.S. appears to have agreed to documents set out by European allies, not a U.S. plan.
  • The hosts debated whether Russia is winning, with some analysts suggesting a stalemate while others believe Russia is closer to victory.
  • Initial reports suggest Russia may not accept the proposed terms, despite similarities to a plan previously floated by Donald Trump.

  • The hosts discussed the idea that biological men cannot compete fairly against women in sports, describing the WNBA as 'mediocre basketball'.
  • They used examples from basketball and baseball to illustrate physical differences in strength and speed between male and female athletes.
  • Historical events like the Billie Jean King vs. Bobby Riggs match and modern examples of women's national soccer teams losing to younger male teams were referenced.
  • The discussion broadened to societal implications of denying biological differences, hindering rational conversation on gender identity and women's sports.
