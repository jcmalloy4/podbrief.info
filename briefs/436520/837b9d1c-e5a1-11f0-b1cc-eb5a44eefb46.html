Justin Barclay filling in for Glenn- Trump Administration Announces Investigation into Fraud in Minnesota

Key Takeaways

  • Federal agencies are investigating an alleged multi-billion dollar fraud scheme in Minnesota with suspected links to foreign terror organizations.
  • Mainstream media outlets are criticized for downplaying or politicizing the significance of the ongoing fraud investigations.
  • Guest Mike Rogers discussed the impact of upcoming elections on national policy, economics, and foreign interventions in Venezuela.
  • Delano Squires suggested national problems are spiritual rather than purely political, advocating for a return to God and foundational family values.
  • Lance Wallnau noted a significant increase in Bible reading and religious engagement among younger generations, signaling a potential "Great Awakening."
  • Widespread fraud beyond Minnesota is alleged across various government programs, including housing and small business loans.

Deep Dive

  • A federal investigation is underway in Minnesota into a significant fraud scheme, involving ICE and Homeland Security, with authorities reportedly aware of the fraud for years.
  • Homeland Security Investigations conducted door-to-door inquiries at suspected fraud sites in Minneapolis, investigating alleged involvement of Somali immigrants.
  • Elon Musk, Donald Trump, and citizen journalist Nick Shirley are credited with bringing attention to the investigation, contrasting with perceived mainstream media inaction.
  • Allegations suggest the fraud benefits foreign terror organizations and implicates individuals like Ilhan Omar and Tim Wallace.

  • The host criticized media outlets like MSNBC for allegedly downplaying fraud allegations involving Somali immigrants in Minnesota.
  • Media coverage is accused of labeling the issue as racist and omitting details of a multi-billion dollar scam.
  • The host emphasized that fraud investigations should occur regardless of political affiliation.

  • US Senate candidate Mike Rogers discussed the upcoming Michigan election's significance for Senate control, linking it to broader corruption including the Minnesota fraud case.
  • Rogers highlighted economic differences, stating 401k plans increased by an average of $26,000 under Trump's first term but decreased by an average of $24,800 under the Biden administration.
  • He warned that losing the House could lead to impeachment proceedings and hinder efforts on border security, combating fraud, and reducing crime.

  • A military operation reportedly destroyed boats and drug implementation infrastructure in Venezuela, referred to as the 'first strike on land' against narco-terrorists.
  • Former Congressman Mike Rogers explained that Venezuela's President Maduro allegedly heads a narco-terrorist group that funnels drugs into the US.
  • Recent strikes reportedly targeted facilities used for drug movement.

  • Delano Squires, a research fellow, questioned whether the nation's problems are political or rooted in deeper issues, suggesting only God can save souls.
  • Squires argued that the pervasive influence of politics in all aspects of American life signifies national decline, not health.
  • He noted increasing numbers of millennials and Gen Z are reportedly turning to spiritual answers and attending church.

  • Delano Squires identified the breakdown of the family as the biggest social issue in the U.S., linking it to national revival and suggesting 'making America great again' starts at home.
  • He cited a significant increase in non-marital birth rates, with approximately 40% of American children born to unmarried mothers.
  • Squires noted that a quarter of children live in single-parent households, attributing this breakdown to divorce, the redefinition of marriage, surrogacy, and abortion.

  • HUD Secretary Turner reportedly identified $5 billion in potential fraudulent payments, including to illegal immigrants and deceased individuals.
  • The host linked rampant rental assistance fraud in cities like New York City and D.C. to a Biden administration program.
  • New mandates for public housing authorities aim to account for residents and ensure taxpayer-funded mortgages go only to American citizens.
  • The host questioned whether government programs are designed to involve fraud, or at least shuffle money without fixing problems.

  • The Small Business Administration is halting all federal funding in Minnesota after identifying $430 million in fraudulent PPP loans.
  • Concerns about fraud in the childcare sector are long-standing, with surveillance videos from 2015 allegedly showing questionable practices and kickbacks.
  • One center, Quality Learning Center, disputes state claims of closure and accuses the state agency of providing false information, raising questions about oversight.

  • Guest Lance Wallnau discussed a perceived 'Great Awakening,' citing increased Bible reading among younger generations (Millennials and Gen Z).
  • He noted a rise in the importance placed on Jesus Christ among Americans.
  • Wallnau suggested the current surge in religious interest might be the early stages of a third such historical movement.

  • Data suggests increased Bible reading and a surprising surge in religious engagement among Gen Z, with one ministry reportedly receiving 10 million digital inquiries last year from young people seeking hope and purpose.
  • Dr. Lance Wallnau noted the significant role of young people in past political movements and their current search for answers.
  • He highlighted both a return to Christ and concerning trends like anti-Semitism within this demographic.

  • Dr. Lance Wallnau described the current period as a political revolution, linking it to Donald Trump's actions against institutional decay in academia, government, media, and big business.
  • Wallnau emphasized the importance of voter turnout in upcoming midterms, stating that a 'bloodbath' is predicted if conservatives lack focus.
  • He urged grassroots efforts, stating that change will not come from traditional news.

  • Dr. Wallnau asserted that the current division within the conservative movement appears to be a deliberate plan, potentially from external forces.
  • He expressed frustration with the lack of unified strategy among conservatives for the midterms, likening the situation to internal conflict and a 'circular firing squad.'
  • Wallnau referenced his book 'The Return of the Lion,' which explores Israel's role in end-times prophecy and Jesus' return as a judging ruler.
