The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | Guest: Michael Iskander | 11/14/25

Key Takeaways

  • Glenn Beck analyzed Dietrich Bonhoeffer's moral conflict regarding pacifism versus confronting extreme evil.
  • The hosts expressed significant concerns about Apple's new digital ID system and potential for government overreach.
  • Actor Michael Iskander discussed portraying David in Amazon's 'House of David,' emphasizing leadership and flawed heroism.
  • The episode cautioned against the erosion of privacy through digital technologies and the challenges of resistance.

Deep Dive

  • Glenn Beck addressed Tucker Carlson's statements regarding Dietrich Bonhoeffer, asserting Bonhoeffer's heroic status.
  • Beck began recounting historical events in Germany during the rise of the Third Reich and changes within Christianity.
  • The discussion explored Bonhoeffer's internal conflict as a pacifist grappling with the morality of resisting Hitler and overwhelming evil.

  • Dietrich Bonhoeffer was imprisoned by the Nazis, surviving for a period due to his family's prominence.
  • During his imprisonment, he wrote extensively, including a notable homily on marriage, and preached to fellow prisoners.
  • Bonhoeffer and other prisoners were systematically hanged in the final weeks before Hitler's death, known for his composed demeanor.

  • The discussion on digital IDs began with concerns about potential government control and an 'all-seeing, all-knowing state.'
  • Hosts linked digital ID initiatives to methods of 'backdooring' them into the population, referencing the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • Apple's new digital ID feature for domestic air travel allows storing government IDs in Apple Wallet for TSA checkpoints at over 250 U.S. airports.

  • Hosts discussed the potential dangers of a universal digital ID system, citing privacy, tracking, and government overreach.
  • Parallels were drawn to historical data collection and control, referencing IBM's role in the Holocaust.
  • Concerns were raised that a digital ID system could centralize control over citizens' finances and activities, furthering authoritarianism.

  • Speakers highlighted that convenience often leads to the acceptance of technologies that erode privacy, comparing it to the Patriot Act.
  • They emphasized the difficulty of resisting widespread digital identification, especially when global actors like China do not adhere to similar restrictions.
  • The discussion underscored the challenges citizens face in pushing back against increasingly centralized digital control.

  • Host Glenn Beck praised Amazon's 'House of David' series, comparing its quality favorably to 'The Chosen.'
  • Michael Iskander, who portrays David, expressed gratitude for the show's positive reception.
  • Iskander discussed Goliath's depiction as a true giant and his personal training, including learning to use a slingshot in three days.

  • Michael Iskander recounted fasting and praying before auditioning for David, a long-held dream inspired by 'The Chosen.'
  • He noted the series resonates because David, a flawed but beloved figure and ancestor of Jesus, embodies human imperfection and forgiveness.
  • Iskander emphasized the importance of showing biblical heroes' failings and the possibility of repentance and grace in storytelling.
