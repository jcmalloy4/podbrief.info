The Glenn Beck Program

Justin Barclay filling in for Glenn- Trump Dollar Coin? | 12/31/25

Key Takeaways

  • Widespread fraud allegations in Minnesota's childcare programs are leading to federal action and anticipated arrests.
  • Michigan's voter rolls are under scrutiny for significant discrepancies, raising election integrity concerns.
  • The 'Trump Economy' is credited with positive economic indicators and projected tax cut benefits for 2026.
  • Plans for US coinage include a 'Trump dollar coin' and commemorative coins for the nation's 250th anniversary.
  • Minnesota Governor Tim Walz faces pressure to resign amid ongoing fraud controversies and a state budget deficit.
  • Adventurer Kevin DeVries is launching a new project exploring spiritual awakening through extreme experiences.

Deep Dive

  • Karoline Leavitt discussed potential fraud arrests in Minnesota, criticizing Governor Walz.
  • The Trump administration has actively addressed fraud since day one, with the Department of Justice executing search warrants.
  • Homeland Security is also involved, and arrests are anticipated in the new year.

  • Federal government introduced new requirements for payment justification and demanded a statewide review from Governor Walz regarding alleged fraud in childcare funding.
  • The current system is described as facilitating the exploitation of federal funds, with arrests predicted.

  • The host critiqued a Politico reporter's tweet suggesting violence against investigative reporters under 'stand your ground' laws.
  • The report was argued to be legally inaccurate and to have mischaracterized small businesses as home daycares.
  • The reporter's comments targeted those investigating Somali daycare centers in Minnesota.

  • An investigation into Michigan's voter rolls found 500,000 more registered voters than voting-age adults before the 2024 election.
  • This number increased by 750,000 between July and October, raising questions about Secretary of State Jocelyn Benson's methods.
  • Tactics used are suggested to be part of a broader Democratic Party strategy.

  • Economic indicators including declining inflation, a 4.3% GDP, and 6.18% 30-year fixed mortgage rates are attributed to the 'Trump Economy'.
  • U.S. Treasurer Brandon Beach discussed 2026 tax cuts from a 'big beautiful bill' that will bring lower interest rates, decreasing gas prices, and a tourism boost from the 2026 World Cup.
  • Tax bill benefits include immediate equipment expensing, permanent factory expensing, no tax on tips/overtime/Social Security, and American-made auto loan deductibility.

  • Discussions cover reducing nickel production costs and creating commemorative coins for the nation's 250th anniversary.
  • A 'Trump dollar coin' featuring President Trump is in development, expected for production within 10 weeks as legal tender.
  • A proposed $250 bill, sponsored by Congressman Joe Wilson, is also under consideration.

  • A criminal complaint was filed against Governor Tim Walz for allegedly violating statute 3.971 and allowing fraud for voting blocks.
  • Minnesota House Speaker Lisa DeMuth states widespread fraud, including $250 million from the 'Feeding Our Future' program and an estimated $9 billion total, has occurred for seven years under Walz's administration.
  • Funds were diverted from vulnerable populations.

  • Minnesota gubernatorial candidate Lisa DeMuth criticized Governor Tim Walz's leadership on COVID-19 restrictions and ongoing fraud.
  • DeMuth called for Walz to resign or withdraw from his re-election bid, citing a failing education system and a state budget shift from surplus to deficit.

  • Alleged fraud involving over 40 daycare centers in Columbus, Ohio, linked to a Somali organization, received $14 million.
  • This type of fraud is noted as becoming more widespread, potentially tied to Democrats.
  • A caller from Ohio later predicted Medicaid fraud in their state would be double Minnesota's, citing a 43.3% improper payment rate on a $40 billion budget in 2022, amounting to $17.7 billion.

  • Adventurer Kevin DeVries, known for Mount Everest climbs and Noah's Ark search efforts, is developing a new project called 'The Journey Within'.
  • This docuseries uses adventure narratives to explore spiritual awakening and healing from trauma.
  • DeVries seeks financial and distribution partners, with a trailer available on YouTube.

  • The host invited listener predictions for 2026, coinciding with the U.S.'s 250th anniversary, and discussed the potential impact of a Trump presidency on national celebrations.
  • A caller expressed concern about Democrats flipping the House in midterms, while another predicted Republicans would hold or narrowly expand their Senate majority.

  • John Justice suggests Governor Tim Walz may withdraw from his re-election bid due to political pressure and fraud revelations.
  • No Minnesota governor has won a consecutive third term since the four-year term law change.
  • Investigations into fraud are urged to expand beyond daycare and autism services to include housing stabilization services and transportation.
