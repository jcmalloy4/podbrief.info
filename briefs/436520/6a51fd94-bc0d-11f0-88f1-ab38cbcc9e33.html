The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | Guest: Noah Oppenheim | 11/7/25

Key Takeaways

  • The host discussed the need for a third American "awakening" to address national challenges.
  • Presidents receive minimal nuclear decision-making training, often just one briefing, with sole authority in crises.
  • Varied claims exist regarding the success rates of ground-based missile interceptors, from 61% to 100%.
  • A government shutdown is projected to impact up to 40% of airline flights, stemming from a dispute over Obamacare subsidies.
  • Elon Musk's potential $1 trillion Tesla compensation package is tied to an $8 trillion company valuation target.
  • The host advocated for cultivating personal virtue and pursuing excellence to counter societal cynicism.

Deep Dive

  • The host discussed a difference between a 'revival' and an 'awakening', referencing two historical American awakenings.
  • These past awakenings were reportedly tied to the American Revolution and the Civil War periods.
  • A potential third awakening is suggested as necessary to address the nation's current challenges and save the nation.

  • The host advised young men to actively seek virtuous and praiseworthy things to develop strength and hope.
  • He described a society characterized by cynicism and spiritual malnourishment, urging pursuit of excellence over superficial distractions.
  • A philosophy for men emphasized clear-sightedness, anchoring to higher principles, and building a life of admiration through hard work and wisdom.

  • Screenwriter Noah Oppenheim shared that most presidents receive minimal training on nuclear decision-making, typically one briefing.
  • Presidents hold sole authority and extremely limited decision time during a potential nuclear attack.
  • Interviews with presidential staff revealed that presidents almost never rehearse nuclear decision-making, with Ronald Reagan reportedly the last to do so.
  • Oppenheim's research for his film, "A House of Dynamite," involved consulting former White House and CIA personnel.

  • President Trump reportedly fears nuclear conflict and has discussed improving missile defense and potentially re-engaging in arms control treaties like New START.
  • Public testing records indicate a 61% success rate for ground-based missile interceptors, while the Pentagon claims 100% based on recent tests.
  • The guest highlighted the difficulty of assessing system effectiveness and making decisions with incomplete information during a crisis.

  • The ongoing government shutdown could affect up to 40% of airline flights due to a lack of safety staff.
  • The dispute centers on Obamacare subsidies, which Democrats initially voted to terminate but now seek to continue.
  • Republicans have suggested maintaining current spending levels to resolve the shutdown.
  • Democratic Senator Chris Murphy reportedly commented on the political damage associated with reopening the government.

  • Elon Musk's potential $1 trillion pay package from Tesla shareholders is linked to the company achieving an $8 trillion valuation.
  • The host suggested Musk is driven by a desire to change the world rather than just accumulate wealth, contrasting him with historical figures.
  • Musk's intense dedication to his companies, including XAI, is described as a key trait for achieving ambitious goals, with compensation tied to 12 performance tranches.
