Is Pam Bondi’s Day of Reckoning Here? | Guests: Peter Schweizer & James Brown | 1/20/26

Key Takeaways

  • DOJ inaction criticized on Epstein, COVID, and Deep State issues.
  • Peter Schweizer details foreign adversaries exploiting U.S. immigration and citizenship laws.
  • Host proposes President Trump deliver a strong, sovereign-nation speech at Davos.
  • Montana Auditor exposes a $50 million Obamacare fraud scheme targeting Native Americans.

Deep Dive

  • The host criticizes the Department of Justice for lack of accountability in cases like the Jeffrey Epstein scandal, the Biden AutoPen scandal, and January 6th events.
  • Concerns are raised about the absence of arrests and prosecutions for alleged lies by Capitol Police and the FBI, and perceived inaction against powerful figures.
  • The host argues that visible accountability is essential for a republic to survive, calling for new leadership at the DOJ to restore public trust.

  • The host criticizes Attorney General Pam Bondi and the Department of Justice (DOJ) for a perceived lack of results on the MAGA agenda.
  • The host referenced unfulfilled expectations for justice and a lack of response to a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request regarding the Jeffrey Epstein case.
  • A FOIA request was filed on July 11th, seeking communications from Dan Bongino related to Epstein, Bondi, and Cash Patel, with no response from the DOJ.

  • The host discusses potential reasons for slow DOJ action on complex corruption cases, citing historical examples like Watergate and the prosecution of John Gotti.
  • Modern corruption is described as intricate, involving shell NGOs and foreign cutouts, which necessitates patient investigation over immediate public indictments.
  • Moving too early on cases is suggested to risk allowing accused individuals to escape prosecution and setting precedents for political motivation within the DOJ.

  • Guest Peter Schweizer, author of 'The Invisible Coup,' discusses how Chinese nationals exploit U.S. birthright citizenship laws.
  • Over 100,000 Chinese children are reportedly born in the U.S. annually and raised in China, becoming potential voters with U.S. citizenship.
  • Schweizer notes over 100 Chinese-owned companies in Southern California are involved in surrogacy, with Chinese nationals using American women as carriers for U.S. citizen children.

  • The host and guest discuss how China uses apps like WeChat to monitor and control Chinese students in the U.S., threatening their families if they do not comply.
  • The conversation highlights China's industrial model for encouraging birth tourism to exploit birthright citizenship, with President Trump addressing the issue.
  • Barack Obama's 2014 10-year visa program for Chinese nationals is mentioned as an enabler, allowing pregnant women to enter the U.S. and providing renewable visas.

  • The host expresses concern that radical Islamists and the far-Left have united with the shared goal of destroying America, Israel, and Western civilization.
  • Guest Peter Schweizer explains this alignment as a 'marriage of convenience' to achieve common goals, despite fundamental ideological differences.
  • The alliance reportedly extends to Mexico, with Islamists supporting the Reconquista movement and advocating for the return of territories from the U.S.

  • The host discusses Diego Garcia, described as a highly strategic U.S. military base in the Indian Ocean.
  • Great Britain's decision to relinquish control of Diego Garcia due to accusations of colonialism is noted.
  • The base is equidistant from major regions, useful for power projection, staging, and protecting international shipping and oil routes.

  • The host criticizes the perceived collapse of European nations, their reliance on outsourced security, and dismantling of defenses.
  • Concerns are raised about Europe's approach to immigration, arguing that unchecked influx leads to parallel societies.
  • The host questions the future of NATO and Europe's ability to survive, suggesting a lack of resolve compared to President Trump's view of defending Western civilization.

  • The host proposes a speech for President Trump at the World Economic Forum, urging him to reject global management by unelected institutions.
  • The proposed speech advocates for an end to globalist ideologies that undermine national sovereignty, culture, and the middle class.
  • It emphasizes a return to strong, sovereign nations cooperating directly, confronting issues like Venezuelan corruption and Iran's nuclear ambitions, and restoring U.S. industrial strength.

  • Montana State Auditor James Brown details an Obamacare fraud scheme targeting Native Americans on reservations, estimated at $50 million.
  • The scheme involved fraudulent enrollment, transportation across state lines for unnecessary rehab, and billing insurance companies.
  • Approximately $23.3 million in fraud has been stopped so far, affecting about 200 individuals through fraudulent policies, with criminal prosecution being pursued.
