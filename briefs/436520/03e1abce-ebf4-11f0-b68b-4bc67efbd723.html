Best of the Program | Guests: Ryan Mauro & Carol Roth | 1/7/26

Key Takeaways

  • A Washington organization is allegedly linked to funding Hamas hostage-holders.
  • Experts discuss how 'capital controls' and government intervention could damage cities like New York.
  • President Trump's strategy aims to counter the 'CRINK' alliance and secure U.S. energy dominance.
  • Iran's severe economic crisis and internal turmoil are reportedly weakening its regional influence.

Deep Dive

  • Counterterrorism expert Ryan Mauro discusses the Palestine Chronicle, which is being sued by freed Israeli hostages.
  • The defense claimed a contributor was voluntary, despite writing multiple times daily after October 7th.
  • Evidence suggests the organization raised funds for an initiative coordinated by a Hamas hostage-holder's wife.
  • An alleged network connects the People Media Project to Hamas, the Muslim Brotherhood, and Palestinian Islamic Jihad through organizations in Turkey and a university in California.

  • Former investment banker Carol Roth explains de facto capital controls as government restrictions on private property and money, drawing parallels to Venezuela.
  • Rent control and similar interventions in New York are predicted to lead to property decay and uninhabitable slums due to landlord disincentives.
  • New York City Councilman Zohran Mamdani's proposals for property owners are cited as examples of impending controls.
  • The discussion links these policies to a proposed retroactive wealth tax in California and the departure of billionaire Ken Griffin from Chicago, impacting tax revenue and philanthropy.

  • The 'CRINK' acronym (China, Russia, Iran, North Korea) is introduced as an 'Axis power' alliance.
  • Donald Trump's strategy is presented as dismantling these powers through energy independence and control for the Western hemisphere.
  • Trump's Middle East strategy reportedly shifts from 'endless wars' to a U.S.-anchored block facilitating normalization between nations like Israel, the UAE, and Bahrain.
  • A U.S. Navy operation involving boarding and impounding a Russian-flagged oil tanker from Venezuela is cited as a serious measure alarming adversaries.
