The Glenn Beck Program

Best of the Program | Guests: Allie Beth Stuckey & Christopher Rufo | 12/4/25

Key Takeaways

  • A new GAO report indicates massive Obamacare fraud, including fictitious applications and payments to deceased individuals.
  • Politicians are criticized for defending corrupt programs and ignoring government incompetence, with calls for indictments.
  • Concerns are raised about the 'Islamization of the West,' cultural shifts, and crime in some Muslim communities.
  • Minnesota taxpayers unknowingly funded Al-Shabaab through welfare fraud schemes involving the Somali community.
  • The immigration system and 'toxic empathy' are cited as factors enabling corruption and cultural shifts.

Deep Dive

  • A new GAO report indicates massive fraud within Obamacare, with 100% of fictitious applications approved and renewed annually.
  • The report also highlighted issues with deceased individuals and non-existent social security numbers receiving funds.
  • Similar GAO reports were issued in 2015 and 2016, pointing to billions in Obamacare fraud with little apparent action.

  • The host criticized the distribution of taxpayer money to 'imaginary citizens' and questioned the rationale behind extending subsidies.
  • Programs like Obamacare are argued not to be about healthcare, but potentially about control and manipulation.
  • There's a strong desire for indictments and jail time for those involved in corruption, specifically mentioning those in power who covered up wrongdoing.

  • The host described how communities can bilk the system when no one is held accountable, referencing alleged fraud within the Somali community.
  • Politicians are accused of avoiding addressing the issue due to the perceived need for votes.
  • Frustration was expressed with the GOP for making promises without taking action, with voters desiring tangible results to protect the Republic.

  • Guest Allie Beth Stuckey noted a growing public awareness of an 'Islamic takeover of the West,' with people noticing changes in their neighborhoods.
  • Concerns were highlighted about mandatory halal food in schools and the spread of Islamic dominance.
  • Stuckey discussed increasing violent crime and sexual assault in areas with large Muslim populations, citing examples like Dearborn, Michigan.

  • The discussion introduced 'toxic empathy,' with the guest arguing political expediency, not empathy, drives politicians to ignore community Islamization.
  • The average person's fear of being labeled Islamophobic is cited as preventing them from speaking out against these changes.
  • Donald Trump's tweet characterizing 53 million immigrants as a burden was interpreted as a critique of weaponized compassion.

  • BlazeTV host Christopher Rufo discussed how Minnesota taxpayers were unknowingly funding the terror group Al-Shabaab.
  • Rufo stated that a significant percentage of the Somali community has participated in schemes looting Minnesota's treasury.
  • Minnesota politicians are criticized for focusing on sheltering the Somali community from criticism rather than addressing the looting.

  • Rufo drew parallels between the situation in Minnesota and Sweden, suggesting that Scandinavian kindness and socialism are being exploited.
  • The issue is described as involving both imported corruption and individual bad actors, with a high percentage of the Somali community participating in fraud schemes.
  • America's immigration system is criticized for group-level analyses and granting special privileges to groups like Somalis based on identity.

  • Taxpayer money, suspected of being funneled to Al-Shabaab through informal Somali banking systems, is confirmed by counterterrorism officials.
  • Two hypotheses for the influx of foreign groups are presented: gaining political power through a dependent voting bloc, or liberal naivete.
  • Former Congresswoman Michelle Bachman reportedly warned in 2008/2009 about the State Department moving entire Somali communities into Minnesota.
