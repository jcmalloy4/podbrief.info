Apple News Today

Trump takes a bulldozer to the White House. What to know.

Key Takeaways

  • White House East Wing demolition proceeds for a new ballroom despite concerns and increased costs.
  • U.S. soybean farmers face significant financial strain due to the ongoing U.S.-China trade dispute.
  • A newly elected Arizona congresswoman's swearing-in is delayed, prompting a state lawsuit and political allegations.
  • Global news includes U.S. sanctions on Russian oil, a drug boat strike, and mosquitoes appearing in Iceland.

Deep Dive

  • Demolition of parts of the East Wing is underway in preparation for President Trump's new ballroom.
  • The project's estimated cost has increased to $300 million, departing from past White House modification approaches.
  • President Trump has a long-held desire for this project, aiming to establish a lasting legacy as a builder.
  • Preservation groups are expressing growing opposition, concerned about the proposed ballroom's scale.

  • China has ceased soybean imports from the U.S. in retaliation for tariffs, impacting American farmers.
  • Soybeans are a major U.S. crop, and falling prices due to the dispute are causing financial strain for farmers.
  • China is increasing soybean imports from South America and investing heavily in Brazil's infrastructure.
  • The administration is releasing $3 billion in aid and considering a multi-billion dollar bailout package for affected farmers.

  • Arizona Democrat Adelita Grijalva won a special election but has not been sworn in by House Speaker Mike Johnson.
  • Speaker Johnson cites the government shutdown for the delay, while Grijalva claims political motivation.
  • Arizona's Attorney General is suing to have Grijalva seated, arguing 800,000 constituents lack representation.
  • Grijalva stated she is the 218th signer for the discharge petition to release Jeffrey Epstein records.

  • The U.S. has sanctioned Russian oil companies, marking a shift from previous trade tactics under President Trump.
  • U.S. forces conducted an eighth strike against a suspected drug boat in the Pacific, resulting in at least 32 deaths.
  • Mosquitoes have been detected in Iceland, a region previously considered mosquito-free, raising concerns.
