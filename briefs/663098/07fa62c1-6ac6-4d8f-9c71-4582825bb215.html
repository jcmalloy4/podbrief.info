Jimmy Kimmel returns to late-night TV. Here’s what he said.

Key Takeaways

  • Jimmy Kimmel returned to his show after a suspension, drawing scrutiny from the FCC.
  • President Trump criticized the United Nations and addressed issues including climate change and national identity.
  • Iowa's major agriculture industry is being investigated for its links to water pollution and high cancer rates.
  • Amazon faces allegations from the FTC of duping millions of customers into Prime subscriptions.
  • A man was found guilty of attempting to assassinate Donald Trump at his Florida golf course.

Deep Dive

  • Jimmy Kimmel returned to his show after a suspension by Disney for comments made following Charlie Kirk's death, expressing disagreement with Disney's decision.
  • FCC Chair Brendan Carr clarified that complaints trigger FCC adjudication if stations and national programmers like Disney cannot resolve issues themselves.
  • Critics accuse Carr of leveraging FCC power, including influence over mergers and acquisitions, to silence dissent, citing past FCC decisions involving media companies and Trump.

  • President Trump criticized the United Nations during his speech at the organization's General Assembly, questioning its effectiveness and its attitude toward the U.S.
  • Trump spoke for an extended period, touching on multiple topics including climate change and migration.
  • He emphasized national identity throughout his address to the General Assembly.

  • Donald Trump's comments at the UN General Assembly suggest Ukraine could reclaim occupied territory, indicating a shift from his previous stance.
  • This follows his withdrawal from UN multilateral structures and cessation of dues.
  • The U.S. currently owes over $3 billion to the UN due to these actions.

  • Iowa's high cancer rate is being investigated for a link to its massive $50 billion agriculture industry.
  • Health experts suspect a connection to billions of pounds of nitrogen fertilizer and manure runoff, which pollutes water and has been linked to health risks including birth defects and blue baby syndrome.
  • Despite EPA regulations enacted in the 1990s, efforts to control nitrate risks in Iowa's water have stalled due to reported influence from state officials and the Iowa Farm Bureau.
  • Interviews with Iowa residents reveal concerns about an 'epidemic' of cancer in rural areas, with one couple knowing 21 diagnosed individuals within a five-mile radius.

  • A man who attempted to assassinate Donald Trump at his Florida golf course was found guilty.
  • Amazon is in court facing Federal Trade Commission allegations of duping over 35 million consumers into Prime subscriptions.
  • The FTC alleges a complex cancellation process, with potential penalties of billions of dollars.
