Nancy Pelosi’s legacy after nearly four decades in Congress

Key Takeaways

  • Nancy Pelosi announced her retirement from Congress after nearly 40 years.
  • Trinidad and Tobago's Prime Minister is backing U.S. military strikes in the Caribbean.
  • A dispute between YouTube TV and Disney has blacked out ESPN and other channels for millions.
  • Tesla shareholders approved a record $1 trillion pay package for CEO Elon Musk.
  • A federal judge ordered full funding for November's SNAP benefits.
  • Security at the Louvre is under scrutiny following a $102 million jewelry heist.

Deep Dive

  • Nancy Pelosi announced her retirement from Congress after nearly four decades, ending her term in 2027.
  • She served as the first female Speaker of the House and led House Democrats for 20 years.
  • Pelosi played a key role in legislative battles, including the Affordable Care Act, and confronted Donald Trump.
  • Discussion includes speculation that she may want to make way for new voices in leadership.

  • Trinidad and Tobago's Prime Minister, Kamla Prasad Biseser, supports President Trump's military strikes against suspected drug boats in the Caribbean.
  • This alignment is partly driven by hopes of accessing Venezuelan gas reserves, crucial for Trinidad and Tobago's economy.
  • The Prime Minister is identified as a risk-taker, potentially aiming for regime change in Venezuela to honor existing business deals.
  • Congress voted against restricting President Trump's military campaign despite concerns from some Republicans.

  • A dispute between YouTube TV and Disney has resulted in 10 million subscribers losing access to ESPN and other Disney-owned channels.
  • The blackout impacts sports viewership, including Monday Night Football, and affects networks like ABC and FX.
  • CNBC reports the core arguments involve carriage fees and the integration of Disney's streaming content.

  • Tesla shareholders approved a record-setting $1 trillion pay package for CEO Elon Musk.
  • The approval is seen as a referendum on Musk's leadership and his vision for humanoid robotics.
  • Musk described the moment as the beginning of a new era for Tesla.
