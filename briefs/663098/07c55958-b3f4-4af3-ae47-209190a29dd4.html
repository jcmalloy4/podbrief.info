Inside the immigration deal Democrats cut to avoid a shutdown

Key Takeaways

  • Government shutdown averted over ICE funding; Republicans agreed to a short-term extension.
  • Iran's government violently suppressed national protests, leading to civilian deaths in Rasht.
  • Significant tax law changes for 2024 returns include new provisions for overtime and tips.
  • A man impersonating an FBI agent attempted to free a suspect from a Brooklyn jail.
  • U.S. life expectancy increased to 79 years in 2024 but still lags other developed nations.

Deep Dive

  • Senate Democrats, Republicans, and the White House reached a deal to prevent a government shutdown over ICE funding.
  • Republicans agreed to a short-term extension for Department of Homeland Security funding while negotiations continue.
  • Democrats are demanding reforms, including ending roving patrols, enforcing agent accountability, and prohibiting masks for federal agents.
  • The Atlantic's Caitlin Dickerson noted that previous legislation provided ICE with significant funding without stipulations regarding use of force or accountability.

  • The Iranian government initiated a communication blackout amid widespread protests driven by economic hardship, which expanded to more affluent classes.
  • The Washington Post's Yeganeh Torbati reported on a violent crackdown by security forces in Rasht on January 8th.
  • Security forces reportedly used tear gas and ammunition against protesters and shop owners who refused to reopen businesses.
  • During clashes in Rasht, civilians were reportedly killed and injured, a bazaar caught fire, and resentment toward the regime increased.

  • A man impersonating an FBI agent was arrested after attempting to free Luigi Mangione from a Brooklyn jail.
  • The individual used a barbecue fork and a pizza cutter in the attempted jailbreak.
  • Mangione is currently awaiting trial for a 2024 killing.
