Apple News Today

A sitting president had never sued a major newspaper for libel. Until now.

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump has launched an unprecedented $20 billion libel lawsuit against The Wall Street Journal over a report linking him to Jeffrey Epstein.
  • The humanitarian crisis in Gaza is escalating as Israeli forces extend operations into previously untouched areas, exacerbating an already dire hunger situation.
  • A critical Air India crash investigation centers on cockpit actions, with pilots questioning each other about key fuel switches moved post-takeoff.
  • House Speaker Mike Johnson adjourned early to prevent votes on Jeffrey Epstein files, as Democrats pressed for more information amidst political pressures.

Deep Dives

Trump's Unprecedented Wall Street Journal Lawsuit

  • Donald Trump has launched an unprecedented $20 billion libel lawsuit against The Wall Street Journal over a report claiming he wrote a birthday note to Jeffrey Epstein.
  • CNN's Brian Stelter suggests the Journal's practice of giving advance notice indicates confidence in their reporting, facing the high bar of proving actual malice.
  • This move is interpreted as either a publicity stunt or a strategic warning to other media organizations, potentially creating a chilling effect on journalism.

Escalating Humanitarian Crisis in Gaza

  • Israeli troops have extended operations into central Gaza's Deir al-Bala, an area previously untouched, striking homes and World Health Organization facilities, intensifying the humanitarian crisis.
  • Aid centers have become perilous, with reports of dozens killed and wounded near a border crossing as aid trucks entered, further exacerbating the dire hunger situation.
  • The UN Secretary General and 28 countries have condemned the escalating conditions, emphasizing collapsing lifelines and calling for an immediate end to the conflict amidst widespread malnutrition.

Air India Crash Investigation Focuses on Cockpit Actions

  • An investigation into the Air India crash, which claimed 260 lives, reveals that critical fuel switches were moved shortly after takeoff, leading to a loss of thrust and altitude.
  • Cockpit recordings capture one pilot questioning the other about the switch movement, which the second pilot denies, prompting deeper inquiry into cockpit actions.
  • The ongoing probe is exploring various contributing factors, from muscle memory to potential design flaws or malfunctions, with new information expected to refine initial assessments.
