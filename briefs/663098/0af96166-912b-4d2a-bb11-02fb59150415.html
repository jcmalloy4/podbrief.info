The suspect in Charlie Kirk’s killing is charged. What we’ve learned.

Key Takeaways

  • Tyler Robinson was charged with aggravated murder in Charlie Kirk's killing.
  • The U.S. labor market faces slower job growth and increasing long-term unemployment.
  • Toxic fumes are reportedly leaking into airplane cabins more frequently.
  • A UN-commissioned report accused Israel of committing genocide.
  • An ICE detention center in Texas violated over 60 federal standards.
  • The world's oldest mummies, dating back 14,000 years, were discovered.

Deep Dive

  • Tyler Robinson appeared in court, charged with aggravated murder in the killing of Charlie Kirk.
  • Prosecutors allege the motive was Kirk's political expression and are seeking the death penalty.
  • Robinson's alleged statements and text messages were detailed in court proceedings.

  • The Federal Reserve is expected to announce an interest rate cut to address a slowing labor market.
  • Despite a low national unemployment rate, job growth is slower than anticipated.
  • Over a quarter of unemployed individuals have been jobless for six months or more.
  • The Washington Post highlights individuals like one relying on SNAP benefits, six months behind on rent.

  • Toxic fumes, sometimes smelling like dirty socks or wet dog, are increasingly leaking into airplane cabins.
  • These fumes, which can originate from leaking engine oil or hydraulic fluid, may contain harmful neurotoxins.
  • Thousands of such incidents have been reported since 2010, affecting both passengers and crew.
  • The Wall Street Journal's reporting suggests manufacturers and airlines have downplayed health risks and lobbied against safety measures.
  • One flight attendant developed a traumatic brain injury and permanent nervous system damage after exposure, a condition her neurologist called a chemical concussion.

  • A UN-commissioned report accuses Israel of committing genocide, citing aid blockages and destruction of facilities.
  • This finding is the strongest to date from the United Nations, though independently commissioned.
  • Thousands of Palestinians are currently fleeing Gaza City as Israel's ground offensive continues.
