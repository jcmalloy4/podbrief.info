The FBI stuns the NBA with Mafia-linked charges against its stars

Key Takeaways

  • NBA coach Chauncey Billups and player Terry Rozier were arrested in a sweeping gambling investigation.
  • A government shutdown continues amidst debate over Affordable Care Act subsidies and Medicaid.
  • President Trump pardoned Binance founder Changpeng Zhao, who admitted to money laundering violations.
  • Silicon Valley leaders lobbied President Trump to halt federal troop deployment to San Francisco.
  • The World Series has begun, featuring the Los Angeles Dodgers against the Toronto Blue Jays.
  • A woman with Parkinson's disease played the clarinet during brain surgery to improve motor skills.

Deep Dive

  • Portland Trail Blazers coach Chauncey Billups and Miami Heat guard Terry Rozier were indicted for alleged gambling schemes and game manipulation.
  • The FBI detailed a coordinated takedown, alleging involvement of organized crime families.
  • The NBA has placed both Billups and Rozier on leave following the charges.

  • The government shutdown, now the second longest in modern history, continues due to an impasse between Republicans and Democrats.
  • Democrats demand extensions of Affordable Care Act (ACA) subsidies and reversal of Medicaid cuts.
  • Republicans advocate maintaining current spending levels, claiming ACA subsidies fund medical care for undocumented immigrants.
  • Washington Post's David Ovalle clarified undocumented immigrants are largely ineligible for federal plans, though Emergency Medicaid covers necessary treatments.
  • Premiums could double for many Americans without ACA subsidy extensions.

  • President Trump pardoned Binance founder Changpeng Zhao, who admitted to money laundering violations.
  • Zhao's company facilitated billions in illicit cryptocurrency transactions, leading to a $4.3 billion fine from the Department of Justice.
  • Binance actively lobbied for the pardon and has connections to a crypto company launched by Donald Trump Jr.
  • The Trump Jr.-linked crypto company reportedly added $5 billion to the Trump family's net worth, sparking claims of corruption from top Democrats.

  • President Trump rescinded plans to deploy federal troops to San Francisco.
  • This decision followed lobbying efforts from Silicon Valley tech leaders.
  • Concerns included potential National Guard deployment or increased immigration enforcement in the city.

  • The World Series has commenced, featuring the Los Angeles Dodgers against the Toronto Blue Jays.
  • The Dodgers swept the Milwaukee Brewers in their previous series.
  • The Blue Jays secured their spot by winning a seven-game series against the Seattle Mariners.
  • Experts anticipate a close series, despite the Dodgers being favored.
