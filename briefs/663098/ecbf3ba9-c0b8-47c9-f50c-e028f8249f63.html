The Gaza ceasefire deal is holding. Here’s what’s next.

Key Takeaways

  • A Gaza ceasefire deal resulted in the release of 20 Israeli hostages, with Israel to free 250 Palestinians.
  • The government shutdown has led to over 4,000 federal worker layoffs and significant public service disruptions.
  • Police reports challenge President Trump's "war zone" characterization of Portland protests, detailing localized demonstrations.
  • Four people died and 20 were injured in a mass shooting at a South Carolina bar during a high school reunion.

Deep Dive

  • The first phase of a Gaza ceasefire deal saw Hamas release 20 Israeli hostages.
  • In exchange, Israel is expected to release 250 Palestinians, following negotiations where President Trump's pressure was key.
  • President Trump visited Israel, met with hostage families, and addressed parliament, calling it a "great and beautiful day."
  • The agreement facilitated the movement of 600 humanitarian aid trucks into Gaza on Sunday, including essential infrastructure repair equipment.
  • Anat Peled of The Wall Street Journal noted that while the initial deal was achieved, future stages, including disarming Hamas, will be significantly more challenging.

  • Over 4,000 federal workers across at least seven departments received layoff notices over the weekend due to the ongoing government shutdown.
  • Unions have filed lawsuits to halt these layoffs, which President Trump described as targeting "Democrat programs."
  • Approximately 1.4 million federal workers are now entering their first week without pay.
  • The shutdown's public impact extends to closed IRS helplines and FAA staffing shortages causing flight delays.
  • Negotiations remain stalled, with Democrats insisting on extending Affordable Care Act subsidies, a $350 billion cost opposed by Republicans like Senator Lindsey Graham.

  • The Wall Street Journal's Zusha Ellenson investigated police reports, which dispute President Trump's characterization of Portland as a "war zone."
  • Reports indicated protests were smaller, less energetic, and concentrated near an ICE facility, not reflecting a city under siege.
  • Federal authorities used pepper balls and tear gas on protesters near the ICE facility, with no arrests made.
  • The Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals heard arguments regarding President Trump's attempt to deploy National Guard troops, with the court appearing skeptical of a block on deployment.
  • Images of demonstrators in costumes aimed to contrast the narrative of them being violent extremists, differing from intense 2020 protests that turned public sentiment.

  • Four individuals were killed and 20 others injured in a mass shooting at Willie's bar and grill in South Carolina.
  • The incident occurred early Sunday morning during a high school reunion attended by approximately 100 people.
