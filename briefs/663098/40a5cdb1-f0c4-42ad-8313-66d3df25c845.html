Democrats won big in last night’s elections. Here’s what you missed.

Key Takeaways

  • Democrats secured election victories in New York, New Jersey, and Virginia.
  • California voters approved Proposition 50, enabling Democrats to redraw electoral maps.
  • The Supreme Court will examine President Trump's authority to impose global tariffs.
  • A UPS cargo plane crash in Louisville, Kentucky, resulted in seven deaths.

Deep Dive

  • Democrats achieved a clean sweep in recent elections, with Zohran Mamdani projected to win the New York mayoral race.
  • The party also secured victories in the New Jersey and Virginia gubernatorial races.
  • California voters approved Proposition 50, a redistricting measure that allows Democrats to redraw electoral maps, potentially impacting future midterm elections.
  • Governor Gavin Newsom described the night as 'extraordinary' following the election outcomes.

  • The Supreme Court is set to hear arguments regarding President Trump's authority to impose sweeping global tariffs.
  • The case centers on whether such actions require explicit congressional authorization.
  • A ruling against the administration could mean businesses that paid nearly $90 billion in tariffs might receive refunds.
  • The outcome could also significantly influence the administration's negotiation tactics with other countries.

  • A UPS cargo plane crashed in Louisville, Kentucky, resulting in at least seven deaths.
  • Additionally, 11 people on the ground sustained significant injuries due to the impact.
  • The crash caused fires that affected nearby buildings, contributing to the severity of the incident.
  • All flight operations in Louisville were halted following the accident, disrupting air traffic.
