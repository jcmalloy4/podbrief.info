Fed Chair Powell is under criminal investigation. What to know.

Key Takeaways

  • Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell faces a criminal investigation, which he publicly rebuked as potentially political.
  • Anti-government protests are escalating in Iran, resulting in reports of up to 500 deaths and 10,000 arrests amid crackdowns.
  • Tens of thousands protested ICE in Minneapolis after an agent fatally shot Renee Good, prompting a federal response.
  • A historic Mississippi synagogue was targeted by arson, and President Trump's National Portrait Gallery portrait was updated.
  • The Golden Globes awarded "Good Hang with Amy Poehler" its inaugural podcast award, alongside other film and TV honors.

Deep Dive

  • Federal prosecutors opened a criminal investigation into Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell.
  • Powell issued a public rebuke, suggesting the action is politically motivated despite no one being above the law.
  • The investigation reportedly focuses on Powell's statements about the Fed's building renovation.
  • The Wall Street Journal reports the DOJ investigation is prioritized by Attorney General Pam Bondi.
  • President Trump has frequently criticized Powell for not lowering interest rates faster.

  • Anti-government protests in Iran have entered their third week, sparked by rising prices.
  • The unrest is met with crackdowns and an internet blackout, with reports of up to 500 deaths and over 10,000 arrests.
  • Iran's inflation rate is reported at 42.2%, with the Grand Bazaar joining protests by striking.
  • Protests challenge theocracy, spreading to multiple cities with chants against Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei.
  • President Trump has threatened action if the regime continues to kill protesters, unsettling regime leadership.

  • Tens of thousands protested ICE's presence in Minneapolis following the fatal shooting of Renee Good by an ICE agent.
  • Demonstrations included confrontations, with federal agents reportedly using force.
  • Homeland Security Secretary Christy Noam announced plans to send hundreds more agents to Minneapolis for a large-scale operation.
  • Local and federal authorities are investigating discrepancies in the circumstances of Good's death.

  • The Golden Globes awarded "Good Hang with Amy Poehler" its first-ever podcast award.
  • Paul Thomas Anderson won Best Director for "One Battle After Another."
  • Tiana Taylor received best supporting actress, Timothy Chalamet for "Marty Supreme," and Rose Byrne best actress for "If I Had Legs, I'd Kick You."
  • Awards were also presented to television shows "The Pit," "The Studio," and "Pluribus."
