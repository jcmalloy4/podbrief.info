Shutdown Deadline, Gaza Deal Reaction, Top Military Leaders Meeting

Key Takeaways

  • Congress faces a looming government shutdown tonight over healthcare funding disagreements.
  • President Trump unveiled a 20-point plan for a Gaza ceasefire and hostage release.
  • Hundreds of U.S. generals and admirals are meeting in Quantico, with President Trump expected to speak.

Deep Dive

  • Federal agencies face a funding cutoff tonight as Congress remains deadlocked on a budget deal.
  • Healthcare provisions are cited as a major obstacle, with Democrats and Republicans blaming each other for the impasse.
  • A potential shutdown could impact federal workers and various government programs.

  • President Trump proposed a 20-point plan to end the Gaza war, including a ceasefire and hostage release.
  • The plan also involves the daily flow of humanitarian aid and the release of Palestinian detainees.
  • Hamas has not yet responded to the proposal, while Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu presents it as an Israeli win.

  • Hundreds of U.S. generals and admirals are attending a rare, unannounced meeting in Quantico, Virginia.
  • President Trump is expected to address the gathering of top military leaders.
  • Speculation surrounds the purpose, including discussions on military ethos or a reduction in high-ranking officers.
