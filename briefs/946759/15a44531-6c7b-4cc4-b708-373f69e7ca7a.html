Up First from NPR

Trump's Defends Affordability, SCOTUS & Presidential Power, Indiana Redistricting

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump is campaigning in Pennsylvania, defending his economic record against voter blame for high prices.
  • The Supreme Court is considering granting presidents more power to remove independent agency members.
  • Indiana lawmakers are proposing new congressional maps that could eliminate the state's two Democratic seats.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump traveled to Pennsylvania to address affordability concerns, a key issue where polls indicate voters blame his policies.
  • The White House claims Democrats caused affordability problems, while Trump previously labeled the issue a 'hoax'.
  • This trip aims to defend his record amidst increasing voter dissatisfaction regarding high prices.

  • The Supreme Court heard arguments on whether the President can remove members of independent agencies, a power currently restricted.
  • The conservative majority on the court signaled support for granting the president greater removal authority.
  • This potential shift could significantly impact numerous federal regulatory bodies and the operation of the federal government.

  • Solicitor General John Sauer argued for greater presidential accountability over independent agencies during Supreme Court arguments.
  • Justices expressed concerns about a potential power vacuum if agencies are not directly accountable to the president.
  • Liberal justices voiced concerns regarding the consolidation of executive, legislative, and judicial power under such a change.
  • A Cato Institute analyst expressed confidence the court will overturn a 90-year-old precedent limiting presidential removal power.

  • Indiana lawmakers are considering new congressional maps that could eliminate the state's two Democratic seats.
  • This move is part of an unprecedented wave of mid-cycle redistricting efforts nationwide, spurred by former President Trump.
  • The proposed map would divide Indianapolis, potentially impacting representation for minority communities.
  • The goal is to aid Republican chances in upcoming elections.

  • A voter argued that Republican control of the House is crucial to prevent constant investigations against the president.
  • Passing Indiana's new congressional map faces uncertainty due to constituent opposition and internal party disagreements.
  • Former President Trump has increased pressure on undecided senators to support the proposed map.
