Up First from NPR

Congressional To-Do List, Trump V. BBC, Ukraine Update

Key Takeaways

  • Congress faces deadlines for federal funding, healthcare subsidies, and a vote on Jeffrey Epstein files.
  • Donald Trump is pursuing a multi-billion dollar lawsuit against the BBC for an edited January 6 speech.
  • Ukraine endures Russian attacks on infrastructure amidst a major corruption probe regarding state energy funds.
  • Ukrainian troops defending Pokrovsk are exhausted and in critical need of resources due to evolving Russian tactics.

Deep Dive

  • House lawmakers expect an imminent vote on releasing government files related to Jeffrey Epstein, a measure causing Republican divisions.
  • Following a 43-day shutdown, Congress has reopened but has not yet addressed expiring Affordable Care Act subsidies.
  • Lawmakers also face a January deadline to fund federal agencies.

  • A vote is anticipated this week on releasing Justice Department documents concerning Jeffrey Epstein.
  • The bill is expected to pass easily in the House, which could increase pressure on the Senate to act.
  • This issue has previously caused divisions within the Republican party regarding the disclosure of these files.

  • President Trump is threatening a $1 billion to $5 billion lawsuit against the BBC, alleging they "cheated" by altering his January 6th, 2021 speech.
  • The BBC had previously apologized for misleadingly editing the speech, splicing remarks made an hour apart, which led to two executive resignations.
  • The BBC acknowledges the edit created an unintentional false impression but states there is no legal basis for defamation.

  • Ukraine is experiencing widespread blackouts due to ongoing Russian attacks on its energy infrastructure.
  • A major corruption probe alleges associates of President Zelensky plotted to skim $100 billion from the state nuclear energy producer.
  • This probe has led to ministerial resignations, though President Zelensky has not been directly implicated.

  • Ukrainian soldiers defending the eastern city of Pokrovsk are exhausted as Russian forces have infiltrated with new tactics, including infantry groups supported by drone swarms.
  • Soldiers report a critical need for more resources to hold their positions against the ongoing Russian assault.
  • Ukrainians associate corruption with Russia and view it as particularly damaging during wartime, potentially leading to soldiers' deaths.
