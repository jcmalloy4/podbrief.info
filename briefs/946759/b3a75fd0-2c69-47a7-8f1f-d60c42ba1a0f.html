Up First from NPR

Senate Funding Vote, ICE Family Detention Protest, Fed Holds Interest Rates

Key Takeaways

  • Senate Democrats are threatening a partial government shutdown by Friday's deadline over immigration enforcement limits.
  • A protest at an ICE family detention center in South Texas confronted police and led to arrests.
  • The Federal Reserve maintained interest rates to combat 3% inflation, resisting pressure from President Trump.
  • A federal judge ruled against transferring Liam Ramos, 5, and his father from the detention facility.

Deep Dive

  • Senate Democrats, led by Chuck Schumer, are threatening a partial government shutdown.
  • The deadline for agreement is Friday, requiring new limits on immigration enforcement by Republicans.
  • This threat follows a protest at an ICE family detention center in South Texas.

  • A protest occurred at the South Texas Family Residential Center in Dilley, where demonstrators demanded the release of 5-year-old Liam Ramos and his father.
  • Police in riot gear confronted protesters, who marched to the facility's gate and encountered state troopers and masked ICE agents.
  • Joey Palacios of Texas Public Radio reported on the demonstration, which included chants to abolish ICE and free Liam Ramos.

  • Protesters at the Dilley facility were dispersed by state troopers using pepper balls, leading to two arrests for resisting arrest and interfering with public duties.
  • Congressman Joaquin Castro visited Liam Ramos and his father, reporting the 5-year-old appeared physically and emotionally affected and expressed a desire to return to school.
  • The family had entered the U.S. legally and was awaiting their asylum case; a federal judge has since ruled they cannot be removed or transferred from the facility.

  • The Federal Reserve decided to keep interest rates unchanged, aiming to balance fighting inflation with avoiding increased unemployment.
  • Inflation is currently at 3%, exceeding the Fed's 2% target, with Fed Chair Jerome Powell attributing some of the overshoot to President Trump's tariffs.
  • Despite recent layoffs at companies like Amazon and UPS, unemployment remains low at 4.4%.

  • President Trump has pressured the Fed to lower interest rates, attempting to influence the board and Chair Powell.
  • The Fed is designed to be politically independent, a principle Powell emphasized as crucial for effective inflation control.
  • Powell's term as Fed chairman ends in May, and his replacement nominee will face scrutiny regarding their ability to act independently from political pressure.
