Up First from NPR

U.S.-Iran Talks Continue, Texas Politics, 2026 Winter Olympics Begin

Key Takeaways

  • U.S. and Iranian envoys conducted intensive six-hour talks in Oman to de-escalate regional tensions.
  • Texas primary elections are featuring contentious debates over immigration enforcement policies.
  • The 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan and the Super Bowl headline a busy sports weekend.
  • NPR's upcoming Sunday show will investigate former President Trump's financial dealings.

Deep Dive

  • U.S. and Iranian envoys held six-hour intensive talks in Oman, aimed at de-escalating regional tensions.
  • President Trump described the talks as 'very good,' following the discussions.
  • Iran's foreign minister indicated a desire for further dialogue focusing on its nuclear program.

  • The U.S. has implemented financial pressure on Iran, creating a dollar shortage, protests, and currency devaluation.
  • New sanctions were levied against companies involved in trading Iranian oil.
  • Regional perspectives on U.S. calls for regime change in Iran are divided, with Israel supportive.
  • Iran insists its nuclear program is for peaceful purposes, a claim disputed by the U.S. but not substantiated by the UN watchdog.

  • Texas primary elections feature Governor Greg Abbott and Senator John Cornyn facing Republican challengers.
  • Immigration enforcement is a central issue, with Republicans advocating for respecting ICE and continuing deportations.
  • Democratic candidates Jasmine Crockett and James Tallarico have expressed a desire to abolish ICE.
  • Protests have erupted across Texas, with participants expressing anger over policies they feel do not solely target criminals.
  • Democratic candidates are appealing to Latino voters reportedly disillusioned with Republican immigration stances.

  • Sports fans anticipate a weekend featuring both the 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan and the Super Bowl.
  • Key storylines for the Winter Olympics include the return of NHL players and Lindsey Vonn's participation despite injury.
  • Russian and Belarusian athletes are excluded from the Winter Olympics.
  • The upcoming Super Bowl features the Seattle Seahawks and New England Patriots.

  • The upcoming Sunday show, 'The Sunday Story,' will explore President Trump's financial dealings.
  • The segment will investigate how his family has profited from the presidency.
  • Reporting for this episode will feature NPR's Planet Money and New Yorker writer David Kirkpatrick.
