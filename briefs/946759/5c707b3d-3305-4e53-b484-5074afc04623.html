Up First from NPR

Ukraine Battlefield, New Immigrant Detention Center, Kennedy Center Honors

Key Takeaways

  • Military conflict intensifies in Ukraine as US-Russia summit nears.
  • New large detention center signals increased deportation capacity in US.
  • President Trump's direct involvement reshapes Kennedy Center Honors.

Deep Dives

Ukraine Conflict

  • Russian forces gain ground near Pakrovsk, increasing drone attacks on civilians, while Ukraine counters with its own drones on supply lines.
  • Ukraine views the upcoming US-Russia summit with skepticism, anticipating no truce and potential pressure for concessions.

Detention Expansion

  • El Paso's Fort Bliss is set to become the nation's largest immigration detention facility, housing 5,000 adults for deportation.
  • The center aims to boost mass deportation efforts, raising questions about its cost and focus on criminal aliens versus broader enforcement.

Honors Handpicked

  • President Trump directly selected this year's Kennedy Center honorees and will host the ceremony, a significant departure from tradition.
  • Honorees include Gloria Gaynor, George Strait, and Kiss, as Trump seeks to make the event "our kind of political".
