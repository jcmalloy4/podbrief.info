Up First from NPR

Guthrie Investigation, Ghislaine Maxwell Deposition, Seahawks Win Super Bowl LX

Key Takeaways

  • The search for Nancy Guthrie, Savannah Guthrie's mother, enters its second week amid a ransom message.
  • Ghislaine Maxwell is slated for congressional questioning about Jeffrey Epstein's crimes and associates.
  • The Seattle Seahawks secured their second championship, defeating the New England Patriots 29-13 in Super Bowl LX.

Deep Dive

  • The search for Nancy Guthrie, mother of Today show co-host Savannah Guthrie, has entered its second week.
  • Her family released a video confirming receipt of a message from the kidnappers and expressed willingness to pay a ransom.
  • Investigators found blood drops at Guthrie's home and evidence of a disconnected doorbell camera and pacemaker app.
  • No suspects have been identified as of the report.

  • Ghislaine Maxwell, serving a 20-year sentence for sex trafficking, is scheduled for a private deposition with House Oversight Committee lawmakers.
  • They intend to question her about Jeffrey Epstein's crimes and powerful associates, including figures like Donald Trump.
  • Maxwell continues to contest her conviction related to Epstein's activities.
  • Documents related to Jeffrey Epstein also mention figures including Elon Musk, Deepak Chopra, Noam Chomsky, and Bill Clinton, though their appearance does not indicate wrongdoing.

  • The Seattle Seahawks defeated the New England Patriots 29-13 in Super Bowl LX.
  • This victory marks the franchise's second championship title.
  • The game was characterized by a dominant Seahawks defense and low scoring.
  • Seattle struggled to convert drives into touchdowns until the fourth quarter.
