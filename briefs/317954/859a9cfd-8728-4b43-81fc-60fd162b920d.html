Dr. Laura Call of the Day

Chronic Pain is Wrecking My Life

Key Takeaways

  • Thalidomide's lifelong impact: Teresa's story illustrates the devastating, multi-generational consequences of the thalidomide tragedy, with nearly 40 surgeries and chronic pain defining her 62-year journey since birth.
  • Resilience through perspective: Despite severe physical limitations requiring mobility aids and spending 40% of her day bedridden, Teresa maintains extraordinary positivity by focusing on others who face greater challenges.
  • Specialist selection is critical: For complex chronic pain management, consulting the right type of specialist—specifically neurologists with brain stimulation expertise rather than general pain doctors—can make the difference between treatment success and failure.
  • Family burden concerns drive motivation: Teresa's primary emotional struggle isn't her own suffering, but her deep worry about becoming a burden to loved ones, highlighting how chronic illness affects entire family systems.

Deep Dive

Teresa's Background and Medical Journey

  • Teresa, a 62-year-old thalidomide baby born in 1962, shares her extensive medical history shaped by the drug's devastating effects
  • Her medical journey includes nearly 40 surgeries throughout her lifetime, demonstrating the ongoing complications from her birth condition
  • Since 1993, Teresa has battled chronic pain, which has become a defining aspect of her daily experience
Current Health Status and Challenges
  • Teresa's current physical limitations are significant: she walks with a cane, uses a wheelchair, and spends approximately 40% of her day in bed
  • Recent medical developments include a double mastectomy, bone issues, fused SI joints, and a new diagnosis of back deterioration discovered at Duke University
  • Her mobility and quality of life continue to be impacted by these compounding medical complications
Psychological Resilience and Coping Strategies
  • Despite her extensive challenges, Teresa maintains a remarkably positive outlook by focusing on the perspective that "there are people with worse troubles"
  • Her primary emotional concern centers on not wanting to become a burden to her family, reflecting her selfless approach to managing her condition
  • Throughout the discussion, Teresa demonstrates extraordinary resilience, warmth, and compassion while discussing her difficult health journey
Pain Management Exploration and Medical Recommendations
  • The conversation shifts to exploring advanced pain management options, specifically discussing brain stimulation treatments
  • Teresa currently uses a topical TENS unit as part of her pain management regimen
  • The discussion emphasizes the importance of consulting with the right type of specialist for optimal treatment outcomes
Expert Medical Guidance
  • A key recommendation emerges: Teresa should consult specifically with a neurologist who has expertise in brain stimulation for pain treatment, rather than other specialists like anesthesiologists
  • The speakers note that many people they know who attempted various pain treatments experienced limited success, potentially due to consulting inappropriate specialists
  • The primary advice focuses on researching and identifying a neurologist who specializes specifically in brain and nerve-related pain treatments, suggesting this targeted approach may yield better results than previous attempts
