Dr. Laura Call of the Day

Refresher: A Business Deal Ruined My Relationship

Key Takeaways

  • Mixing business with close personal relationships carries extreme risk - Steve's partnership with a family friend ultimately destroyed both the business relationship and the personal friendship, with no reconciliation attempts made afterward.
  • Childhood abandonment issues can drive adult relationship patterns - Steve's experience with an absent father led him to unconsciously create "coattails" for his friend to ride, essentially making himself indispensable to prevent being left behind again.
  • Failed partnerships often reveal deeper psychological motivations - What appeared to be a friend taking advantage was actually Steve actively maintaining closeness through dependency, recreating familiar family dynamics from his role as "man of the family" at age 8.
  • Self-awareness can transform regret into understanding - By recognizing his pattern of trying to "rescue" others to maintain relationships, Steve gained clarity about his emotional drivers and can make more conscious choices moving forward.

Deep Dive

Business Partnership Origins and Breakdown

  • Steve called in to discuss a failed business partnership with a close family friend, where he had contributed approximately 80% of the work on an invention he created
  • The partnership deteriorated due to fundamental disagreements:
- Differing risk tolerances between the partners - Conflicts over whether and when to sell the business - The friend questioning Steve's ethics and business decisions

  • The business fallout had severe personal consequences:
- Steve ultimately walked away from the friendship entirely - The breakdown impacted Steve's entire family, not just him personally - The friend made no effort to reconnect after the split - Steve retained mixed feelings about the lost relationship

Current Business Status and Outcomes

  • Steve took sole ownership of the business following the partnership dissolution
  • They had appeared on Dragon's Den (Canadian Shark Tank) but failed to secure funding
  • Steve still owns the patents and may pursue the business independently in the future
  • The host warned against mixing business with personal relationships, noting the high likelihood of losing friendships when business partnerships fail

Emotional Impact and Relationship Dynamics

  • Steve's primary struggle was emotional rather than financial - he was more upset about losing the friendship than any monetary losses from the business
  • A pattern of one-sided relationship maintenance emerged - Steve recognized he had always been the one initiating contact in the friendship
  • Steve expressed uncertainty about whether he should attempt to reconnect with his former friend and business partner

Psychological Roots and Childhood Influences

  • The conversation revealed deep psychological motivations rooted in Steve's childhood experiences:
- Raised by a single mother with an absentee father - His father was potentially threatening and unreliable - At age 8, Steve became the "man of the family," taking on adult responsibilities

  • These early experiences shaped Steve's adult relationship patterns:
- He developed a tendency to try to "rescue" or support his friend - His wife observed that he was actively trying to keep his friend close - This behavior likely recreated familiar family dynamics from childhood

Key Psychological Revelation

  • The host helped Steve understand a crucial insight about his behavior:
- Rather than simply having a friend "riding his coattails" - Steve had actively created "coattails" specifically to keep the friend close - This pattern stemmed from his childhood fear of losing important relationships, similar to losing his father

  • Steve's motivation was rooted in preventing abandonment - he sought to maintain close relationships by making himself indispensable

Resolution and New Understanding

  • Steve gained significant new perspective on his relationship dynamics through the conversation
  • He expressed some potential regret about past business and personal decisions now that he understood his underlying motivations
  • The call provided Steve with clarity about complex emotional patterns that had been driving his behavior in relationships, particularly his need to maintain closeness through creating dependency
