Diddy Pardon? Tapper’s Book BOMBS & Woke Culture BACKFIRES w/ Stephen A. Smith | PBD Podcast | 594

Key Takeaways

  • Media credibility crisis: Traditional news outlets are experiencing significant viewership drops and declining trust, with CNN's Jake Tapper exemplifying broader struggles through poor book sales and 25% ratings decline, reflecting audience skepticism post-election.
  • Political realignment on economics: Democrats have lost their historical 23-point advantage among middle-class voters, now tied with Republicans, signaling a major shift in how economic issues influence voting patterns and party loyalty.
  • Sports as cultural battleground: The WNBA's transformation through Caitlin Clark's impact demonstrates how individual talent can reshape entire leagues, while also highlighting tensions around recognition, fairness, and evolving social dynamics in professional sports.
  • Centrist positioning gains relevance: Smith's independent, nationalist-first approach reflects growing appetite for political voices that prioritize economic stability, border security, and public safety while avoiding extreme partisan positions.
  • Geopolitical complexity intensifies: The Ukraine conflict represents a broader struggle between Putin's ambition to restore Soviet influence and Western resistance, with potential for significant escalation as military readiness increases globally.

Deep Dive

Opening and Show Announcements

The episode begins with Stephen A. Smith announcing his return to Sirius XM Radio, featuring a daily sports show from 1-3 p.m. on Mad Dog Sports Radio and a political show on the POTUS channel, with his contract running through August 2028. The hosts outline potential discussion topics spanning international politics (Ukraine conflict, NATO discussions), sports entertainment (Zion Williamson lawsuit, Deion Sanders, Caitlin Clark), and media stories including Jake Tapper's recent book performance.

Media Credibility and Performance Analysis

The conversation shifts to examining Jake Tapper's struggling book sales and CNN performance metrics:

  • Book Performance: Tapper's Biden book sold only 57,000 units in its first week, compared to Bob Woodward's 1.1 million copies, despite Tapper claiming it wasn't a "money grab"
  • Show Ratings: "The Lead" experienced a 25% viewership drop, averaging 525,000 total viewers—the lowest since 2015—with only 11% cable news audience share, significantly behind Fox (68%) and MSNBC (21%)
Smith suggests this reflects broader damage to media credibility since Trump's election, with viewers becoming increasingly skeptical of mainstream media narratives. He argues that news networks failed to "deliver the goods" to their audience, leading to sustained viewership drops months after the election.

Personal Political Philosophy and Positioning

Smith elaborates on his political stance and approach to journalism:

  • Voting Record: Didn't vote for Trump, voted for Kamala Harris, but maintains personal relationship with Trump from past interactions
  • Core Beliefs: Supports strong national defense, economy, and bipartisan cooperation; criticized Trump's divisive behavior but never called him racist
  • Professional Ethics: Emphasizes separating personal feelings from professional reporting, maintaining objectivity while being true to personal convictions
He describes himself as a centrist and registered independent who would have considered candidates like Nikki Haley or Chris Christie, preferring a third-party option that avoids extreme political positions.

Economic Perspective and Middle-Class Politics

The discussion turns to significant shifts in political perception regarding economic issues:

  • Polling Data: Recent CNN polling shows Democrats losing their historical advantage among middle-class voters, going from a 23-point advantage in 1989 to being tied with Republicans in recent polls
  • Personal Background: Smith shares his economic journey from growing poor in Queens to buying his first home in 2001, using this experience as a lens for understanding broader economic issues
  • Property Ownership Impact: Emphasizes how owning property changes one's perspective on value and community, arguing that economic stability is crucial for societal well-being

Priority Issues and Law Enforcement

Smith outlines his top three priority issues: 1. Economy (top priority) 2. Border security 3. Public safety

On law enforcement, he advocates for strict enforcement with accountability while emphasizing respect for police and proper conduct during interactions. He shares personal strategies for police encounters (rolling down windows, keeping hands visible, being cooperative) while acknowledging issues of police brutality and racial disparities.

Political Transformation and Party Critique

Smith describes his evolving political perspective:

  • Nationalist Positioning: Describes himself as a "nationalist first" but not "America only," advocating for global awareness and having more allies than enemies
  • Democratic Party Criticism: Criticizes internal party dynamics, particularly the perceived unfair treatment of candidates like Bernie Sanders and the left's tendency to block competing candidates
  • Personal Aspirations: While not interested in personally running for office, he believes he has a unique political voice and sees value in a centrist approach
He expresses confidence in his ability to respond to potential attacks from various sectors and views a potential presidential debate as his "Super Bowl" moment, claiming he would thoroughly prepare by mastering every issue.

Social and Cultural Commentary

The conversation addresses various social issues:

  • Civil Rights Evolution: Smith argues that modern social movements have shifted from seeking "acceptance" to demanding "embracement," criticizing binary thinking that labels people as either fully supportive or "anti" a cause
  • Transgender Athletics: Raises concerns about fairness of transgender athletes competing in women's sports, particularly those transitioning and immediately competing at high levels
  • Abortion Rights: Identifies as pro-choice but doesn't consider it a top priority, discussing Planned Parenthood's historical origins and emphasizing personal belief in not dictating women's choices

WNBA and Sports Analysis

A significant portion focuses on women's basketball and the WNBA:

  • Caitlin Clark Impact: Clark, a rookie ranking 4th in MVP voting and compared to Steph Curry for shooting ability, has significantly boosted league popularity and attendance
  • League Transformation: The WNBA, historically struggling financially, is projected to potentially reach $1 billion in revenue this year, largely due to Clark's impact
  • Player Dynamics: Discussion of resentment some WNBA players feel toward Clark's attention, with Smith comparing it to historical sports dynamics where new stars face initial resistance
Specific examples include ticket price drops when Clark was injured (Chicago Sky games from $86 to $25, Washington Mystics from $41 to $14) and criticism of Brittany Griner's complaints about increased fan attention.

NBA Analysis and Coaching Decisions

The NBA discussion covers several key topics:

  • Unexpected Finals: Indiana Pacers vs. OKC represents an opportunity for the league to develop new talent around emerging stars like Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Tyrese Halliburton
  • Tom Thibodeau Firing: The New York Knicks' decision to fire Thibodeau is viewed as potentially unfair, given his four consecutive playoff appearances and consistent team improvement
  • OKC Thunder Analysis: Extensive discussion of Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's potential to become the greatest player in franchise history, with the 2025 team having the highest point differential of all time
Smith compares current players to historical OKC stars, suggesting the 2012 team with Durant, Westbrook, Harden, and Ibaka would still beat the current squad, while praising SGA's unique leadership qualities and consistently calm demeanor.

International Relations and Geopolitical Analysis

The conversation shifts to complex international issues:

  • Ukraine Conflict: Discussion of Ukraine bombing 40-41 Russian bombers, with Senator Blumenthal suggesting Putin is launching major offensives and isn't serious about peace talks
  • UK Military Readiness: Prime Minister Keir Starmer moving military to "warfighting readiness" to deter threats from advanced military forces
  • Business Connections: Smith discusses various business connections in Ukraine, including Kansas Strategic Solutions and Kyivstar telecom company board memberships
The analysis includes concerns about escalating conflict, potential World War III scenarios, and criticism of U.S. involvement as a "military industrial complex" test ground.

Putin's Geopolitical Strategy

Smith provides detailed analysis of Russian ambitions:

  • Historical Context: Putin's primary mission since coming to power has been reversing the Soviet Union's dissolution, aiming to reclaim significant portions of Europe including Ukraine, Poland, and Finland
  • Current Conflict: Russia expected a quick 30-day war but has been fighting for three years, with limited allies (Belarus, North Korea, China with reservations) while Ukraine receives substantial Western support
  • Strategic Implications: The conflict represents a significant geopolitical struggle with long-term implications for global power dynamics

Trump's Influence and Decision-Making

The discussion examines Trump's approach to various issues:

  • Media Influence: High-profile media personalities like Sean Hannity and Bill O'Reilly can significantly impact Trump's thinking and actions
  • Trade Policy: Trump's tariff strategy has increased revenue to $21.7-22 billion per month, viewed as creating "peer pressure" on other countries
  • Personal Relationships: Criticism of Trump's tendency to base decisions on personal treatment and compliments, including his suggestion to potentially pardon Diddy based on their past relationship

Israel-Palestine Complexity

Smith addresses the challenging Middle East situation:

  • Conflicting Narratives: Acknowledges different perspectives from Jewish and Palestinian communities, expressing frustration with the lack of clear, undisputed facts
  • Protest Movements: Discussion of pro-Palestinian protests in American cities and their potential political implications
  • Geopolitical Alliances: Exploration of alleged coordination between Russia, Iran, and China to undermine Western interests

Community Relations and Personal Philosophy

The episode concludes with Smith's reflections on community engagement:

  • Black Community Relations: Expresses genuine pain when criticized by his own community, emphasizing that he critiques individuals to help them succeed, not condemn them
  • Success Philosophy: Advocates for strategic thinking beyond personal feelings, with objectives larger than individual interests
  • Independent Thinking: Emphasizes the importance of having courage to hold independent thoughts without feeling compelled to agree with community collective perspectives
Smith references the "Power versus Force" framework, highlighting that guilt is near the bottom of consciousness levels while enlightenment is at the top, advocating for surrounding oneself with diverse perspectives that challenge and expand thinking.

The conversation ends with warm, collegial remarks between the hosts, referencing future episodes and demonstrating their friendly, familiar relationship built on mutual respect for diverse viewpoints and reasonable decision-making.
