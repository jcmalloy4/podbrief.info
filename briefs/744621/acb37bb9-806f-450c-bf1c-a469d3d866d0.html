Candace Owens SUED, Obama EXPOSED, Hulk Hogan DEAD & Trump In EPSTEIN FILES? | PBD Podcast | Ep. 621

Key Takeaways

  • Candace Owens' defamation lawsuit by the Macrons is viewed as a strategic misstep, poised to uncover potentially critical information during discovery.
  • Allegations of the Obama administration manufacturing intelligence to undermine President Trump are being dismissed as distractions from established facts.
  • The ongoing Epstein investigation continues to reveal new connections and names, sparking debates about accountability and the transparency of legal processes.
  • Recent university settlements highlight growing scrutiny over campus discrimination and the political ramifications of social media narratives.
  • Discussions on economic policies and police interactions underscore a critical need for individual responsibility versus perceived systemic issues.

Deep Dives

Topic 1: The Macron-Owens Defamation Lawsuit

  • The Macrons' defamation lawsuit against Candace Owens regarding allegations about the French First Lady's gender is seen as a strategic misstep, potentially forcing key revelations during the discovery phase.
  • Owens' team was reportedly unaware of the lawsuit until it became public, prompting a defiant response from Owens who challenged the Macrons to provide evidence.
  • The hosts debated the effectiveness of such lawsuits, noting that this action has significantly prolonged the story's lifespan and intensified public interest.

Topic 2: Obama Treason Allegations and Russia Gate

  • Trump's treason allegations against Barack Obama were dismissed by Obama's office as outrageous and a distraction, while asserting Russia's 2016 election interference as fact.
  • Reports from Tulsi Gabbard and declassified intelligence suggest that Obama's national security team allegedly manufactured false intelligence to promote the Russia Gate narrative and undermine President Trump.
  • The panel questioned the lack of accountability for these revelations, despite calls from some for Obama to be held responsible if the allegations of attempting to overthrow the government prove true.

Topic 3: The Epstein Investigation and Unsealed Files

  • A Florida judge denied requests to unseal additional Epstein grand jury transcripts, despite the Justice Department's concession and the administration's desire for public disclosure.
  • Newly unsealed Epstein files reportedly include correspondence from Bill Clinton, and Donald Trump's name has surfaced, though the White House dismissed these claims as 'fake news.'
  • The discussion highlighted frustration with the drawn-out, week-by-week release of information in the Epstein investigation, calling for swift resolution and full accountability for all involved.

Topic 4: University Accountability and Campus Politics

  • Columbia University agreed to pay over $220 million to settle civil rights violations against Jewish students, setting a significant precedent for other Ivy League institutions like Harvard.
  • The settlement includes provisions for independent monitoring and a ban on masked protests, aiming to identify and apprehend individuals involved in disruptive campus activities.
  • Harvard, in contrast, has resisted similar investigations into its visa practices and discrimination claims, prompting criticism and scrutiny from the State Department.

Topic 5: Economic Realities: Minimum Wage and Housing

  • California's $20 minimum wage for fast-food workers reportedly led to 20,000 job losses, accelerating automation and kiosks as businesses seek cost-effective solutions.
  • Home prices hit record highs in June, reaching a median of $435,000, but sales declined due to high prices, mortgage rates, and increased property taxes and insurance.
  • The panel debated whether homeownership is an 'overrated investment' for young, career-focused individuals, suggesting renting offers greater financial flexibility and lower overhead.

Topic 6: Police Encounters and Personal Responsibility

  • A viral video of a police officer punching a driver was analyzed for its deceptive social media portrayal, with the sheriff clarifying the driver's non-compliance and subsequent guilty plea to resisting arrest.
  • The discussion emphasized the importance of complying with law enforcement during traffic stops to avoid escalation, contrasting this with the driver's behavior which led to the confrontation.
  • The conversation expanded to critique a prevalent 'victim mentality,' stressing the importance of personal responsibility and the negative impact of constantly seeking sympathy or blaming others.
