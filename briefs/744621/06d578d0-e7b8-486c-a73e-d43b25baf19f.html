Bongino RESIGNS, Reiner Murders SHOCKING Updates + Shapiro SLAMS Tucker, Candace & Fuentes | PBD 703

Key Takeaways

  • Dan Bongino's FBI resignation and future media role sparked questions about his credibility.
  • Ben Shapiro publicly criticized specific conservative figures for spreading misinformation and platforming controversial guests.
  • The Nick Reiner murder case unveiled unsettling details about the accused's behavior before the killings.
  • A DNC-sponsored poll showing AOC leading J.D. Vance in 2028 ignited debate on conservative unity and strategy.
  • Netflix secured a multi-year deal with Barstool Sports for exclusive video podcast content, reflecting industry shifts.
  • Discussions highlighted declining US consumer sentiment and a 31% drop in dollar purchasing power.

Deep Dive

  • Miriam Adelson pledged $250 million at a White House Hanukkah Party for a potential third Trump term.
  • The pledge was clarified as sarcastic and humorous, intended as a jab at media interpretation.
  • The comment referenced a previous interaction with Donald Trump in the Israeli parliament.

  • Dan Bongino resigned from his FBI deputy director role, with a rumored replacement already in place.
  • Speculation suggests Bongino's passion may have been stifled, or he encountered unexpected realities within the FBI.
  • Discussion arose about his credibility as a "truth teller" and his potential return to hosting a show on Rumble.

  • The host suggested a "cutthroat mentality" is necessary for a leader to navigate current political challenges.
  • This leadership style was contrasted with figures like Mike Huckabee, perceived as not "cutthroat" enough.
  • Donald Trump was defended against accusations of being a Russian asset or selfish, praised for effectiveness.

  • Ben Shapiro directly called out Tucker Carlson, Candace Owens, and Nick Fuentes regarding guest platforms and information dissemination.
  • Shapiro emphasized the importance of truth, criticizing the spread of misinformation by individuals like Fuentes and Andrew Tate.
  • Tucker Carlson expressed amusement, framing calls for deplatforming as a "cultural revolution."

  • The discussion explored the need for a "synergist" within the conservative movement.
  • Participants debated whether Tucker Carlson or Ben Shapiro could fulfill this unifying role.
  • The consensus was that neither figure currently serves as the movement's synergist.

  • A Newsweek Verisight poll indicated Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez leads J.D. Vance in a hypothetical 2028 presidential matchup.
  • The poll was identified as DNC-sponsored, leading to discussions about the need for conservatives to fund their own polls.
  • AOC confidently stated she would "stomp" Vance, fueling speculation she knew of the poll results.

  • Critiques highlighted a lack of unity and overconfidence within the Republican Party, potentially leading to future losses.
  • Speakers emphasized the need for the party to unite and avoid complacency ahead of midterms and the 2028 election.
  • Underestimating opponents and internal divisions were identified as detrimental to the party's success.

  • The University of Michigan's Consumer Sentiment Survey reached an all-time low.
  • The US dollar's purchasing power decreased by 31% since the current administration's inauguration due to inflation.
  • Money printing and immigration policies under the Biden administration were cited as factors impacting the economy.

  • Nick Reiner made his first court appearance, facing murder charges for his parents' killing.
  • He waived his right to a speedy arraignment, with his next court date set for January 7, 2026.
  • Resurfacing videos related to the case were noted during the broadcast.

  • Nick Reiner allegedly approached Bill Hader and others at a Conan O'Brien party, asking confrontational questions hours before his parents' murder.
  • Reports indicated Nick Reiner had been diagnosed with schizophrenia and struggled with addiction, reportedly attending rehab 18 times by age 22.
  • Rob Reiner previously stated he was not worried about his son negatively impacting his career, attributing addiction to pain.

  • Netflix announced a multi-year partnership with Barstool Sports for exclusive video versions of three podcasts, including 'Pardon My Take,' starting in early 2026.
  • This deal follows a similar agreement with Spotify and marks Netflix's entry into the video podcast market.
  • The move reflects the trend of 71% of podcast viewers watching video content, with estimates for the Barstool deal suggesting a nine-figure sum over three years.

  • Special guest "Santa Corky" discussed offering personalized video messages and calls for children.
  • These services are facilitated through a platform called Manect, with contact details and a QR code provided.
  • The conversation included various children's Christmas wishes, such as a 3D printer, dinosaur Legos, and stocks.
