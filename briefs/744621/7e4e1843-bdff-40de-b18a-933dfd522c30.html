Political News and Current Events

* President Trump had a two-hour call with Putin.

* James Comey posted a controversial Instagram photo showing shells arranged to spell "86-47": * The post sparked significant backlash as "86" is slang for removing/eliminating something and "47" could reference Trump as the 47th president * Comey later removed the post and apologized, claiming he didn't realize the potential violent connotation * Trump responded strongly, suggesting Comey knew exactly what the numbers implied and accused him of potentially calling for presidential assassination * The podcast participants were skeptical of Comey's explanation that the shell arrangement was coincidental: * Tom believed Comey likely arranged the shells himself and was lying * Adam suggested it was a deliberate marketing tactic to promote Comey's upcoming book * The hosts referenced a past incident involving Comey hiding behind a curtain in the White House during Trump's first week in office

* President Biden's health issues were extensively discussed: * Biden was diagnosed with aggressive prostate cancer that has metastasized to bone * Medical experts suggested the cancer likely existed for several years, possibly present during his presidency since 2021 * The hosts discussed Biden's broader health history, noting he's the oldest president in history and has had previous skin cancer, a broken foot, COVID-19 twice, and two prior brain aneurysms * Questions were raised about how such aggressive cancer went undetected given Biden's access to medical care * The hosts recommended PSA testing starting in early 40s and tracking PSA score changes over time * Trump offered a sympathetic public response to Biden's diagnosis, described as a mature, traditional response

* A clip was played of Iranian leader Khamenei making inflammatory remarks, including chants of "Death to America," "Death to Israel," and "Death to the infidels" * The discussion centered on diplomatic approaches to Iran, contrasting integration versus refusal to negotiate * Iran was described as a primary state sponsor of terror in the Middle East, funding groups like Houthis, Hamas, Hezbollah, and Islamic Jihad * Policy options discussed included preventing Iran from obtaining nuclear weapons and considering regime change * The hosts emphasized Iran's uranium enrichment program and potential nuclear capabilities as significant security threats

* The Supreme Court ruled 8-1 to allow Trump to end Temporary Protected Status for 320,000 Venezuelans.

* AOC's district was reported to have a 70% crime surge.

* Bernie Sanders criticized the Democratic Party.

* China's drone technology (UAV) was discussed: * A new Chinese unmanned aerial vehicle with a 15,000 ft ceiling, 4,000 km range, and capability to carry over 100 small drones or 1,000 kg of missiles * Expected to undertake its first mission by June * Raised concerns about U.S. air superiority and future warfare shifting toward unmanned, remote-controlled craft designed to disrupt infrastructure * Discussion of U.S. military spending ($1 trillion annually, exceeding the next 10-15 countries combined) and preparedness for technological threats
