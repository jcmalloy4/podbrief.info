ICE Shooting & Protests, Trump Homebuying BAN + Iran's Regime On The Run? w/ Rob Schneider | PBD Podcast | Ep. 715

Key Takeaways

  • Political comedy faces criticism for prioritizing anger over humor, as exemplified by Jimmy Kimmel's recent material.
  • The Minneapolis ICE shooting sparked debate over officer self-defense, public accountability, and media narrative framing.
  • Donald Trump's proposed policies include banning corporate homebuying and pursuing the strategic acquisition of Greenland.
  • Iran's regime faces internal unrest and external pressure, with figures like Reza Pahlavi seeking U.S. and European support.
  • California's budget shifted from a $100 billion surplus to an $18 billion deficit, raising concerns about its fiscal future.

Deep Dive

  • Rob Schneider criticized late-night hosts like Jimmy Kimmel for presenting politically motivated propaganda rather than comedy.
  • A segment from 'Jimmy Kimmel Live' suggested Trump's actions on Venezuela were a distraction from the Epstein files.
  • Conan O'Brien noted that political comedy has become solely angry, prioritizing 'F Trump' slogans over actual humor for nearly a decade.

  • A woman drove over an ICE agent, who then fired a weapon, leading to the woman's death.
  • The incident involved an ICE officer who was injured and assaulted by an illegal immigrant accused of sexually assaulting a minor.
  • The act was identified as a federal crime under 18 USC 111, interfering with federal officers.
  • CNN's headline, 'Outrage after ICE officer kills U.S. citizen in Minneapolis,' was cited as an example of media framing.

  • Donald Trump proposed potentially purchasing Greenland for $3.3 billion, drawing parallels to the Alaska purchase.
  • The U.S. aims to establish a strategic presence similar to Puerto Rico or Guam, with military installations.
  • Greenland is seen as crucial for NATO to observe Russian submarine activity and secure access to rare earth minerals.
  • The move is intended to counter Russian and Chinese influence in the Arctic, preventing China from exploiting critical resources.

  • Supreme Leader Khamenei tweeted claims of U.S. miscalculation, while internet blackouts and over 50 deaths were reported in Iran.
  • Reza Pahlavi, former Crown Prince, was scheduled for a potential meeting with Donald Trump at Mar-a-Lago, later clarified by Trump.
  • Pahlavi publicly expressed gratitude to Trump for promising accountability and urged decisive action from European leaders.
  • Kalshi data predicted Khamenei's potential fall by 2027, with Pahlavi not listed among top candidates for future leadership.

  • The Iranian regime is allegedly using Tucker Carlson's interviews as propaganda on its state-run news channels.
  • The panel debated whether Carlson's interview with the Iranian president presented the regime favorably or was a missed opportunity for critical questioning.
  • Concerns were raised about Carlson's perceived shift in public commentary and potential anti-American sentiments.
  • Some guests compared Carlson's interview to Candace Owens's perceived shift to a 'destructive force'.

  • California Governor Gavin Newsom faces an $18 billion budget deficit, a stark contrast to a previous $100 billion surplus.
  • Critics argue Newsom's policies have driven up the cost of living and pushed opportunities out of the state.
  • The state's revenues were impacted by a declining economy and capital gains taxes.
  • Billionaires like Larry Page and Larry Ellison are reportedly leaving California due to a proposed 5% wealth tax targeting billionaires.

  • Donald Trump proposed banning institutional investors from purchasing single-family homes.
  • Large financial institutions such as BlackRock, State Street, and Vanguard are accused of artificially inflating housing prices.
  • Proposed regulations aim to limit bulk purchases and speculation by removing tax credits for such acquisitions.
  • The policy seeks to restore housing affordability and the 'American Dream' for younger generations.
