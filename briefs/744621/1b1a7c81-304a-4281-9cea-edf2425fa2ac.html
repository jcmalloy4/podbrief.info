Trump vs Mamdani, Pelosi Retires, Epstein & Comey's Deal + Shapiro SLAMS Tucker | PBD Podcast | Ep. 680

Key Takeaways

  • New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's policies and rhetoric have sparked concerns about economic impact and a resident exodus.
  • The conservative media landscape is fractured by Ben Shapiro's criticism of Tucker Carlson for hosting Nick Fuentes.
  • Allegations surfaced regarding a potential deal for Jeffrey Epstein to implicate Donald Trump, involving James Comey's daughter.
  • A government shutdown is impacting air travel, with the FAA cutting 10% of U.S. flights across 40 cities due to unpaid air traffic controllers.
  • Social controversies involving gender identity in public spaces, such as a Gold's Gym locker room incident, are generating debate.
  • Nancy Pelosi's lengthy political career and substantial increase in net worth are highlighted upon her retirement.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump criticized new NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani's 'angry' speech, warning that communism historically fails.
  • The panel noted a lack of unified Democratic support for Mamdani's policies, suggesting Trump's measured response was strategic.
  • Anthony Scaramucci drew parallels to NYC's financial struggles in the late 1960s after a mayor implemented expensive free services.
  • A host predicted NYC's decline due to socialist ideologies gaining traction among younger generations, amplified by movements like Occupy Wall Street.

  • Mayor Mamdani announced plans to increase fines for landlord violations and, if non-compliant, have the city make repairs and potentially take over buildings.
  • The hosts likened Mamdani's plan to 'seizure of the means of production,' noting citizens voted for these policies.
  • Mamdani faces practical challenges, needing Albany's approval for policies like tax increases on the wealthy and corporations, which Governor Hochul is unlikely to grant.

  • Dave Portnoy threatened to move Barstool Sports out of New York City following Mayor Mamdani's victory.
  • A consultant reported a surge in clients seeking to relocate children to private schools outside NYC due to concerns about anti-Semitism and Mamdani's views on Israel.
  • New Yorkers' long-standing trend of relocating to other states, particularly Florida, was noted, accelerated by COVID-19 and compared to California's exodus to Texas.

  • Hosts critiqued the idea that government can run businesses better than the free market, illustrating with a scenario of a landlord forced into unaffordable improvements.
  • They distinguished between intentional government policies for worker control and unintended consequences that drive away talented individuals and harm businesses.
  • Proposed tax increases, like 2% on high earners and 4% corporate tax, were argued to incentivize companies to move jobs away from the state.

  • Ben Shapiro criticized Tucker Carlson for allegedly normalizing Nazism by hosting white nationalist Nick Fuentes, citing fragmentation of the political right.
  • A speaker expressed trusting Nick Fuentes more than Tucker Carlson, whom they considered an 'actor and a faker.'
  • Shapiro's criticism was suggested to protect the conservative movement from internal fractures, focusing on the far-left as the primary adversary before midterms.

  • Hosts questioned if a 'blue wave' is imminent in 2025 due to Republican disunity, mentioning figures like Nick Fuentes, Ben Shapiro, and Tucker Carlson.
  • Concerns were raised that infighting could benefit Democrats and Gavin Newsom in 2028, contrasting splintering factions with perceived unity on the left.
  • The panel critiqued the Republican Party's strategy, noting recent losses and the reliance on figures like President Trump for endorsements.

  • A cellmate's claim alleges prosecutors offered Jeffrey Epstein a deal: freedom if he implicated then-President Trump.
  • James Comey's daughter, Maureen Comey, was mentioned as a prosecutor involved in the Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell cases.
  • Participants questioned Maureen Comey's potential bias, speculating if anti-Trump individuals exploited legal processes to target him.

  • The FAA announced a 10% reduction in flights at 40 major cities, attributed to air traffic controllers not receiving paychecks since October 28th.
  • This situation could escalate to a 50% flight reduction if the government shutdown continues, exacerbating a 2,000-controller shortage.
  • A poll indicated 25% of respondents had flights affected by the longest government shutdown on record, impacting average citizens.

  • Stephen Colbert's claim of being more conservative than perceived was debated, with panel members questioning his sincerity given his criticism of Donald Trump.
  • CNN anchor Kaitlan Collins pushed back on liberal critics, stating her job is not to take down Trump, possibly due to profitability concerns.
  • Broader media trends suggest a move towards neutrality and objectivity, driven by financial pressures and audience considerations, affecting shows like Colbert's.

  • A video showed an individual identified as Grant Freeman, now Alexis Black, in a women's locker room at a Gold's Gym in California.
  • Freeman reportedly has a criminal record, including drug offenses and a domestic violence conviction for breaking an ex-wife's jaw.
  • The gym's locker room access policy, based on gender identity, was criticized for prioritizing identity over safety, leading to frustration.

  • McDonald's reported rising U.S. sales but falling profits, with a 2.4% increase in same-store sales driven by higher average checks.
  • Traffic from lower-income customers declined nearly double-digits for two years, attributed to increased prices and 'Biden inflation.'
  • A poll indicated the audience is dining out less than pre-COVID 2019 levels, with a Big Mac meal price increasing from $2.45 in 1999 to $8.39 in 2023.

  • Nancy Pelosi announced her retirement from Congress, marking the end of a career spanning 38 years.
  • Her net worth reportedly increased from $165,000 in 1987 to $280 million, while her district, San Francisco, is described as 'disgusting' with visible homelessness.
  • While acknowledged for her historic role as the first female Speaker, her policies and perceived divisiveness were critiqued, though some respected her defiance in visiting Taiwan.
