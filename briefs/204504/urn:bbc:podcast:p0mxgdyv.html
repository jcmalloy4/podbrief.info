Key Takeaways

  • EU and India finalized a major trade deal expected to boost diverse economic sectors for 2 billion people.
  • Iran is experiencing violent protest crackdowns, internet blackouts, and severe daily economic challenges.
  • Spain's government plans to grant legal status to 500,000 undocumented migrants to integrate them into the formal economy.
  • Tensions escalated in Minneapolis with federal agents confronting protesters amidst political fallout.
  • A UK study reveals menopause can cause grey matter loss in the brain, impacting memory and emotional regulation.
  • A landmark lawsuit in California alleges social media algorithms "ruined" a teenager's mental health.

Deep Dive

  • India and the European Union finalized a trade deal covering 2 billion people.
  • European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called it the 'mother of all deals.'
  • Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi stated it is India's largest ever free trade agreement.
  • The deal includes tariff cuts and a security partnership.

  • An Iranian human rights group confirmed nearly 6,000 protester deaths amid a violent crackdown.
  • Internet monitoring platform NetBlocks reported daily economic damage of $34 million due to outages.
  • Businesses in Iran struggle with high prices, raw material shortages, and a depreciating currency.
  • Protests reportedly subsided following internet blackouts and violence against demonstrators.

  • Spain plans to grant legal status to approximately 500,000 undocumented migrants.
  • Beneficiaries must have arrived before the current year, resided >5 months, and have no criminal record.
  • The decree will allow beneficiaries to work in any sector and contribute taxes.
  • The policy is also advocated by the junior coalition partner, Podemos, for more open immigration.

  • President Trump deployed border czar Tom Homan to Minneapolis following killings of two U.S. citizens.
  • Republican Chris Meddell withdrew from the state governor's race, citing ICE operations as an "unmitigated disaster."
  • Protesters and federal agents clashed, with agents using irritants to push back crowds.
  • Tensions escalated outside a hotel where Border Patrol Chief Greg Bovino was staying.

  • A UK study revealed menopause can cause a loss of grey matter in the brain, similar to changes in Alzheimer's disease.
  • This brain matter loss impacts memory and emotional regulation.
  • Researchers identified decreased grey matter volume in the anterior cingulate.
  • Lifestyle factors like exercise and learning new things can improve well-being during menopause.

  • A 19-year-old woman is suing Meta and YouTube in California, alleging their algorithms "ruined" her mental health.
  • This marks the first such lawsuit globally to proceed to trial.
  • The plaintiff claims content addiction led to severe harm.
  • Social media companies deny responsibility, while the plaintiff's lawyer expects a jury to find substantial harm.
