Episode

Key Takeaways

  • Russia insists on taking Ukraine's Donbass region by force, coinciding with President Putin's visit to India.
  • The New York Times is suing the Pentagon over new reporting rules restricting journalist access to information.
  • A new study projects a rise in preventable child deaths by 2025, reversing a century of progress, due to aid cuts.
  • US immigration operations target undocumented immigrants, sparking backlash and criticism against President Trump's rhetoric.
  • Meta began removing accounts of under-16s from its Australian platforms ahead of a national social media ban for children.
  • Myanmar has emerged as a major hub for online scams, with criminal groups exploiting the ongoing civil war.

Deep Dive

  • President Putin commenced a two-day visit to India, his first since the 2022 Ukraine invasion.
  • Russia stated its intent to control Ukraine's Donbass region by force if negotiations fail, urging Europe to cease hindering peace.
  • India navigates its role as a major buyer of Russian oil amid US pressure to end trade, facing existing US tariffs.
  • Russia views India as a vital market and partner, with oil purchases acting as an economic lifeline to counter Western isolation efforts.

  • The New York Times is suing the US Department of Defense over new reporting rules implemented in October.
  • These rules require Pentagon reporters to sign a pledge to only disseminate information authorized by officials.
  • The lawsuit alleges these policies violate the First Amendment and hinder lawful news gathering.
  • The Times seeks to stop the enforcement of these policies, arguing they restrict press access to the Pentagon.

  • President Trump criticized the Somali community in Minnesota, labeling them 'garbage' and alleging they 'destroyed' the state.
  • His remarks coincided with reports of planned immigration enforcement and an investigation into alleged tax dollar diversion to al-Shabaab.
  • Somalia's Prime Minister stated such comments insult many countries.
  • Minnesota state senator Omar Fateh criticized Trump's comments as hurtful and untrue, describing ICE agents' operations in Twin Cities.

  • A new report from the Gates Foundation projects a rise in child deaths before age five, reaching 4.8 million in 2025.
  • This reverses decades of decline and is attributed to significant cuts in international aid from wealthy nations.
  • Mark Suzman, CEO of the Gates Foundation, described the situation as a 'temporary setback'.
  • Aid cuts have reduced funding for health programs and led to job losses in countries like Bangladesh.

  • Myanmar has become a major hub for online scams, with criminals exploiting the ongoing civil war to establish scam centers.
  • The KNU, a key insurgent group, has taken control of two such centers, Shunda and Baoli, near the Thai border.
  • This action led to the displacement of residents and artillery exchanges with the Myanmar military.

  • Meta began removing accounts of users under 16 from its Australian platforms.
  • This anticipates a new Australian law banning social media for children, effective next Wednesday.
  • Companies face fines of up to 50 million Australian dollars if they do not comply with the new regulation.
  • Teenagers expressed mixed feelings, some concerned about social isolation, others grateful for the break.
