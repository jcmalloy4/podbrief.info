Deep Dive

  • The US Justice Department released over 3 million additional documents related to Jeffrey Epstein.
  • These documents include references to public figures such as Bill Gates and Donald Trump, though individuals and the Justice Department deny wrongdoing.
  • Survivors' groups and lawmakers criticized the release as incomplete, demanding full transparency.

  • Mexico's President Claudia Sheinbaum warned US tariffs on oil exports to Cuba could cause a humanitarian crisis.
  • Cuba faces its worst economic hardship since the Cold War, exacerbated by a decrease in Venezuelan oil from 100,000 to approximately 35,000 barrels daily.
  • Mexico is now Cuba's largest oil supplier at around 25,000 barrels per day.
  • The situation impacts hospitals, schools, and businesses, with food spoilage and reserves potentially dwindling in two weeks due to tariff threats.

  • Iran's Foreign Minister stated the country is open to discussing its nuclear program with the US, but insists on equal footing without threats.
  • Reports indicate tens of thousands killed and injured in Iran, with thousands detained and subjected to torture, despite an internet shutdown.
  • Unarmed protesters, including women and children, have been targeted with live fire, aiming to quell dissent and create terror.
  • A shift in public mood in Iran shows increased favor for foreign intervention to bring down the regime, provided civilians are unharmed.

  • Protesters in Minneapolis demanded federal immigration agents leave the city following the recent deaths of two individuals during ICE encounters.
  • Police used tear gas against demonstrators, while White House border czar Tom Homan stated operations would continue.
  • Richard Carlbum of the Minnesota DFL criticized ICE for alleged rights violations and aggressive actions, including tear gas near a preschool.
  • Native Americans report being disproportionately targeted and harassed by ICE agents, despite being U.S. citizens and presenting federally recognized tribal identification.

  • A UK court awarded Saudi human rights activist Ghanem al-Masarir nearly $4 million (£3 million) in damages.
  • The ruling found the Saudi royal family used Pegasus spyware to hack al-Masarir's phones and organized an assault on him in London.
  • Al-Masarir, a popular YouTuber, described his phones being infected with Pegasus spyware in 2018, stating the ordeal ruined his life.
  • Saudi Arabia initially claimed sovereign immunity but lost its case in 2022; the activist intends to pursue enforcement measures if damages are not paid.

  • Season four of the Netflix drama 'Bridgerton' is topping streaming charts in over 80 countries.
  • The current season focuses on Benedict Bridgerton's search for a spouse, exploring themes of commitment and societal expectations.
  • Actors Luke Thompson and Yerin Ha worked with an intimacy coordinator, emphasizing sex scenes as an extension of character expression.
  • Based on eight best-selling books by Julia Quinn, the series features historical characters with modern aspirations; its final four episodes will be released in late February.
