Episode

Key Takeaways

  • Iran faces significant protests with approximately 50 reported deaths and widespread internet blackouts.
  • The Iranian government condemned protesters as foreign-backed, while President Trump warned of potential US military strikes.
  • A federal agent fatally shot Renee Goode in Minneapolis, sparking protests and conflicting accounts of self-defense.
  • Iranians abroad express fear for loved ones, struggling with communication due to ongoing internet disruptions.
  • Mentalist Oz Perlman demonstrated skills based on psychology and observation rather than supernatural abilities.

Deep Dive

  • Rights groups report approximately 50 protester deaths since the uprising began.
  • Authorities imposed an internet blackout to curb communication and control information flow.
  • New footage shows large crowds protesting amidst an economic downturn that has escalated into a challenge for the government.

  • Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei condemned protesters as "vandals" and "mercenaries serving foreign powers."
  • US President Donald Trump reiterated warnings of military strikes if Iranian protesters are harmed, a stance reportedly concerning Tehran.
  • Reza Pahlavi, son of the former Shah, calls for Iranians inside and outside the country to unite and challenge the current regime, though he is a divisive figure within the opposition.

  • US immigration agent Jonathan Ross filmed the fatal shooting of Renee Goode in Minneapolis, claiming self-defense in a video shared by US Vice President J.D. Vance.
  • Local officials maintain Goode posed no danger, contradicting the self-defense assertion.
  • Protests erupted in Minneapolis following Goode's death, with Donald Trump blaming 'professional troublemakers' and vowing to protect law enforcement.

  • Internet blackouts in Iran severely disrupt communication, causing fear and anxiety for Iranians abroad.
  • Roya Pirai, who left Iran after her mother was killed in 2022 protests, shared her grief and decision to leave the country.
  • Marsa Ali Mandani, living in the UK, has been unable to contact her family in Iran since Thursday due to these internet disruptions.

  • Mentalist Oz Perlman, known for accurately guessing Joe Rogan's bank PIN, attributes his abilities to psychology and observation, not supernatural powers.
  • Perlman demonstrated his skills by correctly identifying the name 'Ellen' written by a producer during a phone game.
  • His approach involves techniques for reading people and building confidence through psychological insights.
