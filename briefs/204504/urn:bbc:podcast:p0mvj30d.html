Episode

Key Takeaways

  • A former Trump White House official's candid comments about the administration have sparked controversy and pushback.
  • The United States has significantly escalated its economic and military pressure on Venezuela.
  • New research identifies specific midlife cognitive and emotional symptoms linked to increased dementia risk.
  • Sydney continues to process a mass casualty attack, with community mourning and political fallout.
  • A racial controversy involving Miss Finland has led to the removal of her crown and international criticism.
  • Singer Lizzo achieved a partial legal victory, though a sexual harassment lawsuit against her will proceed.

Deep Dive

  • Susie Wiles, former White House chief of staff, reportedly described President Trump's 'alcoholics personality' and called his Vice President a conspiracy theorist.
  • The Vanity Fair article, based on 11 interviews, was accused by Wiles of being a 'hit piece' and intentionally presenting a chaotic administration.
  • President Trump and his cabinet publicly supported Wiles in a New York Post interview, countering Vanity Fair's narrative.
  • Wiles' comments, though not denied, indicate potential internal splits within the Trump White House being challenged by the administration.

  • The U.S. escalated actions against Venezuelan President Nicolas Maduro, announcing a blockade of sanctioned oil tankers.
  • Maduro's government was designated a foreign terrorist organization, following previous actions including alleged drug busts killing at least 90 people.
  • Venezuela condemned the U.S. blockade and designation as an attempt to steal its oil wealth, despite possessing some of the world's largest oil reserves.
  • President Trump used strong rhetoric about the 'largest armada' in South American history for blockade implementation.
  • Questions are being raised regarding the legality of Trump's authority to impose such a blockade and designate Venezuela a terrorist state.

  • Research from University College London suggests that losing confidence or feeling overwhelmed in middle age increases dementia risk.
  • Six specific symptoms were identified two decades before diagnosis as being linked to a higher risk.
  • The study found that general midlife depression symptoms, such as low mood, are not linked to later dementia risk.
  • Specific symptoms like loss of confidence and difficulty coping may serve as independent risk factors or early indicators.

  • The first funeral was held for Rabbi Eli Schlanger, one of 15 victims of the recent attack at Bondi Beach, Sydney.
  • Political fallout emerged, with criticism directed at the government's handling of anti-Semitism.
  • The suspected gunman, whose father was killed by police, is reportedly awake and expected to be questioned by authorities.
  • New Year's Eve events at Bondi Beach have been canceled following the attack.

  • Miss Finland Sara Jafce faced a racism scandal after posting a racially insensitive gesture on social media.
  • The incident led to the removal of her crown and sparked political controversy involving right-wing populist party members.
  • The controversy created a public relations crisis abroad, with media in Asia covering the story and negative comments for the national airline.

  • Singer Lizzo won a legal victory as a judge dismissed fat-shaming claims from three former dancers.
  • However, a lawsuit alleging sexual harassment will proceed against the artist.
  • The dancers claimed they were forced to interact with exotic dancers at a strip club in Amsterdam, which they found inappropriate.
  • Discussion arose around Lizzo's public image regarding body positivity and recent weight loss, drawing criticism from some in the body-positive community.
