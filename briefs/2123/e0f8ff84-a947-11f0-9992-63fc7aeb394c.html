Decoder with Nilay Patel

Announcing an ad-free Decoder feed for Verge subscribers

Key Takeaways

  • Paid Verge subscribers can now access podcasts ad-free.
  • Non-subscribers can subscribe for ad-free content and other exclusive benefits.

Deep Dive

  • The Verge is now offering paid subscribers ad-free listening for its podcasts.
  • This includes popular shows like Decoder, Version History, and The Vergecast.
  • Subscribers must opt-in through their account settings to enable this feature.

  • Non-subscribers can join The Verge by visiting theverge.com/subscribe.
  • A subscription offers ad-free podcasts, exclusive newsletters, and unlimited access to all published content.
