🚨 IMMEDIATE RELEASE 🚨 Rep. Dan Crenshaw Threatens to Sue Shawn Ryan...

Key Takeaways

  • Podcast host Shawn Ryan received a legal demand letter on December 9, 2025, from Congressman Dan Crenshaw's legal team.
  • The letter threatens a defamation lawsuit over podcast comments regarding Crenshaw's wealth and a party.
  • Ryan interpreted an Instagram message from Crenshaw referencing 'my boys at six' as a threat.
  • Shawn Ryan refuses to remove content or apologize, asserting that this is an attempt to silence criticism.
  • Ryan defends his right to question public officials' finances and stock trading, citing transparency concerns.

Deep Dive

  • Podcast host Shawn Ryan announced receipt of a legal demand letter on December 9, 2025, from lawyers representing Congressman Dan Crenshaw.
  • The letter threatens a defamation lawsuit concerning comments made on the podcast and demands content removal and a public apology.
  • Ryan's initial comments questioned Crenshaw's perceived wealth and an expensive party, noting a $174,000 annual congressional salary and referencing media reports on insider trading by members of Congress.

  • Shawn Ryan recounted receiving an Instagram message from Congressman Crenshaw, which mentioned 'my boys at six' told him about the criticism.
  • Ryan interpreted this reference to SEAL Team Six as a threat, particularly in light of Crenshaw's prior public statement about wanting to kill Tucker Carlson.
  • The host published the Instagram message he received from Congressman Crenshaw as evidence.

  • Crenshaw's lawyers claim Shawn Ryan's interpretation of the Instagram message constitutes defamation, demanding an apology and content removal.
  • Ryan stated he refuses these demands and intends to publish both the demand letter and his lawyer's response.
  • He argues that the legal action is an attempt by a congressman to silence criticism through litigation threats.

  • Shawn Ryan clarified that his interpretation of Crenshaw's message referencing SEAL Team Six as threatening is reasonable, irrespective of Crenshaw's intended meaning.
  • He maintains his right to ask questions about a public official's finances, wealth accumulation, and stock trading while in office.
  • Ryan suggested that Crenshaw's resort to legal threats instead of providing answers indicates an avoidance of transparency.
