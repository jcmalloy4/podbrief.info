#265 Lee Strobel - Who is the Real Santa Claus & What Evidence Connects Jesus to Christmas?

Key Takeaways

  • A perceived revival of Christianity is noted, especially among younger generations seeking truth.
  • Investigating doubts about faith is encouraged as a path to a stronger, more resolved belief.
  • New scholarship offers updated insights into the historical context of Jesus' birth and prophecies.
  • The virgin birth is explained through biological and theological perspectives, focusing on divine intervention.
  • Jesus' fulfillment of ancient prophecies, particularly Isaiah 53, is presented as significant evidence.
  • The guest, a former investigative journalist, applies his rigorous methods to Christian apologetics.
  • The historical figure of Saint Nicholas, known for generosity, predates the modern Santa Claus image.
  • Spiritual growth involves receiving God's gift, an indwelling Holy Spirit, and community support.

Deep Dive

  • A survey indicates young people are taking spiritual steps, with increased Bible sales and young men returning to church.
  • Younger generations, feeling misled by AI, media, and politics, are seeking solid truth in faith.
  • A widespread return to church across all age groups prompts new questions and a need for scriptural guidance.
  • The guest encourages investigating faith doubts, likening it to a child sharing a nightmare, to strengthen belief.

  • Isaiah's prophecy of the Virgin Mary, foretold 700 years prior, is discussed, addressing skeptic questions about 'alma' versus 'virgin' translation.
  • Genesis 3:15, concerning the seed of a woman crushing Satan, is presented as an early prophecy of the virgin birth.
  • The biological aspect of the virgin birth is explored, noting Mary's X chromosomes and the divine intervention for Jesus to receive a Y chromosome.
  • The Holy Spirit's intervention ensured Jesus was born without original sin, despite Mary being a sinner.

  • The guest discusses biblical accounts of angels manifesting as males, contrasting with ancient Jewish literature mentioning female manifestations.
  • A personal angelic encounter at age 12, which conveyed that goodness alone does not grant heaven, significantly impacted the guest.
  • Sixteen years later, as an atheist, the guest connected the angel's message with the gospel of grace, realizing its prophetic nature.
  • The societal embarrassment surrounding the supernatural is diminishing, with the guest's book 'Seeing the Supernatural' documenting reality beyond the physical.

  • The significance of Bethlehem as the town of David is highlighted, fulfilling Micah's 500-year-old prophecy of the Messiah's birth.
  • New scholarship supports Luke's account of Mary and Joseph traveling to Bethlehem for an earlier, less documented census under Herod, prior to Quirinius's 6 AD governorship.
  • A Swiss scholar's discovery suggests Quirinius was a procurator at Jesus' birth, aligning with Luke's use of 'governor' and the requirement to return to hometowns.

  • The term 'Magi' referred to astrologers and diviners from the East, not highly regarded by Jewish people.
  • The star guiding the Magi is theorized to be a nova sighted by Chinese astronomers around 5 BC or a Jupiter and Saturn conjunction in 7 BC.
  • The Magi's gifts—gold for royalty, frankincense for priesthood, and myrrh for embalming—theologically foreshadowed Jesus' role and death.
  • The arrival of the Magi, representing pagans and Gentiles, symbolized Jesus' role as a savior for all humankind.

  • Mathematician Peter Stoner calculated the odds of any human fulfilling 48 ancient prophecies as one chance in trillions.
  • Isaiah 53 explicitly describes a suffering Messiah, written centuries before Jesus' birth, leading some Jewish individuals to Christianity.
  • Louis Lepides, a Vietnam veteran, became a Christian pastor after comparing Isaiah 53 with original Hebrew scriptures, confirming its description of Jesus.
  • Prophecies from Isaiah also concern the Messiah's resurrection and conquering death.

  • A common misconception about Jesus' birth involves a mistranslation of the Greek word 'katalima', often rendered as 'inn'.
  • 'Katalima' likely referred to a guest room in a first-century Jewish home, which had living and animal areas with mangers.
  • Mary and Joseph probably stayed in a relative's family living area due to a lack of space in the guest room, with animals potentially present.
  • Turning away a pregnant Jewish woman would have been unthinkable in first-century Jewish culture, supporting the 'guest room' interpretation.

  • The guest, a former investigative journalist trained in law, explains how his journalistic curiosity drives his faith-based investigations.
  • He compares his approach to the host's, using investigative skills to uncover truths regarding Christianity.
  • His writing process involves interviewing leading scholars and transcribing extensive interviews to simplify complex topics.
  • The goal is to make scholarly information accessible, described as 'put[ting] the cookies on the bottom shelf'.

  • The core Christian message is presented: the wages of sin is death, but God's free gift is eternal life through Jesus Christ's perfect life and crucifixion.
  • The 'narrowness' of the path to heaven signifies salvation exclusively through Jesus Christ, not other means or beliefs.
  • Christianity is contrasted with Islam, noting the Quran denies key Christian tenets like Jesus' crucifixion and his divine sonship.
  • The guest recounts sharing historical evidence for Jesus' resurrection with NBA star Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, leading to his girlfriend's conversion.

  • Jesus is discussed as both fully God and fully man, experiencing human temptations and suffering.
  • Hebrews is referenced, stating Jesus learned obedience through suffering, a lesson applicable to believers.
  • The incomprehensible aspects of Jesus' divine experience and sinless existence despite temptation are explored.
  • Philippians chapter 2, an early Christian hymn, describes Jesus' pre-existence, divinity, and voluntary sacrifice.

  • The guest recounts a personal experience of faith after leaving a legal editor career for a church role with a significant pay cut.
  • He describes anonymously sending $500 to a woman in need, which was confirmed by his wife and arrived just in time to prevent her from losing her job and apartment.
  • Such experiences build confidence in God's attentiveness and orchestration of events, enabling individuals to live for God amidst persecution.
  • The guest discusses moments of doubt, questioning if beliefs are genuine or divinely inspired, and methods for testing spiritual guidance.

  • The discussion distinguishes between the folklore of Santa Claus and the historical figure of Saint Nicholas, born in 270 AD in Turkey.
  • Saint Nicholas was a bishop known for his generosity and defense of Christian truth, enduring imprisonment and torture.
  • A story recounts Saint Nicholas anonymously providing dowries for three poor daughters by throwing gold through a window, preventing prostitution and linking to gift-giving traditions.
  • The modern image of Santa Claus, a white-bearded man in a red suit, is credited to Coca-Cola's 1930s print campaign.
