Trump Administration Urges ‘Remigration,’ Borrowing White Nationalist Language

Key Takeaways

  • The Trump administration is prioritizing "remigration," encouraging immigrants to voluntarily leave the U.S.
  • The term "remigration" is linked to European white nationalist movements and their ideologies.
  • Administration policies include pausing applications from specific countries and modifying asylum processes.
  • Official rhetoric frequently employs language associated with white nationalism and "Western values."
  • The U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is shifting towards an enforcement-focused role.

Deep Dive

  • The concept of 'remigration' originates from European white nationalist movements.
  • It aims for the ethnically-driven departure of non-white individuals from traditionally white nations.
  • The term is explicitly linked to the 'Great Replacement' conspiracy theory, popularized by the European Identitarian Movement.

  • The Trump administration implements practical applications like large ad campaigns and modified asylum appointment systems.
  • Policies include monetary incentives for voluntary departure, though effectiveness and numbers are unclear.
  • There has been a sweeping pause on immigration applications from 19 specific countries following a shooting incident.
  • Lawyers report a chilling effect on individuals seeking permanent status, actively pushing immigrants to leave or return.
  • The administration explicitly uses white nationalist-associated terms, including President Trump's 'reverse migration' post and proposed 'Office of Remigration.'

  • U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) documents indicate a preference for immigrants who can "easily assimilate."
  • The criteria for determining an immigrant's ability to assimilate are not explicitly defined by the administration.
  • Immigration lawyers observe a pattern of blocking individuals from poorer, war-torn, or non-majority white countries.
  • Conversely, programs have been created for white individuals, such as white South Africans.

  • The terms "remigration" and "reverse migration" align with the administration's use of language concerning "Western values" and "Western civilization."
  • This includes Vice President Vance's remarks on "civilizational suicide" and White House national security documents referencing "civilizational erasure."
  • This terminology resonates with European white nationalist movements concerned over "reverse colonization" and an "existential urgency."
  • Recent events, like a shooting by an Afghan immigrant, are used by President Trump to denigrate immigrants and tighten policies, mirroring European identitarian tactics.

  • The administration focuses on legal migration avenues, coupled with a willingness to revoke status granted by previous administrations.
  • New roles at USCIS, including Homeland Defenders and Special Agents, are authorized to carry firearms and support border protection and ICE.
  • This represents a significant shift from USCIS's traditional role, potentially creating a policing mindset within the agency.
  • European identitarian leaders are observing U.S. immigration crackdowns as a potential model for their own countries.
