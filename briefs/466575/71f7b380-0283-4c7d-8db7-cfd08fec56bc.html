Roundup: A Trade War With Canada, Inflation Ticks Up, And The Shutdown Persists

Key Takeaways

  • The government shutdown entered its 24th day, causing widespread disruption and economic data delays.
  • President Trump halted trade negotiations with Canada over an advertisement referencing tariffs, calling the ad 'fake'.
  • New sanctions were authorized on two Russian oil companies, signaling a shift in Trump's Russia policy.
  • President Trump's cultivated image of 'winning' influences White House alterations and his governance approach.
  • Trump's intense control over his brand and message impacts his actions and focus on presidential aesthetics.

Deep Dive

  • The government shutdown reached its 24th day, affecting over a million federal workers.
  • Domenico Montanaro reported no signs of resolution, stating the shutdown is on track to be the longest in history.
  • President Trump announced a $130 million private donation from an unnamed individual to cover military shortfalls.

  • President Trump halted trade talks with Canada, reacting to an Ontario advertisement that used Ronald Reagan clips criticizing tariffs.
  • Trump reportedly believed the ad aimed to influence the U.S. Supreme Court on an upcoming tariff case.
  • Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau indicated Canada is prepared to resume negotiations when the U.S. is ready.

  • President Trump made physical alterations to the White House, including a new ballroom and changes to the Oval Office and Rose Garden.
  • These changes are linked to Trump's brand of "winning" and "wealth," with gold accents symbolizing a "golden age" under his presidency.
  • Panelists compared Trump's brand-building to his business dealings, noting his reputation for wealth despite business failures.

  • Trump's approach to controlling his message and brand is described as more intense than other politicians, evidenced by his name on products.
  • He focused on the aesthetics of the presidency, such as mandating architectural styles for federal buildings and replacing tunnel tiles.
  • This focus on image and deference sometimes overshadowed underlying policy issues like homelessness and crime.

  • President Trump reportedly governs as if he has a large mandate, despite not being a broadly popular president.
  • He engaged in efforts such as pressuring states to redraw district lines.
  • His focus included maintaining Republican control of Congress rather than winning over voters broadly.

  • Domenico Montanaro discussed the unexpected arrival of mosquitoes in Iceland, linking it to climate warming.
  • Ashley Lopez detailed a $100 million jewel heist at the Louvre in Paris, carried out by thieves using disguises and an angle grinder.
  • Tamara Keith highlighted Shohei Ohtani's historic baseball performance, achieving 10 strikeouts and three home runs in one game.
