Unpacking The New Jersey And Virginia Races For Governor

Key Takeaways

  • New Jersey's gubernatorial race features Democrat Mikey Sherrill and Republican Jack Ciattarelli, with affordability as a key issue.
  • Sherrill links rising electric costs to green energy, while Ciattarelli is endorsed by former President Donald Trump.
  • Virginia's gubernatorial election involves Republican Winsome Sears and Democrat Abigail Spanberger.
  • Sears prioritizes social and cultural issues like transgender student policies in her campaign.
  • Public polling shows Spanberger leading by over 10 points in Virginia, where the economy is the top voter concern.
  • These gubernatorial races are seen as bellwethers for national midterm elections and presidential popularity.

Deep Dive

  • The New Jersey gubernatorial election features Democratic candidate Congresswoman Mikey Sherrill and Republican Jack Ciattarelli.
  • Ciattarelli has received an endorsement from former President Donald Trump.
  • Voters in New Jersey express concerns about unaffordability and high taxes.
  • The race focuses on voter turnout and contrasting policy visions for the state.

  • Democratic candidate Mikey Sherrill links rising electric bills to New Jersey's green energy investments.
  • Republican Governor Chris Christie previously closed two clean energy plants in the state.
  • The impact of the Trump presidency reportedly resulted in some clean energy projects being canceled or put on hold.
  • The New Jersey race is described as neck and neck, potentially a referendum on Phil Murphy or Donald Trump.

  • Republican Lieutenant Governor Winsome Sears is campaigning on continuing Governor Glenn Young's agenda and social issues.
  • Sears is focusing on policies concerning transgender students in bathrooms and girls' sports.
  • She is trailing in fundraising and polls and does not have an endorsement from former President Trump.
  • This strategy resonates with her base, but the economy is not a strong point for Republicans.

  • Democratic candidate Abigail Spanberger, a moderate and former CIA officer, centers her campaign on affordability issues.
  • Her campaign highlights the impact of federal policies on Virginia's workforce and government contractors.
  • The current federal government shutdown is considered a potential wildcard in the race.
  • Gubernatorial races typically favor the party not holding the White House.

  • Winsome Sears' candidacy generated enthusiasm among supporters at a Moms for Liberty event, highlighting school-related concerns.
  • Sears' campaign used an ad targeting Spanberger's support for LGBTQ+ rights, drawing parallels to a past Trump campaign ad.
  • Public polling indicates Spanberger leads by over 10 points, with the economy, not education or transgender issues, as the top voter concern.
  • Gubernatorial races are often seen as indicators for upcoming midterm elections and presidential popularity.
