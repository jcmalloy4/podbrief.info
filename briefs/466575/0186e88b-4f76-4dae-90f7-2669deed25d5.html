Defiant Trump continues calls for acquiring Greenland

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump expressed a desire to acquire Greenland, citing national security and critical mineral interests.
  • Congressional lawmakers rejected the Greenland acquisition, reaffirming U.S. alliances with Denmark and Greenland.
  • Danish and Greenlandic populations conveyed offense and betrayal regarding the U.S. proposal for acquisition.
  • U.S. public opinion showed significant opposition, with 72% of Americans against acquiring Greenland.
  • Trump's actions risk alienating European allies and potentially undermining long-standing alliances like NATO.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump expressed a desire to acquire Greenland for national security, with officials noting potential critical minerals.
  • During a speech at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, on January 21st, 2026, Trump reiterated his insistence on acquiring Greenland.
  • He stated he would not use military force, a reassurance analysts viewed as insufficient due to his past unpredictable nature.

  • Trump views Greenland as a U.S. territory, influenced by a national security memo suggesting U.S. entitlement to assert control in the Western Hemisphere.
  • His proposal is linked to historical U.S. military presence in Greenland during World War II, bypassing typical foreign policy checks.
  • Canada's Mark Carney observed a global paradigm shift, suggesting middle powers must cooperate due to actions by great powers like the U.S. and China.

  • A U.S. congressional delegation visited Copenhagen to meet with Danish and Greenlandic officials, aiming to reaffirm alliance support.
  • Lawmakers sought to temper tensions from White House statements and fact-check President Trump's acquisition claims.
  • Congress rejected the acquisition, stating no intelligence indicated an imminent threat from Russia or China necessitating such a move.

  • The Economist YouGov polling indicated 72% of Americans oppose acquiring Greenland, with only 9% in favor.
  • Many Danes conveyed a sense of betrayal and sadness, with thousands protesting in Copenhagen to show solidarity with Greenland.
  • Greenlandic individuals expressed deep offense at the concept of land ownership, contrasting with their indigenous population's respect for the land.

  • The U.S. risks alienating European allies, potentially leading them to seek deals with other powers like China if tariff threats persist.
  • This could undermine long-standing alliances like NATO, which is crucial for collective defense against anti-democratic forces.
  • Danish veterans and military families highlighted their sacrifices in conflicts like Afghanistan, underscoring the importance of the U.S.-Danish alliance.
