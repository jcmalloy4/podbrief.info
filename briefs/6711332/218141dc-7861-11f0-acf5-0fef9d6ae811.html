Sen. Eric Schmitt: FBI and DOJ Corruption, and How Politicized Judges Are Undermining America

Key Takeaways

  • The U.S. Senate is "broken," its power ceded to politicized courts.
  • The Biden administration orchestrated widespread censorship with tech companies.
  • Lack of accountability for powerful figures erodes public trust.
  • U.S. reliance on China for vital supplies poses significant national security risks.
  • The Republican Party is transforming under Trump, prioritizing new domestic and foreign policy issues.

Deep Dives

Broken Governance

  • Senator Eric Schmitt contrasts his effective role as Missouri Attorney General with the U.S. Senate's current state of "kabuki theater".
  • Both Carlson and Schmitt agree that governmental power has shifted significantly from the legislative branch to the courts.
  • Congress's consistent abdication of its lawmaking responsibility empowers administrative agencies, necessitating constant legal challenges.

Government Censorship

  • Senator Schmitt details a lawsuit exposing the Biden administration's "vast censorship enterprise" in collaboration with big tech companies.
  • The administration allegedly pressured social media platforms, threatening Section 230 protections to suppress disfavored content.
  • Agencies like the FBI and CDC reportedly provided direct guidelines for censorship, including "pre-bunking" the Hunter Biden laptop story.
  • Despite these alleged illegal government pressures on speech, no accountability for those involved has emerged.

Eroding Trust

  • Carlson questions why figures like Anthony Fauci have faced no consequences for their pandemic-era actions and policy recommendations.
  • Senator Schmitt asserts that law enforcement agencies are weaponized against political opponents, undermining public confidence in the system.
  • Regaining public trust requires tangible accountability for perceived wrongdoing, as inaction further erodes faith in institutions.

China Dependency

  • The U.S. relies on China for a significant 80-90% of its raw materials and finished pharmaceuticals.
  • Senator Schmitt highlights this profound dependency as a critical national security and economic vulnerability for the nation.
  • Missouri's lawsuit against China seeks $24 billion and the ability to seize farmland, alleging a deliberate pandemic cover-up.

Republican Evolution

  • President Trump's influence is transforming the Republican Party, inspiring a new generation of politicians to aggressively fight for their beliefs.
  • The party is shifting towards an "American realism" foreign policy, prioritizing national interests and economic strength over global entanglements.
  • A new Republican coalition champions free speech principles and focuses on addressing working-class concerns.
  • This evolution aims to appeal to ordinary citizens who feel overlooked and to authentically challenge prevailing political narratives.
