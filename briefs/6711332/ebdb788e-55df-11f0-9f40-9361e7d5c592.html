The Tucker Carlson Show

RFK Jr. Provides an Update on His Mission to End Skyrocketing Autism and Declassifying Kennedy Files

Key Takeaways

  • Planned studies aim to re-examine the potential link between vaccines and autism rates.
  • Pharmaceutical companies reportedly influence doctors and media through financial means.
  • The 1986 Vaccine Act provided liability immunity to vaccine manufacturers.
  • Concerns persist regarding the safety data and approval process of COVID-19 vaccines.
  • Historical investigations into political assassinations allegedly reveal suppressed evidence.

Deep Dive

  • The guest suggests that people should not blindly trust experts, stating this is characteristic of totalitarianism, not science or democracy.
  • Plans include implementing "gold standard science" practices like publishing research protocols, raw data, and requiring study replication.
  • It is alleged that the NIH ceased requiring replication and that scientific journals refuse to publish studies critical of vaccines due to pharmaceutical financial pressure.

  • Pharmaceutical companies allegedly pay scientists to validate products, manipulating data for publication in journals and using paid preprints to incentivize doctors.
  • Fifty percent of pediatricians' revenue is reportedly from vaccines, with insurance companies offering bonuses up to tens of thousands of dollars for achieving 95% vaccination rates.
  • The suppression of the autism-vaccine link is attributed to pharmaceutical companies being the largest media revenue source.
  • After 1997, a Supreme Court case granting pharmaceutical advertising First Amendment protection led to an explosion of direct-to-consumer TV ads.
  • It is suggested that many direct-to-consumer drug advertisements, restricted or non-existent outside the US, are paid for by taxpayers via Medicaid/Medicare.

  • The 1986 National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program granted vaccine companies immunity from liability, reportedly contributing to an increased vaccination schedule.
  • American children reportedly now receive between 69 and 92 vaccines for school entry in some states, compared to three vaccines prior to 1986.
  • The Institute of Medicine has reportedly urged the CDC for 25 years to study suspected vaccine injuries, with only 38 of 158 identified in 2013 studied.
  • A 2010 CDC machine-counting system (Lazarus) found the VAERS captured less than 1% of vaccine injuries, while a new system captured over 95%, with a 2.7% injury rate across all vaccines.

  • Analysis of COVID-19 vaccine trial data reportedly showed 23% more all-cause deaths in the vaccinated group than in the placebo group after six months.
  • The guest expressed skepticism within his agency regarding mRNA vaccine safety due to a lack of comprehensive safety studies and numerous anecdotal reports of injuries.
  • A 2002 congressional oversight committee reportedly found 97% of Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) members had undisclosed conflicts of interest.
  • An ACIP member allegedly voted to add a rotavirus vaccine to the schedule, which was later withdrawn, then sold a competitive vaccine for $186 million.
  • Most vaccines approved since 1986 allegedly lacked pre-licensing placebo-controlled safety trials, leading to unknown risk profiles.

  • The guest questioned why former federal employee Anthony Fauci might have needed a "pardon in advance" due to potential liability for "creating the coronavirus."
  • It is alleged that Fauci funded gain-of-function research at the Wuhan lab, providing technology for pathogen development.
  • A Fauci-funded researcher reportedly developed a "seamless ligation" technique to obscure the laboratory origins of manipulated viruses.
  • The discussion suggests economic and professional incentives exist for researchers in this field, and mentions the development of a deadly avian flu virus capable of mammal-to-mammal transmission.

  • The host questions the US's record of revealing truth, referencing a presidential executive order to declassify files related to the murders of RFK's uncle, father, and Martin Luther King.
  • RFK states his conclusions remain unchanged, asserting his uncle's assassination was a conspiracy and his father's murder remains uninvestigated.
  • RFK alleges Sirhan Sirhan fired two shots while his father was shot four times from behind by contact shots, suggesting a security guard named Cesar was the true shooter.
  • It is alleged the LAPD destroyed 2,500 photographs and other evidence, failing to confiscate Cesar's gun, labeling their investigation "malevolent."
  • The guest notes a figure named Cesar's only official employer was the CIA, and expresses confidence that President Trump will release relevant documents.
