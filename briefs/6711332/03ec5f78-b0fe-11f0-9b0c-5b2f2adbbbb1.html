Cheryl Hines: Stories From “Curb Your Enthusiasm” and Sticking by RFK on His Way to the White House

Key Takeaways

  • Cheryl Hines faced a decade of career struggles before achieving acting success in Hollywood.
  • Hollywood is described as a predatory environment, with increasing intolerance towards differing political views.
  • Larry David's on-screen persona is rooted in his real-life intelligent, humorous, and neurotic traits.
  • Bobby Kennedy Jr.'s political views and campaign significantly impacted Cheryl Hines' career and social standing.
  • The denial of Secret Service protection for Bobby Kennedy Jr. raised concerns, especially given his family's history.
  • Cheryl Hines found Donald Trump to be genuine and interested, contrasting with prevailing Hollywood narratives.
  • Discussions around vaccines are perceived by some as rigid doctrine, not open scientific inquiry.
  • Hines experienced physical stress and social ostracism due to her husband's controversial political activities.

Deep Dive

  • Cheryl Hines moved to Los Angeles with two acquaintances, facing challenges such as background acting and difficulty securing bartending jobs.
  • She expressed fear of rejection from talent agents and underwent mental preparation before submitting her headshot and resume.
  • Hines proactively sought an audition for a TV movie about Tanya Harding and Nancy Kerrigan, sending her own headshot with a note.
  • During the audition, she was told she resembled Nancy Kerrigan and improvised a death scene by clutching her chest, simulating a heart attack.
  • It took Hines 10 years to feel successful in the Los Angeles acting industry.

  • Hollywood is characterized as a predatory environment where ambitious individuals are often exploited by those in positions of power.
  • Hines recounted an unsettling encounter with director James Toback approximately 25 years prior, involving strange questions about body hair.
  • During the encounter, Toback requested she remove a boot, prompting Hines to leave with her friends.
  • She initially did not recognize the director's actions as predatory, attributing it to the unusual nature of acting work.
  • Having friends nearby provided a safety net during this and similar instances.

  • Cheryl Hines secured her role in 'Curb Your Enthusiasm' after Larry David recognized her from an improv sketch she had written.
  • She was initially unaware of Larry David's public profile, but her humor reportedly aligned with his creative style.
  • An earlier sketch involving a refusal to drink urine was a key moment that led to her meeting Larry David.
  • 'Curb Your Enthusiasm' has spanned 24 years and 12 seasons, with production breaks attributed to Larry David's creative process.
  • Hines described David as smart, funny, caring, and neurotic, confirming his on-screen persona is rooted in reality.

  • Larry David introduced Cheryl Hines to Robert Kennedy Jr. at a Waterkeeper event, marking their initial acquaintance.
  • Hines initially perceived Kennedy as a great speaker and humorously noted his preference for pleated khakis.
  • They reconnected years later during their respective divorces, leading to their relationship.
  • Hines' friends initially viewed her relationship with Kennedy as casual, citing their different living locations and his work as an environmental attorney.
  • Public scrutiny of their relationship intensified later, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • Public discourse became prolonged and stressful, criticizing perceived hypocrisy in 'my body, my choice' slogans when applied to a spouse's views.
  • Bobby Kennedy Jr.'s process of writing 'The Real Anthony Fauci' involved extensive research and sourcing, significantly impacting their household.
  • The growing controversy around her husband and his book critical of Dr. Fauci created a backlash in Hollywood.
  • Hines noted a contrast between their separate worlds as celebrities and the intense public reaction to Kennedy's views on COVID-19.
  • The difficulty and demanding nature of writing the book affected family dynamics.

  • Cheryl Hines experienced a period where her life felt broken, marked by public reports suggesting work might be withheld due to her husband's political stance.
  • She believes these reports are real and shocking, attributing them to an overemphasis on politics within the industry.
  • Hines received texts from friends calling her husband an 'asshole' after his political actions became public.
  • Bradley Whitford messaged Hines on X (formerly Twitter), criticizing her for not denouncing her husband's actions.
  • Hines defended her support for her husband, stating it sets the best example for children.

  • The entertainment industry in Los Angeles is characterized by a lack of disagreement and monochromatic political views.
  • After the 2016 election, an actor who admitted voting for Donald Trump was ostracized by colleagues.
  • Hines' past perception of Los Angeles as an open-minded city contrasted with instances of intolerance, especially after her husband entered politics.
  • She was surprised by Kennedy's presidential candidacy, noting the controversy within Hollywood for running against Joe Biden.
  • The political climate surrounding Kennedy's campaign led to divisions and affected her friendships.

  • Cheryl Hines noted that people are judged and ostracized based on their association with individuals holding unpopular political views.
  • A friend of 30 years stopped speaking to Hines after the 2020 election due to her marriage to Bobby Kennedy Jr., highlighting a perceived cruel mindset.
  • The Biden administration's denial of Secret Service protection for Bobby Kennedy Jr. caused shock.
  • Speakers expressed surprise at the lack of outcry from prominent individuals, including Kennedy's relatives, given the family's history of political assassinations.
  • The discussion highlighted delays in securing Secret Service protection and perceived political motivations behind it, alongside security threats encountered.

  • Robert Kennedy Jr.'s potential endorsement of Donald Trump was described as a significant development that tested personal boundaries.
  • This development created particular challenges within Hollywood circles, where political views are often polarized.
  • The guest discussed her first meeting with Donald Trump, which occurred shortly after an assassination attempt on Trump.
  • The meeting between Kennedy and Trump took place in mid-July, coinciding with the Republican convention.
  • The circumstances added to a high-stakes and stressful environment for Hines.

  • Stress and security concerns escalated following Bobby Kennedy Jr.'s meeting with Donald Trump.
  • Trump's public call for Kennedy to receive Secret Service protection led to its immediate granting by executive order.
  • The protection only covered Kennedy, not his family, causing disruption and requiring home evacuation for security sweeps.
  • Hines recounted the extreme stress, including Trump's assassination attempt and the security changes, manifesting physically as hives and swollen lips requiring an emergency room visit.
  • She expressed reluctance to share her positive impressions of Trump with most friends due to the polarized environment.

  • During an appearance on 'The View,' Hines faced criticism regarding her husband's stance on vaccines, evolving into a discussion about vaccine safety.
  • The conversation touched on experiences of parents reporting adverse effects in their children and the politicization of vaccine discussions.
  • One speaker questioned why a vaccine with fewer injuries or deaths is met with anger, suggesting it's treated as a religion rather than science.
  • A 1930s Diego Rivera mural depicting a child receiving vaccines was referenced as analogous to a religious icon.
  • The discussion noted that people are often asked to take vaccines without questioning, presenting it as a compulsory act.

  • The concept of vaccines was described as a religious, unchangeable doctrine, akin to holy writ, prompting reflection on life's trajectory.
  • Cheryl Hines reflected on personal growth and evolving perspectives, noting that friendships sometimes ended due to her husband's political activities.
  • She learned to accept these changes without anger or sadness, focusing on the importance of family.
  • Hines detailed her unexpected deep dive into politics, spurred by Kennedy's presidential campaign.
  • She found the experience fascinating, learning about campaign operations, press dynamics, and the legislative process in Washington D.C.
