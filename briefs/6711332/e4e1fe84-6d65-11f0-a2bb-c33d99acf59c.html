John Mearsheimer: The Palestinian Genocide and How the West Has Been Deceived Into Supporting It

Key Takeaways

  • Ukraine faces inevitable defeat; a diplomatic settlement is impossible.
  • NATO expansion, driven by Russophobia, directly caused the Ukraine conflict.
  • US policy empowered China while isolating Russia, forming a dangerous bloc.
  • Israel is committing genocide in Gaza, with the US providing unconditional support.
  • The US foreign policy establishment is reckless and deeply incompetent.

Deep Dives

Ukraine War Outlook

  • Russia is winning the war in Ukraine due to superior weaponry and manpower, rendering Ukraine's situation hopeless without continued Western support.
  • A diplomatic settlement is impossible as Russia's non-negotiable demands for a neutral, demilitarized Ukraine, accepting annexed territory, are rejected by the West.
  • The conflict is predicted to end on the battlefield, resulting in an "ugly Russian victory" and a frozen conflict.

NATO Expansion's Role

  • Western Russophobia prevents accepting Russia's legitimate security concerns, despite its stance on NATO being akin to the U.S. Monroe Doctrine.
  • NATO's eastward expansion, particularly the 2008 decision to include Ukraine and Georgia, was the primary driver of the conflict.
  • The U.S. ignored Russia's clear opposition to Ukraine joining NATO, continuing to push the agenda due to a perceived need to avoid showing weakness.

Geopolitical Missteps

  • The U.S. inadvertently empowered China by pursuing an "engagement" policy in the 1990s, creating a peer competitor to American dominance.
  • Driving Russia into China's embrace was a catastrophic decision, forging a powerful anti-American bloc against U.S. interests.
  • The foreign policy establishment's failures to learn from decades of disasters are likened to structural engineers whose bridges repeatedly collapse without consequence.

Gaza Genocide Allegation

  • Israel is allegedly committing genocide in Gaza, defined by the 1948 convention as attempting to destroy a group's national identity.
  • Current events are linked to a historical Zionist goal of a "Greater Israel," which has always entailed ethnic cleansing to maintain a Jewish majority.
  • The ongoing attacks in Gaza are seen as an opportunity to expel Palestinians, continuing a pattern of ethnic cleansing evident since 1948 and 1967.
  • The U.S. provides unconditional support for Israel, despite its actions often diverging from American strategic interests and moral precepts.

Elite Incompetence

  • The U.S. foreign policy establishment, despite holding perceived discredited views, retains immense power and continues to push reckless policies.
  • A shared hawkish orientation across Republican and Democratic establishments reflects an "addiction to war," prioritizing military intervention.
  • This elite believes projecting strength, even through failure, is more strategic than concealing true power, leading to exposed vulnerabilities.
  • The foreign policy establishment's persistent failures are evident in outcomes like the rise of China and the Ukraine war, yet they face no accountability.
