The Tucker Carlson Show

Tucker’s Brother Buckley Carlson on Dogs, Childhood, Nicotine, Frank Luntz and America’s Future

Key Takeaways

  • Buckley Carlson achieved unexpected Twitter fame, advocating for free speech against perceived societal restrictions.
  • He confronted school administrators over curriculum and asserted individual expression in defiance of conformity.
  • Carlson views truth-telling as the most profound method to combat tyranny, prioritizing words over violence.
  • He expresses deep distrust in U.S. government and intelligence agencies, believing they actively spread untruths.
  • Carlson recounts a personal spiritual awakening that led to his sobriety, influenced by his wife and son's birth.
  • His career includes 25 years as a speechwriter for Republican figures, coining influential political terms like 'death tax'.
  • He shared a harrowing experience of falling asleep while piloting a plane, attributing his safe landing to divine intervention.
  • The episode delves into perceptions of societal decline, including male cowardice and the erosion of post-WWII freedoms.

Deep Dive

  • Buckley Carlson gained unexpected Twitter fame after an initial passive use of the platform since 2010.
  • He transitioned to active engagement, finding creativity and information, describing the platform as addictive.
  • Carlson noted a "tragic" societal shift away from post-WWII freedoms towards increased restrictions on thought and speech, impacting children's upbringing.
  • He argued the sentiment "words will never hurt me" has inverted to a fear of words and acceptance of violence.
  • Carlson asserted that telling the truth is the most profound way to fight tyranny, believing words can change the world.

  • The guest discussed perceived cowardice in men and extreme hostility encountered during COVID-19.
  • He described being the only person without a mask in Chicago's O'Hare airport and experiencing hostile reactions.
  • The conversation also touched on the perceived parental failings contributing to negative child behavior.

  • The discussion included nicotine products, praising a specific item and discussing its usage.
  • A tangent explored dental work and oral hygiene, leading to a broader commentary on disaster preparedness.
  • The segment highlighted the Grid Doctor, a battery backup system from Last Country Supply, emphasizing EMP protection.
  • Carlson reflected on his upbringing, noting his father treated him and his sibling with complete equality, contrasting it with modern DEI.

  • The speaker recounted smoking aggressively and joyfully for 12 years, describing it as a companion and self-defense tool.
  • He shared an anecdote with the host about using a lit cigarette to fend off an unwanted advance at a company picnic.
  • Carlson suggested carrying a cigarette, even unlit, offered a sense of preparedness and expressed a desire to bring back unfiltered cigarettes for perceived health benefits.
  • The discussion referenced a mother who died of lung cancer after smoking unfiltered cigarettes.

  • Buckley Carlson detailed experiences with his off-leash dogs in a Washington D.C. dog park, including one dog catching a squirrel.
  • He recounted being pursued by park police who threatened to 'tase' a dog, leading to a confrontation.
  • Carlson described confronting the officers, noting their initial embarrassment and a perceived lack of genuine authority.
  • He expressed lingering anger for over 20 minutes after the incident.

  • Carlson argued that more people should act assertively in societal situations, reflecting on figures like Trump who embody standing by beliefs.
  • The speakers discussed qualities of courageous and decisive men, contrasting past leadership with the present.
  • They highlighted their father as an example of bravery, decency, and kindness, noting his unafraid demeanor.
  • Carlson recounted a story of being expelled from boarding school at 17, with his father considering enrolling him in the French Foreign Legion.

  • The guest expressed a recent shift in perspective regarding government trustworthiness, influenced by COVID-19 and past experiences.
  • He contrasted the perceived lack of accurate information in Soviet-era countries with a past assumption of truthful U.S. government information.
  • Carlson stated his epiphany that the United States has become like North Korea, with government and intelligence agencies deliberately spreading untruths.
  • This realization, particularly after the 2020 election and information suppression, was described as unsettling and scary.

  • The speaker described a personal spiritual awakening, attributing it to God's intervention in changing ingrained patterns he couldn't alter independently.
  • He reflected on a lifetime of unbidden thoughts, recently recognized as spiritual or divine in nature.
  • Carlson recounted his journey to sobriety, influenced by concerned friends and a pivotal moment at his son's birth 23 years ago.
  • His wife's spiritual experience also led to her quitting drinking, further influencing his decision.
  • Carlson described divine intervention removing his desire for alcohol, noting physical symptoms and nightmares during his difficult first year of sobriety.

  • Carlson recounted falling asleep multiple times while flying a plane due to exhaustion and heavy drinking for two weeks.
  • He described navigating foggy conditions at an international airport and making a difficult return shortly after takeoff.
  • Despite falling asleep between radio communications, he managed to land safely, attributing it to prayers and self-pinching.
  • Following this, he worked as a blackjack dealer and for political campaigns before becoming a speechwriter for 25 years.
  • He mentioned writing incendiary press releases for the Maryland Republican Party and speeches for figures like Michael Steele.
