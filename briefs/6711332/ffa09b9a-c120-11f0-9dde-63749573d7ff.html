Who Is Thomas Crooks?

Key Takeaways

  • The FBI initially presented Thomas Crooks as a lone actor lacking an online presence.
  • Evidence contradicts FBI claims, revealing Crooks' extensive online activity and radicalization history.
  • Crooks' digital footprint shows an evolution from pro-Trump views to advocating political violence.
  • The FBI faces scrutiny over its handling of evidence and alleged obstruction in the investigation.
  • Congressional leaders and the public express dissatisfaction with the FBI's transparency and responsiveness.

Deep Dive

  • The FBI initially claimed Thomas Crooks acted alone, had no online presence, and was a right-winger.
  • Newly obtained YouTube comments from Crooks, posted between 2019 and 2020 as a teenager, suggested anti-Semitic, anti-immigration, and political violence themes.
  • A source reportedly provided access to Crooks' online accounts, including emails, Snapchat, and Venmo, contradicting the FBI's assertion he was a 'ghost online'.
  • Data retrieved from his deactivated YouTube account included 737 public comments and video metadata indicating filming at his parents' home.

  • Thomas Crooks' online comments evolved from pro-Trump sentiments to violent anti-immigrant and anti-Democrat rhetoric, including threats against politicians.
  • He made threats against law enforcement and referenced school shooters, with evidence reportedly available on internet archives.
  • His commentary shifted from pro-Trump views to criticism of Trump and conservative media, embracing left-leaning perspectives on issues like the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • Crooks' online commentary became increasingly radicalized, advocating for terrorism-style attacks against government officials and promoting assassinations.

  • A user named Willie Tepis reportedly pressured Crooks towards violence; Tepis' online activity is linked to foreign Antifa websites and the Nordic Resistance Movement.
  • Crooks' online searches included terms related to Trump, Jack Ruby, and mass shootings, suggesting a pattern of radicalization and interest in violence prior to the shooting.
  • Online searches also covered historical extremist groups and hateful ideologies, contrasting with later FBI claims.

  • The FBI is accused of obstructing the investigation by withholding information and videos related to the July 13th shooting.
  • Crooks' body was released for cremation 10 days after the assassination attempt, on August 5th, the same day House committees began their investigation, precluding independent forensic verification.
  • An FBI agent was reportedly seen hosing down the crime scene where Crooks died the day after the shooting, preventing third-party analysis of evidence.
  • Crooks' phone was analyzed by the FBI in Quantico within 40 minutes, accessing encrypted messages, but this online activity was reportedly not mentioned in the December 2024 congressional report.

  • Public frustration persists regarding the lack of transparency surrounding the assassination attempt on Donald Trump.
  • FBI Director Chris Wray stated under oath that the shooting was not an accident but has not fully answered detailed questions submitted by Congress.
  • A bipartisan group of senators, led by Ron Johnson, issued a subpoena for records after the FBI failed to respond to requests for nearly a year.
  • Congressional representatives expressed dissatisfaction with the FBI's investigation, citing a lack of clear understanding of the shooter's background and motives.

  • The shooter reportedly conducted drone surveillance of the rally site, coinciding with downtime of the Secret Service's anti-drone system.
  • Police identified the shooter as suspicious but subsequently lost track of him, allowing access to a building without surveillance.
  • The shooter was positioned on the American Glass Research Building, outside the security perimeter; police officers assigned to the building reportedly did not report the shooter or left early.
  • The FBI collected surveillance footage from local businesses, state police, and a gun range where the shooter trained, but has reportedly refused to release it.
