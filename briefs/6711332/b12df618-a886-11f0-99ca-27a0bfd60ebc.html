The Tucker Carlson Show

Alex Jones Warns of the Globalist Death Cult Fueling the Next Civil War and Rise of the Antichrist

Key Takeaways

  • The Russia-Ukraine conflict is described as an escalating phase towards a potential new world war.
  • Concerns are raised about US government support for Zelensky and alleged corruption related to aid.
  • Discussions highlight a 'Great Reset' agenda aimed at establishing a totalitarian, cashless, social credit system.
  • Predictions of impending US civil unrest, state secession, and political violence are attributed to a 'Podesta Plan'.
  • Allegations surface regarding the weaponization of the justice system and government agencies against political opponents.
  • The episode delves into spiritual warfare, occult practices among elites, and the nature of good versus evil.
  • Critiques are made regarding military leadership, modern societal values, and the safety of COVID-19 vaccines.

Deep Dive

  • US government's support for Ukrainian President Zelensky is questioned, labeling him an 'unelected dictator'.
  • Concerns were raised regarding potential corruption and money laundering linked to US aid.
  • The situation was compared to historical decadence, referencing the French Revolution.

  • The 'Great Reset' and UN policies, including carbon taxes, are described as aiming for a totalitarian, dystopian society.
  • Claims suggest a plan for reduced populations, controlled city-states, and agrarian living for the masses.
  • This agenda allegedly involves sabotaging the global economy via resource restrictions and farming cuts to establish a cashless, social credit system and neo-feudalism.

  • Alex Jones discusses a scenario of impending civil unrest and war in the US, including state secession and a 'Western Alliance'.
  • He claims this is part of a 'Podesta Plan' and foreshadowed in the movie 'Civil War'.
  • The 'Podesta Plan' report from 2020 allegedly outlined strategies to destabilize the US if Donald Trump won the election.

  • Concerns are raised about a coalition of leftists and Antifa poised to act upon a staged violent event, allegedly outlined in the 'Podesta plan'.
  • The guest claims Antifa has targeted lists, including the host, and is supplied with drugs and weapons.
  • The potential for violence is compared to 'Helter Skelter times 10,000'.

  • Globalist elites, including figures like Larry Fink, are accused of waging war on Western civilization through initiatives like ESG.
  • Current societal trends are compared to the Soviet Union's rise and a global hostility towards Christian white farmers, citing the Rwandan genocide.
  • Corporate interests in resource extraction in Rwanda, South Africa, and Iraq are linked to destabilization benefiting globalist agendas.

  • Strong condemnation of historical figures like Hitler and Stalin is expressed, critiquing the idea of arming Stalin during World War II.
  • The discussion argues against choosing between 'lesser evils' and highlights propaganda tactics by groups like the ADL using historical events.
  • The guest recounts his grandfather's view of Hitler's monstrous nature and extraction of money from German-Americans during wartime.

  • The host and Alex Jones discuss Kanye West's perceived obsessions and the influence of drugs on his behavior.
  • This conversation is contextualized within Jones's erratic conduct during the broadcast, including wearing a gimp mask and repeatedly invoking Hitler.
  • The host observes Jones's pupils appearing completely black, speculating it was due to extreme drug use, possibly ecstasy and methamphetamine.

  • Concerns are raised about a South Korean study suggesting a link between the COVID-19 vaccine and cancer due to immune system effects.
  • An increase in spiritual discussions is noted, exploring near-death experiences and hallucinogens like DMT and ayahuasca.
  • Individuals reportedly encounter entities promoting depopulation during these hallucinogenic experiences.

  • The conversation addresses perceived corruption within established religious organizations, citing influences like the Rockefeller Foundation.
  • It is argued that a personal relationship with God remains accessible despite this 'capture' of institutions.
  • The guest suggests the current situation may be triggering a broader spiritual awakening, emphasizing repentance and divine help in the struggle against evil.

  • Concerns are raised about the loyalty and mindset of individuals in military and government leadership, attributed to globalist ideologies.
  • There is a belief that the COVID-19 vaccine was used to purge men with high testosterone and self-respect from the military.
  • The guest argues that a lack of experience with physical violence makes leaders unqualified to command armies or countries.

  • Alex Jones claims he is being targeted for expressing opinions, citing $1.5 billion in lawsuits and judgments that threaten to shut down his operations.
  • The host reiterates that questioning is a protected right in the United States, citing professors who have debated the Sandy Hook Elementary School shooting.
  • Jones details a multi-year legal and financial battle, alleging coordinated efforts by the Justice Department and other entities to silence him through bankruptcy and asset seizure.

  • The discussion highlights a 'new model' where judges allegedly declare guilt by default, as seen in cases against Alex Jones and Mike Lindell.
  • Allegations include the weaponization of government agencies and the Justice Department funding lawsuits.
  • A deputy Attorney General, Todd Blanche, is accused of intervening to shut down investigations into alleged government overreach and protecting specific agents.
