Whistleblower Tony Aguilar Responds to Attacks After Exposing Israel’s War Crimes in Gaza

Key Takeaways

  • Lt. Col. Aguilar powerfully defends his character and combat record against severe attacks.
  • He exposes the Gaza aid operation as chaotic, under-equipped, and ethically compromised.
  • Horrifying accounts reveal alleged war crimes and IDF control over aid distribution.

Deep Dives

Aguilar's Defense

  • Aguilar clarified he resigned in protest, not fired, providing documentation to refute Johnnie Moore's claims.
  • He detailed his distinguished 25-year military career, including Special Forces command, countering critics questioning his combat experience.
  • Aguilar asserts the attacks stem from his eyewitness accounts of wrongdoing, dismissing online critics as uninformed and lacking credibility.
  • He expressed a desire for a private life post-military, entering the mission to help Gaza and embody American exceptionalism.

Aid Operation Flaws

  • Aguilar characterized GHF as a "shadowy, for-profit" entity with unclear structure and no humanitarian aid experience, run by Johnnie Moore.
  • He cited logistical failures, like contractors providing pizzas for unpaid local workers, and GHF supplying food without water in Gaza.
  • Armed contractors operated on tourist visas with no rules of engagement, using armor-piercing rounds, a grave violation of military practice.

War Crime Claims

  • Aguilar observed IDF forces firing small arms, mortars, and artillery at calm Palestinian crowds without visible resistance.
  • He questioned the deaths of 20 people at a distribution site on July 16th, disputing the "stampede" claim and noting live ammunition use.
  • Aguilar views the US-provided aid as an "appendage of the Israeli Defense Force," facilitating IDF offensive actions and occupation.
  • He notes other contractors and IDF reservists are coming forward with similar accounts, challenging official narratives.
