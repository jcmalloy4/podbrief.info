The Tucker Carlson Show

The 9/11 Files: From Cover-up to Conspiracy | Ep 4

Key Takeaways

  • Government actions post-9/11, including evidence suppression, are posited as fostering conspiracy theories.
  • The collapse of WTC Building 7, with its unique characteristics, remains a point of significant contention.
  • Independent studies challenge official explanations for WTC Building 7's collapse, suggesting alternative causes.
  • Thermitic materials were reportedly found in 9/11 dust samples, contradicting official blast assessments.
  • Significant increases in put options against airlines and financial firms occurred prior to the 9/11 attacks.
  • Foreign intelligence agencies, including France, Israel, and Saudi Arabia, allegedly had foreknowledge of the 9/11 plots.
  • The NSA reportedly withheld 9/11 intelligence from the CIA while sharing it with foreign entities.
  • Questions persist regarding the disposal of WTC debris, limited Pentagon footage, and the 9/11 Commission's scope.

Deep Dive

  • The host outlines actions like suppressing evidence and avoiding investigations as fostering conspiracy theories after 9/11.
  • It is suggested these actions were taken by the U.S. government, facilitating attacks or demonstrating incompetence.
  • The discussion posits that focusing on fringe theories distracts from government's potential role in events.

  • WTC Building 7's collapse at 5:20 p.m. on 9/11 is highlighted as unexplained, omitted from the 9/11 Commission report.
  • FEMA cited debris from the North Tower and subsequent fires; NIST pointed to thermal expansion of steel beams causing progressive collapse.
  • Video evidence appears to show a simultaneous, free-fall acceleration, contrasting with NIST's thermal expansion explanation.
  • A 2020 study by Professor Leroy Hulsey concluded office fires alone could not have caused critical girder failure in Building 7.

  • A 2009 study reported red-gray chips containing energetic and thermitic materials in WTC dust samples.
  • NIST deemed thermite unlikely for Building 7's collapse due to required quantities and logistical challenges.
  • Eyewitness Barry Jennings, who reported hearing explosions in Building 7, died at age 53 shortly before NIST's report release.
  • Media, including the BBC, reportedly announced Building 7's collapse before it occurred on 9/11.

  • Significant increases in put options against United Airlines and American Airlines were observed before 9/11.
  • The identity of one investor behind significant airline put options was reportedly withheld by the U.S. government.
  • Surges in put options against British Airways, Morgan Stanley, and Merrill Lynch were also noted.
  • Call options for gold and oil reportedly increased prior to the 9/11 attacks.

  • Foreign intelligence agencies in Germany, Saudi Arabia, and France reportedly possessed prior knowledge of 9/11.
  • France's intelligence service allegedly learned of an al-Qaeda hijacking plot in January 2001.
  • Michael Scheuer, former CIA official, stated the NSA shared signals intelligence with Israeli and Saudi entities but withheld it from the CIA.
  • The 9/11 Commission's investigation into the NSA's foreknowledge was limited, with no staff assigned to review NSA files.

  • Five Israeli nationals were reportedly seen celebrating the initial World Trade Center explosion on 9/11.
  • FBI documents indicated these individuals possessed videos, box cutters, multiple passports, cash, and photos with the burning WTC.
  • A memorandum to the 9/11 Commission suggested Israeli intelligence assets, posing as art students, were near future hijackers' locations.
  • Israeli intelligence reportedly claimed to have warned the U.S. about large-scale terror attacks within a week of 9/11.
