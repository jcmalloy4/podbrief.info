The Smerconish Podcast

Susie Wiles, Vanity Fair & Trump: Mark Halperin On What You Weren’t Told

Key Takeaways

  • Mark Halperin offered insights on several public figures and their political roles.
  • The discussion highlighted the evolving role of White House Chiefs of Staff and internal dissent.
  • Former President Trump's social media post about Rob Reiner's death generated significant controversy.
  • Trump's approach to death was characterized as viewing it solely as a 'news peg' for counterpunching critics.
  • A poll revealed 90% of over 25,000 voters considered Trump's post about Reiner a 'new low'.

Deep Dive

  • Host introduced a discussion about comments made by Mark Halperin regarding public figures.
  • Figures mentioned included Donald Trump, J.D. Vance, Elon Musk, Russell Vought, and A.G. Pambondi.
  • The comments stemmed from a Vanity Fair interview referenced by the host.
  • The host questioned the context and potential motivations behind Halperin's statements, referencing an article and the idea of 'off the record' remarks.

  • Discussion centered on the role of White House Chiefs of Staff and internal dissent.
  • Contrasted perceived lack of internal dissent in Trump's first term with the current administration.
  • Guest argued figures like Susie Wiles and the Vice President privately offer dissenting opinions in Trump II, providing more checks on decisions.
  • President Trump reportedly called the New York Post to comment on Susie Wiles's 'alcoholic' remark.

  • Host introduced a discussion about former President Trump's social media post following Rob Reiner's death.
  • Trump posted on Truth Social the day after Reiner's passing.
  • The host explained Trump's post was motivated by a desire to 'slap back' at Reiner and gain attention.
  • The host described his personal reaction to the post as 'appalling' and 'an all-time low'.

  • The host elaborated on former President Trump's perspective, suggesting he views death as a 'news peg' for counterpunching critics.
  • Trump's remarks about John McCain were cited as a precedent for this approach.
  • The guest explained the president's perspective on death, suggesting he does not differentiate between recent and distant events.
  • The guest posited that Trump's criticism of Rob Reiner aligns with his general pattern of attacking critics, especially from Hollywood.

  • The host read Mark Halperin's explanation of President Trump's controversial Truth Social post about Rob Reiner.
  • Halperin's written explanation stated Trump views death as a universal experience.
  • The explanation clarified Trump criticized Reiner because he was part of the news cycle and a liberal media figure, not due to the circumstances of his death.
  • A poll question on Smirkanish.com asked if the post represents a 'new low'.

  • Mark from San Diego called in, agreeing Trump's post was a 'new low' and criticizing Halperin's explanation.
  • Poll results showed 90% of over 25,000 voters agreed the post was a 'new low'.
  • Liz from San Antonio agreed it was a new low and predicted more such comments.
  • Republicans reportedly denounced Donald Trump's statement following the murder of Rob Reiner.
