What Did Team Biden Hide? Shocking Insights Revealed

Overview

  • Biden's declining cognitive health has become a major campaign issue, with reports that his top aides attempted to manage his condition by limiting his schedule to 10-4 p.m. and discussing potential wheelchair use after the election, suggesting awareness of his diminished capacity.
  • The George Clooney fundraiser incident stands as a pivotal moment where Biden appeared disoriented and failed to recognize Clooney despite previous meetings, though Smerconish views this as rationalization by aides rather than deliberate deception.
  • A legal education seminar on "Trump 2.0" explored the constitutional implications of Trump's potential first 100 days, covering pardons, birthright citizenship, mass deportations, and executive orders.
  • The Qatari jet controversy raises significant legal concerns among experts and Republican senators alike, with consensus that accepting such a gift would likely violate the Emoluments Clause and create security risks.

Content

Biden's Health Situation and Campaign Concerns

* Michael Smerconish is discussing a daily poll question about President Biden's health situation, asking whether Biden's aides were engaged in rationalization or deliberate scheming regarding his health scrutiny.

* An upcoming book "Original Sin" by Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson about President Biden's potential mental decline is generating significant media attention: * Biden's top aides reportedly tried to limit his work schedule to 10-4 p.m. * David Plouffe (Obama campaign manager) criticized Biden's campaign, saying they were "screwed" by Biden * There are references to Obama potentially helping Biden during public events * Close aides discussed potential use of a wheelchair for Biden, deciding to wait until after the election * White House physician warned that a fall could make a wheelchair necessary * Biden reportedly had moments of being barely audible and trailing off incoherently

* The George Clooney fundraiser incident is highlighted as particularly concerning: * At a 2024 fundraiser, Biden reportedly did not immediately recognize George Clooney despite having met him multiple times over the years * The fundraiser was historically successful, raising $30 million - the most successful American political fundraiser ever * Clooney observed Biden appearing significantly diminished since their last meeting in December 2022 * Biden was walking with small steps, guided by an aide * Biden appeared disoriented and repeatedly said "Thank you for being here" * The incident occurred after Biden had just returned from a G7 summit with limited sleep

* Smerconish's perspective on the situation: * Believes Biden's staff was aware of his diminished capacity * Does not see evidence of a major deliberate cover-up * Does not view the Clooney non-recognition as significant evidence of diminished capacity * Believes Biden should not have run for re-election * Characterizes Biden aides' actions as "rationalization" rather than deliberate scheming * Suggests Biden is stubborn and did not want to step down

Legal Education Seminar on "Trump 2.0"

* Smerconish moderated a Continuing Legal Education (CLE) seminar about "Trump 2.0: The First 100 Days" * The seminar covered various legal topics related to the Trump administration, including: * Pardons * Birthright citizenship * Mass deportations * Executive orders * DEI policies * Transgender service member ban

Qatari Jet Controversy

* The legal panel discussed the Qatari jet gifted to Trump * Consensus among legal experts was that accepting the jet likely violates the Emoluments Clause * Some Republican senators, including John Thune, Rand Paul, and Ted Cruz, have raised concerns about the jet * Concerns include: * Potential espionage risks * Inappropriate signal * Qatar's financial support for Hamas

* Trump's motivation for wanting the jet: * Desires a larger plane due to delays in Boeing's Air Force One order * Currently traveling in the Middle East, being "treated like a king" * Interested in acquiring a bigger aircraft
