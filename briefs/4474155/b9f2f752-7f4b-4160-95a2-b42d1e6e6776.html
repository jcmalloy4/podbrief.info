The Smerconish Podcast

Michael Smerconish: Jimmy Kimmel Should NOT Have Been Suspended

Key Takeaways

  • The host opposes Jimmy Kimmel's suspension for comments, citing First Amendment protections.
  • A debate on hate speech's legality intensified following Charlie Kirk's assassination.
  • ABC pulled Jimmy Kimmel's show, and the FCC Chair suggested regulatory action over his remarks.
  • Media bias and perceived condescension towards public figures were discussed as influencing political outcomes.

Deep Dive

  • The Smerconish.com poll questions if Jimmy Kimmel should be suspended for comments regarding Charlie Kirk's assassination.
  • The host immediately stated 'no,' citing First Amendment principles and promising a full explanation.
  • The poll garnered high public engagement, with the host maintaining his initial 'no' answer.

  • The First Amendment broadly protects speech, but includes exceptions for obscenity, defamation, and incitement to imminent lawless action.
  • The Brandenburg v. Ohio case is referenced as a precedent for these specific speech protection limits.
  • The host questioned if Jimmy Kimmel's comments directly triggered a chain reaction leading to his suspension.

  • Yesterday's poll on 'Hate speech does not exist legally in America' received 34,101 votes, with 71.11% agreeing with the Charlie Kirk-attributed statement.
  • Pam Bondi suggested 'no place for hate speech' following Charlie Kirk's assassination, indicating a willingness to target those using it.
  • The host contrasted Bondi's stance with established First Amendment protections, specifically referencing Brandenburg v. Ohio.

  • ABC indefinitely pulled Jimmy Kimmel's show after conservatives accused him of mischaracterizing the politics of Charlie Kirk's alleged murderer.
  • FCC Chair Brandon Carr suggested regulatory action against ABC for Kimmel's November 15th remarks.
  • Kimmel's monologue accused Trump supporters of 'desperately trying to characterize this kid who murdered Charlie Kirk as anything other than one of them.'
  • He also compared President Trump's reaction to a journalist's question to the fourth stage of grief, akin to a child mourning a goldfish.
