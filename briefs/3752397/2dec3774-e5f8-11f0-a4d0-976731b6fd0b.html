We Can Do Hard Things

Welcome to 2026 (and Hello, YouTube Friends)

Key Takeaways

  • "We Can Do Hard Things" podcast is transitioning back to an audio-only format.
  • The format change aims to enhance host comfort and authenticity.
  • New episodes are scheduled to resume on January 6th, 2026.
  • The podcast is an independent production by Treat Media.

Deep Dive

  • The podcast "We Can Do Hard Things" announced a shift back to an audio-only format, discontinuing video on YouTube.
  • This decision was made because one host reportedly felt more 'embodied' and less self-conscious without considering her physical appearance on camera.
  • The change aligns with what the hosts feel is best for their sharing approach.

  • New episodes of "We Can Do Hard Things" are slated to resume on January 6th, 2026.
  • The hosts expressed gratitude to both new YouTube listeners and long-time audio subscribers.
  • They affirmed their commitment to continuing to face life's challenges together with their audience.

  • "We Can Do Hard Things" is identified as an independent production by 'Treat Media'.
  • Treat Media is described as an entity that creates 'art for humans'.
  • Listeners are directed to follow the podcast on Instagram and TikTok for updates.
