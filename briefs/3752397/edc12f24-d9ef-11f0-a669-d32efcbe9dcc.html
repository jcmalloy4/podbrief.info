Astrology for Skeptics: Sonya Renee Taylor Reads Our Charts

Key Takeaways

  • Astrology is presented as a map for understanding one's soul purpose and reducing suffering.
  • Ancient wisdom, including astrology, is framed as intrinsic knowledge, not repackaged dogma.
  • Glennon's chart reveals a pioneering Aries sun, signifying a new journey of authentic self-expression.
  • Abby's chart highlights a Gemini sun in the 12th house and a Capricorn moon, pointing to duality and structured emotions.
  • The importance of self-nurturing and embracing all aspects of oneself is emphasized for personal growth.

Deep Dive

  • Sonya Renee Taylor defines astrology as a map of the soul's intentional journey for one's highest good.
  • She explains that suffering arises from the gap between an individual's true self and their learned personality.
  • Taylor posits that humans are made of the same materials as stars and planets, suggesting an intrinsic relationship where celestial bodies serve as teachers.

  • An astrological chart visually represents planetary relationships, illustrating energies in conversation.
  • Sonya Renee Taylor emphasizes her intuitive approach to chart readings, complementing traditional astrological study.
  • The chart is likened to a harp, where external events 'pluck' certain strings, and individuals also 'pluck' strings through their choices, allowing for co-creation with life.

  • Glennon's sun sign is identified as Aries, the first zodiac sign, at zero degrees, symbolizing a new beginning and pioneering energy.
  • This placement signifies her transition from a dutiful Christian wife to embracing personal desires and starting a new path.
  • The guest connects this zero-degree Aries sun to a collective message about initiating energy that has not existed before, moving away from Piscean absorption.

  • Glennon's moon sign is Sagittarius in the fourth house, indicating a philosopher archetype, combining animal instinct with human rationality.
  • This fire sign, ruled by Jupiter, embodies expansion, luck, humor, and wisdom, leading to a desire for knowledge and a drive to teach others.
  • Her rising sign is Virgo, a mutable earth sign associated with the maiden archetype and the mind-body connection, occurring in the first house.

  • The higher expression of Virgo's 'virgin' archetype is defined as sovereign, meaning fully belonging to oneself.
  • This involves embracing all parts of oneself, including imperfections and confusion, without judgment, leading to wholeness.
  • Fulfilling one's purpose requires welcoming previously ignored or feared aspects to offer a whole and authentic gift to the collective.

  • Abby's sun is in Gemini in the 12th house, representing duality, the mind, curiosity, and a need to reconcile separation.
  • This placement signifies integrating disavowed aspects of oneself and finding a way to be seen, even when the sun is in a house associated with the unseen.
  • The fulfillment of this aspect is noted to stem from the emotional realm, not necessarily the mind.

  • The hosts express that Sonya Renee Taylor's astrological insights are profound and align with previous discussions.
  • One host reacts to the personal revelations by stating, 'I'm not special,' suggesting a realization of universal patterns within individual experiences.
  • The guest explains that astrology provides a map to an individual's life, offering insights without needing direct personal knowledge.

  • Abby's moon sign is Capricorn in the seventh house, which rules committed partnerships and represents her emotional landscape.
  • This placement indicates a tendency to create structure and compartmentalize emotions, potentially leading to harshness towards oneself, especially within relationships.
  • The guest highlights that this tendency can hide deep vulnerability, particularly in the seventh house, which represents relational intimacy.

  • Abby's Cancer rising, ruled by the moon, denotes deep sensitivities and maternal instinct, challenging her to integrate strength with vulnerability.
  • Glennon's Cancer Rising and Venus in the first house indicate a new assignment to self-nurture, a process with moments of grasping and losing the concept.
  • Current astrological shifts, including lunar nodes in Virgo (south) and Pisces (north), are creating intensified pressure to confront perfectionism and trust, especially with Saturn, Mars, and Jupiter in Virgo in the third house.
