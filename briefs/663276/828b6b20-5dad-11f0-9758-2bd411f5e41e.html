The United States is Southern now

Key Takeaways

  • Northern students increasingly choose Southern colleges for cost savings, laid-back culture, and robust campus life.
  • Southern universities leverage traditions like Greek life and college football for recruitment, actively targeting Northern states.
  • The competitive 'Bama Rush' phenomenon on TikTok highlights the consumer-driven and historical aspects of Southern sorority culture.
  • Decades of structural and legal changes, including civil rights and economic incentives, have propelled the South's growing national influence.
  • Southern hip-hop and country music are significantly shaping mainstream American pop culture, sparking dialogue on heritage and identity.

Deep Dive

  • Over a decade, there has been an 88% increase in students from the North attending Southern colleges, a trend accelerating post-pandemic.
  • Universities like Clemson, Georgia Tech, Ole Miss, and the University of Tennessee at Knoxville have seen significant jumps in Northern enrollment.
  • Students are drawn by factors such as a more laid-back culture, appealing large state schools with strong football programs, and active Greek life.

  • College football is identified as a major driving factor, creating a strong sense of community and acting as a recruitment tactic.
  • Southern universities have actively recruited students from Northern states like New Jersey and New York for over a decade.
  • These institutions appeal to Northern students with lower tuition costs; one student saved nearly $70,000 by choosing South Carolina over Syracuse after scholarships.

  • Southern schools have become the 'Olympics of sorority rush' due to historically large Greek life participation and a changing student demographic.
  • The culture appeals to out-of-state students seeking an idealized 'white Southern womanhood' experience.
  • Rituals like Rush are described as performative symbols that invoke the privilege of the plantation era, with a focus on exclusion and status elevation.
  • Purity is a significant factor in sorority evaluations, emphasizing reputation to attract specific fraternities for social and marital prospects.

  • The visual culture of 'Bama Rush' on TikTok includes 'outfit of the day' and 'get ready with me' videos, attracting significant attention.
  • Successful influencers often receive free clothes from brands seeking exposure, while others face pressure to conform to expensive fashion standards.
  • Students showcase brands like Shoco, Steve Madden, Pandora, Dior, Michael Kors, and David Yurman in their TikTok videos.
  • Bama Rush is described as a 'blood sport' where participants, despite appearances of friendship, are actively competing, with race sometimes cited as a factor by a participant.

  • The South's increasing prominence in American pop culture is attributed to decades of structural and legal changes.
  • Early shifts in the 1960s included the Civil Rights Movement, the end of Jim Crow laws, and technological advancements like air conditioning and the American Highway Program.
  • Southern states such as Florida, Texas, and Georgia actively market themselves with tax rebate and incentive packages to attract industries like car manufacturing and film production, often highlighting union-unfriendly environments.

  • Outkast's 1995 Source Awards statement, 'The South got something to say,' is highlighted as a pivotal moment signaling the rising influence of Southern hip-hop.
  • The rise of Southern artists, extending beyond Atlanta to Houston, has changed the landscape of American music, impacting artists like Justin Bieber.
  • The growing popularity of country music is discussed alongside Southern hip-hop, with controversies surrounding artists like Morgan Wallen and Jason Aldean contrasted with Beyoncé's 'Cowboy Carter,' which re-examines Southern heritage.
