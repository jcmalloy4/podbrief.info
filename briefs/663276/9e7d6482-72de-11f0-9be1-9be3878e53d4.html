Project 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump's administration advanced many Project 2025 objectives despite initial distancing.
  • Project 2025 provides a comprehensive blueprint to reshape government functions and policy.
  • Key policy shifts aligned with Project 2025 were observed in social, economic, and environmental areas.
  • Implementation of Project 2025 goals was systematic, often leveraging executive orders.
  • Despite being unpopular, Project 2025 proposals significantly influenced the conservative agenda.
  • Conservative unity, while fragile, is maintained by Donald Trump amidst internal policy divisions.

Deep Dive

  • Project 2025 is described as a comprehensive plan including policy ideas for government departments, strategies to reduce the administrative state, and a database of potential appointees.
  • The project aimed to prepare conservatives to implement these changes from day one of a new administration.
  • Author David Graham notes Trump embraced many proposals despite prior disavowal, with figures like Russell Vought and John Ratcliffe taking significant roles.

  • Trump's actions in his first year aligned with Project 2025, particularly through executive orders issued on day one of the administration.
  • Progress on policy goals includes pushes against 'wokeness,' attacks on DEI programs, and efforts to restrict transgender rights, alongside ongoing immigration policies.
  • Economic policies featured Trump's pursuit of tariffs, while environmental efforts encouraged oil and gas exploration, reducing focus on renewables by cutting mandates and regulations.

  • The administration's progress on Project 2025 is attributed to systematic preparation and a desire for a powerful, less restricted presidency, often involving veterans from Trump's previous term.
  • This preparedness allowed for swift and organized implementation, described as a 'blitzkrieg' that surprised various government bodies and the public.
  • A tracker suggests over 50% of Project 2025's goals have been addressed, with the ultimate aim being a significant reshaping of society over years or decades.

  • Aspects of Donald Trump's presidency did not align with the Project 2025 agenda, including certain socially conservative policies like abortion bans and pro-family initiatives.
  • The administration saw partial success in targeting the Department of Education but lacked significant action against 'big tech' companies, contrary to expectations.
  • A key tenet of Project 2025, a strategic focus on China, remained a consistent goal for the administration.

  • Project 2025's proposals are described as extreme and unpopular, with favorability ratings more than 30 percentage points underwater, yet they represent Trump's laid-out agenda.
  • Despite unpopularity and Trump's resistance to ceding control, Project 2025 successfully set the broader conservative agenda.
  • Groups like the Heritage Foundation and others on the right continue to navigate how to fit into the 'age of Trump' and vie to shape the future of MAGA and provide policy blueprints.

  • Schisms exist within conservatism regarding issues like U.S. intervention and the Affordable Care Act, with Donald Trump being the primary, though increasingly fragile, unifier.
  • Project 2025 reveals internal policy divisions even as Trump holds the coalition together, noting that figures like J.D. Vance may align with its policy mindset but lack Trump's unifying ability.
  • The permanence of changes made, particularly those reliant on executive orders rather than legislation, is questioned, but broader shifts in government power are difficult to reverse and could take years to restore checks and balances.
