Today, Explained

JD Vance is just getting started

Key Takeaways

  • VP J.D. Vance redefines the role through online engagement and unconventional communication.
  • Vance shapes foreign policy with an "America First" stance, criticizing traditional allies.
  • His coalition management involves navigating controversial figures and identity politics.
  • Vance is a leading 2028 presidential contender, aiming to unite conservative factions.
  • His political future hinges on economic performance and broadening appeal beyond MAGA.

Deep Dive

  • Politico writer Ian Ward notes that young MAGA conservatives view J.D. Vance as encapsulating their worldview, describing his vice presidency as unlike any other.
  • Vance has adopted an unconventional approach, engaging heavily with online discourse, memes, and hosting a podcast.
  • His first year included dressing as a popular meme of himself for Halloween, interpreted as a savvy political communication strategy.
  • This embrace of online cultural dynamics signals Vance's understanding of internet-driven conservative politics and his ability to connect with younger generations.

  • Vance played a role in a meeting where he and President Trump criticized Ukrainian President Zelensky.
  • He views the international order as having benefited Europe at the expense of American working-class individuals facing deindustrialization.
  • Vance categorizes European nations as 'freeloaders' and has justified Trump's military actions, including the Iran strikes, under a 'Trump doctrine' of overwhelming force followed by withdrawal.

  • After the assassination of Charlie Kirk, Vance's public statements on free speech were perceived as hypocritical, citing the 'friend vs. enemy' distinction articulated by Carl Schmitt.
  • Vance downplayed leaked racist and anti-Semitic messages from a young Republican group chat as 'edgy, offensive jokes' of young men.
  • He strategically remained quiet on Tucker Carlson's interview with Nick Fuentes, aiming to manage his coalition without alienating Fuentes's young male followers.
  • At a Turning Point USA event, Vance expressed hope for his Hindu wife to convert to Catholicism and articulated 'Heritage Americans' as white, Protestant descendants of colonial settlers whose values should shape the nation.

  • The Trump administration's immigration policies have reportedly unleashed xenophobia that Vance's family may face.
  • Vance is reportedly pivoting from foreign policy to economic issues, such as affordability, potentially to distinguish "America First" from "MAGA" and address criticisms.
  • Ian Ward notes that Vance's role in the upcoming midterms will include focusing on economic accomplishments and immigration as unifying issues for the MAGA coalition.

  • Politico's Ian Ward suggests Vance's denials of presidential ambitions for 2028 are pro forma, indicating he is widely seen as the likely heir apparent.
  • Ward believes Vance has a strong base among the elite conservative class and MAGA Republicans.
  • However, to unite diverse conservative factions for a 2028 run, Vance will need to appeal to independent or swing voters.

  • Concerns exist that Vance's alignment with the MAGA coalition may create divisions, hindering his goal of achieving a transformative Republican agenda.
  • His political stances on technology, particularly AI, are noted as a point of friction within the conservative movement.
  • Vance's future success is presented as contingent on the economic conditions during the remaining three years of the Trump administration, with positive conditions favoring his succession.
