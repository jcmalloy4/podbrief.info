Deep Dive

  • Google's Gemini app usage surged, surpassing ChatGPT and other applications in the App Store.
  • This growth is significantly attributed to its Nano Banana image model.
  • The Nano Banana model has already recorded over 500 million uses.

  • An OpenAI study of 1.5 million ChatGPT users from May 2024 to June 2025 found 73% of chats were personal.
  • ChatGPT's top use case, at 28.3%, was practical guidance, including how-to advice and schoolwork help.
  • An Anthropic report indicates 36% of Claude users engage in coding, with 77% of enterprise use involving automation.

  • China has initiated a preliminary antitrust investigation into NVIDIA.
  • China's state administration for market regulations found NVIDIA in violation of antitrust rules regarding its Mellanox Technologies acquisition.
  • This investigation coincides with ongoing U.S.-China trade negotiations.

  • Klarna, founded in 2005, has gained 111 million customers by offering flexible online shopping with four interest-free payments.
  • Now listed on the NYSE, Klarna focuses on transparent payment options for consumers.
  • PayPal has launched PayPal Links, enabling users to send and receive payments with a one-time, personalized link without fees or tax forms.
  • PayPal Links is expanding internationally and will include cryptocurrency transactions.

  • A weekend read by Jerry Newman from Colossus argues that generative AI's wealth creation potential will likely disappoint investors.
  • Newman compares AI's impact to containerized shipping, a societal transformation benefiting customers and incumbents more than new independent winners.
  • The analysis suggests the key question for investors is who ultimately captures AI's value.
