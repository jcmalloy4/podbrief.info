PDB Situation Report | April 26th, 2025: Inside China’s Struggling Economy & Kashmir Attack Sparks Crisis Between Nuclear Rivals

Here's a concise briefing based on the podcast transcript:

Key takeaways:

  • U.S.-China trade war remains at an impasse despite both sides expressing openness to dialogue
  • China issued stern warnings to countries considering trade deals with U.S. that could undermine Chinese interests
  • White House reportedly considering significant tariff reductions on Chinese imports to de-escalate tensions
Stalled Negotiations The U.S.-China trade war has reached a stalemate, with China demanding removal of all U.S. tariffs as a precondition for talks. President Trump has indicated willingness to negotiate but insists tariff cuts would depend on reciprocal actions from Beijing. China denies any current negotiations are underway. Markets remain volatile amid speculation rather than substantive progress.

Economic Impacts China remains heavily export-dependent despite efforts to reduce reliance on foreign trade and technology. The trade war has caused significant shifts in global supply chains, with some manufacturing moving to countries like Vietnam and Mexico. However, China has maintained large trade surpluses by increasing exports to other countries to compensate for reduced U.S. trade.

Data Reliability Concerns There are significant doubts about the reliability of Chinese economic data and reporting. With limited transparency and restricted access for foreign journalists, it is difficult to accurately assess China's true economic situation. This lack of reliable information poses challenges for Western companies and policymakers in evaluating risks and opportunities related to China.

Chinese Vulnerabilities A key concern for Chinese leaders is potential unrest among migrant workers in export-focused industries impacted by the trade war. These workers are seen as vulnerable to unemployment and social instability if factory closures and reduced trade persist. The Chinese government fears the potential for large-scale protests or unrest among this population.
