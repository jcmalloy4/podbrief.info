January 8th, 2026: Trump’s Endgame in Venezuela: Forcing Out China, Russia, and Iran & Iranian Protesters Make A Dramatic Plea

Key Takeaways

  • The Trump administration is implementing a strategy to reassert U.S. influence in Venezuela's security and oil sectors.
  • Iranian protesters are appealing to President Trump for help amid a government crackdown on nationwide demonstrations.
  • Kyiv reports "concrete progress" on security guarantees in Paris talks, though territorial integrity issues persist.
  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio states President Trump retains interest in purchasing Greenland for strategic reasons.

Deep Dive

  • The Trump administration's strategy aims to dismantle foreign intelligence networks from China, Russia, Iran, and Cuba.
  • The U.S. seeks to reassert influence over Venezuela's security services and its oil sector.
  • Venezuela's economic recovery is conditioned on severing ties with China and Russia, partnering exclusively with U.S. firms.
  • This approach is described as a test case in great power competition within the Western Hemisphere.

  • Iranian protesters are directly appealing to President Trump, with some reportedly renaming streets after him.
  • Security forces are increasing aggression, deploying armed units and using tear gas in civilian areas.
  • Demonstrations have spread to over 300 locations in 28 provinces, with reports of at least 36 deaths and over 2,100 arrests in 12 days.
  • Exiled opposition figure Reza Pahlavi characterized the protests as a significant opportunity to dismantle the Islamic Republic.

  • Kyiv reported guarded optimism from recent Paris peace talks, focusing on progress regarding post-war security guarantees with allies.
  • Kyiv's chief of staff, Kirillo Budinov, emphasized concrete results from talks, affirming Ukraine's national interests, particularly its territory, will be defended.
  • The U.S. formally backed a coalition pledging binding security guarantees for Ukraine, with White House Special Envoy Steve Witkoff attending.
  • Britain and France expressed readiness to deploy forces post-ceasefire, and all coalition leaders agreed to a U.S.-led ceasefire monitoring mechanism.

  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio informed lawmakers that President Trump has renewed interest in purchasing the Arctic territory of Greenland.
  • President Trump desires to acquire Greenland, not invade it, and has requested updated plans for such an acquisition.
  • Greenland holds strategic importance due to its Arctic location, shipping routes, and mineral wealth.
  • The White House views acquiring Greenland as a national security priority to deter growing Russian and Chinese activity in the Arctic.
  • Leaders from six NATO countries and Denmark have reaffirmed support for Greenland's sovereignty and emphasized collective Arctic security.
