Deep Dive

  • A raccoon was responsible for multiple break-ins in Virginia, targeting a liquor store, various businesses, and a karate studio.
  • The animal was eventually found disoriented and intoxicated.
  • Despite being caught on camera during the liquor store incident, the raccoon was released back into the wild without charges.

  • Nantucket's annual Christmas stroll, a festive tradition, devolved into a street brawl originating at The Boarding House bar.

  • A naked man robbed a meat market in Lake City, Florida, before attempting to flee the scene.
  • Authorities apprehended the individual following the incident.

  • Police in Ramapo, New York, responded to a report of a masked intruder.
  • Officers discovered a deer with a plastic bag over its head.
  • The police successfully removed the plastic bag, ensuring the deer's safety.
