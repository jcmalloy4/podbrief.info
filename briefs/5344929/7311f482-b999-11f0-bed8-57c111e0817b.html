PDB Afternoon Bulletin | November 4th, 2025: Inside The ISIS Inspired Plot Thwarted On U.S. Soil & America’s Longest Shutdown

Key Takeaways

  • Federal agents foiled an ISIS-inspired terror plot targeting LGBTQ bars in Detroit, Michigan.
  • The government shutdown reached 35 days, tying the record for the longest in U.S. history, over healthcare subsidies.
  • FBI intervention prevented a mass casualty attack, highlighting the ongoing threat of online radicalization.
  • Cautious optimism emerged for a government shutdown resolution on Capitol Hill, though specific details remain unclear.

Deep Dive

  • Federal agents thwarted an ISIS-inspired plot by Mohmed Ali and Majed Mahmoud to attack LGBTQ bars near Detroit on Halloween weekend.
  • The alleged plot, codenamed "Pumpkin," involved online extremist propaganda exchanges and plans for a Paris-style assault on crowded nightlife spots.
  • Suspects purchased tactical gear, ammunition, and cameras, practiced shooting, and had stockpiled weapons and body armor.
  • FBI surveillance and intervention before the attack prevented potential mass casualties, highlighting online radicalization risks.
  • The men face federal charges, including conspiracy to transfer firearms for terrorist attacks, with potential life imprisonment if convicted.

  • The U.S. government shutdown reached day 35, tying the record for the longest in American history.
  • The stalemate is primarily over healthcare subsidies for Obamacare, which Democrats demand be extended.
  • Politicians are reportedly concerned about damaging their own political interests, hinting at a potential resolution.

  • The shutdown, which began on October 1st, impacts federal employees, air travel, and SNAP benefits, with some mitigation measures in place.
  • Democrats accuse the White House of politicizing appropriations amid the ongoing impasse.
  • Cautious optimism for a resolution has emerged from Capitol Hill, with Senate Majority Leader John Thune and House Speaker Mike Johnson commenting.
  • Senate Republicans have conducted 14 votes on a House-passed funding bill, all blocked by Democrats, indicating continued legislative gridlock.
