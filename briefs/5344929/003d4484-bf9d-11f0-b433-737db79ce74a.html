November 12th, 2025: Where’s Putin? How Paranoid Vlad Is Hiding His Location & Bombings Rock India and Pakistan

Key Takeaways

  • Russian President Vladimir Putin reportedly uses three identical offices to conceal his movements.
  • Twin bombings in Delhi and Islamabad have reignited tensions between India and Pakistan.
  • The United Kingdom has suspended intelligence sharing with the U.S. over controversial Caribbean drug interdiction strikes.
  • Chinese astronauts are facing a delayed return to Earth due to accumulating space debris in orbit.

Deep Dive

  • Investigators discovered Russian President Vladimir Putin operates from three nearly identical offices.
  • These offices are reportedly designed to conceal his true whereabouts, even from his own government.

  • Twin bombings in Delhi and Islamabad within 24 hours killed a total of 25 people, escalating regional tensions between India and Pakistan.
  • The Delhi bombing killed 13 and wounded over 20, with authorities investigating a transnational terror module linked to Kashmiri doctors and the Pakistan-based Jaisheh Mohammed group.
  • A suicide bombing in Islamabad killed 12; Pakistan accused India, which denied involvement, amid a disavowed claim by a Pakistani Taliban splinter group.

  • The United Kingdom suspended intelligence sharing with the U.S. regarding drug trafficking in the Caribbean due to concerns over U.S. strikes.
  • U.S. strikes have killed at least 76 suspected traffickers since September, raising UK concerns about breaches of international law and extrajudicial killings.
  • The U.S. reclassified drug cartels as foreign terrorist organizations, enabling lethal force under wartime authorities, a doctrine also causing Canada to limit intelligence sharing.

  • China's Shenzhou 18 astronauts face a delayed return to Earth from their Tiangong space station due to space debris in their re-entry path.
  • The crew remains in good condition in orbit, awaiting a safe return window, which impacts the subsequent Shenzhou-19 launch.
  • Low Earth orbit conditions are worsening due to anti-satellite tests and satellite breakups, with U.S. Space Command tracking over 40,000 debris pieces.
