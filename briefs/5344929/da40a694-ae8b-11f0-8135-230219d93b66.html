PDB Afternoon Bulletin | October 21st, 2025: REVEALED: CIA Directing Narco-Boat Strikes Off Venezuela & Europe Endorses Trump’s Ceasefire Plan

Key Takeaways

  • The CIA is leading US counter-drug operations in the Caribbean, targeting ELN-affiliated vessels.
  • This covert involvement signifies a militarization of the war on drugs against the Maduro regime in Venezuela.
  • European powers support President Trump's push for a Ukraine ceasefire, advocating negotiations along current battle lines.
  • Russia maintains its demands for Ukraine to withdraw from the contested Donetsk and Luhansk regions.

Deep Dive

  • The U.S. Navy has executed its seventh strike since September against suspected drug trafficking boats in the Caribbean, allegedly linked to Colombia's ELN.
  • The ELN, designated a terrorist organization by the U.S. State Department since 1997, is known for smuggling narcotics and weapons and has received protection from Venezuela's security forces.
  • An investigation reveals the CIA is providing real-time intelligence, including satellite surveillance and signal intercepts, to identify and target these drug-carrying vessels.
  • This expanded CIA involvement aligns with a secret Trump administration authorization for covert action inside Venezuela, aiming for aggressive operations against the Maduro regime and its allied cartels.
  • At least 32 people have been killed in strikes conducted off Venezuela's coast.

  • European leaders, including London, Paris, and Berlin, are aligning behind President Trump's proposal for a ceasefire in Ukraine.
  • They advocate for negotiations based on current battle lines, deeming this the most viable path to peace.
  • The White House has paused plans for a second summit between President Trump and Vladimir Putin due to a wide gap between Moscow and Kyiv.
  • European capitals express caution regarding potential Trump-Putin summits, fearing a lack of meaningful concessions following prior meetings that yielded no significant progress.
  • Reporting indicates a tense meeting where President Trump allegedly urged Ukrainian President Zelensky to accept Russian demands, including withdrawal from the Donbas, a claim Trump denies.

  • While President Trump denies pressing Ukraine to cede territory, Ukrainian President Zelensky stated that Trump's envoy conveyed Russia's position.
  • The Donbas region has re-emerged as a critical point of contention, with Russia insisting Ukraine withdraw from Donetsk and Luhansk, areas it claims.
  • Ukraine questions the logic of ceding territory based on Russia's constitutional claims.
  • President Trump advocates for an immediate halt to fighting along current battle lines, suggesting negotiations to resolve territorial disputes later.
  • The Kremlin has reiterated its unchanged stance, signaling a desire for more than merely a frozen conflict.
