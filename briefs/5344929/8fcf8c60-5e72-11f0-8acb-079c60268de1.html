PDB Afternoon Bulletin | July 1th, 2025: 'Bomb The Sh*t Out Of Moscow': Trump’s Private Warning To Putin & Secret Service Suspends Agents Over Assassination Attempt On Trump

Podcast Executive Summary: Trump, Secret Service, and Geopolitical Tensions

Topic: Analysis of alleged Trump threats, Secret Service failures, and related news.

Overview

  • Newly released audio suggests President Trump threatened to bomb Moscow if Russia invaded Ukraine, a claim denied by Putin's spokesperson.
  • The Secret Service faced significant criticism for its handling of a failed assassination attempt on President Trump, resulting in suspensions and a resignation.

Content

Trump's alleged threats

  • Allegations that President Trump threatened to bomb Moscow and other locations during conversations with foreign leaders emerge through newly released audio recordings. Putin's spokesperson denies the calls took place.

Secret Service Failures and Aftermath

  • Six Secret Service agents face suspension for systemic failures during a failed assassination attempt on President Trump. The suspensions range from 10-42 days of unpaid leave, prompting criticism of the insufficient punishment and highlighting internal disagreements within the agency.

Conclusion

The podcast concludes with a discussion of the Secret Service failures, highlighting insufficient punishment and internal frustrations. Ongoing reforms are underway, though criticism remains. The episode ends with promotional information and a preview of an upcoming special report on Putin.
