PDB Afternoon Bulletin | April 24th. 2025: FBI: Maduro Weaponizing Gangs Against America & Trump Unloads on Putin & Zelensky

Key takeaways:

  • FBI alleges Venezuelan officials facilitated migration of violent TDA gang members into U.S.
  • Russia launched massive missile and drone attack on Kyiv, killing at least 12 people
Venezuelan Government Accused of Aiding Violent Gang The FBI has alleged that top Venezuelan officials have been facilitating the migration of members of the violent Tranda Aragua (TDA) gang into the U.S. A senior Trump administration official shared the findings, stating the Maduro regime is using TDA as a proxy force to sow chaos on American streets. The FBI warns Venezuelan officials may leverage their relationship with TDA to target regime critics in the U.S. and South America.

Russian Attack on Kyiv Russia unleashed a large-scale missile and drone attack on Kyiv overnight, killing at least 12 people and wounding over 90. The attack, described as one of the deadliest in over three years of war, came hours after peace talks between Washington and Kyiv appeared to fall apart. President Trump condemned the strikes on social media, calling them unnecessary and poorly timed.

Stalled Peace Negotiations The latest U.S. peace proposal for Ukraine, which would grant Russia control over Crimea and end Ukraine's NATO aspirations, was rejected by President Zelensky as unconstitutional. The Trump administration has taken a hardline stance, with Vice President Vance calling it the "final offer" and Trump warning Ukraine risks losing more territory without a deal. However, diplomatic efforts have stalled, with U.S. officials skipping planned talks in London.

Trump Administration's Approach to Ukraine Conflict The Trump administration's peace proposal marks a sharp break from previous U.S. policy on Crimea. Their approach prioritizes securing a quick deal to fulfill campaign promises, rather than considering broader geopolitical implications. This domestic politics-focused strategy clashes with Ukraine's stance and traditional Western approaches to the conflict, complicating negotiations and potentially prolonging hostilities.
