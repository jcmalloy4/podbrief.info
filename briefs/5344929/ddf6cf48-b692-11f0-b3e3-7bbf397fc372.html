PDB Afternoon Bulletin | October 31st, 2025: REPORT: U.S. Military Attack On Venezuela Is Imminent & FBI Foils ISIS-Inspired Halloween Terror Attack

Key Takeaways

  • U.S. reportedly considering airstrikes in Venezuela against drug cartels linked to Nicolas Maduro.
  • The FBI thwarted an alleged ISIS-inspired terror plot in Michigan over Halloween weekend.
  • The Trump administration increased pressure on Maduro regime, including $50 million reward for his capture.
  • U.S. military deployment to the Caribbean aids campaign against Venezuelan military and cartel networks.

Deep Dive

  • The U.S. is reportedly preparing airstrikes against Venezuela's military targets to dismantle the 'Cartel of the Suns' drug network, linked to Nicolas Maduro.
  • The White House has denied these reports, with a spokeswoman stating unnamed sources are misinformed.
  • The Trump administration estimates the Maduro regime moves approximately 500 tons of cocaine annually, with half destined for the U.S.
  • The U.S. previously doubled the reward for information leading to Maduro's capture to $50 million, with additional rewards for other regime figures.

  • Sources indicate the broader U.S. goal is to cripple Venezuela's 'Cartel of the Suns' through targeted operations, similar to the strike on Qasem Soleimani.
  • Recent U.S. military buildup in the Caribbean, including the USS Gerald R. Ford, is part of a campaign to neutralize Venezuela's military and cartel networks.
  • Traffickers are reportedly abandoning sea routes, leading to a shift toward potential inland operations.
  • Potential risks include regional backlash from Latin American governments and condemnation from nations like Russia and China.
  • A Russian transport aircraft linked to the Wagner mercenary group reportedly landed in Caracas, suggesting potential support for Maduro from the Putin regime.

  • The FBI thwarted an alleged ISIS-inspired terror plot in Michigan, planned for Halloween weekend.
  • Five individuals, aged 16 to 20, were arrested in Dearborn and Inkster following simultaneous raids.
  • Suspects were reportedly discussing an attack, referred to as 'pumpkin day,' and had access to guns.
  • FBI Director Cash Patel credited the swift action of agents and law enforcement partners for disrupting the plot.
  • Court documents remain sealed, and the investigation is ongoing.
