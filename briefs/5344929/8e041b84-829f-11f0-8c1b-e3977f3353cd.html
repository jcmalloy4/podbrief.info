PDB Afternoon Bulletin | August 26th, 2025: 'Very Angry' Trump Lashes Out At Putin After Russian Strikes & Iranian Terror in Australia

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump expressed frustration with Russian President Putin over ongoing aerial attacks on Ukraine.
  • The U.S. is supplying Ukraine with over 3,300 extended-range attack munitions (ERAMS).
  • Australia severed diplomatic ties with Iran and designated the IRGC a terrorist organization.

Deep Dive

  • Australia severed diplomatic ties with Iran, expelling its ambassador and three other diplomats.
  • The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) was designated a terrorist organization.
  • This action follows allegations Iran orchestrated antisemitic attacks in Australia, including a synagogue firebombing.
  • Iran rejected the accusations as baseless and promised reciprocal action.

  • President Trump expressed frustration with Russian President Putin over ongoing aerial attacks on Ukrainian cities.
  • Trump believes Putin's actions are undermining diplomatic efforts towards a peace deal.
  • Personal dislike between Putin and Ukrainian President Zelenskyy was cited as a barrier to peace.

  • The U.S. is supplying Ukraine with over 3,300 extended-range attack munitions (ERAMS).
  • These munitions are intended to enhance Ukraine's strike capabilities.
  • Pentagon approval is required for use against certain targets, potentially limiting strikes deep inside Russia.
