June 5th, 2025: How Ukraine Used Artificial Intelligence To Strike Russia & Russia’s One Million Casualties

Key Takeaways

  • Ukraine executed a devastating AI-guided drone strike across 4 Russian military bases using 117 drones, requiring 18 months of planning and potentially disabling 34% of Russia's cruise missile bomber fleet—demonstrating unprecedented operational sophistication in modern warfare.
  • The Ukraine-Russia conflict has become a catastrophic war of attrition with Russian casualties expected to reach 1 million by summer 2024, while both sides engage in WWI-style trench warfare with Russia losing approximately 1,000 troops daily.
  • A domestic terrorism attack in Colorado highlights rising ideological violence in the U.S., as Muhammad Solomon firebombed pro-Israel demonstrators while shouting "Free Palestine," facing up to 624 years in prison amid a broader surge of over 10,000 anti-Semitic incidents since October 2023.
  • China continues systematic suppression of Tiananmen Square anniversary commemorations, maintaining comprehensive censorship and detention tactics 35 years after the 1989 crackdown that killed hundreds of pro-democracy demonstrators.

Deep Dive

Ukraine's Sophisticated Drone Strike on Russian Territory

Ukraine's security service (SBU) executed a highly sophisticated drone attack on Russian military infrastructure, demonstrating unprecedented operational capabilities:

Attack Scale and Execution:

  • Deployed 117 drones across 4 Russian military bases inside Russian territory
  • Struck targets deep within Russia, including a base in Siberia
  • Utilized artificial intelligence to guide drones, enabling mission continuation even if remote signals were lost
  • Required 18 months of meticulous planning
Operational Sophistication:
  • Drones were assembled inside Russia, remarkably near an FSB headquarters
  • Transportation involved container trucks equipped with remote-activated false tops
  • Coordinated simultaneous launches at multiple strategic targets
Damage Assessment:
  • Ukraine claims to have disabled 34% of Russia's cruise missile bomber fleet
  • Estimated $7 billion in damage inflicted
  • Ukrainian reports: 41 aircraft hit
  • Independent verification: 13 aircraft confirmed hit, including 11 strategic bombers
Strategic Response:
  • Russia downplayed the attack's significance, claiming minimal damage and no personnel injuries
  • Putin was informed of the "incident" and ordered investigation as potential terrorist act
  • Zelensky suggested the attack could have been avoided with an earlier ceasefire

Escalating War Casualties and Battlefield Dynamics

The conflict has evolved into a devastating war of attrition with staggering human costs:

Casualty Figures (CSIS Analysis):

  • Russian military casualties expected to reach 1 million by summer 2024
  • Approximately 250,000 Russian troops killed since February 2022
  • Ukraine has suffered around 400,000 casualties, including up to 100,000 killed
  • Russia currently losing about 1,000 troops daily
Battlefield Characteristics:
  • Conflict has devolved into World War I-style trench warfare
  • Russia employing "meat grinder" tactics of mass infantry assaults
  • Since January 2024, Russia has captured only 1,930 square miles (approximately 1% of Ukraine)
Recent Military Actions:
  • Russia launched 95 Iranian-designed drones, killing 10 civilians
  • Ukraine conducted "Operation Spiderweb," targeting Russian air bases with drone swarms
Strategic Outlook:
  • Kremlin believes it can outlast Ukraine due to superior population and industrial capacity
  • Former MI6 chief Richard Dearlove predicts Russian military capabilities will decline from summer 2024
  • Peace negotiations remain stalled, with Russia demanding Ukraine's unconditional surrender

Domestic Terrorism: Colorado Attack and Rising Ideological Violence

A significant domestic terrorism incident has highlighted growing concerns about imported ideological violence:

Attack Details:

  • Muhammad Solomon allegedly conducted a firebombing attack on pro-Israel demonstrators in Boulder
  • Entered the U.S. in August 2022 on temporary visa, filed asylum application, and secured work authorization
  • Attacked rally while shouting "Free Palestine," injuring 12 people
  • Stated intention to "Kill all Zionist people"
Legal Consequences:
  • Facing 16 counts of attempted first-degree murder
  • 2 counts of using an incendiary device
  • Potential maximum sentence of 624 years if convicted
Federal Response:
  • DHS Secretary Kristi Noem announced investigation into potential family complicity
  • Federal authorities have taken Solomon's family into custody
  • Family facing deportation and visa revocation
  • Officials investigating Solomon's network and motivations
Broader Pattern of Violence:
  • Attack represents part of sharp increase in ideologically motivated violence against Jews since Hamas' October 7, 2023 attack
  • Anti-Defamation League reports over 10,000 anti-Semitic incidents in U.S. since that date
  • Simultaneously, anti-Muslim hate incidents rising, with 8,600 complaints recorded in 2024
  • Ted Deutch (American Jewish Committee): "This is a war against people who support Israel. It's a war against the Jewish people"

Tiananmen Square Anniversary and China's Historical Censorship

The discussion concluded with reflection on the 1989 Tiananmen Square incident and Beijing's continued suppression efforts:

Historical Context:

  • Hundreds, possibly over 1,000 unarmed demonstrators were killed in the 1989 crackdown
  • The iconic "tank man" photo remains a powerful symbol of resistance
  • Chinese Communist Party continues comprehensive suppression of information about the event
Current Commemorative Tensions:
  • U.S. Senator Marco Rubio issued a statement marking the anniversary
  • China's foreign ministry responded by accusing Rubio of distorting facts and interfering in internal affairs
Beijing's Ongoing Suppression Tactics:
  • Annual lockdown of Tiananmen Square during anniversary periods
  • Systematic detention of potential dissidents
  • Comprehensive scrubbing of internet references to the event
  • Prevention of public discussion and remembrance activities
The segment emphasized the critical importance of preserving historical memory against the CCP's systematic attempts to erase this pivotal moment in modern Chinese history.
