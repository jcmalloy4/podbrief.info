December 26th, 2025: Cuba Is On The Verge Of Total Collapse & Fighting Erupts In Syria

Key Takeaways

  • Cuba faces an economic collapse and fuel crisis due to U.S. oil sanctions targeting Venezuela, increasing emigration.
  • The Trump administration's recall of nearly 30 career U.S. ambassadors has created diplomatic gaps and drawn criticism.
  • Syria experiences renewed instability, with gun battles in Aleppo and stalled negotiations regarding Kurdish forces.

Deep Dive

  • U.S. pressure on Venezuela's oil trade causes severe fuel shortages in Cuba, leading to widespread blackouts and increased emigration.
  • The situation strains essential services on the island, highlighting unintended consequences of sanctions.

  • The Trump administration recalled nearly 30 career foreign service ambassadors, appointed under the Biden administration, by mid-January.
  • The State Department cited presidential authority to ensure alignment with the 'America First' agenda.
  • The American Foreign Service Association criticized this as an unprecedented mass recall, creating diplomatic gaps.

  • Gun battles erupted in Aleppo between Syrian forces and Kurdish-led SDF, resulting in casualties.
  • Negotiations to integrate Kurdish forces into state institutions have stalled ahead of a March 10 deadline.
  • Turkey's foreign minister visited Damascus, complicating integration talks due to Turkey's opposition to the SDF.
