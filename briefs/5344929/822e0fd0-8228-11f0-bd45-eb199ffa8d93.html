August 26th, 2025: How China Is Rigging Elections in America’s Biggest City & Coast Guard Mega-Seizure

Key Takeaways

  • China's political machine is reportedly manipulating elections in New York City.
  • The U.S. Coast Guard completed its largest-ever drug offload, valued at $473 million.
  • DNI Tulsi Gabbard reportedly restricted Ukraine intelligence sharing with key allies.
  • An Israeli airstrike on a Gaza hospital killed 20 people, including 5 journalists.

Deep Dive

  • A new report details how China's political machine manipulates elections in New York City.
  • Community groups and social media are used to favor pro-Beijing candidates and silence critics.
  • Tax-exempt nonprofits are cited as being used in political activities to further Beijing's influence.

  • The U.S. Coast Guard offloaded 76,140 pounds of illicit narcotics, valued at nearly $500 million, in Port Everglades, Florida.
  • The record-breaking seizure included 61,740 pounds of cocaine and 14,400 pounds of marijuana.
  • This resulted from 19 interdictions in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific.
  • Organized crime groups in Venezuela, specifically the TDA gang, reportedly transported the drugs.
  • The White House deployed naval assets, including guided missile destroyers, off Venezuela in response to the Maduro regime, described as a 'narco-terror cartel'.

  • Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard reportedly restricted access to U.S. intelligence on Russia-Ukraine peace efforts for key allies.
  • Britain, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand were affected, potentially weakening the Five Eyes alliance.
  • A DNI spokeswoman denied the action, stating intelligence sharing relationships remain strong.
  • Former officials expressed concern that this could damage trust and lead to retaliatory actions, undermining the nearly eight-decade-old Five Eyes alliance.
  • Counterarguments suggest intelligence restrictions and 'no-foreign' classifications are common when national interests diverge.

  • An Israeli airstrike on Nasser Hospital in southern Gaza resulted in at least 20 deaths, including five international journalists.
  • The Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) acknowledged a strike in the area, citing civilian casualties and a complex operational reality.
  • The IDF accused Hamas of using hospitals for cover; Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu called it a 'tragic mishap' and promised an investigation.
  • President Trump stated he was unaware but expressed unhappiness, calling for an end to the 'nightmare' in Gaza.
  • The Foreign Press Association condemned the strike, noting at least 192 journalists were killed in the conflict prior to this incident.
