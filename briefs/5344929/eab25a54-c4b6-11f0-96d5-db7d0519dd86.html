PDB Afternoon Bulletin | November 18th, 2025: Russian Oil Sector Continues To Collapse & Berlin Reopens Arms Flow To Israel

Key Takeaways

  • Russia's oil sector is facing significant pressure from Ukrainian drone attacks and new U.S. sanctions.
  • Germany will lift its three-month freeze on arms exports to Israel, citing improved ceasefire conditions in Gaza.

Deep Dive

  • Russia's oil sector faces dual pressure from Ukrainian drone strikes and new U.S. sanctions.
  • Ukrainian drones are hitting targets deep inside Russia, impacting oil infrastructure.
  • New U.S. sanctions are cutting off key buyers of Russian oil in India and China.
  • This combined pressure is posing a serious problem for Moscow's war economy.

  • Germany is preparing to lift its three-month freeze on arms exports to Israel.
  • The decision cites improved Gaza ceasefire conditions and upholds long-standing commitments.
  • Effective November 24th, Germany will revert to case-by-case arms export reviews for Israel.
  • Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Saar welcomed Germany's move and urged other nations to follow.
  • Germany's foreign minister described the decision as responsible and correct.
