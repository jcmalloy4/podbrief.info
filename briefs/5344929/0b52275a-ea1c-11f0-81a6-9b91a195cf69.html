January 5th, 2026: Inside The Delta Force Raid That Captured Maduro & Iranian Protests Continue To Grow

Key Takeaways

  • Covert CIA teams and Delta Force operations led to the capture of Nicolás Maduro after months of tracking and raid rehearsals.
  • Newly unsealed federal charges accuse Nicolás Maduro and his inner circle of narco-terrorism and cocaine trafficking over 25 years.
  • Economic protests in Iran have resulted in at least 16 deaths amid a harsh government crackdown nationwide.
  • Britain and France conducted a joint airstrike on Islamic State weapons facilities in Syria to counter resurgence.

Deep Dive

  • Covert CIA teams tracked Nicolás Maduro's movements for months in Venezuela, gathering detailed intelligence since August.
  • Delta Force, the U.S. military's high-value target unit, rehearsed the raid extensively on a full-scale replica of Maduro's safe house.
  • U.S. Army helicopters, flown by the Night Stalkers, executed the mission, supported by aircraft disabling air defenses.
  • Maduro was reportedly caught by surprise and surrendered at his Caracas compound around 1 a.m. Eastern, unable to reach his safe room.
  • The operation, named Absolute Resolve, concluded by 3:20 a.m. Eastern with no American fatalities, despite hostile engagements during extraction.

  • Maduro's capture marks a first step; Venezuela's future hinges on a democratic transition or the persistence of the existing power structure.
  • The current Venezuelan military and security apparatus may resist ceding power, particularly with Vice President Delcy Rodríguez sworn in as president.
  • The Venezuelan people appear elated by Maduro's capture, but the transition could be complicated if existing security forces do not cooperate.

  • Newly unsealed federal charges detail 25 years of narco-terrorism and drug trafficking allegations against Nicolás Maduro and his inner circle.
  • Prosecutors allege a coordinated network within Venezuela's leadership actively enabled and profited from cocaine shipments to the U.S.
  • Charges include narco-terrorism, cocaine importation conspiracy, and weapons offenses, allegedly used to enforce the trafficking operation through violence.
  • Specific allegations include Maduro selling passports to traffickers and authorizing illicit fund flights while he was foreign minister.
  • Attorney General Barr indicated Maduro will face justice in American courts, with a first appearance expected in New York City, potentially facing decades in prison.

  • Protests in Iran, driven by inflation and economic strain, have intensified, resulting in at least 16 deaths and over 580 arrests.
  • Human rights monitors report rising fatalities and detentions as clashes with security forces escalate, involving the use of live ammunition.
  • Iranian Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei characterized the protests as riots instigated by 'enemy mercenaries,' signaling a harsh government response.
  • Demonstrations have spread to over 100 locations across 22 provinces, with reports of violent dispersals and alleged casualties.
  • President Trump posted a message of solidarity with Iranian protesters, stating the U.S. would intervene if the regime uses lethal force.

  • Britain and France conducted a joint airstrike in Syria targeting an Islamic State weapons depot north of Palmyra.
  • Intelligence indicated the facility was used for storing weapons and explosives by the Islamic State.
  • This operation is part of ongoing efforts by Western allies to prevent ISIS resurgence and keep the terror group off balance.
