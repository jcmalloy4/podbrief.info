PDB Afternoon Bulletin | January 5th, 2026: Iran’s Supreme Leader Is Planning To Flee Tehran & Ukraine’s Power Shakeup

Key Takeaways

  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has appointed military intelligence chief Kyrylo Budanov to run his presidential office.

  • Budanov's new role is presented as a major shakeup at the highest levels of power in Ukraine.
