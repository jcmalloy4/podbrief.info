PDB Afternoon Bulletin | November 25th, 2025: China’s Taiwan Invasion Rehearsal Caught On Satellite & U.S.-Russia Face-To-Face Talks

Key Takeaways

  • China conducted amphibious drills simulating a Taiwan invasion, integrating civilian ferries into military exercises.
  • The United States engaged in rare face-to-face talks with Russian officials in Abu Dhabi regarding a Ukraine peace framework.
  • US and Ukrainian negotiators established a common understanding on a revised peace framework, though territorial issues persist.
  • A potential visit by Ukrainian President Zelensky to the U.S. aims to finalize a peace deal.

Deep Dive

  • New satellite imagery reveals China practicing beach-landing maneuvers required for a Taiwan invasion.
  • Drills off Guangdong province utilized civilian ferries, integrating commercial shipping into military exercises.
  • Analysts state this marks an escalation in Beijing's invasion preparations by addressing previous lift capacity limitations.

  • US Army Secretary Dan Driscoll met with Russian officials in Abu Dhabi regarding a Ukraine peace framework.
  • This "shuttle diplomacy" follows revised 19-point peace plan discussions between US and Ukrainian negotiators in Geneva.
  • The US and Ukraine are aligned on a revised framework, but territorial concessions remain a key issue for direct leader discussions.

  • Ukrainian President Zelensky is expected to visit the US to finalize the revised peace deal.
  • No specific date has been set for this visit.
  • Progress is hampered by ongoing Russian attacks and skepticism regarding Moscow's commitment to negotiations.
