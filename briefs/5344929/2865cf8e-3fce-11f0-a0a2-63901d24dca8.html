PDB Afternoon Bulletin | June 2nd, 2025: New Details Revealed About ‘Russia’s Pearl Harbor’ & A Terrorist In Boulder

Key Takeaways

  • Ukraine executed an unprecedented deep-strike operation using 117 individually piloted drones launched from disguised shipping containers built inside Russia, targeting strategic bomber aircraft and claiming $7 billion in damage to Russia's nuclear-capable fleet.
  • The Ukrainian drone operation required 18 months of meticulous planning and was conducted near FSB headquarters, demonstrating sophisticated intelligence capabilities and marking a significant escalation in Ukraine's ability to strike deep into Russian territory.
  • A targeted terror attack in Boulder, Colorado specifically aimed at a pro-Israel demonstration resulted in eight injuries when an illegal immigrant used Molotov cocktails and a makeshift flamethrower against participants calling for hostage releases during a Jewish holiday.
  • The Boulder attack represents part of a broader pattern of anti-Semitic violence across the United States, with the FBI investigating it as "ideologically motivated violence" amid warnings about the volatile threat environment facing Jewish communities.

Deep Dive

Ukraine's Strategic Drone Operation Against Russian Airfields

Ukrainian forces executed a massive and sophisticated drone attack deep inside Russian territory, representing one of the most significant operations of its kind in the conflict. The operation involved 117 individually piloted drones launched from modified shipping containers that were disguised and transported on trucks. Remarkably, these containers were reportedly built or assembled inside Russia itself, with the truck drivers allegedly unaware of their actual cargo.

Operation Details and Outcomes:

  • The attack targeted Russia's strategic bomber fleet, focusing on nuclear-capable aircraft
  • Ukrainian sources claimed significant success:
- 41 Russian aircraft reportedly hit - 34% of Russia's cruise missile bomber fleet allegedly disabled - Approximately $7 billion in estimated damage
  • Primary targets included Tu-95 and Tu-22M-3 bombers, as well as an A-50 early warning aircraft
Strategic Significance:
  • The operation required 18 months of careful planning
  • Conducted near FSB headquarters, highlighting its audacious nature
  • Ukraine's SBU (Security Service) claimed responsibility for the attack
  • President Zelensky emphasized the operation's boldness and strategic importance
Russian Response:
  • The Defense Ministry confirmed the attacks occurred but claimed all strikes were successfully repelled
  • Russian officials characterized the attacks as "terrorist" actions
  • Despite the military escalation, ongoing peace talks in Istanbul continue, though expectations remain low

Terror Attack in Boulder, Colorado

A targeted terror attack occurred in Boulder, Colorado, specifically aimed at a pro-Israel demonstration. The perpetrator, Mohammed Sabri Solomon (45 years old), attacked participants at the "Run for Their Lives" demonstration on Pearl Street Mall near Denver. The event was calling for the release of Israeli hostages and took place during the Jewish holiday of Shavuot.

Attack Details:

  • Solomon employed Molotov cocktails and a makeshift flamethrower as weapons
  • In a twist of irony, he accidentally set himself on fire during the attack
  • Eight victims, ranging in age from 52 to 88, sustained injuries from minor to critical
  • No fatalities were reported
Legal and Security Response:
  • Solomon was arrested at the scene and faces serious charges including:
- First-degree murder - First-degree assault - Unlawful use of explosive devices
  • Bond was set at $10 million
  • Authorities confirmed Solomon was in the U.S. illegally
Broader Implications:
  • The Israeli government condemned the incident as a "vicious terror attack"
  • The FBI is investigating the case as "ideologically motivated violence"
  • The attack fits into a concerning pattern of recent anti-Semitic incidents across the United States
  • Authorities have issued warnings about the volatile threat environment currently facing Jewish communities
