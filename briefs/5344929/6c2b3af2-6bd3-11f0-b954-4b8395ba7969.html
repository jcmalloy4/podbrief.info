PDB Afternoon Bulletin | July 28th, 2025: Trump Steamrolls Europe On Tariffs & Issues A New Deadline To Putin

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump finalized a historic trade deal with the European Union, averting a transatlantic trade war.
  • Trump delivered Russia a strict 10-12 day ultimatum for a Ukraine ceasefire, threatening severe economic consequences.

Deep Dives

EU Trade Deal

  • The agreement, a result of prolonged pressure, includes a 15% tariff on EU imports and substantial U.S. energy and defense sales.
  • This strategic move, hailed as "the art of the deal," averted a potential 30% tariff, targeting sectors like automobiles, semiconductors, and pharmaceuticals.

Russia Ultimatum

  • President Trump issued a firm 10-12 day deadline for a Ukraine ceasefire, resetting a previous 50-day timeline due to frustration with Putin.
  • Non-compliance threatens a 100% tariff on Russia and its trading partners, aiming for economic isolation, a stance Ukraine supports as "peace through strength."
