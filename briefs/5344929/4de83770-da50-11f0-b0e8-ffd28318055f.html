December 16th, 2025: New Year’s Eve Terror Plot Foiled in the U.S. & Ukraine’s Underwater Drone Strike

Key Takeaways

  • Federal authorities disrupted a New Year's Eve terror plot involving pipe bombs in Southern California.
  • Ukraine claims an underwater drone strike critically damaged a Russian submarine in the Black Sea.
  • An Israeli strike killed a senior Hamas commander, drawing U.S. criticism for alleged ceasefire violation.
  • The Brown University shooting investigation continues with a new person of interest sought by police.

Deep Dive

  • Federal authorities disrupted a New Year's Eve terror plot involving four suspects.
  • The suspects allegedly planned coordinated pipe bomb attacks targeting Southern California logistics facilities.
  • The plot aimed to cause mass casualties and significant disruption.

  • Ukraine claims an underwater drone critically damaged a Russian Kilo-class submarine.
  • The incident occurred in Novorossiysk, a Black Sea port.
  • Video shows an explosion consistent with a subsurface detonation, though external confirmation is pending.
  • If verified, this event could significantly alter modern naval warfare tactics.

  • The White House criticized an Israeli strike that killed senior Hamas commander Rayad Saad.
  • The U.S. deemed the strike a violation of the Trump administration-brokered ceasefire.
  • Israel stated the strike was retaliatory for a recent bomb attack, claiming Saad was rebuilding Hamas's military infrastructure.
  • The White House privately conveyed its frustration to Prime Minister Netanyahu regarding the strike and stalled progress in Gaza.

  • Authorities continue the investigation into the Brown University shooting, having released a person of interest.
  • Evidence now points in a new direction, with a new person of interest described as a male, approximately 5'8" and stocky.
  • The attack resulted in two student fatalities and nine wounded individuals.
  • Police released clearer video footage, and the FBI is offering a reward for information as the suspect remains at large.
  • The university canceled all remaining classes and final exams due to ongoing concerns.
