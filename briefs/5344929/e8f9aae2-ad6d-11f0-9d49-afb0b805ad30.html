October 20th, 2025: CEASEFIRE BREAKS: IDF Launches Airstrikes In Gaza After Hamas Attacks

Key Takeaways

  • Israel launched airstrikes in Gaza, accusing Hamas of violating a Trump-brokered ceasefire.
  • President Trump remains non-committal on sending Tomahawk missiles to Ukraine, desiring to end the war.
  • Pakistan and Afghanistan agreed to a new immediate ceasefire after over a week of border fighting.
  • Historic flooding has devastated remote Alaskan communities, prompting evacuations and disaster requests.

Deep Dive

  • Israel conducted airstrikes in Gaza after accusing Hamas of a "bold violation" of a Trump-brokered ceasefire, though Hamas denied the violation.
  • Israel closed the Rafah crossing due to Hamas's delay in returning hostage bodies.
  • Hamas is accused of internal purges and public executions of rivals, stated by officials as an effort to reassert control.
  • A senior Hamas official stated the group cannot commit to disarming, contradicting the Trump ceasefire framework.
  • The initial phase of the ceasefire, intended for civilian relief and hostage release, resulted in violence and recriminations, deemed a "clear fail."

  • President Trump met Ukrainian President Zelensky to discuss sending Tomahawk missiles to Ukraine, but no decision was made.
  • Trump's tone shifted after a call with Russian President Vladimir Putin, expressing a desire to end the war rather than escalate it.
  • Trump proposed a peace plan to freeze current battle lines, which Putin reportedly dismissed as "damaging to peace."
  • Trump stated the U.S. cannot deplete its own arsenal and expressed uncertainty about further aid to Ukraine; Zelensky seemed to accept these limitations.
  • European capitals, including London, Berlin, and Paris, reaffirmed support for Ukraine, viewing the U.S. White House meeting as a setback.

  • Pakistan and Afghanistan have agreed to an immediate ceasefire after over a week of cross-border clashes, mediated by Qatar and Turkey.
  • Both nations pledged to halt hostilities and work towards stability, though previous truces have failed.
  • The new agreement includes commitments to not support groups attacking the opposing government and to refrain from striking security forces or civilians.
  • Disputes over alleged airstrikes and the sheltering of militant groups have persisted, contributing to previous truce failures.

  • Historic flooding has torn through remote communities in western Alaska, submerging villages and causing widespread destruction.
  • Hundreds of residents have been forced to evacuate their homes due to the severe conditions.
  • Governor Mike Dunleavey has requested a presidential disaster declaration for the affected areas.
