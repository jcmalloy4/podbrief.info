December 18th, 2025: China Accused of Accessing Sensitive U.S. Nuclear Research & A European Ukraine Force

Key Takeaways

  • A congressional report indicates China may be tapping into U.S.-funded research for nuclear technology.
  • European leaders are discussing a multinational force for Ukraine following a potential ceasefire.
  • An MIT professor's murder in Massachusetts is being investigated for possible Iranian involvement.
  • New surveillance footage was released in the Brown University shooting, identifying a second person of interest.

Deep Dive

  • A congressional report indicates China acquires sensitive nuclear technology via U.S.-funded research programs.
  • Investigators found collaborations between Department of Energy-funded scientists and Chinese institutions linked to China's military.
  • Legislation to block federal funding to collaborations with foreign adversaries passed the House but was not included in the annual defense policy package.

  • European leaders are discussing a potential multinational force for Ukraine following a ceasefire.
  • Germany's Chancellor suggested Western troops could be authorized to push back Russian forces if a ceasefire is violated.
  • The plan includes substantial military support, economic recovery aid, and consideration of using frozen Russian assets for reparations.

  • MIT fusion professor Nuno Luero was found shot and later died in his Brookline, Massachusetts home.
  • Israeli officials are reviewing preliminary intelligence suggesting possible Iranian involvement due to Luero's sensitive fusion research and pro-Israel statements.
  • The investigation is active with no arrests; authorities emphasize no determinations have been made regarding Iranian connection.

  • Authorities released new surveillance footage in the Brown University shooting, where two students died and nine were injured in a December 13th attack.
  • A second person of interest was identified near the primary suspect before the incident, and authorities are seeking public assistance.
  • The FBI offers a $50,000 reward for information on the main suspect, whose identity remains unclear amid challenges like misinformation and lack of clear footage.
