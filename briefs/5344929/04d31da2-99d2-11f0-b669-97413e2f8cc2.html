September 25th, 2025: Fuel Shortages In Russia Threaten Putin’s Grip & Iran’s Missile Trouble

Key Takeaways

  • Ukraine's drone attacks on Russian oil refineries are causing nationwide fuel shortages.
  • Iran is rebuilding missile factories but faces critical challenges in producing solid fuel.
  • The Trump administration reports 2 million illegal immigrants removed in 8 months, citing an end to open borders.
  • Central American gang Barrio 18 has been designated a foreign terrorist organization by the Trump administration.

Deep Dive

  • Ukraine's drone strikes on Russian oil refineries are causing fuel shortages across Russia.
  • At least 10 major refineries have been targeted, leading to potential drops in refining capacity.
  • Fuel availability is impacted in regions like Crimea and is spreading westward.

  • Iran is rebuilding missile factories destroyed by Israel, focusing on solid fuel production but lacking industrial mixers.
  • Iran has depleted one-third of its estimated 2,500-missile arsenal in 90 days, firing over 900 ballistic missiles.
  • Production of over 200 solid-fuel missiles per month is frozen, requiring external assistance; China is a likely source for crucial planetary mixers.
  • Evidence suggests a planetary mixer moved to a Syrian facility, indicating a shadow supply chain for Iran's rebuilding efforts.
  • Missile sites show activity and are being prioritized over nuclear facilities, following an April port explosion linked to mishandled chemicals that killed 70.

  • The Trump administration reports approximately 2 million illegal immigrants have been removed from the country in under 250 days.
  • This total includes 1.6 million immigrants who voluntarily left and 400,000 who were deported.
  • Officials state "the era of open borders is over" and credit the president's return for a policy shift.
  • Customs and Border Protection has not released illegal aliens into the country for four consecutive months.

  • The Trump administration is designating the Central American gang Barrio 18 as a foreign terrorist organization.
  • Barrio 18 is involved in extortion, drug trafficking, and violence across Honduras, El Salvador, and Guatemala.
  • This designation, similar to that of MS-13, aims to freeze assets and facilitate prosecutions.
