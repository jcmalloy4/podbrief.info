August 13th, 2025: How Mossad Used Iranian Spies To Sabotage Tehran’s Defenses & Europe’s Arms Race

Key Takeaways

  • Israeli intelligence launched a covert mission, crippling Iran's air defenses and targeting nuclear scientists.
  • Europe's defense industry is rapidly expanding, with manufacturing growing three times faster than before the Ukraine war.
  • President Trump federalized D.C. law enforcement, citing high crime and challenging existing crime statistics.

Deep Dives

Covert Infiltration

  • Mossad's "Operation Rising Lion" infiltrated Iran by recruiting and training Iranian dissidents for sabotage missions.
  • Agents precisely targeted Iranian air defense systems and provided coordinates for nuclear scientists' homes for air-to-ground missile strikes.
  • Israeli cyber operations also lured Iranian military leaders to a bunker, resulting in a targeted airstrike that killed top officials.

Defense Buildup

  • European defense manufacturers are rapidly expanding, tripling their pre-Ukraine war growth rates, particularly in ammunition and missile production.
  • Major expansion projects include a large Hungarian plant for tank rounds, alongside existing efforts by Rheinmetall, Germany's largest defense contractor.
  • EU initiatives like the 'Rearm Europe' program aim to overcome industrial bottlenecks and shift security burdens towards Europe, driven partly by US calls for increased NATO spending.

Federal Control

  • President Trump invoked the Home Rule Act to federalize D.C. police, deploying National Guard troops to restore order.
  • Contrary to claims of low crime, D.C.'s 2023 homicide rate hit a 20-year high, with 2024 rates remaining elevated and vehicle thefts three times the national average.
