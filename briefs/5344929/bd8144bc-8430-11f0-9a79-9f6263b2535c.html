PDB Afternoon Bulletin | August 28th, 2025: Why John Bolton May Be Screwed & China's Xi To Host Putin And Kim At Major Military Parade

Key Takeaways

  • John Bolton is under increased legal scrutiny over alleged mishandling of classified information.
  • Unsecured emails from Bolton reportedly reached a foreign spy service, prompting FBI investigations.
  • Chinese President Xi Jinping will host a military parade with North Korean and Russian leaders.
  • The parade highlights Chinese military modernization and signals a bloc of authoritarian powers.

Deep Dive

  • The probe into John Bolton's alleged mishandling of classified information intensified under the Biden administration.
  • Intelligence indicated unsecured emails containing national security secrets were accessed by an adversarial foreign spy service.
  • FBI raids on Bolton's property and potential charges under the Espionage Act are currently under review.

  • Chinese President Xi Jinping will host a military parade joined by North Korean leader Kim Jong-un and Russian President Vladimir Putin.
  • The event commemorates the 80th anniversary of Japan's World War II surrender and aims to showcase Chinese military modernization.
  • The gathering signals a bloc of authoritarian powers, with notable absences of Western and Indian leaders.
