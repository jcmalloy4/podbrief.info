October 7th, 2025: Thousands Of CUBANS Join Russia’s Fight Against Ukraine & Sticking Points In Hamas-Israel Deal

Key Takeaways

  • Up to 5,000 Cuban fighters are reportedly deploying to Ukraine, aiding Russia due to Cuba's severe economic crisis.
  • Negotiations for President Trump's Gaza peace plan are deadlocked over critical issues like Hamas's disarmament and post-war governance.
  • The U.S. and Ukraine are nearing a significant defense deal for the U.S. to acquire Ukrainian drone technology.
  • French Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu resigned after less than a month, highlighting a period of acute political instability.
  • U.S. policy toward Cuba is under review, considering engagement strategies to foster Western alignment.

Deep Dive

  • Up to 5,000 Cuban fighters are reportedly joining Russian forces in Ukraine, according to a U.S. State Department cable.
  • This deployment is attributed to Cuba's severe economic crisis, with recruits offered approximately $2,000 monthly.
  • Ukrainian officials report 39% of Cuban fighters are confirmed dead, with estimates suggesting numbers could reach 25,000.
  • Havana's participation is viewed as a method to repay debts to Russia and secure essential oil and energy financing.
  • The U.S. State Department stated the Cuban regime has failed to protect its citizens from being used as pawns by Russia.

  • Discussion considers U.S. engagement over embargo to align Cuba with the West and improve Cuban lives.
  • Economically embracing Cuba with Western goods is suggested to reveal opportunities to the population.
  • Potential for tourism and casino industries is mentioned, despite expected pushback from Russia, China, and the Cuban-American lobby.
  • The embargo's ineffectiveness in changing Cuba's communist leadership is highlighted, suggesting a different approach is needed.

  • Negotiators met in Egypt for indirect talks on President Trump's Gaza peace plan, aiming for ceasefire, hostage release, and Israeli withdrawal.
  • Key disputes include Hamas's disarmament, post-war governance for Gaza, and the timeline for Israeli withdrawal.
  • Israel insists on Hamas's destruction and Gaza's demilitarization, while Hamas refuses to disarm without a recognized Palestinian state.
  • Trump's proposal excludes Hamas from post-war governance, suggesting a technocratic body, which Hamas seeks to be part of.
  • The plan outlines Israeli withdrawal based on a three-zone map, but vague timelines raise coordination questions and a deal is not imminent.

  • Ukraine and the U.S. are finalizing a defense agreement for the U.S. to purchase Ukrainian drone technology.
  • This collaboration, backed by Presidents Trump and Zelensky, seeks to integrate Ukraine's defense industry into the U.S. supply chain.
  • Discussions focus on a potential five-year, $50 billion deal for advanced drone systems.
  • The U.S. military is testing Ukrainian drones like the Babayaga bomber and Magura sea drones, noting their effectiveness and lower cost compared to U.S. systems.
  • Challenges include Ukraine's diffuse drone industry, reliance on Chinese components, and the need to meet U.S. procurement standards.

  • Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu resigned after less than a month, marking the shortest government term in modern French history.
  • This is the third prime minister to resign under President Macron in under a year, highlighting significant political instability.
  • The chaos stems from Macron's 2024 snap elections, which led to a deadlocked National Assembly.
  • The resignation blindsided the country and has led to calls for snap elections or Macron's resignation, both rejected by him.
  • The market reacted negatively due to fears of an inability to pass a budget bill to address France's rising debt and deficit.
