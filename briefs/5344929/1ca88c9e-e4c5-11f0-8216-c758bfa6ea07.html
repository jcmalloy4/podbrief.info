PDB Afternoon Bulletin | December 29th, 2025: President Trump Announces Strike Inside Venezuela & Zelensky At Mar-A-Lago

Key Takeaways

  • U.S. forces conducted a strike in Venezuela, targeting a drug facility under Operation Southern Spear.
  • President Trump met with Ukrainian President Zelensky, expressing optimism for a peace deal with Russia.
  • Significant obstacles remain in Ukraine peace talks, including territorial claims and security guarantees.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump announced U.S. forces conducted a strike inside Venezuela, targeting a "major drug-related facility."
  • The action is framed within Operation Southern Spear, aimed at disrupting drug trafficking routes linked to Venezuela.
  • The administration presents this as counterterrorism, not regime change, with few public details available regarding location or means.

  • The U.S. strike in Venezuela follows a broader campaign and increased military presence in the Caribbean.
  • While framed as counterterrorism, President Trump also cited Venezuela's alleged theft of U.S. oil and land.
  • The U.S. has crossed a line in its confrontation with Venezuela, with further details and potential retaliatory actions anticipated.

  • President Trump met Ukrainian President Zelensky, describing negotiations as constructive but complex.
  • Trump expressed optimism, stating the sides "have the makings of a deal" to end Russia’s war in Ukraine.
  • Key obstacles include President Putin's stance on ending the invasion and the future of Ukraine's Donbass region.

  • President Zelensky indicated U.S. and Ukraine agree on the need for security guarantees against Russian aggression.
  • The U.S. is potentially offering a 15-year guarantee, while Zelensky prefers a 50-year commitment.
  • Details on these guarantees, involving international partners, were not publicly released.

  • Moscow's core demands, including territorial claims and no NATO membership for Ukraine, remain significant obstacles.
  • President Putin has not shown interest in a 60-day ceasefire, which Zelensky's peace plan requires for a national referendum.
  • Zelensky sidestepped details on EU membership timelines and military aid pledges.
