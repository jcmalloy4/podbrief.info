  • Ticket acquisition details:

  • Complex logistical challenges getting to the venue:
    • - Coordinating with Rich Nathanson to pick him up on Hollywood Boulevard - Navigating parking restrictions at the Hollywood Bowl (17,000 capacity) - Managing luggage while trying to make the show - Obtaining tickets at Will Call with last-minute changes - Comedic moments with the limo driver's surprise and parking attendant's confusion
  • Concert experience details:
    • - Concert ran precisely on schedule, which impressed the speaker - Billy Strings (progressive bluegrass) performed as an opening act * Used pedals, unusual in traditional bluegrass * Speaker's children enjoyed Billy Strings despite not typically liking his music
  • Bob Dylan's performance highlights:
    • - Arrived on stage exactly at scheduled time - Unique stage presentation with minimal lighting, backlit and hard to see - Played "Don't Think Twice It's All Right" (first time in decades) - Modified lyrics to sound more mature/kind - Performed "Ricky Nelson's "Garden Party," a song about Dylan himself - Opened with "Things Have Changed" - Made a rare audience interaction, casually asking "What are you eating down there?" - Speaker considered it the best Dylan show he'd seen - Sound quality was excellent - Noted for being timeless, performing without nostalgia, always feeling current
  • Notable social aspects:

    Willie Nelson Performance

  • Willie Nelson performed at age 92 with his iconic guitar "Trigger":
    • - Played with a unique band including a bongo player and harmonica player - Guitar playing style described as unconventional, more reminiscent of jazz musicians like Coltrane than traditional country - Plays "out of time and out of key" with a distinctive approach - Guitar "Trigger" extremely worn with a large hole and significant damage - Attempted a sing-along of "Mama, Don't Let Your Babies Grow Up to be Cowboys" that partially failed - Performed cannabis-themed songs like "If I Die When I'm High" and "Roll Me Up and Smoke Me When I'm Dead" - Despite weakened voice, his guitar playing was described as exceptionally inspiring - Played without following traditional formulas, with solos that seemed spontaneous and purely inspired
  • Broader musical reflections:
    • - Speaker discussed respecting aging musicians, even when performances aren't at peak level - Compared Willie's approach to his own bass playing, inspired by his teacher's advice to be creative during solos - Emphasized the value of seeing legendary musicians live for the experience and respect, not technical perfection - Speaker noted his own challenges performing at 70 years old, compared to Dylan (83) and Nelson (92)

  • Reflections on artistic innovation:
