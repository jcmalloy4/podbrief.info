The Hugh Hewitt Show: Highly Concentrated

The 2nd Anniversary of 10/7

Key Takeaways

  • Israel cannot afford to forget the October 7th massacre due to its geopolitical realities.
  • Anti-Semitism in the US has surged unexpectedly post-October 7th, linked to restorationist ideologies.
  • "Operation Arctic Frost" alleges FBI surveillance of US senators and organizations, prompting calls for investigation.
  • A government shutdown is impacting military families, raising concerns about readiness and financial stability.
  • The US military faces calls to de-politicize and refocus on merit and capability over DEI initiatives.
  • Israeli public opinion shows distrust in leadership but optimism for the nation's future.
  • Israel reaffirms its unwavering strategic alignment with the US in the current global order.

Deep Dive

  • Host Hugh Hewitt questioned if Israel would forget October 7th lessons, comparing it to America's 9/11 memory.
  • Guest Haviv Rettig Gur asserted Israel cannot forget, being a small nation surrounded by ideological enemies.
  • Democratic countries focused on individual freedoms are more prone to forgetfulness than dictatorial states.
  • Israel's 1973 Yom Kippur War is cited as a past example of being surprised by an enemy attack.

  • The guest connects current American societal decline to historical restorationist movements in the Muslim world.
  • Figures like Tucker Carlson are accused of promoting similar restorationist ideologies, identifying Jews as the primary enemy.
  • This mirrors the 19th-century Muslim Brotherhood's playbook, blaming external influences and Jews for societal rot.
  • The host expressed skepticism about the success of this confrontational Christianity, defending Carlson's character.

  • Israel has historically won existential wars without American aid; the alliance began in the 1970s.
  • The American 'bear hug' strategy, in place since the alliance's start, aims to rein in Israel.
  • Donald Trump's executive order involving CENTCOM to prevent Israeli airstrikes on Qatar is a recent example.
  • The Pentagon supports Israel due to its usefulness in developing new defense technologies.

  • The Israeli public deeply distrusts current leadership post-October 7th, contrasting with American trends after 9/11.
  • Widespread reserve duty could lead to a new generation of serious leadership in Israel.
  • IDF service terms have already been extended and can be further lengthened in emergencies.
  • Discussion addresses drafting ultra-Orthodox men into national service, with calls for non-combat roles.

  • Israel will always align with the US in a binary world dominated by China and the US.
  • Historical precedents show Israel siding with America even when it was disadvantageous.
  • The American-led democratic world order offers peace among democracies and a global financial system.
  • Democracies are argued to be uniquely capable of solving global challenges like climate change through technology.

  • "Operation Arctic Frost" is described as a mass roundup with potential wiretapping and surveillance.
  • Call logs and location data were allegedly uncovered targeting individuals, including 8 US senators.
  • The operation is linked to January 6th events, potentially providing a pretext for illegal actions.
  • The investigation into "Operation Arctic Frost" is estimated to have a $50 million price tag.

  • Senator Joni Ernst discussed the impact of a government shutdown on military personnel, including her daughter.
  • Junior enlisted soldiers and their families face significant financial hardship, unable to quit.
  • Training and readiness of the military are being negatively affected by the shutdown.
  • Congressman Pat Harrigan criticized the weaponization of government and political games leading to the shutdown.

  • Concerns were raised about DEI (Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion) initiatives within the US military.
  • The problem of politicization is seen as larger than initially perceived, needing a return to merit and capability.
  • The military's core mission to win wars is believed to be failing under the current approach.
  • Leaders must insulate the military and service academies from excessive political swings for future leaders.

  • A thorough, public investigation of "Operation Arctic Frost" is deemed necessary, compared to Iran-Contra.
  • Experienced prosecutors are crucial for the investigation to avoid politically motivated actions.
  • Hugh Hewitt advocates for Senate Leader Thune to establish a select committee with Republican lawyers.
  • FBI Director Christopher Wray and Attorney General Merrick Garland should be investigated for their roles.
  • The surveillance of multiple senators by Jack Smith is considered unprecedented in American history.
