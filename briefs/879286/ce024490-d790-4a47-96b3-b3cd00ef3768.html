The Hugh Hewitt Show: Highly Concentrated

President Trump returns to discuss Cuba, Iran, SCOTUS and more

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump discussed U.S. policy regarding Iran, China, and Cuba.
  • He outlined components of the "Trump doctrine" for responding to threats.
  • Proposed strategies for military operations, defense spending, and shipbuilding were detailed.
  • Judicial appointments, presidential debate strategy, and the future political landscape were topics of discussion.
  • Podcast callers provided feedback on the President's actions and debated Supreme Court structure.

Deep Dive

  • Iran's regime is reportedly failing due to internal shortages and spreading protests.
  • President Trump stated he believes he already weakened Iran.
  • He confirmed Iran is not doing well and warned of severe consequences if protests are suppressed violently.
  • Trump expressed uncertainty about meeting Crown Prince Padlai, preferring to let events unfold naturally.

  • President Trump discussed activist Jimmy Lai's situation with China's President Xi Jinping but received no definitive response.
  • He suggested limited further pressure on Cuba, referencing past actions in Venezuela where Cuban fighters were lost.
  • The "Trump doctrine" is characterized by a firm response to threats against Americans or U.S. security, including drug prohibition.

  • President Trump highlighted a "brilliant attack" operation in Venezuela with no American casualties.
  • He emphasized expanding naval capabilities, including guided missile frigates and attack submarines, to address perceived failures under past Democratic administrations.
  • Plans include boosting shipbuilding by preventing defense companies' stock buybacks and limiting executive salaries, redirecting funds to plant and equipment.
  • A $1.5 trillion defense budget was mentioned, alongside plans to build new battleships and reopen shipyards, including the Philadelphia Navy Yard in partnership with South Korea.

  • President Trump favored addressing healthcare as a standalone, large-scale issue, believing popular initiatives could pass independently.
  • He asserted his administration brought respect back to the U.S. internationally, drawing a comparison to Richard Nixon's "madman theory."

  • President Trump discussed the strategic value of presidential debates, indicating he opted out of some past cycles for advantage.
  • He identified potential 2028 Democratic candidates, including Gavin Newsom and AOC, criticizing their perceived weaknesses.
  • The conversation briefly touched on potential judicial candidates, whose names were withheld, and concerns about New York City under Mayor Adams.
  • Space Force headquarters' potential 'Golden Dome' location was mentioned, with Ohio noted as a possibility.

  • Callers expressed strong support for President Trump's actions, particularly regarding an operation in Venezuela.
  • A caller suggested cutting off funding to Minneapolis, which the host rejected citing principles of individual liberty and the Constitution.
  • A caller questioned if the Supreme Court's nine-justice structure should be legislated, with the host arguing that changing the number would end the rule of law in the United States.
