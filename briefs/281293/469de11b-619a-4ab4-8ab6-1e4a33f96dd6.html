RealClearPolitics Podcast

Only One House Built After Fire in California

Key Takeaways

  • Reconstruction in Pacific Palisades remains stalled, with only one home rebuilt since wildfires.
  • The debate over Obamacare subsidies carries significant political risk for both parties ahead of midterms.
  • A Reagan Institute poll indicates strong American support for global engagement, NATO, Ukraine, and Taiwan defense.
  • Turning Point USA is publicly addressing Candace Owens' conspiracy theories about Charlie Kirk.
  • Public perception of the alleged Charlie Kirk assassin's political views is largely misinformed.

Deep Dive

  • One year after devastating wildfires, only one home has been rebuilt among approximately 7,000 damaged buildings in the Pacific Palisades.
  • Los Angeles city officials are criticized for delaying waivers for reconstruction fees, drawing a historical contrast to rapid construction of the 1,500-mile Alcan Highway in 1942.
  • A resident expressed devastation over the perceived incompetence of city officials regarding the slow rebuilding progress.

  • Democrats are criticized for focusing on national issues like fighting Trump, neglecting state-level disaster response in California.
  • The slow rebuilding efforts in Pacific Palisades are seen as a political vulnerability, with Republican candidate Steve Hilton potentially leveraging the issue against Democratic leadership.
  • Residents face financial urgency as insurance coverage for temporary housing expires amid ongoing delays in rebuilding their homes.

  • A KFF poll reveals 50% of Obamacare beneficiaries consider premium increases a major factor in their midterm voting decisions.
  • The potential failure of a Democratic compromise vote in the Senate raises concerns about extending Obamacare subsidies and a possible government shutdown.
  • Republicans are divided on the issue, with some focusing on reports of significant fraud within the Obamacare program.

  • A new Reagan Institute poll indicates two-thirds of Americans want the U.S. to lead globally from a position of military strength.
  • China is perceived as the greatest threat to the U.S., while public attention also focuses on the Middle East, Ukraine, and South America.
  • Despite perceptions of isolationism, nearly 80% of MAGA voters support American global engagement and military strength.

  • The American public holds mixed views on artificial intelligence in military applications, though comfort has increased over time.
  • Support for military AI rises when framed as a strategic advantage over China or when safeguards are emphasized.
  • A poll indicates one-third of Americans believe the U.S. has AI superiority over China, one-third believe China has it, and one-third believe they are at parity.

  • A Reagan Institute poll shows favorability towards NATO increased to 68% from 59% in 2018, with significant gains among Republicans.
  • Support for Ukraine is rising, with the American public clearly preferring Ukraine to maintain full territorial sovereignty over territorial concessions.
  • Three in four Americans believe it is important for the U.S. military to defend Taiwan, with support for various responses to a potential Chinese invasion rising by 10 points in the last year.

  • Blake Neff, a producer for The Charlie Kirk Show, publicly criticized Candace Owens for spreading 'baseless conspiracy theories' about Charlie Kirk.
  • Owens' allegations about Kirk's alleged assassination have generated a significant volume of threats against Kirk's associates and family.
  • Candace Owens has agreed to participate in a December 15th live stream event to address her accusations directly with Turning Point USA representatives.

  • A Media Research Center poll found only 24% of respondents correctly identified Tyler Robinson, the man accused of attempting to assassinate Charlie Kirk, as holding left-wing views.
  • The accused's background includes a conservative upbringing contrasting with developing left-wing beliefs and potential mental health issues.
  • Public discourse is noted for its tendency towards simplistic political labels, often overlooking such complexities.
