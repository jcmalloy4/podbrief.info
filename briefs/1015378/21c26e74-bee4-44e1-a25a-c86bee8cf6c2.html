All-In with Chamath, Jason, Sacks & Friedberg

California Forever: The Startup Building America's Next Great City

Key Takeaways

  • California Forever plans a new city in Solano County for high-tech manufacturing and innovation.
  • The project includes America's largest advanced manufacturing park and aims to boost US shipbuilding capacity.
  • Backed by Silicon Valley, the new city will house 400,000 people in a walkable, traditionally designed environment.
  • The initiative is presented as a modern manifestation of American reindustrialization and abundance.

Deep Dive

  • Jan Sramek introduced California Forever, a venture to build a new sustainable city in Solano County, California.
  • The project will span over 100 square miles and is supported by Silicon Valley investors.
  • It seeks to revive California's historical leadership in high-tech manufacturing and innovation.

  • California Forever plans to acquire nearly 70,000 acres in Solano County for the new city.
  • The project includes the Solano Foundry, intended to be America's largest advanced manufacturing park.
  • The initiative aims to increase productivity through robotics and AI, with factories located near Bay Area talent.

  • The guest highlighted the national security imperative of expanding U.S. shipbuilding, contrasting it with China's extensive capacity.
  • California Forever owns waterfront land in Solano County sufficient to house all U.S. shipyards, offering a potential solution.
  • New shipyards on the West Coast, particularly the Bay Area, are identified as ideal due to natural harbors and proximity to AI talent.

  • The project proposes building a new, walkable city designed to accommodate up to 400,000 residents.
  • The city will feature traditional architecture, drawing inspiration from historic American neighborhoods.
  • This urban planning aims to create a desirable living environment supporting industrial and technological goals.
