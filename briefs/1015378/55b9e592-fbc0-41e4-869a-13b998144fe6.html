All-In with Chamath, Jason, Sacks & Friedberg

The New Era of the Stock Market with Nasdaq CEO Adena Friedman | All-In Summit 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Nasdaq has transformed into a global technology provider, beyond its exchange function, achieving over 100% growth in five years.
  • Nasdaq plans to integrate tokenized securities and aims for 24/5 trading, pending clear regulatory frameworks.
  • Efforts are underway to simplify the IPO process and adapt regulations for evolving market structures like prediction and private markets.
  • The stock market sees increased index fund dominance, making investing more accessible for retail.
  • The Federal Reserve emphasizes its data-driven approach and advocates for its independence from political influence.

Deep Dive

  • Nasdaq shares increased 14% year-to-date and over 100% in the last five years.
  • Adena Friedman transformed Nasdaq into an "architect of modern markets," extending beyond a stock exchange.
  • Nasdaq provides technology to 17 markets directly and sells it to 135 others globally.
  • Its index business manages $700 billion in assets and offers anti-financial crime and market surveillance technology.

  • Companies like Uber, Stripe, and SpaceX remain private for extended periods before going public.
  • Public markets are emphasized for economic growth, providing billions in investor access and opportunities.
  • The NASDAQ 100 has historically delivered a 14.25% average annual return.
  • Efforts aim to simplify the IPO process through disclosure, proxy, and litigation reforms, reducing burdens for public companies.

  • Nasdaq plans to integrate tokenized securities into core markets to streamline post-trade processing.
  • SEC Chair Gary Gensler is working to modernize IPOs and explore crypto/tokenized security integration.
  • Nasdaq hesitates to directly engage with crypto markets due to a lack of clear regulatory frameworks.
  • The company aims to balance investor protection with innovation, seeking regulatory clarity from Washington.

  • Market types include traditional securities tied to businesses, prediction markets, and crypto.
  • Options markets are viewed as complex prediction markets, offering layered bets.
  • SEC and CFTC collaboration is discussed for overseeing evolving markets and increasing asset accessibility.
  • Nasdaq Private Market prioritizes issuer control and partnerships for private company liquidity, including secondary sales and SPVs.

  • The stock market is dominated by index funds, leading to compressed returns and concentration in top market-cap companies.
  • This trend makes investing more accessible overall despite challenges in finding alpha.
  • The Nasdaq 100 offers accessibility and liquidity for retail investors, contrasting with individual stock picking challenges.
  • Passive market movements can create arbitrage opportunities for active managers.

  • Concerns are raised about the Federal Reserve's access to proprietary data for decision-making.
  • A New York Fed board member stated the Fed uses various public and private data sources and values its data-driven approach.
  • The importance of the Fed's independence from political pressures for long-term economic focus is strongly advocated.
  • The New York Fed's operations are described as data-driven and apolitical, ensuring objective market evaluation.
