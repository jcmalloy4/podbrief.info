All-In with Chamath, Jason, Sacks & Friedberg

Rick Caruso on California’s Collapse: Broken Leadership, LA Wildfire Failures & the Fix

Key Takeaways

  • Los Angeles faced criticism for an incompetent response to the 2024 wildfires and slow rebuilding efforts.
  • The conversation addressed California's one-party system and perceived lack of accountability contributing to urban issues.
  • Rick Caruso articulated a vision for California focusing on competence, public safety, and innovative solutions to homelessness.
  • Caruso's business acumen and public service experience were discussed as qualifications for potential political office.

Deep Dive

  • The 2024 LA wildfires resulted in 7,000 lost homes, with rebuilding efforts eight months later hampered by extensive red tape.
  • Mayor Karen Bass's response was criticized as incompetent, with city plan checkers reportedly still working remotely.
  • Rick Caruso stated he protected his Pacific Palisades properties by deploying private firefighters and water trucks, contrasting with the city's inadequate response.

  • The discussion posits that California's one-party system and lack of competitive elections reduce incentives for officials like Mayor Bass to address issues, leading to stagnation.
  • The Palisades fire was described as preventable, attributed to a smoldered fire the fire department initially failed to extinguish and a lack of water in hydrants.
  • The conversation covered perceived urban decay in cities like Portland, Seattle, Chicago, and New York, debating if it's due to political alignment or broader societal apathy.

  • Politicians like Karen Bass and Gavin Newsom were questioned for using the Palisades fire as an 'opportunity to reimagine,' potentially leading to low-income housing or zoning changes.
  • Rick Caruso argued against making zoning changes at the expense of homeowners, emphasizing the need for government support and infrastructure upgrades.
  • The conversation touched on the perceived ineptitude of Los Angeles and California, and the possibility of Governor Newsom using state authority to expedite infrastructure changes and secure federal funding.

  • The host expressed support for Rick Caruso's potential political aspirations, highlighting his attention to detail observed at his properties like The Grove and Pacific Palisades.
  • Caruso explained his business philosophy centers on enriching people's lives, providing a platform for innovation exemplified by a trolley at The Grove.
  • He reflected on his past public service, working for three Los Angeles mayors, expressing satisfaction in making positive changes for residents.

  • Caruso stated California should lead the nation, excelling in housing, education, and safety, with no homelessness or drug dealing on streets.
  • He criticized the current approach to homelessness, advocating for zero tolerance for street encampments and highlighting Los Angeles wasting $900,000 per person.
  • Caruso recounted an incident in the Sepulveda Basin where homeless individuals allegedly created booby traps, injuring a firefighter, and criticized the mayor's complex response.
