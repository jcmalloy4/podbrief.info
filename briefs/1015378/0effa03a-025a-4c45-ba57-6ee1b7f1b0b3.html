All-In with Chamath, Jason, Sacks & Friedberg

Fed Hesitates on Tariffs, The New Mag 7, Death of VC, Google's Value in a Post-Search World

Here's my structured briefing of the podcast discussion:

Key takeaways:

  • Google faces first-ever search volume decline as AI chatbots gain traction, triggering strategic questions
  • KOTU Management aims to build "new Magnificent 7" portfolio across public/private markets over next decade
  • Miami F1 event featured major tech figures including Sergey Brin, who's deeply engaged in Google's AI efforts
The Future of Google Search The podcast discussed Google's first reported decline in search volume, with Apple executive Eddie Cue noting users are shifting to AI chatbots like ChatGPT. While Google has strong AI capabilities with Gemini, debate centered on whether the company needs more aggressive integration of AI across its products. The hosts suggested YouTube and Google Workspace as key areas for AI enhancement, with Sergey Brin's increased involvement seen as potentially critical for driving change.

KOTU's Investment Strategy Philippe Lafont outlined KOTU Management's approach of building a ~25 company portfolio across both public and private markets, aiming to identify the next generation of tech leaders. He emphasized the need to balance traditional valuation discipline from public markets with the forward-looking perspective gained from private investments. The strategy focuses on companies that could become the next "Magnificent 7," including current private companies like SpaceX and Stripe.

Miami F1 Event Highlights The hosts recapped their Miami Formula 1 experience, which included appearances from major tech figures. Notable guests included Sergey Brin, who demonstrated deep engagement with Google's AI initiatives, Tony Robbins, who led a meditation session, and Nico Rosberg. The event combined tech discussions with social activities, highlighting the intersection of technology and entertainment in modern business culture.
