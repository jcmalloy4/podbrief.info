Jack Hibbs Podcast

A New Beginning That Lasts

Key Takeaways

  • Truth discernment is vital in a deceptive world, requiring spiritual guidance.
  • Effective time stewardship is emphasized, linking daily choices to spiritual impact.
  • Spiritual renewal, guided by God, offers a more impactful year than secular resolutions.
  • Past mistakes should be learning opportunities, not future limitations.
  • Creating personal 'monuments' can serve as reminders of spiritual milestones.

Deep Dive

  • The host illustrates feelings of a 'messed up' life with an analogy of a high-performance Lamborghini crashing and being destroyed.
  • Listeners are encouraged to mitigate past mistakes and seek a fresh start as the year concludes.
  • Psalm 65:11, 'You crown the year with your goodness and your paths drip with abundance,' is presented as a foundational verse for the new year.

  • The host cites Isaiah 9:6, identifying Jesus as the ultimate authority over time, and Romans 15:5-6 regarding God's ability to redeem lost time.
  • Listeners are prompted to consider necessary changes for a new season, suggesting that slowing down is often a resisted but vital step.
  • The host reflects on approaching 68 years old, expressing urgency to give cherished possessions, such as model airplanes, to grandsons.

  • The host questions the value of Christians spending hours on video games, suggesting it represents 'lost time' with no kingdom benefit.
  • Listeners are urged to consider their limited remaining weekends and Christmases, prompting a focus on active use of gifts for the church.
  • The discussion contrasts typical New Year's resolutions, like weight loss, with a spiritual approach rooted in Psalm 65:11 for divine guidance and abundance.

  • The host advocates for creating physical 'monuments' or landmarks to serve as personal reminders of significant spiritual events.
  • Examples include a specific intersection in Newport Beach where he met his wife and a restaurant on Coronado Island linked to his father's former business.
  • A large oak tree in Chino Hills is cited as a personal location for seeking divine guidance.

  • The host warns that navigating the current age requires the Holy Spirit and biblical knowledge to discern truth from widespread deception.
  • Sun Tzu's 'The Art of War' is referenced to illustrate strategic deception, suggesting that Satan employs similar tactics.
  • The episode cites alleged instances of broadcast manipulation to spread misinformation, making truth difficult to ascertain without spiritual guidance.

  • Listeners are advised to move beyond past mistakes, not by forgetting them, but by preventing them from dictating future actions.
  • The focus is on extracting lessons from previous errors to achieve success in the new year.
  • This approach aims to ensure past failures do not lead to their repetition in the future.
