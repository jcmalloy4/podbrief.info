We Hosted an Anime Battle Tournament | Trash Taste #282

Key Takeaways

  • The Trash Taste Podcast reintroduced its "Anime Death Battle" segment after a 10-year hiatus.
  • The revived segment featured anime character matchups across randomized, non-combat categories.
  • Discussions often focused on characters' adaptability, marketability, and social skills beyond their fighting prowess.
  • Key matchups included Kenshin vs. Muhammad Ali Jr. in bobsledding and Eskinor vs. Kenshiro as pop stars.
  • The episode concluded with a final "death battle" debate between Yujiro and Rudeus.

Deep Dive

  • The hosts reintroduced the "Anime Death Battle" concept after five years, initially referring to a "tournament" last used a decade ago.
  • Battles feature randomized categories and stages, moving beyond simple strength comparisons to adaptability.
  • A core rule restricts selections to one character per series, with an exception for Baki to include Muhammad Ali Jr.
  • The first matchup was Kenshin vs. Muhammad Ali Jr. with the unexpected category "fastest at bobsledding."

  • Hosts debated Eskinor from Seven Deadly Sins vs. Kenshiro from Fist of the North Star for who would be a more successful pop star.
  • Eskinor's dual 'twink' and 'daddy' personas were seen as more marketable than Kenshiro's singular masculine image.
  • The viability of Kenshiro's mustache in 2025 was discussed, with comparisons made to 90s porn stars and Ned Flanders.
  • The potential for a Fist of the North Star idol parody and Kenshiro as part of a male idol group was considered.

  • Rudeus from Mushoku Tensei was favored over Gon from Hunter x Hunter for "most likely to pass a job interview" due to Rudeus's character growth and desperation.
  • Mob from Mob Psycho 100 was selected as the "best assassin" over Guts from Berserk, citing Mob's ability to blend in.
  • Guts' past as an assassin was detailed, including the use of his large sword and an instance of accidental child killing.

  • The hosts debated who would lose the most money in Las Vegas between Naruto and Jotaro, with Jotaro identified as the most experienced gambler.
  • Tanjiro from Demon Slayer was ultimately chosen over Takopi for "best international spy," citing his experience in the Entertainment District arc.
  • Light Yagami was favored for charity fundraising due to his manipulative nature, while "Turbo Granny" was noted for potentially cursing people for donations.

  • Lelouch from Code Geass was argued to be a better "class clown" than Kirito due to his calculated humor and use of Geass.
  • Megumin from Konosuba was deemed an untrustworthy "valet driver" over Gabi Madu from Hell's Paradise, due to her destructive magic and likelihood of stealing the car.
  • Haruhi and Shinji were both considered terrible "personal assistants," with Shinji predicted to not show up for work.

  • Kenshiro from Fist of the North Star was chosen over Luffy for "survives the longest on a desert island," citing Kenshiro's mental fortitude and established survival in harsh conditions.
  • Rudeus from Mushoku Tensei was dubbed the "worst internet troll" over Gojo, due to his controversial nature and tendency to unintentionally offend online.
  • Muhammad Ali Jr. versus Aaron in a "gun to a knife fight" scenario concluded with Aaron being more likely to bring a gun.

  • The hosts debated whether Light Yagami or Jotaro Kujo would deliver a pizza fastest, considering Jotaro's time-stopping ability.
  • Tanjiro Kamado was chosen over Ichigo Kurosaki as the better babysitter, based on his perceived attentiveness and caring demeanor.
  • Hypothetical scenarios involving Light Yagami and Yujiro Hanma delivering pizza were also discussed, focusing on their unique abilities.

  • Kenshiro from Fist of the North Star was selected over Aaron for a fashion runway due to his stylish normal clothes and a "notable pair of pants."
  • Rudeus from Mushoku Tensei was chosen as the "best contestant on crazy Japanese game shows" over Guts, considering Rudeus's potential interest in furthering goals.
  • Yujiro Hanma was predicted to win a hot pepper eating contest against Lelouch due to his extreme nature.

  • Tanjiro from Demon Slayer was favored over Light Yagami as the "best DJ" for providing a more universally enjoyable, mainstream experience.
  • Yujiro Hanma was deemed more likely to survive a zombie apocalypse than Gabimaru due to his immense durability and dismissive attitude towards zombies.
  • Rudeus Greyrat was chosen over Kenshiro as the "best dinner party host," citing Rudeus's more established household and hosting experience.

  • Hosts evaluated Rudeus from Mushoku Tensei against Yujiro from Baki for a hypothetical blind date scenario.
  • Yujiro's violent past, including murdering his wife, was contrasted with Rudeus's potential inappropriate behavior and financial struggles.
  • The discussion focused on differentiating their dating liabilities, considering opening lines and potential financial situations during a date.
