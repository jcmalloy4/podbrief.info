Why The Way We Socialize Is Changing & How To Evolve Ft. Jake Bullock, Co-Founder & CEO Of Cann

Key Takeaways

  • Cann successfully identified an unexpected market for alcohol alternatives among parents seeking relaxation without hangovers.
  • The company addressed a significant market gap by offering consistently micro-dosed, sophisticated cannabis beverages.
  • Celebrity endorsements and platform leverage proved crucial for Cann's early scaling, especially given THC advertising restrictions.
  • The fundraising landscape dramatically shifted, forcing companies to balance rapid growth with demonstrated profitability.
  • Cann's beverages provide a distinct, fast-acting, mild experience due to unique absorption mechanisms, differing from traditional edibles.

Deep Dive

  • Cann discovered an unexpected customer base, including mothers in affluent areas like Brentwood and Marin County, seeking hangover-free relaxation.
  • Co-founder Jake Bullock identified a market need for reliably micro-dosed cannabis beverages after observing inconsistent edibles in Colorado.
  • The brand aimed for a potency similar to beer or wine, with an ideal THC dose of two milligrams per serving, metabolized within approximately one hour.

  • Cann executed an unconventional launch by securing a deal with MedMen, California's largest dispensary chain, leading to a three-week sell-out.
  • The company actively sought new customers by engaging individuals in the Los Angeles entertainment industry for product feedback.
  • Celebrity endorsements, beginning with Ruby Rose and including Gwyneth Paltrow's investment, significantly boosted Cann's popularity.
  • This momentum enabled offering investment opportunities to other celebrities, such as Kate Hudson, who collaborated on a holiday campaign.

  • Cann focused on consumer venture investors like Imaginary Ventures and cannabis-specific funds due to challenges with traditional THC investment.
  • The fundraising environment shifted from prioritizing growth-only companies with readily available capital to requiring profitability.
  • Early rapid growth saw sales double annually from $200,000 to $3 million, then $10 million in initial years.
  • Many companies that focused solely on growth failed when the market tightened, emphasizing the importance of balancing growth and profitability.

  • Cann products are federally legal when derived from hemp, allowing online sales in approximately 35 states and retail in 30 states.
  • Upcoming regulations will cap THC from hemp at 0.4 milligrams, starting November 12th of the following year, which Cann is actively trying to influence.
  • Cann's microdosed beverages offer a mild, manageable experience, contrasting with unpredictable and delayed effects from traditional edibles.
  • Unlike edibles processed by the liver, cannabis beverages are absorbed rapidly through the esophagus and stomach lining, taking effect in about 10 minutes and subsiding in an hour.

  • Jake Bullock's hiring philosophy prioritizes energetic, fast-learning individuals willing to adapt, evolving to integrating experienced professionals.
  • The strategy for scaling includes avoiding 'gatekeepers' and embracing experienced hires to create more collective opportunities for existing employees.
  • Cann's long-term goal is to build a global brand and achieve an eventual exit, encouraging early employees to remain for the entire process.

  • Jake Bullock found his undergraduate degree from Duke valuable for foundational skills and maintaining career options.
  • His MBA from Stanford was particularly beneficial due to its strong entrepreneurial culture, deviating from typical business school environments.
  • The MBA was seen as a necessity for his entrepreneurial path, despite emotional and financial challenges faced post-graduation without immediate employment, contrasting a conventional private equity path.

  • Jake Bullock emphasizes humor and the ability to diffuse tension as critical components of effective leadership, particularly in high-stakes environments.
  • Leaders should aim for a fun and irreverent style rather than being overly serious, mirroring Cann's brand approach to cannabis beverages.
  • AI is leveraged for efficient tasks like ingredient photography, freeing human resources for core brand messaging and more impactful content creation.
  • The host, Lauryn Evarts Bosstick, uses AI as a 'psycho assistant' for optimizing her daily schedule and planning efficient transitions.

  • The brand name 'Cann' was chosen for its short, four-letter nature, aiming to define a new category of social cannabis beverages alongside beer and wine.
  • Cann maintains a core mission as a social beverage company, rejecting product ideas that do not align, such as diversifying into psilocybin.
  • Products include a two-milligram THC/four-milligram CBD 'social' size and a five/ten milligram 'high boy' size, with flavors like cranberry sage and grapefruit rosemary.
  • Ingredients feature freshly squeezed juices and all-natural flavors, exemplified by sourcing Sicilian blood orange juice, considered premium due to volcanic soil.
