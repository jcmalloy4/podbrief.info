The Skinny Confidential Him & Her Podcast

Dr. Terry Dubrow And Heather Dubrow Reveal What Really Works: The Truth About GLP-1s, Cosmetic Procedures, Relationship Advice, And Youthful Skin

Key Takeaways

  • Dr. Terry and Heather Dubrow offer insights on human optimization and modern cosmetic procedures.
  • They clarify the truth about GLP-1 medications, their benefits, and common misinformation.
  • The discussion includes new breast implant technology and the evolving "forever 35" aesthetic.
  • Insights are provided on navigating long-term relationships and effective conflict resolution.

Deep Dive

  • Dr. Terry Dubrow, a plastic surgeon, emphasizes advancements in health and wellness while cautioning against surgical disasters.
  • He distinguishes between effective and ineffective treatments, highlighting a lack of scientific backing for some, such as NAD and peptides.
  • Heather Dubrow questions popular wellness trends like salmon sperm facial treatments, noting their dubious efficacy.
  • Dr. Dubrow expresses skepticism about cosmetic threads, citing a history of failure and potential complications.
  • The discussion notes regional differences in cosmetic preferences, with California focusing on body procedures and New York on the face.

  • The "forever 35" face phenomenon is discussed, noting a trend of individuals aiming to maintain a youthful appearance and celebrities admitting to plastic surgery.
  • Concerns about unnatural or altered appearances from procedures like facelifts are addressed, noting risks of feminization for men and dating trends due to past surgery styles (e.g., Heidi Montag's early procedures).
  • Heather Dubrow recounted her experience with breast deflation after childbirth and her initial consideration of implants, eventually recovering without them.
  • Dr. Terry Dubrow expressed initial reluctance for implants due to a 15-20% complication rate, advocating for natural approaches.
  • The Motiva implant, approved in November, is highlighted for its potential to mitigate capsular contracture and improve outcomes in difficult cases.

  • Dr. Terry Dubrow gained board certification in obesity medicine due to his intense interest in GLP-1 medications, which he compares to AI's impact on the industrial revolution.
  • GLP-1s, initially developed for diabetes, were discovered to have significant weight loss benefits and offer multiple ancillary health benefits, including cardiovascular protection and improved liver/kidney health.
  • Many uses of GLP-1s are considered off-label, meaning they are not FDA-approved for a specific indication, such as using them solely for weight loss.
  • Dr. Dubrow distinguishes between Ozempic/Wegovy (semaglutide) and Monjaro/Zepbound (tirzepatide), noting tirzepatide combines GLP-1 and GIP.
  • He mentions standard dosage titration up to 15mg for tirzepatide, suggesting lower 'microdoses' might be effective for some individuals.

  • The stigma around GLP-1 medications is addressed, comparing it to past dangerous weight loss methods and debunking myths about side effects like gallbladder issues and pancreatitis.
  • Heather Dubrow expressed concern about social media misinformation regarding GLP-1s, questioning their safety and necessity for individuals not seeking weight loss.
  • Dr. Terry Dubrow explains GLP-1s control sugar and inflammation, offering potential benefits for cognitive health and addiction disorders, positioning them as an anti-aging drug.
  • His motivation to become triple board-certified (general surgery, plastic surgery, obesity medicine) stemmed from a social media dispute with Jillian Michaels regarding GLP-1 safety.

  • The evolution of weight-loss drugs includes triple-hormone drugs like Retatrutide, which show higher efficacy in clinical trials compared to GLP-1s.
  • Concerns are raised about illegal compounding of these drugs and potential side effects such as skin sagging and hair loss.
  • The 'Ozempic vulva' phenomenon, involving decreased hydration and lubrication, and 'Ozempic face,' characterized by sagging due to rapid weight loss, are discussed.
  • Experts recommend staying hydrated and consuming adequate protein, amino acids, and collagen to mitigate side effects from rapid weight loss drugs.
  • These drugs can also lead to poor tolerance for alcohol, causing severe sickness, and may offer a potential benefit for alcoholism.

  • Heather Dubrow's company, Consult Beauty, focuses on internal and external wellness, integrating ingestible beauty products and discussing injectable peptides like BPC-157 and Thymosin Beta-4.
  • Dr. Terry Dubrow raises concerns about the lack of FDA approval and regulation for peptides, questioning dosage consistency and suggesting some positive effects might be placebo.
  • Heather validates these concerns, highlighting potential unknown long-term risks, such as cancer, contrasting this with the years of data available for GLP-1s due to their use in diabetes treatment.
  • A broader trend of medical professionals offering aesthetic and wellness services with a lack of standardized training is also noted.

  • Heather and Terry Dubrow discuss their accidental success in real estate flipping, detailing how market fluctuations led to selling multiple homes, including "Chateau Dubrot."
  • Heather's meticulous design of their former Orange County home included custom features like heated towel drawers and a champagne ring in the closet.
  • Their home was sold for the third highest price in Orange County history, leading to a quick move within three weeks.
  • They transitioned from a 22,000-square-foot home to a two-bedroom penthouse in Century City, Los Angeles, and now rent a second apartment in the same building for their four children.
  • Heather initially struggled with apartment living but now appreciates the privacy and amenities, comparing the experience to living in a hotel.

  • Heather Dubrow is described as the "voice of reason" on 'Real Housewives', often de-escalating conflicts while maintaining her role on the show.
  • Dr. Terry Dubrow has learned to be more reserved on reality television, anticipating potential revelations about his life due to his entertainment industry experience.
  • The hosts appreciate Heather's glamour and logic on 'Real Housewives of Orange County', noting that diverse opinions enhance the show.
  • Audience fatigue with political news suggests that reality television, with its discussions of plastic surgery and GLP-1s, offers an escape where viewers can aspire to or distance themselves from the subjects.
  • Heather describes experiencing significant emotional distress during Season 17 of 'The Real Housewives' due to conflicts and personal stress from selling their home.

  • The Dubrows' 27-year partnership included a particularly difficult year on the show, which amplified marital strain and led to a critical discussion about their relationship.
  • Guests attribute long-term marriage success to being best friends, mutual respect, and working through inevitable arguments to grow closer.
  • Advice includes prioritizing the marriage, continuing to "date" each other, especially as children leave home, and expecting challenges with faith that they will pass.
  • The importance of evolving together is emphasized, rather than expecting relationships to remain static.
  • Communication methods during arguments are discussed, including individual processing times and the use of AI tools like ChatGPT to craft responses.
