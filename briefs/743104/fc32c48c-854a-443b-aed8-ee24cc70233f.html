Lauryn Bosstick On The Story She's Never Fully Told

Key Takeaways

  • Lauryn Evarts Bosstick shares her personal story of her mother's death by suicide at age 18, a topic she avoided for 10 years.
  • Her mother's passing and family struggles with addiction profoundly shaped Lauryn's identity and fueled her ambition.
  • The episode highlights the isolation of grief and mental health struggles, particularly before widespread public discourse on these topics.
  • Lauryn emphasizes the importance of forgiveness, developing empathy, and consistent long-term support for those experiencing loss.
  • The conversation aims to offer comfort and context, reminding listeners that difficult experiences can inform but not define one's future.

Deep Dive

  • Lauryn Evarts Bosstick decided to share a personal story she avoided for 10 years, aiming to protect her family and her mother's legacy.
  • The hosts intend for the discussion to provide context for Lauryn's identity and support listeners during the holidays.
  • Michael Bosstick describes Lauryn as one of the strongest individuals he knows, stating a significant life event profoundly shaped her thinking.

  • Lauryn's mother died by suicide when Lauryn was 18 and her sister was 12, a loss that felt isolated due to limited mental health awareness at the time.
  • Despite her mother's positive qualities, the suicide was jarring due to the absence of clear warning signs, leading to Lauryn's feelings of guilt.
  • After her mother's death, Lauryn coped by immersing herself in reading for approximately 1.5 hours daily, seeking solace and guidance.

  • The host recalls being on the phone with her mother the night she died by suicide, emphasizing that an 18-year-old would not anticipate such an event.
  • She notes her sister, at age 12, responded to their mother's death by shutting down, contrasting with Lauryn's own coping mechanisms.
  • Now with three children of her own, Lauryn finds it challenging to fully comprehend her mother's state of mind at the time of her death.

  • Lauryn expresses a fear of inherited depression and suicide, stemming from her mother's history, and actively combats negative mental health narratives.
  • She recounts her sister's severe addiction to hard drugs at a young age, noting a frightening personality change, but celebrates her sister's 10 years of sobriety.
  • Michael Bosstick praises Lauryn as the family's grounding force, crediting her resilience and perspective for inspiring him.

  • Forgiveness, particularly of her mother, has been a significant personal help for Lauryn in understanding her mother's struggles with depression and anxiety.
  • Lauryn suggests her mother may have been misdiagnosed with postpartum depression and over-prescribed medication, noting different treatment options 20 years ago.
  • She contrasts her mother's experience with her own postpartum depression, which she learned to manage through wellness practices.

  • The host explains that people often shy away from discussing suicide due to nervousness and uncertainty about how to approach the topic.
  • She advises that consistent check-ins over time, even years after a loss, are more deeply helpful than the initial abundant support.
  • Lauryn acknowledges and appreciates thoughtful gestures from individuals who remember significant dates, like anniversaries, and reach out.
