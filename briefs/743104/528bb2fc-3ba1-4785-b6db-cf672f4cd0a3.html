Mark Consuelos: Built To Win - Success, Love, & Discipline You Can Apply To Your Own Life

Key Takeaways

  • Mark Consuelos defines success as simple, fulfilling family moments without arguments.
  • His 30-year marriage to Kelly Ripa began with an elopement and thrives on mutual respect and shared experiences.
  • Early acting career discipline on daily soap operas instilled a rigorous work ethic in Consuelos.
  • He prioritizes health and fitness, maintaining a strict regimen to ensure activity with future grandchildren.
  • Consuelos co-owns the Italian soccer team Campobasso FC, guiding it to two championships in two years.
  • As co-host of "Live with Kelly and Mark," he aims to provide viewers a reprieve, focusing on daily self-competition.

Deep Dive

  • Mark Consuelos and Kelly Ripa eloped in Vegas after a breakup the week prior.
  • He proposed following a walk in Central Park after a co-starring appearance on "Live with Regis and Kathy Lee."
  • The couple had their first child at 26, effectively growing up together while managing demanding careers.

  • Consuelos filled in over 100 times on "Live," often last minute, before becoming a permanent co-host.
  • He notes Ripa's quick wit and ability to handle his on-air stumbles, highlighting trust in their professional relationship.
  • Commuting together for two years, including an hour each way for a 12-hour workday, was a challenging period.
  • Becoming a permanent co-host brought "real pressure and overthinking," contrasting with consequence-free fill-in roles.

  • Consuelos takes health seriously, noting he was in better shape at 50 than at 30.
  • He prioritizes fitness to remain active with future grandchildren at age 60.
  • His regimen includes taking up golf as a competitive, therapeutic activity, often completing a round in under two hours.
  • He maintains an inner dialogue, using past photos to track his progress and motivate himself.

  • Mark Consuelos wakes at 6 AM to prepare coffee for his wife before heading to the studio by 8 AM for the 9 AM live show.
  • He now avoids discussing work with Kelly Ripa, contrasting with past experiences, due to the demands of the job.
  • Consuelos mentions 'talk show amnesia,' where hosts may forget interview details due to the high volume of content.

  • Consuelos attributes his 30-year marriage success to living in New York, not Hollywood, and observing his parents' long marriages.
  • Deep mutual respect and love, alongside embracing 'boring' chapters and weathering challenges, are key.
  • He values his children choosing to spend time with parents, citing a billionaire's definition of success by this measure.

  • Consuelos acquired a stake in Campobasso FC, an Italian soccer team that had gone bankrupt and re-entered a lower division.
  • He moved from a silent partnership to active involvement in player selection, sponsorships, and daily operations.
  • The team achieved two championships in their first two years, returning to the third division.
  • Consuelos describes the ownership experience as 'an MBA in professional sports,' creatively fulfilling and highly competitive.

  • Consuelos’ non-negotiables include 4-5 days of weightlifting per week and strict diet adherence using a macro calculator.
  • He prioritizes protein intake to combat age-related muscle loss.
  • His sleep routine involves an Eight Sleep mattress with a split cooling feature and a gentle vibration alarm.

  • The Skinny Confidential's brow peptide product uses castor oil and a peptide to promote brow growth.

  • Consuelos advises aspiring interviewers to cultivate genuine interest, curiosity, and flexibility to deviate from a script.
  • He contrasts the podcast's open format with the time constraints of live television, where segments are brief.
  • The pressures of live broadcasting include a lack of control over segment timing and the differing energy levels of weekday audiences.
