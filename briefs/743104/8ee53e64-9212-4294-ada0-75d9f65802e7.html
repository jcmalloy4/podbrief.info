Dana White: A Winner's Mindset On Business, Health, & What It Takes To Get What You Want In Life

Key Takeaways

  • UFC President Dana White prioritizes intuition in business, transforming niche sports like PowerSlap into global powerhouses.
  • White advocates for trusting one's vision, emphasizing hard work, consistency, and resilience in entrepreneurship.
  • He joined Meta's board to contribute a "backbone" against content censorship and learn from industry leaders, including Mark Zuckerberg.
  • White credits alternative health interventions by Gary Brecka for resolving long-standing health issues, including sleep apnea and chronic pain.

Deep Dive

  • Dana White initially expressed skepticism about Gary Brecka's alternative medicine but was convinced after Brecka provided an IV and oxygen, leading to White's full recovery within two hours during an illness.
  • Brecka subsequently helped White resolve sleep apnea and snoring, conditions that had persisted despite conventional treatments like CPAP machines.
  • Brecka's diagnostic process involved analyzing blood work and genetic tests to identify issues, leading to a rigorous workout regimen and specific diet.
  • White's cousin was diagnosed with Lyme disease by Brecka through blood work, after medical professionals at the Mayo Clinic could not diagnose numbness and arm pain.

  • Dana White reflects on the fight business being perceived as a "dumbest idea ever" in its early days, drawing parallels to initial skepticism about the Vegas Golden Knights.
  • He emphasizes trusting one's own capabilities and vision over others' opinions, even from successful businessmen, who may not see potential in unique ideas.
  • White's strategy involves investing in niche sports that others overlook, such as the UFC and PowerSlap, and transforming them into billion-dollar businesses.
  • He discovered PowerSlap through social media, noting its 10 million YouTube viewers despite low production value, and saw potential for a highly produced sport.
  • White's decisions are primarily gut-driven; he identifies concepts that work on social media and can translate into compelling live events.

  • Donald Trump provided crucial early support for the UFC when the organization faced difficulties securing venues and legal challenges.
  • White emphasizes Trump's consistent support throughout his career, including a request to speak at the presidential convention, which White accepted due to loyalty.
  • White reflects that his childhood experiences with his parents and family have shaped his character and steadfastness.
  • He notes his personality has remained consistent over the past 25-30 years.

  • Dana White recalls being in New York for a Conor McGregor fight when Donald Trump won the 2016 election, having dinner at a restaurant called Scalonattella.
  • He received a call from Trump's security and later directly from the newly elected president, strengthening their relationship over the following decade.
  • White recounts attending Trump's after-party in a fight suit, the only attire he had, after receiving an invitation from Trump's security.
  • He was unexpectedly pulled onto the stage to speak at a convention in Mar-a-Lago during a past election, where Elon Musk was also present.

  • Dana White praises the character of Donald Trump and his family, noting that Ivanka Trump had previously been a guest on the podcast.
  • White highlights Jared Kushner's accomplishments in Middle East peace negotiations, describing them as a "miracle" and Kushner as a "brilliant human."
  • White met Ivanka Trump and Jared Kushner through his association with Donald Trump.

  • Dana White defines success as the happiness of his children in their chosen paths, regardless of profession.
  • He states that financial success does not equate to happiness, deriving joy from his work and life accomplishments.
  • White possesses a personal affinity for pressure, actively seeking it out and likening his need for challenge to a 'saber-toothed tiger.'
  • At 56, he contrasts his busy lifestyle with typical retirement expectations, actively adding more responsibilities by expanding into boxing and jiu-jitsu.
  • White highlights Donald Trump's work ethic and resilience, attributing his ability to handle intense pressure without faltering to a 'killer' instinct.

  • Dana White joined Meta's board because he was asked by Mark Zuckerberg, accepting due to the caliber of other members and the opportunity to learn from them.
  • He initially approached the Meta board with an intent to observe and learn, but quickly engaged, asserting his perspective.
  • White notes that initial perceptions of him by other board members evolved positively through personal interaction.
  • Mark Zuckerberg invited White to provide a 'backbone' for Meta, referencing a quote from Zuckerberg's appearance on Joe Rogan's podcast, aligning with White's philosophy of not censoring content.
  • White praises Zuckerberg's leadership, business acumen, and team competence, also noting Zuckerberg's MMA training and physical capability in a potential fight against Elon Musk.

  • Dana White states that time is his primary adversary despite his commitments, aiming to balance work with significant family time, including four major family trips annually.
  • His wife, Lene, manages his schedule, balancing business pitches, advice seekers, and fundraising requests.
  • White highlights his enjoyment of mentoring young people through an intern program, meeting with each intern upon arrival and departure to share insights and learn from them.

  • Dana White believes greatness requires being an 'animal' who loves the grind and demonstrates consistency.
  • He shares his personal discipline, including a recent fluctuating diet and current water fast, in preparation for a demanding three-week trip to the Middle East.
  • White connects entrepreneurs with potential investors not for a personal stake, but to witness their success and provide opportunities, expressing disdain for taking a commission on such deals.
  • When asked for advice for young people starting careers, he states it is a unique and interesting time in history, contrasting it with his upbringing in the 1980s.
