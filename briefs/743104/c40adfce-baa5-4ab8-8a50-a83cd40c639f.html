How To Heal Naturally, Strengthen Your Immune System, & Stop Getting Sick Ft. Carley Kremer Of Beekeeper's Naturals

Key Takeaways

  • Bee products like propolis, royal jelly, and bee pollen offer science-backed health benefits for immunity, brain function, and overall wellness.
  • Beekeeper's Naturals prioritizes ethical sourcing from pesticide-free zones and rigorous third-party testing to ensure product purity.
  • Natural bee-based remedies are presented as alternatives to traditional over-the-counter medications, with documented efficacy for various conditions.
  • Beekeeping is described as a low-maintenance, meditative activity that can yield various beneficial products.

Deep Dive

  • Carley Kremer's personal journey to heal autoimmune issues inspired her research into bee products, leading to the founding of Beekeeper's Naturals.
  • The birth of Kremer's daughter 20 months ago further motivated the creation of a dedicated line of children's health products.
  • Kremer's approach to celebrating her daughter's milestones parallels a childhood memory of her father celebrating her sister's birth.

  • The guest's child recovered from a runny nose within 24 hours using Beekeeper's Naturals kids cough syrup and nasal spray.
  • Propolis's immunomodulatory properties are credited with protecting the guest's child from a severe virus during a trip to Costa Rica, while other children contracted it.
  • Traditional over-the-counter medications, like NyQuil and DayQuil, are questioned for their benefits and risks; ingredients such as phenylephrine have been recalled for ineffectiveness.
  • Bee products are presented as supporting overall immune health for thousands of years, in contrast to traditional medicines that often 'band-aid' symptoms.

  • Starting beekeeping involves acquiring a hive, ordering bees, and essential gear such as a suit and smoker.
  • Beekeeping is described as less high-maintenance than commonly assumed, requiring approximately weekly check-ins.
  • Bees communicate via pheromones, and a calm demeanor from the beekeeper can lead to calmer bees, with temperament also influenced by the bee breed.
  • Beekeepers are noted to have low cancer rates, which the guest attributes to working with bees and consuming products like propolis.

  • Beekeeper's Naturals avoids U.S.-sourced bee products due to concerns about pesticide contamination, as bees forage within a five-mile radius.
  • The company sources ingredients from pesticide-free regions in Europe and Brazil, emphasizing regenerative beekeeping practices.
  • Beekeeper's Naturals conducts third-party pesticide testing on all raw products to ensure purity and quality.

  • Propolis exhibits anti-inflammatory properties and has shown potential for shrinking tumors in studies from Brazil.
  • The guest has been involved in beekeeping and product development since 2012, pioneering the bee product category.
  • Beekeeper's Naturals collaborates with doctors, researchers, and pharmaceutical companies, serving both general wellness consumers and individuals with serious health conditions.

  • Royal jelly plays a crucial role in bee development and longevity, significantly impacting queen bees compared to worker bees.
  • The host humorously discusses royal jelly's benefits for fertility and brain health, linking it to postpartum recovery.
  • Bee pollen is described as 'nature's multivitamin,' rich in nutrients, amino acids, and potentially hormone-balancing effects.
  • The guest uses bee pollen as her sole multivitamin, combining it with coconut oil for energy and endurance.

  • Buckwheat honey is noted for its high antioxidant content and use in cough syrups.
  • Many commercial honeys, including some expensive brands, may be adulterated with corn syrup, pasteurized, or lack beneficial components like propolis.
  • The guest identifies propolis as the most potent antiviral ingredient derived from the hive.
  • Scaling Beekeeper's Naturals faces challenges from 'big pharma' competition and consumer distrust of natural remedies, requiring education and partnerships with medical professionals.

  • The guest highlights concerns about petroleum-based dyes and addictive properties in traditional cough syrups and nasal sprays like Flonase.
  • Beekeeper's Naturals offers a non-habit-forming nasal spray deemed safe for infants by pediatricians, including for the guest's own child.
  • Natural remedies like nebulizers and neti pots are discussed, with the suggestion of adding propolis to a neti pot rinse or even to pet water.

  • A brain health liposomal product is proposed for a month-long trial, based on the belief that past traumas can lead to conditions like migraines.
  • A brain scan study conducted by Dr. Amon reportedly showed improved activity in the prefrontal cortex, temporal lobe, and limbic cortex after a month of using the product.
  • Discussion includes improved sensory filtering in the thalamus and potential healing in areas of the brain damaged by concussions.
  • The product is colloquially referred to as 'Liquid Nature Adderall' due to its nootropic effects and enhanced bioavailability through liposomal delivery.
