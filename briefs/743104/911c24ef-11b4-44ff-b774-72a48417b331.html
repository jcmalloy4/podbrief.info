The Skinny Confidential Him & Her Podcast

Q&A Pt. 1: Michael & Lauryn Bosstick Answer Questions They’re Never Asked

Key Takeaways

  • Michael and Lauryn Bosstick embrace immediate, direct communication, including bickering, as their conflict resolution method.
  • Their public personas as business-focused individuals contrast with a private life prioritizing family and routine.
  • The hosts discuss their parenting philosophy of raising resilient, resourceful children who value hard work.
  • Specific beauty routines for hair and skin health, including supplements and water filtration, were detailed.
  • The discussion included the need for greater recognition of fathers' active contributions to childcare.

Deep Dive

  • Michael Bosstick believes listeners would be surprised by how frequently he and Lauryn fight and bicker.
  • They air grievances immediately, preventing issues from accumulating, a method they acknowledge may seem unusual.
  • The Bossticks consider their argumentative communication style effective for their long-standing relationship.
  • They suggest that podcasting and similar open discussions function as a form of therapy for their dynamic.

  • Michael stated he would prioritize his wife and slow down, contrasting with Lauryn's playful pushback on constant spousal obsession.
  • He described himself as more reclusive and insular than publicly perceived, needing personal time for recharging despite running businesses.
  • Lauryn observed Michael's intentional approach to social engagements, preferring meaningful connections over general networking.
  • If in Michael's role, Lauryn stated she would adopt a slower, more methodical 'sea turtle' pace, in contrast to his 'barracuda' speed.

  • The Bossticks emphasize a discrepancy between their public persona as 'alpha females' or business-focused individuals and their private home life.
  • At home, Lauryn identifies as a homemaker, creating a positive atmosphere for her children and focusing on close friendships.
  • They assert that maintaining a healthy relationship and raising children are more challenging and paramount than their business endeavors.
  • Their business success is viewed as a supplement to, rather than the foundation of, their core values and stable home environment.

  • One host detailed a two-year hair transformation, including scalp massage, stimulation, microneedling, and a diet rich in organ meats.
  • She uses Nutrafol, a dermatologist-recommended hair growth supplement, for issues like thinning and shedding, noting improvement in thickness and length.
  • Nutrafol is backed by studies and third-party certifications, having helped with hair shedding and growth difficulty.
  • The discussion highlighted Jolie filtered shower heads, which remove chlorine and heavy metals, leading to improved hair health, reduced frizz, and better color retention.

  • Michael Bosstick argues that active fathers sometimes do not receive adequate recognition for their contributions to childcare.
  • He acknowledges women's unique role in pregnancy and childbirth but advocates for greater appreciation of fathers' daily efforts.
  • Michael proposes a 60/40 split for parental contributions, noting childbirth temporarily shifts this balance.
  • The hosts discussed the societal acknowledgment of parental efforts, with differing views on which parent does 'more.'

  • The hosts express a desire to raise resilient and resourceful children who understand the importance of hard work.
  • They aim to instill the belief that 'life does not owe them anything,' actively avoiding entitlement.
  • A core parental challenge is withholding the urge to make things easier for their children to foster independence.
  • They reinforce the goal of raising capable, resilient, and independent individuals who no longer need constant parental support.

  • Lauryn expressed strong enthusiasm for The Skinny Confidential's Brow Peptide, describing it as a favorite product.
  • The formula contains castor oil and peptides, designed to enhance brow growth without side effects like fat melting around the eyes.
  • It is described as non-toxic and suitable for use during pregnancy, capable of creating a lifted, feathered brow look.
  • The product also offers multi-use potential, including application on eyelashes, according to Lauryn.

  • A playful hypothetical explored how being 'Lauren's ex' could be a significant draw for a single person in the dating world.
  • The hosts discussed the difficulty of starting over in dating after a long-term relationship.
  • An analogy of a 'muscle car' was used to describe a partner improving another, with concerns about ex-partners benefiting from these changes.
  • The conversation touched on a business perspective, where an ex-partner's association with success might signal value to the market.
