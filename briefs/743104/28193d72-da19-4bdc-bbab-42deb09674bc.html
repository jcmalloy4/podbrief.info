The Future of Health: Why Proactive Care Starts With Your Data Ft. Nate Graville Of Geviti

Key Takeaways

  • Traditional healthcare is reactive; Geviti promotes proactive, data-driven health solutions.
  • Comprehensive bloodwork offers critical insights into underlying health issues beyond symptoms.
  • Personalized, data-driven supplementation is crucial, as many individuals take incorrect or unnecessary products.
  • Declining fertility rates are a significant concern, linked to hormonal imbalances and lifestyle factors.
  • AI-driven preventative care aims to optimize health outcomes and reignite progress in life expectancy.

Deep Dive

  • Nate Graville founded Geviti after his father's death from lung cancer at 58, which highlighted the reactive nature of traditional healthcare.
  • Geviti focuses on preventative bloodwork and personalized data to address health proactively.
  • Blood is considered a critical blueprint for health, providing insights into underlying issues undetectable through feeling fine alone.
  • Comprehensive blood work, ideally done twice a year, is emphasized as the only way to obtain a full health overview.
  • Specific blood markers like CBC, C-reactive protein, and lipoprotein A can signal the need for further investigation.

  • Many individuals take supplements based on word-of-mouth or influencer recommendations, often making incorrect or unnecessary choices.
  • Geviti's data-driven approach uses bloodwork to create personalized supplement plans, highlighting that many take the wrong supplements.
  • Supplementation is deemed crucial due to modern food's lack of nutrients but requires data-driven direction, likened to using a GPS.
  • The guest advises against symptom-based or unverified information for supplementation; excessive Vitamin D intake, for example, can be toxic.
  • Geviti's panel requires about 8 blood vials, less than commercial labs (12-15), to analyze over 100 biomarkers.

  • Basic blood work can indicate hormone levels related to fertility, such as LH and FSH, with Geviti offering more comprehensive tests.
  • The guest agrees with Elon Musk's concerns about plummeting fertility rates, stating more children are needed.
  • Declining fertility is attributed to low testosterone, LH, and FSH in men, and hormonal imbalances in women often linked to prolonged birth control use.
  • Rebalancing hormones without hormone replacement therapy (HRT) is noted as difficult.
  • Lifestyle factors like excessive screen time and lack of physical activity in young men are speculated contributors to reproductive issues.

  • The current healthcare system is described as reactive, leading to increased spending and declining health outcomes.
  • Since 1970, GDP allocation to healthcare and obesity rates have risen, highlighting systemic issues.
  • Societal factors, including the abundance of processed foods, lack of fitness opportunities, and disrupted sleep, hinder healthy choices.
  • The high cost of healthy alternatives compared to cheap, processed foods is identified as a significant barrier.
  • The current system incentivizes cheap, nutrient-poor products that are addictive, contrasting with higher quality, nutrient-dense foods.

  • One speaker criticizes Brian Johnson's extreme longevity approach, referencing his olive oil brand and questioning the pursuit of physical perfection.
  • True longevity is defined as being present and capable for future generations, rather than simply maximizing lifespan.
  • The pursuit of health optimization should have a clear destination and not become an isolated, self-focused endeavor.
  • While optimizing health for a longer, healthier life is positive, the 'don't die' marketing and potential for obsession are questioned.

  • Concerns are raised that reduced alcohol consumption in Gen Z might negatively impact social interactions and opportunities for connection.
  • The wellness and anti-alcohol movements are debated for potentially decreasing human-to-human interactions, where alcohol historically served as a social lubricant.
  • Readily accessible pornography is argued to reduce the need for real-world social interaction and intimacy, particularly among young men.
  • Porn consumption is linked to hormonal imbalances, reduced ambition, and unrealistic expectations for real-life sexual experiences.
  • Brain scans are cited as evidence of the cognitive impact of pornography consumption.

  • The guest advises listeners to begin proactive healthcare by getting their blood work done, emphasizing convenience and scheduling flexibility.
  • Geviti creates customized AM and PM pharmaceutical-grade supplement packs based on individual blood work results, avoiding generic protocols.
  • The guest notes a noticeable difference in the efficacy of Geviti's supplements compared to those from less regulated sources like Amazon.
  • Geviti offers separate tests for toxins such as microplastics and lead, in addition to blood work and gut health analysis via stool samples.
  • Mold toxicity requires personalized protocols considering blood biomarkers, medical history, and wearable data, as lifestyle significantly impacts outcomes.

  • Privacy concerns regarding health data are discussed, citing instances of data leaks from genetic testing companies.
  • Geviti reassures users it will not sell data and will delete it upon request, while acknowledging industry-wide lack of transparency.
  • An open-source health platform is being developed to allow users to verify their data and ensure privacy, leveraging technology expertise.
  • The discussion balances data privacy concerns with the potential benefits of sharing health information for personal optimization and longevity.
  • Geviti emphasizes using AI and data to extend life, contrasting this with potential misuse by other entities.

  • Life expectancy significantly increased from 1900 to 2010 but has since plateaued, despite technological advancements.
  • A paradigm shift towards proactive, AI-driven healthcare is needed to reignite progress in extending lifespan.
  • Geviti aims to achieve highly accurate predictive mortality calculations by analyzing two years of blood work and wearable data.
  • This data allows for personalized health protocols designed to minimize mortality risk over five and ten-year periods.
  • Geviti emphasizes its focus on minimizing mortality risk through proactive health optimization facilitated by AI, rather than commission-driven sales.
