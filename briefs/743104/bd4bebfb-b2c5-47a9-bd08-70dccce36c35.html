The Skinny Confidential Him & Her Podcast

Pia Mance On Turning Your Side Hustle Into A Cash Cow, Leveraging AI, & The Drop Everyone Wants

Key Takeaways

  • Heaven Mayhem grew to a $10M brand in three years with an all-female team by prioritizing quick, decisive choices.
  • Strategic hiring involves testing candidates through freelance or intern projects, akin to 'dating' before committing.
  • Pia Mance manages demanding schedules and protects her energy with meticulous daily planning and a year-round 'out of office' email.
  • AI tools like Fireflies, Otter AI, and ChatGPT are leveraged for transcription, scheduling, and even simulating mentor advice.
  • The Heaven Mayhem x Lauryn Bosstick collaboration introduces 'bedside boxes' for stylish organization, launching December 11th.

Deep Dive

  • Pia Mance built Heaven Mayhem into a $10 million brand within three years without external funding, leading an 8-person all-female team.
  • Success is attributed to avoiding decision fatigue and procrastination, making quick, decisive business choices for most operational decisions.
  • Mance advises aspiring entrepreneurs to start by creating mood boards and swiftly moving to execution, such as choosing and trademarking a business name.
  • She suggests keeping some ideas private initially to avoid false gratification and maintain focus until a tangible product exists.

  • Pia Mance manages her demanding schedule, including events like Paris Fashion Week, by planning the next day the night before in her iPhone notes app.
  • Her routine saves time by using only two hairstyles, completing makeup in 15 minutes, and maintaining a consistent diet of bone broth, matcha, and meat while traveling.
  • She has maintained an 'out of office' email auto-reply year-round for five years to protect her energy, sometimes themed around creative projects.
  • Daily walks, bone broth, and matcha are key to her wellness routine, and she maximizes productivity by utilizing travel time for work tasks like filming and editing TikToks on the metro.

  • Pia Mance is noted for her warm, disarming personality in an often harsh industry environment.
  • She states that she does not tolerate negative energy and addresses rudeness constructively.
  • Mance's initial interest in The Skinny Confidential podcast began in London, solidifying into devoted listenership after moving to Los Angeles and receiving an ice roller.
  • She specifically admires the host's personality, business acumen, and direct communication style, which motivated her to collaborate.

  • The Heaven Mayhem x Lauryn Bosstick collaboration was initiated by Bosstick via text, with Mance quickly agreeing.
  • The collaboration features two 'bedside boxes,' building on the success of Heaven Mayhem's viral 'book boxes' which disguise jewelry storage as coffee table books.
  • One box is cream-colored with compartments for items like mouth tape and supplements, while the Hunter Green crocodile-stamped box holds larger items such as Kindles or books.
  • Designed to organize bedside essentials and jewelry, the limited-edition collaboration launches December 11th and is framed as selling 'peace of mind.'

  • Pia Mance strategically uses AI to dictate ideas during walks, with an AI note-taker transcribing and summarizing these thoughts to identify actionable concepts, such as a Christmas campaign.
  • She employs tools like Fireflies and Otter AI for transcription and ChatGPT for organizing daily tasks using a prompt-less method.
  • Mance customizes AI-generated content to avoid a robotic tone, personalizing it to fit her brand's voice.
  • She utilizes a unique 'Pia Mentor' chat in ChatGPT, trained on her mentor's online presence, to simulate conversations for business advice in the mentor's exact tone and style.

  • Pia Mance aims to arrive at her office by 7 AM on Mondays for focused work, before her 8-person team arrives at 8:30 AM.
  • Her all-female team works in a collaborative office environment, sitting together to facilitate quick questions and shared workspace.
  • Mance's business and personal lives are fluid, with work discussions integrated into everyday conversations.
  • Her complementary business partnership with her husband often involves his opposite perspective, leading to more efficient solutions for operational decisions.
