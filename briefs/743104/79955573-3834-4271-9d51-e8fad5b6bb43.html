Michael Bosstick On How To Make Every Year A Success & Achieve Your Personal Goals

Key Takeaways

  • Focus is a key differentiator for both successful businesses and personal relationships.
  • Adopting a single, overarching yearly theme provides a 'North Star' for decision-making and goal achievement.
  • Intentional theme-setting helps eliminate distractions and concentrate efforts on high-impact goals.
  • Reviewing past themes can reveal effective strategies and facilitate future planning.

Deep Dive

  • Michael Bosstick introduced a year-themed framework designed to provide structure, purpose, and focus for personal and professional success.
  • He stated that focus is a key differentiator for successful businesses and relationships, based on two decades of experience.
  • A lack of intentional direction is identified as a common cause for personal and professional setbacks.
  • The concept references Scott Galloway's idea that achieving everything is possible, but perhaps not simultaneously.

  • The 2020 theme of 'change' guided decisions like moving from Los Angeles to Texas, bringing on new business partners, and raising capital during COVID-19.
  • The 2021-2022 theme of 'health' led to prioritizing personal weight loss, muscle gain, and mental well-being improvements.
  • In 2023, the theme 'family' focused on developing the family unit after the birth of their second child.
  • The host's theme for 2024 is 'growth' and 'maturing,' applied to both personal development and Dear Media.

  • The host plans aggressive business growth in 2025, emphasizing a mindset to overcome entrepreneurial burnout and achieve higher success.
  • He navigated a demanding year with a new child, Dear Media's best year, a business acquisition without a banker, and property purchases.
  • Maintaining show production during his wife's maternity leave was managed by setting a clear theme and mental preparation for challenges.
  • Setting a yearly theme enables individuals to mentally prepare for and pursue specific personal or professional goals.

  • Developing new habits aligned with a yearly theme ensures positive attributes carry forward into subsequent years.
  • This approach builds foundational skills, such as maintaining fitness or developing executive capabilities.
  • Establishing a yearly theme acts as a filter, guiding focus and eliminating non-essential activities.
  • It helps avoid common entrepreneurial pitfalls like losing focus due to boredom or seeking too many new opportunities.

  • The host's upcoming theme for the year is 'elevation,' covering improvements in business, personal brand, family life, and relationships.
  • He plans to communicate this 'elevation' theme to his team and network to build company-wide momentum and shared direction.
  • Sharing a focused theme with others, like a spouse or colleagues, is crucial for gaining buy-in and building a supportive system.
  • This support system proved valuable when the host focused on health and later on business growth.

  • The episode's core takeaway for the new year is to identify a single, most important personal or professional theme.
  • This theme serves to set high-impact goals and priorities, rather than a long list of resolutions.
  • It creates a framework for decisions by aligning actions with the desired personal or professional narrative, eliminating distractions.
  • The speaker suggests reflecting on past years to identify overarching themes and analyze actions that led to specific outcomes.
