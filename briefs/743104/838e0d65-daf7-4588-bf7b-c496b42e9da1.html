Q&A Pt. 2: Michael & Lauryn Bosstick Answer Questions They’re Never Asked

Key Takeaways

  • Michael Bosstick has a daily habit of pretending to be asleep for 30 minutes.
  • A strong partnership can significantly expand business potential and strategic vision.
  • Chronic lateness is viewed as a sign of disrespect that negatively impacts relationships.
  • The hosts discussed hypothetically restarting careers as silent partners.
  • Building resilience involves finding identity in multiple areas beyond one's career.
  • Lauryn and Michael offered candid, distinct perceptions of each other's personalities.

Deep Dive

  • Michael admitted to a daily habit of pretending to be asleep for 30 extra minutes after waking.
  • Lauryn Bosstick was already aware of this practice.
  • The discussion included the difference between "marinating" and "rotting" in bed.

  • One host attributed their expanded business potential to their partner's influence.
  • The partner helped broaden their vision of what was possible in their ventures.
  • The collaboration is described as a successful creative and strategic partnership.
  • They discussed the concept that thinking big is as easy as thinking small for growth.

  • One host suggested the other's chronic lateness might stem from a control issue, despite high performance.
  • They manage schedules by sometimes adjusting reported times or adding buffer periods to accommodate lateness.
  • A past incident involved Jordan Belfort reportedly leaving a podcast recording due to host lateness.
  • Both hosts agreed that chronic lateness signals disrespect for others' time and harms relationships.

  • The hosts considered a hypothetical scenario where all their businesses and platforms vanished overnight.
  • One host expressed confidence in their capabilities, emphasizing self-talk and the belief that 'everything is figure outable'.
  • They explored building resilience by finding identity in various areas like motherhood or marriage, beyond a career.
  • The discussion included whether they would pursue the same industry or an entirely different path if starting over.

  • In a hypothetical career restart, the hosts stated they would avoid public-facing roles.
  • They would instead act as silent partners, leveraging their acquired skills.
  • Their focus would be on supporting emerging entrepreneurs behind the scenes.
  • Operating without a large infrastructure at their current age, with family, was noted as a significant challenge.

  • Michael Bosstick asked Lauryn to describe him to a stranger as the episode concluded.
  • Lauryn used hyperbolic praise for Michael, highlighting his uniqueness and unfiltered personality.
  • Lauryn advised caution when interacting with her, emphasizing her perceptiveness and seriousness.
  • She warned against underestimating her, stating she would reciprocate any judgment and offer direct creative advice.
