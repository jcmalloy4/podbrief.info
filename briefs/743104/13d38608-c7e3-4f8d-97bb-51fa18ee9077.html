Tim Tebow & Demi Tebow On Finding Your True Purpose, Faith, & Everlasting Resilience

Key Takeaways

  • Tim and Demi Tebow leverage their diverse backgrounds in sports and pageantry for global impact.
  • Their relationship is fundamentally built on a shared purpose: fighting human trafficking and advocating for vulnerable populations.
  • Both emphasize that true identity and significance derive from inherent worth and service to others, not personal achievements.
  • The Tim Tebow Foundation has evolved its mission to directly combat trafficking and exploitation.
  • Confronting difficult issues like human trafficking requires public awareness and a willingness to engage, despite discomfort.
  • Their approach to life and work is guided by core values, principles of impactful living, and a holistic focus on health and wellness.
  • Purpose is defined as an urgent, divine invitation to responsibility, found by integrating one's mindset, skills, heart, and placement to benefit a wider community.

Deep Dive

  • A pivotal mission trip to the Philippines at age 15, encountering a boy with backward feet, shifted Tim Tebow's focus from athletic achievement to serving the vulnerable.
  • His foundation evolved from caring for orphans and hospitals to combating human trafficking and exploitation.
  • Tim navigated massive public attention and the 'Tebowing' phenomenon during his NFL transition, emphasizing the importance of wise counsel for managing sudden fame.

  • Demi Tebow grew up in a small farming town in South Africa, experiencing a slower pace and strong community.
  • At age 22, she transitioned to New York City after winning Miss South Africa, viewing her role as an ambassadorship.
  • She temporarily ceased speaking Afrikaans for three months to improve her English fluency for Miss Universe preparations.

  • Tim and Demi's first phone call lasted over two hours, leading them to realize they would marry before meeting in person.
  • Their connection is built on shared purpose, specifically advocating for people with special needs and fighting human trafficking, rather than common interests.
  • They highlight that over 50 million people are trafficked globally, with the issue significantly present in the United States.

  • The conversation addresses the difficulty of discussing human trafficking on mainstream platforms, often due to its overwhelming nature.
  • People may feel helpless or unsure how to make a difference, contributing to a desire to avoid the topic.
  • Tim recounted a golf game where companions refused to hear details about 12 girls in distress, underscoring this public reluctance to engage with serious issues.

  • The Tebows' daughter's middle name, 'Rain,' was inspired by a young girl rescued at just 25 days old, deepening their personal connection to their mission.
  • Tim explains that faith forms the foundation for their philanthropic work, stemming from a belief in every person’s inherent, God-given worth.
  • Serving victims of trafficking is described as their greatest honor, providing profound fulfillment beyond other achievements.

  • The Tebows convey that every person possesses infinite value and worth, irrespective of their actions.
  • Their core values include being 'protectors' of each other and the vulnerable, emphasizing love, faith, and hope.
  • They advocate for valuing and seeking wise counsel, and outline five principles for impactful living: humility, seeking input, compassion, solidarity, and embracing their urgent divine calling.

  • Demi describes Tim at home as consistently authentic, always ready to help, and actively seeking to address problems.
  • Humorous insights reveal Tim's casual habits, including eating snacks in bed.
  • Tim praises Demi as an incredible mom, highlighting her significant passion, drive, compassion, and kindness in all her roles.

  • Demi emphasizes eliminating processed foods and sugar, noting her South African upbringing fostered farm-to-table eating.
  • Tim aims for six weightlifting sessions weekly but currently averages two to three, and consults experts like Dr. Josh Axe for guidance.
  • Tim discussed his post-football brain scan by Dr. Amen, exploring the impact of sports like football and boxing on brain trauma, leading to a focus on brain health over physical appearance.

  • Tim defines purpose as 'the reason something is done, used, created, or exists,' emphasizing it's typically larger than one's immediate job.
  • He outlines a framework for discovering purpose by evaluating one's mindset, skills, heart, and current placement to benefit others.
  • Demi suggests that true purpose is selfless, focused on benefiting others, and often found within one's hidden skills and passions.
