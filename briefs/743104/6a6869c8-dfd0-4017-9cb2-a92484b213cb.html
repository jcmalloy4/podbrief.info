Abe & Erin Lichy On The Truth About Marriage, Ambition, & Life Behind The Reality TV Curtain

Key Takeaways

  • Effective spousal communication is crucial for managing financial decisions and avoiding conflict.
  • Psychedelic experiences can significantly influence personal growth and spiritual awareness for some individuals.
  • Participating in reality television requires authenticity and navigating public scrutiny while dealing with personal issues.
  • Balancing multiple children and businesses requires structured routines and external childcare support.
  • Entrepreneurship offers flexibility but comes with inherent risks, stress, and continuous problem-solving.
  • Maintaining intimacy and shared fun is vital for the long-term health and connection in a marriage.
  • Societal stigmas surrounding sex hinder open dialogue, despite its fundamental role in relationships.
  • Conscious alcohol consumption, like choosing 100% agave mezcal, reflects growing consumer mindfulness.

Deep Dive

  • Abe Lichy's sale of Bitcoin caused frustration for Erin Lichy due to lack of prior communication.
  • Abe explained his actions stemmed from a mindset of avoiding spousal stress, acknowledging past avoidant behavior.
  • Erin recounts an on-camera argument during filming that prompted a deeper discussion about the Bitcoin sale.

  • Erin Lichy was appreciated for her honesty on 'The Real Housewives of New York' about her father and an abortion.
  • Her father's enthusiastic show participation was documented before his passing a year prior.
  • Erin shared unique intuition about conceiving her four children, experiencing a strong connection at conception.
  • She discussed the challenging experience of regaining her body shape after her fourth pregnancy.

  • Abe Lichy expressed reluctance towards vasectomies, citing a feeling of losing 'life force' and psychological leverage.
  • Couples who are also business partners face complexities in blending professional and personal lives.
  • Shared passion for business can increase productivity, but differing work dynamics pose challenges.
  • Setting boundaries for discussing work outside business hours is difficult and often unnatural.

  • Erin and Abe Lichy balance four children and businesses using systems, organization, and hired help, including a night nurse.
  • Erin noted a gendered double standard, with women often questioned for using childcare help more than men.
  • She emphasized that paid staff or family support is essential for her to be a present parent.
  • Negative social media comments were received for discussing nannies, despite their supportive role.

  • Entrepreneurship offers schedule flexibility but involves immense risk and stress compared to traditional employment.
  • It lacks the security of a fixed salary and predictable output, requiring constant problem-solving.
  • The conversation contrasted the control over one's schedule with the continuous challenges of business ownership.

  • Erin Lichy implements a weekly routine for her family, including a printed schedule in English and Spanish.
  • Her children, aged 8 and 11, follow a structured bedtime that encourages early sleep.
  • Meal preparation is delegated, reflecting a methodical approach to family management.
  • The discussion touched on fostering independence in children, referencing Malcolm Gladwell's ideas.

  • Men on 'The Real Housewives' occupy a 'liminal space,' facing public scrutiny without direct compensation.
  • They are exposed to tabloid reporting and public opinion in ways distinct from the primary cast.
  • A discussion arose about a past comment concerning infidelity, clarified as a hypothetical joke about lack of intimacy.

  • Sex is considered a crucial component for connection in intimate relationships, often overlooked.
  • Busy schedules or lack of connection can lead couples to stop having sex, eroding the initial spark.
  • The guests' business, 'Woo,' addresses the neglect of sex lives despite its importance to happiness.
  • Societal shame around sex contributes to discomfort in discussing it, hindering open dialogue and normalization.

  • Abe and Erin Lichy's Mezcalum mezcal is less smoky than traditional varieties, designed for cocktails without overpowering other flavors.
  • It is made from 100% agave, unlike tequila, which can contain additives.
  • Mezcal uses up to 80 different species of agave, while tequila is limited to one (blue weber).
  • Mezcalum is highlighted for its purity, no additives, low glycemic index, probiotic qualities, and low calorie count.
