Jeffree Star: Unfiltered, Uncensored, Speaking His Truth, & Redefining Success on His Own Terms

Key Takeaways

  • Jeffree Star launched his cosmetics brand in 2014, leveraging his YouTube presence for rapid growth.
  • Star's entrepreneurial drive stems from a difficult childhood and a hands-on approach to product development.
  • He views his move to Wyoming and the launch of Yak Star Ranch as a shift from materialism to a simpler, more authentic life.
  • Star emphasizes ethical, free-range yak ranching, contrasting it with factory farming practices.
  • He opened 'The Jeffree Star Store Makeup and Meat' in Casper, Wyoming, combining cosmetics and yak products.
  • Star addresses cancel culture, stressing authenticity and accountability for genuinely harmful actions, not past mistakes.
  • He offers unique perspectives on gender identity, societal polarization, and the amplification of negative online voices.
  • Star engages in extensive, unpublicized charitable work in Wyoming, donating millions to the local community.
  • He shares personal views on modern society's impact on spirituality and seeks grounding in nature.

Deep Dive

  • Jeffree Star started his YouTube channel in 2009 while in 10th grade, initially with vlogs and music videos.
  • He founded Jeffree Star Cosmetics in 2014, aiming to dominate the beauty market amid rising influencer culture.
  • Star's early drive was fueled by a difficult childhood, leading him to offer business advice and work with celebrities.
  • He rejected white-labeling, emphasizing his hands-on approach to product development for items like brow pencils and lip liners from the Czech Republic and Korea.

  • Jeffree Star transitioned from a music career, launching Jeffree Star Cosmetics with just three liquid lipsticks.
  • The brand achieved an immediate sell-out on Black Friday 2014, signaling significant business potential.
  • His business empire includes Killer Merch, which started small and expanded to handle logistics and merchandise for various clients.
  • The brand's rapid growth was attributed to YouTube and audience investment in Star's journey, dominating a market with few competitors.

  • Jeffree Star discussed his past financial behaviors, including 'faking it till you make it' on MySpace and acquiring wealth within five years.
  • He noted a 'trip' from having little to immense wealth, admitting to buying possessions to fill a void from childhood trauma.
  • Star realized materialistic items, like a large home with amenities, did not provide lasting fulfillment.
  • After 7-8 years of brand building, he decided to move from Los Angeles, prioritizing experiences and a simpler life over material possessions.

  • Jeffree Star's move to Casper, Wyoming, was a spontaneous decision facilitated by a private jet trip.
  • He noted initial apprehension from some Casper locals about his move from Los Angeles.
  • Star explained his choice of Wyoming, contrasting it with tourist-heavy areas like Jackson Hole.
  • He described Casper as a 'cute' town with 60,000 people and found its residents to be friendly and helpful, unlike perceived self-interest in Los Angeles.

  • Jeffree Star expanded his Wyoming land from 70 to 1000 acres, creating 'Star Creek Road' and teaching himself ranching.
  • He was inspired by seeing yaks in Switzerland, noting their unique and quirky personalities.
  • Star distinguishes between pet yaks and those raised for meat, emphasizing ethical, free-range, grass-fed practices.
  • He reflected on his past as a 'fast food addict' and aims to provide a healthier alternative to factory-farmed meat.

  • Jeffree Star's acquisition and cross-breeding of yaks disrupted rivalries among older ranchers.
  • He registered his yak herd with the North American Yak Association through GeneSeq in Nebraska, following extensive research.
  • Star began producing and selling yak meat, generating significant revenue and educating consumers on healthier eating.
  • He opened 'The Jeffree Star Store Makeup and Meat' in Casper, Wyoming, a 7,000 square-foot store offering cosmetics and yak products like jerky and moisturizers.

  • Jeffree Star shared that expressing controversial views resulted in losing 120,000 followers, but he subsequently gained half a million more.
  • He discussed his views on gender identity, referencing a Disneyland situation and contrasting it with a past focus on 'passable' transition.
  • Star expressed concern about the encouragement of gender experimentation in young children and the sexualization of content aimed at them.
  • He believes algorithms amplify negative voices, and there's a need for more positive online discourse.

  • Jeffree Star clarified misconceptions about him, stating he cares for people and wants others to have the best life.
  • He revealed extensive charitable work in Wyoming, having donated millions without public acknowledgment.
  • This philanthropy aims to counter perceptions of him being an attention-seeker and to invest in his local community, including the Wyoming food bank.
  • Star contrasted negative Wyoming stereotypes, fueled by media like 'Wind River,' with his positive personal experience in the state.

  • Jeffree Star expressed a belief that 95% of men are not exclusively attracted to women, based on his own research.
  • He, identifying as a feminine man, shared experiences of men struggling with same-sex attraction due to societal shame.
  • Star recounted observing different societal approaches to sexuality during a trip to Senegal, Africa, with Akon.
  • He clarified his own sexual openness, stating he is attracted to all genders, and views sexual and gender identity categories as distinct.

  • Jeffree Star enjoys grounding activities like standing barefoot in nature on his Wyoming ranch, linking it to spiritual practice.
  • He criticized modern society and government actions, such as food/water 'poisoning' and daylight savings, as detrimental to mental well-being.
  • Star finds peace in nature, contrasting it with his high-performance career, and prefers physical books over e-readers.
  • He finds therapy in memoirs about overcoming addiction and trauma, given his family's history.
