The Skinny Confidential Him & Her Podcast

The Conversations Women Only Have In Private Ft. Lauryn Bosstick & Kenzie Burke

Key Takeaways

  • Kenzie Burke transformed from a stylist to a wellness entrepreneur, achieving rapid financial success in 2019-2020.
  • She faced significant online backlash and felt "canceled" after her initial success, leading to personal introspection.
  • Burke navigated financial struggles and bankruptcy, learning valuable lessons about business and personal boundaries.
  • She openly shares her journey, including failures, to inspire resilience and destigmatize financial setbacks.
  • Her current brand, Brulee, reflects an evolved business strategy focusing on direct-to-consumer and intentional design.
  • Burke emphasizes authenticity, self-belief, and the freedom to pivot as crucial elements of her entrepreneurial path.

Deep Dive

  • Kenzie Burke's 2019-2020 PDF guide generated $500,000 in two months, marking her pivot to wellness.
  • This rapid success led to immediate purchases, including a Range Rover and new apartment furnishings.
  • Her strong work ethic, honed from childhood in Wisconsin, drove her to quickly scale into products and an app.
  • Burke admitted initial shock and happiness, followed by rapid spending and a frantic drive to sustain wealth.

  • Burke faced significant "canceled" online backlash after her podcast appearance, accused of promoting eating disorders.
  • Her content on fruits and vegetables, now common, garnered strong negative reactions at the time.
  • The isolation from public criticism prompted her to explore childhood traumas during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • She pursued personal evolution through reading, therapy, and spiritual practices, embracing flexibility.

  • Kenzie Burke launched Brulee independently in Los Angeles, working months to find manufacturers.
  • Early Brulee ventures incurred substantial financial losses from over-purchasing units and high costs due to naivete.
  • She attributed issues to being "screwed over" and not trusting intuition when too many people were involved.
  • Burke learned lessons about boundaries after attracting individuals who left her financially responsible.

  • Burke hit "rock bottom" after a business venture, losing a close friend due to misaligned intentions.
  • She paused her Brulee business, feeling overwhelmed by personal struggles including her parents' divorce.
  • Her financial and personal setbacks contrasted with the perceived linear success of her peers.

  • Burke filed for bankruptcy for one company, calling it a necessary step to relieve overwhelming financial weight.
  • Post-bankruptcy, she became more vocal online during an election, viewing it as a cathartic release.
  • Her strategy during the election involved deliberate posts to encourage disagreement and unity.
  • She created "gone rogue" hats, coinciding with the end of her bankruptcy process.

  • Burke shares her bankruptcy story, emphasizing starting her business with just $500 and working holidays.
  • She identifies as a "wild girl" whose resilience makes her compelling despite public perplexity.
  • Burke's "decisiveness in her indecisiveness" is described as a superpower, guiding her artistic, non-traditional design.
  • She rejects societal expectations, feeling a divine calling to share her entrepreneurial journey openly.

  • Post-bankruptcy, Burke rebuilt Brulee with a direct-to-consumer model, eliminating middlemen.
  • She intensely focused on finding a trustworthy manufacturer and preparing patterns for the relaunch.
  • The brand relaunched on her birthday in March, with Burke independently managing all operations.
  • Downtime provided clarity and preparation, fostering "unstoppable momentum" for the new launch.

  • Burke applies 'Art of War' principles, using limited-time store openings to create demand for Brulee.
  • This strategy manages creative energy, contrasting with her prior wellness business where constant creation led to bankruptcy.
  • She describes her journey as a "360" experience, embracing messiness to achieve desired outcomes.
  • Burke secured a strong partnership with a designer clothing manufacturer for Brulee.

  • Burke acknowledges internet misconceptions about her "pure-hearted" intentions and truthfulness.
  • The host attributes Burke's success to her "indecisiveness" as a superpower, often misunderstood.
  • Burke considers embracing authenticity and individuality, despite doubts, her boldest risk, not bankruptcy.
  • She values engaging with diverse opinions to challenge her own perspectives.
