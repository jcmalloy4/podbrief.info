My First Million

5 App Ideas for ChatGPT’s New App Store ft. Greg Isenberg

Key Takeaways

  • OpenAI's new ChatGPT App Store is identified as a major wealth creation opportunity for developers.
  • The App Store enables ChatGPT to integrate diverse applications and contextually suggest them to users, reaching up to 800 million weekly active users.
  • Brainstormed AI app ideas include specialized tools for tax preparation, healthcare navigation, meme generation, and credit repair.
  • Rapid development of Minimum Viable Products for AI applications is feasible, with some projects estimated to take as little as 2-3 weeks using no-code tools.

Deep Dive

  • The ChatGPT App Store allows integration of tools like Figma for design and Coursera for education, expanding ChatGPT's capabilities.
  • Apps can be discovered by manual addition or contextual suggestions from ChatGPT itself, enhancing user engagement.
  • The platform now supports visual content consumption and introduces new risks related to data sharing and privacy.
  • Developers have a significant opportunity to create apps that can reach ChatGPT's 800 million weekly active users.

  • The 'AI Tax Guy' is proposed as an application designed to simplify quarterly tax filing by connecting to financial platforms.
  • It aims to auto-fill tax forms and streamline the process, addressing complex tax management like K1 forms.
  • An example of a successful niche application, BankStatementConverter.com, earns $40-50k per month by solving a specific financial data problem.

  • An 'AI Healthcare Concierge' app is proposed to help users find local specialists based on insurance coverage.
  • The app would offer booking options and could potentially be monetized through affiliate partnerships or acquisition by platforms like ZocDoc.
  • The guest estimates such an application could be built within 2-3 weeks using no-code development tools.

  • The potential for AI-powered meme generation is discussed, noting that current tools like ChatGPT can facilitate this process.
  • There is an identified opportunity for optimized applications specifically designed for meme creation.
  • This area is linked to OpenAI's broader strategy to increase user engagement and platform utility.

  • The hosts identified a market opportunity for a transparent and effective AI-driven credit repair service.
  • Both hosts shared personal experiences with unexpectedly low credit scores due to overlooked issues, highlighting a widespread problem.
  • The development of such a service would require a strong domain name and potentially significant investment to build trust and efficacy.
