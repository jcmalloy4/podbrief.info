My First Million

5 AI Tools I’d Use to Make $1M (w/o employees, capital, or time)

Key Takeaways

  • AI tools are rapidly advancing, creating realistic deepfakes and automating complex tasks.
  • Specialized AI platforms can significantly boost productivity across browsing, data analysis, and content creation.
  • AI-first browsers and voice dictation tools offer streamlined workflows and increased efficiency.
  • AI facilitates content automation for social media and democratizes opportunities for entrepreneurs and job seekers.

Deep Dive

  • AI-generated media, including a Taylor Swift deepfake using WAN 2.2, is becoming indistinguishable from reality.
  • Concerns exist about misuse, such as a criminal using a deepfake to access a wealthy individual's apartment.
  • Tools like enhancer.ai simplify deepfake creation, enabling commercial applications with public domain figures like JFK.
  • An ad expert reportedly generates hundreds of millions of views for major companies using AI-generated videos.

  • Perplexity Comet is introduced as an AI-first browser that integrates AI capabilities directly into the browsing workflow.
  • The tool streamlines research, exemplified by using it to investigate viral video techniques.
  • It contrasts with traditional browsers like Chrome by embedding AI directly into the user experience.

  • Comet can pinpoint specific moments within YouTube videos, such as Steve Jobs discussing technology and liberal arts.
  • A Chrome plugin generates YouTube video transcripts, allowing analysis via ChatGPT for deeper insights.
  • Perplexity can find LinkedIn profiles matching specific criteria, like former Apple AI employees now at Meta.
  • The tool can draft and potentially send outreach emails to identified talent, comparable to LinkedIn Recruiter functionality.

  • Whisperflow is an AI voice-to-text dictation tool significantly boosting productivity.
  • Users can dictate at 220 words per minute, compared to average typing speeds of 45 words per minute.
  • The tool corrects mistakes mid-sentence and formats text, including bullet points.
  • A snippet library allows quick insertion of common phrases, further accelerating workflow.

  • Shortcut AI is presented as an AI-first alternative to traditional spreadsheet software like Excel.
  • It allows users to interact with spreadsheet data using natural language prompts.
  • The tool can generate complex financial analyses, including Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) models.
  • This offers a solution to limitations of current Excel AI integrations.

  • Reel.Farm automates the creation of TikTok slideshows to drive traffic and promote software.
  • The tool sources images from platforms like Pinterest to create slideshows on various topics.
  • It automates content creation and scheduling, leveraging TikTok's current promotion of slideshow formats.
  • This offers an alternative to expensive agencies for content marketing.

  • Reel.Farm extends its capabilities to offer a User-Generated Content (UGC) ad avatar feature.
  • This feature uses AI to create product videos from templates.
  • Users can provide hooks, background music, and visuals to customize the AI-generated content.
  • This tool is adopted by companies like A16Z and Substack for customer acquisition.

  • AI tools like AI Apply are discussed for their potential to help individuals achieve financial goals.
  • The tools aim to democratize opportunities for entrepreneurs and job seekers without connections or capital.
  • The conversation acknowledges concerns about AI-powered consumerism and potential addiction to AI-generated content.
  • One speaker estimates spending 10 hours weekly exploring new AI applications, proficient in approximately ten tools.
