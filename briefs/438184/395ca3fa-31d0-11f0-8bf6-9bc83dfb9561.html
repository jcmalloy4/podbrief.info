My First Million

How Scott Galloway Turned $8M into $120M Through Investing

Overview

* Distressed investing offers superior returns compared to "sexier" investments, as demonstrated by the speaker's 4.5X return on FTX bankruptcy claims and 30X return on a vaping company sold to Altria for $2.2 billion after obtaining FDA approval.

* The speaker predicts a major capital flow reversal from U.S. markets to international markets in 2024-2025, citing an unusually long 14-15 year U.S. bull market and currently attractive European valuations, and is personally repositioning his portfolio accordingly.

* For most investors, the speaker recommends geographic diversification through index funds and residential real estate, which offers advantages through leverage, depreciation benefits, and less frequent valuation fluctuations.

* Modern society faces a relationship crisis, with declining marriage and childbirth rates (only 27% of 30-year-olds now have children versus 60% forty years ago), requiring both individual initiative in social connections and broader societal solutions.

* The speaker's life philosophy emphasizes that success and failure are rarely entirely your fault, advocating for humility during success, forgiveness during failure, and prioritizing learning, kindness, and meaningful relationships over constant ambition.

Content

FTX Bankruptcy Claims Investment

  • The speaker discusses a successful investment strategy involving FTX bankruptcy claims
  • Purchased $10 million in FTX bankruptcy claims for $2.2 million
  • Identified potential value in FTX's Anthropic investment
  • Estimated creditors might recover 50-160 cents on the dollar
  • Found a broker named Thomas Brazile who specializes in bankruptcy claims
  • Worked with Brazile to purchase and verify individual claims
  • Offered Brazile 10% of potential upside for his assistance
  • The broker would identify and purchase claims, then transfer them to the speaker's account
  • The speaker emphasizes this wasn't pure luck, but the result of careful research
  • Describes the process as complex and risky, involving multiple verification steps

Investment Philosophy and Distressed Assets

  • The speaker argues that distressed investing is the best asset class
  • Key principle: "The sexier an investment, the lower the returns"
  • Has experience across multiple investment stages: seed, venture, growth, public, and distressed
  • Highlights a significant win: 30X return on a consumer company brought out of bankruptcy, partnering with Jason Mudrick
  • Described distressed investing as complex and unappealing, but potentially highly profitable
  • Discussed a case involving a claim potentially sold to multiple parties
  • Emphasized the importance of thorough diligence and being first to file a claim

Personal Investment Experiences

  • Shared a recent investment loss with Oddity (AI beauty company)
  • Lost approximately $2 million by selling covered calls without proper due diligence
  • Missed potential earnings by not researching upcoming company announcements

Enjoy (Vaping Company) Investment

  • Invested in Enjoy, a vaping company focused on smoking cessation
  • Nicotine is addictive but doesn't cause cancer
  • Vaping is considered less harmful than smoking combustible tobacco
  • The speaker was motivated by friends who quit smoking using the product
  • Faced industry challenges:
- Juul previously marketed to teenagers and faced significant backlash - High schools experienced severe vaping problems - "Popcorn lung" incident where kids bought dangerous black market vaping liquids
  • Experienced personal ethical struggle:
- Felt conflicted about potentially harming young people - Resigned from the board of his child's school due to vaping concerns - Attempted to sell his stake but found no market
  • Investment outcome:
- Invested $2.5 million when the company was valued at $60 million - Company pursued FDA approval as a regulatory strategy - Spent approximately $100 million obtaining FDA approval - Sold to Altria for $2.2 billion, representing a 30X return on investment

Other Notable Investments

  • Invested $5 million in a healthcare texting company (98.6) that went to zero in 18 months
  • Invested around $4 million in Dex Media (Yellow Pages):
- Yellow Pages business was declining 12% annually - Revenue of $600 million, EBITDA of $280 million - Bought in at $2-3 per share, stock later rose to $15-20 - Strategy involved buying Yellow Pages companies in different countries, consolidating operations, cutting costs faster than revenue decline, and transitioning from Yellow Pages to CRM software company

Investment Strategy Recommendations

  • Advocates for investing in "unsexy" industries
  • Argues that top talent in less glamorous fields (like tax law) can earn significantly more than top talent in romantic/competitive industries (like acting)
  • For most people: invest in diversified index funds, especially internationally
  • Believes U.S. market may experience a 10-15 year downturn due to economic and political factors
  • Suggests geographic diversification across Asian, European markets
  • Recommends residential real estate as a potentially superior long-term investment strategy:
- Ability to leverage (5:1 ratio) - Annual depreciation benefits - Less frequent valuation fluctuations - Potential for consistent returns

Personal Investment Approach

  • Has unique access to investment opportunities due to professional background (economics degree, Morgan Stanley experience)
  • Transitioning U.S. holdings to international investments
  • Prefers investments not marked daily to reduce emotional stress
  • Current goal is "not getting poor" rather than getting rich
  • No specific target rate of return
  • Loosely aims to maintain financial stability
  • Emphasizes understanding potential portfolio risks through stress testing
  • Aims to find 12 positive, uncorrelated income streams for optimal risk-adjusted returns

2024-2025 Market Predictions

  • Predicts a massive capital flow reversal out of U.S. markets into non-U.S. markets
  • Believes economic cycles typically last ~8 years, but U.S. has been on an unprecedented 14-15 year run
  • Expects significant multiple contraction in U.S. markets (from 26 to high teens or lower)
  • Sees European markets as particularly attractive, with undervalued companies trading at single-digit P/E ratios
  • Selling down U.S. stocks, plans to be almost entirely out of U.S. market within 3-4 months
  • Invested in Vertical Aerospace (VTOL company) as a speculative bet
  • Invested in Atlanta Partners fund focusing on small/mid-cap opportunities in Latin America and Europe
  • Interested in European defense spending (expected to increase from 1.9% to 3% of GDP)
  • Potential incremental defense spending of $200 billion annually
  • Interested in last-mile transportation solutions, particularly electric vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) technologies

Real Estate Investment Strategy

  • Focuses on high-end residential real estate in five key locations billionaires prefer: Dubai, London, Palm Beach, Aspen, and New York
  • Believes these properties will appreciate in value due to limited supply and high demand from wealthy individuals
  • Uses real estate as both a financial investment and a way to maintain family connections

Political and Economic Observations

  • Discusses corruption across political parties, suggesting both Democrats and Republicans engage in unethical practices
  • Highlights growing income inequality and the emergence of "transnational oligarchs" who have detached interests from their home countries
  • Notes significant wealth concentration: billionaires increased from 500 to 2,500 in 10 years
  • Number of millionaires in the US has doubled since 2020
  • Top 0.01% of wealthy individuals have increasing global mobility and resources
  • Discusses Trump's meme coin (Trump coin) and its financial dynamics
  • Suggests potential for foreign interference through cryptocurrency manipulation

Health and Mortality Reflections

  • Emphasizes the importance of health, noting that people with good health have many problems, while those with poor health have one primary problem
  • Shares personal experiences of friends dying unexpectedly in their 50s from sudden health issues
  • Advocates for living in the moment and not postponing life experiences

Personal Freedom and Societal Expectations

  • Reflects on the concept of true freedom beyond financial means
  • Struggles with self-censorship due to fear of criticism from different political sides
  • Aims to catalyze productive conversations while being constrained by fear of judgment
  • Seeks to break free from societal expectations and personal insecurities

Life Philosophy

  • Emphasizes the importance of learning, enjoying life, being kind, and surrounding oneself with loving relationships
  • Critiques the constant pursuit of ambition
  • Key advice: "Nothing's ever as good or as bad as it seems"
  • Major insights include:
- Success and failure are often not entirely your fault - At the end of life, people regret their emotional reactions more than actual events - Be humble during successes and forgiving during failures - Recognize that timing and circumstances play a significant role in outcomes
  • Identifies as an atheist
  • Emphasizes the importance of self-compassion and resilience

Mental Health and Online Interactions

  • Discusses experiencing mental health challenges triggered by online comments, particularly regarding his pro-Israel stance
  • Has been called "Professor Genocide" in online comments, which deeply affected him
  • Now tries to handle online criticism more constructively:
- Reads first 10 comments to understand perspectives - Recognizes many negative comments come from bots - Tries to appreciate positive and constructive feedback - Stopped using X (Twitter), which helped reduce stress

Admired Figures and Inspirations

  • Sam Harris - admired for being disciplined, thoughtful, and unafraid to speak his mind
  • Other respected professionals/writers mentioned:
- Matt Levine (Bloomberg) - Bill Cohen (Puck) - Josh Brown and Barry Ritholtz - David Brooks

Personal Growth and Parenting

  • Reflects on shifting from money-driven decisions in his 20s and 30s to seeking a new "game" or purpose
  • Recognizes two types of people: those continuing to chase money and those who choose to start a new challenge
  • Discusses the challenges of parenting, particularly finding babies (0-18 months) difficult
  • Highlights the importance of taking pictures to capture scarce, fleeting moments with children
  • Notes that the period from ages 3-15 is the most enjoyable for parenting
  • Shares personal experience of becoming more emotionally vulnerable, easily moved to tears by child-related memories
  • Describes human behavior as driven by scarcity (food, mating opportunities, play)
  • Uses Apple memories and photos to preserve and revisit meaningful moments
  • Quotes Gloria Vanderbilt about happiness being most profound during years with young children

Relationship Practices and Societal Trends

  • Discusses a practice learned from Tony Robbins where couples review their best photos/videos together (called "emotional flooding")
  • This helps maintain connection, especially for couples with young children
  • Serves as an alternative to meditation that feels more meaningful
  • Notes significant decline in young people's ability to form relationships and have children
  • Key statistics highlighted:
- 60% of 30-year-olds had children 40 years ago, now only 27% - 51% of men aged 18-24 have never asked a woman out in person - Men who haven't cohabitated/married by 30 have a one-in-three chance of substance abuse problem
  • Loneliness impacts men more severely than women
  • Economic challenges are making relationship formation and family creation increasingly difficult for younger generations
  • Suggests need for programs helping people under 41 meet others and create social connections
  • Emphasizes creating more "third places" for social interaction

Advice for Young People on Networking

  • References "The Game" by Neil Strauss, a book about pickup artistry
  • Advice for young people (particularly men) on personal and professional networking:
- Send LinkedIn profiles and job applications - Seek out and connect with interesting potential friends - Develop confidence in approaching strangers - Be comfortable expressing romantic interest while ensuring others feel safe
  • Success comes through experiencing many rejections ("a shit ton of no's")
  • Recommended conversation starters/techniques:
- Little Dicky's polite pickup line: "What's your availability for being hit on?" - Asking someone to take a photo, then suggesting a joint photo
