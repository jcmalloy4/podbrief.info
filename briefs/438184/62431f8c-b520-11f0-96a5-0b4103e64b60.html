My First Million

From McDonald’s to Queen of Wall St

Key Takeaways

  • Cathie Wood detailed her career journey from early jobs to managing billions in assets.
  • ARK Invest utilizes an open research strategy, incorporating external expert brainstorms.
  • The firm actively trades disruptive innovation stocks, balancing long-term conviction with market volatility.
  • ARK aims for a minimum 15% compound annual return over five years, which it has achieved since inception.
  • Tesla is identified as a significant "embodied AI" investment, focusing on robo-taxis and humanoid robots.
  • Autonomous transportation is projected to become an $8-10 trillion market in the coming years.

Deep Dive

  • Cathie Wood began her career with early jobs at McDonald's and a supermarket.
  • A pivotal moment occurred when Professor Art Laffer introduced her to Capital Group, launching her investment career.
  • Her early strategy involved integrating new technologies, such as time-sharing systems, for economic analysis within her firm.

  • ARK conducts daily research meetings from 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM involving investment and portfolio management teams.
  • Weekly 'brainstorm' sessions on Fridays at 10:30 AM invite approximately 40 external experts to challenge internal research.
  • ARK openly releases its evolving research and uses X (formerly Twitter) as its primary platform for information dissemination and debate.

  • ARK maintains long-term conviction in specific stocks like Tesla but also engages in active trading.
  • The firm utilizes market volatility, driven by over 75% algorithmic trading, to its advantage.
  • Positions, such as Tesla, are rebalanced to manage portfolio percentage and capitalize on lower entry prices.

  • ARK's objective is a minimum 15% compound annual rate of return over five years, which it reports achieving since inception.
  • In 2020, during COVID-19, ARK's fund achieved a 150% return by sharing research and trades publicly.
  • Performance challenges post-2020 were attributed to unexpected supply chain bottlenecks impacting unit growth models.

  • Hedge fund and venture capital fee structures have evolved since the 1980s, driven by increased competition.
  • Some fund models waive carry fees to gain direct access to startup cap tables, such as SpaceX and OpenAI.
  • Established venture capital firms like Sequoia benefit from network effects and brand recognition in securing top startups.

  • ARK initially invested in NVIDIA in 2014 for autonomous driving but has since shifted focus to other AI plays.
  • Tesla is identified as a significant "embodied AI" project, concentrating on developing robo-taxis and humanoid robots.
  • The robo-taxi market is projected to reach $8-10 trillion in revenue within 5-10 years, with Tesla potentially capturing $4-5 trillion.

  • The historical cost per mile for human transportation has remained stable at approximately $1.10 for the past century.
  • Self-driving electric vehicles are projected to reduce this cost to $0.25 per mile, representing a fourfold decrease.
  • Wright's Law supports consistent cost declines with technological advancement, making EVs more cost-effective for future transportation.

  • The projected autonomous transportation market is estimated at $8-10 trillion, significantly larger than current ride-sharing services like Uber and Lyft's $60 billion.
  • Waymo is reportedly surpassing Lyft in daily miles driven in San Francisco, indicating consumer adoption despite geo-fencing limitations.
  • Projections anticipate prices will drop to $0.25 per mile at scale, down from current rates of $2-2.50 per mile, with potential for surge pricing up to $8 per mile.
