My First Million

I Did Nothing For 2 Weeks. It Made Me Better At Everything.

Key Takeaways

  • Sam Parr's two-week paternity leave highlighted differing parental bonding experiences.
  • Deliberate inaction and 'think time' are crucial for fostering creative breakthroughs.
  • Shaan Puri is developing 'One Hour Books' to condense valuable ideas into concise formats.
  • Parental belief and consistent encouragement significantly shape a child's future success.
  • A startup is generating $100 million annually by organizing group travel to combat loneliness.

Deep Dive

  • Sam Parr took a two-week break for his wife's baby and his grandpa's funeral, noting his wife's C-section experience.
  • He did not feel an immediate connection with his newborn, with a deep emotional bond developing after approximately 15 months.
  • The hosts discussed strategies for paternity leave, suggesting a flexible period spread over three to four months postpartum.
  • Sam expressed feeling ready to return to work sooner than two weeks, highlighting differing parental experiences.

  • The hosts advocate for 'dedicated think time' as a crucial 'accident' for breakthroughs, distinct from passive leisure.
  • Tim Ferriss's routine, involving significant 'puttering' and periods of inaction, is cited for fostering deep thinking and decision-making.
  • 'Engineered rest' methods, such as Aaron Sorkin's frequent showers and Einstein's aimless boating, are presented as catalysts for creative thought.
  • This contrasts with modern habits of problem-solving while stationary and staring at screens.

  • Shaan Puri is launching 'One Hour Books,' a series of concise books designed to distill 'life-changing ideas' into single-sitting reads.
  • The project aims to innovate by doing the opposite of lengthy, unsubstantial books, a personal pet peeve for Puri.
  • Puri's goal is to become 'the TED for books,' establishing a consistent format and high-quality content.
  • He plans to dedicate 10,000 hours of research for each book to provide readers with the most valuable ideas and stories in a one-hour read.

  • Writing a book is characterized as a low-odds, long-term endeavor, often undertaken for personal fulfillment rather than external reward.
  • This contrasts with more immediate and potentially lucrative ventures like podcasts or building companies.
  • Authors such as Ron Chernow and Robert Greene are highlighted as craftsmen who create dense, purposeful prose.
  • Creative breakthroughs for authors like J.K. Rowling are suggested to occur during periods of idle motion.

  • The hosts discuss the increased challenge of deep work today due to constant digital notifications from platforms like text, Slack, Twitter, Instagram, and email.
  • Shonda Rhimes's creative process involves two hours of focused deep work daily, a disciplined approach to overcome initial mediocre output.
  • Paul Graham's concept of 'good procrastination' is introduced, where intense focus on one's life's work occurs while societal expectations are ignored.

  • Shaan recounts his grandfather instilling a profound belief in his father's greatness, despite growing up in extreme poverty in rural India.
  • The story of Aaron, a father who created the Instagram account 'Raising Ronaldo' for his talented soccer daughter, illustrates consistent encouragement.
  • Soccer player Marcelo's grandfather sold his car to fund daily training, consistently affirming, 'You're the best' and 'One day you'll be playing for Brazil,' significantly contributing to his success.

  • Shaan's father impulsively took an IIT entrance exam, was accepted, and received a scholarship to the University of Colorado Boulder, arriving with only six dollars.
  • Upon establishing himself, his father successfully sponsored his grandfather and brother to immigrate to the U.S.
  • Shaan's grandfather demonstrated an entrepreneurial spirit, running vending machines and a snack shop, and pursued new skills like typing, working at Circuit City into his 80s.

  • 'Little Blue Books' were a historical publishing success, selling an estimated 200-500 million copies.
  • Many titles featured catchy, copy-worthy phrases and concepts, distilling ideas into concise formats.
  • The historical publishing model demonstrates success in addressing specific consumer needs for accessible information.

  • A company, possibly named Weed Road, reportedly generates $100 million annually by organizing group travel experiences for professionals aged 20-40, primarily women.
  • The business is projected to reach $160 million in revenue this year, up from $100 million, operating with approximately 30% gross margins.
  • The model addresses the scarcity of belonging by facilitating in-person community through curated group trips with 8-15 participants and tour guides.
  • The company, founded around 2020, taps into macro trends of physical experiences and the need for connection.
