Happier with Gretchen Rubin

A Little Happier: After My Beloved Father Died, My Mother, Sister, and I Wrote This Obituary

Key Takeaways

  • Gretchen Rubin and her family collaboratively wrote a detailed obituary for her father, Jack Kraft.
  • Jack Kraft, who died at 87, was a distinguished Kansas City lawyer and influential civic leader.
  • His public service included spearheading the Union Station redevelopment and advising key political figures.
  • Kraft was a devoted family man, known as 'Smiling Jack,' who enjoyed diverse hobbies and supported his daughters.
  • Gretchen Rubin provided guidance on self-reflection as a strategy for navigating significant life transitions.

Deep Dive

  • Gretchen Rubin introduced the episode focusing on her father, Jack Kraft, who passed away in December at age 87.
  • Born in Denver, Colorado, and raised in North Platte, Nebraska, he was married for 64 years to his high school sweetheart, Karen Winfrey.
  • He earned a history degree from the University of Nebraska, where he was president of Beta Theta Pi and played tennis.
  • His early work, including surveying for Union Pacific and working in a bottling plant, influenced his decision to become a lawyer after Northwestern Law School.

  • Jack Kraft served as legal counsel to the Kansas City police board and advised Mayor Richard Berkeley.
  • He assisted John Danforth's campaign for Missouri Attorney General and later joined his office, advocating for bipartisan government.
  • Kraft founded the firm Kraft, Fridken, and Rhine in 1987, which merged with Lathrop Engage in 2004.
  • He chaired the Public Improvements Advisory Committee, the Kansas City Port Authority, and led the successful bi-state initiative to fund Union Station's redevelopment.

  • Known as 'Smiling Jack' by his granddaughter Eliza, he was described as a loving, fair, and fun-loving father who made fudge and read stories.
  • He advised his daughters to 'enjoy the process' and took interest in their professional lives, learning about Hollywood and publishing economics.
  • His diverse interests included golf, tennis, yoga, daily walks in gardens, and leading trips to various attractions.
  • An avid reader, he consumed newspapers daily and enjoyed books from 'Game of Thrones' to 'War and Peace'.
  • He traveled extensively with his wife Karen, visiting museums like the Met multiple times.

  • Gretchen Rubin provided advice to Craig Robinson and Michelle Obama on how to remain grounded during significant life changes.
  • Rubin suggested asking reflective questions about current time usage, personal values, desired habit adjustments, and the balance between solitude and social interaction.
  • She emphasized that increased self-knowledge is key to better decision-making, which in turn leads to greater happiness and stability when navigating transitions.
