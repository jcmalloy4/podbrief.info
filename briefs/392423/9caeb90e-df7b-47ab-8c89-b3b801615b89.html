Happier with Gretchen Rubin

Ep. 567: Do You Feel Unprepared for the New Year? Plus Roller-Derby Names

Key Takeaways

  • Listeners received advice on comprehensive medication lists, including vitamins and supplements.
  • The hosts detailed an annual 'look back, look forward' exercise for personal reflection.
  • A home cotton candy maker was suggested as a unique party entertainment hack.
  • The 'Read 25 in 25' challenge concluded, encouraging engagement with literature.
  • The importance of maintaining exercise for well-being was highlighted.

Deep Dive

  • Listeners emphasized including all forms of medication, vitamins, and supplements in their lists.
  • A suggested hack for easy access was taking a photo of the completed medication list.

  • The exercise includes a 'look back' component, reviewing past year sheets, themes, milestones, and creative projects.
  • The 'look forward' component is aspirational, focusing on future goals like adventures, habits, or larger objectives such as selling a book proposal.
  • The process uses large, high-quality paper and markers to enhance visual engagement and satisfaction.
  • Reflections are consolidated onto a single page, employing visual elements like colors, boxes, and arrows for easy reference.
  • Gretchen Rubin has conducted this exercise with friends for over 10 years, citing accountability and energy benefits.

  • The hosts affirmed the exercise's value, describing it as fun, creative, and illuminating for self-reflection.
  • Performing such exercises with others can deepen connections by fostering discussions about meaningful personal experiences.
  • Elizabeth and her writing partner, Sarah, plan to incorporate this year-end reflection into their routine.

  • The suggested happiness hack is a home cotton candy maker, serving as a fun and special addition to parties.
  • It is described as a crowd-pleaser, particularly appealing to children, providing an exciting quality to gatherings.
  • Its initial cost is considered in comparison to other entertainment expenses for parties.

  • The hosts reflected on the 'Read 25 in 25' annual challenge, where participants read for 25 minutes daily in 2025.
  • The challenge encouraged reading great literature and led to a family 'slow read' of 'War and Peace' over the year.
  • One host discussed rediscovering a love for great literature and the practice of rereading favorite books through the challenge.

  • One host noted difficulty processing new material via audiobooks unless it has been previously read.
  • Both hosts found pleasure in listening to rereads of familiar stories, discovering new aspects.
  • They encouraged listeners to maintain reading habits, suggesting methods like Goodreads goals or book clubs.

  • Elizabeth awarded a gold star for consistently maintaining exercise routines during a difficult period following their father's death.
  • The hosts emphasized the importance of exercise for both physical and mental well-being.
  • They noted that exercise aids with sleep and stress management, and was a value their father upheld.
