Acquired

Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi

Key Takeaways

  • Uber's revenue tripled to over $30 billion, achieving cashflow positivity.
  • Dara Khosrowshahi transformed Uber's financial health and public perception.
  • His early career with Barry Diller at Allen & Company shaped his leadership style.
  • Uber's integrated Rides and Eats strategy now drives marketplace efficiency and growth.
  • Prioritizing a positive earner experience is crucial for Uber's long-term success.
  • Uber successfully navigated a complex and turbulent shareholder base transformation.
  • The company adapted its business model for international markets and suburban expansion.
  • Uber maintains a strong cultural resistance to increasing take rates, favoring volume.
  • Autonomous vehicle adoption faces significant technical and societal hurdles.

Deep Dive

  • IAC signed the term sheet to acquire Expedia before the 9/11 terrorist attacks.
  • Despite the severe impact on the travel industry, IAC proceeded with the acquisition.
  • IAC opted not to invoke the material adverse change clause to withdraw from the deal.
  • Barry Diller's conviction that travel would rebound post-pandemic led to unchanged deal terms.

  • Booking.com's success stemmed from its relentless focus on hotels.
  • The strategy emphasized pursuing large, fragmented markets and optimizing conversion.
  • Uber adopted a similar supply-led growth model, learning from Booking.com.
  • Uber's marketplace now includes 5.6 million drivers and couriers, and nearly one million restaurants.

  • Uber merged technical and marketplace teams for Rides and Eats post-pandemic.
  • This integration allowed Rides to act as a proprietary customer acquisition channel for Eats.
  • Cross-promotional features required extensive experimentation to optimize user experience.
  • The integrated strategy has led to faster margin growth for Uber over competitors.

  • Increased Uber ride costs were linked to higher driver earnings in the past.
  • Prices are currently declining year-over-year as supply begins to outpace demand.
  • Uber's operations are more complex than Expedia's, handling end-to-end service provision.
  • The CEO noted his initial unpreparedness for the challenging yet impactful Uber role.

  • Dara Khosrowshahi informed Barry Diller directly about his potential move to Uber.
  • Diller reacted with disbelief but ultimately offered support and friendship.
  • Diller assisted Khosrowshahi in preparing his presentation for the Uber board.
  • Khosrowshahi initially remained on the Expedia board, highlighting a complex dual role.

  • Uber initially focused on urban areas for Eats, overlooking significant suburban demand.
  • DoorDash leveraged an early focus on suburban markets and broader restaurant selection.
  • Uber is now actively expanding its suburban presence and selection in food delivery.
  • The company projects 50% growth from core rideshare and 15% from international markets.

  • Uber's shareholder base transformed from a hyper-growth focus to disciplined, profitable growth.
  • This process led to ownership by institutional investors like Fidelity and Morgan Stanley.
  • Former CEO Travis Kalanick's post-IPO share sale initially caused panic but allowed him to move on.
  • Leadership faced immense pressure to maintain focus amidst stock price volatility and public drama.

  • Securing SoftBank's investment required a complex negotiation over high-vote shares.
  • A power struggle existed between Benchmark and Travis Kalanick for Uber's control.
  • Unanimous consent was needed from all high-vote shareholders to eliminate the high-vote structure.
  • This unprecedented move facilitated SoftBank's investment and refocused the company on building.

  • Early predictions (2013-2014) for widespread self-driving cars have not materialized.
  • The difficulty lies in solving the 'final 2%' of complex use cases.
  • Societal acceptance of autonomous vehicle fatalities, even if safer, is a key challenge.
  • Uber exited self-driving development post-Phoenix incident, now partnering with Waymo and Aurora.

  • Uber previously overlooked the earner experience due to an oversupply of drivers.
  • Building the best platform for its 5.6 million earners is now a critical focus.
  • The goal is to ensure 90% of earners have a positive experience on the app.
  • Optimizing the 'P95 experience' for earners requires a higher duty of care.
