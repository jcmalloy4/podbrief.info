TBD | 2025: The Meme of the Year

Key Takeaways

  • The 'astronomer CEO Cold Play Kiss Cam scandal' was identified as the meme of the year.
  • Social media facilitates rapid online identification, eroding personal privacy and creating a public surveillance state.
  • Public reactions to high-profile incidents can reflect class consciousness and frustration with those in power.
  • Newer memes, like '6'7'' and 'brain rot', represent distinct forms of youth cultural communication.
  • Memes are predicted to evolve, becoming more audio-based and fragmented due to algorithmic influence.

Deep Dive

  • The discussion defines memes, noting their historical presence and evolution beyond static images.
  • Memes now encompass broader internet moments and interactions, challenging traditional definitions.

  • The 'astronomer CEO Cold Play Kiss Cam scandal' was identified as the meme of the year.
  • The incident involved a CEO and an HR head swiftly identified online via platforms like LinkedIn and Twitter.
  • Information and misinformation spread rapidly, with factual details largely confirmed within two days.
  • Public fascination stemmed from a 'rich person gets theirs' narrative due to the individuals' high-profile status.

  • Online reactions, drawing parallels to incidents like the 'Titan sub-explosion,' demonstrate public schadenfreude.
  • This behavior reflects growing class consciousness and frustration with those in power, particularly regarding issues like healthcare costs.
  • The internet's capacity to 'ruin someone's life' raises questions about the duration and extent of public accountability, even after individuals face consequences.

  • The 'Republican makeup' meme was discussed as a meme of the year contender.
  • It describes a specific aesthetic: thick foundation, heavily lined eyes, and poorly applied blush, emerging after the 2016 election.
  • The meme functions as a commentary on the performance of femininity and women's roles within certain political contexts.

  • Another meme runner-up was '6'7"', originating from a basketball player and a song.
  • Its virality stemmed from a fan cam of the player and a clip of a child saying '6'7"' with a specific hand gesture.
  • This meme is characterized as a shared, nonsensical language among youth, distinct from adult humor, which contributed to its longevity.

  • The '6'7"' meme is considered a 'bird call' for younger generations, signifying shared cultural knowledge inaccessible to older groups.
  • 'Brain rot' memes are explained as an ironic, nihilistic form of online communication requiring deep internet immersion to understand.
  • Kate Lindsay, host of Slate's ICYMI, predicts memes will continue to evolve, becoming more audio-based and fragmented due to algorithms, with monoculture memes diminishing.
