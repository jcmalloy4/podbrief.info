She Won the Election. The GOP Won’t Swear Her In.

Key Takeaways

  • Adelita Grijalva's swearing-in to Congress has been delayed for over six weeks by Speaker Mike Johnson.
  • Democrats speculate the delay is linked to Grijalva's potential vote on releasing Jeffrey Epstein files.
  • Grijalva ran for her late father's congressional seat, driven by family legacy and her children's encouragement.
  • The unprecedented delay prevents Grijalva from performing official duties, impacting constituent services and vulnerable populations.

Deep Dive

  • Adelita Grijalva, elected Sept. 23 to Arizona's 7th district, has been delayed from being sworn in for over six weeks.
  • Speaker Mike Johnson denies the alleged link to a discharge petition vote on Jeffrey Epstein files, calling it absurd.
  • Democrats, including Arizona Senators Mark Kelly and Ruben Gallego, have questioned Speaker Johnson about the unprecedented 41-day delay.

  • Adelita Grijalva won the September 23rd special election for Arizona's 7th congressional district, running for the seat previously held by her late father, Raoul Grijalva.
  • Her father was a co-founder of the progressive movement and served 12 terms in the House of Representatives.
  • Grijalva's decision was influenced by her young children's encouragement and her desire to continue her father's legacy.

  • The guest confirms initial swearing-in dates were set but then postponed without explanation, contrasting with past government shutdowns.
  • Grijalva highlights the importance of being sworn in to assist constituents and co-sponsor legislation.
  • The delay makes it impossible for her to perform official duties, such as printing documents or accessing a government email.

  • The representative-elect clarifies that being sworn in is not merely ceremonial; it enables crucial actions.
  • These actions include signing a discharge petition, accessing budgets, and ordering essential equipment.
  • Grijalva notes she currently lacks an office and is operating from headquarters.

  • Grijalva discusses the balance between supporting established party members and embracing new leadership within the Democratic Party.
  • She emphasizes the importance of standing by ideals, values, and local control, asserting community-elected representatives best serve constituents.
  • Grijalva advocates for primaries for unrepresented politicians and supports training younger, diverse voices.

  • The guest warns of severe impacts on vulnerable populations, including seniors and Head Start participants, due to lack of food.
  • Local communities currently lack the capacity to manage the prolonged issue.
  • This situation is exacerbated by the ongoing government shutdown, which Grijalva cannot address without being sworn in.
