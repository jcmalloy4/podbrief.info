Bonus: The Scariest Halloween Costume

Key Takeaways

  • A special Halloween episode explores the holiday's unique appeal for adults.
  • Halloween costume trends show a shift towards more traditional choices.
  • The current political climate is seen as influencing the popularity of topical Halloween costumes.
  • Listeners suggest topical costumes offer an outlet for collective subversion.

Deep Dive

  • Slate staff writer Luke Winkie, identified as the podcast's 'Halloween person,' cites the holiday as his favorite.
  • Winkie, a former 'werewolf pirate' for Vice, values Halloween for its unpredictability and campiness, allowing for less concern for good taste.
  • Adults particularly appreciate the variety of parties and themes available during Halloween celebrations.

  • The conversation shifted to the types of Halloween parties and evolving costume preferences.
  • There is a noted trend towards more traditional costumes, such as ghosts and werewolves.
  • This follows years of previously themed parties, including a 'Broadway-themed' event hosted by Winkie's wife and a 'clown-themed' gathering.

  • The host and guest debated the appropriateness of politically themed Halloween costumes.
  • Winkie argued that the current 'campy and chintzy' political climate lends itself to parody.
  • He suggested this environment encourages the choice of topical costumes for Halloween celebrations.

  • A listener's input suggested a strong embrace of topical costumes this year.
  • This trend is theorized to stem from a collective feeling of suffering.
  • It is believed to facilitate a 'carnival'-like subversion of power structures, allowing for a day of mockery.
