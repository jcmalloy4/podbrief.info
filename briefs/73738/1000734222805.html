Key Takeaways

  • "Gooning" is an online subculture centered on communal, prolonged masturbation, predominantly among Gen Z men.
  • The subculture utilizes platforms like Discord for group sessions and shares rapidly edited "porn music videos."
  • COVID-19 isolation accelerated engagement, with many finding community and an escape in the practice.
  • The journalist expresses concern about an "emergent slop culture" driven by accelerated short-form content.
  • Reporting on niche online communities can alter their perceived privacy and self-consciousness.

Deep Dive

  • "Gooning" is a subculture focused on prolonged masturbation, often in communal online settings like Discord.
  • It primarily consists of Gen Z men, with the "Goonerverse" potentially encompassing over 1 million participants.
  • Online sessions typically involve multiple cameras: one on the participant, another viewing rapidly spliced pornographic montages.

  • The gooning subculture coalesced during the pandemic, with Discord facilitating private communities for communal porn sharing and discussions.
  • Participants' ages range, with early to mid-20s being the most active, though the community includes individuals in their 20s, 30s, and 50s.
  • Journalists reported by distributing questionnaires within Discord communities, where participants were willing to share due to difficulty discussing the hobby offline.

  • Gooners engage in community rituals such as 'feeding,' which involves sharing curated pornography, and 'wank battling,' a gamified porn trading system.
  • The pornography consumed is characterized as 'porn music videos,' which splice clips from numerous films at a rapid pace, often synchronized to music.
  • This content creation is a vibrant community effort, with makers actively developing and sharing techniques for rapid editing.

  • College student Spischak found community in 'gooning' online after struggling with social interaction exacerbated by COVID-19 lockdowns.
  • Spischak created an elaborate multi-screen setup for gooning, highlighting how pandemic isolation was a significant factor in individuals deepening engagement with online communities.
  • The guest notes that while sexual gratification is a component, 'gooning' also functions like a fandom, providing community and content for participants.

  • The reporter experienced desensitization to the rapid pace and editing of pornography during reporting, questioning if gooners undergo similar changes.
  • 'Gooner' pornography is characterized by rapid editing, with examples cited of 500 cuts in a two-minute clip, making it distinct from conventional porn.
  • This distinct style of online pornography, particularly on platforms like Pornhub, shapes users' conception of sex, potentially diverging from real-time sexual experiences.

  • Reporting on niche communities presents challenges; journalistic descriptions can alter the perceived specialness of these communities, particularly in sensitive areas like sex and pornography.
  • The guest acknowledges that the article's publication likely increased self-consciousness within the 'gooner' community, which was accustomed to operating discreetly.
  • The journalist expresses concern about an "emergent slop culture," where the accelerated pace of short-form content constantly stimulates brains, comparing it to modern cartoons.
