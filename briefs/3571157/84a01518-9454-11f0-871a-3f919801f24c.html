Friendship Failures..

Key Takeaways

  • Friendship betrayals can escalate to workplace hostility, requiring strategic distancing and communication.
  • A partner's secretive communication during significant life events, especially with a history of infidelity, often signals deep trust issues.
  • The label 'high-value man' can mask controlling and manipulative behaviors in relationships, demanding vigilance from partners.
  • Disclosing infidelity, even with good intentions, can expose complex family dynamics and surprising moral stances from partners.
  • Interference in wedding planning, particularly by a future mother-in-law, necessitates clear boundaries and assertive communication.

Deep Dive

  • A Reddit user discovered her best friend, Ellen (22), was spreading rumors that the user's boyfriend, George (24), tried to cheat with Ellen.
  • The three-year friendship with Ellen began deteriorating after the user's one-year relationship with George became official.
  • Ellen allegedly became cold and difficult, later spreading false accusations about George at their shared workplace.

  • The original poster (OP) addressed workplace hostility by informing her supervisor, rather than confronting Ellen or involving HR directly.
  • The OP was accommodated by working remotely for the remainder of her internship, then received a positive recommendation and was hired.
  • As of July 17, 2024, the OP shared positive updates, including acceptance into a top country school and plans to marry George.

  • Hosts discuss the commonality of 'mean girls' creating drama and spreading false information, leading to social distancing.
  • Personal anecdotes included friends exhibiting manipulative behaviors, such as attempting to ruin relationships.
  • The discussion contrasted manipulative behaviors with the importance of supportive friends during challenging times, citing examples like a friend attending a 21st birthday despite health issues.

  • A 36-year-old woman discovered her husband was texting a childhood female friend, whom he had briefly dated, throughout her labor and delivery.
  • The OP expressed anger due to the husband's past infidelity and his continued secretive communication with this friend.
  • The hosts advised the wife to establish firm boundaries, meet the friend, or re-evaluate the future of the relationship given the history.

  • A 28-year-old woman's 35-year-old boyfriend began exhibiting controlling behavior after they moved in together.
  • His dictates included rules on clothing, hair, social interactions, and a demand to stop eating dinner, justified by his self-proclaimed status as a 'high-value man.'
  • Hosts and commenters identified these actions as significant red flags and manipulative tactics, particularly attempts to isolate the OP from friends.

  • A man witnessed his brother-in-law kissing another woman and informed his pregnant sister-in-law, leading to her leaving her husband.
  • This action resulted in a significant marital dispute with the OP's wife, who wanted to protect her sister.
  • The hosts debated the implications of informing a pregnant woman about infidelity for family bonds and trust, considering the brother-in-law's brazen behavior.

  • The original poster's (OP) wife stated they should not interfere in her sister's marriage and would forgive OP if he cheated, believing 'it's just sex.'
  • She also indicated she would hide it if she cheated, revealing a moral stance that led OP to question their marriage.
  • Following an argument, OP's wife left for a week, and he was unable to contact her or her family during that period.

  • The OP and his sister-in-law are both proceeding with divorce, with the sister-in-law seeking child support and alimony.
  • OP invited his sister-in-law to celebrate Christmas, leading to a closer friendship due to their partners' betrayals, with OP providing financial support.
  • OP's ex-wife accused him of cheating, leading to family backlash, despite OP planning to marry the sister-in-law.

  • A future mother-in-law unilaterally distributed revised wedding invitations, altering the dress code and start time from the original invites.
  • The original poster (OP) expressed confusion and frustration over her fiancé's mother's justifications for her actions.
  • Hosts recommended the fiancé directly contact guests to correct misinformation and advised password-protecting wedding details to establish boundaries.
