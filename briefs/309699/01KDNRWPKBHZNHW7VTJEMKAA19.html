A glimmer of hope for the housing market

Key Takeaways

  • The housing market showed modest improvement in November, with rising sales and improving affordability.
  • U.S. oil production is forecast to decline in 2026, with average prices expected to fall.
  • Minimum wage increases are set to take effect in 19 states, impacting both workers and businesses.
  • AI travel influencers are emerging as a cost-effective marketing tool, creating both opportunities and challenges.
  • Tariffs continue to pose significant financial and logistical challenges for small businesses like Busy Baby.

Deep Dive

  • Pending home sales increased over 3% in November 2025, with inventory also showing an increase.
  • Mortgage rates, which peaked near 8% two years prior, have stabilized around 6.2% since mid-October 2025.
  • Housing affordability is improving due to slight price depreciation and falling mortgage rates.
  • Analysts predict further rate decreases in 2026 due to anticipated Federal Reserve interest rate cuts, potentially encouraging more buyers and sellers.
  • Demand may need mortgage rates to dip below 6% for a significant pickup in activity.

  • The U.S. Energy Information Administration forecasts a decline in domestic oil production for 2026, following four years of growth.
  • The average West Texas Intermediate price is estimated to fall to $14 per barrel in 2026, below the 2025 average, driven by global oversupply.
  • Oil prices are projected to range between $50 and $60 per barrel in 2026, with some forecasts suggesting dips into the $40s.
  • These price levels are below the break-even point for some companies to drill new wells, potentially leading to decreasing U.S. production.
  • Decreasing rig counts are expected to impact employment in the oil and gas sector in 2026, but also lead to lower fuel and gasoline prices for consumers.

  • Beth Benneke, founder of Busy Baby, identifies tariffs as a significant economic factor in 2025, impacting small importers.
  • Busy Baby, which started from a solution to prevent babies from dropping items, has expanded internationally with agreements in Australia, the Czech Republic, Hungary, and Slovakia.
  • The company faces significant financial challenges, including a potential loss of its warehouse and depletion of personal savings.
  • Benneke awaits a Supreme Court decision on tariff legality, which could allow her to ship $48,000 worth of inventory currently held back by a 30% tariff.
  • Busy Baby previously lowered prices for the holiday season to boost sales amidst rising costs for essential baby items.

  • Starting Wednesday night, 19 states will implement minimum wage increases.
  • More workers will now earn $15 per hour or more, exceeding the federal minimum wage of $7.25.
  • Experts suggest the impact on employment will vary, with larger increases posing more challenges for businesses.
  • Research indicates some businesses pass increased costs to consumers, while others absorb them through lower profits.
  • Workers report that wage increases are often offset by rising living costs, affecting the overall benefit.

  • AI travel influencers are increasingly present on platforms like Instagram and TikTok, blurring the lines between virtual and human creators.
  • Examples include Sama, a virtual cabin crew for Qatar Airways, who provides travel tips and airline information realistically.
  • 'Emma from Destination Germany,' an AI-generated influencer, mimics human creators with realistic posts, sometimes causing confusion among viewers.
  • AI influencers are a cost-effective marketing tool for the travel industry, as 73% of consumers report influencer recommendations influence their travel decisions.
  • AI enables cheaper and faster content creation, eliminating the need for travel crews and allowing for imagery generation across different seasons.

  • Real travel content creators face increased competition and reduced compensation due to the proliferation of AI influencers.
  • Some human creators are now using AI tools themselves, such as AI schedulers, to manage costs.
  • Advancements in AI influencer technology are noted, though minor glitches like misaligned body parts can still occur in images.
  • Future developments may see AI influencers becoming 'agentic,' capable of booking travel arrangements, marking a significant shift in the industry.
