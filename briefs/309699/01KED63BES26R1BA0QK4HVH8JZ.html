Labor market anxieties grow

Key Takeaways

  • The U.S. job market is experiencing a slowdown with reduced dynamism and growing worker anxieties.
  • Economists are examining why recent tariffs have not yet caused expected widespread price increases.
  • NVIDIA introduced new AI chips designed for lower cooling needs, impacting the HVAC industry and energy use.
  • Strong 2025 car sales reflect a K-shaped economy, driven by higher-income consumers despite rising vehicle costs.

Deep Dive

  • The December jobs report is expected soon, with recent data showing private sector job additions of 41,000 last month.
  • Job openings fell to a one-year low in November, indicating a 'low-fire, low-hire' market.
  • Worker worries about job security have increased since April 2025, impacting personal finance sentiment.
  • The unemployment rate for Black workers rose to 8.3% in November, nearly a 2% increase from a year ago.

  • Harvard economist Gita Gopinath investigated why recent tariffs haven't generated expected price increases.
  • U.S. importers pay an effective tariff rate of about 14%, roughly half the announced statutory rate of 27-28%.
  • Exemptions, shipping lags, and the USMCA trade agreement contribute to these lower effective rates.
  • Economists anticipate an additional 50% price increase from tariffs over the next two years, manifesting gradually.

  • Shalene Hearing is planning to rebuild her Two Dragons Martial Arts studio in Altadena, lost in the Eaton fire a year prior.
  • She envisions a new building with commercial and residential spaces on the former studio lot.
  • Hearing is collaborating with the Greenline Housing Foundation, which purchased the lot for $500,000.
  • She has raised over $100,000 through GoFundMe to support the rebuilding effort, emphasizing community collaboration.

  • The recovery in Altadena extends to residential areas, with a focus on businesses reopening to support community return.
  • Residential properties require remediation for toxic residues from the Eaton fire, including asbestos, lead, and cadmium.

  • NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang announced new AI chips that will require less cooling, potentially impacting the HVAC industry.
  • AI data centers typically operate at high temperatures, demanding substantial energy for cooling systems.
  • The new chips will be cooled by hot water, presented as a more efficient alternative to traditional systems.
  • This innovation offers environmental benefits by reducing energy consumption, indirect carbon emissions, and water usage.

  • Over 16 million new cars were sold in 2025, a 2% rise year-over-year, largely driven by higher-income Americans.
  • The average new car price reached $50,000, up from under $40,000 in 2020, making vehicles unaffordable for many.
  • Average monthly car payments reached nearly $790, a significant increase from $600 pre-pandemic.
  • Falling interest rates could offer some consumer relief, though potential tariff-driven price increases might offset these savings.
