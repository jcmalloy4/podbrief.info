Jeff Bezos’ New AI Startup, Yann LeCun Says LLMs are a Dead End, Thiel’s Fund Sells NVIDIA | Diet TBPN

Key Takeaways

  • OpenAI reintroduced its GPT-4.0 model after user dissatisfaction and a brief sunsetting, amid debates on its capabilities.
  • Jeff Bezos has launched Project Prometheus, an AI startup, marking his return to an operational leadership role.
  • Apple's board is reportedly intensifying succession planning for Tim Cook, while the company faces internal concerns regarding AI advancements.
  • Prominent investors are making significant portfolio adjustments, with Wall Street accelerating trillions into AI infrastructure.
  • The humanoid robotics market is expanding, prompting discussions on long-term profitability and potential security implications.

Deep Dive

  • OpenAI reinstated GPT-4.0 after users expressed dissatisfaction with changes and its brief removal, advocating for its return due to unique personality and capabilities.
  • A debate suggests that completely removing GPT-4.0 could drive users to less regulated open-source models, potentially increasing safety risks.
  • The rapid reintroduction is speculated to be a response to significant user churn or financial impact among ChatGPT's 800 million weekly active users and 20 million paying subscribers.

  • Jeff Bezos is launching Project Prometheus, an AI startup, with $6.2 billion in funding, serving as co-chief executive.
  • This marks Bezos's first formal operational role since stepping down as Amazon CEO in 2021, entering a competitive AI market.
  • Project Prometheus aims to apply AI to physical tasks such as robotics and scientific discovery, building on Bezos's prior AI investments.

  • The Financial Times reported Apple's board is intensifying succession planning for Tim Cook, with John Ternus considered a likely successor.
  • Reports indicate internal shock within Apple over missing AI advancements, perceived as a failure of Apple Intelligence.
  • Apple has released the 'iPhone Pocket,' a fashion accessory designed by the creator of Steve Jobs's iconic turtlenecks, targeting a different consumer base.

  • Peter Thiel sold his entire stake in NVIDIA and 76% of his Tesla stake, according to regulatory filings, prompting speculation on his market outlook.
  • Teal Macro reported a new $500 million position in Core Weave and additions to CRZ and Iron miners in Q3 13F filings.
  • The firm's portfolio notional value doubled from $2.12 billion to $4.15 billion, largely due to a $1.5 billion cash injection.

  • A $40 billion investment is planned in Texas by 2027 to develop cloud and AI infrastructure, creating thousands of jobs and strengthening energy resilience.
  • Wall Street firms like Blue Owl Capital are amassing trillions for AI investments, including a $14 billion package for an Oracle and OpenAI data center in Texas.
  • Major tech companies are increasingly using debt and private equity to fund AI infrastructure, generating substantial fees for financiers amid strong investor appetite.

  • The 1X Home Robot is marketed with a $20,000 upfront cost or a $499/month subscription, raising skepticism about the viability of software-only companies.
  • Shenzhen-based UB Tech claims mass delivery of its Walker S2 humanoid robot, securing over $112 million in orders this year.
  • Concerns are voiced regarding the potential security risks of mass deployment of humanoid robots in homes, drawing parallels to past debates surrounding drone technology.
