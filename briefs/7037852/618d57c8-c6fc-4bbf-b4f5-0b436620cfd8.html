Do Companies Need Storytellers, Ford’s EV Flop, Kushner Paramount Pullout | Diet TBPN

Key Takeaways

  • The demand for corporate "storytellers" is rising, emphasizing narrative's power in business value.
  • Influential founders create "reality distortion fields" to attract talent, capital, and customers.
  • Brands are heavily investing in earlier holiday advertising, projecting over $1 trillion in retail sales.
  • Ford shifted its strategy, reporting a $19.5 billion EV write-down to focus on gas and hybrids.

Deep Dive

  • A Wall Street Journal article highlights a trend of startups hiring "storytellers" to impose meaning on abundance and control narratives.
  • Companies like Google and Microsoft are seeking individuals for roles such as customer storytelling manager to drive acquisition and growth.
  • One contrarian view suggests that once reported in the Wall Street Journal, a trend may be past its peak.

  • Influential figures create "reality distortion fields" that attract talent, capital, and customers, leading to high valuation multiples like 100X PE ratios.
  • The concept of a "Joe Rogan CEO" describes founders capable of captivating audiences through compelling narratives, exemplified by Elon Musk and Palmer Luckey.
  • Elite storyteller founders effectively break through the noisy internet landscape, dominating attention amidst numerous startups and AI-generated content.

  • The discussion analyzed the effectiveness of unconventional marketing campaigns.
  • Examples include the Eagles' Super Bowl commercial and Avi Schiffman's strategy to 'buy every billboard' for awareness.
  • Such campaigns, costing an estimated $1-2 million, successfully achieved household name status for brands.

  • Job postings mentioning 'storyteller' have doubled, sparking debate on their effectiveness versus creating impactful campaigns.
  • The ideal storyteller is rare, bridging technical understanding, people skills, and communication ability.
  • A Wall Street Journal article discusses why individuals with storytelling talent may not always secure 'storyteller' roles, emphasizing genuine passion for writing over titles.

  • OpenAI launched a new version of ChatGPT Images, powered by a faster image generation model for precise edits and easier prompting.
  • The company hired a new executive from Google to lead AI initiatives.
  • OpenAI's first Chief Communications Officer, Hannah Wong, departed at the end of January after guiding major product launches.

  • Advertisers began Christmas campaigns earlier, with a 13% increase in spending, totaling $1.47 billion over nine weeks.
  • Holiday retail sales are projected to exceed $1 trillion this year, a significant increase.
  • Retailers spent $5.8 billion on U.S. digital ads between November 1st and 7th, up 4% year-over-year, despite inflation.
  • Walmart's ad features Walter Goggins as the Grinch, while Target reintroduced a Christmas enthusiast character; Amazon aired its first holiday ad on October 13th.
