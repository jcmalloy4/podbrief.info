The AI Power Backlash, the NYMag David Ellison profile, Google Personal Intelligence | Diet TBPN

Key Takeaways

  • AI data centers face bipartisan community opposition due to increased power prices, leading to project delays and cancellations.
  • Microsoft aims for net-zero carbon emissions by 2030, absorbing higher electricity rates for AI data centers rather than passing costs to consumers.
  • Apple is shifting its $20 billion credit card partnership from Goldman Sachs to JP Morgan after two years of negotiations and valuation disagreements.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic are planning Super Bowl advertisements to reframe AI as a pragmatic, relatable tool for a broader audience.
  • Google's Gemini AI will introduce 'Personal Intelligence,' utilizing user data from Google apps for customized, opt-in responses.
  • Actor Matthew McConaughey secured eight trademarks, including his voice, to prevent unauthorized AI simulation of his likeness and voice.
  • David Ellison, with support from his father Larry Ellison, is pursuing major acquisitions in Hollywood, including Paramount and Warner Brothers.

Deep Dive

  • Microsoft is negotiating political backlash against AI data centers due to increasing power prices.
  • Donald Trump stated he would work with tech companies to prevent Americans from paying more for electricity due to AI power consumption.
  • Wholesale electricity prices have reportedly increased by 267% in areas affected by data centers.
  • Approximately $64 billion in data center projects face delays or cancellations due to local community opposition over rising electricity costs and environmental concerns.

  • Microsoft has committed to net-zero carbon emissions by 2030 and carbon negative by 2050.
  • This initiative involves paying higher electricity rates for data centers to avoid passing costs to consumers.
  • The move is considered a strategic action with potential implications for energy markets and capacity building.

  • Apple's credit card partnership with Goldman Sachs is reportedly ending after two years of negotiations.
  • JP Morgan is set to replace Goldman Sachs as the issuer for Apple's $20 billion credit card program.
  • Negotiations stalled over valuation disagreements and pacing, but a deal was finalized after direct communication between bank executives.

  • OpenAI's Super Bowl advertisement is expected to highlight AI advancement by showcasing human innovation from fire to ChatGPT.
  • The ads aim to reach audiences 'on the fence' about AI, focusing on pragmatic applications and daily integration.
  • Both OpenAI and Anthropic have shifted advertising strategies from framing AI as transformative to presenting it as a relatable tool.

  • David Ellison, supported by his father Larry Ellison, harbors ambitions to become a major studio mogul.
  • His early career included the 2006 film 'Flyboys,' a $60 million production that received poor reception.
  • Ellison built Skydance into a major production company and is now pursuing acquisitions of studios like Paramount and Warner Brothers.

  • Actor Matthew McConaughey secured eight trademark approvals from the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office.
  • These trademarks cover visual and audio elements, including his voice saying 'All right, all right, all right'.
  • The action aims to prevent AI from simulating his likeness or voice without consent, a growing concern for performers.
