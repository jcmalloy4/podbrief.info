Big Tech Earnings, Elon’s SpaceX–xAI Merge, Genie 3 | Diet TBPN

Key Takeaways

  • Apple acquired AI audio startup Q.ai for nearly $2 billion, enhancing audio interpretation.
  • Microsoft's Q4 revenue of $81.3 billion exceeded estimates, but shares fell 12% due to AI investments.
  • Meta's Q4 2025 revenue of $59.9 billion beat expectations, with significant AI program rebuilds underway.
  • Tesla reported $24.9 billion Q1 revenue, pivoting towards autonomy, robo-taxis, and Optimus robots.
  • Elon Musk's XAI is reportedly in merger talks with X, ahead of a planned initial public offering.
  • Epoch's Project Genie allows users to generate entire video game worlds instantly from text prompts.

Deep Dive

  • Apple acquired Israeli AI startup Q.ai for nearly $2 billion, specializing in AI for audio.
  • Q.ai's technology can enhance audio in challenging environments and interpret whispered speech and mouthed words.
  • The acquisition draws parallels to Apple's past purchase of PrimeSense, which supported facial recognition development.

  • Meta reported $59.9 billion in Q4 2025 revenue, exceeding expectations with 21% year-over-year growth.
  • CEO Mark Zuckerberg stated Meta has rebuilt its AI program foundations, with new models and products expected soon.
  • Meta AI currently lacks real-time web search and seamless integration to fully leverage platform content.
  • The company is making a substantial AI investment, exceeding 50% of its projected revenue for the next year.

  • Meta aims to empower creators by integrating AI for generating content, such as drone shots and video transitions.
  • The company owns Manus, a team capable of building AI agents for prompt-to-short-form video generation.
  • Meta's future plans prioritize image and video models for product integration, potentially outpacing competitors.
  • YouTube's new AI features include video summaries and conversational capabilities, like requesting a parts list from a video.

  • Tesla's Q1 revenue was $24.9 billion, a slight beat but down year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter.
  • The company is pivoting towards autonomy, with disclosures on autopilot subscriptions, robo-taxis, and Optimus humanoid robots.
  • Tesla generated $1.4 billion in free cash flow, exceeding analyst expectations and supporting its long-term vision.
  • Plans include transitioning the Fremont facility to produce one million Optimus units annually.

  • Reuters reported that Elon Musk's XAI is in merger talks with X, ahead of a planned IPO.
  • This potential combination would unite SpaceX, X (formerly Twitter), and XAI under a single entity.
  • The move was predicted by some observers months prior, consolidating Musk's ventures.

  • Project Genie, a new world-building AI model from Epoch, powered by Genie 3, renders entire worlds as simple video games.
  • Users can prompt detailed scenarios, which are instantly generated for interactive exploration.
  • The system is experiencing high demand, potentially leading to rate limits for users.
  • World models could accelerate game prototyping and development for platforms like Roblox and Fortnite.
