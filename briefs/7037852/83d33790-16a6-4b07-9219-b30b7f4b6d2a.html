Big Tech turns to mining, Thinking Machines loses top executives, NYC Sauna Wars heat up | Diet TBPN

Key Takeaways

  • Tech companies are vertically integrating into raw material supply chains for critical minerals like lithium and copper.
  • The AI industry is experiencing significant talent mobility and leadership changes, particularly concerning Thinking Machines.
  • New York City's wellness sector is booming with a surge in luxury and traditional bathhouses, dubbed 'Sauna Wars'.
  • Meta is strategically pivoting investments from its metaverse division towards artificial intelligence initiatives.
  • New ventures are exploring advanced human-machine fusion, aligning with long-term AI predictions by industry leaders.
  • Geopolitical directives are impacting cybersecurity firms, alongside significant shifts in U.S. private land ownership trends.

Deep Dive

  • Tesla is advancing its lithium refinery capabilities, aiming for self-sufficiency in battery-grade lithium hydroxide for 500,000 to 1 million EVs annually.
  • AWS partnered with Rio Tinto to underwrite a new copper refining process for low-grade ore, a deal for 1,400 metric tons over four years.
  • This trend signifies tech companies extending beyond software into raw material extraction and processing to regionalize supply chains.

  • A new North American lithium-ion refinery by X-Freeze, operational in 19 months, will produce battery-grade lithium hydroxide for over 500,000 EVs annually.
  • This initiative aims to reduce China's 60% global refining dominance in lithium production.
  • The AWS-Rio Tinto deal focuses on a new copper refining process that could potentially triple global copper supply from currently uneconomical reserves.
  • Elon Musk indicated AI is a significant demand driver for copper, an under-resourced material, contributing to its price nearly doubling in two years.

  • Thinking Machines terminated CTO Barrett Zoff for alleged unethical conduct; Sumith Chintala took over as CEO.
  • Zoff previously served as VP of post-training at OpenAI before joining Thinking Machines in September 2024.
  • More Thinking Machines employees are reportedly joining OpenAI, following the return of three co-founders and Andrew Tullick's departure in late 2023.
  • Thinking Machines secured a seed round valued at $2 billion, with an overall company valuation of $12 billion.

  • New York City is experiencing a 'Sauna Wars' with bathhouses opening in Flatiron and Williamsburg, including high-end clubs like Continuum ($40,000/year).
  • Lore Bathing Club, a 6,200 square foot space in Noho, takes inspiration from traditional baths and communal sweat traditions.
  • The market for bathhouses in North America is considered less saturated than Europe's, driving competitive expansion.
  • Traditional establishments like the Russian and Turkish baths in the East Village also maintain a strong presence, including on TikTok.

  • Meta laid off 1,500 employees from its metaverse division but still maintains 14,000 in Reality Labs, signaling a strategic shift.
  • The company is increasing investment in AI, with capital expenditures reaching $72 billion last year, and plans to acquire AI startups.
  • Separately, Merge Labs, backed by OpenAI and Sam Altman, raised $252 million to focus on fusing humans and machines, aligning with Altman's 2017 writings on 'The Merge'.

  • Beijing has instructed Chinese firms to cease using CrowdStrike services.
  • Stan Kroenke became the largest private landowner in America, acquiring nearly 1 million acres of ranch land in New Mexico, totaling 2.7 million acres.
  • This transaction is the largest U.S. land purchase in over a decade, highlighting the growing popularity of farmland investing.
  • Discussions also touched on traditional whaling and seal hunting practices in Greenland.
