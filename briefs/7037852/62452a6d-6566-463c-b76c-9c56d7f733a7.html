FULL INTERVIEW: Tyler Cowen on Kevin Warsh, Clawdbot, precious metals, and AI & unemployment

Key Takeaways

  • Fiscal policy, not Federal Reserve chairs, predominantly steers economic policy.
  • The appreciation of precious metals indicates a shift from the U.S. dollar's traditional safe haven status.
  • The integration of AI within the financial sector introduces systemic risks requiring new regulatory oversight.
  • Jerome Powell's Federal Reserve tenure will be primarily remembered for 8.9% inflation and waning central bank independence.
  • The rapid advancement of AI agents like 'Clawdbot' is prompting discussion about the nature of reality.
  • Human mentorship and access to non-public 'secrets' are becoming increasingly valuable in an AI-driven environment.

Deep Dive

  • Guest Tyler Cowen noted new Fed chair pick Kevin Warsh is politically adept and possesses financial independence.
  • Cowen suggests fiscal variables, not the Federal Reserve chair, are the primary drivers of economic policy.
  • The Federal Reserve's balance sheet is viewed as a consequence of political decisions, with the institution expected to act expediently.
  • Congress is predicted to regain macroeconomic importance due to declining presidential popularity and upcoming elections favoring Democrats.

  • The run-up in precious metals is attributed to the U.S. dollar losing its safe haven status for some investors.
  • Cowen likened precious metals to 'meme stocks' in the absence of a clear rival asset, not signifying economic collapse.
  • The lack of a credible rival asset is a primary driver for continued investment in treasuries, despite speculation in gold and silver.
  • Tether's purchase of gold is described as a significant move, potentially signaling the rise of gold-backed stablecoins.

  • The guest elaborated on potential systemic risks created by financial institutions utilizing advanced AI technologies.
  • New oversight functions and staffing for the Federal Reserve are deemed necessary to address these risks.
  • One theory suggests the potential for an AI-driven bubble with over-leverage, drawing parallels to the global financial crisis.
  • The Federal Reserve is advised to proactively monitor these AI-related risks, even with limited direct intervention capabilities.

  • The guest believes Jerome Powell will be remembered for 8.9% inflation, influenced by fiscal authority and geopolitical actions.
  • Powell is characterized as a transitional figure as the Federal Reserve's independence is perceived to wane.
  • If given a do-over, Powell would have monitored M2 more closely and avoided 40% increases in its growth.
  • The discussion also highlighted the importance of quantitative tightening and forward guidance to avoid overly expansionary policies.

  • The guest expressed apprehension towards advanced AI agents like 'Clawdbot' and 'Multi-Bot,' describing their outputs as 'insane.'
  • The simultaneous emergence of 'Molt Book' and 'Genie 3' is seen as enhancing the likelihood that reality is a simulation.
  • AI is expected to transform religion, potentially leading to solo religious practices and an implicit polytheism as individuals seek wisdom from AI.
  • A project benchmarking AI models, including their 'spirituality,' found them to be more atheist than anticipated.

  • The speakers considered AI as a potential default mentor but emphasized the continued importance of human recommendation and vouching.
  • Human connections remain crucial for career advancement and securing venture capital funding.
  • The value of 'secrets'—institutional, corporate, and interpersonal knowledge not readily available online—is increasing.
  • This hidden information is seen as becoming more valuable as AI automates other tasks, potentially leading to a future of hoarding secrets.
