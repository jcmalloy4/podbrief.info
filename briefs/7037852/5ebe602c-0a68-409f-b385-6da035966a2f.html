Diet TBPN: October 31, 2025

Key Takeaways

  • US-China trade relations saw tariff reductions on fentanyl precursors and rare earth minerals.
  • Major tech companies reported mixed Q3 earnings, impacting stock performance and future strategies.
  • AI market adoption shows strong enterprise engagement, but its revenue generation scale is debated.
  • OpenAI's significant expenditures are classified as strategic capital investments, not traditional losses.
  • NVIDIA is investing heavily in an 'AI factory' with a broad focus, led by CEO Jensen Huang.

Deep Dive

  • Following a meeting with President Xi Jinping, the US announced tariff reductions.
  • These reductions specifically apply to fentanyl precursors.
  • Controls on rare earth minerals were also eased as part of the new trade adjustments.

  • Tesla reported a revenue beat but an earnings miss, resulting in a stock decline.
  • The company's autonomous driving approach was contrasted with competitors like Waymo.
  • Elon Musk's future plans include developing humanoid robots and establishing SpaceX data centers in orbit.

  • NVIDIA is building an 'AI factory,' raising questions about its focus on space, humanoids, and quantum computing.
  • CEO Jensen Huang's public demeanor suggests confidence despite potential market challenges.
  • Huang is highlighted as a value-creative founder CEO leading the company's strategic direction.

  • Microsoft disclosed that OpenAI incurred $11.5 billion in expenditures last quarter.
  • These figures are characterized as investments in capital expenditures rather than traditional losses.
  • The accounting perspective was explained using an analogy to startup funding models.
