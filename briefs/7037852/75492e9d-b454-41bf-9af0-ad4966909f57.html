Full Interview: Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman on Apple’s AI Stack, Anthropic, and the Future of Siri

Key Takeaways

  • Apple's AI strategy is criticized for poor hiring and reliance on Google models.
  • Siri is expected to gain advanced "World Knowledge Answers" and agent-like capabilities.
  • John Ternus is emerging as a potential successor to Tim Cook for Apple CEO.
  • Apple is developing new hardware, including smart glasses, foldable phones, and a robotic arm.
  • The company faces challenges in the Chinese market and with evolving user tech preferences.

Deep Dive

  • Apple's AI development has struggled since hiring AI lead John Giannandrea in 2018.
  • Siri, a breakthrough in 2011, has fallen behind competitors like Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa.
  • Poor hiring decisions have led Apple to 'screw up AI in every which way,' according to the guest.
  • Despite strong financial performance, these internal AI issues persist, making urgent resolution difficult.

  • OpenAI is reportedly developing hardware, including AirPods competitors, led by former Apple designer Jony Ive.
  • Skepticism exists regarding OpenAI's hardware success due to high entry barriers and questionable market demand for AI earbuds.
  • AI agents could render the current app store model obsolete, with speculation about an OpenAI phone and new operating system.
  • Apple is reportedly moving towards AI agents with Siri improvements, enabling complex tasks such as editing photos.

  • New Siri features, including 'World Knowledge Answers' and agent-like capabilities, are targeted for completion around the end of March.
  • These features aim to provide direct answers to general knowledge questions and enable interactions with apps.
  • Apple reportedly understated these 'game-changing' features at WWDC 2024, suggesting they were not yet fully ready.
  • Siri's underlying model may shift towards Google's Gemini, which is noted for its Google search capabilities.

  • Future iPhones are expected to feature enhanced satellite connectivity, with integration plans reportedly including Starlink.
  • The current satellite service offers slower, emergency-focused communication.
  • This advancement could expand iPhone utility in areas without traditional cellular coverage.

  • John Ternus, age 50, is highlighted as a potential successor to Tim Cook as Apple CEO, favored for his hardware background.
  • Ternus has gained significant responsibilities, including full control of Apple Watch engineering and oversight of robotics and design teams.
  • Unlike other major tech companies, Apple is perceived to lack a clear AI leader, raising questions about whether Ternus could fill that role.
  • Apple's potential acquisition of Perplexity for AI talent reportedly stalled due to increased valuation and the Google search deal.

  • Apple is reportedly developing a robotic arm with a nine-inch display, likened to a 'Pixar lamp,' which is years away.
  • A high-end, expensive foldable phone is anticipated for release in the fall.
  • A 'Vision Air' product was reportedly canceled, with Apple pivoting towards smart glasses development.
  • Apple's smart glasses are expected to outperform Meta's, with iMessage support deemed crucial for success.

  • A societal shift indicates users desire instant gratification and pre-configured technology, contrasting with past user investment in device setup.
  • Apple's market position in China faces challenges, partly due to the company not developing bespoke products for the region.
  • Foldable phones are noted as potentially successful in the highly competitive Chinese market.

  • Apple Vision Pros were out of stock in a Pasadena store, while Mac Minis were readily available for same-day pickup.
  • An expected surge in Mac sales is projected, driven by new MacBook Pros, a lower-cost iPhone-powered MacBook, and updated Mac Minis and Studios.
  • Apple's upcoming quarterly earnings report will provide key metrics, including iPhone sales, particularly in China.
  • New Apple monitors are anticipated, raising questions about their positioning relative to the existing Pro Display XDR.
