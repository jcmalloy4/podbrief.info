Diet TBPN: October 16th, 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Google AI has achieved a breakthrough in cancer research, identifying novel cellular behaviors.
  • Global trade strategies are evolving, with discussions around combating market manipulation and new financial entities emerging.
  • Lead contamination has been reported in leading protein supplements and other consumer foods.
  • OpenAI is experiencing rapid user growth and significant revenue, despite substantial annual losses.
  • Internal debates at X highlight challenges in creator payout models and platform incentives.

Deep Dive

  • Google, in collaboration with Yale, announced its C2S scale 27B foundation model generated a novel hypothesis about cancer cellular behavior.
  • This hypothesis was experimentally validated in living cells, marking a significant advancement.
  • The AI's role is likened to making 'cold tumors' visible to the immune system, similar to 'popping the UAV' in a Call of Duty analogy.

  • A report indicates President Trump claims Indian Prime Minister Modi agreed to stop buying Russian oil.
  • A Polymarket market suggests a trade war with China is underway, with discussions of setting floor prices rather than tariffs to combat market manipulation.
  • Joe Lonsdale emphasized finance's role in job creation; Mr. Beast has filed a trademark for 'Mr. Beast Financial.'

  • A Zava video for a restaurant ERP, integrating payments, point of sale, and AI agents, was noted for its attention-grabbing initial hook.
  • VO3's AI-generated content was praised for character consistency, while questions arose about the overall brand-building effectiveness of some AI outputs.
  • The discussion compared high-budget campaigns from companies like Airbnb and Apple to lower-budget, idea-driven videos from seed-stage companies, emphasizing the importance of innovation over production value.

  • OpenAI reports 800 million weekly active users, with 5% converting to paying users.
  • The company generates $40 million from paying users, resulting in $13 billion in annual recurring revenue (ARR) and an average revenue per user (ARPU) of $325 annually.
  • Despite current losses of $20 billion annually against its $13 billion revenue, OpenAI projects reaching $100 billion in revenue by 2028.

  • A debate between Nikita, Head of Product at X, and Elon centers on creator payouts and allocation accuracy.
  • Nikita suggested an alternative system, while Elon advocated for a model similar to YouTube, which he views as more effective.
  • Polymarket has created a betting market on whether Nikita will be removed as Head of Product at X this year.
