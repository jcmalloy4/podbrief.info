Deep Dive

  • iMessage has evolved into a personal ERP system and data lake for communication and information sharing.
  • Integrating AI assistants like Gemini 3 with iMessage raises privacy concerns due to Apple and Google's "walled garden" ecosystems.
  • Skepticism exists regarding consumer desire for AI access to iMessage data and the feasibility of cross-platform data portability.

  • Google announced 'Anti-Gravity,' a new Agentic development platform, with speculation about forked code from 'Windsurf' or 'Cursor.'
  • Google trained its Gemini 3 Pro on internal TPUs, not NVIDIA chips, marking a significant hardware choice.
  • The 'Gemini' product name signifies the consolidation of Google Brain and DeepMind AI research groups.

  • NVIDIA's earnings call, scheduled for Wednesday, is considered crucial due to its 8% market share of the S&P 500.
  • XAI, led by Elon Musk, is partnering with NVIDIA to develop a 500-megawatt data center in Saudi Arabia.
  • The XAI facility is assessed as an entry into the AI cloud/data center business, leveraging expertise in large-scale physical infrastructure.

  • The hosts searched for a new 'Ultradome V2' space in Los Angeles, encountering a peculiar dome-like structure.
  • During a walkthrough of a potential space, a locked closet contained a mysterious, humming drywall box.
  • The broker identified the box as a "soil cleaning machine" already accounted for in the lease terms, prompting speculation of hazardous waste.

  • The book 'Empire of AI' was cited for incorrectly claiming a data center uses 1,000 times more water than a city.
  • The accurate figure is 22% of a city's water consumption, or 5 billion liters per year compared to 5 million liters for the city in 2019.
  • This highlights a significant miscalculation regarding data center resource usage.

  • Brookfield announced a $100 billion global AI infrastructure program with NVIDIA and the Kuwait Investment Authority.
  • Cloudflare experienced a significant outage, disrupting major websites and services like X and ChatGPT.
  • The incident highlighted the internet's extensive reliance on Cloudflare's services.
