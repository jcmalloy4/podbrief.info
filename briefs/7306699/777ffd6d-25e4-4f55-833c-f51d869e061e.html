Next Up with Mark Halperin

Rubio’s Political Resurrection, the Next Blue Wave & Jessica Reed Kraus Spills Some Political Tea

Key Takeaways

  • Marco Rubio completed a significant political comeback, becoming a key foreign policy figure in the current administration.
  • The Trump administration's foreign policy relies on a 'leverage' doctrine, strategically applied to achieve global objectives.
  • The 2026 midterms are expected to feature significant shifts, driven by retirements, economic factors, and evolving campaign strategies.
  • Public reaction to US intervention in Venezuela was notably subdued, with analysts exploring the underlying reasons.
  • Journalist Jessica Reed Kraus plans to expand her unique cultural-political coverage into high-profile trials and profiles of influential women.

Deep Dive

  • Mark Halperin details Marco Rubio's strategic reinvention following his 2016 presidential primary defeat against Donald Trump.
  • Rubio transformed from a rival, enduring 'Little Marco' insults, to a dominant foreign policy figure.
  • His long game involved building key alliances with Ivanka Trump, J.D. Vance, and political operative Susie Wiles.
  • Rubio's past attempts to retaliate against Trump, including calling him a 'con man,' were unsuccessful in 2016.

  • Rubio gained significant foreign policy power, delegating Middle East and Russia/Ukraine policy to Steve Witkoff.
  • His Cuban-American identity and insights into Latin American authoritarians shaped his influence on Venezuela policy.
  • Credibility is boosted by national security experience and positive television appearances supporting President Trump's Venezuela policies.
  • President Trump trusts Rubio's loyalty and policy expertise for Venezuela, with Rubio deferring 2028 ambitions and supporting J.D. Vance.
  • Rubio's 'long game' approach and use of 'muscle and finesse' align with Trump's foreign policy doctrine, emphasizing 'leverage' for global objectives.

  • Republican strategist Kristin Davison and Democratic strategist Kevin Walling discuss the surprisingly muted public reaction to the US ordering the seizure of a foreign leader in Venezuela.
  • Factors contributing to the subdued response include a 'holiday fog,' the Western Hemisphere's distinct geopolitical context, and legal implications of drug trafficking.
  • It is suggested that stronger outrage may emerge as Congress returns and more information becomes available about the administration's strategy.
  • Discussion includes predictions on Donald Trump's potential actions, ranging from avoiding US troops to utilizing private security firms.

  • Republican strategist Kristin Davison anticipates numerous retirements, a shifting electoral map beyond Georgia and Michigan, and healthcare as a key Republican issue.
  • Democratic strategist Kevin Walling highlights healthcare, GDP, and job growth as critical economic factors influencing the upcoming year.
  • Analysts discuss potential shifts in the House of Representatives due to increasing retirements and a shrinking Republican majority, which could challenge Speaker Johnson's control.
  • The conversation addresses the electoral impact of demographic changes, specifically the Latino vote's alignment with Donald Trump in 2024.

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI) is identified as a significant political and technological story.
  • The Democratic party shows internal divisions on AI policy, while the Republican party, influenced by President Trump, largely adopts a pro-AI stance.
  • Strategists Kevin Walling (Democrat) and Kristin Davison (Republican) debate which party will benefit more from AI as a political issue and a campaign tool.

  • Jessica Reed Kraus plans to shift her content focus in 2026 from politics to trial coverage and profiles of women in the administration.
  • Upcoming trials of interest include the Alexander brothers' case in New York, involving real estate tycoons and accusations of drugging women, and a case related to Rob Reiner's son.
  • Kraus's 'House Inhabit' Substack and Instagram are noted for her prolific content creation and unique perspective.

  • Jessica Reed Kraus expresses interest in Ghislaine Maxwell's history beyond Jeffrey Epstein, noting Maxwell's multiple licenses and potential for a pardon or overturned verdict.
  • Discussion covers Maxwell's prison transfer to a minimum-security facility, with speculation that Donald Trump's past association and appreciation for loyalty might influence a commutation.
  • Conflicting views on Jeffrey Epstein's death are noted, with dialogue from his cellmate and brother who hold differing accounts.
  • Donald Trump's reluctance regarding released Epstein documents is attributed to his past connections and annoyance with 'gossip' rather than a sex trafficking conspiracy.

  • Jessica Reed Kraus's business model makes fans feel included by sharing behind-the-scenes content, particularly from political events.
  • This approach resonates with a predominantly female audience, fostering community and representing their interests, such as details on jewelry.
  • Kraus maintains a multidisciplinary content creation process, focusing on visuals and rapid production, while also engaging in archival projects.
  • She plans to launch 'Palm Beach Diaries,' styled after Truman Capote and Dominick Dunne, in addition to her diverse current content.
