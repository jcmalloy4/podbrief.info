What Were Democrats Thinking?

Key Takeaways

  • Democrats initially led public opinion polls blaming Republicans for the government shutdown.
  • Senate Democrats ended the shutdown with minor gains, failing to secure long-term ACA tax credits.
  • Beyond ACA, Democrats were motivated by opposing Trump's perceived authoritarianism and corruption.
  • Internal divisions and fears of filibuster abolition influenced Democrats' decision to compromise.
  • The shutdown helped Democrats highlight healthcare and link Republican policies to higher premiums.

Deep Dive

  • Public opinion polls during a past government shutdown initially favored Democrats, who successfully blamed Republicans.
  • President Trump's personal spending and falling approval ratings contributed to this trend.
  • Democrats saw gains in elections during the period, benefiting from public sentiment.

  • Many Democrats were primarily motivated by opposing President Trump's perceived authoritarianism and corruption, not solely ACA subsidies.
  • ACA subsidies served as a unifying demand that aligned with broad public opinion, including among MAGA voters.
  • Internal Senate Democratic divisions emerged, with some opposing shutdowns outright and others questioning the strategic value of the narrow ACA demand.

  • Chuck Schumer's proposed compromise for a one-year ACA tax credit extension was seen by some Democrats as politically beneficial to Republicans.
  • The government shutdown caused significant disruption, leading to airports nearing chaos due to unpaid air traffic controllers.
  • Senate Democrats made a deal partly due to the widespread pain inflicted on constituents and fears that Republicans might abolish the filibuster.

  • Some Senate Democrats feared that a prolonged shutdown would lead Republicans to abolish the filibuster, a move they opposed.
  • Democrats surveyed believed they could have secured a slightly better deal by holding out longer, but acknowledged Trump's inflexibility.
  • Concerns arose that Democrats' willingness to compromise taught Trump they would fold under pressure, potentially emboldening him.
