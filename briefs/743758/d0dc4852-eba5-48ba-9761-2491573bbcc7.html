The Daily Beans

Wake-Up Call

Key Takeaways

  • ICE operations in Minneapolis are under scrutiny for alleged legal counsel denial and use of chemical agents.
  • Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell will attend a Supreme Court hearing concerning Governor Lisa Cook's attempted firing.
  • Donald Trump suggested canceling elections if he wins and regretted not seizing voting machines after 2020.
  • Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger ended the state's 287G program, stopping ICE collaboration.
  • Community groups are organizing actions against ICE and providing support to affected immigrant families.

Deep Dive

  • The FBI opened a civil rights investigation into ICE agent Jonathan Ross following the death of Renee Good in Minneapolis.
  • Attorneys allege the Department of Homeland Security is denying legal counsel to Minneapolis detainees, a practice not observed in over a decade.
  • South Dakota Governor Kristi Noem initially denied her department used chemical agents against Minnesota protesters, then backtracked after video evidence.
  • The government has appealed a judge's preliminary injunction against ICE's use of chemical agents to the Eighth Circuit Court of Appeals.

  • Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell will attend a Supreme Court oral argument concerning the attempted firing of Governor Lisa Cook.
  • This unusual show of support follows recent subpoenas and threats of indictment against Powell by the Trump administration.
  • The Supreme Court is considering whether a President can remove Fed Governors, a decision that could give appointees a majority on the Fed's board.
  • Legal analysis suggests the court may rule in favor of Cook, distinguishing the Fed from other multi-member boards.

  • Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger vetoed an executive order on her first day, ending the state's participation in the federal 287G program.
  • This action concludes the program that deputized local law enforcement as ICE agents.
  • The move is seen as a significant win for immigrant advocacy groups.
  • The decision reflects the influence of Latino voters in Governor Spanberger's election.

  • Indivisible has issued a call to action urging Congress to defund ICE, arguing against taxpayer funds for 'terrorizing' citizens.
  • The hosts criticized Senator Corey Booker's suggestion of training and body cameras for ICE, advocating for immediate legislative action.
  • The 'Every Meal' organization in Minnesota is providing food bags to children and families impacted by increased ICE activity and suspended SNAP benefits.
  • A billboard near the Great Lakes Naval Base in North Chicago, Illinois, advises 'Active duty and National Guard: You have a duty to refuse illegal orders,' sponsored by Veterans For Peace.
