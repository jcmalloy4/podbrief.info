Harm’s Way

Key Takeaways

  • The Pentagon reportedly knew of survivors from a September 2nd boat strike before launching a follow-on strike.
  • Ghislaine Maxwell is pursuing a habeas petition, expressing concern over the release of Epstein files.
  • A Department of Defense Inspector General report found that Pete Hegseth's use of an unsecure Signal chat endangered U.S. troops.
  • Republican Representatives Elise Stefanik and Nancy Mace have publicly expressed discontent with Speaker Mike Johnson's leadership.
  • A federal judge has prohibited warrantless arrests of immigrants in Washington D.C. unless they are flight risks.
