The Daily Beans

Refried Beans | The Elevens | 12/2/2022

Key Takeaways

  • The 11th Circuit Court of Appeals vacated Judge Aileen Cannon's Mar-a-Lago special master order.
  • Joel Greenberg was sentenced to 11 years in federal prison for multiple crimes, including sex trafficking.
  • Kari Lake's lawyers faced sanctions and fines for filing a frivolous election lawsuit in Arizona.
  • Listeners contributed various pet photos and stories to the episode's 'Good News' segment.
  • A listener shared a personal story about the passing of their long-time rescue dog, Mongo.

Deep Dive

  • The 11th Circuit Court of Appeals vacated Judge Aileen Cannon's order appointing a special master in the Mar-a-Lago documents case.
  • The court ruled Judge Cannon lacked equitable jurisdiction for the appointment.
  • Both classified and unclassified documents, including 'Celine Dion photos,' will now return to the Department of Justice.
  • Special Counsel Jack Smith will oversee the continuing criminal investigation.

  • Former Seminole County tax collector Joel Greenberg received an 11-year federal prison sentence.
  • The sentence was for multiple crimes, including underage sex trafficking, wire fraud, and identity theft.
  • Greenberg cooperated with the Justice Department's investigations into alleged sex trafficking by Matt Gaetz and provided information related to Jeffrey Epstein.

  • A federal judge sanctioned the lawyers for failed Arizona gubernatorial candidate Kari Lake.
  • The lawyers were ordered to pay fines for filing a frivolous lawsuit that demanded the use of paper ballots.
  • The judge criticized the legal team for failing to conduct basic legal research, noting paper ballots were already in practice.

  • A listener identified as Carmadillo submitted a picture of a goat for the 'Good News' segment.
  • Barb sent in photos of her rescue granddog and grandcat, sparking discussion on their unique appearances.
  • Valerie Klein submitted a humorous photo of a cat sitting in a sink, labeled 'sink, Elon' and 'Void Kitty'.

  • Rob Murray shared the story of his dog, Mongo, who passed away after 17 years.
  • Mongo was adopted from a senior dog rescue and enjoyed daily companionship and ice cream.
  • A listener expressed gratitude for the podcast's comfort during the difficult time of their own pet's euthanasia due to seizures.
