The Daily Beans

It Gets Better (feat. Brian Wenke)

Key Takeaways

  • The Daily Beans announced a $20,000 donation to the "It Gets Better" organization for LGBTQ+ youth support.
  • Authorities released a person of interest in the Brown University shooting, leaving the investigation without a known suspect.
  • A JetBlue flight narrowly avoided a mid-air collision with a U.S. Air Force refueling tanker near Venezuela.
  • The "It Gets Better" project provides grants, mentorship, and a new web-based app to empower young queer individuals.
  • Listeners are urged to contact congressional representatives to reopen the Office of Gun Violence Prevention.
  • Podcast listeners shared positive news including academic achievements, community initiatives, and personal milestones.

Deep Dive

  • The Daily Beans announced a $20,000 donation to the "It Gets Better" organization.
  • The donation aims to support the LGBTQ community and counter negative administration policies.
  • Host Allison Gill shared a personal connection to the cause and an upcoming interview with Brian Wenke, Executive Director.

  • Authorities released the person of interest detained in connection with a shooting at Brown University.
  • The shooting resulted in two student deaths and nine injuries, with one wounded student being a Parkland survivor.
  • Law enforcement found no basis to keep the individual in custody, marking a setback for the investigation.

  • A JetBlue flight from Curaçao to New York City narrowly avoided a mid-air collision with a U.S. Air Force refueling tanker.
  • The JetBlue pilot reported the military plane crossed their flight path at the same altitude, halting ascent.
  • The incident occurred near Venezuela during an increased U.S. military anti-drug trafficking campaign.

  • The It Gets Better project, founded 15 years ago, emerged in 2010 after suicides of young people, including Tyler Clementi, due to bullying.
  • The organization began as a social media storytelling campaign, collecting over 70,000 stories of hope for LGBTQ+ youth.
  • The initiative aimed to provide support and hope to vulnerable LGBTQ+ individuals, earning an Emmy for its social media use.

  • It Gets Better has evolved into a leadership development and youth empowerment organization.
  • The Changemakers program offers grants up to $10,000 for young queer people to fund community art, teacher education, and proms.
  • The organization provides mentorship through its youth voices program and developed IME, a free web-based app for self-guided exploration of identity.

  • The It Gets Better project is hosting 'Queer-a-thon' on Twitch, a six-to-eight-hour live-streamed event on December 17th.
  • The event serves as a year-end fundraiser and safe celebration space for LGBTQ+ youth.
  • A $40,000 donation could fund four youth grants, support 40,000 new IME users, and produce around 10 content series.

  • Listeners are encouraged to help match The Daily Beans' $20,000 donation to It Gets Better.
  • The organization has over 2 million followers across platforms and is the largest LGBTQ+ nonprofit on YouTube, Tumblr, and Twitch.
  • Donations can be made via itgetsbetter.org/dailybeansdonate to support LGBTQ+ youth programs.

  • Listeners are urged to contact Senators and House Representatives to demand action on gun violence.
  • Advocacy includes reopening the Office of Gun Violence Prevention, which was shut down by the Trump administration.
  • Specific policy demands include an assault weapons ban, universal background checks, and safe storage laws.

  • Listener Serenity, a trans woman, shared her journey of starting to stream video games on Twitch.
  • She highlighted Queer Munity, an LGBTQ+ and BIPOC-focused venue in Minneapolis.
  • Another listener, Kate, shared her experience speaking about Regency-era fashion at a Jane Austen-themed tea party and being interviewed by local radio.
