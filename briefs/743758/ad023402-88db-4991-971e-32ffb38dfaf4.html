Block Party

Key Takeaways

  • Minneapolis prosecutors will investigate Renée Good's murder by an ICE agent despite FBI attempts to block state access.
  • The Trump administration is deploying more Border Patrol agents to Minneapolis and restricting congressional access to ICE facilities.
  • Federal courts blocked key Trump administration orders concerning elections, welfare funds, and family immigration parole programs.
  • Virginia Democrats are pursuing a redistricting plan to create more Democratic-leaning congressional seats for the 2026 elections.
  • The National Portrait Gallery removed information about Trump's impeachments, replacing it with a large portrait of him.

Deep Dive

  • Minneapolis prosecutors will investigate the murder of Renée Good by an ICE agent, proceeding with their own review.
  • Hennepin County Attorney Mary Moriarty and Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison announced the review due to concerns about evidence sharing.
  • The FBI attempted to block state investigators by revoking access to case files, a move described as unusual.
  • Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Fry has publicly called for ICE to leave the city.

  • The Trump administration is deploying over 100 U.S. Customs and Border Protection agents to Minnesota following the Renée Good shooting, reassigned from Chicago and New Orleans.
  • DHS Secretary Kristi Noem reinstated a policy requiring seven days' notice for congressional visits to ICE detention facilities, a move seen as circumventing a court ruling.
  • Minnesota Democratic Representatives Angie Craig, Ilhan Omar, and Kelly Morrison were denied access to a Minneapolis detention facility due to Noem's guidance.
  • Federal law has prohibited ICE from denying lawmakers access to facilities since 2020.

  • The National Portrait Gallery replaced text detailing former President Trump's impeachments and the January 6th insurrection with a prominent photo of him.
  • This action is part of a broader effort by the Trump administration to remove perceived 'woke ideology' from cultural institutions.

  • A federal court blocked key provisions of Trump's executive order on elections, ruling it unconstitutional, which would have imposed new rules like proof of citizenship and restricted mail-in ballots.
  • A federal judge also blocked the Trump administration's attempt to freeze $10 billion in welfare funds for five states, including California, New York, and Illinois.
  • The welfare fund freeze impacts child care, temporary assistance for needy families, and social services grants.
  • Additionally, a federal judge signaled blocking the Trump administration's move to end family reunification parole programs for seven Latin American countries, which were initiated in 2021 by the Biden administration.

  • Virginia Democrats are considering a redistricting plan aiming to create 10 out of 11 Democratic-leaning congressional seats.
  • This move seeks to counter Republican gerrymandering efforts nationwide and could significantly impact the control of Congress in 2026.
  • Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger has urged caution due to potential complications with a statewide referendum related to redistricting.

  • A GoFundMe campaign has been created for ICE agent Jonathan Ross, who is accused of murdering Renée Good.
  • Listeners highlighted that this GoFundMe may violate the platform's terms of service and provided information on how to report it.
