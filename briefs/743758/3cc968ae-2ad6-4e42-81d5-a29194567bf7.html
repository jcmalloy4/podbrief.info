Refried Beans | Deep State Resistance (feat. David Rothkopf) | 11/01/2022

Key Takeaways

  • The Department of Justice charged the assailant in the Paul Pelosi attack with federal crimes.
  • Key testimonies detailed actions and encounters during the January 6th Capitol attack in the Oath Keepers trial.
  • Lula da Silva's victory in Brazil's election was presented as a rejection of fascism.
  • Senator Chris Murphy urged a national security review regarding Saudi Arabia's investment in Twitter.
  • David Rothkopf's book, "American Resistance," outlined how civil servants countered the Trump administration's agenda.
  • Podcast listeners shared positive updates on fundraising efforts and personal stories, including Halloween costumes.

Deep Dive

  • The Leguminati surpassed their $200,000 midterm fundraising goal for Democratic candidates.
  • Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva won Brazil's presidential election, with his victory framed as a rejection of fascism.
  • International leaders, including Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and US President Joe Biden, extended congratulations to Lula.

  • The Department of Justice charged the assailant in the Paul Pelosi attack with federal crimes, including attempting to kidnap Speaker of the House Nancy Pelosi.
  • Additional federal charges include retaliating against a federal official's family member.
  • The assailant's confession, detailed in the DOJ affidavit, indicated the attack was an assassination attempt on Nancy Pelosi.

  • Graydon Young, an Oath Keepers member who pleaded guilty, testified against his former allies, detailing his preparations for January 6th.
  • Young described an initial exhilaration upon witnessing the Capitol breach, comparing it to the storming of the Bastille.
  • Police officer Harry Dunn testified about a face-to-face encounter with Oath Keepers members during the January 6th Capitol attack, stating they tried to bypass him.

  • Senator Chris Murphy called for a national security review of Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter due to Saudi Arabia's investment.
  • Saudi entities became the second-largest shareholder in Twitter, raising concerns about potential national security implications.
  • Murphy cited fears of foreign influence and the platform's role in amplifying divisive content, including anti-Semitic and racist remarks.

  • David Rothkopf's book, "American Resistance," highlights how government professionals upheld their constitutional oaths during the Trump administration.
  • Former President Trump's approach to governing, described as that of a business owner, led to immediate challenges such as an attempt to implement an illegal Muslim ban.
  • Career civil servants and other officials utilized established laws and procedures to counteract Trump's unconventional and extreme proposals, mitigating potential damage.

  • The Trump administration was characterized by the existence of 'two governments': official channels and parallel, often unofficial, operations, such as Rudy Giuliani's shadow Ukraine policy.
  • Government employees, including appointees, eventually reached breaking points with Trump's actions, even those ideologically aligned with him.
  • Officials found workarounds to implement policy without direct presidential approval, including processes for election fairness and managing relations with Russia.

  • Listeners submitted Halloween photos, including Monica's daughter in a witch costume and 'Dog and cat mom's pets dressed as Pennywise the clown.
  • Another listener, Peter, shared details of his creative Halloween costume combining Mandalorian, Bruce Wayne, Gandalf, and Stormbreaker.
  • Dana Goldberg raised $250,000 for the Human Rights Campaign, and the 'Luguminati' raised $200,000 for Democratic midterm candidates.

  • Listener Dan, a wind turbine technician, introduced himself and expressed appreciation for the podcast's news delivery.
  • Listener Carol shared a heartwarming story about her daughter's first time voting, which was met with enthusiastic applause at the polling station.
  • The hosts discussed high youth voter turnout, attributing it to the younger generation's dissatisfaction with issues like gun violence and climate change.
