The Daily Beans

Refried Beans | Morally And Financially Bankrupt (feat. John Fugelsang) | 12/22/2023

Key Takeaways

  • Rudy Giuliani filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy after a $148 million defamation judgment.
  • The Pope issued a decree allowing blessings for same-sex unions in the Catholic Church.
  • Florida faces lawsuits over a law restricting pronoun usage for teachers in schools.
  • Georgia's Lt. Governor Burt Jones faces a lawsuit for his role as a 2020 Trump elector.
  • The National Rifle Association reported significant financial decline and membership lows.
  • Donald Trump faces ongoing legal challenges, including seven scheduled trials and an immunity appeal.

Deep Dive

  • Florida's law restricting pronoun usage in schools is generating anger among educators.
  • Math teacher Katie Wood is suing the state, fearing termination for using 'Miss' as her title.
  • Librarians and teachers are initiating lawsuits against these state policies.
  • The hosts expressed strong disapproval of the actions of Florida's governor regarding this issue.

  • A Georgia judge heard arguments in a lawsuit seeking to disqualify Lt. Gov. Burt Jones from office.
  • Four Georgia voters argue Jones violated his oath by participating as a 2020 Trump elector.
  • The lawsuit is part of a nationwide effort to challenge ballot eligibility under the Constitution's insurrection clause.
  • Superior Court Judge Thomas Wilson is allowing additional legal briefs before making a ruling, with an expected appeal to the state Supreme Court.

  • The National Rifle Association (NRA) is experiencing a significant decline, with reduced revenue and membership for the fourth consecutive year, reaching lows not seen since at least 2008.
  • The NRA reported $211 million in income for 2022, less than 60% of its 2016 revenue, and incurred a $22 million deficit, its largest since 2016.
  • Dues and member fees dropped to their lowest point since 2008, representing a nearly 59% decrease from 2016.
  • The organization saw a $36 million loss in value during 2022, with increased fundraising expenses and smaller investment returns.

  • The Pope issued a decree allowing blessings for same-sex unions within the Catholic Church, though marriage is still defined as between a man and a woman.
  • Blessings for same-sex couples must be non-liturgical, representing a significant, albeit small, step forward for the Vatican.
  • Guest John Fugelsang interpreted the Pope's actions as embodying Jesus's message of inclusivity, arguing against biblical justification for homophobia.

  • Rudy Giuliani filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the Southern District of New York, listing $1-10 million in assets and $500 million in debt.
  • This move followed Judge Beryl Howell ordering the immediate enforcement of a $148 million verdict against him.
  • The bankruptcy filing could temporarily halt enforcement, though defamation claims with actual malice are typically bankruptcy-proof.
  • It is expected that Giuliani's future earnings will be directed to Ruby Freeman and Shea Moss.

  • Donald Trump faces seven trials scheduled for the upcoming month, impacting his legal and political standing.
  • Jack Smith's immunity appeal to the Supreme Court could impact the March trial date for the D.C. indictment.
  • Citizens are using the judicial system for redress of grievances, with legal judgments cited against Alex Jones ($1 billion), Donald Trump ($5 million), and Rudy Giuliani ($148 million).

  • Listeners shared good news, including a husband rescuing a Belgian Malinois from a shelter.
  • A listener provided a correction about the ABC affiliate in New York City being Channel 7.
  • Listeners from Australia shared photos of local wildlife and expressed gratitude for the podcast's U.S. political news coverage.
  • The hosts emphasized the importance of chosen families, particularly in the queer community, during the holidays.

  • A listener described encountering a large, unusual turtle-like creature with a long, pointy tail on a bike trail in Augusta, Georgia, seeking identification.
  • One listener shared a personal anecdote about misinterpreting the song 'I Saw Mommy Kissing Santa Claus' until adulthood.
  • Another listener shared a childhood Christmas photo of their mother kissing Santa, asking for help identifying the image due to a possible Mandela Effect.
