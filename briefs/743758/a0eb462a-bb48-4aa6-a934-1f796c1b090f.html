The Daily Beans

Epstein Files Friday (feat. John Fugelsang)

Key Takeaways

  • New photos and documents from Jeffrey Epstein's estate have been released.
  • Federal prosecutors attempted a third felony charge against New York Attorney General Letitia James.
  • Republicans are showing signs of distancing from Donald Trump ahead of the midterms.
  • Speaker Mike Johnson faces challenges over Trump alignment and Affordable Care Act subsidies.
  • President Trump's military bonus is funded by existing housing allowances, not new funds.
  • Community protests are planned against Key Lime Air's contracts with ICE and a university.
  • The 2026 National Parks Pass design featuring Donald Trump is drawing controversy.

Deep Dive

  • December 19th marked 'Epstein Files Day,' the deadline for Pam Bondi to release related documents.
  • House Democrats released over 50 additional photos from Jeffrey Epstein's estate.
  • The released photos included a Ukrainian passport, a picture of Noam Chomsky, and text messages referencing the exchange of money for girls.

  • President Trump announced a $1,776 bonus for over a million military troops, funded by existing housing allowances, not new funds.
  • The administration is criticized for withholding strike video footage, including a September 2nd incident where two men died and two survivors were taken into custody.
  • The handling of these individuals raises questions about transparency and accountability regarding alleged crimes.

  • Federal prosecutors attempted to add a third felony charge, making a false statement to a financial institution, against New York Attorney General Letitia James.
  • This proposed charge could carry a 30-year sentence.
  • Grand jurors had previously rejected earlier charges, and a judge denied the prosecutors' request to seal court records.

  • A Vanity Fair article features Susie Wiles discussing Donald Trump's connection to the Epstein files.
  • The hosts and guest analyzed Wiles's motivations for speaking out.
  • It was suggested Wiles might be positioning herself for future protection by providing her account of events.

  • Analysis suggests Republicans may distance themselves from Donald Trump before the midterms, following Susie Wiles's lead.
  • Wiles reportedly urged Trump not to pardon January 6th rioters, which he allegedly disregarded.
  • The discussion contrasted Trump's approach to personal vendettas with former President Nixon's.

  • House Republicans face a precarious position, with Speaker Mike Johnson criticized for his alignment with Donald Trump.
  • Johnson is accused of shutting down Congress early to avoid discussing Epstein files and potential Affordable Care Act premium increases.
  • The lack of enacted ACA subsidies could lead to rising premiums and voter backlash, particularly in 'Red State' areas.

  • Protests are scheduled for Saturday, December 20th, targeting Denver Air Connection and Key Lime Air.
  • The demonstrations will focus on their contracts with ICE to transport detainees and with the University of Colorado for athletic teams.
  • Protests are planned in multiple Colorado airports, Jackson, Tennessee, and Minneapolis, Minnesota.

  • The 2026 America the Beautiful National Parks Pass features a controversial new design including Donald Trump's image alongside George Washington.
  • An artist has created decals to cover this design.
  • A listener characterized this as 'good trouble'.
