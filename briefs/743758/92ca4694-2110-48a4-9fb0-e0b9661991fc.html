The Daily Beans

TACO Tuesday (feat. Adam Klasfeld)

Key Takeaways

  • Multiple prominent Republicans criticized ICE and called for an independent investigation into Alex Pretti's murder.
  • The Trump administration began withdrawing ICE Director Gregory Bovino and agents from Minneapolis.
  • Senator Jacky Rosen called for Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem's impeachment.
  • A Republican gubernatorial candidate in Minnesota withdrew from the race due to ICE actions.
  • The Department of Homeland Security confirmed possession of body-worn camera footage of Alex Pretti's fatal shooting.

Deep Dive

  • ICE Director Gregory Bovino and agents were withdrawn from Minneapolis, framed as a significant, though small, victory achieved through grassroots community efforts.
  • This development is highlighted as a potential precedent for national resistance.
  • The hosts emphasized maintaining pressure on elected officials and continued advocacy for change.

  • Former Vice President Mike Pence called body-worn camera footage of Alex Pretti's shooting 'deeply troubling,' urging an immediate, full, and transparent investigation.
  • Senator Jacky Rosen called for Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem's impeachment, citing Noem's alleged attempts to mislead the public regarding Pretti's shooting.
  • Republican gubernatorial candidate Chris Madel withdrew from the Minnesota race, criticizing ICE's conduct and "Operation MetroSurge" for expanding beyond its stated focus.

  • The Department of Homeland Security confirmed that body-worn camera footage exists from the fatal shooting of Alex Pretti, and investigators are reviewing the videos.
  • Lindsey Halligan, a former insurance attorney, is no longer employed by the Justice Department after stepping down from her interim U.S. attorney role in Virginia.
  • A judge previously ruled Halligan unlawfully held her position, citing a lack of prosecutorial experience and misrepresentation to the court.

  • A hearing was scheduled regarding a lawsuit filed by Minneapolis, focusing on whether the Trump administration's deployment of ICE violated the 10th Amendment's anti-commandeering doctrine.
  • Legal arguments focused on states' rights and federal coercion, with a letter from Pam Bondi framed as a "quid pro quo" for policy changes.
  • The judge questioned how the ICE surge addressed specific state issues and the extent of federal power to compel state compliance.

  • A federal judge is carefully considering a request for a broader order to halt ICE operations in Minnesota, following an Eighth Circuit pause on a narrower order regarding pepper spray use.
  • The judge is scrutinizing the government's justification for deploying thousands of agents.
  • The judge is questioning the link between voter data collection and the need for increased enforcement.

  • Following a call with Governor Walls, Donald Trump indicated a potential shift in his administration's immigration enforcement in Minnesota, agreeing to consider reducing federal agents.
  • National reaction to the Alex Pretti case, including criticism from Second Amendment supporters, prompted Trump to reconsider ICE's presence.
  • The judge in the lawsuit is reportedly focusing on developing a solid legal record for potential appeals and investigating a secret memo on ICE's warrant-less entry authority.

  • An evidentiary hearing is scheduled for Kilmar Abrego Garcia, focusing on the prosecution's burden to prove they are not acting vindictively.
  • This stems from public statements by Todd Blanche implying the investigation began due to a judge's questioning of Abrego's removal to El Salvador.
  • The hearing will examine evidence and government explanations for alleged coordination.

  • The 'Good Trouble' segment highlights Charlotte Clymer's post on Blue Sky, directing listeners to standwithminnesota.com, a directory for supporting organizations and mutual aid projects in Minnesota.
  • A listener from Minneapolis reported approximately 50,000 people participated in a protest and general strike against ICE in sub-zero temperatures on January 23rd, 2026.
  • Activists are building email lists and engaging in political action, emphasizing the power of activism over passivity.

  • A discussion on resilience during dark times is highlighted by an excerpt from a Minnesota Timberwolves chaplain's statement.
  • The chaplain criticized performative prayers and called for action over inaction.
  • This message underscores the importance of active engagement in response to difficult events.
