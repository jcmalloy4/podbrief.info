The Megyn Kelly Show

Perilous Israel-Hamas Deal, Charlie Kirk Awarded Medal of Freedom, Memphis Success: AM Update 10/15

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump issued an ultimatum to Hamas amid ongoing Gaza ceasefire violations.
  • Charlie Kirk was posthumously honored with the Presidential Medal of Freedom by President Trump.
  • Federal agents conducted a major operation in Memphis, resulting in over 800 arrests and 45 child recoveries.
  • A man received a 25-50 year sentence for arson and attempted murder at Governor Shapiro's home.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump issued an ultimatum to Hamas to disarm, stating, "if they don't disarm, we will disarm them."
  • Israel and Hamas traded accusations of violating the newly brokered Gaza ceasefire.
  • Hamas claimed Israel killed residents, while Israel reported troops fired on suspects crossing a defined line.
  • Israel plans to cut aid due to Hamas's delay in returning hostage bodies, with fewer identified deceased hostages than initially reported.

  • President Trump posthumously awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom to Charlie Kirk at the White House.
  • The award was presented on what would have been the Turning Point USA founder's 32nd birthday.
  • Trump described Kirk as a 'fearless warrior for liberty' and a 'martyr for truth and for freedom.'
  • Kirk's widow, Erica Kirk, accepted the award on his behalf.

  • The DOJ's Memphis Safe Task Force conducted over 800 arrests in two weeks, involving multiple federal agencies.
  • The operation targeted violent crime, narcotics, firearms, and sex offenses.
  • The Director of the Tennessee Bureau of Investigations reported 45 missing children recovered, primarily runaways at risk of human trafficking.
  • Memphis Mayor Paul Young praised the task force's impact, and Governor Bill Lee stated aspects would become permanent.

  • Cody Bomber pleaded guilty to arson and attempted murder charges for attacking Governor Josh Shapiro's home.
  • Bomber stated his motive was anger over Governor Shapiro's stance on the Gaza war.
  • He was sentenced to 25 to 50 years in prison.
  • Governor Shapiro called for accountability against political violence, noting the plea represented a just outcome.
