New MN Shooting Under Investigation, Major Murder Rate Drop, Winter Storm Slams US: AM Update 1/26

Key Takeaways

  • Federal agents fatally shot an anti-ICE protestor in Minneapolis, sparking conflicting narratives and political backlash.
  • The U.S. murder rate has dropped to its lowest point in over a century, with homicides down 21% in 2025.
  • A massive winter storm impacted 40 states, causing widespread power outages, travel disruptions, and school closures.
  • Rock climber Alex Honnold free-soloed Taiwan's 1,667-foot Taipei 101 skyscraper live on Netflix.

Deep Dive

  • Anti-ICE activist Alex Predi was fatally shot by federal agents in Minneapolis, generating conflicting narratives from social media videos.
  • Federal sources expressed frustration over the Department of Homeland Security's initial messaging regarding the incident, describing it as 'catastrophic'.
  • Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey and Minnesota Governor Tim Walz criticized federal actions, with Walz calling for agents' removal from the state.
  • DHS Secretary Kirstjen Nielsen stated the suspect had a handgun and ammunition, suggesting an intent to cause 'maximum damage'.
  • Alex Predi was a nurse and a concealed carry permit holder whose father had previously advised him against engaging in protests.

  • Border Patrol Commander Gregory Bovino defended agents facing hostile protesters, stating Alex Predi 'made the decision to engage'.
  • ICE Associate Director Marcos Charles detailed dangers faced by ICE officers, citing an incident where a protester bit off an agent's finger during an arrest for a violent crime.
  • Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche attributed Minneapolis's crime problems to local leadership's lack of cooperation with federal immigration enforcement.
  • President Trump called on Minneapolis Mayor Frey and Governor Walz to cooperate with federal authorities on issues including sanctuary cities and law enforcement.

  • A new report indicates the U.S. murder rate has fallen to its lowest level in over a century.
  • Homicides were down 21% in 2025 compared to 2024, with other violent crimes also showing significant declines.
  • The report did not identify specific causes for the crime rate drop, while the White House attributed it to federal crackdowns and President Trump's policies.

  • A massive winter storm impacted 40 states with snow, ice, and freezing rain, causing widespread travel disruptions.
  • The storm led to over 12,000 flight cancellations on a single day and numerous school closures across cities including Houston, Dallas, and Boston.
  • Approximately 1 million customers lost power in the South due to the severe weather.

  • Rock climber Alex Honnold free-soloed Taiwan's Taipei 101 skyscraper, a 1,667-foot building.
  • The ascent was performed without ropes or safety gear and broadcast live on Netflix.
  • Honnold completed the climb, lasting 44 minutes, stunning viewers worldwide.
