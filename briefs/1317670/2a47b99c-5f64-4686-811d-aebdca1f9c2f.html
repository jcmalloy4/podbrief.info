Criminal Probe Into Fed Chair Powell, Homan's Warning on ICE, MN Fraud Admissions: AM Update 1/12

Key Takeaways

  • Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell is under criminal investigation for allegedly misleading Congress on renovation costs.
  • Protests erupted in Minneapolis following an ICE agent's fatal shooting, sparking debate over police actions.
  • A federal investigation targets an estimated $9 billion in Medicaid fraud across 14 services in Minnesota.
  • President Trump is reportedly considering military options for Iran and pursuing the acquisition of Greenland.
  • The college football championship features undefeated Indiana against Miami, highlighting quarterback Fernando Mendoza.

Deep Dive

  • Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell confirmed he is under criminal investigation by the Justice Department.
  • The inquiry focuses on whether Powell misled Congress about the Fed's $2.5 billion headquarters renovation costs, which rose from $1.7 billion in 2017.
  • Powell asserts the investigation is a pretext related to his disagreements with President Trump over interest rates.
  • President Trump had previously deemed the rising renovation costs a 'fireable offense' during a July visit.

  • At least 30 arrests were made in Minneapolis after protests erupted following an ICE agent's fatal shooting of a woman.
  • Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey's claim that the agent was not run over was contrasted by audio and video evidence.
  • Border Czar Tom Homan noted the involved ICE agent has been doxxed and received death threats.
  • Homeland Security Secretary Kirstjen Nielsen reported vehicles have been weaponized over 100 times against law enforcement recently.

  • A federal surge into Minnesota is investigating an estimated $9 billion in fraud across 14 Medicaid services.
  • Governor Tim Wals is facing scrutiny over his response, acknowledging the fraud could have been addressed more effectively.
  • The Trump administration implemented measures like freezing payments and creating a new DOJ position to prosecute fraud.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Besant plans to offer rewards to whistleblowers to aid fraud investigations.

  • U.S. foreign policy includes recent airstrikes in Syria, targeting over 35 ISIS sites and weapons caches.
  • President Trump is considering military actions regarding Iran, with options presented by the military including cyber weapons and strikes.
  • Trump is pursuing the acquisition of Greenland for national security, with special operations commanders developing contingency plans.
  • Denmark warned against military aggression regarding Greenland, while European nations consider expanding NATO forces there.

  • The college football playoff championship will feature the undefeated Indiana Hoosiers against the Miami Hurricanes.
  • Indiana's success is attributed to quarterback Fernando Mendoza, whose Stoicism-rooted philosophy guided the team.
  • The game is set for Monday at 7:30 p.m. East, hosted at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami.
