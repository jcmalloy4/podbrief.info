Media Covers Up Their Role in Biden Cover-Up, and Rubio Goes After Dem Senators, with Victor Davis Hanson | Ep. 1077

Overview

  • The media and Democratic Party engaged in a coordinated effort to conceal Biden's cognitive decline until recently, with figures like Jake Tapper now acknowledging what conservative media had reported for years, raising serious questions about journalistic integrity and public trust.
  • Biden's presidency has been characterized by significant governance questions and medical transparency issues, including concerns about who's actually making decisions, potential undisclosed treatments, and inconsistencies in his health reporting, particularly regarding his prostate cancer diagnosis.
  • The Trump administration's foreign policy approach combines strategic unpredictability with strong deterrence, with Marco Rubio emerging as a powerful Secretary of State nominee who represents both restraint and intervention capabilities in international relations.
  • Significant concerns exist regarding the Ukraine-Russia conflict's devastating toll (potentially 1.5 million casualties), with Trump reportedly pursuing diplomatic solutions including territorial compromises, a DMZ, and potential secondary sanctions against Russia.
  • Controversial issues surrounding South Africa's racial tensions have emerged, with Trump granting refugee status to white South African farmers amid reports of targeted violence, while criticism of FBI leadership focuses on alleged political weaponization under directors like James Comey.

Content: Chronological Account of the Conversation

Biden's Cognitive Decline and Media Coverage

  • Megyn Kelly discusses a recent interview with Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson about Biden's cognitive decline, noting that Tapper admitted conservative media was right about Biden's mental state.
  • Victor Davis Hanson criticizes Tapper for previously dismissing and ridiculing those who suggested Biden was cognitively impaired:
- Specifically mentions Tapper being rude to Laura Trump when she raised concerns - Notes Tapper only came forward with criticism after his book's release - Suggests Tapper will likely profit from a situation he was previously complicit in covering up

  • The speaker criticizes President Biden's leadership, citing multiple perceived failures:
- Weakened U.S. deterrence in Afghanistan - Perceived soft approach to Putin - Distancing from Israel - Removing terrorist designations - Contributing to two theater wars - Experiencing hyperinflation

  • The discussion argues there was a coordinated effort to hide Biden's cognitive decline:
- Involving medical team, staff, surrogates, and media - Dismissing concerns as "cheap fake" for months - Suppressing information about the president's potential health issues

Media Accountability and White House Inner Circle

  • The conversation criticizes media outlets for being "late to the party" in reporting on Biden's infirmity:
- Wall Street Journal reporters criticized for delayed reporting - CNN attacked for dismissing concerns about Biden's mental state - Lack of rigorous journalistic follow-up on Biden's presidency and decision-making

  • Book revelations about White House inner circle:
- Mike Donilon, Steve Ricchetti, Jill Biden, and Hunter Biden identified as key figures - Anthony Blinken effectively managed foreign policy - Jake Sullivan was aware of Biden's deterioration - Anita Dunn had a special governmental employee status

  • The broader context of Biden's nomination is discussed:
- Concerns about his cognitive state were evident as early as 2020 - Cory Booker noted Biden's verbal difficulties during debates - Jim Clyburn's intervention in South Carolina primaries effectively "coronated" Biden - Democratic party did not have a truly open nomination process

Media Hypocrisy and Political Dynamics

  • The speaker criticizes media outlets (CBS, Washington Post, CNN) for suddenly discovering Biden's cognitive issues when they were aware of these concerns earlier:
- Media outlets now acting surprised about Biden's condition - These same outlets previously downplayed or ignored signs of Biden's mental decline - Washington Post criticized for hypocrisy, given their historical rigorous reporting on presidential issues

  • The Democratic Party and media are accused of deliberately overlooking Biden's cognitive challenges in 2020 for political reasons:
- Biden viewed as a "wax effigy" useful for pushing a progressive agenda - Obama-aligned groups saw Biden as an opportunity to extend Obama's policy approach - The party coordinated to support Biden's nomination despite his apparent limitations

  • The conversation highlights the contrast in media coverage of cognitive fitness:
- CNN commentators criticized conservative media's portrayal of Biden as mentally unfit - Previous media narratives questioned Trump's mental fitness, including psychiatrist Bandy Lee's public diagnosis - Discussions about using the 25th Amendment against Trump - Trump taking the Montreal cognitive assessment

Biden's Health and Governance Questions

  • Discussion focuses on questions about President Biden's health and potential medical history:
- Specific concerns raised about Biden's PSA (prostate-specific antigen) testing - Claims he had no PSA test since 2014 - Calls for transparency about test results and medical records

  • Speculation about potential undisclosed medical treatments during presidency:
- References to bruises on Biden's hands - Questions about possible IV treatments

  • Suggestions that Biden may not be fully in control of presidential decision-making:
- Claims "auto-pend" signatures were used without Biden's direct involvement - Implies other political actors were making significant governmental decisions

  • The speaker discusses President Biden's prostate cancer diagnosis, questioning the credibility of the official explanation:
- Dr. David Samadhi (a prostate surgeon) reportedly said the official statement about hormone treatment is implausible - The only way to confirm hormone treatment's effectiveness is to see PSA levels decrease - Suggestions the cancer announcement may have been timed to deflect attention from a potentially damaging book

Biden's Communication Challenges

  • Biden reportedly struggled significantly with verbal communication during video recordings:
- Production teams had to use multiple cameras and extensive editing to make his video addresses usable - Often unable to complete short addresses without verbal mistakes - Difficulties attributed not to his stutter, but to inability to find words, trouble remembering what he was saying, and difficulty maintaining a train of thought

  • Biden's teleprompter and media interactions:
- Known to use teleprompters for donor and campaign events - Even with teleprompters, sometimes struggled to read prepared text - Media representatives like TJ Ducklow deflected questions about teleprompter use

  • Speculation about potential underlying medical conditions:
- Effects of past aneurysm surgeries - Possible neurological conditions - Potential medication side effects

Qatar's Gift to Trump and Foreign Policy

  • Qatar gifted a Boeing 747 to the Pentagon/Trump, valued at $400 million:
- 2013 model 747 - Considered a "white elephant" - outdated and expensive to operate - Difficult to maintain - Had been on the market since 2020 with no buyers

  • Key controversies surrounding the gift:
- Qatar's history with terrorism - Potential security concerns about the aircraft - Perception of personal benefit to Trump

  • Trump's response:
- Initially claimed it was a gift to the US/Pentagon - Later suggested it would eventually go to his presidential library - Appeared reluctant to refuse the gift to avoid offending hosts

  • Legal considerations around the gift include:
- Potential emoluments clause concerns - DOJ arguing it's a gift to the Pentagon, not Trump personally - Ethical questions about being "beholden" to Qatar

Marco Rubio and Foreign Policy

  • Marco Rubio is discussed as performing exceptionally well in Senate Foreign Relations Committee:
- Demonstrated strong verbal skills and ability to handle multiple arguments - Faced criticism from Senator Chris Van Holland - Noted as the only Trump administration nominee confirmed unanimously as Secretary of State

  • Rubio is viewed as a powerful Secretary of State:
- Closely aligned with Trump's foreign policy - Seen as competent in handling complex international negotiations - Represents a blend of "restrainer" and interventionist foreign policy approaches

  • Future political prospects for Rubio:
- Mark Halperin suggests Rubio has become a key figure in the MAGA movement - Speculation about a potential 2028 presidential ticket with Rubio and J.D. Vance - Described as a rising star who overcame earlier political challenges

Ukraine-Russia Conflict and Trump's Approach

  • The discussion centers on a foreign policy approach of non-interventionism combined with strong deterrence:
- Referencing Trump's previous strategy of being "unpredictable" but willing to retaliate disproportionately - Trump views war as "bad business" but understands the need to occasionally show force

  • Ukraine-Russia conflict assessment:
- Trump recently spoke with Putin and suggested Russia and Ukraine should resolve the conflict themselves - New York Times reporting suggests Trump is backing away from supporting Ukraine - The conflict has caused massive casualties, potentially more than Stalingrad in 1942 - Estimated 1.5 million people dead, wounded, or maimed

  • Putin's current situation:
- Faces significant challenges despite apparent military advantages - Russia has suffered approximately 1-1.5 million casualties - Military and economy have been severely impacted - Current territorial gains are limited (40-80 miles beyond previous territories)

  • Trump's potential diplomatic solutions:
- Accepting Russia's control of Crimea and Donbass - Preventing Ukraine's NATO membership - Establishing a DMZ and ceasefire - Proposed potential secondary sanctions against banks doing business with Russia

South Africa and Racial Tensions

  • Trump has allowed 59 white South African farmers and families to enter the US as refugees:
- Claims of potential persecution against white minority (8% of population) - Nonprofit Afro forum reported 333 farm attacks in 2022, including 50 murders - South African police stats show 197 farm murders and 154 attempted murders from April-September 2024

  • South African President Rama Foza visited the White House:
- Tensions exist over South Africa's actions regarding Israel at the Hague - Trump showed a documentary about violence against white farmers during the meeting - The South African delegation reportedly watched the video in silence

  • Ratif Goosen, a white South African golfer, provided personal testimony about farm violence:
- His family experiences constant threats - Farms are being targeted with attempts to burn them down - His family lives behind electric fences - His 80-year-old mother was attacked in her house

  • Julius Malema, leader of South Africa's EFF party, made controversial statements about potential violence against white people, saying he's "not calling for slaughter... at least for now"
  • Elon Musk highlighted South Africa's "black economic empowerment" laws:
- Require businesses to have at least 30% black ownership - Preventing him from bringing Starlink to the country

FBI Leadership Criticism

  • The discussion concludes with criticism of recent FBI leadership, particularly focusing on James Comey:
- Misuse of the Steele dossier to obtain FISA warrants - Forging documents (referenced Kevin Clinesmith) - Leaking confidential conversations - Improperly handling investigations of Hillary Clinton and Michael Flynn - Weaponizing the FBI for political purposes

  • Criticism extends to multiple FBI directors, including James Comey, Robert Mueller, Andrew McCabe, and Christopher Wray
  • Proposed solution by Kash Patel:
- Shut down FBI headquarters - Relocate approximately 1,500 employees - Move headquarters out of Washington, D.C. to a more central location like Oklahoma or Kansas
