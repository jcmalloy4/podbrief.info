The Megyn Kelly Show

Hollywood Secrets, GOP Push to Get Crockett to Run, and Keys to Longevity, with Andrew Klavan and Gary Brecka | Ep. 1210

Key Takeaways

  • Megyn Kelly withdrew her podcast from Golden Globe consideration due to perceived campaigning requirements and industry bias.
  • Andrew Klavan discussed the left's cultural dominance and challenges faced by conservative artists in Hollywood.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Netflix's potential acquisition of Warner Bros. and its content strategy.
  • A report alleges national Republicans 'astro-turfed' support for Jasmine Crockett's Senate run.
  • Longevity expert Gary Brecka provided advice on sleep, diet, exercise, and critical nutrients like Vitamin D3.

Deep Dive

  • Megyn Kelly withdrew her podcast from Golden Globe consideration, citing campaigning requirements and personal endorsements.
  • She contrasted this with past practices at Fox News and perceived leftist bias in award systems.
  • Kelly stated a preference for maintaining integrity and speaking honestly over engaging in publicity for awards.

  • The host criticized the Met Gala, questioning the cultural relevance of co-chairs like Beyoncé and the event's high cost for attendees.
  • The perceived decline of the entertainment industry was illustrated by referencing the appearances of Ariana Grande and Cynthia Erivo.
  • Reports of Andy Dick's overdose and Kelly Osbourne's thinness were cited as further indicators of the industry's state.

  • Actress Sydney Sweeney faced criticism over a jeans advertisement following her family's perceived Republican stance.
  • Sweeney initially defended her position but later issued a statement against hate, which was interpreted as 'bending the knee' to industry pressure.
  • The guest criticized People Magazine as 'woke' and claimed its staff 'hate conservatives' regarding Sweeney's statement.

  • Kelsey Grammer has demonstrated increasing political boldness with age, publicly supporting Donald Trump.
  • His public support is presented as an act of courage, contrasting with actors who have faced cancellation, such as Mel Gibson.

  • Netflix is reportedly pursuing a $82.5 billion acquisition of Warner Brothers, which includes franchises like Batman and Harry Potter.
  • The host criticized Netflix for content such as a movie about a 'trans' coal miner and 41% LGBTQ themes in children's content.
  • A competing bid from Paramount Skydance, exceeding $100 billion, is also discussed as a potentially better option for shareholders.
  • Concerns were raised about Netflix's 'woke management' potentially leading to content that instructs rather than entertains.

  • National Republicans allegedly 'astro-turfed' support for Jasmine Crockett's Senate run against John Cornyn.
  • This tactic aimed to encourage stronger Democratic candidates to withdraw, making Crockett an easier opponent to defeat.
  • Crockett appeared with a rapper promoting her, a move criticized by the host as an attempt to appear 'badass'.
  • Donald Trump's insults towards Crockett were also mentioned as a potential misstep in political discourse.

  • Discussion focused on Donald Trump's remarks about Representative Ilhan Omar concerning her background and loyalty to the U.S.
  • Comments attributed to Omar in 2024 suggested prioritizing directives of Somalians in the U.S. to protect Somalia's interests.
  • The guest labeled Omar's alleged statements as 'treason' and questioned her loyalty to the United States.
  • The host criticized a perceived lack of gratitude towards the U.S. from some individuals and politicians.

  • Human biologist Gary Brecka, founder of 'The Ultimate Human,' provides health and wellness advice, with UFC CEO Dana White's transformation cited as an example.
  • Brecka emphasizes sleep, a whole food diet (avoiding processed foods), and non-negotiable exercise as critical for longevity, supported by Blue Zone studies.
  • Vitamin D3 is highlighted as the only nutrient humans can produce internally via sunlight and cholesterol, emphasizing its critical importance.
  • Approximately 85% of the world is deficient in Vitamin D3; daily supplementation of 5,000 IUs, especially with K2, is advised.

  • Genetic methylation tests identify nutrient deficiencies, such as the MTHFR gene mutation affecting 46% of the population, which impairs folic acid conversion.
  • Impaired folic acid conversion can lead to gut issues, anxiety due to reduced serotonin, and high homocysteine levels linked to high blood pressure.
  • Walking is considered a crucial yet overlooked exercise for moving the lymphatic system and aiding detoxification.
  • Walking outside with a weighted vest is proposed as the most efficient exercise, increasing benefits by 40-50%.
  • Osteoporosis requires 12 minerals for hydroxyapatite formation; mineral salts like Baja Gold are recommended over calcium supplements.
