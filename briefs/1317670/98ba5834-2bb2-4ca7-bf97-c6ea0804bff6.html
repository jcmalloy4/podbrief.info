Democrat Rep Charged with FEMA Fraud, Trump vs Dems on Hypothetical "Illegal Order": AM Update 11/21

Key Takeaways

  • A Florida congresswoman was indicted for allegedly stealing $5 million in FEMA funds.
  • Democrats and President Trump clashed over a video encouraging troops to defy orders.
  • Vice President J.D. Vance discussed economic policies and immigration enforcement.
  • A former congressional staffer was charged with staging a politically motivated attack.

Deep Dive

  • Florida Democratic Congresswoman Sheila Serfolis McCormick and co-defendants were indicted for allegedly stealing $5 million in FEMA disaster funds.
  • The funds were reportedly laundered into McCormick's 2021 campaign, charges she denies as a baseless sham.

  • Democrats shifted focus to President Trump's reaction to a video from six lawmakers urging troops to refuse hypothetical illegal orders.
  • Trump denounced the video as 'seditious behavior' from 'traitors,' prompting condemnation from Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer.
  • White House Press Secretary Caroline Levitt stated the President was responding to the 'dangerous video,' which House Speaker Mike Johnson also condemned.

  • Senator Alyssa Slotkin, a former CIA officer, posted a video on X addressing President Trump's threats.
  • She specifically referenced Trump's threats of arrest, trial, and death against her and other lawmakers who released the video.
  • Slotkin characterized President Trump's actions as 'beyond the pale.'

  • Vice President J.D. Vance discussed the September jobs report, noting better-than-expected gains of 119,000 jobs.
  • Vance attributed these gains to Trump's economic policies, leading to increased take-home pay.
  • He addressed the Catholic Church's stance on immigration, agreeing on humane treatment but asserting nations' rights to control borders, citing border security as humanitarian.
  • DHS reported over 527,000 deportations and 1.6 million self-deportations since the start of the second Trump administration.

  • Natalie Green, a 26-year-old former staffer for New Jersey Congressman Jeff VanDrew, faces charges of conspiracy and making false statements.
  • She is accused of staging a politically motivated attack against herself in Egg Harbor Township on July 23, 2025.
  • Green was reportedly found bound and gagged with 'Trump whore' written on her stomach and 'Van Drew is a racist' on her back.
  • Investigators linked the incident to Green's prior payment for a scarification body modification procedure hours before the alleged attack.
