Anti-Israel Attack in DC, Kid Cudi Testifies in Diddy Trial, Trump Admin vs. Harvard: AM Update 5/23

Overview

  • A targeted anti-Semitic attack at the Capitol Jewish Museum resulted in the murder of two Israeli embassy staffers, with the suspect allegedly chanting "Free Palestine" during the attack and now facing multiple serious charges including murder of foreign officials.
  • Sean "Diddy" Combs' criminal trial featured testimony from Kid Cudi about intimidation tactics, including his Porsche being set on fire, while Combs faces potential life imprisonment on charges of racketeering conspiracy, sex trafficking, and fraud.
  • An alarming HHS report reveals that over 40% of American children now suffer from at least one chronic health condition, driven by four key factors: poor diet (particularly ultra-processed foods), environmental chemical exposure, physical inactivity, and over-medicalization.
  • Harvard University faces severe sanctions from Homeland Security, including a prohibition on enrolling international students for 2025-2026, affecting approximately 6,800 students (27% of enrollment) amid accusations of non-compliance with reporting requirements and creating an unsafe campus environment.

Content

Anti-Semitic Attack at Capitol Jewish Museum

* A young couple, Yaron Lashinsky (30) and Sarah Milgram (26), were murdered in a targeted anti-Semitic attack at the Capitol Jewish Museum in Washington, D.C. * Suspect Elias Rodriguez allegedly shot the victims in the back, then advanced to shoot them again as they fell * Rodriguez was arrested at the scene, reportedly chanting "Free Palestine" and wearing a keffiye

Victims' Background: * Both were Israeli embassy staffers * Lashinsky was planning to propose to Milgram the following week * Milgram was Jewish, from Kansas, worked in public diplomacy * Lashinsky was born in Germany, moved to Israel as a teenager, worked as a research assistant

Legal Developments: * Suspect facing charges including: - Murder of foreign officials - Causing death through firearm use - Discharge of firearm during violent crime - Two counts of first-degree murder

Additional Context: * Suspect reportedly previously associated with Party for Socialism and Liberation (connection ended in 2017) * An online manifesto allegedly written by the suspect criticizes Israeli actions toward Palestinians * Attorney General Pam Bondi promised full law enforcement engagement and prosecution

Sean Diddy Combs Criminal Trial

Kid Cudi Testimony: * Kid Cudi testified about his past relationship with Cassie Ventura * Testified that his Porsche was set on fire in January 2012, which he believed was connected to Combs * Described meeting Combs after the car fire, comparing him to a "Marvel supervillain" * Stated he broke up with Ventura in December 2011 due to concerns about her and his own safety * Ventura previously testified about participating in "Freak-Offs" and keeping a secret phone

Combs Criminal Case Details: * Charged with racketeering conspiracy, sex trafficking, fraud, and related crimes * Pleaded not guilty * Faces potential life in prison if convicted

HHS Report on Childhood Chronic Disease

Key Findings: * Identified four key drivers of the epidemic: - Poor diet - Exposure to environmental chemicals - Lack of physical activity and chronic stress - Over-medicalization * Alarming statistics highlighted: - Over 40% of children have at least one chronic health condition - Childhood cancer rates increased by nearly 50% since 1970s - Childhood obesity rates increased from <5% to over 20% - Autism rates increased from 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 31 - 70% of children's daily calories come from processed products

Nutrition Concerns: * Ultra-processed foods cited as a leading culprit * Poor nutrition and excessive calorie intake are significant concerns

Environmental Factors: * Exposure to harmful chemicals, particularly pesticides, is increasing * Glyphosate (herbicide) linked to potential health risks including: - Reproductive disorders - Cancer - Liver inflammation - Metabolic damage

Physical Activity Issues: * Over 70% of children aged 6-17 do not meet federal physical activity guidelines * Declining activity levels attributed to: - Reduced in-school recess - Increased screen time

Medication Usage: * One in five children estimated to have taken a prescription drug in past 30 days * Report highlights "concerning trend" of over-prescribing medications * Potential conflicts of interest in medical research and practice

*Note: A full policy strategy addressing the report's findings is expected in August.*

Harvard International Student Policy

* Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem prohibits Harvard from enrolling international students for 2025-2026 * Approximately 6,800 international students (27% of enrollment) affected * DHS requests records related to: - Illegal/dangerous student activities - Protest footage * Harvard accused of: - Non-compliance with reporting requirements - Creating an unsafe campus environment - Promoting pro-Hamas sympathies - Implementing racist DEI policies
