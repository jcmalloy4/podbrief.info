The Megyn Kelly Show

Truth About the Biden Cover-Up, and Revelations in "Original Sin," with Jake Tapper, Alex Thompson, and Batya Ungar-Sargon | Ep. 1076

Overview

* The book "Original Sin" reveals an extensive cover-up of President Biden's cognitive decline by his inner circle, with over 200 Democratic insiders admitting they misrepresented his condition because they viewed Trump as an existential threat.

* Biden's mental deterioration was managed by a small, powerful group nicknamed the "Politburo" who strictly controlled his schedule, limited cabinet access, and employed elaborate production techniques to mask his condition in public appearances.

* By late 2023, the situation had deteriorated to where cabinet secretaries were kept away from Biden, with sources claiming "five people were running the country" while the president functioned as, at best, a "senior board member."

* The media largely avoided serious coverage of Biden's cognitive issues, dismissing concerns as conspiracy theories until his disastrous debate performance made his condition impossible to ignore.

* The authors argue this represents a systemic failure of democratic accountability, where political and media elites prioritized defeating Trump over transparency about the president's fitness for office.

Content: Biden's Cognitive Decline and Media Coverage

Book Introduction and Central Claims

* Megyn Kelly introduces an interview about "Original Sin," a book exploring President Biden's cognitive decline. * The book's title refers to Biden's decision to run for re-election, which Jake Tapper describes as the "original sin" and the subsequent cover-up of his condition. * Authors interviewed over 200 Democratic insiders post-election who justified misrepresenting Biden's condition by citing the "existential threat" of Donald Trump. * The book claims there were "two Bidens": - One functional and adequate - Another non-functioning (seen during debate and in certain clips) * Key revelations include cabinet secretaries suggesting Biden could not be relied upon for emergency responses by 2024. * A source claimed "five people were running the country" with Biden being, at best, a "senior board member."

Early Signs of Cognitive Decline

* Signs of mental deterioration were evident as early as 2015 after Biden's son Beau's death. * First Lady's office (particularly Anthony Bernal and Annie Tomasini) took significant control of Biden's schedule and interactions. * Jake Sullivan (National Security Advisor) was aware of Biden's memory issues years before the 2024 debate. * In December 2022, Biden struggled to recall names of staff members like Jake Sullivan and Kate Benningfield. * Multiple instances documented Biden: - Forgetting names of world leaders - Asking for a deceased congresswoman (Jackie Walorski) - Struggling to complete sentences - Having what a special counsel report described as a "poor memory"

Biden's Inner Circle ("Politburo")

* Four key advisors formed a tight circle around Biden: - Mike Donilon (top political advisor) - Steve Reschetti (top legislative advisor) - Ron Klain (former chief of staff) - Bruce Reed (longtime policy advisor) * Additional influential figures included: - Annie Tomasini (deputy chief of staff) - Anthony Bernal (Jill Biden's top aide, described as extremely powerful) * This small inner circle firmly decided Biden would run for re-election. * Internal discussions about the wisdom of a re-election bid were quickly shut down. * Bernal advocated for two full terms, while Donilon stated the decision was final.

White House Management and Access

* Cabinet secretaries were largely kept away from President Biden in 2023. * By late 2023/2024, many White House staff had not seen Biden in weeks or months. * Multiple sources described Biden as appearing "disoriented" and "out of it." * White House resident staff reportedly witnessed Biden's cognitive decline. * Resident staff were often kept at bay and sent home early. * Some staff felt they were being prevented from witnessing Biden's deterioration. * Jill Biden actively managed Biden's schedule and interactions, often helping him remember names. * Sources suggest Biden's mental state deteriorated after Hunter Biden's plea deal fell apart. * Cabinet secretaries reportedly described fall 2023 as a "weird period" where they were kept away from Biden.

Campaign Management Tactics

* Biden's campaign events and fundraisers were highly controlled. * Q&A sessions were pre-scripted with pre-written questions. * Donors were given pre-approved questions to ask. * No spontaneous or unscreened questions were allowed. * Campaign had to film multiple takes and use video editing techniques to create usable footage. * Biden struggled to deliver coherent statements without a teleprompter. * Campaign staff were defensive about questions regarding Biden's teleprompter use. * Some campaign videos were deemed unusable or required heavy editing. * Aides sometimes filmed in slow motion to mask walking difficulties. * Biden needed multiple daily "news of the day" cards with basic information.

Presidential Debate Performance

* Biden appeared to struggle significantly during the debate, with noticeable signs of cognitive decline. * Key observations included: - Shuffling walk - Weak, phlegmy voice - Losing train of thought mid-sentence - Mistakenly saying "we beat Medicare" instead of "we beat COVID" * The speaker considers this potentially the worst presidential debate in history since 1960. * Trump was relatively restrained during Biden's apparent difficulties. * Moderators/commentators were shocked by Biden's performance. * Immediately after the debate, Biden and Jill Biden seemed unaware of how poorly he had performed. * Hollywood donors (Rob Reiner, Jane Fonda, Doug Emhoff) were deeply distressed during the debate. * Donors and campaign staff expressed panic and belief that "it's over."

Media Coverage and Criticism

* Initially, discussing Biden's cognitive decline was considered risky. * Democratic officials were privately panicked about his condition. * Media and political allies attempted to: - Dismiss concerns as conspiracy theories - Claim clips were "cheap fakes" or taken out of context - Argue his mistakes were normal for someone with a demanding schedule * Jake Tapper acknowledges that his previous criticism of Laura Trump regarding Biden's cognitive state has "aged poorly." * Tapper admits he called Laura Trump months after their initial confrontation and acknowledged she was "right" about Biden's cognitive issues. * Tapper acknowledges potential shortcomings in CNN's coverage of Biden's apparent cognitive issues. * Multiple incidents were reportedly not adequately covered, including: - Jackie Walorski moment - Freeze-ups at Juneteenth celebration - Wandering off at G7 summit - Incident at George Clooney fundraiser

Health Concerns and Speculation

* Dr. O'Connor noted changes in Biden's gait due to: - Significant spinal arthritis - Mild post-fracture foot arthritis - Mild sensory peripheral neuropathy * Recent announcement of Biden's prostate cancer raised questions about the credibility of health disclosures. * Neurologists speculated about potential cognitive decline (Lewy body dementia, Parkinson's). * Congressman Mike Quigley observed Biden's physical frailty during State of the Union. * Some sources suggested Biden only has 4-6 good hours per day due to his age.

Broader Implications and Reactions

* The authors argue their book is not "mean," but accurate and based on serious medical observations. * Naomi Biden (president's granddaughter) called it "political fairy smut." * The authors have received significant blowback from both left and right. * Vice President Kamala Harris is quoted as strongly defending Biden during a meeting with Democratic governors, saying "We have to have the president's back" and that "it's our fucking democracy at stake." * The speakers suggest a systemic problem where media elites are more aligned with Democratic Party interests due to shared socioeconomic backgrounds. * The discussion implies a broader narrative about media bias and political manipulation of public perception.
