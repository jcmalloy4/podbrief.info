MAGA Fractures Over Epstein, Fetterman Gets 20 Stitches, Newsom Eyes 2028: AM Update 11/17

Key Takeaways

  • GOP fractured over Epstein files, leading to Trump cutting ties with Marjorie Taylor Greene.
  • Trump administration rolled back tariffs on numerous food items to address voter concerns over grocery costs.
  • Senator John Fetterman experienced a serious cardiac incident and fall, adding to his documented health issues.
  • California Governor Gavin Newsom is publicly positioning himself for a 2028 presidential campaign.

Deep Dive

  • The House is reportedly voting on a bill to release remaining Epstein files, a move opposed by Speaker Mike Johnson.
  • President Trump accused Republican Representatives Thomas Massey, Marjorie Taylor Greene, Nancy Mace, and Lauren Boebert of aiding Democrats.
  • Trump requested the Department of Justice investigate Epstein's relationships with Bill Clinton, Larry Summers, and Reid Hoffman.
  • Congressman Thomas Massey stated his belief that President Trump is attempting to protect wealthy friends and donors.

  • President Trump cut ties with Congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene, citing her constant complaints and stating he would endorse a primary challenger.
  • Greene affirmed her support for Trump's MAGA agenda but refused to compromise on the Epstein files issue.
  • A congresswoman reiterated support for victims of trafficking and sex trafficking, emphasizing the public's right to transparency in certain files.

  • President Trump announced the rollback of tariffs on dozens of food items, including coffee, tea, bananas, oranges, tomatoes, and beef.
  • This decision followed new trade agreements with Argentina, Ecuador, El Salvador, and Guatemala.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Besson indicated these agreements aim to lower consumer prices.
  • The White House is addressing bipartisan criticism regarding the cost of living, with tariff rollbacks timed to mitigate these concerns.

  • Senator John Fetterman posted a selfie showcasing 20 stitches after a fall, attributed to lightheadedness and a ventricular fibrillation flare-up.
  • This incident adds to his documented health issues, including a 2022 stroke and related complications.
  • Fetterman's new memoir, 'Unfettered,' details his struggles with post-stroke complications and criticism from the left.
  • Online reactions to Fetterman's cardiac episode and fall ranged from callous to sarcastic.

  • Politico published a lengthy profile on California Governor Gavin Newsom, with the headline declaring him the '2028 frontrunner'.
  • The profile allowed Newsom to promote his delayed memoir, 'Young Man in a Hurry,' and highlighted his social media presence.
  • Newsom has been active in redistricting efforts and international climate summits.
  • His political mentor, Willie Brown, expressed uncertainty about Newsom's core beliefs.
