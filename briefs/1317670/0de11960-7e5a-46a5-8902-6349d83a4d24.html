Mamdani's Election Lead, Trump Warns Nigeria To Protect Christians From Islamists: AM Update 11/4

Key Takeaways

  • New York City's mayoral election features socialist Zohran Mamdani leading polls, with Andrew Cuomo gaining a Trump endorsement.
  • Mamdani faces scrutiny from both supporters and critics regarding his socialist platform and potential policy shifts.
  • A Democracy Matters report reveals 60% of working-class voters perceive Democrats as 'woke, weak, and out of touch'.
  • President Trump has threatened to cut aid and deploy US forces to Nigeria over the 'mass slaughter' of Christians.
  • Trump's intervention reportedly secured critical cancer treatment for Dilbert creator Scott Adams after months of delays.

Deep Dive

  • Socialist frontrunner Zohran Mamdani leads in New York City mayoral polls, facing warnings from a prominent activist supporter about delivering on campaign promises.
  • Andrew Cuomo has gained support, including a late endorsement from Donald Trump; Polymarket predicts Mamdani's victory.
  • Elon Musk publicly endorsed Cuomo and urged voters against supporting third-party candidate Curtis Sliwa.

  • Investor Kevin O'Leary predicts Zohran Mamdani will moderate his socialist platform on housing and rent control if elected, citing financial pressures.
  • Activist Linda Sarsour warned Mamdani against shifting to the center, specifically regarding the retention of NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tish.
  • Sarsour expressed concerns that retaining Commissioner Tish would lead to continued resistance to dismantling specialized NYPD units.

  • A Democracy Matters report found 60% of working-class voters view Democrats as 'woke, weak, and out of touch', unable to define their platform beyond opposing Trump.
  • The report suggests Democratic messaging needs adjustment; candidates focusing on economic improvement for workers garnered 52% support, compared to 43% for 'fighting oligarchy'.
  • Immigration and transgender rights were identified as weaknesses, with one-third of independents more likely to support Democrats if they adopted a specific stance on transgender athletes.

  • President Trump stated on Truth Social that Nigeria is a 'country of particular concern' due to the 'mass slaughter' of Christians by Islamist militants.
  • Trump threatened to cut U.S. aid and potentially deploy U.S. forces, instructing the Department of War to prepare for action if killings continue.
  • Reports indicate over 52,000 Christians were killed in Nigeria between 2009 and 2023; an Observatory for Religious Freedom in Africa report noted 16,769 Christians and 6,235 Muslims killed between October 2019 and September 2023.
  • The Trump administration designated Nigeria a 'country of particular concern' in 2020, a designation reversed by the Biden administration in 2023.

  • Dilbert creator Scott Adams publicly requested former President Trump's intervention to expedite a critical cancer treatment from Kaiser of Northern California.
  • Trump responded 'on it' via Truth Social; Adams confirmed that the Trump administration acted quickly, with Kaiser agreeing to administer the FDA-approved drug the following day.
  • Adams noted that his files may have been misplaced, causing the initial months-long delay in securing his treatment.
