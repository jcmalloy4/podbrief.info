Trump's Historic Middle East Summit, National Guard and Chicago, Biden's Cancer: AM Update 10/13

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump brokered a historic Israel-Hamas ceasefire and hostage release, ending a two-year conflict.
  • A federal appeals court temporarily granted the Trump administration authority to federalize Illinois's National Guard, blocking Chicago deployment for now.
  • Former President Joe Biden is undergoing radiation and hormone therapy for stage 4 prostate cancer.
  • The House Oversight Committee is investigating alleged concealment of Biden's cognitive decline and autopen use.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump is in Israel and Egypt to mark a historic deal with Hamas, ending a two-year war and securing hostage releases.
  • The agreement includes deadlines for releasing living hostages and bodies, with some body recoveries potentially delayed.
  • Trump met with hostages and their families before addressing the Israeli Congress and co-chairing a Gaza Peace Summit in Egypt.
  • Democrats, including Hillary Clinton and Kamala Harris, acknowledge the historic nature of the deal and credit President Trump.

  • Following the hostage release, Israel is expected to free nearly 2,000 Palestinian prisoners.
  • The Wall Street Journal reported Egypt and Qatar pressured Hamas to accept the deal, threatening to withdraw support.
  • This agreement is the first phase of a broader 20-point plan for lasting peace in the region.
  • U.S. Central Command personnel in the region will oversee the ceasefire terms and humanitarian aid, clarifying U.S. ground presence is unnecessary due to offers from Muslim-majority countries.

  • A federal appeals court granted the Trump administration temporary authority to federalize the Illinois National Guard.
  • However, the court currently blocks the Guard's deployment into Chicago.
  • The White House stated President Trump will not ignore lawlessness, while Governor Pritzker deemed the federalization an unconstitutional overreach.

  • Justification for federal intervention in Chicago includes high crime rates, with 573 homicides recorded in 2024.
  • ICE conducted Operation Midway Blitz, leading to protests and arrests, prompting President Trump's criticism of local leaders.
  • Governor Pritzker asserted the President lacks constitutional power to overcome state authority, citing a Ninth Circuit case on National Guard deployment.
  • Vice President J.D. Vance discussed potentially invoking the Insurrection Act due to increased violent attacks against ICE agents.

  • Former President Joe Biden is undergoing radiation and hormone therapy for stage 4 prostate cancer, which has spread to bone.
  • He plans to attend two upcoming events despite the advanced diagnosis.
  • Medical experts expressed surprise at the rapid timeline from discovery to diagnosis, questioning how White House doctors missed the condition.
  • The House Oversight Committee is investigating whether Biden's cognitive decline was concealed while in office, with his White House physician reportedly pleading the fifth.
