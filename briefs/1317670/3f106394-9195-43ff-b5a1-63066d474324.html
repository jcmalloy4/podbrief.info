Anti-ICE Activist Playbook Revealed, MN Legal Fight, Tepe Murders Suspect In Court: AM Update 1/13

Key Takeaways

  • Homeland Security deployed hundreds of agents to Minneapolis, citing obstruction by far-left activist networks.
  • Illinois and Minnesota filed lawsuits against the Trump administration's immigration enforcement tactics.
  • The White House described Minnesota Democrats' lawsuit as a "pathetic stunt" prioritizing "illegal criminals."
  • Michael McKee appeared in Illinois court for the murders of Spencer and Monique Tepe, waiving extradition to Ohio.
  • Meta appointed former Trump official Dina Powell McCormick as President and Vice Chairman.

Deep Dive

  • Homeland Security Secretary Kirstjen Nielsen announced the deployment of hundreds of additional agents to Minneapolis.
  • The deployment aims to ensure safety for ICE and Border Patrol personnel and hold obstructors accountable.
  • Federal officials accuse far-left activist networks, including Minnesota Ice Watch, of deliberately obstructing law enforcement operations.
  • President Trump described Renee Goode, connected to Minnesota Ice Watch, as a "professional agitator" disrespecting law enforcement.
  • ICE focuses on arresting sex trafficking and child trafficking offenders, with a significant increase in arrests under President Trump.

  • Illinois, joined by Chicago, filed a lawsuit against the Trump administration's immigration enforcement tactics.
  • The lawsuit accuses federal agencies of attempting to coerce policy changes and violate the 10th Amendment.
  • Minnesota's Attorney General Keith Ellison called federal actions an 'invasion' and alleged constitutional violations.
  • Law enforcement officials warn that ICE Watch guidance suggesting physical removal of arrestees from officers is illegal and dangerous.

  • A White House spokesperson criticized the lawsuit filed by Minnesota Democrats.
  • The spokesperson called the legal challenge a "pathetic stunt."
  • The White House accused Minnesota Democrats of prioritizing "illegal criminals" over Americans.

  • Michael McKee, accused of the murders of Spencer and Monique Tepe, appeared in an Illinois court.
  • McKee waived extradition to Ohio and faces aggravated murder charges; a not guilty plea is anticipated.
  • The Tepes' bodies were discovered December 30th after Spencer's unexcused absence; their two young children were unharmed.
  • Surveillance footage led to McKee's arrest in Illinois, and charges were upgraded to aggravated murder with premeditation.
  • MK True Crime host Jonna Spilbore noted premeditation factors include travel and the alleged use of a weapon, plus McKee's divorce from Monique Tepe seven years prior.

  • Facebook parent company Meta appointed Dina Powell McCormick as President and Vice Chairman.
  • McCormick previously held leadership roles at Goldman Sachs and served on Meta's board.
  • She served as deputy national security advisor under President Trump and worked on the Abraham Accords.
  • Her husband is Republican Senator Dave McCormick; President Trump congratulated her via Truth Social.
  • The appointment signals a strategic shift for Meta towards global finance and AI expansion amid increasing political scrutiny.
