Content

Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson's Book "Original Sin" on Biden's Cognitive Decline

  • Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson discussed their new book "Original Sin" about President Biden's cognitive decline
  • The book is based on interviews with over 200 Democrat insiders and Biden White House staffers
  • Key revelations include efforts to conceal Biden's mental fitness and pressure on media outlets to limit scrutiny
  • Tapper made several notable admissions:
- Acknowledged legacy media's failure to adequately report on Biden's condition - Called the reporting process "humbling" - Apologized to Lara Trump for previously dismissing concerns about Biden's cognitive state - Described the June 2024 presidential debate as potentially "the worst debate in the history of presidential debates" - Called for "soul searching" among legacy media - Admitted conservative media was "right" about Biden's cognitive decline

Biden Administration Power Structure

  • Thompson revealed that four senior aides effectively ran the White House:
- Mike Donilon, Steve Reschetti, Ron Klain, and Bruce Reed - These aides were referred to as the "Politburo" or "Gray Hares" - Additional powerful aides included Annie Tomasini and Anthony Bernal

  • The book includes a controversial anecdote about George Clooney allegedly not being recognized by President Biden at a fundraiser
- Democratic fundraiser Rufus Gifford disputes the anecdote - Author Jake Tapper stands by the story, citing thorough fact-checking

ICE Detention Facility Incident

- McIver claims the charges are political and denies wrongdoing - House Democratic leadership condemned the charges as "extreme" and "morally bankrupt" - Former President Trump commented on the incident, supporting law enforcement
