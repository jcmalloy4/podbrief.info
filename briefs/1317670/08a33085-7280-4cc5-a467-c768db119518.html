The Megyn Kelly Show

Meghan Markle's $100K Grift, George Clooney's Controlling Wife, and Minnesota Somali Daycare Fraud

Key Takeaways

  • Millions in federal funds were allegedly misdirected in Minnesota daycare fraud.
  • New York City's FDNY commissioner appointment sparked criticism regarding qualifications and appearance.
  • Meghan Markle and Prince Harry faced publicist resignation, a foundation rebrand, and a $100,000 dinner charge.
  • The podcast concludes 2025 with a 'best and worst' review, including public figures and events.

Deep Dive

  • Millions of federal dollars were allegedly disbursed to Somali-owned daycare centers in Minnesota without children present.
  • Independent journalist Nick Shirley investigated by visiting centers and questioning staff.
  • The host criticized alleged involvement of figures like Tim Walls, advocating for investigations into billions lost.

  • Lillian Bonsignor was appointed as the new FDNY fire commissioner in New York City.
  • The host criticized Bonsignor's appointment due to her appearance and perceived lack of firefighting experience.
  • This appointment was linked to diversity initiatives by Zohran Mamdani, which the host implied could lead to negative consequences.

  • Kamala Harris offered relationship advice on a recent podcast, drawing host criticism.
  • The host questioned Harris's qualifications to give such advice, citing her political career and perceived lack of achievements.
  • Allegations against Harris's husband, Doug Emhoff, were also referenced.

  • Meghan Markle and Prince Harry's 11th publicist, Meredith Maine, resigned, reportedly due to Kardashian event drama and photo leaks.
  • Their foundation was renamed from Archwell Foundation to Archwell Philanthropies for its fifth anniversary.
  • The couple is reportedly charging donors $100,000 for dinner to raise charity funds via Charity Buzz.

  • Actor George Clooney has implemented a new rule against kissing women on screen.
  • The rule is attributed to the influence of his wife, Amal Clooney, and his age.
  • His new acting stance was contrasted with Leonardo DiCaprio's age-related dating preferences.

  • The 'worst of 2025' included Michelle Obama and Meghan Markle's 'wickless candle,' with Prince Harry also described metaphorically as a wickless candle.
  • The 'Wicked' press tour was criticized for perceived unhealthy habits of Ariana Grande and Cynthia Erivo.
  • The 'best of 2025' recognized the Coldplay Jumbotron couple, First Lady Melania Trump, and Catherine, Princess of Wales, for authenticity in her cancer journey.
  • The Camp Mystic flooding tragedy in Kerrville, Texas, was also listed for the unity and faith demonstrated by affected families and volunteers.
