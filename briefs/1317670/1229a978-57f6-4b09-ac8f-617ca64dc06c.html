The Megyn Kelly Show

Meghan Markle, Ryan Reynolds, Oprah and Others Compete with Fellow Offenders at The Nerve Awards!

Key Takeaways

  • The 2025 Nerve Awards commenced, satirizing cultural figures across various categories.
  • Awards were presented for 'Arsonist,' 'Best Worst Actress,' and 'Most Unlistenable Podcast,' among others.
  • A running narrative humorously tracked the alleged disappearance and recovery mission for Stedman Graham.
  • Recipients included Ryan Reynolds, Sarah Jessica Parker, Jack Schlossberg, and Lily Allen.
  • The ceremony featured commentary on celebrity actions and controversial public statements.

Deep Dive

  • The inaugural "Arsonist, career or otherwise" award was presented at 3:36.
  • Nominees included Jon Hamm, Megan Markle, and Jeremy Irons.
  • Ryan Reynolds was announced as the winner of this category.

  • Celebrity makeup artist Tim McKay joined to present the award for Best Worst Actress in a Hate Watch at 10:18.
  • Nominees included Sarah Jessica Parker, Jennifer Aniston, and Claire Danes.
  • Sarah Jessica Parker was announced as the winner at 13:25.

  • A segment titled 'Zero Bark 30' detailed a special ops mission to recover Stedman Graham's remains, announced at 18:20.
  • The mission involved an Apache helicopter landing at an unnamed Middle Eastern compound.
  • A dog named Teddy Van Halen assisted in the search at the compound, with Stedman Graham winning 'Celebrity appendage most in need of a SEAL team six extraction' at 18:34.

  • The award for 'Most Unlistenable Podcast of 2025' was presented at 23:28.
  • Nominees included Michelle Obama, Jamie Kern Lima, and Chelsea Clinton.
  • Jamie Kern Lima was announced as the winner of the award.

  • The 'Dwarf Minds Award' was presented at 30:05.
  • Nominees included Jack Schlossberg for a letter to President Putin, Lori Laughlin, and George Clooney for discussions on Darfur.
  • Jack Schlossberg was named the winner of the award.

  • The award for "abortion is my favorite pastime" was introduced at 35:02, with nominees revealed at 36:18.
  • Nominees included Cynthia Nixon for her 'Make abortion great again' hat, Lily Allen, and Chelsea Handler.
  • Lily Allen was announced as the winner for celebrating her abortions.

  • The "repeat offender quote of the year" nominees included Gail King, Meghan Markle, and Whoopi Goldberg, presented at 39:25.
  • Gail King was announced as the winner for her comments about space travel.
  • The host suggested King's comments could potentially lead to her firing from CBS.

  • The "Teddy Award" was presented at 46:06.
  • Teddy was recognized for his efforts in searching for Stedman Graham.
  • He was named "the hero of the Nerve" for the year.
