The Megyn Kelly Show

National Guard to Memphis and Portland, SCOTUS Takes Cook Case, Vance on Memes: AM Update 10/2

Key Takeaways

  • Federal officials launched a crime crackdown in Memphis, resulting in 53 arrests and 20 firearms seized.
  • National Guard troops are deploying to Portland to protect ICE facilities, despite Oregon officials' objections.
  • Oklahoma authorities arrested 130 commercial drivers for lacking legal status or valid licenses.
  • VP J.D. Vance defended President Trump's "sombrero memes" amidst a government shutdown debate.

Deep Dive

  • Attorney General Pam Bondi, Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, and advisor Stephen Miller initiated the crackdown.
  • The operation aims to reduce violent crime in Memphis, Tennessee.
  • Governor Bill Lee announced 53 arrests and 20 firearms seized within 48 hours.

  • President Trump ordered National Guard deployment to Portland to protect ICE facilities, citing ongoing unrest.
  • National Guard troops are training for crowd control ahead of deployment, expected over the weekend.
  • Governor Tina Kotek and Oregon officials object to the federal action, filing a lawsuit for a temporary restraining order.
  • A hearing on Oregon's lawsuit to halt the National Guard deployment is scheduled for Friday.

  • A joint operation by Oklahoma Highway Patrol and ICE arrested 130 alleged illegal alien semi-truck drivers at a border weigh station.
  • Governor Kevin Stitt highlighted that nearly 25% of the arrested commercial drivers lacked legal status or valid licenses.
  • Authorities are cracking down on commercial driver's licenses issued by sanctuary states due to identification and expired license issues.
  • This follows a fatal Florida accident involving an immigrant truck driver who struggled with English and improperly obtained a commercial license.

  • Vice President J.D. Vance addressed the government shutdown, stating President Trump's "sombrero memes" of House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries would stop if Democrats agreed to reopen the government.
  • Vance commented on the memes, calling them funny and a way to highlight Democratic positions.
  • Vance questioned the accusation of racism made by House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries, suggesting the image was AI-generated with a mustache.
  • House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries responded on X, stating Democrats would not "surrender to the Republican effort to gut health care because of a sombrero meme."
