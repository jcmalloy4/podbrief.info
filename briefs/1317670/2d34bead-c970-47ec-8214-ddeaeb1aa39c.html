The Megyn Kelly Show

Mark Halperin on Biden Cover-Up, Link Lauren on Mulvaney and Melania, Maureen Callahan on Harry and Meghan: MK Media Highlights

Overview

* The podcast reveals significant concerns about President Biden's cognitive decline, with Megyn Kelly sharing personal observations dating back to 2017 and discussing a new book that details how Democratic officials actively concealed his condition from the public.

* Kelly delivers a scathing critique of media accountability, arguing that journalists weren't merely hindered in covering Biden's mental state but actively participated in a cover-up to avoid helping Trump, calling for transparency from press outlets.

* The show examines cultural tensions in fashion and entertainment, highlighting perceived bias against conservative women like Melania Trump and criticizing transgender influencer Dylan Mulvaney receiving Melania's former Versace dress.

* Kelly provides candid commentary on celebrity dynamics, including Prince Harry appearing "emasculated" in a BBC interview, Meghan Markle's calculated social media responses, and behind-the-scenes revelations about Taylor Swift's early career and family relationships.

Content

Podcast Introduction and MK Media Network

  • Megyn Kelly introduces her podcast and highlights three new shows in the MK Media network
  • Mentions shows by Mark Halperin, Link Lauren, and Maureen Callahan
  • Expresses gratitude to her audience for supporting the new shows

Joe Biden's Cognitive Decline Discussion

  • Kelly discusses a new book "Original Sin" by Alex Thompson and Jake Tapper about Biden's mental state
  • Shares her personal observation of Biden's cognitive issues dating back to 2017
  • Describes witnessing Biden struggling during a book tour event, appearing glassy-eyed, having trouble following conversations, and speaking haltingly
  • Criticizes claims that Biden's mental decline was only recently apparent

Book Revelations About Biden

  • The book reveals many Democrats were unhappy with Biden continuing to run
  • Internal discussions occurred about Biden potentially needing a wheelchair
  • The Biden campaign limited public appearances and press interactions
  • Senior Biden officials actively worked to cover up the President's cognitive decline
  • Authors interviewed over 200 people, mostly Democrats, who were only candid about Biden's condition after the 2024 election

Media Coverage Criticism

  • Kelly criticizes the press for not sufficiently scrutinizing Biden's condition
  • Notes that reporters allegedly did not adequately hold Biden accountable for signs of mental decline
  • Highlights that the public widely perceived Biden's diminished mental capabilities
  • Points out Biden's limited interviews and press conferences
  • Discusses how the White House consistently deflected or "gaslighted" concerns about Biden's mental state

Jake Tapper's Role and Contradictions

  • References a 2022 interview between Jake Tapper and Biden where Tapper raises concerns about Biden's age
  • Contrasts this with a 2020 interview between Tapper and Laura Trump where:
- Laura Trump suggested Biden was experiencing cognitive decline - Tapper became indignant and accused her of potentially mocking Biden's stutter - Tapper cut off the interview abruptly
  • Notes that Tapper has now written a book acknowledging White House cover-ups regarding Biden's condition
  • Suggests Tapper is now discussing how the White House press corps may have been complicit in hiding information

White House Response to Concerns

  • Biden's press secretary claimed he works tirelessly and "runs circles around" younger staff
  • Kelly suggests these claims lack substantive evidence
  • Cites an incident where Biden appeared to speak about a deceased congresswoman as if she were alive
  • Notes that Biden spokespeople deflect concerns by suggesting voters don't care about minor details

Media Accountability Discussion

  • Kelly argues the press was not merely "stymied" but actively participated in a cover-up
  • States that reporters were threatened with loss of access if they covered unfavorable stories
  • Mentions the New York Post losing access after covering the Hunter Biden story
  • Suggests many in the media avoided reporting on Biden's decline to prevent appearing to help Trump win
  • Calls for the media to use this moment to be transparent and "come clean"

Dylan Mulvaney and Melania Trump's Dress

  • Discusses transgender influencer Dylan Mulvaney receiving a Versace dress previously worn by Melania Trump
  • Notes the dress was sent directly by Donatella Versace herself
  • Kelly criticizes Mulvaney, claiming he mocks women through his content and doesn't understand true womanhood
  • References the Bud Light controversy, where Kelly:
- Partially defended Mulvaney, arguing he was just doing a brand deal - Criticized Bud Light's marketing decision - Noted the brand's stock declined after the partnership
  • Critiques Donatella Versace for choosing to send a dress to Mulvaney instead of other potential recipients

Fashion Media Bias Discussion

  • Kelly criticizes Vogue and fashion media for perceived bias against conservative women, using Melania Trump as a primary example
  • Notes Melania Trump was never featured on Vogue's cover as First Lady, despite previously being featured
  • Contrasts this with Jill Biden and Michelle Obama appearing on Vogue's cover multiple times
  • References Trump's 2020 election performance (77 million votes)
  • Suggests media is alienating approximately half the country by dismissing conservative women

BRICS Summit Mention

  • Notes an upcoming global BRICS nations summit planned for July in Rio de Janeiro
  • The summit aims to potentially challenge the U.S. dollar's global currency status
  • Referred to as the "Rio Reset"
  • Discusses potential implications for U.S. dollar value and savings

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle Segment

  • Discusses Prince Harry's challenging BBC interview
  • Notes he lost a court case regarding royal security
  • Describes Harry as appearing "emasculated" during the interview
  • Mentions Meghan responded by posting Instagram photos from a Beyonce concert

Analysis of Meghan's Instagram Post

  • Kelly critiques several details:
- Harry's perceived subservient behavior towards Meghan - The hat Harry wore with children's names inscribed inside - Their seating in a mid-section of the concert, not a VIP area
  • Suggests their public interactions appear staged and performative
  • References a recent interview where Meghan discussed missing her children

Harry in London Incident

  • Discusses Harry being found wandering alone in a London neighborhood
  • Notes Harry's camp claims he was looking for a friend's last known address
  • Kelly suggests alternative theories about Harry's behavior, including potential partying or being disoriented
  • Implies Harry has a limited support network and cannot easily contact family or friends

Real Housewives of New York Discussion

  • Notes Bravo canceled the rebooted version of the show (referred to as "Roni")
  • Kelly criticizes the recent reboot, which:
- Fired the original cast - Attempted to introduce diversity post-George Floyd - Lacked the original cast's established social dynamics

Tribute to Countess Luann de Lesseps

  • Describes her as a legendary cast member from the original show
  • Notes she was originally a nurse who married a minor royal
  • Known for her "Countess" persona and public persona
  • Experienced public divorce and financial challenges
  • Had a memorable drunk moment during vacation, falling in bushes "like a supermodel"
  • Married Tom, who was known for cheating, leading to:
- Drinking to cope - Getting arrested - Eventually reinventing herself as a cabaret performer

Other Entertainment News

  • Mentions Kelly Clarkson ending her talk show
  • Discusses speculation about Hoda Kotb potentially taking over
  • Expresses skepticism about Hoda's viability for the role, citing her age and conversational skills

Taylor Swift/Blake Lively Legal Drama

  • Notes Taylor Swift is being subpoenaed in a lawsuit involving Blake Lively and Justin Baldoni
  • Swift claims to feel "betrayed" and "exploited" by Blake Lively
  • Mentions Swift was deliberately photographed at a Mother's Day lunch with Travis Kelce

Scott Swift Email Discussion

  • Discusses an email written by Scott Swift (Taylor's father) when Taylor was around 15 years old
  • Scott emphasizes his critical role in Taylor's early career development:
- Spent significant time promoting her music - Funded her trips and appearances - Prepared promotional materials
  • Reveals family dynamics insights:
- Scott feeling underappreciated despite substantial contributions - Suggestions that Taylor's brother Austin was marginalized - Implied tension with ex-wife Andrea about Taylor's career focus
  • Notes the email is from around 20 years ago and reflects the early stages of Taylor Swift's music career
