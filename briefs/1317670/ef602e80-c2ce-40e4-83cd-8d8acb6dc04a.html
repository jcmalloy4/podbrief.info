FBI's Shocking NBA Gambling Bust, Trump's Cartel Roundtable, Adams Endorses Cuomo: AM Update 10/24

Key Takeaways

  • FBI arrested over 30 individuals, including NBA figures, in mafia-linked gambling indictments.
  • President Trump designated cartels as "ISIS of the Western Hemisphere," escalating counter-terrorism efforts.
  • Trump administration implemented significant cartel crackdowns, facing criticism over drug boat strikes.
  • New York Mayor Eric Adams endorsed former Governor Andrew Cuomo for mayor, reshaping the race.
  • Federal prosecutors indicted Decarlos Brown Jr. for the killing of Ukrainian refugee Iryna Zarutska.

Deep Dive

  • FBI arrested over 30 individuals, including NBA coach Chauncey Billups and guard Terry Rozier.
  • An indictment details a sports betting scheme utilizing insider information on NBA games.
  • Charges include wire fraud and money laundering related to the scheme.

  • A second indictment targeted a poker scheme that defrauded victims of millions of dollars.
  • The scheme involved former professional athletes and organized crime families.
  • This was part of a nationwide series of indictments exposing mafia-linked gambling operations.

  • President Trump declared cartels the "ISIS of the Western Hemisphere" at a White House roundtable.
  • The administration announced a new executive order designating cartels and gangs as foreign terrorist organizations.
  • This policy aims to combat drug cartels using counter-terrorism authorities.

  • Crackdowns resulted in over 3,200 arrests and 91 tons of drugs seized, dismantling criminal networks.
  • Operations included the arrest of 'La Diabla,' a trafficker involved in illegal C-sections and organ harvesting from babies.
  • The White House continued striking suspected drug boats, with Secretary of War Pete Hagseth reporting 3 deaths from the ninth strike.
  • Critics questioned White House authority and due process, with Colombian President Gustavo Petro accusing the U.S. of targeting fishermen.

  • New York Mayor Eric Adams endorsed former Governor Andrew Cuomo for mayor, withdrawing his own candidacy.
  • Adams stated his decision was to unite the city against a "socialist and communist agenda."
  • A recent poll indicates Zohran Mamdani leads Andrew Cuomo by 14 points, with Republican Curtis Sleewa polling at 19%.
