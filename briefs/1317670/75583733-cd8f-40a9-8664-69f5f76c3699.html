James Comey Indicted, Trump Paves Way for American TikTok Deal, NJ Gov Race Tightens: AM Update 9/26

Key Takeaways

  • Former FBI Director James Comey indicted on charges related to the Trump-Russia probe.
  • President Trump signed an executive order for a U.S.-based version of TikTok.
  • U.S. second-quarter GDP surged to 3.8%, the best quarter since Q3 2023.
  • The Dallas ICE facility gunman planned his attack for months with an anti-ICE motive.
  • New Jersey's gubernatorial race is a dead heat, according to a new Emerson College poll.

Deep Dive

  • Former FBI Director James Comey has been indicted on charges of making false statements and obstruction related to the Trump-Russia probe.
  • Prosecutors were reportedly facing a statute of limitations deadline to bring these charges.
  • President Trump and others reacted to the indictment following its announcement.

  • President Trump signed an executive order to allow American investors to operate a U.S.-based version of TikTok, continuing a deal considered during his administration.
  • The order aims to comply with a ban signed under the Biden administration, requiring ByteDance to divest significant U.S. assets.
  • TikTok's algorithm must be spun off into a U.S.-based joint venture, with ByteDance owning less than 20%.
  • Oracle will oversee TikTok data and privacy, and ownership is expected to include companies linked to right-wing billionaires, raising some concerns.

  • Revised government data shows second-quarter GDP surged to a 3.8% rate, significantly exceeding initial reports.
  • This marks the best quarter for GDP growth since Q3 2023, representing robust economic expansion.
  • The growth is primarily attributed to increased consumer spending within the U.S. economy.
  • This economic performance is presented as evidence that President Trump's policies can foster strong economic growth.

  • Authorities revealed that the Dallas ICE facility gunman, Joshua Jan, planned his attack for months with the explicit goal of terrorizing ICE agents.
  • Jan's notes indicated a clear anti-ICE motive, a desire to maximize lethality, and an intent to interfere with ICE's work, which he considered human trafficking.
  • The attack, involving Jan firing at the facility from the roof, resulted in one detainee death and two injured detainees, though no ICE agents were harmed.
  • This incident marks the fourth attack on an ICE or CBP facility this year.

  • An Emerson College poll shows the New Jersey governor's race is tied between Democrat Mikey Sherrill and Republican Jack Chitterelli, with 11% undecided.
  • Polling data indicates Chitterelli is gaining momentum, while Sherrill attempts to link him to Governor Murphy, and Chitterelli connects Sherrill to Murphy's energy policies.
  • President Trump has endorsed Chitterelli, calling Sherrill 'incompetent', intensifying the political stakes.
  • This is Chitterelli's third gubernatorial run, following a close 2021 race, with another debate scheduled for next week.
  • Sherrill's campaign highlights her Navy service, but reports surfaced regarding a cheating scandal during her graduation, which she addressed.
