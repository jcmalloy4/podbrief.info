The Megyn Kelly Show

The Mission to Capture and Arrest Maduro, Somali Fraud Fallout, Mayor Mamdani Begins: AM Update 1/5

Key Takeaways

  • U.S. forces apprehended Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro, sparking international condemnation and domestic debate over executive authority.
  • An alleged $110 million fraud in Minnesota's Somali-run childcare and healthcare operations has triggered a federal payment freeze and FBI investigation.
  • New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani immediately reversed his predecessor's key policies after taking office, emphasizing collectivism.

Deep Dive

  • U.S. forces, including Army Delta Force, captured Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro and his wife from a fortified Caracas compound.
  • They were transported to Brooklyn to face federal charges, including narco-terrorism and cocaine importation conspiracy.
  • The operation, authorized by President Trump, was described as a display of American military power executed without U.S. casualties.
  • Chairman of the Joint Chiefs Dan Raisin Kane detailed complex planning involving over 150 aircraft from 20 bases.

  • Russia, Iran, and China condemned the U.S. action as aggression and a violation of Venezuelan sovereignty.
  • Maduro faces federal charges in New York including narco-terrorism, conspiracy, and weapons possession, with potential life imprisonment.
  • Venezuela's Vice President Delcy Rodriguez was appointed acting president with military backing.
  • President Trump indicated U.S. involvement in ensuring leadership changes align with American interests.

  • Secretary of State Rubio stated the U.S. would not govern Venezuela but would press for changes via an oil blockade.
  • The U.S. aims to prevent adversaries from controlling Venezuelan oil and ensure proceeds benefit the Venezuelan people.
  • Critics, including Congressman Thomas Massey, argued the operation lacked congressional approval and was motivated by oil wealth.
  • Rubio defended it as a law enforcement action by FBI agents, not an invasion, not requiring congressional authorization.

  • Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer anticipates a Senate vote this week on a war powers resolution.
  • The resolution aims to prevent further U.S. military action without specific congressional approval.
  • This legislative effort could face constitutional challenges regarding executive branch authority.

  • Independent journalist Nick Shirley's viral investigation alleges up to $110 million in fraud within Somali-run childcare and healthcare operations in Minnesota.
  • Shirley's video, viewed over 137 million times, documented empty facilities and blocked windows.
  • The Department of Health and Human Services has frozen childcare payments to Minnesota following these allegations.
  • FBI Director Cash Patel stated the ongoing investigation is like a "large iceberg."

  • Zohran Mamdani was sworn in as New York City Mayor, with a public oath administered by Bernie Sanders.
  • His inaugural address emphasized 'collectivism' and using government power to improve citizens' lives.
  • Mamdani immediately signed an executive order revoking all prior orders by predecessor Eric Adams, including those concerning Israel.
  • He also established new task forces to address housing affordability and protect tenants.
