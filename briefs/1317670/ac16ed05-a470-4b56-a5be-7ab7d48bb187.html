The Megyn Kelly Show

Shock New Reporting About Biden and Wheelchair, and Disturbing Diddy Trial Details, with Buck Sexton, Mark Eiglarsh, and Mark Geragos | Ep. 1071

Overview

  • Media credibility crisis exposed through revelations about Biden's cognitive decline, with allegations that journalists and White House officials concealed his deteriorating condition since 2021, raising serious questions about who was actually governing during his presidency.
  • The Trump administration's initiative to accept white South African refugees highlights contentious immigration policy debates, with supporters arguing these refugees face legitimate persecution while critics question preferential treatment based on race and skills.
  • In the "Kelly's Court" segment, Sean "Diddy" Combs' defense strategy focuses on separating moral judgments from criminal charges, while prosecutors build a case around witness testimony alleging organized prostitution and abuse.
  • The Menendez brothers case demonstrates evolving perspectives on justice and rehabilitation, with all living victims now supporting their release after 35 years in prison through potential resentencing, clemency, or habeas corpus petition.
  • New evidence in the Idaho college murder case reveals calculated predatory behavior by suspect Brian Kohlberger, including surveillance of victims, Ted Bundy-inspired research, and strategic planning that strengthens the prosecution's death penalty case.

Content

Biden's Cognitive Decline and Media Coverage

  • The Megyn Kelly Show discusses revelations from Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson's book "Original Sin" about President Biden's physical and cognitive deterioration in 2023.
  • Key revelations about Biden's condition:
- Aides were concerned about his physical deterioration and discussed potential wheelchair use if re-elected - They deemed revealing this information politically damaging during the campaign - Biden's team "shielded him from his own staff" according to a senior aide - David Plouffe (Obama campaign strategist) criticized Biden for leaving it too late to drop out of the 2024 election - President Obama reportedly visited the White House in 2023 warning Biden to ensure he could win

  • Democratic donors expressed specific concerns about Biden's:
- Diminishing energy - Cognitive skills - Ability to deliver speeches

  • Kelly expresses outrage about the situation, questioning why these details weren't known earlier and suggesting cognitive decline was obvious since 2021.
  • The discussion criticizes media coverage of Biden's condition:
- Media personalities like Joe Scarborough previously claimed Biden was at his "sharpest" version - Journalists are now accused of trying to "rewrite history" and shift blame - Some media outlets allegedly "sold their souls to defeat Trump" - CNN specifically called out for losing credibility

  • The speakers argue that:
- Biden has shown signs of dementia for years - Media and White House knew about his condition but did not address it - They effectively had a "puppet president" for four years - Right-wing commentators were consistently pointing out Biden's issues while mainstream media denied them

  • Calls for accountability include:
- A federal investigation into Biden's competence while in office - Questions about who was actually making decisions during his presidency - Criticism of Jill Biden for not removing her husband from the presidential race - A "reckoning" for media figures who allegedly misled audiences

South African Refugees and Immigration Policy

  • The discussion shifts to Trump's initiative to bring white Afrikaners from South Africa to the US as refugees:
- Approximately 59 South African refugees have arrived - They are entering legally at presidential invitation - Described as skilled, English-speaking, and grateful to America

  • Context of the South African situation:
- Explicit racial quotas disadvantaging white population (approximately 7%) - State-sponsored disenfranchisement of white citizens - Concerns about potential land expropriation similar to Zimbabwe's model - Ongoing economic challenges including corruption and rolling blackouts

  • The speakers critique Ashley Allison (former Biden-Harris campaign official) for her comments:
- She stated South Africa ended apartheid 35+ years ago and reformed its constitution - Suggested Afrikaners should "go back" to their native lands like Germany or Holland - The speakers counter that Afrikaners have been in South Africa for 300 years

  • Broader immigration discussion:
- Criticism of perceived double standards in immigration policy favoring non-white immigrants - UK illegal immigration reached 906,000 from June 2022 to June 2023, compared to 100,000 in 2010 - Concerns about immigration from Pakistan, Afghanistan, Iran, Bangladesh, and Syria - Argument that mass immigration has been "an abject disaster" for Western countries

  • The speakers highlight Keir Starmer (UK Labour Party) as an example of political flip-flopping:
- Previously held pro-immigration, open borders stance - Recently adopted more restrictive immigration rhetoric

Sean "Diddy" Combs Case

  • Defense strategy focuses on:
- Separating personal moral judgments from criminal charges - Emphasizing that being "deplorable" or having an unconventional lifestyle is not criminal - Humanizing Combs (wearing gray sweater, reading Bible in court) - Arguing that personal sexual choices or anger are not criminal racketeering

  • Case details:
- Involves allegations from two ex-girlfriends and one employee - Prosecution team consists of six white women lawyers - Charges include racketeering, sex trafficking, and transportation for prostitution - Judge is reportedly monitoring podcast discussions about the case

  • Witness testimony from Daniel Phillip (male dancer):
- Claims he was paid to have sex with Cassie - Described multiple sexual encounters at Gramercy Park Hotel in 2013-2014 - Alleged scenarios involving Sean Combs watching/directing sexual acts - Testimony includes allegations of physical abuse of Cassie

  • Legal debate centers on:
- Whether paying for "time and experience" rather than directly for sexual services constitutes prostitution - The applicability of pimping charges in this context - The complexity of prosecuting cases where victims maintained long-term relationships with alleged abusers

Menendez Brothers Case

  • Three potential paths to release are being considered:
- Resentencing (currently in progress) - Clemency from Governor Newsom - Habeas corpus petition based on new sexual abuse evidence

  • Key developments:
- New DA Nathan Hochman is personally invested in the case - Hochman previously represented Jose Menendez's ex-business partner - Jose Menendez's sister Joan supports the brothers' release and has forgiven them - All living victims and relatives reportedly support release - The legal focus is on rehabilitation over 35 years, not just the original crime

Idaho College Student Murders

  • New details about Brian Kohlberger's alleged actions:
- Cased the house multiple times before the murders - Entered through a second-floor sliding door - Maddie Mogan was the primary target, not Kaylee Gonsalves - Encountered a surviving roommate who heard him say "don't worry, you'll be okay"

  • Evidence against Kohlberger includes:
- DNA on a K-bar knife sheath left at the scene - Google searches about Ted Bundy and sociopathic traits - Photos mimicking Ted Bundy's appearance - Searching for female student photos from local universities - Purchasing another K-bar knife online two days after the murders - Calling his father for 30-60 minutes the morning after the murders

  • Legal proceedings:
- No indications of a plea deal currently - Prosecution appears to be seeking the death penalty - Defense may attempt to argue mental competence issues - Evidence suggests premeditated, calculated crime with multiple aggravating factors
