Trump Torches UN In Front of World Leaders, Routh Guilty, Kamala Back on The View: AM Update 9/24

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump criticized the UN's role in mass migration and green energy, also noting security incidents.
  • Ryan Routh was found guilty on all counts for his assassination attempt against Donald Trump.
  • A government shutdown looms as President Trump and Democratic leaders remain deadlocked on spending.
  • Kamala Harris's approval ratings continue to decline despite her defense of past campaign missteps.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump criticized the UN's handling of global issues, specifically mass migration, which he claimed the organization funds and facilitates.
  • He stated the UN budgeted $372 million in 2024 to assist migrants entering the U.S., calling it an invasion and a threat to Western countries.
  • Trump also condemned the green energy agenda as the 'greatest con job ever perpetrated on the world,' citing inefficiencies and costs of renewable sources like windmills.

  • President Trump suggested Ukraine could 'win all of Ukraine back' and advised them to act while Russia is economically vulnerable, referring to Russia as a 'paper tiger.'
  • He also proposed NATO countries should shoot down Russian military aircraft if they intrude on airspace, with U.S. support contingent on specific situations.
  • A Gallup poll indicates 69% of Republicans favor a quick end to the war, even if Ukraine cedes territory, with MAGA-aligned Republicans showing the least enthusiasm for further aid.

  • Ryan Routh was found guilty on all five charges related to the attempted assassination of then-candidate Trump in Florida.
  • As the verdict was read, Routh appeared to attempt self-harm by trying to stab himself with a pen.
  • He now faces the possibility of life in prison for his actions.

  • A government shutdown looms as President Trump canceled a meeting with Democratic leaders ahead of the October 1 deadline.
  • Democrats are reportedly seeking a meeting to discuss healthcare spending, while Trump critiques their demands as 'unserious and ridiculous.'
  • Trump cited concerns over excessive spending on social programs and benefits for undocumented immigrants in their proposals.
  • Calls are growing for Democrats to force a shutdown to gain political leverage, despite warnings of internal criticism.

  • Vice President Kamala Harris appeared on 'The View' to discuss her 2024 campaign, insisting her infamous answer was not a turning point.
  • However, CNN's Harry Enten reported polling data indicating a significant drop in her net favorable ratings.
  • The decline in popularity was particularly noted among independent voters since the interview.
