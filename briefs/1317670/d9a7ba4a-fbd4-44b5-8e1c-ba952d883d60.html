DOJ Puts Don Lemon On Notice, 2028 Dem Contenders Go Quiet, Arctic Blast Incoming: AM Update 1/20

Key Takeaways

  • DOJ is investigating Don Lemon for his involvement in disrupting a Minnesota church service, with potential federal charges.
  • Potential 2028 Democratic candidates are struggling to address questions on gender and biology amid shifting public opinion.
  • A delayed 60 Minutes report on El Salvador's CECOT prison generated controversy regarding its content and initial cancellation.
  • An Arctic blast is projected to bring severe cold, snow, and hazardous travel conditions across much of the United States.

Deep Dive

  • Don Lemon is under investigation by DOJ officials for his involvement in disrupting a Christian church service in St. Paul, Minnesota.
  • Potential federal charges include violations of the FACE Act and the Ku Klux Klan Act.
  • Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights Harmeet Dylan confirmed the investigation into potential conspiracy to violate civil rights.

  • Assistant Attorney General Harmeet Dylan stated the Ku Klux Klan Act, a post-Civil War civil rights statute, could elevate charges from a misdemeanor to a felony.
  • Don Lemon claimed he was practicing journalism, but former Deputy AG Todd Blanche countered that freedom of the press does not extend to trespassing or embedding with groups storming a church.
  • Minnesota leadership, including Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey and Governor Tim Walz, has remained silent regarding the disruption of a Christian worship service.

  • Potential 2028 Democratic candidates reportedly struggle to answer basic questions on gender and biology.
  • A New York Times poll indicates 79% of Americans, including 67% of Democrats, believe male athletes should not compete in women's sports.
  • Axios surveyed 20 potential contenders; eight, including Kamala Harris and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, declined to answer questions on transgender participation in sports and medical interventions for minors.

  • CBS News aired a delayed 60 Minutes report on El Salvador's CECOT prison, focusing on deported individuals from the Trump administration.
  • The report's initial cancellation by CBS editor-in-chief Barry Weiss led to accusations of censorship from newsroom staff.
  • Discussion covered accusations against anchor Scott Pelley and controversial editorial decisions regarding the framing of two deported individuals.

  • An Arctic blast is projected to bring severe cold, snow, and ice to much of the United States starting Friday.
  • Dangerous travel conditions and record low temperatures are anticipated across multiple regions.
  • Affected areas include the northern plains, Midwest, and parts of the South and Northeast.
