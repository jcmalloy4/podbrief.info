The Megyn Kelly Show

Rob Reiner Son's Alarming Court Appearance, “Burn Cage” Found in D4vd’s Home, and Hope for JonBenét Case

Key Takeaways

  • Nick Reiner faces double murder charges with special circumstances, potentially leading to a death penalty.
  • Reiner's legal defense is reportedly funded by his deceased parents' $200 million estate.
  • Singer D4vd is under investigation after a 14-year-old victim's remains were found with an industrial incinerator.
  • New evidence and DNA technology offer renewed hope in the 29-year-old JonBenét Ramsey cold case.

Deep Dive

  • Nick Reiner faces charges for the double murder of his parents, Rob and Michele Reiner, including 'special circumstances' that could lead to the death penalty in California.
  • Defense lawyer Alan Jackson successfully delayed Reiner's arraignment, citing complex issues and requesting public and media restraint.
  • Legal analysts speculated the delay might relate to the client's mental health and ability to assist in his defense.

  • Potential defense strategies include drug-induced psychosis, which might temporarily impair a defendant's ability to assist in their defense.
  • Defense attorneys may strategically delay arraignment in California to gain access to more information from the prosecution's case file.
  • Prosecutors' perspective suggests Reiner's alleged evasive actions after the murders weaken an insanity defense, making competency a more complex issue requiring expert evaluation.

  • Reports indicate Nick Reiner's legal fees are being paid by the $200 million estate of his deceased parents.
  • Family members reportedly intend for Reiner to potentially be placed in a mental institution rather than prison.
  • While common for victims' families to fund a defendant's defense, funding from deceased victims' estates is highly unusual.
  • The estate's value raises potential for disputes from other beneficiaries who may object to funds being used for the alleged perpetrator's defense.

  • The case against Nick Reiner could potentially be a death penalty case due to allegations of 'special circumstances,' such as a double homicide and the use of a deadly weapon.
  • Despite California's current moratorium on executions, a jury could still impose the death penalty, making mitigation evidence crucial for the defense.
  • A death penalty trial proceeds with a guilt phase, followed by a mitigation phase where evidence of abuse, mental health issues, or drug use can be presented.
  • Prosecutors would counter a defense strategy alleging lifetime abuse by emphasizing the need for factual investigation to disprove such claims.

  • An update on singer D4vd's case reveals a 14-year-old victim's remains were found in his car trunk.
  • An industrial burn cage, capable of reaching 1,600 degrees Fahrenheit, was allegedly discovered in D4vd's rental home.
  • Witnesses are reportedly cooperating in an investigative grand jury regarding the case.
  • Industrial incinerators like the one found are illegal in Los Angeles, and standard human cremation requires temperatures of at least 1,400 degrees.

  • The 29-year-old JonBenét Ramsey cold case, where the six-year-old victim was strangled, is being re-examined.
  • The Boulder Police Department has conducted new interviews, re-interviews, collected new evidence, and retested existing evidence to generate leads.
  • Evolving DNA testing technology, including touch DNA and amplification techniques, and investigative genetic genealogy (Othrum Labs) are being utilized.
  • The update suggests a renewed hope for solving the case, similar to its application in the Brian Koberger investigation.

  • Guest Dave Aronberg reflected on a year marked by violence, citing incidents at Brown University, Bondi Beach, and the Reiner murders.
  • He contrasted these perpetrators with acts of heroism, such as a Syrian shop owner disarming a terrorist.
  • Aronberg noted positive responses from figures like James Woods and Rob Schneider regarding Rob Reiner, emphasizing unity in condemning tragedy.
  • He stressed the importance of finding hope during the holiday season amidst the year's events.
