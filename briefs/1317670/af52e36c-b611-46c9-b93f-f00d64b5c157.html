Jasmine Crockett Enters Senate Race, Paramount's Hostile Takeover, Mamdani's Mansion: AM Update 12/9

Key Takeaways

  • Paramount launched a hostile $108 billion all-cash bid for Warner Bros. Discovery, challenging Netflix's prior offer.
  • Texas Congresswoman Jasmine Crockett entered the U.S. Senate race, setting up a contested Democratic primary.
  • Jimmy Kimmel extended his ABC contract for one year after affiliates temporarily pulled his show due to controversy.
  • NYC Mayor-elect Zohran Mamdani will move into Gracie Mansion, contrasting his image as a rent-stabilized apartment dweller.

Deep Dive

  • Paramount launched a hostile takeover bid for Warner Brothers Discovery with a $30 per share, all-cash offer.
  • The bid values WBD equity at $41 billion and the total deal at approximately $108 billion.
  • This offer is higher than Netflix's proposed $83 billion deal, which involves cash and stock.
  • Paramount claims its bid offers faster regulatory certainty and is backed by the Ellison family.

  • The Warner Bros. Discovery Board of Directors has 10 days to respond to Paramount's bid, which expires January 8, 2026.
  • If the Netflix deal fails, Netflix would pay WBD $5.8 billion; if WBD accepts another offer, it would pay Netflix $2.8 billion.
  • President Trump expressed concerns about Netflix's market share if they were to acquire Warner Brothers.
  • Paramount's bid is reportedly funded by foreign sovereign wealth funds and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners.

  • Texas Congresswoman Jasmine Crockett officially launched her bid for the U.S. Senate, challenging State Representative James Talarico.
  • Former Congressman Colin Allred cited Crockett's entry as the reason for his withdrawal from the race.
  • Crockett's political style is characterized by confrontational rhetoric and a strong social media presence.
  • Poll data indicates she is currently leading the primary field.

  • Jimmy Kimmel signed a one-year contract extension with ABC, months after his show was temporarily pulled by affiliates for six days.
  • The preemption by large owners like Nexstar and Sinclair followed controversial remarks made after a murder.
  • The FCC chair reminded stations of their power to preempt programming not in the public good.
  • Kimmel's average viewership fell from 2.4 million in 2015 to 1.6 million before his suspension.

  • New York City Mayor-elect Zohran Mamdani plans to move into Gracie Mansion, the 11,000-square-foot official mayoral residence.
  • Mamdani, a self-described socialist, cited family safety and the need to focus on his agenda as reasons for the move.
  • This decision contrasts with his public image, which highlighted his $2,300/month rent-stabilized apartment in Astoria.
  • Mamdani intends to keep his Astoria apartment while residing in Gracie Mansion.
