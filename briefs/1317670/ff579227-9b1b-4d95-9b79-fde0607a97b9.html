Greenland Showdown in Davos, Rep. Omar Wealth Probe, Manhunt After Judge Shooting: AM Update 1/21

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump's push to acquire Greenland is a global summit focus, raising NATO concerns.
  • Congresswoman Ilhan Omar's husband faces scrutiny over significant wealth amid fraud investigations.
  • Law enforcement nationwide is tracking suspects in two separate shooting incidents involving a judge and police officers.
  • Vice President J.D. Vance and Second Lady Usha Vance announced they are expecting their fourth child.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump escalated his push to acquire Greenland on Wednesday, January 21st, 2026, rattling European leaders and raising questions about NATO’s future.
  • The initiative was a central topic at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, marked by an AI-generated image depicting Trump claiming the territory.
  • European Union head Ursula von der Leyen advocated for European independence, while Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney affirmed support for Greenland and Denmark's self-determination.
  • California Governor Gavin Newsom criticized European leaders' responses, calling them pathetic and embarrassing, with a Danish EU Parliament member issuing a blunt rejection.

  • President Trump's pursuit of Greenland, including considerations of potential military force, is raising questions about NATO's future.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Besant framed the initiative as a proactive measure designed to strengthen the alliance.
  • President Trump argued for U.S. acquisition citing strategic and historical reasons, stating the U.S. 'has to have it' and Denmark cannot protect it.
  • The acquisition is presented as a preemptive measure against potential moves by Russia or China, intended to offer U.S. protection.

  • Congresswoman Ilhan Omar is reportedly under investigation by House oversight and federal law enforcement regarding significant wealth accumulation by her husband, Tim Minett.
  • Financial disclosures indicate Minett's businesses, including Rose Lake Capital and a winery, generated between $6 million and $30 million in one year.
  • Investigators are probing potential links between Omar and individuals charged in a multi-million dollar pandemic fraud scheme in Minnesota.
  • Omar maintains transparency and denies wrongdoing, noting she has been referred to ethics committees numerous times previously.

  • Authorities are searching for a suspect in Lafayette, Indiana, who shot Tippecanoe Superior Court Judge Stephen Meyer and his wife, Kimberly.
  • The victims were lured to their front door by a false report about their dog before being shot.
  • Both Judge Meyer and his wife are reported to be in stable condition.
  • The FBI is assisting in the ongoing investigation and manhunt for the suspect.

  • Two Portland, Oregon police officers were shot after responding to a late-night 911 call about a man with a knife.
  • The suspect remains at large following a large-scale overnight manhunt conducted by authorities.
  • Police have released images of the suspect, described as a white male in his mid-30s.
