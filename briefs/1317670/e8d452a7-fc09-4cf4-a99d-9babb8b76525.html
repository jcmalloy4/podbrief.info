The Megyn Kelly Show

3 Arrests Linked to ISIS-Inspired Plot, FAA Reducing Flights, Pelosi to Retire: AM Update 11/7

Key Takeaways

  • Five suspects have been charged in a foiled ISIS-inspired terror plot.
  • The FAA is cutting 10% of flights at 40 major airports amid a government shutdown.
  • President Trump announced a deal to reduce GLP-1 weight-loss drug costs to $350 monthly.
  • Former House Speaker Nancy Pelosi will not seek re-election after nearly four decades.
  • Preventative healthcare solutions are being offered to address the current reactive system.

Deep Dive

  • Three additional suspects were charged in a foiled ISIS-inspired terror plot, bringing the total to five.
  • Two 19-year-old New Jersey residents were among those arrested.
  • One suspect allegedly planned to travel to Syria for ISIS training and discussed a Boston Marathon-style attack.

  • The current healthcare system is described as reactive, leading to expensive treatments rather than prevention.
  • Geviti offers at-home testing for over 100 blood markers to facilitate data-driven health decisions.
  • The service provides personalized supplement packs, biannual retesting, and virtual consultations for ongoing care.

  • The FAA is reducing up to 10% of flights at 40 major airports due to staffing shortages caused by a six-week government shutdown.
  • Air traffic controllers are working without pay, leading to staffing shortfalls that could affect 3,500 to 4,000 flights daily.
  • Secretary Duffy stated this data-driven decision aims to prioritize safety over preventing gridlock, acknowledging potential delays.

  • President Trump announced a deal with Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk to reduce monthly costs of GLP-1 weight-loss drugs.
  • Popular drugs like Wegovy and Ozempic will be cut to approximately $350 per month through the new TrumpRX program.
  • The initiative aims to address high U.S. drug prices by aligning them with lower international costs and combating the national obesity crisis.

  • Former House Speaker Nancy Pelosi, 85, announced she will not seek re-election after nearly four decades in Congress.
  • Pelosi was the first woman to serve as Speaker of the House, first elected in 1987 and holding the position in 2007 and 2019.
  • During her tenure, she presided over two impeachment efforts against President Trump, who subsequently called her an "evil woman" and expressed gladness at her retirement.
  • Newsweek estimates Nancy and Paul Pelosi's net worth to be over $230 million.
