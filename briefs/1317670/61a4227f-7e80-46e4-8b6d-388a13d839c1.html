Deadly Shooting at Dallas ICE Facility, FBI Seized "Classified" Docs in Bolton Raid: AM Update 9/25

Key Takeaways

  • A shooting at a Dallas ICE facility killed one detainee and critically wounded two before the shooter died by suicide.
  • FBI agents seized classified documents, some decades old, from John Bolton's Washington D.C. office.
  • Ukrainian President Zelensky expressed surprise at President Trump's suggestion that Ukraine could win the war without ceding territory.
  • Ben Affleck's daughter, Violet, called for the reinstatement of mask mandates at the United Nations.

Deep Dive

  • A shooting at a Dallas ICE facility resulted in one detainee's death and two critical injuries before the shooter's suicide.
  • The deceased shooter was identified as Joshua Yon, 29, and 'anti-ICE' shell casings were recovered.
  • The FBI is investigating the incident as targeted violence, referencing a similar July 4th attack in Alvaredo, Texas.

  • Assaults on ICE agents have increased by 1,000%, coinciding with doxing campaigns.
  • The segment highlighted Democratic rhetoric critical of ICE, contrasting it with rising violence.
  • Vice President J.D. Vance condemned the 'rhetorical assault on law enforcement,' stating it encourages violence.

  • FBI agents seized documents marked 'confidential' and 'secret' from John Bolton's Washington D.C. office last month.
  • Some seized records were decades old, though an attorney stated they were previously approved for publication.
  • The ongoing investigation is under the Espionage Act, with no charges filed to date.

  • Ukrainian President Zelensky expressed surprise at President Trump's suggestion that Ukraine could win without land concessions.
  • Zelensky believes increased communication led to Trump's evolved stance and understanding of Putin's intentions.
  • He noted that Putin has historically misled President Trump regarding the conflict.

  • Vice President J.D. Vance stated President Trump's position on the Russia-Ukraine war reflects an acknowledgment of current reality.
  • Vance indicated Trump's impatience with Russia's lack of negotiation in the conflict.
  • He believes the ongoing war is detrimental to Russia, Ukraine, and the United States.

  • Violet Affleck, daughter of Ben Affleck and Jennifer Garner, addressed world leaders at the UN, advocating for mask mandates.
  • Wearing a mask, she called for action on airborne disease transmission, drawing parallels to Greta Thunberg's 2019 climate speech.
  • These comments were made nearly 2.5 years after the COVID-19 national health emergency was declared over.
