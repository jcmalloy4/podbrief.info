The Skeptics' Guide to the Universe

The Skeptics Guide #1073 - Jan 31 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Artemis program is preparing for its February 6th launch after extensive crew training and health stabilization.
  • Untreated dental infections pose serious risks like jaw bone loss, requiring specialized endodontist care.
  • An extinct, 8-meter-tall organism, Prototaxites, is now classified as a unique, multicellular eukaryotic lineage.
  • Wikipedia, celebrating 25 years, serves as a vital antidote to misinformation in the current information ecosystem.
  • AI's heavy reliance on Wikipedia for training data raises concerns about its financial sustainability if direct traffic decreases.
  • Research findings can be subtly influenced by ideological bias through researchers' methodological design choices.
  • The habitable zone for exoplanets may be wider, as tidally locked worlds could support liquid water.
  • Kesterite solar panels offer a promising lead-free alternative, though efficiency and stability need further development.
  • Virtual jury deliberations showed no significant difference in attention, fairness, or outcomes compared to in-person.
  • The stratospheric ozone layer is recovering, with full restoration expected by 2066, debunking reversal claims.

Deep Dive

  • Untreated dental infections pose dangers including jaw bone loss and severe complications in the head region.
  • Endodontists, 'super dentists' specializing in tooth roots and pulp, utilize CT scans for clearer views of roots and canals.
  • An infection can erode the thin bone between a tooth root and the sinus cavity, potentially creating an open channel.
  • A dental abscess, indicated by a gum blister, signals a deep infection requiring immediate attention.

  • New research on a fossil from Rhiny Church, Scotland, reclassified Prototaxites, an organism from the Devonian period (approximately 400 million years ago).
  • Prototaxites was a complex, multicellular eukaryote that stood about 8 meters tall and over a meter wide, lacking branches and a root system.
  • Scientists used advanced imaging, chemical analysis, and AI to determine it represents an entirely extinct eukaryotic lineage.
  • The latest study concluded Prototaxites is chemically and structurally distinct from all known fungal groups, both modern and ancient.

  • Wikipedia, which turned 25 on January 15th, 2001, has evolved into a crucial information source, described by an editorial in Nature as an 'antidote to a poisoned information ecosystem'.
  • The platform encompasses over 65 million entries across at least 300 languages, with the English version adding approximately 500 new entries daily.
  • Maintained by nearly 300,000 regular volunteer editors and the non-profit Wikimedia Foundation, Wikipedia emphasizes neutral points of view, verifiability, and referenced published material.
  • Editorial discussions and changes are transparently available on 'talk pages', contrasting with opaque 'black box' scenarios.

  • Wikipedia serves as a critical primary source for AI training data.
  • Concerns are raised that AI search engine summaries, by preempting Wikipedia, may lead to fewer clicks and financial contributions.
  • This reduction in traffic and donations could potentially impact Wikipedia's sustainability as a self-funding, non-commercial platform.
  • Users are encouraged to support Wikipedia through donations or by contributing expertise as editors.

  • A study examined ideological bias in research by having 158 researchers analyze immigration policy data, divided into 71 teams.
  • Results showed a significant correlation between researchers' personal attitudes toward immigration and their findings.
  • This bias is attributed to 'endogeneity of research design,' where researchers make methodological choices influenced by early data indications.
  • With at least 1,253 different regression models calculable for the study, flexibility allows bias to influence scientific outcomes, often subconsciously.

  • New research published in The Astrophysical Journal by Amri Wandel challenges the traditional narrow definition of the habitable zone for exoplanets.
  • The study suggests that tidally locked exoplanets, common around red dwarf stars (the most abundant star type), could support liquid water.
  • Thick atmospheres and oceans on these planets could redistribute heat from the star-facing side to the night side, potentially creating habitable conditions.
  • The James Webb Space Telescope has detected water vapor and complex atmospheres on exoplanets, including those initially deemed unlikely for habitability.

  • Kesterite solar panels represent a promising technology that does not contain lead and is durable.
  • Theoretical models suggest a 33.56% efficiency, but practical lab efficiencies are currently lower at 14.3%.
  • This discrepancy is attributed to manufacturing imperfections and chemical instabilities within the crystal lattice.
  • Significant development is required to achieve efficiencies above 20% and long-term stability comparable to silicon panels, potentially taking another 10 years for market entry.

  • A study comparing 54 mock juries found no significant difference in attention, deliberation, or outcomes between in-person and virtual groups.
  • Initial skepticism regarding virtual jury effectiveness was addressed, as the study revealed similar levels of engagement and fairness.
  • The virtual mock juries operated under constraints on distractions, distinguishing them from typical remote home settings.
  • Further research is noted as necessary to expand on these findings regarding virtual legal proceedings.

  • A recent report suggesting the stratospheric ozone layer was reversing its recovery trend since 2020 was debunked.
  • The stratospheric ozone layer is continuing to repair itself and is expected to be fully restored by 2066.
  • This stratospheric ozone layer is crucial for blocking harmful UV radiation, essential for life on Earth.
  • Ground-level ozone, a distinct component of air pollution, has negative health effects and is subject to different regulatory strategies.
