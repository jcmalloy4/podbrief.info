Terminally Online: Live from Crooked Con (Subscription Preview)

Key Takeaways

  • A special live episode of "Terminally Online" from CrookedCon blind-ranked 2025's most online politicians.
  • J.D. Vance was identified as the number one most online politician, followed by Nancy Mace.
  • A Crooked Con straw poll indicated Pete Buttigieg as the top choice for a 2028 presidential run with 31% of votes.
  • Audience members at CrookedCon nominated various politicians for the 2028 presidential race.

Deep Dive

  • The segment featured a live recording of the subscriber-exclusive show "Terminally Online" from CrookedCon.
  • Hosts Elijah Cohn, Jon Favreau, Dan Pfeiffer, Tommy Vietor, and Jon Lovett blind-ranked the "most online politicians of 2025."
  • The game's objective was to achieve a consensus ranking among the hosts without prior knowledge of the full list.

  • Congresswoman Nancy Mace's online presence was described as "uncomfortable" by the hosts.
  • Discussions included an incident at the Charleston airport involving a police escort, which led to a Wired reporter's firing.
  • Mace posted a video about the incident, prompting bipartisan defense of airport staff from Tim Scott and Lindsey Graham.
  • Her online persona was characterized as demonstrating "internet personality disorder" and "main character energy."

  • J.D. Vance was ultimately ranked as the number one "most online" politician of 2025.
  • The top five also included Nancy Mace, Donald Trump, Zoran Montani, and Gavin Newsom.
  • Chuck Schumer was mentioned as another politician with a significant online presence.
  • Donald Trump was initially placed at a three for his online activity, including all-caps posts.

  • Grant Plattner was recognized as an honorable mention for his activity on Reddit.
  • Mike Lee was discussed, characterized as a "protégé of J.D. Vance" and easily influenced online.
  • Valentina Gomez, who ran for Secretary of State in Missouri, was mentioned for attempting to primary Dan Crenshaw in Texas.
  • Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez was cited for her past online impact and ability to "break the internet."

  • The Crooked Con Straw Poll surveyed attendees on their preferred 2028 presidential candidates.
  • Pete Buttigieg secured the most votes with 31%, followed by Andy Bashir at 22%.
  • Wes Moore and Gavin Newsom each received 15% of the vote.
  • Anderson Clayton and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez each garnered 7%, though only 13 people voted in total.

  • Audience members at CrookedCon participated in a live poll for 2028 presidential candidates.
  • Suggestions included Chris Murphy, Andy Beshear, and a joint ticket of Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Jon Ossoff.
  • Other nominations featured Gretchen Whitmer, Corey Booker, and J.B. Pritzker.
  • One audience member noted J.B. Pritzker's past $1.4 million casino winnings, donated to charity.
