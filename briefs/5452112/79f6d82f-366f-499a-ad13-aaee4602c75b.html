Live Show Announcement

  • Lulu announces an upcoming live taping of The Interview podcast at the Tribeca Festival in New York City, scheduled for Thursday, June 12th
  • The featured guest will be Sandra Oh, acclaimed actress known for her starring roles in Grey's Anatomy and Killing Eve
  • Sandra Oh will also be performing in Shakespeare in the Park's production of Twelfth Night later in the summer, adding context to her current theatrical work
  • Tickets for the live show are available through TribecaFestival.com/The Interview, with Lulu encouraging local New York area listeners to attend the event
