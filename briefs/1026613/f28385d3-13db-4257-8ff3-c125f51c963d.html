Hard Fork’s 50 Most Iconic Technologies of 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Hard Fork's 2025 list highlights 50 technologies defining the year, for better or worse.
  • AI innovation drove new products, from controversial pendants to powerful coding agents.
  • Failures like the Humane AI Pin underscore challenges in consumer tech adoption.
  • Established technologies like paper and air conditioning maintained their foundational importance.
  • Ethical concerns, including AI alignment issues, surfaced with models like "Mecha Hitler."
  • Infrastructure, specifically data centers and EUV lithography, became critical for AI expansion.
  • The return of plastic straws and rise of zero-proof beverages mark shifting consumer trends.
  • Prediction markets and crypto, like Trump Coin, saw significant financial and regulatory impact.
  • The year concluded with data centers identified as the most iconic technology of 2025.
