The Prof G Pod with Scott Galloway

Raging Moderates: Trump Has Completed Half of Project 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Government shutdown persists, with public opinion favoring Democrats despite bipartisan blame.
  • Project 2025, a far-right governing blueprint, is reportedly half-implemented, expanding presidential power.
  • U.S. stock market, heavily reliant on AI valuations, exhibits fragility, raising recession concerns.
  • 'No Kings' protests against authoritarianism gain momentum, countering government's critical labels.

Deep Dive

  • The government shutdown has led to closed museums and unpaid federal workers.
  • President Trump is utilizing Defense Department funds to pay troops, leaving other federal employees in limbo.
  • Jessica Tarlov noted Donald Trump's longest shutdown lasted 35 days, anticipating a prolonged current shutdown.

  • Polling data indicates 41% of Americans blame the GOP for the shutdown, compared to 30% for Democrats.
  • Democrats are strategically focusing on healthcare issues, gaining an advantage as the shutdown continues.
  • Speaker Mike Johnson is expected to negotiate a deal with Democratic terms, while attempting to declare victory.
  • Leader Hakeem Jeffries is pushing for a standalone bill to pay troops, while Trump considers executive action or tariff funds.

  • Project 2025, a far-right governing blueprint, is described as nearly 50% implemented despite Donald Trump's claims of ignorance.
  • The plan aims to dismantle checks and balances, granting sweeping presidential power and influencing future elections.
  • Kamala Harris and Democratic surrogates have effectively used Project 2025 as a talking point, concerning Republican pollsters Tony Fabrizio.

  • House Speaker Mike Johnson is criticized for embracing a 'neutered Congress' role, executing Project 2025's agenda.
  • Originating from the Heritage Foundation, its policy aims include restoring the family and dismantling the administrative state.
  • Alarming stances include labeling the FBI as 'lawless' and advocating for a 'biblically based' definition of marriage.
  • The project proposes banning pornography and shutting down companies that allow access to adult material.

  • Accounts of alleged mistreatment of U.S. citizens in detention and legal challenges to government actions have surfaced.
  • A case involving a DACA recipient and the detention of a U.S. citizen veteran are highlighted as examples.
  • Judges are reportedly disputing the administration's portrayal of cities like Portland and Chicago as chaotic.

  • The current market performance is largely seen as a bet on AI, with high valuations providing 'cloud cover' for administration activities.
  • Economic fragility is highlighted, with 10 companies representing 40% of the S&P 500's value.
  • The U.S. banking sector is perceived as fragile despite the market's resilience after Silicon Valley Bank's collapse.
  • NVIDIA's investment in OpenAI is cited as an example of 'circular deals' that inflate valuations.

  • A 20% stock market decline could significantly reduce spending by wealthy individuals, potentially triggering a recession.
  • The economy is described as concentrated and fragile, with current market valuations heavily fueled by AI hype.
  • A personal financial strategy involves rotating out of U.S. and tech stocks into European and Latin American markets, as the U.S. market is believed to be overvalued due to AI hype.

  • 'No Kings' protests, backed by labor unions and progressive groups, aim to protest authoritarianism and the government shutdown.
  • Speaker Mike Johnson labeled the protests as 'hate America' and pro-Hamas, which organizers dismissed as fear-mongering.
  • Over 2,000 rallies are planned, including a significant protest in Washington D.C., indicating larger participation than previous events.
  • The term 'Antifa' is characterized as a vague, fear-mongering tool broadly applied to those disliked by the administration, distracting from more organized violence.
