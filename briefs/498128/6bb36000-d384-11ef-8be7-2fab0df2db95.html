The Prof G Pod with Scott Galloway

No Mercy / No Malice: America’s Best Bet

Key Takeaways

  • America's major bet on AI is questioned; GLP-1 drugs offer greater economic and well-being upside.
  • U.S. obesity affects over 100 million adults, projected to cost more than $20 trillion by 2060.
  • GLP-1 drugs induce significant weight loss by suppressing hunger and increasing satiety.
  • Limited insurance coverage restricts broad access to GLP-1s, despite their efficacy and potential benefits.
  • Widespread GLP-1 access could offset costs through increased GDP and health productivity gains.

Deep Dive

  • Scott Galloway introduces the idea that while America's current major bet is on AI, GLP-1 drugs could be a better bet for transforming the economy and well-being.
  • The host elaborates that GLP-1 drugs offer more asymmetric upside than GPT-5, particularly for addressing obesity.
  • Over 100 million U.S. adults are affected by obesity, with projected healthcare costs potentially exceeding $20 trillion by 2060.

  • U.S. obesity rates may have peaked, with GLP-1 drugs, which mimic a hunger-suppressing hormone, presented as a revolutionary tool to reverse the trend.
  • GLP-1 drugs reduce hunger and increase satiety, leading to significant weight loss (15-20%).
  • Coverage for GLP-1s for obesity is limited to 30-40% of commercial plans and 14 state Medicaid programs.
  • Medicare covering GLP-1s is estimated to have a net cost of $48 billion over a decade, though prices are decreasing, with some drugs costing under $7,000 annually or $499 per month at Costco.

  • Former President Trump's proposal to lower GLP-1 prices to $150 per month, aligning with other developed nations, caused a sharp stock drop for Novo Nordisk and Lilly.
  • Economist Emily Oster argues that concerns about the budget impact of expanding GLP-1 access are overstated, suggesting increased GDP from widespread use could offset costs.
  • The obesity epidemic costs Britain £31 billion annually, with Henry Dimbleby viewing GLP-1s as a crucial, though not cure-all, tool.
  • GLP-1 drugs are presented as a vital weapon against obesity, complementing lifestyle changes and counteracting ultra-processed foods.

  • Expanding access to GLP-1s could significantly reduce adult obesity rates in the U.S., addressing a major public health issue.
  • A potential $12 billion investment over two years in obesity treatments is discussed, offering significant health and productivity gains.
  • GLP-1s are highlighted as a more impactful engine of progress compared to AI, especially for health and economic benefits.
