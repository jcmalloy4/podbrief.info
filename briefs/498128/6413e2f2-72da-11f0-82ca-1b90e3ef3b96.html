The Prof G Pod with Scott Galloway

Raging Moderates: Trump Pulls Back in Minneapolis as Democrats Turn Up Pressure on ICE

Key Takeaways

  • Democrats threaten a government shutdown to rein in ICE following the Alex Pretti killing.
  • The GOP faces internal conflicts over gun rights and federal agent actions.
  • Public opinion is shifting towards greater ICE accountability and reform.
  • Melania Trump's documentary generates criticism for its reported $40 million payday.
  • Discussions highlight the disproportionate online misogyny women face and the need for male empathy.

Deep Dive

  • Jessica Tarlov noted Heather Cox Richardson's comparison of ICE officials' training needs to those of Dachau guards.
  • Catherine Dillon observed the U.S. executing its citizens, similar to Iran.
  • These impactful statements were made during Scott Galloway's absence.

  • Scott Galloway criticized the reported $40 million payday for Melania Trump's new documentary as 'soft corruption.'
  • Galloway labeled her the 'worst first lady in history' due to perceived inaction and the failure of her 'Be Best' initiative.
  • He suggested the documentary was an attempt by Amazon to curry favor.

  • Democrats are threatening to block DHS funding unless ICE is reined in, risking a government shutdown.
  • Proposed reforms include DHS cooperation with state probes, CBP remaining at the border, warrants for arrests, agents wearing IDs and body cameras, and ICE ceasing operations in churches and schools.
  • ICE is already funded at a significantly higher level than the previous year, even if the current $10 billion is not approved.
  • A partial shutdown related to DHS funding would not impact federal services on the same scale as a full government shutdown.

  • Senator Rand Paul demanded the suspension of agents involved in Alex Pretti's killing, labeling Pretti a domestic terrorist.
  • Kyle Cheney's reporting indicated 2,300 alleged illegal ICE detentions since July.
  • Acting ICE director Todd Lyons faces a judge in an unprecedented situation regarding alleged violations.
  • Articles of impeachment against Governor Kristi Noem are being considered for obstruction of Congress, violation of public trust, and self-dealing related to ICE recruitment contracts.

  • Polling data indicates 46% support abolishing ICE, with 41% opposed, showing increased support among independents and Republicans.
  • Most Democrats and independents differentiate abolishing ICE from abolishing immigration enforcement.
  • Democratic proposals for ICE reform suggest clear demands, including removing undocumented immigrants who commit crimes, potentially with cooperation from 'Blue City' mayors.
  • Tom Holman is reportedly attempting to sideline factions led by Stephen Miller and Kris Kobach within government over immigration policy.

  • The killing of Alex Pretti exposed a contradiction within the Republican Party regarding gun rights and federal overreach.
  • Republicans are defending federal agents who killed a lawful gun owner, challenging their anti-tyrannical government stance.
  • Jessica Tarlov suggests many Republicans prioritize gun rights over loyalty to Donald Trump.
  • An official's tweet implied lethal force is justified when approaching law enforcement with a gun.

  • Jon Stewart contrasted gun owners' embrace of the Second Amendment with their perceived willingness to abandon other amendments at Trump's behest.
  • Jessica Tarlov presented a historical argument referencing how Nazis disarmed populations in occupied countries.
  • Legally licensed gun owners are less likely to commit crimes than ordinary citizens, though a correlation exists with increased suicide risk.
  • Backlash occurred against Secretary Neocim for labeling Alex Pretti a domestic terrorist.

  • Tech CEOs, often from privileged backgrounds, may lack understanding of societal issues.
  • The discussion highlighted a disproportionate amount of misogynistic and threatening online commentary directed at women compared to men.
  • A call was made for greater empathy and awareness from men regarding these issues.
