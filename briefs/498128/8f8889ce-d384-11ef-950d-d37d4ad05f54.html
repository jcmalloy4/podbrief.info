The Prof G Pod with Scott Galloway

Raging Moderates: How Trump Remade America in Just One Year

Key Takeaways

  • Trump's 2025 return reshaped US power, foreign policy, and challenged established norms.
  • The US economy's reliance on AI may be shielding President Trump from greater scrutiny despite economic anxieties.
  • Trump's political standing shows signs of strain, with declining support linked to voter concerns over the economy.
  • The debate over AI regulation highlights tensions between federal oversight, states' rights, and potential societal impacts.
  • There is a growing global push for social media regulation, especially for minors, to combat negative societal effects.
  • The year 2025 included notable moments of heroism, significant philanthropy, and cultural highlights.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump's return to office in 2025 reshaped American power, strained relations with Europe, and introduced new tariffs.
  • His administration implemented executive actions on immigration and the Justice Department, challenging established norms.
  • The host criticized Trump for disrupting the post-World War II order and for monetizing the presidency and pardons.
  • Actions in the Middle East, including bombing Iranian nuclear facilities, reflected a transactional foreign policy approach.

  • The US economy is heavily reliant on AI, with projections suggesting a government bailout for AI in 2026 to sustain its rapid growth.
  • AI and big tech companies are largely unaffected by tariffs, implying the AI boom is propping up the economy.
  • A rising stock market, attributed to AI, is proposed as a factor shielding President Trump from greater scrutiny.
  • One host speculated that a fee between $500,000 and $3 million could leverage connections to overcome legal consequences.

  • President Trump signed 218 executive orders in 2025, a historical high, yet his support is reportedly weakening.
  • 57% of voters believe Trump is losing the battle against inflation, with 68% viewing the economy as poor.
  • Trump's communication style, focusing on trivial matters rather than voter concerns, has alienated potential supporters.
  • Support among 2024 voters has declined by 16 percentage points since March, particularly among younger and Latino demographics.

  • President Trump's executive order aims to create a single national AI framework, potentially preempting 47 state-level regulations.
  • Concerns exist that Trump's federal AI standard, influenced by AI company donations, could favor deregulation and national security risks.
  • The impact of AI on the workforce includes accelerating layoffs, prompting moderate House Democrats to seek information from tech companies.
  • Specific AI policy suggestions include age-gating synthetic relationships and regulating 'character AI' due to potential societal harm.
  • Users spend an average of 75 minutes on 'character AI' platforms compared to 15 minutes on ChatGPT, raising concerns about synthetic relationships.

  • A critique suggests that the 'cult of therapy' and supplements distract from addressing structural issues like economic precarity, housing, and accessible healthcare.
  • A quote from Esther Perel argued that the rise of therapy culture makes Americans 'sicker, weaker, and more divided'.
  • This perspective contends that modern therapy can treat disagreement as trauma, fostering manufactured fragility among individuals.

  • Australia is considering a ban on social media for minors under 16, with some politicians advocating for similar measures, citing positive school phone ban results.
  • Research indicates individual parental restrictions on social media are ineffective, suggesting a collective ban is necessary to mitigate negative impacts.
  • Jonathan Haidt's 'The Anxious Generation' advocates for a social media ban for those under 16 to restore childhood activities and real-world interactions.
  • The proliferation of smartphones and unregulated social media is linked to declining societal well-being, including increased division, polarization, and extremism.

  • Hosts reflected on various positive moments from 2025, including election results, protests, and legislative changes.
  • Cultural highlights mentioned included Beyoncé's Grammy win and notable celebrity relationships, prompting personal anecdotes.
  • The discussion covered a range of humorous observations about the past year's events, focusing on moments that provided hope.

  • Jessica Tarlov highlighted the World Series, featuring Shohei Ohtani and Vladimir Guerrero Jr., and the Chris Martin-Gwyneth Paltrow 'kiss cam' incident.
  • Scott Galloway noted his son's college acceptance and the heroism of Ahmed Al-Ahmed, a fruit shop owner who intervened during an attack in Bondi Beach.
  • Philanthropist MacKenzie Scott made significant donations, including $15 million to the Jet Foundation for teen suicide prevention.
  • Scott's philanthropic approach was praised and contrasted favorably with that of traditional male billionaires.
