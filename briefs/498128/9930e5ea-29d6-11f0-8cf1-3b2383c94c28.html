The Prof G Pod with Scott Galloway

Scott’s Thoughts on Joe Rogan, Year-End Reflections, and Hiring Great People

Key Takeaways

  • The tech industry is experiencing an "AI Gold Rush" with widespread product integration.
  • Scott Galloway explains his refusal to appear on The Joe Rogan Experience podcast.
  • Galloway holds Spotify and Joe Rogan responsible for amplifying health misinformation.
  • Scott Galloway reviewed his 2025, detailing professional achievements and personal challenges.
  • Small businesses can attract talent through continuous outreach and strong referral systems.
  • Commitment to employee growth is crucial for long-term talent retention.

Deep Dive

  • The host describes an "AI Gold Rush," noting companies are integrating AI into various products and platforms.
  • A two-week series on The Vergecast is exploring developers' approaches to AI and its potential impact.
  • Looking back at 2025, the tech industry focused on AI, NVIDIA's rise, and the launch of the Switch 2.

  • Scott Galloway is reluctant to appear on The Joe Rogan Experience due to concerns about misinformation.
  • He cites instances where Rogan presented "quacks" alongside experts, creating false equivalences, particularly regarding mRNA vaccines.
  • Galloway removed his podcasts from Spotify after a loved one died of COVID-19 following anti-vax narratives.
  • He holds Spotify and Rogan responsible for not adequately fact-checking sensitive issues on their large platform, stating they caused "death, disease, and disability."

  • Professionally, the host's book became a New York Times bestseller, and a 'Pivot' live tour attracted 15,000 attendees across 7 cities.
  • He earned less money than usual due to flat investments, including in a Bitcoin Treasury company.
  • Personally, his father passed away at 95, acknowledging a complicated relationship.
  • A personal highlight was a 10-day college tour with his oldest son, visiting 7 towns.

  • Scott Galloway advises small businesses to attract great talent through continuous outreach and strong referral systems.
  • He emphasizes reliance on reference hiring, citing an example of hiring an employee based on a trusted referral.
  • The process of vetting potential hires involves trusted contacts and achieving team consensus.

  • Demonstrating excellence and a commitment to employee growth is crucial for retention.
  • Many employees at PropG Media have stayed with the company long-term due to this focus.
