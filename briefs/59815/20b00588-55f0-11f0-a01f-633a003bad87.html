Andrew Schulz's Flagrant with Akaash Singh

Jordan Jensen wants to Smash her Dad, Bad Acid Trips, & God is a Woman?

Key Takeaways

  • The episode features unfiltered discussions on family dynamics, online culture, and personal experiences.
  • Topics range from comedians' stage anxieties and intimate hygiene to substance use and societal observations.
  • Conversations delve into ethical considerations regarding consent, child-rearing decisions, and gender roles.
  • Hosts share explicit anecdotes about relationships, career challenges, and public confrontations.
  • Perceptions of intelligence while under the influence of various substances are debated.
