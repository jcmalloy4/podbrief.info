This Past Weekend w/ Theo Von

#583 - Chad "Ochocinco" Johnson

Overview

* The NFL has undergone significant rule changes driven by player safety concerns, creating a more pass-friendly game that favors offensive players while restricting defensive tactics, fundamentally altering how the sport is played compared to previous eras.

* Chad Johnson's football success stemmed from his meticulous preparation and psychological warfare - studying film extensively to predict opponents' moves while using creative tactics like sending "gifts" to opposing defenses to gain mental advantages.

* Despite witnessing his mother's struggle with alcoholism until her 2021 death from cirrhosis, Chad developed a disciplined personal lifestyle that includes daily cigar smoking but completely avoids alcohol and drugs.

* Johnson approaches family planning with strategic intentionality, selecting athletic partners specifically for genetic potential rather than appearance, with eight children currently and plans for more through IVF.

* Post-NFL, Chad has built a more lucrative media career than his playing days, working with NFL Films and ESPN while maintaining his authentic personality and pursuing diverse interests including soccer, the WNBA, and his cigar brand.

Content: Chad Ochocinco Johnson Podcast Conversation

Introduction and Background

* Guest is Chad Ochocinco Johnson, former NFL wide receiver, commentator, and podcast host * The interview is casual and free-flowing, covering various topics from sports to personal life * Initial discussion includes tour dates, upcoming shows, and casual conversation about racing, animals, and betting

NFL Evolution and Rule Changes

* Chad discusses how football has evolved, particularly for receivers * NFL rule changes were primarily driven by a massive lawsuit about player injuries: - Filed in 2011-2012 - Approximately 4,500 former players involved - Resulted in significant settlement - Led to softer rules protecting players, particularly around contact and head injuries * Modern football throws more passes compared to Chad's era * Current rules favor offensive players with defensive players facing more restrictions * Players have become faster, stronger, and bigger, while field dimensions remain unchanged * The game is described as a "gladiator sport" that is somewhat "barbaric"

Defensive Players and Adaptation

* Defensive cornerbacks and safeties have been significantly impacted by rule changes * Notable defensive players mentioned include Jalen Ramsey, Sauce Gardner, Christian Gonzalez * Modern defensive players are technically adapting to new rules * Less opportunity for "hard-hitting" defensive play compared to previous eras * Offensive players (like Mahomes) strategically use rules to draw penalties * Quarterbacks are advised to slide early to avoid dangerous hits

Chad's Approach to Football

* Always stayed engaged even when not directly receiving the ball * Enjoyed "talking shit" during plays * Emphasized his preparation and study habits: - Success was due to extensive film analysis - Could predict opponents' moves based on detailed study - Particularly enjoyed watching film and anticipating defensive strategies * Used psychological tactics to get into opponents' heads: - Sent "gifts" to opposing defenses (e.g., Pepto-Bismol to the Browns) - Believed causing mental distraction created competitive advantage - Described his approach as "mental gymnastics" * Felt most comfortable with Carson Palmer as his quarterback * Believed his role was to make the quarterback's job easier

Personal Lifestyle and Habits

* Owns a cigar brand (85 Cigars) * Has been smoking cigars since 1991 * Smokes 2-3 cigars daily, including during his playing career * Never drinks alcohol or does drugs * Pairs cigars with coffee * Enjoys various cigar flavor profiles * Passionate about having an even, dark skin tone * Compares his ideal skin color to Lindor chocolate

Mother's Influence and Family Dynamics

* Mother struggled with alcoholism: - Drank heavily ("morning, noon, night") - Passed away in 2021 from cirrhosis/alcohol poisoning - Her drinking transformed her into someone he "didn't recognize" * Never drank alcohol himself after witnessing his mother's addiction * Attempted intervention through a family TV show, but she was uncooperative * Described her as "Hurricane Paula" with a strong, confrontational personality * Despite challenges, he loved her authenticity * She was proud of his NFL and off-field accomplishments * Too unpredictable to bring to formal events * Has saved voicemails of her "cursing him out" * Developed personal philosophy from upbringing: - Comfortable in chaotic environments - Maintains humor during challenging situations - Purposely created chaos during NFL career for entertainment

Experience with Terrell Owens

* Had an early podcast with T.O. called the "Teotihuahua show" around 2010 * Advocated for bringing T.O. to the Cincinnati Bengals * Admired T.O. as a top-three all-time player * Was willing to take a "backseat" role to T.O. * Contrasted their approaches to athletic preparation: - T.O. was disciplined: used massages, hyperbaric chambers, ate healthy - Chad was the opposite: ate McDonald's, smoked cigars * Considers T.O. a very good friend to this day

College and Early Career

* Attended Santa Monica College (SMC) * Discussed challenges of attending SMC, like parking difficulties * Mentioned needing to leave Miami's environment * Later attended Langston University (NAIA school in Oklahoma) * Got expelled for fighting * Moved to LA after grandmother "washed her hands" of him

Relationships with Other Athletes

* Knows Antonio Brown (AB) from early on * AB's father is Eddie Brown, who holds arena football touchdown records * Views AB positively despite controversial public persona * Describes AB as a long-time friend from before his football career

Relationships and Family Planning

* Currently has eight children, wants two more (specifically twins via IVF) * Reveals a deliberate strategy in choosing partners: - Only dated athletic women - Goal was to create children with strong athletic potential - Focused on genetic potential over physical appearance - Looks at physical attributes like ankles as indicators of athletic ability * Only dates women who already have children (minimum two kids) * Believes having kids means more stability * Tests potential dates' athletic skills through activities like: - Dave & Buster's - Bowling - Roller skating - Gym dates * Takes his children on annual group trips (recently Mexico/DR, planning Dubai) * Supports each child's individual interests and talents: - Daughter running track at University of Kentucky - Another daughter who is a dancer at Prairie View A&M - Son graduating from Arizona State University * Allows the mothers of his children to choose their names

Views on Current NFL and Sports

* Discussed quarterback tiers, identifying "tier one" quarterbacks: - Patrick Mahomes, Joe Burrow, Lamar Jackson, Josh Allen - Jalen Hurts, Jordan Love, Justin Herbert, Trevor Lawrence, CJ Stroud * Emphasized how difficult it is to find truly good quarterbacks * Most NFL teams are "a quarterback away" from being competitive * Highlighted the 2005 Bengals season as particularly strong * Believes they would have won if Carson Palmer hadn't been injured in playoffs * Maintains a group chat with former teammates

Other Sports Interests

* Huge soccer enthusiast who has traveled extensively for the sport * Believes soccer is like "painting a picture" * Has visited famous stadiums worldwide: - River Plate vs. Boca Juniors in Argentina - Every English Premier League stadium - La Liga stadiums * Attended Qatar World Cup as a Fox analyst * Also expressed enthusiasm for WNBA: - Attended games in Atlanta and Las Vegas - Praises the WNBA's style of play as reminiscent of early NBA basketball

Current Career and Media Work

* Makes more money now than during his playing career * No interest in coaching due to insufficient pay * Works with NFL Films and ESPN with Stephen A. Smith during football season * Appreciates maintaining a voice in sports after playing professionally

Controversial Personal History

* Worked previously as a gigolo for wealthy women * Earned $3,500-$5,000 per hour * Also worked as a stripper in LA with the nickname "Twix" * Discussed being entrepreneurial and working on a cash-only basis

Perspectives on Recognition and Loyalty

* Spoke about Hall of Fame induction and his perspective on such honors * Expressed dislike for the power dynamics involved in award nominations * Emphasized not needing external validation for his achievements * Discussed loyalty to his podcast/show (Nightcap) despite personal challenges * Draws parallel to Bill Belichick's stance on friendship with Donald Trump * Maintains an "even keel" approach and remains "authentically myself at all times"
