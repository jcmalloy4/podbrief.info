This Past Weekend w/ Theo Von

#626 - Baby Me

Key Takeaways

  • A charity jersey auction successfully raised approximately $40,000 for Cumberland Heights, with an additional donation made to the Gary Sinise Foundation.
  • The host acknowledged Thanksgiving's complex historical context while expressing gratitude for unique human interactions and life's simple pleasures.
  • Producer Riley Mau announced a significant life update: he and his partner are expecting a son, due at the end of the year.
  • Discussions centered on the current 'wild time' marked by information overload and a perceived 'awakening,' emphasizing focus on personal priorities.
  • Listeners shared personal stories, including one individual's journey of overcoming Borderline Personality Disorder and reflections on challenging family dynamics.
  • The host elaborated on the concept of 'meeting God halfway' as maintaining a consistent spiritual connection through prayer and community engagement.

Deep Dive

  • A charity jersey auction, supported by listeners, raised approximately $40,000.
  • Proceeds from the auction benefited Cumberland Heights, a local rehabilitation center.
  • An additional donation was made to the Gary Sinise Foundation.

  • Producer Riley Mau announced that he and his partner are expecting a baby.
  • The couple is anticipating the birth of a son, due by the end of the year.
  • Mau expressed excitement about upcoming fatherhood, sharing thoughts on purpose and preparing for the child.

  • The host emphasized the importance of life insurance, citing a friend's recent passing and its financial support.
  • Ethos, an online platform, was highlighted for its quick and easy life insurance application process, often without medical exams.
  • Ethos has been recognized by Business Insider for its efficient service, offering up to $3 million in coverage.

  • A clip from comedian Sebastian Maniscalco's new special, 'It Ain’t Right,' was played.
  • The special is currently streaming on Hulu and is also available to Disney Plus bundle subscribers.
  • The host characterized Maniscalco's comedic style as meticulously detailing unusual everyday occurrences.

  • A 23-year-old listener named Bailey shared a personal story of overcoming depression after a Borderline Personality Disorder diagnosis.
  • Bailey credited the podcast for providing hope and perspective, stating 'shit does get better' after being in a psychiatric ward the previous Thanksgiving.
  • The host acknowledged the importance of shared experiences and encouragement in mental health recovery.

  • The host responded to a listener voicemail from Anna, who expressed sadness and dislike for her family during the holidays.
  • The host validated the need to express difficult emotions regarding family dynamics.
  • He shared personal insights on showing up for family as an act of service, while acknowledging struggles with self-pity.

  • A caller named Cage from North Carolina announced he and his partner are expecting twin girls, adding to their existing son.
  • The host and Riley Mau offered congratulations, with the host humorously expressing skepticism about twins, referring to it as 'wizardry.'
  • The discussion touched on the unique challenges and joys of raising multiple children.

  • A listener, Danielle from Southern California, inquired about the host's statement on trying to 'meet God halfway.'
  • The host defined this concept as maintaining a warm relationship through consistent prayer and consultation.
  • He explained it also involves 'getting outside of oneself' to check on others, facilitating guidance when needed.

  • Producer Riley Mau, 27, articulated his primary wish for his soon-to-arrive baby boy: the child's health.
  • Mau confirmed the baby's due date is December 30th.
  • The conversation reflected on past times and present changes, with focus on the upcoming birth.
