This Past Weekend w/ Theo Von

#627 - Leanne Morgan

Key Takeaways

  • Comedian Leanne Morgan achieved her career breakthrough in her early 50s, leading to a Netflix special and series.
  • She began her comedy career at age 32, encouraged by her husband Chuck Morgan, despite early touring difficulties.
  • Morgan's Southern upbringing in a small Tennessee farming community and her family life inform much of her material.
  • Her husband's financial stability allowed her to pursue less lucrative comedy gigs in her early career.
  • Morgan reflects on the evolving role of women in comedy, noting the shift from historical biases to greater acceptance.
  • The Bell Witch legend from her hometown of Adams, Tennessee, is a significant part of local culture and personal history.

Deep Dive

  • Leanne Morgan started her comedy career at age 32, 18 months after her baby was born, performing at local events like the Rotary club for small fees.
  • She met her husband, Chuck, at the University of Tennessee while finishing her undergraduate studies.
  • Morgan met country singer Morgan Wallen at a charity event after his elimination from 'The Voice' and promised him a casserole.

  • Her career breakthrough arrived in her early 50s, following a viral clip of her discussing a Def Leppard and Journey concert, which led to sold-out shows.
  • This success came after considering quitting comedy due to low pay, demanding travel, and uncomfortable accommodations in early touring days.
  • Morgan even contemplated opening a hardware store instead of continuing with comedy due to these challenges.

  • Leanne Morgan recounts childhood experiences on a farm with an electric fence and her family's non-confrontational nature.
  • She lived in a double-wide mobile home while pregnant, encountering challenges like a charging potbelly pig and retrieving stolen building materials.
  • Morgan also shared an anecdote about someone attempting to buy a cat from Facebook Marketplace, cautioning against meeting strangers.

  • Leanne Morgan's family owned a grocery store and meat processing plant near Knoxville, Tennessee.
  • She contrasts her upbringing with her sister Beth's high school success, including being homecoming queen.
  • Morgan also briefly recounts her first marriage in her early twenties, describing the relationship as difficult.

  • Leanne Morgan met her husband, Chuck, while waitressing at a restaurant in Knoxville, Tennessee.
  • She explains her early career involved taking any available job and staying on stage when possible.
  • Morgan initially aspired to perform at the Comedy Store in Los Angeles, questioning how having children impacted her ability to pursue comedy during those years.

  • Netflix released all 16 episodes of Leanne Morgan's show simultaneously, a first for the platform.
  • Her daughter is training for a union film job and will accompany her as a makeup artist on future seasons.
  • Morgan's oldest child is married with two grandchildren, her middle child works at a New York food bank, and her youngest studies makeup for television and film in Manhattan.

  • Leanne Morgan recounts the local lore of the Bell Witch in her hometown of Adams, Tennessee, noting its integration into local culture through mock rituals.
  • She shares family history about the Bell Witch Cave, mentioning fraternities would visit, leading to vandalism at the local church.
  • Morgan offers an alternative theory attributing the events to a ventriloquist and mathematician who allegedly poisoned John Bell.

  • Leanne Morgan never felt gender-based disparity in her career, attributing any missed opportunities to personal readiness or skill level.
  • She discusses the historical perception of jesters being male and society's discomfort with women performing vulgar acts, noting this view has shifted.
  • Morgan credits advice from Christian Johnson to focus on humor, citing Lucille Ball and Carol Burnett, and appreciates that the pressure to be conventionally attractive has lessened with age.

  • Chuck Morgan initially seemed like a 'butthole' due to comments about Leanne's food choices but pursued her with gifts and support despite her initial reluctance stemming from a prior divorce at age 23.
  • He humorously presented an invoice for expenses incurred while pursuing her and later proposed marriage contingent on him getting a job after NBA school.
  • Chuck broke up with her, citing her smoking smell, then purchased a mobile home business in Bean Station, Tennessee.
