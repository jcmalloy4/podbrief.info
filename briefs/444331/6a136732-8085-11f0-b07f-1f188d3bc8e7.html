This Past Weekend w/ Theo Von

#605 - Memphis Crisp

Key Takeaways

  • Theo Von shared observations from recent tour stops, including a detailed account of Philadelphia experiences.
  • The host attended Hulk Hogan's funeral in Florida, reflecting on the wrestler's personal impact and legacy.
  • News segments covered a missing Texas woman found with a Scottish tribe and an FDA warning about radioactive shrimp.
  • Discussions included modern dating challenges, personal battles with anger/alcohol, and insights from a Joaquin Phoenix interview.

Deep Dive

  • The host recounted a 'crazy couple of weeks of shows,' specifically detailing his experience in Philadelphia.
  • He described Philadelphia as a 'tough' city where children appeared to work early and everything was 'puffing.'
  • A dating experience with a Philadelphia woman involved her repeatedly inquiring about 'bottomless' food and drink options, including mimosas, raviolis, and even methadone.

  • The host attended Hulk Hogan's funeral in Largo, Florida, at Indian Rocks Baptist Church, alongside Kid Rock.
  • The service featured footage of Hogan's baptism and a large attendance including wrestling figures like Hacksaw, Jimmy Hart, Ric Flair, and Dennis Rodman.
  • He reflected on how wrestling, particularly Hulk Hogan, provided an escape and inspiration during his challenging childhood, impacting friends like Will Teague.

  • A Texas woman, previously reported missing, was found living with the 'Kingdom of Kubala,' an African tribe.
  • The tribe was located in Scottish woodlands and reportedly intended to reclaim land stolen 400 years prior.
  • The group was described as living a 'simple life' in nature with 'Zulu warrior type of energy' and grilling food.

  • The FDA warned the public against consuming Great Value raw frozen shrimp sold at Walmart.
  • The warning was due to potential contamination with cesium-137, a radioactive isotope.
  • U.S. Customs and Border Protection detected cesium-137 in shipping containers at four U.S. ports, leading to a recommendation to discard matching shrimp products.

  • A listener sought advice on a two-month relationship where her partner had not initiated a kiss, questioning his interest.
  • The host humorously suggested the man might be sick or that kissing is an outdated practice in current dating.
  • He attributed some male hesitation in dating and physical intimacy to the influence of the #MeToo movement.

  • A caller sought advice on managing anger and an alcohol problem negatively impacting his relationship.
  • The host acknowledged his own long-standing struggle with anger, identifying it as 'rage from being young' and 'unrealistic expectations of others' as an adult.
  • He stated he has addressed these issues through participation in a 12-step recovery program, emphasizing that 'nothing changes if nothing changes.'

  • The host discussed a past episode featuring Joaquin Phoenix, noting Phoenix's interest in a video on the Middle East.
  • He praised Phoenix's unique acting talent and unconventional lifestyle, reflecting on challenges of authentic conversation during interviews.
  • The host also mentioned physical boundaries in public interactions and recommended Phoenix's movie 'Eddington'.
