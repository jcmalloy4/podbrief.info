This Past Weekend w/ Theo Von

#614 - New York Circus

Key Takeaways

  • The host detailed personal challenges, including stopping antidepressants and dealing with emotional stress before his comedy special.
  • A viral Homeland Security video featuring the host caused public backlash and paranoia prior to his special taping.
  • The comedy special taping in New York City was reportedly disorganized, with the host experiencing manic symptoms and memory lapses.
  • The host clarified a controversial suicide joke and shared a personal story to promote mental health awareness.

Deep Dive

  • The circus arriving in Nashville is described as a 'rave for children,' deviating from traditional circuses of the host's youth.
  • Modern circus features progressive elements such as gender-neutral performers and lacks traditional animals.
  • The show also highlighted current dietary considerations, offering gluten-free options and focusing on peanut allergies.
  • The host reminisced about receiving circus tickets in school and observing animals in past circus experiences.

  • The host stopped taking antidepressants a month before taping his comedy special to experience a wider range of emotions for the performance.
  • He described the taping as messy and confusing, experiencing mild manic symptoms and physical discomfort on stage due to being off medication.
  • The first show began with a disorganized intro featuring Joey Diaz due to lack of rehearsal, and the host struggled with memory lapses during both shows.
  • Meeting Chris Rock backstage between shows inspired the host but also led to him feeling overexcited for the second performance.

  • The Department of Homeland Security utilized a year-and-a-half-old video of the host in a 'trap beat' production concerning evictions and deportations.
  • The viral video garnered millions of views across platforms, including the White House's social media, prominently featuring the host.
  • The host, whose father immigrated from Nicaragua, received hateful messages and felt paranoid, despite his lack of involvement or prior knowledge.
  • A government official offered the host extra security following the incident, which he found confusing and unnerving before his comedy special taping.

  • The host addressed audience reactions and media articles regarding a joke about suicide, clarifying he does not personally support suicide and has many reasons to live.
  • He acknowledged the performance was not perfect and expressed openness to reshooting the special.
  • The host assured listeners he is not in a dark place and uses humor as a coping mechanism for serious subjects, recognizing their gravity.
  • He shared the story of his friend's sister, Christianne, who died by suicide, emphasizing the importance of communicating distress.

  • The host recounted a previously undisclosed doctor's visit that caused distress.
  • An invasive comment from a nurse during the visit made the host question his safety and led to him feeling tearful when discussing it with his mother.
  • He clarified that he holds no ill will towards the nurse, believing her intentions were good, but the incident contributed to his overall stress.

  • Caller Tom expressed gratitude for the podcast, crediting it with providing laughter and helping him maintain sobriety for five and a half years after his son's suicide.
  • The host responded with empathy, acknowledging Tom's profound loss and expressing appreciation for the podcast's role in offering comfort.
  • He offered words of comfort and acknowledged the difficulty of finding appropriate responses to such personal tragedies.
