Office Ladies

Holiday Chit Chat with Kate Flannery

Key Takeaways

  • Jenna Fischer, Angela Kinsey, and Kate Flannery shared holiday memories and traditions.
  • Behind-the-scenes details of Meredith's bald cap transformation were revealed.
  • The hosts and guest debated their favorite Dunder Mifflin holiday party episodes.
  • Unique gift wrapping strategies, including a 'Lazy Genius' method, were discussed.

Deep Dive

  • Guest Kate Flannery discussed her holiday music preferences, referencing 'A Swinging Little Christmas'.
  • She also mentioned her touring schedule with actress Jane Lynch.

  • Historically, Christmas trees were decorated with edible items and often put up on Christmas Eve to preserve freshness, a practice extending into the Victorian era.
  • Some parents maintained a tradition of Santa Claus bringing the tree, decorating it overnight for children.
  • A survey indicated most people put up trees between December 2nd and 23rd, with one in four decorating on December 1st.

  • Jenna Fischer showed behind-the-scenes photos of Angela Kinsey from the Meredith bald cap episode.
  • Makeup artist Ed French spent 4.5 hours applying the realistic bald cap and 3 hours for removal.
  • Show photographer Chris Hasten captured many of these behind-the-scenes images.

  • Kate Flannery discussed Meredith Palmer's memorable holiday moments, including her New Year's resolution about drinking.
  • Flannery noted the show's strong writing foundation allowed for improvised lines, specifically Meredith removing her top.

  • Jenna Fischer noted her father, an engineer, meticulously wraps gifts, aligning paper prints for seamless transitions.
  • Pam's talking head about using only three pieces of tape per gift was recalled.
  • Kendra Adachi from 'The Lazy Genius' watches the entire 'Lord of the Rings' trilogy annually while wrapping presents.

  • The hosts and guest discussed a 40-minute YouTube compilation of Michael Scott ruining holidays.
  • A clip from the compilation was played, featuring scenes from 'Christmas Cancelled' and 'Phyllis' Santa'.

  • The hosts discussed the 'Moroccan Christmas' episode, highlighting Meredith's intervention.
  • Kate Flannery detailed her only improvised scene where Michael Scott drags Meredith to rehab, noting chaotic filming.
  • Director Paul Feig supported the improvised dialogue and filming process for the scene.

  • The podcast posed a question to listeners: which Dunder Mifflin holiday party would they attend?
  • Options included 'Yankee Swap,' 'Bennihana Christmas,' 'Moroccan Christmas,' and 'Secret Santa.'
  • Jenna Fischer favored the first Christmas episode, while Angela Kinsey preferred 'Beni Hana Christmas' for its dual parties and alcohol.
