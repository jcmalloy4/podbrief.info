Introducing Simpsons Declassified with Nancy Cartwright

Key Takeaways

  • Nancy Cartwright, voice of Bart Simpson, hosts new podcast 'Simpsons Declassified'.
  • Podcast provides behind-the-scenes stories and fan theories from 'The Simpsons' 35-year history.

Deep Dive

  • The new podcast, 'Simpsons Declassified,' is hosted by Nancy Cartwright, known as the voice of Bart Simpson for over 35 years.
  • It aims to provide an all-access pass to 'The Simpsons,' covering its 36 seasons and more than 800 episodes.
  • Content includes exclusive behind-the-scenes stories, wild predictions, and fan conspiracies.

  • Host Nancy Cartwright will delve into how 'The Simpsons' has predicted future events.
  • The podcast explores the inner workings of creating iconic fictional characters.
  • It features exclusive stories from creators, performers, and celebrity superfans who were involved with the series.
