Second Drink: The Negotiation with David Denman

Key Takeaways

  • Actor David Denman offered insights into Roy Anderson's character and departure from the show.
  • The episode "The Negotiation" highlighted Michael Scott's unusual negotiation tactics and personal moments.
  • Behind-the-scenes details included wardrobe specifics and actor off-camera memories.
  • Key character interactions, particularly Pam's emotional arc, were a central focus.
  • Discussions extended to fan theories and movie tangents, including Keanu Reeves's filmography.

Deep Dive

  • A fan identified actor Nicholas DeGosto, who played Hunter in "The Office," also appeared in the TV show "Heroes."
  • The discussion covered how actors appearing on one successful NBC show might later be cast on others.

  • Actor David Denman was introduced to the British "The Office" by a friend before auditioning for the American version.
  • Denman secured the role of Roy Anderson, acknowledging its significant career impact.
  • He was among the first cast members, attending the initial table read, and had a unique Polaroid taken by casting director Allison Jones.
  • Denman envisioned Roy and Pam's engagement as a passive progression, with Roy realizing his feelings only when he was on the verge of losing her.

  • Guest David Denman shared an off-camera memory from "Booze Cruise": a sunrise boat ride with Steve Carell.
  • He discussed a fan theory regarding a secret relationship between Angela and Roy as a potential unwritten storyline.
  • Denman recounted discussions with Craig Robinson about participating in a "muddy buddy" obstacle race involving electrocution.

  • David Denman discussed the emotional experience of filming his last scene on "The Office."
  • He kept the original jacket from his audition, which was later replicated for the warehouse staff's costume.
  • Denman is currently involved in projects such as the movie "Greenland" and the series "Mayor of East Town" starring Kate Winslet.

  • Angela expressed missing the fight between Roy and Dwight, prompting Oscar to describe Dwight's actions in detail.
  • Angela was visibly affected and aroused by Dwight's "gallant behavior" during the confrontation.
  • The hosts connected this reaction to a future plot point involving two men dueling over her, suggesting a character pattern.
  • Angela wore a heart-shaped necklace she used to convey emotion, clarifying it was a wardrobe item, not a gift from Dwight.

  • Daryl attempted to negotiate a raise with Michael, who used delaying tactics like changing meeting locations and observing silence.
  • Michael's negotiation style contrasted with Daryl's direct approach, which featured clear objectives.
  • Daryl questioned Michael's suit as "lady clothes"; Michael claimed it was found in a bin and humorously speculated about its "bisexual" or "European" cut, noting the buttons were on the wrong side.
  • The wardrobe designer confirmed Steve Carell was fitted with a real women's suit, and an identical one was sourced for a background actress.

  • Pam experienced heartbreak in the breakroom, underscored by Josh Compton's song, "Heartbreak in the Breakroom."
  • The hosts analyzed Pam's vulnerability after declaring her relationship with Roy was over.
  • Jim's perceived "cold reaction" was interpreted as an attempt to protect himself from further emotional distress.

  • The hosts discussed the movie "Speed," drawing parallels to an episode car scene and praising its action, romance, and Keanu Reeves's performance.
  • They also mentioned "Point Break," directed by Kathryn Bigelow and starring Reeves, highlighting a notable foot chase scene.
  • One host expressed intent to watch "John Wick" and discussed other Reeves films including "Bill & Ted" and "The Matrix."
  • A friend's experience kissing Keanu Reeves in "Bill & Ted: Face the Music" was also shared.

  • The hosts discussed Nicholas D'Agosto's role in "Final Destination 5," leading to a conversation about the franchise.
  • They clarified the films involve characters who survive a catastrophe due only to a premonition, but are subsequently killed by elaborate accidents.
  • The movies do not involve asteroids or science fiction elements, focusing instead on death hunting those who cheated it.

  • Michael successfully negotiated a raise with Jan, securing an increase despite struggling to understand Jan's strategy of asking for 15% to receive 12%.
  • Daryl also received a raise as a result of the negotiation.
  • Jim witnessed Dwight kiss Angela in the office bathroom, leading to discussions about intentional staging for actor proximity.
  • Jim vowed to remain silent regarding the discovery of Dwight and Angela's secret relationship.
