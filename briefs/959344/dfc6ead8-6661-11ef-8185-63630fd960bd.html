Office Ladies

Office Meet Cutes

Key Takeaways

  • The "Office Ladies" podcast won a Webby Award for Best Comedy Podcast (People's Voice Award 2025).
  • Michael Scott's condo from "The Office" is currently listed for sale in Reseda, California.
  • The Office Ladies Network is expanding with a new podcast, 'How We Made Your Mother'.
  • Michael Scott had the most romantic relationships of any character on "The Office."
  • The episode explored the origin of the term "meet cute" and its examples in "The Office."
  • Listeners and the hosts shared personal stories of how they met their romantic partners.

Deep Dive

  • The hosts discussed movie 'meet-cutes,' with Angela highlighting a scene from 'Crazy Stupid Love' where Ryan Gosling's character charms Emma Stone's character.
  • The conversation included a personal anecdote about a "scooch famous" friend's memorable encounter with a very famous and attractive co-star, describing the experience as exceeding expectations.

  • Michael Scott had the most romances on "The Office," including major relationships with Jan Levinson and Holly Flax.
  • The top ten fan-favorite romantic scenes from "The Office" included Jim's video to Pam, Dwight's proposal to Angela, and Michael's proposal to Holly.

  • Dwight Schrute's chaotic proposal to Angela Martin involved driving her off the road and a dramatic declaration of love.
  • He accepted her son, Philip, as part of the proposal.
  • The hosts praised the writing and emotional impact of this scene, as well as Jim's toast to Pam.

  • Rachel M. from Los Angeles shared her story of meeting her now-husband at her first office job in 2008, inspired by Pam Beesly's arc to leave an unhappy relationship.
  • Ana C. from Brazil met her husband in 2021 through a Reddit post for an "Office"-themed birthday party, which inspired her to move to Belgium.

  • Jenna Fischer explained the term "meet cute" originated from the 1938 film "Bluebeard's Eighth Wife," and was used in "The Office" episode "Training Day."
  • The hosts analyzed Kelly Kapoor's manufactured "meet cute" with D'Angelo Vickers, where she dropped files for his attention, to his obliviousness.

  • Podcast team members shared their favorite movie meet-cutes, with Sam detailing the "As you wish" exchange between Buttercup and Westley from "The Princess Bride."
  • Cassie highlighted the premise of "The Holiday," where two women swap homes and find love.

  • The hosts discussed the movie "Moonstruck," detailing the meet-cute between Cher's character and Nicholas Cage's character, a baker who lost a hand.
  • The conversation highlighted the character's belief that love is messy and destructive, contrasting with fairy tale notions.

  • Kaylee from Wisconsin shared her "first kiss and last kiss" story, meeting her husband in kindergarten.
  • They bonded over tractors and shared a memorable, albeit embarrassing, kiss on the school bus.

  • Jenna Fischer shared her personal meet-cute with her husband, Josh, whom she first noticed as a parent at her daughter's preschool during story time.
  • She later encountered Josh again when volunteering for a Lego-building event for Star Wars models.
  • Angela Kinsey recounted reconnecting with her husband after knowing him for 10 years, with their relationship evolving during movie production.
