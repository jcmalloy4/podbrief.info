Office Ladies

Friday Chit Chat 7

Key Takeaways

  • Angela Kinsey detailed her five-day food journal for New York Magazine's Grub Street Diet.
  • Podcast producer Cassie Jerkins shared plans for upcoming improv and puppetry performances.
  • Jenna Fischer provided an update on her breast cancer survivorship journey, one year post-diagnosis.
  • The Office Ladies podcast introduced a new segment for listener-submitted 'Chit Chat Ideas'.
  • Jenna Fischer discussed the challenges and incidents encountered while traveling with pets.

Deep Dive

  • Macy's Fall Fashion Guide now incorporates home decor items for the autumn season.
  • Specific highlighted products include floral bedding, kitchenware, and tabletop accessories.
  • Brands such as Fileroy and Boch were featured within the fall home collection.

  • Angela Kinsey is participating in a five-day food journal for New York Magazine's Grub Street Diet.
  • The food journal commenced on a Southwest Airlines flight from Chicago to Burbank.
  • This feature release coincides with the upcoming publication of her and Josh's cookbook.

  • Cassie Jerkins announced upcoming participation in an improv show and puppetry for a DD show.
  • She will be performing with the comedy group Asian AF.
  • Jerkins detailed her background in puppetry, including building and performing with puppets.

  • Jenna Fischer sought listener experiences regarding flying with pets, sharing her own difficulties.
  • She described the complexities of pet travel, including specific security procedures.
  • Fischer recounted a past incident where her cat, Andy, escaped his carrier during airport security.

  • October marks one year since Jenna Fischer announced her breast cancer diagnosis.
  • She stressed the critical importance of early detection through self-exams and mammograms.
  • Fischer has completed Herceptin infusions, had her port removed, and is currently on tamoxifen.
  • She manages medication side effects with methods like weightlifting, acupuncture, and lifestyle changes.

  • The hosts announced a new podcast segment called 'Chit Chat Ideas' for future episodes.
  • Listeners are invited to submit slice-of-life topic suggestions for discussion.
  • A swipe-up link for submission will be made available on the 'Office Ladies' Instagram page.
