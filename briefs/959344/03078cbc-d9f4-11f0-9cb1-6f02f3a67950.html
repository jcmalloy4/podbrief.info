Office Ladies

Friday Chit Chat 8

Key Takeaways

  • The hosts initiated the first "Friday Chit Chat" of 2026, focusing on "little fixes, big wins."
  • Fan questions explored ideal date scenarios and the ethical dilemma of returning shopping carts.
  • The podcast announced a producer transition, with Cassie Jerkins departing and Matt Beagle joining.

Deep Dive

  • The 'Office Ladies' hosts launched their first 'Friday Chit Chat' of 2026.
  • They introduced a shared resolution to adopt a 'little fixes, big wins' approach to achieving goals.
  • One host cited replacing dingy guest bathroom hand towels as an example of a small task yielding unexpected happiness.

  • A fan question from Freya in Phoenixville, Pennsylvania, prompted discussions on the hosts' ideal dates with their significant others.
  • One host shared their "Movie Monday" tradition involving daytime matinee showings and dinner in a food court.
  • The other host described an ideal weekend getaway: a morning hike, lunch in a local town, hotel relaxation, movie, and early bedtime.

  • Peter from Chandler, Arizona, submitted a fan question regarding the social obligation to return shopping carts.
  • The hosts referred to Mike Scher's book, "The Correct Answer to Every Moral Question," which covers this topic.
  • The 'shopping cart theory of morality' posits that returning a cart indicates an individual's capacity for self-governance.

  • The discussion continued regarding the 'shopping cart theory of morality,' with both hosts confirming they always return carts.
  • The Instagram account 'Cartnarks,' which shames individuals for not returning shopping carts, was mentioned.
  • The hosts concluded that returning carts is still expected, even when stores remove dedicated cart returns, potentially due to labor cuts.

  • Producer Cassie Jerkins is departing 'Office Ladies' after five years; her last day in the studio was today.
  • Cassie started as a transcriber and became a producer in November 2020.
  • Matt Beagle was introduced as the new senior podcast producer, taking over from Cassie.
