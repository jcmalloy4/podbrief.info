Office Ladies

Friday Chit Chat 4

Key Takeaways

  • First-time perspectives can offer fresh insights - Jenna's unique take on Lord of the Rings as a "survivalist zombie film in a fantasy world" demonstrates how approaching beloved content without preconceptions can reveal new dimensions.
  • Personal connections amplify entertainment experiences - The hosts' relationships with cast members like Elijah Wood and Sean Astin, plus their son's deep engagement with the franchise, show how personal ties transform passive consumption into meaningful relationships.
  • Mindful ownership reflects deeper values - Josh's meticulous care of his new car represents a thoughtful alternative to throwaway culture, suggesting that how we treat our possessions can be a form of intentional living.
  • Life transitions deserve recognition - Whether it's Josh moving from a family minivan to an "adult" car or fostering rescue kittens, the hosts celebrate meaningful moments that might otherwise go unnoticed in daily life.

Deep Dive

Lord of the Rings Discussion and Personal Connections

The conversation begins with an enthusiastic discussion about The Lord of the Rings/The Hobbit, sparked by Jenna's recent first-time viewing of the film at age 51. She found the movie compelling and offers a unique perspective, describing it as "a quest, survivalist, zombie film set in a fantasy world."

Jenna's favorite movie elements included:

  • The elaborate birthday party in the Shire
  • Impressive fireworks sequences
  • Hobbits eating cheese
  • The concept of "second breakfast"
  • The frightening orcs
The discussion takes a personal turn as they share family connections to the franchise:
  • Their son Jack previously learned Elvish and requested a Legolas wig
  • Jack would play in the front yard dressed as the character
  • They have personal relationships with cast members Elijah Wood and Sean Astin
  • Sean Astin recorded a birthday cameo for their friend
  • Elijah Wood created a birthday video specifically for their son Jack
Character analysis focuses on Sam, whom they highlight as "the truest friend," discussing his unwavering loyalty and specifically referencing a scene where Sam tries to help Frodo.

New Zealand Travel Aspirations

The Lord of the Rings conversation naturally transitions to New Zealand's official hiking trail based on the films. The speakers express genuine interest in potentially organizing an "Office Ladies" trip to walk the Shire path, showing how their podcast could incorporate travel content.

Josh's New Car Journey

The discussion shifts to a detailed exploration of Josh's recent car purchase - a new Kia that represents a significant life transition from their long-time family minivan to an "adult" car.

Josh's meticulous car care routine includes:

  • Personal car washing (no professional services)
  • Using a special "floofy" duster/cleaning tool multiple times daily
  • Constant vigilance about pollen and cleanliness
  • Installing a special trash bag/caddy system in the interior
The speaker appreciates Josh's careful attention to his new vehicle, viewing his behavior as a positive contrast to "throwaway culture." She notes the symbolic importance of this transition from a kid-focused family vehicle and mentions plans to monitor how frequently he continues his cleaning routine. The fact that they park outside without a garage adds context to his extra care efforts.

Current Life Updates: Foster Kittens

The conversation concludes with current fostering responsibilities - the speaker is caring for 4 kittens plus 1 adult cat and 1 dog, creating a household of 6 animals and 4 people. She humorously describes the kittens as "zombie-like" in their movement and behavior, drawing a comparison to The Last of Us fungus.

The "Modern Family" litter adoption details:

  • Kitten names: Manny, Claire, Haley, and Chinny D (Phil)
  • Located in Los Angeles
  • Available through kittenrescue.org
  • Actively seeking adoptive homes
The segment ends with standard podcast production credits for Office Ladies, produced by Jenna Fisher and Angela Kinsey, with Executive Producer Cassie Jerkins and Audio Engineer Sam Kiefer, including a noted emotional "aww" reaction from Cassie during the kitten discussion.
