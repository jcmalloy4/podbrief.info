All About Creed Bratton

Key Takeaways

  • A fan-compiled "Creed Bratton Fact List" proved more comprehensive than the show bible.
  • Creed Bratton clarified numerous character details, distinguishing fiction from his real life.
  • Actor Creed Bratton discussed his band history with The Grass Roots and shared "The Office" set stories.
  • The real Creed Bratton revealed his creative routines, travel, and upcoming international tour dates.
  • Discussions covered Creed's post-series fate, with the actor suggesting an escape from prison.

Deep Dive

  • A fan-compiled "Creed Bratton Fact List," created over six years by a family from Sydney, Australia, was deemed more comprehensive than the official show bible.
  • The official show bible revealed the character's alias as William Charles Schneider and detailed his penchant for crime and shenanigans.
  • Additional character details from the show bible included his history of transferring debt.

  • The "Office Ladies team" shared favorite Creed moments, including the line "Someone making soup?" from season two.
  • Favorite scenes included Creed claiming asthma to scuba dive in season four and immediately running away during "Murder."
  • Additional highlights included Creed's New Year's resolution to do a cartwheel in season seven and his remarks on cults.

  • The character Creed Bratton originated from writers discovering him as a background actor, leading to the development of his unique persona.
  • The "Creed Thoughts" blog, active since June 26, 2008, was a fan area of interest.
  • A blog post detailed Creed's uniform preference: navy blue sweatpants, a leather vest, and comfy slippers.

  • The hosts prepared to interview Creed Bratton about the blurred lines between his real life and his "Office" character.
  • Questions focused on distinguishing fictional elements from the actor's actual experiences.
  • The discussion anticipated clarifying various character details with the real Creed Bratton.

  • Creed Bratton confirmed his membership in The Grass Roots band, releasing four albums in four years.
  • He clarified that the "Wacky Weed Creed" radio show was a character detail, not a real experience.
  • Bratton confirmed growing and eating mung beans and is a grandfather of two children, Bo and Ami.

  • Creed Bratton confirmed he has ten toes, debunking the four-toe claim, which was an ad-libbed story for "The Office."
  • He recounted a broken toe incident in Scandinavia, which inspired a scene where his character was homeless.
  • Bratton stated he once caught two squirrels with his bare hands while working on fire breaks.

  • Creed Bratton enjoyed his spotlight role as acting manager in season seven, particularly driving the Porsche.
  • Many of his deleted scenes, including a guitar performance on a 'boost cruise,' are available in Peacock's Superfan episodes.
  • Bratton clarified his unique right-handed but left-handed guitar technique and why live music was mimed on set due to logistical challenges.

  • Creed Bratton kept a samurai warrior figurine and his tailored finale suit from "The Office" set.
  • His first entertainment job in 1964 was as an extra in "Cast a Giant Shadow" with Frank Sinatra and John Wayne.
  • Sinatra reportedly saved Bratton from injury by pulling him from an exploding wall on the 1964 film set.
  • Bratton primarily plays guitar, piano, mandolin, and trumpet, which was his first instrument.

  • Creed Bratton's writing routine includes an hour of writing daily, starting with coffee and gluten-free muffins.
  • He uses a "cheat sheet" from his day planner for journaling and task accomplishment.
  • Bratton announced upcoming international tour dates in October for Hawaii, New Zealand, Australia, Singapore, and Japan.
  • He maintains a 15-year tradition of hosting "Yankee Swap" Christmas parties for "The Office" cast.
