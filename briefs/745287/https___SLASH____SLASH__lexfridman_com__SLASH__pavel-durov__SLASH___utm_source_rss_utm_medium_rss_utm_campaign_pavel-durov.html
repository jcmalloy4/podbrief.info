#482 – Pavel Durov: Telegram, Freedom, Censorship, Money, Power & Human Nature

Key Takeaways

  • Pavel Durov advocates for freedom of speech and user privacy as paramount principles for Telegram's operation.
  • Durov practices an ascetic, self-disciplined lifestyle, avoiding addictive substances, processed foods, and distractions like phones.
  • Telegram operates with a lean engineering team of approximately 40 people, prioritizing efficiency and automation.
  • Telegram employs distributed infrastructure and strong encryption, making it impossible to share private user messages with governments.
  • The platform has faced significant government pressure and ban attempts in countries like Russia and Iran, which it resisted to protect user freedom.
  • Telegram achieved profitability in 2024 through a subscription model and context-based ads, without exploiting user data.
  • Durov is an early investor in Bitcoin and believes in its long-term value as a censorship-resistant asset.
  • Durov's education emphasized intensive math and language studies, fostering logical thinking and competitive drive.

Deep Dive

  • Pavel Durov values freedom above money, citing childhood experiences in the Soviet Union versus Italy.
  • He identifies fear and greed as freedom's primary enemies, advocating confronting worst-case scenarios to overcome fear.
  • Durov believes living by principles is more valuable than a longer life lived in servitude, viewing freedom as essential for contribution.
  • He minimizes external negative influences, including 20 years of abstinence from alcohol, tobacco, coffee, and drugs, crediting it for his success.

  • Durov advocates for self-analysis, avoiding short-term pleasures like processed foods and alcohol, and not relying on painkillers.
  • He emphasizes understanding incentives behind media consumption to avoid manipulation and maintain independent thought.
  • Durov adopts a strategic, long-term life approach, prioritizing future well-being over immediate gratification.
  • Telegram operates with a lean philosophy, employing a core engineering team of approximately 40 people for its vast infrastructure.

  • Durov observes governments build larger bureaucracies, leading to gradual censorship and limitations on freedoms and free markets.
  • He was arrested in France in August of the previous year on 15 serious charges related to alleged user actions on Telegram.
  • Durov spent four days in police custody, noting investigators' limited understanding of technology and encryption.
  • He likened the bureaucratic legal situation to absurd, Kafka-esque scenarios where individuals are caught without clear understanding.

  • Following his arrest, French intelligence requested Telegram remove specific channels related to Moldovan elections, which Telegram did if rules were violated.
  • Durov refused a broader request to remove channels expressing political views, suspecting ulterior motives from French intelligence.
  • He resisted systematic attempts by the French government to censor political voices, upholding Telegram's commitment to freedom of speech.
  • Telegram protects all users who do not call for violence, regardless of their political affiliation or whether they are peaceful protesters.

  • Durov experienced intensive education at an experimental school in St. Petersburg at age 11, focusing on math and foreign languages.
  • As a teenager, he set an ambitious goal to achieve top marks in every subject, requiring intense effort and studying during breaks.
  • He emphasizes mathematics' role in shaping the brain, teaching logical thinking, problem decomposition, and perseverance.
  • Math education fosters independent thinking, problem-solving, and is comparable to programming and entrepreneurship.

  • Pavel Durov was the sole employee during VK's early development, handling all aspects including backend, frontend, design, and customer support.
  • VK initially used PHP and MySQL, later optimizing with Memcached and Nginx, with critical assistance from his brother Nikolai Durov.
  • As VK scaled to over a million users and dozens of servers, Nikolai provided algorithmic and engineering solutions, including database sharding.
  • This experience taught Durov efficiency and the ability of a single person to achieve significant results, applied later to Telegram.

  • Telegram pioneered features like the auto-delete timer in 2013 and message replies with visual indicators, later copied by other apps.
  • Its aesthetic design, including subtle animations and dynamic background gradients, enhances user experience without sacrificing clarity.
  • Implementing these technically challenging design elements efficiently across diverse devices is a key focus.
  • Telegram uses complex, infinitely scalable, vector-based patterns with four shifting colors for chat backgrounds, allowing user customization.

  • Telegram introduced end-to-end encryption in secret chats in 2013, predating competitors like WhatsApp.
  • Secret chats include additional security measures like preventing screenshots and message forwarding, offering distinct utility from standard chats.
  • Telegram is the only popular messaging app with reproducible builds for Android and iOS, allowing users to verify its source code integrity.
  • The team builds its entire software stack internally, minimizing reliance on external open-source libraries to reduce attack surface and enhance efficiency, informed by Edward Snowden's revelations.

  • Durov confirmed an alleged assassination attempt via poisoning in the spring of 2018 while fundraising for the TON blockchain.
  • He attributes a near-death experience in France to a neighbor, which occurred amidst government attempts to ban Telegram.
  • This event, combined with an earlier existential threat related to his work, paradoxically led to a greater sense of freedom.
  • Following recovery, Durov realized he could no longer operate freely in Russia, intensifying Telegram's geopolitical pressures.

  • During the Ukraine war, Durov considered suspending political channels to prevent Telegram's use as a war tool.
  • Users from both Ukraine and Russia objected, asserting their right to access information and form their own conclusions.
  • Durov compares his leadership to Elon Musk's, noting that personal traits like emotionality can drive strong vision and commitment.
  • He believes humanity's diverse traits complement each other, with perceived negative traits often being sources of strength.

  • The Open Network (TON), an open-source blockchain, powers Telegram's tokenized usernames, accounts, and smart contracts.
  • TON facilitates third-party developer earnings withdrawals and in-platform transactions like ad purchases.
  • Telegram 'gifts,' socially relevant NFTs built on TON, became a top blockchain for daily NFT trading volumes within six months.
  • Snoop Dogg 'gifts' generated $12 million in sales within 30 minutes and have secondary market prices exceeding $10,000 per item.
  • Durov emphasizes user-benefiting monetization, enabling individuals to earn millions through Telegram's mini apps and gifts.

  • The conversation references the 'Universe 25' mouse experiment, where conditions of abundance led to social collapse and extinction.
  • This experiment illustrates potential negative impacts of excessive comfort and lack of scarcity on a society.
  • Durov and the host reflect on how future abundance, possibly driven by AI, could challenge human society.
  • They emphasize the need for self-restriction and purpose to navigate potential issues arising from extreme comfort.
