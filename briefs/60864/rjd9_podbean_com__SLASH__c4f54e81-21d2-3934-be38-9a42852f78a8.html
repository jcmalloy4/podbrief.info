Key Takeaways

  • Stratum Capital, managing over $1 billion, focuses on B2B software with a hyper-transparent investment process.
  • The firm employs a 'five-point plan' to provide clear, actionable guidance for portfolio company growth.
  • AI is viewed as an 'invention' currently undergoing 'innovation' through business model experimentation and application.
  • AI integration has driven efficiency gains and enhanced product capabilities for Stratum's portfolio businesses.

Deep Dive

  • Host RJ Lumba introduced Bob Morse, co-founder and managing partner at Stratum Capital, a private equity firm.
  • Stratum Capital manages over $1 billion in assets, specializing in B2B software and technology companies.
  • Morse's background includes roles at Oak Hill Capital Partners, GCC Investments, and Morgan Stanley, and he has been recognized as a top software investor.

  • Stratum Capital was founded to add more value post-global financial crisis, emphasizing a hyper-transparent investment process.
  • The firm positions itself as a better choice for founders than traditional private equity, prioritizing belief in founders and their industry experience.
  • Stratum cultivates a culture of continuous improvement, questioning existing methods, and valuing founders who solve customer problems with new technologies.

  • Stratum utilizes a 'five-point plan' agreed upon before a deal signs, limiting actionable items to five from an initial list of 50.
  • This plan is written in the founder's voice and communicated to the team, providing clarity and direction to reduce employee stress during control investments.
  • A specific example showed a founder-led business's revenue grow from $10 million to $54 million within four years by implementing a demand-generation strategy outlined in the plan.

  • The guest proposed a framework distinguishing 'invention' (a new capability like AI) from 'innovation' (market introduction and business models around a capability).
  • Companies are currently in a phase of experimenting with AI business models, drawing an analogy to the application of the steam engine to ships.
  • The focus is on adapting AI to deep industry expertise to solve customer problems, rather than simply delivering AI tools.

  • Stratum Capital seeks founders who prioritize their innovative spark and customer focus when considering a capital partner.
  • The firm offers a bespoke investment approach, emphasizing product, transparency, and empowering founders with choices, often without banker involvement.
  • Stratum gravitates towards vertical-specific software, with investments in supply chain solutions and organizations with extended constituent bases, such as government, hospitals, and universities.
