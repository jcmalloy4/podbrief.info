Key Takeaways

  • Adam Street Partners manages over $60 billion in global private market investments.
  • The firm prioritizes growth equity in specific tech and healthcare sectors.
  • Adam Street actively supports portfolio companies in revenue growth and human capital.
  • Agentic AI is viewed as a transformative technology for enterprise productivity.
  • Division 1 athletic discipline develops crucial skills for investment success.

Deep Dive

  • Adam Street Partners is a global private markets investment manager with $62 billion in assets under management.
  • The firm, founded 50 years ago, manages $60 billion across five distinct strategies, benefiting from collective insights.
  • Approximately $30 billion is managed in Funds of Funds investing in early-stage venture capital and buy-out funds globally.
  • The remaining $30 billion is invested directly into companies, focusing on growth equity.
  • Adam Street has scaled from a small Chicago office to 350 employees in 12 global offices.

  • Adam Street focuses on growth investments within four key sectors: application software, infrastructure software (cybersecurity, DevOps), fintech, and healthcare technology.
  • The firm seeks companies with a repeatable and scalable business model that have achieved product-market fit and an 'unfair advantage'.
  • Investment analysis prioritizes the CEO's trajectory and personal growth, alongside the business's financial profile, including growth rates, scale, and unit economics.
  • The objective is to identify companies at an inflection point to achieve attractive risk-reward profiles.
  • Understanding management team traits like vision, passion, and drive is crucial for securing top talent in competitive markets.

  • Adam Street Partners actively assists portfolio companies, acting as the opposite of a passive asset manager, even without always holding board seats.
  • Leveraging over 50 years of private market investing experience, the firm has developed internal resources for scaling companies.
  • Support focuses on two key areas: facilitating revenue growth and enhancing human capital.
  • For revenue growth, Adam Street utilizes its extensive global network to make warm introductions to potential customers and partners.
  • In human capital, the firm maintains a curated list of C-suite executives to advise or join portfolio companies in leadership roles.

  • The guest expresses excitement about Agentic AI, a next wave of generative AI, and its potential to create dynamic systems that complete complex objectives.
  • Startups integrating AI to make employees 10 times more productive, accurate, or creative are identified as future successes.
  • Glean, an enterprise search platform powered by generative AI, is highlighted as a portfolio example.
  • Glean connects to all enterprise data to help users find, create, or automate, aiming to become a general-purpose assistant for knowledge workers.

  • The guest, a former Division 1 water polo athlete at UC Berkeley, balanced a rigorous practice and academic schedule.
  • A strong correlation exists between the demands of D1 athletics and professional success, particularly in the investment ecosystem.
  • Transferable skills include persistence, effective time management, operating in ambiguity, and self-motivation.
  • These skills are crucial for navigating the investment ecosystem, which often features long feedback cycles.
