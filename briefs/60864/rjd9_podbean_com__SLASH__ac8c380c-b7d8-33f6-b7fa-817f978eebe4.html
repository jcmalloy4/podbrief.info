Key Takeaways

  • Altamont differentiates through sector expertise and a "transform and scale" business-building strategy.
  • The firm frequently builds businesses from scratch, a unique approach in private equity.
  • Altamont prioritizes a culture of business building, focusing on increasing fundamental company value.
  • Philanthropic efforts include the Altamont Foundation and support for employee-wide ownership initiatives.

Deep Dive

  • Altamont differentiates itself in a competitive private equity market through sector expertise.
  • The firm employs a 'transform and scale' business-building strategy.
  • This strategy includes transforming businesses not yet PE platforms into scalable entities, then driving growth through organic means and acquisitions.
  • Altamont partners with management teams, often as the first institutional capital, using repeatable sector playbooks.

  • Altamont uniquely builds businesses from scratch, a strategy implemented about a dozen times since 2002.
  • A playbook for these startups includes entity formation, regulatory licensing, recruiting management teams, and setting up basic operations.
  • Accelerant, a digital risk exchange, grew from three executives and an idea in early 2019 to a global business writing nearly $4 billion in premium.
  • Accelerant recently had a successful IPO on the NYSE, a rare achievement for a mid-market private equity firm with a sub-billion-dollar fund.
  • This "build from scratch" approach is applied to proven operators in market dislocations, not just product-market fit, occurring 1-2 times per fund.

  • Altamont utilizes "Altamont Ascend," a portfolio operations capability with internal and external operating partners.
  • These experts provide functional expertise in areas like finance, IT, and HR, aiding in value creation and talent recruitment.
  • The firm maintains flexible capital deployment, not budgeting by sector, allowing pivots between industries such as financial services and industrials.
  • This flexible approach results in a balanced allocation across sectors in any given fund, driven by market conditions and deal flow.

  • Altamont's culture prioritizes business building over financial engineering, focusing on fundamentally increasing company value, revenue, and EBITDA.
  • This approach has contributed to consistent returns across various economic cycles.
  • The firm actively recruits and develops team-oriented individuals, often former athletes, who thrive in collaborative environments.
  • Altamont emphasizes its deep, talented, and aligned team, with over 50 investment professionals and partners averaging over 12 years of tenure.
