Key Takeaways

  • The firm focuses on sectors with identified opportunities, avoiding commodity-heavy or regulated industries.

Deep Dive

  • Transom Capital Group specializes in complex middle-market companies, corporate carve-outs, and lender-owned businesses.
  • The firm utilizes an in-house operations team to transform and grow companies across various sectors.
  • Transom focuses on sectors with identified opportunity or discontinuity, avoiding heavily commodity-exposed or regulated industries.
  • Specific expertise areas include hardware technology, IT services, and specialty distribution.

  • Transom implements five core operational transformations: driving revenue growth, improving gross margin, and reducing costs.
  • Revenue growth strategies include Salesforce implementation, digital transformation, and new product innovation.
  • Gross margin improvement is achieved through supply chain optimization.
  • Cost reduction focuses on SG&A analysis, with a sixth area dedicated to talent acquisition via a proprietary methodology.

  • Transom acquired Pelco, a security camera and software provider, as a corporate carve-out facing declining revenue and high SG&A costs.
  • A new sales-focused CEO, Kurt Takahashi, was installed, stabilizing and growing revenue within 12-15 months.
  • A significant supply chain overhaul unified US-based engineering and Taiwan-based manufacturing teams, accelerating product development.

  • An acquisition example demonstrated a $10 million reduction in SG&A costs within 60 days through responsible operation.
  • The business was ultimately sold to Motorola within 15 months following these operational improvements.
  • A strategic calculus was present from the beginning, identifying a viable customer base and potential for resale to strategic buyers.

  • Transom's model involves acquiring complex businesses, performing "heavy lifting," and selling to strategic acquirers; all sales in the last five years have been to strategics.
  • The guest states there is limited competition for their specific investment niche, with prospective acquisitions receiving fewer Letters of Intent.
  • Transom's advantage includes flexibility to operate across diverse industries like hardware technology, audio, security, specialty distribution, and education.
