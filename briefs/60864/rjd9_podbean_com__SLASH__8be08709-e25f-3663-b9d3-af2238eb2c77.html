Key Takeaways

  • Long Ridge Equity Partners, a 2024 top growth equity firm, specializes in financial and business technology.
  • The firm's 15-year track record and internal promotion culture attract discerning founders.
  • Strategic investments target GRC, cybersecurity, and unique niche 'end-of-one' businesses.
  • The guest stresses interpersonal fit and strong partnerships for company growth and investment success.

Deep Dive

  • Long Ridge Equity Partners, specializing in financial and business technology, was founded in 2007.
  • The firm aims to provide growth capital and operational expertise to companies in its target sectors.
  • A strong culture of continuity exists, with all senior staff promoted internally from junior positions.

  • Long Ridge partnered with Broadway Technology, a provider of trading infrastructure for global banks.
  • After 1.5 years of relationship building, the firm invested in 2018.
  • They professionalized the C-suite, bringing in a new president and COO, and augmented go-to-market functions.
  • This led to a successful transaction, highlighting strategic importance in FinTech.

  • The firm specializes in governance, risk, and compliance software, and cybersecurity within financial technology.
  • They also target "end-of-one" businesses dominating niche, strategically important markets.
  • These 'end-of-one' companies are cultivated for future strategic value, despite potentially small Total Addressable Markets.

  • The guest's firm attracts founders by emphasizing integrity, strong partnerships, and a concentrated investment approach.
  • Firm members demonstrate personal alignment by investing over 10% of their own capital in each fund.
  • This strategy aims to differentiate them in the competitive growth equity market.

  • The guest's father influenced him, imparting that 90% of life's happiness comes from choosing the right partner.
  • A deeply personal cause for the guest is pediatric cancer research.
  • This cause is motivated by his daughter's survival from stage four kidney cancer.
