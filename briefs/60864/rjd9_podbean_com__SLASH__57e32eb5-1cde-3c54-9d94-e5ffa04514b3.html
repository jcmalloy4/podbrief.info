Deep Dive

  • AE Industrial Partners manages $7.2 billion in assets, focusing on national security, aerospace, and industrial services.
  • The firm operates as a buyout specialist, making control investments in companies addressing national security challenges.
  • Since 1998, AEI has completed over 140 investments, leveraging extensive industry expertise.
  • The guest, Kirk Conner, joined AEI in 2014, having previously worked at Sun Capital Partners and Wells Fargo Securities.

  • AEI prioritizes investments with bipartisan consensus, particularly in cybersecurity and artificial intelligence (AI) sectors.
  • The firm actively invests in space technology, including companies such as Redwire Space, Firefly Aerospace, and York Space.
  • Firefly Aerospace successfully landed autonomously on the moon in March, while York Space is disrupting satellite manufacturing.
  • Other key investment domains include autonomy, applied in drones and ground-based systems, and cybersecurity, focused on defending national infrastructure against daily attacks.

  • AE Industrial Partners provides operational support to its portfolio companies through a deeply integrated team.
  • This team comprises individuals with diverse backgrounds, including engineers and former business leaders.
  • They develop detailed operational playbooks before deal closure, working directly with companies post-acquisition to foster long-term growth.
  • The firm maintains a large operational team relative to its assets under management (AUM) to assist companies in scaling and executing growth plans, recognizing lengthy development and sales cycles, particularly with government customers.

  • Kirk Conner began his career in banking during the 2008 financial crisis, then transitioned to private equity at Sun Capital before joining AEI 11 years ago.
  • The firm was founded by a father-son team with an initial focus on middle-market commercial aerospace companies.
  • Around 2015, AEI expanded its investment strategy to include national security and space sectors.

  • The guest relocated to Washington D.C. two months prior to be at the center of national security investments and better serve portfolio companies.
  • This move led to establishing a local team in D.C. focused on government relations and business development.
  • The guest cited their father, an electrical lineman, as a profound influence, instilling values of perseverance and hard work.
  • Involvement in youth sports and a college football career at Davidson, a university also attended by NBA player Steph Curry, reinforced lessons in teamwork and resilience.
