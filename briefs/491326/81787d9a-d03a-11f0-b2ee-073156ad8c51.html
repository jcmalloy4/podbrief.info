Trump Calls Somali Immigrants ‘Garbage’

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump made disparaging remarks about Somali immigrants in Minneapolis ahead of an expected ICE operation.
  • Peace talks between Russia and Ukraine failed, while Lithuania began preparing for potential conflict.
  • U.S. auto sales are slumping due to high prices, inflation, and consumer reluctance.
  • Waymo's self-driving vehicles are adopting a more assertive driving style while reporting fewer serious crashes.

Deep Dive

  • President Trump made disparaging remarks about Somali immigrants in Minneapolis, ahead of an expected ICE operation.
  • He stated they are not wanted in the U.S. and should return to their home countries.
  • Trump linked Somali immigrants to crime, while Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey affirmed support for the local Somali community.

  • Peace talks between Russia and Ukraine concluded without an agreement, with disputed territory in the Donbass region remaining a major sticking point.
  • Lithuania is preparing for a potential conflict by constructing defensive barriers along its border with Russia, collaborating with other Baltic states and Poland.
  • NATO foreign ministers met, though the absence of the U.S. Secretary of State raised questions about American commitment.

  • U.S. auto sales are unexpectedly slumping, with forecasts indicating stagnant sales for the next two years.
  • Consumer reluctance is attributed to high prices, inflation, and an uncertain job market, leading some buyers to hold out for lower prices.
  • High prices stem from pandemic factory shutdowns, a semiconductor shortage, and lingering effects of tariffs on materials and parts.
  • Electric vehicle sales are notably falling, particularly in the U.S. after a tax credit expired, leading dealers to offer discounts off sticker prices.

  • Waymo's self-driving vehicles in San Francisco are adopting a more assertive driving style to improve traffic flow and avoid disruptions.
  • This change follows an incident where a Waymo executed an illegal U-turn and was pulled over by police.
  • Waymo reports a 91% reduction in serious injury crashes compared to human drivers across its operational cities.
