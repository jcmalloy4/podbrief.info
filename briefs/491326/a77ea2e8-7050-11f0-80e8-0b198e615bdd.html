How Summer Travel Is Changing

Key Takeaways

  • Summer travel costs are rising as companies target upscale consumers.
  • Economic uncertainty drives a clear split in the travel market.
  • Airlines now unbundle services, adding fees for basic amenities.

Deep Dives

Summer Costs

  • Economic uncertainty and tariff news caused some travelers to reconsider plans, leading to scaled-back or domestic trips.
  • Travel companies are adapting to more affluent consumers, indicating a potential new normal for vacation expenses.

Market Split

  • Wealthier consumers continue unaffected international travel, creating a clear bifurcation in the travel industry.
  • Budget-conscious domestic travelers are adjusting plans, often opting for shorter or less expensive road trips.

Airline Strategy

  • Major airlines like Delta and United focus on premium cabins, while Southwest introduces new bag fees and assigned seating.
  • Airlines are expected to continue segmenting offerings, potentially even charging for business class seat assignments or reducing amenities.
