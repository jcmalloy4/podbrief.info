What to Expect From President Trump’s Domestic Agenda This Year

Key Takeaways

  • Trump's first year saw significant policy changes in taxes, tariffs, and immigration.
  • Affordability is a key voter concern for the administration in 2026.
  • The administration is expected to continue its influence over corporate America.
  • Aggressive immigration policies and deportations are under review for continuation.
  • President Trump's 2026 political strategy includes midterm engagement and future positioning.

Deep Dive

  • The Trump administration characterized its first year as successful, marked by significant policy changes in taxes, tariffs, and immigration.
  • Tariffs, a key economic policy in 2025, appear to have had a less severe economic impact than initially feared.
  • Affordability is identified as a critical voter concern for 2026, with the administration finding it difficult to lower prices.
  • Democrats are likely to use rising costs as a point of criticism against Trump's policies in 2026.

  • The Trump administration is expected to continue its engagement with the private sector.
  • This includes interventions in corporate deals and the promotion of initiatives such as the Trump RX program.
  • A key question for 2026 is the extent to which the administration will push its influence over corporate America.

  • The Trump administration pursued aggressive immigration tactics, leading to increased deportations.
  • Questions persist regarding whether these policies will continue amidst public outcry and specific incidents.
  • The ouster of Venezuelan President Nicolas Maduro and potential instability could influence U.S. immigration patterns.

  • Predictions for 2026 include President Trump's engagement in midterm elections and potential endorsements.
  • Discussions center on whether he will position himself for a future presidential run or focus on regaining lost voter bases.
  • The U.S.'s 250th birthday in 2026 is anticipated to be a highly theatrical and celebratory year under Trump's influence, potentially serving as a political advantage.
