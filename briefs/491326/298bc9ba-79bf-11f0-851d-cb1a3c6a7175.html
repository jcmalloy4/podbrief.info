Could the U.S. Government Be Considering Buying a Stake in Intel?

Key Takeaways

  • Trump and Putin's summit faces long odds for Ukraine ceasefire, as Russia seeks sustained influence.
  • US government considers Intel stake, citing national security and "America-first" tech manufacturing.
  • Apple reinstates watch feature amid patent spat; federal overreach sparks D.C. controversy.

Deep Dives

Ukraine Diplomacy

  • President Trump seeks a ceasefire and inclusion of Ukrainian President Zelensky, while Putin aims to reassert Russian dominance and delay sanctions.
  • Journal analysts suggest a lasting peace is unlikely, as Ukraine slowly accepts potential territorial losses and Russia persists in its political objectives.

Chipmaker Intervention

  • Washington's potential financial stake in Intel supports President Trump's "America-first" manufacturing agenda and eases pressure on its CEO.
  • The move underscores national security concerns about AI capabilities and reliance on foreign chip production, driving broader government influence in tech.

Tech & Policy

  • Apple reinstated its blood oxygen monitoring feature on some watches after resolving a patent dispute.
  • In Washington D.C., federal intervention in local policing by the Attorney General drew strong criticism from the Mayor.
