Witkoff-Putin Meeting Fuels Fresh Hope for Ukraine Peace Talks

Key Takeaways

  • Ukraine peace talks are gaining momentum, involving U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff and President Trump.
  • Medicare has negotiated lower prices for 15 high-selling drugs, including Ozempic, saving an estimated $12 billion.
  • Retailers Kohl's and Abercrombie & Fitch reported better-than-expected earnings, boosting stock prices.
  • Former Fox News host Tucker Carlson launched a precious metals company amid gold price benefits from rate cut expectations.
