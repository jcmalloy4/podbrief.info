Why Earning More Isn’t Saving Some American Families From Poverty

Key Takeaways

  • Child poverty has surged, with nearly 10 million American children now living below the poverty line.
  • President Trump gained a pivotal opportunity to influence the Federal Reserve's future monetary policy.

Deep Dives

Rising Poverty

  • Ending pandemic-era aid, relentless inflation, and rising housing costs push millions of families to the brink, even those with increased earnings.
  • Reporter Dan Frosch detailed struggles of working families in Broome County, NY, battling daily to afford food and manage bills.

Fed Reshaping

  • An unexpected resignation created a vacancy on the Federal Reserve's Board of Governors.
  • WSJ's Nick Timiraos indicated Trump might appoint a critical "firebrand" to challenge Chair Jerome Powell's current approach.
