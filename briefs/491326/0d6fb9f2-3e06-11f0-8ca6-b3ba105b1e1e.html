WSJ What’s News

What’s News in Markets: Temu’s Pressures, Nvidia Delivers, Beautiful E.l.f.

Key Takeaways

  • Trade tensions remain a dominant market force, with U.S.-China tariffs creating clear winners and losers as companies either adapt strategically or struggle under pressure from ongoing restrictions and policy uncertainty.
  • May delivered exceptional market performance across all major indexes, with the Nasdaq leading at over 9% gains, demonstrating investor optimism despite geopolitical headwinds.
  • Supply chain diversification is becoming critical, as evidenced by Elf Beauty's $1 billion acquisition to reduce China dependence, while companies like Teemu face severe profit declines from tariff exposure.
  • Tech resilience shines through restrictions, with NVIDIA posting record $44 billion sales and 69% revenue growth despite China sales limitations, proving strong demand can overcome regulatory challenges.

Deep Dive

Trade Policy Developments and Legal Framework

The conversation began with significant developments in U.S. trade policy, where the U.S. Court of International Trade initially invalidated most of Trump's tariffs. However, this decision was subsequently overturned when a federal appeals court allowed the tariffs to remain in effect, maintaining the ongoing trade tensions between the U.S. and China that continue to shape the business landscape.

Market Performance Overview

Against this backdrop of trade uncertainty, the hosts discussed May's strong market performance across all major indexes:
  • Dow Jones: Gained 3.9% for the month
  • S&P 500: Rose 6.2%
  • Nasdaq: Jumped over 9%, leading the pack

Company-Specific Impact Analysis

Teemu (PDD Holdings) - Trade War Casualties

The discussion highlighted how trade tensions are creating clear winners and losers, with Teemu serving as a prime example of the latter:
  • Financial Impact: Profit dropped nearly 50% in Q1
  • Root Causes: Directly impacted by U.S. tariffs and loss of duty exemptions
  • Market Reaction: Stock dropped 14% on Tuesday and 19% for the entire week

NVIDIA - Resilience Despite Restrictions

In contrast, NVIDIA demonstrated remarkable resilience:
  • Record Performance: Reported record-breaking sales of $44.06 billion
  • Growth Metrics: Achieved 69% revenue increase despite facing limitations on China sales
  • Market Response: Stock gained 3% on Thursday and 2.9% for the week

Elf Beauty - Strategic Diversification

The hosts examined Elf Beauty's proactive approach to trade tensions:
  • Major Acquisition: Announced $1 billion acquisition of Hailey Bieber's Rode brand
  • Strategic Rationale: Aims to diversify supply chain away from China
  • Market Enthusiasm: Stock rocketed 24% on Thursday and 34% for the week

Overarching Theme

The conversation concluded with the recognition that ongoing U.S.-China trade tensions continue to be a dominant force significantly impacting companies' performance and strategic decisions, creating a clear divide between those adapting successfully and those struggling under the pressure.
