Can Perplexity’s Longshot Bid for Google Chrome Succeed?

Key Takeaways

  • AI startup Perplexity made a surprising $34.5 billion bid for Google's Chrome browser.
  • President Trump publicly challenged Goldman Sachs over its chief economist's tariff predictions.
  • The White House seeks to reshape Smithsonian museum narratives ahead of the country's 250th anniversary.

Deep Dives

Chrome Bid

  • AI startup Perplexity made an unsolicited $34.5 billion offer for Google's Chrome, despite being valued at $18 billion.
  • The bid emerges as Google faces a judge's ruling that might force a Chrome spin-off due to antitrust violations.

Trump vs. Goldman

  • President Trump publicly urged Goldman Sachs CEO David Solomon to replace his chief economist over past tariff predictions.
  • Trump asserted tariffs have not negatively impacted the U.S. economy, also questioning Solomon's outside career.

Smithsonian Review

  • The White House plans a comprehensive review of Smithsonian museums' exhibitions and materials.
  • This aims to align museum content with President Trump's interpretation of American history for the nation's 250th anniversary.
