WSJ What’s News

Would Denmark Ever Sell Greenland?

Key Takeaways

  • President Trump is reportedly pursuing a purchase of Greenland, facing Danish resistance to negotiations.
  • Venezuela is set to provide the U.S. with up to 50 million barrels of oil, with proceeds benefiting both nations.
  • Global stock markets are experiencing a record rally, with major banks predicting further S&P 500 growth for the year.
  • The healthcare sector anticipates a significant boost from new weight loss drugs, highlighted by Eli Lilly's $1 trillion valuation.

Deep Dive

  • The White House is reportedly pursuing the purchase of Greenland, with threats about the island intended to pressure Denmark into negotiations.
  • Heightened security concerns in the Arctic, due to Russian and Chinese military expansion, are cited as a factor.
  • WSJ's Brussels bureau chief Daniel Michaels discussed a 'compact of free association' as a potential deal, though currently unaccepted by Denmark or Greenlanders.

  • President Trump announced Venezuela will provide the U.S. with up to 50 million barrels of oil, with proceeds benefiting both nations.
  • This volume represents a significant portion of Venezuela's current production and is valued at $1.5 to $2.5 billion by energy consultants.
  • Major oil companies are expected at a White House meeting to discuss potential Venezuelan investments.

  • Stock markets worldwide have hit fresh highs, extending a record rally despite uncertainties surrounding Venezuela.
  • The Dow shows its best start since 2003, and indexes in Japan, Singapore, South Korea, and London reached record closing highs.
  • Big banks predict a significant rise for the S&P 500 for the year, anticipating strong corporate earnings, tax cuts, and Federal Reserve rate cuts.

  • The healthcare sector is expected to be significantly boosted by new weight loss drugs, including Novo Nordisk's Wagovi and a forthcoming pill from Eli Lilly.
  • Eli Lilly's valuation recently crossed the $1 trillion threshold, highlighting the market's anticipation for success in the obesity drug market.
  • Biotech companies are also seeing increased interest due to potential acquisitions fueled by this market trend.
