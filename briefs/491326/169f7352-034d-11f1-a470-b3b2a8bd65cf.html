Why MAGA Is Fuming Over Bad Bunny’s Halftime Show

Key Takeaways

  • Tech stocks, including Amazon, fell due to high AI spending and cloud growth concerns.
  • Global markets experienced a downturn linked to AI spending and new model releases.
  • White House launched TrumpRX website to reduce prescription drug costs for uninsured Americans.
  • Bad Bunny's Super Bowl performance drew conservative backlash over his political statements.

Deep Dive

  • Amazon shares fell over 7% after announcing a 60% increase in planned AI spending, exceeding Wall Street expectations.
  • Amazon's cloud computing growth also lagged behind competitors such as Microsoft and Alphabet.
  • Concerns over AI spending contributed to a global stock downturn, with South Korea's tech index halting trading.
  • Stellantis shares plunged to pandemic-era lows following a $26 billion write-off due to overestimated U.S. EV demand.

  • The White House launched TrumpRX, a government-run drug buying website offering roughly 40 medications.
  • This initiative aims to lower healthcare costs, particularly for uninsured Americans.
  • High-stakes talks between Iran and the U.S. concerning Tehran's nuclear program concluded after 90 minutes.
  • The U.S. has increased military forces near Iran amid escalating tensions and rising public anger within Iran.

  • Bad Bunny's upcoming Super Bowl halftime performance generated controversy among conservative circles due to his political stances.
  • His 'ice out' comment at the Grammys, protesting the Trump administration's immigration agenda, reignited backlash.
  • WSJ reporter Sabrina Rodriguez noted that Bad Bunny's comments align with a shift in public opinion, with a majority disapproving of ICE's actions.
  • White House Press Secretary Karine Jean-Pierre criticized celebrities for demonizing law enforcement while living in luxury.

  • The Milan-Cortina Winter Olympics have begun, with Vice President J.D. Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio attending.
  • American athletes have an opportunity to break the record for most gold medals.
  • U.S. figure skater Ilia Malinin, known as the 'quad god' for his quadruple axel, is expected to win gold.
  • Skier Lindsey Vonn, 41, is attempting a comeback on a reconstructed knee, with experts unsure if she can medal.
