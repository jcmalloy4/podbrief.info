Verdict with Ted Cruz

Daily Review With Clay and Buck - Nov 10 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Democrats' government shutdown strategy was criticized for causing chaos, public frustration, and internal party dissent.
  • The Affordable Care Act is scrutinized for increasing premiums and benefiting insurance companies over patients.
  • President Trump's NFL appearance was highlighted as a moment signaling his growing cultural acceptance.
  • Michelle Obama's public persona and media treatment were critically examined by the hosts for perceived ungratefulness.
  • Allegations of media bias in covering presidential families were discussed, favoring some while criticizing others.

Deep Dive

  • Democrats were criticized for intentionally causing chaos and disruption during the government shutdown, particularly affecting air travel and essential workers.
  • The shutdown was framed as a strategic misstep by Democrats ahead of elections, with eight Democrat senators reportedly breaking ranks.
  • President Donald Trump's response, including calls for bonuses for working air traffic controllers, was presented as a leadership moment.

  • Hosts argue the Affordable Care Act (Obamacare) has led to skyrocketing premiums and increased insurance company profits without improving care quality.
  • The CDC estimates that extreme obesity costs the U.S. healthcare system $200 billion annually.
  • The discussion suggests Democrats are attempting to force a transition to a single-payer healthcare system.

  • President Trump appeared in the Fox Sports broadcast booth during an NFL game, an event described as fun and signaling his growing cultural acceptance.
  • His commentary on football, high school playing days, and interactions with players like Drew Brees were highlighted.
  • The Detroit Lions' 'Trump dance' was noted as emblematic of a shift in how young men and sports figures view the president.

  • Democrats were criticized for a strategic miscalculation during the government shutdown, lacking an exit strategy and failing to achieve objectives.
  • The shutdown was characterized as 'kabuki theater' and 'pantomime,' creating public frustration over airport disruptions.
  • The outcome allegedly benefited Republicans by highlighting Democrat disarray, with eight Democratic senators breaking ranks.

  • The 40-day government shutdown concluded due to Democrats recognizing their lack of progress and significant public frustration.
  • Widespread air travel disruptions, including thousands of flight cancellations and delays, were cited as a key factor in the resolution.
  • President Trump's position was seen as strengthened, citing his actions regarding SNAP benefits and payment of ICE agents.

  • The stock market saw substantial gains as the government shutdown ended.
  • Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer's political future is debated, with hosts speculating on challenges from figures like Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez.
  • The Senate is viewed as a stepping stone for higher office, and Schumer's potential re-election in 2028 is questioned.

  • Eight Democratic senators who voted to end the government shutdown are noted as not being up for re-election in the upcoming year.
  • Hosts argue the Democratic Party's focus on Obamacare may be problematic heading into the midterms, recalling healthcare was a key issue in 2018.
  • The Affordable Care Act is claimed to empower insurance companies, potentially paving the way for a single-payer system.

  • Kim Kardashian's repeated attempts to pass the California bar exam are acknowledged for her persistence and ambition.
  • Michelle Obama is characterized as ungrateful and perceived to have a victimhood narrative despite her former First Lady status.
  • Her media treatment is contrasted with other presidential families, including the Bushes, Clintons, and Trumps, suggesting unprecedented grace.

  • The hosts criticize the perceived attitude of the Democratic Party, particularly among women, describing it as ungrateful and lecturing.
  • Media coverage of presidential children is contrasted, with claims of more favorable treatment for Obama daughters compared to Bush or Trump children.
  • Hunter Biden is noted for receiving significant negative attention despite alleged felonies, contrasting with other presidential children.
