Verdict with Ted Cruz

Trump’s Plan to Rebuild Venezuela’s Broken Oil Empire plus Maduro in the Big Apple

Key Takeaways

  • Donald Trump outlined a plan for U.S. oil companies to rebuild Venezuela's oil infrastructure, emphasizing its trillions in reserves.
  • Nicolas Maduro pleaded not guilty to narco-terrorism and drug trafficking charges in Manhattan, claiming 'prisoner of war' status.
  • The FBI Director credited Trump's leadership for U.S. counter-narcotics successes, including fentanyl seizures and addressing Venezuela as a transshipment point.

Deep Dive

  • Donald Trump outlined a plan for U.S. oil companies to rebuild Venezuela's 'broken' oil infrastructure.
  • Trump stated elections should occur 'at the right time' after stabilization.
  • He corrected a reporter, emphasizing Venezuela's oil reserves are worth 'trillions of dollars'.

  • Nicolas Maduro made his first court appearance in Manhattan on narco-terrorism and drug trafficking charges.
  • He pleaded not guilty, declared himself a 'prisoner of war,' and asserted he remains Venezuela's legitimate president.
  • Maduro's wife also appeared and pleaded not guilty, reportedly with visible injuries.
  • His next hearing is scheduled for March 17, 2026.
  • Protests occurred outside the courthouse during the proceedings.

  • The FBI Director praised Donald Trump's leadership, citing agreements with Chinese officials that reduced precursor chemicals for fentanyl.
  • This led to the seizure of enough fentanyl to kill 175 million Americans.
  • Venezuela is described as a major transshipment point for illicit narcotics, including cocaine and fentanyl, with traffickers operating with impunity.
  • Trump's leadership is credited for a significant reduction in narcotics on U.S. streets.
