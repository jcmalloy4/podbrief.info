Verdict with Ted Cruz

Oil Means National Security, Rep. Jim Jordan vs Jack Smith & Crime Crashes Coast‑to‑Coast Week In Review

Key Takeaways

  • U.S. energy policy aims to maintain oil prices at $60-$70/barrel to weaken adversaries while supporting domestic producers.
  • Venezuela's oil infrastructure is severely degraded, requiring over a decade and $100 billion+ to significantly increase production.
  • Cuba faces severe economic challenges following the cessation of Venezuelan support, with its survival now dependent on Mexican oil supplies.
  • Special Counsel Jack Smith's January 6th investigation is criticized for alleged political motivation and reliance on contested testimony.
  • Reported U.S. murder rates decreased by approximately 20% nationwide from 2024 to 2025, alongside declines in other violent crimes.

Deep Dive

  • Lower global oil prices weaken adversarial regimes, including Iran, Russia, and Venezuela, by reducing their revenue.
  • The Trump administration aimed for a 'sweet spot' oil price between $60 and $70 per barrel.
  • This price point was intended to weaken adversaries while preventing the bankruptcy of U.S. independent oil producers in regions such as the Permian Basin.

  • Venezuela possesses the world's largest proven oil reserves, but decades of mismanagement have severely degraded its infrastructure.
  • Increasing Venezuela's oil production from 1 million to 3 million barrels per day could require 10 years and over $100 billion in investment.
  • Even a more modest increase to 2 million barrels per day is estimated to take 5-7 years due to the degraded state of infrastructure.

  • Cuba is experiencing an economic freefall after Venezuela ceased financial and oil support.
  • Historically, Cuba relied on external support, first from the Soviet Union and subsequently from Venezuela under Chávez and Maduro.
  • Mexico is currently providing oil to Cuba, which helps sustain the Cuban regime.
  • The Trump administration may exert pressure on Mexico to cut this supply, a move that could potentially lead to the Cuban regime's political collapse.

  • Special Counsel Jack Smith is accused of leading a politically motivated prosecution against Donald Trump regarding January 6th.
  • Smith allegedly relied on 'questionable or disproven testimony,' notably from Cassidy Hutchinson.
  • During a congressional hearing, Jim Jordan questioned Smith about Hutchinson's secondhand hearsay accounts, including Trump's alleged lunge for a steering wheel, which another witness in the vehicle did not confirm.

  • The host and Senator Ted Cruz discussed the perceived weaponization of the justice system, drawing parallels to the 2016 Russian collusion allegations.
  • Critiques included the reliance on questionable witnesses and testimonies, such as Cassidy Hutchinson's, to target Donald Trump.
  • Senator Cruz personally noted that a dramatic passage in Cassidy Hutchinson's book, describing an alleged conversation with him, was fictional and never occurred.

  • Nationwide murder rates reportedly declined approximately 20% from 2024 to 2025, representing an estimated 1,400 fewer murders.
  • Major cities including Chicago (-30%), New York City (-20%), Baltimore (-31%), Oakland (-33%), and Washington D.C. (-31%) reported significant decreases in homicides.
  • Other violent crimes also declined, with motor vehicle theft down 25%, robbery down 18%, and aggravated assault down 8%.
  • Law enforcement statistics cited a 100% increase in violent crime arrests, a 210% increase in disrupted gangs, and a 31% increase in fentanyl seizures.
