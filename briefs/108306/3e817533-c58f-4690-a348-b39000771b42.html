Verdict with Ted Cruz

Terror Comes to America from Biden Afghan Terrorists

Key Takeaways

  • A recent attack on National Guard members by an Afghan national highlights security concerns stemming from refugee vetting processes.
  • Critics contend the Biden administration's Afghanistan withdrawal prioritized political optics over thorough security, leading to potential risks.
  • Humanitarian issues, including child marriage and sexual abuse, have been reported at Afghan refugee intake centers.
  • The administration's approach is framed by critics as ideologically driven, downplaying radical Islamic terrorism and border security risks.

Deep Dive

  • Two National Guard members, Sarah Beckstrom and Andrew Wolf, were shot by an Afghan national; one died, and the other was critically injured.
  • The incident is attributed to inadequate vetting of Afghan nationals who allegedly entered the U.S. through resettlement programs.
  • Senator Ted Cruz and co-host Ben Ferguson stated they predicted such outcomes for over four years due to perceived vetting failures.

  • The 2021 Afghanistan withdrawal was criticized as chaotic, abandoning Bagram Airfield in favor of Kabul Airport, leading to 13 service member deaths.
  • Senator Cruz warned on August 27, 2021, about vetting Afghan refugees, suggesting alternative housing in third countries.
  • Concerns were raised about a perceived lack of security and vetting at the Fort Bliss facility in El Paso, Texas, intended for 10,000 Afghan refugees.
  • The host criticized the withdrawal as a 'numbers game' that failed to evacuate allies, referencing a cross-examination of then-Secretary of State Tony Blinken.

  • Humanitarian concerns stemming from Afghan refugees entering the U.S. include reports of widespread child marriage and domestic abuse.
  • Reports indicate instances of sexual abuse at intake centers involving Afghan men allegedly claiming young children as wives.
  • Speakers highlighted a perceived lack of urgency and proper vetting by the Biden administration regarding these issues.

  • Warnings of increased terror risks and potential attacks in the U.S. were issued due to the administration's handling of the Afghanistan withdrawal.
  • Concerns include inadequate vetting of Afghan evacuees, potential for individuals with criminal records, and terror attacks at U.S. facilities like Fort Bliss.
  • Observations at a Donna tent facility cited a lack of security measures and a casual approach to detaining evacuees.
  • An Afghan national in Texas was arrested for making terroristic threats after allegedly appearing in a TikTok video referencing Fort Worth as a target.

  • An arrest in Texas and the D.C. National Guard attack are linked to a perceived pattern of insufficient vetting and an open southern border.
  • The Biden administration's policies are characterized by critics as driven by a left-wing ideology that ignores radical Islamic terrorism.
  • Speakers claimed the administration's actions are a calculated decision to prioritize 'flooding America with illegal immigrants' over safety.

  • The Democratic Party's ideological stance is criticized, with concerns raised about its approach to radical Islamic terrorism.
  • Representative Rashida Tlaib was criticized for not condemning 'Death to America' chants at a rally in her district.
  • This political stance is linked to the perceived dangers of unvetted Afghan immigrants, citing the National Guardsman incident.
