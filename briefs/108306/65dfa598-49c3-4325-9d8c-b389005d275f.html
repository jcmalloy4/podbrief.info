Verdict with Ted Cruz

MTG Resigns-Why & What it Means, plus Minnesota Taxpayers are the LARGEST Funders of Al-Shabaab Terrorists

Key Takeaways

  • Marjorie Taylor Greene announced her resignation from Congress, effective January, following shifts in her political positions and a withdrawal of support from Donald Trump.
  • A report claims massive welfare fraud in Minnesota allegedly funneled billions from taxpayers, with millions potentially reaching the terrorist group Al-Shabaab.
  • Texas's newly redrawn congressional map, which adds five Republican seats, remains in effect after Justice Alito issued a stay on a federal court's decision.

Deep Dive

  • Congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene announced her resignation from Congress, effective January.
  • Her departure is framed as a moment of Republican internal accountability, contrasting with perceived Democratic inaction on radical elements.
  • Greene's shift to anti-Israel and anti-capitalist stances was a key factor in her decision.
  • Donald Trump's withdrawal of support, citing her changed positions and frequent complaints, was highlighted as decisive.

  • Texas redrew its congressional map to add five Republican seats, a move initially struck down by a federal district court.
  • Justice Alito issued a stay on the district court's decision, keeping the new map in place.
  • This intervention could determine control of the House of Representatives.
  • The decision is viewed as a victory for the rule of law against activist judges, potentially preserving Republican control.
  • Texas's redistricting efforts are contrasted with alleged Democratic gerrymandering practices in states like California.

  • A report alleges that Minnesota taxpayers indirectly funded the terrorist group Al-Shabaab through massive welfare fraud.
  • Billions of dollars were reportedly stolen from programs like Housing Stabilization Services by members of the Somali community.
  • Millions of dollars were allegedly routed to Somalia and subsequently to Al-Shabaab via informal money networks.
  • Democratic leadership and media were criticized for allegedly ignoring this issue, framing it as a security threat and governance failure.
  • Minnesota Governor Tim Walz and Democrats were specifically criticized for allegedly ignoring the fraud, attributed to a desire for votes.
