    Verdict with Ted Cruz

    Shutdown Dems Gaslight, claiming they DON'T Want Free Healthcare for Illegals, plus Fighting the Crime Epidemic in Blue Cities

    Key Takeaways

    • Democrats are blamed for intentionally causing the government shutdown for political gain.
    • Taxpayer-funded healthcare for undocumented immigrants is cited as a key Democratic policy demand.
    • Rising crime rates in Democrat-led cities are attributed to progressive District Attorneys and soft-on-crime policies.
    • Left-wing rhetoric is claimed to incite violence, with various incidents cited as examples.
    • Senator Cruz proposed the "Clean DC Act" to address crime by reversing current laws and supporting law enforcement.

    Deep Dive

    • Hosts Ted Cruz and Ben Ferguson attributed the ongoing government shutdown to Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer and the Democratic Party.
    • They claimed Democrats are misrepresenting the situation using "Orwellian doublespeak" to advance their agenda.
    • The shutdown was framed as Chuck Schumer's attempt to appease progressive factions, including Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, to maintain his leadership position.
    • House Republicans reportedly passed a "clean CR" (continuing resolution) to keep government funding stable, but Democrats rejected it.

    • Hosts claimed Democrats' policy demand for taxpayer-funded healthcare for undocumented immigrants was a primary driver of the shutdown.
    • Past Democratic presidential debates were cited where all candidates reportedly raised hands in support of healthcare coverage for undocumented immigrants.
    • California and New York are referenced for already providing state-level healthcare to undocumented immigrants, with federal funds indirectly supporting these programs.
    • Hosts argued that Democrats are "gaslighting" the public by denying this stance and are willing to shut down the government to remove eligibility requirements for benefits.

    • A Senate Judiciary Committee hearing highlighted rising crime rates in Democrat-led cities.
    • Progressive District Attorneys, allegedly funded by George Soros, were accused of refusing to prosecute violent criminals, leading to a pattern of repeat offenses.
    • Examples cited included cases in New York, Georgia, Wisconsin, and the Milwaukee DA, who allegedly allowed a criminal to be released twice before a fatal Christmas parade incident.
    • Statistics presented showed high homicide rates per capita in 2023 for cities like New Orleans, Memphis, and St. Louis, attributed to Democrat crime policies.

    • Criticism was directed at Democratic-led efforts to defund or reduce police funding, citing examples in Minneapolis, New York, and Austin.
    • Soros-backed District Attorneys were accused of eliminating cash bail, downgrading felonies, and prioritizing criminals over victims.
    • A Senate Judiciary Committee hearing discussed how prison and bail reform policies contribute to the release of violent criminals.

    • Senator Cruz discussed his proposed "Clean DC Act," aimed at reversing soft-on-crime laws enacted in Washington, D.C. in 2022.
    • The act advocates for reinstating tougher penalties and providing more support for law enforcement.
    • Former President Trump was credited with deploying the National Guard to Washington, D.C., which reportedly led to a 58% drop in murder rates.

    • The discussion linked Democratic rhetoric to violent outcomes, citing incidents such as BLM riots, anti-Semitic incidents, and attacks on ICE facilities in Chicago.
    • Claims were made that left-wing activists celebrate violence, contrasting with conservative condemnation.
    • Examples of alleged left-inspired violence included assassination attempts on former President Trump and Justice Kavanaugh, and the murder of Charlie Kirk.
