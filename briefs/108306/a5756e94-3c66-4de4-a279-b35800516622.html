Verdict with Ted Cruz

Schumer Shutdown Continues...How it will end, plus Biden Judge Gives Light Sentence to Justice Kavanaugh's attempted Murder-because the Killer is Transgender

Key Takeaways

  • Democrats are blamed for the ongoing government shutdown, facing claims of "panic" and a predicted "capitulation."
  • The eight-year sentence for Nicholas Roske, who plotted to assassinate Justice Kavanaugh, sparked outrage and accusations of "left-wing bias."
  • AI-generated memes depicting Democratic leaders with sombreros and mustaches were used in shutdown messaging.
  • The Kavanaugh sentencing is tied to broader critiques of Democratic "soft-on-crime" policies and perceived cultural double standards.

Deep Dive

  • The ongoing government shutdown, now in its sixth day, is attributed to Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer and the Democratic Party.
  • Democrats are described as panicking and lacking a political "off-ramp," with hosts predicting "complete capitulation" from their side.
  • Polling data is cited, allegedly showing 64% perceive the Democratic Party as "weak" and 46% as "extreme."
  • The situation is framed as a political win for Republicans, suggesting it reveals public indifference to big government.

  • Nicholas Roske, who plotted to assassinate Supreme Court Justice Brett Kavanaugh, received an eight-year sentence from a federal judge.
  • Hosts Ted Cruz and Ben Ferguson expressed outrage, calling the sentence unjustly lenient and indicative of "left-wing bias" from Biden appointee Judge Deborah Boardman.
  • Senator Cruz detailed Roske's plot, which included weapons and targets, and noted the prosecution's initial request for a 30-year sentence.
  • The judge's decision to deviate 22 years from the recommended sentence was criticized, with her reasoning interpreted as leniency based on Roske's transgender identity.

  • Discussion focused on AI-generated memes, created by Trump and their campaign, featuring Democratic leaders Hakeem Jeffries and Chuck Schumer in sombreros and mustaches.
  • The hosts stated these memes were humorous, while Democrats reportedly reacted by calling them racist.
  • A song used in a meme campaign featuring 44 Democratic senators with sombreros and mustaches, including visuals of Chris Van Holland and Hillary Clinton, was played.

  • Senator Cruz recounted an interaction with Senator Tim Kaine regarding the government shutdown, where Kaine joked about Cancun and Cruz responded by noting half of Virginia's state employees were not getting paid.
  • Cruz described Republican reactions to Senator Schumer's statements on polling data and the New York Times as "spontaneous laughter."
  • Cruz predicted Democrats would concede to reopen the government this week, citing internal pressure from members concerned about re-election.
  • It was noted that 83% of government services, including military and essential services, continue to operate during the shutdown.

  • The judge's statement referring to Nicholas Roske as 'Ms. Roski' and emphasizing the family's acceptance of their daughter's transgender identity was highlighted as a factor in sentencing.
  • Statements from Roske's mother and the judge regarding the family's journey with LGBTQ+ issues and the desire for Roske to be housed in a women's facility were detailed.
  • The incident was expanded into a broader critique of Democratic "soft-on-crime" policies, with claims that the public desires law and order.
  • Senator Cruz expressed strong personal reaction to the sentencing due to his long-standing relationship with Justice Kavanaugh and his family.
