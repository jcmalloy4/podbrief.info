Verdict with Ted Cruz

Comey Indicted for Lying under Oath in response to my Questioning plus Sniper Targets Dallas ICE Facility

Key Takeaways

  • Former FBI Director James Comey faces indictment for alleged false statements to Congress.
  • Conflicting testimonies from Comey and McCabe form the basis of the indictment.
  • A politically motivated sniper attack occurred at a Dallas ICE facility.
  • Podcast hosts assert divisive political rhetoric fuels violence against law enforcement.
  • The hosts frame current events through a lens of political weaponization.

Deep Dive

  • Senator Ted Cruz and co-host Ben Ferguson discussed former FBI Director James Comey's indictment on charges of lying under oath and obstruction of justice.
  • The indictment stems from Comey's denial of authorizing leaks to the press, contradicting Andrew McCabe's statements.
  • The hosts frame the charges as proof the FBI was politicized against former President Donald Trump.

  • Hosts characterized James Comey's public statement following his indictment as political, not fact-based.
  • Comey's response was criticized for framing him as a victim and using charged language against Donald Trump.
  • The discussion highlighted Comey's past tweet celebrating Trump's indictment in the Alvin Bragg case.

  • The podcast covered a politically motivated sniper attack at an ICE facility in Dallas.
  • Senator Cruz recounted being on the scene shortly after the incident.
  • The hosts criticized Democratic rhetoric for allegedly fueling anti-ICE sentiment and violence.

  • Senator Cruz read a December 2020 letter to then-AG Barr and Director Wray regarding Comey's alleged conduct.
  • A 2016 Wall Street Journal article quoted an anonymous source about the Hillary Clinton email investigation, reportedly authorized by Andrew McCabe.
  • McCabe initially denied authorizing the leak but later confessed, stating Comey approved the disclosure.
  • McCabe reiterated Comey's involvement in a Senate hearing, contrasting Comey's denials under oath.

  • The hosts frame discussed events within a narrative of political weaponization, left-wing hypocrisy, and anti-Trump bias.
  • Senator Cruz portrayed James Comey as a partisan actor, comparing his own questioning of Comey with Andrew McCabe's admissions.
  • Violence against law enforcement is positioned as a consequence of inflammatory rhetoric by Democratic leaders.

  • Hosts predicted James Comey's defense will try to discredit Andrew McCabe's testimony, who is expected to testify Comey authorized leaks.
  • Senator Cruz predicted Comey will not testify to avoid cross-examination and potential further perjury charges.
  • Cruz stated Comey's alleged hatred for Trump is not a defense, asserting Comey deliberately lied five years ago and expects conviction.

  • Senator Cruz connected the Dallas ICE facility shooting to rhetoric from politicians like Gavin Newsom, who demonized ICE agents.
  • The hosts linked the gunman to online searches and an app for tracking ICE agents.
  • They argued that divisive language from Democratic leaders fuels violence against law enforcement, noting the Dallas incident was the third such shooting in Texas.
