Verdict with Ted Cruz

Inside the Room with the Heroic Soldiers Wounded in the Maduro Raid & with Venezuelan Opposition Leader Maria Corina Machado

Key Takeaways

  • Three U.S. soldiers wounded in a Venezuela raid are recovering, demonstrating heroism and discipline.
  • Venezuelan opposition leader Maria Corina Machado is advocating for free elections and bipartisan U.S. support from exile.
  • Senator Cruz anticipates potential democratic transitions in Iran, Venezuela, and Cuba within a year due to strong U.S. leadership.
  • The episode discusses the importance of U.S. defense innovation and supporting dissidents against communism.
  • Iran is experiencing protests, internet shutdowns, and a shifting U.S. policy response.

Deep Dive

  • Senator Ted Cruz visited three U.S. soldiers wounded during a raid to apprehend Nicolás Maduro.
  • All soldiers were reported recovering in stable condition, with none suffering life-threatening injuries.
  • One soldier helped plan and lead the raid and continued fighting after being shot.
  • Two soldiers requested and received approval from Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth to keep the bullet and shrapnel from their injuries as mementos.

  • Venezuelan opposition leader Maria Corina Machado met with former President Donald Trump, symbolically presenting him with her Nobel Peace Prize.
  • Machado presented the prize as gratitude for U.S. support and advocated for free and fair elections in Venezuela.
  • The host discussed Machado's courage and humility in presenting the Nobel Peace Prize while living in exile.

  • Following the reported removal of the Maduro regime, numerous political prisoners were released, though they remain under surveillance.
  • Maria Corina Machado met with approximately a dozen U.S. senators on Capitol Hill to discuss Venezuela's future.
  • Machado strongly advocates for swift, free, and fair elections, ideally within six months.
  • Senator Cruz noted his presence at the meeting due to the significant Venezuelan population in Texas and his past advocacy for Americans imprisoned in Venezuela.

  • Senator Ted Cruz discussed his long-standing opposition to communism, stemming from his family's history of imprisonment and torture.
  • He emphasized the importance of supporting dissidents and highlighting their struggles against tyrannical regimes worldwide.

  • Senator Ted Cruz suggested that under strong U.S. leadership, democratic transitions could occur in Iran, Venezuela, and Cuba within a year.
  • He noted that a Democrat senator present in a meeting agreed with his assessment of these potential changes' historical significance.
  • Cruz expressed optimism about pro-American governments in these regions within six months, believing it would significantly enhance global safety and security.

  • The discussion covered ongoing protests in Iran, the regime's violent response, and the difficulty in obtaining accurate casualty figures.
  • Elon Musk's Starlink was noted as a means to bypass internet blockages amidst rapidly changing events.
  • A State Department official suggested a regional shift away from Iranian influence could be imminent.
  • The current U.S. administration's approach to the Iranian protests was contrasted with the Obama administration's actions in 2009, highlighting a shift in support for protesters.
