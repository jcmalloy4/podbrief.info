Verdict with Ted Cruz

A Historic Year of Victories

Key Takeaways

  • The "Working Families Tax Cut" permanently extends Trump-era tax cuts and eliminates taxes on tips, overtime, and Social Security benefits.
  • Significant federal investments are allocated to border security, military rebuilding, and a U.S. return to the Moon by 2028.
  • New legislative initiatives include nationwide "Trump Accounts" for child investments and expanded school choice programs.
  • Policies address online safety, modernize air traffic control, and involve auctioning federal spectrum for technology development.
  • Regulatory rollbacks include the elimination of CAFE standards, aiming to reduce car costs and enhance vehicle safety.

Deep Dive

  • Reported illegal immigration decreased by over 99% due to policy changes.
  • More than $100 billion was invested in border security infrastructure, including walls, technology, and personnel.
  • Claimed economic benefits include reduced housing costs and rents due to deportations.

  • The "Working Families Tax Cut" allocates $150 billion to rebuild the military, with $24.5 billion specifically for the Coast Guard.
  • Funds support new ships, helicopters, and polar ice cutters for Arctic operations to counter Chinese and Russian presence.
  • The investment aims to enhance capabilities and stimulate U.S. shipbuilding.

  • The government is auctioning 800 MHz of federal spectrum to the private sector.
  • This initiative is projected to generate over $100 billion for the U.S. Treasury.
  • It aims to boost 5G and 6G development, create jobs, and provide a competitive advantage against China.

  • The Rotor Act mandates ADS-B technology for all aircraft, including military, to enhance collision avoidance.
  • This technology enables real-time tracking for planes and air traffic controllers.
  • Legislation was prompted by a near-collision involving an Army helicopter with its ADS-B turned off.
  • A $12.5 billion investment in modernizing air traffic control systems is also included.

  • The "Working Families Tax Cut" includes a school choice expansion providing dollar-for-dollar tax credits up to $1,700 for scholarship organization donations.
  • This provision is projected to generate billions for K-12 education and expand opportunities for millions of students.
  • The measure faced significant opposition and was nearly removed three times during the reconciliation process.

  • The 'Trump Accounts' provision, authored by Senator Cruz, will provide every child in America a $1,000 initial investment.
  • These tax-advantaged accounts allow up to $5,000 in annual contributions, invested in the S&P 500.
  • Projections suggest accounts could grow to $170,000 by age 18 and $700,000 by age 35, fostering new capitalists.

  • A $10 billion investment targets NASA and commercial space programs for lunar exploration.
  • The U.S. aims to return to the Moon by 2028, preceding China's 2030 target, with a focus on sustained human habitation.
  • This initiative is deemed strategically vital to national security and linked to over 50,000 jobs in Texas.

  • The elimination of CAFE (Corporate Average Fuel Economy) standards is presented as a regulatory rollback.
  • This action is claimed to lower car costs by thousands of dollars and enhance vehicle safety.
  • The change aims to allow for more steel and less plastic in car construction, countering a perceived push towards electric vehicles.

  • The 'Take It Down Act' criminalizes the posting of non-consensual intimate imagery, including revenge porn and deepfake pornography.
  • The legislation mandates immediate removal of such content from online platforms.
  • Championed by Melania Trump, this act seeks to protect victims and potentially save lives.
