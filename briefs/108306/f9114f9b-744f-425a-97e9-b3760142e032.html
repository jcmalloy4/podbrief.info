Verdict with Ted Cruz

Bonus: Daily Review with Clay and Buck - Oct 14 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Former President Donald Trump's 36-hour diplomatic mission resulted in a Gaza peace deal, securing Israeli hostages and Palestinian detainees.
  • Left-leaning media figures and Democrats, including Stephen Colbert and Jimmy Kimmel, publicly acknowledged Trump's diplomatic success.
  • New York Attorney General Letitia James faces mortgage fraud allegations concerning a Virginia property, including claims of misrepresentation.
  • Eric Trump discussed the substantial "lawfare" and legal expenditures faced by his family, estimated at $400 million, in his new book.

Deep Dive

  • Former President Trump's 36-hour diplomatic trip to Israel and Egypt led to a Gaza peace deal, which secured the release of Israeli hostages and nearly 2,000 Palestinian detainees.
  • Media figures like Stephen Colbert, Jimmy Kimmel, and Joe Scarborough acknowledged Trump's success in brokering the ceasefire.
  • This achievement is highlighted as surpassing that of previous presidents, from Carter to Obama, in Middle East peace negotiations.

  • The discussion explored the potential impact of Trump's Middle East peace deal on average American voters.
  • The hosts questioned if the news would resonate beyond engaged political circles and the Jewish American community.
  • They predicted the story might be quickly overshadowed by domestic concerns, suggesting a shift back to issues like Portland, Chicago, and trade with China.

  • Alyssa Farah Griffin, a former Trump White House communications director, publicly made a bet to wear a MAGA hat if President Trump secured the release of Israeli hostages.
  • Following the diplomatic success, the hosts urged Griffin to fulfill her public promise.

  • New York Attorney General Letitia James faces allegations of mortgage fraud tied to a Virginia property.
  • The indictment details claims James misrepresented the home as a second residence to obtain preferential loan rates.
  • It is alleged that James allowed family members, including a convicted felon, to live rent-free in the property, potentially falsifying loan applications.

  • Eric Trump discussed his new book, "Under Siege: My Family's Fight to Save Our Nation," which has become an Amazon bestseller.
  • He detailed the "lawfare" and legal challenges faced by his family, estimating $400 million was spent on defenses against politically motivated investigations and claims like the "Russia collusion hoax."
  • Eric Trump attributed his father's diplomatic successes and political resilience to a business-minded, deal-making approach and "superhuman" focus.
