Verdict with Ted Cruz

Deportations Cure Rent Inflation, When We Have No Evidence meets Do It Anyway plus the Final 3 Historic Victories of 2025 Week In Review

Key Takeaways

  • Mass immigration under the Biden administration is cited for increasing U.S. rental and housing prices.
  • Newly released emails suggest FBI doubts regarding probable cause for the 2022 Mar-a-Lago raid.
  • Upcoming 2026 tax policy changes propose no federal tax on tips, overtime, or Social Security for seniors.
  • Key legislative achievements include significant space investment, relaxed automotive standards, and the 'Take It Down Act'.

Deep Dive

  • A $10 billion investment in NASA and commercial space targets a U.S. lunar landing by 2028, aiming to surpass China's 2030 goal.
  • CAFE (Corporate Average Fuel Economy) standards were 'zeroed out' to reduce car costs and counteract Biden administration fuel economy rules.
  • The bipartisan 'Take It Down Act' makes posting non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI deepfakes, a felony and grants victims immediate removal rights.

  • Mass immigration under President Biden is linked to increased rental demand and housing prices across the U.S.
  • A Wharton study suggests a 1% population increase correlates with a 1% rise in rents, with recent rent declines (-1.1% YoY) attributed to Trump-era deportations.
  • A HUD report indicates Biden's immigration policies significantly raised housing demand, particularly for low-income Americans without assistance.
  • Between 2021 and 2024, the foreign-born population increased by over 6 million, contributing to higher housing demand and prices.

  • Upcoming 2026 tax policy changes include proposals for no federal tax on tips, overtime, or Social Security benefits for seniors.
  • These changes are anticipated to result in millions of IRS refunds for Americans starting in January 2026.
  • The host suggests that economic growth, spurred by tax cuts and deregulation, is seen as the primary solution to 'affordability' concerns.

  • Newly released emails indicate FBI officials doubted probable cause for the 2022 Mar-a-Lago raid, noting 'little had been developed' regarding culpability.
  • Despite FBI concerns, the raid proceeded under pressure from the Biden Department of Justice.
  • The situation has raised questions about potential political motives behind the search and alleged abuse of power.

  • Despite FBI field office reservations and suggestions for less intrusive options, the DOJ concluded probable cause for the Mar-a-Lago search warrant existed.
  • The wide-ranging search was described by an FBI agent as 'stunning' and potentially counterproductive.
  • Discussion highlights calls for accountability and potential congressional hearings, particularly by the Senate Judiciary Committee.
  • The release of these emails is attributed to the Trump Justice Department, with suggestions the Biden DOJ attempted to conceal them.
