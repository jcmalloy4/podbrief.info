    Verdict with Ted Cruz

    Trump's Art of the Peace Deal , Charlie Kirk Honored plus Eric Trump & the cost of Political Warfare Week In Review

    Key Takeaways

    • President Trump's "peace through strength" policy is credited for Mideast stability and decisive foreign actions.
    • The government shutdown, dubbed "Schumer Shutdown," is impacting federal employees and military families, with blame placed on Democrats.
    • Charlie Kirk was posthumously honored with the Presidential Medal of Freedom, and his widow, Erica, publicly forgave his assassin.
    • A philosophical difference emerged between Erica Kirk's Christian forgiveness and Donald Trump's combative stance on enemies.
    • Eric Trump detailed the family's significant legal expenditures, totaling $400 million, citing political motivations.
    • Donald Trump's parenting style emphasized discipline, hard work, and instilling values from a young age.

    Deep Dive

    • The discussion highlights President Trump's "peace through strength" policy as crucial for a Middle East peace agreement.
    • Historical examples cited include the defeat of ISIS, strikes on Iranian targets, and actions against narco-terrorists in Venezuela.
    • Speakers contrasted this approach with Joe Biden's perceived weakness, claiming it led to conflicts in Ukraine and Gaza.

    • The government shutdown, in its second week and referred to as the 'Schumer shutdown,' is affecting healthcare and military families.
    • Republicans have repeatedly voted to fund the government, but Democrats are reportedly blocking efforts, needing 60 Senate votes.
    • Congressional members, including Democrats, continue to receive paychecks, while Senator Ted Cruz has requested his be withheld until the shutdown ends.

    • A memorial service for Charlie Kirk at the White House included President Donald Trump posthumously awarding him the Presidential Medal of Freedom.
    • The event was attended by cabinet members and politicians, reflecting on Kirk's vision for mobilizing young conservatives.
    • Speakers highlighted Kirk's entrepreneurial spirit, noting his early movement-building at 18 years old.

    • Charlie Kirk's widow, Erica, delivered a speech at his funeral emphasizing Christian forgiveness for her husband's murderer.
    • This moment, drawing parallels to Jesus's words from the cross, was described as deeply moving and a powerful testament to the gospel.
    • It is suggested that Kirk's death amplified his message, inspiring more people than in his 31 years of life.

    • A contrast was drawn between Erica Kirk's public forgiveness of her husband's murderer and Donald Trump's more combative stance.
    • Trump reportedly admitted to hating his enemies and liking to fight them, differing from Erica's stated prayer for her enemies.
    • This theological and personal difference in approach to adversaries was a striking element observed during the service.

    • Eric Trump discussed the financial and legal battles faced by his family, stating they spent approximately $400 million on legal defense.
    • He criticized the legal system for perceived bias and overreach, citing the Manhattan District Attorney's case as a political prosecution.
    • Prosecutors like Letitia James, Alvin Bragg, Fani Willis, and Jack Smith were named in relation to ongoing legal challenges.

    • Eric Trump reflected on Donald Trump as a father, emphasizing discipline, hard work, and a focus on instilling values.
    • He recounted childhood experiences, including working on construction sites from age 11 and receiving practical lessons on earning money.
    • Eric Trump highlighted his father's advice for parents: "Keep them poor, make them work early."
