    Verdict with Ted Cruz

    Eric Trump-Exclusive Interview on his new book Under Siege

    Key Takeaways

    • Eric Trump's book, "Under Siege," details alleged governmental corruption and political targeting of his family.
    • The Trump family reportedly spent approximately $400 million defending against investigations and lawsuits.
    • Hundreds of Trump-affiliated bank accounts were allegedly closed by major financial institutions due to political pressure.
    • Eric Trump claims extensive 'weaponization' of government and private sectors against conservatives.
    • The episode includes critiques of progressive policies and an endorsement of cryptocurrency for financial freedom.

    Deep Dive

    • Eric Trump's book, "Under Siege: My Family's Fight to Save Our Nation," debuted as the number one best-selling book in America.
    • He describes his family as a "pinata" in a fight against governmental corruption.
    • Trump claims his family was targeted because they lacked constitutional protections while running the Trump Organization outside of Washington, D.C.

    • Eric Trump reports receiving over 100 subpoenas as part of what he calls political persecution.
    • The FBI raid on Mar-a-Lago involved 30 agents searching personal spaces, including Melania's and Barron's rooms.
    • Attorney-client and HIPAA-related documents were reportedly seized during the raid, which Trump described as politically motivated.

    • Hundreds of bank accounts for Trump businesses and affiliates, including at Capital One, JP Morgan, Bank of America, and First Republic, were reportedly closed due to political pressure.
    • Shopify also canceled accounts linked to the Trump name, according to Eric Trump.
    • Bank employees allegedly confirmed these actions were politically motivated, impacting numerous employees and vendors.

    • Alleged pressure extended to employees and vendors, forcing reliance on smaller, local banks due to widespread account closures, even impacting the cryptocurrency sector.
    • Reporters from publications like The Washington Post allegedly harassed vendors and employees, attempting to solicit negative testimony against Donald Trump.
    • District attorneys and attorneys general were implicated in strategies to pressure businesses and individuals.

    • The Trump family allegedly spent approximately $400 million defending themselves against investigations and lawsuits from various prosecutors.
    • The organization faced difficulty securing a $600 million bond due to alleged pressure on insurance companies.
    • Banks, courts, and insurance companies were reportedly pressured to avoid doing business with the Trump Organization.

    • Eric Trump describes his father, Donald Trump, as a disciplined parent who instilled a strong work ethic.
    • The Trump children were taught the value of earning money rather than inheriting it.
    • The family emphasizes "moral integrity," contrasting it with alleged misconduct by Hunter Biden.

    • Eric Trump recounts his father's decision to enter politics, noting initial unfamiliarity with issues such as immigration.
    • Donald Trump's background was in building hotels before transitioning into public life.
    • Eric Trump also contrasts his family's upbringing, focused on discipline and work ethic, with Hunter Biden's alleged misconduct.

    • Eric Trump denounces progressive policies like DEI initiatives and "wokeness" as threats to American values.
    • He frames the Trump family's experiences as part of a national struggle against "corrupt elites."
    • The book's popularity is seen as reflecting public anger over issues like IRS weaponization and financial freedom suppression.
