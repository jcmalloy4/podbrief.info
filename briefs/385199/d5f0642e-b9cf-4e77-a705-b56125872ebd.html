Fast Money Live! The Desk Trades the Holidays as the S&P and Dow Close at Records 12/11/25

Key Takeaways

  • Broadcom reported better-than-expected revenue, projecting doubled AI chip sales and increasing its dividend by 10%.
  • Lululemon's stock surged on strong earnings but faces a CEO transition and competitive market share losses.
  • Disney announced a $1 billion investment in OpenAI, aiming to integrate its IP into AI video generation.
  • The broader AI trade saw a rotation, with Microsoft and Meta experiencing relief rallies after recent sell-offs.
  • Analysts are advising diversification for 2026, citing a fully priced-in bullish consensus.

Deep Dive

  • Broadcom's shares rose after reporting better-than-expected revenue and forecasting a doubling of AI chip sales.
  • The company's report highlighted significant AI revenue growth and a 10% dividend increase.
  • Key questions include the identity of a fourth major custom AI chip customer and potential cannibalization from its Google relationship.

  • Lululemon's stock surged post-earnings, but CEO Calvin McDonald is stepping down next month, staying as a senior advisor until March.
  • The CEO transition and a disappointing Q4 forecast are seen as overshadowing the earnings beat.
  • Founder Chip Wilson is reportedly considering a proxy campaign, having criticized the company as 'in a nosedive' in October.

  • Lululemon's Q4 guidance reflects a slowdown post-Thanksgiving, with August being the strongest month.
  • Margins face a 410 basis point drag in Q4 due to the elimination of the de minimis exemption and tariffs.
  • Gross margin deterioration and an 11% year-over-year inventory increase persist, impacting competitive positioning.

  • Disney announced a $1 billion investment in OpenAI to integrate its characters into AI video generation.
  • CEO Bob Iger stated the goal is to reach younger audiences and capitalize on AI growth.
  • Analysts expressed mixed views, calling the deal unusual and potentially 'circular vendor financing' but acknowledging IP value.

  • The panel discussed investing in Constellation Energy versus GE Vernova for powering AI data centers.
  • Tim Seymour favored Constellation Energy, citing its valuation and dual nuclear/natural gas strategy.
  • GE Vernova's stock pulled back after strong guidance from its recent earnings report.

  • Rebecca Patterson, former Bridgewater chief investment strategist, outlined a 'tempered bull' stance for 2026.
  • She anticipates 2% GDP growth and stronger earnings per share, supported by Fed cuts, stimulus, and buybacks.
  • Patterson advised diversification into gold, overseas investments, and sectors like banks and global defense.

  • Concerns were raised about the sustainability of current AI-driven market growth.
  • Potential risks include significant market declines if AI CapEx expectations are not met.
  • Analysts compared potential outcomes to the dot-com bubble or a less severe market correction.

  • Listeners asked about potential IPOs, specifically naming OpenAI, SpaceX, and Anthropic.
  • A question on Meta's budget reallocation from the metaverse to AI and advertising was largely seen as positive.
  • Discussions covered small-cap stock viability and Generation Jones retirement spending in casinos and cruises.
