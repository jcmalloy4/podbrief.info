CNBC's "Fast Money"

Fast Money 9/17/25

Key Takeaways

  • The Federal Reserve cut rates by 25 basis points, a move largely anticipated by the market.
  • Lyft shares surged 13% after announcing a strategic partnership with autonomous vehicle company Waymo.
  • Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly presented promising oral weight loss pill trial results, facing market concerns about competition and pricing.
  • Meta Platforms unveiled new AI-powered smart glasses, part of its Reality Labs division's significant hardware investment.
  • Walmart's stock reached an intraday record high, driven by its leadership in AI commerce and advertising technology.

Deep Dive

  • The Federal Reserve implemented a widely anticipated quarter-point interest rate cut, despite internal disagreements on future rate paths.
  • The Dow reached a new high, and small caps gained, while some tech stocks pulled back following the announcement.
  • Bond yields remained steady, and oil and gold fell as the U.S. dollar strengthened.
  • One governor advocated for a larger rate cut, indicating differing views within the Fed committee.

  • Tim Seymour characterized the 25-basis-point rate cut as 'hawkish,' despite market expectations for more aggressive cuts.
  • The S&P 500 had risen 10% and gold 11% in the three months preceding the cut, suggesting anticipation.
  • Yields on 30-year and two-year bonds fell approximately 10% in two months, reflecting pre-emptive market adjustments.
  • Chair Powell's statement about an 'insurance cut,' rather than the start of a cutting cycle, was perceived as a bearish signal.

  • Richard Fisher, former Dallas Federal Reserve President, commended governors Bowman and Waller for adhering to economic logic in their support for rate cuts.
  • The discussion addressed Stephen Myron's perceived dual role within the White House and the Federal Reserve, raising questions about objectivity.
  • The president's focus on employment data is linked to voter demographics and desires for rate cuts to alleviate economic distress.
  • Fisher stressed that central bank decisions should be guided by economic reasoning and persuasion, not external influence.

  • Job numbers were revisited, with revisions totaling nearly one million jobs, suggesting a weaker labor market than previously stated.
  • Participants debated the Federal Reserve's rate cut strategy, with some advocating for larger 50-basis-point cuts due to weakening economic data.
  • The Fed's wariness of inflation caused by tariffs was highlighted as a factor influencing its cautious approach.
  • It was noted that current economic conditions, arguably weaker than the previous year, are only seeing one rate cut compared to a 75-basis-point cut then.

  • Lyft's stock surged 13% today, reaching its highest level in three years, due to a partnership with driverless car company Waymo, expanding into Nashville.
  • Analysts attribute the stock's rise to increased credibility with Wall Street following a management change and a history of insider buying.
  • Lyft's stock had already experienced a 65% increase in the prior month, driven by growth factors including international expansion and the acquisition of the Free Now app.
  • The partnership is framed as a response to a deal not involving human drivers, with speculation about Waymo's capital position influencing partner selection.

  • Alibaba's stock rallied over 7% this week, nearing four-year highs, following reports of a major customer signing for its AI chips.
  • One analyst suggested trimming positions due to the stock's steep, uncorrected advance, recommending selling calls as an option.
  • Another perspective indicated declining Chinese earnings growth, raising doubts about the sustainability of the current momentum.
  • Chasing the stock now was cautioned against, given the broader Chinese market sentiment and ongoing trade talks.

  • Novo Nordisk announced its higher-dose oral obesity pill helped patients lose nearly 17% of their body weight over 64 weeks.
  • Eli Lilly's weight loss pill trial results showed patients losing about 12% of body weight after 72 weeks.
  • A Novo executive stated their oral obesity drug is the first comparable to injectables in efficacy, tolerability, and safety, with fewer side effects than Eli Lilly's in a head-to-head trial.
  • Both drugs are expected to launch in 2026, targeting a market estimated to impact tens of millions of Americans, with Eli Lilly noted for its scalability advantage.
  • Market concerns about pricing, potentially lower than injectables, and intense competition from companies like Amgen and Roche are dampening stock reactions.

  • Meta Platforms unveiled new AI-powered smart glasses, codenamed HyperNova, expected to cost around $800 with an integrated display.
  • The new glasses build on previous Ray-Ban collaborations and are part of Meta's Reality Labs division's increasing focus on hardware and long-term AI integration.
  • Reality Labs has incurred significant financial losses, leading to scrutiny regarding the return on investment for Meta's AI and metaverse initiatives.
  • Analysts discussed the potential price point and functionality, comparing them to previous models and the substantial investment required.
