Can Oracle Get Shares Back in Rally Mode, and a Trade School on the Netflix/Warner Brothers Deal 12/5/25

Key Takeaways

  • Oracle's stock dropped 40% amid AI investment and debt concerns before its earnings report.
  • Broadcom's earnings are a key indicator for broadening the AI trade beyond NVIDIA's performance.
  • The Federal Reserve anticipates rate cuts, moving towards a 3% neutral rate, supported by productivity tailwinds.
  • Ulta Beauty reported strong earnings and raised its outlook, signaling resilient consumer spending in beauty.
  • Defense contractor AeroVironment plans to double its size, driven by demand for drone technology.
  • Netflix made a $72 billion offer for Warner Brothers Discovery's studio and streaming assets.
  • Alphabet received a bullish rating, citing strong AI monetization, market share gains, and chip advancements.

Deep Dive

  • Oracle's stock has declined nearly 40% since its last positive earnings report in September.
  • Concerns include the financing for its AI build-out and soaring credit default swap (CDS) spreads, which hit highs not seen since 2009.
  • Debate exists on whether Oracle acts as an AI barometer or represents an idiosyncratic story in the market.
  • Analysts note a $70 billion increase in net debt over five years, with projections for another $10 billion increase next year.

  • Upcoming earnings reports for Oracle and Broadcom are highlighted as crucial for assessing the broader AI trade.
  • Broadcom's stock is near all-time highs, and its performance is seen as a barometer for market sentiment on AI beyond NVIDIA.
  • A strong Broadcom report could signal broader market strength, though some express concern over debt rather than demand in AI.
  • Analysts noted a significant valuation discrepancy between Broadcom and NVIDIA.

  • Panelists predict a Federal Reserve rate cut, anticipating a move towards a neutral rate around 3% over time.
  • The market's rallies are partly driven by expectations of Fed dovishness and favorable strategist calls for 2026.
  • An expert anticipates slow expansion for 2026, noting the Federal Reserve's flexibility due to productivity tailwinds.

  • Ulta Beauty shares surged nearly 13% to a record high after reporting strong earnings, beating estimates and raising its full-year outlook.
  • The company cited strong performance during Black Friday and Cyber Monday, with shares up 38% year-to-date.
  • Ulta reported higher average ticket prices and transaction numbers, alongside market share gains in both mass and luxury beauty segments.
  • Analysts view Ulta's stock valuation as reasonable given its strong execution and consumer spending resilience.

  • AeroVironment CEO Waheed Nawabi outlined plans to double the company's size in three to five years.
  • Growth is driven by organic expansion and strong demand for drone technology due to geopolitical threats.
  • The company possesses capacity to ramp up production significantly, from thousands to potentially millions of units.
  • AeroVironment has expanded its export reach to over 100 countries, with Ukraine now representing less than 5% of revenue.

  • CACI is presented as a more attractive investment than AeroVironment in the defense sector.
  • This assessment is based on CACI's valuation, despite AeroVironment's extraordinary growth.

  • Netflix made a surprising $72 billion offer for Warner Brothers Discovery's studio and streaming businesses.
  • The move is perceived as both an offensive strategy for content acquisition and a defensive move against competitors.
  • Analysts discussed regulatory and antitrust aspects, with arguments that Netflix's market share might not concern regulators.
  • Concerns were raised regarding Paramount's financial stability compared to Netflix in a potential bidding war, which included a $5.8 billion breakup fee.

  • Pivotal Research issued a bullish call on Alphabet, raising its price target to $400 per share, citing market share gains and AI monetization.
  • Google is highlighted for its advancements in the AI race, leveraging cheaper chips and profitability gains.
  • The significance of the TPU protocol and YouTube's advantage in content creation were mentioned as positive factors.
