The Battle For Warner Brothers Discovery… And The Outlook For Banks In 2026 12/22/25

Key Takeaways

  • Larry Ellison's $40 billion backing strengthens Paramount Skydance's bid for Warner Brothers Discovery.
  • Gold and silver prices have reached record highs, with silver's industrial demand exceeding supply.
  • Alphabet is acquiring Intersect for nearly $5 billion to secure AI data center power and capacity.
  • Skepticism grows over Tesla's stock performance amid increasing EV market competition and delivery concerns.
  • The banking sector, particularly small caps and regional banks, shows potential for significant growth in 2026.

Deep Dive

  • Paramount Skydance increased its bid for Warner Brothers Discovery.
  • Larry Ellison personally guaranteed over $40 billion in equity financing, addressing previous funding concerns.
  • An analyst predicted an Ellison win, emphasizing the critical role of content and streaming competitiveness for the next five years.

  • Lionsgate Vice Chairman Michael Burns believes the Warner Brothers Discovery battle validates premium content value.
  • He noted Lionsgate's library represents a significant asset with strong revenue and margin potential.
  • Lionsgate is now positioned as a pure-play content company after separating from Starz.

  • Lionsgate's stock is perceived as undervalued, with market recognition expected soon due to its extensive content library.
  • Projections for fiscal years 2026 and 2027 indicate a strong movie slate and twice the television output from the previous year.
  • The company is focused on consolidating its businesses and strengthening earnings projections across management, television, and film divisions.

  • Gold and silver reached record highs, marking their best year since 1979, with mining stocks also hitting all-time highs.
  • Silver's annual demand is exceeding supply, with its price expected to rise considerably due to significant industrial applications.
  • Bitcoin, tracked by the IBIT ETF, is underperforming, suggesting investors are favoring gold as a diversification asset due to market nervousness.

  • Alphabet is acquiring data center and energy infrastructure company Intersect for nearly $5 billion.
  • The acquisition aims to secure power and capacity for Alphabet's AI build-out, addressing surging demand.
  • This move represents vertical integration to secure energy supply, managing a projected U.S. power deficit where demand outstrips supply.

  • Analysts express skepticism about Tesla's continued stock growth, citing competitors like Ford and GM rolling back EV investments.
  • Concerns include potential double-digit delivery declines due to the expiration of EV tax credits and unpopularity in Europe.
  • Robo-taxi limitations and challenges with battery capacity for Optimus robots are highlighted as significant hurdles.

  • KBW's Chris McGratty highlights Citi's ongoing turnaround, business simplification, and projected return on equity improvement, noting it trades below its tangible book value.
  • He sees continued opportunity in the bank trade due to excess capital, with large bank earnings revisions outperforming smaller ones.
  • McGratty favors a selective approach for regional banks, specifically liking Citizens Bank for its organic momentum and capital markets exposure.

  • The Russell 2000 has shown strong performance, hitting all-time highs and pacing for its best monthly gain streak since mid-2021.
  • The outlook for small caps in 2026 is considered positive, especially with potential benefits from lower interest rates due to their sensitivity to variable rates.
  • Three small-cap stock picks include Ollie's (discount retailer), LeMetch (healthcare), and Molis (acquiring bankers during private equity downturn).
