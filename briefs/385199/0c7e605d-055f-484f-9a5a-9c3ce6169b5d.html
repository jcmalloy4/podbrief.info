The Latest AI Investments From Tech Giants… And A Bull Case Still In Place

Key Takeaways

  • Dow Transports index surged, signaling potential broader economic health and manufacturing sector expansion.
  • Oracle plans to raise up to $50 billion for AI infrastructure, amid evolving and scrutinized AI financing models.
  • The AI revolution faces headwinds including CapEx monetization, energy shortages, and geopolitical tensions.
  • Waymo secured $16 billion in new funding, tripling its valuation to $126 billion for global expansion.
  • Palantir exceeded earnings expectations and provided strong guidance, driven by U.S. commercial growth.
  • Disney's stock fell on weaker guidance and ongoing concerns about its direct-to-consumer business and CEO succession.
  • A bullish market outlook persists despite significant volatility in gold, silver, and cryptocurrency markets.
  • NVIDIA's potential $100 billion OpenAI investment is complicated by reported chip performance issues and strategic concerns.

Deep Dive

  • The Dow Transports index reached a new record high, gaining over 3%, with all constituent stocks showing positive movement.
  • The ISM Manufacturing Index posted its best reading since August 2022, marking the first expansion since February of the previous year.
  • This data suggests a potential inflection point for sectors like airlines and shippers, which historically outperform in the 12 months following such an economic turn.
  • Specific transportation stocks like FedEx rallied, while UPS was noted for its compelling valuation and dividend yield, showing signs of a trendline breakout.

  • Oracle plans to raise up to $50 billion for its AI infrastructure build-out.
  • This strategic move positions Oracle as a core player in the AI revolution, with potential for trillions in spending over time.
  • NVIDIA's CEO indicated an intention to invest, potentially NVIDIA's largest, impacting the broader AI ecosystem including OpenAI.

  • OpenAI's business model faces skepticism, particularly its pivot to an advertising-based approach, raising questions about its potentially inflated private market valuation.
  • Headwinds for the AI revolution include Capital Expenditure versus monetization challenges, energy shortages for data centers, and US-China relations.
  • Oracle's equity-linked issuance for AI infrastructure is being scrutinized for potential dilutive effects on traditional investors and sector valuations.

  • Waymo closed a new $16 billion funding round, nearly tripling its previous valuation to $126 billion post-money.
  • The round was three times oversubscribed and led by new investors Dragoneer, DST Global, and Sequoia.
  • This capital injection will fund expansion into over 20 new cities globally, including Tokyo and London, to support fleet growth.

  • Palantir shares rose after beating revenue and earnings expectations and providing strong guidance.
  • CEO Alex Karp stated Palantir's U.S. business is reaching "escape velocity," with significant commercial revenue growth.
  • The company's AI applications are noted for enabling upskilling and job creation in manufacturing.

  • Disney shares fell significantly after issuing weaker-than-expected guidance, raising concerns about its overall business model.
  • The direct-to-consumer (DTC) business shows momentum, but international headwinds affect the parks division.
  • Investors await news on CEO succession, with the board expected to vote on a replacement and John DeMaro reportedly a frontrunner.

  • Evercore ISI's Julian Emmanuel maintains a bullish market outlook, predicting the S&P 500 will climb despite volatility in gold, silver, and Bitcoin.
  • Gold and silver settled lower, reaching their lowest levels since early January, with silver dropping 30% on Friday.
  • Emmanuel believes the market's dip was a one-day event influenced by psychology, not fundamental economic weakness, citing strong trucking rates.

  • OpenAI CEO Sam Altman tweeted positively about NVIDIA, but a Reuters report claims OpenAI is dissatisfied with NVIDIA's AI chip speed for specific tasks.
  • NVIDIA clarified its CEO's $100 billion investment mention in OpenAI was an intention, not a commitment.
  • Sources confirm NVIDIA's participation in OpenAI's latest funding round, which could be NVIDIA's largest investment ever, despite reported chip performance issues.
