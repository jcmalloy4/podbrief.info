Fed Flying Blind Ahead Of Rate Decision… And A Homebuying Bummer 10/28/25

Key Takeaways

  • NVIDIA's market cap surged, driven by AI chip orders and strategic partnerships with major companies.
  • The Federal Reserve navigates an interest rate decision amid limited official data and rising corporate layoffs.
  • Mortgage rates are predicted to remain above 6% until 2028, impacting housing market dynamics.
  • PayPal announced integration with OpenAI's ChatGPT for in-chat payments, boosting its stock and growth outlook.

Deep Dive

  • NVIDIA's stock rose nearly 5%, adding over $230 billion in market cap and approaching $5 trillion.
  • CEO Jensen Huang announced cumulative orders for Blackwell and Rubin chips reached $500 billion through calendar 2026.
  • Partnerships with Nokia, CrowdStrike, Palantir, Lowe's, and Uber were unveiled at the GTC event.
  • Analysts re-evaluated NVIDIA's valuation, projecting 25-50% higher shipments by the end of 2026.

  • Concerns were raised about NVIDIA's high valuation, with its price-to-sales ratio approaching 19 times revenue.
  • A contrasting view suggests NVIDIA's expansion into AI infrastructure creates a 'flywheel' effect, supporting a higher valuation.
  • The company's strong gross margins and central role in the AI ecosystem are cited as mitigating valuation concerns.

  • The Federal Reserve faces an interest rate decision with limited official jobs data due to a government shutdown.
  • Amazon and UPS announced significant layoffs, signaling pressure on the employment market.
  • Analysts debate if job cuts signal positive news for Fed rate cuts or broader economic trouble from rising unemployment.

  • UBS's Evan Brown notes a strong GDP coupled with a soft labor market, influencing Federal Reserve actions.
  • The speed of increase in the unemployment rate is more critical than a specific level for market impact.
  • A chart shows a 70% stock market increase since ChatGPT's October 2022 launch, concurrent with a 30% drop in job openings.

  • President Trump is set to meet China's President Xi to discuss trade, TikTok, rare earths, fentanyl, and shipping fees.
  • During his Asia tour, Trump announced Toyota's $10 billion investment in the U.S., with potential to reach $400 billion.
  • A U.S.-China trade deal could boost both U.S. and Chinese stock markets, with K-Web (China) identified as a potential buy.

  • PayPal shares surged after announcing integration with OpenAI's ChatGPT for in-chat payment transactions.
  • The company reported better-than-expected earnings and raised guidance.
  • This integration is viewed as a significant development, potentially positioning ChatGPT as an 'everything app'.

  • Housing economist Mike Fratentoni predicts mortgage rates will remain above 6% until 2028, citing rising term premiums and inflation fears.
  • He described 2023 as the housing market's low point due to high mortgage rates.
  • Home sales are forecast to increase by 5% in 2026, driven by increased inventory from new construction and existing homeowners.

  • Visa reported earnings per share and revenue that exceeded Wall Street estimates.
  • The company provided guidance for fiscal year 2026, projecting revenue growth in the 'low double digits,' surpassing analyst expectations.
  • October spending trends were solid, with U.S. spend growing approximately 7% year-over-year.
  • Visa's CEO described consumer spending as healthy, highlighting investments in payment services for the AI industry.
