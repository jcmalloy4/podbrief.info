AI Ripple Rally Boosts Copper Miners… And The Fed’s Next Move As Rally Rolls On 10/8/25

Key Takeaways

  • AI-driven data center demand is significantly boosting copper prices and mining stocks.
  • Gold reached new record highs, with analysts debating its sustainability and profit-taking advice.
  • Federal Reserve minutes reveal a divided outlook on interest rate cuts, impacting market expectations.
  • Power companies are emerging as beneficiaries of increased electricity demand from AI infrastructure.
  • NVIDIA explores new strategies to expand AI infrastructure access and manage GPU obsolescence risks.
  • Zeta Global showcases consistent growth and profitability through its AI-powered marketing cloud.

Deep Dive

  • AI-related demand from data centers is significantly increasing copper prices, benefiting miners like Freeport McMoran and Southern Copper.
  • BHP's CEO forecasts a 70% increase in copper demand by 2050 due to data centers and energy transition.
  • Copper prices have risen 12% in October and 27% year-to-date, exceeding $5 per pound.
  • Data centers may require 3 to 10 times more copper than standard facilities, exacerbating supply constraints.

  • Gold achieved a record high for the 44th time this year, surpassing $4,000, driven by dollar debasement and central bank purchases, particularly from China.
  • Analyst Carter Worth advised booking profits after predicting a breakout, citing a measured move objective.
  • Overbought conditions, with RSI above 90, indicate potential for a market sell-off in tandem with broader market declines.
  • Gold is being explored as a market hedge amid continued PE expansion, contrasting with industrial metals and Bitcoin.

  • Power companies like Quanta Services, Constellation, Vistra, GE Vernova, and NRG are benefiting from increased demand for power and copper driven by the AI boom.
  • Analysts differentiate between companies with regulated energy contracts and those able to sell to the highest bidder.
  • The inflationary impact of rising energy and utility costs is highlighted, with Constellation Energy (CEG) specifically mentioned to benefit from these trends.

  • NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang expressed surprise at AMD's deal with OpenAI, while NVIDIA invested $2 billion in Elon Musk's XAI.
  • OpenAI is reported to lose billions annually despite significant revenue, raising questions about AI infrastructure sustainability.
  • NVIDIA's strategy involves expanding its customer base beyond hyperscalers, enabling companies to lease GPUs and reduce capital expenditures.
  • This leasing model shifts the risk of GPU obsolescence to leasing companies, rather than NVIDIA directly.

  • Guggenheim raised its price target for Eli Lilly to $948, citing strong U.S. sales and volume.
  • Lilly's high valuation is primarily driven by its weight-loss drugs, positioning it alongside Novo Nordisk.
  • There is speculation about a potential valuation catch-up for other pharmaceutical companies like Merck and Bristol Myers in the weight-loss drug market.

  • Federal Reserve minutes indicate a divided outlook on interest rates, with a narrow majority expecting two more cuts this year.
  • A segment of participants favored holding rates steady, while the 10-year Treasury yield remains largely unchanged for 18 months.
  • The Fed's balance sheet and quantitative tightening (QT) continuation are noted, with reserves approaching a critical level below $3 trillion.
  • The market anticipates lower rates due to a potential change in Fed leadership and stable longer-term rates, pushing the stock market to new record highs.

  • Zeta Global, an AI marketing cloud company, uses AI since 2017 to process trillions of signals, predicting consumer behavior for marketing efficiency.
  • The company reports a $5 to $7 return for every dollar spent by clients, aiming for $10 through recent acquisitions.
  • Zeta Global has achieved 16 consecutive quarters of beating guidance and raising forecasts, with over 20% organic compounded growth for four years.
  • Its competitive advantage stems from owning one of the world's largest first-party data clouds and an integrated AI platform launched in 2021.

  • Upcoming third-quarter earnings reports from Delta, Pepsi, and Levi's will be scrutinized for commentary on the government shutdown, tariffs, and consumer spending.
  • Delta is a key focus for fuel costs and revenue segmentation.
  • Pepsi is noted for insights into consumer behavior and margins, with potential impacts from GLP1 drugs.
  • Zeta Global's 'publisher cloud' aims to help publishers monetize content as search engines answer more queries directly.
