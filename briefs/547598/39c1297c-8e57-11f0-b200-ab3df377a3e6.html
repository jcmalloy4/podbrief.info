Introducing: Camp Swamp Road

Key Takeaways

  • A man was killed in a shooting on Camp Swamp Road following a 911 call reporting a reckless driver.
  • Police initially ruled the shooting a justifiable homicide under South Carolina's stand-your-ground law.
  • A Wall Street Journal investigation, aided by secret recordings, suggests a potential cover-up.

Deep Dive

  • On September 9, 2023, a 911 caller reported a reckless driver pointing a gun on Camp Swamp Road in rural South Carolina.
  • The situation escalated rapidly, resulting in a man being shot dead seconds after the report.

  • WSJ reporter Valerie Bauerlein is investigating the Camp Swamp Road shooting, which police initially ruled a justifiable homicide.
  • The initial ruling was made under South Carolina's stand-your-ground law, but a potential cover-up is now suggested.
  • Secret recordings and a grieving sister's quest for truth are key factors challenging the initial police findings.
