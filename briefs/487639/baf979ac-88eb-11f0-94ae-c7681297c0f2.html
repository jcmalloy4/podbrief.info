The 25 Best Movies of the Century: No. 9 - 'Mad Max: Fury Road’

Key Takeaways

  • "Mad Max: Fury Road" ranks as the ninth-best 21st-century film.
  • The film employs visual storytelling and minimal dialogue for a "pure cinema" experience.
  • Charlize Theron's performance as Furiosa is considered a career-defining role.
  • "Mad Max: Fury Road" earned six Academy Awards from 10 nominations in 2015.
  • Director George Miller's career spans from the "Mad Max" trilogy to family-friendly animation.

Deep Dive

  • Conceived 35 years before its 2015 release, the film reflects director George Miller's long-term vision.
  • Miller's return to the "Mad Max" universe followed a 28-year gap since "Beyond Thunderdome."
  • Hosts Sean Fennessey and Amanda Dobbins highlight the film's unique sound design and unprecedented filmmaking achievements.
  • The theatrical experience of the film was immediately impactful, likened to receiving an "adrenaline shot."

  • The film achieves "pure cinema" through its visual storytelling, drawing parallels to early film masters like Eisenstein and Griffith.
  • It conveys narrative and emotion with minimal dialogue, almost functioning as a silent film despite its loud sound.
  • The host notes the film uses a high visual information density and a sped-up frame rate to create a hyper-realistic feel.
  • "Fury Road's" straightforward methodology enhances its unique artistic and craft elements within modern cinema.

  • "Mad Max: Fury Road" is described as a "mini epic" due to its singular, day-long chase structure.
  • This contrasts with the "Furiosa" episode's origin story and longer timeline, which garnered a 'cult fandom.'
  • George Miller's distinctive sensibility creates a world of visceral, odd characters and unusual comic timing.
  • Imperator Furiosa's characterization remains grounded amidst the surrounding extravagance, maintaining relatability.

  • Charlize Theron's performance as Imperator Furiosa is characterized by her imposing physical presence and minimal dialogue.
  • Her role in "Mad Max: Fury Road" is widely seen as a career-defining moment, building on her Oscar win for "Monster."
  • The film established Theron as a powerful female lead in Hollywood's action genre, capable of demanding roles.
  • Post-"Fury Road," Theron's career trajectory included successful action films like "Atomic Blonde" and the "Fast and Furious" franchise.

  • Hosts debate if Furiosa is Charlize Theron's signature role, comparing it to her Oscar-winning turn in "Monster."
  • Tom Hardy's portrayal of Max is contrasted with Mel Gibson's, noting Hardy's voice acting was perceived as distracting.
  • The film's focus on Furiosa and female power is enhanced by a less central performance from Max.
  • Nicholas Hoult's energetic and transformative performance as 'Nux' is praised by the hosts.

  • "Mad Max: Fury Road" received 10 Academy Award nominations at the 2015 Oscars.
  • The film secured six "below-the-line" awards, including for sound design, but not major categories.
  • "Spotlight" won Best Picture, while "The Revenant" earned Best Director and Cinematography.
  • Hosts suggest "Fury Road" did not win major awards because it was not primarily an acting showcase, unlike "Spotlight."

  • George Miller's career trajectory includes the original "Mad Max" trilogy, then films like "The Witches of Eastwick."
  • He later shifted focus to family-friendly features, producing "Babe" and directing "Babe: Pig in the City."
  • Miller also directed the "Happy Feet" animated films before his eventual return to the "Mad Max" universe.
  • Despite artistic achievements, "Mad Max: Fury Road" also succeeded as a blockbuster, grossing nearly $400 million.

  • "Mad Max: Fury Road" is noted for its blend of practical effects, CGI, and score, differentiating it from typical CGI usage.
  • The film's practical effects and stunt work draw parallels to the "John Wick" franchise and "Mission: Impossible - Fallout."
  • Hosts compare "Fury Road" to other action films, including "Edge of Tomorrow," "RRR," and "Top Gun Maverick."
  • It is distinguished by its mythic and poetic storytelling, setting it apart from action films focused on simpler narratives.
