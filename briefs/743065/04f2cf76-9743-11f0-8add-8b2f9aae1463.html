The Bill Simmons Podcast

Are the Chargers for Real? Plus: the Tommy Boy Titans, a New York Swoon, Throwback Pats PTSD, and Guess the Lines With Cousin Sal.

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 3 concluded with unexpected outcomes and significant gambling losses reported by the hosts.
  • The discussion solidified top Super Bowl contenders, highlighting Buffalo and Baltimore with 5-to-1 odds.
  • Quarterback performances were closely analyzed, praising Justin Herbert's improvement while questioning Patrick Mahomes' current form.
  • Hosts provided early predictions and betting line analyses for key Week 4 NFL matchups.
  • The 'Parent Corner' segment featured humorous personal anecdotes, including Bill Simmons' mother investigating an old injury.

Deep Dive

  • Recording at 8:30 PM Pacific on Sunday, the hosts analyzed Week 3 NFL results, including a 'terrible' Chiefs-Giants game.
  • Justin Herbert showed significant improvement this season, with a key pre-halftime play shifting momentum, and his MVP odds adjusted to 6-to-1.
  • Hosts questioned Patrick Mahomes' performance after three weeks, considering the impact of Tyreek Hill's departure and a less experienced supporting cast.
  • Cousin Sal critiqued the Chiefs' offensive style, suggesting they are forced into situations like prime-time games against weaker opponents.
  • The Chiefs' overall standing and potential as a Super Bowl contender were debated following their Week 3 performance.

  • The Green Bay Packers' game against the Browns remained anticlimactic despite a betting line of 7 or 7.5 points.
  • Jordan Love threw a critical interception, and Browns running back Pierre Strong Jr. emerged unexpectedly during the game.
  • The Browns' defense was highlighted for its strong and competitive play, compensating for offensive struggles.
  • The Packers' depleted receiving corps limited their explosiveness, contributing to the difficulty of winning at the Browns' home field.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles staged a significant comeback against the Rams, overcoming a 26-7 deficit in Week 3.
  • The game concluded chaotically with a blocked field goal returned for a touchdown, directly impacting betting outcomes.
  • The improbability of the specific player executing the blocked kick and return was noted by the hosts.
  • Rams' wide receiver Puka Nacua's performance was highlighted, drawing comparisons to top receivers, with his Offensive Player of the Year odds at +750.

  • The Jacksonville Jaguars secured a 17-10 victory over the Texans, showcasing their defensive capabilities against Houston's struggling offense.
  • The Texans' offensive line and lack of running backs were highlighted as team struggles, despite draft picks on receivers, with CJ Stroud's performance questioned.
  • The Tennessee Titans faced fan frustration with coach Callahan due citing numerous penalties and a critical mismanaged end-of-half field goal attempt resulting in a delay of game penalty.
  • The Colts began the season undefeated, with a statistic comparing their lack of punts to the 2007 New England Patriots, and their division odds at -135.

  • The San Francisco 49ers faced an unexpected 1-point loss to the Arizona Cardinals, despite being favored by 1.5 points.
  • Mac Jones's performance included a critical interception and a safety, yet he played through a previous injury.
  • The 49ers' defensive effectiveness was impacted by the loss of Bosa, while the Cardinals' defense rose to the occasion, particularly in the red zone.
  • Christian McCaffrey maintained consistent performance, noted for his effective dump-off passes, and the 49ers' defense exceeded expectations.

  • The Cowboys' defense was questioned, with observations that it might be among the worst in decades after allowing 7 yards per play in a recent game.
  • Betting lines were discussed for the Cowboys and Dolphins, including projections for their regular season win totals.
  • Tua Tagovailoa's play was critiqued, noting his low yardage per completion and 'hospital balls,' contrasting with announcers' perceived command of the game.
  • Week 3 saw significant blowouts, specifically Washington over the Raiders and the Vikings against the Bengals, marked by Bengals' turnovers.

  • The hosts began guessing NFL Week 4 betting lines, starting with the Steelers vs. Vikings matchup, which they noted was a pick'em.
  • The Ravens vs. Chiefs game was highlighted as a marquee matchup, despite potential viewer fatigue, with the betting line debated between Ravens favored by 2.5 and 3 points.
  • For the Eagles at Buccaneers game, the Eagles were favored by 2.5 points, with discussions including Mike Evans' recurring hamstring injuries and the Buccaneers' undefeated record with three game-winning drives.
  • The Falcons vs. Commanders game was discussed, noting uncertainty about Jaden Daniels' availability and a Washington -3 line.

  • The 'let's be careful out there' game of the week was identified as Chargers at Giants, with predictions of Chargers favored by 5 to 6.5 points.
  • For the Broncos-Jets game, the hosts questioned if it was time to switch to backup quarterback Will Lock, noting his athleticism compared to Russell Wilson.
  • The Texans versus Titans game was deemed undesirable for betting, with Vegas setting the line at Texans by 7.5 points, sparking skepticism.
  • The betting line for the Raiders vs. Bears game had the Raiders favored by 1.5 points.

  • The Patriots vs. Panthers game saw the Patriots favored by 3, later shifting to 4.5 points, as hosts recapped their weekly betting predictions.
  • Discussion confirmed two Monday night games were scheduled for Week 4, leading to commentary on broadcasting duration.
  • For the Packers vs. Cowboys game, the betting line was 5.5, with speculation on the upcoming narrative surrounding Jordan Love and the Dallas defense.
  • The Dolphins vs. Jets Monday night game was predicted to have the Dolphins favored by 2.5 points, noting its later East Coast start time.

  • In 'Parent Corner,' the guest recounted his son Jack's interaction with Jimmy Kimmel after a 'tumultuous week.'
  • Bill Simmons' mother texted him about a bruise on his eye, referencing an old incident where his wife Carrie accidentally hit him with a shoe.
  • The discussion covered mothers' evolving use of technology, from incoherent texts to direct comments, and the receipt of AI-generated content like a fabricated video of Bill Belichick and Jordan.
  • Simmons' mother continued to investigate the eye incident, prompting Simmons to deny a thrown shoe and state he was fine.
