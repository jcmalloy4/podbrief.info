The Bill Simmons Podcast

Round 1 Winners (Da Bears!) and Losers (the Eagles!), Plus Guess the Lines With Cousin Sal

Key Takeaways

  • The NFL wild-card round featured major upsets and surprising team performances.
  • The Green Bay Packers' long history of playoff collapses was extensively analyzed.
  • Offensive struggles defined the Philadelphia Eagles' unexpected playoff exit.
  • Bill Simmons identified three NFL teams as "fraudulent" heading into the playoffs.
  • The hosts predicted lines for upcoming divisional round games in a traditional segment.
  • "Parent Corner" returned with humorous family anecdotes and personal stories.

Deep Dive

  • The Chicago Bears achieved an improbable wild-card victory over the Green Bay Packers, scoring 25 fourth-quarter points.
  • Cousin Sal identified the Bears' resilience and historical rivalry with the Packers as the most compelling storyline.
  • The hosts debated whether the Bears' win or the Packers' late-game collapse was more significant, with the guest emphasizing Green Bay's devastating defeat.

  • Since their 1996 Super Bowl win, the Green Bay Packers have experienced numerous playoff losses, including five NFC title game defeats.
  • The team has also suffered five overtime losses in playoff contests, contributing to a pattern of January collapses.
  • Hosts questioned Coach Matt LaFleur's future and contract extension given the recent playoff results and recurring struggles.

  • The Philadelphia Eagles' offense exhibited lethargic play, lacking pace and utilizing minimal trick plays against the 49ers.
  • High-priced receivers had limited impact, and the offense struggled on crucial fourth-down attempts.
  • The 49ers secured a win despite Brock Purdy's two interceptions and Christian McCaffrey's limited rushing yards, highlighting Eagles' shortcomings.
  • Tom Brady critiqued Jalen Hurts' decision-making during the game, questioning offensive strategy.

  • The Panthers vs. Seahawks game was noted for the largest road favorite line ever.
  • The hosts discussed watching the Panthers vs. Rams game at a Shaky's restaurant, a long-standing tradition with friends.
  • Memorable past playoff games viewed at Shaky's included Tebow's playoff moment and Marshawn Lynch's 'Beast Mode' run.
  • The viewing experience was distracted by friends discussing the American Revolution and complaints about TV channel selection.

  • The hosts ranked the greatest NFL running back runs, with Marshawn Lynch's 'Beast Quake' run being a strong contender.
  • Other notable runs included Eric Dickerson's 99-yarder, Marcus Allen's Super Bowl run, and Franco Harris's playoff run.
  • Bo Jackson's runs against Brian Bosworth and against the Raiders were also highlighted as significant.

  • Drake May, despite effective scrambling, was critiqued for missing a touchdown throw to Hooper and displaying less accuracy than usual.
  • The Patriots had protected May's scrambling throughout the season to prevent hits, but he played more aggressively in this playoff game.
  • The hosts discussed how playoff games often show quarterbacks, like Patrick Mahomes, adapting their rushing strategies.

  • The hosts reviewed the performance of rookie quarterbacks in their first playoff games.
  • Historical examples like Peyton Manning and Tom Brady were cited to contextualize current rookie performances.
  • Key plays from the Patriots' win, including a fumble recovery, were also discussed as part of the review.

  • The hosts shifted to previewing the upcoming Steelers vs. Texans game, with the line settled at three points.
  • They analyzed the potential for the Steelers' offense to struggle with blocking against the Texans.
  • Bill Simmons identified three teams he considered fraudulent in the playoffs: the Panthers, Bears, and Steelers, noting two played tough.

  • The discussion shifted to the emergence of a new generation of quarterbacks, including Caleb Williams and Drake May.
  • Their impressive plays and potential for future success were highlighted by the hosts.
  • Potential playoff paths for teams like the Rams and Bears were also considered in relation to evolving talent.

  • The host recounted tricking his wife into a surprise party by fabricating a "Jury Duty" season two premiere with a mafia theme.
  • The elaborate ruse involved his mother as a decoy and specific costume requirements for the attendees.
  • Cousin Sal shared a story about his son Harrison winning a championship game and a retracted joke about a private jet.
  • One son, now 18, requested privacy and to be excluded from future "Parent Corner" stories.
