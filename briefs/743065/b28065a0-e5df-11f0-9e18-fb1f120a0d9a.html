The Bill Simmons Podcast

NBA Panic Teams, Best QBs Ever, and Sorry Seattle With Rob Mahoney, Chris Russo, and Danny Kelly

Key Takeaways

  • Warriors face dimming playoff hopes; trade scenarios, including Paul George, are under consideration.
  • The New York Knicks' 2-9 slump highlights significant synergy issues between Jalen Brunson and Karl-Anthony Towns.
  • Orlando Magic navigates roster decisions for promising young core, debating upgrades and Paolo Banchero's development.
  • Lakers' defensive woes persist despite winning record, prompting discussions on roster construction and trade targets.
  • Atlanta Hawks' inconsistency leads to talks of a potential summer reboot, with Anthony Davis trade viability debated.
  • Oklahoma City Thunder explore significant trades to consolidate assets despite being a top Western Conference team.
  • NFL quarterback legends are debated, with Super Bowl wins and clutch play defining Joe Montana, Tom Brady, and Patrick Mahomes.
  • Josh Allen's playoff struggles and the Bills' history of close losses are analyzed, drawing comparisons to Peyton Manning.
  • The podcast scrutinizes high-pressure performances of elite athletes like James Harden, Karl Malone, and Wilt Chamberlain.

Deep Dive

  • Discussion on Paul George's trade value and recent strong play over 17 games, despite a torn ACL.
  • Concerns about the Golden State Warriors' struggles and potential end to their season following Jimmy Butler's injury.
  • Exploration of trade targets like De'Anthony Melton or bench players to complement Stephen Curry and Draymond Green.
  • Consideration of high-risk, high-reward options like Zion Williamson or Trey Murphy III, but Stephen Curry is unlikely to be traded.

  • The New York Knicks hold a 2-9 record in their last 11 games, with losses to teams like Phoenix, Spurs, Atlanta, and Dallas.
  • Guests suggest core players Jalen Brunson and Karl-Anthony Towns lack synergy, leading to two years of suboptimal play.
  • Opponents are reportedly targeting Towns to disrupt his play, exemplified by Draymond Green's actions.
  • Discussions included the viability of hypothetical trades involving Anthony Davis or LeBron James for Karl-Anthony Towns.

  • The Orlando Magic show promise with a 23-19 record and a key five-man lineup having a plus-18 rating per 100 possessions.
  • A need for a backup guard is identified, with players like Anthony Simons and Malik Monk discussed as trade targets.
  • One speaker advocates retaining Anthony Simons due to improved shooting and first-step ability, citing potential negative team morale if traded.
  • Critiques are raised regarding Paolo Banchero's late-game performance compared to his prior season, despite his popularity among fans.

  • The Los Angeles Lakers hold a 25-16 record but rank 29th defensively and 25th in net rating at -4.7.
  • The team struggles with low rankings in rebounds, blocks, and made three-pointers.
  • Expiring contracts for players like Rui, Vincent, and Kleber are noted as potential trade assets.
  • The Lakers need dynamic athletes, such as Tari Eason or De'Anthony Melton, to improve their transition defense.

  • The Atlanta Hawks exhibit an 11-9 record over their last 20 games, with questions surrounding their identity and defensive struggles.
  • The current season is viewed as a failed experiment following prior acquisitions, prompting a potential summer reboot focused on Jalen Johnson.
  • Discussions include the potential acquisition of Anthony Davis, but concerns exist about his long-term health and age.
  • One guest compares the risk of investing in Davis to Blake Griffin's injury-plagued career.

  • The Oklahoma City Thunder, currently the top team in the Western Conference, are debating whether to remain static or make a trade.
  • One analyst suggested trading players like Dort, Caruso, or Wallace for assets like Trey Murphy, potentially involving multiple first-round picks.
  • The Pelicans are discussed as potentially trading Trey Murphy if an overwhelming offer of picks is received from a team like OKC.
  • The guest argues that a non-All-NBA talent could be traded for multiple first-round picks to consolidate draft capital or acquire supporting players.

  • The debate features historical benchmarks like Otto Graham and Johnny Unitas, focusing on post-1970 merger quarterbacks.
  • Tom Brady, Joe Montana, and Patrick Mahomes are identified as top contenders, with Peyton Manning, John Elway, and Dan Marino also considered.
  • Super Bowl championships are a key ranking criterion, with discussions noting Terry Bradshaw's four titles and Aaron Rodgers' single appearance.
  • Brett Favre and Steve Young were debated but faced scrutiny for turnovers, while Dan Fouts was excluded from the top 10 due to a 3-4 playoff record.

  • Analysis of Josh Allen's versatile playstyle and his role in the Buffalo Bills' recent playoff exit is discussed.
  • The Bills' franchise history of close losses and Super Bowl defeats, including Norwood's missed field goal, is highlighted.
  • The sustained playoff contention due to Allen's play is noted, with a projection for future success despite recent losses.
  • A comparison is drawn between Allen's career trajectory and Peyton Manning's, suggesting eventual championships can alter historical perception.

  • The discussion identifies athletes who possessed immense talent but faltered in crucial moments, citing James Harden's Game 7 performances.
  • Karl Malone's career is analyzed, contrasting his statistical achievements with high-pressure game performance compared to Tim Duncan.
  • Wilt Chamberlain's championships and dominant statistics are acknowledged, but his passivity in a pivotal Game 7 against the Celtics is scrutinized.
  • Clayton Kershaw's postseason performance is questioned, noting a 4.45 ERA and critical moments in past games.

  • The Knicks' current performance is criticized, along with past management decisions such as the firing of coach Thibodeau.
  • The trade for Mikal Bridges is cited as a move that has limited the team's potential.
  • Speculation arises about potential trade targets like Giannis Antetokounmpo, alongside ongoing criticism of the Mikal Bridges acquisition for multiple first-round picks.
  • The Knicks are projected as a consistent 50-win team in the coming years but may struggle to achieve championship contention.

  • Initial concerns for the Seahawks' Super Bowl run centered on quarterback Sam Darnold and running back Charbonnet's torn ACL.
  • The team's strong defense is acknowledged as an advantage, but offensive inconsistency and lack of spark are key worries.
  • Concerns are raised about the Rams' defense containing JSN and the potential of McVay and Stafford to exploit the Seahawks' defense, as Stafford previously threw for 500 yards.
  • The Seahawks are expected to adopt a physical strategy, relying on close games rather than deep passes, with Seattle weather noted as a factor.

  • A preview of the Patriots-Broncos game sets the line at 5.5 points, with Jared Stidham starting for Denver.
  • The Patriots' playoff path is defended, citing wins against top DVOA teams like the Seahawks and Rams.
  • The Broncos' defense is identified as a major threat to the Patriots, with turnovers and quarterback pressure noted as critical variables.
  • Quarterback prospect Mendoza is strongly supported as a number one pick due to his accuracy, anticipation, and athleticism in college football playoffs.
