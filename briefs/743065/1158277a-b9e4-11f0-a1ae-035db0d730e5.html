The Bill Simmons Podcast

OKC’s Better, Jokic Isn’t Real, a Clippers Swoon, and Detroit’s Rise With Tim Legler, Plus Week 11 Picks With Joe House and Billy Gil

Key Takeaways

  • The Oklahoma City Thunder demonstrates elite defense and deep roster talent, securing wins without key players.
  • Nikola Jokic is performing at a career-best level, showing exceptional offensive efficiency and confidence in his team.
  • The Los Angeles Clippers are facing significant challenges, positioning them as the league's most "desperate" team for trades.
  • Preseason NBA predictions were often incorrect, with the Miami Heat and Detroit Pistons exceeding initial expectations.
  • The criteria for the NBA Most Improved Player award are debated, suggesting an alternative "Where Did You Come From" award.
  • NFL discussions focused on improving injury report clarity, analyzing uniform trend correlations, and Week 11 betting strategies.
  • The Ringer 107 NFL Week 11 picks included Vikings -2.5, Browns +7.5, Seahawks +3, Jaguars +3, and Chiefs -2.5 (combo).

Deep Dive

  • A.J. Mitchell has provided unexpected contributions in Jalen Williams's absence, showcasing offensive and defensive capabilities.
  • Mitchell's play, including his shooting and ability to absorb contact, contributes to OKC's roster depth.
  • The team's success in defeating the Lakers was achieved without key players Jalen Williams and Aaron Wiggins.

  • Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's consistent high-level play and efficient scoring draw comparisons to elite guards like Michael Jordan and Kobe Bryant.
  • He is considered among the league's best guards of the past decade due to his impact and statistical achievements.
  • SGA recently delivered a 30-point, 10-assist performance through three quarters, demonstrating his current form.

  • Nikola Jokic demonstrated a "playoff mode" performance against the Clippers, scoring 25 points in the first quarter and 52 after three quarters.
  • His effectiveness is attributed to a stronger supporting cast, allowing him more decisive play and rest between games.
  • Jokic's shooting percentages are exceptionally high: 92% from 0-3 feet and 73% from 10-16 feet.

  • The Los Angeles Clippers are facing struggles, including potential player availability issues for James Harden and a challenging upcoming road schedule.
  • The team is identified as the league's most desperate due to a lack of draft picks, hindering easy rebuilding options.
  • Their offense has been poor, and their defense has declined significantly from the previous season, necessitating a trade for talent.

  • The definition and validity of the Most Improved Player award are debated, questioning if a player meeting rookie expectations qualifies.
  • A suggestion is made for a "Where the Fuck Did You Come From" award, with A.J. Mitchell cited as a prime candidate.
  • Davion Mitchell's situation is also discussed, questioning if his improved play stems from increased opportunity rather than inherent skill growth.

  • The Oklahoma City Thunder hold a surprising 10-2 record and a high standing in the Western Conference.
  • Houston and Cleveland are highlighted as surprising teams, with Houston adding a player to improve decision-making.
  • Reed Shepard's emergence, marked by improved three-point shooting and growing court confidence, is detailed.

  • Critiques of the NFL's injury report system call for more detailed information beyond vague designations like 'questionable'.
  • Suggestions for improved clarity include numerical scales (1-5 or 1-10) or AI-processed real-time updates to mitigate betting confusion.
  • A trend indicates that teams wearing alternate or throwback uniforms have a poor success rate this season, notably the Steelers and Cardinals.

  • The group agreed on several Week 11 NFL picks: Jaguars plus three, Browns plus seven and a half, and Minnesota minus two and a half.
  • Considerations included the Eagles minus one and a half against Detroit and the Seahawks plus three against the Rams.
  • Analysis for Chargers vs. Jaguars focused on the Chargers' offensive line issues, while Rams' secondary vulnerability was noted for Seahawks pick.

  • Skepticism is expressed regarding Sean Payton's 'genius' status and game management, despite the Broncos' overall success.
  • Concerns are noted about coaching decisions, particularly concerning Bo Nix's limitations as a quarterback.
  • Betting strategies discussed included the Chiefs moneyline and potential bets involving the Green Bay Packers and Houston Texans.
