Eagles Spit Takes, Week 1 NFL Picks, and a Possible Clippers Scandal With Joe House

Key Takeaways

  • The Eagles-Cowboys game featured critical turnovers, a strong Cowboys defensive performance, and a controversial Jalen Carter ejection.
  • The NFL's new taunting rules were criticized for inconsistent enforcement and their potential to unfairly influence game outcomes.
  • Bill Simmons and Joe House detailed their Ringer 107 NFL betting picks, discussing strategies, specific game analyses, and 'stayaway' calls.
  • Allegations of improper payments to Kawhi Leonard, involving Steve Ballmer's company, are expected to trigger an NBA investigation.

Deep Dive

  • The Eagles-Cowboys 'TNF' game experienced a lightning delay and featured a critical fumble by Miles Sanders.
  • The Cowboys' defense, particularly Micah Parsons, was noted for its impact, while the Eagles' offense drew criticism.
  • The hosts described the Week 1 matchup as even but uninformative, with broadcasting errors also discussed.

  • The NFL's new taunting rules faced criticism for inconsistent enforcement during the Eagles-Cowboys game.
  • Nolan Smith received a penalty for celebration after a tackle, contrasting with a later unpenalized choreographed celebration.
  • Hosts expressed concern that the subjective rule, dependent on referee judgment, could significantly impact game outcomes and predictability.

  • The hosts detailed the Ringer 107 contest format, involving picking five NFL games weekly with two agreed selections.
  • Guest Joe House expressed nervousness about a line move on the Giants from plus 7.5 to under 6 points.
  • Season win targets for the betting contest were debated, with one host aiming for 60 wins and another considering 16 wins acceptable.

  • The Raiders were favored due to Christian Gonzalez's injury affecting the Patriots' defense, with a lean toward the Raiders.
  • High hopes were expressed for the Raiders' quarterback, citing strong performance despite a weak supporting cast last season.
  • One host favored the Cardinals -6.5 against the Saints, despite historical trends with Kyler Murray and concerns about the Saints' offensive line injuries.

  • Joe House selected the Kansas City Chiefs minus 3 points against the Los Angeles Chargers for his Ringer 107 pick.
  • Reasoning cited Chiefs' historical dominance over the Chargers, particularly Patrick Mahomes and Andy Reid's Week 1 success.
  • Concerns included the Chargers' significant offensive line issues, including the loss of their left tackle.

  • A controversial Ringer 107 pick involved the Seahawks +2.5 against the 49ers.
  • One host expressed strong belief in the Seahawks' defense and offensive potential, citing Mike McDonald and favorable player matchups.
  • The other host vehemently disagreed, highlighting concerns about the Seahawks' offensive line and Geno Smith's performance under pressure.

  • Both hosts favored the Baltimore Ravens against the Buffalo Bills, despite Buffalo being a Super Bowl contender.
  • A 'revenge factor' and concerns about the Bills' secondary injuries, including starting cornerbacks Benford and White, were cited.
  • The hosts also discussed betting on Lamar Jackson's consistent performance in regular season games.

  • The hosts explored parlay options involving the Jaguars, Broncos, and Falcons, analyzing offensive potential.
  • Confidence was expressed in the Jacksonville Jaguars against the Carolina Panthers, citing Bryce Young's situation and Panthers' receiver injuries.
  • Final Ringer 107 picks included the Chiefs, Falcons, Bears, Ravens, Jaguars, and Broncos, with justifications based on matchups.

  • A journalistic report alleged improper payments to Kawhi Leonard involving Steve Ballmer's company, Aspiration.
  • The NBA is expected to investigate these claims, which highlight potential circumvention of league rules regarding financial dealings and player benefits.
  • The discussion debated Steve Ballmer's potential awareness of the significant investment and the likelihood of league punishment.
