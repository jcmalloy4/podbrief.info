The Bill Simmons Podcast

Niners Magic, Schlitt Happens, a Red Sox Postmortem, the Seahawks Ceiling, and Week 5 NFL Picks With Kevin Hench and Joe House

Key Takeaways

  • The Red Sox season concluded with critical defensive errors and widespread player injuries.
  • Historical Red Sox moments, especially those from October 2nd, were contrasted with the team's current performance.
  • NFL Week 5 picks and early NBA MVP odds were analyzed, including specific team and player betting lines.
  • NFL 'stayaway' picks were identified due to game uncertainties and team performance concerns.
  • NBA over-under win totals were debated for several teams, alongside player contract and trade speculation.

Deep Dive

  • The Red Sox's season ended due to a four-run inning and critical defensive lapses in a series loss.
  • Key plays included a missed pop fly that allowed a run and a failed bunt attempt, preventing a game-winning scenario.
  • Player Jaren Duran's performance and significant injuries to Roman Anthony and Tristan Casas were scrutinized.
  • The team's pitching depth was notably challenged, operating with its 11th starter during the season.

  • October 2nd holds historical significance for Red Sox fans, referencing the 1978 Bucky Dent game as a source of past distress.
  • The 2004 Red Sox season is recalled as a "balm" for previous losses, contrasting their crucial plays with current shortcomings.
  • Strategic decisions from the 2004 team, like Pedro Martinez's Game 7 appearance, were revisited.
  • The host noted October 2nd still evokes negative memories despite the 2004 success.

  • The current Red Sox roster's struggles, attributed to injuries and poor roster moves, were compared to less talented 1990s teams.
  • Potential future core players like Tristan Casas and Marcel Le Maire were evaluated based on health and durability.
  • Defensive miscues, including outfield issues and Nathaniel Lowe's performance, were highlighted as team weaknesses.
  • Pitching depth issues with players like Quinn Priester and rookie Conley Early were discussed.

  • Roman Anthony had an exceptional rookie season with the Red Sox, drawing comparisons to Nomar Garciaparra's 1997 season.
  • He demonstrated resilience and clutch hitting, including game-winning hits against high-velocity pitcher Johan Duran.
  • A season highlight involved Anthony hitting a home run off Paul Skeens during Peyton Tolley's dad's live interview.
  • His strong performance preceded a subsequent collapse and an injury impacting the team.

  • The conversation shifted to NFL Week 5 picks and early NBA bets, including MVP odds.
  • Josh Allen was listed at -105, Justin Herbert at 6-1, and Patrick Mahomes at 650 for MVP.
  • AFC North odds placed Pittsburgh at +185, with Lamar Jackson's availability impacting the Ravens.
  • Houston had +650 odds in the AFC South, and the Indianapolis Colts were at even money.

  • The hosts reviewed their Ringer 107 picks, noting a 3-2 record before designating 'stayaway' games for Week 5.
  • The Browns-Vikings game was identified as a 'stayaway' pick due to uncertainty.
  • The Chargers-Washington game, with the Chargers favored at -2.5, was also deemed a 'stayaway' due to Jalen Daniels' injury and receiver issues.
  • Concerns about the Chargers' offensive line, potentially without both tackles, led to reluctance in betting on their offense.

  • The Saints (+1.5) versus Giants game was discussed, highlighting the Giants' historically poor run defense as a key factor.
  • In the Lions versus Bengals game, the spread was considered too high given Detroit's cornerback issues.
  • The Colts (-6.5) versus Raiders game also drew attention, with doubt expressed about the Raiders' overall capability.
  • Specific prop bets for the Saints and Jackson Dart's performance were mentioned.

  • The Patriots, listed as 8.5-point underdogs against the Bills, were discussed as a team capable of keeping the game close.
  • A potential for a backdoor cover was noted for the Patriots, with a narrative suggesting the Bills may not be as dominant as their record indicates.
  • Betting strategies included a parlay involving the Patriots and an over/under total, as well as a bet on the Chiefs minus three against the Jaguars.
  • Analysis focused on quarterback play, comparing mobility to Patrick Mahomes and discussing betting strategies.

  • The Ringer 107 segment, sponsored by FanDuel, detailed official NFL Week 5 picks, with the team's current record at 8-12.
  • Selections included Seahawks minus three against the Buccaneers, Texans minus 1.5 against Baltimore, and Chiefs minus three against the Jaguars.
  • Patriots plus 8.5 against the Bills was also chosen, with the hosts believing the Patriots could keep the game close and betting the over.
  • Joe House's picks included a moneyline parlay of the Colts and Eagles, acknowledging it as a 'square' bet.

  • NBA over-under win totals were debated, with skepticism regarding the Atlanta Hawks' line of 47.5 wins.
  • Victor Wembanyama's MVP odds at 12-to-1 were mentioned, with one host stating they would bet on him annually.
  • The Washington Wizards' over-under of 21.5 wins was discussed, with a tentative lean towards the over.
  • The Golden State Warriors' 45.5 win total also faced skepticism, considering Klay Thompson's contract and Draymond Green's extension.
