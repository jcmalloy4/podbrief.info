The Bill Simmons Podcast

The Juggernaut Packers, Realistic Clippers Punishments, and Week 2 NFL Picks With Joe House, Jason Concepcion, and Chris Ryan

Key Takeaways

  • Green Bay's defense and Jordan Love's improved play led to a decisive TNF win over the Washington Commanders.
  • The Clippers' alleged scandal raises concerns about NBA transparency and the role of player representatives.
  • NBA investigations face skepticism for their prolonged nature, with comparisons drawn to past controversies.
  • Experts debated potential severe penalties for the Clippers, including owner bans and draft pick fines, rather than just monetary fines.
  • NFL Week 2 betting analysis covered 'stayaway' games, 'possible' bets, and detailed breakdowns of key matchups.
  • Final NFL Week 2 picks included underdogs and parlays, with a focus on perceived value despite Week 1 outcomes.

Deep Dive

  • The Green Bay Packers defeated the Washington Commanders in a TNF matchup.
  • Host Bill Simmons noted his bets on the Packers; guest Joe House attributed the Commanders' loss to a difficult schedule and early-season injuries.
  • Green Bay's defense, particularly cornerback Jaire Alexander, showcased superior speed and effectiveness.
  • Washington struggled with offensive execution, including an inability to run the ball and convert on short-yardage situations.
  • Jordan Love's performance and the emergence of Tucker Kraft as an offensive weapon for the Packers were highlighted.
  • start Rowland: 03:30.
  • teaser: Simmons and House react to Packers' victory over Commanders, analyzing team performances.

  • Chris Ryan and Jason Concepcion discussed the alleged "smoke" surrounding the Clippers' scandal involving Steve Ballmer.
  • Questions were raised about the lack of transparency and public statements from Kawhi Leonard and his agent, Uncle Dennis.
  • Guests expressed surprise at the NBA's lack of official comment or action, contrasting it with how similar situations involving other star players might be handled.
  • One guest speculated Kawhi Leonard is content with financial obligations and benefits from maintaining a low profile.
  • start Rowland: 20:00.
  • teaser: Analysts question the lack of transparency surrounding the Clippers' alleged scandal.

  • Participants expressed skepticism regarding the NBA's investigation timeline and its approach to scandals, suggesting prolonged legalistic responses are ineffective.
  • The unfolding situation was likened to a conspiracy in a movie like 'JFK,' questioning whether high-level officials or individuals lower in the organizational structure, like minority owner Dennis Wong, orchestrated the actions.
  • The Clippers' recent scandal was compared to past controversies involving Donald Sterling and Steve Ballmer, noting the team's history of struggles to establish a strong identity in Los Angeles.
  • start Rowland: 30:30.
  • teaser: Skepticism mounts over the NBA's investigation tactics regarding the Clippers' history of controversy.

  • A guest proposed a theory that Kawhi Leonard may have been unaware of alleged illegal activities conducted by his uncle, suggesting Dennis freelanced the scheme.
  • This perspective posits Leonard's desire to avoid significant work and his team's efforts to shield him from such responsibilities.
  • The discussion touched upon the potential for athletes at Leonard's level to delegate financial opportunities to their representatives.
  • Guests analyzed the reporting on the Clippers' potential involvement in a money laundering scheme, debating who the key figures might be and whether Steve Ballmer or Kawhi Leonard were aware of the alleged illegal activities.
  • start Rowland: 37:00.
  • teaser: Debate arises on Kawhi Leonard's awareness and involvement in his uncle's alleged illegal activities.

  • Discussion focused on potential punishments for the Clippers' scandal, noting the ineffectiveness of fines for billionaires.
  • The possibility of forcing an owner to sell was raised, referencing the Donald Sterling case, along with the competitive landscape of the NBA.
  • Speculation included owner Steve Ballmer potentially facing a ban from the team and a significant fine of first-round picks.
  • Historical precedents like George Steinbrenner's ban and Adam Silver's handling of similar situations were discussed.
  • Guests concluded that the scandal does not provide a substantial competitive advantage, despite Ballmer's wealth.
  • start Rowland: 46:30.
  • teaser: Speculation on severe penalties for the Clippers' scandal, including owner bans and draft pick fines.

  • The 'Ringer 107 picks' segment for NFL Week 2 commenced with a recap of the previous week's 1-4 record.
  • Hosts acknowledged being on the right side of most games but regretted specific losses, including a Chargers bet and a Falcons upset over the Bucs.
  • A close Bears game that "slipped away late" was also discussed during the recap.
  • The discussion shifted to "stayaway" bets for Week 2, starting with the Jaguars at Bengals matchup due to concerns about both teams' offensive and defensive lines.
  • start Rowland: 58:00.
  • teaser: Ringer 107 Week 2 NFL picks begin with a 1-4 recap and "stayaway" selections.

  • "Possible" bets included the Titans-Rams game, with analysts arguing the Titans, despite losing their right tackle and having offensive line issues, could cover the 5.5-point spread.
  • Critiques of a coach's decision-making, specifically a pass play near the goal line and rules regarding player possession, were noted when considering Tennessee bets.
  • Betting values for the AFC South were discussed, with Tennessee at +950 identified as a potential long-shot value pick despite coaching concerns.
  • The Jets' solid offense and Justin Fields' strong play against the Bills were highlighted, though concerns about the Jets' defense struggling against Aaron Rodgers were raised.
  • start Rowland: 1:06:00.
  • teaser: In-depth analysis of "possible" NFL Week 2 bets, including Titans and AFC South long shots.

  • The Eagles versus Chiefs matchup saw the Chiefs as surprisingly underdogs at home.
  • Historical data showing Patrick Mahomes' strong record as a home underdog and his streak of not losing three consecutive games was noted.
  • The Eagles' offensive performance against Dallas in Week 1 was questioned, with speculation they may have been holding back.
  • Analysts debated the game, with one expressing strong conviction in the Eagles due to their perceived dominance in the trenches and the Chiefs' vulnerable secondary.
  • Both analysts revealed a strong lean towards the Eagles for Week 2, despite acknowledging the difficulty in definitively betting on the game.
  • start Rowland: 1:16:30.
  • teaser: Chiefs are home underdogs against the Eagles; analysts debate Mahomes' record and team performance.

  • One host selected the Atlanta Falcons, citing a belief that the team is better than their previous performance indicated and that the number is wrong.
  • Other proposed picks included the New England Patriots and Denver Broncos.
  • A parlay pick focused on the Jets plus 6.5 points against the Buffalo Bills, citing concerns about the Bills' depleted defense.
  • The parlay was finalized to include the Arizona Cardinals moneyline and the Philadelphia Eagles plus 4.5 points against the Kansas City Chiefs.
  • The Broncos at -2.5 against the Colts were also picked, with confidence in Denver's defense and Sean Payton's potential adjustments.
  • start Rowland: 1:27:30.
  • teaser: Hosts finalize their NFL Week 2 picks, including a parlay with Cardinals and Eagles.
