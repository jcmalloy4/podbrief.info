The Bill Simmons Podcast

The Rejuvenated Cowboys, Most Desperate NFL Teams, Lane Kiffin’s LSU Move, and Guess the Lines With Cousin Sal, Todd McShay, and Van Lathan

Key Takeaways

  • NFL Week 13 analysis covered playoff implications, inconsistent team performances, and debated coaching decisions across several matchups.
  • The podcast explored the most 'depressed' NFL fan bases, citing team performance, playoff odds, and future outlooks for teams like the Steelers and Lions.
  • Hosts reviewed their season-long NFL future bets, discussing Week 13 upsets and the unpredictability of certain divisions like the AFC South.
  • The 'Parent Corner' segment provided personal anecdotes on familial football loyalties, including a son's surprising allegiance to the Dallas Cowboys.
  • A significant portion focused on college football, analyzing Lane Kiffin's move to LSU and its implications for the transfer portal, NIL deals, and coaching fame.
  • The discussion highlighted the growing financial demands in college football and the potential need for government intervention to regulate the sport.

Deep Dive

  • The Broncos' overtime win against the Commanders initiated the Week 13 NFL recap, impacting playoff discussions.
  • Scenarios like the Bears' playoff seeding, AFC South competitiveness, Chiefs' wildcard chances, and Cowboys' NFC East potential were considered.
  • Week 14 was identified as a significant weekend for the NFL, with major playoff implications, especially for teams like the Chiefs.
  • The host questioned the Dallas Cowboys' legitimacy as a playoff contender after recent wins, including one against the Eagles.
  • The Philadelphia Eagles' inconsistent offense and defense, despite strong receivers, were scrutinized, alongside Jalen Hurts's effectiveness.

  • Josh Allen's perceived decline in physical dominance this season led to questions about his status as the league's best quarterback.
  • Patrick Mahomes's record was acknowledged, while cautioning about the Buffalo Bills' late-season surges in previous years.
  • The Pittsburgh Steelers' offensive line issues and a historic number of rushing yards allowed were highlighted.
  • Quarterback Kenny Pickett's perceived decline was noted, with an assessment that the Steelers' season is 'done' due to offensive struggles.

  • Discussion centered on the Steelers, Ravens, Lions, and Chiefs as having the most depressed fan bases.
  • The Steelers' lack of a clear future and the Ravens' missed window with Lamar Jackson were cited as contributing factors.
  • The Detroit Lions' recent struggles after a promising period and the Kansas City Chiefs' uncharacteristic losing streak were also mentioned.
  • NFL playoff odds for the Ravens, Chiefs, Lions, and Steelers were updated, reflecting recent team performances.

  • Hosts reported season-long NFL future bets records of 7-5 and 6-6, mentioning specific picks like New England over and Minnesota under.
  • Week 13 successful picks included the Patriots and Seattle, with the AFC South division identified as particularly difficult to predict.
  • The Carolina Panthers were highlighted for their 10.5-point underdog win against the Rams, despite an inconsistent season.
  • The Houston Texans' 7-5 record and wins against strong opponents were contrasted with the Kansas City Chiefs' struggles.

  • Exceptional plays from Week 13 included a 'pick six' described as the play of the year and remarkable catches by players like Bowers and Aiyuk.
  • The host secured a crucial fantasy football win, attributing it to 48 points from the Seahawks defense and kicker Myers.
  • Debate occurred regarding the scoring system for shutouts in fantasy football, with one suggesting 20 points.
  • A unique statistic was mentioned: a team having two quarterbacks throw a pick-six in their first start in the same year.

  • Bill Simmons initially set the Packers-Bears line at Packers minus 5.5, while Cousin Sal picked the Jaguars minus 1 against the Colts.
  • Concerns were raised about Trevor Lawrence's performance and the Jaguars' offensive line issues, leading to advice against betting on that game.
  • The Ravens-Steelers game line moved from Ravens minus 4.5 to 6.5, with speculation on Lamar Jackson's potential performance after a 10-day break.
  • The Bills-Bengals game had odds set at Bills minus 5.5, with consideration given to teasing the Bengals up to 11.5 points.

  • The Seahawks-Falcons game was favored by the host by four, against a line of 8.5, noting the Falcons' history of 'fishy lines.'
  • The Buccaneers were favored by 8.5 (line at 7.5) against the Saints, while the Rams were favored by 8.5 (line at 7.5) against the Cardinals.
  • The Browns-Titans game, though unappealing, was discussed for betting, with the Browns favored by 5.5 (line at 3.5).
  • Bo Nix's inconsistent performance in the Vikings-Commanders game was analyzed, noting a rally in overtime after earlier struggles.

  • The 'Parent Corner' segment began with the host discussing the enjoyment of the football weekend.
  • The host shared a story about his youngest son, Harrison, unexpectedly becoming a Dallas Cowboys fan, despite attempts to dissuade him.
  • An anecdote involved one of the host's sons cheering for an opposing team's touchdown due to fantasy football reasons.
  • A story about the host's dog, Murph, possibly consuming turkey parts and Italian cookies during Thanksgiving was recounted.

  • Todd McShay and Van Lathan joined to discuss the anticipated news of Lane Kiffin leaving Ole Miss to coach at LSU.
  • The situation was compared to Brian Kelly's 2021 move to LSU, noting similarities in coaching changes during potential playoff runs.
  • Van Lathan commented on LSU's history of such coaching changes and called the current state of college football 'abject insanity'.
  • Lathan had learned about Kiffin's potential move through contacts in Baton Rouge a week and a half prior, indicating the deal was finalized.

  • Factors influencing Kiffin's decision included LSU's stronger NIL war chest and more aggressive support compared to Ole Miss's historical performance.
  • LSU's program resources potentially include NIL deals reaching $20-25 million annually.
  • Louisiana is highlighted as a fertile recruiting ground for NFL talent, benefiting LSU as the state's dominant SEC school with a lack of other in-state SEC programs.
  • Kiffin's ability to develop players, as demonstrated with quarterback Trinidad Chambliss, and his schematic offensive capabilities were praised.

  • Discussion centered on Lane Kiffin's potential impact on college football fame, drawing parallels to LeBron James's career trajectory.
  • The current state of college football is perceived as lacking 'sex appeal' due to a focus on playoff implications and cultural clashes, overshadowing individual stories.
  • The transfer portal and NIL deals are noted for creating a 'system of mercenaries' among players and coaches.
  • Kiffin's departure from Ole Miss allegedly involved attempts to leverage the system by taking his staff and players, which ultimately did not succeed.

  • Challenges of evaluating NFL draft prospects were discussed in light of young players receiving sudden wealth, fame, and free time.
  • Participants agreed government intervention may be necessary for college football regulation due to the significant financial benefits for those in power.
  • The rise of 'sleazy' handlers and the dramatic increase in financial demands for players, from $50,000 in the past to millions for top prospects, were highlighted.
  • The influence of agent Jimmy Sexton in college football coaching hires was noted, with guests agreeing he is currently the most powerful figure.
