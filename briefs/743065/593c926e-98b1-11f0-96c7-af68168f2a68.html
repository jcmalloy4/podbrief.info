The Bill Simmons Podcast

A Rockets Gut Punch, Expectations for the Celtics and Pacers, and a Vegas Tourism Slump With Zach Lowe, Sean Fennessey, and Chris Ryan

Key Takeaways

  • Fred VanVleet's torn ACL significantly impacts the Houston Rockets' championship aspirations.
  • The Rockets possess valuable draft assets for future trade flexibility and player acquisition.
  • NBA championship odds show perceived undervaluation of teams like the Minnesota Timberwolves.
  • The Celtics and Pacers face uncertain season outlooks due to injuries and roster depth.
  • Las Vegas's identity has shifted from a gambling mecca to a broader, more upscale entertainment hub.

Deep Dive

  • Fred VanVleet's torn ACL significantly impacts the Houston Rockets' season, potentially derailing championship aspirations.
  • His absence removes a crucial traditional point guard, leaving a void in playmaking and potentially overtaxing Kevin Durant.
  • The Rockets may struggle for playoff seeding against Western Conference rivals like the Lakers and Timberwolves without VanVleet's low-mistake play.
  • Despite potential development for Amen Thompson and Reed Shepard, relying on inexperienced players in crucial roles raises concerns.

  • The Houston Rockets hold future first-round picks from Brooklyn, Phoenix, Dallas, and their own, offering significant trade flexibility.
  • The Brooklyn Nets-Houston Rockets trade resulted in Brooklyn regaining its 2025 and 2026 first-round picks.
  • In return, Brooklyn surrendered Phoenix's 2027 unprotected first-round pick and top picks in 2029 from Dallas, Phoenix, and Houston.
  • This strategy allowed the Rockets to acquire Kevin Durant, with Phoenix's recent struggles making their future picks potentially more valuable.

  • The Houston Rockets are exploring trade targets to address their point guard needs following Fred VanVleet's injury.
  • Potential stopgap options include Ayo Dosunmu, whose contract situation was analyzed, and Jose Alvarado.
  • Higher-impact targets considered include Jaden Ivey from the Detroit Pistons and Derrick White from the Boston Celtics.
  • Peyton Pritchard and TJ McConnell were also mentioned, with their trade value compared to Reed Shepard.

  • The host suggests the Atlanta Hawks should offer Trae Young a contract extension.
  • Young's potential fit with the Rockets alongside Kevin Durant and Alperen Şengün is questioned due to defensive concerns.
  • Integrating Young with other high-usage players like Durant and Şengün presents challenges in offensive scheme and financial implications.
  • Peyton Pritchard is proposed as a lower-risk trade target for the Rockets, potentially benefiting Boston as well.

  • Minnesota Timberwolves' 20-to-1 NBA Finals odds are highlighted as a surprising value bet.
  • The Golden State Warriors' odds are questioned, being placed behind teams like Atlanta and Detroit despite Steph Curry's presence.
  • One host expressed skepticism about Joel Embiid playing over 40 games, ranking him 63rd among players.

  • Concerns are raised about the Denver Nuggets' supporting cast and their reliance on Nikola Jokic.
  • The Oklahoma City Thunder are noted for having seven players in the top 90, including Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Jalen Williams, and Chet Holmgren.
  • Player availability and performance metrics for Jalen Suggs, LaMelo Ball, and Tyler Herro are discussed.
  • Projections for potential impact players like Nikola Jokic, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Luka Doncic, Anthony Edwards, and Victor Wembanyama are made.

  • The Boston Celtics' season outlook is uncertain due to player availability concerns, including Jayson Tatum's potential sidelining and roster depth limitations.
  • Jaylen Brown's potential scoring increase is debated, with 29 points per game suggested as an attainable high target if Tatum is out.
  • The Indiana Pacers' identity is tied to Tyrese Haliburton, whose absence is a significant factor despite Coach Rick Carlisle's intensity.
  • Betting lines for Boston are at 41.5 wins, while Indiana is at 37.5 wins, with a lean towards the under for Indiana due to depth concerns without Haliburton.

  • Discussions included "The Great British Bake Off," noted as a favorite with all seasons viewed by one host.
  • "Only Murders in the Building" was mentioned, with a note that its charm might be fading.
  • "Morning Show" was described as preachy by one host but self-parodying and enjoyable by another, particularly its current season set during the 2024 Olympics.
  • "Party Down" was recommended and praised for its consistent humor, with a specific "porn party" episode highlighted as a personal favorite.

  • Las Vegas's identity has shifted from a unique gambling mecca to a broader entertainment and sports hub, with events like F1 and major sports teams.
  • Perceived difficulty in winning at casinos has increased, with contactless payments potentially encouraging more spending, contrasting with earlier cash-envelope strategies.
  • The city has transitioned from an affordable getaway to an upscale destination, marked by high-end hotels and restaurants, replacing easily comped drinks and meals.
  • The traditional sports book experience has declined, with less populated venues even during significant sporting events, such as a sports book with only four people during Cinemacon.

  • Las Vegas is transitioning from a focus on "degenerate gambling" and bachelor parties to a more family-friendly environment with broader entertainment options.
  • Questions arise whether the city is successfully catering to a wider audience or losing its core identity with attractions like the Sphere and major sporting events.
  • The cost of visiting Las Vegas, including parking and hotels, has increased, contrasting with a past era focused on saving money.
  • Speakers debate if Las Vegas can transition from a destination to a residential city, noting the addition of the Raiders and events like WrestleMania.
