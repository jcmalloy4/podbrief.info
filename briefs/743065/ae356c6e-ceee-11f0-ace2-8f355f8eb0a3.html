The Bill Simmons Podcast

A Crazy 49ers Win, Baltimore’s Alive, Week 18 Stakes, and Guess the Lines With Cousin Sal

Key Takeaways

  • Week 17 analysis revealed unusual playoff scenarios and key quarterback performances across the NFL.
  • NFL officiating faced criticism for inconsistent calls, particularly on roughing the passer and pass interference.
  • Fantasy football outcomes, including Christian McCaffrey's significant impact, were a major point of discussion.
  • Many teams' playoff and draft implications for Week 18 remained highly unpredictable.
  • The Buccaneers and Seahawks are under scrutiny for inconsistent performance and coaching decisions.
  • Parent Corner highlighted personal fantasy football frustrations and family dynamics during the holidays.

Deep Dive

  • The 49ers defeated the Bears; Caleb Williams' play was described as a "roller coaster," with comparisons to Patrick Mahomes for arm strength and elusiveness.
  • The 49ers demonstrated a "bulletproof" offense, moving the ball effectively despite offensive line injuries and an early pick-six.
  • Brock Purdy recorded 303 passing yards, 3 touchdowns, and 2 rushing touchdowns in the game.
  • Caleb Williams' season statistics included over 3,700 yards, 25 touchdowns, and 6 interceptions.
  • The Bears' consistent close games led to debate over whether they were the season's most entertaining team.

  • The Bills' Week 18 playoff seeding scenarios were analyzed, with potential matchups against the Patriots and Jaguars.
  • Considerations included the implications of the Chargers' game and the Bills' option to rest starters against the Jets.
  • The host questioned why the Patriots might prefer facing the Jaguars over the Chargers, citing injury concerns for the latter.
  • Josh Allen and the Bills achieved five consecutive AFC East titles prior to this season.
  • Despite a 13-3 record, the team was noted for only beating one opponent with a winning record.

  • The host expressed strong disdain for NFL "tanking," suggesting it should be exclusive to the NBA, and questioned the scheduling of games with clear draft order implications.
  • Criticism was directed at teams that focus on tanking, attributing poor performance to coaching and free-agent signings while relying on draft picks.
  • NFL draft prospects were discussed, including an Oregon quarterback and FanDuel odds for the number one pick, with Mendoza favored.
  • The Giants were noted as potentially benefiting from uncertainty surrounding a top quarterback's draft decision.

  • Buccaneers players reportedly held a press conference to express frustration after losing seven of their last eight games.
  • Disbelief was expressed regarding the team's performance, particularly consistent penalties and inability to capitalize on opportunities.
  • Play-calling and coaching decisions were questioned following multiple significant losses in December.
  • Todd Bowles could potentially secure his second division title with an 8-9 losing record, a rare achievement.
  • Speculation arose regarding Todd Bowles's job security, even if he were to win the division.

  • Uncertainty was expressed about trusting any team in the upcoming playoffs due to a perceived lack of dominant quarterbacks.
  • Favored teams, such as the 49ers, were noted for inconsistent performance, particularly concerning their defense.
  • The Steelers were identified as a potential first-round playoff exit, often playing close games.
  • Derrick Henry's dominant four-touchdown performance was highlighted, alongside the Texans' surprising win over the Chargers.

  • The inconsistency of roughing the passer penalties and taunting calls in the NFL was a point of critique.
  • Officiating decisions regarding pass interference and offensive pass interference, and their game-deciding impact, were discussed.
  • The ambiguity of "continuation catches" and the strategy of attempting to draw teams offsides were also examined.
  • Players are now able to remove helmets on the field without penalty, contrasting with other call strictness.

  • The Tampa Bay Buccaneers are favored by three points at home against the Carolina Panthers in a game with NFC South implications.
  • The San Francisco 49ers vs. Seattle Seahawks game betting line was debated, with the 49ers ultimately favored by 1.5 points.
  • Considerations for the 49ers included their short turnaround after a physically demanding game against the Bears and Christian McCaffrey's travel.
  • Quarterback situations involving Sam Darnold and Brock Purdy were discussed for the 49ers-Seahawks matchup.

  • The Jaguars are heavily favored to secure the three seed against the Titans, though some skepticism was noted regarding the large point spread.
  • The Bills are favored by 12.5 points against the Jets, despite the potential for resting players.
  • The Bears, tied at 11-5 with the Eagles, could secure the two seed with a win over the Lions.
  • The complexity of AFC seeding and the potential for "wonky" games were highlighted as factors affecting betting.

  • The Packers are noted to be locked into the seventh seed regardless of their game outcome against the Vikings, potentially impacting roster decisions.
  • The Chiefs at Raiders matchup was analyzed, with discussion focusing on the Raiders' recent performance and the point spread.
  • The Cowboys at Giants game was discussed, noting the Giants' recent win affected their draft pick position.
  • Predictions were provided for the Bengals versus Browns game, with the Bengals identified as "peaking at the right time."

  • The host recounted a painful fantasy football loss after starting Christian Watson over Alec Pierce, despite advice from his son Archie.
  • Christian Watson's 23-point performance contributed to the host's loss in a high-stakes fantasy league.
  • Cousin Sal discussed his 2007 fantasy championship team, featuring LaDainian Tomlinson and Adrian Peterson, contrasting it with current guillotine league struggles.
  • The host's adult children prioritized watching new "Stranger Things" episodes over family time on Christmas evening.
