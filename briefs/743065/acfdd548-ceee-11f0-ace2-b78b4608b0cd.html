The Bill Simmons Podcast

An NBA Six-Pack, Netflix’s WBD Pursuit, NFL Picks, and John Cena Finally Stops By, With Joe House and Matt Belloni

Key Takeaways

  • NBA discussions covered player performance, MVP considerations, and Giannis Antetokounmpo trade scenarios.
  • Matt Belloni detailed the impending Warner Bros. Discovery sale, identifying Paramount, Netflix, and Comcast as key suitors.
  • The challenges and historical success rates of media mergers were debated, including impacts on content strategy and brand identity.
  • Bill Simmons and Joe House provided NFL betting strategies and finalized their Ringer 107 picks for upcoming games.
  • John Cena discussed his decision to depart WWE, emphasizing physical limitations and a desire for a respectable exit.
  • Cena shared insights into the evolution of professional wrestling, his career philosophy, and experiences with Vince McMahon.
  • The conversation touched on the physical toll of wrestling and Cena's vision for his final WWE appearance, focusing on new talent.

Deep Dive

  • Giannis Antetokounmpo's potential trade destinations included Atlanta, Houston, and the Miami Heat.
  • A specific Miami Heat scenario involved acquiring Giannis and his brothers, plus Bobby Portis, in exchange for Tyler Herro, Bam Adebayo, and future draft picks.
  • News of a calf strain for Giannis was noted, with projections suggesting the issue could extend through January.

  • The host expressed pessimism about the Clippers and advised against early NBA betting due to unpredictable player availability.
  • NFL betting picks included reservations about the Ravens and a debate on including the Bengals in a parlay, citing the Bills' run game.
  • The Philadelphia Eagles' matchup against the Chargers was discussed, considering Jalen Carter's absence and Justin Herbert's broken hand as potential factors favoring the Eagles.

  • The Ringer 107 picks included Bears +6.5, a parlay of Texans +7.5 with the under 49.5 in Kansas City, Jaguars +1.5, and Eagles -2.5.
  • The final pick was Jets +3 against the Dolphins, with discussion focusing on Tua Tagovailoa's potential cold weather performance.
  • The hosts highlighted the difficulty in selecting a confident fifth pick due to various team dynamics and player performances.

  • Three main suitors for Warner Bros. Discovery were identified as Paramount, Netflix, and Comcast, with David Zaslov leading the bid evaluation.
  • Paramount is considered a likely suitor due to perceived political advantages, having settled a lawsuit involving Trump and broadcasting a fight on the White House lawn.
  • Potential acquisition scenarios include Netflix absorbing HBO Max or operating it as a separate tile, primarily to gain control of valuable franchises like Batman and Wonder Woman.
  • Concerns were raised that an HBO acquisition by Netflix could dilute HBO's distinct brand identity and audience appeal, comparing it to WWE's assimilation of WCW.

  • Discussion on Netflix's movie strategy questioned if the company would continue theatrical releases for Warner Bros. films post-acquisition.
  • One guest argued Netflix might benefit from a model where its movies receive a limited theatrical run before streaming, but acknowledged that theater chains might resist such releases.
  • Concerns were raised about Netflix's ability to create memorable, franchise-worthy content compared to traditional theatrical releases, citing 'Extraction' versus older films with cultural impact.

  • Professional wrestling has experienced multiple "golden ages" or "boom periods," including WWE's nationalization, the Monday Night Wars, and the current era with various competing promotions.
  • The early 2000s saw a downturn following the absorption of ECW and WCW, leading to an oversupply of talent and decreased audience size, alongside events like the XFL's failure.
  • Recent growth is attributed to improved TV deals, expanded global reach, a new generation of stars, and increased opportunities for performers.

  • John Cena underwent neck fusion surgery in 2007 or 2008 and returned to SmackDown the same week, a decision that surprised colleagues.
  • Cena emphasizes storytelling and character motivation over high-risk, athletic stunts in his wrestling performances.
  • He views his role as enhancing the narrative provided by management, rather than dictating his character (face or heel) or opponents.
  • Cena respects other performers' in-ring choices but is clear about understanding his own physical limitations, recounting a near-fatal flip attempt during a suplex.

  • John Cena asserted that WWE is thriving without Vince McMahon, reiterating McMahon's lesson that 'no one is irreplaceable' and highlighting the capable individuals now running the company.
  • Cena discussed potential future wrestling opponents like Gunther and LA Knight, his upcoming 'Little Brother' project with Eric Andre, and his desire to remain involved with WWE as a personality or announcer.
  • He recounted the challenges of juggling multiple commitments, including extreme fatigue from filming 'Fast & Furious' in Budapest and Morocco while also working WWE dates.
