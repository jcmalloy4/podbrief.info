A Crazy Good NBA Rookie Class With J. Kyle Mann, Plus Tua’s Cleveland Adventure, and NFL Week 7 Picks With Joe House

Key Takeaways

  • Dylan Harper showcased advanced skills and composure for a rookie in preseason NBA.
  • Victor Wembanyama's defensive prowess is a significant factor in Spurs' roster construction.
  • Rookie 'Flag' demonstrated versatile, high-value play comparable to a max contract asset.
  • NBA preseason assessments highlighted Harper and 'Flag' as having potential for All-NBA honors.
  • Analysts reconsidered initial NBA over-under predictions for teams like the Magic and Spurs.
  • NFL betting discussions identified 'stayaway' games and strategic parlays for the week.
  • The Kansas City Chiefs were predicted to win convincingly against the Las Vegas Raiders.
  • The New Orleans Saints were favored against the Chicago Bears, despite public betting trends.
  • The Philadelphia Eagles' defense was critiqued for injuries and below-average performance.
  • Cleveland Browns' home defense and adverse weather were projected to impact the Dolphins game.

Deep Dive

  • J. Kyle Mann praised Dylan Harper's poise in the paint and physical play, comparing his early scoring ability to Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.
  • Harper's crafty and angular playstyle, including unique layups, was noted as a key strength, surprising observers for a 19-year-old.
  • A specific play demonstrated Harper's advanced skillset, typically seen from veteran players.
  • The Spurs' securing the second pick was re-evaluated given Harper's impact and the unique nature of guards immediately handling significant ball-handling duties.

  • Rookie 'Flag' was described as a versatile 'Swiss Army knife' whose value on a rookie contract is likened to a max contract player.
  • The reporter expressed newfound respect for 'Flag,' noting his consistent progression and crafty offensive moves.
  • His ability to create space and hit shots off screens was highlighted, indicating an efficient play style.
  • The discussion touched on potential interesting lineup combinations involving 'Flag' alongside players like D'Lo and Cooper.

  • Rookie Ace Bailey's scoring ability and athleticism were identified as immediate strengths, drawing comparisons to players like Rashard Lewis.
  • His limitations in ball-handling and defensive awareness were noted.
  • The Utah Jazz was described as a 'perfect team' for Bailey to develop, offering minutes and shots due to their low-stakes environment.
  • The group agreed the Jazz could benefit from his potential 'messiness' and athleticism without significant downside.

  • Dylan Harper and 'Flag' were identified as the major headlines from NBA preseason rookie assessments.
  • Both rookies were suggested to possess first-team All-NBA potential based on their early performances.
  • The pairing of Harper with Victor Wembanyama in San Antonio was seen as a strong foundational piece for the Spurs' future.

  • The podcast guests recapped a successful NBA over-unders podcast with a 4-0 record for the hosts.
  • The host expressed doubt about their initial under pick for the Orlando Magic (50.5 wins), citing injury potential and a strong young core.
  • A guest reconsidered their under pick for the San Antonio Spurs, opting to support Victor Wembanyama's success.
  • A FanDuel super boost was mentioned, focusing on over-unders for Milwaukee Bucks (45+ wins), Golden State Warriors (45+ wins), and Oklahoma City Thunder (60+ wins).

  • The hosts discussed a betting pact involving $500 per person and analyzed the Patriots' schedule.
  • The possibility of the Patriots achieving an 11-6 record was mentioned, alongside a 'revenge game' narrative for the Titans vs. Patriots matchup.
  • The 49ers vs. Falcons game was assessed as a 'stayaway' despite the Falcons' strong recent performance, due to 49ers injuries and Falcons' road performance.
  • A parlay strategy for the Texans-Seahawks game was proposed, involving Houston at +7.5 and the under at 47.5, suggesting a 'full teaser' option.

  • The Kansas City Chiefs were favored at -11.5 against the Las Vegas Raiders.
  • Confidence was expressed in the Chiefs' strong offense, contrasting with the Raiders' struggles, particularly without tight end Michael Bowers.
  • Analysts anticipated a convincing Chiefs victory, even if they ease up in the second half.

  • One host questioned the five-and-a-half-point spread for the Saints vs. Bears game.
  • The guest argued the Bears' defense is statistically weak, especially against the run, and highlighted recent close wins as fortunate.
  • The Saints' competent offense and ability to gain yards on the road were cited as reasons to favor them despite the spread.
  • Betting trends showed 60% of FanDuel Sportsbook tickets on Chicago, validating a contrarian bet on New Orleans.

  • Concerns were raised about the Eagles' offensive line and injuries to key defensive players like Mitchell and Dickerson.
  • The retirement of Zadarius Smith was noted as significantly weakening the Eagles' pass rush.
  • The Eagles' defense was described as below average, ranking in the third tier of NFL defenses, with poor statistics (29th against pass, 25th against rush).

  • Weather forecasts predicted rain and potential wind for an upcoming game, impacting passing games.
  • The Cleveland Browns' strong defense against the run, particularly at home, was noted as a significant factor.
  • Tua Tagovailoa's performance in cold weather was questioned, contrasting with the Dolphins' struggles in adverse conditions.
  • The Dolphins' poor run defense, ranking 30th in the league, was highlighted, predicting a significant game for a specific running back.
