The Bill Simmons Podcast

The Rockets' Rise and NBA League Pass Rankings With Zach Lowe, Plus Week 12 NFL Picks With Joe House

Key Takeaways

  • The NBA League Pass draft highlighted Denver, Houston, and San Antonio among top watchable teams.
  • Kevin Durant is lauded for his team-first approach and engagement with the Houston Rockets.
  • Debate surrounds the optimal length of the NBA season and potential expansion to Seattle and Las Vegas.
  • Ringer 107 player rankings ignited discussion on balancing veteran experience with rising talent.
  • NFL betting strategies focused on identifying "stay away" games and capitalizing on perceived mispriced lines.
  • Concerns persisted regarding player availability and injury histories for key NBA stars.
  • Trade assets and roster flexibility are key topics for teams like the Atlanta Hawks and Los Angeles Clippers.
  • Toronto Raptors and Phoenix Suns were identified as undervalued teams showing strong commitment.

Deep Dive

  • The Denver Nuggets were selected as the number one team for the NBA League Pass draft due to consistent entertainment and Nikola Jokic's play.
  • Nikola Jokic is considered the decade's clear top player, compared to Stephen Curry, for his on-court performance.
  • Despite injuries, the Nuggets' performance, including Jamal Murray's improved speed, is stable, allowing the team to manage without major disruptions.
  • David Adelman's potential to pair Jokic with other players like Valentunas was also discussed as a strategic point.

  • The Houston Rockets display toughness and an aggressive style, gaining coaching staff approval, reminiscent of a hockey team mentality.
  • They secured a road win against the Cleveland Cavaliers with clutch plays from Aaron Holiday and Alperen Şengün, executing in critical moments.
  • Kevin Durant reportedly enjoys a "big brother" role with younger players like Jabari Smith Jr. and Reed Shepard, a contrast to his prior team experiences.
  • The Rockets' offensive efficiency and league-leading offensive rebounding rate are attributed to their large starting lineup and Alperen Şengün's versatility.

  • The Detroit Pistons were chosen fourth for entertainment value, recognized for team chemistry and potential for physical play, particularly with Isaiah Stewart and Ron Holland.
  • Jalen Thompson's emergence and the team's defensive capabilities were noted as factors contributing to their watchability.
  • Discussions surrounding Troy Weaver's management and draft picks like Cade Cunningham raise questions about his track record and approach in Detroit.
  • New additions like Simone Fontecchio and Monte Morris have contributed to the team's competitive nature.

  • A debate suggests the NBA season is too long, with a proposal for 76 games or fewer to improve the league, a sentiment shared by many.
  • Owner finances are highlighted, with one team selling for over $7 billion and player salaries reaching $75 million annually, leading to questions about acceptable income levels.
  • Momentum is noted for potential NBA expansion in Seattle and Las Vegas, with speculation about New Orleans relocation or a 31-team league.
  • Owners may hesitate to divide TV revenue with new teams, impacting expansion fees.

  • The Atlanta Hawks' assets, including a large trade exception and the New Orleans Pelicans' unprotected pick, make them candidates for a significant trade.
  • Hypothetical trades for players like Zach LaVine or DeMar DeRozan were considered, with questions raised about the wisdom of certain deals.
  • The potential pairing of Trae Young and James Harden on the same team was deemed problematic, sparking discussion on who would be traded.
  • Trey Young's three-point shooting percentage prior to his injury was noted as significantly lower than typically perceived, impacting team identity without him.

  • The Toronto Raptors' depth and the improved play of Scottie Barnes contribute to a more enjoyable viewing experience.
  • Scottie Barnes is now perceived as a more unselfish, physical, and defensively engaged player, shifting earlier opinions on his watchability.
  • The team's depth, particularly an advantage against injured opponents like the Cleveland Cavaliers, could lead them to 20 wins before a tough January schedule.
  • Players like Jacoby Walter and Grady Dick were mentioned in discussions about the team's upcoming performance.

  • The Portland Trail Blazers are recognized as a fun, competitive team despite injuries to key players like Scoot Henderson and Anfernee Simons.
  • The team consistently plays close games, showcasing their resilience in challenging situations.
  • The development of player Toumani Avdia is highlighted, with his contract described as a significant value.
  • Portland was selected among teams due to their consistent involvement in exciting games.

  • The Boston Celtics' watchability is linked to player metrics and the performances of Jalen Brown and Derrick White.
  • Concerns arose regarding coach Joe Mazzulla's handling of player Simons' playing time, citing a 25-point first-half performance followed by reduced minutes.
  • Jordan Walsh's defensive potential, particularly a specific defensive stretch against Tyrese Maxey, suggests he could become an 'SGA stopper.'
  • A scout's suggestion to start Simons over Pritchard was mentioned in the context of player development.

  • The Indiana Pacers are discussed as a team potentially tanking, drawing parallels to the San Antonio Spurs' acquisition of Tim Duncan in 1997.
  • The team's young core and strategic acquisition of high draft picks are noted as part of their rebuilding efforts.
  • The impact of Tyrese Haliburton's injury and the team's 'what if' playoff run are also acknowledged.
  • A past trade allowed the Pacers to regain their 2026 draft pick, contributing to their future potential.

  • Key NBA league stories being monitored include the Detroit Pistons' win streak and the Toronto Raptors' performance.
  • The status of LeBron James and the Milwaukee Bucks' ability to perform without Giannis Antetokounmpo are also under discussion.
  • Upcoming matchups, such as Denver versus Houston, are highlighted for their significance in the league.
  • The conversation also touched on the experience of doing live podcast shows in Minnesota and New York.

  • The first parlay pick combined the Chiefs minus 1.5 points with the Lions moneyline against the Giants, accepting a higher risk.
  • Additional picks included the Jacksonville Jaguars minus 2.5 points on the road against the Arizona Cardinals.
  • The Patriots minus 6.5 points against the Bengals in Cincinnati was a final pick, influenced by Jamar Chase's absence and potential team morale issues.
  • Hesitation regarding betting on the Chiefs was expressed due to their inconsistent performance, particularly their defense.

  • Specific NFL betting recommendations included the Buccaneers plus 6.5 points against the Rams, citing a perceived inflated line.
  • The Saints minus 1.5 points against the Falcons was another potential bet, considering the Falcons' offensive struggles without Drake London and betting against Kirk Cousins.
  • Concerns about the Rams' offense and the Buccaneers' defensive capabilities were factors in the analysis.
  • The Jacksonville Jaguars minus 2.5 points against the Arizona Cardinals was picked despite potential inconsistencies, projecting a 10-7 record for Jacksonville.
