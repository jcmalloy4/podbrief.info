Beyond the Talk: Deja Foxx on finding alternative online spaces

Key Takeaways

  • Teen girls offer vital solutions to global problems, yet their ideas are frequently dismissed.
  • New online architecture, built by and for women, is essential for safe and equitable digital spaces.
  • Alternative digital platforms are emerging to counter censorship and foster diverse representation online.
  • Civic engagement, both online and offline, empowers young people to drive meaningful change.
  • AI-generated deepfakes pose a significant threat to women's participation in public life and leadership.

Deep Dive

  • Deja Foxx, a 25-year-old activist with a decade of experience, highlights that teen girls possess solutions to major problems.
  • Her core message emphasizes personal experiences with online activism and political engagement.
  • Foxx gained public attention at age 16 for confronting a senator about birth control access.

  • Alternative digital platforms are emerging, allowing women to counter censorship prevalent on major sites.
  • DM, a Reddit alternative, reports that 25% of searches focus on reproductive rights, built from women's conversations.
  • Platforms like Sunroom emerged from issues where women's 'hotness' is commodified without creators receiving profit.
  • Sunroom implements content moderation through a 'woman's lens' with zero tolerance for harassment and hate speech, fostering diverse representations.

  • The guest advocates for tech regulations led by elected officials who understand social platforms, noting that attempts like a TikTok ban were poorly received.
  • A diversified platform ecosystem is proposed to prevent new platforms from inheriting problems of current dominant providers.
  • This model promotes user choice in information and community building, moving away from 'hate-for-profit' models and encouraging more women builders.

  • Online platforms often use gender and age to customize content feeds, leading to vastly different digital experiences.
  • The guest advocates for new online architecture that provides safe spaces for girls and women to build communities and drive real-world change.
  • Current major platforms are described as 'boy-built and boy-focused,' highlighting a fundamental design disparity.

  • The guest decided to run for office to provide young people with a candidate they could be excited about and see themselves in, addressing Gen Z's underrepresentation in Congress.
  • She encourages cynical young people to recognize their personal experiences as expertise, qualifying them for civic involvement.
  • Advocacy can manifest through various forms, including sharing one's story via public speaking, art, or content creation.

  • Foxx highlights a lack of discussion surrounding AI-generated deepfakes, particularly those targeting women.
  • This digital threat contributes to gender-based violence and can discourage women from participating in public discourse and leadership.
