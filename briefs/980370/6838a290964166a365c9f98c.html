How do you take the right risks? A photographer and brand builder answer | David Suh and Molly Graham

Key Takeaways

  • Authenticity drives unexpected success - David Suh's viral TikTok breakthrough came not from his technical photography skills, but from boldly expressing his authentic self, revealing that personal value extends far beyond professional expertise alone.
  • Fear serves as a growth catalyst - Both speakers identified fear and risk-taking as essential tools for self-discovery, helping individuals develop their authentic voice and build intrinsic confidence through successfully navigating challenging scenarios.
  • Content creation becomes a learning journey - Publishing and sharing knowledge online creates unexpected connections across diverse audiences, often leading to deeper personal growth as creators must research and develop responses to human-centric questions they hadn't initially considered.
  • Success definitions evolve with maturity - The conversation revealed a shift from external markers (titles, fame, community expectations) to internal metrics focused on joy, authenticity, and positive impact, with success increasingly defined by time allocation and meaningful relationships.
  • Individual approaches matter over universal solutions - Both speakers emphasized the importance of helping people as unique individuals rather than applying one-size-fits-all approaches, recognizing that authentic growth requires shedding past assumptions and remaining adaptable.

Deep Dive

David Suh's Viral TikTok Breakthrough

The conversation begins with portrait photographer David Suh sharing a pivotal moment during COVID quarantine. Unable to take client photos, he turned to TikTok and created an innovative video where he played both photographer and client roles. In a bold creative choice, he wore a red dress, wig, and flower crown to demonstrate client transformation. The video unexpectedly went viral overnight, teaching Suh a crucial lesson about authenticity and personal value.

  • Initially believed his worth was solely in his photography skills
  • The viral success revealed the importance of being authentically himself
  • Discovered he could bring more value by fully exploring and expressing his identity
  • Learned that embracing fear could serve as a catalyst for learning and growth

The Power of Unexpected Content Impact

The discussion shifts to another transformative moment involving the publication of an article about "giving away your Legos" - a metaphor for adapting and letting go as companies grow and change. This piece resonated far beyond its intended audience, reaching people from diverse companies and countries, including a founder in Nigeria.

  • The article's success led to speaking engagements called "Legos talks" at companies experiencing growth
  • Eventually facilitated a transition to coaching and mentoring startup founders and leaders
  • Demonstrated how sharing knowledge can connect people across different contexts
Both speakers reflect on how publishing content became an unexpected learning journey, with audience responses often focusing on deeply personal and human-centric questions that required additional research and growth.

Navigating Online Presence and Personal Growth

The conversation explores the challenges of maintaining authenticity in digital spaces. Suh discusses his evolution from initially building confidence through positive comments to developing a more stable foundation for self-worth.

  • Learned to manage negative comments and online criticism effectively
  • Developed a perspective of viewing fear as a trigger for learning and personal growth
  • Recognized the importance of helping people as individuals rather than applying one-size-fits-all approaches
  • Emphasized the need to shed past knowledge and remain adaptable

Risk-Taking as Self-Discovery

Both speakers identify risk-taking as a crucial method for authentic self-discovery and confidence building. They discuss how taking risks helps individuals:

  • Hone their authentic voice and make more confident decisions
  • Develop deep self-understanding and define who they are (and are not)
  • Build intrinsic confidence through successfully navigating challenging scenarios
Suh shares a specific example of posting content about Kamala Harris that triggered significant online criticism, ultimately leading him to remove the content due to anxiety. This experience sparked discussion about when to "fight" for authentic expression versus letting go.

Evolving Definitions of Success

The conversation culminates in a thoughtful exploration of how success definitions evolve with personal growth and age.

David's Perspective:

  • Seeks to discover his authentic self, especially after growing up in homogenous Korean culture with predefined success paths
  • Defines success as being more of himself and helping others
  • Believes authenticity enables greater positive impact on others
  • Values finding individual voice beyond community-prescribed expectations
Host's Perspective:
  • Recognizes that success definitions change with maturity
  • Shifted from external markers (titles, fame) to internal metrics
  • Now defines success primarily through joy and time allocation
  • Aims for joy in 80-85% of daily experiences after realizing time's finite nature
  • Prioritizes working with enjoyable people and spending time with loved ones

Conclusion and Mutual Learning

The conversation closes with both speakers expressing genuine enjoyment of their discussion and acknowledging their mutual learning potential. They express interest in continuing conversations about comfort, risk, and the distinction between joy and happiness - themes that clearly resonated throughout their exchange about authenticity, growth, and redefining success on personal terms.
