How to recapture the joy of the early internet | Michael Sun

Key Takeaways

  • Early internet fostered spontaneous connections, a stark contrast to today's algorithmic feeds.
  • Reclaim digital spontaneity through niche online communities or by sharing feeds with friends.

Deep Dives

Wild West

  • Michael Sun recounts a profound early online connection in 2008, experiencing "first online heartbreak" when it vanished.
  • He contrasts this with the current internet, where algorithmic feeds and rapid micro-trends create a sense of lost control and instant obsolescence.

Digital Reclamation

  • Sun proposes utilizing niche Facebook groups, akin to 2000s forums, as utopian spaces for genuine subcultures away from mainstream pressures.
  • He also suggests simply sharing unique algorithmic feeds with friends to discover unexpected content and reintroduce online randomness and connection.
