How video games can power up your parenting | Hannah Boquet

Key Takeaways

  • Engaging with children in their digital world, like video games, can foster strong family connections.
  • Esports are recognized for developing crucial life skills such as teamwork, leadership, and emotional regulation.
  • Video games can bridge generational and geographical distances, strengthening family relationships.
  • Parental participation in gaming can lead to a deeper understanding of children's online interactions and interests.

Deep Dive

  • Host Elise Hu introduced Hannah Bouquet's method of connecting with her children by playing video games.
  • Bouquet noted the challenge of maintaining connection with children as they became digital natives, feeling disconnected from their technology immersion.
  • To bridge this gap, Bouquet decided to join her children in their video game world, specifically Fortnite, despite her initial unfamiliarity.

  • Playing video games enabled Bouquet to understand her children's in-game communication and build sought-after connections.
  • An anecdote highlighted how her brother Amos used video games to connect with his nieces and nephews across geographical distance.
  • Esports are defined as competitive video games, encompassing genres from shooters to digital chess, emphasizing the competitive element.

  • Esports are recognized in South Dakota as a sanctioned high school activity, developing leadership, teamwork, communication, and emotional regulation.
  • The activity is highly inclusive, accessible to individuals of any age, background, or ability.
  • The speaker found joining her children in online spaces alleviated parental fears, leading to connection and understanding, even using Fortnite to introduce a new significant other.

  • The speaker contrasted her 1990s rural South Dakota childhood with modern gaming experiences.
  • A family Christmas gift of a Nintendo 64 enabled her to play video games with her father.
  • Her father's effort to connect through gaming revealed his playful side, creating cherished memories.

  • Video games foster a sense of belonging and teach valuable life skills, with discussions often emphasizing connection over gameplay.
  • The speaker's journey began by playing Fortnite with her sons, leading to research and organizing a competition in Sioux Falls.
  • Saying 'yes' to opportunities, even uncertain ones, resulted in connecting with her sons and being perceived as 'cool.'
