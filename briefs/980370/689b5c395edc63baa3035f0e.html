A multigenerational musical journey | Yijia Tu

Key Takeaways

  • Yijia Tu creatively fuses ancient East Asian traditions with modern musical forms.
  • Preserving tradition means keeping its spirit alive, not merely mimicking past forms.
  • Music powerfully bridges generations, cultures, and memories, transcending physical presence.

Deep Dives

Musical Blend

  • Initially, Yijia Tu found success with Western-influenced music but felt disconnected from her Chinese heritage, which she once viewed as outdated.
  • She consciously reconnected with her roots by learning traditional instruments and folk music, creatively blending these elements with her own compositions.

Living Tradition

  • Tu emphasizes Gustav Mahler's idea that tradition is about "keeping the fire alive," not "worshipping ashes," advocating for relevance over rigid adherence.
  • She highlights how the preference for "trendy" Western culture over "earthly" Chinese traditions risks their extinction, stressing the need for personal connection to preserve them.

Transcendent Power

  • Tu believes music transcends barriers of language, culture, time, and space, offering a profound way to connect people and consciousness.
  • A multi-generational lullaby allowed her to connect with her grandmother, who had Alzheimer's, demonstrating music's unique ability to maintain bonds beyond memory and loss.
