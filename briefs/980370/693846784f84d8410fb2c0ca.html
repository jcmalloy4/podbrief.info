Schools urgently need a redesign. Here's how | Aylon Samouha

Key Takeaways

  • Traditional education models are disengaging students and failing to prepare them for an AI-driven world.
  • Community-based design offers a robust approach to school redesign, blending local wisdom with research.
  • Successful implementations demonstrate improved student engagement, academic performance, and skill development.
  • Integrating AI with community-designed education holds potential to accelerate learning and purpose-finding for students.

Deep Dive

  • The Brooklyn STEAM Center, a public high school, exemplifies non-traditional education where students engage in real-world projects.
  • Projects include digital marketing and cybersecurity, connecting learning to practical applications.
  • Aylon Samouha introduces Transcend, his organization, which uses a community-based design approach to school redesign.
  • Transcend involves listening to students, families, and employers to create relevant and engaging learning experiences.

  • A survey of 70,000 students revealed less than half love school, and two-thirds find it boring.
  • Student engagement significantly declines by high school, with few feeling in charge of their learning.
  • Samouha argues that traditional schooling, designed for the factory age, is outdated for today's AI-driven world.
  • He advocates for redesigning education to prioritize critical thinking, collaboration, and adaptability.

  • Community-based design is presented as a third way that merges community wisdom with research and proven educational practices.
  • Van Ness Elementary in Washington, D.C., an example of this approach, focuses on the whole child, integrating mindfulness and restorative circles, leading to zero suspensions and improved academic performance.
  • The community-based design approach is expanding to over 100 schools nationwide.
  • A rural North Dakota school used this method to help a student find purpose and develop entrepreneurial skills through blacksmithing.

  • Integrating community-based design with technology, specifically AI, can accelerate learning and create immersive experiences.
  • The goal is to enable more students to find purpose and succeed in a rapidly changing world.
  • Aylon Samouha advocates for a continuous, community-based approach to redesigning education.
  • This approach aims to move beyond periodic overhauls to foster engaged, thriving students who can transform the world.
