The daily practice that could rewire your brain | Timm Chiusano

Key Takeaways

  • Timm Chiusano identifies "addiction to appreciation" as a personal superpower.
  • This insight emerged after a challenging day in 2018.
  • Appreciation, distinct from gratitude, can be a cultivated disposition.
  • Consistent appreciation enhances happiness, presence, and empathy.

Deep Dive

  • Chiusano experienced a difficult day in 2018, marked by team meltdowns and impending layoffs.
  • While walking home, he became fascinated by a manhole cover, prompting a shift in focus.
  • This unexpected observation occurred despite significant professional stress.

  • Chiusano identified his "addiction to appreciation" as a tendency to notice and value the good in everyday items.
  • He shared this realization on social media, framing it as a deliberate choice for a freeing practice.
  • Appreciation is defined as foundational and always present, differing from transactional gratitude.

  • Research from a Rutgers professor indicates appreciation is a disposition, with gratitude being only one of its eight components.
  • Consistent daily noticing can cultivate this appreciative mindset.
  • This practice led Chiusano to gain a large social media following through daily vlogs of his corporate life, which started with the hook, "This is a dope-ass Monday in your 40s."

  • Chiusano demonstrates appreciation by focusing on mundane objects like light bulbs, considering their representational progress.
  • He also applies this practice to people, advocating for empathy even towards those who have caused conflict.
  • Consistent appreciation is said to lead to increased happiness, wonder, presence, and an enhanced ability to encourage others.
