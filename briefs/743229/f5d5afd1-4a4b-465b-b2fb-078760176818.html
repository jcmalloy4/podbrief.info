“A Terrifying Line Is Being Crossed”: Mayor Jacob Frey on the Turmoil in Minneapolis

Key Takeaways

  • Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Fry detailed the city's conflict with federal immigration enforcement.
  • Federal "Operation Metro Surge" deployed thousands of agents, causing fear and local protests.
  • Mayor Fry refused to enforce federal immigration laws, prioritizing local crime over federal mandates.
  • Concerns emerged over ICE violating court orders and the political intent behind federal actions.
  • A federal investigation targets Minneapolis leadership amidst rising state-federal tensions.

Deep Dive

  • Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Fry discussed "Operation Metro Surge," which involved thousands of federal agents in his city.
  • ICE and CBP actions generated fear among residents and led to protests in Minneapolis.
  • Border Czar Tom Homan announced a drawdown of agents, but President Trump later denied this claim.
  • The host recounted nationwide protests against ICE and a Justice Department investigation targeting Minnesota officials.

  • Mayor Fry stated his refusal to enforce federal immigration laws, asserting that Minneapolis police should prioritize local crime like homicides.
  • The federal government proposed drawing down its substantial presence of 3,000-4,000 agents in Minneapolis.
  • The mayor expressed serious concern over reports indicating ICE had violated nearly 100 court orders since its crackdown began.

  • The guest posited that the federal deployment in Minneapolis was intended to create a political narrative and exact retribution, rather than for safety or immigration enforcement.
  • An initial large-scale federal deployment in Minneapolis was perceived as targeting the Somali community.
  • The guest supports citizens documenting federal agents' actions, likening it to police body cameras and viewing it as a "moral impediment" to unjust actions.

  • The guest reflected on his third term as mayor, noting past calls for police reform and improved community relations within the Minneapolis police department.
  • The guest criticized the Department of Justice for allegedly weaponizing information against peaceful individuals, citing the Alex Predi case.
  • While acknowledging valid criticisms of ICE, the mayor stated he does not favor abolishing the agency entirely, viewing it as a practical governance matter.

  • A federal investigation is targeting Mayor Fry and Governor Waltz, with a subpoena issued to the mayor's office for speaking for constituents.
  • The guest described a request from Attorney General Pam Bondi for Governor Waltz to turn over voter registration records as "wildly unconstitutional."
  • The mayor expressed concern that the federal government is using its power to coerce local cities, referencing a 'might is right' philosophy.

  • Constituents in Minneapolis are experiencing fear due to federal actions, with some avoiding leaving their homes or seeking medical care.
  • The guest highlighted the community's activism, resilience, and efforts to rebuild trust with the police department.
  • The guest expressed deep concern over the explicit conflict between federal and state authorities, comparing the situation to the start of the Civil War.
