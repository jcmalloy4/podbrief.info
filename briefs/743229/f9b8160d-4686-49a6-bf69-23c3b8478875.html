Peace in Ukraine Is Harder Than Trump Thought

Overview

* Trump's relationship with Ukraine has undergone a significant transformation - from initially suspending aid to Zelensky while seeking to please Putin, to becoming increasingly disillusioned with Putin's unwillingness to negotiate in good faith.

* Zelensky demonstrated strategic diplomacy by changing his public tone, showing openness to negotiations, and agreeing to a ceasefire proposal, effectively exposing Putin's lack of genuine interest in peace while maintaining U.S. support.

* A pivotal one-on-one meeting between Trump and Zelensky at Pope Francis's funeral granted Zelensky legitimacy in Trump's eyes, leading to improved relations and a minerals access deal that gave Trump a stake in Ukraine's future.

* Putin appears to be playing a long-term waiting game, taking Trump's calls casually without making concessions, seemingly anticipating that Trump will eventually become frustrated and reduce U.S. support for Ukraine.

* The future of U.S. support for Ukraine remains uncertain, with Trump oscillating between optimism, anger, and potential apathy, while European allies may not fully compensate if American assistance diminishes.

Content

Trump-Zelensky-Putin Dynamics

* The relationship between Trump, Zelensky, and Putin regarding the Russia-Ukraine conflict has evolved significantly over time. * Initially, Trump had a contentious meeting with Zelensky, suspending military aid and intelligence sharing. * Trump was eager to please Putin and believed he could quickly broker a peace deal. * European leaders acted as "marriage counselors" between Trump and the Ukrainian government.

Zelensky's Strategic Response

* Zelensky changed his public tone to be more careful about criticizing the U.S. * He showed openness to negotiating with Russia and agreed to a 30-day ceasefire proposal on March 11th. * This approach was strategically shrewd, effectively exposing Putin's unwillingness to genuinely negotiate. * Zelensky's cooperation put pressure on Putin while demonstrating Putin's lack of genuine interest in negotiations.

Shifting Dynamics

* Trump became increasingly disillusioned as Putin rejected meaningful peace negotiations. * It became apparent that Putin had been "stringing Trump along" without serious intent to compromise. * By late March/April, Trump began showing frustration with Putin. * Trump publicly criticized Putin on Truth Social, telling him to "stop" attacks on Ukraine—a significant departure from his previous Putin-friendly rhetoric.

Vatican Meeting

* Trump and Zelensky had an impromptu, one-on-one meeting at Pope Francis's funeral. * The meeting was intense but not angry, with a notable photo showing them in close, private conversation. * This meeting granted Zelensky legitimacy in Trump's eyes.

Diplomatic Developments

* Trump's administration signed a deal with Ukraine for access to critical minerals. * Ukrainians viewed the deal positively as giving Trump a stake in their country's future. * Trump became increasingly impatient with Putin's lack of concessions.

Putin-Trump Call

* The call occurred with mismatched expectations and approaches. * Putin treated the call casually, taking it from a school in Sochi. * Putin described the call as "informative" but made no significant concessions. * No ceasefire or breakthrough was achieved.

Trump's Negotiation Approach

* Trump expressed continued hope for a deal with Putin. * He directly asked Putin about ending the "bloodbath." * Trump appeared frustrated but not overtly angry. * He suggested this should have remained a "European situation." * Trump's approach was primarily motivated by a desire to "cut a deal." * He tended to posture publicly but was less confrontational in direct interactions. * Trump demonstrated a pattern of shifting between optimism, anger, and potential apathy.

U.S. Support Implications

* Uncertainty remains about continued U.S. support for Ukraine. * The U.S. is currently still providing previously approved Congressional assistance and critical intelligence sharing. * There's a potential risk of Trump reducing or cutting support. * European allies might not fully compensate for reduced U.S. assistance.

Strategic Observations

* Putin appears to be playing a long-term strategic game, waiting for U.S. disengagement. * An analogy about patience in diplomacy references a Taliban saying: "You have the watches, but we have the time." * Putin seems to expect Trump to become frustrated and abandon negotiations. * Trump's negotiation approach may not effectively pressure Putin. * This situation could create a potential long-term strategic advantage for Russia if U.S. support wanes.

Other International Developments

* A federal judge in Boston criticized the Trump administration for potentially violating a deportation order. * The judge ordered an investigation into a flight deporting migrants and warned of potential criminal contempt sanctions. * Canada, Britain, and France condemned Israel's proposed Gaza war escalation, calling the plans "disproportionate and egregious."
