Project 2025’s Other Project

Key Takeaways

  • Republican lawmakers are leveraging congressional hearings to accuse universities of failing to combat antisemitism, a strategy linked to a conservative strategic initiative.
  • The Heritage Foundation's Project Esther, initially presented as an anti-antisemitism plan, primarily aims to dismantle pro-Palestinian and broader progressive movements.
  • Project Esther broadly labels critics of Israel and pro-Palestinian groups as a "Hamas Support Network," extending targets to include progressive philanthropists and Democratic politicians.
  • The project proposes punitive government actions against activists, including deportation and defunding, with some of these proposals already mirroring Trump administration actions.

Deep Dives

Unveiling Project Esther's Core Mission

  • The Heritage Foundation's Project Esther, distinct from Project 2025, was developed as a national strategy to combat antisemitism following the October 2023 Hamas attack and subsequent campus unrest.
  • Despite its stated purpose, the project's true aim is to dismantle the pro-Palestinian movement and broader progressive causes, labeling them as anti-capitalist, anti-Western, and threats to Judeo-Christian values.
  • Named after a biblical figure, the document brands critics of Israel and pro-Palestinian activists as a "terrorist support network" or "Hamas Support Organization" to broadly define its perceived enemy.

Expanding the Definition of "Enemy"

  • Project Esther defines its "Hamas Support Network" to include prominent pro-Palestinian groups like National Students for Justice in Palestine and anti-Zionist groups like Jewish Voice for Peace, despite their denials of affiliation.
  • The network also targets progressive philanthropic groups and Democratic politicians, referred to as the "Hamas caucus," even suggesting Jewish figures like George Soros are part of this network.
  • This broad labeling aims to shift public perception by associating the pro-Palestinian movement directly with terrorism, thereby framing it not just as a threat to Jews but to America itself.

Translating Strategy into Action

  • The project outlines severe punitive measures against targeted individuals and groups, including deportation, defunding, lawsuits, firing, and expulsion, to discourage any association with critics of Israel.
  • Actions by the Trump administration, such as arresting protestors, threatening deportation for visa holders, and freezing university grants for alleged inaction against harassment, align with over half of Project Esther's objectives.
  • While direct ties remain unconfirmed by officials, a co-author of Project Esther indicates that its proposals are being implemented at federal, state, and private levels, suggesting a coordinated agenda.

Leveraging Antisemitism for Broader Agendas

  • Project Esther reflects a fundamental distrust of higher education, viewing college campuses as hotbeds of progressivism, and leverages concerns about Jewish safety to weaken these institutions.
  • The strategy uses antisemitism as a means to undermine broader democratic principles, including due process, free speech, and the integrity of the press, aligning with a wider conservative agenda.
  • Some Jewish groups have cautioned that this project exploits genuine fears of antisemitism for partisan goals, urging Jewish leaders to resist such political manipulation.
