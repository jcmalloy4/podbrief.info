The 100 Best Movies of the 21st Century

Key Takeaways

  • The New York Times' top 100 movie list reaffirmed cinema's enduring cultural significance and public engagement.
  • The diverse list reflected personal tastes, surprising with its strong representation of popular comedies.
  • Auteur filmmakers and unconventional blockbusters dominated the selections, highlighting distinct cinematic visions.

Deep Dives

Cinema's Relevance

  • The New York Times' top 100 films list generated immense public interest, with millions engaging across platforms.
  • This widespread discussion, even on TikTok, underscored movies' ongoing cultural importance to audiences.

Curated Choices

  • Approximately 500 film industry figures, including actors and directors, contributed their personal movie ballots.
  • The list surprisingly featured many popular comedies like 'Bridesmaids,' showing genuine personal resonance over critical prestige.
  • These diverse selections revealed individual sensibilities and reflected broader life experiences through film choices.

Auteur Vision

  • The list heavily featured films by auteur directors, recognized for their distinct personal vision and consistent style.
  • Directors like Christopher Nolan and the Coen Brothers had multiple entries, demonstrating their influential filmmaking.
  • Top films such as "Mulholland Drive" and "Parasite" showcased the list's embrace of unique, impactful cinematic works.
