The New Co-Hosts of 'The Daily'

Key Takeaways

  • Diverse journalistic origins shape perspective: The new co-hosts bring unique inspirations—from witnessing genocide survivor interviews in Guatemala to finding purpose through comic book heroes—demonstrating how varied paths can lead to impactful journalism.
  • High-stakes investigative work defines their approach: Both hosts have tackled dangerous, complex stories—from tracking down GM crash victims' families to reporting inside fentanyl laboratories—showing their commitment to immersive, accountability journalism.
  • The host serves as listener advocate**: Their hosting philosophy centers on clarifying complex information, uncovering deeper meanings, and adapting communication styles across diverse subjects while maintaining the listener's perspective throughout each episode.
  • Audio storytelling offers unique power: The transition to The Daily represents their recognition that podcast format can convey complex narratives in ways other media cannot, particularly for sensitive topics like the Me Too movement.

Deep Dive

Introduction and New Co-Hosts

Michael Barbaro introduces Natalie Kittroweff and Rachel Abrams as the new co-hosts of The Daily podcast, setting the stage for a conversation about their journalistic backgrounds and approach to hosting.

Journalistic Origins and Inspirations

Natalie's Path to Journalism:

  • Inspired by her mother, a Latin American politics professor who brought a scholarly perspective to complex regional issues
  • Gained early exposure to investigative work by accompanying her mother to Guatemala
  • Witnessed firsthand interviews with genocide survivors, guerrilla fighters, and ex-army commanders
  • Learned the critical importance of understanding complex stories from multiple perspectives, recognizing that truth often emerges from examining all sides of a conflict
Rachel's Journalism Inspiration:
  • Found her calling through an unexpected source: comic books, particularly the character of Lois Lane
  • Viewed journalism as a powerful force for good in the world through uncovering and revealing truth
  • Saw reporters as essential truth-tellers with the ability to make meaningful impact

Defining Professional Moments

Rachel's Impactful Investigation:

  • Conducted a significant investigation into General Motors car malfunctions that were causing fatal accidents
  • Demonstrated persistence by tracking down the family of an early crash victim
  • Provided both closure and potential financial compensation by revealing the mechanical failures responsible for the tragedy
  • Exemplified journalism's power to bring accountability and justice to affected families
Natalie's Recent High-Stakes Reporting:
  • Undertook dangerous investigative work on the Sinaloa cartel to understand the roots of the fentanyl crisis
  • Conducted risky field reporting, including visits to actual fentanyl laboratories
  • Aimed to provide comprehensive understanding of the drug trade's complex dynamics and supply chains
  • Demonstrated commitment to immersive journalism despite significant personal risk

Transition to The Daily

Personal Connection to the Show:

  • Natalie describes The Daily as feeling like a "home away from home," expressing enthusiasm about making her role permanent
  • Rachel was initially drawn to the podcast through a Me Too movement story, recognizing audio's unique power to convey complex narratives in ways other media cannot

Hosting Philosophy and Approach

Core Hosting Principles:

  • The host's primary responsibility is serving as an advocate for the listener throughout each episode
  • Key hosting duties include:
- Clarifying potentially confusing information or complex topics - Uncovering deeper meanings and implications within conversations - Maintaining presence while effectively representing the listener's perspective and questions - Adapting communication style across diverse interview subjects, from farmers to university presidents

Team Dynamic:

  • The conversation reveals a warm and collegial relationship between all participants
  • Mutual excitement about working together is evident throughout their discussion
  • The group engages in playful, friendly banter, including jokes about thanking each other
  • The transition represents colleagues moving into new collaborative roles with shared enthusiasm for the work ahead
