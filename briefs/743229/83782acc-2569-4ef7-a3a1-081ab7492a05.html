What C.E.O.s Really Think About Trump’s Tariffs

Key Takeaways

  • CEOs view Trump's aggressive tariffs as the "new normal," adapting rather than resisting.
  • Widespread fear of presidential criticism shapes corporate decisions, including hiring and pricing.
  • The booming AI sector is currently offsetting tariff impacts, but economic gravity looms.

Deep Dives

Tariff Reality

  • President Trump has implemented significantly high tariffs, some exceeding 100%, now including a surprising 50% on goods from India.
  • Business leaders view these tariffs as a permanent economic reality, shifting their focus from lobbying efforts to adjusting supply chains and operations.

Corporate Caution

  • CEOs harbor a significant fear of President Trump targeting them or their industries with public criticism or adverse actions.
  • This climate of fear influences even hiring decisions, with companies making back-channel calls to the White House to gauge potential reactions to candidates.
  • Companies are delaying price increases to avoid presidential conflict, absorbing tariff costs even when financially unsustainable, as seen with Ford.
