How Mintlify Is Rebuilding Documentation for Coding Agents

Key Takeaways

  • Mintlify's co-founders iterated through eight pivots to develop a documentation platform for developers.
  • Documentation has evolved into critical infrastructure, powering AI tools and support agents.
  • Mintlify's early "do things that don't scale" sales approach secured its first customers.
  • AI advancements are enabling "self-healing" documentation, addressing outdated information issues.
  • Mintlify now supports external and internal documentation for millions of users monthly.

Deep Dive

  • The role of documentation has shifted from a human-readable resource to essential infrastructure for AI tools, coding agents, and support bots.
  • Accurate and reliable documentation is crucial as outdated information can lead to system failures in AI-powered workflows.
  • Mintlify's success is attributed to its "do things that don't scale" sales motion and iterating through eight pivots.

  • A two-day prototype developed by Mintlify co-founders led to their first customer, Hyper Beam.
  • Mintlify's early sales motion included manually migrating customer documentation and offering reviews, a strategy termed 'do things that don't scale'.
  • This manual approach, including pre-building documentation for potential clients, helped Mintlify scale to thousands of customers.

  • The vision of AI agents using documentation as a source of truth has become a reality, increasing its importance for human users and AI.
  • The demand for documentation has significantly increased due to the rise of AI agents and new developer personas like 'vibe coders'.
  • AI tools are now helping to automate the update process for documentation to maintain accuracy.

  • Mintlify's scope dramatically expanded in 2023 to include help centers, internal documentation, and replacing internal knowledge stacks.
  • The platform's AI tooling, QA bots, and Slack integration for natural language commands are key features driving broader adoption.
  • Engineers are increasingly involved in support product decisions because AI agents now handle a significant portion of support tickets.

  • Context and documentation are crucial for AI agents to perform effectively, highlighting the continued need for excellent tooling.
  • The persistent problem of outdated documentation is attributed to engineers, who possess the most context, being disincentivized to maintain docs.
  • Incorrect or outdated documentation leads to real-world consequences when relied upon by AI tools like support agents.

  • The convergence of advanced model capabilities, specifically GPT-4.5, and increased enterprise comfort sharing data with AI agents is enabling 'self-healing' documentation.
  • Mintlify's design focus has shifted from visual appeal to prioritizing content itself and access points for AI agents.
  • The role of documentation is evolving to primarily serve AI tools, potentially becoming 90% for AI and 10% for human consumption.

  • Mintlify serves demanding customers, including prominent AI labs and companies like Anthropic, Microsoft, and Coinbase.
  • To meet client needs, especially from fast-moving organizations, Mintlify prioritizes responsiveness and speed in its operations.
  • Anthropic complimented Mintlify's quick replies, setting a high standard for startups, inspired by the pace of large companies.

  • Customer usage from early adopters like Anthropic has directly influenced Mintlify's product development, such as inspiring a pipeline for automated documentation translation into 12 languages.
  • Mintlify's platform powered sites that were encountered by 20 million people last month.
  • The co-founders are motivated by empowering builders, including individuals like an 11-year-old learning to code.
