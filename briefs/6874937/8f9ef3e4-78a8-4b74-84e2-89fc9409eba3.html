Material Security CEO: How To Find Your Ideal Customer

Key Takeaways

  • Frothy markets can obscure true customer needs and delay Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) definition.
  • A clear ICP is crucial for efficient customer acquisition, marketing spend, and post-sales retention.
  • ICP definition is a company-building step, impacting product roadmap, sales, and investment decisions.
  • Founders must balance creating a new category with meeting immediate market needs to drive adoption.
  • Identifying ICP goes beyond company size, focusing on subtle factors like team structure and buying behavior.

Deep Dive

  • In a market boom around 2018, early customers had discretionary budgets, allowing easy sales of solutions to ubiquitous problems.
  • Material Security experienced 180% net revenue retention, primarily from hypergrowth tech startups like DoorDash and Stripe.
  • Rapid success and demand from early customers obscured the need for rigorous ICP definition, despite providing learning opportunities.

  • The market downturn accelerated the realization that a lack of clear ICP definition impacted downstream decisions like marketing and investment.
  • Declining seat-based Net Revenue Retention forced Material Security to focus on new business and pipeline generation.
  • ICP clarity became critical for investment decisions, affecting marketing messaging, capital deployment, and activities like self-serve trials.

  • Founders' instinct to solve problems for everyone can be counterproductive; ICP is about doubling down on what works.
  • Defining an ICP requires historical analysis of successful customers and efficient engagements, complicated by evolving products.
  • Sales team reactions during pipeline reviews can provide a 'kinesthetic sense' for viable opportunities, contrasting with founders' emotional attachments.
  • ICP definition helps identify customers who prefer a product over alternatives due to its unique appeal, influencing market segmentation.

  • Material Security initially served a wide range of customer sizes without a defined ICP.
  • They realized their product appeals most to companies with consolidated security needs, often correlated with smaller security teams, not just company size.
  • ICP complexity extends beyond simple metrics, involving nuanced factors like team structure and buying behavior.
  • Sales teams are more efficient with a clear, singular focus provided by a defined ICP, preferring it over multiple strategies.

  • Material Security's initial concept involved novel access controls for inboxes, prompted by breaches like the Sony Pictures hack in 2016.
  • They learned to 'meet the market where it is' by offering solutions addressing immediate customer needs before guiding them to a broader vision.
  • Material Security wins deals by beginning with competitive offerings but ultimately securing customers with their unique, differentiated capabilities.
  • Identifying an ICP impacts all company aspects, from product roadmap and messaging to sales and team culture, as seen in a prospect choosing Material Security partly due to team 'vibe'.

  • Customers outside the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) require budgeting and resource allocation, but not all will evolve into ICP.
  • Managing non-ICP customers necessitates transparency and clear communication about exceptions to company strategy.
  • The guest advises founders to consider ICP definition earlier; the host cautions against premature ICP definition before customer data is gathered.
