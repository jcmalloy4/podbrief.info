The Information Problem Professionals Face

The best leaders, founders, and executives are voracious learners. But between back-to-back meetings, deliverables, and actually running a business, podcast backlogs grow to hundreds of unheard episodes. The guilt is real. The time isn't.

PodBrief gives you the curated intelligence without the time cost. Our AI reads, analyzes, and distills every episode into a clean 2-minute summary — the key arguments, insights, and takeaways, nothing else. You decide if it's worth the full listen. Most of the time, the summary is enough.

Think of it as having a brilliant assistant who listens to everything and briefs you on what matters. That's PodBrief.
