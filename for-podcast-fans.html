The Superfan's Problem

You love podcasts. Maybe too much. Your queue is hundreds of episodes deep. You follow shows religiously but can't possibly keep up. You feel guilty when you skip an episode. You miss references your friends make to podcasts you technically subscribe to but haven't listened to in weeks.

PodBrief is the superfan's secret weapon. Instead of choosing between shows, stay across all of them with summaries. Know every key episode, every hot take, every guest — without the time cost of full listens. Then go deep on the episodes that truly deserve it.

It's not replacing listening. It's making you a better, more informed listener with a wider range of shows than anyone you know.
