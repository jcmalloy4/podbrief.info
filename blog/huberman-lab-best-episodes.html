Best Huberman Lab Episodes: Essential Science Protocols

Published February 11, 2024 · 12 min read

Dr. Andrew Huberman's podcast has become essential listening for anyone interested in neuroscience, health, and performance. With 90+ minute episodes packed with research-backed protocols, it can be overwhelming to know where to start.

Here are the must-listen Huberman Lab episodes organized by topic.

Best Sleep Episodes

Sleep is foundational to everything. These episodes give you actionable protocols for better sleep:

  • Master Your Sleep: The comprehensive sleep toolkit
  • Sleep Supplements: What works and what doesn't
  • Optimize Your Sleep Environment: Temperature, light, and timing

Best Focus & Productivity Episodes

Learn to control your attention and get more done:

  • Focus Toolkit: Science-backed strategies for concentration
  • Dopamine & Motivation: How to maintain drive
  • ADHD & Focus: Understanding attention mechanisms

Best Exercise & Performance Episodes

Optimize your training with neuroscience:

  • Exercise for Longevity: What science says about optimal training
  • Build Muscle: The neuroscience of hypertrophy
  • Endurance Training: Maximize cardiovascular health

Best Mental Health Episodes

Science-based approaches to stress, anxiety, and mood:

  • Stress Toolkit: Real-time stress management
  • Depression & Anxiety: Understanding and addressing mood
  • Breathwork: Powerful protocols for state management

Why Huberman Lab Episodes Are So Long

Most episodes run 90-120 minutes because Huberman:

  • Explains the science thoroughly
  • Gives context for why protocols work
  • Addresses common questions and misconceptions
  • Provides multiple protocol options for different situations

How to Get the Most from Huberman Lab

These episodes are dense with information. Here's how to maximize value:

  • Read briefs first: Know what protocols are covered before listening
  • Focus on one topic: Don't try to implement everything at once
  • Take notes: Write down specific protocols you want to try
  • Re-listen: These episodes reward multiple listens
