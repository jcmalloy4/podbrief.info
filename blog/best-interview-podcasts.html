Best Interview Podcasts (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

The best interview podcasts go beyond surface-level questions to create meaningful conversations. These shows give guests time to think, share stories, and dive deep into topics that matter.

Top Interview Podcasts

1. Lex Fridman Podcast

MIT researcher Lex Fridman conducts marathon conversations (often 2-4 hours) with scientists, philosophers, engineers, and cultural figures. Deep, thoughtful, and intellectually rigorous.

Best for: Science, AI, philosophy, deep thinking

Episode length: 2-4 hours

Browse Lex Fridman summaries →

2. The Tim Ferriss Show

Tim Ferriss deconstructs world-class performers to extract tactics, tools, and routines. Guests include everyone from Arnold Schwarzenegger to Brené Brown.

Best for: Productivity, success habits, life optimization

Episode length: 90-180 minutes

3. The Joe Rogan Experience

The biggest podcast in the world features 3-hour conversations on every topic imaginable. Joe's curiosity and diverse guest list create unpredictable, fascinating discussions.

Best for: Long-form conversations, variety

Episode length: 2-3 hours

4. SmartLess

Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes surprise each other with celebrity guests, creating genuine, funny, and insightful conversations.

Best for: Celebrity interviews with humor

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

5. On Being with Krista Tippett

Krista Tippett explores the big questions of meaning, purpose, and human connection with poets, scientists, theologians, and thinkers.

Best for: Meaning, spirituality, humanity

Episode length: 50-60 minutes

6. WTF with Marc Maron

From his garage, Marc Maron conducts raw, honest conversations with comedians, actors, musicians, and cultural figures. Vulnerable and authentic.

Best for: Real talk with comedians and artists

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

7. Fresh Air

Terry Gross is a legendary interviewer who brings curiosity, preparation, and empathy to every conversation. NPR excellence.

Best for: Authors, artists, newsmakers

Episode length: 30-45 minutes

8. Armchair Expert

Dax Shepard and Monica Padman create a judgment-free zone for celebrities to discuss failures, vulnerabilities, and growth.

Best for: Honest celebrity conversations

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

9. Rich Roll Podcast

Ultra-endurance athlete Rich Roll talks wellness, nutrition, mindfulness, and peak performance with experts and inspiring individuals.

Best for: Health, wellness, inspiration

Episode length: 90-150 minutes

10. How I Built This

Guy Raz interviews founders and entrepreneurs about building iconic companies. Inspiring origin stories of brands you know.

Best for: Entrepreneurship, business stories

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

How to Choose

For Intellectual Depth

Start with Lex Fridman or On Being. These shows prioritize thoughtful exploration of complex topics.

For Practical Advice

The Tim Ferriss Show and Rich Roll extract actionable wisdom from every conversation.

For Entertainment

SmartLess and Armchair Expert balance insight with humor and celebrity appeal.

For Business Inspiration

How I Built This tells the stories behind successful companies in compelling narrative form.

What Makes a Great Interview Podcast?

The best interview shows share these qualities:

  • Prepared hosts: They've done their homework
  • Genuine curiosity: They actually want to learn
  • Time for depth: No rushing through topics
  • Follow-up questions: They listen and respond
  • Guest variety: Different perspectives and fields
  • Letting guests think: Silence is okay

Getting Started

New to interview podcasts? Try these episodes:

  • Lex Fridman: Elon Musk or Sam Altman episodes
  • Tim Ferriss: "How to Master Any Skill" with Josh Waitzkin
  • WTF: President Barack Obama episode
  • How I Built This: Airbnb or Instagram episodes
