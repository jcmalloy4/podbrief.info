Best Finance Podcasts 2024

Published February 15, 2024 · 11 min read

Master investing, markets, and personal finance with these top finance podcasts. From beginners to pros.

Best Overall: All-In Podcast

Four tech investors debate markets, economy, tech, and policy. Weekly deep dives into what's happening in finance and business.

Hosts: Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg

Format: 60-90 min weekly

Best For: Market analysis, tech investing, macro trends

Why It's #1

  • Multiple perspectives on every topic
  • High-level investor insights
  • Covers markets, AI, policy, economics
  • Timely analysis of current events

Best for Economics: Planet Money

NPR's economics podcast makes complex topics accessible. Stories about how the economy actually works.

Format: 20-30 min, 2-3x per week

Best For: Understanding economics, financial history, market mechanics

Why People Love It

  • Explains economics through stories
  • Short, digestible episodes
  • No finance jargon required
  • Great for beginners

Best for Personal Finance: The Dave Ramsey Show

Debt-free living, budgeting, and building wealth. Ramsey answers caller questions about real financial situations.

Format: Daily episodes, 45-60 min

Best For: Getting out of debt, budgeting, personal finance basics

Key Philosophy

  • Baby steps to financial freedom
  • Zero-based budgeting
  • Emergency fund first
  • Debt snowball method

Best for Investing: We Study Billionaires

Learn from the world's best investors. Deep dives into investment strategies and legendary investors.

Hosted by: The Investor's Podcast Network

Format: 60-90 min interviews

Best For: Value investing, stock market, investment philosophy

Episode Types

  • Founder's Notes (Warren Buffett deep dives)
  • Bitcoin Fundamentals
  • Millennial Investing
  • Real estate investing

Best Daily News: Morning Brew Daily

Business news in 15 minutes. Perfect morning briefing on markets, companies, and economy.

Format: Daily, 15-20 min

Best For: Staying current on business news without reading financial news sites

Best for Markets: Odd Lots

Bloomberg's deep dive into markets and economics. Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal interview experts.

Format: 45-60 min, 2-3x per week

Best For: Understanding market mechanics, Fed policy, economic trends

Best for Crypto: Bankless

Crypto, DeFi, and blockchain explained. Weekly analysis of crypto markets and technology.

Hosts: Ryan Sean Adams, David Hoffman

Format: Weekly episodes, 45-90 min

Best For: Crypto investing, DeFi, blockchain technology

Best for Beginners: ChooseFI

Financial independence for regular people. Practical strategies to retire early and build wealth.

Format: 3x per week, 45-60 min

Best For: FIRE movement, index investing, saving strategies

How to Choose Your Finance Podcast

If You're New to Finance

Start with: Planet Money + Morning Brew Daily

Planet Money explains concepts, Morning Brew keeps you current.

If You Want to Invest

Start with: We Study Billionaires + All-In

Learn investment philosophy from billionaires, get market analysis from All-In.

If You're in Debt

Start with: The Dave Ramsey Show

Practical debt payoff strategies and real caller examples.

If You Follow Markets

Start with: Odd Lots + Morning Brew Daily

Deep market analysis + daily news coverage.

Finance Podcast Listening Strategy

For Beginners

  • Morning Brew Daily (daily, 15 min) - current events
  • Planet Money (2x/week, 25 min) - concepts
  • Dave Ramsey (as needed) - personal finance

For Investors

  • All-In (weekly, 90 min) - market analysis
  • We Study Billionaires (weekly, 60 min) - strategy
  • Odd Lots (3x/week, 45 min) - deep dives

For FIRE Seekers

  • ChooseFI (3x/week) - strategies
  • We Study Billionaires (weekly) - investing
  • Planet Money (2x/week) - economics

Common Mistakes

Following Too Many News Podcasts

Pick one daily news podcast (Morning Brew or similar). More = noise.

Only Listening to Bulls or Bears

Get multiple perspectives. All-In is great because hosts disagree.

Ignoring Personal Finance

Investment podcasts are fun, but budgeting/debt payoff matters more for most people.

Treating Podcasts as Financial Advice

These are educational, not personal financial advice. Do your own research.
