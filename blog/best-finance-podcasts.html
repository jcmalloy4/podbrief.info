Best Finance Podcasts in 2026

Published February 13, 2026 · 10 min read

Whether you're learning to invest, following market trends, or building wealth, finance podcasts deliver expert insights without the jargon overload. From beginner-friendly shows to deep-dive market analysis, here are the best finance podcasts to grow your financial knowledge.

Top Finance Podcasts

1. The Compound and Friends

Josh Brown and Michael Batnick deliver market commentary with personality. The Compound blends serious financial analysis with humor, making complex market dynamics accessible and entertaining.

Why listen: Real talk about markets, entertaining hosts, practical investing advice.

2. Planet Money (NPR)

Planet Money makes economics and finance fascinating through storytelling. Each episode tackles economic concepts, financial history, or market phenomena with wit and clarity.

Why listen: Economics made fun, storytelling approach, short episodes perfect for learning.

3. Motley Fool Money

Weekly market recap with investing insights from the Motley Fool team. Discusses stocks, market trends, and investing strategies with long-term focus.

Why listen: Stock analysis, market news, long-term investing perspective.

4. Rational Reminder

Evidence-based investing podcast focusing on academic research and behavioral finance. Cameron Passmore and Benjamin Felix dive deep into portfolio construction, risk management, and financial planning.

Why listen: Academic rigor, evidence-based approach, portfolio construction insights.

5. We Study Billionaires

Preston Pysh and Stig Brodersen analyze investing through studying the world's best investors. Episodes include Warren Buffett deep-dives, interviews with investors, and market analysis.

Why listen: Learn from billionaire strategies, value investing focus, quality interviews.

6. Chat With Traders

Aaron Fifield interviews professional traders across all markets and strategies. Practical trading insights from those making money in markets.

Why listen: Real trader experiences, diverse strategies, practical trading insights.

7. Animal Spirits

Ben Carlson and Michael Batnick discuss markets, investing, and life. Casual but informed conversations about financial planning, market behavior, and investor psychology.

Why listen: Behavioral finance insights, market psychology, relatable financial advice.

8. Invest Like the Best

Patrick O'Shaughnessy interviews investors, entrepreneurs, and thinkers. Deep conversations about investing philosophy, mental models, and decision-making.

Why listen: High-quality guests, investing philosophy, mental models for decision-making.

9. The Indicator (NPR)

Daily 10-minute economics podcast explaining economic indicators, market trends, and financial news. Quick, informative episodes from Planet Money's team.

Why listen: Daily economics news, bite-sized episodes, timely topics.

10. Afford Anything

Paula Pant explores financial independence, real estate investing, and lifestyle design. Practical advice on building wealth while enjoying life.

Why listen: Financial independence focus, real estate investing, work-life balance.

11. BiggerPockets Money Podcast

Mindy Jensen and Scott Trench discuss financial independence through real estate, side hustles, and smart money management. Interviews with people achieving FI through various paths.

Why listen: Financial independence strategies, real estate focus, diverse money stories.

12. The Money Guy Show

Brian Preston and Bo Hanson deliver financial planning advice and wealth-building strategies. Comprehensive personal finance guidance with clear frameworks.

Why listen: Financial planning process, wealth-building frameworks, comprehensive advice.

13. Masters in Business

Barry Ritholtz interviews leaders in finance, investing, and business. High-quality conversations with legendary investors, economists, and business leaders.

Why listen: Legendary investor interviews, market history, insider perspectives.

14. Acquired

Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal deep-dive into company histories and business strategy. While not purely finance, Acquired delivers the best business analysis for understanding investments.

Why listen: Company deep-dives, business strategy analysis, investment research quality.

15. Bloomberg Odd Lots

Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway explore markets, economics, and finance with expert guests. Tackles unconventional topics and explains complex market dynamics.

Why listen: Deep market analysis, unconventional topics, expert guests.

What Makes a Great Finance Podcast?

Actionable insights: The best shows provide practical advice you can implement.

Evidence-based: Quality podcasts cite research and data rather than hot takes.

Clear explanations: Finance jargon made accessible without dumbing down concepts.

Diverse perspectives: Multiple viewpoints help you form your own investment philosophy.

Consistent quality: Regular episodes with high standards for research and production.

How to Learn from Finance Podcasts

Take notes: Write down key concepts, ticker symbols, and book recommendations.

Verify claims: Don't take investment advice at face value; do your own research.

Diversify sources: Listen to multiple perspectives to avoid echo chambers.

Focus on principles: Learn frameworks and principles over specific stock picks.

Apply what you learn: Use podcasts to inform your financial decisions and strategy.

Common Finance Podcast Topics

Market analysis: Weekly or daily market recaps and trend analysis

Investing strategies: Value investing, index investing, dividend strategies, trading

Personal finance: Budgeting, debt management, savings strategies

Financial independence: FIRE movement, early retirement, passive income

Real estate: Property investing, rental strategies, real estate markets

Behavioral finance: Psychology of investing, avoiding mistakes, decision-making

Cryptocurrency: Bitcoin, Ethereum, blockchain, digital assets

Red Flags to Watch For

Not all finance podcasts are created equal. Watch for:

Too many "hot tips": Quality shows teach principles, not just stock picks.

Lack of disclosure: Hosts should disclose conflicts of interest and holdings.

Guarantees: No legitimate financial expert guarantees returns.

Overly complex jargon: Good educators explain clearly, not to confuse.
