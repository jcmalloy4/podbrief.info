Best Finance Podcasts 2026 - Investing, Money & Business

Published February 24, 2026

Finance podcasts can transform your relationship with money, teaching you about investing, personal finance, economics, and wealth building. Whether you're just starting your financial journey or managing significant wealth, these are the best finance podcasts of 2026.

What Makes a Great Finance Podcast?

  • Credible experts: Hosts with real financial expertise and track records
  • Actionable insights: Practical advice you can apply to your finances
  • Balanced perspective: Multiple viewpoints on complex financial topics
  • Current market coverage: Up-to-date analysis of economic trends

Top Finance Podcasts in 2026

1. All-In Podcast

The "besties" — Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, and David Friedberg — discuss markets, tech, and policy. All-In offers insider perspectives from successful investors and entrepreneurs.

Best for: Tech investing and macro analysis

2. Planet Money

NPR's Planet Money makes economics entertaining and accessible. From the origin of money to modern financial crises, they explain complex topics through compelling stories.

Best for: Economics education

3. The Dave Ramsey Show

Debt elimination, budgeting, and building wealth through proven principles. Dave Ramsey has helped millions get out of debt and take control of their finances.

Best for: Debt elimination and budgeting basics

4. BiggerPockets Money Podcast

Financial independence and real estate investing from the BiggerPockets team. Interviews with people at every stage of their FI journey provide relatable insights.

Best for: Financial independence and real estate

5. Invest Like the Best

Patrick O'Shaughnessy interviews top investors, CEOs, and thinkers. Invest Like the Best offers deep dives into investment philosophy and business strategy.

Best for: Investment philosophy

6. The Indicator from Planet Money

Daily 10-minute economics explainers from NPR. The Indicator breaks down one economic idea, trend, or news story each day.

Best for: Daily economic news

7. Acquired

Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal dive deep into company histories and business strategies. Acquired episodes run long (often 3+ hours) but provide unmatched depth.

Best for: Business history and strategy

8. ChooseFI

The financial independence movement explained through interviews and tactical advice. ChooseFI makes early retirement and financial freedom accessible.

Best for: FIRE movement strategies

9. The Motley Fool Money

Weekly stock market analysis and investment ideas from The Motley Fool team. Entertaining discussions mixed with serious investment research.

Best for: Stock market analysis

10. How I Built This

Guy Raz interviews founders about building iconic companies. How I Built This focuses on entrepreneurship but offers valuable financial lessons.

Best for: Entrepreneurship and business building

Finding Your Perfect Finance Podcast

For beginners: Start with Planet Money for economics basics

For debt elimination: The Dave Ramsey Show provides step-by-step guidance

For investing: All-In or Invest Like the Best offer advanced perspectives

For real estate: BiggerPockets specializes in property investing

For daily updates: The Indicator delivers economics news in 10 minutes

For entrepreneurs: How I Built This and Acquired tell business stories

Learning Finance Through Podcasts

Podcasts are one of the best ways to build financial literacy. You can learn during your commute, while exercising, or doing chores. The key is consistency — listening regularly compounds your knowledge just like compound interest grows your wealth.
