Best Finance Podcasts (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

Whether you're learning to budget, building an investment portfolio, or tracking market trends, finance podcasts deliver expert insights on money, investing, and wealth building. These shows turn complex financial topics into actionable knowledge.

Top Finance Podcasts

1. All-In Podcast

Four successful tech investors (Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg) discuss markets, technology, economics, and politics with insider perspectives and no filter.

Best for: Tech investing, macro trends, market analysis

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

Browse All-In summaries →

2. Planet Money

NPR's economics podcast makes finance entertaining through storytelling. Learn economic principles through real-world examples.

Best for: Economics fundamentals, accessible learning

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

3. Acquired

Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal analyze the business and financial strategies behind the world's greatest companies. Deep dives into Apple, Amazon, Nvidia, and more.

Best for: Business strategy, company analysis

Episode length: 2-4 hours

4. The Dave Ramsey Show

Dave Ramsey delivers straight-talking debt elimination and personal finance advice through listener calls and clear principles.

Best for: Debt payoff, personal finance basics

Episode length: 180 minutes (daily)

5. BiggerPockets Money

Financial independence and wealth building through real estate, investing, and side hustles. Practical advice for building wealth.

Best for: FIRE movement, wealth building

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

6. The Indicator from Planet Money

Daily 10-minute episodes explaining one economic indicator or financial concept. Perfect for busy schedules.

Best for: Quick daily finance learning

Episode length: 10 minutes

7. Motley Fool Money

Stock market analysis, investing advice, and company earnings discussions from The Motley Fool team.

Best for: Stock market investing

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

8. We Study Billionaires

The Investor's Podcast Network dives deep into value investing, studying Warren Buffett and other successful investors.

Best for: Value investing, investor mindsets

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

9. Afford Anything

Paula Pant explores financial independence, real estate investing, and life optimization. "You can afford anything, but not everything."

Best for: Financial independence, life design

Episode length: 45-75 minutes

10. Animal Spirits

Ben Carlson and Michael Batnick discuss markets, behavioral finance, and investor psychology with humor and insight.

Best for: Behavioral finance, market commentary

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

What Makes a Great Finance Podcast?

The best finance shows share these qualities:

  • Actionable advice: Practical steps you can take
  • Clear explanations: Complex topics made simple
  • No conflicts of interest: Transparent about affiliations
  • Diverse perspectives: Different investing philosophies
  • Long-term focus: Building wealth, not quick schemes
  • Regular updates: Current market commentary

Finance Podcast Strategies

Build Financial Literacy

Start with beginner-friendly shows like Planet Money and The Indicator to build foundational knowledge.

Match Your Goals

Investing in stocks? Focus on We Study Billionaires. Want financial independence? Choose BiggerPockets Money.

Balance Entertainment & Education

Mix analytical shows like Acquired with lighter, story-driven shows like Planet Money.

Take Action

Don't just listen—implement advice. Open that investment account, create that budget, start that side hustle.

Important Disclaimers

  • Podcasts are educational, not personalized financial advice
  • Do your own research before investing
  • Consider consulting a financial advisor for major decisions
  • Past performance doesn't guarantee future results
  • Different strategies work for different people

Getting Started

New to finance podcasts? Try these episodes:

  • Planet Money: "The Indicator" daily episodes (start anywhere)
  • All-In: "E1-100 Recaps" for recent market analysis
  • Acquired: "Nvidia" episode (recent hit)
  • The Dave Ramsey Show: Any "Debt-Free Scream" episode
