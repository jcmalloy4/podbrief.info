All-In Podcast vs My First Million 2026

Published March 10, 2026

All-In Podcast and My First Million are two of the most popular business podcasts, but they serve different audiences. All-In focuses on tech, investing, and macro trends with four successful investors. My First Million breaks down business ideas and entrepreneurial tactics with Sam Parr and Shaan Puri. Which one should you listen to?

Quick Comparison

Feature All-In Podcast My First Million
Hosts Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg Sam Parr & Shaan Puri
Focus Tech, investing, macro trends, politics Business ideas, entrepreneurship, tactics
Length 90-150 minutes 60-90 minutes
Frequency Weekly 2-3x per week
Best For Investors, tech leaders, macro thinkers Entrepreneurs, side hustlers, idea people

All-In Podcast Overview

Hosts: Chamath Palihapitiya (Social Capital), Jason Calacanis (angel investor), David Sacks (Craft Ventures), David Friedberg (Production Board)

What They Cover

  • Tech Trends: AI, crypto, SaaS, emerging technologies
  • Investing: Public markets, venture capital, IPOs, SPACs
  • Politics & Policy: Elections, regulations, geopolitics
  • Economics: Inflation, interest rates, Fed policy
  • Science: Climate, biotech, energy, food tech

Strengths

  • High-level insights from successful investors with real track records
  • Debates between four smart people with different perspectives
  • Coverage of macro trends that shape markets and society
  • Inside Silicon Valley perspective on tech and startups
  • Data-driven analysis with nuanced takes

Who Should Listen

  • Investors (public markets, VC, or angel)
  • Tech startup founders and executives
  • People interested in macro trends and policy
  • Those who enjoy intellectual debates
  • Anyone wanting to understand where tech/markets are heading

My First Million Overview

Hosts: Sam Parr (founder of The Hustle, sold to HubSpot) and Shaan Puri (ex-Twitch, Bebo, Monkey Inferno)

What They Cover

  • Business Ideas: Breakdown of companies, trends, and opportunities
  • Entrepreneurship: How to start, grow, and sell businesses
  • Marketing Tactics: Growth strategies that actually work
  • Creator Economy: Building audiences and monetizing
  • Personal Stories: How Sam and Shaan built their businesses

Strengths

  • Actionable business ideas you can actually steal and execute
  • Entertaining chemistry between Sam and Shaan
  • Focus on small-to-mid-size businesses (not just billion-dollar unicorns)
  • Marketing and growth tactics you can use immediately
  • More frequent episodes (2-3x per week)

Who Should Listen

  • Aspiring entrepreneurs looking for business ideas
  • Small business owners seeking growth tactics
  • Side hustlers and solopreneurs
  • Content creators building audiences
  • Anyone who loves brainstorming business opportunities

Key Differences

1. Scope & Scale

All-In: Focuses on billion-dollar companies, public markets, and macro trends. Discussion of policy, geopolitics, and how big systems work.

MFM: Focuses on smaller businesses ($1M-$100M), specific tactics, and opportunities accessible to normal people.

2. Actionability

All-In: Insights for understanding trends, but not always directly actionable unless you're investing millions.

MFM: Highly actionable—specific business ideas, marketing tactics, and frameworks you can use today.

3. Tone & Style

All-In: Serious, debate-focused, intellectual. Can get heated when hosts disagree on politics or policy.

MFM: Casual, entertaining, high-energy. Feels like listening to two friends brainstorming over coffee.

4. Time Commitment

All-In: Weekly 2+ hour episodes. Requires focus and time.

MFM: 2-3 episodes per week, 60-90 minutes each. More content, shorter episodes.

5. Political Content

All-In: Significant political and policy discussion, especially elections, regulations, and geopolitics.

MFM: Minimal politics—stays focused on business and entrepreneurship.

Topic Overlap

Both shows sometimes cover similar ground:

  • Tech trends (AI, crypto)
  • Startup stories and funding rounds
  • Business strategies and models
  • Creator economy and personal brands

But they approach these topics differently—All-In from an investor/macro lens, MFM from an operator/builder lens.

Which Podcast Should You Choose?

Listen to All-In if you:

  • Are investing in public markets or venture capital
  • Want to understand macro trends shaping tech and society
  • Enjoy intellectual debates and different perspectives
  • Work at a tech company or startup at a senior level
  • Care about policy, politics, and how they affect business

Listen to My First Million if you:

  • Want specific business ideas to launch or copy
  • Are building a small-to-mid-size business
  • Need marketing and growth tactics
  • Enjoy brainstorming and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Prefer actionable content over macro analysis

Listen to Both if you:

  • Want both macro trends and tactical execution
  • Are a founder who also invests
  • Have time for 3-4 episodes per week
  • Enjoy diverse perspectives on business and tech

Popular Episodes

All-In Best Episodes

  • AI debates with detailed technical discussion
  • Election coverage and political analysis
  • Fed policy and economic predictions
  • Crypto bull/bear debates

MFM Best Episodes

  • Business breakdown series (how companies grew)
  • Failed business confessions
  • Idea generation episodes (10 business ideas)
  • Interviews with successful entrepreneurs

The Verdict

Both are excellent, but they serve different needs:

All-In: Best for understanding where the world is going—macro trends, investing, tech shifts, policy. Top-down thinking.

My First Million: Best for figuring out what to build next—specific ideas, tactics, execution. Bottom-up thinking.

The ideal combination? Listen to All-In to understand the landscape, then listen to MFM to figure out what to build within that landscape.
