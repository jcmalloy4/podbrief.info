# Best Marketing Podcasts in 2026

Whether you're a marketer, entrepreneur, or business owner, staying current with marketing trends and tactics is essential. These marketing podcasts deliver expert insights, case studies, and strategies from industry leaders who are actually doing the work.

## Top Marketing Podcasts

### 1. Marketing Against the Grain
Kipp Bodnar (HubSpot CMO) and Kieran Flanagan (SVP Marketing at HubSpot) discuss marketing strategy, growth tactics, and industry trends with insider perspectives from leading a major marketing platform.

**Why listen:** Insider HubSpot insights, modern marketing strategy, entertaining banter.

### 2. My First Million
Sam Parr and Shaan Puri discuss business ideas, marketing tactics, and entrepreneurship. While broader than pure marketing, MFM delivers the best marketing ideation and growth strategy content in podcasting.

**Why listen:** Creative marketing ideas, growth tactics, entrepreneurial thinking.

### 3. Marketing School
Neil Patel and Eric Siu deliver daily 5-10 minute marketing lessons covering SEO, content marketing, social media, and growth tactics. Bite-sized, actionable advice every day.

**Why listen:** Daily marketing tips, SEO and content focus, quick actionable lessons.

### 4. The Marketing Millennials
Daniel Murray explores modern marketing with a focus on social media, content creation, and emerging platforms. Particularly strong on TikTok, Instagram, and creator economy trends.

**Why listen:** Social media marketing, creator economy insights, modern platform tactics.

### 5. Everyone Hates Marketers
Louis Grenier helps marketers create better campaigns by learning what actually works (and calling out what doesn't). Tactical, no-BS marketing advice with guest interviews.

**Why listen:** Practical marketing tactics, honest assessments, cutting through hype.

### 6. The Growth Show (HubSpot)
HubSpot's podcast features interviews with marketing and sales leaders about growth strategies, customer acquisition, and scaling businesses.

**Why listen:** Growth strategy insights, customer acquisition tactics, scaling advice.

### 7. The GaryVee Audio Experience
Gary Vaynerchuk discusses social media marketing, branding, entrepreneurship, and attention economics. High-energy insights on modern marketing and platform trends.

**Why listen:** Social media insights, personal branding, attention strategy.

### 8. Akimbo
Seth Godin explores marketing, culture, and making change happen. Short, philosophical episodes that challenge conventional marketing thinking.

**Why listen:** Marketing philosophy, cultural insights, thought-provoking perspectives.

### 9. Masters of Scale
Reid Hoffman interviews founders about scaling companies, including deep dives into marketing and growth strategies that powered major companies.

**Why listen:** Scaling insights from successful founders, growth strategy case studies.

### 10. Social Pros Podcast
Jay Baer and guests discuss social media marketing strategy, platform updates, and corporate social media management. Focused on practical social media tactics.

**Why listen:** Social media strategy, platform updates, corporate marketing insights.

### 11. The Smart Passive Income Podcast
Pat Flynn shares online business and marketing strategies, with a focus on passive income, content marketing, and audience building.

**Why listen:** Content marketing, audience building, online business tactics.

### 12. Demand Gen Visionaries
B2B marketing leaders discuss demand generation, marketing automation, and growth strategies. Particularly valuable for SaaS and tech marketers.

**Why listen:** B2B marketing insights, demand gen tactics, marketing automation.

### 13. The Hustle Daily Show
Sam Parr and the HubSpot team discuss business news with marketing insights, trends analysis, and entrepreneurial thinking. Daily newsletter companion podcast.

**Why listen:** Marketing trends, business news analysis, quick daily insights.

### 14. Online Marketing Made Easy
Amy Porterfield teaches digital marketing courses and shares marketing tactics focused on course creation, email marketing, and digital products.

**Why listen:** Digital product marketing, email tactics, course creation strategies.

### 15. The Tim Ferriss Show
While not purely marketing-focused, Tim's interviews with entrepreneurs often reveal marketing insights and growth strategies from world-class operators.

**Why listen:** Growth strategy insights, entrepreneurial marketing, world-class tactics.

## What Makes a Great Marketing Podcast?

**Actionable insights:** The best shows provide tactics you can implement immediately.

**Current trends:** Marketing changes fast; quality shows stay on top of platform updates.

**Real examples:** Case studies and real campaigns beat abstract theory.

**Diverse perspectives:** Learning from different marketing approaches prevents echo chambers.

**Honest assessments:** Great marketing podcasts admit failures and call out what doesn't work.

## Key Marketing Podcast Topics

**SEO and organic growth:** Keyword research, content strategy, link building, technical SEO

**Social media marketing:** Platform strategies, content creation, influencer marketing, community building

**Content marketing:** Blogging, video, podcasting, content distribution, storytelling

**Email marketing:** List building, automation, copywriting, segmentation

**Paid advertising:** Facebook Ads, Google Ads, creative testing, attribution

**Growth marketing:** Experimentation, funnel optimization, retention, viral loops

**Branding:** Positioning, messaging, brand voice, storytelling

**Analytics:** Data analysis, attribution, conversion optimization, metrics

## How to Learn from Marketing Podcasts

**Take notes:** Write down specific tactics, tools, and campaigns to explore.

**Test tactics:** The best way to learn is by implementing what you hear.

**Follow the experts:** Note which guests and hosts resonate, then follow their work.

**Stay skeptical:** What works for one company may not work for yours.

**Join communities:** Many podcasts have communities where you can discuss tactics.

## Common Marketing Mistakes to Avoid

**Chasing every new tactic:** Focus on fundamentals before jumping to the latest hack.

**Copying without adapting:** What works for big brands often doesn't work for startups.

**Ignoring metrics:** Track results to know what's actually working.

**Platform dependence:** Build owned channels (email, website) alongside rented ones (social).

**Neglecting fundamentals:** SEO, email, and content marketing still drive results.
