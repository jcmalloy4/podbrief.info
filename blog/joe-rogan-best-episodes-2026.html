Joe Rogan Best Episodes 2026: Essential Listening Guide

Published March 11, 2026 | Podcasts & Entertainment

The Most Popular Podcast in the World

The Joe Rogan Experience has over 2,000 episodes spanning comedy, science, politics, sports, and everything in between. Where do you start? These are the episodes that define JRE.

Best Science & Health Episodes

1. Dr. Andrew Huberman (Multiple Episodes)

Stanford neuroscientist Dr. Huberman breaks down optimization protocols for sleep, focus, fitness, and mental health. Every episode is packed with actionable science.

Start with: Episode #1513 (First appearance, covers everything)

Browse Joe Rogan + Huberman episodes →

2. Dr. Rhonda Patrick (Multiple Episodes)

Nutrition, longevity, and health optimization. Rhonda brings the research and makes it practical. Dense with information.

Standout episode: #1474 (Sauna, nutrition, immune health)

3. Dr. Peter Attia (Multiple Episodes)

Longevity doctor discussing how to live longer and better. Exercise, nutrition, sleep, and metabolic health.

Start with: #1513 (Comprehensive health overview)

4. Dr. Matthew Walker (#1109, #1109)

Sleep scientist author of "Why We Sleep." Life-changing information about sleep's impact on health, performance, and longevity.

Essential listening for: Anyone who sleeps (so everyone)

Best Comedy Episodes

5. Joey Diaz (Multiple Episodes)

Uncle Joey's stories are legendary. Hilarious, raw, and completely unfiltered tales from a life fully lived.

Fan favorite: #1435 (Peak Diaz energy)

6. Bill Burr (Multiple Episodes)

One of the best comedians working today. Bill and Joe's chemistry is perfect—they riff on everything.

Start anywhere: Every Burr episode is gold

7. Bert Kreischer (Multiple Episodes)

The Machine himself. Bert's storytelling and infectious laughter make for consistently entertaining episodes.

8. Tom Segura & Christina P (Multiple Episodes)

Comedy couple with great chemistry with Joe. Always funny, often absurd.

Best Intellectual Conversations

9. Elon Musk (#1169, #1470)

The most famous JRE moments. Elon discussing Tesla, SpaceX, AI, and yes, smoking weed on camera.

Cultural moment: #1169 (The meme-generating episode)

10. Sam Harris (Multiple Episodes)

Philosopher and neuroscientist discussing consciousness, free will, meditation, and making sense of the world.

For deep thinkers: #1658 (Free will debate)

11. Naval Ravikant (#1309)

Angel investor and philosopher. Wealth creation, happiness, reading, and how to think better.

Life advice goldmine: One of the most re-listened episodes ever

12. Jordan Peterson (Multiple Episodes)

Clinical psychologist discussing meaning, responsibility, mythology, and psychology. Love him or hate him, these are substantive conversations.

Start with: #1208 (First appearance, foundational ideas)

Best Athlete Episodes

13. Mike Tyson (Multiple Episodes)

Iron Mike discussing boxing, his life's journey, philosophy, and redemption. Surprisingly deep and thoughtful.

14. Lance Armstrong (#1173, #1382)

Controversial but fascinating. Lance discusses cycling, doping, cancer, and his complicated legacy.

15. David Goggins (Multiple Episodes)

Ultra-endurance athlete and motivational force of nature. These episodes will make you want to run through a wall.

For motivation: #1212 (Peak Goggins intensity)

16. Alex Honnold (#1188, #1423)

Free solo climber discussing fear, risk, and doing the impossible. Calm demeanor discussing terrifying feats.

18. Edward Snowden (#1368)

19. Tulsi Gabbard (Multiple Episodes)

How to Navigate 2,000+ Episodes

Start With Your Interests

Pick a category that resonates:

  • Want to get healthier: Huberman, Rhonda Patrick, Peter Attia
  • Want to laugh: Joey Diaz, Bill Burr, Bert Kreischer
  • Want to think: Sam Harris, Naval, Jordan Peterson
  • Want motivation: David Goggins, Jocko Willink

Check Guest Credentials

Look for guests who are:

  • Actual experts (PhDs, practitioners, athletes)
  • Doing interesting things currently
  • Have been on multiple times (Joe re-invites the best guests)

Use Episode Length Strategically

Most JRE episodes are 2-3 hours:

  • Long commute: Perfect fit
  • Workout: Break it into segments
  • Work session: Background for deep work
  • Short on time: Maybe not JRE's day

Speed Up Playback

1.2x-1.5x speed makes 3-hour episodes more manageable without losing comprehension.

Why JRE Works So Well

Long-Form Format

2-3 hours lets conversations go deep. Guests relax, drop their guard, and have real conversations.

Joe's Curiosity

Joe asks questions regular people want answered. He's genuinely interested, not performing.

Diverse Guests

One day it's a physicist, next day a comedian, then a UFC fighter. You discover new interests.

No Corporate Filter

Say what you want about Joe's opinions—the show is authentically unfiltered. People speak freely.

Common Criticisms & How to Approach Them

"Joe platforms controversial people"

True. But long-form conversation lets you form your own opinion. You're not required to agree with every guest.

"Episodes are too long"

Listen in segments. Or use 1.5x speed. Or pick shorter interviews (some are 90 minutes).

"Joe spreads misinformation"

He's not a journalist, he's a comedian having conversations. Verify claims that matter to you.

"It's bro culture"

Yes, Joe loves MMA, hunting, and comedy. But he also features scientists, intellectuals, and diverse voices. Cherry-pick what interests you.
