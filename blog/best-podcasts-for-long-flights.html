Best Podcasts for Long Flights: 15 Shows to Pass the Time (2026)

Engaging, entertaining podcasts that make hours fly by—literally

Updated February 25, 2026

Long flights are perfect for podcast binge-listening. With hours of uninterrupted time and limited entertainment options, the right podcasts can transform a tedious journey into productive or deeply entertaining experience. Here are the best podcasts to download before your next flight.

What Makes a Great Flight Podcast?

  • Deeply engaging – Captures attention without requiring intense focus
  • Long-form content – Multi-hour episodes or binge-worthy series
  • Downloadable – Works offline (airplane mode)
  • Not too complex – Can follow even when drowsy or distracted

Best True Crime Podcasts for Flights

Serial

The podcast that started the true crime boom. Season 1 is perfect for a long flight—12 episodes investigating a single murder case. Incredibly addictive storytelling.

Flight time coverage: ~12 hours (entire Season 1)

S-Town

7-episode masterpiece from the creators of Serial. Starts as a murder mystery, becomes something much deeper. Stunning storytelling and production.

Flight time coverage: ~7 hours (complete series)

Crime Junkie

Consistent, well-produced true crime stories. Each episode is standalone (30-60 min), so you can listen to as many as your flight allows. Download 5-10 episodes for a transatlantic flight.

Best Comedy Podcasts for Flights

SmartLess

Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes interview celebrities. Light, funny, and perfect for keeping your mood up during a long flight. Download a season for continuous entertainment.

View SmartLess episodes on PodBrief →

Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

Conan's wit makes any flight better. Celebrity interviews with genuine laughs. Download 5-6 episodes for a cross-country flight.

How Did This Get Made?

Comedians dissect terrible movies. Hilarious, no visual required (perfect for flights). Each episode is standalone, so download favorites from their extensive catalog.

Best Story-Based Podcasts

This American Life

Award-winning storytelling. Each episode features multiple stories around a theme. 800+ episodes to choose from—download 6-8 for a long flight.

Radiolab

Science and philosophy through storytelling. Engaging production and fascinating topics. Episodes are 30-60 minutes—perfect flight-sized chunks.

The Moth

True stories told live on stage. Episodes are short (10-20 min individual stories), making them perfect for napping between stories on a red-eye.

Best Educational Podcasts for Flights

Hardcore History

Dan Carlin's epic, multi-hour deep dives into history. A single episode can cover your entire flight—some run 4-6 hours. Immersive historical storytelling.

Huberman Lab

Neuroscience and health optimization. Episodes are 1.5-3 hours of deep dives into sleep, focus, fitness, and brain function. Learn something valuable while you fly.

Stuff You Should Know

Two guys explain random topics. Engaging, educational, and entertaining. Download 8-10 episodes for variety during a long flight.

Flight-Length Podcast Guides

Short Flight (1-3 hours)

  • 3-4 episodes of Crime Junkie (30-60 min each)
  • 1-2 episodes of The Tim Ferriss Show
  • 4-6 episodes of The Moth

Medium Flight (4-7 hours)

  • Complete Serial Season 1 (12 episodes, ~12 hours—start it!)
  • 2-3 Joe Rogan episodes (6-9 hours)
  • Complete S-Town series (~7 hours)
  • 1 Hardcore History episode (~5 hours) + 2 SmartLess episodes

Long International Flight (8-15 hours)

  • Serial Season 1 + S-Town (~20 hours total, covers entire trip)
  • 4-5 Joe Rogan episodes (~12-15 hours)
  • 2 Hardcore History episodes + some SmartLess for laughs
  • Mix: 10 Crime Junkie + 5 Stuff You Should Know + 3 Conan episodes

Pre-Flight Podcast Tips

  • Download everything on WiFi – Don't rely on airplane WiFi for streaming
  • Download 2x what you need – Delays happen, and you might not want to listen to what you planned
  • Mix genres – Comedy, true crime, education—mood changes during long flights
  • Bring backup entertainment – Podcasts + music + audiobooks for variety
  • Test your headphones – Make sure they work before takeoff
  • Charge everything – Phone, headphones, battery pack
