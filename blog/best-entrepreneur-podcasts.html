Best Entrepreneur Podcasts: 15 Shows for Startup Founders & Business Builders (2026)

Published February 28, 2026 • 14 min read

Building a business? These podcasts deliver ideas, tactics, and lessons from successful entrepreneurs. From bootstrapping to raising millions, here are the essential shows for founders in 2026.

💡 Best Podcasts for Business Ideas

1. My First Million

Hosts: Shaan Puri, Sam Parr

Length: 45-60 min

Frequency: 2-3x per week

The idea generation machine. Every episode features fascinating businesses, brainstorming sessions, and opportunities you could pursue. Energetic, inspiring, tactical.

Best for: Finding your next business idea

Vibe: "Holy shit we should do this!" energy

Compare to All-In Podcast →

2. Indie Hackers

Host: Courtland Allen

Length: 30-60 min

Bootstrapped founders share how they built profitable businesses without VC funding. Real revenue numbers, real tactics, no BS.

Best for: Bootstrappers and solo founders

Focus: SaaS, online businesses, indie products

📖 Best Founder Story Podcasts

3. How I Built This

Host: Guy Raz (NPR)

Length: 45-60 min

The iconic founder interview show. Airbnb, Spanx, Instagram, Warby Parker—founders tell the real story of building their companies. Inspiring and educational.

Best for: Inspiration and resilience lessons

Companies covered: Household names + hidden gems

4. Acquired

Hosts: Ben Gilbert, David Rosenthal

Length: 2-4 hours

Epic deep dives into the greatest companies ever built. NVIDIA, Microsoft, Costco, Disney—complete company histories with MBA-level analysis.

Best for: Understanding business strategy at scale

Learn: Decision-making, pivots, competitive moats

Browse Acquired briefs →

5. The Foundr Podcast

Host: Nathan Chan

Length: 45-60 min

Successful entrepreneurs share how they started and scaled. Tactical advice mixed with inspiration.

Best for: Early-stage founders looking for playbooks

🚀 Best Startup/VC Podcasts

6. All-In Podcast

Hosts: Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg

Length: 90-120 min

Four tech billionaires debate markets, tech, and startups. Insider perspective on Silicon Valley, fundraising, and tech trends.

Best for: Understanding the VC/startup ecosystem

Topics: Markets, AI, tech policy, investment landscape

Browse All-In briefs →

7. This Week in Startups

Host: Jason Calacanis

Length: 60-90 min

Weekly startup news, founder interviews, and VC insights. Jason's been angel investing for 20+ years (Uber, Robinhood early investor).

Best for: Weekly startup ecosystem pulse

8. 20VC (20 Minute VC)

Host: Harry Stebbings

Length: 30-60 min

VC and founder interviews. How to raise money, build teams, scale companies. Harry's built relationships with top VCs and founders.

Best for: Fundraising insights and VC thinking

🎯 Best Marketing & Growth Podcasts for Entrepreneurs

9. Marketing Against the Grain

Hosts: Kipp Bodnar (HubSpot CMO), Kieran Flanagan

Length: 30-45 min

Marketing trends, growth tactics, and contrarian takes. These guys run marketing at scale and share what actually works.

Best for: Growth and marketing strategy

10. The Growth TLDRNewsletter Podcast

Length: 20-30 min

Quick hits on growth tactics from successful companies. What they did, how it worked, what you can apply.

Best for: Actionable growth tactics

💼 Best Business Strategy Podcasts

11. The Prof G Pod

Host: Scott Galloway (NYU professor, entrepreneur)

Length: 50-70 min

Business strategy, tech trends, and market analysis. Galloway's sharp, entertaining, and brutally honest. Breaks down what big companies are doing right/wrong.

Best for: Strategic thinking and market analysis

12. Invest Like the Best

Host: Patrick O'Shaughnessy

Length: 60-90 min

Investors, operators, and thinkers share their frameworks. Not just startups—also investing, decision-making, and systems thinking.

Best for: Mental models and frameworks

🛠️ Best Tactical/Execution Podcasts

13. The SaaS Podcast

Host: Omer Khan

Length: 40-60 min

SaaS founders share how they got from 0 to product-market fit to growth. Tactical, honest, detailed.

Best for: SaaS founders at any stage

14. Startups For the Rest of Us

Hosts: Rob Walling, Mike Taber

Length: 35-50 min

Bootstrap-focused entrepreneurship. Two experienced founders sharing lessons and tactics for building profitable businesses.

Best for: Bootstrappers avoiding the VC path

🧠 Best Personal Development for Entrepreneurs

15. The Tim Ferriss Show

Host: Tim Ferriss

Length: 90-150 min

World-class performers (entrepreneurs, athletes, artists) share their routines, tactics, and mental models. Less about specific business tactics, more about high performance.

Best for: Life optimization and mental models

Entrepreneurs covered: Naval Ravikant, Reid Hoffman, Marc Andreessen, many more

🎯 Choosing the Right Podcast for Your Stage

💭 Idea Stage (Pre-Launch)

Listen to:

  • My First Million (idea generation)
  • Indie Hackers (bootstrapped validation stories)
  • How I Built This (inspiration)

Goal: Find ideas, validate markets, build confidence

🛠️ Building Stage (0 to First Customers)

Listen to:

  • The SaaS Podcast (tactical execution)
  • Indie Hackers (0 to $10k MRR stories)
  • Startups For the Rest of Us (bootstrap tactics)

Goal: Execution, customer acquisition, product-market fit

📈 Growth Stage (Scaling)

Listen to:

  • Marketing Against the Grain (growth tactics)
  • All-In Podcast (market trends)
  • Acquired (company case studies)

Goal: Scale, hire, systems, strategy

💰 Fundraising Stage

Listen to:

  • 20VC (VC perspectives)
  • This Week in Startups (fundraising insights)
  • All-In Podcast (investor thinking)

Goal: Understand VC mindset, pitch better, negotiate terms

📚 Sample Weekly Entrepreneur Listening Plan

Monday Morning (Start Week Inspired)

How I Built This (45 min) - Founder story during commute

Tuesday (Idea + Tactics)

My First Million (60 min) - New ideas during workout/commute

Wednesday (Growth Focus)

Marketing Against the Grain (40 min) - Growth tactics mid-week

Thursday (Market Context)

This Week in Startups (70 min) - Ecosystem news during commute

Friday (Strategic Thinking)

The Prof G Pod (60 min) - Big picture strategy to end week

Weekend (Deep Dive)

Saturday: Acquired (3 hours) - Epic company deep dive during long walk/chores

Sunday: The Tim Ferriss Show (2 hours) - Performance optimization

Total: ~8-10 hours/week of entrepreneur education

💡 How to Get Maximum Value

Take Notes

  • Keep a "business ideas" note: Add every interesting idea you hear
  • Track tactics: Write down specific growth tactics to test
  • Capture frameworks: Mental models from investors and founders

Apply Immediately

  • One tactic per week: Listen to growth podcast → test one tactic
  • Share with team: Discuss episodes in team meetings
  • Reach out to guests: Many podcast guests are accessible on Twitter/LinkedIn

Follow the Guests

  • Guest appears on multiple shows → search their other appearances
  • Follow on Twitter/LinkedIn for ongoing insights
  • Read their books/newsletters

🔥 Hidden Gem Entrepreneur Podcasts

  • The Small Business Big Marketing Podcast: Practical marketing for small businesses
  • eCommerce Influence: Building online stores and eCommerce brands
  • The $100 MBA: Quick daily business lessons (10 min)
  • Business Wars: Dramatic retellings of business rivalries (Nike vs Adidas, Netflix vs Blockbuster)
  • Masters of Scale: Reid Hoffman (LinkedIn founder) interviews entrepreneurs about scaling

For: Solo founders building profitable businesses without VC

Conclusion

Entrepreneur podcasts are like having the world's best business advisors in your pocket. While you commute, work out, or do chores, you're absorbing lessons that cost others years to learn.

Start with these three (covers all bases):

  1. My First Million - Ideas and inspiration (3x/week, 60 min)
  2. How I Built This - Founder stories (weekly, 45 min)
  3. Marketing Against the Grain - Growth tactics (weekly, 40 min)

Then add stage-specific podcasts as you grow. The average entrepreneur spends 5-7 hours/week in transit or doing chores—turn that time into your competitive advantage.
