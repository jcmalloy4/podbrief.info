# Best My First Million Episodes: Must-Listen Business Ideas & Interviews

My First Million, hosted by Shaan Puri and Sam Parr, delivers some of the best business idea breakdowns and entrepreneurial insights in podcasting. Here are the episodes that stand out.

## Top Episodes by Category

### Business Idea Goldmines

**"The Milk Road Breakdown"**
Shaan and Sam break down how they built and sold The Milk Road, a crypto newsletter, for millions. Pure tactical gold on building and selling digital assets.

**"Brainstorming Million-Dollar Business Ideas"**
Classic MFM format - rapid-fire business ideas ranging from the practical to the wild. Several listeners have actually built businesses from ideas in this episode.

**"Billion Dollar App Ideas"**
The hosts dissect overlooked app opportunities and business models. Great for anyone looking to build in the app space.

### Founder Interviews

**"How Steph Smith Built a Million-Dollar Newsletter"**
Deep dive into how Steph Smith built her newsletter business, including specific tactics for growth and monetization.

**"The Hustle's Story with Sam Parr"**
Sam tells the full story of building and selling The Hustle for $27M. Candid, tactical, and inspiring.

**"Neville Medhora on Copywriting Millions"**
One of the best copywriting breakdowns you'll ever hear. Practical frameworks you can use immediately.

### Wealth-Building Strategies

**"How to Get Rich Without Getting Lucky"**
Breaking down Naval Ravikant's famous tweetstorm with practical applications.

**"Making Money While You Sleep"**
Passive income strategies that actually work, from people who've done it.

**"The Sweaty Startup"**
Why boring businesses can make you rich. Eye-opening for anyone overlooking "unsexy" opportunities.

### Framework Episodes

**"The 4 Types of Luck"**
How to engineer luck in business and life. Highly actionable framework.

**"The Side Hustle Framework"**
Step-by-step guide to starting a side business while keeping your day job.

**"How to Spot a Trend Before It Blows Up"**
Pattern recognition for business opportunities. Essential listening for entrepreneurs.

## Why These Episodes Work

The best MFM episodes share common traits:

1. **Actionable Ideas** - You can actually build these businesses
2. **Real Numbers** - Shaan and Sam share actual revenue, costs, and metrics
3. **Pattern Recognition** - Teaching you to spot opportunities yourself
4. **Candid Conversations** - No fake guru nonsense, just real talk

## How to Listen

**For Idea Generation:**
Listen to the brainstorming and business breakdown episodes. Keep a notebook ready.

**For Learning:**
Focus on the founder interview episodes. Study the specific tactics and frameworks.

**For Motivation:**
The success story episodes when you need a reminder that it's possible.

## MFM Listening Strategy

1. **Scan summaries first** - Not every episode will be relevant to you
2. **Take notes** - The value is in the implementation
3. **Act on one idea** - Don't just consume, execute
4. **Follow the guests** - Many become regular sources of insight
