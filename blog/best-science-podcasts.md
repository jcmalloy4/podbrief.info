# Best Science Podcasts in 2026

Science podcasts make complex topics accessible, fascinating, and entertaining. From physics and biology to neuroscience and space exploration, these podcasts will expand your understanding of the world. Here are the best science podcasts to feed your curiosity.

## Top Science Podcasts

### 1. Radiolab
**The gold standard for science storytelling.** Radiolab blends cutting-edge science with sound design, philosophy, and human stories. Hosts Lulu Miller and Latif Nasser explore questions that span biology, physics, ethics, and everything in between.

**Why listen:** Innovative sound design, interdisciplinary approach, makes science feel magical.

### 2. Huberman Lab
Dr. Andrew Huberman, a Stanford neuroscientist, delivers deep dives into neuroscience, health, and human performance. Episodes are detailed, actionable, and backed by peer-reviewed research.

**Why listen:** Science-based health advice, expert guests, actionable protocols, free and accessible.

### 3. Ologies with Alie Ward
Alie Ward interviews scientists from every "-ology" you can imagine: from volcanology to thanatology (death), etymology to ornithology. Ologies makes science approachable, fun, and deeply human.

**Why listen:** Covers every scientific field imaginable, hilarious host, makes scientists relatable.

### 4. Science Vs
Wendy Zukerman tackles controversial topics with scientific rigor. Is organic food really better? Do detoxes work? Science Vs separates fact from fiction with humor and thorough research.

**Why listen:** Myth-busting with evidence, fun and accessible, covers trending topics.

### 5. StarTalk
Neil deGrasse Tyson brings astronomy and astrophysics to the masses. StarTalk blends science with pop culture, featuring comedians and celebrity guests alongside deep space discussions.

**Why listen:** Legendary host, makes astrophysics accessible, entertaining format.

### 6. This Podcast Will Kill You
Two disease ecologists explore the history, biology, and impact of diseases. Each episode examines a different disease with scientific depth, dark humor, and cocktail recipes.

**Why listen:** Deep dives into epidemiology, engaging hosts, blend of history and science.

### 7. Science Friday
Ira Flatow's long-running NPR show covers the latest science news, interviews with researchers, and listener questions. Science Friday is your weekly roundup of what's happening in science.

**Why listen:** Trusted source, current science news, accessible explanations.

### 8. The Infinite Monkey Cage
Brian Cox (physicist) and Robin Ince (comedian) host this BBC Radio 4 show blending science with comedy. The panel format features scientists and comedians discussing topics from black holes to consciousness.

**Why listen:** British humor meets serious science, panel format, world-class guests.

### 9. Stuff to Blow Your Mind
Hosts Robert Lamb and Joe McCormick explore science through the lens of culture, history, and the bizarre. Topics range from neuroscience to mythology, always with thorough research.

**Why listen:** Interdisciplinary approach, explores unusual topics, consistent quality.

### 10. Short Wave
NPR's daily science podcast delivers 10-15 minute episodes on current research and discoveries. Short Wave makes science news accessible during your commute.

**Why listen:** Daily updates, bite-sized episodes, covers breaking science news.

### 11. The Skeptics' Guide to the Universe
A panel of science enthusiasts and skeptics discuss the latest science news, pseudoscience, and critical thinking. Hosted by Dr. Steven Novella, this podcast promotes scientific skepticism and rational thinking.

**Why listen:** Critical thinking skills, debunks pseudoscience, weekly science news roundup.

### 12. Planetary Radio
The Planetary Society's podcast covers space exploration, astronomy, and the search for life beyond Earth. Host Mat Kaplan interviews scientists, engineers, and astronauts.

**Why listen:** Space exploration focus, expert interviews, inspiring missions.

## What Makes a Great Science Podcast?

**Accuracy:** The best science podcasts cite research, interview experts, and correct mistakes.

**Accessibility:** Complex topics explained clearly without dumbing down the science.

**Engaging storytelling:** Science is fascinating when presented as stories, not just facts.

**Diverse topics:** From quantum physics to marine biology, variety keeps things interesting.

## Science Podcasts by Interest

**For health and medicine:** Huberman Lab, This Podcast Will Kill You, ZDoggMD

**For space and astronomy:** StarTalk, Planetary Radio, Daniel and Jorge Explain the Universe

**For general science news:** Science Friday, Short Wave, The Naked Scientists

**For deep dives:** Radiolab, Ologies, Stuff to Blow Your Mind

**For skepticism and critical thinking:** The Skeptics' Guide to the Universe, Science Vs

## How to Get the Most from Science Podcasts

**Take notes:** Science podcasts often pack in information worth remembering.

**Follow up:** Many podcasts link to research papers and additional resources.

**Start with topics you love:** Pick an area you're already curious about, then branch out.

**Don't rush:** Complex topics deserve time to sink in; re-listen if needed.
