Best Podcasts to Fall Asleep To (2026)

Can't sleep? The right podcast can be better than any sleep medication. Here are the most effective shows for drifting off, from intentionally boring to wonderfully soothing.

😴 Podcasts Designed for Sleep

Sleep With Me

Host: Drew Ackerman (Dearest Scooter)
Why it works: Intentionally boring bedtime stories that wander aimlessly. Drew's droning voice and pointless tangents are scientifically engineered to bore you to sleep. It's brilliant.

Best for: People who need distraction from racing thoughts

Nothing Much Happens

Host: Kathryn Nicolai
Why it works: Cozy bedtime stories for adults. Each episode tells the same simple story twice—the second time even slower. Kathryn's warm voice is pure ASMR.

Best for: Anxiety, stress, overthinking

Sleepy

Host: Otis Gray
Why it works: Classic literature read in a monotone voice. Think "Pride and Prejudice" but make it a lullaby.

Best for: People who like stories but need them slow and calm

Get Sleepy

Format: Meditation + story
Why it works: Guided relaxation followed by a soothing story. Production quality is excellent, voices are deeply calming.

Best for: People who need help winding down before the story

📖 Soothing Storytelling

LeVar Burton Reads

Host: LeVar Burton
Why it works: Short fiction read by the most soothing voice in the universe. Not designed for sleep, but his voice is magic.

Best for: Gentle, engaging stories that ease you into sleep

The Classic Tales Podcast

Host: BJ Harrison
Why it works: Classic short stories (Poe, Lovecraft, Dickens) read with calm narration. Long enough to fall asleep to.

Best for: Literature lovers who want familiar stories

Myths and Legends

Host: Jason Weiser
Why it works: Folklore and fairy tales told in a soothing, conversational tone. Interesting but not stimulating.

Best for: People who want something mildly engaging but not exciting

🎙️ Calm Conversations

The Slow Show

Format: Slow living, creativity, mindfulness
Why it works: Gentle conversations about intentional living. No drama, no excitement—just calm.

Best for: Evening wind-down, pre-sleep relaxation

Sleeping Forecast

Format: Shipping forecasts read slowly
Why it works: UK shipping forecasts are famously boring and rhythmic. Pure sleep fuel.

Best for: People who find repetition soothing

Meditation Minis

Host: Chel Hamilton
Why it works: 10-minute guided meditations. Short, sweet, effective.

Best for: Quick relaxation before bed

🌊 Nature & ASMR Sounds

Slow Radio

Format: BBC nature soundscapes
Why it works: Pure nature sounds—rain, waves, forests. No talking, just soothing ambiance.

Best for: People who need white noise but prefer nature

ASMR & Chill

Format: Whispered stories, tapping, soft sounds
Why it works: ASMR triggers designed for sleep.

Best for: ASMR lovers

🧘 Meditation & Mindfulness

Daily Meditation Podcast

Host: Mary Meckley
Why it works: Guided meditations that ease you into sleep. Calming voice, no ads mid-episode.

Best for: Nightly meditation routine

Tracks to Relax

Format: Sleep meditations + hypnosis
Why it works: Professionally designed for deep relaxation and sleep induction.

Best for: People who respond well to hypnosis

⚠️ Podcasts to AVOID Before Bed

  • True Crime: Serial killers = bad dreams
  • News/Politics: Stress and anxiety spike
  • Comedy: Laughing wakes you up
  • High-Energy Hosts: Joe Rogan, Lex Fridman—save for daytime
  • Cliffhangers: Serialized dramas that make you want "just one more episode"

🎯 How to Use Sleep Podcasts Effectively

Set a Sleep Timer

Most podcast apps have a sleep timer. Set it for 30-45 minutes so the podcast doesn't play all night.

Start Before You're Tired

Don't wait until you're desperate. Start listening 20-30 minutes before you want to sleep.

Same Show, Same Time

Use the same podcast every night to train your brain. It becomes a sleep cue.

Volume Matters

Keep it low—just loud enough to hear but not loud enough to focus on every word.

No Blue Light

Turn your screen face-down or use a smart speaker. Looking at your phone defeats the purpose.

💤 Best Sleep Podcast by Need

If you love ASMR: ASMR & Chill
