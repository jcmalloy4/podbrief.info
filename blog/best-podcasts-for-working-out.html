Best Podcasts for Working Out: 12 High-Energy Shows (2026)

Ditch the playlist. These high-energy podcasts will keep you motivated through your toughest workouts—from running to lifting to HIIT.

What Makes a Good Workout Podcast?

The best workout podcasts have:

  • High energy: Dynamic hosts with engaging delivery
  • Good pacing: Fast-moving conversation that matches workout intensity
  • Right length: 20-60 minutes (most workout durations)
  • Easy to follow: No complex ideas requiring deep focus
  • Motivating content: Stories, comedy, or debates that keep you going

Avoid: Slow-paced interviews, meditative content, or anything requiring note-taking.

Pro Tips for Workout Podcasts

  • Pre-download episodes: Don't rely on gym WiFi
  • 1.2-1.5x speed: Faster playback = more energy
  • Skip intros/ads: Maintain momentum with the 30-sec skip button
  • Use one earbud: Stay aware of your surroundings (especially at gym)
  • Create workout playlists: Queue multiple episodes for long sessions
  • Test before race day: Don't try new podcasts during important runs

Music vs Podcasts: When to Use Each

Use music for:

  • High-intensity intervals (when podcasts are too distracting)
  • PRs and max effort lifts
  • Sprints and speed work
  • When you need pure hype

Use podcasts for:

  • Steady-state cardio
  • Long runs (60+ minutes)
  • Lifting between sets
  • Walking/incline treadmill
  • Recovery days

Podcasts to Avoid During Workouts

  • Meditation/sleep podcasts: Will put you to sleep, not pump you up
  • Slow-paced interviews: Energy mismatch kills motivation
  • Complex technical content: Save learning code for desk time
  • News analysis requiring charts: "As you can see in the graph..." doesn't work while running
