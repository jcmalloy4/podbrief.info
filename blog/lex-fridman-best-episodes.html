Best Lex Fridman Podcast Episodes of All Time

Published March 9, 2026 · 12 min read

The Lex Fridman Podcast delivers 2-4 hour conversations with the world's top minds in AI, science, and technology. Here are the essential episodes.

Top 10 Must-Listen Episodes

#367 - Sam Altman: OpenAI CEO on GPT-4, ChatGPT, and the Future of AI

The OpenAI CEO discusses GPT-4's capabilities, AGI timeline, AI safety, and the future of artificial intelligence.

Why listen: Best explanation of GPT-4's capabilities and limitations directly from OpenAI's CEO.

#252 - Elon Musk: SpaceX, Mars, Tesla Autopilot, Self-Driving, Robotics, and AI

Elon Musk on Tesla's FSD progress, SpaceX's Mars mission, AI risks, and robotics.

Why listen: Elon at his most technical, discussing engineering challenges and AI safety.

#398 - Mark Zuckerberg: First Interview in Metaverse

Historic interview conducted entirely in Meta's VR metaverse. Zuckerberg discusses Meta's AI strategy, Llama models, and the future of social platforms.

Why listen: Rare candid conversation about Meta's pivot to AI and the metaverse vision.

#309 - John Carmack: Doom, Quake, VR, AGI, Programming, Video Games, and Rockets

Legendary programmer John Carmack on game development, VR, and his journey toward AGI.

Why listen: One of the greatest programmers of all time shares deep technical insights.

#73 - Andrew Ng: Deep Learning, Education, and Real-World AI

AI pioneer Andrew Ng on deep learning fundamentals, education, and deploying AI in production.

Why listen: Best introduction to practical AI and machine learning from a master teacher.
