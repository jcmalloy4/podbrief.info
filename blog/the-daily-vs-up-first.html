The Daily vs Up First: Which News Podcast is Better?

Published February 14, 2024 · 10 min read

Both The Daily and Up First dominate the morning news podcast space, but they serve different purposes. Here's how to choose the right one for your routine.

Quick Comparison

Feature The Daily Up First
Length 20-30 minutes 10-15 minutes
Format Deep dive into 1-2 stories Quick hits on 3 stories
Host Michael Barbaro (NYT) Rotating NPR hosts
Release 6 days/week (M-F + weekend) 7 days/week
Best For Understanding one story deeply Fast news overview

The Daily: Deep Dive Journalism

What Makes It Great

  • Depth: Explores one major story with context, interviews, and analysis
  • Production quality: Excellent sound design and storytelling
  • Michael Barbaro: Distinctive voice and interviewing style
  • NYT resources: Access to Times reporters and exclusive audio

When to Choose The Daily

  • You have 25-30 minutes in your morning routine
  • You want to deeply understand major news events
  • You prefer narrative storytelling over quick updates
  • You like consistency (same host daily)

Up First: Fast News Briefing

What Makes It Great

  • Speed: Three stories in 10-15 minutes
  • Breadth: Covers more topics per episode
  • NPR quality: Trusted reporting and analysis
  • Daily availability: Never misses a day

When to Choose Up First

  • You have limited time in the morning (10-15 min)
  • You want a broad overview of current events
  • You prefer quick hits to deep dives
  • You listen every single day

The Verdict

Choose The Daily If:

  • You have time for a longer listen
  • You want in-depth understanding
  • You prefer storytelling and narrative
  • One well-told story is more valuable than three quick updates

Choose Up First If:

  • You're short on time
  • You want broad news coverage
  • You listen during a quick commute or workout
  • You need news every single day (including weekends)

Or Listen to Both

Many people start with Up First for a quick overview, then listen to The Daily later when they have time for deeper context on the day's biggest story.
