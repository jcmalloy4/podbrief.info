Best SmartLess Episodes 2026 - Top Celebrity Interviews

Published February 24, 2026

SmartLess has become one of the most popular celebrity interview podcasts, thanks to the chemistry between hosts Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes. Each week, one host surprises the other two with a mystery guest, leading to hilarious, genuine conversations with A-list celebrities. Here are the best SmartLess episodes worth listening to.

What Makes SmartLess Special?

  • Host chemistry: Three longtime friends with genuine affection and humor
  • Mystery guest format: Two hosts don't know who's coming, creating authentic reactions
  • A-list access: Major celebrities feel comfortable opening up
  • Balance: Smart questions mixed with silly banter

Best SmartLess Episodes by Guest Type

Best Comedy Legends Episodes

Conan O'Brien

The legendary late-night host discusses his career, comedy philosophy, and the evolution of entertainment. When comedy titans meet, magic happens.

Why listen: Comedy royalty sharing war stories

Tina Fey

SNL and 30 Rock creator Fey discusses writing, comedy, and navigating Hollywood. Her wit and intelligence shine through.

Why listen: One of comedy's sharpest minds

Steve Martin

The comedy legend discusses his extraordinary career spanning stand-up, film, music, and art. A masterclass in reinvention.

Why listen: Learn from a true renaissance entertainer

Best Actor/Actress Episodes

George Clooney

Clooney's appearances are always gold. He's charming, funny, and surprisingly candid about his career and friendships.

Why listen: Hollywood royalty at his most relaxed

Meryl Streep

The most celebrated actress of her generation discusses craft, career choices, and what drives her continued excellence.

Why listen: Acting wisdom from the GOAT

Ryan Reynolds

Reynolds brings his signature humor while discussing the business side of Hollywood and building brands.

Why listen: Comedy and business insights combined

Best Music Industry Episodes

Paul McCartney

A Beatle on SmartLess! McCartney discusses the Beatles, Wings, and his legendary songwriting partnership with John Lennon.

Why listen: Music history from someone who made it

Taylor Swift

Swift opens up about songwriting, fame, and navigating the music industry. One of SmartLess's most downloaded episodes.

Why listen: Insight into one of music's biggest stars

Elton John

The legendary performer discusses his incredible career, sobriety, and enduring passion for music.

Why listen: Rock and roll history and wisdom

Best Business & Tech Episodes

Mark Cuban

The billionaire entrepreneur discusses business, Shark Tank, and his approach to investing and life.

Why listen: Business wisdom with personality

Gwyneth Paltrow

Love her or hate her, Paltrow's discussion of building Goop into a lifestyle empire is fascinating.

Why listen: Learn about building controversial brands

Best Political & Newsmaker Episodes

Kamala Harris

The Vice President appears in a surprisingly relaxed conversation about her life, career, and path to national politics.

Why listen: See a political figure in a different light

Anderson Cooper

The CNN anchor discusses journalism, truth-telling, and his unique career path.

Why listen: Journalism insights from a trusted voice

Best Sports Star Episodes

Tom Brady

The NFL legend discusses competition, longevity, and what drove his unprecedented success.

Why listen: Peak performance mindset from the GOAT

Serena Williams

Tennis legend Williams discusses her career, motherhood, and life beyond tennis.

Why listen: Greatness and grace combined

Best "Surprise Factor" Episodes

Some of the best SmartLess moments come from unexpected guests who surprise even the most jaded Hollywood insiders. Watch for episodes where the hosts genuinely don't know who's coming—their authentic reactions create the best moments.

How to Listen to SmartLess

For comedy fans: Start with Conan, Tina Fey, or Steve Martin

For music lovers: Paul McCartney and Taylor Swift are essential

For business insight: Mark Cuban and Gwyneth Paltrow

For Hollywood stories: George Clooney and Meryl Streep

For sports: Tom Brady and Serena Williams

For pure entertainment: Ryan Reynolds never disappoints

What Makes the Hosts Special

Jason Bateman

The straight man who asks smart questions and keeps conversations on track. His decades in Hollywood give him unique credibility with guests.

Will Arnett

The wild card who brings energy and unpredictability. His absurd humor balances Jason's seriousness.

Sean Hayes

The heart of the show who brings warmth and genuine curiosity. His emotional intelligence draws out vulnerable moments.

The SmartLess Formula

What makes SmartLess work:

  • Genuine friendship: The hosts clearly enjoy each other
  • No pretense: They're not trying to be journalists; they're having fun
  • Celebrity comfort: Guests know the hosts and relax
  • Smart enough: Thoughtful questions when they matter
  • Silly enough: Willing to be ridiculous and laugh
