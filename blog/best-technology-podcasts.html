🚀 Best Startup & Business Podcasts

All-In Podcast

Why it's great: Four successful tech investors (Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg) debate markets, tech trends, and politics. Unfiltered takes on the biggest tech and economic stories.

Best for: Venture capital perspective, market analysis, startup ecosystem insights

Frequency: Weekly (every Friday)

My First Million

Why it's great: Shaan Puri and Sam Parr share business ideas, breakdown successful companies, and interview entrepreneurs. High energy, actionable insights.

Best for: Business ideas, entrepreneurship, case studies of successful businesses

Frequency: 2-3 episodes per week

This Week in Startups

Why it's great: Jason Calacanis interviews founders, investors, and tech leaders. Covers funding news, startup strategies, and emerging trends.

Best for: Startup news, founder interviews, fundraising insights

Frequency: 3-4 episodes per week

💻 Best Software Development Podcasts

The Changelog

Why it's great: Deep dives into open source projects, developer tools, and software engineering practices. Conversations with creators of popular frameworks and libraries.

Best for: Open source, developer tools, software architecture

Frequency: Weekly

Software Engineering Daily

Why it's great: Daily episodes covering different aspects of software engineering. Topics range from databases and cloud infrastructure to programming languages and DevOps.

Best for: Broad software engineering topics, technical depth, daily learning

Frequency: Daily

Syntax.fm

Why it's great: Wes Bos and Scott Tolinski discuss web development, JavaScript, React, tooling, and the modern web stack. Mix of "Hasty Treats" (quick tips) and longer "Tasty Treats" (deep dives).

Best for: Web development, JavaScript ecosystem, practical coding tips

Frequency: Twice weekly (Monday & Wednesday)

📱 Best Product & Design Podcasts

Lenny's Podcast

Why it's great: Lenny Rachitsky interviews product leaders from companies like Airbnb, Netflix, Stripe, and more. Tactical advice on product management, growth, and career development.

Best for: Product management, growth strategies, PM career advice

Frequency: Weekly

The Product Podcast

Why it's great: Stories from product managers at top tech companies. Covers product strategy, user research, roadmapping, and cross-functional collaboration.

Best for: Product management best practices, real-world PM experiences

Frequency: Weekly

🔐 Best Cybersecurity Podcasts

Darknet Diaries

Why it's great: True stories from the dark side of the internet. Jack Rhysider investigates hackers, breaches, cybercrime, and digital espionage. Engaging storytelling makes complex security topics accessible.

Best for: Security awareness, hacking stories, cybercrime investigations
