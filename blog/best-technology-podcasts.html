Best Technology Podcasts in 2026

Published February 13, 2026 · 10 min read

Technology shapes our world, and staying informed about tech trends, innovations, and implications is essential whether you're a developer, entrepreneur, or curious observer. These technology podcasts deliver expert insights, industry analysis, and deep dives into the tech shaping our future.

Top Technology Podcasts

1. Acquired

Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal deliver the best deep-dive technology company analysis in podcasting. Multi-hour episodes exploring tech giants' histories, strategies, and business models.

Why listen: Definitive tech company deep-dives, business strategy analysis, exceptional research.

2. Hard Fork

Kevin Roose and Casey Newton discuss the biggest tech news, AI developments, and Silicon Valley culture. Weekly tech news with insider perspectives from New York Times tech columnists.

Why listen: Current tech news analysis, AI coverage, Silicon Valley insider views.

3. The Vergecast

The Verge's tech editors discuss the week in technology, gadget reviews, and industry trends. Essential listening for consumer tech and platform news.

Why listen: Consumer tech reviews, platform updates, entertainment and analysis.

4. All-In Podcast

Chamath, Jason, Sacks, and Friedberg discuss tech, markets, and politics. Silicon Valley insiders debating the week's biggest tech and business stories.

Why listen: Silicon Valley insider perspectives, tech business analysis, unfiltered debate.

5. a16z Podcast

Andreessen Horowitz's podcast covers technology trends, startup strategy, and future innovations from one of Silicon Valley's top venture firms.

Why listen: VC perspectives on tech trends, startup insights, future technology analysis.

6. Techmeme Ride Home

Brian McCullough delivers daily 15-20 minute summaries of the day's most important tech news. Perfect for staying current without information overload.

Why listen: Daily tech news summary, time-efficient, comprehensive coverage.

7. The Info Podcast

Founder and tech reporter interviews exploring tech industry news, startup stories, and Silicon Valley dynamics. Premium podcast from The Information.

Why listen: Tech journalism excellence, startup stories, industry insider access.

8. Reply All

While on hiatus, Reply All's archives remain the best exploration of internet culture, technology, and weird online phenomena. Investigative and entertaining.

Why listen: Internet culture deep-dives, tech investigations, entertaining storytelling.

9. Exponent

Ben Thompson and James Allworth discuss tech strategy, business models, and industry analysis. Deep analysis of technology business strategy.

Why listen: Strategic business analysis, tech business models, thoughtful discussion.

10. The Talk Show

John Gruber discusses Apple, technology, and culture. The definitive Apple-focused tech podcast with insider perspectives and analysis.

Why listen: Apple analysis, technology commentary, industry insider perspective.

11. Lenny's Podcast

Lenny Rachitsky interviews product leaders, tech founders, and growth experts. Focused on product management, growth, and building tech companies.

Why listen: Product management insights, growth strategies, tech company building.

12. This Week in Tech (TWiT)

Leo Laporte and rotating panelists discuss the week's tech news. Long-running, comprehensive tech news roundtable.

Why listen: Comprehensive tech news, multiple perspectives, long-running show.

13. The Twenty Minute VC

Harry Stebbings interviews VCs, founders, and tech leaders about startups, investing, and technology. Focus on venture capital and startup ecosystem.

Why listen: VC insights, startup funding, tech ecosystem perspectives.

14. Land of the Giants

Seasonal deep-dive series exploring major tech companies and industries. Each season examines one tech giant or trend in detail.

Why listen: Deep tech industry investigations, seasonal deep-dives, excellent production.

15. Darknet Diaries

Jack Rhysider explores cybersecurity, hackers, and digital crime. Gripping stories about technology's dark side and security implications.

Why listen: Cybersecurity stories, hacking tales, technology security insights.

What Makes a Great Technology Podcast?

Accurate reporting: Quality shows verify facts and correct mistakes.

Industry access: The best podcasts feature insider perspectives and industry sources.

Strategic analysis: Beyond news, great shows explain why tech developments matter.

Diverse topics: From consumer gadgets to enterprise software, coverage breadth matters.

Clear explanations: Technical concepts explained accessibly without dumbing down.

Key Technology Topics

Artificial Intelligence: GPT models, machine learning, AI safety, AGI predictions

Consumer tech: Smartphones, laptops, gadgets, reviews, platform updates

Enterprise tech: Cloud computing, SaaS, infrastructure, B2B technology

Startups: Funding rounds, startup strategy, founder stories, VC trends

Platforms: Apple, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon ecosystem updates

Emerging tech: Crypto, VR/AR, quantum computing, biotech

Regulation: Antitrust, privacy, Section 230, tech policy

Cybersecurity: Data breaches, hacking, security practices, vulnerabilities

How to Stay Current with Tech Podcasts

Pick your cadence: Mix daily summaries (Techmeme) with weekly analysis (Hard Fork).

Choose your depth: Balance news (The Vergecast) with deep-dives (Acquired).

Follow your interests: Focus on areas relevant to your work or curiosity.

Take action: Apply insights to your products, investments, or understanding.

Stay skeptical: Tech hype is common; look for evidence-based analysis.

Red Flags in Tech Podcasts

Uncritical boosterism: Quality shows question claims and examine downsides.

Conflicts of interest: Hosts should disclose investments and relationships.

Hype over substance: The best shows explain technology, not just promote it.

Outdated information: Tech moves fast; check episode dates for news-focused shows.
