What Makes a Great Tech Podcast

Timely Coverage

Tech moves fast. The best podcasts cover current events (AI breakthroughs, policy changes, acquisitions).

Insider Access

VCs, founders, and researchers provide insights you can't get from news articles.

Multiple Perspectives

All-In is great because the hosts disagree. Avoid echo chambers.

Technical Depth

Best podcasts balance accessibility with technical accuracy. Not dumbed down, not jargon-heavy.

Common Mistakes

Only Following Hype

Everyone talks about the latest AI model. Follow podcasts that explain why it matters.

Ignoring Long-Form Content

Lex Fridman episodes are 2-4 hours, but worth it for deep understanding.

Not Staying Current

Tech podcasts from 2+ months ago might be outdated. Prioritize recent episodes.
