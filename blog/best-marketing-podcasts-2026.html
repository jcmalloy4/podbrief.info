Best Marketing Podcasts 2026

Published March 10, 2026

Marketing podcasts keep you current with the latest strategies, tools, and tactics in digital marketing, growth, branding, and advertising. Whether you're running a startup, managing campaigns, or building a personal brand, these podcasts deliver actionable insights from top marketers.

Why Listen to Marketing Podcasts?

  • Stay Current: Marketing changes fast—new platforms, algorithms, and strategies emerge constantly
  • Learn from Practitioners: Hear from marketers running real campaigns with real budgets
  • Actionable Tactics: Not just theory—specific strategies you can implement today
  • Cross-Channel Insights: SEO, paid ads, social media, email, content—learn it all

Top Marketing Podcasts

Marketing Against the Grain

Kipp Bodnar (HubSpot CMO) and Kieran Flanagan (SVP Marketing at HubSpot) discuss unconventional marketing strategies. They share what's working in growth, product-led growth, and B2B marketing with humor and insider knowledge.

My First Million

Sam Parr and Shaan Puri break down business ideas and marketing tactics behind successful companies. They discuss viral marketing campaigns, content strategies, and how founders grow their businesses from zero.

Everyone Hates Marketers

Louis Grenier interviews marketers who reject conventional tactics like growth hacking and spam. Focus on authenticity, customer research, and marketing that actually helps people. Anti-BS marketing advice.

The GaryVee Audio Experience

Gary Vaynerchuk on social media marketing, personal branding, and entrepreneurship. Daily episodes covering content creation, platform strategies (TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn), and building attention.

The Growth Show (HubSpot)

Stories from real companies about growth, marketing, and sales. Interviews with founders and marketers on how they acquired customers, scaled, and navigated challenges.

Online Marketing Made Easy

Amy Porterfield teaches digital marketing tactics for course creators, coaches, and entrepreneurs. Episodes cover email marketing, webinar funnels, list building, and social media strategies.

The Marketing Companion

Mark Schaefer and Brooke Sellas discuss current marketing trends, news, and strategies. Covers content marketing, social media, branding, and emerging platforms with thoughtful analysis.

Perpetual Traffic

The paid advertising podcast from DigitalMarketer. Ralph Burns and team break down Facebook ads, Google ads, YouTube ads, and traffic strategies that drive conversions.

The Smart Passive Income Podcast

Pat Flynn shares strategies for building online businesses through content marketing, email, affiliates, and passive income. Transparent case studies and income reports.

Marketing Podcast Categories

Digital Marketing Strategy

The Digital Marketing Podcast from Target Internet covers all aspects of digital marketing. The Marketing School by Neil Patel and Eric Siu delivers daily 10-minute marketing tips.

Content Marketing

Content Inc. by Joe Pulizzi focuses on building audiences through content. The Content Strategy Podcast explores content planning, creation, and distribution.

SEO & Search Marketing

The Search Engine Journal Show covers SEO news and strategies. Authority Hacker discusses SEO tactics for affiliate marketers and niche site builders.

Social Media Marketing

Social Media Marketing Podcast by Michael Stelzner (Social Media Examiner founder) interviews top social media marketers. The Science of Social Media from Buffer shares data-driven strategies.

Email Marketing

Email Marketing Hero with Adam Erhart focuses on email strategy and automation. ConvertKit Craft & Commerce features creators building businesses with email.

Brand Building

The Futur with Chris Do explores branding, design, and creative entrepreneurship. Building a StoryBrand by Donald Miller teaches narrative-driven marketing.

Growth Marketing

The Growth TLDRShow by Neil Patel discusses growth tactics and experiments. Growth Everywhere features interviews with growth marketers and startup founders.

What Makes a Great Marketing Podcast?

  • Actionable Advice: Specific tactics you can implement immediately
  • Real Results: Case studies and data from actual campaigns
  • Current Trends: Coverage of new platforms and changing algorithms
  • Diverse Perspectives: B2B, B2C, startup, enterprise—different contexts
  • Honest Failures: What didn't work and why

Marketing Skills to Learn

  • Acquisition: Paid ads, SEO, content, social media
  • Conversion: Landing pages, email funnels, CRO
  • Retention: Email nurture, community building, loyalty
  • Analytics: Measuring ROI, attribution, experimentation
  • Brand: Positioning, messaging, storytelling

How to Choose the Right Marketing Podcast

  • Your Business Model: B2B vs B2C? SaaS, e-commerce, services, or creator?
  • Channel Focus: Which channels do you use? Paid, organic, social, email?
  • Experience Level: Beginner-friendly or advanced tactics?
  • Industry: Some podcasts focus on specific industries (SaaS, e-commerce, local)
  • Budget: Bootstrapped tactics or big-budget campaigns?

Common Marketing Topics

  • TikTok & short-form video
  • LinkedIn marketing for B2B

  • Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
  • Product-led growth
  • Community building

Marketing Podcast Formats

  • Solo Teaching: One host sharing strategies (Online Marketing Made Easy)
  • Co-Host Discussion: Two marketers debating tactics (Marketing Against the Grain)
  • Interviews: Conversations with successful marketers (Social Media Marketing Podcast)
  • News & Analysis: Discussing current marketing trends (The Marketing Companion)
  • Daily Tips: Short, actionable episodes (Marketing School)

The Value of Marketing Education

Marketing podcasts help you:

  • Learn new tactics without expensive courses
  • Stay ahead of algorithm changes and platform updates
  • Discover tools and automation that save time
  • Understand what's working for other businesses
  • Build a competitive edge through continuous learning
