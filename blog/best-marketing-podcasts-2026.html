Best Marketing Podcasts (2026)

Published February 21, 2026

Want to master marketing? The best marketing podcasts of 2026 cover SEO, content marketing, social media, branding, and growth strategies from practitioners who've done it successfully.

Why Listen to Marketing Podcasts?

Marketing podcasts help you:

  • Stay current with platform algorithm changes
  • Learn proven tactics from successful marketers
  • Understand customer psychology and behavior
  • Master new channels before competitors
  • Avoid expensive mistakes through case studies

Top Marketing Podcasts of 2026

1. Marketing Against the Grain

Kipp Bodnar (HubSpot CMO) and Kieran Flanagan share contrarian marketing takes, experiments, and what's actually working. Data-driven and practical.

Best for: Growth marketing, SEO, content strategy, marketing experiments

2. My First Million

While primarily business-focused, Shaan Puri and Sam Parr break down viral marketing campaigns, growth tactics, and creative marketing ideas.

Best for: Creative marketing, growth hacks, trend analysis

3. The GaryVee Audio Experience

Gary Vaynerchuk shares daily insights on social media marketing, personal branding, and attention arbitrage. Raw and unfiltered.

Best for: Social media, personal branding, content creation

4. Everyone Hates Marketers

Louis Grenier interviews marketers who've achieved real results. No fluff, just tactics and strategies that work.

Best for: Practical marketing, case studies, anti-hype approach

5. The Marketing Millennials

Daniel Murray breaks down what top brands are doing on social media and how to apply those strategies to your business.

Best for: Social media marketing, brand strategy, Gen Z/Millennial marketing

6. The Growth Show

HubSpot's podcast featuring conversations with successful entrepreneurs and marketers about growth strategies.

Best for: Inbound marketing, growth frameworks, customer acquisition

7. The Copyblogger Podcast

Tim Stoddart and the Copyblogger team discuss content marketing, copywriting, and building audiences online.

Best for: Content marketing, copywriting, audience building

8. The Smart Marketer Podcast

Ezra Firestone shares e-commerce marketing strategies, Facebook ads, email marketing, and conversion optimization.

Best for: E-commerce, paid advertising, conversion optimization

9. The Science of Social Media

Buffer's podcast about social media marketing trends, tools, and tactics. Data-backed insights and quick tips.

Best for: Social media strategy, platform updates, practical tips

10. Demand Gen Chat

Kaylee Edmondson interviews B2B marketers about demand generation strategies that drive revenue.

Best for: B2B marketing, demand generation, lead nurturing

SEO & Content Marketing

  • Keyword research and content strategy
  • Link building tactics
  • Technical SEO optimization
  • Content distribution strategies

Social Media Marketing

  • Platform-specific strategies (TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter/X)
  • Organic growth tactics
  • Paid social advertising
  • Influencer marketing

Email Marketing

  • List building strategies
  • Email sequence design
  • Deliverability optimization
  • Conversion-focused copywriting

Paid Advertising

  • Facebook/Instagram ads
  • Google Ads strategy
  • Attribution and analytics
  • Creative testing frameworks

Brand & Positioning

  • Brand differentiation
  • Messaging and positioning
  • Community building
  • Personal branding

How to Choose the Right Marketing Podcast

For SEO & Content: Marketing Against the Grain, Copyblogger

For Social Media: GaryVee, Marketing Millennials, Science of Social Media

For E-commerce: Smart Marketer

For B2B Marketing: Demand Gen Chat, The Growth Show

For Creative Ideas: My First Million, Everyone Hates Marketers

For Personal Branding: GaryVee Audio Experience

What You'll Learn

The best marketing podcasts teach:

  • Frameworks: Repeatable systems for testing and scaling
  • Case studies: Real examples with numbers and results
  • Platform updates: Algorithm changes and new features
  • Psychology: Why people buy and share
  • Tools: Software and resources that save time

Pro Tip: Apply as You Learn

Don't binge podcast episodes. Listen to one, implement one tactic, measure results, then move to the next episode. Execution beats consumption.
