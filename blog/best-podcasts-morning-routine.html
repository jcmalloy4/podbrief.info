  • The Daily (25-30 min)

  • Huberman Lab (60-90 min)

  • Joe Rogan Experience (2-3 hours - start and continue)

Tips for Morning Podcast Listening

  • Download the night before - Avoid buffering during your rushed morning
  • Use 1.5x speed - Fit more content into your morning
  • Queue tomorrow's episode today - One less decision in the morning
  • Pair with a morning habit - Coffee making, shower, commute
