Best Podcast Apps Compared: Apple Podcasts vs Spotify vs Overcast (2026)

A detailed comparison of the top podcast apps—features, pricing, and which one is right for you

Updated February 25, 2026

Choosing the right podcast app can dramatically improve your listening experience. From playback features to discovery tools, each app has strengths and weaknesses. Here's an in-depth comparison of the best podcast apps in 2026.

Quick Comparison Table

Apple Podcasts: Best for iPhone users, free, simple interface

Spotify: Best for music + podcasts in one app, free with ads

Overcast: Best for power users, advanced playback features, free/$10/year

Pocket Casts: Best cross-platform app, beautiful design, $4/month

Castro: Best inbox/queue management, iOS only, $5/month

Apple Podcasts

Platform: iOS, macOS
Price: Free
Best for: iPhone users who want simplicity

Pros

  • Pre-installed on iPhone/Mac – No download needed
  • Clean, simple interface – Easy for beginners
  • Seamless Apple ecosystem sync – Works across all Apple devices
  • Largest podcast directory – Over 4 million shows
  • Completely free – No ads, no premium tier

Cons

  • Limited playback customization (basic speed control)
  • No silence trimming or voice boost
  • Discovery features are basic
  • No web player

Verdict: Great default option for iPhone users. Simple, reliable, and free—but lacks advanced features.

Spotify

Platform: iOS, Android, Web, Desktop
Price: Free with ads / $11/month Premium
Best for: People who want music + podcasts in one app

Pros

  • Music + podcasts in one app – No switching between apps
  • Exclusive shows – The Joe Rogan Experience, Lex Fridman, and more
  • Great discovery algorithm – Personalized recommendations
  • Cross-platform sync – Works on everything
  • Web player – Listen in your browser

Cons

  • Ads on free tier (even for podcasts)
  • Limited playback features (no silence trimming)
  • Podcast search is less comprehensive than dedicated apps
  • Video podcasts eat battery faster

Verdict: Best if you already use Spotify for music. Convenient all-in-one app, but podcast features lag behind dedicated apps.

Overcast

Platform: iOS, Apple Watch
Price: Free / $10/year premium
Best for: Power users who want advanced playback features

Pros

  • Smart Speed – Automatically trims silence without changing pitch
  • Voice Boost – Enhances vocal clarity
  • Granular speed control – Adjust playback speed precisely
  • Excellent queue management – Custom playlists and prioritization
  • Free with minimal ads – Premium removes ads and adds features

Cons

  • iOS only (no Android)
  • No web player
  • Interface can feel dated
  • Discovery features are basic

Verdict: The best podcast app for serious listeners. Smart Speed alone saves hours per year. iOS-only is the main limitation.

Pocket Casts

Platform: iOS, Android, Web
Price: Free / $4/month Pocket Casts Plus
Best for: Cross-platform users who want a beautiful interface

Pros

  • Beautiful, modern design – Best-looking podcast app
  • Cross-platform sync – iOS, Android, and web
  • Silence trimming – Similar to Overcast's Smart Speed
  • Volume boost – Enhance quiet podcasts
  • Custom filters and playlists – Advanced organization

Cons

  • Premium features require subscription ($4/month)
  • Free version limits some customization
  • Desktop apps require Plus subscription

Verdict: The best all-around podcast app if you use multiple platforms. Worth the subscription if you listen a lot.

Castro

Platform: iOS
Price: Free trial / $5/month
Best for: People overwhelmed by too many podcasts

Pros

  • Inbox approach – Triage new episodes like email
  • Queue management – Fine control over what plays next
  • Clean design – Minimal, focused interface
  • Enhanced audio – Volume boost and trimming

Cons

  • iOS only
  • Subscription required for full features ($5/month)
  • Learning curve for inbox workflow

Verdict: Perfect if you subscribe to many podcasts and need a system to manage them. The inbox workflow is polarizing—you'll love it or hate it.

Other Notable Apps

Google Podcasts (Discontinued)

Google shut down Google Podcasts in 2024 and moved podcast functionality to YouTube Music. If you used Google Podcasts, migrate to YouTube Music or choose another app from this list.

YouTube Music

Includes podcasts but primarily a music app. Good if you already subscribe to YouTube Premium, but dedicated podcast apps offer better features.

Which Podcast App Should You Choose?

Choose Apple Podcasts if: You're on iPhone and want something simple and free

Choose Spotify if: You want music + podcasts in one app and listen to Spotify exclusives

Choose Overcast if: You're on iOS and want the best playback features (Smart Speed is a game-changer)

Choose Pocket Casts if: You use multiple platforms and want a beautiful, feature-rich app

Choose Castro if: You subscribe to many podcasts and need inbox-style management

Final Thoughts

There's no single "best" podcast app—it depends on your platform, listening habits, and priorities. iPhone users should start with Overcast or Apple Podcasts. Android users should try Pocket Casts. If you use both iOS and Android, Pocket Casts or Spotify are your best bets.
