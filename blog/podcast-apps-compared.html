Best Podcast Apps Compared: Apple Podcasts vs Spotify vs Overcast (2026)

Choosing the right podcast app can transform your listening experience. We tested the top 8 podcast apps to help you find the perfect fit.

Quick Comparison Table

Best Overall: Apple Podcasts (Free, best integration for iPhone users)

Best for Music + Podcasts: Spotify (Free with ads, $11.99/mo Premium)

Best for Power Users: Overcast ($9.99/year)

Best for Discovery: Pocket Casts (Free, $39.99/year Plus)

1. Apple Podcasts

Price: Free

Best For: iPhone, iPad, Mac users who want simple, reliable podcasting

Pros:

  • Pre-installed on all Apple devices
  • Seamless sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, HomePod
  • CarPlay integration
  • Large podcast library
  • Clean, intuitive interface
  • Smart Speed and voice boost

Cons:

  • No Android version
  • Limited customization
  • Occasional sync issues
  • No cross-platform support

2. Spotify

Price: Free (with ads), $11.99/month Premium

Best For: People who want music and podcasts in one app

Pros:

  • Combines music + podcasts
  • Exclusive shows (Joe Rogan, Call Her Daddy, Armchair Expert)
  • Great discovery algorithm
  • Works on iOS, Android, desktop
  • Free tier available
  • Video podcasts

Cons:

  • Missing some independent podcasts
  • No chapter markers support
  • Limited playback speed options (0.5x-3.5x)
  • Ads in free tier

3. Overcast

Price: Free (with ads), $9.99/year Premium

Best For: Power users who want advanced features

Pros:

  • Smart Speed (removes dead air without distortion)
  • Voice Boost (normalizes volume)
  • Powerful playlist tools
  • Chapter support
  • Clean, minimal interface
  • Excellent playback controls

Cons:

  • iOS only (no Android)
  • Can feel overwhelming for beginners
  • Limited discovery features

4. Pocket Casts

Price: Free, $39.99/year Pocket Casts Plus

Best For: Cross-platform users, podcast enthusiasts

Pros:

  • Works on iOS, Android, web
  • Excellent discovery features
  • Powerful filters and playlists
  • Dark mode
  • Chapter markers
  • Sleep timer
  • Cloud storage for custom files (Plus)

Cons:

  • Some features require Plus subscription
  • Interface can feel cluttered

5. Google Podcasts (Discontinued)

Note: Google Podcasts was discontinued in 2024. Google now recommends YouTube Music for podcasts.

6. YouTube Music

Price: Free (with ads), $10.99/month Premium

Best For: Android users, YouTube fans

Pros:

  • Combines music, podcasts, and YouTube content
  • Good discovery features
  • Video podcast support
  • Works on iOS and Android

Cons:

  • Still building out podcast features
  • Not as polished as dedicated podcast apps
  • Ads in free tier

7. Castro

Price: Free (limited), $24.99/year Castro Plus

Best For: Inbox zero enthusiasts

Pros:

  • Unique "inbox" approach to episode management
  • Triages new episodes
  • Chapter support
  • Playback enhancements

Cons:

  • iOS only
  • Requires active management
  • Not ideal for casual listeners

8. Stitcher (Discontinued)

Note: Stitcher was shut down in 2023. Content moved to SiriusXM.

How to Choose the Right Podcast App

If you're an iPhone user: Start with Apple Podcasts. It's free, reliable, and works perfectly with your devices.

If you use both music and podcasts: Spotify or YouTube Music combine everything in one app.

If you're a power listener: Overcast or Pocket Casts offer advanced features like Smart Speed, custom playlists, and better organization.

If you switch between Android and iPhone: Pocket Casts or Spotify work across all platforms.

If you're new to podcasts: Apple Podcasts or Spotify have the simplest learning curves.

Key Features to Look For

  • Playback Speed: All major apps support variable speed (0.5x to 3x)
  • Offline Downloads: Essential for flights and commutes
  • Auto-Download: Automatically download new episodes
  • Sleep Timer: Stop playback after a set time
  • Chapter Markers: Jump to specific segments
  • Cross-Platform Sync: Pick up where you left off on any device
  • CarPlay/Android Auto: Safe listening while driving

Our Recommendation

For most people, we recommend starting with Apple Podcasts (iPhone) or Spotify (all platforms). Both are free, widely supported, and cover 95% of use cases.

If you listen to 5+ hours of podcasts per week, upgrade to Overcast or Pocket Casts. The Smart Speed feature alone will save you hours over time.
