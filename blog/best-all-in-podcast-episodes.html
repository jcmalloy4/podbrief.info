Best All-In Podcast Episodes of All Time

Published February 13, 2026 · 10 min read

The All-In Podcast brings together four successful investors—Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, and David Friedberg—for unfiltered discussions about tech, politics, business, and culture. Known for their "besties" dynamic and willingness to debate controversial topics, All-In has become essential listening for anyone interested in Silicon Valley thinking.

Here are the best episodes that showcase what makes All-In so compelling.

Top All-In Podcast Episodes

1. "Silicon Valley Bank Collapse" (Episode E122, March 2023)

The besties broke down the SVB collapse in real-time, offering insider perspectives on what happened, why it mattered, and what it meant for the startup ecosystem. This episode showcased their ability to analyze breaking news with depth and clarity.

Why listen: Real-time analysis of a major financial crisis, insider perspectives, practical implications explained.

2. "The Great Resignation" (Episode E53, October 2021)

This episode explored the massive shift in how people think about work post-pandemic. The hosts debated remote work, labor dynamics, and the future of employment with characteristically diverse perspectives.

Why listen: Prescient analysis of workplace trends, debate on productivity and culture, still relevant today.

3. "Ukraine Crisis & Conflict" (Episode E69, February 2022)

Just as the Ukraine situation escalated, the All-In crew analyzed the geopolitical dynamics, economic implications, and potential outcomes. Their willingness to discuss controversial angles sparked significant debate.

Why listen: Geopolitical analysis from a tech/business lens, multiple perspectives, controversial but thoughtful.

4. "The Climate Debate" (Episode E94, August 2022)

David Friedberg's expertise shines in this deep dive into climate science, policy, and solutions. The episode exemplifies All-In's ability to tackle complex topics with intellectual honesty.

Why listen: Science-based climate discussion, policy analysis, solutions-oriented.

5. "Biden vs. Trump 2024" (Episode E138, June 2023)

The besties analyzed the political landscape ahead of the 2024 election with their characteristic frankness. This episode highlights their willingness to critique both sides and discuss taboo political topics.

Why listen: Nonpartisan political analysis, predictions, Silicon Valley political perspective.

6. "The AI Revolution" (Episode E115, January 2023)

Right as ChatGPT exploded, the All-In crew discussed AI's implications for business, society, and the future. Their debate on AI safety, regulation, and opportunity was prescient.

Why listen: Early AI analysis that aged well, multiple expert perspectives, forward-looking.

7. "Breaking Down the FTX Collapse" (Episode E109, November 2022)

The FTX implosion provided All-In with a perfect case study in fraud, venture capital, and crypto hype. The hosts didn't hold back in their criticism and analysis.

Why listen: Thorough breakdown of a major fraud, crypto skepticism meets crypto enthusiasm, lessons for investors.

8. "The Twitter/Elon Saga" (Episode E74, April 2022)

Before Elon's acquisition completed, All-In analyzed the Twitter takeover attempt, debated content moderation, and discussed free speech. Sacks' perspective as a future Twitter executive adds fascinating context in retrospect.

Why listen: Inside look at a historic tech acquisition, free speech debate, aged interestingly.

9. "Tech Layoffs & Economic Reality" (Episode E116, January 2023)

As big tech began massive layoffs, the besties discussed overhiring, efficiency, and the end of the ZIRP (zero interest rate phenomenon) era. Brutally honest about tech excesses.

Why listen: Inside perspective on tech industry dynamics, economic analysis, honest assessment of tech bubble behavior.

10. "Education Reform" (Episode E82, May 2022)

The crew tackled education, school choice, and the future of learning. Chamath and Jason's personal experiences with education added depth to policy discussions.

Why listen: Personal stories meet policy analysis, passionate debate, practical solutions.

What Makes All-In Special?

Unfiltered conversation: Unlike polished corporate podcasts, All-In feels like eavesdropping on smart people arguing.

Diverse perspectives: The four hosts disagree regularly, creating dynamic debates rather than echo chambers.

Inside access: As successful investors and operators, they offer perspectives you won't get from traditional media.

Willingness to be controversial: All-In tackles topics others avoid, from politics to culture wars.

Real-time analysis: Breaking news episodes showcase their ability to analyze complex situations quickly.

The "Besties" Dynamic

The chemistry between Chamath, Jason, David Sacks, and David Friedberg is what makes All-In work:

Chamath Palihapitiya: Bold takes, macro focus, willing to challenge conventional wisdom

Jason Calacanis: Moderator and provocateur, startup expertise, occasionally plays devil's advocate

David Sacks: Conservative perspective, product thinking, free speech advocate

David Friedberg: Science and data focus, climate expertise, usually the voice of reason

All-In by Topic

For startup/VC insights: Episodes on fundraising, exits, and startup strategy

For political analysis: Election coverage, policy debates, geopolitical discussion

For economic analysis: Fed policy, inflation, market dynamics, crypto

For tech industry: Big tech regulation, AI, social media, platform dynamics

For science/climate: Friedberg-heavy episodes on climate, agriculture, biotech

How to Listen to All-In

Expect debate: The hosts disagree frequently; that's a feature, not a bug.

Check your biases: All-In challenges assumptions across the political spectrum.

Follow the news: Episodes are most valuable when you're familiar with the topics.

Don't expect consensus: The best episodes end with the hosts still disagreeing.
