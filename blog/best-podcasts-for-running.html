Best Podcasts for Running (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

The right podcast transforms a hard run into an adventure. These shows match running's rhythm—engaging enough to distract from discomfort, but not so complex that you lose focus on form and pace.

Best Running Podcasts by Distance

For Short Runs (20-30 Minutes)

1. The Daily

Perfect for a 3-mile morning run. One compelling news story delivered in 20-30 minutes.

Why it works: Engaging but not mentally taxing, complete in one run

Browse The Daily summaries →

2. Up First

10-15 minute news brief for quick runs or warm-ups.

Why it works: Quick, energizing, informative

3. Ted Radio Hour (Segments)

Individual TED Talk segments run 10-15 minutes—perfect for shorter distances.

Why it works: Inspiring ideas, motivating energy

For Medium Runs (45-60 Minutes)

1. SmartLess

Funny celebrity interviews that make a 6-mile run fly by.

Why it works: Conversational, entertaining, no heavy thinking

2. How I Built This

Entrepreneur stories that inspire you to push through the hard miles.

Why it works: Motivating, engaging narratives, good pacing

3. Hidden Brain

Fascinating psychology that keeps your mind engaged while legs work.

Why it works: Interesting without requiring intense focus

For Long Runs (60-90+ Minutes)

1. The Joe Rogan Experience

3-hour conversations perfect for marathon training runs. Time disappears.

Why it works: Never boring, conversational, makes long runs manageable

2. Lex Fridman Podcast

Deep conversations that occupy your mind during endurance efforts.

Why it works: Intellectually engaging, long-form, varied topics

3. Acquired

2-4 hour business deep dives perfect for Sunday long runs.

Why it works: Fascinating stories, substantial length, educational

Why Certain Podcasts Work Better for Running

Conversational > Narrative

Interviews and conversations work better than tightly-produced narratives that require close listening.

Engaging > Complex

Interesting topics that don't require intense mental focus. Save deep philosophy for stationary listening.

Match Pace to Content

Easy runs = comedy. Tempo runs = news. Long runs = epic stories.

Complete Episodes

Nothing worse than stopping mid-story. Choose episode lengths that match your planned distance.

Podcasts vs. Music for Running

Podcasts Win For:

  • Long runs: Stories make time disappear
  • Easy pace: No need for music's rhythm
  • Mental engagement: Distraction from discomfort
  • Learning: Productive use of training time

Music Wins For:

  • Speed work: Rhythm helps maintain pace
  • Racing: Energy and motivation
  • Focus on form: Less mental distraction

Tips for Running with Podcasts

Safety First

  • Use bone conduction headphones or one earbud
  • Keep volume low enough to hear traffic/surroundings
  • Extra caution at crossings and intersections

Download Everything

  • Don't rely on cell coverage during runs
  • Download episodes the night before
  • Build a "running queue" for the week

Speed Settings

  • Try 1.25-1.5x speed for faster consumption
  • Match speed to run intensity (slower for hard efforts)
  • Some apps have "smart speed" to remove dead air

Episode Planning

  • Sunday: Queue episodes for the week's runs
  • Match episode length to planned distance
  • Save exciting episodes for hard workout motivation

Sample Weekly Running + Podcast Plan

Monday: Easy 30 min

The Daily or Planet Money (complete story)

Tuesday: Tempo 45 min

Hidden Brain or Freakonomics (engaging but not distracting)

Wednesday: Easy 40 min

SmartLess or Conan (entertaining, light)

Thursday: Intervals 30 min

Short Wave or The Indicator (short bursts)

Friday: Rest/Cross-train

Save complex podcasts for stationary bike

Saturday: Easy 45 min

The Moth or 99% Invisible (stories)

Sunday: Long run 90+ min

Joe Rogan, Lex Fridman, or Acquired (marathon episodes)
