Best My First Million Episodes of All Time

Shaan Puri and Sam Parr turn business brainstorming into appointment listening. These are the episodes that spark ideas, challenge assumptions, and make you want to start something.

🔥 The Absolute Best

"Brainstorming The Best Startup Ideas" (Multiple Episodes)

Why it's iconic: This is the show's bread and butter. Shaan and Sam riff on business opportunities they see in the market—some genius, some absurd, all entertaining.

Key takeaway: Ideas are cheap. Execution is everything. But good ideas are a starting point.

Best quote: "Here's a business idea worth $100 million..."

"The Milk Road Story - How Shaan Made $500k in 6 Months"

Why it's iconic: Shaan breaks down exactly how he built and sold a crypto newsletter for life-changing money. Pure tactical gold.

Key takeaway: Small, focused newsletters can print money if you understand the audience.

Best quote: "I spent 30 minutes a day and made half a million dollars."

"The Hampton Story - Sam's $10M+ Mastermind"

Why it's iconic: Sam shares how he built The Hampton, an exclusive mastermind for 8-figure entrepreneurs. The business model is brilliant.

Key takeaway: High-ticket communities scale better than courses.

"Trends We're Betting On"

Why it's iconic: Shaan and Sam call shots on emerging industries: Web3, AI tools, remote work infrastructure, niche media. Some hit, some miss, all fascinating.

Key takeaway: Study trends before they're obvious. Early movers win.

"Andrew Wilkinson - Building a $1B Holding Company"

Why it's iconic: Andrew runs Tiny, a Berkshire Hathaway for internet businesses. He breaks down his acquisition playbook.

Key takeaway: Buy boring, profitable businesses and hold them forever.

💡 Best Business Idea Episodes

"Boring Businesses That Print Money"

Shaan and Sam celebrate unsexy, profitable businesses: car washes, laundromats, HVAC companies. Inspiration for operators, not just tech founders.

"Underrated Business Models"

Holdco structures, roll-ups, aggregators, and other meta-businesses that buy/build companies at scale.

"Opportunities in AI"

Early takes on AI tools, vertical SaaS powered by LLMs, and picks-and-shovels plays in the AI gold rush.

"Newsletter Businesses"

The Morning Brew model dissected. How to build, grow, and monetize a media business in 2020s.

👤 Best Founder Interviews

"Sahil Bloom - How He Built a 1M+ Twitter Following"

Sahil breaks down content strategy, personal branding, and how he monetized attention into businesses.

"Codie Sanchez - Buying Boring Businesses"

Codie teaches how to acquire cash-flowing small businesses (laundromats, car washes) instead of building startups.

"Alex Hormozi - $100M Offers"

Alex shares his framework for creating irresistible offers. One of the most tactical episodes ever.

"Naval Ravikant - Building Wealth"

Naval's philosophy on leverage, specific knowledge, and long-term thinking. Timeless wisdom.

"Nick Huber - Storage Unit Empire"

Nick built a storage facility business to $100M+ valuation. Unsexy, profitable, genius.

🧠 Best Mental Models & Frameworks

"The Billionaire's Framework for Decision Making"

Shaan breaks down Charlie Munger's mental models applied to modern business.

"How to Validate Business Ideas Fast"

Testing demand before building. Pre-sales, landing pages, smoke tests—all the hacks.

"The 4 Types of Luck"

Naval's framework on luck: random, persistent motion, prepared mind, unique skill. Game-changing perspective.

"Shaan's Life Frameworks"

How Shaan thinks about time, money, relationships, and happiness. Philosophical yet practical.

💰 Best "How We Made Money" Stories

"The Hustle - How Sam Built & Sold for 8 Figures"

Sam's origin story: building a media company from scratch and selling to HubSpot.

"Shaan's Twitch Failure → Success"

Shaan tried to build a Twitch competitor, failed spectacularly, then sold Blab to Twitter. Lessons from the trenches.

"How We Made $1M in 30 Days"

Rapid execution stories from Sam and Shaan's playbook. Move fast, test, scale.

🎙️ Best Debates & Hot Takes

"Is College a Scam?"

Shaan and Sam debate ROI on college vs. starting businesses. Polarizing and fun.

"Remote Work: The Future or a Fad?"

Recorded during COVID. Some predictions aged well, others didn't. Still fascinating.

"Crypto: Revolution or Ponzi?"

Pre-FTX collapse. Shaan's bullish, Sam's skeptical. The debate format works perfectly.

🚀 Best Industry Deep Dives

"The Creator Economy Explained"

Platforms, monetization models, and why everyone is becoming a media company.

"SaaS Metrics That Actually Matter"

CAC, LTV, churn, retention—explained for non-finance people. Super useful.

"E-Commerce in 2024"

DTC brands, Amazon aggregators, Shopify ecosystems, and where the puck is going.

"Web3 Opportunities"

NFTs, DAOs, tokenization. Some aged poorly, but the frameworks still apply.

🎯 What Makes a Great MFM Episode

The Chemistry

Shaan and Sam finish each other's sentences. They riff, disagree, build on ideas in real-time. The dynamic is everything.

The Tactical Insights

Unlike most business podcasts, MFM gives you actual tactics: "Here's how to do X. Here's the cost. Here's the timeline."

The Idea Generation

Every episode sparks at least one "wait, that's actually genius" moment. Even if you don't execute, your brain gets rewired.

The Vulnerability

Shaan and Sam share failures openly. The Twitch competitor that flopped. The newsletter that didn't work. Real talk.

📚 Lessons from 500+ Episodes

1. Speed Beats Perfection

Launch fast, iterate faster. Both Shaan and Sam move at 10x speed compared to normal founders.

2. Ideas Are Abundant, Execution Is Rare

They generate 10 business ideas per episode. Only execution separates winners from dreamers.

3. Boring Can Be Brilliant

Car washes, laundromats, storage units—unsexy businesses print money while startups burn cash.

4. Build in Public

Shaan and Sam share their wins, losses, and live experiments. Transparency builds trust and opportunity.

5. Niche Down, Then Scale

The Milk Road started as a tiny crypto newsletter. Focused execution → massive outcome.

🎧 How to Listen to MFM

Start with Idea Episodes

The brainstorming episodes are the most fun and immediately valuable. Jump in anywhere.

Take Notes

Ideas fly by fast. Pause and write down the ones that spark something. Revisit your notes monthly.

Listen at 1.5x

Shaan and Sam talk fast already. 1.5x keeps the energy high without losing comprehension.

Don't Binge

Give yourself time to digest ideas. One episode per commute/workout is perfect.
