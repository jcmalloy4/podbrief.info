Best Podcasts to Fall Asleep To 2026

Published March 11, 2026 | Health & Wellness

Let Your Mind Drift Off

Struggling to fall asleep? The right podcast can quiet your racing thoughts and lull you into deep sleep. These shows are specifically designed (or perfectly suited) to help you drift off peacefully.

Best Podcasts Designed for Sleep

1. Sleep With Me

The king of sleep podcasts. Dearest Scooter tells meandering, pointless stories in a soothing monotone voice specifically designed to bore you to sleep. Sounds weird, works brilliantly.

Why it works: Engaging enough to stop anxious thoughts, boring enough to let you drift off

Episode length: 60 minutes

2. Nothing Much Happens

Kathryn Nicolai reads cozy bedtime stories for grown-ups. Each story is read twice—the second time slower. Soft voice, gentle stories, pure relaxation.

Why it works: Creates safe, calm mental images that replace worry

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

3. Sleepy

Classic literature and public domain books read in a deliberately sleep-inducing voice. Great if you want something substantive but delivered soothingly.

Why it works: Familiar stories told slowly with no sudden changes in volume

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

4. Get Sleepy

Original bedtime stories combined with relaxation techniques. British narrator with an incredibly calming voice.

Why it works: Combines guided relaxation with storytelling

Episode length: 30-45 minutes

Best Calming Story Podcasts

5. LeVar Burton Reads

Yes, the Reading Rainbow guy. LeVar reads short fiction with his iconic soothing voice. Not designed for sleep, but perfect for it.

Why it works: That voice. You know the one.

6. The Moth (Select Episodes)

Real people telling real stories. Pick slower-paced, personal stories (not intense or dramatic ones). The human voice telling stories is naturally calming.

Why it works: Authentic human connection without screens

7. Story Time with Seth Rogen

Seth Rogen reads children's books and bizarre stories. Unexpectedly perfect for sleep.

Why it works: Gentle humor without excitement, familiar voice

Best Meditation & Mindfulness for Sleep

8. Sleep Meditation Podcast

Guided meditations specifically for falling asleep. Body scans, progressive relaxation, and breathing techniques.

Episode length: 15-30 minutes

9. Meditation Minis

Short 10-15 minute guided meditations. Great for quick sleep induction or middle-of-the-night wake-ups.

Episode length: 10-15 minutes

10. Tracks to Relax

Premium sleep meditations, bedtime stories, and soundscapes designed by sleep experts.

Episode length: 20-60 minutes

Best "Accidentally Good for Sleep" Podcasts

These weren't designed for sleep, but they work perfectly:

11. In Our Time (BBC)

British intellectuals discuss historical and philosophical topics. Substantive but delivered in calm, measured tones. Perfect for the overthinking mind.

Why it works: Interesting enough to follow, calm enough to drift off

12. 99% Invisible

Design and architecture stories told with a soothing voice. Roman Mars' voice is legendary for its sleep-inducing quality.

Why it works: That voice + interesting but not exciting content

13. The History of Rome

Mike Duncan's epic Roman history series. Educational, detailed, and delivered in an even, undramatic tone.

Why it works: Learn while you drift off, wake up smarter

14. Hardcore History (Dan Carlin)

Long-form history (3-6 hours). Fascinating content but Dan's measured pacing and deep voice make it surprisingly good for sleep.

Why it works: So long you'll definitely fall asleep before it ends

What Makes a Good Sleep Podcast?

Calm, Consistent Voice

No sudden volume changes, no high-pitched voices, no excitement. Monotone is your friend here.

Engaging But Not Exciting

You want something to occupy your mind (so it stops racing) but not so exciting that you stay awake.

Good: Bedtime stories, history, gentle philosophy
Bad: True crime, thriller audiobooks, intense debates

Appropriate Length

Ideally 30-60 minutes so it's still playing as you drift off, but not so long you wake to it at 3 AM.

No Ads or Sudden Noises

Premium versions or ad-free podcasts are worth it for sleep. Nothing ruins sleep faster than a loud ad.

How to Use Podcasts for Better Sleep

Set a Sleep Timer

Most podcast apps have sleep timers. Set it for 30-45 minutes so the podcast shuts off after you're asleep.

Apple Podcasts: Built-in sleep timer
Overcast: Sleep timer in player
Pocket Casts: Sleep timer in settings

Use a Low Volume

It should be barely audible. You're not trying to hear every word, just occupy your mind gently.

Pick Familiar Content for Repeat

Once you find an episode that works, save it and reuse it. Familiarity is soothing for sleep.

Avoid Visual Stimulation

Use audio-only apps. Don't watch YouTube videos—the screen light will keep you awake.

Create a Routine

Same podcast at the same time trains your brain: "This voice = sleep time."

Why Podcasts Help

  • Reduces anxiety: Occupies the default mode network (mind-wandering areas)
  • Consistent audio: Masks environmental noises
  • Ritual signaling: Cues your brain that it's sleep time
  • Distraction from thoughts: Breaks the worry loop

Best Practices

  • Dim lights 30 minutes before bed
  • Keep room temperature cool (65-68°F optimal)
  • Start podcast as you're settling in, not wide awake in bed
  • Combine with sleep hygiene: no caffeine after 2 PM, no screens 1 hour before bed

Best Podcasts for Different Sleep Needs

Trouble Falling Asleep

Try: Sleep With Me, Nothing Much Happens

These are specifically designed to help you drift off in the first place.

Middle-of-Night Wake-Ups

Try: Meditation Minis, Sleep Meditation Podcast

Shorter episodes perfect for getting back to sleep quickly.

Anxious Mind

Try: In Our Time, Sleepy

Substantive content occupies your thoughts without exciting them.

Prefer Stories

Try: Nothing Much Happens, Get Sleepy, LeVar Burton Reads

Narrative format provides comforting structure.

Like Learning

Try: History of Rome, Hardcore History, 99% Invisible

Educational content delivered calmly.

Avoid These for Sleep

  • True crime: Too suspenseful, triggers stress response
  • Comedy: Laughter wakes you up
  • News/politics: Anxiety-inducing
  • Business/productivity: Engages problem-solving brain
  • Intense music: Stimulating rather than calming

Sample Sleep Podcast Rotation

Monday-Wednesday

Thursday-Friday

Nothing Much Happens (variety while staying in sleep mode)

Weekend

As Needed

The Sleep Podcast Starter Kit

  1. Download 3-4 episodes of Sleep With Me
  2. Download 3-4 episodes of Nothing Much Happens
  3. Download 2-3 Meditation Minis for emergencies
  4. Set up sleep timer on your podcast app
  5. Tonight: start with Sleep With Me, volume barely audible
  6. Give it 3-4 nights to become a habit
