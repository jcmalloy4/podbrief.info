# All-In vs My First Million: Which Business Podcast Is Right for You?

Both All-In and My First Million have become essential listening for entrepreneurs, investors, and business enthusiasts. But which podcast should you choose? Let's compare these two powerhouse shows to help you decide.

## Overview

**All-In Podcast**
- Hosted by Jason Calacanis, Chamath Palihapitiya, David Sacks, and David Friedberg
- Focus: Tech, markets, politics, and venture capital
- Format: Weekly roundtable discussion
- Length: 90-120 minutes

**My First Million**
- Hosted by Shaan Puri and Sam Parr
- Focus: Business ideas, entrepreneurship, and wealth-building
- Format: Conversational deep-dives
- Length: 45-75 minutes

## Content Style

### All-In
The "besties" bring high-level insights from the world of venture capital and tech. Expect:
- Deep dives into market trends
- Political and economic analysis
- Insider perspectives on tech deals
- Debates on policy and business strategy

### My First Million
More focused on actionable business ideas and entrepreneurship:
- Business breakdowns and opportunities
- Startup ideas and trends
- Interviews with successful founders
- Practical wealth-building strategies

## Who Should Listen to Each?

**Choose All-In if you:**
- Want insider tech and VC perspectives
- Follow markets and macroeconomics
- Enjoy political and policy debates
- Prefer longer, in-depth discussions
- Are interested in high-level strategy

**Choose My First Million if you:**
- Want to learn about business opportunities
- Prefer actionable entrepreneurial insights
- Enjoy exploring new business ideas
- Like shorter, more focused episodes
- Want practical wealth-building advice

## Production Quality

Both podcasts have professional production values:
- All-In: High-quality video and audio, occasional live events
- My First Million: Excellent audio quality, regular cadence

## Monetization & Business Angle

**All-In:** The hosts are all successful investors and entrepreneurs, bringing real-world credibility to their discussions about markets, tech, and policy.

**My First Million:** Focuses heavily on business opportunities and entrepreneurship, with hosts actively building and investing in businesses themselves.

## Best Episodes to Start With

### All-In
1. "Bestie Brawl" episodes (debates on hot topics)
2. Market analysis episodes during major events
3. Tech company deep-dives

### My First Million
1. "Business Idea" episodes
2. Interviews with successful entrepreneurs
3. "Trends" episodes highlighting opportunities

## The Verdict

You don't have to choose! Many listeners subscribe to both:

- **All-In** for macro trends, market insights, and high-level tech/policy discussion
- **My First Million** for business ideas, entrepreneurial tactics, and opportunity spotting

If you only have time for one:
- **Choose All-In** if you're more interested in investing, markets, and tech industry insider perspectives
- **Choose My First Million** if you're looking to start a business or find new opportunities
