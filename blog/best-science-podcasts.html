Best Science Podcasts 2026 - Top Shows for Curious Minds

Published February 24, 2026

Science podcasts make complex topics accessible, bringing cutting-edge research, fascinating discoveries, and expert insights to your commute. Whether you're interested in neuroscience, physics, biology, or space exploration, these are the best science podcasts of 2026.

What Makes a Great Science Podcast?

  • Expert hosts: Scientists and researchers who truly understand the material
  • Clear explanations: Complex topics made accessible without dumbing down
  • Up-to-date research: Coverage of the latest scientific discoveries
  • Engaging storytelling: Science as narrative, not just facts

Top Science Podcasts in 2026

1. Huberman Lab

Dr. Andrew Huberman brings neuroscience and biology to life in Huberman Lab. From sleep optimization to dopamine regulation, each episode provides science-backed protocols you can apply to your life.

Best for: Applied neuroscience and health optimization

2. Radiolab

The pioneers of narrative science storytelling. Radiolab blends investigation, philosophy, and cutting-edge sound design to explore scientific and philosophical questions.

Best for: Creative science storytelling

3. Science Vs

Wendy Zukerman takes on fads, trends, and controversial topics with rigorous scientific analysis. Science Vs separates fact from fiction on everything from detox diets to gun control.

Best for: Myth-busting and critical thinking

4. StarTalk Radio

Neil deGrasse Tyson makes astrophysics fun and accessible. StarTalk blends celebrity guests, comedy, and serious science for an entertaining exploration of the cosmos.

Best for: Space and astrophysics

5. Ologies

Alie Ward interviews experts in every "-ology" imaginable, from volcanology to synesthesiology. Ologies proves there's no such thing as a boring science if you have the right guide.

Best for: Exploring diverse scientific fields

6. The Infinite Monkey Cage

Physicist Brian Cox and comedian Robin Ince bring British wit to science discussions. The Infinite Monkey Cage makes physics, astronomy, and mathematics hilarious.

Best for: Science with humor

7. Science Friday

The NPR classic covering the latest science news and discoveries. Science Friday has been making science accessible since 1991.

Best for: Weekly science news

8. Hidden Brain

Shankar Vedantam explores the unconscious patterns that drive human behavior. Hidden Brain uses science and storytelling to reveal how our brains shape our lives.

Best for: Psychology and behavioral science

9. The Portal (when active)

Eric Weinstein brings together physics, mathematics, economics, and philosophy in deep conversations with brilliant thinkers. The Portal isn't for casual listening, but rewards patient attention.

Best for: Deep theoretical discussions

10. Stuff You Should Know (Science Episodes)

Josh and Chuck from Stuff You Should Know make science accessible and fun. Their chemistry and thorough research make complex topics digestible.

Best for: Accessible science explainers

Finding Your Perfect Science Podcast

For practical health tips: Start with Huberman Lab

For space and physics: Try StarTalk Radio or The Infinite Monkey Cage

For psychology: Hidden Brain is your best bet

For general science news: Science Friday covers everything

For myth-busting: Science Vs uses rigorous research to debunk claims

Why Listen to Science Podcasts?

Science podcasts help you understand the world better, make informed decisions about your health, and satisfy your curiosity about how things work. They're perfect for commutes, workouts, or anytime you want to learn something new.
