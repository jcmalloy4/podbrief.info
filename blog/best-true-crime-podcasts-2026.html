Best True Crime Podcasts 2026 - Mysteries, Investigations & Cold Cases

Published March 9, 2026 | 10 min read

Obsessed with true crime? These podcasts deliver gripping investigations, cold case mysteries, criminal justice deep dives, and stories that will keep you listening episode after episode.

🔍 Serial

Why listen: The podcast that started the true crime boom. Sarah Koenig investigates one case per season with journalistic rigor and narrative storytelling.

  • Host: Sarah Koenig, investigative journalist (This American Life)
  • Best for: Deep investigations, season-long narratives, journalistic quality
  • Format: Season-long investigations (8-12 episodes per case)
  • Famous seasons: Season 1 (Adnan Syed murder case), Season 3 (Cleveland courthouse)
  • Production: Highest quality investigative journalism
  • Impact: Literally created the modern true crime podcast genre

🎙️ Crime Junkie

Why listen: Ashley Flowers delivers tightly produced, binge-worthy true crime stories every week. Perfect pacing and storytelling.

  • Host: Ashley Flowers (with co-host Brit Prawat)
  • Best for: Weekly true crime fix, well-researched cases, great pacing
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min case investigations
  • Style: Ashley tells the story, Brit reacts (like you would)
  • Coverage: Unsolved cases, cold cases, solved mysteries
  • Audience: Massive fanbase, one of the biggest true crime podcasts

🔪 Casefile True Crime

Why listen: Anonymous Australian host delivers meticulously researched cases with no banter, just pure storytelling. Respectful and gripping.

  • Host: Anonymous narrator (Australian accent)
  • Best for: Detailed case narratives, international cases, respectful approach
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min case deep dives
  • Style: Serious, no-nonsense, fact-focused storytelling
  • Coverage: Global cases (US, Australia, Europe, Asia)
  • Why it works: Lets the story speak for itself, no unnecessary commentary

🎧 My Favorite Murder

Why listen: Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark blend true crime with comedy, mental health discussions, and genuine friendship. Stay sexy, don't get murdered.

  • Hosts: Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark (comedians)
  • Best for: True crime + comedy, mental health awareness, community
  • Episode format: Weekly 90-120 min episodes (2 stories + banter)
  • Format: Each host tells a murder story, lots of tangents and laughs
  • Community: "Murderinos" - massive, engaged fanbase
  • Approach: Respectful to victims, irreverent about everything else

🔦 In the Dark

Why listen: APM Reports investigates wrongful convictions and criminal justice failures with Pulitzer-level journalism.

  • Host: Madeleine Baran, investigative reporter
  • Best for: Investigative journalism, wrongful convictions, systemic failures
  • Format: Season-long investigations (2 seasons so far)
  • Season 1: Jacob Wetterling abduction case
  • Season 2: Curtis Flowers wrongful conviction (led to Supreme Court case)
  • Impact: Real-world change from investigations

🎭 Criminal

Why listen: Phoebe Judge's soothing voice explores crime stories from unexpected angles - not just murder, but all kinds of crime and justice.

  • Host: Phoebe Judge (voice like butter)
  • Best for: Unique crime stories, varied perspectives, beautiful production
  • Episode format: Weekly 20-40 min standalone stories
  • Topics: Not just murder - fraud, heists, odd laws, justice system quirks
  • Tone: Thoughtful, empathetic, never sensational
  • Production: Radiotopia network, exceptional quality

🔍 S-Town

Why listen: From the Serial team, this 7-episode series about a murder in Alabama becomes something much deeper and more human. Unforgettable.

  • Host: Brian Reed, journalist
  • Best for: Narrative storytelling, small-town mystery, human complexity
  • Format: Limited series (7 episodes, all released at once)
  • Story: Starts as murder investigation, becomes profound character study
  • Warning: Deeply emotional, not typical true crime
  • Legacy: One of the most downloaded podcasts ever

🎙️ Dateline NBC

Why listen: TV's most famous true crime show in podcast form. Keith Morrison's voice, Andrea Canning's investigations - full TV episodes as podcasts.

  • Hosts: Keith Morrison, Andrea Canning, Josh Mankiewicz (NBC correspondents)
  • Best for: TV-quality investigations, full cases per episode
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min full episodes
  • Content: Audio versions of TV episodes + podcast-exclusive content
  • Strength: NBC's investigative resources and access

🔪 Bear Brook

Why listen: New Hampshire Public Radio investigates bodies found in barrels, using genealogy and DNA to solve a decades-old mystery.

  • Host: Jason Moon, investigative reporter
  • Best for: Cold case investigation, DNA genealogy, scientific detective work
  • Format: Limited series (8 episodes)
  • Story: Unidentified bodies in New Hampshire woods, solved via genetic genealogy
  • Significance: Pioneering use of DNA in cold cases

🎧 Sword and Scale

Why listen: Uses 911 calls, police interviews, and court audio to tell disturbing true crime stories. Not for the faint of heart.

  • Host: Mike Boudet (controversial host)
  • Best for: Raw audio evidence, disturbing cases, unfiltered content
  • Episode format: Bi-weekly deep dives using real recordings
  • Warning: Graphic content, actual crime scene audio
  • Approach: Heavy use of primary audio sources
  • Note: Host is polarizing, content is intense

🔦 Dr. Death

Why listen: Wondery's investigation into Dr. Christopher Duntsch, a neurosurgeon who maimed and killed patients. Medical malpractice thriller.

  • Host: Laura Beil, journalist
  • Best for: Medical crime, systemic failures, narrative storytelling
  • Format: Limited series (10 episodes)
  • Story: Surgeon who intentionally(?) harmed patients, system that protected him
  • Production: High-budget Wondery production
  • Impact: Led to TV adaptation on Peacock

🎭 Accused

Why listen: Elizabeth Vargas (journalist) investigates cases where guilt is uncertain. Are they innocent or guilty?

  • Host: Elizabeth Vargas, journalist
  • Best for: Ambiguous cases, wrongful convictions, criminal justice
  • Format: Season-long investigations
  • Approach: Questions whether convicted person is actually guilty
  • Production: High-quality investigative journalism

🔍 The Clearing

Why listen: A daughter investigates her father, a suspected serial killer. Deeply personal and haunting.

  • Host: Josh Dean, with April Balascio (daughter of suspect)
  • Best for: Personal investigations, family secrets, serial killers
  • Format: Limited series (8 episodes)
  • Story: Woman realizes her father might be a serial killer, reports him to police
  • Emotional depth: First-person account from daughter

🔑 Key Themes Across Top True Crime Podcasts

  • Victim-focused: Best podcasts respect victims and families
  • Investigative rigor: Real research, not just Wikipedia summaries
  • Criminal justice issues: Many explore systemic problems (wrongful convictions, police failures)
  • Narrative storytelling: Best shows tell stories, not just recite facts
  • Ethical responsibility: Top podcasts balance entertainment with respect

📚 Recommended Listening Path

For beginners: Serial Season 1 → Crime Junkie → Criminal

For deep investigations: Serial → In the Dark → Bear Brook

For comedy + crime: My Favorite Murder → Wine & Crime → And That's Why We Drink

For serious journalism: In the Dark → S-Town → Dr. Death

🚀 Start Here

New to true crime podcasts? Begin with these:

  • Serial: Season 1, Episode 1 (the podcast that started it all)
  • Crime Junkie: "MURDERED: Danielle Hallan" (classic episode)
  • Criminal: "Episode 51: Money Tree" (unique, not typical murder)
  • S-Town: Chapter 1 (binge the whole thing if you love it)

Bottom line: The best true crime podcast depends on your style. Want investigative journalism? Serial or In the Dark. Want weekly cases? Crime Junkie or Casefile. Want comedy? My Favorite Murder. Start with Serial Season 1 to understand why everyone's obsessed.
