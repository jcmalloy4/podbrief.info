Best True Crime Podcasts 2026

Published March 10, 2026

True crime podcasts have become one of the most popular genres, offering deep dives into real mysteries, investigations, and criminal cases. Whether you're interested in cold cases, serial killers, or wrongful convictions, these podcasts deliver gripping storytelling backed by thorough research.

Why Listen to True Crime Podcasts?

  • Real Stories: Learn about actual crimes, investigations, and the criminal justice system
  • Deep Investigation: Detailed research and interviews reveal new perspectives on cases
  • Mystery & Suspense: Follow along as hosts uncover clues and pursue justice
  • Social Impact: Many podcasts help solve cold cases or expose wrongful convictions

Top True Crime Podcasts

Serial

The podcast that started the true crime boom. Serial investigates one story over multiple episodes, diving deep into complex cases with meticulous reporting. From the Adnan Syed case to the Bowe Bergdahl story, each season is a masterclass in investigative journalism.

My Favorite Murder

Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark blend true crime with comedy, creating a unique format that's both entertaining and respectful. They cover famous murders and lesser-known cases, with a devoted fanbase called "Murderinos."

Crime Junkie

Ashley Flowers and Brit Prawat deliver weekly episodes covering disappearances, murders, and mysteries. Known for their storytelling and "full body chills" moments, Crime Junkie is one of the most popular true crime podcasts.

Morbid: A True Crime Podcast

Hosted by Alaina Urquhart (autopsy technician) and Ash Kelley, Morbid combines true crime with dark humor. They cover serial killers, hauntings, and mysterious deaths with detailed research and engaging banter.

Casefile True Crime

An anonymous Australian host delivers fact-based, detailed case studies in a serious tone. Casefile is known for its thorough research and respectful treatment of victims, covering cases from around the world.

Detective

From The New Yorker, Detective follows real detectives as they investigate unsolved murders. It offers an inside look at how homicide investigations actually work, with access to case files and detective interviews.

Bear Brook

This investigative podcast unravels a decades-old cold case involving four bodies found in barrels in New Hampshire. It's a gripping example of how DNA technology and persistent investigation can solve cold cases.

In the Dark

APM Reports investigates flawed criminal investigations. Season 1 examined the Jacob Wetterling case, while Season 2 exposed major problems in the Curtis Flowers case, leading to his release after decades in prison.

What Makes a Great True Crime Podcast?

  • Thorough Research: Access to case files, interviews, and expert analysis
  • Respectful Storytelling: Treating victims and families with dignity
  • Compelling Narrative: Clear storytelling that keeps you engaged
  • New Information: Original reporting or fresh perspectives on cases
  • Social Justice Focus: Advocating for victims or exposing injustice

True Crime Subgenres

Cold Cases & Unsolved Mysteries

Podcasts like The Vanished and Trace Evidence focus on unsolved disappearances and murders, often bringing new attention to forgotten cases.

Serial Killers

Last Podcast on the Left and Serial Killers (Parcast) dive deep into the psychology and crimes of history's most infamous killers.

Wrongful Convictions

Wrongful Conviction and Truth & Justice investigate cases where innocent people were imprisoned, often working to exonerate them.

Historical True Crime

Red Collar covers white-collar and corporate crimes, while Criminal tells lesser-known stories from throughout history.

How to Choose the Right True Crime Podcast

  • Tone Preference: Do you want serious journalism or comedy mixed in?
  • Case Type: Serial killers, missing persons, legal injustice, or historical crimes?
  • Format: Single-episode stories or multi-part deep dives?
  • Episode Length: Quick 30-minute stories or 2-hour investigations?

The Impact of True Crime Podcasts

True crime podcasts have had real-world impact:

  • Helped solve cold cases by bringing new attention and tips
  • Exposed wrongful convictions, leading to exonerations
  • Raised awareness about missing persons cases
  • Highlighted systemic issues in criminal justice
  • Created communities of engaged listeners who support victims' families
