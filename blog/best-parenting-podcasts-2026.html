Best Parenting Podcasts 2026 - Expert Advice for Modern Parents

Published March 9, 2026 | 9 min read

Navigating parenthood? These podcasts deliver evidence-based advice, practical strategies, and reassurance from pediatricians, psychologists, educators, and experienced parents on raising happy, healthy kids.

👶 The Longest Shortest Time

Why listen: Honest, funny, and deeply researched stories about the early years of parenting. Makes you feel less alone in the chaos.

  • Host: Hillary Frank, parent and journalist
  • Best for: New parents, toddler years, real parenting stories
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min narrative episodes
  • Topics: Pregnancy, birth, infancy, toddlerhood, work-life balance
  • Tone: Vulnerable, funny, relatable, evidence-based
  • Note: Show ended but huge archive worth exploring

🧠 Good Inside with Dr. Becky

Why listen: Clinical psychologist Dr. Becky Kennedy delivers practical scripts and strategies for tough parenting moments with empathy and boundaries.

  • Host: Dr. Becky Kennedy, clinical psychologist
  • Best for: Discipline strategies, tantrums, sibling rivalry, emotional regulation
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min episodes (advice + Q&A)
  • Philosophy: Kids are good inside; behavior is communication
  • Topics: Tantrums, boundaries, anxiety, big feelings, scripts for parents
  • Popularity: Massive following, evidence-based + compassionate

🎙️ Unruffled by Janet Lansbury

Why listen: RIE parenting expert Janet Lansbury answers parent questions with respectful, calm approaches to discipline and development.

  • Host: Janet Lansbury, parenting educator and RIE associate
  • Best for: Respectful parenting, toddler behavior, boundaries
  • Episode format: Weekly 20-30 min Q&A episodes
  • Philosophy: Respect-based parenting, trust the child
  • Topics: Setting limits, tantrums, sleep, independence
  • Tone: Calm, reassuring, practical

🔬 Parenting Beyond Discipline

Why listen: Erin Walsh brings neuroscience and psychology research to parenting with practical, brain-based strategies.

  • Host: Erin Walsh, parenting educator
  • Best for: Brain-based parenting, emotional intelligence, discipline
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min episodes
  • Approach: Neuroscience + attachment + practical strategies
  • Topics: Tantrums, power struggles, sibling conflict, screen time

📚 What Fresh Hell: Laughing in the Face of Motherhood

Why listen: Two moms (Amy and Margaret) tackle parenting challenges with humor, research, and reassurance. You're not alone!

  • Hosts: Amy Wilson and Margaret Ables (comedians and moms)
  • Best for: Parenting humor, common challenges, sanity checks
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-40 min episodes
  • Topics: Picky eating, bedtime battles, school issues, mom guilt
  • Tone: Funny, relatable, evidence-informed but not preachy
  • Community: Makes parenting feel less isolating

🎧 The Mom Hour

Why listen: Two friends (Meagan and Sarah) discuss the realities of motherhood, from toddlers to teens, with honesty and warmth.

  • Hosts: Meagan Francis and Sarah Powers (moms and writers)
  • Best for: Mom friendship, real talk, all ages of kids
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min conversations
  • Topics: Parenting stages, self-care, relationships, work-life balance
  • Vibe: Like coffee with friends who "get it"

👨‍⚕️ Pediatrics on Call

Why listen: Pediatrician Dr. Jennifer Shu answers parent questions about child health, development, and medical concerns.

  • Host: Dr. Jennifer Shu, pediatrician and medical editor
  • Best for: Health questions, developmental milestones, medical advice
  • Episode format: Weekly 15-20 min Q&A
  • Topics: Illness, vaccines, growth, sleep, nutrition
  • Authority: AAP medical editor, evidence-based advice

🎙️ The Mindful Mama Podcast

Why listen: Hunter Clarke-Fields teaches mindfulness practices for parents to reduce reactivity and increase presence.

  • Host: Hunter Clarke-Fields, mindfulness mentor and parent educator
  • Best for: Mindful parenting, emotional regulation, reducing yelling
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min episodes
  • Approach: Mindfulness + positive discipline
  • Topics: Managing anger, staying calm, presence, self-compassion

📖 Read-Aloud Revival

Why listen: Sarah Mackenzie inspires families to build read-aloud habits with book recommendations and literacy strategies.

  • Host: Sarah Mackenzie, author and reading advocate
  • Best for: Family reading, book recommendations, literacy
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min episodes
  • Topics: Read-aloud strategies, age-appropriate books, raising readers
  • Bonus: Incredible book lists and resources

🧒 Raising Good Humans

Why listen: Aliza Pressman (Mount Sinai psychologist) translates child development research into actionable parenting strategies.

  • Host: Dr. Aliza Pressman, developmental psychologist
  • Best for: Evidence-based parenting, child development, modern challenges
  • Episode format: Weekly interviews + solo episodes
  • Topics: Screen time, social media, resilience, emotional health
  • Guests: Researchers, educators, psychologists

🎧 One Bad Mother

Why listen: Biz Ellis and Theresa Thorn celebrate imperfect parenting with humor and encouragement. You're a good mom having a bad day.

  • Hosts: Biz Ellis and Theresa Thorn
  • Best for: Parenting humor, guilt relief, real talk
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min conversations
  • Catchphrase: "You're a good mom having a bad day"
  • Topics: Mom guilt, parenting fails, self-care, honesty

👪 The Dad Edge

Why listen: Larry Hagner interviews fathers and experts about being present, intentional dads while balancing work and family.

  • Host: Larry Hagner, father and coach
  • Best for: Fatherhood, work-life balance, intentional parenting
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min interviews
  • Topics: Father-child bonding, marriage, career, health
  • Community: Active dad community

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Parenting Podcasts

  • Connection over perfection: Best parenting podcasts emphasize relationship-building
  • Evidence-based + compassionate: Research-backed advice delivered with empathy
  • You're not alone: Parenting is hard; community and honesty matter
  • Practical scripts: Top shows give you actual words to say in tough moments
  • Age-specific advice: Different stages need different strategies

📚 Recommended Listening by Age

Pregnancy & babies: The Longest Shortest Time → Pediatrics on Call

Toddlers (1-3): Good Inside with Dr. Becky → Unruffled → What Fresh Hell

Preschool & elementary (3-10): Good Inside → Raising Good Humans → The Mom Hour

Tweens & teens: Raising Good Humans → Good Inside (teen episodes)

For dads specifically: The Dad Edge

🚀 Start Here

New to parenting podcasts? Begin with these episodes:

  • Good Inside: "Tantrums: What's Really Going On" (immediately useful)
  • What Fresh Hell: "Picky Eating" (relatable and reassuring)
  • Unruffled: "Setting Limits Without Stress" (game-changing approach)
  • The Longest Shortest Time: "The First Six Weeks" (essential for new parents)

Bottom line: The best parenting podcast depends on your kid's age and your biggest challenge. Toddler tantrums? Dr. Becky. Need reassurance? What Fresh Hell. Want research? Raising Good Humans. Feel alone? The Mom Hour. Start with what you're struggling with right now.
