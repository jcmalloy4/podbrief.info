Best Technology Podcasts 2026: Top Shows for Tech Enthusiasts

Technology podcasts have become essential listening for anyone working in or following the tech industry. From AI developments to startup strategy, these shows keep you informed, inspired, and ahead of the curve. Here are the best technology podcasts in 2026.

🚀 Best Tech Industry & Startup Podcasts

1. All-In Podcast

Why it's essential: Four billionaire tech investors (Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg) debate technology, markets, politics, and the future. Unfiltered Silicon Valley insider perspectives.

Best for: Understanding tech trends and VC thinking

Format: 90-120 minute weekly roundtables

Browse All-In episodes on PodBrief

2. Acquired

Why it's valuable: Deep dives into technology companies and their stories. From NVIDIA to Microsoft, each episode is an MBA-level case study.

Best for: Understanding how tech giants were built

Format: 2-4 hour company deep dives

Browse Acquired episodes on PodBrief

3. My First Million

Why it's actionable: Shaan Puri and Sam Parr break down tech business ideas, startup trends, and opportunities with zero BS.

Best for: Aspiring tech entrepreneurs

Format: 60-90 minute idea sessions

Browse My First Million episodes on PodBrief

🤖 Best AI & Machine Learning Podcasts

4. Lex Fridman Podcast

Why it's fascinating: MIT researcher hosts deep conversations with AI researchers, engineers, and thinkers. From Sam Altman to Elon Musk.

Best for: Understanding AI's future and implications

Format: 2-4 hour long-form interviews

Browse Lex Fridman episodes on PodBrief

5. The Cognitive Revolution

Why it's timely: Nathan Labenz explores AI developments, capabilities, and implications in real-time as the field evolves.

Best for: Staying current on AI progress

Format: Weekly AI news and interviews

6. Latent Space

Why it's technical: For engineers building with AI. Covers LLMs, agents, tools, and the state of the art.

Best for: AI builders and researchers

Format: 60-90 minute technical discussions

💻 Best Software Engineering Podcasts

7. The Changelog

Why it's comprehensive: Covers open source, software engineering, and the people behind the code. Long-running and consistently excellent.

Best for: Software developers

Format: Weekly developer interviews

8. Software Engineering Daily

Why it's consistent: Daily technical interviews covering databases, infrastructure, programming languages, and more.

Best for: Daily technical learning

Format: 45-60 minute daily episodes

9. Syntax - Tasty Web Development Treats

Why it's practical: Wes Bos and Scott Tolinski cover web development, JavaScript, React, and modern dev tools.

Best for: Web developers

Format: Twice weekly, 30-60 minutes

📱 Best Product & Design Podcasts

10. Lenny's Podcast

Why it's insightful: Lenny Rachitsky interviews product leaders from Airbnb, Stripe, and top tech companies about building products.

Best for: Product managers and designers

Format: Weekly PM interviews

11. The Product Podcast

Why it's practical: Product School hosts discussions with product leaders about strategy, roadmaps, and career growth.

Best for: Aspiring and current PMs

Format: 45-60 minute interviews

🎙️ Best Tech News & Analysis Podcasts

12. Hard Fork

Why it's entertaining: New York Times tech columnists Kevin Roose and Casey Newton break down the week's biggest tech stories with humor.

Best for: Weekly tech news roundup

Format: Weekly, 45-60 minutes

13. The Vergecast

Why it's authoritative: The Verge's editorial team discusses consumer tech, gadgets, platforms, and tech culture.

Best for: Consumer tech enthusiasts

Format: Weekly tech news and reviews

14. Decoder with Nilay Patel

Why it's revealing: Verge editor-in-chief interviews tech CEOs and leaders, asking tough questions about strategy and decision-making.

Best for: Understanding tech leadership

Format: Weekly CEO interviews

🔐 Best Security & Privacy Podcasts

15. Darknet Diaries

Why it's gripping: True stories from the dark side of the internet - hacking, cybercrime, and security breaches told like thrillers.

Best for: Security enthusiasts

Format: 45-60 minute story episodes

16. Security Now

Why it's authoritative: Steve Gibson and Leo Laporte discuss security news, vulnerabilities, and privacy with deep technical knowledge.

Best for: Security professionals

Format: Weekly 2-hour technical discussions

🎯 Best Cloud & Infrastructure Podcasts

17. The Cloudcast

Why it's comprehensive: Weekly discussions about cloud computing, AWS, Azure, DevOps, and infrastructure.

Best for: Cloud engineers and architects

Format: Weekly 30-45 minute episodes

18. Screaming in the Cloud

Why it's entertaining: Corey Quinn interviews cloud professionals with humor and sharp insights about AWS, costs, and cloud culture.

Best for: Cloud practitioners

Format: Weekly interviews

By Tech Interest:

  • Tech News: Hard Fork, The Vergecast, Decoder
  • Security: Darknet Diaries, Security Now

By Time Commitment:

  • Short (30-45 min): Syntax, Hard Fork, The Cloudcast
  • Medium (60-90 min): All-In, My First Million, Lenny's Podcast
  • Deep Dive (2+ hours): Acquired, Lex Fridman, Security Now

🎧 Tips for Tech Podcast Listeners

1. Stay Current But Don't Chase Hype

Tech moves fast, but not everything is as revolutionary as claimed. Balance breaking news with timeless principles.

2. Learn from Multiple Perspectives

Listen to both optimists and skeptics. Tech leaders, engineers, and critics all offer valuable viewpoints.

3. Take Notes on Tools & Resources

Podcasts often mention frameworks, libraries, and tools. Keep a running list to explore later.

4. Apply What You Learn

The best learning comes from building. Use podcast insights to inform your projects.

5. Follow Up on Guests

If a guest fascinates you, follow them on Twitter/X, read their blog, or explore their projects.

Final Thoughts

The technology podcast landscape in 2026 is richer than ever. Whether you're building AI applications (Lex Fridman, Latent Space), starting a tech company (All-In, My First Million), or staying current on industry trends (Hard Fork, The Vergecast), there's a show delivering cutting-edge insights.
