Best Technology Podcasts: 20 Shows for Tech News & Innovation (2026)

Stay ahead of the curve with the best technology podcasts. From AI breakthroughs to startup stories, gadget reviews to Silicon Valley insights—these shows keep you informed on everything tech.

Best Technology Podcasts Overview

1. All-In Podcast

Best for: Tech, business, and politics from Silicon Valley insiders

Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, and David Friedberg debate technology trends, startups, economics, and current events. Unfiltered opinions from successful founders and investors.

2. Hard Fork (New York Times)

Best for: AI news and tech policy

Kevin Roose and Casey Newton break down the biggest tech stories each week, with a focus on AI, social media, and how technology is changing society.

3. The Vergecast

Best for: Consumer tech and gadget reviews

The Verge's flagship podcast covering smartphones, laptops, apps, services, and everything tech consumers care about. Great for staying current on releases.

4. This Week in Tech (TWiT)

Best for: Weekly tech news roundup

Leo Laporte and rotating tech journalists discuss the week's biggest tech stories. Long-running (since 2005), comprehensive coverage.

5. Acquired

Best for: Deep dives on tech companies

Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal tell the complete stories behind iconic tech companies—from founding to IPO and beyond. 2-3 hour episodes packed with research.

6. The Changelog

Best for: Software engineering and open source

Conversations with software developers building interesting tools and platforms. Great for developers who want to stay current on new technologies.

7. Waveform (MKBHD)

Best for: Consumer electronics and reviews

Marques Brownlee (MKBHD) and team discuss the latest gadgets, tech trends, and internet culture. Accessible tech talk from one of YouTube's biggest tech creators.

8. a16z Podcast

Best for: Venture capital perspectives on tech trends

Andreessen Horowitz partners and portfolio founders discuss emerging technologies, business models, and where tech is headed.

9. Darknet Diaries

Best for: Cybersecurity and hacking stories

True stories from the dark side of the internet—hackers, data breaches, cyberattacks, and digital espionage. Tech thriller in podcast form.

10. The Tim Ferriss Show

Best for: Tech founder interviews and productivity

While not exclusively tech-focused, Ferriss interviews many tech leaders and explores tools, tactics, and habits of successful people.

11. Reply All

Best for: Internet culture and tech storytelling

Stories about how technology affects our lives—from scams to viral moments to deep investigations. Recently relaunched after hiatus.

12. Decoder (The Verge)

Best for: How tech leaders make decisions

Nilay Patel interviews CEOs and decision-makers at major tech companies about how they run their businesses and navigate challenges.

13. The AI Breakdown

Best for: Daily AI news (15 minutes)

Quick daily updates on AI developments, tools, and implications. Perfect for keeping up with the fast-moving AI space.

14. No Priors (Sarah Guo & Elad Gil)

Best for: AI and machine learning deep dives

Investors Sarah Guo and Elad Gil interview AI researchers, founders, and engineers building the next generation of AI products.

15. Lex Fridman Podcast

Best for: Long-form AI and tech philosophy

Deep conversations with AI researchers, tech leaders, scientists, and thinkers. Technical but accessible discussions about artificial intelligence and the future.

16. The Practical AI Podcast

Best for: Applied AI and machine learning

How businesses and developers are actually using AI today. Practical insights beyond the hype.

17. Techmeme Ride Home

Best for: Daily tech news (15 minutes)

Quick daily rundown of the top tech news stories. Perfect for a commute or afternoon catch-up.

18. The 20VC

Best for: Venture capital and startup insights

Harry Stebbings interviews VCs and founders about startups, fundraising, and building companies. Tech-heavy with business focus.

19. Accidental Tech Podcast

Best for: Apple ecosystem and software development

Three developers discuss Apple, programming, technology, and their lives. Great for Mac/iOS enthusiasts and developers.

20. Land of the Giants

Best for: Deep dives on Big Tech companies

Seasonal podcast from Recode and The Verge examining one major tech company per season (Amazon, Apple, Netflix, etc.). Investigative journalism meets tech history.

How to Choose the Right Tech Podcast

For daily news:

  • Techmeme Ride Home (general tech)
  • The AI Breakdown (AI-specific)
  • Hard Fork (twice weekly, bigger stories)

For consumer tech and gadgets:

  • The Vergecast (comprehensive)
  • Waveform (MKBHD's perspective)
  • This Week in Tech (roundtable format)

For AI and machine learning:

  • The AI Breakdown (daily updates)
  • No Priors (investor/founder insights)
  • Practical AI (applied use cases)
  • Lex Fridman (philosophical deep dives)

For startups and venture capital:

  • All-In Podcast (founder perspectives)
  • a16z Podcast (VC insights)
  • The 20VC (fundraising and growth)

For tech history and storytelling:

  • Acquired (company deep dives)
  • Land of the Giants (investigative seasons)
  • Reply All (internet culture stories)

For developers:

  • The Changelog (software engineering)
  • Accidental Tech Podcast (Apple ecosystem)
  • Practical AI (applied ML)

Final Thoughts

Technology moves fast—these podcasts help you keep up without drowning in noise. Start with 2-3 shows that match your interests, then branch out as you discover specific areas you want to explore deeper.

Daily shows work great for commutes. Long-form shows (Acquired, Lex Fridman, All-In) are better for weekends or dedicated listening time.
