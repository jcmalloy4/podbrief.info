Best Technology Podcasts (2026)

Published February 21, 2026

Stay ahead of the curve. The best technology podcasts of 2026 cover AI, startups, software development, consumer tech, and the future of innovation from industry insiders.

Why Listen to Technology Podcasts?

Technology podcasts help you:

  • Stay current with rapid tech changes
  • Understand AI and emerging tech before they're mainstream
  • Learn from tech leaders and startup founders
  • Spot trends early for career or investment opportunities
  • Deepen technical knowledge in specific domains

Top Technology Podcasts of 2026

1. All-In Podcast

Four tech investors (Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg) debate tech, business, economics, and politics. Insider perspectives on Silicon Valley and beyond.

Best for: Tech industry analysis, venture capital, macro trends

Browse All-In episodes →

2. Lex Fridman Podcast

MIT researcher Lex Fridman interviews scientists, engineers, and thinkers about AI, robotics, physics, and the future of intelligence.

Best for: AI/ML, deep tech, philosophy of technology

3. Hard Fork (The New York Times)

Kevin Roose and Casey Newton break down the biggest tech news each week with humor and insight.

Best for: Tech news, AI developments, consumer tech

4. Acquired

Deep dives into the history and strategy of tech companies like NVIDIA, Apple, Microsoft, and Amazon. 2-4 hour episodes.

Best for: Tech company history, business strategy, competitive analysis

5. Pivot

Kara Swisher and Scott Galloway dissect tech news, business, and politics with sharp analysis and predictions.

Best for: Tech news commentary, market analysis, bold predictions

6. The Vergecast

The Verge's tech journalists discuss the week's biggest tech stories, product reviews, and gadget news.

Best for: Consumer tech, gadgets, product reviews

7. Decoder (The Verge)

Nilay Patel interviews tech CEOs and leaders about how they make decisions and build products.

Best for: Product strategy, leadership, tech CEO interviews

8. This Week in Tech (TWiT)

Leo Laporte and tech journalists roundtable the week's tech news. Long-running (since 2005) and comprehensive.

Best for: Weekly tech news roundup, multiple perspectives

9. Talk Python To Me

Michael Kennedy interviews Python developers and data scientists. Technical but accessible.

Best for: Software development, Python, data science

10. No Priors: AI, ML, Tech

Sarah Guo and Elad Gil interview AI researchers and founders building the future of AI.

Best for: AI/ML, frontier tech, AI startups

Staying Current Without Overwhelm

Tech moves fast. Here's how to keep up:

  • Pick 2-3 podcasts max to follow regularly
  • Choose by interest: AI? Startups? Consumer tech?
  • Skip episodes on topics outside your focus
  • Use 1.5x-2x speed to save time
  • Take notes on actionable insights
