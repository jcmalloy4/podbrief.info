Best Technology Podcasts 2026 - AI, Startups, Silicon Valley & Tech News

Published March 9, 2026 | 10 min read

Stay ahead of tech trends with these podcasts covering artificial intelligence, startups, Silicon Valley culture, and breakthrough innovations from industry insiders and expert journalists.

🚀 All-In Podcast

Why listen: Four elite investors (Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg) debate tech, markets, AI, and policy with insider knowledge and zero filter.

  • Hosts: Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg
  • Best for: AI trends, tech policy, venture capital, market analysis
  • Episode format: Weekly 90-120 min discussions
  • Topics: AI regulation, big tech, startups, crypto, macro trends
  • Unique: Contrarian takes, insider VC perspective, debates between hosts
  • Audience: Founders, investors, tech executives

🤖 Lex Fridman Podcast

Why listen: MIT researcher Lex Fridman interviews AI researchers, tech founders, and scientists about artificial intelligence, robotics, and the future.

  • Host: Lex Fridman, AI researcher at MIT
  • Best for: AI research, deep learning, robotics, autonomous vehicles
  • Episode format: 2-4 hour long-form interviews
  • Guests: Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Yann LeCun, Demis Hassabis, top AI researchers
  • Depth: Technical but accessible, philosophical + practical
  • Unique: Explores both technical details and human implications

🎙️ Acquired

Why listen: Deep-dive case studies on tech companies and acquisitions. Harvard Business School-level analysis in podcast form.

  • Hosts: Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal
  • Best for: Tech company histories, M&A strategy, business building
  • Episode format: Monthly 2-4 hour deep dives
  • Recent tech episodes: NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta
  • Research quality: Months of research per episode
  • Audience: Founders, investors, tech strategists

📰 Hard Fork

Why listen: New York Times tech columnists Kevin Roose and Casey Newton discuss the week's biggest tech stories with insider access and sharp analysis.

  • Hosts: Kevin Roose and Casey Newton (NYT tech journalists)
  • Best for: Weekly tech news, AI developments, big tech scandals
  • Episode format: Weekly 60 min news + analysis
  • Topics: AI regulation, social media, crypto crashes, tech layoffs
  • Production: NYT quality reporting
  • Vibe: Informed but accessible, funny but serious

🧠 The Knowledge Project (Tech Episodes)

Why listen: Shane Parrish interviews tech leaders and thinkers about decision-making, mental models, and building great products.

  • Host: Shane Parrish (Farnam Street)
  • Best for: Strategic thinking, decision frameworks, product building
  • Episode format: Weekly long-form interviews
  • Tech guests: Naval Ravikant, Tobi Lütke, tech founders, VCs
  • Focus: How great leaders think, not just what they built

🎧 The Vergecast

Why listen: The Verge's editorial team discusses the week's tech news, product launches, and industry trends with journalist expertise.

  • Hosts: Nilay Patel, David Pierce, Alex Cranz (The Verge editors)
  • Best for: Consumer tech, product reviews, industry analysis
  • Episode format: 2-3x per week, 60-90 min discussions
  • Topics: iPhone launches, AI products, app reviews, tech policy
  • Strength: Journalist perspective, product expertise

💡 This Week in Tech (TWiT)

Why listen: Leo Laporte and rotating panel of tech journalists discuss the week's biggest tech stories. Running since 2005.

  • Host: Leo Laporte + rotating tech journalist panel
  • Best for: Weekly tech news roundup, broad coverage
  • Episode format: Weekly 2-hour panel discussions
  • Topics: Everything tech - hardware, software, policy, business
  • Legacy: One of the original tech podcasts still going strong

🚀 Lenny's Podcast

Why listen: Product and growth strategies from leaders at the fastest-growing tech companies (Airbnb, Stripe, Notion, Figma).

  • Host: Lenny Rachitsky, former Airbnb PM
  • Best for: Product-led growth, PLG strategies, product management
  • Episode format: Weekly interviews with product leaders
  • Guests: PMs and growth leads from top tech companies
  • Topics: User onboarding, retention, pricing, product strategy

🎙️ The Logan Bartlett Show

Why listen: Redpoint partner Logan Bartlett interviews tech founders and investors about building companies and navigating markets.

  • Host: Logan Bartlett, Redpoint Ventures partner
  • Best for: VC insights, startup strategy, tech investing
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min interviews
  • Guests: Tech founders, VCs, operators
  • Topics: Company building, market timing, fundraising, exits

🤖 The AI Podcast

Why listen: NVIDIA's Noah Kravitz interviews AI researchers, developers, and entrepreneurs building with artificial intelligence.

  • Host: Noah Kravitz (NVIDIA)
  • Best for: AI applications, machine learning, developer perspectives
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min interviews
  • Topics: AI use cases, machine learning, GPU computing
  • Insider access: NVIDIA's position in AI gives unique access

🎧 Decoder with Nilay Patel

Why listen: The Verge's editor-in-chief interviews tech CEOs about how they make decisions and run their companies.

  • Host: Nilay Patel, The Verge Editor-in-Chief
  • Best for: CEO interviews, company structure, decision-making
  • Episode format: Weekly 60 min CEO interviews
  • Guests: Tech CEOs (Spotify, Reddit, Shopify, etc.)
  • Signature: "How do you make decisions?" framework

💻 The Changelog

Why listen: For developers and tech enthusiasts - interviews with open source creators, maintainers, and software leaders.

  • Hosts: Adam Stacoviak and Jerod Santo
  • Best for: Open source, software development, dev tools
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min interviews
  • Audience: Developers, engineers, tech builders
  • Topics: Programming languages, frameworks, dev tools, OSS

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Tech Podcasts

  • AI dominates 2026: Every tech podcast now covers AI developments
  • Insider access matters: Best podcasts have connections to founders/investors
  • Long-form wins: Tech topics need depth; 2+ hour episodes are common
  • Technical + business: Best shows blend engineering with strategy
  • Contrarian thinking: Top podcasts challenge conventional wisdom

📚 Recommended Listening Path

For tech news: Hard Fork → The Vergecast → This Week in Tech

For AI focus: Lex Fridman → The AI Podcast → All-In (AI episodes)

For startup insights: All-In → Lenny's Podcast → Logan Bartlett Show

For company deep dives: Acquired → Decoder → The Knowledge Project

For developers: The Changelog → Syntax → Lex Fridman (tech episodes)

🚀 Start Here

New to tech podcasts? Begin with these episodes:

  • All-In: Any recent episode (pulse on tech industry)
  • Acquired: "NVIDIA Parts 1-3" (masterclass in tech strategy)
  • Lex Fridman: "Sam Altman: OpenAI CEO" (AI future)
  • Hard Fork: Any recent episode (accessible tech news)

Bottom line: The best tech podcast depends on your focus. Want AI deep dives? Lex Fridman. Want tech news? Hard Fork. Want startup strategy? All-In. Want product insights? Lenny's Podcast. Start with weekly news shows, then go deeper.
