Best Technology Podcasts 2026 - Tech News & Innovation

Published February 24, 2026

Technology moves fast, and tech podcasts help you keep up. Whether you're interested in AI, startups, software development, or the latest Silicon Valley drama, these are the best technology podcasts of 2026.

What Makes a Great Technology Podcast?

  • Expert insights: Hosts who understand technology deeply, not just surface-level news
  • Timely coverage: Rapid response to breaking tech news and trends
  • Diverse perspectives: Multiple viewpoints on controversial tech topics
  • Practical takeaways: Actionable insights you can apply to your work or investments

Top Technology Podcasts in 2026

1. All-In Podcast

The "besties" — Chamath, Jason, Sacks, and Friedberg — dissect tech, business, and politics. All-In offers insider perspectives from successful Silicon Valley investors.

Best for: Tech business and venture capital

2. Lex Fridman Podcast

Deep conversations with leading AI researchers, scientists, and technologists. Lex Fridman brings intellectual rigor to discussions about artificial intelligence and the future of technology.

Best for: AI and deep tech discussions

3. Hard Fork

New York Times tech columnists Kevin Roose and Casey Newton cover the week's biggest tech stories. Hard Fork makes complex tech news accessible and entertaining.

Best for: Weekly tech news roundup

4. Pivot

Kara Swisher and Scott Galloway deliver sharp analysis on tech business, policy, and culture. Pivot doesn't hold back on criticizing big tech when warranted.

Best for: Tech business analysis with attitude

5. The Vergecast

The Verge's flagship podcast covers consumer tech, gadgets, and internet culture. The Vergecast combines product reviews with broader tech trends.

Best for: Consumer tech and gadgets

6. This Week in Tech (TWiT)

Leo Laporte has been covering tech news since the podcast's early days. TWiT brings together a panel of tech journalists for lively weekly discussions.

Best for: Comprehensive tech news panel discussions

7. Acquired

Deep dives into tech company histories and business strategies. Acquired episodes run long (3+ hours) but provide unmatched depth on companies like NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Apple.

Best for: Tech company deep dives

8. The TWIML AI Podcast

"This Week in Machine Learning & AI" interviews researchers and practitioners building AI systems. TWIML is essential for anyone working in or studying artificial intelligence.

Best for: Machine learning professionals

9. Accidental Tech Podcast

Three developers (Marco Arment, Casey Liss, John Siracusa) discuss Apple, programming, and tech. ATP offers deep technical insights wrapped in entertaining banter.

Best for: Apple and software development

10. Darknet Diaries

Jack Rhysider tells true stories from the dark side of the internet: hacking, cybercrime, and security breaches. Darknet Diaries makes cybersecurity fascinating.

Best for: Cybersecurity and hacking stories

Finding Your Perfect Technology Podcast

For AI and machine learning: Start with Lex Fridman or TWIML AI

For tech business: All-In or Pivot offer insider perspectives

For weekly news: Hard Fork or TWiT provide comprehensive coverage

For developer content: Accidental Tech Podcast dives deep into programming

For cybersecurity: Darknet Diaries tells gripping security stories

For company analysis: Acquired provides exhaustive business histories

Why Listen to Technology Podcasts?

Technology shapes every aspect of modern life. Tech podcasts help you understand where the industry is heading, make informed investment decisions, and stay relevant in your career. Whether you're a developer, investor, or simply tech-curious, these podcasts provide the insights you need.
