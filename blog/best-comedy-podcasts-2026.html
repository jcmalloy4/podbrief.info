Best Comedy Podcasts 2026

Published March 11, 2026

Comedy podcasts offer laughs, entertainment, and escape from daily stress. From stand-up legends to improv masters, celebrity interviews to absurd character work, these shows deliver consistent hilarity. Whether you want intellectual humor or pure silliness, there's a comedy podcast for you.

Why Listen to Comedy Podcasts?

  • Stress Relief: Laughter reduces cortisol and improves mood
  • Entertainment: Makes commutes, workouts, and chores enjoyable
  • Behind the Scenes: Comedians sharing stories and creative processes
  • Discovery: Find new comedians before they hit mainstream

Top Comedy Podcasts

SmartLess

Jason Bateman, Sean Hayes, and Will Arnett interview celebrities—with a twist: only one host knows who the guest is. The chemistry between the three hosts creates hilarious moments as they navigate surprise guests from all walks of life.

Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend

Conan interviews celebrities, comedians, and interesting people in his signature style—playful, self-deprecating, and genuinely curious. The dynamic with his assistant Sona and producer Matt adds layers of humor to every episode.

Comedy Bang! Bang!

Scott Aukerman hosts this absurdist comedy podcast featuring celebrity interviews mixed with improv character work. Comedians drop in as ridiculous characters, creating unpredictable and hilarious conversations.

WTF with Marc Maron

Stand-up legend Marc Maron conducts deep, honest conversations with comedians, actors, and musicians in his garage. While introspective, Maron's neurotic humor and guest banter create laugh-out-loud moments.

The Daily Show Podcast

Extended segments and behind-the-scenes content from The Daily Show. Political satire, cultural commentary, and interviews with newsmakers—comedy that makes you think.

My Brother, My Brother and Me

Three brothers (the McElroys) give hilariously bad advice to listener questions. Their chemistry, running jokes, and absurd Yahoo Answers create a unique comedy experience that's spawned a passionate fanbase.

How Did This Get Made?

Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael, and Jason Mantzoukas dissect terrible movies with comedic brilliance. Their incredulous reactions and deep dives into plot holes make bad movies hilarious.

The Dollop

Dave Anthony reads a story from American history to Gareth Reynolds, who knows nothing about it. The absurdity of real historical events combined with Gareth's reactions creates comedy gold.

2 Dope Queens

Jessica Williams and Phoebe Robinson host a stand-up showcase featuring diverse comedians. Recorded live with great energy, covering everything from dating to race to pop culture.

The Bonfire

Dan Soder and Big Jay Oakerson deliver uncensored comedy, stories, and commentary. Raunchy, honest, and hilarious—not for the easily offended.

Comedy Podcast Categories

Stand-Up & Comedy Showcases

All Things Comedy, Kill Tony (live stand-up with roasting), and Ari Shaffir's Skeptic Tank feature comedians doing what they do best.

Improv & Character Work

Comedy Bang! Bang!, improv4humans, and Hello from the Magic Tavern (fantasy improv comedy) showcase improvisation at its finest.

Celebrity Interviews

SmartLess, Conan O'Brien, Armchair Expert (Dax Shepard), and Off Camera with Sam Jones blend humor with celebrity conversations.

Bad Movie/TV Reviews

How Did This Get Made?, The Flophouse, and We Hate Movies turn terrible entertainment into comedy.

Pop Culture Commentary

Pod Save America (political comedy), Las Culturistas (pop culture), and Keep It! (culture and politics) mix humor with commentary.

True Crime Comedy

My Favorite Murder, Last Podcast on the Left, and Crime in Sports blend true crime with comedy—dark humor for fans of both genres.

What Makes a Great Comedy Podcast?

  • Host Chemistry: Natural rapport creates spontaneous humor
  • Consistent Quality: Episodes reliably deliver laughs
  • Unique Voice: Distinct style that stands out
  • Good Pacing: Knows when to land a joke and move on
  • Replay Value: Episodes you want to relisten to

Comedy Styles

Intellectual & Witty

Clever wordplay, cultural references, and smart observations. Think Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! or The Bugle.

Absurdist & Surreal

Nonsensical premises, character work, and pure silliness. Comedy Bang! Bang!, Hello from the Magic Tavern.

Observational & Relatable

Everyday situations turned funny. You Made It Weird (Pete Holmes), The Joe Rogan Experience (comedy episodes).

Dark & Edgy

Taboo topics, offensive humor, no sacred cows. Legion of Skanks, The Bonfire, Are You Garbage?

Wholesome & Uplifting

Positive humor without meanness. The Good Place: The Podcast, Everything is Alive (objects being interviewed).

How to Choose the Right Comedy Podcast

  • Humor Style: Witty, absurd, dark, or wholesome?
  • Format: Interviews, improv, storytelling, or commentary?
  • Length: Quick 20-minute laughs or hour-long deep dives?
  • Hosts: Do you already love certain comedians?
  • Offensiveness: Clean comedy or uncensored chaos?

Comedy Podcasts by Mood

Need a Quick Laugh

  • My Brother, My Brother and Me (1 hour, wholesome absurdity)
  • The Daily Show Podcast (short segments)
  • Comedy Bang! Bang! (excerpts)

Long Commute/Road Trip

  • The Dollop (1-2 hours, storytelling)
  • How Did This Get Made? (1.5 hours)
  • WTF with Marc Maron (90 minutes)

Background Entertainment

  • SmartLess (easy to follow)
  • Conan O'Brien (conversational)
  • Armchair Expert (long-form interviews)

Focus Listening

  • Comedy Bang! Bang! (improv requires attention)
  • Hello from the Magic Tavern (ongoing narrative)
  • The Dollop (historical stories with running gags)

Discovering New Comedians

Comedy podcasts are launchpads for rising talent:

  • Kill Tony showcases new comedians every week
  • 2 Dope Queens features diverse up-and-comers
  • All Things Comedy network promotes fresh voices
  • Comedy Bang! Bang! regulars often become stars

The Value of Comedy Podcasts

  • Free entertainment anytime, anywhere
  • More intimate than specials—unfiltered conversations
  • Discover comedians before they blow up
  • Community and inside jokes with fellow listeners
  • Mental health benefits of regular laughter

Start with These Accessible Shows

New to comedy podcasts? Try these beginner-friendly options:

  • SmartLess: Celebrity guests, easy to follow, consistently funny
  • Conan O'Brien: Familiar host, great guests, high production
  • How Did This Get Made?: Bad movies you might know, hilarious reactions
  • My Brother, My Brother and Me: Wholesome, absurd, welcoming community
