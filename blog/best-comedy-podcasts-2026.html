Best Comedy Podcasts 2026 - Funniest Shows to Listen To

Published March 6, 2026 | 7 min read

Need a laugh? Comedy podcasts deliver everything from stand-up sets to hilarious conversations to absurdist improv. Here are the funniest podcasts to brighten your day.

Why Listen to Comedy Podcasts?

Comedy podcasts offer:

  • Stress relief: Laugh away anxiety and tension
  • Free entertainment: Hours of comedy at no cost
  • Diverse styles: From dry wit to slapstick to dark humor
  • Insider access: Comedians being themselves, off-stage
  • Perfect background: Great for commutes, chores, workouts

Top Comedy Podcasts of 2026

1. SmartLess (Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes)

Why it's great: Three comedy legends interview celebrities, with one host surprising the others with the guest. Natural chemistry and genuine laughs.

Best for: Celebrity interviews with comedy gold

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

Memorable guests: Conan O'Brien, Kamala Harris, Margot Robbie, Jimmy Kimmel

2. Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

Why it's great: Conan's genuine curiosity and quick wit make every conversation hilarious. Great mix of comedy and real talk.

Best for: Fans of late-night comedy and clever banter

Episode length: 60 minutes

Highlights: Conan's relationship with assistant Sona, chemistry with guests, random tangents

3. Comedy Bang! Bang! (Scott Aukerman)

Why it's great: Absurdist comedy with comedians playing bizarre characters. Unpredictable, silly, and often brilliantly weird.

Best for: Fans of improv and surreal comedy

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

Style: Fake interviews, character work, running gags

4. My Dad Wrote a Porno (Jamie Morton, James Cooper, Alice Levine)

Why it's great: Jamie reads his father's self-published erotic novel while his friends react in horror and hysterics. Unintentionally hilarious writing.

Best for: Cringe comedy and British humor

Episode length: 30-40 minutes

Warning: NSFW content, definitely headphones-only

5. Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! (NPR)

Why it's great: News quiz show with comedians and celebrity guests. Smart, topical humor about current events.

Best for: News junkies who want to laugh

Episode length: 50 minutes

Format: Weekly news quiz with panelists and listener call-ins

6. The Bugle (Andy Zaltzman, Various Co-hosts)

Why it's great: Satirical news podcast with British wit and wordplay. Political comedy that's smart and silly.

Best for: International news satire

Episode length: 40-50 minutes

Style: Puns, satire, absurdist takes on world events

7. Bad Friends (Andrew Santino & Bobby Lee)

Why it's great: Two comedians with opposite personalities roast each other and riff on life. Chaotic, unpredictable energy.

Best for: Fans of roast-style comedy and chaos

Episode length: 90 minutes

Vibe: Unfiltered, sometimes controversial, always funny

8. No Such Thing As A Fish (QI Elves)

Why it's great: Four researchers from QI share the week's most interesting facts with humor and wit. Educational comedy at its best.

Best for: Trivia lovers who want laughs with their facts

Episode length: 40 minutes

Style: British humor meets fascinating facts

9. How Did This Get Made? (Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael, Jason Mantzoukas)

Why it's great: Comedians dissect bad movies with hilarious commentary. Perfect if you love laughing at terrible films.

Best for: Movie fans and schadenfreude enthusiasts

Episode length: 75-90 minutes

Movies covered: The Room, Face/Off, Jaws: The Revenge, Cats

10. The Dollop (Dave Anthony & Gareth Reynolds)

Why it's great: American history podcast where one host tells bizarre true stories while the other reacts in disbelief. Comedy from real events.

Best for: History buffs with dark humor

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

Topics: Weird historical figures, forgotten scandals, absurd events

Tips for Finding Your Comedy Podcast

1. Match Your Humor Style

  • Dry/British: The Bugle, No Such Thing As A Fish
  • Absurdist: Comedy Bang! Bang!, Hello from the Magic Tavern
  • Roast/Aggressive: Bad Friends, Kill Tony
  • Wholesome: SmartLess, My Brother My Brother and Me

2. Start with a Topic You Like

If you love movies, try "How Did This Get Made?". History fan? "The Dollop". This makes comedy more engaging.

3. Consider Episode Length

  • Short (30-40 min): Wait Wait, My Dad Wrote a Porno
  • Medium (60 min): SmartLess, Conan O'Brien
  • Long (90+ min): Bad Friends, How Did This Get Made?

4. Check Content Warnings

Many comedy podcasts have explicit content. Check ratings before listening at work or around kids.

What Makes Great Comedy Podcasts?

Chemistry

The best comedy podcasts have hosts with natural rapport. SmartLess, Bad Friends, and How Did This Get Made? thrive on host dynamics.

Consistency

Regular release schedules keep audiences engaged. Most top comedy podcasts release weekly.

Authenticity

Listeners can tell when comedians are genuinely having fun vs. phoning it in. The best shows feel like eavesdropping on friends.

Variety

Mixing formats—interviews, improv, commentary—keeps shows fresh over hundreds of episodes.

Laugh More with Comedy Podcasts

Comedy podcasts turn boring commutes into laugh sessions and mundane chores into entertainment. With thousands of hours of free content, you'll never run out of reasons to smile.

From Conan's clever banter to the absurdity of Comedy Bang! Bang!, there's a comedy podcast for every sense of humor. Try a few, find your favorites, and let the laughter begin.
