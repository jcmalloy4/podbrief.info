Best Comedy Podcasts 2026 - Stand-Up, Improv & Hilarious Conversations

Published March 9, 2026 | 9 min read

Need a laugh? These comedy podcasts feature stand-up comedians, improv legends, and hilarious conversations that will have you cracking up during your commute, workout, or downtime.

😂 SmartLess

Why listen: Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes interview celebrities with charm, wit, and genuine chemistry. One host brings a mystery guest the others don't know.

  • Hosts: Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes (A-list actors/comedians)
  • Best for: Celebrity interviews, Hollywood stories, chemistry between hosts
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min interviews
  • Guests: A-listers from film, TV, music, sports (Obama, Conan, Kimmel, etc.)
  • Format: One host surprises the others with mystery guest
  • Vibe: Friends hanging out, genuinely funny banter

🎙️ Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

Why listen: Conan's quick wit and genuine curiosity make every interview hilarious. Plus his assistant Sona and producer Matt add extra comedy.

  • Host: Conan O'Brien, legendary late-night host
  • Best for: Celebrity interviews, improvisational comedy, Conan being Conan
  • Episode format: Weekly 1-hour interviews + banter
  • Guests: Comedians, actors, musicians, politicians
  • Structure: Guest interview + arguing with Sona and Matt
  • Why it works: Conan's improv skills make even boring guests hilarious

🍻 Your Mom's House

Why listen: Tom Segura and Christina P (married stand-up comedians) share wild videos, inside jokes, and absurd humor. Not for the easily offended.

  • Hosts: Tom Segura and Christina Pazsitzky (married comedians)
  • Best for: Dark humor, viral videos, inside jokes that build over years
  • Episode format: 2x per week, 90-120 min episodes
  • Content: Reacting to wild videos, guest comedians, listener stories
  • Warning: Very adult humor, not politically correct
  • Cult following: Massive fanbase with deep lore

🎭 Comedy Bang! Bang!

Why listen: Scott Aukerman hosts absurdist improv comedy with comedians playing characters. Weird, hilarious, and unpredictable.

  • Host: Scott Aukerman (comedy writer, improv legend)
  • Best for: Improv comedy, character work, absurdist humor
  • Episode format: Weekly interviews that devolve into chaos
  • Format: Real celebrity interview + comedians playing characters
  • Guests: Paul F. Tompkins, Lauren Lapkus, Jason Mantzoukas (improv masters)
  • Vibe: If you like "I Think You Should Leave," you'll love this

🎬 WTF with Marc Maron

Why listen: Marc Maron's vulnerable, honest conversations with comedians, actors, and musicians. Deep dives into creativity, addiction, and the comedy world.

  • Host: Marc Maron, stand-up comedian and actor
  • Best for: In-depth comedian interviews, vulnerability, raw honesty
  • Episode format: 2x per week, 90 min interviews (recorded in his garage)
  • Guests: Comedians, actors, musicians (Obama, Robin Williams, Conan, etc.)
  • Style: Confessional, therapeutic, genuine connection
  • Legendary: The podcast that proved podcasts could be serious interviews

🎤 The Joe Rogan Experience

Why listen: When Rogan has comedians on, it's hilarious. Long-form conversations that go anywhere.

  • Host: Joe Rogan, comedian and UFC commentator
  • Best for: Comedian episodes (avoid the political ones if you just want laughs)
  • Episode format: 2-4 hour free-flowing conversations
  • Comedian guests: Bill Burr, Tom Segura, Joey Diaz, Bert Kreischer, Duncan Trussell
  • Format: No structure, just hanging out and talking
  • Pro tip: Look for episodes with comedians you already know

🍺 The Bonfire with Big Jay Oakerson & Dan Soder

Why listen: Two NYC comedians riff on news, pop culture, and listener stories. Fast-paced, high-energy comedy.

  • Hosts: Big Jay Oakerson and Dan Soder (stand-up comedians)
  • Best for: Quick wit, riffing, NYC comedy sensibility
  • Episode format: Daily SiriusXM show, also podcast
  • Style: Rapid-fire jokes, building on each other's bits
  • Topics: News, viral videos, listener calls, improv storytelling

🎙️ 2 Bears 1 Cave

Why listen: Tom Segura and Bert Kreischer (best friends, stand-up comedians) tell stories, watch videos, and crack each other up.

  • Hosts: Tom Segura and Bert Kreischer (comedian friends)
  • Best for: Comedian chemistry, storytelling, viral videos
  • Episode format: Weekly 90-120 min hangouts
  • Format: Watching videos, telling stories, making each other laugh
  • Vibe: Feels like listening to your funniest friends

🎭 How Did This Get Made?

Why listen: Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael, and Jason Mantzoukas hilariously dissect terrible movies. Film criticism meets comedy gold.

  • Hosts: Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael, Jason Mantzoukas
  • Best for: Bad movie commentary, improv comedy, film nerd humor
  • Episode format: Bi-weekly deep dives on awful movies
  • Movies covered: The worst films ever made (The Room, Cats, etc.)
  • Format: Hosts watch bad movie, then roast it hilariously
  • Cult classic: Huge fanbase, live shows

🍿 The Dollop

Why listen: Comedian Dave Anthony tells wild true stories from history to comedian Gareth Reynolds, who reacts in real-time. History + comedy.

  • Hosts: Dave Anthony and Gareth Reynolds (comedians)
  • Best for: True historical stories, comedy history, absurd facts
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min storytelling
  • Format: Dave tells Gareth a crazy historical story he's never heard
  • Topics: Bizarre historical events, forgotten disasters, weird people
  • Why it works: Gareth's genuine reactions to insane history

🎧 My Dad Wrote a Porno

Why listen: Jamie Morton reads his dad's terrible erotic novel to his friends Alice Levine and James Cooper. Hilariously awkward and absurd.

  • Hosts: Jamie Morton, Alice Levine, James Cooper
  • Best for: Cringe comedy, British humor, so-bad-it's-good writing
  • Episode format: Chapter-by-chapter readings with commentary
  • Premise: Jamie's dad self-published terrible erotic fiction
  • Warning: Very adult content (but hilariously bad writing)
  • Cult hit: One of the biggest comedy podcasts globally

🎤 The Monday Morning Podcast

Why listen: Bill Burr rants about life, sports, relationships, and society. One of the funniest solo podcasts ever.

  • Host: Bill Burr, legendary stand-up comedian
  • Best for: Rants, observational comedy, Boston attitude
  • Episode format: 2x per week, 60 min solo rants
  • Format: Bill talks into a mic about whatever's on his mind
  • Topics: Sports, marriage, social issues, advertising (he roasts his own sponsors)
  • Why it works: Bill's unique voice and take on everything

🎭 Heavyweight

Why listen: Jonathan Goldstein helps people resolve long-standing conflicts or questions. Funny, emotional, beautifully produced.

  • Host: Jonathan Goldstein, writer and comedian
  • Best for: Storytelling, resolution, comedy + heart
  • Episode format: Bi-weekly narrative investigations
  • Premise: People with unresolved issues from years ago get closure
  • Tone: Equal parts funny and touching
  • Production: Gimlet-quality storytelling

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Comedy Podcasts

  • Chemistry matters: Best comedy podcasts have great host dynamics
  • Long-form = funnier: Comedy improves when there's no time limit
  • Inside jokes build: Long-running podcasts develop their own lore
  • Authenticity wins: Comedians being themselves, not performing bits
  • Conversation > monologue: Riffing together beats solo rants (usually)

📚 Recommended Listening Path

For celebrity interviews: SmartLess → Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

For dark humor: Your Mom's House → 2 Bears 1 Cave → The Bonfire

For improv comedy: Comedy Bang! Bang! → How Did This Get Made?

For stand-up comedian insights: WTF with Marc Maron → The Joe Rogan Experience (comedian episodes)

🚀 Start Here

New to comedy podcasts? Begin with these episodes:

  • SmartLess: "Will Ferrell" (hilarious guest, great host chemistry)
  • Conan O'Brien: "Timothy Olyphant Returns" (perfect example of Conan's style)
  • How Did This Get Made: "The Room" (iconic bad movie breakdown)
  • The Dollop: "The Rube" (incredible true story told hilariously)

Bottom line: The best comedy podcast depends on your humor style. Want celebrity charm? SmartLess. Want dark humor? Your Mom's House. Want improv? Comedy Bang! Bang! Want solo rants? Monday Morning Podcast. Start with what sounds funniest to you.
