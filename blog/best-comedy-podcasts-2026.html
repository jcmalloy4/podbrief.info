Best Comedy Podcasts 2026 - Stand-Up, Interviews & Hilarious Conversations

March 12, 2026 • 12 min read

Need to laugh? Comedy podcasts deliver hilarious conversations, celebrity interviews, and absurd storytelling directly to your ears. Whether you love stand-up, improv, or just funny people talking, there's a podcast for you.

Here are the best comedy podcasts in 2026, organized by style so you can find exactly what makes you laugh.

Best Celebrity Interview Comedy Podcasts

1. SmartLess

Hosts: Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes
Length: 45-60 minutes
Frequency: Weekly (Mondays)
Best for: A-list celebrity interviews with humor

What makes it great: Three famous friends take turns surprising each other with mystery celebrity guests. The chemistry is incredible, the guests are massive (Obama, Conan, Tom Hanks), and it's both funny and surprisingly heartfelt.

Listen if: You want Hollywood stars letting their guard down with comedian friends.

2. Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend

Host: Conan O'Brien
Length: 60-75 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Conan's unique interview style

What makes it great: Conan brings his late-night brilliance to podcasting. He's genuinely curious, wildly funny, and gets celebrities to open up in unexpected ways. His assistant Sona and producer Matt add hilarious dynamics.

Listen if: You're a Conan fan or love smart, absurdist humor.

3. WTF with Marc Maron

Host: Marc Maron
Length: 90-120 minutes
Frequency: 2x per week
Best for: Deep, honest conversations with comedians and artists

What makes it great: Maron is a master interviewer who gets people to be vulnerable and real. His garage conversations have become legendary (he got Obama for a garage interview). Funny but also philosophical.

Listen if: You want substance with your comedy—real talk about creativity, mental health, and the comedy world.

4. Armchair Expert

Host: Dax Shepard
Length: 90-120 minutes
Frequency: 2x per week
Best for: Honest celebrity conversations

What makes it great: Dax and co-host Monica Padman interview celebrities, experts, and interesting people. Dax's openness about addiction and personal struggles creates surprisingly honest conversations.

Listen if: You want funny interviews that also get real about life challenges.

Comedian Conversation Podcasts

5. The Joe Rogan Experience

Host: Joe Rogan
Length: 2-4 hours
Frequency: 3-5x per week
Best for: Long-form conversations with comedians (and everyone else)

What makes it great: When Rogan has comedians on (Bill Burr, Dave Chappelle, Joey Diaz), it's comedy gold. They riff, tell stories, and just hang out for hours. His comedian episodes are the funniest.

Listen if: You want to hear comedians being themselves for hours.

6. 2 Bears 1 Cave

Hosts: Tom Segura, Bert Kreischer
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Absurd comedian banter

What makes it great: Two hilarious stand-ups just riffing, telling stories, and making each other laugh. No agenda, no structure—just funny friends being ridiculous.

Listen if: You want chaos and laughter without any pretense.

7. The Bonfire with Big Jay Oakerson & Dan Soder

Hosts: Big Jay Oakerson, Dan Soder
Length: 2-3 hours
Frequency: Monday-Thursday
Best for: Unfiltered comedian radio

What makes it great: SiriusXM show released as a podcast. Raw, hilarious, often outrageous. They bust each other's balls, tell ridiculous stories, and make fun of everything.

Listen if: You want edgy, unfiltered comedy.

Story-Based Comedy Podcasts

8. The Moth

Hosts: Various storytellers
Length: 45-60 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: True stories told live

What makes it great: Real people tell true stories on stage without notes. Some are hilarious, some are heartbreaking, all are compelling. The comedy episodes are genuinely funny.

Listen if: You love storytelling and want variety (not all comedy, but the funny ones are great).

9. Risk!

Host: Kevin Allison
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Outrageous true stories

What makes it great: People tell their most embarrassing, shocking, and hilarious true stories on stage. Often NSFW but always entertaining.

Listen if: You want stories that make you laugh and cringe.

Improv & Scripted Comedy

10. Comedy Bang! Bang!

Host: Scott Aukerman
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Absurdist improv comedy

What makes it great: Interviews with celebrities mixed with improvisational comedy characters. Weird, hilarious, and beloved by comedy nerds. Guests play along with the absurdity.

Listen if: You love weird, experimental comedy.

11. Hello from the Magic Tavern

Hosts: Arnie Niekamp, Matt Young, Adal Rifai
Length: 60 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Fantasy improv comedy

What makes it great: A man falls through a portal behind a Burger King and interviews creatures in a magical land called Foon. Completely improvised fantasy world-building that's hilarious and surprisingly deep.

Listen if: You want something totally unique and ridiculous.

News Satire & Commentary

12. The Daily Show: Ears Edition

Host: Various (The Daily Show team)
Length: 25-30 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Thursday
Best for: Daily Show audio versions

What makes it great: The Daily Show's best segments in podcast form. Political satire and commentary from one of the longest-running comedy institutions.

Listen if: You want your news with comedy.

13. Pod Save America

Hosts: Jon Favreau, Jon Lovett, Dan Pfeiffer, Tommy Vietor
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Best for: Political commentary with humor

What makes it great: Former Obama staffers make politics funny while staying substantive. Smart analysis with great jokes.

Listen if: You want politics that doesn't make you want to cry.

Niche & Specialty Comedy

14. The Dollop

Hosts: Dave Anthony, Gareth Reynolds
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Comedy + American history

What makes it great: Dave reads a crazy American history story to Gareth, who knows nothing about it. Gareth's reactions are hilarious. You'll learn bizarre history while laughing.

Listen if: You want education disguised as comedy.

15. My Dad Wrote A Porno

Hosts: Jamie Morton, James Cooper, Alice Levine
Length: 30-45 minutes
Frequency: Completed series (bingeable)
Best for: British humor + absurdity

What makes it great: Jamie's dad wrote an erotic novel. Jamie reads it to his friends, and they all die laughing at how bad it is. Tears-streaming-down-your-face funny.

Listen if: You want British comedy and don't mind adult content.

How to Choose Comedy Podcasts

If You're New to Comedy Podcasts

Start with:

  • SmartLess — Accessible and consistently funny
  • Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend — A comedy legend at his best
  • The Daily Show — Familiar format if you watch the show

If You Love Stand-Up Comedians

Listen to:

  • WTF with Marc Maron — The comedian's comedian
  • 2 Bears 1 Cave — Stand-ups being themselves
  • The Joe Rogan Experience — When he has comedians on

If You Want Something Weird

Try:

  • Comedy Bang! Bang! — Experimental absurdity
  • Hello from the Magic Tavern — Fantasy improv
  • My Dad Wrote A Porno — Unique British humor

If You Want Stories

Go for:

  • The Moth — True stories, some hilarious
  • Risk! — Outrageous true stories
  • The Dollop — Historical comedy

Building Your Comedy Podcast Rotation

The Daily Laugh (30-60 min)

Pick one show for your commute or workout:

  • SmartLess (Mondays) — Start your week with celebrity laughs
  • The Daily Show (M-Th) — News with jokes
  • Conan O'Brien (Weekly) — Reliable quality

The Weekend Binge

Longer episodes for when you have time:

  • WTF — Deep 2-hour conversations
  • Armchair Expert — Substantive and funny
  • Joe Rogan — When comedians are guests

The Absurdist Queue

For when you want something different:

  • Comedy Bang! Bang!
  • Hello from the Magic Tavern
  • My Dad Wrote A Porno

Tips for Comedy Podcast Listeners

Know Your Comedy Tolerance

  • Clean: SmartLess, Conan, The Moth
  • Some swearing: WTF, Armchair Expert, The Dollop
  • Very NSFW: 2 Bears 1 Cave, The Bonfire, Risk!, My Dad Wrote A Porno

Don't Force It

Comedy is subjective. If a podcast doesn't make you laugh after 2-3 episodes, move on. There are hundreds of comedy podcasts—find YOUR funny.

Save Favorites

When you find a particularly funny episode:

  • Star it in your app
  • Keep a list of "re-listen worthy" episodes
  • Share with friends who'd appreciate it

Why Listen to Comedy Podcasts?

  • Stress relief: Laughter reduces cortisol
  • Makes boring tasks fun: Chores become entertainment time
  • Commute therapy: Turns traffic into laugh time
  • Social connection: Feel like you're hanging with funny friends
  • Mood boost: Hard to stay in a bad mood when laughing

The Verdict: Which Comedy Podcasts Should You Choose?

For celebrity interviews: SmartLess or Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend

For comedian conversations: WTF with Marc Maron or 2 Bears 1 Cave

For storytelling: The Moth or The Dollop

For weird/experimental: Comedy Bang! Bang! or Hello from the Magic Tavern

For political satire: The Daily Show or Pod Save America
