Best Writing Podcasts 2026: Craft, Inspiration & Career Advice for Writers

March 4, 2026 9 min read

Writing podcasts offer invaluable guidance for novelists, journalists, screenwriters, and anyone passionate about the written word. Whether you're working on your first draft or your fifteenth book, 2026's best writing podcasts provide craft lessons, publishing insights, author interviews, and the inspiration to keep writing.

Why Writing Podcasts Are Essential

Writing podcasts help you:

  • Learn craft: Story structure, character development, dialogue, and style
  • Overcome blocks: Practical advice for getting unstuck
  • Navigate publishing: Agents, editors, self-publishing, and marketing
  • Find inspiration: Hear how successful authors work
  • Build community: Connect with other writers

Best Writing Craft Podcasts

1. Writing Excuses

Why it's great: Brandon Sanderson, Mary Robinette Kowal, and team deliver 15-minute masterclasses on writing craft. "15 minutes long, because you're in a hurry, and we're not that smart."

Best for: Fiction writers, especially sci-fi/fantasy

Topics: Plot, character, worldbuilding, revision, publishing

Format: Short, focused episodes with homework prompts

2. The Creative Penn Podcast

Why it's great: Joanna Penn covers the full spectrum of author business: writing craft, publishing, marketing, and making a living as a writer. Practical, business-focused.

Best for: Self-published authors and hybrid authors

Topics: Book marketing, AI for writers, indie publishing

View Creative Penn summaries →

3. The Shit No One Tells You About Writing

Why it's great: Bianca Marais and CJ Lyons dish honest, unfiltered advice about the writing life, publishing realities, and industry truths.

Best for: Writers who want real talk, not sugarcoating

Vibe: Frank, funny, encouraging

Tips for Getting the Most from Writing Podcasts

Active Listening

  • Take notes: Jot down key insights and exercises
  • Do the homework: Many podcasts include writing prompts
  • Apply immediately: Try new techniques in your current project
  • Revisit favorites: Re-listen to episodes when facing specific challenges

Balance Learning & Writing

  • Set limits on podcast consumption (don't let learning replace writing)
  • Use podcasts during commutes, not prime writing time
  • Choose 2-3 favorites; don't try to keep up with everything
