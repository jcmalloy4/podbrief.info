Best Podcasts for Learning: 15 Shows to Expand Your Mind (2026)

Educational podcasts that make you smarter while you commute, exercise, or do chores

Updated February 25, 2026

The best podcasts for learning transform idle time into education. Whether you're commuting, working out, or doing chores, these shows deliver knowledge from world-class experts in science, history, psychology, business, and more.

Why Podcasts Are Perfect for Learning

  • Learn while multitasking – Turn commutes, workouts, and chores into education time
  • Access world-class experts – Hear directly from researchers, authors, and thought leaders
  • Go deep on topics – Long-form conversations reveal nuance books can't capture
  • Free education – Most podcasts are free, making learning accessible to everyone

How to Learn More from Podcasts

Maximize your learning with these strategies:

  • Take notes – Pause and write down key insights you want to remember
  • Listen at 1.5x speed – Cover more content without losing comprehension
  • Re-listen to favorites – Dense episodes reward multiple listens
  • Read summaries first – Preview episodes to find the most relevant ones for you
  • Follow up with books – Many guests have written books on their topics
