Best Podcasts for Learning (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

Podcasts are one of the most efficient ways to learn while commuting, exercising, or doing chores. These shows transform dead time into education, delivered by expert hosts who make complex topics accessible and engaging.

Top Learning Podcasts

1. Huberman Lab

Stanford neuroscientist Andrew Huberman explains how the brain and body work, with actionable protocols for sleep, focus, fitness, and mental health. Dense science made practical.

Learn about: Neuroscience, health, performance

Episode length: 90-150 minutes

Browse Huberman Lab summaries →

2. Lex Fridman Podcast

MIT researcher Lex Fridman conducts deep conversations with scientists, philosophers, engineers, and thinkers. Intellectual rigor meets accessible storytelling.

Learn about: AI, science, philosophy, technology

Episode length: 2-4 hours

3. Hardcore History

Dan Carlin's marathon episodes deliver comprehensive historical education through compelling narrative. Learn more from one episode than most history classes.

Learn about: History, wars, civilizations

Episode length: 3-6 hours

4. Freakonomics Radio

Stephen Dubner explores the hidden side of everything through economics and data. Eye-opening insights about how the world really works.

Learn about: Economics, human behavior, data

Episode length: 40-50 minutes

5. RadioLab

Jad Abumrad and team investigate scientific and philosophical questions with innovative sound design. Science storytelling as art.

Learn about: Science, philosophy, human nature

Episode length: 30-60 minutes

6. The Jordan Harbinger Show

Jordan Harbinger interviews experts across fields—psychology, business, science—extracting practical wisdom and fascinating stories.

Learn about: Psychology, business, self-improvement

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

7. 99% Invisible

Roman Mars explores the design and architecture of the world around us. Learn to see the invisible decisions that shape our environment.

Learn about: Design, architecture, urban planning

Episode length: 20-40 minutes

8. Planet Money

NPR's economics podcast makes finance, business, and economic principles entertaining and understandable.

Learn about: Economics, finance, business

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

9. Hidden Brain

Shankar Vedantam explores unconscious patterns that drive human behavior using psychology and behavioral science.

Learn about: Psychology, human behavior, decision-making

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

10. The Tim Ferriss Show

Tim Ferriss deconstructs world-class performers to extract their tactics, tools, and habits. A masterclass in learning from the best.

Learn about: Success habits, optimization, skills

Episode length: 90-180 minutes

How to Learn Effectively with Podcasts

Choose the Right Speed

Start at 1x for dense content, increase to 1.25-1.5x as you get comfortable. Some listeners go to 2x for familiar topics.

Take Notes

For actionable content, pause and capture key insights. Most podcast apps let you save timestamps.

Mix Difficulty Levels

Balance challenging shows like Lex Fridman with more accessible ones like 99% Invisible.

Revisit Key Episodes

Great learning podcasts reward multiple listens. Revisit episodes on topics you want to master.

Why Podcasts for Learning?

  • Efficiency: Learn during commutes, workouts, chores
  • Expert access: World-class teachers for free
  • Depth: Long-form allows nuance and detail
  • Variety: Learn about anything imaginable
  • Engaging: Story and conversation beat textbooks
  • Updated: Current information, not outdated books

Getting Started

New to educational podcasts? Try these starter episodes:

  • Huberman Lab: "How to Optimize Your Sleep"
  • Freakonomics: "Why is My Life So Hard?" (decision-making)
  • 99% Invisible: "The Amen Break" (music/culture)
  • Planet Money: "The Indicator" (daily, digestible)
