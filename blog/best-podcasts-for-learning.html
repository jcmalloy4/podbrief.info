Best Podcasts for Learning: 15 Educational Shows (2026)

Turn commute time into learning time. These educational podcasts make complex topics accessible, engaging, and actually fun to learn about.

Why Podcasts Are Great for Learning

Unlike textbooks or online courses, podcasts let you learn while:

  • Commuting to work
  • Working out at the gym
  • Doing household chores
  • Walking the dog
  • Cooking dinner

The best educational podcasts don't feel like school—they're conversations, stories, and explorations that happen to teach you something valuable.

Best Science Podcasts for Learning

1. Radiolab

Hosts: Jad Abumrad, Lulu Miller, Latif Nasser

Length: 30-60 minutes

Topics: Science, philosophy, human experience

Why it's great: Mind-bending stories about science and philosophy. Amazing sound design makes complex ideas come alive. You'll think about episodes days later.

Learn about: Quantum mechanics, memory, consciousness, ethics, evolution

2. Science Vs

Host: Wendy Zukerman

Length: 30-40 minutes

Topics: Science fact-checking popular claims

Why it's great: Takes controversial topics (vaccines, GMOs, detoxes) and breaks down the actual science. No BS, just facts.

Learn about: Nutrition myths, medical claims, environmental issues, technology hype

3. Ologies

Host: Alie Ward

Length: 60-90 minutes

Topics: Every "-ology" you can imagine

Why it's fun: Alie interviews experts about their niche fields—from volcanology to puppetry to death. Her enthusiasm is contagious.

Learn about: Meteorology, entomology, criminology, astrobiology, marine biology

Best History Podcasts for Learning

4. Hardcore History

Host: Dan Carlin

Length: 3-6 hours per episode

Topics: Major historical events

Why it's the best: Dan Carlin is the greatest history storyteller alive. His multi-part series on WWI, WWII, and ancient Rome are legendary.

Learn about: World War I, Genghis Khan, nuclear weapons, ancient empires

5. Revolutions

Host: Mike Duncan

Length: 30-45 minutes

Topics: History's great revolutions

Why it's great: Mike Duncan tells the story of major revolutions (American, French, Russian, etc.) in chronological detail. Learn how societies transform.

Learn about: French Revolution, Haitian Revolution, Russian Revolution, English Civil War

6. Throughline

Hosts: Rund Abdelfatah, Ramtin Arablouei (NPR)

Length: 40-50 minutes

Topics: History of current events

Why it's relevant: Connects today's headlines to historical context. Understand why things are the way they are.

Learn about: Roots of modern conflicts, political movements, cultural shifts

How to Learn Effectively from Podcasts

Take notes:

  • Use a note-taking app (Apple Notes, Notion, Obsidian)
  • Write down key insights immediately after listening
  • Review your notes weekly

Listen actively:

  • Pause and think about what you just heard
  • Ask yourself: "How does this apply to my life?"
  • Discuss episodes with friends or online communities

Build a learning routine:

  • Morning commute: News and current events
  • Gym: General knowledge (Stuff You Should Know, Ologies)
  • Evening walk: Deep learning (Lex Fridman, Hardcore History)

Match Podcasts to Your Learning Goals

If you want to understand the world better: Hidden Brain, Freakonomics, Planet Money

If you want to learn about everything: Stuff You Should Know, Ologies, 99% Invisible

If you want deep expertise: Lex Fridman, Hardcore History, Radiolab

If you have short commutes: Science Vs (30 min), The Indicator (10 min), 99% Invisible (20-40 min)

If you have long commutes: Hardcore History (3-6 hours), Lex Fridman (2-4 hours), Revolutions (series)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Passive listening without retention: Take notes or you'll forget everything
  • Trying to learn too much at once: Pick 3-5 podcasts max
  • Never reviewing what you learned: Revisit notes monthly
  • Only listening to familiar topics: Challenge yourself with new subjects
