SmartLess Best Episodes 2026

Published March 11, 2026 | Comedy & Entertainment

Three Friends, One Surprise Guest

Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes have created podcast magic. Each episode, one host surprises the other two with a mystery celebrity guest. The result? Authentic, hilarious conversations with A-listers.

Here are the best SmartLess episodes to start with.

Best Celebrity Interviews

1. Tom Hanks

America's dad gets the SmartLess treatment. Tom's warmth and storytelling shine through. Hilarious and heartwarming.

Why it's great: Tom Hanks being Tom Hanks—humble, funny, and full of great stories

2. Harrison Ford

Han Solo himself, opening up in ways you rarely see. Harrison is funny, thoughtful, and surprisingly candid.

Why it's great: Getting past Harrison's legendary grumpiness to real conversation

3. Conan O'Brien

Comedy royalty meeting comedy friends. The chemistry is perfect, the laughs are constant.

Why it's great: Four masters of comedy riffing together

4. Will Ferrell

One of the funniest humans alive in conversation with three comedic actors. Exactly as good as you'd hope.

Why it's great: Pure comedy gold from start to finish

Why it's great: Behind-the-scenes Hollywood stories from someone who's been everywhere

7. Ryan Reynolds

Why it's great: Ryan's humor and business savvy make for fascinating conversation

8. Dax Shepard

Actor, podcaster, and motorcycle enthusiast. Honest discussion about addiction, relationships, and personal growth.

Why it's great: Raw honesty about life's challenges

What Makes SmartLess Special?

The Surprise Element

Two of the three hosts don't know who the guest is. This creates genuine reactions and authentic curiosity.

Real Friendships

Jason, Will, and Sean are actual friends. Their chemistry isn't manufactured—it's decades of friendship.

Celebrity Access

The hosts' Hollywood connections bring A-list guests who don't normally do podcasts. When Harrison Ford says yes to SmartLess, you know the show is special.

No Pretense

The hosts make fun of themselves constantly. There's no celebrity ego—just friends having fun.

Short Enough

45-60 minutes is perfect. Long enough for substance, short enough to fit your commute.

How SmartLess Compares to Other Interview Podcasts

vs Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

SmartLess: Three hosts, shorter format, surprise element
Conan: One-on-one, deeper dive, more structure
Both are great: Pick based on mood and time

vs Armchair Expert

SmartLess: Light, fun, celebrity-focused
Armchair Expert: Deep, therapeutic, longer conversations
SmartLess wins for: Easy listening and pure entertainment

vs WTF with Marc Maron

SmartLess: Upbeat, friendly, Hollywood access
WTF: Raw, vulnerable, comedy-focused
SmartLess wins for: Feel-good conversations

Why SmartLess Works for Everyone

Even If You Don't Know the Guest

Two hosts don't know either! You discover together.

Light Without Being Shallow

The show is fun, but guests open up about meaningful things. It's not just fluff.

No Controversy

Perfect Length

Tips for Enjoying SmartLess

Don't Skip Based on Guest

Even guests you don't think you'll like often surprise you. The hosts make everyone interesting.

Listen to the Opening Banter

The first 10 minutes of the hosts catching up is often the funniest part.

Pay Attention to the "Wait, who is it?" Moment

When the clueless hosts find out who the guest is, their reactions are priceless.

Notice the Dynamic

Watch how the hosts bring different energies—Jason is thoughtful, Will is boisterous, Sean is enthusiastic. Together they're perfect.

The SmartLess Effect

SmartLess proved podcasts could be:

  • Celebrity-driven but authentic - A-listers being real
  • Short-form but substantive - Quality over marathon length
  • Multiple hosts done right - Three people without chaos
  • Wholesome entertainment - Funny without being mean

The show has launched a successful tour, become one of the top podcasts globally, and set a standard for celebrity interview shows.
