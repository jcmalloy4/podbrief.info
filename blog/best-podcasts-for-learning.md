# Best Podcasts for Learning: Expand Your Knowledge in 2026

Want to learn while you commute, exercise, or do chores? These podcasts deliver high-quality educational content across science, technology, business, history, and more.

## Science & Technology

### **Huberman Lab**
Dr. Andrew Huberman breaks down neuroscience and biology into actionable protocols for health, performance, and well-being.

**Best for:** Health optimization, neuroscience, evidence-based protocols  
**Episode length:** 90-150 minutes  
[Huberman Lab Briefs](/podcast/huberman-lab)

### **Lex Fridman Podcast**
Deep conversations with leading scientists, researchers, and thinkers about AI, technology, and the future.

**Best for:** AI, philosophy, big ideas  
**Episode length:** 120-240 minutes  
[Lex Fridman Briefs](/podcast/lex-fridman-podcast)

### **Radiolab**
Investigative journalism meets storytelling. Complex scientific concepts made accessible and entertaining.

**Best for:** Science storytelling, curiosity-driven learning  
**Episode length:** 30-60 minutes

### **Science Vs**
Myth-busting show that examines popular claims with actual scientific evidence.

**Best for:** Critical thinking, debunking myths  
**Episode length:** 30-45 minutes

## How to Learn Effectively with Podcasts

### 1. **Match Content to Activity**
- **Deep learning:** Save complex podcasts for focused listening
- **Light learning:** Background-friendly shows for chores/exercise
- **Quick updates:** Short daily podcasts for commutes

### 2. **Take Notes**
- Use voice memos to capture insights while listening
- Create a simple note-taking system
- Review and implement what you learn

### 3. **Adjust Speed**
- 1.5x-2x for familiar topics
- 1x for complex new material
- Slow down for critical information

### 4. **Be Selective**
- Use summaries to preview episodes ([PodBrief](/))
- Skip episodes that aren't relevant
- Focus on high-signal content

### 5. **Apply What You Learn**
- Learning without action is entertainment
- Implement one insight per podcast
- Share what you learn to reinforce it

## Learning Strategies by Goal

**Building expertise in one area:**
- Choose 2-3 podcasts in that domain
- Listen to every episode for 6 months
- Take notes and create a knowledge base

**Broad learning across topics:**
- Subscribe to 10-15 diverse shows
- Be selective about which episodes you finish
- Use summaries to navigate efficiently

**Staying current:**
- Daily/weekly news podcasts (The Indicator, The Daily)
- Industry-specific shows for your field
- 10-20 minutes per day
