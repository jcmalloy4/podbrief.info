Best Podcasts Before Bed: 12 Shows for Better Sleep (2026)

Struggling to fall asleep? These calming podcasts help you wind down, relax your mind, and drift off naturally—no sleep medications required.

What Makes a Good Bedtime Podcast?

Not all podcasts work for sleep. The best bedtime podcasts have:

  • Calm, soothing voices: No loud, energetic hosts
  • Gentle pacing: Slow, relaxing delivery
  • Non-stimulating content: No debates, drama, or cliffhangers
  • No sudden sounds: Consistent volume, no loud ads or music stings
  • Right length: 20-60 minutes (enough time to fall asleep)

Avoid before bed: True crime, news, business podcasts, anything that makes you think too hard or gets your adrenaline pumping.

Best Sleep Podcasts (Designed for Sleep)

1. Sleep With Me

Host: Drew Ackerman (Scooter)

Length: 60+ minutes

Frequency: 3x per week

Why it works: Drew deliberately tells boring, meandering stories in a soothing voice. The point is to give your mind something to listen to without engaging it. Falls asleep to it, every episode.

Perfect for: Anyone who needs to occupy their mind to fall asleep

2. Nothing Much Happens

Host: Kathryn Nicolai

Length: 20-30 minutes

Frequency: Weekly

Why it's calming: Kathryn reads gentle bedtime stories for adults. Each story is read twice—the second time slower. Incredibly soothing voice.

Perfect for: People who love bedtime stories

3. Sleepy

Host: Otis Gray

Length: 30-60 minutes

Frequency: Weekly

Why it works: Otis reads classic literature and old texts in a deliberately boring, monotone voice. Pride and Prejudice, Greek myths, travel logs—all designed to lull you to sleep.

Perfect for: People who like listening to books but need something dull

Best Meditation & Relaxation Podcasts

Host: Mary Meckley

Length: 20-30 minutes

5. The Meditation Podcast

6. Tracks to Relax

Length: 10-30 minutes

Frequency: Daily

Why it's versatile: Sleep meditations, bedtime stories, white noise, nature sounds. Different episodes for different moods.

Perfect for: Variety—try different styles to see what works

How to Use Podcasts for Better Sleep

Create a bedtime routine:

  • 30 minutes before bed: Turn off screens (except podcast app)
  • Dim the lights
  • Get in bed
  • Start podcast with sleep timer set for 30-45 minutes
  • Close your eyes and listen

Use a sleep timer:

  • Most podcast apps have sleep timers
  • Set for 30-45 minutes
  • Podcast stops automatically after you fall asleep
  • Saves battery and prevents waking you up later

Experiment with volume:

  • Volume should be just loud enough to hear comfortably
  • Too loud = stimulating
  • Too quiet = straining to hear keeps you awake

Best Podcast Apps for Sleep

Features you need:

  • Sleep timer: Essential (all major apps have this)
  • Volume boost: For quiet voices (Overcast has this)
  • Offline downloads: No internet = no interruptions
  • Auto-play queue: Multiple episodes in a row

Recommended apps:

  • Apple Podcasts (free, simple sleep timer)
  • Overcast (voice boost feature, $9.99/year)
  • Pocket Casts (great sleep timer, free)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Choosing stimulating content: Avoid news, true crime, debates before bed
  • Playing at high volume: Keep it low and soothing
  • No sleep timer: Waking up at 3 AM to a loud ad ruins the benefit
  • Watching screens while listening: Defeats the purpose—close your eyes
  • Switching podcasts constantly: Stick with one style for a week to see if it works

Podcasts to Avoid Before Bed

  • True crime: Too stimulating, might cause nightmares
  • News podcasts: Stress-inducing
  • Business/entrepreneurship: Gets your mind racing about work
  • Comedy: Laughing keeps you awake
  • Anything with cliffhangers: You'll want to keep listening

Why Podcasts Work Better Than Music for Sleep

  • Occupies your mind: Prevents racing thoughts
  • Consistent sound: No sudden changes in volume or tempo
  • Familiar voices: Create a sense of comfort and routine
  • No lyrics to sing along to: Music with lyrics can be distracting

Build Your Perfect Bedtime Routine

Week 1-2: Try Sleep With Me or Nothing Much Happens (designed for sleep)

Week 3-4: Add meditation podcasts to your rotation

Week 5+: Experiment with gentle storytelling or nature sounds

Everyone is different—what puts one person to sleep might keep another awake. Experiment to find your perfect bedtime podcast.
