Best Health & Wellness Podcasts (2026)

Published February 21, 2026

Looking to optimize your health? The best health and wellness podcasts of 2026 deliver science-backed advice on nutrition, fitness, sleep, mental health, and longevity from leading experts.

Why Listen to Health Podcasts?

Health podcasts offer:

  • Evidence-based protocols backed by research
  • Expert interviews with doctors and scientists
  • Practical strategies you can implement immediately
  • Latest research translated into actionable advice
  • Holistic approaches to physical and mental well-being

Top Health & Wellness Podcasts of 2026

1. Huberman Lab

Stanford neuroscientist Dr. Andrew Huberman explains the science of sleep, stress, focus, exercise, and habits. Dense, detailed protocols for optimizing biology and behavior.

Best for: Science-backed protocols, neuroscience, performance optimization

Browse Huberman Lab episodes →

2. The Peter Attia Drive

Dr. Peter Attia dives deep into longevity, metabolic health, exercise, nutrition, and disease prevention. In-depth conversations with leading researchers.

Best for: Longevity, metabolic health, disease prevention

3. The Model Health Show

Shawn Stevenson covers nutrition, sleep, fitness, and lifestyle optimization. Accessible and actionable health advice.

Best for: Sleep optimization, nutrition, fitness, lifestyle

4. FoundMyFitness

Dr. Rhonda Patrick explores nutrition, aging, and disease prevention through a scientific lens. Dense with research but incredibly valuable.

Best for: Nutrition science, aging, disease prevention

5. The Doctor's Farmacy

Dr. Mark Hyman interviews experts on functional medicine, nutrition, and root-cause health. Holistic approach to wellness.

Best for: Functional medicine, nutrition, chronic disease

6. Mind Pump: Raw Fitness Truth

Four trainers discuss fitness, nutrition, and health myths. Entertaining while debunking common misconceptions.

Best for: Fitness training, muscle building, fat loss

7. The Genius Life

Max Lugavere explores brain health, nutrition, and lifestyle factors for cognitive performance and longevity.

Best for: Brain health, cognitive performance, nutrition

8. The Rich Roll Podcast

Ultra-endurance athlete Rich Roll interviews wellness experts, athletes, and thought leaders on plant-based nutrition and optimal living.

Best for: Plant-based nutrition, endurance, mindset

9. Ten Percent Happier

Dan Harris and meditation teachers discuss mindfulness, meditation, and mental health. Skeptical but open-minded approach.

Best for: Meditation, mindfulness, mental health

10. The Nutrition Diva

Quick, evidence-based nutrition tips from registered dietitian Monica Reinagel. Short episodes (under 10 minutes).

Best for: Quick nutrition tips, myth-busting, practical advice

How to Apply What You Learn

The best approach:

  • Pick ONE protocol to implement per month
  • Track your metrics (sleep, energy, mood, performance)
  • Give each change 4-6 weeks before adding another
  • What works for others may not work for you—experiment
