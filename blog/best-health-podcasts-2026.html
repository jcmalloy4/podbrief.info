Best Health & Wellness Podcasts 2026 - Nutrition, Fitness & Mental Health

Published March 9, 2026 | 10 min read

Want to optimize your health, fitness, and well-being? These podcasts deliver evidence-based advice on nutrition, exercise, sleep, mental health, and longevity from leading doctors and researchers.

🧠 Huberman Lab

Why listen: Stanford neuroscientist Dr. Andrew Huberman translates neuroscience research into actionable protocols for sleep, focus, stress, hormones, and performance.

  • Host: Dr. Andrew Huberman, Stanford professor of neurobiology
  • Best for: Sleep optimization, stress management, focus, hormones, vision
  • Episode format: Weekly 2-3 hour deep dives (solo + expert guests)
  • Approach: Science-based, zero-cost protocols you can implement
  • Topics: Dopamine, sleep, sunlight, cold exposure, exercise, supplements
  • Audience: Massive (one of the biggest health podcasts globally)

🎙️ The Peter Attia Drive

Why listen: Dr. Peter Attia explores longevity, healthspan, and performance with deep dives into nutrition, exercise, sleep, and preventive medicine.

  • Host: Dr. Peter Attia, longevity physician
  • Best for: Longevity, preventive medicine, metabolic health, exercise science
  • Episode format: Weekly 2-3 hour interviews with experts
  • Guests: Top researchers, doctors, scientists in health/longevity
  • Depth: Extremely detailed, medical-level discussions
  • Philosophy: Optimize healthspan, not just lifespan

🥗 FoundMyFitness

Why listen: Dr. Rhonda Patrick breaks down nutritional science, genetics, and health optimization with research-backed insights.

  • Host: Dr. Rhonda Patrick, biomedical scientist
  • Best for: Nutrition science, micronutrients, genetics, longevity
  • Episode format: Monthly deep dives + interviews with researchers
  • Topics: Fasting, omega-3s, vitamin D, heat/cold exposure, autophagy
  • Approach: Extremely detailed, science-heavy but accessible
  • Bonus: Free research reports and newsletters

💪 The Model Health Show

Why listen: Shawn Stevenson covers nutrition, fitness, sleep, and lifestyle optimization with practical advice and inspiring stories.

  • Host: Shawn Stevenson, nutritionist and bestselling author
  • Best for: Practical health advice, sleep optimization, nutrition basics
  • Episode format: 2x per week, 45-90 min episodes
  • Style: Accessible, motivational, actionable
  • Topics: Sleep, nutrition, fitness, mindset, relationships
  • Audience: Beginners to intermediate health enthusiasts

🧘 Ten Percent Happier

Why listen: Dan Harris (ABC News anchor) explores meditation, mindfulness, and mental health with scientists, monks, and practitioners.

  • Host: Dan Harris, journalist and meditation advocate
  • Best for: Meditation, mindfulness, mental health, stress reduction
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min interviews
  • Guests: Meditation teachers, neuroscientists, psychologists, authors
  • Approach: Skeptical journalist learns meditation, shares journey
  • Topics: Anxiety, stress, focus, compassion, Buddhist psychology

🏃 The Rich Roll Podcast

Why listen: Ultra-endurance athlete Rich Roll interviews thought leaders on fitness, nutrition, mindfulness, and plant-based living.

  • Host: Rich Roll, ultra-endurance athlete and author
  • Best for: Plant-based nutrition, endurance training, wellness, mindset
  • Episode format: Weekly 2-3 hour long-form conversations
  • Guests: Athletes, doctors, authors, spiritual teachers
  • Topics: Nutrition, fitness, recovery, purpose, transformation
  • Vibe: Inspiring, deep conversations about health and meaning

🎧 The Doctor's Farmacy

Why listen: Dr. Mark Hyman (functional medicine pioneer) discusses food as medicine, chronic disease prevention, and holistic health.

  • Host: Dr. Mark Hyman, functional medicine doctor
  • Best for: Functional medicine, nutrition, chronic disease, gut health
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min interviews
  • Guests: Doctors, researchers, authors in integrative medicine
  • Focus: Food as medicine, root causes of disease
  • Topics: Gut health, inflammation, autoimmune, metabolic health

🧬 The Drive (Peter Attia)

Why listen: Deep dives into the science of longevity with world-class researchers and practitioners.

  • Host: Dr. Peter Attia
  • Best for: Advanced health optimization, longevity science
  • Episode format: Weekly long-form interviews + solo deep dives
  • Topics: VO2 max, Zone 2 training, cancer prevention, Alzheimer's, metabolic health
  • Audience: Serious health optimizers willing to go deep

🥑 Nutrition Facts Podcast

Why listen: Dr. Michael Greger reviews the latest nutrition research with evidence-based, plant-forward recommendations.

  • Host: Dr. Michael Greger, physician and nutrition researcher
  • Best for: Evidence-based nutrition, plant-based diets, disease prevention
  • Episode format: Weekly research reviews + Q&A
  • Approach: Reviews peer-reviewed studies, no industry funding
  • Topics: Nutrition science, longevity, disease prevention
  • Bonus: Free website (nutritionfacts.org) with video summaries

🧠 The Mind Pump Podcast

Why listen: Sal, Adam, and Justin deliver no-BS fitness advice, debunk health myths, and interview experts on training, nutrition, and hormones.

  • Hosts: Sal Di Stefano, Adam Schafer, Justin Andrews (fitness trainers)
  • Best for: Strength training, muscle building, fat loss, fitness industry truths
  • Episode format: Daily episodes (short Q&A + longer interviews)
  • Style: Entertaining, myth-busting, practical
  • Topics: Training programs, nutrition, hormones, supplements

🎙️ Armchair Expert (Health Episodes)

Why listen: Dax Shepard's "Experts On Expert" series features deep dives with health researchers and doctors.

  • Host: Dax Shepard (actor, podcast host)
  • Best for: Accessible health science, addiction, mental health
  • Format: Mix of celebrity and expert interviews
  • Health experts: Dr. Attia, Dr. Huberman, addiction researchers
  • Vibe: Vulnerable, curious, relatable

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Health Podcasts

  • Evidence-based: Best health podcasts cite research and interview credentialed experts
  • Personalization: One-size-fits-all is dead; bio-individuality matters
  • Foundational pillars: Sleep, nutrition, exercise, stress management, relationships
  • Preventive focus: Optimize health now to prevent disease later
  • Actionable protocols: Best shows give you specific things to implement

📚 Recommended Listening Path

For beginners: The Model Health Show → Huberman Lab basics → Rich Roll

For deep optimization: Huberman Lab → Peter Attia Drive → FoundMyFitness

For mental health: Ten Percent Happier → Huberman Lab mental health episodes

For nutrition: The Doctor's Farmacy → Nutrition Facts → FoundMyFitness

For fitness: Mind Pump → Huberman Lab training episodes → Rich Roll

🚀 Start Here

New to health podcasts? Begin with these episodes:

  • Huberman Lab: "Master Your Sleep & Be More Alert When Awake" (foundational)
  • The Model Health Show: "10 Ways to Improve Your Sleep" (accessible intro)
  • Ten Percent Happier: "Meditation for Skeptics" (mental health gateway)
  • Peter Attia: "Exercise Framework" (longevity foundations)

Bottom line: The best health podcast depends on your goals. Want actionable protocols? Huberman Lab. Want longevity deep dives? Peter Attia. Want nutrition science? FoundMyFitness. Want fitness training? Mind Pump. Start with the basics (sleep, exercise, nutrition) then go deeper.
