Best Startup Podcasts for Founders (2026)

Building a company is one of the hardest things a person can do. These podcasts won't do it for you — but they'll make sure you're learning from people who've already made the mistakes you're about to make. From billion-dollar origin stories to bootstrapped indie builds, here's the startup podcast canon.

🚀 The Essential Startup Podcast List

How I Built This

Host: Guy Raz
Network: NPR / Wondery
Why it's great: The gold standard for founder origin stories. Guy Raz has an extraordinary ability to get founders to be honest about their failures, near-death moments, and the role of luck in their success. These aren't highlight reels — they're full narratives. You'll finish episodes feeling both inspired and grounded.

Best episodes:

  • "Spanx: Sara Blakely" — bootstrapped to a billion from $5,000
  • "Airbnb: Brian Chesky & Joe Gebbia" — the cereal box that saved a startup
  • "Patagonia: Yvon Chouinard" — building a values-driven company for 50 years
  • "Warby Parker: Neil Blumenthal & Dave Gilboa" — disrupting a monopoly from dorm rooms

Perfect for: Founders who want perspective, inspiration, and honest stories of struggle before success

My First Million

Hosts: Shaan Puri & Sam Parr
Network: HubSpot Podcast Network
Why it's great: Pure founder energy. Shaan and Sam brainstorm business ideas live on air, break down market opportunities, and interview operators who built things fast and scrappy. No VC polish, no corporate speak — just two guys who love building things talking about the grind. The idea generation sessions alone are worth the subscription.

Best episodes:

  • "We Made $5M Selling a Newsletter" (the Trends story)
  • "How to Build a $1M Newsletter Business"
  • "The Business of Boring Companies" (the roll-up strategy)
  • Any "brainstorm" episode where they riff on new business models

Perfect for: Idea-stage founders, indie hackers, anyone who wants to think like an operator

Acquired

Hosts: Ben Gilbert & David Rosenthal
Network: Independent
Why it's great: The deepest, most researched company histories in podcasting. Acquired episodes run 3–6 hours and cover the full arc of iconic companies — NVIDIA, Apple, Amazon, Berkshire Hathaway, Nike, Hermès. Ben and David don't just cover what happened; they dissect the strategy, the competitive dynamics, and the decisions that compounded into dominance. Once you listen, you can't go back to surface-level business content.

Best episodes:

  • "NVIDIA" — the most important company in tech, told properly
  • "Apple" — four-part series on the greatest comeback in business history
  • "Berkshire Hathaway" — understanding compounding and capital allocation
  • "Amazon" — Jeff Bezos's operating principles in action over 30 years

Perfect for: Founders who want to think long-term, investors, anyone obsessed with strategy

The Indie Hackers Podcast

Host: Courtland Allen
Network: Independent (Stripe origins)
Why it's great: The bootstrapped founder's bible. Courtland interviews founders who built profitable internet businesses without VC funding — and they share exact revenue numbers, traffic sources, and growth tactics. No vanity metrics, no fundraising theater. Just people who built something real and sustainable.

Best episodes:

  • Pieter Levels (Nomad List, Remote OK) — building in public before it was a thing
  • Josh Pigford (Baremetrics) — transparent SaaS metrics and founder mental health
  • Sahil Lavingia (Gumroad) — the honest story of a "failed" unicorn that became a great lifestyle business
  • Any episode where founders share exact MRR and growth channels

Perfect for: Bootstrappers, solopreneurs, SaaS founders, anyone building without VC

Lenny's Podcast

Host: Lenny Rachitsky (ex-Airbnb PM)
Network: Independent
Why it's great: The best podcast for product thinking, growth, and the craft of building software products. Lenny interviews PMs, founders, and growth operators from the best product companies in the world — and asks the specific, tactical questions that other interviewers don't know to ask. If you're building a product, this is required listening.

Best episodes:

  • "Finding Product-Market Fit" with Rahul Vohra (Superhuman)
  • "How Duolingo Grew to 500M Users" with Jorge Mazal
  • "The Art of the Pivot" with multiple founders
  • "How to Run Better 1:1s" — management craft for founder-CEOs

Perfect for: Product managers, technical founders, growth operators, Series A/B stage companies

20VC with Harry Stebbings

Host: Harry Stebbings
Network: 20VC (independent)
Why it's great: Harry interviews the biggest names in venture capital and startups with a prep intensity that's almost absurd — he researches every guest for 20+ hours. The result is unusually revealing conversations with investors and founders who rarely speak this openly. You'll learn how VC actually works, how term sheets are structured, and how the best investors think about markets.

Best episodes:

  • Marc Andreessen on what makes great founders
  • Benchmark's Bill Gurley on bubbles, valuations, and founder mistakes
  • Any founder episode where Harry pushes on the "what went wrong" moments
  • The annual "State of Venture" episodes for market context

Perfect for: Founders raising VC, aspiring investors, anyone who wants to understand the startup funding ecosystem

Masters of Scale

Host: Reid Hoffman (co-founder of LinkedIn)
Network: Wondery
Why it's great: Reid is uniquely positioned to host this show — he's been in the room at PayPal, LinkedIn, Greylock, and more. Each episode is built around a counterintuitive theory of scaling (e.g., "Do things that don't scale," "The best founders are cockroaches"), illustrated with stories from Airbnb, Netflix, Google, and others. The production quality is exceptional.

Best episodes:

  • "The Scrappy vs. the Polished" with Mark Zuckerberg
  • "Fake It Till You Make It" with Eric Schmidt
  • "Do Things That Don't Scale" with Brian Chesky
  • "Rapid Response" series — how companies handled COVID, crises, and existential moments

Perfect for: Growth-stage founders, anyone thinking about culture and scale, aspiring operators

My First Million for idea generation and market spotting

Masters of Scale for organizational design

⚡ Rapid-Fire Honorable Mentions

The Tim Ferriss Show

World-class performers across every field. Not startup-specific, but the operating principles translate directly. Essential for founder mindset.

The Knowledge Project (Shane Parrish)

Mental models and decision-making from top investors, founders, and scientists. Improves how you think, not just what you know.

Lex Fridman Podcast

Long-form conversations with founders and scientists at the edge of technology. Essential for understanding where tech is going.

Y Combinator's Startup School Podcast

YC partners share direct, tactical advice for early-stage founders. No fluff — just what works at the world's best accelerator.

📋 What Every Founder Should Listen To First

If you're just starting out, here's a curated starter pack:

  1. How I Built This — Airbnb: Understand that every great company looked like a failure first
  2. Indie Hackers — Sahil Lavingia (Gumroad): Learn to redefine success on your own terms
  3. Lenny's Podcast — Product-Market Fit: Understand the most important milestone in startup life
  4. Acquired — Amazon: Study a company that compounded for 30 years
  5. My First Million — Any brainstorm episode: Train your idea-generation muscle

🎧 Podcast Listening Tips for Founders

Listen at 1.5x–2x Speed

You're busy. Most conversations have dead air. Speed it up and get more signal per hour.

Keep a "Founder Notes" File

When an episode triggers an insight about your business, pause and write it down. Passive consumption is entertainment; active consumption is learning.

Revisit Relevant Episodes by Stage

The Acquired episode on Amazon will mean something completely different when you're at $1M ARR vs. $10M. Re-listen as you grow.
