Best News Podcasts (2026)

Published February 21, 2026

Stay informed without doom-scrolling. The best news podcasts of 2026 deliver daily briefings, in-depth analysis, and investigative journalism in digestible audio format.

Why Listen to News Podcasts?

News podcasts offer:

  • Curated information without the noise
  • Context and analysis beyond headlines
  • Time efficiency during commutes or workouts
  • Multiple perspectives on important issues
  • Investigative depth impossible in 30-second clips

Top News Podcasts of 2026

1. The Daily (The New York Times)

Hosted by Michael Barbaro, The Daily delivers one deep dive into a major story each weekday. 20-30 minutes of context and analysis.

Best for: Daily news deep dives, context, quality journalism

Browse The Daily episodes →

2. Up First (NPR)

NPR's morning news briefing in 10-15 minutes. Three top stories, updated by 6 AM ET every weekday.

Best for: Quick morning briefings, national news, balanced coverage

3. The Journal (The Wall Street Journal)

Business and finance news with deeper context. Daily episodes explore how major stories impact markets and your money.

Best for: Business news, markets, economics

4. Today, Explained (Vox)

Vox journalists explain one big story each day. Approachable, well-researched, and context-rich.

Best for: Explainers, context, understanding complex issues

5. The NPR Politics Podcast

NPR's political team breaks down what happened in politics and why it matters. Multiple episodes per week during major news cycles.

Best for: U.S. politics, elections, policy analysis

6. Post Reports (The Washington Post)

Washington Post journalists tell the stories behind the news. Daily deep dives into major stories.

Best for: Political news, investigative journalism, D.C. coverage

7. The Indicator from Planet Money (NPR)

Quick (under 10 minutes) daily episodes explaining one economic story with clarity and wit.

Best for: Economics, business, understanding numbers

8. The Intelligence (The Economist)

Global news from The Economist's perspective. Three stories daily with international focus.

Best for: International news, global perspective, business

9. BBC Global News Podcast

Twice-daily global news from the BBC. Comprehensive international coverage.

Best for: International news, global perspective, breaking news

10. What Next (Slate)

Mary Harris explores the story everyone is talking about and asks: what happens next?

Best for: Forward-looking analysis, implications, what to watch

How to Choose the Right News Podcast

For Quick Morning Updates: Up First, BBC Global News

For Deep Understanding: The Daily, Today Explained

For Business News: The Journal, The Indicator

For Politics: NPR Politics, Post Reports

For International News: BBC Global News, The Intelligence

For Balanced Coverage: Up First, The Daily

Building a News Podcast Routine

Morning Routine

Start with a brief (Up First or BBC) for headlines, then add a deep dive (The Daily) if time allows.

Commute Routine

Queue 2-3 deep dive episodes on topics that interest you. Let curiosity drive your listening.

Evening Catch-Up

If you missed morning news, Today Explained or Post Reports provide context without overwhelming detail.

Avoiding News Fatigue

  • Limit to 1-2 news podcasts per day
  • Choose quality over quantity
  • Skip days when you need a mental break
  • Balance news with other content types
  • Focus on stories you can act on or learn from
