Best News Podcasts 2026

Published March 11, 2026 | News & Current Events

Stay Informed Without Doomscrolling

News podcasts let you stay informed during your commute, workout, or morning routine—without the anxiety of social media feeds. These are the best ways to get your news in 2026.

Best Daily News Podcasts

1. The Daily (New York Times)

The gold standard for daily news podcasts. Host Michael Barbaro takes one major story and goes deep with NYT reporters. 20-30 minutes every morning.

Perfect for: Understanding one story deeply rather than skimming headlines

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

Browse The Daily episodes →

2. Up First (NPR)

Quick 10-15 minute rundown of the three biggest stories. Perfect for busy mornings when you need the headlines without deep dives.

Perfect for: Getting up to speed fast

Episode length: 10-15 minutes

3. Today, Explained (Vox)

The story everyone's talking about, explained with context. Vox's signature explainer style applied to current events.

Perfect for: Understanding why something matters, not just what happened

Episode length: 20-25 minutes

4. Morning Brew Daily

Business and tech news with personality. Two hosts banter through the day's business headlines with wit and context.

Perfect for: Business-focused news without being dry

Episode length: 15 minutes

5. BBC Global News Podcast

International perspective on world events. Less US-centric, more global coverage.

Perfect for: Understanding news beyond American borders

Episode length: 30 minutes, twice daily

Best Weekly News Analysis

6. The Economist - Intelligence

Global affairs and business analyzed by Economist editors and correspondents. Smart, international, authoritative.

Perfect for: Understanding the bigger picture

7. On the Media (WNYC)

How news is made and reported. Media criticism and press analysis. Essential for media literacy.

Perfect for: Understanding how to consume news critically

8. The Weeds (Vox)

Policy deep dives with wonky detail. Not just politics—actual policy substance.

Perfect for: Policy nerds who want to understand how things actually work

Best Political News Podcasts

9. Pod Save America

Former Obama staffers discuss politics with progressive perspective. Entertaining and informative.

Perspective: Left-leaning/progressive

10. The Dispatch Podcast

Conservative perspective from Never-Trump conservatives. Principled right-of-center analysis.

Perspective: Conservative (anti-populist)

11. The Ezra Klein Show

Big ideas and deep conversations about politics, policy, and culture. Thoughtful interviews with a liberal perspective.

Perfect for: Understanding complex issues through conversation

12. The Ben Shapiro Show

Conservative commentary on news and culture. Fast-talking, opinionated analysis.

Perspective: Conservative

Best International News

13. The Guardian Today in Focus

British perspective on UK and world news. Different angle than American news sources.

14. The World (PRX/BBC/WGBH)

International news collaboration. Global stories with on-the-ground reporting.

15. Financial Times News Briefing

Global business and economic news. Essential for understanding markets and international business.

Best Investigative & Deep Dive News

16. Slow Burn (Slate)

Season-long investigations into major historical events (Watergate, Clinton impeachment, etc.). Present feels like history in the making.

17. The Journal (Wall Street Journal)

Deep dives into business stories and economic trends. Great storytelling meets financial journalism.

18. Reveal (Center for Investigative Reporting)

Hard-hitting investigative journalism on important issues. Documentary-style reporting.

19. Frontline (PBS)

Podcast version of the legendary documentary series. In-depth investigations that matter.

Best Tech & Science News

20. Hard Fork (NYT)

Tech news with Kevin Roose and Casey Newton. AI, social media, Silicon Valley, and tech's impact on society.

21. The Vergecast

Consumer tech news and analysis. Gadgets, platforms, and tech culture.

22. Short Wave (NPR)

Quick daily science news. 10-15 minutes of fascinating science stories.

How to Build Your News Podcast Routine

Morning: Quick Update

Start with 10-15 minutes of headlines:

  • Up First - Three stories, 15 minutes
  • OR Morning Brew Daily - Business focus, 15 minutes

Commute: Deep Dive

One story with context:

  • The Daily - 20-30 minutes
  • OR Today, Explained - 20 minutes

Evening/Weekend: Analysis

Bigger picture understanding:

  • Ezra Klein Show - Deep conversations
  • On the Media - Media criticism
  • Economist Intelligence - Global perspective

Avoiding News Fatigue

Limit News Consumption

Pick 1-2 news podcasts max per day. You don't need every perspective on every story.

Choose Depth Over Breadth

One story deeply understood (The Daily) beats 20 headlines skimmed (doomscrolling).

Take News Breaks

It's okay to skip the news for a day. The truly important stuff will still be there tomorrow.

Mix in Non-News Content

Balance news podcasts with comedy, stories, or educational content. All news all the time is exhausting.

Understanding Bias in News Podcasts

No Source is Perfectly Neutral

Every news source has perspective. The key is understanding what it is.

Build a Balanced Diet

If you lean left, occasionally listen to The Dispatch or Ben Shapiro.
If you lean right, occasionally listen to Pod Save America or Ezra Klein.

Prioritize Reporting Over Opinion

The Daily, Up First, and The Journal focus on reporting.
Pod Save America and Ben Shapiro are explicitly opinion/commentary.

International = Different Lens

BBC, The Economist, and Guardian provide non-American perspectives valuable for understanding how others see US events.

What Makes a Great News Podcast?

Strong Reporting

Look for podcasts from established news organizations (NYT, NPR, WSJ) with real reporters in the field.

Appropriate Length

Match length to your available time:

  • 10-15 min: Up First, Morning Brew
  • 20-30 min: The Daily, Today Explained
  • 45-60 min: Ezra Klein, On the Media

Good Hosting

Clear voice, good pacing, asks questions you'd ask. Michael Barbaro (The Daily) and Lulu Garcia-Navarro are masters of this.

Context, Not Just Headlines

The best news podcasts explain why things matter, not just what happened.

News Podcast Starter Kit

  1. Subscribe to Up First (quick morning update)
  2. Subscribe to The Daily (deep dive on one story)
  3. Pick one analysis show based on your interests:
    • Policy wonk → The Weeds
    • Tech focus → Hard Fork
    • Global perspective → Economist Intelligence
    • Political left → Pod Save America
    • Political right → The Dispatch
  4. Set up morning routine: Up First while getting ready, The Daily during commute
