Best News Podcasts 2026 - Daily News, Politics & Current Events

March 12, 2026 • 12 min read

Staying informed has never been easier—or more overwhelming. News podcasts deliver expert journalism directly to your ears, whether you want a quick daily briefing or deep political analysis.

Here are the best news podcasts in 2026, organized by format and focus so you can find exactly what you need.

Quick Daily News Briefings (10-30 Minutes)

Perfect for morning routines or commutes when you want to know what happened without investing hours.

1. The Daily (The New York Times)

Host: Sabrina Tavernise (and rotating hosts)
Length: 20-30 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday
Best for: One in-depth story explained well

What makes it great: The Daily picks the most important story of the day and goes deep. NYT reporters explain the context, history, and why it matters. It's journalism you can trust, packaged for busy people.

Listen if: You want quality over quantity and trust The New York Times.

2. Up First (NPR)

Hosts: Leila Fadel, Steve Inskeep, A Martinez
Length: 10-15 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday (released by 6 AM ET)
Best for: Quick morning news catch-up

What makes it great: Three stories in 10 minutes. Up First is the fastest way to know what happened while you slept. NPR journalists break down the news clearly and without fluff.

Listen if: You're in a rush and want the essentials before work or school.

3. Post Reports (The Washington Post)

Hosts: Martine Powers
Length: 20-25 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday
Best for: Washington Post readers who want audio versions

What makes it great: WaPo reporters tell the stories they're covering, often with new angles or behind-the-scenes details. Strong on politics and policy.

Listen if: You prefer Washington Post journalism and want insider perspectives.

4. Today, Explained (Vox)

Hosts: Noel King
Length: 20-25 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday
Best for: Understanding *why* the news matters

What makes it great: Vox's signature explainer style applied to daily news. They don't just tell you what happened—they explain the bigger picture, trends, and implications.

Listen if: You want context and analysis, not just headlines.

Deep Political Analysis & Commentary

For when you want to go beyond headlines and understand the nuances of politics and policy.

5. Pod Save America

Hosts: Jon Favreau, Jon Lovett, Dan Pfeiffer, Tommy Vietor
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Best for: Progressive political analysis with humor

What makes it great: Former Obama staffers dissect the week in politics with insider knowledge and sharp wit. They interview politicians, activists, and journalists. Unapologetically left-leaning but thoughtful.

Listen if: You're progressive and want smart, entertaining political commentary.

6. The Ezra Klein Show

Host: Ezra Klein
Length: 60-90 minutes
Frequency: 2x per week
Best for: Intellectual policy discussions

What makes it great: Klein brings on experts, authors, and thinkers to explore ideas shaping politics and society. Thoughtful, nuanced conversations about policy, philosophy, and culture.

Listen if: You want to understand the ideas behind the headlines.

7. All-In Podcast

Hosts: Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg
Length: 2-3 hours
Frequency: Weekly (Fridays)
Best for: Tech industry perspective on news and politics

What makes it great: Four successful tech investors debate current events from a Silicon Valley lens. They cover markets, tech policy, geopolitics, and economics with insider knowledge.

Listen if: You want contrarian takes from people with skin in the game.

8. The Argument (The New York Times)

Hosts: Jane Coaston
Length: 45-60 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Hearing multiple political perspectives

What makes it great: Coaston moderates debates between thinkers across the political spectrum. It's one of the few places you'll hear good-faith arguments from left, right, and center.

Listen if: You want to escape echo chambers and understand other viewpoints.

Investigative & Long-Form Journalism

For deep dives, investigations, and stories that take months to report.

9. Slow Burn (Slate)

Hosts: Varies by season
Length: 40-60 minutes per episode
Frequency: Seasonal (8-10 episode seasons)
Best for: Historical context on current issues

What makes it great: Each season revisits a pivotal moment in recent history (Watergate, Bill Clinton impeachment, LA riots, etc.) with new reporting and archival audio. Helps understand how we got here.

Listen if: You want to understand how today's politics echo the past.

10. Reveal (Center for Investigative Reporting)

Hosts: Al Letson
Length: 45-60 minutes
Frequency: Weekly
Best for: Investigative journalism

What makes it great: Award-winning investigations into corruption, inequality, and injustice. Original reporting that often leads to real-world change.

Listen if: You value accountability journalism and in-depth investigations.

11. Radiolab

Hosts: Lulu Miller, Latif Nasser
Length: 60 minutes
Frequency: Bi-weekly
Best for: Science and philosophy meets current events

What makes it great: While not strictly news, Radiolab often tackles current issues through scientific and philosophical lenses. Beautifully produced storytelling.

Listen if: You want news stories told with cinematic production and deep curiosity.

International & Global News

12. The Global Story (BBC)

Hosts: Katya Adler
Length: 25-30 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday
Best for: International news perspective

What makes it great: BBC correspondents explain the biggest global story each day. Less US-centric than American podcasts.

Listen if: You want news beyond American borders.

13. The World (PRX/BBC/WGBH)

Hosts: Marco Werman
Length: 45 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday
Best for: Global news from an American public radio perspective

What makes it great: Partnership between American and international journalists covering global stories. Bridges US and international news.

Listen if: You want international news tailored for American listeners.

Business & Economics News

14. Planet Money (NPR)

Hosts: Various
Length: 20-30 minutes
Frequency: 2x per week
Best for: Economics made accessible

What makes it great: Complex economic stories explained with humor and creativity. They find fascinating angles on seemingly dry topics.

Listen if: You want to understand the economics behind the headlines.

15. The Journal (Wall Street Journal)

Hosts: Ryan Knutson, Kate Linebaugh
Length: 20-25 minutes
Frequency: Monday-Friday
Best for: Business news deep dives

What makes it great: WSJ reporters tell one big business or economic story each day. Excellent for understanding markets, companies, and economic policy.

Listen if: You care about business, markets, and economic trends.

How to Build Your News Podcast Routine

The Morning Stack (15-30 minutes total)

Pick one quick briefing:

  • Up First (10 min) + coffee = perfectly timed
  • The Daily (25 min) = deeper on one story
  • Today, Explained (20 min) = context and analysis

The Weekly Deep Dive

Pick 1-2 longer shows for weekends or when you have time:

  • Pod Save America = political week in review
  • The Ezra Klein Show = big ideas
  • All-In = tech/business perspective

The Investigation Queue

Subscribe but don't feel pressure to stay current:

  • Slow Burn (seasonal)
  • Reveal (when topics interest you)
  • Radiolab (when you want something different)

Tips for News Podcast Listeners

Avoid News Overload

  • Pick ONE daily briefing, not three
  • Unsubscribe from shows you're not listening to
  • It's okay to skip episodes on topics you don't care about
  • Take news breaks on weekends if needed

Balance Your Perspective

  • Follow at least one show outside your political bubble
  • Mix hard news (The Daily) with analysis (Ezra Klein)
  • Include international perspectives (BBC)

Speed Up Playback

  • 1.2x-1.5x speed saves time without losing comprehension
  • Most podcast apps have speed controls
  • Try slightly faster—you'll adjust quickly

The Verdict: Which News Podcasts Should You Choose?

For busy mornings: Up First (10 min) or The Daily (25 min)

For political junkies: Pod Save America + The Ezra Klein Show

For international news: The Global Story or The World

For business focus: The Journal + Planet Money

For deep investigations: Reveal + Slow Burn (seasonal)

For balanced perspectives: The Argument + Today, Explained
