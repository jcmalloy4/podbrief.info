Best Health & Fitness Podcasts 2026 - Top Wellness Shows

Want to optimize your health? These podcasts deliver science-backed advice on fitness, nutrition, mental health, sleep, and longevity from leading experts and researchers.

Why Listen to Health & Fitness Podcasts?

Health podcasts offer:

  • Expert knowledge: Learn from doctors, researchers, and trainers
  • Science-based advice: Evidence-backed strategies, not fads
  • Actionable tips: Practical tools you can use immediately
  • Motivation: Stay inspired on your wellness journey
  • Free education: University-level health knowledge at no cost

Top Health & Fitness Podcasts of 2026

1. Huberman Lab (Andrew Huberman)

Why it's great: Stanford neuroscientist breaks down how your brain and body work, with actionable protocols for sleep, focus, exercise, and more.

Best for: Deep scientific understanding with practical applications

Episode length: 90-150 minutes

Popular topics: Sleep optimization, dopamine, light exposure, cold exposure, focus protocols

Unique feature: Time-stamped protocols you can implement immediately

2. FoundMyFitness (Dr. Rhonda Patrick)

Why it's great: PhD biochemist explores longevity, nutrition, and disease prevention through cutting-edge research.

Best for: Science nerds who want deep dives into health research

Episode length: 60-120 minutes

Focus areas: Nutrition, micronutrients, sauna use, fasting, longevity

3. The Peter Attia Drive

Why it's great: Longevity physician interviews experts on living longer and better. Focus on the "marginal decade" of life quality.

Best for: Serious health optimization and longevity

Episode length: 90-180 minutes

Topics: Cardiovascular health, cancer prevention, exercise science, metabolic health

4. Feel Better, Live More (Dr. Rangan Chatterjee)

Why it's great: British GP makes health accessible with practical, holistic advice. Warm, conversational style.

Best for: Approachable health advice for everyday people

Episode length: 45-75 minutes

Approach: Four pillars of health—food, movement, sleep, relaxation

5. The Model Health Show (Shawn Stevenson)

Why it's great: Nutritionist and fitness expert delivers motivational, actionable health advice with infectious energy.

Best for: Fitness enthusiasts wanting lifestyle optimization

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

Style: Mix of solo shows and expert interviews

6. The Proof with Simon Hill

Why it's great: Physiotherapist and nutritionist explores plant-based nutrition and health science with balanced, evidence-based approach.

Best for: Plant-based eaters or anyone curious about nutrition science

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

Focus: Nutrition research, longevity, gut health

7. Mind Pump: Raw Fitness Truth

Best for: Gym-goers wanting better training strategies

Episode length: 45-90 minutes

Topics: Strength training, program design, body composition, supplements

8. Maintenance Phase (Aubrey Gordon & Michael Hobbes)

Why it's great: Journalists debunk wellness fads and diet culture with research and humor. Critical thinking about health trends.

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

9. ZOE Science & Nutrition (Jonathan Wolf)

Why it's great: Scientists from ZOE health study explain personalized nutrition, gut health, and metabolic responses.

Best for: Understanding individual responses to food

Episode length: 30-45 minutes

Focus: Gut microbiome, blood sugar, personalized nutrition

Tips for Using Health Podcasts Effectively

1. Start with One Area

Don't try to overhaul everything at once. Pick one focus area (sleep, nutrition, exercise) and go deep.

2. Take Notes

Health podcasts are dense with information. Use a notes app to capture key protocols and studies mentioned.

3. Verify with Your Doctor

Podcasts provide education, not medical advice. Discuss major changes with healthcare providers.

4. Look for Show Notes

Many health podcasts provide time-stamped notes, studies, and resources. Use them!

5. Be Patient

Health optimization takes time. Don't expect overnight transformations from podcast advice.

What Makes Great Health Podcasts?

Evidence-Based

Top health podcasts cite research, invite credentialed experts, and distinguish between data and speculation.

Actionable

The best shows give you protocols you can implement, not just theory. Huberman Lab excels at this.

Balanced

Great hosts acknowledge uncertainty and present multiple perspectives, avoiding dogma.

Accessible

Complex science explained clearly without dumbing down. Feel Better, Live More does this well.
