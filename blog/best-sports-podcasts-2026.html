Best Sports Podcasts 2026 - NFL, NBA, Soccer & Fantasy Sports

Published March 9, 2026 | 9 min read

Whether you're into football, basketball, soccer, or fantasy sports, these podcasts deliver expert analysis, insider stories, and entertaining sports talk from athletes, journalists, and superfans.

🏈 Pardon My Take

Why listen: Big Cat and PFT Commenter blend sports analysis with comedy, interviewing athletes and covering all major sports with a satirical edge.

  • Hosts: Big Cat and PFT Commenter (Barstool Sports)
  • Best for: Sports comedy, NFL, college football, interviews with athletes
  • Episode format: 3x per week, 90-120 min episodes
  • Coverage: All major sports, heavy on football (NFL/college)
  • Guests: NFL players, coaches, sports media personalities
  • Vibe: Bros who actually know sports, satirical sports media takes

🏀 The Lowe Post

Why listen: Zach Lowe (ESPN's top NBA analyst) delivers the smartest basketball analysis and interviews in podcasting.

  • Host: Zach Lowe, ESPN senior NBA writer
  • Best for: Deep NBA analysis, tactical breakdowns, insider access
  • Episode format: 2x per week during season, 60-90 min episodes
  • Guests: NBA coaches, GMs, players, top basketball minds
  • Depth: X's and O's, roster construction, league trends
  • Audience: Serious NBA fans who want substance

⚽ Men in Blazers

Why listen: Roger Bennett and Michael Davies make soccer accessible and hilarious for American audiences. Premier League, Champions League, World Cup coverage.

  • Hosts: Roger Bennett and Michael Davies (Brits in America)
  • Best for: Soccer/football, Premier League, international tournaments
  • Episode format: Multiple episodes per week during season
  • Coverage: Premier League focus, Champions League, World Cup
  • Style: Witty British humor, accessible for casual fans
  • Audience: Americans getting into soccer + serious fans

🎙️ The Bill Simmons Podcast

Why listen: The godfather of sports podcasting discusses NBA, NFL, pop culture, and media with A-list guests and hilarious hypotheticals.

  • Host: Bill Simmons, founder of The Ringer
  • Best for: NBA, NFL, sports + pop culture crossover
  • Episode format: 3-4x per week, 60-120 min episodes
  • Guests: Athletes, coaches, celebrities, sports media
  • Signature: "Rewatchables" movie pods, trade ideas, Mount Rushmore debates
  • Legacy: Pioneered long-form sports podcasting

🏈 The Pat McAfee Show

Why listen: Former NFL punter Pat McAfee brings chaos, energy, and insider NFL access with daily shows featuring surprise guests.

  • Host: Pat McAfee (former Colts punter, WWE personality)
  • Best for: NFL insider info, high-energy sports talk, unpredictability
  • Episode format: Daily 3-hour shows (yes, daily!)
  • Guests: Aaron Rodgers (regular), coaches, players, sports media
  • Vibe: Locker room energy, former player perspective
  • Unique: Daily show with massive production value

🏀 All The Smoke

Why listen: Former NBA players Matt Barnes and Stephen Jackson interview current and former players with unfiltered conversations.

  • Hosts: Matt Barnes and Stephen Jackson (ex-NBA players)
  • Best for: NBA insider stories, player perspective, unfiltered talk
  • Episode format: Weekly 90-120 min interviews
  • Guests: NBA stars (Kobe, LeBron, Durant, Curry, etc.)
  • Why it works: Players open up to former players differently
  • Topics: On-court + off-court life, beefs, behind-the-scenes stories

📊 Fantasy Footballers

Why listen: Andy, Jason, and Mike deliver daily fantasy football analysis, rankings, and strategy with humor and expertise.

  • Hosts: Andy Holloway, Jason Moore, Mike Wright
  • Best for: Fantasy football strategy, waiver wire, start/sit decisions
  • Episode format: Daily during NFL season, 45-60 min episodes
  • Coverage: Rankings, injury updates, matchup analysis, trades
  • Community: Massive fantasy football community
  • Production: High-quality, consistent, timely

⚾ Effectively Wild

Why listen: Ben Lindbergh and Meg Rowley discuss baseball with statistical analysis, historical context, and genuine love for the game.

  • Hosts: Ben Lindbergh and Meg Rowley (FanGraphs/The Ringer)
  • Best for: Sabermetrics, MLB analysis, baseball history
  • Episode format: 3-5x per week, 60-90 min discussions
  • Approach: Stats-driven but accessible, nerdy but fun
  • Topics: MLB trends, player analysis, rule changes, "Stat Blast"

🏈 Around The NFL

Why listen: NFL.com's official podcast with Gregg Rosenthal, Dan Hanzus, and team delivers comprehensive NFL coverage with personality.

  • Hosts: Gregg Rosenthal, Dan Hanzus, Marc Sessler, Erica Tamposi
  • Best for: NFL news, game recaps, team analysis, fantasy impact
  • Episode format: Daily during season, weekly in offseason
  • Coverage: Every team, every week, draft, free agency
  • Tone: Serious analysis with humor and camaraderie

🏀 The Mismatch

Why listen: Kevin O'Connor and Chris Vernon (The Ringer) debate NBA with strong opinions, analytics, and entertainment.

  • Hosts: Kevin O'Connor (analytics nerd) and Chris Vernon (old-school)
  • Best for: NBA debates, analytics vs. eye test, draft coverage
  • Episode format: 2-3x per week, 60-75 min episodes
  • Dynamic: Young analytics guy vs. veteran traditional perspective
  • Topics: Games, trades, awards, playoff predictions

🎾 No Challenges Remaining

Why listen: Ben Rothenberg covers professional tennis with insider access and thoughtful analysis of the tour.

  • Host: Ben Rothenberg, tennis journalist
  • Best for: Tennis analysis, Grand Slam coverage, player interviews
  • Episode format: Weekly during tournaments
  • Coverage: ATP, WTA, Grand Slams, tour politics
  • Guests: Players, coaches, tennis insiders

🏁 Pardon My Take (Extended Universe)

Why listen: Barstool Sports has podcasts for nearly every sport and team. Find your niche.

  • Network: Barstool Sports
  • Options: Team-specific pods for NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college teams
  • Examples: Spittin' Chiclets (NHL), Bussin' With The Boys (NFL), Starting 9 (MLB)
  • Vibe: Fan-first perspective, humor, inside access

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Sports Podcasts

  • Player perspective: Ex-athletes bring insider knowledge casual fans don't have
  • Daily vs. weekly: Some sports (NFL) work weekly, others (NBA) need daily coverage
  • Analytics revolution: Best podcasts blend stats with eye test
  • Fantasy impact: Many pods now include fantasy analysis
  • Entertainment + analysis: Top shows balance fun with substance

📚 Recommended Listening by Sport

NFL: Pardon My Take → Around The NFL → Pat McAfee Show

NBA: The Lowe Post → All The Smoke → The Mismatch

Soccer: Men in Blazers → The Totally Football Show → Counter Attack

Fantasy Football: Fantasy Footballers → The Ringer Fantasy Football Show

MLB: Effectively Wild → Starting 9

🚀 Start Here

New to sports podcasts? Begin with these:

  • Pardon My Take: Any recent episode (accessible, funny, multi-sport)
  • Bill Simmons: Post-NBA Finals or Super Bowl episodes (big events)
  • The Lowe Post: Any playoff preview episode (smart NBA talk)
  • Men in Blazers: World Cup or Champions League final episode (soccer gateway)

Bottom line: The best sports podcast depends on your team and sport. NFL? Pardon My Take or Around The NFL. NBA? The Lowe Post. Soccer? Men in Blazers. Fantasy? Fantasy Footballers. Pick your sport and dive in.
