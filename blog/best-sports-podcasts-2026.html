Best Sports Podcasts 2026 - NFL, NBA, Fantasy & More

Published March 5, 2026 • 8 min read

Whether you're a fantasy sports junkie, a die-hard NFL fan, or someone who loves deep dives into sports culture, 2026's sports podcast landscape offers something for everyone. From daily news shows to long-form storytelling, these are the best sports podcasts to follow this year.

Best Daily Sports Podcasts

The Bill Simmons Podcast

Host: Bill Simmons
Frequency: 3-4x per week
Perfect for: NBA fans, pop culture enthusiasts, Boston sports diehards

The godfather of sports podcasting continues to dominate with a mix of NBA analysis, NFL playoffs coverage, and celebrity interviews. Simmons' ability to connect sports moments to broader cultural trends makes every episode essential listening. Recent guests include Kevin Durant, Jimmy Kimmel, and Malcolm Gladwell.

Pardon My Take

Hosts: Big Cat, PFT Commenter
Frequency: Daily
Perfect for: Fans who don't take themselves too seriously

The most downloaded sports podcast in America blends comedy, satire, and genuine sports insight. Daily interviews with athletes and coaches mixed with recurring segments like "Guys on Chicks" and "Who's Back of the Week" create a unique formula that's spawned countless imitators but no equals.

The Dan Le Batard Show with Stugotz

Hosts: Dan Le Batard, Stugotz, ensemble cast
Frequency: Daily (3 hours)
Perfect for: Listeners who want sports intersecting with culture, politics, and absurdity

After leaving ESPN and going independent, Le Batard's show has only gotten better. The ensemble cast creates radio-quality chaos while tackling serious issues. It's sports talk for people who want more than just scores and hot takes.

Best Football Podcasts

The Ringer NFL Show

Hosts: Various (Robert Mays, Nora Princiotti, Danny Kelly)
Frequency: Daily during season
Perfect for: Film room junkies, prop bet enthusiasts

Multiple shows under one feed covering every angle of NFL fandom. Monday morning recaps, Wednesday film breakdowns, Friday gambling guides, and Sunday morning previews. The Ringer's stable of writers brings actual X's and O's analysis mixed with narrative storytelling.

Around the NFL

Hosts: The Podcast Heroes (Gregg Rosenthal, Dan Hanzus, Marc Sessler)
Frequency: 3-5x per week
Perfect for: NFL completists who care about all 32 teams

The personalities shine through while covering every team, every week. These guys genuinely watch every game and care about the Jaguars as much as the Chiefs. Their playoff predictions and offseason coverage are legendary in NFL podcast circles.

Best Basketball Podcasts

The Lowe Post

Host: Zach Lowe
Frequency: 2x per week
Perfect for: Film nerds, trade machine addicts, actual basketball analysis

Lowe is ESPN's best basketball writer and his podcast matches that quality. Deep tactical breakdowns, GM interviews, and smart guests make this required listening for anyone who wants to understand the modern NBA beyond just box scores.

The Old Man and the Three

Host: JJ Redick
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Fans who want player perspectives

Redick's player-to-player conversations reveal insights you'll never get from traditional media. His recent series on the evolution of the three-point shot featured interviews with Ray Allen, Steph Curry, and Damian Lillard that changed how many fans view the modern game.

Best Fantasy Sports Podcasts

The Fantasy Footballers

Hosts: Andy Holloway, Mike Wright, Jason Moore
Frequency: Daily (September-December)
Perfect for: Fantasy football obsessives

The most popular fantasy football podcast combines entertainment with actionable advice. Daily waiver wire pickups, start/sit decisions, and trade evaluations delivered with genuine chemistry between hosts who've been doing this together for over a decade.

The Ringer Fantasy Football Show

Hosts: Danny Heifetz, Danny Kelly, Craig Horlbeck
Frequency: 3x per week (in season)
Perfect for: Dynasty league players, film watchers

More analytical than most fantasy podcasts, the Ringer crew dives deep into film study and advanced metrics. Their offseason dynasty rankings and rookie draft coverage are especially valuable for serious players.

Best Soccer Podcasts

Men in Blazers

Hosts: Roger Bennett, Michael Davies
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Perfect for: Premier League fans, Americans new to soccer

Two witty Brits make soccer accessible and entertaining for American audiences. Their Premier League recaps, Champions League coverage, and celebrity interviews bring personality to a sport that can sometimes take itself too seriously in U.S. media.

The Totally Football Show

Hosts: James Richardson, various journalists
Frequency: Daily
Perfect for: European football fanatics

Comprehensive coverage of Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and Champions League. The rotating cast of British and European journalists provides insider knowledge and tactical analysis that goes beyond what casual fans see.

Best Baseball Podcasts

Effectively Wild

Hosts: Ben Lindbergh, Meg Rowley
Frequency: 3-5x per week
Perfect for: Sabermetrics nerds, baseball historians

FanGraphs' flagship podcast brings statistical rigor without losing the fun. Lindbergh and Rowley answer listener questions, break down trades, and explore obscure baseball history in marathon episodes that somehow never feel too long.

Starting 9

Hosts: Dallas Braden, Jared Carrabis
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Perfect for: Fans who want player perspectives and gambling insight

A former MLB pitcher and a lifelong Red Sox fan create an entertaining mix of insider knowledge and passionate fandom. Their playoff coverage and World Series predictions are must-listen content.

Best Sports Storytelling Podcasts

30 for 30 Podcasts

Network: ESPN
Frequency: Seasonal series
Perfect for: Long-form narrative fans

ESPN's documentary brand extends to audio with deep dives into sports stories that transcend the game. Recent series on the XFL, the WNBA's growth, and the 1990s NBA create binge-worthy seasons that appeal even to casual sports fans.

The Lead

Host: Brian Curtis
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Media nerds, industry insiders

Curtis interviews sports media personalities about the business of sports coverage. Recent conversations with Tony Romo about NFL broadcasting and Mina Kimes about building a multimedia career reveal the machinery behind sports media.

How to Find the Right Sports Podcast

Consider Your Sport

Start with your primary interest (NFL, NBA, soccer) and find the flagship show in that category. Most sports have at least 3-5 excellent podcasts with different vibes.

Match the Tone

Do you want serious analysis or comedy? Daily quick hits or weekly deep dives? Some shows lean heavily into entertainment while others prioritize tactical breakdowns.

Check the Schedule

Daily podcasts like Pardon My Take require commitment but keep you constantly updated. Weekly shows like The Lowe Post are easier to maintain but might miss breaking news.

Sample Different Hosts

Chemistry matters in sports podcasts. Try 2-3 episodes of different shows to see whose personalities click with you. Some hosts banter, others bring guests, others go solo.

Start Listening Today

Sports podcasts have revolutionized how fans consume content, offering deeper analysis, better storytelling, and more personality than traditional sports media. Whether you're commuting, working out, or cooking dinner, these shows make you feel connected to the games and communities you love.
