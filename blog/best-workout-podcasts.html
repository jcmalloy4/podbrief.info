Match Podcasts to Your Workout Type

Cardio (30-60 Minutes)

  • Running: Crime Junkie, The Daily, SmartLess
  • Cycling: All-In Podcast, My First Million (business debates keep you pedaling)
  • Treadmill: Pardon My Take, Comedy podcasts

Weightlifting (45-90 Minutes)

  • Between sets: Joe Rogan (pause-friendly long conversations)
  • Continuous: Huberman Lab (learn while lifting)
  • High-energy: Dan Le Batard Show

HIIT/Circuit Training (20-30 Minutes)

  • Fast-paced: Up First (news briefing)
  • Motivational: TED Talks Daily
  • Engaging: Short comedy podcast episodes

Workout Podcast Tips

  • Download before you go - Gym WiFi is terrible
  • Start episodes fresh - Don't resume mid-workout; start clean
  • Match energy levels - High-energy for cardio, learning for lifting
  • Use bone conduction headphones - Stay aware of your surroundings
  • Create a workout playlist - Queue 2-3 episodes for long gym sessions

What NOT to Listen to at the Gym

  • Complex business/finance: Save Invest Like the Best for desk work
  • Slow-paced interview: Terry Gross is amazing, but not for cardio
  • Heavy emotional content: Crying on the treadmill is awkward
