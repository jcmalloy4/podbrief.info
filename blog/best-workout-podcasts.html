Best Workout Podcasts: 15 Shows to Fuel Your Exercise (2026)

Motivating, energizing, and engaging podcasts perfect for runs, lifts, and cardio

Updated February 25, 2026

The right podcast can transform your workout from a grind into an experience. Whether you're running, lifting, or doing cardio, these podcasts provide the perfect mix of motivation, entertainment, and information to keep you moving.

What Makes a Great Workout Podcast?

  • Engaging enough to distract from physical discomfort
  • Not too complex – You can follow while your heart rate is elevated
  • Good pacing – Matches the rhythm of your workout
  • Motivating content – Keeps you pushing when you want to quit

Best Podcasts for Running

The Joe Rogan Experience

Long-form conversations perfect for long runs. When you're 5 miles into a run, Rogan's 3-hour conversations with comedians, scientists, and athletes keep you engaged.

Crime Junkie

True crime stories told at the perfect pace for running. Each episode is 30-60 minutes—ideal for a 5K or 10K run. The storytelling is so engaging you forget you're running.

SmartLess

Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes interview celebrities. Light, funny, and entertaining—perfect for taking your mind off the miles.

Best Podcasts for Weightlifting

All-In Podcast

Tech investors debate business, markets, and technology. The passionate arguments and disagreements match the intensity of a heavy lift. Perfect rest-period listening.

The Tim Ferriss Show

World-class performers share their routines and tactics. Listening to high achievers discuss discipline and excellence is naturally motivating during a tough workout.

Huberman Lab

Neuroscience and fitness optimization from Stanford professor Andrew Huberman. Learn about muscle growth, recovery, and performance while you train. Meta-level motivation.

Best Podcasts for Cardio & HIIT

My First Million

High-energy business podcast with rapid-fire conversations. The hosts' enthusiasm matches the intensity of interval training.

Stuff You Should Know

Educational podcast covering random topics. Episodes are 30-60 minutes—perfect for a full cardio session. The hosts' chemistry makes the time fly.

Best Motivational Podcasts for Workouts

The David Goggins Podcast

Former Navy SEAL and ultramarathon runner David Goggins on mental toughness and pushing past limits. Listen to this when you need to dig deep.

Jocko Podcast

Retired Navy SEAL Jocko Willink on discipline, leadership, and ownership. "Discipline equals freedom" is the perfect workout mantra.

Best Comedy Podcasts for Workouts

Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

Conan interviews celebrities and comedians. Laughing makes the workout easier—scientifically proven endorphin boost on top of exercise endorphins.

How Did This Get Made?

Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael, and Jason Mantzoukas dissect bad movies. Hilarious, energetic, and perfect for forgetting you're on mile 3 of the treadmill.

Best Story-Based Podcasts for Exercise

S-Town

A 7-episode mystery/true story from the makers of Serial. Each episode is gripping enough to make you extend your workout just to hear what happens next.

The Moth

True stories told live on stage. Episodes are short (10-20 min) and emotionally engaging—perfect for shorter workouts or cool-downs.

Best Science & Health Podcasts for Gym Sessions

FoundMyFitness with Dr. Rhonda Patrick

Deep dives into longevity, nutrition, and exercise science. Learn while you train—the ultimate efficiency.

Tips for Listening to Podcasts During Workouts

  • Download episodes beforehand – Avoid buffering at the gym
  • Use wireless earbuds – Cables are annoying during exercise
  • Enable playback speed control – 1.2x-1.5x if you need extra energy
  • Create workout playlists – Queue up episodes before you start
  • Match content to intensity – Complex topics for low-intensity cardio, entertaining shows for high-intensity work
