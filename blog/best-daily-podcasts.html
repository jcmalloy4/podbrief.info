Best Daily Podcasts (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

Daily podcasts become part of your routine—reliable companions for commutes, workouts, or morning coffee. These shows publish every weekday (or every day) with fresh content you can count on.

Best Daily News Podcasts

1. The Daily (New York Times)

The gold standard for daily news podcasts. Host Michael Barbaro explores one story deeply each weekday morning in 20-30 minutes.

Schedule: Monday-Friday, 6 AM ET

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

Browse The Daily summaries →

2. Up First (NPR)

Your morning news brief. Three stories in 10-15 minutes, perfect for quick updates before your day starts.

Schedule: Every day, 6 AM ET

Episode length: 10-15 minutes

3. Today, Explained (Vox)

Vox's daily explainer dives into one story with context and clarity. Great for understanding why news matters.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 20-25 minutes

4. NPR News Now

5-minute news updates, updated hourly. Perfect for the quickest possible news catch-up.

Schedule: Updated hourly

Episode length: 5 minutes

5. Post Reports (Washington Post)

Washington Post journalists tell the stories behind their reporting.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

Daily Business & Economics

1. The Indicator from Planet Money

One economic idea explained clearly in 10 minutes. Makes economics accessible and interesting.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 10 minutes

2. Marketplace (NPR)

Daily business news and economic stories that affect your life.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 30 minutes

3. WSJ What's News

Wall Street Journal's business and finance news twice daily—morning and evening editions.

Schedule: Twice daily

Episode length: 10-15 minutes

Daily Talk Shows

1. The Dan Le Batard Show

Sports, culture, and comedy. Daily 3-hour show that's more about life than just sports.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 180 minutes

2. The Pat McAfee Show

Former NFL punter Pat McAfee brings energy and NFL insider knowledge daily.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 180 minutes

3. The Rich Eisen Show

Sports talk with charm, knowledge, and A-list guests every weekday.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 180 minutes

Daily Science & Learning

1. Short Wave (NPR)

Daily science stories in 10-15 minutes. Makes science news accessible and exciting.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 10-15 minutes

2. 60-Second Science

Science news in literally 60 seconds. Perfect for daily learning in tiny doses.

Schedule: Daily

Episode length: 1 minute

3. TED Talks Daily

One TED Talk per weekday. Daily inspiration and ideas worth spreading.

Schedule: Monday-Friday

Episode length: 10-20 minutes

Why Daily Podcasts Work

Build Habits

Daily shows become rituals—morning coffee with The Daily, commute with The Indicator.

Stay Current

Never fall behind. Fresh content every day means you're always up to date.

Manageable Length

Most daily shows are 10-30 minutes—short enough to finish daily without falling behind.

Reliable Quality

The best daily shows maintain consistent quality despite the demanding schedule.

How to Build a Daily Podcast Routine

Morning Ritual

6:00-6:15 AM: Up First (news brief)
6:15-6:45 AM: The Daily (deep dive)

Commute

Short (15 min): The Indicator or Short Wave
Medium (30 min): Today, Explained or Marketplace
Long (60+ min): The Pat McAfee Show or The Dan Le Batard Show

Lunch Break

The Indicator (10 min) or TED Talks Daily (15 min)

Evening

Post Reports or WSJ What's News evening edition

Best Daily Podcast Combinations

The News Junkie Stack

  • Morning: Up First (15 min)
  • Deep dive: The Daily (30 min)
  • Evening: Post Reports (20 min)
  • Total: 65 minutes, fully informed

The Quick Learner Stack

  • News: Up First (15 min)
  • Economics: The Indicator (10 min)
  • Science: Short Wave (15 min)
  • Total: 40 minutes, diverse topics

The Business Professional Stack

  • Morning: WSJ What's News (10 min)
  • Market open: The Indicator (10 min)
  • Afternoon: Marketplace (30 min)
  • Total: 50 minutes, business-focused

Tips for Daily Podcast Listening

Auto-Download

Set your podcast app to auto-download daily shows overnight. Wake up to fresh content.

Queue Management

Delete after listening. Daily shows add up fast—don't let them pile up.

Don't Feel Guilty

Miss a day? Skip it. Daily shows are designed to be stand-alone episodes.

Speed Settings

1.25-1.5x speed helps you keep up with multiple daily shows without overwhelming your schedule.

Seasonal Adjustment

Subscribe to 2-3 daily shows, not 10. Quality over completionism.

Daily Podcasts vs. Weekly Podcasts

Daily Podcasts Win For:

  • Current events and news
  • Building habits and routines
  • Consistent companions for daily activities
  • Staying informed without effort

Weekly Podcasts Win For:

  • Deep dives and investigations
  • Narrative storytelling
  • Long-form interviews
  • Topics that don't change daily

Sample Weekly Schedule

Monday-Friday Routine

  • 6:00 AM: Up First (news brief)
  • 6:15 AM: The Daily (deep dive)
  • Commute: The Indicator (economics)
  • Lunch: Short Wave (science)

Weekends

Switch to weekly shows for longer, deeper content:

  • Saturday: Acquired, Lex Fridman, Joe Rogan
  • Sunday: Long-form interviews, narrative series
