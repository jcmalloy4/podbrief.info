Best Comedy Podcasts in 2026

March 3, 2026

Whether you need a laugh during your commute or just want to escape reality for an hour, these comedy podcasts deliver consistent laughs from professional comedians, actors, and naturally funny people.

🎭 Best Celebrity Comedy Interview Podcasts

SmartLess

Why it's great: Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes interview celebrities with genuine chemistry and improvised humor. One host doesn't know who the guest is until they walk in.

Best for: Celebrity interviews, Hollywood stories, natural banter, A-list guests

Notable episodes: Conan O'Brien, Michelle Obama, Tom Hanks, Kevin Hart

Frequency: Weekly

Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend

Why it's great: Conan's absurdist humor, self-deprecation, and genuine curiosity make every interview hilarious. His chemistry with assistant Sona Movsesian adds another layer.

Best for: Absurd humor, celebrity vulnerability, late-night comedy style

Notable episodes: Will Ferrell, Stephen Colbert, Michelle Obama, Seth Rogen

Frequency: Weekly

Armchair Expert

Why it's great: Dax Shepard and Monica Padman create a comfortable space for celebrities to be vulnerable and funny. Honest conversations with comedic moments.

Best for: Honest celebrity interviews, addiction/recovery stories, relationship humor

Notable episodes: Barack Obama, Bill Gates, Brené Brown, Kristen Bell

Frequency: Twice weekly

WTF with Marc Maron

Why it's great: Marc Maron's neurotic, brutally honest style leads to surprisingly funny and deep conversations. The garage setting creates intimacy.

Best for: Comedian interviews, behind-the-scenes stories, neurotic humor

Frequency: Twice weekly

🎙️ Best Stand-Up Comedy Podcasts

The Bonfire with Big Jay Oakerson and Dan Soder

Why it's great: Two stand-up comedians riff on current events, tell road stories, and roast each other. Unfiltered, spontaneous comedy.

Best for: Stand-up comedian banter, roasting, improvised humor

Frequency: Weekdaily (SiriusXM)

Kill Tony

Why it's great: Open mic comedians do 60 seconds, then get interviewed and roasted by Tony Hinchcliffe and guests. Chaotic, unpredictable, often hilarious.

Best for: Live comedy, roasting, unpredictability, discovering new comedians

Frequency: Weekly

🎬 Best Improv & Sketch Comedy Podcasts

Comedy Bang! Bang!

Why it's great: Scott Aukerman hosts a mix of interviews and improvised characters. Absurdist, weird, and consistently funny.

Best for: Improv comedy, character work, absurdist humor

Spontaneanation with Paul F. Tompkins

Why it's great: Entirely improvised stories based on audience suggestions. Paul F. Tompkins and improv comedians create hilarious narratives on the fly.

Best for: Improv storytelling, comedy nerds, unpredictable episodes

Frequency: Weekly

🎯 How to Choose Your Comedy Podcast Stack

For Celebrity Comedy Fans

  • Start with: SmartLess (polished, A-list guests)
  • Add: Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend (absurdist humor)
  • Deeper: WTF with Marc Maron (vulnerable, honest)

For Stand-Up Comedy Fans

  • Comedian banter: The Bonfire
  • Live comedy: Kill Tony
  • Long conversations: Joe Rogan (with comedian guests)

For Improv & Sketch Lovers

  • Characters: Comedy Bang! Bang!
  • Improvised stories: Spontaneanation

For True Crime Comedy

  • Women-focused: My Favorite Murder
  • Dark humor: Last Podcast On The Left

For Unfiltered Chaos

  • Relationship comedy: Tigerbelly or Your Mom's House
  • Personal stories: Risk!
  • Anything goes: The Joe Rogan Experience

💡 How to Get the Most Laughs

  1. Start with guests you already like - Browse PodBrief to find episodes with your favorite comedians or actors.
  2. Don't force it - Not every comedy podcast will match your humor. Try 2-3 episodes before deciding.
  3. Listen at normal speed - Comedy timing matters. Unlike educational podcasts, stick to 1x speed for best laughs.
  4. Find your humor style - Do you like absurdist? Dark? Observational? Self-deprecating? Match podcasts to your preferences.
  5. Save the best for workouts/commutes - Laughing makes boring tasks disappear.

⚠️ Comedy Podcast Red Flags

Skip if:

  • The humor feels forced or scripted
  • Hosts talk over each other constantly
  • It's more about shock value than actual jokes
  • The vibe is mean-spirited rather than funny
  • Inside jokes dominate and new listeners feel excluded

Good signs:

  • Natural chemistry between hosts
  • Guests seem genuinely comfortable and funny
  • You laugh out loud within 10 minutes
  • Stories and jokes feel spontaneous, not rehearsed
  • The humor matches your personal taste
