Best Comedy Podcasts: 18 Hilarious Shows to Listen to in 2026

Published February 28, 2026 • 14 min read

Need a laugh? These comedy podcasts deliver everything from clever conversation to absurdist humor to stand-up sets. Whether you want witty banter or chaotic energy, here are the funniest podcasts of 2026.

😂 Best Comedian Interview Podcasts

1. SmartLess

Hosts: Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes

Format: 50-60 min celebrity interviews

Three comedic actors interview surprise guests (one host doesn't know who's coming). A-list stars, hilarious chemistry, genuinely funny conversations.

Why it's great: Celebrities being funny and vulnerable with their friends

2. Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

Host: Conan O'Brien

Format: 60-75 min interviews

Conan interviews celebrities and comedians with his signature absurdist humor. One of the smartest, funniest people in comedy having loose conversations.

Why it's great: Conan is genuinely curious and hilariously self-deprecating

3. WTF with Marc Maron

Host: Marc Maron

Format: 90-120 min deep conversations

Maron's garage conversations with comedians, actors, musicians. Funny but also deeply personal and vulnerable.

Why it's great: Gets beyond the PR talking points to real stories

🎙️ Best Comedian Conversation Podcasts

4. The Joe Rogan Experience (Comedy Episodes)

Host: Joe Rogan

Format: 2-3 hour comedian hangouts

When Rogan has comedians on (Bill Burr, Tom Segura, Joey Diaz), it's just funny people being funny for hours. No structure, pure conversation.

Best comedy guests: Bill Burr, Dave Chappelle, Tom Segura, Bert Kreischer

5. Fly on the Wall with Dana Carvey and David Spade

Hosts: Dana Carvey, David Spade

Format: 60-75 min SNL/comedy stories

SNL legends share behind-the-scenes stories and interview fellow comedians. Insider perspective on comedy's biggest moments.

Why it's great: Stories from the comedy trenches

🤣 Best Improv & Scripted Comedy Podcasts

6. Comedy Bang! Bang!

Host: Scott Aukerman

Format: 60-90 min improv/interview hybrid

Part interview, part improv show. Comedians play absurd characters alongside real interviews. Unpredictable and hilarious.

Why it's great: No podcast is weirder or funnier

7. The Bonfire with Big Jay Oakerson and Dan Soder

Hosts: Big Jay Oakerson, Dan Soder

Format: 2-3 hour comedy radio show

Two stand-ups riff on news, life, and random topics. Fast-paced, irreverent, genuinely laugh-out-loud funny.

Why it's great: Natural chemistry and zero filter

8. Smartless

Hosts: Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes

Format: 50-60 min surprise guest interviews

Already mentioned above but worth repeating—one of the best comedy podcasts running.

📻 Best Comedy News & Commentary

9. Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me!

Host: Peter Sagal

Network: NPR

Format: 50 min news quiz show

Week's news told through comedy panel game. Smart, funny, informative.

Why it's great: Learn about current events while laughing

10. The Bugle

Hosts: Andy Zaltzman + rotating co-hosts

Format: 45-60 min satirical news

British satirical take on global news. Clever wordplay, absurdist humor, sharp political comedy.

Why it's great: Smart satire without being preachy

11. The Daily Show Podcast

Host: Various

Format: 25-35 min comedy news

Extended interviews and segments from The Daily Show. Political comedy for the podcast era.

Why it's great: Sharp political satire

🎭 Best Comedy Storytelling Podcasts

12. Risk!

Host: Kevin Allison

Format: 60-90 min true stories

People tell hilariously embarrassing, dangerous, or bizarre true stories on stage. Raw, honest, and very funny.

Why it's great: No topic is off-limits

13. This is Important

Hosts: The Workaholics cast

Format: 60-75 min comedy banter

Four friends from Workaholics talk about nothing and everything. Pure chaotic comedy energy.

Why it's great: Feels like hanging with funny friends

🎤 Best Stand-Up Comedy Podcasts

14. The Stand-Up Podcast

Format: Stand-up sets + interviews

Actual stand-up comedy sets from touring comedians, plus behind-the-scenes interviews about the craft.

Why it's great: Pure stand-up in podcast form

15. All Fantasy Everything

Hosts: Ian Karmel, Sean Jordan, David Gborie

Format: 75-90 min comedy draft show

Comedians "fantasy draft" random topics (sandwiches, action movies, vegetables). Hilarious and surprisingly competitive.

Why it's great: Simple format, maximum laughs

🎬 Best Pop Culture Comedy Podcasts

16. How Did This Get Made?

Hosts: Paul Scheer, June Diane Raphael, Jason Mantzoukas

Format: 75-90 min bad movie breakdowns

Comedians dissect hilariously bad movies. Educational and hysterical.

Why it's great: Makes terrible movies entertaining

17. The Rewatchables

Hosts: Bill Simmons + rotating guests

Format: 90-120 min movie deep dives

Not strictly comedy, but Simmons and guests are hilarious breaking down classic movies. Sports meets pop culture meets comedy.

Why it's great: Funny takes on movies you love

🔥 Best Rising Comedy Podcasts (2026)

18. The Basement Yard

Hosts: Joe Santagato, Frankie Alvarez

Format: 60-75 min comedy conversations

Two friends talk about life, weird news, random topics. Massive Gen Z/Millennial following.

Why it's great: Relatable humor for younger audiences

💡 How to Choose Your Comedy Podcast

If You Like Smart Comedy

→ Wait Wait Don't Tell Me, The Bugle, Comedy Bang! Bang!

If You Like Celebrity Gossip + Humor

→ SmartLess, Conan Needs a Friend, Fly on the Wall

If You Like Chaotic, Unfiltered Comedy

→ The Bonfire, This is Important, Risk!

If You Like Stand-Up

→ The Stand-Up Podcast, WTF (for comedian interviews)

If You Like Pop Culture Humor

→ How Did This Get Made, The Rewatchables

🎧 Building Your Comedy Podcast Rotation

Weekly Plan

  • Monday: Wait Wait Don't Tell Me (news + comedy)
  • Wednesday: SmartLess or Conan (celebrity interview)
  • Friday: The Bonfire or This is Important (pure comedy)
  • Weekend: How Did This Get Made (movie comedy) or WTF (deep conversation)

Mood-Based Selection

  • Need quick laugh: Wait Wait (50 min), All Fantasy Everything
  • Long commute: Joe Rogan (comedy episodes), WTF
  • Background work: SmartLess, Conan
  • Full attention: Comedy Bang! Bang!, How Did This Get Made

🎙️ Honorable Mentions

Other great comedy podcasts to explore:

  • Your Mom's House: Tom Segura & Christina P (dark humor)
  • 2 Bears 1 Cave: Tom Segura & Bert Kreischer (comedian buddies)
  • Tigerbelly: Bobby Lee & Khalyla Kuhn (chaotic Asian-American comedy)
  • Armchair Expert: Dax Shepard (celebrity interviews with humor)
  • The Fighter and The Kid: Brendan Schaub & Bryan Callen (MMA + comedy)

Conclusion

Comedy podcasts prove you don't need a visual component for great comedy—just funny people talking. Whether you want sophisticated satire, celebrity gossip, or chaotic energy, there's a comedy podcast for every taste.

Start with these three:

  1. SmartLess - Accessible, celebrity-driven, consistently funny
  2. Wait Wait Don't Tell Me - Smart news comedy, short format
  3. Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend - One of the best interviewers in comedy

From there, branch out based on your humor preferences. You'll never run out of laughs.
