How to Listen to Podcasts Efficiently

Published February 11, 2024 · 8 min read

Podcasts offer incredible value—expert knowledge, engaging stories, and fresh perspectives. But the average podcast episode is 40+ minutes, and with dozens of great shows, it's impossible to listen to everything.

Here's how to get maximum value from podcasts in minimum time.

1. Use Episode Summaries

Don't commit to a 2-hour episode blindly. Read an AI-powered summary first to decide if it's worth your time. You can get the key insights in 2-3 minutes and only listen to episodes that truly matter.

2. Speed Up Playback

Most people can easily understand podcasts at 1.5x-2x speed. This cuts listening time in half without losing comprehension. Start at 1.25x and gradually increase.

3. Be Selective About Shows

You don't need to follow every episode of every show. Choose 3-5 must-listen podcasts and sample others based on specific topics or guests.

4. Listen During Dead Time

Maximize efficiency by listening during:

  • Commuting
  • Exercise
  • Household chores
  • Walking

5. Skip What Doesn't Serve You

Many episodes have 10 minutes of ads, intros, and tangents. Don't feel obligated to listen to everything. Jump to the content that matters.

Best Podcasts by Time Commitment

Short Daily Briefs (10-20 min)

  • Up First from NPR: 15-minute morning news
  • Today, Explained: 20-minute news deep dives

Medium Episodes (30-45 min)

  • The Daily: 30-minute news analysis
  • Morning Brew Daily: 30-minute business news

Long-Form (60+ min)

  • Joe Rogan Experience: 2-3 hours (sample based on guest)
  • Huberman Lab: 90+ minutes (worth the time for health topics)
  • Acquired: 3+ hours (only for true business nerds)
