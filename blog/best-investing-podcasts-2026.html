Best Investing Podcasts 2026 - Stocks, Crypto, Real Estate & Wealth Building

Published March 10, 2026 | 10 min read

Want to build wealth through investing? These podcasts deliver expert strategies, market analysis, and actionable advice on stocks, crypto, real estate, and long-term wealth building from professional investors and financial advisors.

📈 Invest Like the Best

Why listen: Patrick O'Shaughnessy interviews elite investors, operators, and allocators about frameworks for investing and business building.

  • Host: Patrick O'Shaughnessy (CEO of O'Shaughnessy Asset Management)
  • Best for: Investment frameworks, business moats, strategy
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min deep dives
  • Guests: Top investors, CEOs, fund managers, allocators
  • Topics: Compounding, competitive advantage, capital allocation
  • Audience: Serious investors seeking high-level insights

💰 The Motley Fool Money

Why listen: Motley Fool analysts discuss the week's stock market news, earnings, and investment ideas with accessible analysis.

  • Hosts: Chris Hill and rotating Motley Fool analysts
  • Best for: Weekly market recap, stock picks, investing basics
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min discussions
  • Approach: Long-term investor perspective, not trading
  • Topics: Earnings reports, market trends, stock ideas
  • Philosophy: Buy and hold quality companies

📊 Animal Spirits

Why listen: Michael Batnick and Ben Carlson discuss markets, behavioral finance, and investing with humor and honesty.

  • Hosts: Michael Batnick and Ben Carlson (portfolio managers)
  • Best for: Behavioral finance, market commentary, realistic investing
  • Episode format: Weekly 60 min conversations
  • Style: Honest about what works and what doesn't
  • Topics: Market psychology, portfolio management, wealth building
  • Vibe: Two smart investors talking like real people

🏠 BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast

Why listen: Real estate investing strategies from investors who've built wealth through property—rentals, flips, commercial, and more.

  • Hosts: David Greene, Rob Abasolo (real estate investors)
  • Best for: Real estate investing, rental property, passive income
  • Episode format: 2x per week, 60-90 min interviews
  • Topics: House hacking, BRRRR method, syndications, multifamily
  • Community: Massive BiggerPockets investor network
  • Actionable: Specific tactics and case studies

🔐 We Study Billionaires

Why listen: The Investor's Podcast Network interviews billionaires, investors, and authors about wealth-building strategies.

  • Hosts: Stig Brodersen, Trey Lockerbie, Clay Finck
  • Best for: Value investing, billionaire strategies, long-term wealth
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min episodes
  • Guests: Billionaire investors, hedge fund managers, authors
  • Topics: Value investing, Bitcoin, markets, biographies
  • Philosophy: Learn from the best investors in history

💡 The Compound and Friends

Why listen: Josh Brown (Ritholtz Wealth Management) and guests discuss markets, investing, and wealth management with Wall Street insider perspective.

  • Host: Josh Brown "The Reformed Broker" + guests
  • Best for: Market analysis, wealth management, investing trends
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min discussions
  • Guests: Portfolio managers, analysts, financial advisors
  • Topics: Markets, ETFs, asset allocation, financial planning
  • Unique: Practitioner perspective from working wealth manager

⚡ Bankless

Why listen: Ryan Sean Adams and David Hoffman cover crypto, DeFi, and blockchain investing for the "bankless" future.

  • Hosts: Ryan Sean Adams and David Hoffman
  • Best for: Crypto investing, DeFi, blockchain technology
  • Episode format: Multiple episodes per week
  • Topics: Ethereum, Bitcoin, DeFi protocols, NFTs, crypto investing
  • Approach: Long-term crypto thesis, not day trading
  • Warning: High-risk asset class; understand before investing

📚 Meb Faber Show

Why listen: Meb Faber (Cambria Investments) interviews investors, researchers, and authors about global investing and strategies.

  • Host: Meb Faber, quantitative investor and author
  • Best for: Global macro, tactical allocation, research-based investing
  • Episode format: Weekly 60 min interviews
  • Topics: Asset allocation, value investing, momentum, global markets
  • Guests: Academic researchers, fund managers, investors

🎙️ Masters in Business

Why listen: Barry Ritholtz interviews leaders in finance, investing, and economics with deep, wide-ranging conversations.

  • Host: Barry Ritholtz (Ritholtz Wealth Management)
  • Best for: Investment legends, market history, career insights
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min interviews
  • Guests: Ray Dalio, Howard Marks, Bill Ackman, top investors
  • Depth: Goes beyond investing into life, career, philosophy
  • Production: Bloomberg Radio quality

💼 The Rational Reminder

Why listen: Evidence-based investing advice from financial advisors Cameron Passmore and Ben Felix—no hype, just research.

  • Hosts: Cameron Passmore and Ben Felix (portfolio managers)
  • Best for: Academic research, passive investing, evidence-based strategy
  • Episode format: Weekly 60-90 min discussions
  • Approach: What does the research actually say?
  • Topics: Factor investing, asset allocation, behavioral finance
  • Philosophy: Low-cost, diversified, evidence-based

🏦 Afford Anything

Why listen: Paula Pant covers real estate investing, financial independence, and building wealth through multiple income streams.

  • Host: Paula Pant, real estate investor and FIRE advocate
  • Best for: Real estate, FIRE movement, passive income
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min episodes
  • Topics: Rental properties, index funds, financial independence
  • Audience: People building wealth to achieve freedom

📈 The Acquirers Podcast

Why listen: Tobias Carlisle interviews deep value investors about finding undervalued companies and contrarian strategies.

  • Host: Tobias Carlisle, value investor and author
  • Best for: Value investing, contrarian strategies, stock picking
  • Episode format: Weekly 30-45 min interviews
  • Topics: Stock analysis, value metrics, investing frameworks
  • Philosophy: Buy undervalued businesses and wait

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Investing Podcasts

  • Long-term > short-term: Best investors focus on years, not days
  • Behavior matters: Psychology often determines investment success
  • Diversification is key: Don't put all eggs in one basket
  • Low costs compound: Fees matter enormously over time
  • Know yourself: Best strategy is one you'll stick with

📚 Recommended Listening by Goal

For stock investing: Invest Like the Best → Motley Fool Money → Animal Spirits

For real estate: BiggerPockets → Afford Anything

For crypto: Bankless → We Study Billionaires (Bitcoin episodes)

For passive investing: The Rational Reminder → Animal Spirits

For value investing: We Study Billionaires → The Acquirers Podcast

🚀 Start Here

New to investing podcasts? Begin with these episodes:

  • The Rational Reminder: "Your Complete Guide to Factor-Based Investing" (fundamentals)
  • Animal Spirits: "Things Everyone Should Know About Investing" (accessible intro)
  • BiggerPockets: "House Hacking 101" (real estate intro)
  • Motley Fool Money: Any recent episode (weekly market overview)

Bottom line: The best investing podcast depends on your strategy. Want stocks? Invest Like the Best or Motley Fool Money. Want real estate? BiggerPockets. Want crypto? Bankless. Want evidence-based advice? The Rational Reminder. Start with your investment goals and go from there.
