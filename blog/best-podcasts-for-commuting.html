Best Entertainment for Long Commutes

Got a longer drive? These shows keep you engaged:

  • SmartLess: Celebrity interviews with humor
  • The Joe Rogan Experience: Long conversations (sample episodes)
  • Armchair Expert: Honest celebrity conversations

Best Learning Podcasts for Drive Time

  • Huberman Lab: Health and neuroscience (long episodes, save for longer drives)
  • How I Built This: Entrepreneur origin stories
  • TED Talks Daily: Big ideas in 15-20 minutes

Tips for Commute Listening

  • Match episode length to your commute time
  • Download episodes beforehand (avoid data/signal issues)
  • Use 1.25x-1.5x speed to fit more content
  • Read episode briefs to pick the best content
