SmartLess vs Armchair Expert: Which Celebrity Podcast is Better? (2026)

Two of the biggest celebrity interview podcasts—SmartLess (Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes) and Armchair Expert (Dax Shepard and Monica Padman). Both feature A-list guests and insider Hollywood stories, but they couldn't be more different. Here's how to choose.

Quick Comparison

Feature SmartLess Armchair Expert
Hosts Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, Sean Hayes Dax Shepard + Monica Padman
Format Surprise guest reveal Deep 2-hour conversations
Length 60-75 minutes 90-150 minutes
Frequency Weekly (Mondays) 2x weekly (Mon + Thu)
Vibe Fun, light, comedy-focused Vulnerable, introspective, therapeutic
Guest Type A-list celebrities, athletes, politicians Celebrities + experts + academics

SmartLess: The Fun, Surprise Celebrity Hangout

The Format

Each week, one of the three hosts invites a surprise guest—the other two hosts don't know who's coming. This creates genuine reactions and keeps conversations fresh and unpredictable.

The Hosts

  • Jason Bateman: The straight man, keeps things moving
  • Will Arnett: The goofball, always cracking jokes
  • Sean Hayes: The enthusiastic one, biggest reactions

What Makes It Great

  • Chemistry: The three hosts are genuinely friends—the banter is natural
  • Access: These guys can book anyone—Obama, Oprah, Kamala Harris, Paul McCartney
  • Fun: It's a comedy podcast first, interview second
  • Digestible: 60-75 minutes feels breezy

Potential Downsides

  • Can feel superficial—more entertainment than depth
  • The surprise format sometimes feels gimmicky
  • Hosts occasionally talk over guests
  • Chemistry is so strong it can overshadow the guest

Best Episodes

  • Will Ferrell (peak comedy chaos)
  • Barack Obama (surprisingly loose and funny)
  • Margot Robbie (charming and hilarious)
  • Conan O'Brien (comedy legends colliding)

Armchair Expert: The Deep, Vulnerable Conversation

The Format

Dax Shepard conducts 90-150 minute interviews, often diving deep into childhood trauma, addiction, relationships, and personal growth. Monica Padman co-hosts and adds perspective. Follow-up "Fact Check" segment at the end where Monica corrects Dax's mistakes.

The Hosts

  • Dax Shepard: Vulnerable, self-deprecating, loves therapy-speak
  • Monica Padman: The fact-checker, balances Dax's energy

What Makes It Great

  • Depth: Guests actually open up about real struggles
  • Vulnerability: Dax shares his own addiction, failures, and insecurities
  • Variety: Celebrities, scientists, authors, experts—broad guest mix
  • Expert episodes: "Expert on X" episodes are legitimately educational
  • Fact Check: The post-interview segment is often as entertaining as the main interview

Potential Downsides

  • Can feel preachy or therapy-heavy
  • Dax sometimes dominates with his own stories
  • Long runtime isn't for everyone
  • The earnestness can feel forced

Best Episodes

  • Kristen Bell (Dax's wife, incredibly honest)
  • Brené Brown (vulnerability expert talking vulnerability)
  • Matthew McConaughey (surprisingly deep)
  • Dr. Laurie Santos (expert on happiness science)

Head-to-Head: Which is Better?

Interview Style

SmartLess: Conversational, playful, lets the guest shine
Armchair Expert: Therapeutic, probing, digs into psychology and backstory

Winner: Depends on your mood. SmartLess for fun, Armchair Expert for depth.

Guest Quality

SmartLess: A-list movie stars, politicians, athletes
Armchair Expert: Celebrities + scientists, authors, experts

Winner: SmartLess for celebrity access, Armchair Expert for variety.

Host Chemistry

SmartLess: Three best friends with perfect comedic timing
Armchair Expert: Close partnership, but Dax dominates

Winner: SmartLess. The trio chemistry is unmatched.

Rewatchability

SmartLess: Light and fun, easy to revisit
Armchair Expert: Heavy topics make re-listening less appealing

Winner: SmartLess.

Educational Value

SmartLess: Minimal—mostly entertainment
Armchair Expert: High—especially "Expert on X" episodes

Winner: Armchair Expert.

Length

SmartLess: 60-75 minutes (digestible)
Armchair Expert: 90-150 minutes (commitment)

Winner: SmartLess for busy schedules, Armchair Expert for deep dives.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose SmartLess if you:

  • Want to laugh and be entertained
  • Prefer shorter episodes you can finish in one sitting
  • Like the surprise element
  • Care more about celebrity access than depth
  • Want something light for commutes or workouts

Choose Armchair Expert if you:

  • Want deep, meaningful conversations
  • Enjoy therapy-speak and vulnerability
  • Are interested in psychology, science, and expert insights
  • Don't mind 2+ hour episodes
  • Like the "Fact Check" segment and behind-the-scenes banter

Why not both?

They serve completely different needs. SmartLess is your fun Friday night; Armchair Expert is your Sunday morning coffee deep-dive. Subscribe to both and pick based on your mood.

The Verdict

For pure entertainment: SmartLess wins
For depth and learning: Armchair Expert wins
For host chemistry: SmartLess wins
For guest vulnerability: Armchair Expert wins

Both are excellent. SmartLess is the celebrity podcast for people who want to have fun. Armchair Expert is the celebrity podcast for people who want to understand what makes people tick.
