Best Finance Podcasts: 18 Shows for Investing & Money Management (2026)

Whether you're learning to invest, managing wealth, or staying on top of markets, these finance podcasts deliver expert insights without the jargon. From beginner-friendly personal finance to advanced investing strategies.

Best Finance Podcasts Overview

1. The Motley Fool Money

Best for: Stock market news and stock picks

Weekly market analysis, stock recommendations, and interviews with business leaders. Great for active investors who want actionable ideas.

2. Planet Money (NPR)

Best for: Understanding economics

Makes economics entertaining and accessible. Perfect for understanding how money, markets, and the economy actually work.

3. Rational Reminder

Best for: Evidence-based investing

Academic, research-backed approach to investing. Deep dives into portfolio construction, behavioral finance, and market efficiency.

4. We Study Billionaires

Best for: Value investing and Warren Buffett disciples

In-depth investor interviews and book studies. Learn from the best investors in history.

5. BiggerPockets Money

Best for: Financial independence and early retirement

Personal finance, real estate investing, and FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) strategies from people who've done it.

6. The Indicator from Planet Money

Best for: Daily economic news (10-15 minutes)

Short, daily episodes explaining one economic indicator or market trend. Perfect for staying informed without time commitment.

7. Animal Spirits

Best for: Market commentary and investor psychology

Ben Carlson and Michael Batnick discuss markets, investing, and financial planning with humor and common sense.

8. InvestED

Best for: Father-daughter investing lessons

Phil Town teaches his daughter (and listeners) value investing principles. Beginner-friendly and conversational.

9. Afford Anything

Best for: Real estate and passive income

Paula Pant covers real estate investing, financial independence, and lifestyle design. "You can afford anything, but not everything."

10. Chat with Traders

Best for: Trading strategies and active investing

Interviews with professional traders sharing their strategies, setups, and lessons learned from wins and losses.

11. The Dave Ramsey Show

Best for: Debt payoff and budgeting basics

Live calls with people getting out of debt. Strong on discipline, controversial on investing advice. Great for motivation.

12. ChooseFI

Best for: Financial independence strategies

Actionable tactics for saving more, investing smarter, and building wealth to retire early or work optionally.

13. Masters in Business (Bloomberg)

Best for: Wall Street insider insights

Barry Ritholtz interviews top investors, economists, and business leaders. High-level finance and investing wisdom.

14. Stacking Benjamins

Best for: Personal finance with humor

Money management, investing, and wealth-building advice delivered with jokes and pop culture references.

15. The Money Guy Show

Best for: Financial planning and wealth accumulation

CFPs Brian Preston and Bo Hanson break down investing, retirement planning, tax strategies, and financial decision-making.

16. Invest Like the Best

Best for: Venture capital and growth investing

Patrick O'Shaughnessy interviews investors, founders, and thinkers. Covers public markets, venture capital, and business strategy.

17. MarketFoolery

Best for: Daily stock market wrap-up (15 minutes)

Quick daily take on market news and individual stocks. Great for active investors who want a daily pulse check.

18. The Personal Finance Podcast

Best for: Beginner-friendly money management

Andrew Giancola covers budgeting, debt, investing, side hustles, and building wealth from scratch.

How to Choose the Right Finance Podcast

If you're new to investing:

  • Start with Planet Money or The Indicator (economics basics)
  • Move to InvestED or Stacking Benjamins (beginner investing)
  • Avoid trader-focused shows until you understand fundamentals

If you're building wealth:

  • BiggerPockets Money or ChooseFI (financial independence)
  • Afford Anything (real estate and passive income)
  • The Money Guy Show (comprehensive financial planning)

If you're an active investor:

  • The Motley Fool Money or MarketFoolery (stock picks)
  • We Study Billionaires (value investing deep dives)
  • Chat with Traders (if you're interested in trading)

If you want to understand markets:

  • Rational Reminder (academic, evidence-based)
  • Animal Spirits (behavioral finance and psychology)
  • Masters in Business (Wall Street perspectives)

Final Thoughts

The best finance podcast is the one you'll actually listen to consistently. Start with one from the beginner category, then branch out as your knowledge grows. Avoid podcasts that promise "get rich quick" schemes—wealth building takes time and discipline.

Remember: podcasts are education, not investment advice. Always do your own research before making financial decisions.
