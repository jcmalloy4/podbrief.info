Best Finance Podcasts 2026: Top Shows for Investors & Entrepreneurs

Finance podcasts have become essential tools for investors, entrepreneurs, and anyone looking to build wealth. From market analysis to business strategy, these shows deliver insights from billionaires, venture capitalists, and financial experts. Here are the best finance podcasts in 2026.

💰 Best Venture Capital & Tech Finance Podcasts

1. All-In Podcast

Why it's essential: Four billionaire tech investors (Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg) debate markets, politics, technology, and business. Unfiltered insights you won't get elsewhere.

Best for: Tech investors and startup founders

Format: 90-120 minute weekly roundtables

Browse All-In episodes on PodBrief

2. Acquired

Why it's valuable: Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal deliver the deepest company histories and business strategy analysis available. Each episode is an MBA-level case study.

Best for: Understanding how great companies are built

Format: 2-4 hour deep dives on single companies

Browse Acquired episodes on PodBrief

3. My First Million

Why it's actionable: Shaan Puri and Sam Parr break down business ideas, trends, and strategies with zero fluff. Every episode sparks entrepreneurial thinking.

Best for: Aspiring entrepreneurs and idea generators

Format: 60-90 minute conversations

Browse My First Million episodes on PodBrief

📊 Best Investing & Market Analysis Podcasts

4. Invest Like the Best with Patrick O'Shaughnessy

Why it's insightful: O'Shaughnessy interviews the best investors, allocators, and business builders. Deep conversations about strategy and mental models.

Best for: Serious investors wanting framework-level thinking

Format: 60-90 minute interviews

5. The Compound and Friends

Why it's real: Josh Brown (The Reformed Broker) and Michael Batnick discuss markets, investing, and wealth management with humor and honesty.

Best for: Retail investors and financial advisors

Format: Weekly market commentary

6. Animal Spirits

Why it's balanced: Ben Carlson and Michael Batnick cut through market noise with practical investing wisdom and behavioral finance insights.

Best for: Long-term investors avoiding hype

Format: 30-45 minute weekly discussions

💼 Best Business & Entrepreneurship Podcasts

7. How I Built This with Guy Raz

Why it's inspiring: Founders of iconic companies share their journeys from startup to success. Stories behind Airbnb, Instagram, Spanx, and more.

Best for: Entrepreneurs seeking inspiration and lessons

Format: 45-60 minute founder interviews

8. Masters of Scale with Reid Hoffman

Why it's strategic: LinkedIn founder explores how companies grow from zero to billions. Features iconic founders and CEOs.

Best for: Scaling businesses and strategy

Format: 30-45 minute highly produced episodes

9. The Tim Ferriss Show (Business Episodes)

Why it's tactical: Deep dives with world-class performers, extracting tactics, tools, and routines. Some of the best business interviews available.

Best for: Optimizers and high achievers

Format: 2-3 hour long-form conversations

📈 Best Personal Finance & Wealth Building Podcasts

10. The Money Guy Show

Why it's practical: Brian Preston and Bo Hanson deliver actionable financial advice for every life stage. From 401(k)s to tax strategy.

Best for: Building wealth systematically

Format: Weekly financial planning guidance

11. ChooseFI

Why it's comprehensive: Financial independence strategies covering saving, investing, real estate, and lifestyle optimization.

Best for: FIRE movement followers

Format: 60-90 minute episodes

12. BiggerPockets Money Podcast

Why it's relatable: Real people share their financial journeys, mistakes, and successes. Covers budgeting to multimillion-dollar net worths.

Best for: Learning from others' financial stories

Format: Weekly financial journey interviews

🏦 Best Macro Economics & Markets Podcasts

13. Odd Lots

Why it's essential: Bloomberg's Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal explore markets, economics, and finance in depth. The best macro podcast.

Best for: Understanding market structure and economics

Format: 45-60 minute expert interviews

14. The Prof G Pod with Scott Galloway

Why it's provocative: NYU professor offers unfiltered takes on business, tech, and markets with sharp analysis and predictions.

Best for: Big-picture market perspectives

Format: Weekly commentary and Q&A

🎯 Best Real Estate Investing Podcasts

15. BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast

Why it's comprehensive: The definitive real estate investing podcast. Strategies from house hacking to commercial deals.

Best for: Real estate investors at any level

Format: Weekly investor interviews

📊 How to Choose the Right Finance Podcast

By Your Financial Goal:

  • Build startup: All-In, My First Million, How I Built This
  • Invest in stocks: Invest Like the Best, The Compound, Animal Spirits
  • Achieve FIRE: ChooseFI, Money Guy Show
  • Real estate: BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast
  • Understand markets: Odd Lots, Prof G Pod
  • Study great companies: Acquired

By Time Commitment:

  • Short (30-45 min): Animal Spirits, Prof G Pod
  • Medium (60-90 min): All-In, My First Million, Invest Like the Best
  • Deep Dive (2+ hours): Acquired, Tim Ferriss Show

By Experience Level:

  • Beginner: Money Guy Show, ChooseFI, How I Built This
  • Intermediate: The Compound, My First Million, BiggerPockets
  • Advanced: All-In, Invest Like the Best, Odd Lots, Acquired

🎧 Tips for Finance Podcast Listeners

1. Don't Follow Blindly

Even expert investors make mistakes. Use podcasts for ideas and frameworks, not direct financial advice.

2. Track Actionable Ideas

Keep a note of book recommendations, investing strategies, and business insights mentioned.

3. Diversify Your Sources

Different perspectives prevent echo chambers. Balance bull and bear viewpoints.

4. Focus on Principles Over Predictions

Market predictions are often wrong. Timeless principles about investing and business are more valuable.

5. Apply What You Learn

Listening without action is entertainment. Implement at least one insight per week.
