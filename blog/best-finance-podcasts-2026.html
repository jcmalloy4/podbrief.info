Best Finance Podcasts 2026: Money, Wealth & Personal Finance

March 7, 2026 • 12 min read

Your financial education doesn't stop after college—or start there, for most people. The best finance podcasts deliver what traditional education misses: practical money management, real investing strategies, and economic context that actually affects your life.

Whether you're learning to budget for the first time, building an investment portfolio, or trying to understand why your retirement account just dropped 15%, there's a podcast that can help. This guide covers the best finance podcasts for personal finance, investing, economics, and wealth building.

Personal Finance & Money Management

The Ramsey Show

Host: Dave Ramsey (and team)

What you'll learn: Debt elimination, budgeting, emergency funds, avoiding financial traps

Why it's essential: Love him or hate him, Dave Ramsey's debt snowball method has helped millions get out of debt. The show features real callers with real financial problems. His approach is conservative and disciplined—perfect if you need motivation to pay off debt.

ChooseFI

Hosts: Jonathan Mendonsa & Brad Barrett

What you'll learn: Financial independence (FI), early retirement strategies, tax optimization, frugality

Why it's essential: The gold standard for the FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) movement. Practical strategies for saving 50%+ of your income and retiring decades early. Real interviews with people who've done it.

BiggerPockets Money

Hosts: Mindy Jensen & Scott Trench

What you'll learn: Personal finance, real estate investing, side hustles, wealth building

Why it's essential: Weekly interviews with people on different financial journeys. From $20k in debt to $5M net worth—you'll hear real strategies and real numbers.

More personal finance:

  • Afford Anything - Paula Pant on building wealth through real estate and investing
  • The Money Guy Show - Financial planning and wealth accumulation
  • Stacking Benjamins - Entertaining personal finance with humor

Investing & Stock Market

Invest Like the Best

Host: Patrick O'Shaughnessy

What you'll learn: Investment frameworks, portfolio strategies, market insights from top investors

Why it's essential: Interviews with legendary investors, fund managers, and business operators. High-level conversations about what actually drives long-term returns. Not for beginners, but incredible for intermediate+ investors.

The Compound and Friends

Host: Josh Brown (& team)

What you'll learn: Stock market analysis, investing psychology, portfolio construction

Why it's essential: Reformed broker Josh Brown delivers smart, no-BS market commentary. Daily and weekly shows covering what's happening in markets and what it means for your portfolio.

Animal Spirits

Hosts: Michael Batnick & Ben Carlson

What you'll learn: Market commentary, behavioral finance, investor psychology

Why it's essential: Weekly discussions on markets, money, and life. Honest takes on why most investing advice is useless and what actually matters.

More investing podcasts:

  • All-In Podcast - Tech investors discuss markets, economy, and tech (covered in investing guide)
  • We Study Billionaires - Preston Pysh on value investing and Bitcoin
  • Motley Fool Money - Weekly stock market roundup
  • InvestED - Phil Town on value investing principles

Economics & Big Picture

Planet Money

Network: NPR

What you'll learn: Economics, trade, markets, business, how money works

Why it's essential: Makes economics fascinating and accessible. Episodes explain everything from why gas prices spike to how the Fed actually works. Perfect for understanding the economic context behind financial decisions.

Freakonomics Radio

Host: Stephen Dubner

What you'll learn: Behavioral economics, hidden incentives, how people really make decisions

Why it's essential: Economics isn't just about money—it's about how people behave. Understanding incentives helps you make better financial decisions.

More economics:

  • The Indicator - NPR's daily 10-minute economics podcast
  • Odd Lots - Bloomberg's deep dives on markets and economics
  • Macro Musings - David Beckworth on monetary policy and macroeconomics

Real Estate Investing

BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast

What you'll learn: Rental properties, house flipping, REITs, real estate strategies

Why it's essential: 600+ episodes interviewing successful real estate investors. From house hacking your first duplex to building a $50M portfolio.

More real estate:

  • Afford Anything - Paula Pant's real estate and FI strategies
  • The Real Estate Guys - Wealth building through property

Entrepreneurship & Business Finance

My First Million

Hosts: Shaan Puri & Sam Parr

What you'll learn: Business ideas, revenue models, bootstrapping, making money online

Why it's essential: Two successful entrepreneurs break down businesses and share ideas you can actually execute. Episodes include real revenue numbers and growth strategies.

More business finance:

  • How I Built This - Guy Raz interviews founders (funding, scaling, exits)
  • Acquired - Deep dives on how great companies were built
  • The Tim Ferriss Show - Entrepreneurs and investors share strategies

Cryptocurrency & Digital Assets

Bankless

Hosts: Ryan Sean Adams & David Hoffman

What you'll learn: Crypto markets, DeFi, Ethereum, Web3, digital assets

Why it's essential: The leading podcast for understanding cryptocurrency beyond hype. Deep technical knowledge balanced with practical investing strategies.

More crypto:

  • The Pomp Podcast - Anthony Pompliano on Bitcoin and markets
  • We Study Billionaires (Bitcoin series) - Preston Pysh's Bitcoin deep dives
  • Unchained - Laura Shin on crypto and blockchain

How to Learn from Finance Podcasts

1. Start with fundamentals: If you're new to finance, begin with personal finance podcasts (Ramsey, ChooseFI) before diving into investing.

2. Take action on what you learn: Listening to investing podcasts won't grow your wealth. Opening a brokerage account and investing will.

3. Diversify your sources: No single podcast has all the answers. Different perspectives prevent blind spots.

4. Ignore noise, focus on principles: Daily market commentary is mostly noise. Focus on episodes about long-term strategy and proven principles.

5. Track your financial education: Keep notes on key concepts and tactics you want to implement. Your podcast notes become your financial playbook.

6. Verify claims: Podcasts are entertainment first. Always verify investment advice with multiple sources or a qualified financial advisor.

Common Finance Podcast Mistakes

1. Listening without executing: Financial education is worthless if you don't act on it. Start small, but start.

2. Following advice blindly: What works for a 30-year-old tech worker in SF won't work for a 50-year-old teacher in Iowa. Adapt strategies to your situation.

3. Getting caught up in hype: Crypto, meme stocks, day trading—podcasts often chase trends. Stick to proven principles.

4. Ignoring personal finance basics: You can't invest your way out of overspending. Master budgeting and saving before worrying about asset allocation.

5. Treating podcasts as financial advice: Podcasts are educational content, not personalized financial advice. Consult a qualified advisor for major decisions.

Final Thoughts

Financial literacy is one of the most valuable skills you can develop, and podcasts are one of the most accessible ways to learn. The difference between someone who understands money and someone who doesn't compounds over decades.

Start with 2-3 podcasts that match your current financial stage. Listen consistently. Take notes. Execute on what you learn. Your future self will thank you.
