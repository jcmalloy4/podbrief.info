Best Interview Podcasts: 12 Shows with Incredible Conversations (2026)

The art of conversation at its finest. These interview podcasts feature world-class hosts asking great questions and getting fascinating answers.

What Makes a Great Interview Podcast?

The best interview podcasts have three ingredients:

  • Skilled host: Asks insightful questions, listens actively, knows when to dig deeper
  • Interesting guests: World-class performers, experts, or people with unique stories
  • Real conversation: Not a sales pitch or promotion tour—actual dialogue

Best Long-Form Interview Podcasts

1. The Tim Ferriss Show

Host: Tim Ferriss

Length: 2-3 hours

Guests: World-class performers, investors, athletes, artists

Why it's the best: Tim deconstructs excellence. He asks guests about their routines, habits, beliefs, and failures. Every episode has actionable insights.

Notable guests: Arnold Schwarzenegger, Jamie Foxx, Brené Brown, Naval Ravikant, Susan Cain

2. Lex Fridman Podcast

Host: Lex Fridman

Length: 2-4 hours

Guests: AI researchers, scientists, philosophers, tech leaders

Why it's great: Lex asks deep, thoughtful questions. Long silences. Philosophical tangents. Guests open up in ways they don't elsewhere.

Notable guests: Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, Sam Altman, Yuval Noah Harari, Joe Rogan

3. The Joe Rogan Experience

Host: Joe Rogan

Length: 2-3 hours

Guests: Comedians, fighters, scientists, politicians, entrepreneurs

Why it's iconic: The most popular podcast in the world. Joe's curiosity and conversational style make guests feel comfortable sharing unfiltered opinions.

Notable guests: Elon Musk, Bernie Sanders, Neil deGrasse Tyson, Jordan Peterson, Mike Tyson

Best Business & Entrepreneurship Interview Podcasts

4. How I Built This

Host: Guy Raz

Length: 45-60 minutes

Guests: Founders of iconic companies

Why it's great: Guy Raz gets founders to tell the complete story—from idea to exit. Honest about failures and pivots.

Notable guests: Brian Chesky (Airbnb), Sara Blakely (Spanx), Kevin Systrom (Instagram), Stewart Butterfield (Slack)

Browse How I Built This summaries →

5. The Knowledge Project

Host: Shane Parrish

Length: 60-90 minutes

Guests: Thinkers, investors, leaders

Why it's great: Shane focuses on mental models, decision-making, and timeless wisdom. Conversations go deep on first principles.

Notable guests: Ray Dalio, Daniel Kahneman, Patrick O'Shaughnessy, Annie Duke

6. The Diary of a CEO

Host: Steven Bartlett

Length: 60-90 minutes

Guests: Entrepreneurs, psychologists, celebrities

Why it's great: Steven asks vulnerable questions about mental health, relationships, and success. Guests share things they don't say elsewhere.

Notable guests: Mo Gawdat, Simon Sinek, Matthew McConaughey, Rory Sutherland

Why it's fun: One host picks a mystery guest for the other two. Friendly banter, inside Hollywood stories, genuine laughs.

Best Niche Interview Podcasts

10. On Being

Host: Krista Tippett

Length: 50 minutes

Guests: Spiritual leaders, poets, scientists, activists

Why it's profound: Krista asks about meaning, purpose, and the human experience. Thoughtful, contemplative conversations.

Notable guests: Mary Oliver, Brené Brown, David Whyte, Ruby Sales

11. WTF with Marc Maron

Host: Marc Maron

Length: 90 minutes

Guests: Comedians, actors, musicians

Why it's raw: Marc records in his garage. Intimate, unpolished, honest conversations about comedy, creativity, and life.

Notable guests: Barack Obama, Robin Williams, Louis CK, Dave Chappelle, Jerry Seinfeld

12. The Ezra Klein Show

Host: Ezra Klein

Length: 60-90 minutes

Guests: Authors, thinkers, policymakers

Why it's smart: Ezra reads everything his guests have written. Deep, well-researched conversations about ideas and policy.

Notable guests: Tyler Cowen, Sam Harris, Yuval Noah Harari, Tressie McMillan Cottom

How to Choose the Right Interview Podcast

If you want business insights: Tim Ferriss, How I Built This, The Knowledge Project

If you want intellectual depth: Lex Fridman, Ezra Klein, On Being

If you want entertainment: SmartLess, Conan O'Brien, Armchair Expert

If you want long-form exploration: Joe Rogan, Tim Ferriss, Lex Fridman

If you want quick insights: How I Built This (45-60 min), SmartLess (45-60 min)

What Makes These Hosts Great

  • Tim Ferriss: Obsessive preparation, pattern recognition, actionable frameworks
  • Lex Fridman: Deep curiosity, comfortable with silence, philosophical
  • Joe Rogan: Conversational, curious, makes guests comfortable
  • Guy Raz: Storytelling structure, narrative arc, warm tone
  • Dax Shepard: Vulnerable, honest about flaws, relatable
  • Conan O'Brien: Witty, self-deprecating, genuinely funny

Pro Tips for Interview Podcast Listening

  • Start with episodes about guests you know: Then branch out to unfamiliar names
  • Read episode descriptions: Not every guest is interesting to everyone
  • Use 1.2x speed: Most interview podcasts work well at slightly faster speed
  • Take notes: Great interviews have quotable insights—write them down
  • Follow guests: If you love a guest, search for their other podcast appearances
