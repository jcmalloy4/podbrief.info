Best Interview Podcasts 2026 - Deep Conversations & Famous Guests

Published March 5, 2026 • 9 min read

The best interview podcasts create space for conversations that traditional media can't match. With episodes running 1-3 hours, hosts and guests can explore ideas deeply, share personal stories, and go far beyond soundbites. Whether you're interested in celebrity profiles, entrepreneurial wisdom, or intellectual discussions, these podcasts consistently deliver compelling conversations.

Best General Interview Podcasts

The Joe Rogan Experience

Host: Joe Rogan
Frequency: 4-5x per week
Length: 2-3 hours
Perfect for: Uncensored conversations, eclectic guest mix

Love him or hate him, Rogan's podcast is the biggest in the world for a reason. His curiosity and willingness to let conversations flow naturally creates moments you won't find elsewhere. Recent guests include Elon Musk, Bernie Sanders, Neil deGrasse Tyson, and UFC fighters, reflecting the show's unique range from comedy to science to politics.

What makes it work: Rogan's genuine curiosity and ability to make guests comfortable enough to share unguarded thoughts. The 2-3 hour format allows for depth impossible in traditional media.

SmartLess

Hosts: Jason Bateman, Sean Hayes, Will Arnett
Frequency: Weekly
Length: 45-60 minutes
Perfect for: Celebrity interviews with humor, guests who are "smart" and "less" known to one host

Three comedy stars take turns surprising each other with mystery guests. The twist: only one host knows who's coming, creating genuine reactions and fresh dynamics. Recent episodes with Harrison Ford, Billie Eilish, and Michelle Obama showcase their ability to balance entertainment with substance.

What makes it work: The chemistry between three established stars puts guests at ease, and the surprise element keeps conversations spontaneous.

WTF with Marc Maron

Host: Marc Maron
Frequency: 2x per week
Length: 60-90 minutes
Perfect for: Introspective conversations, comedy world insiders, emotional depth

Maron pioneered the intimate podcast interview format from his garage, conducting over 1,400 episodes with everyone from President Obama to unknown comedians. His neurotic honesty encourages guests to be vulnerable, resulting in conversations that reveal the person behind the persona.

What makes it work: Maron's therapeutic interview style creates emotional safety. Guests open up because Maron openly discusses his own struggles first.

Best Business & Entrepreneur Interviews

How I Built This

Host: Guy Raz
Frequency: Weekly
Length: 45-60 minutes
Perfect for: Startup stories, founder journeys, business inspiration

Guy Raz interviews founders about their origin stories, from Airbnb and Spanx to Patagonia and Ben & Jerry's. The narrative format tracks each company from idea to success, including the failures, pivots, and lucky breaks along the way.

Notable episodes: Sara Blakely (Spanx), Brian Chesky (Airbnb), Howard Schultz (Starbucks)

The Tim Ferriss Show

Host: Tim Ferriss
Frequency: 2x per week
Length: 90-120 minutes
Perfect for: Performance optimization, tactics from world-class performers

Ferriss "deconstructs world-class performers" by digging into their routines, habits, and decision-making frameworks. His prepared questions and obsessive research create interviews that are both tactical and philosophical. Guests span tech, sports, arts, and military.

Signature format: "What would you put on a billboard?" and other recurring questions create comparable insights across episodes.

My First Million

Hosts: Shaan Puri, Sam Parr
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Length: 45-60 minutes
Perfect for: Business ideas, market analysis, internet entrepreneurs

Two successful entrepreneurs brainstorm business ideas, break down companies, and interview founders. The conversational format feels like listening to smart friends debate opportunities, with specific numbers and frameworks rather than vague inspiration.

Best Intellectual & Academic Interviews

Lex Fridman Podcast

Host: Lex Fridman
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Length: 2-4 hours
Perfect for: AI, science, philosophy, existential questions

MIT researcher Lex Fridman conducts marathon conversations with the world's leading thinkers. His calm demeanor and genuine curiosity create space for guests to explore ideas at their deepest level. Recent interviews with Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Yuval Noah Harari, and Richard Dawkins are master classes in long-form intellectual conversation.

What makes it work: Fridman's technical background lets him go deep with scientists, while his philosophical interests create bridges to broader questions about humanity and existence.

The Ezra Klein Show

Host: Ezra Klein
Frequency: 2x per week
Length: 60-90 minutes
Perfect for: Books, ideas, policy, understanding complex systems

Klein interviews authors, researchers, and policymakers about big ideas. His preparation is legendary—he reads entire books before interviewing authors and asks questions that push conversations into unexpected territory. Topics range from AI safety to urban housing to consciousness.

On Being with Krista Tippett

Host: Krista Tippett
Frequency: Weekly
Length: 50-60 minutes
Perfect for: Meaning, spirituality, wisdom, the human condition

Tippett explores "the big questions" through conversations with poets, theologians, scientists, and activists. Her thoughtful questions and contemplative pacing create meditative listening experiences that contrast with the rapid-fire style of most podcasts.

Best Comedy & Entertainment Interviews

Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend

Host: Conan O'Brien
Frequency: Weekly
Length: 60 minutes
Perfect for: Comedy, celebrity stories, Conan being Conan

Conan brings his late-night charm to long-form conversations with fellow comedians, actors, and musicians. His self-deprecating humor and genuine interest in his guests create interviews that are both hilarious and surprisingly intimate.

Standout moments: The running gag with his assistant Sona and producer Matt adds extra layers of comedy.

Armchair Expert

Host: Dax Shepard
Frequency: 2x per week
Length: 90-120 minutes
Perfect for: Celebrities discussing addiction, relationships, and personal growth

Shepard's openness about his own recovery and relationship struggles creates permission for guests to discuss topics typically avoided in promotional interviews. The "fact check" segment with Monica Padman adds humor and corrections to the main conversation.

Best Industry-Specific Interviews

Acquired

Hosts: Ben Gilbert, David Rosenthal
Frequency: Monthly
Length: 2-4 hours
Perfect for: Deep dives on companies and acquisitions

While not traditional interviews, Acquired's occasional founder conversations (like their recent episodes with Charlie Munger) are extraordinary. Their research-heavy approach means they ask questions no one else thinks to ask.

The Knowledge Project

Host: Shane Parrish
Frequency: Weekly
Length: 60-90 minutes
Perfect for: Mental models, decision-making, wisdom

Parrish explores how successful people think through conversations with investors, scientists, and leaders. His focus on frameworks and principles over tactics creates timeless content.

What Makes a Great Interview Podcast?

The Host's Preparation

The best interviewers do their homework. Ezra Klein reads entire books. Tim Ferriss researches obsessively. Preparation shows respect for guests and audiences alike, enabling conversations that go beyond what's already been said elsewhere.

Creating Safety

Guests open up when they feel safe. Marc Maron shares his own vulnerabilities first. Dax Shepard discusses his struggles openly. This emotional safety leads to the memorable moments that transcend typical PR interviews.

The Long-Form Advantage

Traditional media interviews run 5-10 minutes. Podcast conversations run 1-3 hours. This time allows guests to move past prepared talking points into actual thought, creating the spontaneous insights that make interviews memorable.

Genuine Curiosity

The common thread among great interviewers: they're genuinely curious. Joe Rogan, Lex Fridman, and Krista Tippett ask questions because they want to know the answers, not because they're performing journalism.

How to Choose Interview Podcasts

Follow Guests, Not Just Hosts

Use PodBrief or podcast apps to search for specific guests across shows. Your favorite author or entrepreneur has probably done multiple podcast appearances—compare how different hosts bring out different sides.

Match Energy Levels

Some shows are high-energy and fast-paced (SmartLess, My First Million). Others are contemplative and slow (On Being, The Knowledge Project). Match the vibe to your mood and activity.

Consider Interview Style

Do you prefer structured questions (Tim Ferriss) or free-flowing conversation (Joe Rogan)? Comedic (Conan) or serious (Lex Fridman)? Different styles serve different purposes.

Length Matters

Commuters might prefer 45-60 minute episodes (SmartLess, How I Built This). Weekend listeners can handle 2-3 hour marathons (Rogan, Lex Fridman). Most apps let you speed up playback if time is tight.

Start Listening Today

Interview podcasts have revolutionized how we access brilliant minds and fascinating people. The long-form format creates space for genuine connection, unexpected insights, and conversations that stick with you long after the episode ends.
