Best Tim Ferriss Show Interviews 2026 - Top Episodes

Published February 24, 2026

The Tim Ferriss Show has featured over 700 episodes with world-class performers from every field imaginable. From Navy SEALs to neuroscientists, entrepreneurs to artists, Tim extracts the tactics, routines, and habits that drive success. Here are the best Tim Ferriss episodes worth your time.

What Makes Tim Ferriss Interviews Special?

  • Actionable tactics: Tim focuses on specific techniques and routines you can implement
  • Deep preparation: Extensive research means informed, specific questions
  • Diverse guests: From athletes to investors, scientists to artists
  • Vulnerability: Tim shares his own struggles and experiments openly

Top Tim Ferriss Podcast Episodes by Category

Best Business & Entrepreneurship Episodes

Naval Ravikant Episodes

Multiple conversations with the AngelList founder cover wealth creation, happiness, and philosophy. Naval's insights on leverage, specific knowledge, and building wealth are legendary.

Why listen: Life-changing philosophy on wealth and happiness

Peter Thiel Episodes

PayPal co-founder and venture capitalist Thiel discusses contrarian thinking, startup strategy, and building monopolies. Essential listening for entrepreneurs.

Why listen: Learn contrarian thinking from a legendary investor

Seth Godin Episodes

Marketing legend Godin shares insights on shipping, finding your tribe, and doing work that matters. His perspective on creativity and marketing is invaluable.

Why listen: Master class in marketing and creativity

Best Health & Fitness Episodes

Dr. Peter Attia Episodes

Longevity expert Attia discusses exercise, nutrition, sleep, and extending healthspan. These episodes are packed with actionable health optimization tactics.

Why listen: Science-backed health and longevity strategies

Dr. Andrew Huberman Episodes

Neuroscientist Huberman shares protocols for optimizing sleep, focus, and performance. His practical, science-based advice has helped millions.

Why listen: Actionable neuroscience protocols

Kelly Starrett Episodes

Physical therapist Starrett discusses mobility, movement, and maintaining your body. Essential for anyone who sits at a desk or exercises regularly.

Why listen: Fix your movement patterns and prevent injury

Best Psychology & Mental Performance Episodes

Brené Brown Episodes

Vulnerability researcher Brown discusses courage, shame, and authentic leadership. Her work has transformed how millions think about human connection.

Why listen: Understand vulnerability and courage

Cal Fussman Episodes

Master interviewer Fussman shares insights on asking questions, listening deeply, and extracting wisdom from conversations. A podcast about podcasting, done brilliantly.

Why listen: Learn the art of conversation

Maria Popova Episodes

Brain Pickings creator Popova discusses literature, philosophy, and living meaningfully. Her perspective on creativity and learning is unique and inspiring.

Why listen: Expand your intellectual horizons

Best Personal Development Episodes

Derek Sivers Episodes

CD Baby founder Sivers shares unconventional wisdom on business, life, and learning. His ability to distill complex ideas into simple principles is remarkable.

Why listen: Simple, powerful life principles

Ryan Holiday Episodes

Stoic philosopher and author Holiday discusses ancient wisdom applied to modern life. His perspectives on ego, obstacles, and stillness resonate widely.

Why listen: Apply Stoic philosophy to your life

Tim's Personal Episodes

Tim's solo episodes where he shares personal struggles with depression, psychedelics, and optimization are among his most vulnerable and valuable content.

Why listen: Raw honesty about mental health and growth

Best Creative & Writing Episodes

Legendary author Gaiman discusses the creative process, storytelling, and maintaining creativity over decades. Essential for any writer or creator.

Why listen: Master storyteller shares his craft

Malcolm Gladwell Episodes

Bestselling author Gladwell discusses research, writing, and how to tell compelling stories with data. His approach to non-fiction is masterful.

Why listen: Learn narrative non-fiction from the best

Bridgewater founder Dalio discusses principles, economic cycles, and building systems. His systematic approach to life and investing is fascinating.

Performance coach Robbins shares insights on psychology, business, and financial planning. Love him or hate him, his tactics work for millions.

How to Approach The Tim Ferriss Show

For entrepreneurs: Naval, Peter Thiel, and Seth Godin are must-listens

For health optimization: Peter Attia and Andrew Huberman episodes

For writers: Neil Gaiman, Malcolm Gladwell, and Maria Popova

For investors: Ray Dalio, Naval, and finance-focused episodes

For personal growth: Brené Brown, Ryan Holiday, and Derek Sivers

For Tim's journey: Listen to his personal/solo episodes

The Tim Ferriss Method

Tim's interview approach focuses on extracting actionable insights:

  • Morning routines: What do peak performers do first thing?
  • Favorite books: What books have shaped their thinking?
  • Failure stories: What did they learn from setbacks?
  • Specific tactics: Exact protocols, not vague advice
  • Billboard advice: If you had a billboard, what would it say?
