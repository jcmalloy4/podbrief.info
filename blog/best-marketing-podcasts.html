Example episodes: "How Morning Brew Grew to 4M Subscribers", "The Best Businesses Nobody Talks About"

💡 How to Learn Marketing from Podcasts

  1. Focus on actionable episodes - Look for case studies, tactical breakdowns, and specific strategies you can implement.
  2. Browse summaries first - Read AI summaries on PodBrief to find episodes with tactics relevant to your business.
  3. Take implementation notes - Don't just listen passively. Write down specific actions to test in your marketing.
  4. Follow up on resources - Podcasts often mention tools, frameworks, and templates. Actually download and use them.
  5. Test what you learn - The best marketing podcast listeners run experiments based on what they hear.

🎓 From Podcast to Practice

Weekly routine that works:

  1. Monday: Listen to 1-2 growth/strategy podcasts during commute
  2. Tuesday: Implement one tactic from Monday's episodes
  3. Wednesday: Check channel-specific podcast (SEO, social, email)
  4. Thursday: Apply what you learned
  5. Friday: Review results, plan next week's tests
