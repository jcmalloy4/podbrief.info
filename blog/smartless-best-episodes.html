Best SmartLess Episodes (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

SmartLess features Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes taking turns surprising each other with mystery celebrity guests. The chemistry between these three friends creates genuine laughter and surprisingly deep conversations. These are the episodes worth your time.

Essential First Episodes

1. Will Ferrell

Will Ferrell's energy perfectly matches the show's vibe. Chaos, improvisation, and genuine friendship on display.

Why start here: Pure comedy gold, shows what makes SmartLess special

2. Conan O'Brien

A podcast legend talks podcasting with three of his comedy peers. Meta and hilarious.

Why start here: Comedy royalty, inside baseball, great chemistry

3. Barack Obama

The former president opens up about leadership, family, and life after the White House.

Why start here: Shows the show's range—serious and substantive

4. Shaquille O'Neal

Shaq's larger-than-life personality fills the room (virtually). Business, basketball, and brotherhood.

5. Kevin Hart

Comedian Kevin Hart talks hustle, success, and staying hungry.

What Makes SmartLess Work

Genuine Friendship

The chemistry between Jason, Will, and Sean is real. They're actually friends.

Mystery Guest Format

One host knows who's coming, two don't. Creates authentic surprise and spontaneity.

Balance of Funny and Serious

They can make you laugh and then deliver a profound conversation in the same episode.

A-List Access

Their Hollywood connections mean they get guests who rarely do podcasts.

Respectful but Playful

They honor guests while keeping things light and fun.

How to Listen to SmartLess

Start with People You Love

See a guest you admire? Start there. The format is consistent enough that any episode works as entry.

Match Your Mood

  • Want laughs? Will Ferrell, Conan, comedians
  • Want inspiration? Obama, Malala, athletes
  • Want stories? Actors and musicians

Perfect for Commutes

45-60 minutes fits most commutes perfectly. Complete episodes, not cliffhangers.

Listen with Partners

Great for couples—accessible, entertaining, often touching.

Why SmartLess Became Huge

  • Authentic chemistry: Real friends, not podcast partners
  • Celebrity access: A-listers who trust the hosts
  • Quality production: Professional without being overproduced
  • Consistent schedule: Weekly episodes, reliable
  • Accessibility: You don't need Hollywood knowledge to enjoy
  • Heart: They genuinely care about guests and stories
