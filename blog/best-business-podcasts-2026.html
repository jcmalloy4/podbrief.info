Best Business Podcasts 2026 - Entrepreneurship, Strategy & Leadership

Published March 9, 2026 | 10 min read

Whether you're building a startup, running a company, or climbing the corporate ladder, these business podcasts deliver insider strategies, real founder stories, and actionable advice from the world's top entrepreneurs and executives.

🚀 All-In Podcast

Why listen: Four elite investors (Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg) debate business, tech, markets, and policy with insider knowledge and zero filter.

  • Hosts: Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg
  • Best for: Market analysis, tech trends, venture capital, macro economics
  • Episode format: Weekly 90-120 min discussions
  • Unique angle: Contrarian takes, debates between hosts, insider VC perspective
  • Audience: Founders, investors, tech executives

💰 My First Million

Why listen: Shaan Puri and Sam Parr brainstorm business ideas, analyze successful companies, and share unfiltered founder insights.

  • Hosts: Shaan Puri (former CEO) and Sam Parr (founder of The Hustle)
  • Best for: Business idea generation, trend spotting, copycat businesses
  • Episode format: 2x per week, conversational and entertaining
  • Signature segments: Business breakdowns, "I'd Buy That" idea sessions
  • Vibe: Friends talking business, highly relatable

📈 Acquired

Why listen: Deep-dive case studies on the biggest company acquisitions and business stories in history. Harvard Business School quality, podcast format.

  • Hosts: Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal
  • Best for: Business history, M&A strategy, company building playbooks
  • Episode format: Monthly 2-4 hour deep dives (seriously deep)
  • Recent episodes: NVIDIA, Costco, Nike, LVMH, Berkshire Hathaway
  • Research quality: Months of research per episode, incredibly thorough

🎯 How I Built This

Why listen: NPR's Guy Raz interviews founders of household-name companies about their origin stories, early struggles, and breakthrough moments.

  • Host: Guy Raz, master interviewer
  • Best for: Founder inspiration, startup journeys, perseverance stories
  • Episode format: Weekly 45-60 min narrative interviews
  • Featured companies: Airbnb, Instagram, Spanx, Patagonia, Bumble, Canva
  • Why it works: Real struggles, pivots, near-death moments

🧠 The Knowledge Project

Why listen: Shane Parrish interviews CEOs, investors, and thinkers about decision-making frameworks and mental models for business.

  • Host: Shane Parrish (Farnam Street founder)
  • Best for: Strategic thinking, decision-making, leadership frameworks
  • Episode format: Weekly long-form interviews
  • Focus: How great leaders think, not just what they did
  • Guests: Warren Buffett's inner circle, top CEOs, military strategists

💡 Masters of Scale

Why listen: Reid Hoffman (LinkedIn founder) reveals counterintuitive strategies for scaling companies from startup to empire.

  • Host: Reid Hoffman, legendary entrepreneur and investor
  • Best for: Scaling strategies, growth tactics, network effects
  • Episode format: Weekly narrative-driven episodes with founder interviews
  • Production: Highly produced, storytelling format
  • Themes: Blitzscaling, platform strategies, hiring, culture

🎙️ The Tim Ferriss Show

Why listen: Tim deconstructs world-class performers to extract their tactics, routines, and decision-making frameworks.

  • Host: Tim Ferriss, author of "The 4-Hour Workweek"
  • Best for: Deep dives, optimization, unconventional strategies
  • Episode format: 2-3 hour long-form interviews, very detailed
  • Guests: Tech founders, investors, CEOs, Navy SEALs, scientists
  • Signature questions: "What do you do daily?" "Biggest failures?" "Book gifts?"

📊 20VC (20 Minute VC)

Why listen: Harry Stebbings interviews top VCs and founders about fundraising, venture capital, and startup strategy.

  • Host: Harry Stebbings (started at age 19, now runs a VC fund)
  • Best for: Fundraising advice, VC insights, pitch strategies
  • Episode format: 3-5x per week, 30-60 min interviews
  • Guests: Sequoia, a16z, Benchmark partners + top founders
  • Topics: Cap tables, valuations, board management, exits

🏢 Invest Like the Best

Why listen: Patrick O'Shaughnessy interviews investors, operators, and allocators about business building and investing frameworks.

  • Host: Patrick O'Shaughnessy (CEO of O'Shaughnessy Asset Management)
  • Best for: Investment frameworks, business moats, strategy
  • Episode format: Weekly deep dives with elite investors and operators
  • Quality: Extremely high signal-to-noise ratio
  • Topics: Compounding, competitive advantage, capital allocation

🎯 The Prof G Pod

Why listen: Scott Galloway delivers sharp analysis on business, tech, and markets with entertaining, no-BS commentary.

  • Host: Scott Galloway, NYU professor and entrepreneur
  • Best for: Tech industry analysis, market trends, business hot takes
  • Episode format: 2x per week (solo + interview)
  • Style: Provocative, funny, data-driven
  • Topics: Big tech, antitrust, brand strategy, market predictions

🚀 Lenny's Podcast

Why listen: Product and growth strategies from leaders at the fastest-growing companies (Airbnb, Stripe, Notion, Figma).

  • Host: Lenny Rachitsky, former Airbnb PM and newsletter writer
  • Best for: Product-led growth, PLG strategies, product management
  • Episode format: Weekly interviews with product and growth leaders
  • Audience: Product managers, founders, growth marketers
  • Topics: User onboarding, retention, pricing, product strategy

💼 The Founders Podcast

Why listen: David Senra reads and summarizes biographies of history's greatest entrepreneurs. Learn from Rockefeller, Musk, Jobs, Bezos.

  • Host: David Senra (obsessive biography reader)
  • Best for: Learning from history's best entrepreneurs
  • Episode format: Solo episodes, 60-90 min deep on one founder
  • Featured: Steve Jobs, Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos, Henry Ford, Sam Walton
  • Philosophy: All great founders studied previous generations

🎧 Business Breakdown by The Motley Fool

Why listen: Weekly deep dives into how specific public companies actually make money and compete.

  • Hosts: Motley Fool analysts
  • Best for: Understanding business models, competitive strategy
  • Episode format: Company-specific deep dives
  • Recent episodes: Costco, Amazon, Meta, Google, Uber

🔑 Key Themes Across Top Business Podcasts

  • Founder-market fit matters: Great founders solve problems they deeply understand
  • Distribution > Product: Best product doesn't always win; best distribution wins
  • Network effects are king: Winner-take-most markets favor platforms
  • Cash flow fundamentals: Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is reality
  • Hiring is everything: A-players hire A-players, B-players hire C-players

📚 Recommended Listening Strategy

For early-stage founders: My First Million → How I Built This → 20VC

For growth-stage operators: All-In → Lenny's Podcast → Masters of Scale

For investors: Invest Like the Best → Acquired → 20VC

For strategic thinkers: The Knowledge Project → The Founders → Acquired

🚀 Start Here

New to business podcasts? Begin with these episodes:

  • My First Million: "Billion Dollar Business Ideas" (fun, accessible)
  • How I Built This: "Airbnb: Brian Chesky" (inspiring founder story)
  • All-In: Any recent episode (pulse on tech/markets)
  • Acquired: "NVIDIA Parts 1-3" (masterclass in business strategy)

Bottom line: The best business podcast depends on your stage. Early founders need inspiration and tactics (MFM, HIBT). Growth operators need playbooks (Lenny, Masters of Scale). Investors need frameworks (ILTB, Acquired). Pick your lane and binge the archive.
