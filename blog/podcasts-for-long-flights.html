Best Podcasts for Long Flights

Published February 14, 2024 · 10 min read

A long flight is perfect for catching up on podcasts. Here are the best shows to download before boarding your next 5-10+ hour flight.

Long-Form Storytelling (2-3 Hours Each)

The Joe Rogan Experience

2-3 hour conversations that make flights disappear. Download 2-3 episodes and you'll cover a 6-9 hour flight.

Best for flights because:

  • Episodes are 2-3 hours long (perfect for long hauls)
  • Conversational style is easy to follow
  • Diverse topics keep you engaged
  • No need to switch podcasts mid-flight

Flight Length Recommendations

Short Haul (2-3 Hours)

  • 3-4 episodes of The Daily (30 min each)
  • 2 episodes of SmartLess (45 min each)
  • 1 Huberman Lab episode (90 min)

Medium Haul (5-7 Hours)

  • 2 Joe Rogan episodes (2.5 hours each)
  • 3 Huberman Lab episodes (90 min each)
  • 5 How I Built This episodes (60 min each)

Long Haul (10+ Hours)

  • 4 Joe Rogan episodes (10 hours total)
  • 6 Huberman Lab episodes (9 hours total)
  • Mix: 2 Rogan + 3 Huberman + 2 SmartLess (10+ hours)

Flight Podcast Strategy

Download Before Security

  • Airport WiFi is slow and crowded
  • Plane WiFi is expensive and unreliable
  • Download at home or hotel the night before

Queue Smartly

  • Takeoff: Start with something engaging (SmartLess, Conan)
  • Cruise: Long-form content (Rogan, Huberman)
  • Mid-flight slump: Switch to lighter content
  • Landing: News briefings (The Daily, Up First)

Backup Content

  • Download 50% more than you think you'll need
  • Delays happen — extra episodes are insurance
  • Better to have too much than run out mid-flight

What NOT to Listen to on Flights

  • True crime before bed flights: Makes it hard to sleep
  • Heavy emotional content: Crying in 32B is awkward
  • Work-related podcasts: Flights are for escaping work
  • Anything requiring note-taking: Hard to write on a plane

Pro Tips

  • Use noise-canceling headphones (engine noise is exhausting)
  • Start at 1.2x speed (helps you stay focused when tired)
  • Download a mix of genres (your mood will change mid-flight)
  • Bring a backup charger (international flights drain batteries)
