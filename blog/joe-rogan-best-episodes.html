Best Joe Rogan Experience Episodes (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

The Joe Rogan Experience is the biggest podcast in the world, with 2,000+ episodes covering comedy, science, politics, sports, and everything in between. With 3-hour conversations and guests from every field, knowing where to start can be overwhelming. These are the essential episodes.

Essential First Episodes

1. Elon Musk (#1169, #1470, #1609)

Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk's appearances became cultural moments. From smoking weed to discussing AI and Mars, these episodes showcase Rogan's ability to get A-listers to open up.

Topics: AI, Tesla, SpaceX, neural links, future of humanity

Why start here: Historic episodes, massive cultural impact

2. Neil deGrasse Tyson (#919, #1159, #1347)

Astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson makes space, science, and the cosmos accessible and fascinating across multiple appearances.

Topics: Space, science, cosmos, critical thinking

Why start here: Educational, entertaining, mind-expanding

3. Graham Hancock (#142, #725, #1284, #1543)

Controversial historian Graham Hancock discusses ancient civilizations, lost history, and alternative archaeology theories. Fascinating whether you agree or not.

Topics: Ancient history, lost civilizations, archaeology

Why start here: Mind-bending theories, compelling storytelling

4. Jordan Peterson (#877, #958, #1006, #1769)

Clinical psychologist Jordan Peterson discusses meaning, responsibility, psychology, and how to live a meaningful life.

Topics: Psychology, meaning, responsibility, philosophy

Why start here: Deep wisdom, practical advice, cultural significance

5. Joey Diaz (Multiple)

Comedian Joey Diaz tells wild stories from his life with incredible energy and humor. Pure entertainment.

Topics: Comedy, life stories, street wisdom

Why start here: Hilarious, authentic, shows Rogan's comedy roots

How to Listen to Joe Rogan

Start with Topics You Care About

Love comedy? Start with Bill Burr. Into science? Try Neil deGrasse Tyson or Brian Cox.

Embrace the Length

3-hour episodes allow depth impossible in traditional media. Spread across multiple sessions if needed.

Speed Settings

Many listeners use 1.25-1.5x speed. Joe's conversational pace handles it well.

Don't Feel Obligated to Finish

With 2,000+ episodes, it's okay to bail if a guest doesn't click.

Use Spotify Chapters

Skip around to topics that interest you—you don't have to listen chronologically.

Why Joe Rogan Works

  • Long-form: 3 hours allows real depth
  • Curiosity: Joe asks questions everyone wants answered
  • Variety: Every topic imaginable
  • Authenticity: Unscripted, unfiltered conversations
  • Access: Gets the biggest names to open up
  • Everyman perspective: Asks layperson questions
