Best True Crime Podcasts 2026 - Top Shows Ranked

Published February 24, 2026

True crime podcasts have exploded in popularity, offering gripping stories of murder mysteries, cold cases, and investigative journalism. Whether you're a seasoned true crime fan or just getting started, this guide will help you discover the best true crime podcasts of 2026.

What Makes a Great True Crime Podcast?

  • Compelling storytelling: Expert narrative that keeps you hooked episode after episode
  • Thorough research: Deep dives into cases with attention to detail
  • Respectful approach: Handling sensitive topics with care for victims and families
  • Quality production: Clear audio, good pacing, and immersive sound design

Top True Crime Podcasts in 2026

1. Serial

The podcast that started it all. Serial investigates one case per season with unprecedented depth. From the Adnan Syed case to the Cleveland kidnappings, Sarah Koenig's investigative journalism sets the standard for the genre.

Best for: Deep investigative journalism

2. Crime Junkie

Crime Junkie covers a different case each week with Ashley Flowers and Brit Prawat. Their conversational style makes complex cases accessible, and their "full body chills" moments have become iconic.

Best for: Weekly dose of true crime

3. My Favorite Murder

Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark blend true crime with comedy in My Favorite Murder. Their "hometown murders" segment lets fans share local stories, creating a vibrant community.

Best for: True crime with humor

4. Dateline NBC

The classic TV show adapted for podcasts. Dateline NBC brings decades of investigative experience to your earbuds, with Keith Morrison's distinctive narration.

Best for: Classic investigative journalism

5. Casefile True Crime

Anonymous Australian narrator, meticulous research, and international cases make Casefile stand out. The serious tone and attention to detail appeal to hardcore true crime fans.

Best for: International cases

6. Cold

Season one investigated the Susan Powell case with unprecedented access and research. Cold represents the gold standard for limited-series true crime podcasts.

Best for: Limited series deep dives

7. Morbid: A True Crime Podcast

Autopsy technician Alaina Urquhart and hairdresser Ash Kelley bring unique perspectives to Morbid. Their chemistry and thorough research have built a devoted fanbase.

Best for: Medical/forensic insights

8. True Crime Obsessed

Patrick Hinds and Gillian Pensavalle recap true crime documentaries with hilarious commentary. If you love both true crime and comedy, True Crime Obsessed is perfect.

Best for: Documentary recaps

Finding Your Perfect True Crime Podcast

If you want investigative journalism: Start with Serial or Dateline NBC

If you prefer lighter content: Try My Favorite Murder or True Crime Obsessed

If you want weekly episodes: Crime Junkie releases like clockwork every Monday

If you love international cases: Casefile covers stories from around the world

How to Listen Responsibly

True crime is entertainment, but it's important to remember these are real stories about real people. The best podcasts handle cases with respect, avoid sensationalism, and often work to bring attention to unsolved cases or wrongful convictions.
