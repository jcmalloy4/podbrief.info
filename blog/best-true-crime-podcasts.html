Best True Crime Podcasts (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

True crime podcasts have exploded in popularity because they combine compelling storytelling with real stakes. These shows investigate murders, mysteries, and the criminal justice system with journalistic rigor and narrative craft.

Top True Crime Podcasts

1. Serial

The podcast that launched the true crime boom. Sarah Koenig investigates one case across an entire season, uncovering new evidence and challenging assumptions.

Best for: Deep investigative journalism

Format: Season-long investigations

Browse Serial summaries →

2. Criminal

Phoebe Judge explores the strange world of crime—not just murders, but fascinating stories about people who've done wrong, been wronged, or gotten caught somewhere in the middle.

Best for: Unusual crime stories

Episode length: 25-40 minutes

3. My Favorite Murder

Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark discuss true crime cases with humor, empathy, and a devoted fan community. Part comedy, part crime, all compelling.

Best for: Lighthearted true crime

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

4. Casefile True Crime

An anonymous Australian host delivers meticulously researched cases with straightforward, no-nonsense narration. International cases, excellent production.

Best for: Serious, detailed investigations

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

5. Dateline NBC

The legendary TV show's podcast version brings the same investigative depth and Keith Morrison's unforgettable voice to audio format.

Best for: Classic true crime storytelling

Episode length: 40-50 minutes

6. In the Dark

Investigative journalists dig into miscarriages of justice, uncovering new evidence that has led to real-world legal changes.

Best for: Criminal justice investigations

Format: Season-long investigations

7. Crime Junkie

Ashley Flowers and Brit Prawat present well-researched cases every week with engaging storytelling and consistent quality.

Best for: Weekly true crime fix

Episode length: 40-60 minutes

8. Bear Brook

A New Hampshire cold case becomes a groundbreaking investigation using genetic genealogy. Gripping season-long storytelling.

Best for: DNA investigation stories

Format: Limited series

9. Dr. Death

The terrifying true story of a neurosurgeon who harmed patients and the system that failed to stop him. Medical malpractice meets true crime.

Best for: Medical true crime

Format: Limited series

10. Morbid

Autopsy technician Alaina Urquhart and hairdresser Ash Kelley combine expertise with dark humor to explore true crime cases.

Best for: Detailed research with personality

Episode length: 90-180 minutes

How to Choose

For Investigative Depth

Start with Serial, In the Dark, or Bear Brook. These shows conduct original investigations that sometimes solve cases.

For Weekly Listening

Criminal and Crime Junkie deliver consistent, well-produced episodes every week.

For Entertainment Value

My Favorite Murder and Morbid balance dark subjects with humor and personality.

For Serious Storytelling

Casefile and Dateline deliver straightforward, respectful coverage without humor.

Why True Crime Podcasts?

These shows resonate because they offer:

  • Real stakes: These stories actually happened
  • Mystery: The human desire to solve puzzles
  • Justice: Holding systems accountable
  • Psychology: Understanding human behavior
  • Safety: Learning from others' experiences
  • Community: Shared interest and discussion

Content Warnings

True crime content can be intense. Consider these factors:

  • Graphic descriptions of violence vary by show
  • Some shows handle victims with more sensitivity than others
  • Comedy-focused shows may not suit everyone's taste
  • Choose shows that match your comfort level

Getting Started

New to true crime podcasts? Try these starter episodes:

  • Serial: Season 1 (the Adnan Syed case)
  • Criminal: "Episode 51: Money Tree" (lighter story)
  • Bear Brook: Episode 1 (gripping from the start)
  • Dr. Death: Season 1 (binge-worthy)
