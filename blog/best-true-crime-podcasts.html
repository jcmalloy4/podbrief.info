Best True Crime Podcasts in 2026

Published February 13, 2026 · 10 min read

True crime podcasts have exploded in popularity, offering gripping stories of mystery, investigation, and justice. Whether you're looking for deep investigative journalism, cold case mysteries, or compelling courtroom drama, here are the best true crime podcasts to add to your queue.

Top True Crime Podcasts

1. Serial

The podcast that started it all. Serial revolutionized true crime storytelling with its season-long deep dives into individual cases. Season 1's investigation into Adnan Syed's murder conviction captivated millions and set the standard for investigative podcasting.

Why listen: Groundbreaking investigative journalism, immersive storytelling, sets the gold standard.

2. My Favorite Murder

Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark blend true crime with comedy in this wildly popular podcast. Each episode features the hosts sharing their favorite murder stories with humor, empathy, and a dedicated community of "Murderinos."

Why listen: Perfect balance of dark stories and light banter, supportive community, relatable hosts.

3. Criminal

Phoebe Judge's soothing voice guides you through fascinating criminal cases that often challenge your assumptions about crime and justice. Criminal explores the gray areas of the justice system with empathy and nuance.

Why listen: Unique perspectives, exceptional storytelling, explores the human side of crime.

4. Case file

Australian host's anonymous narration delivers meticulously researched true crime cases with a no-frills approach. Casefile is known for its thorough investigation and respect for victims.

Why listen: Comprehensive research, respectful approach, global cases, anonymous narrator keeps focus on the story.

5. Dateline NBC

The beloved TV franchise translates perfectly to podcast form. Dateline delivers the same high-quality investigative journalism you expect, with deep dives into mysterious disappearances, murders, and investigations.

Why listen: Trusted journalism, well-produced, extensive archive of cases.

6. Crime Junkie

Ashley Flowers and Brit Prawat deliver tightly edited true crime stories weekly. Crime Junkie is known for its consistent format, clear storytelling, and active advocacy for victims.

Why listen: Consistent schedule, clear storytelling, victim advocacy focus.

7. Bear Brook

This investigative podcast uncovers the mystery of four bodies found in barrels in New Hampshire and connects to a broader investigation spanning decades. Bear Brook won the Peabody Award for its exceptional journalism.

Why listen: Award-winning investigation, deeply researched, connects multiple cases.

8. In the Dark

Investigative reporters examine how the American justice system failed in specific cases. Season 1 focuses on the abduction of Jacob Wetterling, while Season 2 investigates Curtis Flowers, tried six times for the same crime.

Why listen: Investigative excellence, systemic analysis, real-world impact.

9. Dr. Death

The terrifying true story of Dr. Christopher Duntsch, a neurosurgeon who maimed and killed patients. Dr. Death explores how the medical system failed to stop him despite numerous red flags.

Why listen: Unbelievable true story, examines systemic failures, gripping narrative.

10. The Murder Squad

Hosted by retired cold case investigator Paul Holes and investigative journalist Billy Jensen, The Murder Squad aims to solve actual cold cases with help from listeners.

Why listen: Expert hosts, active case solving, community involvement.

What Makes a Great True Crime Podcast?

Respectful storytelling: The best podcasts honor victims and their families while telling compelling stories.

Thorough research: Quality true crime requires extensive investigation, fact-checking, and interviews.

Fresh perspectives: Whether it's underreported cases or new angles on famous crimes, originality matters.

Production quality: Clear audio, good pacing, and thoughtful editing enhance the listening experience.

How to Listen Responsibly

True crime can be addictive, but it's important to remember these are real people's tragedies:

  • Take breaks: True crime content can be emotionally heavy
  • Support victims: Many podcasts highlight ways to help or donate to victim advocacy groups
  • Fact-check: Not all true crime podcasts have the same journalistic standards
  • Respect privacy: Avoid amateur sleuthing that could harm investigations or families
