Best Sports Podcasts (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

Whether you're a die-hard fan, fantasy player, or casual observer, sports podcasts deliver analysis, humor, and insider access. From game breakdowns to athlete interviews, these shows cover every angle of the sports world.

Top Sports Podcasts

1. Pardon My Take

Big Cat and PFT Commenter bring irreverent humor to sports talk. Athlete interviews, recurring segments, and a perfect mix of analysis and comedy.

Best for: Sports humor and personality

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

Browse Pardon My Take summaries →

2. The Bill Simmons Podcast

The godfather of sports podcasting discusses basketball, football, pop culture, and entertainment with guests ranging from NBA stars to Hollywood directors.

Best for: NBA, NFL, pop culture crossover

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

3. The Dan Le Batard Show

Less about scores and more about the culture of sports. Funny, insightful, and unafraid to tackle social issues alongside game analysis.

Best for: Sports culture commentary

Episode length: 120-180 minutes (daily)

4. The Pat McAfee Show

Former NFL punter Pat McAfee brings energy, humor, and insider NFL knowledge. Daily shows featuring breaking news and colorful commentary.

Best for: NFL, high-energy sports talk

Episode length: 180 minutes (daily)

5. Around the Horn / PTI

ESPN's legendary debate shows in podcast form. Quick-hitting sports arguments and passionate disagreements.

Best for: Sports debate and discussion

Episode length: 25-30 minutes

6. The Lowe Post

Zach Lowe delivers the smartest NBA analysis in podcasting. Deep tactical discussions with coaches, players, and basketball minds.

Best for: NBA X's and O's

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

7. Fantasy Footballers

Andy Holloway, Mike Wright, and Jason Moore provide expert fantasy football advice with entertainment value. Essential for fantasy players.

Best for: Fantasy football

Episode length: 60-75 minutes

8. Jomboy Media Podcasts

Jimmy O'Brien's baseball breakdowns went viral, spawning a podcast network covering MLB with humor and analysis.

Best for: Baseball analysis

Episode length: Varies

9. The Ringer NBA Show

Bill Simmons' network delivers multiple NBA podcasts daily with different hosts and angles. Comprehensive basketball coverage.

Best for: Daily NBA updates

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

10. The Rich Eisen Show

Former ESPN anchor Rich Eisen brings charm, knowledge, and A-list guests to daily sports talk.

Best for: All sports, great interviews

Episode length: 180 minutes (daily)

Sports-Specific Recommendations

NBA Podcasts

  • The Lowe Post - Tactical analysis
  • The Bill Simmons Podcast - Culture and storylines
  • The Ringer NBA Show - Daily coverage

NFL Podcasts

  • The Pat McAfee Show - Insider perspective
  • Pardon My Take - Humor and interviews
  • Around the NFL - Expert analysis

MLB Podcasts

  • Jomboy Media - Analysis and breakdowns
  • Effectively Wild - Stats and discussion

Fantasy Sports

  • Fantasy Footballers - NFL fantasy
  • Fantasy Baseball Today - MLB fantasy
  • The Ringer Fantasy Football Show - Strategy

How to Choose

For Humor

Start with Pardon My Take or The Dan Le Batard Show. These shows make you laugh while covering sports.

For Analysis

The Lowe Post and The Ringer network deliver expert tactical breakdowns.

For Daily Updates

The Pat McAfee Show and The Dan Le Batard Show provide daily sports news and reactions.

For Fantasy Help

Fantasy Footballers is essential for competitive fantasy players.

Why Sports Podcasts?

Sports podcasts offer unique advantages:

  • Depth: More analysis than TV highlight shows
  • Personality: Get to know hosts over time
  • Insider access: Athlete interviews and locker room stories
  • On-demand: Listen when convenient, not during live broadcasts
  • Community: Feel connected to other fans
  • Fantasy edge: Better information for fantasy decisions

Getting Started

New to sports podcasts? Try these episodes:

  • Pardon My Take: Any big athlete interview (LeBron, Tom Brady)
  • The Bill Simmons Podcast: NBA trade deadline episodes
  • The Lowe Post: Season preview episodes
  • The Pat McAfee Show: NFL Draft coverage
