Best Design Podcasts 2026: Inspiration for Creatives & Designers

March 4, 2026 9 min read

Design podcasts offer invaluable insights into creativity, problem-solving, and the hidden details that shape our world. Whether you're a UX designer, architect, graphic designer, or simply design-curious, 2026's best design podcasts combine inspiration, education, and practical wisdom from the industry's leading voices.

Why Design Podcasts Matter

Design podcasts help you:

  • Stay current: Learn about design trends, tools, and methodologies
  • Think differently: Understand design thinking and creative problem-solving
  • Build skills: Get practical advice from experienced designers
  • Find inspiration: Hear stories behind great design work
  • See the invisible: Notice design details everywhere

Best Architecture & Urban Design Podcasts

1. 99% Invisible

Why it's great: Roman Mars's legendary podcast about the hidden design that shapes our world. From architecture to infrastructure to everyday objects, 99PI reveals the thought behind what we take for granted.

Best for: Anyone curious about design, architecture, and urban planning

Notable episodes: "The Accidental Room," "Unpleasant Design," "The Smell of Concrete After Rain"

Episode length: 20-40 minutes

View 99% Invisible summaries →

2. The Urbanist

Why it's great: Monocle's exploration of cities, urbanism, and the future of metropolitan life. Features mayors, architects, and city planners discussing what makes cities work.

Best for: Urban planning enthusiasts and city lovers

View The Urbanist summaries →

Best UX & Product Design Podcasts

3. Design Better Podcast

Why it's great: InVision's podcast featuring conversations with design leaders at companies like Airbnb, Google, and Netflix. Practical advice on design process, careers, and leadership.

Best for: UX designers, product designers, and design leaders

Notable topics: Design systems, research methods, design careers

View Design Better summaries →

4. User Defenders

Why it's great: Jason Ogle interviews UX designers and researchers about creating human-centered products. Focuses on empathy, accessibility, and user advocacy.

Best for: UX designers committed to user-centered design

View User Defenders summaries →

5. High Resolution

Why it's great: Bobby Ghoshal and Jared Erondu interview designers about their career journeys, creative processes, and lessons learned. Honest, reflective conversations.

Best for: Designers seeking career inspiration and mentorship

View High Resolution summaries →

Best Graphic Design & Typography Podcasts

6. Scratching the Surface

Why it's great: Jarrett Fuller explores design's role in culture and society through conversations with designers, writers, and educators. Intellectually rigorous and culturally aware.

Best for: Designers interested in theory, criticism, and cultural context

7. The Futur Podcast

Why it's great: Chris Do and team cover graphic design, branding, business, and creativity. Practical advice for freelancers and creative entrepreneurs.

Best for: Freelance designers and creative business owners

Notable topics: Pricing, client relationships, portfolio building

View The Futur summaries →

Design Podcast Learning Paths

For Aspiring Designers

  1. Start with: 99% Invisible (learn to see design everywhere)
  2. Then: Design Matters (understand creative careers)
  3. Deep dive: Design Better or User Defenders (specific skills)

For Experienced Designers

  1. Leadership: Design Better, Hello Monday
  2. Theory: Scratching the Surface
  3. Business: The Futur

For Design-Curious Non-Designers

  1. Start: 99% Invisible (accessible, fascinating)
  2. Explore: Design Matters (creative inspiration)
  3. Apply: Design Sprint Podcast (design thinking)
