Best Tim Ferriss Show Episodes 2026

Published March 10, 2026

The Tim Ferriss Show deconstructs world-class performers to extract tactics, tools, and routines you can use. Tim interviews billionaires, icons, and world-class performers from dozens of areas to discover what makes them successful. With 700+ episodes, here are the best.

Why Listen to Tim Ferriss?

  • World-Class Guests: Billionaires, athletes, authors, scientists, artists
  • Tactical & Actionable: Specific tools, books, habits, and routines
  • Deep Preparation: Tim researches guests thoroughly, asks better questions
  • Diverse Disciplines: Business, health, creativity, philosophy, sports

Most Popular Tim Ferriss Episodes

Derek Sivers - The Quiet Revolutionary

Derek Sivers (CD Baby founder) shares unconventional wisdom on business, life, and decision-making. His philosophy of "hell yes or no" and focus on simplicity has influenced millions. Multiple episodes with Derek are fan favorites.

Kevin Kelly - The Technium

Wired co-founder Kevin Kelly discusses long-term thinking, technology's trajectory, and "1,000 True Fans." His 68 bits of advice and 103 bits of advice episodes went viral for their timeless wisdom.

Naval Ravikant - Wealth and Happiness

Multiple episodes with Naval exploring wealth creation, happiness, reading, meditation, and philosophy. Naval's insights on leverage, specific knowledge, and compound interest are legendary.

Jamie Foxx - Creating Like Crazy

Actor/comedian Jamie Foxx shares his creative process, work ethic, and stories from his career. One of the most entertaining and inspiring episodes covering art, hustle, and staying hungry.

Arnold Schwarzenegger - Psychological Warfare

The Governator discusses bodybuilding, Hollywood, politics, and his relentless drive. Tactical advice on goal-setting, visualization, and never quitting from someone who conquered three careers.

Maria Popova - Writing, Wisdom, and Wonder

Brain Pickings creator Maria Popova discusses her reading process, writing discipline, and how to find meaning through literature and art. Perfect for writers and lifelong learners.

Rick Rubin - The Creative Act

Legendary music producer Rick Rubin shares his philosophy on creativity, taste, and the artistic process. Discussions of reducing, eliminating ego, and creating from a place of truth.

Susan Cain - The Power of Introverts

Author of "Quiet" discusses introversion, creativity, and how to thrive as an introvert in an extroverted world. Tactical advice for introverts in business and life.

Tony Robbins - Giant Steps

Life coach Tony Robbins breaks down his morning routine, physiology hacks, and strategies for peak performance. High-energy discussion of state management and personal change.

Brené Brown - Vulnerability and Courage

Researcher Brené Brown discusses shame, vulnerability, courage, and leadership. Essential listening for anyone leading teams or struggling with perfectionism.

Productivity & Performance

Discussions with doctors, researchers, and athletes on nutrition, exercise, sleep, and longevity. Kelly Starrett, Dr. Peter Attia, Dr. Rhonda Patrick.

Investing & Wealth

Billionaire investors and entrepreneurs share strategies for building wealth. Ray Dalio, Marc Andreessen, Chris Sacca, Tony Robbins on money.

Creativity & Writing

Authors, artists, and creators discuss their craft and creative process. Seth Godin, Neil Gaiman, Malcolm Gladwell, Steven Pressfield.

Philosophy & Wisdom

Psychedelics & Consciousness

Common Questions Tim Asks

Tim's signature questions that extract tactical insights:

  • "What does your morning routine look like?"
  • "What book have you gifted the most?"
  • "What is the book (or books) you've given most as a gift?"
  • "What purchase of $100 or less has most positively impacted your life?"
  • "What advice would you give to your 30-year-old self?"
  • "When you feel overwhelmed or unfocused, what do you do?"
