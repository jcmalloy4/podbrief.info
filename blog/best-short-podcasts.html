Best Short Podcasts Under 30 Minutes (2026)

Quick, valuable podcasts you can finish during your commute or lunch break

Updated February 25, 2026

Don't have time for 2-hour podcast episodes? You're not alone. The best short podcasts deliver maximum value in minimal time—perfect for busy schedules, quick commutes, or lunch breaks.

Why Short Podcasts Are Perfect for Busy People

  • Easy to fit into your schedule – Finish an episode during your commute, workout, or lunch break
  • High signal-to-noise ratio – Short formats force hosts to cut the fluff and deliver value
  • Less commitment – Start and finish in one sitting without interruption
  • More variety – Sample multiple shows instead of committing to long-form content

Best Daily News Podcasts (5-15 Minutes)

The Daily by The New York Times (20-30 min)

One of the most popular news podcasts, The Daily covers a single major story each weekday. While sometimes pushing 30 minutes, most episodes deliver deep context in under 25 minutes.

Up First by NPR (10-15 min)

The fastest way to start your day informed. Three major stories in under 15 minutes, published by 6 AM ET every weekday.

WSJ What's News (5-10 min)

Twice-daily business and world news from The Wall Street Journal. Morning and evening editions, each under 10 minutes.

Best Short Business Podcasts

HBR IdeaCast (15-25 min)

Harvard Business Review's flagship podcast. Weekly interviews with business leaders and researchers on management, leadership, and strategy.

Planet Money by NPR (15-30 min)

Economics explained through fascinating stories. Most episodes run 20-25 minutes and make complex topics accessible.

Best Short Technology Podcasts

Techmeme Ride Home (15-20 min)

Daily tech news roundup published every afternoon. The most important tech stories of the day, no fluff.

Hard Fork (30-40 min)

While occasionally longer, many episodes hit the 30-minute mark. Kevin Roose and Casey Newton discuss the week's biggest tech stories with humor and insight.

Best Short Self-Improvement Podcasts

The Daily Stoic (10-15 min)

Daily stoic philosophy lessons for modern life. Short, actionable wisdom from ancient philosophers.

Hidden Brain (20-30 min)

NPR's psychology and behavioral economics show. Some episodes run longer, but many hit the sweet spot around 25 minutes.

Best Short True Crime Podcasts

Criminal (20-30 min)

Fascinating true crime stories told with empathy and depth. Most episodes run 25-30 minutes.

How to Find Short Podcasts

When browsing for new shows:

  • Check episode length – Most podcast apps show average episode duration
  • Look for daily news formats – Daily shows tend to be shorter (5-20 min)
  • Filter by "Daily" or "Brief" – These keywords signal short-form content
  • Sample recent episodes – Episode length can vary season to season
