How to Use Short Podcasts

Morning Routine (15-30 min total)

  • Up First (12 min) - news briefing
  • Morning Brew Daily (15 min) - business news

Commute (25-30 min)

  • The Daily (25 min) - deep dive
  • Or: TED Talks Daily (15 min) + Up First (12 min)

Lunch Break (20-30 min)

  • Science Vs (25 min) - weekly treat
  • Or: Two TED Talks (20 min total)

Before Bed (10-15 min)

  • TED Talks Daily (lighter topics)
  • Switched on Pop (music analysis)

Benefits of Short Podcasts

Easy to Finish

No guilt about not finishing episodes. Complete satisfaction in under 30 minutes.

Fit Any Schedule

Commute, workout, chores, lunch break - there's always time for a short podcast.

Less Overwhelm

Easier to try new podcasts when episodes are only 15 minutes.

Higher Quality

Shorter = more editing. Less rambling, tighter content.

Building a Short Podcast Routine

Daily Habits (60 min/day total)

  • Morning: Up First (12 min)
  • Commute: The Daily (25 min)
  • Lunch: TED Talk (15 min)

Weekly Variety (3-4 hours/week)

  • 5x Up First (60 min total)
  • 5x Morning Brew (75 min total)
  • 5x The Daily (125 min total)
  • 1x Science Vs (25 min)

Short vs Long Podcasts

Choose Short When:

  • You're new to podcasts
  • You have a busy schedule
  • You want daily news/updates
  • You're doing quick tasks (commute, chores)

Choose Long When:

  • You want deep dives
  • You're on a long drive/flight
  • You're learning complex topics
  • You have dedicated listening time

Common Mistakes

Subscribing to Too Many

Even short podcasts add up. Start with 3-5 dailies max.

Only Listening to News

Mix in learning (TED) or culture (Switched on Pop) for variety.

Feeling Rushed

Short doesn't mean background noise. Give them your attention.

Speed Listening Tips

1.5x for News Podcasts

Up First and Morning Brew are easily consumed at 1.5x speed. Turns 15 min into 10 min.

1.2x for Story Podcasts

The Daily and This American Life work at 1.2x without losing quality.

1x for Learning

TED Talks and Science Vs deserve full attention at normal speed.
