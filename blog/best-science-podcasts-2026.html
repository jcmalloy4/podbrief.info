Best Science Podcasts 2026 - Physics, Biology, Space & More

Published March 6, 2026 | 8 min read

Science podcasts make cutting-edge research accessible. From quantum physics to evolutionary biology, these shows bring you expert insights, breakthrough discoveries, and the wonder of scientific exploration.

Why Listen to Science Podcasts?

Science podcasts offer:

  • Expert knowledge: Learn from PhDs, researchers, and Nobel laureates
  • Breakthrough discoveries: Stay current with latest scientific findings
  • Accessible explanations: Complex topics made understandable
  • Diverse fields: Physics, biology, chemistry, astronomy, and more
  • Critical thinking: Learn the scientific method and evidence-based reasoning

Top Science Podcasts of 2026

1. StarTalk (Neil deGrasse Tyson)

Why it's great: Astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson brings science to everyone with humor and celebrity guests. Makes complex topics entertaining.

Best for: General science enthusiasts and space lovers

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

Topics: Astrophysics, space exploration, science in pop culture

Guests: Scientists, comedians, actors, musicians

2. Lex Fridman Podcast (Science Episodes)

Why it's great: Deep technical conversations with leading scientists, researchers, and AI experts. Long-form discussions that go beyond surface level.

Best for: Those who want technical depth without dumbing down

Episode length: 2-4 hours

Notable science guests: Roger Penrose, Eric Weinstein, Brian Greene, Michio Kaku

3. Science Vs (Wendy Zukerman)

Why it's great: Evidence-based approach to trending topics and controversial claims. Rigorous fact-checking with engaging storytelling.

Best for: Skeptics who want science-backed answers

Episode length: 30-45 minutes

Topics: Debunking myths, examining fads, investigating health claims

4. Radiolab (WNYC Studios)

Why it's great: Award-winning production that explores scientific and philosophical questions through sound-rich storytelling. Creative, thought-provoking, beautifully produced.

Best for: Those who appreciate narrative science storytelling

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

Style: Investigative, experimental audio production

5. The Infinite Monkey Cage (BBC Radio 4)

Why it's great: British physicist Brian Cox and comedian Robin Ince discuss science with humor and expert guests. Smart and funny.

Best for: Science with British wit

Episode length: 30-45 minutes

Format: Panel show with scientists and comedians

6. This Week in Virology (TWiV)

Why it's great: Deep dive into virology and infectious diseases from working virologists. Became essential listening during COVID-19 pandemic.

Best for: Biology enthusiasts and those interested in infectious diseases

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

Hosts: Dr. Vincent Racaniello and virology experts

7. The Science Show (ABC Radio National)

Why it's great: Australia's longest-running science show covers the week's most interesting research across all scientific disciplines.

Best for: Broad science coverage and international perspective

Episode length: 30 minutes

Coverage: Weekly science news and discoveries

8. Sean Carroll's Mindscape

Why it's great: Theoretical physicist explores big ideas at the intersection of science, philosophy, and society. Intellectual and accessible.

Best for: Philosophy of science and theoretical physics

Episode length: 90-120 minutes

Topics: Quantum mechanics, cosmology, philosophy, complexity

9. Ologies (Alie Ward)

Why it's great: Science communicator interviews experts ("-ologists") in obscure and fascinating fields. Fun, accessible, and surprisingly educational.

Best for: Discovering new scientific fields you didn't know existed

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

Topics: Everything from volcanology to astrobiology to jellyfish research

10. The Skeptics' Guide to the Universe

Why it's great: Critical thinking, scientific skepticism, and debunking pseudoscience. News, interviews, and science education.

Best for: Learning critical thinking and spotting bad science

Episode length: 80 minutes

Format: Panel discussion of week's science news

Tips for Science Podcast Listening

1. Start Broad, Then Specialize

Begin with general science shows like RadioLab or StarTalk, then dive into specific fields that capture your interest.

2. Don't Fear Technical Content

Shows like Lex Fridman or Sean Carroll can be technical, but you'll understand more than you expect. Context builds over time.

3. Listen Actively

Pause to look up concepts or visualize what's being described. Science podcasts reward engaged listening.

4. Supplement with Reading

Many science podcasts provide show notes, papers, and resources. Use them to deepen understanding.

5. Embrace Confusion

Not understanding everything is normal. Scientists often discuss cutting-edge research that's still being figured out.

What Makes Great Science Podcasts?

Expert Hosts

The best science podcasts are hosted by working scientists or experienced science communicators who understand the material deeply.

Clear Explanations

Great hosts use analogies, examples, and step-by-step reasoning to make complex topics accessible without oversimplifying.

Source Citations

Quality science podcasts cite studies, provide references, and distinguish between established science and speculation.

Current Research

The best shows cover recent discoveries and ongoing research, keeping you at the cutting edge of scientific knowledge.
