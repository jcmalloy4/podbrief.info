Best Science Podcasts 2026

Published March 10, 2026

Science podcasts make complex research and discoveries accessible to everyone. Whether you're curious about quantum physics, fascinated by space exploration, or want to understand the latest breakthroughs in medicine, these podcasts deliver expert knowledge in engaging formats.

Why Listen to Science Podcasts?

  • Stay Current: Learn about the latest discoveries and breakthrough research
  • Expert Insights: Hear directly from scientists, researchers, and Nobel laureates
  • Make Sense of Complexity: Complex topics explained in accessible language
  • Fuel Curiosity: Explore everything from black holes to consciousness

Top Science Podcasts

Lex Fridman Podcast

Deep conversations with leading scientists, researchers, and thinkers. Lex explores AI, physics, neuroscience, and the nature of intelligence with guests like Elon Musk, Richard Dawkins, and Brian Greene. Known for long-form, thoughtful discussions.

Huberman Lab

Dr. Andrew Huberman, a Stanford neuroscientist, breaks down how the brain and body work. Episodes cover sleep optimization, focus enhancement, stress management, and practical protocols backed by peer-reviewed research. Science you can immediately apply to your life.

Radiolab

WNYC's award-winning podcast blends science, philosophy, and storytelling. Each episode investigates questions like "What is consciousness?" or "How do we make decisions?" with immersive sound design and narrative creativity.

StarTalk

Astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson makes space science entertaining. With celebrity guests and comedians alongside scientists, StarTalk covers everything from cosmic phenomena to the science behind movies and pop culture.

Science Vs

Wendy Zukerman takes on fads, trends, and controversies by examining the actual scientific evidence. Does intermittent fasting work? Are NFTs bad for the environment? Science Vs separates fact from hype with humor and thorough research.

The Infinite Monkey Cage

Physicist Brian Cox and comedian Robin Ince host this BBC Radio 4 show featuring scientists and celebrities discussing topics from quantum mechanics to the origins of life. Intelligent and hilarious in equal measure.

Ologies

Alie Ward interviews experts in obscure scientific fields (-ologies) you didn't know existed. From spiders to sinkholes, volcanoes to slime molds, each episode is a deep dive with a passionate expert who loves their weird specialty.

Sean Carroll's Mindscape

Theoretical physicist Sean Carroll explores the boundaries of knowledge with scientists, philosophers, and artists. Topics range from quantum mechanics and cosmology to consciousness and the nature of reality.

Science Friday

NPR's flagship science show covers the week's top science news with interviews and expert analysis. From climate change to medical breakthroughs, Science Friday makes current research accessible and engaging.

Science Podcast Categories

Physics & Cosmology

Daniel and Jorge Explain the Universe breaks down particle physics and cosmology with humor. The Joy of Why from Quanta Magazine explores fundamental questions about the universe.

Biology & Neuroscience

This Podcast Will Kill You examines diseases throughout history with two disease ecologists. Brain Science explores neuroscience, cognition, and consciousness.

Space & Astronomy

Planetary Radio from The Planetary Society covers space exploration missions. AstronomyCast explains astronomy concepts from basics to advanced topics.

Medical & Health Science

The Peter Attia Drive focuses on longevity and optimizing healthspan. ZDoggMD provides medical commentary with humor and music parodies.

Environmental Science

How to Save a Planet explores climate solutions that actually work. Outside/In from NH Public Radio investigates environmental stories.

What Makes a Great Science Podcast?

  • Accuracy: Based on peer-reviewed research and expert sources
  • Accessibility: Complex concepts explained without dumbing down
  • Engaging Format: Storytelling, interviews, or thought-provoking discussions
  • Current Research: Coverage of recent discoveries and breakthroughs
  • Practical Application: How science applies to your life

Science Communication Styles

  • Expert Interviews: Direct conversations with researchers (Lex Fridman, StarTalk)
  • Solo Explanations: One host breaking down topics (Huberman Lab)
  • Investigative Journalism: Researching and debunking claims (Science Vs)
  • Narrative Storytelling: Science through stories (Radiolab, 99% Invisible)
  • Panel Discussions: Multiple experts debating topics (The Infinite Monkey Cage)

How to Choose the Right Science Podcast

  • Interest Area: Physics, biology, space, medicine, or general science?
  • Depth Level: Beginner-friendly or advanced technical discussions?
  • Format Preference: Interviews, solo episodes, or investigative stories?
  • Episode Length: Quick 20-minute updates or 3-hour deep dives?
  • Tone: Serious academic or entertainment-focused?

The Value of Science Literacy

Science podcasts help you:

  • Make informed decisions about health and technology
  • Understand global challenges like climate change
  • Appreciate the scientific method and critical thinking
  • Stay current with breakthrough discoveries
  • Spark curiosity and lifelong learning
