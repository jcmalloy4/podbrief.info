Best Science Podcasts: 17 Shows to Expand Your Mind (2026)

From black holes to brain chemistry, these science podcasts make complex topics accessible and fascinating. Whether you're a curious beginner or science enthusiast, these shows deliver cutting-edge research and big ideas without the jargon.

Best Science Podcasts Overview

1. Radiolab

Best for: Sound-rich science storytelling

WNYC's legendary show blends science, philosophy, and sound design into immersive stories. Topics range from particle physics to ethics to biology. Unique production makes it feel like audio art.

2. Huberman Lab

Best for: Neuroscience and health optimization

Stanford neuroscientist Andrew Huberman breaks down brain science, sleep, focus, exercise, and health protocols. Deep dives (2+ hours) with actionable takeaways.

3. Science Vs

Best for: Myth-busting and evidence-based science

Takes popular claims (fasting, meditation, detoxes, aliens) and examines what the science actually says. Fun, skeptical, and rigorously researched.

4. Lex Fridman Podcast

Best for: AI, physics, and conversations with leading scientists

MIT researcher Lex Fridman interviews top scientists, engineers, and thinkers. Deep, technical conversations made accessible. Essential for AI and physics enthusiasts.

5. Sean Carroll's Mindscape

Best for: Physics, philosophy, and big ideas

Caltech physicist Sean Carroll explores quantum mechanics, cosmology, consciousness, and philosophy with world-class scientists and thinkers.

6. The Infinite Monkey Cage

Best for: Funny, accessible science from BBC

Physicist Brian Cox and comedian Robin Ince discuss science with humor and wit. British charm meets astrophysics, biology, and everything in between.

7. StarTalk

Best for: Astrophysics with humor

Neil deGrasse Tyson explains space, physics, and science with comedians and celebrity guests. Makes astrophysics entertaining and accessible.

8. Ologies

Best for: Every -ology you can imagine

Alie Ward interviews experts in obscure fields—volcanology, etymology, mycology, criminology. Each episode is a deep dive into a different science.

9. Science Friday (NPR)

Best for: Weekly science news

Ira Flatow covers the week's top science stories—from climate to space exploration to new discoveries. Accessible, timely, and well-produced.

10. The Knowledge Project (Shane Parrish)

Best for: Cognitive science and decision-making

Interviews with scientists, thinkers, and experts on mental models, learning, and how to think better. Great intersection of science and practical wisdom.

11. Stuff to Blow Your Mind

Best for: Weird science and fascinating tangents

HowStuffWorks' deep dive into strange science—from cryptozoology to neuroscience to space mysteries. Perfect for curious minds who love rabbit holes.

12. This Week in Virology (TWiV)

Best for: Virology and infectious disease

Panel of virologists discuss the latest research in virology, vaccines, and infectious diseases. Technical but accessible. Became essential during COVID-19.

13. The Nature Podcast

Best for: Cutting-edge research from Nature journal

Weekly episodes covering the latest papers published in Nature—one of the world's top scientific journals. For people who want the bleeding edge.

14. Planetary Radio

Best for: Space exploration and astronomy

The Planetary Society's show covering NASA missions, space policy, and planetary science. Interviews with mission scientists and astronauts.

15. Hidden Brain (NPR)

Best for: Psychology and behavioral science

Shankar Vedantam explores the unconscious patterns that drive human behavior. Psychology, neuroscience, and social science made fascinating.

16. The Science of Everything

Best for: Beginner-friendly science across all fields

James Fodor (PhD candidate) explains scientific concepts in clear, jargon-free language. Great for building foundational knowledge.

17. In Our Time: Science (BBC)

Best for: History and philosophy of science

Melvyn Bragg and expert guests discuss major scientific ideas and discoveries throughout history. Intellectually dense but rewarding.

How to Choose the Right Science Podcast

For beginners:

  • Radiolab (accessible storytelling)
  • Science Vs (myth-busting fun)
  • The Infinite Monkey Cage (humor + science)
  • Science Friday (current events)

For neuroscience and health:

  • Huberman Lab (protocols and optimization)
  • Hidden Brain (psychology and behavior)
  • The Knowledge Project (cognitive science)

For physics and cosmology:

  • Sean Carroll's Mindscape (deep physics)
  • StarTalk (accessible astrophysics)
  • Lex Fridman (AI and physics conversations)

For biology and life sciences:

  • Ologies (every field imaginable)
  • This Week in Virology (infectious disease)
  • The Nature Podcast (latest research)

For space and astronomy:

  • Planetary Radio (missions and exploration)
  • StarTalk (astrophysics entertainment)
  • Science Friday (space news coverage)

For AI and technology:

  • Lex Fridman (AI researchers and engineers)
  • Sean Carroll's Mindscape (philosophy of AI)

Final Thoughts

The best science podcast is the one that keeps you curious and engaged. Start with something entertaining like Radiolab or The Infinite Monkey Cage, then branch into more specialized shows as you discover what fascinates you.

Science podcasts work great for:

  • Commutes: Turn dead time into learning time
  • Workouts: Many are engaging enough to distract from exercise
  • Cooking or chores: Background learning while doing tasks
  • Bedtime: Some (like Huberman Lab) are perfect for winding down

Don't be intimidated by technical topics—the best science podcasters know how to make complex ideas accessible. Start listening, stay curious.
