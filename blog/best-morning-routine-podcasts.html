Best Podcasts for Your Morning Routine (2026)

Start your day informed, motivated, and energized with these morning podcasts

Updated February 25, 2026

Your morning sets the tone for the entire day. The right podcast can transform your morning routine from groggy autopilot into an intentional, energizing start. Whether you need news, motivation, or learning, these podcasts are perfect for your AM ritual.

Best Morning News Podcasts

Up First by NPR (10-15 min)

The ultimate morning news podcast. Three major stories in under 15 minutes, published by 6 AM ET every weekday. Quick, informative, and perfect while making coffee or getting ready.

Best for: People who want to start the day informed without a time commitment.

The Daily by The New York Times (20-30 min)

Deep dive into a single major story each weekday morning. While longer than Up First, The Daily provides context and depth that makes you truly understand what's happening.

WSJ What's News (5-10 min)

Wall Street Journal's morning briefing. Business and world news in under 10 minutes. Perfect for the shower or morning commute.

Best Motivational Morning Podcasts

The Daily Stoic (10-15 min)

Daily philosophy lessons from ancient Stoics applied to modern life. Short, actionable wisdom to set your mindset for the day. Perfect breakfast listening.

Why it works: Starts your day with intentionality and perspective, not just information.

The Tim Ferriss Show (varies)

While episodes are long (1-3 hours), listening during your morning workout or commute exposes you to world-class performers' routines and mindsets. Naturally motivating.

Best Morning Learning Podcasts

Huberman Lab (varies)

Neuroscience professor Andrew Huberman's episodes on sleep, focus, and morning routines are essential listening. Learn how to optimize your biology while you optimize your morning.

Try these episodes first: Morning sunlight exposure, caffeine timing, cold exposure benefits.

Hidden Brain (20-30 min)

NPR's psychology and behavioral science show. Start your day understanding human behavior better. Episodes are fascinating without being too heavy for morning listening.

Planet Money (15-30 min)

Economics explained through storytelling. Learn about markets, trade, and money over your morning coffee. Makes complex topics accessible and entertaining.

How to Build a Morning Podcast Routine

The 5-Minute Morning (Minimal Time)

  • WSJ What's News – 5-10 min of business news while making coffee

The 15-Minute Morning (Balanced)

  • Up First (10-15 min) – News while getting ready
  • The Daily Stoic (10 min) – Philosophy over breakfast

The 60-Minute Morning (Extended Routine)

  • The Daily (20-30 min) – Deep news dive during breakfast
  • Huberman Lab clip (15 min) – Learning during morning workout
  • SmartLess (20 min) – Entertainment during commute

Morning Podcast Tips

  • Download overnight – Set podcasts to auto-download so they're ready when you wake up
  • Create a morning playlist – Queue up your preferred morning shows
  • Pair with activities – Coffee = news, workout = motivation, commute = learning
  • Adjust playback speed – 1.2x-1.5x to fit more in limited time
  • Keep it consistent – Same shows at same times build a sustainable routine
