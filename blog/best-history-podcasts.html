Best History Podcasts (2026)

Published February 22, 2026

History comes alive through podcasting. Whether you're fascinated by ancient Rome, World War II, or American revolutions, there's a history podcast that will transport you back in time with expert storytelling and deep research.

Top History Podcasts

1. Hardcore History

Dan Carlin's legendary podcast delivers marathon episodes (often 3-6 hours) that dive deep into historical events. His dramatic storytelling and meticulous research make complex history accessible and gripping.

Best for: Deep dives into major historical events

Episode length: 3-6 hours

Browse Hardcore History summaries →

2. The History of Rome

Mike Duncan's comprehensive narrative traces Roman history from the founding of the city through the fall of the Western Empire. A masterclass in long-form historical storytelling.

Best for: Ancient history enthusiasts

Episode length: 20-30 minutes

3. Revolutions

Also by Mike Duncan, this podcast examines major political revolutions throughout history, from the English Civil War to the Russian Revolution.

Best for: Understanding political upheaval

Episode length: 30-45 minutes

4. The History Chicks

Beckett Graham and Susan Vollenweider tell the stories of women throughout history with warmth, humor, and thorough research.

Best for: Women's history

Episode length: 60-90 minutes

5. In Our Time: History

Melvyn Bragg and expert guests discuss major historical topics with academic rigor and British charm. Part of the BBC's In Our Time series.

Best for: Academic discussions

Episode length: 45 minutes

6. Fall of Civilizations

Paul Cooper explores the collapse of great civilizations with cinematic production and compelling narration.

Best for: Rise and fall of empires

Episode length: 2-3 hours

7. The Rest is History

Historians Tom Holland and Dominic Sandbrook discuss everything from ancient battles to pop culture with wit and expertise.

Best for: Entertaining historical conversations

Episode length: 45-60 minutes

8. American History Tellers

Lindsay Graham narrates dramatic moments in American history with immersive storytelling and careful research.

Best for: American history

Episode length: 40-50 minutes

How to Choose

For Deep Dives

Start with Hardcore History or Fall of Civilizations. These marathon episodes provide comprehensive coverage of single topics.

For Daily Listening

The History of Rome or In Our Time offer digestible episodes perfect for commutes.

For Conversation-Style

The Rest is History and The History Chicks feel like listening to smart friends discuss fascinating topics.

Why History Podcasts?

History podcasts offer several advantages over books or documentaries:

  • Storytelling: Great hosts bring historical figures and events to life
  • Accessibility: Learn while commuting, exercising, or doing chores
  • Depth: Long-form episodes allow for nuance and complexity
  • Variety: Cover any historical period or topic imaginable
  • Expert knowledge: Access to historians and researchers

Getting Started

New to history podcasts? Try these starter episodes:

  • Hardcore History: "Wrath of the Khans" (Mongol Empire)
  • Fall of Civilizations: "The Sumerians"
  • The Rest is History: "The Fall of Rome"
  • American History Tellers: "The Gold Rush"
