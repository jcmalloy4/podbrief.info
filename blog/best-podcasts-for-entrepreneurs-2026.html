Best Podcasts for Entrepreneurs 2026 - Startup & Business Advice

Published March 5, 2026 • 11 min read

Building a business is lonely, challenging, and full of questions no one prepares you for. The best entrepreneur podcasts connect you with founders who've faced the same struggles, investors who understand what works, and operators who've built successful companies. Whether you're starting your first business or scaling your third, these podcasts deliver actionable insights, strategic frameworks, and the motivation to keep going.

Best Startup & Tech Entrepreneur Podcasts

My First Million

Hosts: Shaan Puri, Sam Parr
Frequency: 3x per week
Perfect for: Business ideas, market analysis, internet businesses

Two successful entrepreneurs (Sam built and sold The Hustle, Shaan led product at Twitch) brainstorm business opportunities, break down companies, and interview founders. Unlike inspirational business podcasts, MFM gets specific: they share numbers, frameworks, and tactical moves. Recent episodes broke down companies like Liquid Death, Duolingo, and OpenAI with revenue estimates and growth strategies.

Why it works: Feels like eavesdropping on smart friends debating ideas. They share real numbers, admit mistakes, and think through opportunities in real-time.

Key segments: Business idea breakdowns, "Billion Dollar Business Ideas," personal portfolio reveals

How I Built This

Host: Guy Raz
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Founder origin stories, perseverance inspiration

Guy Raz interviews founders about their company journeys from idea to success. Airbnb, Spanx, Patagonia, Warby Parker—each episode tracks the full arc including the near-death moments, lucky breaks, and pivots. The narrative format reveals patterns: most successful founders faced rejection, ran out of money multiple times, and considered quitting.

Notable recent episodes: Sara Blakely (Spanx), Brian Chesky (Airbnb), Stewart Butterfield (Slack)

All-In Podcast

Hosts: Jason Calacanis, Chamath Palihapitiya, David Sacks, David Friedberg
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Tech trends, market analysis, VC perspectives

Four successful investors and entrepreneurs ("the besties") debate tech, markets, politics, and business. Their insider perspectives on AI, crypto, regulation, and startup valuations provide context you won't find in traditional news. Episodes often feature heated debates about Fed policy, immigration, AI regulation, and which companies are overvalued.

Value for entrepreneurs: Understanding how investors think about markets, valuations, and trends helps founders position companies and time fundraising.

Best Marketing & Growth Podcasts

Marketing Against the Grain

Hosts: Kipp Bodnar (HubSpot CMO), Kieran Flanagan (HubSpot SVP Marketing)
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Modern marketing tactics, growth strategies, B2B marketing

Two HubSpot executives break down marketing campaigns, growth experiments, and industry trends with a mix of data and contrarian takes. They'll explain why a TikTok strategy worked, how to build communities, or why traditional SEO tactics are dying—backed by numbers from running marketing at scale.

Lenny's Podcast

Host: Lenny Rachitsky
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Product management, growth, user research

Former Airbnb product manager Lenny interviews PMs, founders, and growth leaders about building and scaling products. Episodes cover retention tactics, pricing strategy, onboarding flows, and growth experiments—always with specific examples and metrics.

Standout episodes: Interviews with PMs from Notion, Figma, Stripe, and Ramp

Nudge

Hosts: Phil Agnew, Rory Sutherland
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Behavioral psychology, creative marketing, outside-the-box thinking

Behavioral science applied to marketing and business. Rory Sutherland (Ogilvy Vice Chairman) shares counterintuitive insights about consumer behavior, branding, and persuasion that challenge conventional marketing wisdom.

Best Early-Stage & Bootstrapping Podcasts

Indie Hackers

Host: Courtland Allen
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Bootstrapped businesses, solopreneurs, indie makers

Indie Hackers interviews founders building profitable businesses without venture capital. Guests share revenue numbers, growth tactics, and honest discussions about the challenges of building alone. If you're bootstrapping or considering it, this community and podcast is essential.

Key topics: Finding your first customers, building in public, productized services, SaaS growth

The Bootstrapped Founder

Host: Arvid Kahl
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Self-funded startups, audience building, selling a business

Arvid built and sold a SaaS business for life-changing money without raising capital. His podcast covers finding opportunities, building audiences, creating products people want, and eventually exiting—all from a bootstrapper's perspective.

Startups For the Rest of Us

Hosts: Rob Walling, Mike Taber
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Solo founders, B2B SaaS, sustainable growth

One of the longest-running startup podcasts focuses on building sustainable, profitable businesses. Topics include pricing, customer acquisition, hiring, and scaling without burning out—practical advice for founders building real businesses, not moonshots.

Best Strategy & Operations Podcasts

Acquired

Hosts: Ben Gilbert, David Rosenthal
Frequency: Monthly (2-4 hour deep dives)
Perfect for: Business history, competitive strategy, understanding moats

Acquired isn't technically a "podcast for entrepreneurs," but founders consistently cite it as influential. Ben and David research companies exhaustively (NVIDIA, Costco, Berkshire Hathaway) to reveal strategic lessons about competition, pricing, culture, and building lasting businesses.

Why entrepreneurs love it: Every episode is a business school case study with lessons applicable to any business.

Invest Like the Best

Host: Patrick O'Shaughnessy
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Strategic thinking, understanding businesses, investor mindset

O'Shaughnessy interviews investors, founders, and thinkers about how they evaluate businesses and opportunities. Episodes develop frameworks for thinking about competitive advantages, market timing, and long-term value creation.

Best Fundraising & VC Perspectives

The Twenty Minute VC

Host: Harry Stebbings
Frequency: 3-4x per week
Perfect for: Understanding VCs, fundraising strategy, startup landscape

Stebbings interviews VCs, founders, and operators about what actually matters in fundraising, building startups, and scaling companies. Episodes cover pitch decks, valuation, board management, and how investors really make decisions.

Pro tip: If you're fundraising, listen to episodes featuring VCs in your target sector to understand their thesis and priorities.

This Week in Startups

Host: Jason Calacanis
Frequency: 2-3x per week
Perfect for: Startup news, founder interviews, angel investing

Angel investor Calacanis (Uber, Robinhood, Calm) covers startup news and interviews founders. His experience funding hundreds of companies provides unique insight into what investors look for and how great founders operate.

Best Personal Development for Entrepreneurs

The Tim Ferriss Show

Host: Tim Ferriss
Frequency: 2x per week
Perfect for: Routines, tactics, mental models, optimization

Ferriss "deconstructs world-class performers" to extract their habits, routines, and decision-making frameworks. While not exclusively about business, the tactics from billionaires, athletes, and creators apply directly to entrepreneurship. His recurring questions create comparable insights across episodes.

Entrepreneur favorites: Episodes with Reid Hoffman, Tony Robbins, Naval Ravikant, and Seth Godin

The Knowledge Project

Host: Shane Parrish
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: Decision-making, mental models, wisdom

Farnam Street founder Shane Parrish explores how successful people think. Conversations focus on first principles, avoiding mistakes, and building better mental models—essential skills for founders making high-stakes decisions with incomplete information.

Best Industry-Specific Business Podcasts

Ecommerce Influence

Host: Austin Brawner
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: D2C brands, ecommerce marketing

Focused specifically on ecommerce and DTC brands, covering customer acquisition, email marketing, subscription models, and scaling ecommerce profitably.

The SaaS Podcast

Host: Omer Khan
Frequency: Weekly
Perfect for: SaaS founders, B2B software

Interviews with SaaS founders about building and scaling software businesses. Topics include pricing, churn, sales processes, and product-led growth strategies.

How to Get the Most from Business Podcasts

Take Notes

Don't just passively listen. When you hear a tactic or framework that resonates, pause and write it down. Many entrepreneurs use apps like Notion or Readwise to capture podcast insights.

Implement Immediately

The best learning comes from applying ideas. After hearing a growth tactic or marketing strategy, try it within 48 hours while it's fresh. Execution beats consumption.

Follow the Guests

When a guest shares valuable insights, follow them on Twitter, subscribe to their newsletter, or check out their other podcast appearances. The best guests appear across multiple shows.

Match Stage to Content

Pre-launch: How I Built This, Indie Hackers, Bootstrapped Founder
Early traction: My First Million, Lenny's Podcast, Growth marketing shows
Scaling: All-In, Acquired, Twenty Minute VC
Anytime: Tim Ferriss, Knowledge Project (mental models apply at all stages)

Speed Up Playback

Most entrepreneurs listen at 1.5-2x speed to consume more content. Podcast apps make this easy, and you adjust quickly. Valuable if you're treating podcasts as education rather than entertainment.

Create a Listening Routine

Morning commute: Motivational/mindset (Tim Ferriss, How I Built This)
Workout: High-energy tactical content (My First Million, All-In)
Evening walks: Strategic deep dives (Acquired, Invest Like the Best)
Focused work sessions: Industry-specific shows relevant to current challenges
