All-In vs My First Million: Which Business Podcast is Better?

Published February 28, 2026 • 10 min read

Two of the biggest business podcasts, two very different vibes. All-In brings billionaire insider perspectives. My First Million delivers scrappy entrepreneurial ideas. Here's how they compare and which one you should listen to.

Quick Comparison

Feature All-In Podcast My First Million
Hosts 4 tech billionaires (Chamath, Jason, Sacks, Friedberg) Shaan Puri & Sam Parr
Format 90-120 min roundtable 45-60 min conversation
Topics Markets, tech, politics, macro trends Business ideas, startups, tactics
Vibe Insider takes, debating billionaires Brainstorming friends, idea generation
Frequency Weekly (Saturdays) 2-3x per week
Best For Macro understanding, VC/startup world Idea inspiration, tactical execution

📊 All-In Podcast Deep Dive

The Hosts

  • Chamath Palihapitiya: Social Capital founder, former Facebook exec
  • Jason Calacanis: Angel investor (Uber, Robinhood), "Moderator"
  • David Sacks: Craft Ventures, PayPal mafia, enterprise SaaS expert
  • David Friedberg: The Production Board, science/climate investor

What Makes All-In Unique

✅ Strengths:

  • Insider perspective: These guys manage billions and know founders/CEOs personally
  • Macro analysis: Deep dives on markets, Fed policy, geopolitics
  • Debate format: Four smart people disagree—you learn from the arguments
  • Political diversity: Rare mix of left/libertarian/center perspectives in tech
  • Science segments: Friedberg brings biotech/climate depth

❌ Weaknesses:

  • Long episodes: 2 hours is a commitment
  • Billionaire bubble: Sometimes out of touch with median listener
  • Politics creep: Increasingly political (some love it, some don't)
  • Inside jokes: "Besties" humor can feel cliquish
  • Weekly only: Just one episode per week

Typical All-In Episode Structure

  1. Opening banter (5-10 min): Jokes, poker stories, lifestyle
  2. Markets/Economy (20-30 min): Fed, inflation, tech stocks
  3. Tech topic (20-30 min): AI, crypto, Big Tech regulation
  4. Politics/Policy (30-40 min): Elections, free speech, governance
  5. Science corner (10-20 min): Friedberg on climate/biotech
  6. Closing (5-10 min): Rapid takes, sponsor thank-yous

Who All-In Is For

  • VCs, founders, tech employees wanting insider context
  • Investors tracking macro trends and market sentiment
  • People who enjoy smart debate and multiple viewpoints
  • Those comfortable with 2-hour deep dives

💡 My First Million Deep Dive

The Hosts

  • Shaan Puri: Ex-Twitch, sold Bebo to Twitch, serial entrepreneur
  • Sam Parr: Founded The Hustle (sold to HubSpot), copywriter

What Makes MFM Unique

✅ Strengths:

  • Idea generation: Constant stream of business ideas you could actually start
  • Tactical insights: How to validate ideas, find customers, build businesses
  • Relatable scale: Focus on $1M-$100M businesses, not just unicorns
  • Storytelling: Fascinating profiles of unknown successful businesses
  • Frequent episodes: 2-3 per week keeps content fresh
  • Energy: Enthusiastic, positive, "anything is possible" vibe

❌ Weaknesses:

  • Less depth: Stays surface-level compared to All-In's analysis
  • Idea overload: So many ideas, hard to know which to pursue
  • Hype factor: Sometimes oversells concepts or opportunities
  • Repetitive format: "Here's a cool business" formula can feel same-y

Typical MFM Episode Structure

  1. Opening hook (2-5 min): Interesting business or person they discovered
  2. Deep dive (15-25 min): How this business works, revenue model, founder story
  3. Idea riff (10-20 min): "What if we did this..." brainstorming
  4. Trends section (5-15 min): What's working now, opportunities they see
  5. Personal stories (5-10 min): Their own business experiences

Who MFM Is For

  • Aspiring entrepreneurs looking for ideas and validation
  • Small business owners seeking growth tactics
  • Side hustlers wanting inspiration and frameworks
  • Anyone energized by possibility and creativity

🎯 Head-to-Head: When to Choose Each

Choose All-In If You Want...

  • Macro understanding: Fed policy, market cycles, geopolitics
  • Insider context: What VCs and billionaires actually think
  • Deep analysis: 30-minute debates on single topics
  • Multiple perspectives: Four smart people disagree productively
  • Tech/VC world insight: Silicon Valley from the inside

Choose My First Million If You Want...

  • Business ideas: Actionable concepts you could start tomorrow
  • Tactical advice: How to validate, launch, grow businesses
  • Inspiration: Stories of scrappy entrepreneurs who made it
  • Frequent content: 2-3 episodes per week
  • Shorter episodes: 45-60 min fits more schedules

💼 Content Comparison

Example Topics

All-In might cover:

  • "Should the Fed raise rates? Chamath vs Sacks debate"
  • "China-Taiwan tensions and tech supply chains"
  • "AI regulation: Free market vs government intervention"
  • "Climate tech investment landscape"

MFM might cover:

  • "This guy made $20M selling plants online - here's how"
  • "5 boring businesses printing money right now"
  • "How to validate your startup idea in 48 hours"
  • "The newsletter business model breakdown"

🎧 Listening Experience

All-In

Best listened to: Weekend long drive/walk, full attention

Requires: Context on current events, some financial literacy

Energy level: Intense debates, serious topics

Takeaway: Deeper understanding of how the world works

My First Million

Best listened to: Commute, workout, background work

Requires: Basic business interest, entrepreneurial mindset

Energy level: Upbeat, enthusiastic, casual

Takeaway: Ideas and inspiration to act on

🏆 Verdict: Can You Listen to Both?

Yes! They complement each other perfectly:

  • All-In gives you the macro context and market understanding
  • MFM gives you the ideas and tactical execution

Recommended Combo

Saturday/Sunday: All-In (understand the landscape)

Monday/Wednesday/Friday: MFM (generate ideas within that landscape)

You'll understand both the big picture (All-In) and the actionable opportunities (MFM).

Similar Alternatives

If you like All-In's depth:

  • Acquired (business deep dives)
  • Invest Like the Best (investor interviews)
  • The Prof G Pod (Scott Galloway on markets + tech)

If you like MFM's idea generation:

  • Foundr (entrepreneur interviews)
  • How I Built This (founder stories)
  • Indie Hackers (bootstrapped founders)

Final Recommendation

Start with My First Million if you're new to business podcasts. It's more accessible, actionable, and energizing.

Graduate to All-In when you want deeper understanding of markets, tech trends, and macro forces shaping business.

Listen to both if you're serious about entrepreneurship or investing. The combination gives you ideas (MFM) and context (All-In).
