All-In vs My First Million: Which Business Podcast is Better? (2026)

A detailed comparison of two top tech/business podcasts—which one should you listen to?

Updated February 25, 2026

Both All-In and My First Million are top-tier business podcasts, but they serve different audiences and offer distinct experiences. Here's everything you need to know to choose which one (or both) to add to your rotation.

Quick Comparison

All-In: Tech investors debate markets, technology, and politics. Formal, polished, macro-focused.

My First Million: Entrepreneurs discuss business ideas and strategies. Casual, energetic, micro-focused.

All-In Podcast

The Hosts

  • Chamath Palihapitiya – Former Facebook exec, venture capitalist (Social Capital)
  • Jason Calacanis – Angel investor, founder of Launch
  • David Sacks – Former PayPal COO, Craft Ventures
  • David Friedberg – Founder of The Production Board, former Google executive

Four experienced investors with different perspectives—leads to great debates.

Format & Topics

  • Weekly roundtable discussion (90-120 minutes)
  • Topics: Technology, markets, politics, economics, science
  • Tone: Intellectual, sometimes heated debates, formal production
  • Structure: Segmented topics, the "besties" banter between topics

Strengths

  • Macro perspective – Understanding markets, technology trends, policy
  • Diverse viewpoints – The hosts often disagree, providing multiple angles
  • High-level insights – These are active investors managing billions
  • Current events analysis – Weekly format keeps pace with news
  • Production quality – Professional, polished, well-edited

Weaknesses

  • Can get political (some listeners love this, others don't)
  • Less actionable for small business owners
  • Episodes are long (90-120 min)
  • Can feel like watching billionaires talk about billionaire problems

Best for: People interested in macro trends, markets, and high-level technology strategy.

My First Million Podcast

The Hosts

  • Shaan Puri – Former founder (Bebo, Monkey Inferno), investor
  • Sam Parr – Founder of The Hustle (sold to HubSpot)

Two entrepreneurs who've built and sold companies—practical experience with startups.

Format & Topics

  • 2-3x per week episodes (45-90 minutes)
  • Topics: Business ideas, entrepreneurship, specific companies/founders
  • Tone: Casual, energetic, conversational
  • Structure: Brainstorming sessions, interviews, business breakdowns

Strengths

  • Actionable ideas – Specific business models you can study or copy
  • Entrepreneurial mindset – How to spot opportunities, think differently
  • Great chemistry – Shaan and Sam's banter is entertaining
  • Accessible – More relatable than billionaire investor talk
  • Idea generation – Sparks creativity and entrepreneurial thinking

Weaknesses

  • Less macro/policy discussion
  • Can be repetitive (business idea brainstorms follow similar patterns)
  • Some ideas are aspirational but not practical
  • Less formal structure—sometimes rambles

Best for: Aspiring entrepreneurs, people interested in business ideas and startup tactics.

Head-to-Head Comparison

Topic Coverage

All-In wins for: Markets, policy, macro technology trends, current events

My First Million wins for: Specific business ideas, entrepreneurial tactics, startup stories

Actionability

All-In: High-level insights, useful for understanding context but less immediately actionable

My First Million: Highly actionable—specific business models, frameworks, and strategies

Entertainment Value

Tie. Both are highly entertaining. All-In has passionate debates; MFM has great banter and energy.

Production Quality

All-In wins. More polished, professional production. MFM is more raw and conversational.

Time Commitment

All-In: Weekly (90-120 min) = manageable
MFM: 2-3x week (45-90 min each) = more content to keep up with

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose All-In if you:

  • Want to understand markets and macro trends
  • Enjoy intellectual debates and multiple perspectives
  • Are interested in policy, technology, and economics
  • Prefer weekly listening (not daily)

Choose My First Million if you:

  • Want actionable business ideas you can study or pursue
  • Are interested in entrepreneurship and startups
  • Prefer casual, high-energy conversations
  • Want more frequent episodes

Can You Listen to Both?

Absolutely. Many people do. They serve different purposes:

  • All-In on Fridays – Weekly macro/markets catch-up
  • MFM 2-3x week – Entrepreneurial inspiration and ideas

They complement each other well—one for strategy, one for tactics.

Final Thoughts

All-In and My First Million are both excellent business podcasts, but they cater to different needs. All-In is for understanding the big picture—markets, trends, and macro strategy. My First Million is for entrepreneurs who want specific ideas and tactics.

If you can only choose one, pick based on your current goals. If you're building a business, start with MFM. If you're investing or want to understand the world, start with All-In. Better yet, listen to both and get the full picture.
