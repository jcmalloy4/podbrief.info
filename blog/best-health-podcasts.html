💡 How to Apply Health Podcast Knowledge

  1. Implement one protocol at a time - Don't try everything at once. Pick one strategy, test for 4-8 weeks, measure results.
  2. Browse summaries for your current health goal - Use PodBrief to find episodes about specific topics (sleep, fat loss, stress, etc.)
  3. Prioritize evidence-based content - Look for MDs, PhDs, and researchers with published work. Avoid "wellness influencers" without credentials.
  4. Track your results - Health podcasts provide strategies. You provide the data. Track sleep, energy, performance, bloodwork.
  5. Consult your doctor - Podcasts are education, not medical advice. Discuss protocols with your healthcare provider.

⚠️ Health Podcast Red Flags

Be skeptical when podcasts:

  • Promote miracle cures or quick fixes
  • Sell supplements without disclosing conflicts of interest
  • Dismiss mainstream medicine entirely
  • Cherry-pick studies to support pre-existing beliefs
  • Make absolute claims ("carbs are poison," "meat causes cancer")

Trust podcasts that:

  • Cite peer-reviewed research
  • Acknowledge uncertainty and limitations
  • Present nuanced, individualized approaches
  • Disclose sponsorships and conflicts of interest
  • Update recommendations when new research emerges
