Best Podcasts for Long Flights 2026 - Travel Entertainment

Published February 24, 2026

Long flights can feel endless, but the right podcast can make hours disappear. Whether you're crossing the Atlantic or heading to Asia, these are the best podcasts for long flights—engaging, binge-worthy, and perfect for air travel.

What Makes a Great Flight Podcast?

  • Engaging storytelling: Keeps you hooked for hours without needing to see anything
  • Multiple episodes: You can binge an entire series or season
  • Good pacing: Not too intense (you might want to sleep), not too boring
  • Audio-only friendly: No need for show notes or visuals

Best Podcasts for Long Flights by Type

Best True Crime for Flights

Serial

The podcast that started the true crime boom. Serial investigates one case per season with deep, episodic storytelling. Perfect for binging an entire season during a long flight.

Flight time: 8-10 hours per season

S-Town

From the Serial team, this wild story starts with a murder investigation and goes places you won't expect. Seven episodes that tell one complete, unforgettable story.

Flight time: 7 hours

Dr. Death

Medical malpractice thriller that reads like fiction but is terrifyingly real. Multiple seasons, each covering different cases.

Flight time: 6-8 hours per season

Best Storytelling Podcasts for Flights

This American Life

The gold standard of narrative podcasts. This American Life tells compelling stories about ordinary people. Download 10+ episodes and you're set for any flight.

Flight time: 1 hour per episode, endless backlog

Radiolab

Radiolab explores scientific and philosophical questions with incredible sound design. Episodes range from 20 minutes to an hour.

Flight time: Customizable based on your flight length

The Moth

True stories told live without notes. The Moth features people from all walks of life sharing pivotal moments. Each episode contains multiple stories.

Flight time: 1 hour per episode

Best Comedy for Flights

SmartLess

SmartLess features Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes interviewing celebrities. Light, funny, and engaging—perfect when you want entertainment without heavy thinking.

Flight time: 1 hour per episode

Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend

Conan interviews celebrities with his signature humor. These episodes are consistently funny and uplifting—great for travel mood.

Flight time: 1 hour per episode

Best Interview Podcasts for Flights

Lex Fridman Podcast

Long-form conversations with scientists, philosophers, and thinkers. Lex Fridman episodes run 2-4 hours—perfect for international flights.

Flight time: 2-4 hours per episode

The Tim Ferriss Show

Tim Ferriss extracts tactics and routines from world-class performers. Episodes are long and packed with value.

Flight time: 2-3 hours per episode

WTF with Marc Maron

Intimate conversations with comedians, actors, and musicians. Maron's authenticity creates memorable moments.

Flight time: 1.5 hours per episode

Best Educational Podcasts for Flights

Hardcore History

Dan Carlin's deep dives into historical events run 3-6 hours per episode. One episode could cover your entire transatlantic flight.

Flight time: 3-6 hours per episode

Huberman Lab

Huberman Lab teaches neuroscience and health optimization. Dense content perfect for learning during travel.

Flight time: 2-3 hours per episode

99% Invisible

Design and architecture stories that reveal the hidden world around us. Short episodes (20-40 minutes) you can chain together.

Flight time: 20-40 minutes per episode

Best Business Podcasts for Flights

Acquired

Acquired tells the full story of iconic companies. Episodes run 3+ hours—perfect for long flights when you want to learn.

Flight time: 3-5 hours per episode

How I Built This

Guy Raz interviews founders about building companies. Inspiring and entertaining—great travel listening.

Flight time: 45 minutes per episode

Best Limited Series for Flights

Slow Burn

Each season explores a major political scandal (Watergate, Clinton impeachment, etc.) with fresh reporting and storytelling.

Flight time: 6-8 hours per season

The Habitat

Six strangers spend a year in a dome simulating Mars. Fascinating social experiment told in real-time.

Flight time: 5 hours

Bear Brook

Investigative journalists solve a decades-old cold case using genealogy. Gripping from start to finish.

Flight time: 8 hours

How to Build Your Flight Playlist

For Short Flights (1-3 hours):

  • 3-4 episodes of This American Life
  • 2-3 SmartLess or Conan episodes
  • Multiple 99% Invisible episodes

For Medium Flights (4-7 hours):

  • Full season of Serial or Dr. Death
  • 2-3 Tim Ferriss or Lex Fridman episodes
  • One Hardcore History episode
  • Multiple interview podcasts

For Long International Flights (8+ hours):

  • Entire limited series (S-Town, Slow Burn)
  • 3-4 Lex Fridman episodes
  • 2 Hardcore History episodes
  • Full season of Serial plus extras
  • Mix of long and short episodes for variety

Pro Tips for Flight Podcast Listening

  • Download in advance: Don't rely on airplane WiFi
  • Bring backup episodes: Download 2x what you think you'll need
  • Mix content types: Combine heavy (educational) with light (comedy)
  • Check episode lengths: Match content to your remaining flight time
  • Use good headphones: Noise-canceling makes a huge difference
  • Start with something engaging: Help distract from takeoff anxiety
