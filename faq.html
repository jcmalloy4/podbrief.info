General

What is PodBrief and how does it work?

+

PodBrief is an AI-powered podcast app that helps you stay on top of your favorite shows with smart, concise briefings. Instead of spending hours listening to every episode, you can quickly read AI-generated summaries that capture the key insights, main topics, and important takeaways.

Using advanced AI technology, PodBrief analyzes full podcast episodes to create accurate and meaningful summaries that respect the original context and intent of the hosts. Here’s how it works:

  • Subscribe to podcasts: Add your favorite shows to your feed
  • Daily feed: See all new episodes in one scrollable feed
  • AI briefings (AI summaries): Get summaries with key takeaways and deep dives into episode topics
  • Smart listening: Decide what to listen to based on the brief, or skip ahead

PodBrief blends a podcast player with AI-driven briefings and summaries, helping you stay informed, save time, and listen with purpose.

What makes PodBrief different from other podcast apps?

+

PodBrief is built for people who love podcasts but struggle to keep up with all their shows. Unlike traditional podcast apps that only organize your subscriptions, PodBrief gives you:

  • AI Briefings (AI summaries): Instant summaries so you know what's worth listening to
  • Daily Feed: All your new episodes in one unified, easy-to-scan feed
  • PodBrief saves time: PodBrief saves time by turning long episodes into smart briefs
  • Better Discovery: Follow more podcasts without feeling overwhelmed

We combine the best of traditional podcast players with modern AI assistance and episode summaries to help you get more value from every minute you spend listening.

AI Briefings

What information is included in a briefing?

+

Each PodBrief briefing gives you a clear overview of an episode so you can quickly understand what it’s about and decide how to listen. Here’s what you’ll find:

  • Key Takeaways: A concise breakdown of the full episode, summarized into easy-to-scan bullet points.
  • Deep Dives: Detailed sections that explore each topic covered in the episode, along with their key insights.
  • Smart Listening: Skip straight to the parts you care about most. In the Deep Dives section, you can jump directly to that segment of the episode.
  • Why You Should Listen: A short overview of who will get the most value from the episode, helping you decide if it’s worth a full listen.

How accurate are the AI briefings?

+

Our AI briefings are designed to be highly accurate and true to the original content. We use state-of-the-art language models that have been specifically tuned for podcast content analysis.

However, AI-generated summaries should be viewed as helpful guides rather than perfect transcriptions. They capture the essence and key points of episodes, but may not include every detail. We recommend listening to the full episode for topics that require deep understanding or nuanced context.

Are briefings available for all podcasts?

+

PodBrief can create briefings for any podcast that’s publicly available, no matter how big or small the show.

Once you favorite a podcast, PodBrief automatically generates briefings for new episodes as they’re released. That way, when they appear in your daily feed, the briefs are ready for you to read right away.

Briefing generation may take a few minutes for newly released episodes, but once complete, they’re instantly available for all users who follow that show.

Can I share briefings with friends?

+

Yes! PodBrief makes it easy to share episode briefings with friends, colleagues, or on social media. When you share a briefing link, it displays a rich preview with the podcast artwork, episode title, and a clip of the brief, perfect for iMessage, Twitter, or any other platform.

Recipients can click the link to view the full briefing or open the episode directly in the PodBrief app.
